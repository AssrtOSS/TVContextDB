## S02E02 Summary — The Gentlemen

### Episode Plot
Eddie and Susie host a high-stakes shooting party at Halstead Manor to advance two agendas: secure Lord Hawthorne's backing for Eddie's hereditary peerage (needed to kill the marijuana decriminalization bill in the Lords), and resolve a crisis in their Italian expansion. Their Italian fixer, Francesco "Cico" Maldini, reveals that Mafia boss Marco Moretti (the Contessa's godfather) has discovered their cannabis farms under aristocratic palazzos and demands €40M/year. Cico proposes buying a Botticelli painting (€6M) to appease him. At the Beaumont's auction, Susie gets caught in competitive bidding and drives the price to £16M. Bobby, consulting his "Bannik" bathhouse-spirit ritual from prison, confirms the purchase. Post-auction, the painting is nearly stolen by thieves hired by art dealer Robert Brooks — on Cico's orders. Eddie and Susie intercept the thieves, confront Cico, and call Moretti directly. Moretti arrives, sees through Cico's double-cross (Cico owned the painting, inflated the price, planned to steal it back), has Enzo execute Cico, and agrees to a new deal: £10M/month for Moretti's protection and support expanding into southern Italy.
