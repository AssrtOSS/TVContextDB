## S01E05

## S01E05 — Episode Summary

### Plot

Neagley has been drugged with Tranqlara (via milk Annika's team laced at her apartment). The drug stimulates the amygdala, surfacing traumatic childhood memories. Neagley hallucinates violently, is drawn toward light sources, and nearly walks into traffic. Hudson breaks down the bathroom door and keeps her safe. Meanwhile, Keno escapes the farm's burn pit after being dumped among dismembered human remains, but has no photos or physical evidence.

The team pieces together that the barn sits atop a decommissioned 1965 government missile silo, purchased at auction by Lawrence Cole ~30 years ago and converted into a climate-controlled underground lab. Rye flour found at the farm links to ergot cultivation — the fungus used to synthesize LSD/psychedelics — which is nearly impossible to grow outside a sealed lab. Neagley deduces that Pierce Woodrow (Invection Pharmaceuticals) is not the top of the chain; someone above him bankrolls the operation through a side agreement outside Invection's public-company books.

Neagley confronts her dying father. Under the drug's influence she has recovered a childhood memory: her father, a cop, killed a man in a basement boiler room while young Frances watched. He screamed "No one ever touches you!" — the origin of her lifelong haphephobia. Her father denies everything and throws her out, choosing to "die with his secrets."

Keno and Renée coordinate an extraction plan for the next day's farmers market, but discover Mandy has already been moved to the "sister farm" — too late to save her through that channel. Keno refuses to leave without Mandy.

The episode ends with Neagley deciding to dose Pierce with Tranqlara herself to invade his mind the way he invaded hers, bypassing traditional interrogation.

### Important Character Actions

- **Annika** (operative): reports killing York to her unnamed employer; her team tries to run Neagley down but aborts to preserve the "accident" narrative. Speaks Swedish in the car.
- **Pierce Woodrow**: presents Tranqlara to Invection's board and a secret superior ("sir" / Rawlings). Board threatens to cut funding and audit by Jan 1 if no human-trial results. The superior doesn't care if the drug works — only if it makes money.
- **Hudson Riley**: kicks in the door to save Neagley; asks his Narcotics contact about rye flour; argues they cannot tell Nora York about her husband's death yet (Neagley looks guilty — York's DNA is on her apartment floorboards). Suggests Renée tear up and dispose of the bloodstained floor.
- **Renée Birdwhistle**: tracks York's phone ping; relays Keno's situation; believes Keno's testimony; helps coordinate extraction plan.
- **Keno**: escapes the burn pit, reveals human remains being incinerated; learns his criminal record makes his testimony worthless to a judge; insists on rescuing Mandy.
- **Neagley**: confronts her father about the childhood murder memory; decides to use Tranqlara on Pierce.

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Tranqlara** — Invection's psychedelic drug: 1 oz clear liquid in any beverage, 3×/week for 6 weeks; stimulates amygdala, surfaces traumatic memories; variant causes compulsive attraction to light
- **Invection Pharmaceuticals** — publicly traded company; Pierce works here; board is a "veil of legitimacy"
- **The Project** — what the cult calls its operation
- **Sister farm** — secondary location where advanced "probationers" (like Mandy) are sent
- **Probationer / Neophyte** — farm hierarchy ranks; probationers have restricted off-site access
- **Annika** — lead operative hunting Neagley; reports to Pierce's unnamed superior
- **Judith** — farm assignment manager who controls market rotations
- **Rawlings** — Pierce's contact; had mics on during the board meeting
- **Jerry O'Connell** — celebrity spokesperson filming Tranqlara infomercial (planned Super Bowl ad)
- **"Bob and Carol"** — Keno's invented names for his unknown biological parents
- **Ergot** — rye-grown fungus for synthesizing psychedelics; requires underground temp-controlled lab

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- York is dead in Neagley's apartment; his blood is on the floorboards. Riley has Neagley's house keys and plans to destroy the evidence.
- Neagley's haphephobia origin: her cop father killed a man in front of her as a child and conditioned her with "no one ever touches you."
- Neagley's father is alive but terminally ill (referenced as "deathbed").
- Tommy Crane was dosed with high-dose psychedelics and set up to die via headlights (same method tried on Neagley).
- The farm cult targets vulnerable people (runaways, transients, DV survivors) as lab subjects.
- Invection's board will cut Tranqlara funding and launch an SEC/FBI audit on Jan 1 if no human-trial results appear.
- The unnamed superior bankrolls the operation through back-channel capital and threatens Pierce if it fails.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later

- **Hudson's "Joseph Tillman" story**: fabricates an elaborate analogy about a vet, a constipated cow named Daisy, and the Great Chicago Fire — then admits he made it up entirely. Uses it to tell Neagley York's death isn't her fault. The real fire origin (illegal card game knocked over a lantern, blamed on O'Leary's cow to fuel anti-Irish sentiment) is also delivered as a punchline-within-a-punchline.
- **Keno's "Bob and Carol"**: recurring bit — the placeholder names for his absent parents, invoked in moments of bitterness about his lack of credibility.
- **Tranqlara infomercial**: the satirical ad-within-a-show format ("Find your tranquility. Find your clarity.") recurs; Jerry O'Connell's cameo is self-aware.
- **"Ham candle" callback**: Keno's earlier euphemism for the man who smashed his genitals while high, referenced in the evidence discussion.

### Unresolved Threads

- Who is Pierce's unnamed superior / "sir"? The person who bankrolls everything and threatens Pierce.
- Mandy has been moved to the "sister farm" — location unknown; Keno refuses to leave without her.
- Neagley's plan to dose Pierce with Tranqlara — will it work, and what will she learn?
- Neagley's father's full secret — he was a cop who killed someone with two partners; Frances witnessed it. What was the context?
- Nora York still doesn't know her husband is dead; she's calling repeatedly and has contacted police.
- No physical evidence of the farm's crimes — Keno's testimony alone won't hold up; extraction of evidence remains the core obstacle.
- Invection's Jan 1 deadline for human-trial results creates a ticking clock for all parties.
- Who is the ultimate client that both Annika and Pierce serve ("We both work for the same person")?
- The "sister farm" — its location, purpose, and what happens to people sent there.

## S01E02

# Neagley S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Neagley and Detective Hudson Riley investigate the murder of Tommy Crane, an Echo Canyon resident and Neagley's old friend. They discover Tommy was a bagman collecting for illegal operations, and his killer had an Echo Canyon tattoo with teeth pulled post-mortem. The investigation leads to a pawnshop where the killer sold gold fillings from victims' teeth. Meanwhile, Neagley receives an envelope from Tommy (postmarked the day before his death) containing rye flour — a deliberate message. Keno goes undercover at Echo Canyon farm, posing as a homeless addict, and is accepted by Judith Gray and Mason. Lawrence Cole, the charismatic but sinister leader, orders a hit on Neagley through hitmen Davos and Ford. Pierce Woodrow, a corrupt federal official on Cole's payroll, tries to renegotiate his bribe; Cole kills him with a digitalis patch disguised as a heart attack. The episode ends with Keno inside Echo Canyon, Neagley and Riley reluctantly formalizing their partnership, and Cole aware of Neagley's investigation.

## Important Character Actions
- **Neagley**: Investigates Tommy's death with Riley; visits her dying father, revealing her haphephobia began at age 5 (not infancy); deduces Tommy sent flour as a clue about Echo Canyon's bakery/food operations; accepts Keno as undercover asset.
- **Riley (Hudson)**: Partners with Neagley despite family tension (cousins, "share a last name"); threatens to arrest her if she reveals their connection; runs pawnshop sting using "two car doors" tactic from his father.
- **Keno**: Infiltrates Echo Canyon by staging a leak that ruins Stukov Brothers' cocaine stash; creates track marks with irritant to sell addict cover; gains entry after proving desperation.
- **Lawrence Cole**: Orders Neagley's assassination; kills Woodrow when he demands more money; presents as "visionary" and "portal" to followers.
- **Judith Gray**: Operations director at Echo Canyon; recruits Keno; aware of mail surveillance and secret burials (Lulu).
- **Ford & Davos**: Hitmen for Echo Canyon; Ford is the muscle (broke, not bright), Davos the brains (sports coat, leads interactions).

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Echo Canyon** — cult-like recovery farm/front; takes "junkies, homeless, the wayward"; mail searched; residents who join "the Project" never return
- **The Project** — secret program at sister farm; participants get private dorms; rumored to involve sexual exploitation by Cole
- **Davos & Ford** — hitmen aliases (first/last names unknown)
- **Pierce Woodrow** — corrupt federal official (agency unspecified) taking bribes from Cole
- **Stukov Brothers** — drug dealers hiding cocaine in walls
- **Quill Labs** — forensic lab Neagley uses (off-book)
- **Grellong & Rome** — high-end law firm representing Ford
- **Charlie Halle** — bagman colleague of Tommy, also murdered
- **Lulu** — Echo Canyon resident who died of fentanyl-laced speed; buried secretly on western acreage; had an uncle

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Neagley and Riley are **cousins** (share last name); Riley: "I don't care we share a last name" / "I'll see you at Thanksgiving, asshole"
- Neagley's **haphephobia** onset: age 5 (not birth); father lies about not remembering when it started; father dying (chemo/meds, foggy memory)
- **The 110th** connection: Riley knows Neagley's military background; "Not trained like me" — Neagley's investigative methods exceed standard police work
- Tommy Crane was Neagley's **best friend**; hadn't seen him in years; father notes: "Tommy was your best friend, and you haven't even seen him in years"
- Neagley's mother died when she was young; father: "You've been afraid of being alone ever since your mother died"
- Keno: foster kid, group homes; living in abandoned house for a year; stole water/electric; owes Stukov Brothers $150K for ruined cocaine
