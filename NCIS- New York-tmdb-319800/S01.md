## S01E01

# NCIS: New York — S01E01

## Plot
Navy reservist Jack Halloway, 36, a tugboat captain in Gerritsen expecting a daughter, is initially ruled a probable suicide by NYPD. Sam Hanna — former Navy SEAL, now a senior NCIS special agent — missed three panicked voicemails from Jack (the godson of Sam's late SEAL teammate) and arrives at ASAC Robyn Wells's office requesting an NCIS reciprocal investigation. Wells approves one case and pairs him with Nick Schaeffer.

Jimmy (NYC ME) confirms staging: the bullet entered at a downward off-angle inconsistent with a self-inflicted shot from Jack's dominant hand. Addy and Wyatt at the Central Park bench recover the killer's brass casing — the assailant fired once, planted the gun, and discharged a second shot into the air to leave residue on Jack's right hand. Sean, working an outdoor terminal, pulls a grainy halal-cart witness image showing a fleeing figure in a hoodie whose hat logo he identifies as the Norfolk Eels (Minor League Baseball mascot). The Norfolk connection loops to Jack's first mate Dave Ballerini, originally from Norfolk, VA.

Sam and Nick interview Jack's wife Janice, who is visibly nervous about the boat. They board the tug at Red Hook Pier North — registered to an operating company, not the Halloways. Inside a newly locked box, ~$7,000 cash. Nick pushes a smuggling theory (drugs, then people): a shipping boat fakes distress, the tug runs out, the contraband rides back to port without a manifest. Sean ties the Norfolk Eels hat to Ballerini; the team picks him up.

Ballerini breaks under interrogation: he has been catfished by a "girlfriend" named Katrina supposedly held in Minsk, and a British mercenary forced him to ferry two operatives onto Jack's boat. He met the "British commando" in the park only to scare Jack off — Chilton pulled the trigger. The van he rented for them racked three parking tickets on Broadway in Bushwick, leading to a vermin-closed club called Saint Violet (a cousin's place). The team raids it, finding surveillance of a SoHo kidnapping-in-progress: gunmen snatch a woman in a brown dress from a birthday brunch.

The team identifies the target as Alina Azarin, a recent NYU grad born in Russia, daughter of Saint Petersburg mining oligarch Danill Azarin, who is feuding with rival oligarch Pavel Dronin over a billion-dollar mining site. Dronin's private-security hire is Ian Chilton — ex-SAS, kicked out a decade ago after eight years for executing a translator in Kabul, now a freelance operator across Africa, Malaysia, and Eastern Europe.

With no time for backup (Robyn's order), Sam overrules and the team ambushes the returning van in Bushwick. Flash-bang breach; Sam and Nick take down the mercs, Addy talks Alina down. Sam is given Jack's folded flag in a quiet memorial scene. Nick then drives Sam to a Brooklyn overlook (his late father's old spot) and pitches him to formally rejoin the team. Sam resists — "I'm a stranger now" — citing his preference for covert assignments and his finance career that collapsed twice. Nick appeals to Sam's sense of duty to the people and places that made him. Sam relents with "Let's see how it goes"; the two head for soul food breakfast.

## Important character actions
- Sam Hanna — emotionally compromised but insists on leading the case; overrules Robyn to breach without backup; receives Jack's flag; opens to coming home.
- Nick Schaeffer — delivers the interrogation of Ballerini with a steadily escalating "mass murderer" riff; presses Sam to stay.
- Addy Ross — does the bench reconstruction with Wyatt, talks Ballerini into honesty at his home ("exposure therapy"), calms Alina post-rescue.
- Wyatt Sellers ("Milkman") — runs bench forensics with Addy; flanks the breach.
- Sean Sullivan — pulls the parking-ticket trail and the Norfolk Eels ID; runs the cloud dump on Jack's missing boat camera showing the two mercs stripping gear before slipping into the harbor.
- ASAC Robyn Wells — opens the reciprocal probe, signs the warrant, attempts to enforce "wait for backup."
- Janice Halloway — confirms Jack was stressed; tells a suspect three calls to Sam.
- Dave Ballerini — first mate turned coerced mule; pitches the catfish story; identifies Chilton and Saint Violet.
- Ian Chilton — British ex-SAS killer; Chilton-shoots-Jack perp.
- Alina Azarin — kidnap target; speaks for more than one line.
- Jimmy (ME) — confirms staged suicide.

## Continuity facts worth remembering
- Sam grew up in Queens; Nick in Brooklyn (Bay Ridge), family long police/fire. Sam's history in L.A. (OSP, Callen, Hetty) is referenced only by omission here — he is introduced by reputation only. Sam has been on special-assignment / covert-ops work for years; he is not a permanent field-office agent and dislikes office-bound postings. He tried finance twice and lost both times.
- Sam's promise to Jack's late SEAL father is the emotional anchor of the episode and the reason he stays on the case.
- The tugboat is owned by an operating company, not the Halloways; that corporate front is unexplained.
- Sean is on an ongoing deal: his hacking of the NYPD police pension fund bought him NCIS technical-operator work instead of prison. That debt is the team's leverage over him.
- Ballerini's cap is the "Norfolk Eels" Minor League Baseball hat — reappearances of the cap will always ID him.
- Nick was twice rejected proposing with a cheap ring he was trying to sell in the Diamond District; he ends up partnered with Sam anyway.
- The bench plaque ("To my bride. You're my today and all of my tomorrows") becomes a recurring image for the Halloway case.

## S01E02

## Plot

Cold open: Ben Gelman proposes to girlfriend Corinne in the family jewelry-store vault (Greenpoint, family business since 1950). Four masked robbers blow through the floor with military C-4; Corinne escapes, Ben is caught in the blast. Sam and Nick are assigned to the case by ASAC Robyn Wells. A four-man crew used coded microplastics traced to Naval Weapons Station Earle — making it a federal NCIS matter. Addy/Wyatt head to the armory; Sam/Nick interview Corinne at 51st Street Hospital.

Ben dies (age 22), escalating the heist to a homicide. Sam and Nick walk the crime scene: water bag to focus the blast, cardboard stacked to catch debris, twelve minutes in-and-out. The getaway van is registered to Wallshill Commercial Cleaning and found parked near a factory in Long Island City — keys still in ignition, three cigarettes (two brands), and Carlos and Vilma Molina (cleaning-crew husband/wife) bound alive in back. The crew left witnesses alive instead of killing them — a deviation from the plan.

At the armory, Senior Chief Leonard and PO2 Joaquin Ricci show Wyatt/Addy the inventory. Wyatt deduces falsified paperwork as the only way C-4 was skimmed. Joaquin ducks out citing a family emergency and bolts. Sam/Nick reach the Ricci family home; brother Matteo (accountant at Castle Hill & Seward) is on his lunch break. Their parents died when Joaquin was 12 (car accident); Matteo stepped up at 19 but never reached his brother, who latched onto a father figure: high-school football coach Alex Villante. The jewelry-store manager card from the security company reads Alex Villante — and a team photo shows him as Joaquin's coach.

The crew takes shape: Villante is the ringleader, Joaquin the demo expert (he stole and set the C-4), Roderick Gaines and Michael Norton (assault/burglary priors) are muscle. Matteo is in over his head — banned from sports-betting apps, owes Villante $80K, and Villante put a gun to his head to force Joaquin to cooperate. Nick finds a workbook at Villante's place with recon for the next target (dated today) plus forged passports. Sean Sullivan decodes "PC" as Proper Care Marijuana Dispensary on Bruckner Boulevard — federal banking restrictions keep dispensaries cash-heavy.

Simultaneous raid: Addy/Wyatt take Gaines and Norton; Sam/Nick take Villante. Nick goes in alone (slow backup) and takes fire in the alley, eventually pinning one suspect. Addy/Wyatt chase Villante east in the van; he crashes and is cuffed. Sam corners Joaquin at a dead end. Joaquin confesses he let Corinne go and refused to kill the Molinas — Villante made him ditch the van. Sam sees himself in the boy and walks him out in handcuffs. Nick took a concussion and possible internal bleeding in his car crash during the chase.

## Important character actions

- Sam Hanna: emotional investment in Joaquin — recognizes a kid from his own neighborhood shaped by absent fathers and crooked mentors. Promises Joaquin "the world will have context."
- Nick Schaeffer: solo entry into the dispensary; gets pinned down, then crashes the car during the Villante pursuit; ends up ambulance-bound with a concussion.
- Addy Ross: bags the cigarette evidence, calls EMS for the Molinas, helps take Villante down.
- Wyatt Sellers: spots the falsified-paperwork angle at the armory; banter with Addy about dating profiles ("plaid boy").
- Robyn Wells: runs point, briefs the team, orders the simultaneous takedown.
- Sean Sullivan: identifies the van, decodes "PC," traces the dispensary.
- Matteo Ricci: turns on Villante in interrogation; still claims no knowledge of Joaquin's location.
