## S01E01

# S01E01 Summary — *It Gets Worse*

## Episode Plot
Ethan (Leo Reich) opens in therapy, narrating his "easy life" but crushing hopelessness — triggered by their landlord selling the flat. The trio (Ethan, Abi, Sam) must vacate by tonight; the new place isn't ready until the 27th (~3–4 weeks), so they plan to crash with Jesse, Ethan's hot-but-awful casual partner. Ethan drilled a hole in the wall trying to hang a photo; they need it fixed to get their deposit back. Abi reveals she's moving in with her boyfriend Tom, blowing up the new flat contract (needs three incomes). A disastrous night at club "Slut Pig" ends in a screaming row: Abi feels pushed around; Ethan and Sam feel blindsided. Ethan goes to Jesse's, has humiliating sex, gets kicked out. The episode closes with the trio back in the old flat, Ethan asking to stay with one of them, and Sam producing proper repair materials from her warehouse job.

- **Tom**: Abi's boyfriend, 32, low libido, HSBC compliance, bouldering.
