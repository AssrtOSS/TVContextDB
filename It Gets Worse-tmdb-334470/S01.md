## S01E04

# It Gets Worse — S01E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
Ethan visits his parents, who announce they're selling the family home to downsize to Kent ("the Garden of England"), triggering his anxiety about displacement. At a queer art party/performance night, Ethan screens his experimental film *Bodies, Bodies, Bodies, Bodies*. The episode interweaves three threads: Ethan's fraught family dynamics, his tutoring of a potentially radicalized 16-year-old boy (Jake), and a charged late-night encounter with a man from the party that blurs vulnerability, attraction, and self-sabotage. The trio's friendship fractures further — Sam resents Ethan and Abi's new domestic "cat stage" with Abi's boyfriend Tom, while Abi and Sam clash over Sam's behavior at the party. The episode ends with a fragile truce: Ethan plans a canal kayaking date with his party connection, while the flatmates negotiate dishes and carbonara leftovers.

## Important Character Actions
- **Ethan**: Confronts parents about selling childhood home; performs/reads at art event; screens his film; has raw post-party conversation with a man (kiss implied); plans ambitious second date (market, picnic, kayaking).
- **Abi**: Defends her relationship with Tom and their cat; clashes with Sam about loyalty; reveals she tells Sam "everything."
- **Sam**: Explodes at Abi for "abandoning" the trio for Tom and a cat; accuses Ethan of performing vulnerability; leaves party early, hurt.
- **Ethan's parents (Roger & Sarah)**: Cheerfully selling up for Kent; Roger's "accountancy conference in Peterborough" becomes a running joke.
- **Party guy (unnamed)**: Engages in escalating "who likes who" confrontation with Ethan; admits "I do" (fancy/kiss Ethan); agrees to next-day date.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Jake** — Ethan's 16-year-old tutee, showing misogynistic/red-pill rhetoric; "radicalised into someone who just didn't care about other people"
- **Zara** — Sam's girlfriend (first named appearance); Sam's "first person who would never judge her"; also Ethan's therapist (or trainee therapist) — "practising on me"
- **Lightning** — Makeup artist at party; mononym legal name ("like Cher or Rylan"); SEO joke
- **PrEP** — Ethan takes it "so I don't get HIV if I decide to fuck a man tonight"
- **"Bodies, Bodies, Bodies, Bodies"** — Ethan's film title (extended from working title *Bodies, Bodies, Bodies* due to pre-existing work)
- **Pastel de nata bao buns** — Portuguese custard tart inside a bao; date destination
- **"Lurks in the deep"** — Shared inside joke/fear about open water (callback established)

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Abi and Tom are in "a really great place" — living together, cat, rim job disclosed casually
- Sam and Zara's relationship deepening (Christmas break daily texts, "never judge her")

## S01E06

# S01E06 Summary — *It Gets Worse*

## Episode Plot
The season finale interweaves multiple storylines over a single chaotic day/night. Ethan has a Grindr hookup (Jesse) at a club called **Slutpig**, where he bottoms for the first time. Sam conducts her final therapy session with a patient (Jake), then navigates a polyamorous proposal from Tess and Zaz that would relegate Ethan to "secondary" partner — he kicks them out. Abi celebrates her promotion to **Head of People** at a pub with Lou (just made redundant via "voluntary redundancy") and Sam (new receptionist at her dad's office, a "sad nepo baby"). Abi also reveals she broke up with Tom. The trio plus friends gather for a house party at the soon-to-be-vacated flat: coke in Abi's room, nitrous balloons, Ethan's coming-out as bisexual to Tom (he's seeing a man who "reminds me a bit of you"), and the new boyfriend en route — until police arrive and shut it down. The episode closes with Zadie Smith's narrator delivering a bleak retrospective: the characters' suspicions that they'd "achieve exactly nothing," never heal or grow, and watch the world crumble were, years later, confirmed correct. Ethan's final line: "Oh, fuck everything!"

## Important Character Actions
- **Ethan**: First-time bottoming; rejects polyamorous "secondary" role; comes out as bisexual to Tom; sends the trio a PDF of his writing (mocked as "poem — in a derogatory sense"); final words express total defeat.
- **Abi**: Promoted to Head of People; breaks up with Tom (32, tracks step count); invites Tom to the party anyway; defends Ethan's writing as "romantic."
- **Sam**: Ends therapy practice; takes dad's receptionist job; mediates party chaos; sets boundary: "no coke" for shrooms.
- **Lou**: Accepts voluntary redundancy ("fresh start"); lease ending, Railcard expired — "Hello, square one."
- **Tom**: Shows up post-breakup; does balloons with Abi; supportive of Ethan's coming-out; new boyfriend expected but interrupted.
- **Jesse**: Ethan's hookup; asks if it was really Ethan's first time bottoming; Ethan leaves before breakfast.
- **Tess & Zaz**: Propose structured poly schedule (3/2/1/1 day split) with boundaries list (phone access, dairy); Ethan ejects them.

- **Slutpig** — gay club/sex venue where Ethan meets Jesse
- **Head of People** — Abi's new corporate title ("sinister," "terrifying")
- **Skinny-fat** — body-type descriptor Ethan applies to himself
- **Easy peeler** — citrus fruit Ethan eats alone in his room
- **The PDF** — Ethan's creative writing piece circulated to the group
- **Primary / secondary partner** — polyamory hierarchy Tess/Zaz propose
- **Rory effing Stewart** — political comparator for Abi's speech ("Sounded like Rory effing Stewart")

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Ethan's mother appears (let a guest in, called him "handsome"); Ethan: "No-one should have to speak to her."

## S01E03

# It Gets Worse — S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode centres on a **sex party** organised by Leon, who is filming an "art film about sex" for **Dust Jacket** (a multimedia scratch night he runs). The three leads attend, each navigating their own crises: Sam hooks up with Jake and confronts her "type"; Ethan spirals over his ex Tom (now with a girlfriend of three years, also named Abi); Abi tells Tom about the party and he gets turned on by it. The night devolves into awkward encounters, drunk arguments, and no actual sex. Ethan returns home to find a stranger, **Roger**, has moved into his room.

## Important Character Actions
- **Sam**: Sleeps with Jake (bi/pan: "I'm attracted to people, not genitals"); discusses boundaries, emotional maturity; meets **Tess** (the woman she's seeing) who turns out to be **Zara's ex** — a "twincest" lookalike situation; destroys her father's flute in a flashback (narrator: "setting healthy boundaries… and destroying the flute").
- **Ethan**: Receives a voice note from ex **Tom Dillon** wanting to move back in; visits Tom's flat, meets Tom's girlfriend **Abi** (3 years), tells her about the sex party; at the party, pursues **Aaron** (who isn't gay), gets drunk, fights with Leon, ends up on the sofa at home.
- **Abi**: Tells Tom she's going to the sex party; at the party, chats with Zara about the dynamic; later asks Ethan "Did you have a good sex party?" — he lies ("Just went for a walk").
- **Leon**: Filming the party on a '90s camcorder for Dust Jacket; presses attendees to perform for B-roll; argues with Ethan.
- **Narrator (Zadie Smith)**: Bookends the episode with Sam's backstory — parents' divorce, mother dating 24-year-old **Lachlan**, father learning flute — framing Sam's "emotional maturity" as hard-won.

## S01E05

# It Gets Worse — S01E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
The trio attends the funeral of **Timmo**, a university acquaintance who died at 24. The service — led by a newly qualified celebrant, **Graham** — features a brutally honest eulogy from Timmo's father **Sandy** ("I loved him. Didn't understand him at all... But I loved him"). Afterward, the three fracture into separate orbits: Ethan hooks up with **Roger** at the wake; Abi reveals she called off her wedding to **Sven** (Swedish royalty, Lake Como/Amal Clooney adjacent) and later sleeps with her boyfriend **Tom**; Sam meets **Zara** and attempts a "radical honesty" accountability conversation about their past. The episode closes with Ethan and Roger bonding in Roger's flat, a musical rendition of "Meaningless," and a narrator button on cliché and aliveness.

## Important Character Actions
- **Ethan**: Sparks a vicious row with Abi over his pattern of chasing straight men (Finn, Wagamama waiter, Mcat dealer, Aaron); hooks up with Roger; shares his 17-year-old suicide attempt (paracetamol/Boots pharmacy cap); stays at Roger's.
- **Abi**: Admits she ended the engagement because "what is our relationship even based on?"; tells Ethan she doesn't love Tom — she loves her Dyson, natural wine, and "feeling like you've won"; sleeps with Tom anyway.
- **Sam**: Tries to "take accountability" with Zara; admits she still feels the loss; deflects with "things usually end when they need to."
- **Roger**: Reveals he's in love with a married woman (strongly implied to be Ethan's mother); calls Ethan "the strapping buck"; offers oddly tender post-coital wisdom ("love is never a mistake").
- **Sandy (Timmo's dad)**: Delivers the emotional core — a father confessing he wasted time trying to mould his son into himself, realizing "what made me happy was him."
