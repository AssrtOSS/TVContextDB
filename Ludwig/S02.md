## S02E03

# Ludwig S02E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
John (as James) investigates a wedding murder: Owen Doyle, the bride's abusive ex, is stabbed during a choreographed Highland fling with 30+ witnesses circling the dance floor. No one saw the killing. John models the dance as a gear puzzle — dancers as rotating, mobile cogs with shifting fields of vision — and deduces the killer must be right-handed, ~6 ft tall, and wearing a cummerbund (used to staunch the wound). The "Guess Who?" elimination leaves only the best man, **Aiden Mooney**, who confesses: he killed Owen in a delayed reaction after a fight the previous night (Owen had checked into the hotel under a false name). Meanwhile, Lucy secures an interview with MP **Joanne Kemper** by trading John's bespoke "Slidey Circles" puzzle to her editor.

## Important Character Actions
- **John** solves the "impossible" locked-room-style murder via puzzle logic; has a panic attack ("too many variables") at the reception.
- **Lucy** stakes out Kemper's office, trades the puzzle for an interview, learns Kemper's PA is Hannah Foster.
- **Carter** visibly unraveling — fiancée Mandy Ziegler died in a car crash ~1 year ago; drops "Adam Newsum" to test John; suspicious of John's digging.
- **Aiden Mooney** confesses; framed as protective brother/son-in-law, not cold-blooded killer.
- **Shaw** informs Carter/John that CC Ziegler is on "official leave" (suspension) during the Pinder inquiry; baseline gossip protocol issued.
- **Gareth Fisher** introduced as new DCC overseeing the Pinder taskforce — also Kemper's personal security liaison.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Owen Doyle** — victim, bride's controlling ex (coercive control, 8 years of abuse reports ignored).
- **Aiden Mooney** — best man, killer (confesses).
- **Sienna** — bride, Aiden's sister.
- **Mrs Price** — mother of the bride.
- **Hannah Foster** — Kemper's PA.
- **Gareth Fisher** — Deputy Chief Constable, Pinder taskforce lead, Kemper's security liaison.
- **Mandy Ziegler** — Carter's deceased fiancée, Ziegler's daughter.
- **Slidey Circles** — John's gear-puzzle variant (mobile, rotating cogs).
- **Ceilidh** — the Scottish folk dance (Highland fling) central to the murder mechanics.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Ziegler suspended ("official leave") mid-investigation — not normal; basement (Pinder's computers) sealed.
- Mandy Ziegler = Carter's fiancée + Ziegler's daughter; car accident ~1 year ago.
- Carter knows John is digging into Sinclair / Adam Newsum; John's reaction to "Adam Newsum" was "appalling."
- Every thread (Sinclair, Pinder, Newsum, James's disappearance, Ziegler's suspension) points to **Joanne Kemper**.
- Kemper's father held the same Cambridge St Michael's seat.
- Sinclair file entry: "ritual of human sacrifice by each Kemper bloodline member once a decade guarantees eternal life."
- John still searching for James; Lucy reminds him he hasn't stopped.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- John's **"too many variables"** meltdown — puzzle brain vs. real people.

## S02E04

# Ludwig S02E04 摘要

## 剧情
John（以"James"身份）正与Lucy追查一条新线索：James加密便条里的两词短语"Knight's Path"——正是局长Ziegler的女儿Mandy Ziegler丧生的车祸地点，而他们的头号嫌疑人、连环谋杀疑凶MP Joanne Kemper听到此路名时明显有反应。同一时间，Carter私下约见James失踪前联系过的旧搭档DI Matt Neville，选在寿司店"Pazuru"，结果碰上现场一起谋杀：数据分析师Jonathan McQuade在旋转传送带餐桌上中毒（乌头碱/monkshood提取物，下在一瓶酱油里）身亡。Shaw因Carter案发在场且先打电话给John而非她，亲自接管案件并撤掉Carter。John通过一张无人认领的餐巾纸（上面有概率算式和速记）破解手法：凶手花了八个月记录团队每周五午餐的习性数据，精确预测谁能拿到唯一有毒的那盘菜。凶手是团队里最具竞争力的Milo Gesalan，被当场识破。Matt Neville被Lucy以一万英镑诱出，透露James失踪后曾见过他一次并交给他一个加密U盘（"保险"）；James的编码笔记副本此前就存在Holly Pinder的电脑上。结局炸弹：Chief Constable Ziegler昨晚被谋杀——John和Russell恰在尸体被发现前砸他家门，John还梦见过这个场景。

## 重要角色行动
- **John**：向Lucy承认已基本向Carter"直说"Kemper涉嫌多起谋杀；破解寿司店毒杀案手法并指认Milo Gesalan；被Shaw评价"千万别去打扑克"。
- **Lucy**：私下把整个冒充James的故事告诉报社记者线人（off the record）；垫出自己攒的一万英镑收买Matt Neville；把Matt藏进报社让他老板看着；拒绝把U盘交给Russell前先让John解读。
- **Carter（Russell）**：因秘密会面被Shaw停职、被建议调职（他要求Ziegler当面签字才走）；读U盘解读后得知未婚妻Mandy死前正要去见James、持有指控其父的"未指明证据"，情绪崩溃冲去砸Ziegler家门。
- **Shaw**：主导寿司店谋杀案，力推调走Russell，划出24小时窗口；宣布Ziegler死讯并称Kemper无辜、将排查其他嫌疑人。
- **Matt Neville**：透露Sinclair案内幕——Sinclair被枪杀却被记为钝器击打、栽赃的窃贼死于锁闭牢房；James因追查相同关联被Shaw命令停手（泄密者是Holly Pinder，现已死）；警告Kemper家族"几代人都有权势，查不得"；收钱后留话"越挖越多人死"。
- **Henry**：在家撞见"长得像James"的John和他的证据板。

- Matt Neville——James旧搭档DI，携妻儿东躲西藏，最怕Matt式多疑；妻子称他"Uncle Matt"（Henry叫他马特叔叔）
- Mandy Ziegler——局长之女、Carter的未婚妻；"Ms Bako"提及的华盛顿选举分析公司挖角

- East Anglia Advertise(r)——报社；老板"The Skipper"（没人真这么叫）

## 连续性相关事实
- James失踪后至少见过Matt一次（几周前主动来电约见面），交出U盘作为"保险"，指定要交给John。
- U盘内容全是密码，与Lucy家证据板上那张（出自Holly Pinder电脑的James编码便条）同源；U盘里还夹着2024年2月3日的旧报纸。
- Mandy死于车祸当晚正要带着"未指明的证据"去见James——证据涉及其父Ziegler的涉案；她认识James（Russell此前不知情）。
- Sinclair案扩写：Sinclair遭枪击（后脑）却被官方记为钝器伤，替罪窃贼被杀于锁闭牢房；Shaw曾命令James停止追查；泄密者Holly Pinder已死。
- Shaw持续逼Russell调职；Ziegler对Russell仍是persona non grata；Kemper被Shaw改口认定无辜。
- Lucy对记者的off-the-record关系（"golden rule of journalism"）成为后续泄密/取证渠道；Lucy已辞职转型记者。
- "我们是同一个案子"阵营扩大：John、Lucy、Russell（现知情）、Matt。
