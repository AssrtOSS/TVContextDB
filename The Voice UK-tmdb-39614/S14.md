## S14E10 — The Voice UK Final (aligned EN/ZH subtitles)

### Episode plot
The season finale, presented by will.i.am, with five coaches: Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones (co-coaching one team), Kelly Rowland, will.i.am, and Sir Tom Jones. After an opening medley ("I Want Your Love" / "Dream On" / "Four voices, one dream"), four finalists each perform a solo, then a duet with their coach. Guest performer Ella Eyre sings "Hell Yeah". Studio audience voting opens, closes, is counted and independently verified, and Cate McHugh is crowned champion. Note: the broadcast captions read "The winner of The Voice UK 2026" — likely a subtitle slip, as this is the Season 14 final.

### Finalists and teams
| Finalist | Team | Key details |
|---|---|---|
| Cate McHugh | Team Tom (Tom Fletcher) | Welsh; daughter Lily, 5; husband Nathan; sings in pubs |
| Monique Nicholson-Porter | Team Kelly (Kelly Rowland) | Husband Jason, a former Voice contestant; four chair turns in the Blinds |
| Frank Raven | Team Tom and Danny | 17, music student; youngest in the competition; family in audience |
| Nana | Team Will (will.i.am) | Latin/Spanish-language artist; performs Spanish + English |

### Solo performances (VT context)
- Monique — Tom Odell's "Heal". Kelly flies to Paris (rehearsing her tour with Robert) and rehearses choreography with her. Monique explains the song's origin: her father was hospitalised, she and her mother sang to him, he died "four weeks to the day"; the experience "almost healed me as well". She calls winning life-changing for her and her family.
- Frank — Britney Spears' "…Baby One More Time", reworked as a slowed-down, darker piano version ("I gotta make it feel me"). Rehearsal footage at Sheffield Arena, where he has gone from watching Busted soundcheck to playing the gig with Busted; his "whole gang" is brought along. Coaches judge the ending as losing his edge ("the Frank has gone"); Frank answers "Go big or go home".
- Nana — Adele's "Hello", initially planned fully in English because she has "been doing Spanish throughout". will.i.am advises mixing Spanish with English rather than losing her identity; she takes the advice and the sung performance uses Spanish verses ("Hello, soy yo"). will.i.am turns up unannounced at her gig and jams/dances; both wear matching orange then green outfits ("we connected to the chocolate universe").
- Cate — The Beatles' "Blackbird", a song she had never sung; Tom chose it because "it's about finding your confidence" and she "feels like blackbird". Tom tells a story about singing in a pub in Pontypridd with a blind pianist ("blind people learn everything about life through music") and about not being able to see the crowd from the stage.

### Guest performance
Ella Eyre — "Hell Yeah", from her album "Everything, In Time" (her first full album after ten years, now independent). Jokes about the all-red dress and red nails ("I didn't get the red memo").

### Duets
- Frank + Tom & Danny — Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off Of You", twisted from its usual major key into a dark, haunting arrangement. Frank says he never expected a chair turn in the Blinds ("you were battling yourself"), and that the semi-final was the most fun he has ever had on stage.
- Nana + will.i.am — a Black Eyed Peas / J Balvin mash-up of "The Rhythm of the Night" and "RITMO"; Nana writes her own verse ("we've got homework"), will.i.am calls her "an honorary Black Eyed Pea" and "la persona originale". He notes the Black Eyed Peas had gone eight and a half years without a record before RITMO.
- Cate + Sir Tom — John Denver's "Country Roads, Take Me Home". Tom closes every one of his gigs with it and had John Denver on his show in 1971. Six months earlier Cate sang at the Brewhouse with a mural of Tom Jones behind her; "now I'm gonna be standing next to the real deal". Tom says the sentiment of the song is "take us home... to Wales".
- Monique + Kelly — Mary Mary's "Shackles", chosen for contrast with "Heal" and to show her range. Kelly reveals her voice "just completely went" in the run-up to the semi-final and that watching Monique sing through it was "like watching a miracle take place". Monique is a big Destiny's Child fan; Kelly forgot some dance steps while Monique nailed them. Kelly: "she deserves to win".

### Vote, result and prizes
- Studio audience decides ("so no pressure at all (!)"); vote opens, then closes, votes counted and independently verified.
- Winner: **Cate McHugh** (Team Tom). Frank, Monique and Nana are congratulated but not individually ranked on screen.
- Winner takes: Universal Music Group record deal; £50,000 cash; seven nights at Universal Orlando Resort with flights courtesy of Virgin Atlantic Holidays, all four theme parks, a Universal Express Unlimited Pass, the Hogwarts Express between Diagon Alley and Hogsmeade, Jurassic Park, five immersive worlds at Universal Epic Universe, and a live performance on the Hard Rock Hotel's Velvet Sessions stage.
- Her version of "Blackbird" becomes her first ever single, available to download and stream after the show.
- Studio competition promoted at £120,000, tax-free — "more cash than we have ever done before on The Voice UK".
