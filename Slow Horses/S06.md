## S06E01

# Slow Horses S06E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The season opens with the murder of **Struan Loy**, a former Slow Horse, who is burned alive in a shipping container by two men posing as prospective buyers. Meanwhile, **David Cartwright** (former MI5 Director General, "OB") is buried at 83. At the funeral, **River Cartwright** delivers a eulogy maintaining the cover that his grandfather worked for the Department of Transport. **Diana Taverner**, now First Desk, oversees the "Park funeral" and hosts a delegation of political donors at Regent's Park, showcasing a Russian bot farm linked to Novichok attacks.

Back at Slough House, the team discovers they've been **completely erased from MI5 systems** — payroll, records, service history, all gone. **Jackson Lamb** goes on strike over non-payment. **Catherine Standish** learns from a contact that the expungement order came from "the very top" (Taverner). 

**River visits his father, Frank Harkness** (revealed in S5 as his biological father), who appears at the funeral disguised as a care worker. Frank is rebuilding a team and warns Lamb he's back in business. 

Lamb investigates Struan's death — the container door was unlocked, yet Struan didn't escape despite only a broken arm. **A second ex-Slow Horse, Kay White, drowns after falling from her canal boat**. Lamb tasks Roddy Ho with checking on other former Slow Horses.

## Important Character Actions
- **Taverner**: Orders Slough House's erasure from MI5 databases; hosts political donors at the Park; maintains the "Department of Transport" cover at David's funeral.
- **Lamb**: Refuses to work without pay; investigates Struan's death off-book ("Moscow rules"); confronts Taverner at the wake.
- **River**: Gives the eulogy; discovers Frank Harkness at his grandfather's house; presses Taverner on the promised return to the Park.
- **Frank Harkness**: Attends the funeral; tells Lamb he's doing "business" and his team can be rebuilt; warns Lamb not to interfere.
- **Standish**: Secures confirmation the purge came from the First Desk.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Struan Loy** — ex-Slow Horse, fired by Lamb for betrayal, murdered in shipping container
- **Kay White** — ex-Slow Horse, drowned in canal (second death)
- **Moira Tregorian** — referenced as having "grassed" on Taverner
- **"Studios in the Sky"** — shipping container housing development where Struan died
- **Code Vulcan** — Taverner's cat (fake alert code)
- **Nosher Nash** — old school nickname for Peter Judd

## Continuity-Re
levant Facts
- **David Cartwright is dead** (83, natural causes); the "Department of Transport" cover story is maintained at his Park funeral.
- **Slough House has been administratively erased** — not a glitch, a deliberate order from First Desk (Taverner).
- **Frank Harkness is alive and active** — operating independently, recruiting, and explicitly back in London.
- **River remains at Slough House** despite Taverner's prior promise to return him to the Park.
- **The "Dogs" (Emma Flyte's team)** provide security at the funeral; Nick Duffy is gone.
- **Peter Judd** (now Home Secretary) attends the Park tour with donors Jeremy Yeo (steel), Sylvia Delaney (Treasury), Damien Cantor (TV channel owner).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- Lamb's "Moscow rules" — operating off-book, no Taverner knowledge.
- "We've all faked our own death at one time or another" (Standish/Lamb banter).
- Lamb's strike: "Christ. Whatever happened to the unions in this country?"
- "Nosher Nash" — Judd's school nickname, referenced by Frank.
- Frank's "circle of life" line at his son's grandfather's burial.
- Taverner's cat = "Code Vulcan" fake alert.
- River's hip flask / "small bottle" pedantry with Standish.
- Lamb almost missing David Cartwright: "It's times like this I almost miss the fucker."

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Who ordered the hit on Struan Loy?** The two "buyers" work for someone; the container was unlocked — why didn't he escape?
2. **Kay White's death** — accident or second murder? Pattern suggests targeting ex-Slow Horses.
3. **Taverner's motive** for erasing Slough House — leverage? Cleanup? Pressure from Judd/PM?
4. **Frank Harkness's "business"** — who is his backer? (He refuses to name them, says identity kept "sub-rosa").
5. **River's mother** — absent from funeral; Frank asks "Your mother didn't show, huh?"
6. **Standish's contact** who confirmed the purge order — who is it?
7. **Roddy's check on other ex-Slow Horses** — who's next?
8. **The political donors at the Park** — what do they want from Taverner?

## S06E02


## Episode Plot

The episode opens with **Andrew Nesmith**, a recently dismissed Dog, meeting an unknown contact about compensation for stealing a file. He's killed shortly after. At Slough House, Lamb convenes the team: River and Roddy are tasked with tracking Harkness's car; Coe and Dander investigate Kay White's death. River discovers Harkness rented a car now parked in Tower Hamlets — inside the flat above, they find **Nesmith's body**, staged to look like suicide or a sex accident. All personal effects (cash, phone, laptop) are missing. River obtains CCTV footage showing **a child** entering the building.

Meanwhile, **Peter Judd** pressures Taverner into a TV interview by dangling the fact that his money man **Damien Cantor** (right-leaning news channel owner) funded the cash Judd passed to her. On air, interviewer **Serena** ambushes Taverner with questions about nerve-agent retaliation and a rumor that a former First Desk was a foreign asset — visibly rattling Diana.

**Coe and Dander** visit Kay White's canal boat and learn from a neighbor (with an expired visa) that **a man and a woman were seen leaving the boat** the night Kay died, confirming it was no accident. **Roddy** identifies the CCTV child as **Reece Nesmith**, the dead Dog's son, currently housed at **Brighter Start Children's Home** in Ealing.

Lamb visits **Moira Tregorian** (Park archivist) to pull paper files on Struan and Kay — only to discover **all Slough House personnel files have been physically removed**. Combined with the digital wipe from E1, the team is now completely invisible to MI5. Lamb invokes **"Blake's grave"** — an emergency go-dark protocol — ordering all Joes to dump phones and scatter to a safe house.

River receives a second call from Harkness (white noise, 3 seconds). Roddy traces it: **the call originates from inside David Cartwright's house**. River ignores Lamb's orders and drives there alone. The episode ends with River entering the dark house and calling out: "Frank!" — Harkness answers: "Hello, River."

## Important Character Actions

- **Lamb**: Assigns investigative tasks; visits Moira Tregorian at the Park; discovers all SH paper files are missing; invokes Blake's grave; picks up Standish (tempted to drink) and forces her into the car.
- **River**: Tracks Harkness's rental car; finds Nesmith's body; obtains CCTV footage; goes to OB's house alone against orders.
- **Roddy**: Traces Harkness's phone calls (declares himself "Obi-Wan fucking Kenobi," then says he hates "Star Trek"); identifies Reece Nesmith; locates the call origin.
- **Taverner**: Agrees to Judd's TV interview under duress; gets grilled on nerve-agent ops and predecessor corruption; confronts the interviewer about sources afterward.
- **Judd**: Leverages Cantor's money connection to force Taverner onto television; seats Cantor at their lunch to apply maximum pressure.
- **Standish**: Remains AWOL through most of the episode; Lamb finds her holding a bottle of Margaux (bought at a wine shop, reminiscing about drinking it with "an old boss" — likely Charles Partner); he talks her into the car.
- **Coe and Dander**: Investigate Kay White's boat; discover the man-and-woman witness lead.
- **Moira Tregorian**: Confirms Nesmith was fired from the Dogs for aggressive behavior at her office; her paper archive has been stripped of all SH files.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Andrew Nesmith** — Dog, fired for misconduct, murdered in Tower Hamlets flat; father of Reece
- **Reece Nesmith** — minor/child, son of Andrew, in care at Brighter Start Children's Home, Ealing; seen on CCTV at the murder scene
- **Damien Cantor** — owner of a right-leaning British news channel; Judd's money man; "huge fan" of Taverner; funds channeled through Judd to Diana
- **Serena** — TV interviewer who ambushes Taverner on air
- **"Blake's grave"** — emergency go-dark protocol; safe house location for the team
- **"Five Fs"** — Lamb's investigative framework: Who the fuck, What the fuck, Where the fuck, Why the fuck (and "Fuck off" as the fifth)
- **"Crypto-Rod"** — Roddy's self-styled cryptocurrency persona
- **"Womble of Wall Street"** — Lamb's nickname for Roddy
- **"Tiny Dander"** — Lamb's nickname for Shirley
- **"Mr. Bundy"** — Lamb's nickname for Coe (serial-killer reference)
- **Brighter Start Children's Home** — facility in Ealing where Reece Nesmith is housed

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- **All Slough House records — digital and now physical — have been completely purged.** Taverner ordered the digital wipe; the paper files vanished separately, suggesting a second actor (possibly Harkness's network).
- **Harkness is operating out of David Cartwright's house
, using it as a base and taunting River by calling from there.
- **Nesmith was fired from the Dogs** for getting aggressive with Moira Tregorian at the Park, then murdered — the stolen file and his death are connected.
- **Kay White's death confirmed as murder** — a man and woman were seen leaving her boat the night she died.
- **Taverner is compromised by Judd/Cantor** — she accepted money channeled through Judd from Cantor; Cantor's media platform gives him leverage to expose her at will.
- **Standish is struggling with sobriety** but hasn't relapsed; Lamb showed a rare moment of genuine concern (knowing her history with alcohol and self-destruction).
- **River gives a building security guard a tip to join the Dogs**, passing his own name — potentially a recruit for later episodes.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- **Roddy's "Obi-Wan fucking Kenobi" / "I fucking hate Star Trek"** — Roddy quotes Star Wars, Lamb misidentifies it as Star Trek. Callback to Roddy's pop-culture illiteracy.
- **Lamb's "five Fs"** — "Who, What, Where, Why" plus "Fuck off" as the fifth. The team counts only four; Lamb insists it's five.
- **"Womble of Wall Street"** — Lamb's layered insult merging the Wombles (children's TV characters who scavenge) with Wall Street. Roddy is unbothered: "Thank fuck for that."
- **"Tiny Dander"** — diminutive for Shirley; paired with "Mr. Bundy" for Coe, painting them as an odd-couple investigative pair.
- **Lamb's "go for Ho"** — Roddy answers his phone like a radio operator.
- **"Crypto-Rod"** — Roddy's delusional trading persona; he skips a crisis meeting because "Bitcoin's about to pop off."
- **Standish buying Margaux** — she tells the wine-shop clerk she "used to drink it with an old boss," almost certainly Charles Partner. Her sobriety arc remains central.
- **Lamb on Standish's drinking**: "Second bottle in, you'll be under a trucker. We both know that." — brutal but effective at snapping her out of it.

## Unresolved Threads

1. **Who stole the paper Slough House files?** Taverner ordered the digital wipe, but the physical files disappearing suggests a second actor — Harkness's network, or someone else?
2. **Who commissioned Nesmith to steal the file from the Park?** The opening scene shows him being paid off by an intermediary; the "big man" behind the theft remains unnamed.
3. **Who killed Andrew Nesmith?** And was the witness (Reece) the reason the killer staged it as suicide rather than a cleaner disposal?
4. **What happens when River confronts Harkness at OB's house?** River is alone, against orders, walking into a trap or a conversation — either way, Harkness has been waiting for him.
5. **Can the trace on Harkness's call be completed?** Roddy had to abandon the trace mid-way when Blake's grave was called; the partial data may still yield something.
6. **What are Judd and Cantor's endgame with Taverner?** One TV interview is done; Cantor now has leverage. The "muscular, retributive response" question suggests they may be fishing for confirmation of covert operations.
7. **Who were the man and woman on Kay White's boat?** The neighbor saw them but couldn't describe them in detail.
8. **Where is Reece Nesmith now, and is he safe?** The killer (or the person who hired the killer) knows he's a witness; River identified him but hasn't reached him yet.
9. **Standish's full situation** — why has she been absent? She's holding a corkscrew and a bottle of Margaux but hasn't opened it yet. Her arc is poised between relapse and recovery.

## S06E03

# Slow Horses — S06E03 Summary

## Plot
Sid Baker, officially listed as dead since the S1 shooting, is revealed alive and hiding in rural seclusion. River, fleeing masked gunmen who crashed David Cartwright's funeral, finds her, and they go on the run together. Meanwhile someone is systematically killing former Slow Horses: Struan Loy (burned alive in a shipping container) and Kay White (drowned off her boat) are dead, and the killers move on to Moira Tregorian, gassing her in her home. Shirley and Catherine extract Moira, then collect Louisa from her private-sector job, and the group heads for a safe house, stopping to buy illegal guns from a shady dealer. Lamb confronts Taverner; Sid and River work out she's being hunted because someone using her identity did an off-books hit in Kazan, Russia.

## Character Actions
- **River**: Survives a gun-and-cattle chase with Sid; reveals he screamed "Frank" (Frank Harkness, his biological father) during the funeral attack; recognizes the Kazan news item as a staged "robbery" hit ("twice in the head").
- **Sid**: Bitter about being written off as dead for three years; suffers PTSD tremors from gunfire; her bullet fragment is nicknamed "Poirot". A travel movement order to Kazan, Russia was sent to her address weeks before — dismissed as a "glitch" by the Park.
- **Lamb**: Realizes a kill list targets everyone who ever worked at Slough House; gatecrashes Taverner's home, accusing her of leaking the Slow Horses list via ex-Dog Andy Nesmith (who stole the hard-copy files); tells her to have Flyte dig into who hired Frank Harkness.
- **Taverner**: Under fire for a TV interview where she said "retaliation" about the nerve-agent response; demotes her own head of security (her clipboard-wielding subordinate) to Slough House as punishment, insisting the "leak" accusation is nothing.
- **Louisa**: Pulled out of her corporate compliance job via a fake "Uncle Blake" accident pretext.
- **Shirley & Catherine**: Escort the traumatized Moira (sprayed in the face by the "gas leak" intruder) toward the safe house; Shirley buys a cheap illegal pistol.
- **Coe**: Sent by Lamb to collect River and Sid; monologues that killing makes him "feel more human"; has a tattoo reading "Pain is strength."
- **Ho & Lamb**: Guard Reece Nesmith, the dwarf son of murdered ex-Dog Andy Nesmith; Reece reveals his dad was blackmailing someone over a stolen file when a money-carrying ex-CIA man killed him.

## New Names / Terms
- **Reece Nesmith** — Andy Nesmith's dwarf son, taken into Lamb's protection.
- **Andy Nesmith** — ex-Dog, murdered by Frank Harkness; stole the hard-copy Slow Horses files.
- **Kay White** — ex-Slow Horse, killed (drowned off her boat).
- **Struan Loy** — ex-Slow Horse, burned alive in a shipping container (returning character).
- **Flyte** — Park investigator, dismissed for "performance issues."
- **Oliver** — government mandarin who berates Taverner over the interview.
- **Kazan (Russia)** — site of the hit done under Sid's identity; a woman was shot twice in the head there.
- **Albert Eric Smith** — Anne's late husband (b. 9 May 1938); his birthday was Anne's password.
- **Anne** — elderly woman whose cottage shelters River and Sid.
- **"Blake's grave"** — the Park's codename for the safe-house/protective-pickup operation.
- **White Hart** — pub the killers falsely claim to be seeking (cover story for door-knocking).

## Continuity Facts
- The Park declared Sid dead after her S1 shooting; her survival is now known to River, Lamb's team — and eventually reported to Taverner.
- Taverner is now Head of MI5 (First Desk-equivalent) and damaged politically by the "retaliation" TV interview; her security chief is demoted to Slough House.
- Moira Tregorian was never formally a Slow Horse — her inclusion on the kill list is "a big mistake," per her.
- Frank Harkness is doing a cleanup/kill operation for an unknown employer, and personally killed Andy Nesmith.
- The team is "dark": phones off, no Park contact; River insists on warning Taverner, Sid warns him Taverner may have set the Kazan hit using her "dead agent" status.
- Lamb's group refuses the Park's invitation to shelter there ("You've got a leak Julian Assange would be proud of").

## Jokes / References Worth Keeping
- Sid's bullet fragment is named "Poirot" and "tells" her not to get shot.
- Cow-price argument: "Cows are like three grand" — recurring bicker while hiding.
- River's cows fear ("Look at the size of them") vs. Sid's "Grow up!"; cow dung ruins his funeral suit.
- "Danger Mouse" taunt at Reece; Reece corrects: "It's dwarf."
- The gun dealer's "dynamic pricing" (sold Marcus his own gun back for double); Gollum/"precious" jab about searching Reece's dad's stuff.
- David Cartwright's maxim: "News on the front page, sport on the back, and assassinations buried on page 12."
- Ho's "Padawan" / Sid's "Pain is strength" tattoo exchange; Lamb's fart on demand in the car.

## Unresolved Threads
- Who hired Frank Harkness for the cleanup, and why the full ex–Slow Horses roster must die.
- What the Kazan hit under Sid's identity was really about, and who inside the Park forged her travel order / "glitch."
- Where the stolen Slow Horses hard-copy files ended up, and who inside the Park is leaking.
- Moira's poisoning — sprayed substance left her badly ill in the car; her condition is uncertain.
- Whether Taverner (or someone above her) orchestrated Sid's "death" and the hits.
- River and Sid's next move: payphones dead, they are walking into a shop at the retail park as the episode cuts.

## S06E05

# S06E05 Summary — Slow Horses

## Plot

Three strands run in parallel and collide by episode's end. **(1)** Two Russian operatives kidnap Sid Baker and drive her into the countryside, debating how festive to make it (tree, fire, or lake) while she jokes about skinny-dipping and British prudery. J.K. Coe and Lamb close in. **(2)** Lamb gatecrashes Diana Taverner's home after her disastrous Cantor interview, accuses her of laundering Cantor's money to fund the Kazan hit, points out that re-wiping Sid's records painted a target on every Slow Horse, and walks out to investigate Cantor's Russian links himself. **(3)** The Slough House group — Louisa, Shirley, Moira Tregorian, Standish, Roddy Ho — hold up in a "safe house" (Shirley making petrol bombs) while the Slow Horses' bodies and identities are re-fed into Taverner's hands. Sid and River are delivered, handcuffed, into the custody of the two Russian operatives who intercepted them.

The mid-episode pivot: Diana calls Vassily (her GRU back-channel) admitting "a development," and later confesses to the O&L Committee that she ordered the Kazan nerve-agent retaliation off-budget and off-books. Meanwhile Cantor meets Peter (an ousted mandarin) and secures Cantor's offer of media-backed rehabilitation in exchange for staying silent about funding the Kazan hit. Cantor is summoned to Quaglino's to close "the biggest media distribution deal in Russian history."

Sid and River's last quiet scene — joking about romantic death spots, three-year sanatorium abstinence, David's old habit of leaving one light on — is broken by "Both of you, up!" Sid is marched off to a Bulgaria-bound plane (Sofia, not Moscow, where no rules apply), while River is left behind. A final pursuit scene ends with Sid crashing, fighting, and vanishing from her Russian escort. Episode closes on the Russians demanding "Where the fuck is she?"
