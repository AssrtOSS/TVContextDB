## Las Azules S02E08 — Episode Summary

### Plot
The season finale resolves the **Executioner (Goyo Cadenas)** arc. After Commander **Aldo Herrera** (Gabina's father) is murdered, a confession tape arrives for Gabina. The Azules must decide whether to leak it (exposing police corruption) or hand it to superiors (who will bury it). Meanwhile, the President installs **Commander Cobra** (DFS) to take over the investigation, forcing the Azules underground. They deduce Goyo is holding **Chief Escobedo** at **Macaria's abandoned factory** (inherited, closed). A raid ensues: Goyo is killed, Escobedo rescued, Macaria arrested. In a presidential showdown, Escobedo admits he **created the original Tlalpan Undresser (Filiberto Guerra)** to later "catch" him for glory — which inadvertently spawned Goyo's Executioner persona. Escobedo gets a quiet 3-year sentence to close the case. The Azules leverage their success to secure **Gerardo's release/reinstatement** and **Romandía's transfer to detectives**. María gives Aldo's confession tape to journalist **Lucas** for publication. The series ends with the Azules formally established as a **Behavioral Sciences Division**, back on patrol together.

### Important Character Actions
- **Gabina**: Receives father's confession tape; struggles to watch it; leads raid on factory; arrests Macaria; chooses to move back home, prioritizing family.
- **María**: Negotiates with Cobra/President; hands Aldo's confession to Lucas (non-negotiable condition: publication); affirms identity as "a Woman in Blue."
- **Ángeles**: Spots the **3-minute pattern** in confession tapes (Portapak standard); notices Filiberto's tape was **cut to 2:37** — key forensic break.
- **Valentina**: Confronts Gabina about avoiding the tape; pushes the group to leak the truth.
- **Escobedo**: Confesses to President he manufactured the Undresser case; receives 3-year sentence in exchange for silence.
- **Cobra (DFS)**: Installed by presidential order; shuts down Azules' autonomy; ultimately credits them publicly.
- **Romandía**: Officially transferred to Detective Division per Azules' demand.
- **Gerardo**: Released from prison, reinstated as detective per Azules' demand.
- **Macaria Cadenas**: Exposed as Goyo's true manipulator — wrote letters from 1940 onward, pushed him to kill, staged her own "abandonment."

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Commander Cobra (柯步拉指挥官)** — DFS operative, takes over investigation by presidential decree.
- **Martín Guerra (马丁·格拉)** — Filiberto Guerra's son; visited Filiberto in Lecumberri biweekly; last visit day before Filiberto's death.
- **Cochon d'Or (金猪餐厅)** — Restaurant where Escobedo frequents (matchboxes found).
- **Division of Behavioral Sciences (行为科学分部)** — Formalized unit for the Azules at series end.
- **Sodium Pentothal / 戊硫喷妥钠** — Truth serum used by Goyo on victims.
- **Portapak tape (标准 Portapak 录像带)** — 3-minute runtime standard; Filiberto's edited tape reveals Goyo censored something.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Executioner's victim escalation**: Filiberto Guerra (officer) → Rómulo Quintanilla (lieutenant) → Plutarco Lozano (detective) → Aldo Herrera (commander). Next target would have been **Escobedo** (only higher rank).
- **Macaria's backstory**: Parents died ~1940; began writing to imprisoned Goyo same year; hybridized hybristophilia with active encouragement.
- **Escobedo's origin sin**: Created Filiberto Guerra as a controllable "Undresser" to solve and win glory — the "Frankenstein" behind both serial killers.
- **President (Luis Echeverría)** directly orchestrates the cover-up: Escobedo serves 3 years, case closed, Azules rewarded but contained.
- **Aldo Herrera's confession tape** (content unseen) given to Lucas for public release — María's final act of defiance.

### Jokes / References for Later
- **"蓝衣女警" (Women in Blue)** used officially by Cobra in closing ceremony — institutional adoption of the once-mocking label.
- **Coffee gag**: Ángeles' deadpan "we make great coffee" → Cobra orders "always a full pot" — becomes running acknowledgment of their support role.
- **Romandía called "Detective" twice** in final montage — payoff for S1-S2 arc from dismissive peer to accepted partner.
- **Valentina's "I'll try not to judge you" / "I said I'll try"** — sister dynamic callback, unresolved tension preserved.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Content of Aldo Herrera's confession** — given to Lucas; publication implied but not shown.
- **Martín Guerra's role** — Filiberto's son, imprisoned for robbery/murder; visited Filiberto before death; connection to Goyo suggested but unexplored.
- **DFS / Cobra's long-term agenda** — "sensitive issues" mandate; control of media cited; institutional power intact.
- **Escobedo's 3-year sentence** — "nothing serious"; likely early release; corruption network (names he offered Goyo) uninvestigated.
- **María & Alejandro's marriage** — separated; she moves back home; final "we both deserve that, but not together" with Romandía closes romantic arc but not domestic.
- **Gabina's mother** — "bad" condition mentioned; off-screen.
- **The "Dirty War" / 1968 Tlatelolco / 1971 Corpus Christi** — structural backdrop unresolved; Azules now inside system but truth partially buried.
