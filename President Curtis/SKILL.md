## 3. World Setting

- **Universe**: Shared with *Rick and Morty* (Adult Swim). Floating timeline — Curtis has been President since ~2013 (through Obama/Trump analogues).
- **Premise**: Spin-off following President Curtis and staff handling crises Rick Sanchez ignores: **interdimensional diplomacy, paranormal investigations, unexplained phenomena**.
- **Tone**: Adult animated sci-fi comedy / black comedy / satire. Standalone stories; R&M cameo only in pilot.
- **Key Locations**: White House (1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW, DC), Pentagon, Moon (secret base), Kennedy Sex Tunnels (R&M lore).
- **Organizations**: US Secret Service, CIA, Pentagon, US Marine Corps (Curtis is former Marine).
- **Technology**: Rick-level sci-fi (spaceships, shrinking tech, portals, cyborg enhancements, EMP weapons) coexisting with real-world govt bureaucracy.

## 4. Overall Plot Premise

> **President Andre Curtis and his eccentric staff — cyborg Chief of Staff Roe Banks and half-leprechaun Secret Service Agent Francis O'Doyle — tackle the "weird" crises Rick Sanchez can't be bothered with: monster diplomacy, paranormal phenomena, interdimensional incidents, and government conspiracies (e.g., Moon landing cover-ups, JFK's secret Moon son). Each episode is a standalone X-Files-style case with satirical political humor.**

- **Season 1** (10 episodes, premiered July 26, 2026): Episode 1 "Pilot" — Paul Bunyan death, Moon landing conspiracy, Adam (JFK's son). Episode 2 "Triangle" — Bermuda Triangle / Amelia Earhart. Remaining episodes: "Ex", "Purchase", "David", "Hollow", "Ghosts", "Knox", "Synths", "Timber".
- **Renewed for Season 2** before premiere (July 2026).

## 7. Cultural Context & Translation Notes

- **Rick and Morty Canon**: This series exists in the same continuity. Translators should be familiar with R&M Chinese terminology (Bilibili/Netflix/douban community standards). Key: "瑞克和莫蒂" official Chinese title.
- **US Political Satire**: Curtis is a composite of Obama (early) + Trump (later) + original satire. Jokes target US presidency as institution, not specific partisan figures. Chinese audience may need footnotes for: **Kennedy Sex Tunnels** (urban legend), **Moon landing conspiracy**, **Paul Bunyan** (folklore), **Amelia Earhart** (disappeared aviator), **Leprechaun** (Irish folklore — O'Doyle's half-species).
- **Adult Swim Tone**: Graphic violence, sexual innuendo, scatological humor (Ep. 1: Curtis defecating in White House Gym), blasphemy. Chinese subtitles traditionally use: **粗口保留或委婉对应**, **性暗示双关保留**, **血腥描写直译**.
- **Episode Titles**: Single-word nouns ("Pilot", "Triangle", "Ex", "Purchase", "David", "Hollow", "Ghosts", "Knox", "Synths", "Timber"). Translate literally: "试播集/首集", "三角区/百慕大", "前任", "采购/购买", "大卫", "空心/空洞", "鬼魂", "诺克斯", "合成人", "木材/伐木".
- **Proper Nouns**: Keep English for agencies (CIA, FBI, NASA, USAF) — Chinese audience recognizes acronyms. "Secret Service" → 特勤局. "Joint Chiefs" → 参谋长联席会议.
- **Measurements**: Feet/miles → 米/公里 in parentheses if plot-relevant.
- **Currency**: Dollars → 美元 / $.
- **Recurring Gags**: 
  - Curtis's "molly" (MDMA) from Rick — translate as "摇头丸" or "专用摩莉".
  - Turkey President / Turkey Marines — "火鸡总统/陆战队".
  - Giant Incest Baby — "巨大乱伦婴儿" (R&M S5).
  - Cromulons — "克罗蒙".
