## S01E03

# S01E03 — "Ex" Summary

## Plot

President Curtis prepares to attend the inauguration of his ex-wife, **Beverly Mills**, newly elected Governor of Illinois (formerly mayor of Chicago). Before he can leave, O'Doyle is sent to **West Wing World** — a robot-populated Secret Service reevaluation simulator — for breaking a congressman's arm in five places during a political stunt. Banks is also ordered to reevaluation for "snitching" after honestly answering an inquiry about O'Doyle's conduct. Curtis is left with replacement agent **Special Agent Alan "Chomps"**, whom he despises as a normie "Chad."

At Beverly's inauguration gala in Chicago, Curtis learns of her accomplishments: the **CPEP (Candyman Perpetual Energy Project)** — capturing Candyman's murdering energy via mirrors and hooks — plus minimum-wage hikes, Babadook book drives, and the **MoonRight Initiative** for werewolf reform. A serving staff member bearing the mark of the **Chicago Werewolf Syndicate** attacks Curtis with hot sauce; Curtis kills him. Banks contains the alarm pheromone by stuffing the body inside the building, and O'Doyle (meanwhile acing his simulator test, including solving "gun baby" by entering a "leprechaun trance") calls in a DoD "Code Silver" rooftop drop.

O'Doyle's reevaluation is sabotaged when a robot named **Tom** baits him into revealing insider information about the President. Realizing the whole sim is full of robots, O'Doyle triggers a diagnostic reset that bleeds into the real world — multiple robots in Chicago lose memories and go searching for humans.

At the party, **Werewolf Anonymous** reveals themselves: all servers were in on it. They've erected an EMP field around the block, and a werewolf-tooth bomb sits on the center table. They demand Beverly transfer all MoonRight funds. Beverly, who had secretly built a "remote-retrieval prepper den" hidden inside Chicago's **The Bean** (Cloud Gate), evacuates everyone into the sculpture. **Chomps takes a werewolf bite protecting Curtis** and begins transforming. Beverly improvises silver bullets from the building's pre-1985 radiator pipes and kills the lead wolf; Candyman finishes the rest. O'Doyle arrives with actual silver ammo from the DoD drop.

Afterward, Curtis slips out the back to give Beverly her night. He asks her: if he weren't President, could they work? She deflects — "If you weren't President, who would you be?" Curtis leaves with Chomps, who is now infected with lycanthropy.

## Character Actions

- **President Curtis**: Pompous, insecure, and clearly still in love with Beverly. Kills a werewolf waiter; asks Beverly to abandon the presidency for him.
- **Francis O'Doyle**: Completes reevaluation by overcoming his half-leprechaun honesty compulsion (entered by his mother) to shoot lies through the robots, saving Curtis and Banks.
- **Roe Banks**: Contains the werewolf pheromone, helps manage the crisis, and is forced into reevaluation alongside O'Doyle.
- **Beverly Mills** (new): Curtis's ex-wife; pragmatic, politically savvy, secretly runs monster reform programs. Saves the gala via The Bean bunker.
- **Special Agent Alan "Chomps"** (new): Curtis's replacement bodyguard. Loyal, earnest, two months from retirement — now a werewolf.
- **Candyman** (returning): Reformed monster, hook replaced with a "champagne flute holder." Provides cover inside The Bean.

## S01E04

# President Curtis S01E04 "Purchase" — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
President Curtis and staff discover the original Louisiana Purchase receipt in the White House attic. Napoleon scribbled a "Frontier Forfeit" clause on it — an 1800s law letting the seller reclaim land after contract breach. Under Napoleonic Code, Napoleon's preserved penis legally *is* Napoleon; if certified as a French citizen in a U.S. court, France can reclaim the entire Louisiana Territory (15 states). The team tracks the penis to Armand Russo's "Famous Organs Wing" in New Orleans (next to Tutankhamun's brain and Bette Davis' eyes). They ally with vampire Salem Montgomery ("Monty"), but the vampires double-cross them, working with French Commandant Dulac. After a break-in, outdoor court ruling (gavel on car hood), and battle, Curtis calls France to reject the claim — offering "his ass" as the only American land France can have.

## Important Character Actions
- **Banks** finds the Louisiana Purchase receipt; hacks security; gets called a "narc at heart" by Curtis.
- **O'Doyle** eats poisonous "lethal chicken" hot wings (half-leprechaun stomach digests molten metal); forges wooden-tipped bullets from brass alloy for the vampire fight.
- **Chomps** embraces permanent werewolf status; survives death by focusing regeneration on arm bones; gets an Innocence Detector collar (chicken-and-rice flavored suppressant) from Dr. Finch.
- **Curtis** negotiates with Marc Luc de Bergeron; ends with crude "eat my ass" diplomacy.
- **Monty** betrays the team but claims ignorance of the double-cross; banned from U.S. forever.

- **Hemogoblin (血魔妖)** — vampire subtype.

## S01E06

## S01E06 "Hollow"

### 剧情梗概
柯蒂斯总统正在直播玩《Battleswag》（战利品大作战），美其名曰"我的炉边谈话"，此时四个州突发大停电。国防部下属的离异科学家 Dr. Keel（基尔博士）带着图表警告：美国电网超负荷运行，面临无限期崩溃，唯一解法是能源上限与征税。柯蒂斯当场拒绝——"限制电力是不爱国的"——转而采纳 Captain Marks 提到的替代方案：前往 Hollow Earth（地心世界），获取当地人称作 Life Tree（生命之树）的无限能源。

代表团由柯蒂斯、Banks、O'Doyle 和老秘书 Janice 组成（Janice 是唯一懂当地语言文化的人）。抵达后柯蒂斯发现能源链条是一层套一层的转包噩梦：Daaboo 族（大布族）的树早已耗尽，实际供电来自下层的 Moss People（苔藓人）；苔藓人又接自更下层；层层都要求"分润"。柯蒂斯为达成协议按当地习俗喝了自己的尿，最终换来的却是要给 Daaboo 族五万辆1996款丰田 RAV4 的荒唐承诺（下一层直接开价六万辆）。

真相在最底层揭晓：所谓"无限电池"其实是几个世纪前坠毁的外星飞船，能量来自船内可爱的小生物——Banks 当场命名为 Gobobears（高高熊/戈戈熊），它们通过有丝分裂快速繁殖。地心居民从未想过打开"电池"看一眼。柯蒂斯只带走两只（足够修复全美电网），其余放养，并对所有地心人翻脸走人。

带走 Gobobears 引来了真正的灾难：Arch-ians（阿尔奇人/阿奇人）舰队降临地球，宣称检测到稀有物质 Cantgetium，要"收割"整个星球，并当场杀死一只名叫 Crinkle Ear（皱耳）的高高熊。人类武器完全无效。Banks 为破解外星代码选择"降低怀疑度、增强适应力"，结果人格彻底 Archian 化，在敌舰上完成刺杀，找到通往引擎核心的热通风口弱点。地心各族坐电梯上地表加入战斗，但战力约等于零。最终由柯蒂斯宠爱的 Gobobear Two-Toes（双趾/两趾）——通过被亲吻抚摸学会了人类语言——自愿被当作弹药射入弱点，条件是让族人带着"爱"这一发现回家。清洁能源，零辐射，舰队被摧毁。

结局讽刺满分：能源危机毫无解决，反而更糟——美国还得给地心人建发电厂赔罪、把90年代款 RAV4 重新投产。柯蒂斯终于问"美国最大的能源消耗是什么"，答案是 AI 色情内容（占全国约95%用电）。于是他实施 AI 色情税：每位公民每天限用两个关键词，每家公司两个字母——正是他一开始拒绝的那种无聊而负责任的限电政策。

### 重要人物行动
- **柯蒂斯**：为逃避"无聊但负责任"的方案而发动整场远征；喝自己的尿以示"内心纯洁"；对 Gobobears 一见钟情式的疯狂亲吻（Mwah 贯穿全集）；面对灾难罕见地自我反省——"我只是想当个很酷的美国老爹"；结尾把政治羞辱转化为游戏 ID 素材。
- **Banks**：被柯蒂斯任命为"得力助手"，负责"确保美国行事光明磊落"；下单五万辆 RAV4 眼都不眨；为破解外星系统主动放弃怀疑与人格边界，"全 Archian 化"后在对方独白途中开枪爆头，被敌方军官临死前表白"我爱你"；结尾早已抢注三个"CumTaxer"系列游戏 ID。
- **O'Doyle**：极不情愿接下保护 Janice 的任务，为此走了一套解除对总统个人保护誓言的半精灵仪式；发现 Janice 的地心旧情人 Toomoo 出轨后威胁揍他，自以为在保护她，反被 Janice 痛斥"有毒的、交易式的情感表达方式"，并被解除保护职责；战斗中冒死把 Toomoo 拖回来给 Janice 作为道歉。

- **Dr. Keel**：全集唯一说对话的人，全集唯一被无视的人；被柯蒂斯在游戏里拉黑，被玩家集体"干掉"，还没被邀请参加远征。

## S01E05

# President Curtis S01E05 "David" — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
President Curtis is summoned to Camp David for a mandatory "test of mental will" against **David**, an ancient water witch bound to the grounds by FDR (the wheelchair Roosevelt). The pact: survive until sunrise without surrendering his "kingdom" (the presidency), or the witch goes free. Meanwhile, the White House staff — led by new Chief of Staff Roe Banks — contend with **The Lifers**, career staffers loyal to the institution, not the president. The witch uses mist-form mind manipulation to manifest each person's deepest fears and shames (Curtis's mother, his burrito-shop past, a ghost baby; Banks's fear of firing; the Lifers' secrets). Curtis initially dismisses the test, but the witch tricks him into verbally quitting during a hallucination of his old manager Chet Simmons. The staff rallies, using **collective vulnerability + copper + a Latin binding incantation** ("Nostra fragilitate, te alligamus") to trap the witch back in the swamp. Curtis emerges owning his insecurities and tells The Party he no longer cares about their re-election support.

## Important Character Actions
- **Curtis**: Dismisses the ritual, gets mentally dismantled by the witch (mother, Chet Simmons, ghost baby), briefly "quits" under hallucination, then leads the vulnerability charge to rebind the witch. Calls The Party and declares independence from their support.
- **Banks**: Feels excluded by Curtis's secrecy. Bonds with Lifers via the "undie flagpole hoist" initiation (wearing Curtis's Batman underwear). Admits fear of firing → earns Lifer shirt. Stands up to The Party on phone at end.
- **O'Doyle**: Brings hemlock/mirrored/nightshade bullets — useless against a water witch. Notes his "ab hole."

# President Curtis S01E10 "Timber" Summary

## Episode Plot
Paul Bunyan returns from the dead as a tree-man hybrid ("Botanicoid"), triggering a multi-state manhunt. President Curtis recklessly pursues him into Illinois, where Governor Beverly (Curtis's ex-wife) invokes **White Flag laws** granting monsters legal right to rehabilitation upon surrender. The trees are revealed to be a collective consciousness that claimed Bunyan's head as "raw power" and used him as a puppet to eat humans and eventually reach space. Curtis kills Bunyan (again), holds a brutally honest press conference admitting it was a personal vendetta, and the Lincoln's Hat bill passes unanimously with $10B in eco-legislation attached. The Tree collective escapes, vowing to return.
