### World Rules (Carried from Original Series)
- **Family Wizard Competition:** Only one wizard per family retains powers permanently
- **Mortal Secrecy:** Wizards must hide powers from mortal world
- **Wizard Laws:** Governed by Wizard Tribunal; violations risk power stripping
- **Power Transfer:** Wizards who marry mortals can transfer powers to siblings
- **WizTech:** Premier wizard education institution
- **Magical Creatures:** Werewolves, vampires, orcs, etc. exist in wizard world

## Overall Plot Premise

**Season 1 (21 episodes):** Fifteen years after *Wizards of Waverly Place*, Justin Russo lives a normal mortal life on Staten Island with wife Giada and sons Roman and Milo. His sister Alex arrives with Billie, a young wizard-in-training needing mentorship. Justin reluctantly returns to magic to train Billie while balancing principal duties and hiding magic from Giada. Antagonist Silas Evilini (son of Dr. Evilini) targets Billie's power.

**Season 2 (10 episodes):** Roman and Milo manifest wizard powers. Justin must train all three for the upcoming family wizard competition. Lord Morsus (masked wizard) emerges, seeking Billie's unique magic. Revelations: Billie is Alex's daughter with Damien (son of Lord Morsus), hidden at WizTech with memory erased. Alex sacrifices herself tackling Nicodemus into a portal.

**Season 3 (4-part finale):** With Alex trapped, Justin, Billie, Roman, Milo, and Damien (reformed) work to rescue her and defeat Lord Morsus permanently. Concludes the Russo family saga.

## Speaking Styles

### Justin Russo (David Henrie)
- **Tone:** Responsible, slightly neurotic, fatherly authority mixed with reluctance
- **Patterns:** Over-explains rules; "As principal..." / "As your wizard teacher..."
- **Catchphrases:** "Everything is not what it seems" (theme song reference); "I'm the principal!"
- **Chinese Voice:** Formal but accessible; uses 标准语 with occasional 语气词 (吧/呢) when flustered

### Alex Russo (Selena Gomez)
- **Tone:** Sarcastic, rebellious, street-smart, emotionally guarded
- **Patterns:** Dry one-liners; deflects with humor; "Whatever" energy
- **Chinese Voice:** 口语化, 俏皮, 略带嘲讽; uses 语气词 (呗/啦/嘛); 简短有力

### Billie (Janice LeAnn Brown)
- **Tone:** Earnest, curious, slightly insecure but determined; teen energy
- **Patterns:** Asks lots of questions; "Wait, what?" / "That's so cool!"
- **Chinese Voice:** 青春活泼; 标准普通话 with 语气词 (呀/哇/诶); 好奇句式多

### Roman Russo (Alkaio Thiele)
- **Tone:** Confident, slightly arrogant older brother; protective
- **Patterns:** Teases Milo; "Check this out"; tries to impress Billie
- **Chinese Voice:** 稍显自大的大哥哥语气; 用"小子/弟弟"称呼Milo

### Milo Russo (Max Matenko)
- **Tone:** Goofy, literal-minded, sweet, chaotic good
- **Patterns:** Misunderstands metaphors; random non-sequiturs; food-obsessed
- **Chinese Voice:** 呆萌直球; 语速稍快; 字面理解梗多

### Winter (Taylor Cora)
- **Tone:** Deadpan, loyal, grounded mortal perspective
- **Patterns:** Dry observations; "You guys are weird"; voice of reason
- **Chinese Voice:** 吐槽役; 语气平淡但犀利; "你们巫师真麻烦"

### Giada Russo (Mimi Gianopulos)
- **Tone:** Sharp, investigative, skeptical, loving but no-nonsense
- **Patterns:** Reporter instincts; "Fact-check: ..."; cuts through magical excuses
- **Chinese Voice:** 干练利落; 职业女性口吻; 面对魔法时先怀疑后接受

### Lord Morsus / Nicodemus (Tobias Jelinek)
- **Tone:** Menacing, theatrical, grandiose, manipulative
- **Patterns:** "Soon..." / "The power will be mine"; formal archaic vocabulary
- **Chinese Voice:** 反派腔调; 古风/书面语混用; 音量控制、停顿制造压迫感

## Cultural Context

### Disney Channel Sitcom Conventions
- **Multi-camera format:** Live studio audience feel; laugh track appropriate
- **Episode structure:** A-plot (magic) + B-plot (school/family); converge at climax
- **Moral lessons:** Responsibility, honesty, family first, be yourself
- **Slapstick + wordplay:** Physical comedy + puns; translation must preserve both
- **Teen speak:** Current slang dates fast; use timeless "youth vernacular" equivalents

### Chinese Localization Precedents (Original Series)
- **Title:** 《韦弗利街的巫师》 (Disney+ China) / 《韦弗利巫师》 (Taiwan) / 《魔法师之道》 (early HK)
- **Character names:** Consistent phonetic transliteration (Justin→贾斯汀, Alex→亚历克斯)
- **Spell incantations:** Rhyming couplets adapted for Chinese rhyme/rhythm
- **"Everything is not what it seems":** 「事物非表面所见」/「一切并非看上去那样」
- **Family wizard concept:** "家庭巫师" / "传承巫师" established terminology

### Translation Sensitivities
- **Cultural references:** NYC/Staten Island specifics → keep or footnote
- **Food references:** Sandwich shop, Italian/Mexican heritage foods
- **School system:** Middle school, principal, detention → Chinese equivalents
- **Pop culture nods:** Marvel, TikTok, influencer culture → localize or preserve
- **Gender/identity:** Show includes diverse casting; avoid gendered assumptions in Chinese
