# S06E02

## Plot
The episode runs three parallel storylines around the business growing up:

1. **Lake District with Les.** Richard drives to his oldest friend's farm to tinker with Les's ancient Riley race car, after selling his own race car for £15,000 and effectively ending Cog Racing. The pair fit an FIA-approved catch tank to the crankcase breather (a race-supplied "old polish tin" adapted as a bulkhead oil collector), drill a bracket from wood, and head to Silverstone for an Auto Solo.
2. **Two customer jobs at the workshop.** Toby's pre-war Sunbeam (overheating, suspected head gasket, pitted exhaust valves) and a rare Shelby Mustang for a Danish car museum, found by Nikolaj in Sweden and brought over for repaint.
3. **Copenhagen.** Richard travels to Tivoli Gardens with the finished Mustang for a Danish breakfast TV appearance, then to the museum for the unveiling and press event.

## Important character actions
- Richard announces a "grown up" step: Roy is brought in to run the workshop and deal with clients, while Richard takes the office and stays "in the driving seat," doing the front-of-house PR himself ("the song and dance bit at the front"). Richard later admits Roy frees him to have fun, and that Roy's Sunbeam experience lets them face customers confidently.
- Les: farm visit (cows' milk fed to pigs, quad bike ride nearly tipping over backwards), bought a tractor with no key, engine not bolted in, no air in the tyres, bought for lawn-mowing work around a customer's big house. He hosts Richard, provides the catch tank, and drives the Riley in the Auto Solo.
- Richard drives the Shelby Mustang briefly (left-hand drive, "pulls like a train"), kisses a cone, and tells Nikolaj the car's owner wants it repainted to look like 1965, in Ken Miles livery.
- Jamie does the glass-fibre bonnet work (Dremel out a spider crack, filler, sand side-to-side to preserve the swage line) and the topcoat; Isaac transports the car by van and trailer.
- John and Matt are on the team; Matt uses a blowtorch in the toilet; Jamie sprays paint with a Pet Shop Boys playlist on repeat for ~17.5 hours.
- Cameron at a precision engineering shop refaces one usable exhaust valve; Roy inspects the rest and reports "one good, five wonky."
- Terry the sticker maker corrects the racing numbers (the 9 is the wrong length) for the Ken Miles stripes, which are applied as stickers rather than paint so the blue doesn't need re-spraying after race damage.
- Richard appears on Danish breakfast TV, talks Top Gear, and uses the Mustang as leverage to plug The Smallest Cog.

## S06E04

# Richard Hammond's Workshop — S06E04 Summary

## Episode plot
Richard Hammond's classic car restoration workshop, The Smallest Cog, is turning five, and he wants to "grow up" and grow the business. He tasks new manager Roy with stabilizing and profiting the workshop while he focuses on events and diversification under the "brand Cog" umbrella. Richard visits nearby Lowe Classics, run by Oli, to study their diversified model — car sales, mechanical work, storage, monthly car meets, and a cafe that draws petrolheads who might leave with a car. Meanwhile, the workshop takes in a Top Gun–themed Kawasaki GPZ900R replica bike owned by Andy, who wants it restored to proper movie-spec paint and decals. Jamie plastic-welds the cracked front valance, Jon handles the paint and stickers, and Jon turns up to the handover dressed as Top Gun's Maverick. Richard also works after hours on his personal Morgan three-wheeler, deliberately leaving a distressed, wrinkled finish under lacquer. Lewis organizes a Japanese car meet to prove the Cog brand can draw a crowd without Richard present, securing four standout Japanese cars from Lowe Classics (including a Datsun 240Z with a Watson Racing American V8) for display and sale. The episode ends on a crisis: Richard's Jaguar E-Type, meant to be the Cog's first show-car restoration, returns from soda blasting almost completely destroyed — bare, rusted "Swiss cheese" metal with shoddy prior repairs — and Jamie and Roy advise it won't be finished this year.

## Important character actions
- Richard: pushes diversification, visits Lowe Classics, personally paints his Morgan after hours to stay on Roy's good side, skips the Japanese meet so the brand can stand on its own, and faces the bad news on his E-Type.
- Roy: new manager; stabilizes finances, reprimands Richard (roleplaying as "El Capitan") about Jamie and Jon missing morning workshop hours, and co-assesses the E-Type damage.
- Jamie: plastic-welds the Kawasaki valance, helps Richard prep and paint the Morgan, and co-leads the E-Type inspection.
- Jon: restores and paints the Top Gun bike, dresses as Maverick (dog tags, glasses — "method painting"), and applies the stickers.
- Lewis: runs the Japanese car meet, arranges the airfield-style Top Gun bike handover with a flypast, and manages the chaotic event-day arrivals.
- Andy: owner of the Kawasaki replica, delighted with the restoration and runway handover.
- Oli: Lowe Classics owner, shows Richard the diversified model and later supplies Japanese cars for the meet.
- Hadrian Spooner: landowner who permits the meet but refuses to move his steam engines; his steam engine ends up blocking the prime parking zone and must be moved by the team in a risky operation.

## Newly established names/terms
- The Smallest Cog — Richard's workshop brand
- brand Cog / Operation Diversicog — diversification and events push
- Lowe Classics — diversification benchmark visited by Richard
- Caffeine&Machine — event hosts (Zog and Phil) Richard arranges to observe
- Kawasaki GPZ900R Top Gun replica — Andy's bike project
- Operation Get Ready Super Quickly — Japanese meet setup
- E-Type show car — Richard's first full restoration project, now in doubt

## Continuity-relevant facts
- The workshop is five years old and still in business.
- Roy is the new manager at The Smallest Cog; it is "not haemorrhaging money at the same rate."
- Lowe Classics has no body shop or paint booth — a potential B&B (business-to-business) referral channel for The Smallest Cog's paint/body work.
- Jamie and Jon were absent from the workshop all morning for the bike handover, creating a productivity friction with Roy.
- The E-Type restoration is the Cog's planned show car and "calling card"; after soda blasting, little remains, and Richard accepts it won't be done this year.
- Richard's Morgan three-wheeler is a long-running personal project still unfinished at home.
- The team concludes "brand Cog stands on its own" after attendees say they would have come even without Richard.

## Unresolved threads
