# S04E01 Summary

**Plot:** Ted Lasso is now back in Kansas City (Missouri side), working part-time as a grocery clerk at Foch's Fine Foods (福奇氏精品食品) and living for Henry. He gets caught on camera stealing snacks with coworker Tyler (a self-confessed kleptomaniac who hasn't paid for razors in three years), meets former player Joe Conrad and his family, then gets called to Manning Elementary with Michelle because Henry wrote anti-government poetry in his notebook (actually Public Enemy lyrics the principal doesn't recognize). Rebecca, Keeley, and Higgins surprise him in Kansas, eat KC barbecue, and — after a "10 seconds, no work talk" catch-up game — offer him the job of coaching Richmond's new women's team: "Last time I hired you for all the wrong reasons. This time I want to hire you for all the right ones." Ted asks for time to think. They watch Henry's soccer game (he gets benched for showboating), visit the Negro Leagues Baseball Museum when the jazz museum is closed, tour CPKC Stadium for a KC Current match, and at the airport Ted declines — "I can't take the job. I got to stay put. This is home." Rebecca counters: "Sometimes home is just a state of mind." Ted's mother Dottie had earlier pushed him to take it ("It's good for a boy to see his father do something he loves" — a callback to Ted's own father taking him to work). The episode ends with a note from Rebecca — "Ted, let the tea steep. —R" — and a final "Come on, man." / "Nope." exchange, leaving the decision open.

## S04E02


# Ted Lasso S04E02 — Episode Summary

Ted's first day coaching the Richmond Women's Team begins at Mae's pub (Mae now charges rent upstairs; Ted is still living there). He gives Mae a case of Gates BBQ for Rebecca, the men's team kicks off against Liverpool this weekend, and a brand-new press conference introduces the women's team to the media.

## Main Plot

- Assistant coach Alice Chilton resigns on Ted's first day, claiming she was passed over for the head coach job. She wants bigger things; her last day is Friday. She hands Rebecca a long strategic report (the "Higgins Report" / KSSASLVPWFLTPLRHBAR) and asks for a letter of recommendation.
- Ted meets the squad in their cramped shared locker room, which they have to share with the academy on Mon/Wed/Fri and every other Saturday. Chilton is cold and abrasive; Ted tries to befriend her with a Beastie Boys reference, to no effect.
- Ted's press conference for the women's team is constantly derailed. Reporters keep asking about the men's season opener vs. Liverpool, the Champions League, and Jamie Tartt's departure to Barcelona. When Ted finally gets a women's-team question, a reporter asks if the men are underpaid, which Ted refuses to answer. Rebecca awkwardly holds forth on feminism, the future being "female," and is mocked with names like Ghislaine Maxwell and Eva Braun.
- At Rebecca's suggestion, Ted has been reading a bell hooks book (revealed to be "Ain't I a Woman" or similar). He picks up where Chilton left off by getting to know the players: striker Josie Gordon (from Watford, 0.78 xG/90), couple Mady and Margy, and the twin center-backs Siobhan and Niamh. The most intriguing is goalkeeper Katie "Boots" Quinn, who refuses to wear gloves. Boots prefers feeling the ball, the pain at the dentist, the cold, everything unfiltered.
- Keeley plays hardball with Rebecca to get a new women's locker room, securing a "Bait and Switch": she asks for 300k (denied), then asks for the men's room (refused), before settling on the visitors' locker room, which Rebecca agrees to ("I don't fucking care"). Coach Kent bakes high-protein, no-sugar, beef-tallow biscuits; Roy brings them. Keeley tells Ted she's now with Roy but invites him to stay friends.
- Ted asks Roy for advice on Beard, who is moping. Beard wanted to be alone, and Ted fears losing him. Roy says keeping two people who belong together apart is bad for everyone; he also says Keeley's heart "is with another" (i.e., Beard).
- Roy preps the visitors' locker room with elaborate pranks, including a trained rat named Jonathan from a Harry Potter play he saw with Phoebe. He also clears the gum from the showerheads and fixes the lights so Ted can address the team on match day.
- Josie tears her ACL in training, ending her season. Ted accompanies her to the hospital; Caroline Seaward (the team's head physio) tends to the team. Rebecca quotes Eleanor Roosevelt, confides to Higgins that she's "lost touch" with what being pro-women means, and reads his women's team viability report.
- Rebecca fires Kent, citing a long-ago Section 6B bylaws violation (he said "wrong call" to a ref at Villa Park); she then reveals the visitors' locker room is now the women's team room with a swanky reveal. The team finds eight showers and unlimited tampons.
- Higgins finishes his report: the women's team has a low chance of long-term viability and could hurt the men's program. Rebecca says she'll burn it and dump the ashes in the Thames.
- Ted asks Chilton to stay, saying he wants a woman on staff and that her detailed dossier is why he knows the team. He recites her playing-career arc (Lewisham, Arsenal Academy, captain material, ACL at 22, tore the other one back, two years later tore the left one again, career ended). He gives her a "going-away gift" (later reclaimed), then asks her to stay as assistant. She accepts.

## Important Character Actions

- Ted meets the team, attends the press conference, and recruits Chilton to stay. He brings Gates BBQ brisket to Rebecca and notices a bull statue the team's gift came from "Mr. Gladstone." He notes "everything seems heavier" around women's football.

- Keeley secures the locker room through a bait-and-switch on Rebecca. She also tells Ted she is with Roy now but wants to remain friends.

- Caroline Seaward, the new head physio for the women's team, is introduced. She is mute/vow-of-silence (she cannot talk), wears a hat and sunglasses, and has a particular interest in Ted's "middle-aged man" health, including "bum stuff."

# Ted Lasso S04E03 — Episode Summary

**Plot:** With the women's team's first match against Hayes & Yeading just days away, Richmond has been reduced to zero available strikers: Josie Gordon is out for the season after her ACL (S04E02), and a second striker — Keeley's old teammate — has just discovered she is pregnant. Alice Chilton half-jokingly suggests "open tryouts," Ted picks up the bit, and Rebecca greenlights the idea. While Keeley self-funds £4,000 worth of rally towels Rebecca won't approve, the season's first shareholders' meeting goes off the rails when the men's fan-base revolts against the women's team, citing an online "viability report" the executives claim never to have read.

Meanwhile, Ted tries to convince his ex-teammate Gemma — a retired striker who quit to study law and now won't speak to Alice — to come out of retirement; Rebecca jets to Canary Wharf to meet Pinter & Sons CEO Elaine Pinter in person to save the sponsorship; and Roy wrestles with his niece Phoebe's first day of school in a new uniform. Ted runs the open tryouts, the team shows up in DIY sparkly kits because the real ones are stuck in a Derek-related shipping snafu, Gemma shows up unannounced to play just in time, and Richmond win their debut 1-0 on her debut goal — broadcast only by stream and commentated by a faux "Rebecca Lowe / Jill Scott" duo.

**Important character actions:**

- Ted pitches open tryouts to Rebecca on the floor outside her office (he tripped and lay there "reminding himself about the value of knocking"); Rebecca approves once Matthijs de Cagliari — her on-off boyfriend, in town from a trip he has spent trying to have sex in her office — endorses it. Ted runs the tryouts himself in a tracksuit, drops a "second time's a charm" malaprop, and is the one to tell Shannon she didn't make it ("I'm good at this stuff… I've broken a lot of hearts out there").

She later takes the train to Canary Wharf alone — Ted calls her bluff that she knows how to handle Elaine ("You people bankroll wars" / "Wars are profitable") — and closes the meeting with a "give us a chance / I'll make a call" speech that lands Elaine ("I was wrong about Brexit").
- Keeley is hit by three crises at once: she's just started dating Roy, she self-funded the £4,000 rally towels Rebecca vetoed (and plans to bill the club back), and the team's official kits are stuck on a truck after a logistics worker called Derek ignored her instructions. Roy reminds her she's the Vanity Fair "Woman on Top," not the failed report.
