# R.J. Decker S02E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Season 2 opens with R.J. Decker (PI, ex-con on conditional release) helping his old prison friend **Stevie Marsh**, who's being blackmailed by **Chip Lankershim** into driving for the **"Bottomless Bandits"**—bank robbers who hit branches pantsless as a distraction. R.J. and Stevie stake out Chip's house to steal his phone and destroy the blackmail video, but find Chip and two other Bandits dead. FBI Special Agent **Winwood** (Mel's former fling) takes the case, identifying the Bandits' original driver **Liam Jansen** via his dog **Zardoz** (named after the Sean Connery film). Jansen reveals a mysterious **"Mr. Big"** (initials **P.C.M.**) armed the crew and ordered the hit when Chip got greedy. R.J. and Catherine deduce **P.C.M. = Panhandle Citizen's Militia**, an anti-government paramilitary group. The guns trace to an ATF raid on gun modifier **Ezra Peters**—and the banana-vape scent on Chip fingers **Special Agent Clark** as Mr. Big, a dirty Fed running bank robberies for off-book cash. Clark tries to kill Mel and Virgil Osgood (ex-militia junkyard owner); R.J. warns Mel via landline. Clark is arrested. Stevie takes a deal: 90 days rehab instead of prison.

## Important Character Actions
- **R.J.** risks his conditional release to help Stevie; identifies Clark via the banana-vape clue; calls Mel on a landline to save her.
- **Mel** stands up to Winwood's jurisdictional bullying; arrests Clark.
- **Stevie** confesses his alcoholism (drinking since release to cope with prison trauma); accepts rehab.
- **Catherine** pushes R.J. to be a friend to Emi, not just "give her space."
- **Emi** admits she needs a friend after her father's murder and the collapse of Ochoa Development.
- **Winwood** tries to sideline R.J.; Mel shuts her down ("Not in charge here. I am").

## Episode S02E03 Summary — R.J. Decker

### Plot
Emi Ochoa is arrested for murdering her father, State Senator Victor Ochoa. The task force claims to have found the murder weapon — a 9mm Beretta compact hidden in a wall vent of her empty condo — with ballistics matching the victim. The heated pool at the crime scene prevented narrowing time of death, undermining her party alibi. R.J., Catherine, Mel, and Wish work to prove she was framed.

A mysterious man, **Augustine**, approaches R.J. at Wish's bar, offering $50,000 to find the real killer. He represents **Nan la Selle**, a Haitian gang that was bribing Victor to keep ports open for smuggling U.S. weapons (AR-15s, RPGs) hidden in "toy" crates via the charity front **World of Hope** (rabbit/flamingo emblem). R.J. investigates Augustine's rental car, finding a Lucky Bird Inn valet tag.

R.J. and Mel raid a World of Hope warehouse, arresting gun dealer **Doyle Koven**. He reveals he was coached to lie about selling a gun to Emi two years ago — the break-in story she told only to DA **Janis Lake**. Emi confronts Lake, accusing her of the murder. R.J. and Mel deduce Lake buried the gun under a pickleball court under construction at **Coconut Harbor** (where Lake's parents live), then dug it up and planted it when Augustine arrived in town. Forensic residue on the gun (acrylic, sand, blue pigment) matches the court surface material.

Confronted with the evidence and the threat of Augustine's "justice," Lake confesses to killing Victor. All charges against Emi are dropped. Augustine leaves a package for R.J. — unclear if threat or gesture.

### Plot
R.J.'s conditional release officially ends Friday; Wish throws him a celebration party ("cheese from the fancy section") with Mel, Catherine, Sofia, and Emi. Wish teases R.J. about "vibing" with CSU tech Trudy Harris. Meanwhile, R.J. takes a new client: **Arlen "Flea" Voss**, director of the **Everglades Bass Championship (EBC)**, whose previous lake-watcher **Finch Palmer** disappeared on patrol. Finch's empty boat was found drifting with an empty whiskey bottle. Voss wants R.J. on land as a set of eyes for the $250K-prize tournament.

At the tournament expo, R.J. runs into his estranged father **Reggie Decker**, now flogging **MuskMaster Select** fish attractant as one of **Dickie Dupree**'s sponsors — a serial winner whose streak is fueling the cheating surge. Reggie is defensive, lets slip his past failures (alpaca farm, wind farm, teeth-whitening litigation), and introduces R.J. to old buddy **Eddie**. The conversation confirms Reggie left R.J. and his brothers when they were teens after the divorce, vanishing in and out of state chasing schemes like french-fry-grease engines, truffle orchards, and blimp advertising — sending flowers only after R.J.'s mom nearly died in a car wreck. Despite the bitterness, Reggie reveals Finch was found shot twice in the chest and stashed up a tree.

Mel (working Finch's homicide) catches R.J. at the tree; together they question Dickie, who gives a weak alibi (he claims he was asleep at cousin Larry's place in Pembroke Pines, but Larry's a long-haul trucker who was out of town). Finch's camera was found at home — meaning he wasn't photographing cheaters the night he died. Finch's separated wife gives Mel an alibi (Orlando wedding) and notes Finch's "conspiracy-minded" friend group, particularly **Merle Crews**.

Reggie is brought in for questioning and cleared: he and Dickie were at Dickie's storage unit heat-gunning Kaneshiro rod labels onto competitor rods — corroborated by the night attendant. The medical examiner confirms Finch's bullet trajectory was even — victim and shooter at the same height — and that no one climbed the tree (all low branches intact), so the corpse had to be lifted into place. Finch's camera showed him photographing someone the previous night (Tuesday), suggesting blackmail material against a tournament cheat.

Merle Crews is tracked down via his mom **Dottie**'s address (the bartender preserved his smashed phone). Merle spins an outrageous story: **Finch discovered an actual UAP underwater, was abducted by a beam of light, and the body in the morgue is a "replicant."** The photo on Merle's phone shows a beam lifting something and carries valid timestamps. The image is consistent with the Drone Nova heavy-lift prototype R.J. and Mel encountered. **Riz Bashir** and **Walt Taylor**, former **Argus** defense-contractor employees now running **Drone Nova**, are questioned. Their website touts a "next-gen autonomous heavy lifting platform" — a drone big enough to move a person. The crash test dummy pulled from the lake was their prototype. They have motive (Argus lawsuit over alleged stolen tech). Merle is dismissed as a crank.

At R.J.'s release party, Reggie pulls off a master con: he tapes the Drone Nova conversation, gets Bashir and Taylor to admit they killed Finch and reveal where the gun is hidden, then has Mel's team waiting outside. Reggie negotiates himself a 3% stake in the company, then gives it up for the satisfaction of proving he can still pull one off — without even needing a clipboard. The episode closes with father and son fishing together, duetting the Alan Parsons Project "Eye in the Sky" hook. Reggie hints he'll move on to a "self-cleaning toilet wand" venture but says he wants to "reassess" what he really wants for once.

## S02E05

# R.J. Decker S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Sam Ellery, manager of Pelican Bend Senior Villas, hires R.J. Decker believing he's trapped in a time loop ("The Matrix meets Groundhog Day"). Daily "glitches" include: identical package deliveries, waking to Dolly Parton's "Here You Come Again," a daily "meteorite" (small explosives on rocks), and neighbor Burt Landry dying in a boat explosion each night—only to reappear alive next morning. R.J. investigates with help from his father Reggie and Wish, systematically debunking each phenomenon. The "meteorites" are staged charges; the boat explosions use mannequins until the final night when the real Burt is aboard and dies. The gaslighter is Mack Reddington, Sam's neighbor and a theater stagehand with pyrotechnics experience. Mack wants Sunlux (a hotel company) to buy his underwater mortgage property, but Sunlux's offer is contingent on acquiring Pelican Bend—requiring Sam's commitment so his sister Lila inherits and sells. Mack paid Burt with Sunlux gift cards to stay away nightly; when Burt tried to quit, Mack murdered him midday and staged the nightly explosion. R.J. catches Mack using UV-enhanced fish oil (Reggie's "MuskMaster Select") on Sam's spark plugs—residue on Mack's hands under blacklight provides proof. Mack is arrested; Sam voluntarily checks into St. Barnes Psychiatric; Lila refuses to sell Pelican Bend.

- **Reggie Decker**: Assists surveillance, devises the MuskMaster/spark-plug trap, meets Trudy (CSU tech), ultimately decides to visit grandson Tommy instead of pursuing PI work.
- **Wish Aiken**: Provides bar sanctuary for Burt, backs R.J. on stakeout.

- **Catherine Delacroix**: Supports R.J., prepares care package for Sam.
- **Sam Ellery**: Hires R.J., spirals under gaslighting, ultimately chooses voluntary commitment.
- **Lila Ellery**: Initially suspected, reveals Sunlux offered double market value, ultimately rejects sale to protect Sam's home.
- **Mack Reddington**: Gaslights Sam for weeks, murders Burt, caught via forensic residue.
