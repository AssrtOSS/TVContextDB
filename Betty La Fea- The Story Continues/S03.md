## S03E03

# S03E03 Summary — Betty La Fea: The Story Continues

## Episode Plot
Betty juggles a clothing-inventory crisis at Eco Moda while her personal life erupts: her father Hermes announces he's marrying Sofía López (Betty's mother's friend) this week; Armando shows up unannounced, apologizes for the Freddy/disguise fiasco, seals a cork as a pledge to stop meddling, and proposes they fly to New York together to meet the Spaniard Serrano face-to-face. Meanwhile, Patricia drags Marcela to the notary expecting a wedding to potato-seller Pascual — but Pascual ghosts her (second time). Armando gets a tarot reading from Mariana Valdés; he draws The Lovers upright, but it's revealed reversed — signaling "destructive indecision" and a love triangle. The episode ends with Armando and Betty in New York, while crisis-manager Román Duarte (Stop Crisis, Madrid) arrives at Eco Moda looking for Betty.

## Important Character Actions
- **Hermes**: declares love for Sofía, proposes immediate marriage, moves into Betty's house (plumbing disaster at his place), brings grandfather Lázaro's ashes and Betty's framed degrees.
- **Betty**: refuses Hugo's 50% price slash on her designs; gives blessing to Hermes/Sofía; accepts Armando's New York trip as friends.
- **Armando**: apologizes to Betty, initiates New York plan, gets reversed Lovers tarot card, dismisses Mariana's warning.
- **Marcela**: stood up at notary by Pascual (again); Patricia buys own expensive dress for the non-wedding.
- **Nicolás**: drinking heavily; Betty confronts him.
- **Freddy**: acting "weird" toward Aura María; she's determined to find out why.
- **Román Duarte**: introduces himself as crisis-management/brand-rehab expert from Stop Crisis (Madrid).
- **Mila**: staying with Betty; designs shoes complimented by Sofía.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Román Duarte** — Stop Crisis manager, crisis-management strategist & brand-rehabilitation expert (Madrid).
- **Stop Crisis** — Spanish firm specializing in crisis management and brand rehabilitation.
- **Serrano** — the Spaniard in New York Betty/Armando must meet to resolve Eco Moda crisis.
- **Pascual Pabón** — full name of "the potato seller" (recurring).

## S03E09

# S03E09 — Alliances & Ambushes / 结盟与伏击

- **Betty**: Rejects sheikh's lowered offer; in therapy, admits she can't forgive past trauma, considers Michel's New York study offer.
- **Ángel**: Reveals he possesses ALL Hugo's designs; accepts representation by a Madrid-connected crisis manager who calls "a scandal a good seed."
- **Patricia**: Serves Nicolás divorce papers at his doorstep — "what's given cannot be taken back."
- **Hugo**: Theatrically mocks Armando's sexuality; his lawsuit jeopardizes the sale.
- **Michel**: Promoted to executive producer; tells Armando the only thing between him and Betty is "death."

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Patricia actively sabotaged Pascual–Marcela reconciliation.
- Hugo's plagiarism lawsuit directly devalues Ecomoda in the sheikh's eyes.

## S03E02

## S03E02 Summary — Betty La Fea: The Story Continues

### Episode Plot
The episode opens amid the fallout from the **Hugo Lombardi plagiarism scandal**: Ángel Pulido has publicly accused Hugo of stealing his "Tribute" collection designs, triggering social-media cancellation, protests at Eco Moda points of sale, and a looming lawsuit. Betty, overwhelmed by the corporate crisis, her strained marriage, and daughter Mila's return, begins therapy but suffers a panic attack mid-session. Meanwhile, Hugo spirals after discovering Ángel deleted every photo of them together from social media. In a pivotal boardroom moment, Betty reverses her plan to step down and reclaims the presidency with Armando and Nicolás's support. The episode closes with Betty announcing she has bought her own house and will live alone at 52 — a declaration of independence that shocks her family but earns the Cuartel's cheers. Armando, unsettled, secretly dispatches Freddy to tail Betty (which she catches him lying about). Hugo, unraveling, trashes the showroom as customers return merchandise en masse.

### Important Character Actions
- **Betty**: Starts therapy; has a panic attack; reclaims Eco Moda presidency; buys a house solo; confronts Armando about spying on her.
- **Armando**: Supports Betty's return as president; lies about sending Freddy to follow her; shows jealousy masked as indifference.
- **Hugo Lombardi**: Breaks down over Ángel's social-media erasure; files police report for stolen sketches/computer; insists Ángel isn't the thief; later destroys inventory in a meltdown.
- **Ángel Pulido** (off-screen): Accuses Hugo of plagiarism; hires a lawyer; deletes all shared photos; becomes the target of Freddy's surveillance.
- **Freddy**: Recruited by Armando to shadow Betty; reports her movements to "Mancho" (nickname for Armando).
- **Mila**: Briefly checks on Betty; supports her mother's independence.
- **Cuartel (Patricia, Aura, Bertha, Sandra, Carmen, Mariana, Sofía)**: Rally around Betty's house announcement; plan a housewarming; podcast topic "Independence after 50" seeded.
- **Nicolás**: Advises caution on lawsuits (bankruptcy risk); backs Betty's presidency return.

- **Ángel Pulido (安赫尔·普利多)** — New designer/antagonist; Hugo's former lover; accuses Hugo of IP theft.

## S03E06

# Betty La Fea: The Story Continues — S03E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Román Duarte, a figure from Betty's past (20 years ago) whom she deeply resents, appears at Eco Moda claiming to be a reformed marketing/crisis-management expert offering to save the company. Betty violently rejects him, triggering a flashback confrontation where Román apologizes for past trauma but refuses to beg. Meanwhile, the arbitration hearing over the **Tribute Collection** designs reaches a crisis: Hugo Lombardi, pressured in the courtroom setting, suffers a creative block and cannot reproduce his own sketches, giving the opposing side (Ángel / "Exiliatus caelis" and Mr. Pulido) a decisive advantage. Armando and Hugo hatch a desperate scheme: Armando will **seduce Ángel** (who is attracted to men) to undermine the opposition. Separately, the **Cuartel's podcast** airs a live episode where Marcela Valencia is guest-interviewed about Pascual standing her up at the notary to return to his ex-wife Estefanía; Patricia calls Pascual mid-cruise and publicly excoriates him. The episode closes with **Michel**, a charming French listener of the podcast, arriving unannounced at Patricia's home; he and Patricia share wine, reminisce about Cartagena and New York, and acknowledge unresolved romantic tension — "the last Mendoza and the last Valencia."

## Important Character Actions
- **Betty**: Rejects Román Duarte's offer to help Eco Moda; refuses his apology; later shares vulnerable conversation with Michel about being the last of their families to care about the company.
- **Román Duarte**: Returns after 20 years; presents himself as crisis-management expert; apologizes for past trauma but insists on dignity; Mila tries to persuade him to stay.
- **Hugo Lombardi**: Fails to draw Tribute Collection designs under arbitration pressure; claims creation is "intimate" and requires his workshop/shower; admits shock at seeing Ángel.
- **Armando**: Proposes and agrees to seduce Ángel to win the lawsuit; practices "gym seduction" routine with Freddy and Hugo coaching.
- **Marcela**: Appears on Cuartel podcast; publicly recounts Pascual's betrayal (stood her up at notary for Estefanía).
- **Patricia**: Calls Pascual on live podcast from his honeymoon cruise; confronts him brutally; hosts Michel at her home; shares intimate evening with him.
- **Mila**: Brought Román to Eco Moda without asking Betty; apologizes to Román; urges him not to leave.
- **Michel**: French podcast listener; arrives at Patricia's unannounced; spends night drinking and reminiscing; implies desire to "restart" their past story.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Román Duarte** — Betty's past antagonist; "neighborhood gangster" per Nicolás; now self-styled marketing/crisis expert.
- **Exiliatus caelis** — Hugo's mock-Latin nickname for Ángel ("Lucifer"); used in arbitration.
- **ULTRAGE** — *Unión de Trabajadores Libres Jubilados de Ecomoda* (Union of Retired Free Workers of Eco Moda); Hugo invokes it theatrically.
- **Tribute Collection (Colección Homenaje / 致敬系列)** — Central IP dispute; designs allegedly stolen.
- **Michel** — Frenchman; listener of Cuartel podcast; history with Patricia in Cartagena and New York.
- **"Bettycave" (贝蒂洞)** — Román's nickname for Betty's home/office sanctuary.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Betty and Román share a traumatic history from ~20 years ago (original series era).
- Hugo's creative process is established as fragile outside his workshop/shower — weaponized by opposing counsel.
- Pascual (Carlos "Pity" Camacho) married Estefanía (his ex-wife) after jilting Marcela at the notary; they are on a honeymoon cruise.
- Patricia is divorced ("divorcee house"); her podcast with the Cuartel has international listeners.
- Armando and Hugo's "seduce Ángel" plan is a secret alliance excluding Betty and Mila.
- Betty and Marcela acknowledge being "the last Mendoza and the last Valencia" who truly care about Eco Moda — callback to family lineages.
- References to **Cartagena** and **New York** arcs from previous seasons/episodes (shared history between Patricia and Michel).

## Jokes / References Useful Later
- Hugo: "Creation is an intimate act. I can only create in my workshop or… in the shower."
- Armando practicing squats/curls to "mark territory" near Ángel at the gym — Hugo coaching him like a dating coach.
- Patricia: "You can't even touch him with potato peel" (Colombian idiom: you're not in his league).
- Cuartel podcast format: "from love to hate, there are potato wedges, diced potatoes, potato omelet, any type of potato."
- Michel: "Our story should be called *Gone With the Time* (*随光阴逝去*)."
- Hugo's theatrical invocation of ULTRAGE and "Mephistopheles/Quasimodo/Notre-Dame" references.

## Unresolved Threads
- **Arbitration outcome**: Hugo's failure to draw designs heavily favors Ángel/Pulido; Armando's seduction plan is the only counter-move.
- **Román Duarte's fate**: Rejected by Betty, urged to stay by Mila; unclear if he remains in Bogotá or departs.
- **Patricia & Michel**: Romantic tension reignited; Michel suggests "restarting" their past; Patricia's divorce status vs. his invitation to share her home.
- **Pascual & Estefanía**: On honeymoon cruise; Pascual humiliated publicly by Patricia; potential fallout for Eco Moda (he is an executive/partner).
- **Mila's secret engagement** (referenced in earlier episodes): Still undisclosed to Betty; Román's presence may intersect.
- **Eco Moda's 50% devaluation**: Mentioned in magazine; existential threat looming over all characters.
