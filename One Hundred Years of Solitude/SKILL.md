## World Setting

**Macondo (马孔多 / 馬孔多)**
- Fictional Colombian river town, founded by José Arcadio Buendía after leaving Riohacha
- Isolated by swamp/mountains; connected to outside world only by gypsy caravans, later railroad
- Microcosm of Latin American history: colonization, civil wars, foreign capital (banana company), dictatorship, massacre, oblivion
- Magical realism permeates: insomnia plague, levitating priest, raining flowers, ghosts, prophetic manuscripts, pig-tailed child

**Historical Allegories**
- Thousand Days' War (Colombian civil war 1899–1902) → Colonel's 32 uprisings
- Banana Massacre (1928, Ciénaga) → Banana Company massacre, 3,000 dead denied by government
- Conservative/Liberal party strife → endless cycles of rigged elections, violence
- Foreign exploitation → American banana company, segregated zones, rubber boom
- Biblical/Genesis parallels: founding, incest taboo, flood (rain 4 years 11 months), apocalypse

## Overall Plot Premise

Seven generations of the Buendía family in Macondo, from founding to annihilation. Patriarch José Arcadio Buendía dreams of a city of mirrors, establishes Macondo. Family cursed by incest fear (pig-tailed child prophecy). Cycles of solitude: power (Colonel), knowledge (José Arcadio Buendía), love (Amaranta), memory (Úrsula), forgetting (town). Melquíades' Sanskrit prophecies, deciphered only at the end by Aureliano Babilonia, reveal entire history was predetermined. Final Aureliano born with pig's tail, eaten by ants as Macondo is wiped from memory by hurricane.

**Themes**: Solitude (inherent, inescapable), circular time, magical realism as historical testimony, impossibility of love, fatalism vs. agency, Latin American identity.

## Cultural Context for Translators

1. **Colombian Caribbean Culture**: Vallenato rhythms, costeño dialect (not directly in subtitles but informs tone), oral storytelling tradition.

2. **Catholic Ritual Language**: Mass Latin, saints' days, confession, processions, miracles — use standard Catholic Chinese terminology (弥撒, 忏悔, 圣人, 奇迹).

3. **Indigenous/Wayuu Elements**: Series includes Wayuu language; Wayuu characters (Visitación, Cataure) — note in subtitles if Wayuu spoken.

4. **Historical References**: 
   - **Rafael Núñez** (regeneration president)
   - **Rafael Uribe Uribe** (Liberal general)
   - **Tomás Cipriano de Mosquera** — use standard Chinese historical names.

5. **Incest Taboo**: Central anxiety; "pig's tail" = genetic curse. Translate with clinical/anthropological tone, not sensational.

6. **Magical Realism Convention**: Supernatural treated as mundane. No "magical" adjectives in narration; straight description. Subtitles must not exoticize.

7. **Time Circularity**: "Many years later, facing the firing squad..." — past/present/future blur. Tense consistency in Chinese (often aspect markers 了/过/着 rather than tense).

8. **Names as Fate**: Repetition of names = repetition of destiny. Subtitles should preserve name repetition visibly.

9. **Oral History Frame**: Novel/series framed as Melquíades' prophecies being read. Final revelation: "The first of the line is tied to a tree, the last is eaten by ants."

10. **Author's Sons as Producers**: Rodrigo & Gonzalo García Barcha executive produced; series authorized, faithful adaptation. Respect textual fidelity.
