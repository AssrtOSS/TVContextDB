## Strictly Come Dancing S24E03 — Movie Week

### Plot
Series 24, episode 3 is the first themed week: Movie Week. Alan Dedicoat announces the live show from Elstree; this is the first episode where all 15 remaining couples perform on the same themed night, and crucially the first episode where the public vote opens (closes9:15pm). Scores carry over from last week, so the leaderboard reflects two weeks combined. Tomorrow night's pre-recorded Results Show will feature the first Dance-Off of the series; the casting vote for that Dance-Off is held by Craig Revel Horwood.

### Key actions / developments

- **Openers**: Josh Widdicombe introduces the celebrities in a boot-camp parody ("Recruits, stand-up"), playing the villain role. Josh is billed as a "Strictly alumni with real-world experience" — a wink at his non-dancer background. The group Pro number is set to "Turn the Floor Into a Zoo" / "Try Everything."
- **Coaches**: John Nellis and Chadley (sp?) is out — actually the co-host lineup is now Emma Willis (lead host), Josh Widdicombe (co-host), and Johannes Radebe (co-host). The four judges remain Craig Revel Horwood, Motsi Mabuse, Shirley Ballas, Anton Du Beke. Alan Dedicoat is back as announcer.
- **Dance trainer / choreographer**: Matt Flint staged the opening number.

2. **John Nellis & Katya Jones** — Couple's Choice to *Titanic*'s "below deck party scene" / Gaelic Storm's "The Jig" (composed by James Horner). John is from Belfast (Titanic built there); a second baby is due in February; he says the toughest part of his career is time away from family. Katya is dropped at the very end of the routine — judges all note it. Score: **27** (two 7s plus higher marks).
3. **Delta Goodrem & Kai Widdrington** — American Smooth to *A Star is Born*. Delta mentions "Born to Try" was her song at 17; Kai shows her new ballroom shoes and uses a "banana with a shelf" image to teach posture. Motsi calls her a "force to be reckoned with"; Shirley wants stronger centre. Score: **36** (the night's high — Delta tops combined leaderboard at 59).
4. **Chris Appleton & Amy Dowden** — Cha Cha Cha to *Baywatch*. Chris is described as a complete beginner; Amy Dowden returns as pro after her own health battle last series. The "red bad boys" line (swim trunks) gets audience laughing. Motsi notes Chris went out of time at the end. Score: lower, no explicit number read but combined-leaderboard bottom (Melanie/Aljaz and Chris/Amy are tied at the foot after tonight).
5. **Lawrence Robb & Jowita Przystał** — Viennese Waltz to *Ratatouille*. Jowita plants a "Jowita" hat that Lawrence tries to dance without her; he jokes he sounds like Alan Partridge. Anton says he's "magic" out of hold but in-hold arm position is poor; Motsi says he has "a beautiful rat" personality. Score not read directly.

Score **in the 20s** (e.g., 28+).

Score: **8 from Craig on week two** = "like a ten"; Anton says Graeme could be one of the best ballroom dancers in Strictly history. Combined score puts them joint 2nd.

Judges split on it: Anton says "tale of two dances"; Shirley scolds him for flat feet and visible foot errors ("no trousers to hide them"). Score one more than week one.
9. **Jaime Winstone & Carlos Gu** — Cha Cha Cha to *Pretty Woman*. Jaime is the daughter of Ray Winstone. She's a former S23 winner (with Carlos Gu) — referred to on-screen as "in the arms of the past champion." Anton calls her "a pocket rocket." Score: **21**.
10. **Melanie Walters & Aljaz Skorjanec** — Waltz to *The Sound of Music* / "Edelweiss". Melanie's late mother had Alzheimer's and loved the film; it was played at her funeral. She wanted to make her mum proud. Craig says "you have potential — I'm gunning for you." Score: **15** (lowest of the night — Melanie and Chris are joint bottom on combined leaderboard).
11. **Cach Mercer & Maddie Ingoldsby** — Samba to *Grease*. Cach is young; he jokes his character's name is "Danny Zucchini" / corrects to "Zuko" (Zuko from Wicked). Craig says he "reinvents dance on week two"; Shirley gives a higher mark than last week but wants basic samba technique. Score: **29**.
12. **Tabby Stoecker & Mark Karmalita** — American Smooth to *The Greatest Showman* / "Rewrite the Stars". Tabby (a former Winter Olympian skeleton racer) uses circus hoop in rehearsal; Mark is a new pro. Score: **six more than last week** — significant jump.
13. **Bethany Antonia & Nancy Xu** — Paso Doble to *Wicked: For Good* / "No Good Deed". Josh offers "a wicked old witch who's defensive and cruel but really wants to be loved." Bethany and Nancy go indoor skydiving to prep; Shirley notes they were "in the arms of a 'Spanish shaping' missing" — wants more classic paso. Score: **27**.

Sunday Results Show after S24E05 (Week 3 themed live).
