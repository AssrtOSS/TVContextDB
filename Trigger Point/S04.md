## S04E01

Episode Summary — Trigger Point S04E01

Plot

The episode opens with Lana Washington in a return-to-duty therapy session, candidly admitting she is "a little bit broken" but determined to keep working. A ticking-clock motif and radio countdown ("Twenty seconds. Prepare for impact") bridge her session directly into an active EXPO callout: at 03:45 an MI5/CTSFO anti-smuggling raid uncovered documentation showing a large explosive device has been supplied to an unidentified group, with intent to detonate in the City of London that morning. Bronze Command (DC Neil, Inspector Brenton) deploys EXPO 1 (Lana) to a warehouse where the device was supposedly loaded onto pallet India 7 of a 7.5-tonne lorry — but the pallet and lorry have vanished.

A CCTV frame shows the lorry left 20 minutes earlier. ANPR picks it up approaching Wandsworth Roundabout. Lana, Danny, Hass and "Wren" set off in pursuit. ANPR tracks it heading east toward Central London. A surveillance drone shadows the lorry, believed to carry remote-detonation control; approaching the lorry will trigger detonation. Gold Command is declared at 08:32 under Commander Nia Quentin, with Phil ("Trojan 1") leading CTSFOs and Amar Batra liaising with MI5 ("Mr Oldfield") at Thames House. MI5 later confirms the target is the Bank of England.

Anti-drone ammunition is coming from Heathrow and won't arrive in time. Lana's "plan B": force the lorry into a tunnel (Waterloo Bridge route) so the drone loses sight, then conduct a covert interception with road closures disguised as construction/RTAs to keep the lorry on route. The tunnel hard-stop succeeds: CTSFOs neutralise the driver (single gunshot wound, non-life-threatening, one T1 CTSFO casualty) and Lana climbs aboard with Hass following, Danny on jammers, driving the live bomb toward the Bank while EXPO 8 (Hass) drives. Target destination confirmed by satnav.

Gold Command also dispatches Trojan 3 to raid the "Quick 2 Carry Couriers" office (London, no contact details on card) — believing the bombers may be operating from there. The lower ground floor and basement are empty; Trojan 3 finds evidence of a contact IED and Bronze Investigation 1 is hit on the fourth floor, comms lost, casualties incurred.

On the lorry, Lana finds multiple pallets. The decoy pallets contain only empty small boxes. A main charge wired to a sat phone (so ECM/jammers are useless — signal is satellite) sits amid decoys, with three detonators hidden in a "collapsing circuit" anti-tamper trap: cutting the wrong wire would have detonated it. With about 20 seconds to spare before reaching the Bank, Lana successfully disarms all three detonators.

The team pulls over. Wren's anti-drone gun has arrived and the threat drone is finally taken out. As they debrief, a detonation echoes from elsewhere in the City. Another device, out east. The episode ends with a boom and Lana's hollow "Fuck." The final scene cuts back to the therapy room — "When we've got more time" — before the second explosion pulls Lana back to operational reality.

Important Character Actions

- Lana Washington: completes therapy intake, leads EXPO 1 through pursuit, tunnel hard-stop planning and on-lorry render-safe; defuses a sophisticated anti-tamper collapsing-circuit device against a satellite-trigger deadline; is visibly stretched ("white-knuckling it").
- Hassan "Hass" Rahim: drives the live bomb as EXPO 8 to maintain the route so the drone doesn't notice the lorry has been intercepted; keeps Lana steady under stress.
- Danny Markham: rides with the jammers on the lorry, confirms they won't work because the device is satellite-detonated.
- "Wren": CTSFO support who follows in the unmarked convoy and later arrives with the anti-drone gun that finally kills the surveillance drone.
- Phil (Trojan 1): CTSFO lead, takes down the driver in the tunnel, sustains or attends the T1 CTSFO casualty, repeatedly chases the missing anti-drone ammunition.
- Amar Batra (DCI): MI5/Thames House liaison; pushes Mr Oldfield for device details, redirects resources to the courier office.
- Mr Oldfield: MI5 representative at Thames House; gatekeeps device intelligence, frustrates EXPOs and CT Command.
- DS Helen Morgan: stays in the ops room collating detail rather than going out; relays status to Gold Command.
- DC Neil / Inspector Brenton: stand up Bronze Command, set up the ops room on scene after India 7 comes up empty.
- DS Hackett: CCTV/ANPR officer; tracks the lorry in real time.
- Commander Nia Quentin: takes Gold Command at 08:32; records decisions, ultimately orders EXPO 8 off route to prevent a Bank detonation, reversing the order when Lana insists she can render safe in time.
- Trojan 3 / Bronze Investigation 1 & 2: CTSFOs raiding the courier office, suffer a contact IED on the fourth floor; comms lost; multiple casualties including a coughing/rattling BI officer; a woman CTSFO's mic captures grunts, screaming and breathing through the blast.

Newly Established Names / Terms

- Eddie: Lana's new therapist/counsellor (return-to-duty sessions). Distinct from DS Helen Morgan (the recurring CT Command DS) — Morgan here is Eddie's surname, used by Trojan colleagues.
- Nia Quentin: Gold Commander for the day.
- Mr Oldfield: MI5 desk officer at Thames House.
- Neil (DC) and Brenton (Inspector): Bronze Command leads.
- Hackett: DC handling CCTV/ANPR.
- Wren: CTSFO on Lana's convoy.
- "Quick 2 Carry Couriers": fictitious front company used to disguise the lorry; HQ in London, near the warehouse; no contact details on the business card.
- "India 7" / pallet designation: warehouse grid reference for the device's expected location.
- "Bronze Investigation 1/2" / "Trojan 1/2/3": tactical callsigns for CTSFO teams and investigation cells.
- STRAP 3 source: STRAP 3 (Security Service Threat Risk Assessment Priority 3 — UK Top Secret grade) is the classification attached to the device intelligence, signalling a state-level threat.
- "Make Hass Great Again": running joke coined by Danny in the aftermath, riffing on the Trump slogan.

Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Lana is mid-return-to-work from PTSD and a past "self-medicating" phase; her notes flag drug abuse and Afghanistan trauma, but she insists both are under control. Her therapist Eddie accepts her back conditionally ("I need you to want to be here") and confirms "every single bit of progress has been down to you, you alone." This is the framing for her arc this series.
- Joel Nutkins remains referenced as killed; Hass has fully inherited the partner/deputy role, and Danny/Wren function as the close team. Hudson is not present — Lana's core trio here is Lana, Hass, Danny, with Wren as CTSFO attachment.
- No mention of Billy, Karl Maguire, or Val in this episode; Jeff Washington is not present. Lana's home support network has not yet resurfaced.
- Helen Morgan is established as the DS handling detail inside Gold Command, mirroring her S2 role.
- The MI5 relationship is openly hostile: Oldfield withholds the device type, Amara/Batra and DC Neil push back; "MI5 assurances" is now a house-hold grievance.
- The Bank of England is named as the bombers' target, raising the strategic stakes; Gold Command explicitly accepts civilian-casualty risk by ordering EXPO 8 to deviate from route.
- The device used a sat-phone trigger and a collapsing-circuit anti-tamper trap — a sophistication marker that the bombers are professionals, not opportunists.

Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- "Gotta love the challenge, right?" — Lana's dry quip when told she has 90 seconds to defuse on a moving lorry; signature gallows humour.
- "Did you fill your colostomy bag?" / "Many, many, many times." — Hass–Danny banter after the render-safe; recurs as in-team decompression language.
- "Make Hass Great Again" — Danny coins it; Hass retorts with a weary "Again?" — clearly a recurring team joke.
- "Fruit goes off… and bombs explode" — Lana's trademark line delivered jointly with Phil, bracketing the tunnel assault.
- Lana asking her mum to wake her up / "Lucky your mum was there to wake me up" / "Arsehole." — establishes that Lana is currently living with her mother (Val) again, a domestic-status fact for S4.
- "No. Fucking Bake Off." — Lana's therapist-session levity; returns to a quietly domestic register amid the chaos.

Unresolved Threads

- The bombers: the "unidentified group" behind the lorry and device remains unknown; the courier front company is empty and Bronze Investigation 1 was wiped out by a contact IED before extracting intel. No suspect identified by episode end.
- The drone: brought down at the very end, but who was piloting it and who it answers to is not addressed.
- Mr Oldfield / MI5: withholds the device composition throughout; this friction is left open as a recurring institutional obstacle.
- The second device: a fresh detonation goes off out east while the team is still recovering from the Bank run — the opening of a larger, multi-device campaign.
- The courier office raid: Trojan 3's fourth-floor contact IED and the fate of Bronze Investigation 1/2 (the coughing, grunting woman CTSFO on comms) are unresolved.
- The driver: detained but "refusing to talk," with no ID — a live interrogation thread.
- Lana's mental health: the therapy arc is set up but explicitly deferred ("We start afresh when you come again"); her stability under escalating S4 pressure is a live concern flagged by Eddie.
- Anti-drone logistics: the Heathrow-to-scene ammo delay nearly cost the operation; institutional readiness against airborne threats is an unresolved capability gap.
