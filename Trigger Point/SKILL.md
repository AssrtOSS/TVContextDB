# Trigger Point — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Overview
**Trigger Point** (2022– ) is a British police procedural crime thriller created by Daniel Brierley, executive-produced by Jed Mercurio (*Line of Duty*). It follows Metropolitan Police Explosives Officers (EXPOs) in London counter-terrorism operations.

- **First air date**: 23 January 2022  
- **Broadcaster**: ITV (linear), ITVX (streaming)  
- **Structure**: 4 series × 6 episodes = 18 episodes (Series 4 scheduled Aug–Sep 2026)  
- **Running time**: ~60 minutes  
- **Production**: HTM Television; filmed in London

## Core Premise
Ex-military bomb-disposal operative **Lana Washington** leads a Met Police EXPO team responding to escalating terrorist bombing campaigns. Each series centres on a distinct threat network — far-right extremists (S1), organised crime/foreign interference (S2), domestic radicalisation (S3) — with continuing personal arcs for Lana and her colleagues.

## Speaking Styles & Register
- **Lana**: Direct, clipped, military precision; swears under stress (“shit”, “fuck” — translate naturally, not euphemistically). Internal monologue rare; emotion suppressed until breaking points.
- **Joel**: Warmer, gallows humour, mentor tone.
- **Youngblood / Batra / Morgan**: Police procedural register — “We need to…”, “Authorise…”, “Secure the perimeter.” Formal but urgent.
- **Sonya**: Analytical, calm, exposition-heavy; explains bomb tech in accessible language.
- **Hassan**: Measured, faith-inflected (“Inshallah” occasionally), professional.
- **Hudson**: Ambitious, bureaucratic, later coldly ideological.
- **Extremists (Maguire, Crusaders)**: Rhetorical, grievance-fuelled, self-justifying.
- **Family scenes (Jeff, Val, Billy)**: Working-class London vernacular; overlapping, interrupted, emotional.

## Cultural / Institutional Context
- **Metropolitan Police structure**: CTC (Counter Terrorism Command) leads; EXPOs are specialist uniformed officers, not detectives. Rank friction: EXPOs operate autonomously on scene; DI/DCI authorise wider ops.
- **UK bomb-disposal doctrine**: “Render safe” > “controlled explosion”; preservation of forensic evidence emphasised.
- **Domestic terrorism landscape**: Series reflects real UK threat evolution — far-right (S1), Islamist (referenced), state-sponsored/gang (S2), lone-actor/online radicalisation (S3).
- **Class & region**: Lana’s family = working-class South London; Met hierarchy = Oxbridge/white-collar. Accent variation matters (McClure’s Nottingham accent vs. London cast).
- **Jed Mercurio DNA**: Institutional corruption, bent coppers, opaque chains of command, “AC-12”-style internal investigations (preliminary hearings in S1 finale).

## Translation Guidelines
1. **Rank/role**: Keep DI/DCI/Commander in English; translate EXPO as 排爆员 unless character says “EXPO”.
2. **Technical dialogue**: Preserve accuracy (wire colours, timer values, component names). Use standard Chinese EOD terminology (参考《爆炸物处理教程》/ 公安部标准).
3. **Profanity**: Retain force; “fuck” → “他妈的/操” per intensity; “shit” → “见鬼/完了”.
4. **Acronyms**: CTC, IED, MOD, SCO19 — keep Latin; add Chinese gloss first occurrence.
5. **“The Crusaders”**: 十字军 (historical resonance intended by writers).
6. **Billy’s arc**: Handle radicalisation vocabulary carefully (“groomed”, “radicalised” → 诱导/极端化).
7. **Continuity**: Character relationships carry across series; maintain consistent address forms (兰娜 vs. 华盛顿 vs. 排爆组长).
