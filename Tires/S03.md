# Tires S03E12 Summary

## Episode Plot
Phil and Dave run a "secret shopper" program across Valley Forge Automotive stores, sending actress Holly (wired with mic/earpiece) to evaluate managers. Previous stores failed spectacularly—managers flirted instead of upselling, with Monaco's Chuck making horrific comments to Holly's "boyfriend" (an actor). Phil reveals he's in "active sex addiction recovery" after an affair. They target Will and Shane's store: Shane coaches Will via earpiece to flirt and extract a discount. Will awkwardly plays "aloof," fails to offer a discount, and Holly leaves. Meanwhile, Dave sits in a surveillance van outside with a persistent erection (blames ginkgo biloba). Police are called, chaos erupts, and Holly reveals she was the secret shopper. Shane learns it was all a setup.
