## Best Medicine S02E01 Summary

### Episode Plot
Season 2 opens with a "Previously on" recap of Martin's arrival, blood phobia, and the Louisa/Mark triangle. Martin's medical board monitor **Renee** visits — she's pleased with his practice but reminds him of three months remaining on probation. A woman in a mermaid tail (**Cecilia**) washes ashore; Martin diagnoses **ichthyosis** (fish-scale skin condition) and **red tide poisoning** (toxic algae bloom causing seizures, garbled speech, wheezing). Cecilia flees the clinic, leaving a note: "Please don't look for me. I'm not worth the trouble." The town mobilizes a search; Martin finds her on a roof/lighthouse. To talk her down, he publicly reveals his **blood phobia**. Cecilia descends, agrees to inpatient care. Louisa announces she's pregnant and marrying her donor-match; Al and Elaine commit to moving to New York. ## S02E02

# Best Medicine S02E02

## Plot
A Brooklyn pizza vendor, **Gina Marie Afrigola**, parks her food truck on The Spruce Sea Breeze (the Salty Breeze)'s property line and trades1980s-movie insults with Greg and George ("Cher from Moonstruck," "man from Cocoon"). At the restaurant, Bert and the regulars prank Martin with a ketchup "blood" wound; Martin publicly shames them ("obscene waste of condiments"). His new girlfriend **Eden**, a doctor who has just arrived unannounced from Boston, defends him and vows to cure his blood phobia and take him home.

Sally's pharmacy is robbed: a suture kit, bandages, rubbing alcohol, five vials of morphine, and a box of extra-large adult male diapers. The thief left $23.75 on the counter (exact cost of the diapers). No pills, no cash taken. Louisa discovers her fertility trigger shot (from St. Louis) is also gone — she has a strict 36-hour window before egg retrieval. They visit **Old Man Collins**, who confesses to taking only the diapers (he uses them to retain moisture in his begonias and didn't want to be seen buying them as a single man); he denies touching the morphine or Louisa's medication.

The Salty Breeze is then looted of vodka, rum, and Benadryl cream. Gina is initially suspected, but Mark clears her truck; she has only a red sauce dot on her chin. Gina reveals she has **Tourette's with echolalia** — she unconsciously repeats phrases — which explains her earlier muttered "Oh God, I shot him." Through deduction Martin concludes the thief is trying to save a gunshot victim allergic to morphine: Benadryl counters the reaction, alcohol dulls the pain, and a stolen albuterol inhaler treats what looks like asthma but is actually a lung nicked by the bullet. The patient is dying of a collapsed lung.

Bert apologizes for the prank. Greg confesses he thinks Gina is a killer (just echoed phrasing). Eden and Sarah share a tense lunch; Eden claims Martin will return to Boston once cured, and asserts she once heard Louisa muttering "I shot him" repeatedly (in fact, just Gina's echolalia echoing nearby).

A pizza/hot-dog price war erupts in the parking lot when Gina expands into hot dogs and a rival (**Irma** with partner **Frankie**) sets up across from her. During Gina's stress-induced echo breakdown, Martin formally diagnoses latent echolalia (triggered by her father's death, brothers taking over the family shop, and the pressure of the move to Maine).

Elaine, who has been hiding a urinary tract infection out of spite for Martin, finally lets him examine her. She lets slip that Al saw Martin and Louisa running into the woods — toward **Nibbler's Trail**, an ivy-choked ridge with Bert's old hunting cottages.

Martin and Louisa find the patient: two teenagers who snuck away from a Vermont field trip. The boy, **Kyle**, was accidentally shot by his girlfriend, who then tried to extract the bullet, stitched the wound (puncturing his lung), and stole from the pharmacy and the Salty Breeze because she knew hospitals must report gunshot wounds. Kyle's "asthma" is the collapsed lung, with pressure building in his chest cavity.

Martin performs an emergency needle decompression but cannot watch — Eden coaches Louisa through breathing exercises while Martin closes his eyes, and Louisa delivers the chest needle. Louisa finds her stolen fertility medication on the scene, nearly panics and abandons the motherhood plan, but Martin steadies her and she self-injects the trigger shot. Life Flight evacuates Kyle.

Aftermath: Gina's truck is relocated to the Legion Hall parking lot with a peace-offering bottle of wine from Greg. Elaine privately tells Sarah that Eden was nice to her and wonders if Martin is "supposed to be with her." Sarah insists Martin is "Port Wenn through and through." A snake hisses in the bushes as an unseen person offers him a coffee — ominous beat.

## S02E03

# S02E03 — Lobster Crate Race

## Plot
The episode orbits Port Wenn's annual lobster crate race while Martin juggles three threads: a karaoke-night fainter, a "pregnant men" medical mystery at the processing plant, and Peter's first race. Martin agrees to enter at the last minute and breaks Aunt Sarah's 50-year record with 32 crates. Louisa's bad online date in Millbridge and Al's reconciliation with Bert play out as side plots.

## Main Beats

- Karaoke night, Salty Breeze. Eden, Martin's girlfriend (new this season), sings "Chattanooga Choo Choo" so badly that a patron faints. Martin, mid-exposure therapy for hemophobia, is caught off-guard but holds it together.
- Lorenzo's fainting. Diagnosed first as dehydration/overexertion; faints again. Martin suspects arrhythmia or heart attack, sends him for an EKG, then refers him to Bar Harbor for stress echo and CTPA.
- Mandatory race physicals. Martin requires exams for entrants; Elaine, optimising for efficiency, tests every sample for everything. Three from-away men at the local processing plant test positive on pregnancy assays — flagged as possible testicular cancer.
- The pee mystery. Confronted at the Salty Breeze, the men refuse retests. Lab bloodwork comes back HCG-zero (no cancer), and the urine analysis shows all three samples came from one person: Hal, the plant owner, who gave them his own urine so they'd pass drug tests. Hal's urine itself tests positive for pregnancy. He refuses Bar Harbor and dubs Martin "Dr. Pees himself."
- Peter's practice. Coached by Louisa, he hits 30 crates and cries "I almost beat your record!" before falling in. Aunt Sarah, off-screen, is un-rigging the famously unstable 30th crate so he gets a fair shot.
- Race day. Mark Mylow 15, Kenji Wan 21, Peter Cronk 26, Glendon Ross 28 (overtaking Peter late). Lorenzo, unable to race, is tapped to ring the victory bell; he collapses mid-"choo choo" arm pull. Martin diagnoses a totally occluded carotid on one side — the arm motion kinks the remaining patent vessel. Lorenzo needs an emergency endarterectomy in Bar Harbor.
- Martin's race. Hal bets Martin: if Hal beats him head-to-head, Martin goes to Bar Harbor for testing; if Martin wins, Hal goes. Martin enters on the spot and runs 32 crates — breaking Sarah's record. Hal concedes and agrees to see a doctor.
- Louisa's date. Millbridge, with Alec, a twice-divorced man. She carpooled there with her ex-fiancé (Mark). Date collapses when she mentions pursuing solo IVF and harvesting eggs mid-dating.
- Al's anniversary. Saturday is the 7th anniversary of Al's biological father's death. Bert (the man who raised him) and Al reconcile at the race; Al calls the crate race "our thing."

## S02E06

# Best Medicine S02E06 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode centers on three converging crises: **Savannah** (the ex-con naturopath who previously caused a bubonic plague outbreak) returns to Port Wenn running a fraudulent healing practice; **George** loses his sense of taste right before a presumed **New York Times food critic (Carson Macleish)** arrives at The Salty Breeze; and **Martin** discovers he can draw blood without fainting — suggesting his haemophobia may be lifting.

Savannah's "miracle cure" of a wheelchair-bound woman is exposed: the woman actually has **MOGAD** (myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody-associated disease), an autoimmune disorder mimicking stroke. Martin's earlier cortisone shot temporarily restored her mobility, not Savannah's herbs. Savannah admits she's a fraud with no medical training (one prison naturopathy class). She's arrested for parole violation after a date with Mark.

Meanwhile, the "food critic" turns out to be **Carl Mooney**, a dentist from Bangor whose wife monogrammed "C.M." shirts for his 50th birthday. The real Carson Macleish never shows. George cooks through the crisis with Gina (his hostile ex-wife) as taster, and the meal succeeds.
