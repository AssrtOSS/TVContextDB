# Ghosts US S06E02 — "Halloween / The Mirror"

## Episode Plot
Halloween at Woodstone. Hetty's sister Margaret lures Hetty into an antique mirror and traps her inside, exploiting the one night the veil between worlds is thin enough for ghosts to swap places. The mirror shatters, "sharding" Hetty's consciousness across the fragments. The household must reassemble the mirror and get Margaret back inside before midnight to reverse the swap. A missing shard hitchhikes on departing guest Evan; Jay, Bela, and Kyle track him to a Sonic on Route 9 using Kyle's ghost-hearing and Jay's encyclopedic fast-food knowledge. With the final piece recovered, the swap is tested — but Hetty, having seen a vision of her past betrayal (she loved a painter named Julian; Margaret stole him and Hetty reported the affair to their father), chooses to stay in the mirror so Margaret can go free. Margaret, moved by Hetty's apology, opts to return to the mirror so she can be shipped to Paris (via Trevor's daughter Abby). The episode ends on Pete's 41st death-anniversary vigil — interrupted by Pete himself reappearing, but with total amnesia: he believes it's 1985, thinks he was just shot with an arrow, and doesn't recognize anyone.

## Important Character Actions
- **Margaret**: Tricks Hetty into the mirror; plays hide-and-seek with Flower/Thorfinn; reconciles with Hetty; chooses to re-enter mirror for Paris trip.
- **Hetty**: Sees mirror-vision of her past; apologizes to Margaret for lifelong neglect; sacrifices her freedom so Margaret can go.
- **Jay / Bela / Kyle**: Road-trip to Sonic; Jay identifies location via "Tropical Heat Smasher" + "Slush Float" menu cross-reference; retrieves shard from Evan's costume.
- **Isaac**: Tests reconstructed mirror; gets vision of his nape being touched (Julian reference).
- **Thorfinn / Flower**: Track Margaret via scent (patchouli) and "Marco Polo" hide-and-seek.
- **Pete**: Returns at episode end — alive(?) but with 1985-era memory, no recognition of the household.
