## S04E01

# Yellowjackets S04E01 Summary

## Episode Plot

**Wilderness Timeline (Flashback):** The group holds a trial for Coach Scott ("The People versus Benjamin Scott"), finding him guilty by majority vote. They prepare a "feast" to honor a deceased child ("now with the Wilderness"). Natalie is tasked with cooking. The next morning they'll draw lots for who hunts — someone has already rigged the draw. A transponder is discovered.

**Present Day (Rescue):** After 19 months, the Wiskayok Yellowjackets are found in the Canadian Rockies. Canadian authorities (CTSB, Investigator Donald Kundla) and Ambassador Thomas Bouchard coordinate repatriation. Parents gather at a clinic; Shauna, Taissa, Natalie, and Melissa (living as "Kelly") are among the survivors. Mari Ibarra is confirmed dead — her mother breaks down. The girls give identical, rehearsed statements: "We're the only ones left." Kundla reveals a *researchers'* distress call — not the girls' — brought search teams to the area.

**Melissa's Revelation:** Melissa (real name, not Kelly) confesses to her husband Alex: she was a Yellowjacket, changed her identity, "rebooted" her life. Her mother made the distress call that saved them. The other survivors want to *kill* her, not just hurt her. They flee to a motel; she later moves into the house next door to Shauna: "Now I know where you are. And everyone else will, too."

**Misty & Walter:** They confirm Vanessa Palmer (Van) died of cardiac arrest with a DNR (terminal cancer). No autopsy. Misty needs access to Shauna's house for journals and a DAT tape; she has an appointment with a PI at 1 PM.

**Lottie's Breakdown:** During questioning, Dr. Brannack notes fresh injuries on Lottie (scratches, bruises, broken finger, black eye). Lottie refuses to speak, screaming "No!" repeatedly. Her father (Mr. Matthews, connected to Ambassador Bouchard via Yale) pulls her out.

**Shauna's Arc:** X-ray reveals pelvic separation — she gave birth in the wilderness. She tries to buy a gun. Her husband Jeff has left with their daughter; she threatens a PI who can't locate Melissa.

## S04E02

## Yellowjackets S04E02 Summary

### Episode Plot
Melissa Kaplan (formerly "Kelly," the Yellowjacket who faked her death 25 years ago) goes public with a televised interview, revealing her true identity, her connection to Hannah Finch (the researcher whose distress call saved them), and that she married Hannah's daughter Alex. In the present, Melissa has moved in next door to Shauna. Jeff's furniture business opens a second location without Shauna's knowledge, and he serves her divorce papers. In the teen timeline, Natalie briefly makes contact via satellite phone using coordinates from Kodi, but the connection lasts only a minute before failing. Lottie's hold over the group deepens with a "Wilderness" ritual.

### Important Character Actions
- **Melissa/Kelly**: Goes public on national TV; moves next door to Shauna; claims she wants to "tell a love story" and rekindle friendships
- **Shauna**: Confronts Melissa at the furniture store; discovers Jeff's divorce filing; reads her mother's journals; threatens Melissa but is checked by media presence
- **Jeff**: Opens second Sadecki Furniture location secretly; hires divorce lawyer; tells Shauna "I can't do it anymore" re: their destructive cycle
- **Natalie (teen)**: Operates broken satellite phone; makes brief contact ("It worked! Only for like a minute"); gives Kodi's coordinates to Hannah
- **Lottie (teen)**: Escalates cult behavior; orders Shauna and Melissa thrown in "the pit"; leads "We hear the Wilderness" chant
- **Taissa (adult)**: Comforts Akilah; sleeps with Van; supports Melissa staying over
- **Van (adult)**: Reconnects with Taissa; stays over; discusses difficulty of reintegration
- **Shauna's mother**: Admits to affair (not the cause of divorce); confesses she read Shauna's journals "trying to know you"

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Hannah Finch** — Research scientist whose expedition got lost in the same mountain range; her distress call rescued the Yellowjackets
- **Alex** — Hannah's daughter, Melissa's wife
- **"The Wilderness"** — Entity/force the girls now worship/channel ("We hear the Wilderness, and it hears us")
- **The Pit** — Punishment hole Lottie uses for dissenters
- **Sadecki Furniture second location** — Opening off Route 36 (west side)

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Melissa chose Hannah's hometown after faking death "maybe I felt like she could keep me safe here too"
- Melissa and Alex met years later by "coincidence"; Melissa didn't know the connection initially
- Shauna's mother's affair was with a coworker; occurred when Shauna was in middle school; not the divorce cause
