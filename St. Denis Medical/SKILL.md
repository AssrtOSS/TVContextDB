Synopsis (Overall Premise)
St. Denis Medical follows the doctors, nurses, and administrators of St. Denis Regional Medical Center, an underfunded, understaffed hospital in Oregon. The series uses a mockumentary format (talking-head confessionals, handheld camera, fourth-wall breaks) to depict the daily chaos of an overburdened public healthcare system. The ensemble balances absurd workplace comedy with genuine emotional stakes: patients suffer from systemic neglect, staff burn out, and management chases accolades over care. The tone echoes The Office and Parks and Recreation — cringe humor grounded in character warmth.
