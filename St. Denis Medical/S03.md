## S03E02

Denis Medical S03E02**:

---

## Episode Plot

Ron returns to the ED after an 8-month absence following his heart attack. Joyce orchestrates an over-the-top "spirit tunnel" welcome with *Celebration* playing, which Ron resents. Meanwhile, a patient (Lucas) arrives with a snapping turtle (named Maureen) latched onto his arm — he claims he was "freeing" it from a zoo/sanctuary. The turtle won't release, and Ron's heart rate spikes dangerously while treating it. Joyce insists on monitoring Ron via a wearable ring ("Ron-itor") and assigns Keith to track his vitals. The team eventually sedates the turtle with ketamine/hydromorphone and intubates it; Maureen survives, Lucas is arrested (FBI involved — CITES Appendix II species), and Ron nearly collapses afterward but refuses to make a big deal of it.

**B-plot:** Bruce obsesses over creating an "inside joke" with Brooke (his girlfriend) after seeing Matt and Serena's dynamic. He ropes Matt into workshopping typo-based jokes ("typo positive"), panics when Brooke edits her text, and eventually spirals into a fake HR complaint against her for "ableist" teasing — which backfires when Joyce takes it seriously.

**C-plot:** Val discovers her paychecks have been going to "Mark Pearson in Radiology" (actually Matt's dad). She forces Matt to confront Payroll, extracting a "clean slate" on his IOU ledger and a promise to never take "New Year's Eve eve" off again (a fake Hawaiian holiday Val invented).

## S03E03

# St. Denis Medical S03E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
Joyce launches **“YO! St. D.”** (Youth Outreach) — a teen field-trip/internship-recruitment day that accidentally brands itself **STD**. High-school students tour the ED while staff scramble to make medicine look cool. Joyce leans on a *K-Pop Demon Hunters* movie metaphor that falls flat; Bruce tries to mentor a goth kid named **Garrett** (“Garrett the Ghoul”) and injures himself doing “The Worm” dance, going viral as “Old Doctor eats [bleep].” A real anaphylaxis case (tree-nut allergy) gives the teens an unfiltered look at the job. Six students sign up for summer internships, including Garrett — for the morgue.

## Important Character Actions
- **Joyce**: Unveils YO! St. D., embarrasses herself with K-pop references, spirals while writing a closing speech (reveals she’s menopausal), ends with a standing ovation after speaking from the heart.
- **Bruce**: Forces “cool” mentorship on Garrett, attempts *Austin Powers* “Mini-Me” bit, hurts himself break-dancing, becomes a TikTok meme.
- **Alex**: Runs the wound-care demo, shuts down a student’s “nurses are assistants” remark, earns rare praise from Ron (“one of the smartest people I know”).
- **Ron**: Doubles down on “doctors = brains, nurses = hands” hierarchy, gets called out by Alex, backpedals clumsily with baseball metaphors (bat boys, groundskeepers), eventually admits Alex could’ve been a doctor.
- **Matt**: Tries to help Joyce’s speech, suggests a funny song, gets brutally roasted (“clunky,” “not funny”).
- **Serena**: Naturally bonds with teens (assimilates like she did in high school), gives Joyce a “normal person” perspective on the speech.
- **Garrett**: Starts friendless, ends with one friend (Bruce), signs up for morgue internship.

## S03E04

## S03E04

Romance-themed episode built around "mating season" at St. Denis: Bruce and Emerson, Matt and Serena, and Vince with his online Thai girlfriend. Joyce, newly single and jealous, dives into dating apps while Ron's post-surgery "Where's my Joyce?" becomes hospital-wide gossip.

### Episode Plot
- Cold open: Bruce treats Mr. Calloway's distal scaphoid fracture; an odd smell is blamed on butane/chlorine before being revealed as "the intoxicating aroma of true love." Keith recounts putting a rotisserie chicken in his dying dog's bowl and driving it to the vet as a family.
- Bruce announces he has converted last year's compass tattoo (commemorating getting lost in the woods) into a human heart: "double meaning" because Emerson is a heart surgeon and hearts mean love. He calls Brooke "the one" and "the future Mrs. Bruce Schweitz."
- Joyce signs up for every hot dating app; her profile is terrible (repeated name, "Biggest weakness: my GERD," Prilosec/statins/Lipitor as desert-island items, Zoom screenshot photo). Serena "hacks the algorithm": favorite movie must be "The Fifth Element," not "Babe: Pig in the City."
- Alex offers offline alternatives (Dr. Pitter in Derm; Ella's algebra teacher Mr. Cardinelli) and tries to coach Joyce's profile, but Joyce insists no one meets in real life anymore.
- Bruce, horrified that Joyce is "trolling the internet for easy men" while Ron pines, breaks his "bro code" promise and leaks Ron's confession to Brandon, giving it 10–15 minutes before it goes hospital-wide.
- Ron insists "Where's my Joyce?" was the incoherent rambling of a heavily drugged man, then claims Joyce is a mother figure, then finally admits to her alone that he asked for her because she is "kind of, like, my best friend."
- Patient Mr. Aiken dislocates his shoulder for the third time in a month; Val and Serena tell Matt it is a "golden shoulder" scam for pain drugs. Aiken confesses, then reveals the meds are for his mother, who has cancer and raised eight children. Matt: "It is now, Mr. Aiken."
- Val repeatedly tricks Matt (lunch money, "break this for singles") to prove "everyone has an angle, and you're an easy mark," then softens ("I'm not a monster"). Matt confronts cafeteria worker Bonnie over withheld chicken strips, makes her cry, and realizes he misread the special (bologna, not bolognese).
- Joyce matches with Evan, a claims adjuster her age, sends a chaotic message barrage (tennis/bunions, fake bathroom/Verizon outage, zip code), and gets blocked ("User not found"). She quits apps: $800 on a five-year subscription to save $12.
- Alex sets Joyce up with Dean, owner of House of Dean across the street. Joyce immediately blurts that the hospital will stop dumping medical waste in his dumpster, revealing an existing practice Dean knew nothing about.
- Final reveal: Bruce tells the camera that he and Emerson "decided to call it quits" ("mewtch decizh"). She took a three-month job at a teaching hospital in Seattle; they are in "two very different places," no long-distance (one-hour flight, but TSA is a mess and "the unions are taking it too far"). He insists "this is what I want" while asking the crew for privacy and his mic off.
