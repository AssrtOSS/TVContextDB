## S03E02

Denis Medical S03E02**:

---

## Episode Plot

Ron returns to the ED after an 8-month absence following his heart attack. Joyce orchestrates an over-the-top "spirit tunnel" welcome with *Celebration* playing, which Ron resents. Meanwhile, a patient (Lucas) arrives with a snapping turtle (named Maureen) latched onto his arm — he claims he was "freeing" it from a zoo/sanctuary. The turtle won't release, and Ron's heart rate spikes dangerously while treating it. Joyce insists on monitoring Ron via a wearable ring ("Ron-itor") and assigns Keith to track his vitals. The team eventually sedates the turtle with ketamine/hydromorphone and intubates it; Maureen survives, Lucas is arrested (FBI involved — CITES Appendix II species), and Ron nearly collapses afterward but refuses to make a big deal of it.

**B-plot:** Bruce obsesses over creating an "inside joke" with Brooke (his girlfriend) after seeing Matt and Serena's dynamic. He ropes Matt into workshopping typo-based jokes ("typo positive"), panics when Brooke edits her text, and eventually spirals into a fake HR complaint against her for "ableist" teasing — which backfires when Joyce takes it seriously.

**C-plot:** Val discovers her paychecks have been going to "Mark Pearson in Radiology" (actually Matt's dad). She forces Matt to confront Payroll, extracting a "clean slate" on his IOU ledger and a promise to never take "New Year's Eve eve" off again (a fake Hawaiian holiday Val invented).

## S03E03

# St. Denis Medical S03E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
Joyce launches **“YO! St. D.”** (Youth Outreach) — a teen field-trip/internship-recruitment day that accidentally brands itself **STD**. High-school students tour the ED while staff scramble to make medicine look cool. Joyce leans on a *K-Pop Demon Hunters* movie metaphor that falls flat; Bruce tries to mentor a goth kid named **Garrett** (“Garrett the Ghoul”) and injures himself doing “The Worm” dance, going viral as “Old Doctor eats [bleep].” A real anaphylaxis case (tree-nut allergy) gives the teens an unfiltered look at the job. Six students sign up for summer internships, including Garrett — for the morgue.

## Important Character Actions
- **Joyce**: Unveils YO! St. D., embarrasses herself with K-pop references, spirals while writing a closing speech (reveals she’s menopausal), ends with a standing ovation after speaking from the heart.
- **Bruce**: Forces “cool” mentorship on Garrett, attempts *Austin Powers* “Mini-Me” bit, hurts himself break-dancing, becomes a TikTok meme.
- **Alex**: Runs the wound-care demo, shuts down a student’s “nurses are assistants” remark, earns rare praise from Ron (“one of the smartest people I know”).
- **Ron**: Doubles down on “doctors = brains, nurses = hands” hierarchy, gets called out by Alex, backpedals clumsily with baseball metaphors (bat boys, groundskeepers), eventually admits Alex could’ve been a doctor.
- **Matt**: Tries to help Joyce’s speech, suggests a funny song, gets brutally roasted (“clunky,” “not funny”).
- **Serena**: Naturally bonds with teens (assimilates like she did in high school), gives Joyce a “normal person” perspective on the speech.
- **Garrett**: Starts friendless, ends with one friend (Bruce), signs up for morgue internship.
