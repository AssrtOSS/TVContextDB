# Minerva - La scuola S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The scandal over hazing, illegal weapons, and a leaked video reaches the Ministry of Defense in Rome. Commander Achille Giordano refuses to resign, opting to expel the cadets involved—including his own son Diego—to contain the fallout. Diego, already fracturing after Rachele reveals a medical condition that ends her pilot aspirations, is expelled when Max testifies against him. Meanwhile, Salvo discovers his missing friend Francesco alive; Francesco fled after Diego publicly humiliated him over a love letter, feeling the school never accepted him. Diego steals a gun from the armory and barricades himself in a confrontation with Achille, Teresa, Francesco, and Salvo. Francesco's forgiveness ("Let's start all over again") breaks the standoff. The episode closes on the last day of school: cadets depart for summer, Camilla asserts independence from her father, Paola breaks with her boyfriend over the school's toxicity, and the first-years receive final orders to keep training for academy exams.

## Important Character Actions
- **Achille Giordano**: Sacrifices Diego to protect the institution; later faces him at gunpoint, admitting fear of his son's "weakness."
- **Diego Giordano**: Expelled, discovers Francesco alive, spirals into armed standoff, ultimately stands down after Francesco's forgiveness.
- **Teresa Bonaiuto**: Defies Achille's order to leave; stays to continue her reform-minded work.
- **Salvo Fusco**: Finds Francesco; uses military discipline as a tool to investigate ("Rules and discipline can be very powerful. Now I'm gonna use them to find out what happened to Francesco Russo").
- **Francesco Russo**: Reveals he vanished from shame, not suicide; his forgiveness resolves the climax.
- **Rachele**: Discloses a career-ending medical condition; rejects Diego's help, prioritizing her F-35 dream.
- **Camilla Savino**: Tells her father her future is her decision, not his.
- **Paola Giordano**: Breaks up with her boyfriend, declaring the school "ruins everyone."

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Francesco Russo** — Salvo's missing best friend, confirmed alive; former cadet humiliated by Diego.
- **F-35 pilot ambition** — Rachele's stated goal ("5 women in the world, I'll be the 6th").
- **Formentera** — Vincenzo's summer destination; he invites Salvo to his beach house.
- **London** — Camilla's original post-Minerva plan, now uncertain.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Diego is **expelled** (not voluntarily leaving); his cadet career ends.
- Achille and Teresa's trust is **broken**; she remains but as an internal opponent.
- Francesco is **alive and outside the school**; his whereabouts known to Esposito and Achille.
- Salvo explicitly commits to using the system's own rules to uncover the truth.
- The **missing gun** from the armory is accounted for (Diego took it; standoff resolved without shots).
- Rachele's medical condition disqualifies her from pilot track—major arc shift for a third-year.
- Camilla's father acknowledges her progress but tries to control her exit; she refuses.
