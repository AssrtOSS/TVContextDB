# Minerva - La scuola S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode covers the **three-day entrance trial** and **Investiture Day** at Minerva Military High School. It opens with the official entrance exam and Commander Giordano's opening address: three days to prove worthiness, after which surviving recruits will be paired with senior mentors and receive a dress sword.

A flashback set **one year earlier** reveals Salvo's background — his troubled mother, foster-home life, and friendship with **Francesco Russo**, a Minerva senior who encouraged Salvo to apply and promised to recruit him. Salvo arrives at school already investigating Francesco's disappearance.

The three-day trial tests physical endurance (dawn anthems, push-ups, inspections), discipline (the marking system: three red marks = expulsion), and mental resilience. **Vincenzo Cipriani** struggles physically but pushes on for family tradition. **Camilla Savino** rebels against authority and risks expulsion. **Laura Canali**, a new civilian philosophy teacher, introduces concepts like Foucault's "disciplinary society" and challenges the school's methods.

Salvo confronts Commander Giordano directly about Francesco's disappearance; Giordano claims Francesco "left without saying a word." Salvo secretly obtains an archive key card and searches the school records. He also meets **Paola Giordano** (the commander's daughter, Diego's sister), who is hiding on campus smoking marijuana and appears to be an insider who knows more than she lets on.

By Investiture Day, the surviving recruits are formally welcomed: seniors choose their mentees, each receiving a symbolic dress sword and taking an oath of duty. The episode ends with the three protagonists — Salvo, Vincenzo, and Camilla — confirmed as first-year cadets, their motivations and vulnerabilities established for the season ahead.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Salvo entered Minerva specifically to investigate Francesco Russo's disappearance — this is his secret agenda from the start.
- Francesco was a third-year cadet at Minerva who vanished one morning "without saying a word to anyone." The school's official position is that he chose to leave.
- Commander Giordano claims not to know what happened to Francesco, but his dinner conversation with Salvo reads as calculated and evasive.
- Salvo obtained an **archive key card** and has accessed personnel files — potential evidence for his investigation.
- Vincenzo's family has generational ties to Minerva; his father attended; this is "the only promise" Vincenzo made to his dad.
- Camilla was sent to Minerva by her father as punishment; her friends back home want her expelled so she can attend school in London. She has a pending incident ("after what you did") referenced in texts.
- Laura Canali is new to the school and new to motherhood; her first lesson on Foucault's disciplinary society directly mirrors the cadets' experience.
- Paola Giordano is Diego's sister and the Commander's daughter; she lives/hides on campus and has intimate knowledge of the school's layout and secrets.
- The three protagonists each represent a distinct archetype: Salvo (survival/investigation), Vincenzo (family tradition), Camilla (punishment/reform).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **"You're a thorn in my side"** — both Francesco (in flashback) and Commander Giordano (to Salvo) use this exact phrase; the echo links past and present, suggesting Salvo is repeating Francesco's pattern.
- **Sun Tzu quotation** — Vincenzo reads "In the middle of the chaos, there is also an opportunity"; signals his intellectual side beneath the anxious exterior.
- **Camilla's baking soda hack** — learned from housekeeper Brenda; class-coded humor (privileged girl with a "basically my mother" housekeeper); she later teaches it to Salvo and Vincenzo.
- **Laura's "my breasts were gonna explode"** — she opens her first class with an unapologetically candid line about pumping milk, immediately establishing her as the anti-military-protocol teacher.
- **"You never met my mother?" / "You've clearly never met my mother"** — Salvo's deadpan retort to the flag-as-mother comparison; dark humor rooted in his foster-care reality.
- **Paola's claustrophobia act** — she fakes a panic attack to get out of a locked room, then lights a joint; signals she is a wildcard who operates outside all hierarchies.
- **"You go straight for Paris Hilton, right?" / "You've got a pet chihuahua"** — seniors' cafeteria insults encode class and appearance markers; "Paris Hilton" likely targets Camilla's privileged persona.
- **"I'm your bitch"** — forced hourly text to Max; degrading ritual that Vincenzo endures; Salvo later helps send the messages, showing early alliance-building.

## Unresolved Threads

1. **What happened to Francesco Russo?** The central mystery — the school claims he left voluntarily, but Salvo doesn't believe it. Commander Giordano's evasiveness suggests a cover-up.
2. **What does the archive contain?** Salvo accessed personnel files — what did he find, and will the key card or his searches be discovered?
3. **Who is Paola Giordano really?** She hides on campus, knows hidden spaces, and seems connected to the school's underbelly. What is her relationship to the Francesco mystery?
4. **Diego's connection to Francesco** — glimpsed in a photo and referenced by Salvo; Diego appears to know something but hasn't been confronted directly yet.
5. **Camilla's London school and "what she did"** — her texts reference a past incident and a pending opportunity in London; the full backstory remains unrevealed.
6. **Vincenzo's physical limitations** — he can barely do push-ups; will his struggle with the physical demands threaten his place at Minerva?
7. **Laura Canali vs. the institution** — her philosophical critique of disciplinary society sets up a season-long tension with Commander Giordano's authoritarian model.
8. **Salvo's mother** — the flashback shows her in legal trouble ("if they arrest her again, I won't see her anymore"); her fate is left dangling.
9. **The mentor pairings** — which senior chose which freshman, and what dynamics will those relationships produce?
