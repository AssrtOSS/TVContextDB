## S06E01

# S06E01 — Abbott Elementary

## Plot
Opening day of the new school year. The building is sweltering because the old portable AC units are dead. Ava greets teachers by claiming a playground is being built by the Philadelphia Eagles, then 76ers; an imposter "Dean Wade" tries to scam the school into paying up front. The real76ers players (Tyrese Maxey, VJ, new teammate Jaylen) show up later and Ava, still not believing them, runs them off after asking where LeBron is ("running up the Rocky Steps"). The development meeting establishes: Gregory is the new assistant principal, two new hires are coming (his first-grade replacement and second-grade teacher Elsa Hardaway from the just-closed Bates Academy), and Barbara was hit by a car in front of the school that morning (Neighborhoods app alert). Melissa, now a union rep, enforces strict eight-hour limits. Three room-setup subplots play out: Janine builds an elaborate TikTok-style "jungle" classroom; Jacob's history room features dinosaurs Barbara deems "historically inaccurate"; Melissa installs misters, vines, and a fire pit in her new upstairs room. Ava unveils "Project Sweeney Todd," a plan to swat Superintendent Collins's barber shop so he switches to her estranged father's shop, then appears with the AC ask while a razor is at his throat. Melissa drops that Gregory is planning to propose to Janine (her friend O'Shon told her). Barbara and Gregory conduct a crossing-guard interview, hiring Abdul, who demonstrates a stop sign (Gregory shoots down Sarah McLachlan jokes). Gregory and Ava drive to the district to demand ten portable AC units but find Collins on a golf course; Collins's secretary redirects them to developer Judd Allen's meeting about replacing two closed schools. Gregory simply asks for the ACs and Allen agrees instantly, humiliating Ava. Barbara tries to reclaim her old first-floor classroom from Hardaway, citing her ankle; Hardaway refuses, dropping "not my ankle, not my stairs, not my problem" and teasing Melissa's crush on a handsome teacher named Morton upstairs. Barbara is forced to admit she was looking at her phone when hit by the car — the truth is now on Neighborhoods video. Ava reveals the hired first-grade replacement is a ChatGPT deepfake Gregory interviewed on Zoom and recorded. Gregory will teach first grade himself for now. The episode ends with Janine's jungle classroom wowing the second graders on day one.

## Important character actions
- Gregory: Bumped up to assistant principal after the Miami teachers' conference saved Abbott; took continuing-ed courses, built rapport with Interim Superintendent Collins, and personally hired his own first-grade replacement (who turns out to be a deepfake). Pressed Collins/Allen for ten ACs and got them on the spot. Decides to teach first grade himself until a real hire is found. Secretly planning to propose to Janine.

- Melissa: Newly minted union rep, blocks any work past the eight-hour development day. Tries to swap classrooms with Hardaway using Abbott-flood lore. Declares Ava "the union rep" and won't move.
- Barbara: Hit by a car, claims it was the driver's fault, eventually admits she was on her phone after the video surfaces. Volunteers as51st District Board of School Commissioners liaison representative to push for a crossing guard; conducts the interview with Gregory and hires Abdul.

## S06E02

## S06E02 — Episode Summary

### Plot overview

Gregory Eddie, officially Assistant Principal while still teaching, attempts the school's first-ever teacher "check-ins" in a corner classroom. The line grows long, interruptions stack up (a dance demo from a student, Barbara's tape request, Jacob's gum grievance, Melissa barging in), and Gregory melts down. Ava — reinstalled as principal — pulls him aside, tells him to pick a substitute from a list (a couple of AI candidates slipped in to check if he learned his lesson from hiring an AI teacher), and reassigns him to full-time AP.

Three side plots braid through the day:

**Justin and the alopecia question.** Janine's class gains Justin, a new student with alopecia areata. His parents asked her not to mention it. Barbara's kindergarteners start staring and pointing, so Barbara consults Mr. Johnson (bald solidarity), then pivots to a lesson on hair coming in many sizes — citing Dwayne "the Rock" Johnson and Christopher Reeve (Superman had alopecia). A parent confronts Barbara because her class inspired another kid to shave his head ("I just wanted to be like Mike" — the Bulls/Gatorade reference). Barbara recovers with a parenting tip: hide the clippers.

**Courtney's home trouble.** Courtney has been lying about being in Podcast Club, stayed late after school, then left campus. Ava learns from her parents that they are divorcing and gives her a hard-line pep talk: skipping class isn't an option, "Abbott's shining star" carries expectations. Janine opens up one-on-one instead, learns Courtney skipped to Ishkabibble's on South Street for a cheesesteak, and promises the same. End of episode: Courtney asks Janine for a "rain check" after school — she's ready to talk.

**Jacob vs. Melissa.** Jacob is furious she has been handing his second-graders gum, which they stick under desks. Melissa defends it as a phone- and focus-saver. The fight leaks into their shared apartment (spaghetti dinners, accusations of disrespect) until they "renew vows" by bingeing Couples Therapy. Melissa brings Gregory a plate of spaghetti and asks him to handle the gum. Jacob, meanwhile, runs through his pet theory about how men react to being proven wrong — "stage one, rewrite history; stage two, double down; stage three, self-destruction" — and Melissa has actually written a book on it.

**Soap-opera loop.** The lounge gag: the staff watches General Hospital obsessively, fixated on the Cassius/Nathan twin reveal. Mr. Johnson has the TV remote all day.

- Barbara: leads the alopecia inclusion lesson; defuses an angry parent.
- Mr. Johnson: offers bald solidarity, finds the lost remote, wins an "S-Tier" trophy (his first), references a backstory as a champion free diver.
- Melissa: doubles down on the gum, brings Gregory spaghetti, "renews vows" with Jacob.
- Jacob: escalates the gum grievance, lectures Gregory on men's denial stages, agrees to get kids to throw gum out before leaving class.
- Ava: handles Courtney, pulls Gregory aside, slips AI subs into the list as a loyalty test.

# Abbott Elementary S06E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Abbott step team competes in a citywide dance competition with a grand prize of an all-expenses-paid trip to Disneyland. Gregory plans to propose to Janine at Disorto restaurant that evening. When the team's best dancer Nevaeh injures her ankle, Ava (coaching the step team) recruits Janine to help rally the kids. Despite setbacks — including a near-empty auditorium after Tariq emailed the wrong PTA — the Abbott community shows up. The team wins first place, earning the Disneyland trip for the first weekend in December. Melissa secures Gregory's reservation at Disorto for that same weekend, meaning Gregory and Janine will chaperone the trip instead of getting engaged privately.
