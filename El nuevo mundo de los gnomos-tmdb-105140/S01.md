## S01E05

## Plot

The small town of Nompton is besieged by murderous garden gnomes led by an awakened "Gnome Queen." Two paranormal investigators, Dana and Gillian, arrive to help and reveal that Arnold, a thirty-something virgin, accidentally freed the queen by touching her obsidian hatchet Bluttrinker on the winter solstice. The curse is mutual: Arnold is now petrifying. With sunrise as the deadline, the group splits: Josie races to Tobias's "gnome factory" to fire up a kiln hot enough to melt the indestructible obsidian, while Arnold and Ellie search Ethel's house for the missing hatchet. The Gnome Queen herself (called "Queenie" by the locals) appears at the end of the episode and Lachlan confronts her directly.

## Important Character Actions

Arnold confesses to waking the queen, learns he is turning to stone, and tells Josie how Jenny gave Lachlan the nickname "Lucky" after he saved her at Nompton Gorge. Josie volunteers for the kiln mission and openly grieves Devon's death from the escape. Ellie helps Arnold search and repeatedly warns "They're coming!" as gnomes close in. Gillian and Dana pose as "Better Gnomes and Gardens" writers, explain the queen's origin, and Gillian carries her great-grandfather's diary containing the re-trapping spell. Lachlan ("Lucky") rallies townsfolk and reveals he became a first responder after saving Jenny at the gorge following his parents' death. Devon dies during the gas-and-escape sequence and is mourned throughout. Tobias runs the "gnome factory," supplies weedkiller for mass extermination, and cheerfully starts the kiln. Ethel briefs Arnold on local gnome activity at her home. Mr Ravi offers a single-shot crossbow. Mr Tingles is an eccentric who chants "All cops are bastards!" and rambles about yo-yos.

Bluttrinker is the indestructible obsidian hatchet, source of the queen's power. Better Gnomes And Gardens is the fake magazine the investigators used as cover. Nompton is the besieged small town. The Gnome Factory is Tobias's weedkiller-and-kiln operation. Queenie is the Gnome Queen's nickname as used by the townsfolk. Nompton Gorge is the gorge where Lucky saved Jenny.
