## Overall premise

Gnomes are a benevolent species, 15 cm tall, weighing 250–300 g, identifiable by their omnipresent conical red cap. They marry at age 100 and live exactly 400 years. By habitat they are classified into forest, garden, farm, house, dune and Siberian gnomes. They live as couples in caves or burrows beneath a large tree, accompanied by a pair of mice and a guardian cricket. Their diet is ovovegetarian. They are telepathic, know nature's secrets and oppose human damage to the environment. Their natural enemies are trolls, who foul, pollute and destroy nature.

In this third series, David the Gnome, Royal Knight of the Environment, is tasked by Millius, head of the council, with traveling the world to solve environmental emergencies. Every episode follows the same pattern: David receives a problem report from somewhere on Earth and travels there to fix it, usually accompanied by his nephew Tomte. Episodes move from jungle (gorillas, chimps, Bengal tigers, Amazon rainforest, jaguars) to oceans (whales, coral reefs, sea turtles, Mediterranean) to wetlands, savannas, deserts, Australia, and the far north. Main human antagonists are poachers, illegal hunters, polluters and careless campers; main non-human antagonists are three trolls — Babas (leader, with a pet spider named Patas), Bruto (with a lizard named Garto) and Tufo (with a bat named Vampi).

## World setting

Gnomes are a benevolent species standing 15 cm tall and weighing 250–300 g, identified by their omnipresent conical red cap. They are seven times stronger than humans relative to size but risk being stepped on in human spaces. They mate at age 100 and live exactly 400 years. Habitat-based subspecies include forest gnome, garden gnome, farm gnome, house gnome, dune gnome and Siberian gnome. They live as couples in caves or burrows beneath a large tree, accompanied by a pair of mice and a guardian cricket. Their diet is ovovegetarian (nature's produce plus surplus bird eggs). They are telepathic, know nature's secrets and oppose human damage to nature. Their natural enemies are trolls, depicted as smelly and clumsy. Gnomes ride birds and enlist forest animals for long-distance travel.

The series' headquarters for global environmental coordination is an unnamed council center led by Millius, who receives distress reports from around the world and assigns them to David.

## Speaking styles

- Narrator: third-person omniscient, warm didactic tone, sets up each episode's ecological lesson at the start. Render as 旁白 with a calm, slightly storybook register.
- David: authoritative, measured, paternal. Uses formal "we" and "the gnomes" when speaking as Royal Knight; otherwise conversational and gentle with animals. Mix of usted and tú; treat both as 你 in Chinese unless an elder-to-child dynamic is explicit.
- Tomte: youthful, eager, occasionally clumsy; short exclamations, occasional awkward phrasing. Render with casual 你 and colloquial tone.
- Lisa: warm, maternal, calm. Polite direct address.
- Millius: official, dispatch-style, short clauses, urgent but composed. Often issues mission briefings.
- Trolls (Babas, Bruto, Tufo): gruff, crude, malaprop-prone; the localized English forms Stinky / Brute / Drool reflect smelly/stinky vocab. Render with coarse 你, vulgar but child-appropriate register, frequent interjections (啊哈, 呸, 嗷呜).
- Animals: anthropomorphized but species-faithful speech (hoots, chirps as interjections rather than full sentences).
- WWF-flavored conservation messaging: educational register, declarative, occasionally rhymed in the original; keep dignified but accessible.

## Cultural context

- The show is the third and final BRB Internacional adaptation of the Wil Huygen / Rien Poortvliet gnome book cycle, succeeding David, el Gnomo (1985) and Wisdom of the Gnomes / La llamada de los gnomos (1987). David and Lisa return from the original; the franchise's environmental-mission framing is preserved and modernized.
- The David el Gnomo franchise has long been a staple of Spanish-language children's television and was widely syndicated in Latin America and the US Hispanic market via Mi Tele on TeleFutura.
- In mainland China, Taiwan and Hong Kong the show aired under multiple Chinese titles; the most common franchise labels are 大卫小矮人 / 小矮人大卫 / 聪明的小矮人. There is no single dominant Chinese title for El nuevo mundo de los gnomos specifically; prefer 《小矮人的新世界》or 《小矮人大卫新篇》 when localization is required, with subtitles clearly distinguishing it from the 1985 original.
- WWF co-production status means ecological accuracy (species names, habitats, threats) is treated as factual and should not be paraphrased away.
- Spanish honorifics: David is "don David" in some episode intros; preserve 阁下 / 大卫先生 in Chinese when used ceremonially. Trolls never receive honorifics.
- Music: Marta Sánchez and Albert Hammond contributed to the soundtrack; opening theme is in the style of the franchise's signature "Soy un gnomo" lineage. Theme song lyrics can be rendered in rhymed Chinese.
- Measurement conventions: heights in cm, weights in grams as per original; do not auto-convert to imperial.
