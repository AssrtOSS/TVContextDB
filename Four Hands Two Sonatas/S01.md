## S01E01

# Four Hands Two Sonatas — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode opens with a flash-forward to the **2017 Prague Harmonie International Competition** finals, then rewinds **six months** to Korea Arts High School. Piano prodigy **Kang Bi-o** (Song Kang) receives a commendation for winning the 27th Lake Erie International Junior Competition Grand Prize, but throws the certificate in the trash — resenting the mandatory assembly and his classmates' jealousy. His background as the grandson of world-renowned conductor **Kang Seung-woon** makes him a target.

Meanwhile, **Choi Jeong-yo** (Lee Jun-young) attends Songmin High (a regular school). His father has fled loan sharks, leaving Jeong-yo to face collectors alone; he's beaten and threatened. Jeong-yo plays piano instinctively at a musical instrument market, where Bi-o and violist **Hong Jae-in** (Jang Gyu-ri) hear him. Jae-in, desperate for an accompanist, tries to recruit him with money and an audition offer through her mother's agency. Jeong-yo rejects her.

Bi-o confronts his grandfather, demanding acknowledgment. Kang Seung-woon cruelly refuses — he doesn't know who Bi-o's father is, and dismisses Bi-o's talent as "empty technique." Bi-o vows to play at the **Noblesse Concert**; if his grandfather still denies him, he'll quit piano. He also asks to switch from Professor Seo to pianist **Hyun-soo**, angering his mother and current teacher.

Director **Yoon Seon-ju** of Korea Arts High recognizes Jeong-yo from a competition seven years prior and offers a full scholarship + dorm. Jeong-yo initially refuses (cynical about "artists needing money"), but after his father disappears and he's left homeless, he calls to accept.

The episode ends with Jeong-yo transferring into Bi-o's class (Year 1, Class 8). He refuses to play for the class, earning instant hostility. Bi-o watches silently.

## Important Character Actions
- **Kang Bi-o**: Throws away award certificate; confronts grandfather; demands teacher change; declares he'll quit piano if not acknowledged at Noblesse Concert.
- **Choi Jeong-yo**: Fends off loan sharks; plays piano at market (raw, untrained genius); rejects Jae-in's offer; initially refuses scholarship; later accepts dorm/transfer after father vanishes.
- **Hong Jae-in**: Defends Bi-o from bullies; discovers Jeong-yo; aggressively recruits him as accompanist (money + agency audition).
- **Kang Seung-woon (grandfather)**: Publicly humiliates Bi-o — denies paternity, calls his playing "vanity without soul," agrees to attend Noblesse Concert as a test.
- **Yoon Seon-ju (Director)**: Recruits Jeong-yo pragmatically ("looks good for my record"), offers full support.
