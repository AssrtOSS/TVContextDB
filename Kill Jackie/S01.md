## S01E03

# She's Gone (S01E03)

## Plot

Jackie recovers in a hospital-area bolthole she prepared twenty years ago. An ally confirms she is the Seven Demons' next contract, and reveals the journal she stole from Linden names the client who hired the Demons. With Billy already dead and Charlie killed by the latest assassin (a Yank codenamed "The Yank"), Jackie lays out a "keep the faith" counter-plan.

She begins with a confidence exercise: a flashback to twenty years earlier at St Thomas Hospital, where a roster of black-list targets is recited (Le Cubain, the knife guy, Karenina, La Doc) over the line "there were seven, then six." Jackie coldly picks "yellow" coffee over Colombian, declaring she's ready to operate again.

To seed her trap, Jackie visits a vintage Northern Soul shop ("Vinyl Vultures — Garde la Foi") and offloads one of only four known copies of the 1976 Hamilton Movement reissue of "She's Gone" for €1,000, in exchange for the owners texting her whenever a serious buyer surfaces.

She then turns to her hardest lead: Zigor, the retired Demon boss, who has been hiding so long that "anyone who finds me dies." Using a service called Poltergeist (a "banking/concierge" outfit that secretly locates people who don't want to be found), she tracks him down, threatens him with a throat-slitting, and offers him a chance at revenge. Zigor warns her the new Demons are a "capitalist killing machine" with no code. They toast with what Zigor assumes is brandy but turns out to be pure ecstasy; both get high, dance, and talk about ghosts — Zigor sees his murdered wife everywhere.

Meanwhile, Sam is sent to a pharmacy with a prescription for the same restricted antidepressants. He charms the pharmacist while cross-dressed (fake breasts, the lot), walking out with both the antidepressants and a stash of sildenafil (Viagra). The pharmacist warns him never to mix the two.

Back at the record shop, the bait catches: Li Dong (the knife guy) buys the rare vinyl for €9,000. Jackie, Sam, and Zigor intercept him and dose him with the antidepressant-Viagra cocktail, which Zigor and Jackie observe through a livestream. Li Dong, predictably, enters a self-destructive frenzy and begins peeling himself with a kitchen peeler ("économe"). The footage reaches The Doc, who recognises the method as her own: "Someone is stealing my techniques. Show the others, I'm coming."

The episode closes on Sam, horrified: he realises Jackie never intended to hand Li Dong to the police and that he is now an accessory. An end card dedicates the episode to Ana Fabo (1967–2025).

## Important Character Actions

- **Jackie** — Ditches Gwen's demand for transparency with a false promise to "hand the killers to police." Rigs the Vinyl Vultures bait, calls in Poltergeist, and executes the drug/peeler broadcast against Li Dong.
- **Gwen** — Confronts Jackie about being kept in the dark; refuses to be told what to do with her body; is sent off to fence a controlled-substance prescription.
- **Zigor** — Recruited back into action; reveals the Demons killed his wife when he tried to quit; high on ecstasy, sees ghosts of her everywhere.
- **Sam** — Cross-dresses to obtain restricted antidepressants + Viagra; later realises he's been made complicit in Li Dong's death.
- **Li Dong** — Takes the bait at Vinyl Vultures; ingests the cocktail and self-mutilates on camera with a peeler.
- **The Doc** — Watches the Li Dong footage, identifies the technique as her own, and announces she is coming to confront whoever stole her method.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **The Yank / Sean Harper** — the new assassin Jackie's team identifies after Charlie's death.
- **Poltergeist** — nominally a banking/concierge outfit, actually a "find anyone" surveillance resource; Jackie's go-to for locating hidden targets.
- **Vinyl Vultures (Northern Soul — Garde la Foi)** — the record shop used as bait.
- **The Hamilton Movement, "She's Gone"** — the 1976 reissue; only four copies in existence, $9,000 auction price; the episode's title reference.
- **Galeretta family** — Galician mafia syndicate (referenced via Zigor's myth of eyes-in-a-bucket).
- **Black List numbering** — "1. Le Cubain, 2. Le type au couteau, 3. Karenina, 4. La Doc" presented as a hit parade in the flashback.
- **"Garde la foi" / Keep the faith** — recurring mantra (shop slogan, Zigor's affirmations, Jackie's own closer).
- **Cosaque witch** — Karenina's nickname.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The hospital-area safehouse was built twenty years ago — long-standing Jackie infrastructure.
- Linden's stolen journal is the proof of who commissioned the Demons; still in Jackie's hands.
- "There were seven, then six" — at least one Demon slot is already empty before the season's hunt began.
- Zigor's wife was killed by the Demons when he tried to leave; he has lived in hiding ever since, and his guilt/mourning is unresolved.
- The Yank killed Billy and Charlie (per Jackie's lines and Gwen's earlier confrontation).
- Sam is now, in his own words, "the transvestite proxy killer" — his moral and legal exposure becomes a season-long liability.
- Jackie explicitly lied to Gwen about delivering the assassins to police; Gwen's trust is now a live thread.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- "Garde la foi" doubles as shop slogan, Zigor's affirmation tape, and Jackie's house style — useful verbal tic to track.
- Zigor's myth about harvesting Galician mobsters' ears/noses/testicles ("braised with garlic and rosemary… the ones I didn't eat") is the kind of legend Jackie calls "everybody loves myths"; the same line recurs as Demon branding.
- The peeler (économe) and the antidepressant-Viagra cocktail are now a documented Demon-adjacent technique — The Doc's "this is mine" line means the playbook is exposed and escalating.
- Sam's running line "I'm a straight man. These days, and it's great" / the fake-breasts gag — recurring Sam-vs-knife-guy humiliations to mine later.
- Jackie calling Poltergeist the way most people call a plumber normalises it as an ongoing resource, not a one-off.

## Unresolved Threads

- Who hired the Demons — Linden journal still unopened on screen.
- The Yank / Sean Harper is on the loose, untargeted this episode.
- The Doc is en route after watching the Li Dong footage — direct Doc vs. Jackie collision queued.
- Sam's status as an unwilling accomplice, his fury at Jackie, and the live phone-call link to the safehouse during the Li Dong murder.
- Gwen is furious, armed with a sellable controlled-substance script, and no longer trusts Jackie.

- The full current seven-person roster beyond Le Cubain, Li Dong, Karenina, The Doc remains unnamed on screen.
- Dedication to Ana Fabo (1967–2025) — real-life crew memorial that may recur in closing credits if the series continues the tribute pattern.

## S01E01

# S01E01 《Burning Down The House》— 《火烧连营》

## Plot

Jackie Price runs a tax-free art auction at the Bilbao freeport. She rejects a $60M bid from "the Yankie" over bad manners (and his wife's eyebrows, and him being "weird"), selling instead to an Indian buyer for $58M. Eight years of sourcing wasted. Charlie confirms the triptych funds cleared via the Mumbai bank; they plan lapsang souchong at the Dorchester.

In London, police have cordoned Jackie's building. Her across-the-hall neighbor Didi Fraser has been shot twice in the chest and once in the head — in that order. Inspector Leo Baxter covers for her. Jackie suspects a message meant for her, since she lives on the same floor.

At the cordon, Jackie is wounded in what appears to be a staged/flashback beat, then wakes in hospital. Charlie has packed her hyaluronic acid, exfoliant, pretty underwear, and a prepaid phone. Jackie tells Charlie her past: cocaine queenpin, twenty years ago, "what's harder than being good is getting out," her past has caught up. She tells Charlie not to go home tonight — go to a hotel.

Jackie confronts Mr. Linden, a broker in the art world. She claims one of his clients tried to kill her, gives him until tomorrow to name them, or she unleashes a "controlled vortex of destruction." She leaves with part of someone's ear lobe; Leo (whom she pays via mortgage and school fees) extracts her.

At the station, her tax lawyer Sam arrives with an "error of identity" defense, then confesses he's "a little in love with her." She rejects.

Jackie rings her art dealer Tucker: organize a secret auction of her entire collection to one buyer, cash, fast. He pushes commission to 20%, passphrase "Jelly rolls and cream tonight."

Billy (Welsh enforcer, waxing-salon mogul with Ms. Ho's team) is cleaning Jackie's flat. He mentions Karenina, Jackie's former security consultant, now on probation with the Seven Demons. Jackie asks Karenina to hire a Demon for personal security — and discovers she herself is the next contract. Assigned to her: the Cuban. Jackie reasons the real attackers were ordinary thugs, not Demons, or she'd be dead. Her contract starts at midnight; until then they're friends.

The Cuban visits Billy in Wales, asking where Jackie is. Billy repeatedly answers "Wales." Billy is offered exile — Vancouver (needs new face), Iceland (too cold), Brazil (beach) — chosen. He and Jackie share a loaded memory of last Christmas alone, turkey in gravy, keys never returned. He goes.

Jackie corners Leo: was he paid to let the attackers in? He admits "like always." "Flic pourri un jour, flic pourri toujours." She lets him board the plane anyway. Then: "Leo has been delayed. Permanently."

Jackie rings Sam: liquidate everything, cash, she needs to survive. Sam's office is on fire. Her apartment has already been blown up. He protests — adult, job, career, Friday friends, his mother and Barbara. He reveals: Charlie is twelve. Jackie's plan: sell the art, take the cash, run. Old school. After twenty years she's herself again, and it's exciting.

Final beat: Charlie urgently needs a toilet.

# S01E05 — Here She Comes

## Plot

Cold open: Jackie and Zigor in a Bilbao-area safehouse she set up 20 years ago. She declares she's going to destroy the Seven Demons; Zigor warns the Doc is hunting Poltergeist's servers and Jackie will be broke by nightfall. Cut to Swansea: a market shooting flashes, then Jackie's estranged father (a Scotsman from Strathbungo) — recently shot — hobbles into his old house, greets a girl in a wedding dress, and is immediately jumped by Dai's crew (Aled and Colin among them). They want £50,000 of "Dai's money," have Ewan Price (Jackie's grandfather) locked in a water tank out back, and tie up the father and the bride to wait for Dai.

Jackie arrives. The father is mystified — he abandoned her, lost a Crete villa gambling, has been stripping the family's copper pipes to sell. He thinks Gwen is "his" granddaughter and rants about Gwen's real mother being "lazy" for giving her up. Gwen suspects her "grandfather" lied about her origins. Mid-standoff, while thugs burn time smoking Gorseinon Green vs. Afghan Black and debating Dai's methods, Paley privately confesses he was mailed a revolver by courier the week before, was told Jackie's arrival was imminent, and has already tipped off "the Demons." When Dai finally pulls up, a second, external team opens fire on the house — Aled is hit, chaos erupts. The father sends Gwen through his kitchen trap door (behind the bin, into next door's garden, where his escape bicycle is parked), apologizes for ruining her mother, says "remember me like this," and stays to draw fire.

Closing beat: Gwen, instead of running, walks up to the gunmen, tells them "I'm her daughter, get me out of this hole, I'll lead you to her." They offer her a ride. Last line, a callback, "À l'ancienne, c'est la meilleure école," as the car pulls away.

## S01E07

## Plot

The episode opens with Jackie Price and her daughter Gwen held captive in Karenina's van. Karenina radios her Demons handler with triumphant "I have Jackie Price" call-ins, earning praise and receiving coordinates. Aboard the van, mother and daughter bicker bitterly: Gwen accuses Jackie of abandoning her as a baby and ruining her life, while Jackie insists the separation was the only way to keep her alive from people who wanted her dead the day she was born. They argue about TikTok and small talk, neither able to connect.

Meanwhile, Don Markel Junior is in the Markel family wine estate trying to push through a shady sale. His father, Don Markel Senior, dresses him down with disgust for his "shitty tennis coach" mustache, reveals that Junior's mother had a half-brother with Mitch, and audits his accounts — exposing repeated $500,000+ payments to a shell company that has since vanished. Junior confesses he hired the Seven Demons to kill the owner of an Almoril painting (one of four, the family already owns one) so he could complete the set. Markel Senior, furious his son "couldn't even murder properly," cuts him off financially, citing past prostate cancer ("Monsieur Prostate") and irradiated testicles that left Junior as his only heir. He confesses he "hated that fucking painting" all along.

In Saint-Sébastien (Spain), Karenina keeps Jackie and Gwen cuffed with Magnitogorsk steel and cheap Japanese Bluetooth earpieces, monologuing about loyalty and pitching Jackie to "change sides" and join the Demons. She mocks Karenina as "the weak link." Junior, broke and panicking, calls the Yank (Sean Harper) to cancel his hit contract — then admits his father has kidnapped him in retaliation. Sean realizes the Demons took Senior as collateral.

In parallel, Zigor and Ander track Jackie's GPS signal (she had a traceur hidden) from a car; Jackie's playlist yields a full unabridged audiobook of Anne of Green Gables ("Anne... la maison aux pignons verts, version intégrale") — a running joke about her wildly tasteful taste. The trace stops, and they fear the worst.

In Bilbao, Sean Harper Senior meets the Demons team for a parley over Don Markel Sr.'s ransom. Sean Sr., a sharp philosophical negotiator with a terminal colon cancer diagnosis (three months to live, drinking morphine, not Scotch), refuses to pay a single cent to extortion, refuses to renegotiate Junior's contract, but reveals that Junior remains in his will — handing Sean Jr. a major negotiation lever. He lectures Sean Jr. on "how men negotiate" with mutual respect, and warns Sean not to tempt him to rewrite the will. The Demons, unable to shake him, ultimately consider compromise.

Back in the van, Jackie's Bluetooth exploit — the cheap Japanese earpieces can't pair to two devices at once — gives Gwen an opening. She flees into woods. Karenina discovers a cold wallet in Jackie's phone with "many zeros" (crypto Poltergeist paid her after she trashed their silos), and warns that the dead Poltergeist courier is a bigger problem. She smashes Jackie's phone to kill the GPS trace. Jackie tells Gwen the Poltergeist operation cost them a man, making Jackie a much bigger threat than Karenina.

Junior arrives at the Markel estate with a gun, furious Sean Sr. won't pay. Sean Sr. calmly disarms the situation by reminding Junior he's still in the will, tells the Demons to "find common ground," and then collapses — Junior screams, "Papa, non!" Sean and his crew run. The episode ends with Karenina still holding Jackie and Gwen, a fresh Demons vs. Markel crisis unfolding, and the cold wallet unspent.

## S01E08

## S01E08 — Wind of Change

### Plot

Cold-open voiceover teases the season arcs: who hired the Demons, Gwen's "je te déteste," Friedrich cutting Gwen's funds, Poltergeist's servers attacked, Jackie's plan to bankrupt the Demons.

Jackie retreats to a Spanish Pyrénées vineyard ("Le Don") and buys Poltergeist's "Sécurité Maximale" package — an unmarked van loaded with Glock, MP5K, AK-47, Negev LMG, ammo, C4, tactical gear, sauna, bamboo fittings, bulletproof glass. Account balance: 0.00.

Don Markel Junior, on the run after Friedrich killed his father, is captured by Markel's people and handed to Jackie. He breaks down in the back of the van, offering his father's billions.

Don Markel Senior reconciles with Jackie and offers his docks.

Jackie recruits The Doc (Friedrich's ex-wife) to switch sides. The Doc agrees for survival ("Je choisirai le camp des vainqueurs") and accepts a mission: take Friedrich for a drive tomorrow and plant the C4.

Zigor is broken. He froze on a clean sniper shot at Karenina yesterday, saw his dead wife in the woods, and refuses to kill. He hands over his knives (one is a wedding-day gift) and walks away. "Laisse-le partir."

Friedrich grooms Gwen via texts, £500,000 Welsh-bank deposits, and Malibu rental offers — tracking her purchases.

Climax: Friedrich lures the survivors (Jackie, Sam, The Doc, Karenina, Sean the Yank, Junior in the van) to a sit-down. Game-theory ultimatum — kill another or die. Sean yields first ("Tout est de ma faute... ja"). Three-minute timer. Zigor, who'd left, returns and snipes Friedrich from a distance. "C'est l'heure."

## Plot

Cold open / context. Under the drug Sam completely breaks: he confesses his life was a one-woman show for his mother (law exams, breaking up with Hannah, his class-president campaign), that he "cancelled" his wedding to Hannah and is still in love, and rhapsodises into "free at last at last." He then reveals Jackie's emergency pager — hidden in his rectum before capture.

Karenina, the Demons' Russian-trained special-forces sniper stuck on technical work, is furious she's been benched ("this is a sensitive subject for me"). She acquires one of Jackie's prepay burners and saves the number as "Maman," betting Jackie will call back. Jackie sees through it; their ping-pong escalates to "you really want to fight? come on then / I'll kill you with one finger / great." Karenina also pitches Fred to let her take the shot; the bid is held for later.

In San Sebastián Jackie and Zigor strategise. She is in the Demons' blind spot — they think she has fled to Brazil or Lagos while their AI and crypto traces run. Zigor warns "the Demons have your daughter"; Jackie concedes "she hates me and she's not wrong" but commits to playing the long game. Zigor peels off to intercept; Jackie holds the line.

Sam uses the recovered rectal pager to call Zigor, claiming he has "escaped the Demons" and is "in the woods." Jackie ("good work") had slipped a tracker into Sam's bag earlier — they set off to retrieve him while Karenina peels off to "handle the Doc."

The Doc also hints Gwen has "a personality disorder" — "it's my fault… or maybe trauma, guilt, shame."

Mid-confession the Doc is violently interrupted. Cliffhanger montage: a Demons roll call (The Cuban, The Knife Guy [Li Dong], Karenina, The Doc, Akilles, Tuukka — Friedrich conspicuously absent), and Sam screams "Help me!" Cut to black: To be continued.
