## S18E58

## Episode Summary — S18E58: Judges vs Semifinalists

### Episode Plot

This episode features a two-round special pitting the judges against the three semifinalists (Vinnie, Aaron, Petro). In Round 1, judges Poh Ling Yeow and Jean-Christophe Novelli cook head-to-head with **potato** as the hero ingredient. The three semifinalists join Sofia Levin as judges. The winner earns 12 months of bragging rights; the loser does the washing-up. There is no elimination.

In Round 2, it is a team challenge: Poh and Jean-Christophe vs the "boy band" (Vinnie, Aaron, Petro). Both teams have **75 minutes** to produce a finals feast — at least two mains, three sides, and a dessert each. Andy Allen is on paternity leave. Sofia is joined at the judging table by guest judges Dot McHugh and former contestant Luke. Both feasts are declared exceptional, but on execution the **judges' feast is deemed flawless**, winning the round. The episode closes with the semifinal brief: the three semifinalists must prepare a two-course dining experience (main + dessert). Only two spots remain in the Grand Finale.

### Important Character Actions

- **Poh** cooks her "redemption dish" — Sardinian **culurgione** (semolina potato dumpling with mint and pecorino), the very dish that eliminated her in Season 12 due to raw pasta dough. This is her third attempt. She wins Round 1. In Round 2 she produces a Jerusalem artichoke velouté, radish with anchovies, fennel with ouzo reduction, and an **ile flottante** (floating island) dessert, all described as a "full package" of support acts that perfectly complement Jean-Christophe's mains.
- **Jean-Christophe** attempts potato four ways — steamed dariole with chicken mousse, sliced Desiree potatoes, crispy potatoes, potato pearls — plus a layered sauce made with various alcohols poured by feel. He runs catastrophically out of time, throws long chives over the plate at the last second, and is chaotic ("Cyclone Jean-Christophe"). Despite the messy plating, his technique and sauce are praised as Michelin-star level. In Round 2 he makes crispy chicken gigolettes, ratatouille, gratin dauphinois, red pepper brandade, and a fish dish with sweet potato and mozzarella.
- **Vinnie** relishes sitting on the judging side. In Round 2 he cooks rib-eye steak on the hibachi with red wine jus and makes gratin dauphinois for the first time — it comes out too wet and he desperately tries to press out moisture and blast it in the oven.
- **Aaron** is on butterflied herb prawns and the red wine jus in Round 2; his jus is called "Louvre-worthy" by the judges. He also judges in Round 1.
- **Petro** makes an olive-oil and **mastiha** chiffon cake with strawberries and figs — **using Poh's own recipe**, which he got from her directly. The cake is oversized, still warm when he ices it, and nearly collapses, but the result is declared "exceptional" and "perfect." He also tells an emotional story about growing up as a massive fan of Poh, calling her the reason he fell in love with cooking; they share a hug.

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Culurgione** — Sardinian semolina potato dumpling, Poh's redemption dish (previously failed in Season 12).
- **Autour du monde sur une pomme de terre / Around-the-world flying with potatoes** — Jean-Christophe's Round 1 dish name.
- **Ile flottante / Floating island** — Poh's Round 2 dessert (mandarin-steeped custard).
- **Gratin dauphinois** — made by both Vinnie and Jean-Christophe in Round 2; Vinnie's first attempt.
- **Brandade** — red pepper brandade, Jean-Christophe's fish accompaniment.
- **Mastiha** — Greek tree resin used in both Petro's chiffon and referenced in Poh's desserts.
- **Cyclone Jean-Christophe / The Hurricane** — nickname for his frantic late-cook chaos.
- **"Poh it"** — the rapid crimping technique Poh uses to seal dumplings.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Poh's Season 12 elimination was specifically due to raw pleating on her pasta dough — she finally lays it to rest in this episode.
- Petro's entire arc culminates here; he describes this semifinal challenge as the one he has "dreamt of" that "could rewrite their history."
- The next episode is the **Semifinal**: Vinnie, Aaron, and Petro must prepare a two-course menu (main + dessert). Only **two** will advance to the Grand Finale.
- Andy remains on paternity leave; his absence becomes a running joke about needing to "think twice about having another baby."
- Sofia is now the de facto lead judge and host.
- Luke (former contestant) and Dot McHugh sit as guest judges in Round 2 — Luke's enjoyment of the power becomes a comic beat.
- Poh and Jean-Christophe are both described as solo workers who barely communicate yet trust each other completely; their combined feast is praised as "speaking the same language."
- The semifinalist "boy band" has been cooking together for six months and displays near-telepathic teamwork.

### Unresolved Threads

- The **Semifinal** is set: Vinnie, Aaron, and Petro must design a two-course menu (main + dessert) to prove they deserve one of only **two Grand Finale spots**.
- Petro's emotional payoff is building — he is living his dream but still needs to secure a finals berth.
- Aaron feels he has "earned, not just a jacket, but a place in the top three."
- Vinnie declares there is "no turning back now."
- Andy's return timeline is unclear; he is expected back for the Semifinal or Grand Finale.
- The Petro-Poh fan-to-peer relationship is newly cemented and may carry into future episodes.
