## S03E01


Episode S03E01 of Beauty in Black

Plot
The episode opens on the immediate aftermath of the Season 2 finale arrests. FBI agents are escorting Norman, Jules, Olivia, and Roy out in cuffs. Kimmie, having turned state's evidence, is the only one walking free — Agent Paul offers her a deal (no jail time in exchange for intel), but she tries to dictate terms and gets dragged back into holding. The episode then cuts between several storylines running in parallel during the same 24-hour window.

At the strip club, Rain arrives to discover she now owns the building. A woman named Gina walks into Rain's office, claims Delinda owed her organisation a fortune, and announces that the sale makes Rain her new "partner" — meaning a cut of every dollar that flows through the club goes to Gina's shadowy boss. Rain refuses, throws her out, and tells her loyalists Big J and Michael to lock the office. The two men quietly warn Rain that Gina is a figurehead for something far bigger; Rain dismisses them as irrelevant.

Meanwhile Mallory, also out on bail, gets a call from an unknown woman claiming Jules has been released. Mallory checks with Kimmie, who confirms everyone is still locked up — the call was a trap. A separate call from Officer Trackson lures Mallory out to meet him. Officer Alex is ordered by Kimmie to get to Rain immediately; Alex is in the middle of his own problems, since Jules's people are hunting him for a perceived betrayal.

At the Bellarie mansion, Charles turns up drunk at Mallory's door, confesses he killed two would-be robbers in self-defence, and admits he was dismembering the bodies in the basement when Attorney Varney walked in and saw everything. Olivia (his mother) had it cleaned up and is now extorting him for $250,000. Charles asks Mallory for the money, threatens suicide when she refuses, and storms out. Mallory calls Varney for help and, on his urging, drives out with him to check on Charles.

Adeline argues with her husband Giovanni that he is too soft and trusting. The argument is interrupted by a teenage girl, Evan, who witnessed Giovanni's two killings and fears she will be silenced. Giovanni offers her money and a hotel; she tearfully asks to stay, and when he refuses she asks to come back in the morning — Giovanni says no again, calling her pathetic.

A new S1 thread: Kimmie's younger sister Sylvie has learned that Rain killed a boy named Glen in self-defence and is cracking under the weight of the family's secrets. Kimmie reassures her that Jules is in jail, then drops the bombshell that Jules once stuffed Sylvie in a car trunk after she went to the hospital and watched him taken off life support. Kimmie tells Sylvie she will one day be free of all of it and sent to college with therapy — but insists on silence for now.

In the holding cell, Norman and Roy scheme to escape using the ties they weren't stripped of (a hanging hazard that was overlooked). Roy is spiralling into heroin withdrawal, which Norman exploits. Norman — who has somehow been given a cell phone by the agents — uses the promise of drugs to extract Roy's confession about the Season 1 hit-and-run: Roy says Jules ran the victim over in an SUV, then had the junkyard attendant killed and stuffed the still-alive victim in the trunk of Roy's car.

Jules calls Kimmie from jail to scream about the betrayal. Kimmie hangs up on him.

In a separate holding cell, Olivia (out on bail for her leg injury, demanding painkillers) meets a chatty cellmate named Lindsay, who cheerfully reveals that Olivia owes Gina $250,000 by Friday and that "tick tock" is coming.

The episode ends with Horace and the wayward young man he has been bankrolling. The kid confesses that he stole the cars and money to feel like a man and only ran because Horace had the club shot up. Horace tells him to get out; the kid refuses to leave and begs to stay. Horace yells "Enough!" The scene cuts to a second confrontation in which the kid is now hooked on heroin and Horace demands he pick up the cane dropped during the argument.

Character actions worth tracking
- Kimmie: tries to negotiate her way out of FBI custody by demanding they show their cards first; tells Sylvie the full truth about Jules and the trunk; orchestrates Alex's deployment to protect Rain; fields Jules's angry call from jail and hangs up on him.
- Rain: becomes the new club owner; stands alone against Gina's extortion; demands loyalty from her crew ("your boss is a bad bitch too"); reveals she is keeping a secret stash skim — for every $20 a dancer puts in the drawer she takes $2 for herself.
- Officer Alex: ordered to Rain by Kimmie; being hunted by Jules's people for an alleged betrayal that is hinted at but not yet explained.
- Mallory: receives the trap call about Jules's release, then a second call luring her out to meet Trackson; refuses to give Charles the $250k; drives out with Varney to find Charles.
- Charles: confesses to Mallory the full chain — robbery, shooting, basement dismemberment, Varney witnessing it, Olivia's cover-up and $250k extortion; threatens suicide and storms off drunk.
- Varney: voicemail-only at first, then takes Mallory's call; tells her flatly that she loves Charles and needs to go check on him.
- Norman: emerges in jail as a schemer, leveraging Roy's heroin addiction, the smuggled cell phone, and his supposed contact with the lead agent to extract the hit-and-run confession.
- Roy: finally breaks under heroin withdrawal and spills the full hit-and-run story to Norman.
- Horace: violently rejects the young man he has been bankrolling, then coldly orders him to pick up his cane as the man writhes in withdrawal.
- Jules: calls from jail to threaten Kimmie, who is unmoved.
- Adeline and Giovanni: established as a couple running together; Adeline accuses Giovanni of trusting people too easily.
- Evan (and her sister): teen witnesses of Giovanni's double murder; accept the hush money but want to stay near him.

Newly established names and terms
- Agent Paul: the lead FBI interrogator ("Agent Paul, Agent Paul" — Kimmie keeps repeating his name mockingly). Kimmie calls him Peter once; he corrects her. Norman later refers to him as "Paul or Peter, whatever his name is," hinting that the agent's identity is unsettled or that Norman is being deliberately slippery.
- Gina: representative of a powerful criminal organisation. Claims Delinda owed her boss; arrives at the club to install herself as Rain's "partner." She is a protégé of Delinda's and has historically come by the club to collect "bags of cash" in person. Uses the phrase "Tick tock."
- Lindsay: a chatty Black woman sharing a holding cell with Olivia, used to deliver the $250k-by-Friday threat. She also nicknames Horace's butler "butler nigga" before correcting herself to "James."
- Big J and Michael: Rain's two loyal enforcers at the club. Kimmie dismisses them as "them niggas are the worst," but Horace rates them as "good."
- Evan: a teenage girl who, with her unnamed sister, witnessed Giovanni commit two murders. She wants protection, not just money; begs Giovanni to let her stay.
- Glen: a teenage boy Sylvie reveals was killed by Rain. Sylvie says he "was gonna kill me" and she "should've just let him" — the self-defence framing mirrors Charles's story and may itself be unreliable.
- The unnamed "young man" Horace is bankrolling: he stole Horace's cars and money, fled after the club was shot up, and is now revealed to be a heroin addict. He has a sister who travels with him; Evan is his sister by adoption/fellow-traveler — the dialogue is ambiguous.
- "Caged birds" / "this shit is for the birds": Norman and Roy's shared joke about the holding cell.
- "The Mafia" (used loosely by Alex): the criminal organisation Gina works for; Alex says it without irony.
- Korean barbecue spot on Wilson: Alex's known hangout, used as a setup location.
- "That little bitch" / "she stays in the shadows": Rain's nickname for Gina before she knows her name.
- "Bad bitch": Rain's self-identifier when establishing dominance over her own staff.

Continuity-relevant facts
- Jules, Norman, Olivia, and Roy are all in federal custody at the start of the episode; Kimmie, Mallory, and Horace are free.
- Kimmie is confirmed to have "turned state's evidence" — Roy references this directly to Norman.
- The Season 1 hit-and-run: Roy now confirms in detail that Jules ran the victim over in an SUV, had the junkyard attendant murdered, and put the still-alive victim in the trunk of Roy's car before returning the car to Roy.
- Attorney Varney is still in play and is now aware of Charles's basement dismemberment; he is also being used by Mallory as a sounding board and is apparently late-night contactable.
- Officer Trackson is now openly antagonistic toward Jules's network — Alex warns his crew that Trackson is "after me over Jules."
- The Season 2 finale loose end of Adeline and Giovanni being a couple is reinforced; Adeline speaks fluent Spanish ("Ay, Dios") when annoyed.
- Charles's Bellarie shares have just been diluted, which is part of why Mallory refuses his $250k loan.
- The strip club's money pipeline is now framed explicitly as organised-crime laundered cash, not just Delinda's personal slush fund.
- Kimmie refers to the agent as "Paul or Peter, whatever his name is" — possibly a continuity seed, possibly just Norman's dismissive speech.

Unresolved threads
- Who is Gina's boss? Alex calls them "the Mafia" but the organisation is unnamed; the threat is open-ended.
- Will Rain pay Gina the demanded cut, or escalate to open war? Her skim operation is now an added pressure point.
- Kimmie's negotiation with Agent Paul — she demanded he show his cards first, then got pulled back. Her deal is still in limbo.
- The truth of Glen's death: Sylvie claims self-defence, but Glen was a teenager and Rain did the killing. Kimmie's promise of therapy and college for Sylvie is the first time a S3 endpoint for the sisters has been hinted at.
- The "Paul or Peter" confusion around the lead agent — possibly a planted identity thread for later.
- The heroin plotline with Horace's young man: the kid is now hooked, dependent on Horace, and Horace is using the cane scene to re-establish dominance. Whether Horace is enabling or trapping him is ambiguous.
- Jules's people are hunting Alex for a "betrayal" that hasn't been explained on screen yet; Alex is also a cop and therefore double-exposed.
- Charles's whereabouts after he storms out drunk and suicidal — Mallory and Varney are driving out to find him, but the episode cuts away before the resolution.
- Olivia's $250k debt to Gina, due Friday — the same number Charles tried to borrow. Whether this is coincidence, Olivia leaking Charles's request to Gina, or a deliberate squeeze is unresolved.
- The teen witness Evan and her sister: paid off and sent to a hotel, but Evan promises to "come back" — she is almost certainly going to reappear.
- Roy's confession to Norman: the hit-and-run detail (junkyard attendant killed, victim alive in the trunk) is new testimony that will matter if either testifies. Roy believes Norman can deliver drugs, but Norman is bluffing — what happens when Roy figures that out?
- Norman's smuggled cell phone: he claims to have an agent's phone and uses it to extract the confession. If the feds realise he has it, the leverage disappears.

## S03E06 Summary

### Episode Plot
Horace Bellarie, recovered from his poisoning, storms Stanley Heart hospital and summons the board. He identifies Larry as the inside man who facilitated injecting him with cancer cells — orchestrated by Norman. With Officers Alex and Trackson as muscle, Horace brutally beats a confession out of Larry: Norman has four+ federal judges, officers, lawyers, and doctors in his pocket, plus blackmail files on women (some underage). Horace orders Trackson to murder Larry's wife and children in front of him, then make Larry's death maximally painful.

Meanwhile, Olivia and Norman trade threats in jail; Norman refuses to help her. Kimmie officially takes over the strip club, renaming it **Make It Rain** and asserting authority over staff (Rain, Angel, Adeline). Mallory bonds with a female friend over their disastrous Bellarie marriages (Roy, Horace), planning to visit Roy in jail to taunt him. Charles and Varney execute a tense cash-for-evidence drop in an alley; they're flanked, Charles initially refuses to pay, revealing he wants both parents dead. Varney warns Olivia likely has "more body parts" hidden. The episode closes with Horace demanding to see Judge Henry Kilwins at his home, and Norman telling someone (likely Horace on the phone): "Time to pay the devil."

### Important Character Actions
- **Horace**: Tortures a board member, orders extrajudicial killings of innocents, asserts total control over hospital and family.
- **Norman**: Confesses (via Larry) to masterminding the poisoning; holds blackmail leverage over judges/officers; refuses Olivia aid; taunts her from jail.
- **Olivia**: Withholds remaining evidence as leverage; threatens Norman from her cell.
- **Kimmie**: Buys/rebrands club to "Make It Rain"; installs new sign; manages staff dynamics.
- **Charles**: Sabotages the drop, declares he wants Horace and Olivia dead; Varney forces him into the car.
- **Varney**: Brokers the exchange; warns Charles about Olivia's insurance (more bodies).
- **Mallory**: Forms alliance with friend over shared Bellarie-husband trauma; plans jail visit to Roy.
- **Officers Alex & Trackson**: Fully complicit in Horace's brutality.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Stanley Heart** — hospital where Horace was poisoned; board meeting site.
- **Henry Kilwins** — federal judge assigned to the case; Horace summons him to his house.
- **"The package"** — physical evidence/bodies being traded (partial delivery; rest held by Olivia).
- **Make It Rain** — Kimmie's new name for the strip club (formerly Jules's).

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Horace was poisoned with cancer cells during his hospitalization; Norman orchestrated it via compromised hospital staff.
- Norman controls ≥4 federal judges, plus officers, lawyers, doctors; holds files on women (some underage) as blackmail.
- Both Olivia and Norman are incarcerated.
- Kimmie now owns the club; Horace is her husband (forced marriage S1/S2).
- Charles explicitly wants both parents dead; Varney implies Olivia has additional bodies hidden.
- Mallory and her friend (unnamed) bond as Bellarie wives; both husbands are addicts/criminals.
