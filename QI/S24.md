## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Caroline Herschel** — first female professional astronomer (£50/yr from George III); discovered 8 comets, 500+ missing stars; 168-year gap until next woman won RAS gold medal.
- **Thermicon** (Hincks & Co, 1880s) — stoneware bottle filled with boiling water, brushed through hair; asbestos-insulated DIY vacuum-toaster hairdryer in *Popular Mechanics Handbook for Women* (1928).
- **Red colobus monkeys (Zanzibar)** — learned to eat charcoal (adsorbs cyanide from almond/mango leaves); population density jumped 50→550/km² in a decade.
- **Microplastics** — 2025 French study: glass bottles leach 5–50× more microplastics than plastic (polyester cap paint). UNM study: human brains contain up to 10 g microplastics (~1 plastic spoon).
- **Water myth** — "8 glasses / 2 L daily" is a forfeit; most need 1.5–1.8 L, largely from food.
- **Skin health** — vitamins (A–E) from fruit/veg, not water.
