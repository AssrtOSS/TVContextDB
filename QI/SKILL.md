## World Setting & Premise
- **Studio panel show** recorded at The London Studios (Series A–P) then BBC Television Centre (Series Q–).  
- **Format**: Host poses obscure / counter-intuitive questions to four comedians (1 permanent + 3 guests).  
- **Scoring philosophy**: “Everything is interesting if looked at in the right way.” Points awarded for *correct* answers AND for *interesting* (quirky, tangential, humorous) answers. Points deducted for **forfeits** — answers that are “not only wrong, but pathetically obvious” (common misconceptions).  
- **Klaxon**: Loud alarm + flashing lights + on-screen text signals a forfeit.  
- **Final round**: “General Ignorance” — seemingly simple questions where the obvious answer is usually wrong.  
- **Tone**: Intellectual comedy, improvisational banter, British self-deprecation, erudite digressions.

## Speaking Styles
- **Stephen Fry**: Elevated, articulate, playful authority; precise diction; frequent Latin/Greek etymologies; gentle teasing of panellists; “Now…” as transition.  
- **Sandi Toksvig**: Warm, quick-witted, inclusive; Danish-accented English; more conversational, less lecturing; uses “Right then,” “Off you go.”  
- **Alan Davies**: Deadpan, self-deprecating, sarcastic; frequent “I don’t know” / “That’s bollocks”; often triggers klaxons deliberately.  
- **Guest comedians**: Improvisational, anecdotal, competitive banter; British panel-show rhythm (overlap, callbacks, running gags).  
- **Narration (Elves/voice-over)**: Factual, neutral, occasionally wry; appears in XL “behind the scenes” segments.

## Cultural Context
- **British panel-show tradition**: Descendant of *Just a Minute*, *Have I Got News for You*, *Mock the Week*; values wit over winning.  
- **Class & education references**: Oxbridge, public-school, Latin tags — often lampshaded or subverted.  
- **Science & history trivia**: Emphasis on debunking myths (e.g., “Great Wall visible from space,” “humans use 10% of brain”).  
- **Self-referential mythology**: “Pliny the Elder” as patron saint; recurring in-jokes (Alan’s buzzers, Sean Lock’s “cheating,” Jimmy Carr’s tax jokes).  
- **Audience**: Studio audience (pre-2020); virtual audience (Series S–T pandemic era); laughter tracked but not canned.  
- **Spin-offs**: *The Museum of Curiosity* (Radio 4), *No Such Thing as a Fish* (podcast), *No Such Thing as the News* (TV), *The Unbelievable Truth* (Radio 4).

## Translation Notes for Subtitlers
1. **Preserve “QI”** — do not translate the abbreviation; “奇” only for series title in Chinese UI.  
2. **Klaxon moments**: Keep timing; add “【扣分警报】” or SFX note if audio not subtitled.  
3. **Wordplay / puns**: Translate sense + add translator note for untranslatable puns (e.g., “lobbadob”).  
4. **Cultural references**: Gloss obscure British figures (e.g., “Lord Reith,” “Blue Peter”) inline or in notes.  
5. **Running gags**: Maintain consistent Chinese handles (e.g., Alan’s “buzzer” → “按钮/抢答器”; “elephant in the room” → “房间里的大象”).  
6. **Measurements**: Convert imperial to metric in parentheses where helpful (e.g., “6 ft (1.83 m)”).  
7. **Latin / scientific terms**: Keep original italicised; add Chinese common name first occurrence.
