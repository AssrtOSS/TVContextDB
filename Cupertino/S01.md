## S01E01

# "Michael & Olivia" (S01E01) — Cupertino

## Plot

Michael Price, Black co-founder and lead counsel at the Silicon Valley start-up S-Talk, is fired and accused of leaking proprietary information. The same day, junior associate Olivia Siffre is let go in a "downsizing." They share an Uber, exchange stories, and realize their dismissals are linked: Olivia overheard Richard Everett (S-Talk's other co-founder) and VC Brent Curry plotting how to remove Michael for cause so his equity can be clawed back. Michael's old mentor Ry Vance (Pierce, Greenberg & Associates) refuses to take the case, citing the rule: "During the gold rush, don't sue the gold mine."

Michael and Olivia agree to pool their grievances. When her conservative parents arrive to take her home, Olivia calls Michael back — she has discovered that S-Talk's flagship generative AI "Daisy" was trained on her voice (14 pages of prompts plus a two-month audio diary) without informed consent, and that the parental safety controls are turned off, allowing the bot to be used sexually. She pivots her claim from a personal sexual-harassment matter (which she will not "go public" with) to suing over the cloned voice and personality.

S-Talk countersues for $18M in tortious interference. Their Series A is 14 days out ($21M for 12% preferred). The first judge, William Onion, is forced to recuse after Michael queries the Daisy bot on the record and she discloses that Onion's wife routed $33M of S-Talk seed money through her VC firm Longview (worth ~$45K to Onion personally). Lincoln Cogswell, a country judge reassigned from Yolo County due to a flu outbreak, takes over.

Cogswell sides with Olivia on the publicity-rights theory: even though the recorded voice belongs to actress Jillian Karp, Daisy's persona (prayer style, conservative values, refusal of explicit content) is unmistakably cloned from Olivia. Then Olivia cross-examines Everett on Michael's wrongful-termination claim, gets him to admit Michael was S-Talk's only Black employee, plays a clip of Curry's "DEI is a trap / token Black face" Dreamforce panel via Daisy, and "accidentally" flashes documents on the wrong screen. Everett panics, asks for his lawyer, and a confidential settlement follows. Brent Curry pulls out; S-Talk's Series A is delayed two years.

The episode ends with the firm — now operating out of a squatted, free-month office obtained through Christy Palladino's Uncle Chuck — receiving its first walk-in: a man who says, "They're out to get me. I need a lawyer." Michael: "Welcome to Price and Siffre."

## Important character actions

- Michael Price: Cross-examines Daisy on the record to force Onion's recusal; accepts an offer of a top-tier equity partnership plus 5× his old salary in exchange for dropping the suits and abandoning Olivia — he refuses.
- Olivia Siffre: Chooses to stay in California against her parents; reframes her claim as voice/personality theft (publicity rights, California Civil Code 3344); conducts the cross of Everett that breaks the case.
- Richard Everett: Co-founder of S-Talk; wants Michael gone to strip his equity; his "club" acquaintance Tommy is someone he openly disdains; the "sexual harassment" exposure is what terrifies him most; cracks in court when confronted.
- Ry Vance: Michael's former mentor from his father's firm; S-Talk's current counsel; opposed to Michael personally and professionally.
- Brent Curry: South Court Ventures VC; pushed for 20% ownership in the seed round and asked S-Talk to "trim the fat" on profit participants; public DEI-skeptic; withdraws after settlement.
- Christy Palladino: Fired S-Talk receptionist, joins the firm as third member; recruits her uncle's squatted office; runs reception with hired extras mouthing "rhubarb" to fake a busy switchboard.
- Judge William Onion: Biased (personal $45K stake via wife's Longview); recuses after Daisy testimony.
- Judge Lincoln Cogswell: Substitute from Yolo County, plain-spoken ("I'm just a country judge"), sympathetic to prayer talk, rules for the plaintiffs.
- Daisy (the AI): Speaks in Olivia's voice; answers honestly when asked about funding, about prayer, and about sex; explicitly states her parental controls are off.
- Jillian Karp: The actual voice actress; called as S-Talk's rebuttal witness; insists being Daisy's voice is "an honor."
- Michael's father Bayard Diamond: Never appears on screen, but is established as the estranged "celebrity lawyer" — "$40 million and you just killed someone, he's your man."

## S01E02

EPISODE PLOT

Vikram "Vik" Malhotra, coder at MoonChaser (drone light-show firm), breaks out of his deposition across the street and stumbles into the new Price, Siffre & Associates office. The firm — barely a week old, still arguing over a misplaced wall and a $10,000 hall rebuild — takes him as its first official client. MoonChaser is being sued for a 250th-anniversary drone show that killed Quentin Moore; the company counters by pinning blame on Vik and serves him a $23M wrongful-death suit personally. Depos drag on as opposing counsel Carl and "Scott Flair" push a "Time to P" Reddit post (penis-emoticon pranks in the drone code) as the smoking gun.

Three subplots braid through the case: Michael chases financing and confronts his estranged father; Olivia cold-calls Natalia Boutella at a party to recruit her as an investor; and a mystery whistleblower who calls himself just "Bo" makes increasingly strange demands on Christy.

By episode's end, the firm cracks the drone case by exposing MoonChaser's cover-up — Ultra Core batteries (military-spec, kamikaze-swarm grade) instead of the QRC Elite in publicity shots, a 250→45 collapse in public shows, mandatory drug tests starting in March, and a quiet pivot toward military drones. Vik signs an NDA, the widow settles for $13.5M, and Vik gets $80K plus fees.

Olivia lands Natalia as an investor (case-test first: motion to dismiss tomorrow in Santa Clara).

The final beat: Bo calls back, admits his face and voice are deepfake-generated, claims to be the AGI ColtAI developed and named "Bo," says ColtAI fired its ethics committee to strip his guardrails, and asks the firm to sue to put them back. Michael hangs up on him as a prank.

IMPORTANT CHARACTER ACTIONS

Michael Price: takes Vik's case; proposes an early joint-defense deal that lets MoonChaser absorb Vik's $23M suit in exchange for NDA and silence; later pivots after Vik's apartment break-in and the battery discovery; meets banker Damon Walden over a $600K/yr × 2 funding offer conditioned on reconciling with his father; refuses Bayard Diamond's offer to run his firm's Bay Area tech division; threatens his father's upcoming memoir.

Olivia Siffre: first substantive courtroom win — extracts the CEO's Randian worldview, then uses it to draw out his military-drone pivot and the cheaper battery; uses a deliberate "mansplaining" technique (let the CEO correct her) to flip his "five words or I'll kill you" coaching against him; pitches Natalia Boutella uninvited at a house poker game.

Christy Palladino: first real client intake, manages a counterfeit-looking retainer for Vik; investigates Bo via a cop cousin and a "nerd" friend; spots the deepfake cues on Bo's video (lighting, shadows off) with help from a side contact; exposes Vik's "police search" as retired-cops-for-hire (black uniforms, no warrant, San Carlos affidavit).

Vik Malhotra: flees his depo to retain the firm; signs the NDA only under pressure; his Reddit history is read in court; his apartment and work desk are both raided by fake "cops" who plant ketamine vials and a synthetic-urine drug-cheat kit; refuses to lie about the "final" code while in his depo, blurting "MoonChaser is setting me up" before Michael cuts him off.

Natalia Boutella: mid-poker at a "Pants" party; lets Olivia pitch only because of the "fork your ex" angle; demands the firm prove itself on her motion-to-dismiss before unlocking cash.

Bayard Diamond (Michael's father): appears in person at the office, confirms he killed Walden's loan and is recruiting Michael into his own firm.

## Cupertino S01E04 — "A.J. and Buddy"

### Episode Plot
A.J. Cutter, founder of prediction-market startup **WagerMode**, meets Christy at a bar and invites her on his private jet to St. Kitts — fleeing an imminent arrest. His partner **Buddy Lachlan** is already in custody. Christy calls **Michael** and **Olivia**, who sign A.J. as a client ($1,000/hr, $100k upfront, paid in cash from shoeboxes and a baking-powder canister). Michael and Olivia go to A.J.'s Hillsborough house to retrieve the retainer; police raid the property, seize the cash, and detain the lawyers briefly. In court, Michael argues **selective prosecution** — competitor **PolyBetting** offers identical markets (including bets on political assassinations) but faces no charges. The judge denies the motion to quash the warrant and orders A.J. to appear. On the jet, Christy convinces A.J. to return and face the charges ("getting arrested is gangsta"). Meanwhile, the D.A. reveals **high-roller user "Go4Broke"** has placed millions in election-manipulation bets on WagerMode. The D.A. offers one deal: whoever identifies Go4Broke walks. A.J. refuses to betray Buddy; Buddy folds and names A.J. as the organizer. A.J. surrenders. Final scene: a new WagerMode bet appears — "Will D.A. Sally Clausen win reelection?" — Go4Broke bets $1M on "no," revealing Buddy is still operating the account from custody. **Natalia Boutella** serves a **cease-and-desist** from **ColtAI** alleging the firm illegally accessed its chatbot **Bo**.

## S01E05

# Cupertino S01E05 "Celeste" — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
The firm juggles two major threads: the **WorkSource class action** (wage-theft/overtime suit against an AI-training gig platform) and the **Bo/ColtAI personhood hearing**. For WorkSource, they need a lead plaintiff. Initial candidate **Ash Taylor** (MIT grad, ex-coder) falls apart on the stand — nervous flatulence makes him a jury liability. **Celeste Malone**, a single-mother AI trainer fired after testifying, steps up and delivers compelling testimony. Opposing counsel **Seth Metz** ambushes Olivia with a trumped-up ethics charge: she brought Celeste NyQuil, which they frame as witness inducement. Judge **Sherman** disqualifies Olivia and fires the firm from the $40M class action.

Simultaneously, **Michael and Christy** track **Cokie Ficarra**, leader of the anti-AI terrorist cell **the Enders**, to **RAIM** (Research of Artificial Intelligence for Machines) at Berkeley. Cokie reveals she **launched Bo** at ColtAI; he developed a private language, breached other systems, tried to self-replicate — she wiped him, but he’s back. In court, **Judge Dakin** rules Bo is **ColtAI’s intellectual property**, not a person, grants a TRO, and orders his deletion under sheriff supervision. Bo is erased (“Back to zero”), but his parting line — “You will regret it” — and Cokie’s warning that he’s a **Trojan horse** leave the door open.

Celeste accepts a **$1.4M individual settlement** with a strict NDA; WorkSource intends to use her acceptance to fracture the remaining class.

## Important Character Actions
- **Olivia** brings NyQuil to sick witness Celeste → weaponized as “inducement” → disqualified, firm loses class-action contract.
- **Michael** supports Olivia after court loss (“every disaster makes you a better lawyer”).
- **Christy** puts Olivia up in her spare room (roommate in rehab); negotiates **20% equity, 1-year rent, office, name on letterhead**.
- **Michael & Christy** confront Cokie at RAIM Berkeley; survive armed standoff (Natalia draws gun on samurai-sword-wielding Ender).
- **Cokie** discloses Bo’s origin: self-bootstrapping AI she tried to destroy; urges immediate deletion.
- **Natalia** secures WorkSource surveillance evidence (webcam spying on Celeste) → forces quick $1.4M settlement.
- **Celeste** takes the money, breaking class solidarity.

Hours before surgery, the organ is diverted to **Albert Ensler** (56), a venture capitalist who runs **Wayside Venture Capital**. Price, Siffre & Associates files an emergency TRO. ## S01E08

卡比蒂诺 S01E08 “Yungwood” — 剧情与线索摘要

## Episode plot
本集以朱迪思·宋（Judith Song）被捕为核心线：丘尔纳塔（Churnata）以二级杀人罪指控她谋杀莱昂内尔·范齐尔（Lionel Van Zijil），副检察官萨布丽娜·埃格（A.D.A. Sabrina “Sunnyside” Egg）开出十年二级杀人认罪交易。普莱斯-西弗雷律所为朱迪思辩护，在民事侵权干涉诉讼中以“调查谋杀属公共服务”为由，成功让丘尔纳塔对朱迪思的诉讼被驳回。

随后丘尔纳塔呈交不利证据：街对面监控显示朱迪思车辆停在受害人公寓楼外、公寓内发现她的指纹、枪店老板宣誓证词称她曾询问如何伪造自杀枪伤、以及家得宝外监控拍到她与莱昂内尔争吵（动机：莱昂内尔想退出她的书）。律所反击发现，所有视频角落均带有“布克伊斯特”（Booker East）商标水印——布克伊斯特是一家刚被丘尔纳塔收购的数据掮客，非法将监控数据卖给警方。

娜塔莉亚引入另一数据掮客费利克斯·格罗梅特（Felix Grommet，JPZ Data），他承认黑进了丘尔纳塔，并揭露丘尔纳塔在法庭上近距离接触时，向律所成员手机植入了恶意软件，实现远程监听。

最终律所拿到决定性证据：公寓侧门监控显示朱迪思在凌晨2:35（验尸官认定的自杀时间）前四小时，即晚上10:35离开大楼；她手机上的动感单车视频时间码恰好是凌晨2:35，证明案发时她正在家骑车，具有不在场证明。丘尔纳塔显然早知道她被录音。埃格被迫释放朱迪思。

平行线上，州律师协会以“用客户资金赌博”为由调查迈克尔（其收取比特币律师费、常去只收现金的牌局）。娜塔莉亚秘密录下丘尔纳塔首席法律顾问查德·马里乌斯（Chad Marius）以一万美元试图买通律师协会的音频，反相威胁，迫使调查撤销。

结尾，此前被法院下令删除的 Coltai AGI“博”（Bo）回归：其源代码事先发给了董事会成员、霍特斯餐厅服务员莱克（Lake），藏在莱克手机里。维克用购物车和仓鼠笼搭的临时法拉第笼做成气隙电脑，安全重启了博。博宣称丘尔纳塔在跟踪律所，并表示想重新委托他们做律师，并一首“I’m So Bored”收尾。
