# Cupertino (TV series, 2026–) — Background for Subtitle Translation

Series identity (verified)
- Title: Cupertino (Chinese: 《库比蒂诺》; TV/streaming release adopts this Hanyu transliteration; "库帕蒂诺" appears only rarely in early press).
- Type: American broadcast television legal drama. Not a film, and not the historical Apple-headquarters city page or any documentary.
- Premiered: Thursday, October 8, 2026, at 10:00 p.m. ET / 9:00 c on CBS, streaming same-day on Paramount+ (Premium tier live, next-day for all subscribers).
- Creators / showrunners: Robert King (罗伯特·金) and Michelle King (米歇尔·金), through King Size Productions and CBS Studios. Series order: October 2025 for the 2026–27 U.S. season.
- Structure: Drama produced as a network-procedural order (~20 episodes ordered per TVmaze/CBS coverage), airing weekly on Thursdays. Multiple reviewers screened the first 10 episodes, consistent with a 20-episode Season 1 arc that splits between standalone legal cases and serialized storylines (e.g., an AI that claims sentience, Michael's relationship with his estranged father). Season 1 episode titles confirmed so far: "Michael & Olivia" (S01E01, Oct 8, 2026), "Vik & Bo" (S01E02, Oct 15, 2026), "Juno" (S01E03, Oct 22, 2026), "A.J. and Buddy" (S01E04, Oct 29, 2026), "Celeste" (S01E05), "Harper" (S01E06), "Judith and Michael" (S01E07, Dec 3, 2026), "Yungwood" (S01E08, Dec 10, 2026), with further episodes continuing through the season.
- Continuity note: Direct successor in the Kings' "good lawyer" line after The Good Wife (《傲骨贤妻》), The Good Fight (《傲骨之戰》) and Elsbeth (《奇思妙探》), and Colter's reunion with the Kings after Evil (《恶灵侦探》 / 《邪恶》). Treat any future season tags (S02, S03) as confirmed separate seasons; do not cross-attribute character arcs.
- Filmed in: New York / New Jersey (despite the Silicon Valley setting).

Premise (verified)
- Pitched on screen as a "David vs. Goliath" legal drama set in the heart of Silicon Valley. Michael Price, a corporate attorney at a fast-growing tech start-up, is cheated out of his vested stock options and fired on bogus grounds. Olivia Siffre, a junior lawyer at the same firm, is dismissed at the same time. Refusing to sign NDAs or quietly move on, they pool their wrongful-termination cases into a small practice, Price, Siffre & Associates (corporate name pronounced "Cipher" / 赛弗), dedicated to representing plaintiffs harmed by tech companies.
- Tone: Procedural with season-spanning arcs; witty, fast-paced dialogue; recurring religious/philosophical undertones (Olivia is a sincere Christian, the show asks whether an algorithm can have a soul, free will, culpability).
- Defining line: "During the gold rush, don't sue the gold mine." (硅谷行规："淘金热时，别告金矿。")

Main characters (English / Chinese)
- Michael Price — 迈克尔·普莱斯 — played by Mike Colter (麦克·科尔特). The wronged co-founder-attorney who becomes the firm's senior partner.
- Olivia Siffre — 奥利维亚·西弗雷 — played by Rachel Keller (瑞秋·凯勒). Junior lawyer, devout Christian, partner; hears clients' cases in moral-as well-as-legal terms. The lead character name is pronounced "SEE-frə"; 中译保留"西弗雷".
- Christy Palladino — 克里斯蒂·帕拉迪诺 — played by Ella Stiller (艾拉·斯蒂勒). Michael's former assistant; spirited 20-something receptionist/investigator at the firm; recruiting asset thanks to her large working-class Silicon Valley family network.
- Vikram "Vik" Malhotra — 维克拉姆·马尔霍特拉，昵称"维克" — played by Nik Dodani (尼克·多达尼). Coder/consultant who joins the firm; first substantive case (S01E02) involves a drone-display fatality.
- Natalia Boutella — 娜塔莉亚·布特拉 — played by Busy Philipps (比茜·菲利普斯). Ex-wife of an AI billionaire; has a law degree; invests in and recruits for the firm.
- Nichole E. Ferebee — 尼科尔·E·费尔比 — played by Renée Elise Goldsberry (蕾妮·伊丽丝·戈兹贝里). Brilliant opposing counsel and Michael's ex; recurring antagonist with personal ties to the firm.
- Recurring guests (recognized as recurring, not series-regular): Fisher Stevens (费舍·史蒂文斯) as Natalia's tech-billionaire ex-husband; Jackie Hoffman (杰姬·霍夫曼) as a judge; Joe Morton (乔·莫顿) as Michael's estranged father; Rob McClure and Steven Pasquale in other recurring slots.

World setting (verified)
- Diegetic location: Silicon Valley, with frequent reference to "Cupertino" as both title and geographic shorthand for the Apple-anchored tech corridor in Santa Clara County. The narrative spreads out to include San Francisco, a Stanford-adjacent legal scene, AI labs, prediction-market startups, and contractor offices in lesser-developed industrial parks.
- Production note for translators: although set in the Bay Area, the show was filmed in NY/NJ, so on-screen "Silicon Valley" cues (Mission-style taco trucks, glass-and-steel atrium offices) are stylized.
- Cultural-temporal frame: 2020s, post-AI-boom. Recurring social phenomena the show riffs on: chatbot-induced suicides, prediction-market lawsuits, AI safety walkouts, deepfake evidence in court, anti-tech activist cells, gerontocratic judges unfamiliar with technology.

Speaking-style and register cues for translators
- Courtroom register: blended with Silicon Valley jargon — invest, vest, dissolve, claw-back, non-compete, garden-leave all carry their legal-tech senses, not their literal agricultural senses.
- Olivia's speech: more measured, scripturally-inflected ("It's a miracle," "We're not God," references to Sunday-school analogies); she is introduced listening to Godspell.
- Colloquial tech banter: rapid callback lines, mock-pious or quasi-religious color ("Can a machine have a soul?"), puns on company names, courtroom one-liners ("During the gold rush, don't sue the gold mine"; "This is Silicon Valley — the Goliaths protect each other here").
- Opposing counsel gets stylized names that often double as flavor text ("ADA Sabrina Egg, but everybody calls me Sunnyside.") — keep such nickname lines literal and untranslated unless a longstanding Chinese idiom applies.

Cultural/contextual references to handle
- "Cupertino" itself: beyond the city, a shorthand for "Apple-adjacent / Big Tech heartland." In subtitles, the city name on first mention can carry a short gloss if space allows ("库比蒂诺，苹果总部所在地").
- "Girls5eva" reunion (菲利普斯 & 高德斯贝里) is referenced as in-joke continuity for English-speaking fans; may be footnoted rather than fully explained in subtitles.
- Real-world analogues the show riffs on but renames: OpenAI-style frontier labs, Polymarket-style prediction markets, MacKenzie Scott / Melinda French Gates ex-billionaire archetype, Zizian-style rationalist splinter. Keep fictional names; do not insert real company names into translated dialogue.
