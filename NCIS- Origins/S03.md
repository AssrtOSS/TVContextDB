## S03E01

# Plot

Cold open, Alaska 2025: older Gibbs lives alone with a dog. A shooter kills the dog, mutters "one down, one to go," and targets Gibbs — someone from his 1991 NIS past has tracked him down.

Main story, 1991: the Pendleton office rebrands from NIS to NCIS. A party celebrating the new "C" leaves everyone drunk and distracted.

- Marine MP Sergeant Zachary "Zach" Crudo, 23, is found shot dead in a filthy public bathroom, wallet missing.
- Probationary agent Randy Randolf is kidnapped and stuffed into the same SUV Lala thought was tailing her about Manny/the Chacales.

Lala almost quit to join Manny, but stays after Gibbs asks her to stay and they kiss.

Investigation:
- Lala lifts a latent print with a dirt/dust technique; it turns out to be Crudo's own. Woody finds a mystery substance mixed in the dirt (later: ash).
- Franks and Vera interview former Regional Director Ron Barrett (Crudo's old boss-driver). Barrett lies about a six-figure corporate-security offer and claims no contact with Crudo in months.
- BOLO hits at a Quick N' Swift gas station. Randy, hidden in the back seat, flashes the SUV's faulty taillights in Morse code: U.S.S.R. Russians grabbed him.
- Vera's real informant, Vladimir "Vlad" Volkinov (KGB-trained, planted 1986, now works at Blockbuster), identifies the limping "Maksim" — KGB, hunting Navy secrets.
- Lala and Gibbs raid a trailer. Randy is found chained inside, alive — he played dead using a Marine breathing technique Gibbs taught him at Daly's. A hired torch arrives to burn the trailer (asbestos cover story) and is captured.

Randy reveals: the kidnappers wanted his password to "Martha II" (his classified-data computer, named after Martha Stewart). The password "GingersJustWanttoHaveFun321" was written on a card his colleague Gail filed away in his wallet as an "intuitive" safe place. The Russians killed Crudo (loose end), stole the computer at the party, and got the password from the wallet.

Smoking gun: Woody matches ash from the scene to Barrett's rare Colombian cigar. Crudo had made 17 calls the previous week to a pay phone two blocks from Barrett's house. Crudo's hand carries an Ordane Tactical bag imprint, suggesting a beach meet-up that went bad. Barrett is arrested. Mary Jo delivers a "my name is Mary Jo Hayes, not honey, doll, or baby" speech on the way out.

Coda: Gibbs and Lala confirm the relationship. Gibbs promises "you're the one, not the next one." Lala asks him not to get married again; he agrees — with visible hesitation.

Final cut back to Alaska: the shooter is still out there. Older Gibbs voiceover: "Saving Randy came at a price… 34 years later, I'd be paying mine."

## S03E02

# NCIS: Origins S03E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Woody (Woodrow Browne) is framed for the strangulation of Sheila Hoffman, a fellow member of his bowling team "The Chalk Outlines." Her body is found in Woody's house with his fingerprint on her gold necklace. The FBI (Agent Casey Bloom, Swanson's new partner) takes jurisdiction. The NIS team works covertly to prove Woody's innocence, discovering the killer used bowling wax from Woody's locker to make a wax mold of his house key, created a duplicate, planted Sheila's body, and transferred Woody's fingerprint onto the necklace. The culprit is Ray Calhoun, the bowling alley owner—a former Marine who lost 30% of his income when Sheila reported health violations that shut down his restaurant. Ray had an affair with Melissa (Woody's teammate, a pattern-analysis expert), who showed him how to transfer latent prints. Woody is exonerated and released.

## Important Character Actions
- **Gibbs & Lala**: Heart-to-heart—Lala admits confusion over their hookup, needs time; Gibbs says he'll wait "as long as it takes."
- **Franks**: Defies Wheeler/FBI, sneaks into holding with Gibbs to give Woody his blood-pressure meds (actually dog heartworm pills) and extract intel.
- **Wheeler**: In therapy, using mantra "I am capable, I'm competent"; orders no NIS contact with Woody.
- **Woody**: Reveals childhood trauma—father died, mother gave him a notebook to write feelings instead of talking; uses it to analyze forensic evidence while detained.
- **Phil**: Returns from anime expo in San Francisco; analyzes Woody's keys, finds bowling-wax residue in grooves proving key was copied via wax mold.
- **Lala**: Gets timeline from Tijuana bartender—Woody arrived ~11:30 PM, tournament ended 9:00 PM, but drive is only 30 minutes.
- **Gibbs**: Realizes lock wasn't picked—key was duplicated; connects wax residue to bowling alley; identifies Ray as only other person with locker access.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Casey Bloom** — FBI, Swanson's partner, spent past year undercover with Barstow biker gang; called "Johnny Utah from *Point Break*" inspiration.
- **Ray Calhoun** — Kingpin Lanes owner, former Marine, killer.
- **Melissa Jenkins** — "Chalk Outlines" teammate, pattern-analysis major, had affair with Ray, taught him print transfer (not complicit in murder).
- **Graham Taylor** — Former teammate, quit after Woody publicly eviscerated his fingerprint-transfer paper.
- **The Chalk Outlines** — Woody's all-forensic-analyst bowling team.
- **Kingpin Lanes** — Bowling alley.
- **Nacho** — Federale Franks owes a favor to (got Woody out of Mexican custody).
- **Blue** — Retired agent with high-level AFIS access Phil takes the mystery print to at episode's end.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Gibbs/Vegas marriage to Diane Sterling referenced (bourbon-fueled mistake).
- Woody's notebook origin established (grief processing since childhood).
- Wheeler's therapy/mantra arc continues.
- Franks' "off-books" dealings with Mexican Federales.
- Older Gibbs voiceover bridges to flagship *NCIS*: Ducky, McGee, Ziva welcome Gibbs "home."
- Phil's anime-expo gag pays off (he was genuinely in San Francisco).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **Anime expo** — Phil's "cartoon" obsession becomes team shorthand.

## Unresolved Threads

2. **Gibbs & Lala** — Relationship in limbo; she needs time, he'll wait.
3. **Wheeler's therapy** — Progress uncertain; mantra may not hold.

## S03E05

# NCIS: Origins S03E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
A pregnant woman dies in a car crash near Camp Pendleton, but the autopsy reveals she was shot first and the pregnancy was faked (wig, colored contacts, fake belly). The victim, **Lily Wiggins** (alias "Lucy Williams"), was running a **Royal Bastard con** — posing as the pregnant mistress of recently deceased oil magnate **Victor Banks** to extract $500K from his greedy family. Her partner, a Mexican lawyer calling himself **Juan Perez**, disappears with the money. The team traces forged letters to their CI **Callum "The Bagpiper"**, who admits Lily paid him with a fake 1912 George Washington stamp. A sting at a stamp collectors' expo (a **King Farouk con** — selling counterfeit collectibles) nabs **Pablo**, who was only hired to play the lawyer. The real mastermind: **Audrey Banks** (wife #4), who orchestrated the con with Lily, then killed her at the fishing cabin "Pearl on the Pond" to keep the $500K. Luminol reveals the blood trail; Audrey is arrested.

## Important Character Actions
- **Lala Dominguez** runs point on the case, confronting her past as a con artist's daughter. She identifies the Royal Bastard and King Farouk mechanics, interviews the Banks family, and gets the break from the former cook Maria.
- **Gibbs** supports Lala throughout, notices the fishing cabin's location between crash site and compound, and gives her Samuel's phone number at the end.
- **Franks** mentors Lala, proudly telling Wheeler "Dominguez ran point," and acknowledges Gibbs pushed to hire her.
- **Randy** plays the gullible trust-fund mark at the stamp expo, delighting in calling Franks "dumbass hick."
- **Vera** has a raw heart-to-heart with Lala about carrying her father's betrayals, urging her to let go so new love can grow.
- **Cliff Wheeler** obsesses over the new NCIS seal (NIS→NCIS transition).

## S03E06

NCIS: Origins — S03E06 Summary

## Episode plot
FBI Agent Casey Bloom, undercover for a year with the Barstow biker gang the Steel Vultures under the alias "Miles Lewis," turns up at Franks' place with a gunshot wound to the chest, shot at 4041 Hillcrest Avenue. His last word is "Tropicana" — the team's code word, born from an old witness job at the Tropicana hotel in Vegas, meaning "hide him, from everyone." The team treats him, then smuggles him into Tri-City Hospital as a John Doe: stripped, dirtied, fingerprints kept off the books, because Bloom is FBI and his prints are already on file.

Gibbs is also wounded and bleeding, drifting in and out of consciousness, and dreams of Lala Dominguez — believed killed years ago in a plane crash. Through the fever dream and Franks, Gibbs learns Lala faked her death; Franks helped her disappear. The opening mentions "one down… one to go": Briggs is in CIA custody ("one down"), and Lala was the intended second target.

At Bloom's apartment the team finds $100,000 in cash, a secret non-FBI cell phone (the Bureau still uses pagers in 1991), an empty weapon magazine, blood on the car door handle, case files on the Steel Vultures, and Patchouli, a cockatoo Bloom rescued from an exotic-animal raid. FBI Agents Swanson and O'Brien arrive with a warrant: Bloom is wanted for stealing $100,000 from the FBI evidence locker, his signature on the log. Franks orders the team to trust no one but themselves.

Woody and Phil match a print from Bloom's car to Cynthia Aldridge — possibly "Brunette Randy," a man in Bloom's photos who resembles a dark-haired Rando. Bloom's undercover partner alias was supposedly "Ritchie Lifson," a coke supplier — an alias Franks himself once used. The Steel Vultures are led by Thomas "Tommy" Aldridge, "top dog" outside Wynola; Miles ran off with Aldridge's wife Cynthia and daughter Elizabeth. Cynthia filed four domestic-violence reports against Tommy; no charges were ever brought, the last assault three days ago. Aldridge put $75,000 on each of them (double if alive, so he can kill them personally).

The team concludes Bloom didn't run off with Cynthia — he extracted her and Elizabeth from an abuser; the money was for a fresh start. Aldridge found them at FBI safe house 1241 Lindon Street and shot Bloom; Cynthia and Elizabeth escaped in a gold Ford Taurus kept at the safe house. Only the FBI should know the safe house location, so the Vultures have a mole in the Bureau: Agent O'Brien (divorced, driving a new Beamer despite claiming his ex took every penny), who phoned in the safe-house BOLO at 9:00 a.m. and gave Aldridge a 30-minute head start. Fornell is clean — "a Boy Scout."

Franks goes undercover at Big Red's bar outside Wynola as Ritchie Lifson with Lala posing as "Monica." Big Red confirms Miles stole from Aldridge too — he took off with Aldridge's wife and child. A brawl erupts when a drunk regular, "Porkchop," declares that "Brunette Rando ain't the victim — he's the head of the damn Vultures."

O'Brien's BOLO leads the team to the Holloway Motel off Route 79, where Aldridge holds Cynthia and young Elizabeth at gunpoint. NIS and FBI move in and arrest Aldridge. Cynthia shot Tommy three times in the back — not self-defense. A conviction means roughly 15 years, and the Steel Vultures control half the prison system; she wouldn't survive. Franks refuses to cover up a murder, but Lala argues the girl becomes an orphan either way. They settle on a cover story: when the three of them arrived, "Aldridge, Cynthia and that little girl — they were already gone." They also leave the $100,000 in Bloom's trunk out of the report, presumably for Cynthia and Elizabeth's new life.
