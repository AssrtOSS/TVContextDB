## S01E06

The episode follows two adult brothers (Matthew and Woodrow) co-parenting teens, with a parallel storyline about Jimmy trying to win back Olive.

---

## Episode Plot

**A-Plot: The High School Dance**  
Woodrow (Woody) helps his son **Wyatt** prepare for a school dance with **Kai** — Matthew's adopted, homeschooled daughter who lives in a converted bus on their property and uses crutches. Kai feels wildly out of place ("caged animal led into the wild"). Woodrow coaches Wyatt to "own the dance floor" and dance *for* Kai, not for the crowd. The dance is subdued; Woodrow blames COVID for killing teens' social skills. Matthew and Woodrow serve as chaperones alongside Mrs. Ogilvy (a teacher who hotboxed the faculty lounge) and a gym teacher rumored to have stolen from Nancy Pelosi's office.

**B-Plot: Jimmy & Olive**  
**Jimmy** (Olive's ex) tries to reconcile via a go-between, delivering an "Olive you" sticker (olive + "I love you" pun). He reveals he won a hefty Taco Bell settlement (NDA-signed, plus $1,600 in "Baja bucks") and is now on disability. He shows up at the dance, gets swarmed by hornets/wasps drinking from a hose, and Olive tends to him. He pours out his heart; she listens but doesn't relent.

**Closing Beat**  
Matthew and Woodrow hold a sealed DNA test envelope. Matthew refuses to open it: "I don't need a piece of paper to tell me whether or not Matthew and I are brothers." They agree to a "fresh start" and recycle the unopened results.

## S01E08 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode centers on the fallout from the revelation that **Matthew McConaughey** and **Woody Harrelson** are half-brothers (sharing a biological father, Clive). Matthew has punched Woody on live TV, then gone missing ahead of his gubernatorial campaign rally in Texas. Woody, Matthew's mother "Ma Mac" (Kayte), Olive, Kai, Jimmy, and the kids scramble to find Matthew and get him to the rally. The episode culminates at the rally where Matthew announces his run for Governor of Texas, he and Woody publicly reconcile as brothers, and the campaign appears to be faltering in real-time vote counts — but the pair decide to embark on a road trip to visit their biological father in prison (Boulder State Penitentiary, Colorado).

### Important Character Actions
- **Woody Harrelson**: Punched by Matthew; spends episode managing the crisis, protecting Matthew's campaign, and ultimately delivering an emotional rally speech acknowledging their brotherhood. Confronts a hostile reporter at the victory party.
- **Matthew McConaughey**: Disappears to his childhood treehouse after the punch/biological father revelation. Confronted by his mother; processes anger over the 50-year secret. Returns to rally, announces gubernatorial run, and publicly embraces Woody as brother.
- **Ma Mac (Kayte)**: Reveals she kept the secret for decades; explains Big Mac (her husband, Matthew's presumed father) knew and chose to raise Matthew. Cooks chili as emotional coping. Drives to treehouse to retrieve Matthew.
- **Olive & Kai**: Navigate their own "cousins-but-dating" dynamic (revealed they're not biologically related since Olive is adopted). Olive gives herself a black eye in solidarity with Matthew. Kai initially runs away but returns.
- **Jimmy**: Olive's partner; supportive, gets caught in family chaos.
- **Big Mac (deceased/mentioned)**: Ma Mac's husband who raised Matthew; knew the truth, chose love, "is your father" per Ma Mac.
