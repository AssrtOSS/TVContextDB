# S02E03 Summary — Where's Wanda

## Episode Plot
Wednesday. Wanda is in prison with new cellmate **Lenka**. Dedo, Carlotta, and Ole confirm **Eyepatch Man** exists (via Ole’s footage) and trace him to a hotel off Main Street. They launch **Project Honey Trap**: Carlotta poses as “Kiki” at the hotel bar to seduce info from him. Meanwhile, Dedo visits **Leni Brauer** in prison, pressing her on Katarina Vinson; Leni admits she was Katarina’s *partner*, not subordinate, and is rattled to learn Wanda claims Katarina was **King**. Ole is cornered by Lukas’s crew — his sister is in the same prison as Lukas — and forced to complete a missed delivery. Detective **Rauch** digs into port records under **E.A. Krone AG**; his partner finds a post-arrest shipment to **W. O. Den Cargo Transporte / “Woden”** (Anglo-Saxon god, leader of the Wild Hunt) picked up PumpkinFest morning with missing paperwork. At the hotel, Carlotta-as-Kiki meets **Victor** (Eyepatch Man), who claims to be an energy exec discussing a wind farm with Katarina. The honey trap yields little. In the elevator, an **Interpol agent** confronts Carlotta: she investigated Katarina, confirms Katarina was King, and offers to help free Wanda — *if Wanda helps her*. Dedo’s mother (Oma) visits unexpectedly; late-night revelation shows she has **advanced, incurable gynecologic tumor, 6–12 months to live**.

## Important Character Actions
- **Wanda**: In prison, maintains Katarina-was-King claim; new cellmate Lenka introduced.
- **Dedo**: Visits Leni Brauer in prison; navigates mother’s surprise visit; learns of her terminal diagnosis.
- **Carlotta**: Runs honey trap as “Kiki”; meets Victor; confronted by Interpol agent in elevator; agrees to secrecy.
- **Ole**: Provides Eyepatch Man visual ID; threatened by Lukas’s gang (sister leverage) to finish a delivery.
- **Rauch**: Pushes shipment investigation despite Chief Inspector Schmidt’s warning; gets Woden lead.
- **Leni Brauer**: Admits partnership with Katarina; shaken by Wanda’s King accusation.
- **Victor (Eyepatch Man)**: Flirts aggressively; gives wind-farm cover story; real name revealed.
- **Interpol agent (unnamed)**: Reveals Katarina = King; recruits Carlotta/Wanda quid pro quo.
- **Oma (Dedo’s mother)**: Arrives unannounced; terminal cancer revealed via referral form.

## S02E04

## Episode Plot
- The episode opens from a deaf character’s perspective, emphasizing that listening goes beyond words and that secrets can be read in expressions, behavior, and private messages about a meeting with “W” and a missing delivery.
- Wanda is attacked in the prison canteen by two girls sent to find a tracker she supposedly stole from Katarina Vinson. When Dedo and Carlotta visit, Wanda insists it was a misunderstanding but warns someone is after the tracker; the family also learns their house was broken into and Wanda’s room searched.
- At school, Kimi pressures Ole to finish a job, threatening Wanda if he fails. Alex, already furious over Katarina’s death, lashes out and hits Ole, leading to a meeting where Alex is given a leave of absence until end of term rather than a formal suspension.
- Jonas invites Dedo and Carlotta to Katarina’s memorial, framing it as a chance for the community to come together, but the Klatt family remains wary of how the town sees them.
- Rauch follows a lead to City Hall and, with Rocco’s remote help, accesses Katarina’s computer. They uncover extensive stalking emails and a formal complaint that got security employee G.B. fired immediately. Dedo, Carlotta, and Rüdiger pursue this lead and identify G.B. as Gus Bremen.
- Rauch is sent to interview “new witness” Astrid Goldammer, who turns out to be a dog; Hester later reports the dog swallowed a mobile phone, which Rauch rushes to recover.
- Ole confides in Chris that Alex agreed to drop a bag for Lukas Novak in prison, the drop went wrong, and Lukas is now threatening Wanda if the task is not completed within the week. Ole and Chris track the backpack’s signal.
- In prison, Wanda is harassed in the bathroom by other inmates but is unexpectedly helped by a fellow prisoner. The woman refuses to be Wanda’s protector for free but offers a deal: if Wanda can break her out before she is transferred to a high-security facility, she will ensure Wanda is treated well.
- The search for Gus Bremen leads to a former residence by the power plant and then to a tip that he now lives in the woods in a van painted with a wolf. Recognizing the wolf connection, Ole and Chris realize the danger and flee as Gus spots them.

## Important Character Actions
- **Wanda**: Endures a targeted prison assault over an alleged stolen tracker, reassures her parents, and begins probing a possible escape arrangement after a fellow inmate intervenes in her defense.
- **Dedo and Carlotta**: Visit Wanda in prison, gather information, follow the Katarina/G.B. lead, and track down Gus Bremen’s possible whereabouts while hosting Carlotta’s mother Inga.
- **Ole**: Is threatened by Kimi on Lukas’s behalf, gets into a confrontation with Alex, reveals the botched prison drop plan to Chris, and helps locate both the backpack signal and Gus Bremen.
- **Alex**: Refuses to reconcile with Ole, blames the Klatt family for his mother’s death during the school meeting, and is removed from school until term’s end.
- **Rocco**: Assists remotely by talking Dedo through social-engineering access at City Hall and searching Katarina’s emails for evidence.
- **Detective Rauch**: Investigates a possible second phone tied to Katarina, uncovers the stalking/termination trail on Katarina’s computer, interviews the false lead Astrid, and moves to seize the swallowed phone.
- **Jonas**: Tries to de-escalate after the school incident and invites the Klatts to Katarina’s memorial.
- **Fellow inmate (unnamed)**: Stops Wanda’s bathroom harassment, makes clear she will not babysit her, and proposes protection in exchange for help escaping before a transfer.
- **Hester**: Reports that her dog Astrid swallowed a mobile phone, giving Rauch a new physical lead.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Kimi**: Person threatening Ole to complete the delivery job for Lukas Novak.
- **G.B. / Gus Bremen / Günther Begemann**: Former Town Hall security guard who stalked/harassed Katarina and was fired; later identified as Gus Bremen.
- **Astrid Goldammer**: Presented as a new witness but revealed to be Hester’s dog.
- **Hester**: Astrid’s owner, who brings the swallowed-phone lead to Rauch.
- **Dominik Silbermann**: Prison staff member whose name appears during the cell inspection/key card moment.
- **Woden**: Confirmed as a shell company owned through further shell companies, with no clear trail.
- **Tracker**: Item Wanda is accused of stealing from Katarina Vinson, motivating the prison attack and linked to the house break-in.
- **Wolf-painted van**: Gus Bremen’s reported current residence in the woods, tying him to the earlier “wolf” clue.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Wanda is assaulted in prison by attackers claiming she stole a tracker from Katarina Vinson.
- The Klatt home was broken into and Wanda’s room searched, consistent with someone hunting the same item.
- Katarina’s official file records only one recovered phone, but suspicion of a second phone persists; a dog is later found to have swallowed a mobile phone.
- Katarina maintained a “stalking evidence” record showing repeated abusive messages from G.B.; she filed a complaint that led to his immediate termination from Town Hall security.
- Woden is not a usable lead in itself: it is buried under layers of shell-company ownership.
- Ole admits Alex had agreed to deliver a bag to Lukas Novak in prison; the failed/mishandled drop now puts Wanda at risk if not resolved within the week.
- A prison inmate facing transfer to a high-security facility offers Wanda protection only if Wanda helps her escape.
- Gus Bremen, linked to Town Hall security and Katarina’s harassment, is said to be living off-grid in a wolf-painted van in the woods.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes
- Opening “Spiderman”/heightened-senses framing from the deaf narrator’s perspective on listening and secrets.
- Katarina’s password being a leetspeak version of her slogan (“Better Together”).
- Rocco’s over-the-top IT cover story about “Blaster Worms, Slammer Worms, Trojan Worms.”

- Is the mobile phone swallowed by Astrid Katarina’s missing second phone, and what evidence does it contain?

## S02E05

Wheres Wanda — S02E05

## Episode Plot

The Klatts prepare to attend the funeral of Katarina Vinson. Dedo's mother Leni, whose cancer has returned, refuses further chemotherapy and tells Dedo she wants to spend her remaining time living well rather than "getting pumped full of chemicals." In jail, Wanda tells Dedo that Alex, together with her cousin Lukas, arranged a "prison drop" — smuggling items over the prison walls using Ole's drone — and pulled Ole into it. Dedo finds Katarina's second phone, a "bat phone" with only one saved number, and plans to call it during the funeral, betting the caller won't want to miss the service. At the service, widower Jonas Vinson eulogizes Katarina and daughter Lucie speaks, while townspeople glare at the ostracized Klatts. Rüdiger, alerted by a hot mic that Gus Bremen (GB) is present, follows GB's wolf-painted van to a woodland men's group preaching "radical responsibility," where GB reveals he was a City Hall security guard fired by Katarina, met her the night she died, and hated only himself. Ernie's background check shows newcomers George Kimble and Marlene, living in the Hessel house on Tannenring, don't officially exist — "ghosts." A monitored prison call explains drops use a three-word location code (one trio per square meter, e.g. "hat, mouse, umbrella"). Wanda then receives a threatening call demanding Ole finish "the drop" by tomorrow. The episode ends with Dedo being urged to talk to his mother, then finding her unresponsive: "Mom? Can you hear me?"

## Key Character Actions

- Dedo argues with Leni over chemo, visits Wanda in jail, retrieves the bat phone, presses Alex on Lukas, and follows GB to his men's group.
- Wanda, from jail, explains the prison drop and later receives a threat that Ole must complete a drop by tomorrow.
- Ole admits to the drone drop, reconciles with Alex in the bathroom, and attends the funeral.
- Alex confesses he lied about Lukas and is forgiven by Ole.
- Leni declines further cancer treatment and chooses quality of remaining life.
- Rüdiger causes a scene at the funeral about Katarina being a criminal and Jonas being the statistical suspect, then runs the Kimble background check with Ernie.
- Lucie notices George Kimble at the funeral and delivers a speech about her mother.
- Victor secretly asks Dedo to obtain information from Wanda that could "blow the case wide open," proposing a meeting at hotel Room 908.
- Carlotta breaks up with Dedo, saying she can't trust him.

- Katarina Vinson (deceased), Jonas Vinson (widower), Lucie (daughter)
- George Kimble and Marlene — fake identities; unregistered, "ghosts"; in the Hessel house on Tannenring
- Gus Bremen (GB) — leader of the "pack" men's group; ex-City Hall security guard
- Prison drop — contraband dropped over prison walls; Ole's drone was to be used
- Bat phone — Katarina's second phone with one saved number

- Team Klatt — the family's self-described investigative unit
- Social radioactivity — the Klatts' pariah status in town
- Radical responsibility — GB's group philosophy
- Room 908 — Victor's hotel meeting room

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Dedo has a new prison-related job (inmates call him "new fish"); he was recently out with "Chris," which Ole hid.
- Alex knows Katarina's cousin Lukas through football.
- The Klatts are socially canceled in Sundersheim; only newcomers will speak to them.
- Wanda's parents told her about Dedo's new job.
