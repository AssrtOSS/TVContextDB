# Pierre S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Pierre juggles the firm's financial crisis (late legal-aid filings costing £200 each) with his promise to Michael's aunt Brenda to investigate her nephew's stabbing. Michael's friend Chantelle hands over a memory stick of unreleased videos Michael made documenting police harassment—specifically targeting PC Mark Tanner. Pierre enlists Lish to gather character testimony on the estate and asks Dan to pull strings with Amar Chadi at Internal Investigations. Meanwhile, he defends Amir, whose father Usman staged a break-in at his own dry-cleaners as an insurance scam to repay an £18k debt to loan-shark Sean Lambert. Lambert sends high-end solicitor Daniela Harcourt to pressure Amir into dropping assault charges against the "intruder" (Lambert's nephew Anthony). Pierre exposes the set-up, warns Usman to settle with Lambert, and confronts Tanner at Michael's roadside memorial. Tanner claims a clean record and cryptically cites criminal psychology: "narcissists always return to the scene of the crime." The episode ends with Eli discovering Michael's secret video database on Pierre's laptop—rating officers on brutality, road rage, and stop-and-search, with Tanner scoring a perfect 100 for "hassling proud black youth."

## Important Character Actions
- **Pierre**: Investigates Michael's death despite firm pressure; confronts Tanner; diagnosed with Type-2 diabetes; hides condition from Eli initially.
- **Ronnie**: Checks Tanner's internal record (clean); reveals Michael had four bags of cocaine when killed; supports Pierre personally.
- **Charlotte**: Pressures Pierre on filing deadlines and firm finances; shows personal concern for his wellbeing.
- **Brenda**: Demands truth about Michael's school exclusion (art-class scalpel incident, not a weapon); feels betrayed when Pierre learns it second-hand.
- **Amir**: Discovers father's debt and staged burglary; furious at being kept in the dark; trashes shop in rage.
- **Usman Malik**: Admits £18k debt to Lambert; agrees to call Lambert after Pierre's warning.
- **Sean Lambert**: Orchestrates pressure via Harcourt; orders arson attack on Usman's shop as "message."
- **Eli**: Finds Michael's police-rating videos on Pierre's laptop; reacts with shock ("That is sick, man!").

## S01E01

# Pierre S01E01 Summary

## PlotThe episode follows a single night in the life of London duty solicitor Pierre Jeffreys. It opens with him fielding a panicked 1am call from his adult son Eli, who has been detained and is shouting that police are trying to plant drugs on him. Pierre takes over Eli�s shift at the station (replacing the absent Mr Hammond) and works a string of small cases.

Early in the night he handles Miriam, a regular eccentric client who has been caught shoplifting from Bryford's supermarket for a second time. Pierre secures her release. Around5am, PC Tanner brings in Michael, a young Black man stopped driving a car registered to his aunt Brenda Deane. Pierre initially negotiates Michael's release on the basis that the car is legitimately his aunt's, but Brenda arrives, refuses to back her nephew (she has warned him before about borrowing the car without permission), and Michael is re-arrested.

Pierre finds Michael in custody and begins representing him. Their early clashes are sharp: Michael accuses Pierre of "playing the white man's game" and of having lost touch with the community he serves. Pierre defends himself, tries to talk Michael down, and later meets Michael at a basketball court where Michael has been coaching local boys (including Dexter). There Michael reveals he has been keeping detailed logs and video recordings of repeated stop-and-search harassment by police, especially PC Tanner, and is asking Pierre to help him bring a harassment case against the force.

After a brief confrontation with Charlotte at the office about money, Pierre leaves a voicemail for Michael saying he will take the case. That evening, at his wife Celeste's surprise birthday party, Pierre is called away: Michael has been stabbed to death. He is initially described by police contact Amir as a "gang-related" incident, which Pierre rejects outright. He goes to Brenda Deane, who is devastated, tells Pierre her last words to Michael were skeptical of his plans, and blames Pierre for advising her to give Michael another chance. Pierre promises Brenda he will find out who did this. The episode closes on a roulette table where Pierre bets everything on black, intercut with Michael's voiceover about defying the odds and standing on truth.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Pierre has been covering for his son Eli's problems since at least the start of this night; Eli has dropped out of university and is drifting, which Celeste and Ronnie both flag.
- Pierre's relationship with his late-night police station is established: he knows the duty sergeant, the desk officer Carol, and DS Ronnie Miller well, and banter freely.
- The firm (Pierre, Charlotte, Danny, Alecia) is in financial distress: clients behind on payment, rent arrears, payroll glitches, threat of redundancies, and Charlotte is steering them toward VAT and fraud work to survive.
- Pierre is a habitual gambler (betting shop scenes, the roulette closing bet, the "tables" Charlotte accuses him of). His gambling winnings are being funnelled into the business account covertly to cover payroll, which Charlotte discovers and rejects as a business model.
- Pierre's health: suspected type 2 diabetes, blood test booked for Friday 11am; Celeste and Ronnie already suspecting it before the test result; Pierre is hiding it from Celeste until Ronnie reveals it at the party.
- Pierre's late-father care duties: looking after Eli (vascular dementia and Alzheimer's mentioned in connection with someone bringing him food at the office, contextually Pierre's dad).
- QPR fandom is established as a small character beat for Pierre.
- Pierre's "lower leagues" speech to Michael positions him as a fixer, not a showman: this is the philosophy he later applies to taking Michael's case.
- Michael is established as having been stopped multiple times by PC Tanner before this incident, and he and his friends are being stopped repeatedly as a pattern, not an isolated event.
- Pierre and Michael's relationship arc moves from antagonism ("playing the white man's game", "you've given up", "you forgot who you were") to agreement: by the evening of the same day Pierre leaves Michael a voicemail committing to bring the harassment case.
