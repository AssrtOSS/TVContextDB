## S02E01

# S02E01 — "Cyclone"

## Plot
The episode picks up the morning after the S1 finale blackout. Dr. Galloway is treating Lieutenant Boone, who was shot less than 72 hours ago and is already ignoring medical advice. Mickey, meanwhile, is missing — last seen storming out of Deputy Director Eva Santos's office. She resurfaces having gone on an overnight bender in Drake County, the next county over. Sheriff Jim Wade, a long-time friend who'll later prove useful, quietly brings her in via his jail's side entrance, gets her cleaned up by a retired-deputy bartender, and hands her back to Boone and Cassidy early Friday morning.

Back home, Wes is waiting because "Skye called Cassidy." Mickey orders Wes out — furious at both her father and the woman who abandoned her. Wes leaves peacefully, but Skye is now openly furious at Eva/Maria for the 40-year lie; she rehashes that her middle name was given to a dead woman and tells the family that, for her, Eva doesn't exist.

Dispatch hits with a 10-56A at Van Hollen Dam: Donna Hawkins is threatening to jump. Mickey talks her down, reaching her and holding on as Donna slips, ultimately saving her. Donna is 5150'd and held for a 72-hour psych eval. Dale ("Deadwater Dale") stays by her side.

The episode then fans out into three threads running in parallel:

- The cold-case thread: Cassidy and Hank quietly re-open Mary Elliot's 28-year-old murder. Andy (Mary's mentally ill son) gives them everything he's got — drug-zoomed, he heard people fighting downstairs, then silence, then Mary on the floor of his room begging him to "Call 911." Mary died of massive blunt-force trauma; murder weapon never found. Brother "Punch" (Ruben, the DA) walks in and is furious — Andy had broken Punch's wrist two weeks before the murder, beating their mother, and the brothers haven't spoken since. Cassidy and Hank track down Sylvia Bekins, Mary's "best friend" and the person who drove Punch to the hospital that night. Andy says Sylvia and his mother were "like sisters," working the church bulletin together every Tuesday night — the same weeknight Mary was killed. Sylvia clams up the second her son Owen shows up, cut short the interview, and confirms a Tuesday-night meet-up with Mary. Cassidy flags her as the prime new suspect.

- The "Mom" thread: Eva insists the Stoyanovs are an existential threat. Mickey tells her to pound sand — "you couldn't hurt me if you tried." Eva pivots to Boone, leaking Stoyanov intel and asking him to talk Mickey into joining the task force. Wes mostly stonewalls Skye about Maria/Eva — "I don't know an Eva, I know a Maria," and shares tender Moonstone Beach memories, but it's clear the past is still raw for both of them. Eva visits Skye directly, in Spanish ("Dios mío"), and reveals for the first time the full backstory of her own survival: she was a DEA agent sent to Oakland to identify the Green Giant; she fell in love with Wes and had Mickey; the feds raided the grow house and "took" her, gave her a 10-year Bogotá interdiction offer, and she made a deal that kept Wes down to 24 months. Then her superiors told her to fake her own death so she could go undercover long-term. Choosing to disappear for nearly 40 years was the only way to keep either parent out of prison. Eva now needs Wes's and Mickey's help to dismantle the Stoyanovs.

- The new threat: Boyd Henshaw at the morgue (the ME — a joke lands badly because her name is Emmy, "M-E, Emmy, get it?") confirms that Donna's tox screen shows cychlorphine, a synthetic opioid several times more lethal than fentanyl. A county-jail inmate also OD'd overnight on it. Cychlorphine is being delivered covertly: liquid distilled onto book pages, magazines, "letters from home," burned and inhaled — a delivery method that's been moving down in SoCal for weeks under the street name "Cyclone." That same morning, Donna escapes the secure ward by faking stomach pain, and her dad Dale disappears with her. A bundle of items delivered to her at the hospital turns out to include a photo of Dale, clearly abducted to flush Donna out. A car with no plates took them.

Mickey and Boone track Donna via a burner she stole from a diner regular, Carl, who never bothered to lock his phone. They raid a stash house where Iggy, a Stoyanov enforcer, is planning to torch the place with Dale inside. Mickey bursts in ("Sheriff's office! Don't move!"), takes down one of them, and saves Donna and Dale.

Afterward, Donna confirms her ex-boyfriend is Michael Ivanov, who works at Little Sofia, the bakery — which Cassidy identifies as a Stoyanov front. She recants that the drug felt like a "game," that her debt was paid with work at a Deadwater grow house where young women are kept locked up to "service the trimmers" — sex slaves, not prostitutes. Michael told her to bring friends instead. With the volatile sexual-trafficking angle in play, Cassidy and Boone observe that Edgewater has never faced a threat on this scale.

A separate showdown at the DA's office: Travis meets with Eva and announces that the case against Wes and the Co-Op was thrown out because the lead investigator, Alec Kane, is now known to be both a criminal and dead, and the U.S. Attorney dropped charges. Travis sneers at Travis — and Eva hits back that Fraley Horizons is still on the DEA's target list. Wes, freshly released, confronts Travis ("Did you sell us out?"), Travis pleads ("Always," about his love for Wes), and Wes returns the question: how many times has he broken Mickey's heart? "Once."

Mickey, after all of this, walks into the Edgewater town meeting and announces she's joining the Stoyanov task force — not for Eva, but for Edgewater. Wes, to Eva's visible surprise, also volunteers: "There are some things more important than my reputation." Santos, however, points out that with Wes's case falling apart, she has no leverage to compel his cooperation. Wes retorts, "Screw your leverage," and says he's in anyway.

The episode ends mid-ambush in the woods, with another young girl fleeing a Stoyanov crew — a clear tease toward the sex-trafficking plot.

## Character actions

- Mickey: leaves Eva's office in a rage; blacks out in Drake County; Wade drives her back to Edgewater. Talks Donna down off the dam and physically catches her when she slips. Refuses to join the task force "for Eva" — but then changes her mind at the town meeting and signs up, framing it as protecting Edgewater rather than reconciling.
- Boone: shot under 72 hours ago, checks himself out of the hospital, argues with Dr. Henshaw (still owes him $50), insists on being in the field. Acts as Mickey's escort back from Wade's, accompanies the Donna raid and the morgue visit. Is in a tighter spot after his confession — Mickey uses the downplaying "This never happened" — and the hangover bait jokes that "the 49ers were gonna win the Super Bowl."
- Cassidy and Hank: go around Mickey's back to re-open Mary Elliot's case. Cassidy interviews Andy at the ECSO; Hank later interviews Sylvia Bekins. Hank is stung when he realizes Cassidy/Mickey/Boone kept the Drake County blackout from him, and he doubles down on feeling left out of the inner circle — partly over the surprise-party cover (which never existed; his birthday is Saturday).
- Wes: barred from Mickey's home after the blackout. Reminisces about Maria/Eva — Moonstone Beach walks, the pancake trick she taught him — but refuses to let Skye define Eva as her grandmother. Down at the DA's office, accepts Travis's "Always," then privately turns the question on him.
- Skye: now in active conflict with Eva over the cover-up; the photo she was raised on was of a "dead" woman. Mid-episode, she cuts through the family to demand the truth.
- Eva / "Maria": reveals the full 40-year backstory to Skye: Green Giant assignment, love for Wes, Mickey's birth, faked death for long-term cover. Approaches Boone separately to recruit Mickey. Threatens Travis that Fraley Horizons is still on the DEA's radar.
- Travis: gets Wes's case dismissed on a technicality (Alec's criminality makes conviction impossible), defends it to Cassidy/Hank with a slick "ZipRecruiter" quip, then faces down both Eva and Wes.
- Donna: jumps from the dam, is 5150'd, mixes with Michael Ivanov, gets cychlorphined, is lured out of the secure ward by a Stoyanov photo of Dale, gets kidnapped, escapes by stealing a diner phone, and finally opens up about Michael and the Deadwater trafficking house.
- Dale ("Deadwater Dale"): at the dam with Donna; later abducted; recovered in the raid.
- Andy: repeats his statement about a fight downstairs and a woman's scream. Confirms Sylvia was "like a sister" to Mary, and that he and Mary worked the church bulletin every Tuesday — the night of the murder.
- Punch (Ruben Massey, DA): off-screen in the episode's first half after learning Cassidy and Hank have been interviewing Andy. Wears a broken wrist he got from Andy two weeks before Mary's murder.
- Sheriff Jim Wade (Drake County): quietly brings Mickey back from her bender, acknowledging decades of mutual back-scratching. He'll be "even" — but "Not even close."
- Dr. Henshaw: still owed $50 by Boone; clears Boone for light duty over the phone.
- Gina: present in the office, but mostly comic relief — fighting cancer (per S1 lore), and a Chuck/Sylvia Bekins housekeeper moment alludes to it.
- Emmy / M.E.: Boyd Henshaw, the medical examiner, gets the weak "M-E = Emmy" callback from Mickey; her willingness to clear Boone surfaces.
- Carl: diner regular whose phone Donna steals; unwilling hero of the Donna tracking thread.

## Continuity facts

- Mickey's blackout was in Drake County, not Edgewater — i.e., outside her jurisdiction. Wade used his "old side entrance" to keep her out of the system.
- Boone was shot fewer than 72 hours before the episode. We're not told when or by whom — likely the S1 finale shoot-out with Alec Kane, since Alec's death is referenced later in this same episode as the reason Wes's case was dropped.
- Alec Kane is dead. His criminality taints the entire Wes/Co-Op case, leading the U.S. Attorney to drop charges.
- Edgewater County jail — at least one inmate has died this week from cychlorphine.
- The County Sheriff's dispatch calls itself "Adam One" for non-sheriff patrol units and "Sheriff One" for Mickey. "Adam One, Sheriff One" with a "Code 3 medical" call to dispatch is established ECSO jargon.
- Fraley Horizons — still on the DEA's target list. Wes's case falling apart actually helps Fraley Horizons's case rather than harming it.
- Cyclone / cychlorphine has been moving through SoCal "for weeks" before reaching Edgewater.

## Unresolved threads

- Was Sylvia Bekins Mary's killer? New prime suspect, but no proof. Andy remembers her too well — used to work on the church bulletin with Mary, including Tuesday nights. Her son Owen is suspiciously protective. "What are you trying to hide, Owen?"
- Was the Tuesday-night meet-up real? Sylvia admitted to a planned meeting with Mary the night of the murder, then shut the interview down. Andy doesn't suspect her, but his mental state makes his read unreliable.
- The Deadwater grow-house sex-trafficking ring. Women are being kept locked up to service the trimmers. Donna only saw it once. Mickey and co. need to find it. The episode ends mid-chase in the woods with another young woman fleeing.
- Who is Iggy, and who is "Man 2"? Stoyanov foot soldiers, presumably working under Michael Ivanov or higher up. Iggy is killed/arrested in the raid.
- The Stoyanov organization at large. They run cychlorphine (Cyclone) distribution, kidnapping, sex trafficking through the Little Sofia bakery front, and have a major grow site in Deadwater. The actual leadership hasn't been named yet.
- Will Eva ever reconnect with Skye? Skye told her, on the doorstep, "neither do you [exist]." Eva hasn't responded to the rejection yet.
- Fraley Horizons as a DEA target. Eva warned Travis that his family's old-money criminal enterprise is still under investigation — Travis brushed it off as "see you in court," but is this season's Fraley-fallback arc?
- The surprise party that wasn't. Cassidy and Boone had a real conversation going, but their cover for it being about a party for Hank (Saturday birthday, red velvet) turned into Hank feeling excluded.
- Hank vs. the inner circle. Hank is out of the loop, and resents being kept out — partly for the surprise party, partly because Mickey, Boone, Cassidy kept the Drake County blackout from him.
- Boone's last night with Mickey. He confessed his feelings; she deflected; the next morning they're back to buddy-cop mode. Does Mickey actually regret the choice?
- Mick and Wes. Mickey hasn't forgiven Wes and Eva loves them both enough to call that out. Wes still lives "in the house" — both physically and metaphorically — and Eva's leverage over him just disappeared with the dropped case.
- The FEMA / California rainy-season / Cyclone-Storm overlay. The episode's natural-disaster framing hasn't materialized yet beyond the Van Hollen Dam scene, which sets the "things fall apart, fast" tone for S2.
- Donna is still in the hospital for the rest of her 72-hour hold, but the abuse she took — Stoyanov kidnapping, exposure to a lethal synthetic opioid, and surviving a bombing — will likely surface in later episodes.

## S02E04

# Sheriff Country S02E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
A joint DEA–ECSO raid on a **cychlorphine** (synthetic opioid) lab in **Deadwater**—1,400 sq mi of booby-trapped wilderness run by the **Bulgarian cartel (Stoyanovs)**—rescues four trafficked girls who flee on foot. **Wes Fox**, held by the Stoyanovs as a DEA confidential informant, escapes just before the raid and shepherds the girls through old grower cabins. **Mickey** coordinates search-and-rescue with **Cal Fire (Jake, Station 42)**, **Boone** (back from a gunshot wound), **Cassidy**, and **Peña**. **Parker Chase** (CEO of **Skotos AI**, introduced by **Deputy Director Santos/Maria**) deploys a military-grade thermal-mapping drone and pitches *predictive policing* to Mickey. The girls are recovered; Wes survives. The Stoyanov leaders (**Sonny, Anna, Konstantin**) escape via a decoy flight to Peru—actually still in Edgewater eliminating witnesses. **Anna** is captured and flipped by Mickey and Santos. **Gina** learns her cancer scan is clear.

## Important Character Actions
- **Mickey**: Runs the manhunt, clashes with Boone over procedure vs. instinct, later reconciles; greenlights Skotos AI drone; apologizes to Skye for withholding Wes’s CI status.
- **Boone**: Returns from injury, argues he saved a deputy by entering solo; accepts Mickey’s order to stand down, later gets heart-to-heart about burnout; takes 1–2 weeks leave.
- **Wes**: Navigates Deadwater’s trapped cabins, splits from group to draw pursuers, saves pregnant **Priya** (sprained ankle), reunites with Mickey.
- **Skye**: Demands answers about Wes, learns he’s a DEA informant, reconciles with Mickey (“90% of family is showing up”).
- **Santos/Maria**: Runs DEA side from D.C., captures Anna, reveals she returned for Mickey, gives Skye 10-year sobriety chip and her address for letters.
- **Parker Chase**: Demos Skotos AI (license-plate readers, facial recognition, predictive policing via phone data); Mickey asks for follow-up call.
- **Cassidy**: Secures girls’ protective custody, talks Priya into hiding, takes down a Stoyanov gunman; considers dating again.
- **Gina**: Cancer-free; jokes about “finding next husband” at bell-ringing ceremony.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Cychlorphine** — synthetic opioid manufactured by Bulgarians in Deadwater.
- **Skotos AI** — Parker Chase’s startup selling predictive-policing suite to LE.
- **Parker Chase** — CEO, Palo Alto, connected by Santos.
- **Predictive policing** — AI forecasting of suspect movements from phone/app metadata.
- **Sonny / Anna / Konstantin Stoyanov** — Bulgarian cartel heads (Sonny = boss, Anna = wife, Konstantin = son/lieutenant).
- **Petar** — Sonny’s underboss, now in DEA protective custody.
- **Deadwater** — 1,400 mi² forest; canopy blocks helicopters; riddled with old grower cabins & booby traps.

- Maria Fox = Deputy Director Santos (alive, revealed S1 finale); Mickey still calls her “Maria,” not “Mom.”
- Boone shot in previous episode (“Cyclone” / S02E03); on light duty but pushed back early.

## S02E05

# Sheriff Country S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
A charity bachelor auction for Cal Fire and ECSO is interrupted when a wildfire erupts in **Cribb Valley**, triggering a High Wind Alert extending to Edgewater County. Simultaneously, a **prison transport van crashes on Route 9**—three inmates escape (Kendra Worley, Paige Dalton, Crystal Wallace) along with a complicit guard, Gordon. The fugitives carjack a vehicle and hole up at **The Meat Up** restaurant, taking 16 hostages including **Skye** (Mickey's daughter) and **Eve**. One Three Rock inmate firefighter, **Tex**, suffers a head injury during the takeover.

Mickey and Manny clash over command: Mickey prioritizes evacuation credibility and hostage negotiation; Manny pushes fire resources. The wind shifts—the fire now bears down on The Meat Up, giving ~20 minutes before arrival. The inmates demand a fire engine to flee through **Deadwater** (lawless, unmarked roads) to Cribb Valley and onward to the Dominican Republic. Mickey negotiates Tex's release as a good-faith gesture, but **Skye volunteers to remain as sole hostage** to free the others. Manny arrives with Engine 42 and volunteers to drive it. The inmates escape in the fire engine with **Skye and Manny as hostages**, heading toward Deadwater. Mickey orders all units to stand down and focus on the fire/evacuations.

## Important Character Actions
- **Mickey**: Commands ECSO, negotiates directly with Kendra, refuses tactical entry (Boone's SWAT proposal) to protect hostages, lets inmates leave to save lives.
- **Manny**: Secures Engine 42 from returning Station 42 crew, drives it to The Meat Up, volunteers as hostage/driver to protect Skye.
- **Skye**: Texts "Help" to Mickey, helps treat Tex's wounds, deliberately stays behind as sole hostage—"You have to be the sheriff."
- **Boone**: Pushes for breach/rescue; overruled. Partners with Bode on downed power line and hospital transport.
- **Bode**: Responds to power line, secures evacuation routes, drives Boone to interview van driver.
- **Eve**: Treats Tex's scalp laceration/possible concussion, keeps him conscious.
- **Kendra (ex-ER doctor, opioid supplier to Criminales Cartel)**: Lead negotiator for inmates, demands fire engine, distrusts Mickey.
- **Paige (arson/murder/armed robbery)**: Enforcer, skeptical of police promises.
- **Crystal (kidnapping/homicide, life without parole)**: Mickey's past arrest; recognizes Skye as Mickey's daughter, threatens to kill her.
- **Gordon (prison guard)**: Inside man, helped plan escape.
