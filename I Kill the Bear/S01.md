## S01E01

# I Kill the Bear — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Thibault siblings — fourth-generation Franco-Ontarian bear wranglers for film — announce their retirement after 100 years in the business. Andrée introduces the family: eldest Cédric, middle Gabby (her favorite), and youngest Olivier, who lives by the mantra "the bear needs calm." They've worked on *Grizzly Adams*, *The Edge*, *The Great Outdoors*, *The Revenant*, consulted on *Ted*, and count *Legends of the Fall* as their crowning achievement. Their final film will be shot under "New Hollywood" conditions — corporate HR culture, sensitivity readers, and animal-welfare scrutiny — which they find hostile to bears. After this movie, they plan to release their bear into the wild.

## Important Character Actions
- **Andrée** delivers the framing narration, establishing family history and the retirement decision.
- **Olivier** lives in the bush building tree forts; embodies the "bear needs calm" philosophy.
- **Gabby** is positioned as the conflicted lead — his "problems began when it became unacceptable to disagree with someone."
- **Cédric** (off-screen) referenced as eccentric, comic relief.
- **Bob** appears briefly as a Hollywood fixer type ("Relax. It's under control").

## S01E03

# I Kill the Bear — S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Thibault siblings (Gabby, Andrée, Olivier, Cédric) are interviewed by documentary crew (Alex, Ricky) about their history with rival wrangler **Son of Grizzly Adams**. Andrée reveals that 10 years ago, a bear went hostile on his set; the crew refused the Thibaults' family calming method, forcing Gabby to shoot the bear — the family's first incident in nearly 100 years. Since then, "when Son of Grizzly Adams comes, bad things happen."

Meanwhile, Hollywood producers (Timothy, Rose, Edward, Kathy) plot to leak the Thibaults' checkered track record (*Dr. Doolittle 7*, *Homeward Bound 5*, *MVP 8*, *Air Bud 16*, *The Great Outdoors*) to deflect PR heat onto them. A **territory recognition** ceremony on set derails when the Thibaults mistakenly claim "coyote territory," prompting coyote howls and Indigenous crew member Ben Runningbull's discomfort.

The network demands: (1) fatten the bear to match a polar bear co-star; (2) make the bear pee on Timothy for a "cute" *Free Willy*-style bonding beat. The Thibaults refuse both — the bear's health is their livelihood ("bread and butter"), and a male bear cannot pee backward like a female. Edward fires Gabby, Andrée, and Cédric on camera after Cédric's anatomically explicit demonstration of what compliance would require. **Olivier stays**, consenting to continue wrangling alone.

## Important Character Actions
- **Andrée**: Discloses the 10-year-old shooting incident; expresses worry for Olivier's continued association with Son of Grizzly Adams.
- **Olivier**: Defends Son of Grizzly Adams ("he doesn't like rules"); ultimately breaks with siblings to stay on the film.
- **Gabby**: Leads refusal to fatten bear or force unnatural peeing; delivers "we cannot make a male pee like a female" line.
- **Cédric**: Provides the "twizz it back" anatomical demonstration that gets them fired.
- **Edward/Kathy/ Rose/ Timothy**: Orchestrate the Thibaults' ouster; Timothy threatens to quit if they aren't fired.
- **Son of Grizzly Adams** (off-screen): Positioned as replacement wrangler.
