## S02E01

# Boston Blue S02E01 — The Crash

## Plot

The episode runs two parallel strands: a copycat "Boston Strangler" murder investigation, and personal/family arcs as Danny's first day back from leave coincides with pressure on the Silver and Reagan circles.

**Case arc.** Danny returns from leave with Lena, immediately pairing up on a homicide — Frannie Swan, 24, found asphyxiated in her apartment with yoga pants tied in a bow around her neck, matching an earlier Beacon Hill strangulation. The clothes choice signals a copycat of the 1962–64 Boston Strangler (Albert DeSalva): same victim profiles, same neighborhoods, but worked in reverse chronological order. With four women already dead and pattern projection pointing to a 25-year-old in West Fenway next, Lena and Danny race the timeline.

A podcaster, Jeremy Rinzano, livestreams from the crime scene and broadcasts the headline that a new Boston Strangler is loose, turning public panic into media pressure on the BPD. Meanwhile, Sergeants' Detective exam results and Brian Rodgers's reinstatement paperwork force Sarah Silver and Danny into direct conflict. Sean Reagan and Jonah Silver, now year-two officers, get chewed out by Sergeant Karim for an off-the-books sting that caught a bike thief. Karim drops that he's transferring to Hyde Park's E-17.

Danny and Lena call Henry Reagan for old-case help. Henry recalls the 1960s task force's "girlfriend" — a woman who believed she was dating the Strangler and was pregnant with what she called "his legacy." Tracking Lynn Crawley down at a nursing home (LBD / Lewy body dementia), they learn she has a grandson Brad who visits daily. Rinzano's tip line surfaces a caller claiming "it's in my blood… the Boston Strangler." Sean and Jonah run down a shady locksmith who copied building master keys stamped "Do Not Duplicate" and ID Brad's car. They catch Brad in the act of strangling his grandmother Lynn, who he believes was DeSalvo's lover. Brad is arrested; Lena and Danny close the case.

**Family arc.** Mae Silver (DA) is running for reelection against Thad Longfellow ("Boston Brahmin," deep pockets, draconian law-and-order stance). She recruits journalist fiancé Seth Yates as her debate-prep surrogate, prompting Edwin to joke Seth has a "similarly punchable face." During prep, Seth drops a bomb: he dug up a June 4, 1985 record of a family-planning-clinic visit tied to Mae's license plate. Mae finally confesses she was driving her sister Jill to the clinic that day — not getting the procedure herself (she had a school oral exam that afternoon). She kept the secret to protect Jill from further harm.

After Shabbat dinner, Sarah drops a forensic find at BPD: the blue truck that rammed Brian Rodgers had been parked an hour outside The Hawke restaurant — the same restaurant where the leaked photo of Mae with "Chris" was taken. The same truck, the same driver, both the photo leak and the hit-and-run. Lena and Danny confront Thad at his campaign rally; Thad claims he only received the photo anonymously from someone signing "a friend of the cause," back-traced to Eastborough — where Chief Chris Williams (Lena's father) lives. His parting line: "Someone is coming for your family."

## Character actions

- **Danny Reagan** — Returns to duty and immediately runs point on the Strangler copycat case. Henry Reagan (Gramps) provides the 1960s "girlfriend" lead. Off-screen he was studying for the sergeant's exam and passed first try; he initially turns down Sarah's offer of Sergeant Detective in the Major Case Unit out of loyalty to Lena, then accepts when Lena reveals she didn't apply for the same slot.
- **Lena Silver** — Danny's partner; pushes the case forward on the Strangler side, including locating Lynn Crawley. Holds onto something Brian said about his claustrophobia at a Shabbat dinner and feels betrayed when Sarah uses it. Confronts Thad directly at his rally; Thad hints Chris Williams may be in the photo-leak crosshairs.
- **Sarah Silver** — BPD Superintendent. Offers Danny the promotion. Denies Brian Rodgers's reinstatement (modified duty) on the grounds that his doctor's clearance doesn't address the psychological aftermath. At the Shabbat table, after Lena confronts her on Rodgers, she surfaces the blue truck / Hawke restaurant link, escalating the family threat.
- **Mae Silver** — District attorney, candidate for reelection. Puts Seth on debate prep; defends her sister Jill by absorbing the 1985 clinic scandal herself.
- **Seth Yates** — Mae's fiancé; journalist with research and debate skills. Walks into the role of debate-surrogate for Thad Longfellow (ironically, while later unmasking photo-leak evidence implicating the Longfellow camp).
- **Edwin Peters** — Pastor; at Mae's he jokes Thad has an "infinitely punchable face" and that Seth has a similar one; supplied Mae's research materials (boxes of Longfellow biography and closing statements).
- **Jonah Silver** — Year-two officer; partner of Sean. Tracks the lead to a shady locksmith via CCTV at the locksmith's shop and later pushes the case on Brian Rodgers's car registered to a "little old lady."
- **Sean Reagan** — Danny's son; year-two officer; runs the Julian/Jonah partnership through the off-duty bike sting that earns Karim's rebuke, then helps Jonah ID Brad's car.
- **Brian Rodgers** — Lena's former partner; still recovering from the hit-and-run (three months in a wheelchair previously; sling still on). Suffering nightmares / claustrophobia / anxiety, sleeping outside under windows. Doctor cleared him for modified duty; Sarah overrode it on the day of the call.
- **Henry Reagan** — Danny's grandfather; worked the 1964 task force, remembers the delusional "girlfriend" of the Strangler and her pregnancy claim; will dig through old journals.
- **Jeremy Rinzano** — Boston podcaster doing "an oral history of Boston." Recorded the BPD scene and went live; filmed Lynn Crawley for his podcast; later played a threatening tip-line voice recording for Lena and Danny.
- **Thad Longfellow** — Rival candidate. Campaign ad claims "My family built this city." Open war with Mae. At the confrontation he insists the photo came anonymously, and points the trail toward Eastborough / Chris Williams.
- **Maria Baez** — Off-screen in New York; Danny hasn't told her about the promotion yet. Cited as a keyperson for Danny's anxiety ("no sleep since her and Baez got to town… realized how small my apartment actually is").
- **Elena** — Danny's partner / girlfriend(?); new to Boston with Maria Baez. Sleeping on the balcony to control anxiety by watching the sky.

## Newly established names and terms

- **Frannie Swan (24)** — latest Strangler-copycat victim, found in her apartment.
- **Beacon Hill strangulation (earlier)** — the case whose M.O. the team links Frannie's to.
- **Boston Strangler / Albert DeSalvo (1962–1964)** — historical case the copycat mimics (real history: DeSalvo confessed but was never convicted; DNA later linked him to the last victim; died in prison 1973). 13 women murdered across sprees.
- **Yoga-pants bow signature** — killer ties victims' athleisure pants around the neck in a bow (modern substitute for DeSalvo's stockings).
- **Lynn Crawley (a.k.a. "the girlfriend")** — woman who in 1964 convinced herself she was dating the Strangler and was pregnant with "his legacy." Now elderly, in a nursing home with Lewy body dementia. Believes Albert was "my sweet, sweet Albert."
- **Gale Crawley** — Lynn's daughter (Lynn says he was "Gale's father"); visits daily.
- **Brad Crawley** — Gale's son, Lynn's grandson (mid-30s, white, short brown hair). Believes the Strangler lineage story; murdered four women using illegally copied building master keys.
- **"Do Not Duplicate" master keys** — keys copied from the building-management ring; central break-in mechanism.
- **Jeremy Rinzano** — Boston true-crime podcaster; "a friend of the cause" anonymous e-mailer sends him the Mae/Chris photo (he relays it on-air).
- **Thad Longfellow** — Boston Brahmin, deep pockets, draconian crime stance; framed as Mae's election opponent; political slogan "My family built this city… a safe and secure future for yours."
- **Seth Yates** — Mae's fiancé; journalist.
- **Judge Eli Robinson** — Mae's current boyfriend (referenced as her "type" by Thad in prep); it's Mae's conflict-of-interest pattern that her late husband was also a judge.
- **Chief Chris Williams** — Lena's father; lives in Eastborough, named as the figure Thad implies is implicated by the Eastborough trace of the photo.
- **Jill** — Mae's sister; had an abortion in 1985; Mae has been absorbing the secret.
- **The Hawke (restaurant) / 67 Hawkes Point Drive** — location where the incriminating photo of Mae + Chris was taken; also where the blue truck was parked for an hour the day it hit Brian.
- **Eastborough** — Chris Williams's neighborhood; mentioned as the trace origin of the anonymous "a friend of the cause" e-mail.
- **E-17, Hyde Park** — Sergeant Karim's transfer destination.
- **Major Case Unit (MCU)** — the unit Danny is being promoted into (and where Lena is based).

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Danny passed the sergeant's exam first try; offered the Sergeant Detective promotion in the Major Case Unit; now holds the rank.
- Lena didn't apply for the same promotion because she is "finally happy right where I am"; her decision is the linchpin for Danny accepting.
- Sarah Silver denied Brian Rodgers's reinstatement (modified-duty request) the same day; Brian is still recovering from the hit-and-run with sling, nightmares, and claustrophobia; he is doing mandated therapy but wants to return to active duty to catch his own assailant.
- Sergeant Karim is in his last week with Sean and Jonah; transferring to E-17 Hyde Park next week. He tells the two rookies that "year two is when you decide what kind of cop you're gonna be."
- The earlier Strangler-copycat murder pattern is solved: Brad Crawley used illegally copied "Do Not Duplicate" building master keys sourced via a shady locksmith.
- Henry Reagan's "old journals" hold the real 1964 "girlfriend" file; he will look up Lynn's actual name.
- The blue truck (registered to Lynn Crawley / her household) simultaneously links two open threads: the hit-and-run on Brian Rodgers, and the leak of the Mae/Chris photo that ended up in Thad's hands.
- Mae's 1985 family-planning-clinic record is actually her sister Jill's abortion; Seth Yates holds the secret; Mae is the one Thad's team thinks they have leverage over, but Seth now knows the truth.
- Mae's election remains in active conflict with Thad Longfellow; her partner Seth Yates doubles as debate prep and investigative digger.
- Lena's father, Chief Chris Williams, surfaced as the Eastborough resident tied to the photo-leak chain; his role is unresolved at the close of the episode.
- Elena sleeps outdoors (balcony/visibility to sky) to manage anxiety; she and Maria Baez recently moved into Danny's small apartment.

## Jokes and references useful for later episodes

- "Brooklyn" / "Beantown" — Danny/Lena's running New-York-City-vs.-Boston antagonism; this is the default insult they lean on when chiding each other ("keep up, Brooklyn" / "I know a lot about it, Beantown").
- Sarah jokingly tells Danny he'd "still ride" with Lena in MCU but as her boss; Lena's response: "No, I'll be your boss."
- Mae on Thad Longfellow: "Boston Brahmin, deep pockets, draconian idea of crime and punishment… and an infinitely punchable face" — reusable line.
- "You can never trust a guy named Thad" — Jonah's quip about Longfellow.
- "Riggs and Murtaugh" — Lena calls Jeremy Rinzano this (Lethal Weapon reference) when he tries to claim police brutality.
- "There's no dumb ideas, just ideas that aren't smart yet" — Jonah's mantra offered to Sean when their locksmith leads stall.
- "You didn't want to tell me she was coming up behind me?" / Danny's standard reluctance / Henry's line "So go get the bastard" — Reagan-family tonal beats.
- "ass over teakettle" — Sarah's warning to the year-two rookies about overreaching.
- The Hawke restaurant named at a moment of dramatic irony (the venue name and "67 Hawkes Point Drive" tie-in).
- The BPD's post-leak line: "Recording BPD with or without our knowledge… legal." Sets up jurisdictional friction with Rinzano going forward.
- Edwin's "divine inspiration" gag whenever he nudges Mae toward an ask — recurring silver-familial nudge.
- Henry Reagan / "Gramps" is the dossier for 1960s Boston lore; reference him when future Boston-history cases come up.

## Unresolved threads

- Who ran Brian Rodgers off the road? The blue truck's path links to The Hawke, where the Mae/Chris photo was shot — implying the driver is the same person who photographed Mae and Chris. Driver identity unresolved.
- Who actually leaked the Mae/Chris photo? Thad received it anonymously; he claims the e-mail trace points to Eastborough (Chris Williams's neighborhood). The story is deliberately framed as "someone is coming for your family" without confirmation.
- Is Chief Chris Williams complicit, framed, or unaware? Thad's insinuation leaves Lena with a family mystery.
- Mae's political vulnerability vs Thad Longfellow — Seth Yates's leak of the 1985 record, and Thad's now-possible counter-leverage. Edwin is still in the dark on the clinic's real identity (thinks Mae was the patient).
- Brian Rodgers's return to active duty: Sarah's denial will have downstream effects; Brian is still determined to hunt his own assailant.
- Sergeant Karim's transfer and its impact on Jonah and Sean's day-to-day supervision — mentoring vacuum as year-two decisions loom.
- Henry Reagan's old journal entry — finding the real name and any surviving 1960s paperwork tied to Lynn Crawley.
- The Lewy body dementia diagnosis of Lynn Crawley as a plot device for unreliable testimony / flashback handling in future episodes.
- Jeremy Rinzano's continuing role — he has the Mae/Chris photo, livestreamed the Lena/Danny Strangler reveal, and is now positioned as an information vector the BPD cannot easily muzzle.
- Elena's anxiety / sleep situation and Maria Baez's arrival as a family-dynamic driver in Danny's household.
- "Mccall over" jokes / "Jennifer Reagan" reference — Mae uses "Jennifer Reagan" sarcastically; cross-family teasing patterns to keep tracking.
- Whether the yoga-pants signature implicates only Brad or a wider pattern; closure on the copycat case beyond Brad.

## S02E02

## 剧情梗概

冷开场在纽约：Danny 与 Baez 陪养女 Elena 玩耍时，被一位名叫 Dania 的妇人跟踪——她是 Elena 的生母之外祖母。Elena 之母当年为照顾病重的她提前脱离 WITSEC，随后被 "Mano Sangriento"（血手）杀害，Elena 由 Danny 与 Baez 合法收养。Baez 查证 Dania 身份属实，最终同意让 Elena 与外祖母相见。主线案件：Lena 拿到线索，Danny 周六返岗，二人追查将 Brian Rodgers 的 SUV 撞进海港的凶手。通过方向盘血迹与额头伤疤锁定飞车党 "Cain's Disciples"（该隐门徒）的见习成员 Jeffrey Viltz；经 Eastborough 局长 Chris Williams（Lena 的生父）私下找 Jackie Blaylock 医生缝合后，Lena 攻心审讯让 Viltz 吐露真相：他本只负责跟踪局长拍照，后被命令枪杀缉毒警 Rodgers，因下不了手才撞车入海。Viltz 以减刑加保护性拘留为条件交出冰毒实验室位置（高速旁自助仓储大楼地下室）。返程途中该隐门徒用信号干扰器在桥头设伏，Danny 与 Lena 弹尽之际 BPD 增援解围。事后资金链查出仓储公司属开曼空壳、一笔误转的保险付款退回 Eastborough Community Health——正是 Jackie 的诊所，Lena 断定她向帮派通风报信。DEA 高级特工 Tony Rushton 到场接管全案；Rodgers 正式接受 DEA 邀约并与 Lena 告别。副线：新任警长 Bet Ryan 到任严查制服，让 Sean 当"制服总管"；Sean 与 Jonah 撞破嫖客 Brett Keller 白嫖性工作者，违令执法反被 Ryan 狠批；Mae 与竞选对手 Thad Longfellow 从互相攻击达成 "détente"（缓和）；Mae 就多年隐瞒 Lena 生父一事道歉，Lena 表示原谅；Shabbat 家宴收束全篇。

## 重要角色行动

- Danny：冷开场陪 Elena 玩游戏、直面 Dania；应 Lena 之请返岗；俱乐部现场克制不强行抓人（避免 Viltz 被灭口）；桥头血战掩护 Lena。
- Lena：主导对 Viltz 的攻心审讯（"Deep calls to the deep"、"坏人与做了坏事的善良人有区别"）；此前单独施压 Thad 被指 "threat under color of law"；案情收官时推断 Jackie 是内鬼。
- Mae：拒绝调查 Lena，信任女儿；反向挖掘 Thad 家族黑料，主动提出 détente 并以 "almonds/耶利米书" 立誓护家；向 Lena 道歉并获原谅。
- Sarah：因 Lena 透露的私密信息拒绝 Rodgers 复职；桥头率 BPD 解围；与某人育有/将育有家庭线（Phoebe 之语）。
- Sean/Jonah：追捕砸车女子（实为被白嫖的性工作者）、扣押 Brett 车辆、安排其未婚妻 Val 当场揭穿；被 Ryan 训斥，Sean 上了她的 "list"。
- Rodgers：确认加入 DEA，与 Lena 和平分手（"Hopefully not forever"）。
- Chris Williams：以地头关系安排缝合、联络法官；请求 Lena 叫他 "Chris"；因信任 Jackie 而被辜负。
