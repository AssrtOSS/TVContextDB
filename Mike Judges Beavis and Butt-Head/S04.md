## S04E03

## 剧情梗概

Butt-Head 之前在停车场刮擦了某人的侧视镜，两人正躲在家中怕对方寻仇，Butt-Head 声称"厌倦了逃跑"，要"破坏音乐"（deface the music）。此时 Stewart 上门宣布与妻子 Kaitlyn 离婚，带着一箱啤酒和 CPAP 呼吸机搬来合住。Beavis 想到 Stewart 既然有孩子就"得手过"，也许能教他们把妹，便留他住下。三人在家看一段 John Brookfield 的家庭健身房绳索健身视频，两人全程往淫秽方向吐槽。两周过去依旧没人得手，Stewart 被打发去夜店实战；Stewart 反以为两人是高手，透露秘诀是戴结婚戒指装已婚（妞儿知道你会那啥）。两人把他踢开，自己去酒吧冒充已婚男，Beavis 还编造了老婆 Cindy 和儿子 Greg、Warrior，结果被轰。他们转念想去勾搭现在单身的 Stewart 老婆，对方以为他们是来劝复合的；正当她快要答应抛下孩子跟两人回家时，Stewart 回来恳求原谅（发誓再不买刮刮乐），两人复合，Beavis 和 Butt-Head 被赶出门。片尾那名被刮坏镜子的家伙找上门来："你就是砸了我镜子的混蛋！"

## 重要角色行为

- Butt-Head：闯祸刮镜后躲债，想出"破坏音乐"；在酒吧跟风声称已婚；最后提议去勾搭 Stewart 的老婆
- Beavis：提议跳窗逃跑；在酒吧自我介绍并虚构妻子 Cindy（"super hot"）、儿子 Greg 和 Warrior；抱怨 Stewart 坏了两人的好事
- Stewart：离婚后搬入，误以为两人是把妹高手，暴露婚戒秘诀；酒吧里教排舞、念着 Kaitlyn；结尾回心转意求复合成功，重新成为"有妇之夫"

## 新建立的名字/术语

- Kaitlyn（凯特琳）：Stewart 的妻子，本集才具名
- John Brookfield（约翰·布鲁克菲尔德）：健身视频中的教练
- Cindy（辛迪）、Greg（格雷格）、Warrior（勇士）：Beavis 虚构的妻子与儿子
- DILF（离异老爹）：Stewart 自称
- CPAP machine（呼吸机）、scratch-off ticket（刮刮乐）、Yelp、line dance（排舞）、safe word（安全词）、noob（新手）
- 口头梗："deface the music"、"the three amigos"、"spank my monkey"、"around the world"、"International Chest Day"、"monkey spanker"

## 连续性事实

- Stewart 与 Kaitlyn 已婚、有孩子，本集离婚后又复合；两人曾短暂成为 Beavis 和 Butt-Head 的室友
- 两人依旧"从未得手、永远得手不了"的设定未变
- Butt-Head 刮镜结仇的对象在本集结尾再次出现，且已找上家门

## 可供后续使用的梗与引用

- 装已婚/婚戒把妹法的连续失败（可在后续把妹题材中复用）
- Beavis 虚构家庭成员（Cindy、Greg、Warrior）的设定可被再次引用
- John Brookfield 绳索视频的淫秽化吐槽风格（"精英手淫训练""见过最污的东西"）
- "Stewart sucks" 与"没有他这儿显得有点空"的对照

## 未解决线索

- 侧视镜被刮的男子上门寻仇，结局停在"Uh-oh"，冲突未解决
- Stewart 虽与 Kaitlyn 复合，但其临时寄居打乱的生活如何收场未交代
- 两人"学把妹"的目标彻底失败，秘密知识始终没到手

# S04E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Beavis and Butt-Head are assigned to clean the Burger World walkway with a pressure washer by their manager. They mishear "pressure washer" as "pleasure washer" and immediately begin misusing it — spraying each other's groins, customers, food, and eventually the restaurant itself. A fire breaks out in the kitchen; rather than extinguish it, they avoid using the washer on the fire because "we don't want to wash the fire" — it would make the manager notice it more and make them wash it daily. They take the washer to the parking lot to spray ants until the water runs out.

## Important Character Actions
- **Beavis & Butt-Head**: Misinterpret "pressure washer" as "pleasure washer"; spray each other's testicles repeatedly; spray a customer's change at drive-thru; spray a hamburger into a bun; spray shake mix into the machine (impossible but they make it "work"); start a kitchen fire; debate whether to wash the fire; spray ants in parking lot.
- **Tom Anderson**: Opens episode with a "humorous military tale" about a private and a farmer's three daughters (classic dirty joke setup).
- **Burger World Manager**: Assigns them the cleaning task; unseen but referenced as someone who would make them "wash the fire every day."
- **Customers**: Drive-thru patron gets change sprayed at them; bathroom occupant gets blasted.

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **"Pleasure washer"** — Beavis and Butt-Head's mishearing/misnaming of "pressure washer," used throughout the episode.
- **Behemoth** — Polish death metal band featured in the music video segment; Butt-Head claims they're "the fifth best heavy metal band in Poland" but "number one if you ask the chicks."
- **"I am the shit ov God"** — Lyric from Behemoth's song, tattooed on the band leader's son's face (per Butt-Head's joke).

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Beavis and Butt-Head still work at Burger World (consistent with Season 4 setting).
- Their pattern of destroying workplace equipment and creating hazards continues.
- The "pleasure washer" joke reinforces their chronic sexual misunderstanding of mundane objects.
- Butt-Head's "hose for your hose" line (line 216) exemplifies their crude metaphor style.

## Jokes/References Useful for Later Episodes
- **Tom Anderson's military joke** — Recurring character trait: Anderson tells rambling, inappropriate stories (established in earlier seasons, continues here).
- **Behemoth music video** — Real band referenced; the "Polish death metal" joke and "chicks love blood/skulls/albinos" bit could be callbacks.
- **"Wash the fire" logic** — Absurdist workplace avoidance logic; reusable as a running gag about dodging responsibility.
- **Pressure washer misuse** — Physical comedy template: dangerous equipment + stupidity = chaos (recyclable for other tools/appliances).

## Unresolved Threads
- The Burger World kitchen fire is left burning — no resolution shown (manager's reaction, fire department, consequences).
- The pressure washer runs out of water in the parking lot; its fate/unreturned status is unaddressed.
- No consequence for assaulting customers with the washer (drive-thru, bathroom).
- Tom Anderson's joke is left unfinished (cuts to Burger World before punchline).

## S04E05 Summary

### Episode Plot
Beavis and Butt-Head attempt their weekly Sunday morning beer run, only to discover the local liquor store is closed due to blue laws. They must drive to the next county over — "the county that doesn't let Jesus tell them when to drink beer" — but Butt-Head's license was revoked for a DUI and he wears an ankle monitor too large to operate a gas pedal. Beavis has no license and never learned to drive, haunted by a traumatic driver's ed incident: he used to sneak off at lunch to masturbate in the driver's ed car, accidentally started it, rolled into a ditch, and "stubbed [his] schlong." Butt-Head forces Beavis to drive anyway. Beavis predictably crashes, runs himself over, and panics at a red light. Butt-Head then employs reverse psychology — relentlessly insulting Beavis to "give him confidence" — which bizarrely works. Beavis learns to drive competently enough to reach the liquor store. The episode intercuts a Bryan Johnson / "Blueprint" longevity documentary (the music video segment) featuring schlong detectors, collagen peptides, hyperbaric oxygen, and a father who wants his son's organs. They succeed in buying beer; Beavis declares he'll be ready to drive home after one more 12-pack.

### Important Character Actions
- **Butt-Head**: Reveals DUI + ankle monitor; refuses to drive; coaches Beavis abusively; uses insult-as-encouragement tactic; identifies "R" as "the letter that has a schlong."
- **Beavis**: Admits fear of driving rooted in driver's ed masturbation accident; repeatedly "stubs schlong"; panics at low speeds; responds to verbal abuse with improved driving; ends episode planning to drive drunk.
- **Both**: Treat the county line as a theological boundary ("they don't care about the Lord"); watch/roast the Blueprint longevity documentary.

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **"Schlong detector"** — Device in Blueprint protocol claiming "Schlong not found."
- **"Blueprint Longevity Protein and Berry nut mix"** / **"Don't Die"** — Bryan Johnson's branded supplements/protocol.
- **"Gimp harness"** — Butt-Head's term for the documentary subject's wearable (corrected by Beavis).
- **County-line beer run** — Established as a 20-year Sunday tradition thwarted by blue laws.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Butt-Head has a canonical DUI conviction and ankle monitor (first explicit mention).
- Beavis has never had a license; his driving trauma originates in high school driver's ed.
- The "Sunday beer run" is a 20-year standing ritual.
- Butt-Head's "R = schlong letter" visual mnemonic.

### Jokes/References Useful for Later Episodes
- **Reverse-psychology driving coaching** — "You suck / but maybe others suck more / your mom's only sort of a slut" → Beavis drives perfectly.
- **"Stubbed my schlong"** — Recurring physical gag (happens twice this episode).

### Unresolved Threads

- Butt-Head's ankle monitor / DUI legal status (never resolved, just ignored).
- Whether Beavis retains driving ability or reverts to incompetence next episode.

# S04E08 Summary

## Episode Plot
Beavis and Butt-Head are sitting in their beat-up car on a scorching day. Butt-Head parks too close to a pole, trapping Beavis inside when the door lock breaks and the keys are left in a convenience store. ## S04E07

# S04E07 — Lost in the Woods / Schlongmerica

## Episode Plot
Beavis and Butt-Head wander into the woods behind the school and get lost. While waiting for rescue, they discover a music video by Hardy featuring "Jim Bob" — Beavis recognizes his grandfather, **James Roberts Beavis**, who was arrested by the FBI and later died of a heart attack while having sex with Beavis's grandmother (Beavis was trapped under the body for 12 hours, thinking it was "sex sounds"). Inspired by the video's rustic vibe, they declare the woods their own sovereign nation: **Schlongmerica**. They draft absurd laws, crown Butt-Head king, craft spears, and fight over a coffee cup Beavis names "Marsha." A teacher (visible from the school parking lot) interrupts after only 45 minutes, ordering them back to class. They abandon Schlongmerica's laws but keep the "shafts" joke.

## Important Character Actions
- **Beavis**: Reveals backstory — mom abandoned him for a year-long cruise; grandfather died mid-coitus; mistakes coffee cup for a woman ("Marsha").
- **Butt-Head**: Immediately sells out Beavis to hypothetical FBI; appoints himself king of Schlongmerica; vetoes "no spanking your monkey" law; tries to execute Beavis for hoarding the cup.
- **Teacher (unnamed)**: Spots them from school grounds, ends the fantasy in 45 minutes flat.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Schlongmerica** — their short-lived woodland nation.
- **James Roberts Beavis** — Beavis's grandfather (FBI arrestee, died during sex with grandmother).
- **Marsha** — a coffee cup Beavis anthropomorphizes as a sexual partner.
- **Laws of Schlongmerica**: (1) Obey laws, (2) Share everything, (3) Don't be a butthole, (4) No spanking your monkey (vetoed).

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Beavis's mother left him with his grandfather outside Houston for ~1 year.
- Grandfather arrested by FBI (shown in music video footage).
- Grandfather died of heart attack during sex with grandmother; Beavis trapped underneath 12 hours.
- Beavis's family history of criminality / dysfunction established.
- The woods are directly adjacent to the school — not a remote wilderness.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "Shafts" / "arrowheads" running gag (phallic wordplay).
- "I pledge of allegiance to the schlong" — mock patriotism.
- Coffee cup as girlfriend ("Marsha's a dirty girl") — precedent for Beavis anthropomorphizing objects.

## Unresolved Threads

- Grandmother's fate after being trapped 12 hours.
- Whether Schlongmerica concept returns (abandoned by episode's end).
- The Hardy / Jim Bob music video — one-off or recurring artist?

The episode includes a music-video-style segment: "Swipe Right with David Van Driessen," a dating-show parody where Van Driessen goes on a vegan dinner date.

## Important Character Actions
- **Beavis & Butt-Head**: Intentionally crash their car multiple times to get women's phone numbers; end up concussed in an ambulance still attempting to "score"
- **Beavis**: Delivers extended gross-out monologue at dentist about "poop fairy," "money fairy," his mother's crystal meth use, bed-wetting habits, and not brushing teeth
- **Butt-Head**: Calls 911 to flirt with operator ("emergency in my pants"); claims he has a "boner in my head made of pain"
