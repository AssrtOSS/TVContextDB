## S02E01

EPISODE PLOT

The SendOff 300, a nonstop 300-mile mountain bike race, opens with rookie star Eli Cooper going off course on a turn and dying. Race medic Nick Sinclair is first on scene and fails to revive him. Jodi Larsen and interim sheriff Ryan Miller (covering the unresolved Al scandal) treat the death as a potential crime scene after spotting an older black eye inconsistent with the fall and unusual cherry-red discoloration. The medical examiner confirms poisoning by tetrahydrozoline, a chemical common in eye drops, lethal in small doses, with no injection marks, so administered orally through a water bottle.

Over one day, three suspects surface. Dana Chalke (SendOff CEO) owed Eli money and secretly took out a $4.5M life insurance policy on him, refuses to halt the race. Chad Michaelis, Eli's freshly fired trainer, is caught on video punching him, receives a threatening note on his windshield, and flees in a beige Sprinter van (plate XGP352). Declan Payne, the veteran racer being cut from the team to promote Eli, handed his SendOff water bottle to Eli and is curt when interviewed.

Tyson scrapes SendOff footage and confirms the handoff. Blue locates the discarded bottle; forensics find Eli's prints, Declan's prints, and a third unmatched set. The break comes when Meeks recognizes Sinclair as the former pro mountain biker whose career Declan ended years ago. Sinclair poisoned Declan's bottle for revenge; Declan gave it to Eli by chance. Jodi races to intercept Declan before he drinks from a second bottle, and Ryan arrests Sinclair for Eli's murder.

IMPORTANT CHARACTER ACTIONS

Jodi leads the case, identifies the cherry-red hue as off, and saves Declan by flagging him off the trail. Ryan operates as interim sheriff, runs the parallel Steel Reapers intel thread with Jodi, delivers the ballistic report on Cam Bergdorf, and arrests Sinclair. Nora guards the body and talks to Chad. Meeks handles trash duty and cracks the case by remembering Sinclair's prior career. Tyson pulls footage, runs drone Betty, builds the bottle timeline, and locates Chad's van. Bill attends his grief-support group, meets Francine Hill, and gets teased about "the rugged new sheriff." Blue tracks Chad's scent off Eli's clothing and locates the discarded bottle but refuses one command, earning a back-room threat from Jodi.

NEWLY ESTABLISHED NAMES AND TERMS

Dana Chalke (SendOff CEO); Declan Payne (veteran racer, the actual target); Eli Cooper (victim, rookie star); Chad Michaelis (Eli's former trainer); Nick Sinclair (race medic, former pro mountain biker, the actual killer); Superintendent Clive Fujihara (retired, visiting, insists on "Clive," no work before nine); Francine Hill (Bill's new acquaintance from the support group); Ernie Estevez (Jodi's former Military Police partner, arrives at the very end to "report a crime"); Steel Reapers biker gang with member Cam Bergdorf; Betty (Tyson's drone); tetrahydrozoline (the poison, an eye-drop ingredient); beige Sprinter van plate XGP352 (Chad's); forensic file SD202500338 (Bergdorf case).

CONTINUITY-RELEVANT FACTS

The Al scandal remains open, keeping Ryan in the interim sheriff seat. Liza King has been promoted and is back in DC; her absence explains why Fujihara's visit matters. Bill is running a grief-support group and easing into Claire's old community-leader role; Francine's interest in Bill foreshadows the Bill-Liza thread. Ryan's Rock N' Roast shop surfaces only via banter. Blue's prior military call sign Maverick and handlers Randall and Andrews are not referenced. Whispering Pines and Claire's death sit in the background, untouched this hour. The morning scene where Jodi pulls back from telling Ryan something is left dangling for a later beat.

JOKES AND REFERENCES USEFUL FOR LATER EPISODES

"Don't tell me we have robot detectives now" — Tyson's awkward delivery when meeting Clive. Bill: "I like the new officer. Smells kind of funny. Doesn't know his own strength" — plays on Blue as much as Clive. Jodi to Blue: "That's twice in one day. You keep up staging me, you'll be sitting in the back room now." Clive's "Just not before 9:00" establishes the no-mornings trait. Bill's "rugged new sheriff" tease is a Jodi-Ryan friction beat. Meeks on Declan: "mountain biking god, not good person." The team's "never meet your heroes" reflection after the Declan interview. Bill and Clive's "man of mystery has a daughter" / "we are peeling back layers" exchange seeds their friendship. Meeks' "run fast, don't run far" and "peak of human performance" salad jokes. Steel Reapers chant "Steel Reapers, baby" with revving bikes as a recurring flavor beat.

UNRESOLVED THREADS

Steel Reapers hold military-grade 55A1 EPR rounds traced to US military supply chains — the seed of the season's wider conspiracy. Cam Bergdorf's death (file SD202500338) is open. The third fingerprint on the poison bottle has no database match, hinting at an off-books pipeline. Dana Chalke has lawyered up over the $4.5M policy and the threat to Chad. Chad Michaelis has been told twice not to leave the area but cannot be held yet. Ernie Estevez's arrival at the end is the hook into Jodi's MP past and whatever crime he is bringing. Liza's promotion and DC posting reshape the chain of command. The Al scandal still needs closure. Bill, Francine, and the grief-support thread keep the Claire arc simmering.
