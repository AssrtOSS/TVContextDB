## Vigil S03E02 — Plot Summary

**Investigation in Glasgow (Silva & Longacre)**
DCI Amy Silva and DI Kirsten Longacre investigate the kidnapping of Energy Minister Gordon Laity by eco-activist Jason Collyer. Collyer demands the government publish the truth about secret seabed-mining activities on Svalbard within 24 hours or he will kill the minister. He also insists his and Professor Vincent Mair's stories be placed on a public website. The phone trace fails to locate him. Silva and Longacre piece together that Collyer emailed Laity about "Rosyth 17" — evidence that a mining fleet is already being built at Rosyth dockyard under the Department of Energy's commission, despite the minister publicly promising no mining would proceed until an independent report confirmed safety. Forensics find explosive residue on Collyer's yellow "Soda Bang" sweatshirt. His girlfriend, Chloe Ackroyd — PhD in environmental sciences, two arrests for climate protests — is placed under surveillance. When Silva confronts Chloe, she denies involvement but visibly recognizes Professor Mair's name. Chloe goes on the move; Silva follows on foot. Eventually Silva tracks Collyer and Laity to a hideout and enters the tense negotiation. Longacre interrogates Collyer, who denies planting the bomb and insists he was the target of the blast. He reveals Laity admitted the mining fleet is already under construction and that he is desperate to save green energy from its carbon costs. Collyer also accuses Laity of having Mair assassinated. During the confrontation, Chloe arrives with the sweatshirt — it turns out the yellow "Soda Bang" sweatshirt is hers; she says someone told her "no one would get hurt" and she never meant for this to happen. Chloe then shoots herself; she has a pulse and is rushed to hospital. Laity is rescued.

**Investigation on Svalbard (Longacre at the hub)**
Longacre continues investigating Daniel Hodge's murder. She concludes Hodge was shot in Shen Jia's room (two shots to the chest, ten centimetres apart) and his body then thrown out the window and collected from the ground outside to avoid moving it through the corridor. Two sets of footprints are found — one size-ten (male), one size-five. The smaller boot matches Shen Jia's foot. Longacre searches the building and finds signs someone recently lived in a hidden space (stale food, no electronics), confirming an insider at the hub has been sheltering Shen Jia. She interrogates the SBS soldiers: Anderson and Cunningham report back after the ambush, but Hodge never does. Longacre suspects someone on the team gave Shen Jia orders to run during the firefight — contradicting the official story that "nobody noticed" her leave. A night-vision video from the Russian side (shown to Longacre by Evgeni Korotayev) confirms a soldier gave Shen Jia the signal and watched her flee. The man is too blurred to identify. Longacre interviews Lorna McAllister, lead scientist at the hub, who admits there were "errors" in the environmental report on seabed mining and blames her predecessor, Siobhan Wilkinson. Longacre also visits the Rosyth dockyard and discovers the Energy Department commissioned three high-spec ships there a year ago; a staff member named Tamsin has been working on the project but is currently off sick.

**Russian-side subplot (Korotayev)**
Russian mayor/detective Evgeni Korotayev tells Longacre he has proof Hodge was killed at the institute hub, not Baltegrad. He offers an exchange: Shen Jia for Hodge's body and all evidence — which would spare the UK and NATO an embarrassing diplomatic incident. Longacre agrees to share the identity of the blurred soldier from the footage. Korotayev has night-vision video showing the soldier giving Shen Jia orders to flee, and bets that the man wears size-ten boots — linking him to the footprints in Shen Jia's room. Korotayev also tells someone (likely a superior) to "keep stringing her along" on the Longacre front, suggesting he may be manipulating her.

**SBS / Special Forces subplot**
Mark Cunningham, an SBS soldier, is questioned by Longacre and becomes evasive. She orders a background check on him. Cunningham warns Longacre that searching for Shen Jia will get her killed. An SBS soldier threatens her directly: "If you find her, they'll kill you." After Korotayev shows the footage of the mystery soldier giving Shen Jia the order to run, suspicion turns to Cunningham (who wears size-ten boots). At the episode's end, Longacre brings Russian analyst Polina to a hotel room (ostensibly to identify the blurred soldier), but Polina is stabbed and dies. Longacre and others arrive at the scene; Kirsten is told to "stop it."

## S03E01

## S03E01 Summary

### Plot

The episode cuts between two threads.

**Prologue – Svalbard extraction.** A four-man British SF team ("Gannet") led by Pickford tries to evacuate Chinese scientist Shen Jia (沈佳) from Svalbard by helicopter ("Blue Jay"). The flight is moved to a secondary LZ that turns out to be compromised. Armed Russians already on site demand Shen Jia, threaten to shoot down the helo and to "bury you in the ice" because "your government won't acknowledge you were ever here". Pickford aborts the helicopter. A firefight breaks out (grenades, propane tanks); in the chaos Shen Jia disappears; the team splits for a 90-minute radial search to a hub ERV. One British soldier is killed; Shen Jia remains missing.

**Main – two investigations.**

- *Domestic (Longacre home).* Early morning, Sir Ian and Jasmine Kelly turn up at DI Kirsten Longacre's (朗埃克) house with news that British contractor Daniel Hodge has been shot dead on Svalbard. Hodge was a four-man SF operative under civilian cover, "allegedly" shot breaking into a Russian building. Svalbard is demilitarised under an historic treaty; the cover must be a police investigation. Longacre (lower profile than a DCI) goes to Svalbard with Norway's consent; DCI Amy Silva (席尔瓦) oversees from the UK. UK is running a seabed-mining trial for rare metals tied to green tech, led by Minister for Energy and Green Transition Gordon Laity (戈登·莱蒂).

- *UK lab (Silva).* Calls Svalbard chief Henrik Becken; flags that Professor Vincent Mair (文森特·梅尔), the seabed-mining adviser, chartered out of Svalbard hours before Hodge's body was found. Silva visits Mair at his university lab; minutes later a bomb goes off – Silva survives, Mair dies. A colleague named Mitchell supplies intel on activist suspect Jason Collyer (eco-activist, prior trespass/criminal-damage arrests; photographed at a demo with Laity). Silva confronts Laity and produces a phone-record match between his number and Mair's phone immediately before the blast. Mair's dying words recorded: "There's people on the… I believe this is their land."

- *Svalbard (Longacre).* Met by Becken, Mayor Korotayev (Russian enclave Baltegrad), and Colonel Fyodor Zamarin (a Russian investigator brought by boat after flights were stopped). Russians refuse autopsy and withhold casings; the rifle placed beside Hodge is a SIG Sauer MCX (British SF issue). Witnesses: coal-miner Arseny (hospitalised, "shot by Hodge") and his wife Polina, who saw a man inside the building ~05:45. Longacre notices the body's drop position is unnatural and blood was trodden in – Korotayev's "untouched body" claim is false. Later, searching Shen Jia's lodgings, Longacre finds wiped blood and a jacket/item with two close-spaced subsonic bullet holes matching Hodge's wounds – so Hodge was killed in this room with a suppressed weapon, moved to Baltegrad, then staged.

- *Becken's private read.* Once the Russians leave, Becken tells Longacre the cover story is "rubbish" and that the body was moved to Baltegrad as a trap – "for us" – to push Russia and NATO toward conflict.

**Cliffhanger.** Silva tracks Jason Collyer and confronts him with "You murdered Vincent Mair." A shot rings out; an unseen attacker forces Collyer outside ("Now. … You should have done your job"). Collyer screams that he is a victim: "They killed the professor and they tried to kill me." Final lines are his calls for help.

# Vigil S03E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
On demilitarized Svalbard, the investigation into the biofuel discovery and related killings comes to a head. Chinese scientist **Jia (沈佳)** discovered a bacteria in Arctic ice that can produce a carbon-neutral biofuel replacement for fossil fuels — a breakthrough threatening petrostates globally. Multiple factions converge: China (Chen), Russia (Korotayev, FSB), CIA (Garcia), and UK police (Silva, Longacre). 

**Director Becken** is arrested after a Russian burner phone belonging to dead SBS soldier **Daniel Hodge** is found on him. **Garcia** reveals himself as CIA, claiming he was ordered to stop Jia leaving "by any means necessary." Surveillance footage initially implicates **Pickford** (SBS), but the camera timestamps were fabricated by Garcia. The real shooter is **Garcia himself** — he killed Hodge when Hodge discovered Garcia was leaking extraction intel to the Russians, shot at Jia on the airstrip, and hit the Director. Garcia framed Hodge as the Russian spy. He's arrested but warns Jia won't be safe off-island. The episode ends with Silva's vehicle ambushed en route to a safe house.

- **Garcia (CIA)**: The mole. Killed Hodge, shot at Jia, shot the Director, leaked intel to Russians (Korotayev). Motivated by US geopolitical interest: a post-oil world would collapse economies of allies (Kuwait, Wudyan) and adversaries (Russia), destabilizing US hegemony. Disavowed by CIA after Silva confronts him with evidence.
- **Silva & Longacre**: Piece together the frame-up; Garcia gives Korotayev's phone (with messages proving Garcia's leaks) to Longacre in exchange for… something unstated.
- **Pickford**: Cleared — the video showing him leaving the hub was doctored by Garcia.
- **Anderson**: SBS, initially suspicious of Pickford; eventually stands down.

## S03E06

## Vigil S03E06

### Episode Plot

The episode opens in the aftermath of the Svalbard extraction. Silva and Jia escaped the vehicle ambush from E05 and are hiding. China formally accuses the UK of abducting Shen Jia and demands her return, leveraging their dominance in rare-earth metal exports as a threat during the COP31 climate conference in Glasgow. Sir Ian Downing tells Silva the Home Secretary has pre-authorised Jia's deportation. Silva refuses to hand Jia over, goes off-grid, and is joined by Longacre, who traces her via a burner phone.

Jia explains her original plan with Vincent Mair: to distribute the biofuel bacteria samples at COP so no single country could control development. The UK instead secretly developed it at a lab called GenFusion east of the city. Silva and Longacre discover that Gordon Laity retrieved a carving from Vincent Mair's lab — the same carving Jia used to hide her sample — implying the sample survived the explosion and is now in UK hands.

Longacre visits Chloe Ackroyd, who has been conscious for 48 hours but undisclosed. Chloe reveals she was recruited by UK intelligence handler Scott Matthews, who ran her as a double agent after she was caught selling information to China. Crucially, Matthews inspected Jason Collyer's laptop (rigged with explosives by the Chinese) for an hour before Chloe returned it to Jason — meaning UK intelligence knew about the bomb and allowed Mair's death. Matthews then tries to kill Chloe in hospital; Longacre intervenes and Chloe names him.

Silva confronts Sir Ian at his home. He triggers a silent alarm; Silva and Jia flee. They raid the GenFusion lab with Mark Cunningham's help, recovering Jia's research samples and proof the UK was developing her biofuel in secret. Sir Ian and Gordon Laity are arrested. Jia makes it to COP31 and publicly distributes live bacteria samples to delegates, fulfilling her and Mair's original vision. The episode ends with Longacre telling Silva she won't face charges — provided she resigns.

### Important Character Actions

- **Silva**: Defies orders, shelters Jia, orchestrates the GenFusion lab raid, arrests Sir Ian and Laity.
- **Longacre**: Traces Silva's burner, saves Chloe from assassination, confronts Jasmine Kelly about intelligence service involvement, races to the GenFusion lab when she realises Silva is under threat.
- **Shen Jia**: Escapes to COP31, publicly distributes live biofuel bacteria samples to the world.
- **Sir Ian Downing**: Revealed as having known about the bomb on Mair's laptop and allowing Mair's death to protect the secret biofuel development. Arrested for accessory to murder, attempted murder, and conspiring with a foreign power.
- **Gordon Laity**: Arrested for fraud and conspiracy to murder. He retrieved the carving to hide evidence of the stolen sample.
- **Scott Matthews**: UK intelligence handler for Chloe. Examined the rigged laptop before the bombing. Attempts to kill Chloe in hospital.
- **Chloe Ackroyd**: Fully confesses — double agent recruited by Chinese, caught by UK intelligence, run by Matthews. Reveals UK knew about the bomb.
- **Jasmine Kelly**: Visited Chloe in hospital before Longacre; her involvement remains murky but she appears to be acting under Sir Ian's direction.
- **Mark Cunningham**: Helps Silva and Jia access the GenFusion lab.
- **Mitchell**: Provides lab explosion inventory updates and traces Silva's burner phone.
- **Teddy Ward**: Has a phone call with an unknown party (likely American) who warns about the biofuel disrupting Washington's interests and demands a "lead" on Jia's location.
- **Kirsten (Longacre's partner)**: Supports Silva's decision; helps with the lab investigation from home.

- **Scott Matthews** — UK intelligence officer, Chloe Ackroyd's handler, involved in the cover-up of Mair's murder.
- **PC Marks** — Police constable assigned to protect Chloe.
- **PC Gregory** — Fake police officer (actually an assassin sent by Matthews) who infiltrates Chloe's hospital room.
- **COP31** — The climate conference in Glasgow where Jia ultimately distributes her samples.
- **Bruce** — An Arctic scientist from the 1890s who worked on Svalbard (used as a reference point by Jia at the GenFusion lab).
- **Teddy Ward** — Appears to be a UK government or intelligence figure communicating with American interests.
- **Rare-earth metals** — China's leverage over the UK; essential for solar panels, wind turbines, and batteries.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The biofuel bacteria consumes CO2 and produces clean fuel, emitting only water when burned.
- Jia split two samples — she kept one, gave one to Vincent Mair. Mair's sample was hidden inside a carving. Gordon Laity retrieved that carving from police evidence after the bombing.
- The UK began developing the biofuel at GenFusion *the day before* Mair died — meaning they stole the sample while he was still alive.
- Chloe was a Chinese intelligence asset who was "turned" by UK intelligence (Matthews), making her a triple agent of sorts.
- UK intelligence knew about the Chinese-rigged bomb on Jason's laptop before it killed Mair, and permitted it to happen to protect the secret biofuel programme.
- Sir Ian authorised Jia's deportation under Chinese pressure to keep rare-earth metal trade flowing.
- The Americans (via Teddy Ward's contact) are also interested in controlling the biofuel and sent mercenaries to the GenFusion lab.
- Longacre tells Silva she should resign; Silva agrees and doesn't regret it.
- Jasmine Kelly's loyalty and knowledge remain ambiguous — she may have been kept in the dark by Sir Ian.
