# Vigil translation context

From Bilibili results I can see:
- 《不眠》(Vigil) is the established Chinese title for the BBC series
- Multiple Chinese subtitle groups use it (甜饼字幕组, 我叫阿搬, 月林亭, etc.)
- "不眠号" is also referenced in fan content
- The fans use "silvacre" as a ship name for Silva/Longacre
- Chinese name references seen: "席尔瓦" (Silva), "朗埃克" (Longacre), "守夜号" (HMS Vigil)

However, I have:

1. Verified the title "Vigil" is a BBC British TV series (police procedural), created by Tom Edge, produced by World Productions
2. First aired 29 August 2021 on BBC One
3. Three series, six episodes each (13 currently aired, 6 pending)
4. Cast, characters, and plot all confirmed via Wikipedia and Bilibili search results
5. Chinese title 《不眠》 confirmed via multiple Bilibili subtitle groups (甜饼字幕组)
6. Chinese renderings seen in Bilibili sources: 席尔瓦 (Silva), 朗埃克 (Longacre), 守夜号 (HMS Vigil)
