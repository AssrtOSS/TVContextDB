# S02E05 剧情摘要

## 剧情梗概
R.J. 接了一位新客户 Sam Ellery——鹈鹕本德高级别墅（Pelican Bend Senior Villas）的经理。Sam 患有精神分裂症，坚信自己身处“模拟程序故障”的时间循环：每天同一时间收到空包裹、早上被同一首 Dolly Parton《Here You Come Again》吵醒、洒水器乱喷、屋顶定时掉落“流星”，以及住户 Burt Landry 的船连续五晚爆炸、次日 Burt 又完好归来。R.J. 逐一拆解，证明全是人为戏法：流星是提前埋在屋顶木板里的炸药，“流星”不过是障眼法。 Burt 的船则是假人与仿制船。R.J. 和父亲 Reggie 在 Sam 车火花塞上涂了夜光鱼油（MuskMaster），锁定穿雨披的跟踪者；追逐中 R.J. 误闯邻居 Mack Reddington 家，被对方用电击枪放倒（Mack 以为他是闯入者，实则自导自演）。R.J. 上门质问酒店公司 Sunlux 的 Terry Washburn，发现 Sam 的姐姐 Lila 持有 Sunlux logo 的笔。当晚"假伯特"船再次爆炸，但 R.J. 发现船上坐的是真 Burt——法医确认 Burt 系中午死于后脑钝器击打，晚间爆炸只是毁尸布局。最终证据链指向 Mack：他房贷资不抵债、急需卖掉房产，而 Sunlux 的收购价 contingent on Pelican Bend 易主；他是社区剧院舞台技术员（刚排完《绿野仙踪》，烟火效果被剧评人诟病），有手段制造爆炸。他付钱（Sunlux 像撒糖一样散发的礼品卡）让 Burt 每晚避嫌，Burt 前一日打电话退出，遂遭灭口。警方凭指甲屑与鱼油残留的比对令搜查，在 Mack 家中发现 Burt 的血迹与凶器，Mack 被起诉。Sam 在 R.J. 劝说下自愿入住 St. Barnes 精神病院；Lila 放弃卖房，告知 Sunlux 不卖。Reggie 放弃当私家侦探的念头，考虑去看望另一个儿子 Tommy，转而尝试"爷爷"角色。

## 重要角色行动
- R.J.：接下 Sam 的委托，用"烟与镜"逻辑拆穿全部循环假象；被 Mack 电击后仍推进案情；单枪匹马闯入 Sunlux 办公室虚张声势套取信息；拼出 Mack 的动机-手段链，促成逮捕。
- Sam Ellery：新客户，病史六年，曾因崩溃住院，父母死于车祸；按信托规定经营 Pelican Bend，若再被强制住院则姐姐继承；危机中主动选择入院，并请 R.J. 代签文件。
- Lila Ellery：Sam 之姐，关系疏远，曾与 Sunlux 接触（Sunlux 签字笔）；一度为支付弟弟护理费决定卖房，结尾拒绝出售。
- Reggie Decker：R.J. 之父，已在本地逗留约一周半，常驻 Wish 酒吧（累计 42 杯免单）；本想转行做私家侦探（甚至规划去坦帕/奥兰多），结尾主动放弃，并劝 R.J. 约 Trudy 出去。
- Mack Reddington：邻居、剧院舞台技师，其母是别墅住户；雨披跟踪者+爆炸装置实施者+杀害 Burt 的凶手。
- Burt Landry：住户，被雇"每晚消失"（报酬为 Oyster Room、Biscayne Grill 等餐厅礼品卡），与 Boca Raton 的 Cecilia 交往；退出后被 Mack 中午杀害。
- Terry Washburn：Sunlux 高管，"桑拿时间神圣不可侵犯"，最初被 R.J. 列为嫌疑人，后证实并非杀 Burt 之人。
- Mel Abreu：询问 Burt 女友 Cecilia，拼出"拿钱避嫌"线索；与人有赌注（洗碗一周）。
- Trudy：CSU 技术员，从实验室给 R.J. 带了干衣服，已见过 Reggie。
- Catherine：为 Sam 准备三明治和水（仅被提及）。

# RJ Decker S02E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Kayla Treadwell discovers a severed hand in a cooler with a championship weightlifting ring on it. Her brother Jake takes the cooler, removes and sells the ring, but disappears before turning in the hand. Kayla and RJ are shot at by a black pickup driver. Jake crashes his car fleeing; the shooter (Eduardo Morales) pulls him from the flames but then screams "Which one?" at him repeatedly. Jake ends up in a medically induced coma. The ring belongs to John Fender, a world-champion weightlifter — and the hand is his. Eduardo (Nicaraguan consulate employee, steroid dealer) is later found dead, also missing his right hand. Bowling-lane wax on the ring leads RJ and partner Arj to Kayla's uncle's alley ("Les Feux de la RAMPE"). They uncover a $23M crypto wallet seized by the consulate from a Managua scammer; Eduardo knew the 5-digit combo (actually 5 fingers for a biometric palm scanner). Carmine (gym owner, ex-NJ mobster) sent his enforcer Todd Prentice (missing a hand) after the hand/fingers to access the wallet. State Police arrest Emi Ochoa at episode's end — Ryan Breen claims irrefutable evidence she killed her father Victor.

## Important Character Actions
- **RJ Decker**: Investigates the hand/ring case, protects Kayla, coordinates with Arj/Wish, gets Trudy (lab) to analyze wax residue, realizes the "5 digits" = fingers for biometric access.

- **Jake Treadwell**: Steals evidence, sells ring, crashes car, placed in induced coma; wakes briefly at end.

- **Carmine**: Gym owner, former mobster; orchestrates Todd's retrieval of the hand/fingers; "biblical" eye-for-an-eye punishment.
- **Todd Prentice**: Carmine's enforcer, missing right hand; sent after Kayla and Eduardo's hand; escapes police raid.
- **Eduardo Morales**: Shooter/savior; consulate staffer, steroid dealer; stole $23M crypto access, killed by Fender (theory).

## S02E04

# S02E04 Summary — RJ Decker

## Episode Plot
R.J. Decker, fresh off conditional release, is hired by Everglades Bass Classic (EBC) director Voss to spot cheaters during the two-day tournament. The case pivots when night patrol guide **Finch Palmer** is found dead — shot twice, body wedged high in a tree with no climb marks. Initial suspects: rising star angler **Dickie Dupree** (4 wins in 6 tournaments, assault record) and his sponsor **Reggie Decker** (R.J.'s estranged father), who sells "MuskMaster Select" attractant. Both have alibis (relabeling Kaneshiro rods at a storage unit). The breakthrough comes via **Merle Crews**, Finch's conspiracy-minded friend: Finch saw a "UAP" crash in the lake, went back to retrieve it, and was killed. The "UAP" is a **Drone Nova** prototype — a heavy-lift autonomous drone (people-mover) developed with stolen Argus defense-contractor tech. Drone Nova principals **Riz Bashir** and **Walt Taylor** killed Finch to protect their IP, then used a second drone to dump the body in the tree. Reggie wears a wire, lures them into revealing the murder weapon's location, and they're arrested. Episode closes at R.J.'s release party with tentative father-son reconciliation.

## Important Character Actions
- **R.J. Decker**: Takes the EBC security gig; partners with Det. Mel Abreu; deduces the body was placed from above (no broken branches on trunk); orchestrates the sting with his father.
- **Reggie Decker (R.J.'s father)**: Provides Dickie's alibi; brokers the Drone Nova sting by posing as a fixer selling "miracle solvent" and a patsy (Merle); wears a wire to recover the gun; admits he's "tricking himself" with schemes; chooses to "reassess" instead of chasing another venture.
- **Det. Mel Abreu**: Leads the homicide investigation; clears Dickie/Reggie via storage-unit witness; pivots to Drone Nova after ME confirms level trajectory (body moved post-mortem) and drone experts confirm heavy-lift capability.
- **Merle Crews**: Finch's only witness; reports the UAP crash, 29 calls with Finch day of death; destroys his phone; hides at mother's house; provides cloud photos of the drone lifting Finch.
- **Riz Bashir & Walt Taylor (Drone Nova)**: Ex-Argus engineers sued for IP theft; killed Finch to stop exposure of stolen tech; used prototype to move body; arrested after Reggie's sting.
