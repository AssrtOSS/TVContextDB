## S10E01

# S10E01 — Episode Summary

## Plot
- **Sinkhole at Second Street (downtown LA):** A father, Morty, his pregnant/estranged wife, son Billy, and teenage daughter are moving cross-country to California because Morty's brother Pat offered him a job after Morty lost his six months ago. Their first day, the road caves in. LAFD Station 118 rescues 36 people with zero fatalities.
- **Parallel storyline — Maddie's audit:** OEM runs a postmortem. Ty Worrell notes a 20% uptick in 911 calls from the area two days before the collapse and asks Maddie Buckley Han (covering dispatch management while Sue Blevins recovers) to audit all 911 calls in a two-mile radius going back 6–8 weeks, due Monday.
- **Aquarium incident:** The next day, an automated call reports a 300,000-gallon tropical tank whose pH crashed from 8.1 to 0.6 (turning acidic). One diver, "Scuba Stu," is trapped. 118 rescues him; Chim performs an emergency cricothyrotomy after burns melt his skin and standard intubation fails. Cause unknown; salinity issues since automation, water drawn from an underground aquifer (LEED-certified facility).
- **Athena's first detective case:** Athena Grant, newly promoted from 30 years on patrol (after being shot in the chest), gets handed a "kidnapping" — a Shih Tzu named Pickles that wandered into a neighbor's shed. Real case arrives: a John Doe, 80% second/third-degree burns, wearing a small but real diamond ring. Co-workers confess to a construction accident with an asphalt melter and beers. Athena gets the celebratory "W" but won't fully take it.
- **Cliffhanger escalation:** Maddie's audit surfaces a pattern of unrelated anomalies downtown — shadows in peripheral vision, sleeplessness, mass anxiety, dead foliage at a dog park, silver tarnishing, acidic tank water, strange hums. Worrell eventually walks the Metro tunnels himself. The Second Street sinkhole is expanding, emitting ~1500 Hz, trace sulfur dioxide, harmonic tremors (not San Andreas).4,000 people evacuated within a 10-block radius. The episode ends with the reveal: **it's a volcano under downtown LA.**

## Important Character Actions
- **Captain Bobby Han, Chim, Hen, Eddie Diaz, Buck, Ravi, Harry, Martinez, Probie** — sinkhole rescue and perimeter operations.
- **Hen** — recognizes Morty's seat-warmer electronics have created a current loop with his ICD (delivering 800 V shocks), uses a "doughnut magnet" to temporarily deactivate it before extraction.
- **Eddie** — descends into the sinkhole to extract a woman trapped in a blue car ten feet underwater beneath a bus.
- **Chim** — performs field cricothyrotomy on the aquarium diver after burns obliterate normal airway landmarks.
- **Maddie** — pulls the call audit together; pushes Worrell past initial dismissal.
- **Athena** — closes the Corbett case officially, then keeps digging because the burn pattern doesn't match the asphalt-melter confession; raids a hit jewelry store and goes into the sinkhole to recover evidence at the end.
- **Buck** — broken nose from his son Theo; record shows four non-work ER visits in three months.
- **May (Athena and Bobby's daughter)** — goads Athena into taking a real case instead of waiting for one.

## S10E03

# 9-1-1 S10E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The volcanic eruption continues. Lava threatens Presbyterian Hospital where casualties were evacuated. Bobby devises a plan: use K-rails to divert flow down Crocker to Sixth, then blast a path into the abandoned **Union Street Metro Station expansion** (deep, empty, connecting to the E Line) to drain lava back underground. Meanwhile, **Athena** is trapped in a subway tunnel with two injured Metro workers—**Harvey** (TBI, short-term memory loss, believes his wife Genevieve is alive) and **Sparks** (internal bleeding from a broken rib). Athena keeps Harvey moving by invoking Genevieve; Sparks fixes an old emergency phone (ex-Ma Bell tech) to call for help. **Maddie** at dispatch coordinates the rescue, linking Athena's location to the lava diversion target. **Buck** rescues student **Peyton** from a dorm fire, promising she's not alone. The diversion succeeds—explosives open the Metro shaft, lava drains in. 118 breaches from a Prohibition-era speakeasy tunnel (1175 Channel Ave) to extract Athena, Harvey, and Sparks. The eruption ends abruptly. Maddie is fired by **Sue** but rehired as co-manager with **Josh**. Buck and **Eddie** clash over parenting **Theo** (Buck treats trauma as jokes; Eddie says the kid needs boundaries, not a buddy).

## Important Character Actions
- **Athena**: Refuses to leave Harvey/Sparks; uses Genevieve as anchor for Harvey's failing memory; talks Sparks through shock.
- **Bobby**: Commands lava diversion; orders hoses off when water creates vog (sulfuric acid); coordinates K-rail + explosives plan.
- **Buck**: Enters burning dorm, seals Peyton's room with wet shirts, talks her down from panic, escapes before collapse.
- **Eddie**: Runs hospital triage; spots lava converging on Third/Alameda; proposes "move the lava" idea; confronts Buck on parenting.
- **Hen/Chimney/Ravi**: Execute K-rail placement and detonation under lava heat.
- **Maddie**: Tracks Athena via 911 callback; connects Metro worker's business card to Athena's mom; patches Athena through to May.
- **Sue**: Fires Maddie "for the mayor," then reveals she insisted on returning as a team—Maddie + Josh as co-managers.
- **Sparks**: Fixes landline despite internal bleeding; apologizes to Athena for past mistakes; asks her to tell Martha Bailey he's sorry.

## S10E04

# 9-1-1 — S10E04 Episode Summary

## Plot overview

Three threads interleave:

1. **Buck adopts Theo.** Buck's sister Maddie is away, and Buck takes Theo to a new preschool where Theo refuses to go, hides in a delivery truck, tries to climb a fence, and needs to be physically restrained. Theo calls Buck "a kidnapper" — he believes Buck stole him from his birth parents (Connor and Cameron). During a doctor visit, Theo tells Dr. Murphy "he's not my dad," and Buck is arrested by police after a park altercation with a school aide. His mother Athena visits and reveals she adopted Theo and previously lost her own son to a water-park incident where he called her a stranger — so she understands Theo's rejection.

2. **Mikey Jones collapses on the basketball court.** The star point guard at Point Sereno High catches a ball, clutches his chest, and collapses. Hen and Eddie treat him. His "coach" turns out to be a 35-year-old impostor who had injected himself with silicone-based illicit fillers — a silicone embolism causing respiratory failure. The team transports him to Presbyterian, where he is arrested for impersonating a high school student (his third such arrest).

3. **The multi-vehicle accident at Kosciuszko and Flower.** A man calling himself "Jerry," claiming medical training, performed catastrophically wrong interventions at the scene: tourniquet below the wound (causing a compound fracture), pulling a glass shard near an iliac artery, prying a car door on an injured bystander, and removing a motorcyclist from a bike. Maddie, taking the 911 call, noticed the inconsistency: he claimed to have no kit yet gave a blood pressure reading. Dispatcher Sue and Detective Jace Newell pursue him via traffic camera and license plate.

## Important character actions

- **Buck**: restrains Theo repeatedly; arrested for resisting/kidnapping accusation; ultimately tells Hen/Athena "I am a kidnapper… I can't parent a kid I traumatized," and resolves to give Theo back to Connor and Cameron. Also discusses leaving 118 to stay home with his kids with a part-time job, prompting the Maddie conversation about his confidence.
- **Maddie Han**: back on 911 phones after a period as supervisor; handles the carbon monoxide call and the "Jerry" call; identifies the missing blood pressure cuff; expresses that the Jerry case shook her confidence ("I missed all the signs") and had doubted she belonged as a supervisor.
- **Hen & Eddie**: treat Mikey (cardiac vs. panic vs. clot vs. embolism), manage the chaotic accident scene (C-collar/backboard, compound fracture, abdominal hemorrhage, a bystander injured prying a door).
- **Athena**: assesses Jerry Thomas; rules out relapse/withdrawal, diagnoses **Korsakoff syndrome** (thiamine/B1 deficiency with fabricated alternate histories); tracks him via a BOLO on clinics, med spas, and any future crash site.
- **Athena (mom)**: explains her history of losing her son in a lazy river, and being a "stranger" to him.
- **May Grant**: student nurse stocking the PPE cart; gets bullied by Dr. Fortner; gets drawn into "Dr. Thomas's" solo appendectomy; refuses to hand over the scalpel, sedates the patient, and walks him into LAPD.
- **Detective Jace Newell & Detective Grant**: identify the suspect's car; confront Nurse Bradley at the hospital.
- **Jerry Thomas**: captured in the OR hallway; treated with a thiamine drip (brain swelling down 20%); his wife visits.

## S10E06

## Episode plot

The 118 (Chimney, Harry, Ravi, Hen, Eddie) responds to a 911 call from brothers Burke and Floyd of Huell Brothers Fertilizer: their truck is hauling two tons of manure and ammonium sulfate down Birch Avenue when Floyd's lit cigar ignites the load, made worse because Burke forgot the tarp. The brothers are breaking up — Floyd admits to sleeping with Burke's wife Justine and is poaching their Santa Clarita clients (Marty among them). Athena reroutes them to an abandoned lot on Booker, but Burke loses consciousness at the wheel; Floyd takes over and they coast to a stop. The crew puts everyone through full decontamination because the burning fertilizer is a toxic hazard near a school zone, and the reconciled brothers agree to split the cost of a new truck.

In parallel, Buck — on medical leave with a hand broken in two places — attends a recovery meeting and flirts with a fellow attendee on crutches. Later he finds Nadia (Markie's mother, in opioid recovery) overdosing and revives her with Narcan; she makes him promise not to tell anyone.

Eddie and Hen respond to a community-theater production of Julius Caesar where Brutus actor Jason has genuinely stabbed co-star Isidor; Jason had earlier cut a cake with the real knife and left it among the props. The blade rests against Isidor's aorta, so the crew extracts it a millimeter at a time under ultrasound using trauma gel. Prop master Kathleen is briefly suspected, but it was Jason's own accident.

Hen transports Calvin Orr (52, terminal pancreatic cancer) to hospice. He asks for one last sunset on Mulholland Drive and confesses to the March 9, 1998 hit-and-run of Haley Gustafson, who survived (broken leg, staples in her head) and reunites with her parents Jed and his wife; Haley forgives him. Hen admits she herself killed a young woman, Evelyn Parker, in an accident years ago and has carried the guilt.

Finally, FBI agents interrogate Athena about Ben Hooks' prison murder (19 stab wounds during private yard time). Detective Grant — whom Hooks once shot, the bullet piercing her vest — attends with counsel and suggests an inside man. Athena collapses during the session.

## Important character actions

- Floyd confesses to the affair with Justine and to ditching the tarp; Burke admits he wanted out of the partnership so the brothers wouldn't end up hating each other; Floyd takes the wheel when Burke blacks out.
- Buck attends a recovery meeting against captain's orders, flirts with Nadia, then saves her from an opioid overdose with Narcan and keeps it secret.
- Jason (Brutus) unknowingly kills Isidor onstage with a cake knife; Kathleen the prop master is suspected and cleared.
- Calvin confesses his 28-year-old crime to Haley's parents and gets his sunset; Haley forgives him.
- Hen reveals her own past fatal hit-and-run (Evelyn Parker) and urges Calvin to put the past behind him.
- Athena faces the FBI over Hooks' death; Grant asserts her own motive (Hooks shot her) and floats an inside-man theory before Athena collapses.
