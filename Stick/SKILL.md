# Stick (TV Series) — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Overview
- **English Title**: Stick
- **Chinese Title**: 《一杆入魂》 (Mainland) / 《高杆拍档》 (Taiwan)
- **Genre**: Sports comedy / Drama
- **Creator**: Jason Keller
- **Original Network**: Apple TV+
- **First Air Date**: June 4, 2025
- **Country of Origin**: United States
- **Original Language**: English
- **Seasons**: 2 (Season 1 aired 2025; Season 2 premiered November 4, 2026)
- **Episodes per Season**: 10
- **Total Episodes**: 20 (as of Season 2)
- **Running Time**: 29–45 minutes
- **Production Companies**: Propagate Entertainment, Entertainment 360, Kellerama, Apple Studios
- **Filming Location**: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada (under working title "Rambler & the Birdie Machine")

## Series Premise
Pryce Cahill, a washed-up former professional golfer whose career derailed after an on-course meltdown 20 years ago, now sells clubs at a golf store in Indiana. His marriage has collapsed, and he's struggling to stay afloat. When he discovers Santi Wheeler, a 17-year-old golf prodigy with immense raw talent but deep anger and abandonment issues, Pryce bets everything on mentoring him. Together with Santi's mother Elena, Pryce's old friend and former caddy Mitts, and the genderfluid bartender-turned-caddy Zero, they travel across the U.S. in an RV for the amateur tournament circuit. What begins as a transactional arrangement becomes a found-family road trip about second chances, mentorship, and confronting past failures — on and off the course.

## Key Relationships
- **Pryce ↔ Santi**: Mentor/protégé; uneasy father-son dynamic; Pryce projects his lost son Jett onto Santi
- **Pryce ↔ Mitts**: Lifelong friends; former player-caddy duo; Mitts is the pragmatic anchor to Pryce's schemes
- **Pryce ↔ Amber-Linn**: Divorced but emotionally entangled; shared grief over Jett; $100k house sale drives S1 plot
- **Pryce ↔ Clark Ross**: Former partners turned rivals; Clark succeeded where Pryce failed; complex respect/resentment
- **Santi ↔ Zero**: Romantic tension → intimate connection; Zero is the only one Santi truly listens to
- **Santi ↔ Elena**: Mother/son; Elena pushes Santi hard, sometimes exploitatively; protective but controlling
- **Elena ↔ Mitts**: Unexpected romantic spark in S1; both tired of men making decisions for them
- **Pryce ↔ Amanda (S2)**: Past relationship; becomes his coach for comeback attempt; "tough love" dynamic

## World Setting
- **Primary Locales**: Indiana (home base) → cross-country RV road trip → various U.S. amateur/PGA tournament venues
- **Key Setting**: The RV (Mitts' "Rambler") serves as mobile home/base for the found family
- **Golf World**: Authentic depiction of amateur circuit (U.S. Amateur, qualifiers) and PGA Tour entry pathway; real pros and commentators appear as themselves
- **Tone**: "Feel-good comedy with heart" — compared to *Ted Lasso* and *Shrinking*; balances humor with genuine emotional weight (grief, addiction, abandonment, midlife crisis)
- **Timeframe**: Season 1 spans ~8 weeks (June–July 2025); Season 2 picks up months later (November 2026)

## Cultural Context & Translation Notes
1. **Golf Authenticity**: Real pros (Morikawa, Homa, Bradley, Clark, Nantz, Immelman) play themselves. Keep names as-is; tournament formats accurate.
2. **American Road Trip Trope**: RV journey = found family bonding; diner scenes, campgrounds, roadside attractions — translate cultural touchstones (e.g., "pickleball," "arcade," "swimming quarry").
3. **Grief & Masculinity**: Pryce's arc centers on repressed grief (son Jett) and toxic masculinity in sports. "What if" episodes (S1E7) are structural — don't flatten emotional beats.
4. **Gender Identity**: Zero uses she/they. Chinese has no grammatical gender for pronouns (TA covers both), but honorifics and self-references matter. Zero explicitly corrects misgendering.
5. **Class & Labor**: Elena (Latina, single mom) navigates white male golf establishment; Mitts (working-class Jewish) vs. Clark (WASP elite). Preserve class markers in dialogue.
6. **Anti-Capitalist/Environmental Themes**: Zero's rhetoric (degrowth, climate justice) — translate terminology accurately (not caricature).
7. **Title Double Entendre**: "Stick" = flagstick / golf club / Pryce's nickname / "stick with it" / "stick to your guns." Chinese titles split this: 《一杆入魂》 (one stroke enters the soul) emphasizes golf + redemption; 《高杆拍档》 (tall pole partners) emphasizes buddy comedy.
8. **Episode Titles**: Often golf puns or thematic — translate meaning over literal (e.g., "Grossweiner's Law" → 格罗斯维纳定律; "Zero Sum Game" → 零和游戏; "RV Shangri-La" → 房车仙境/桃花源).
9. **Music**: Caamp (theme), Zachary Dawes (score) — folk/indie Americana vibe. Song lyrics in episodes may need subtitle treatment.

## Season Structure Summary
- **Season 1 (10 eps, Jun–Jul 2025)**: Formation of the group; RV road trip to U.S. Amateur; Santi's breakthrough; betrayal/reconciliation; ReadySafe Invitational climax; Pryce chooses Santi over his own comeback.
