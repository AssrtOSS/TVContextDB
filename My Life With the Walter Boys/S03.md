## S03E09

**S03E09 — "Ghosts" (心魔/幽灵), per the series background's ordered S3 title list.** Note up front: the stored S03.md notes for this episode describe a *different* episode (Jackie–Cole breakup, car wash, C grade, Hannah, Will/Atlanta) and do not match this aligned transcript; everything below is taken directly from the transcript.

**Episode plot**

- Cole teaches Jackie her first oil change at Tony's garage; Tony walks in on them mid-kiss and passes on Mac's message that he's needed at the track. Jackie tags along and watches Cole test-drive a race car with a shaking-front-wheel problem (5,000 RPM). Mac calls it the best test drive he ever saw and begs Cole to race — "you were born to do this" — with opening day at the Routt Racetrack next week. Jackie is rattled to learn Cole has driven a race car before ("I thought you were just fixing them").
- Katherine and George hold a family meeting about dating: be kind, take care of each other's hearts, and "no sex in the house." Afterwards Katherine privately tells Jackie she saw her kissing Cole; Jackie explains Alex is okay with it and that the relationship is real.
- Wylder invites everyone to his season-opening barbecue at the old shack. Alex refuses ("they treat me like I'm some little kid"); Wylder and Cole push him to come, smile, and make friends.
- Nathan has a mild seizure at the school paper office while checking out the Electric Enemy CD for a concert review. He admits to Skylar he stopped his epilepsy medication ("tired of feeling like the weird kid") and begs her to tell no one.
- George, still in recovery (sleeping on a college-era cot by Katherine's bed, doing physical therapy, unable to manage stairs), plans a The Birds date with Katherine and finally climbs the stairs with her; Danny tries to buy tickets (bad reception) and quizzes George about Hitchcock.
- Kiley invites Alex to Wylder's barbecue and volunteers as his wingwoman. Jackie and Grace pitch the empty Main Street general store as a community space; owner Mr. Gannon refuses, then relents after Jackie visits him at home and opens up about selling her late mom's designer dresses.
- At the barbecue, Alex takes Wylder's hot-sauce dare and earns a grudging "real funny." Mac shows up and pushes Cole again to drive for him; Cole says no — for now.
- Jackie tells Cole she wants him to do the race: "I don't want you to do what's best for me. I want you to do what's best for you."
- Final scene: Jackie pulls up to the ranch with an unidentified guest (a first-time visitor — "It's definitely a change of pace") to see Uncle Richard, who has brought his new intern Eliot, who greets Jackie like an old acquaintance.
