## S02E02

## Episode Plot

J.D. is pleased that Dr. Cox's treatment for microscopic polyangiitis is working, but Cox responds by calling him "a huge disappointment" and advises him that the point of his job is to find someone who drives him as crazy as J.D. drove Cox. J.D. decides he needs a mentee and latches onto Grant Summers, the beloved new Chief Resident, assaulting him with nicknames ("G-spot" fails) and an awkward cheek grab ("Cheek Resident"). Grant invites J.D. to round with him, apparently mistaking J.D. for the Chief of Medicine.

In the OR, Turk runs a surgery with mentee Dashana to "Electric Relaxation," critiques her knot-tying, and banters about reading glasses; intern Tosh faints while drawing blood from donor Mr. Pataky. Meanwhile J.D. and Elliot ("Barbie") discuss choosing a specialty mid-intern year, and J.D. compares the decision to his late dirt-bike ex Craig — "give your career a helmet."

The central crisis: Dr. Cox's insurance denies coverage for his $35,000-per-infusion treatment (12 infusions remain), and he declares he'd rather die than go bankrupt. J.D.'s workaround — admit Cox as an inpatient so insurance must cover it — is refused by Grant as "super illegal." J.D. delivers a speech about the broken healthcare system framed as "jazz improvisation"; Grant is moved, permits the long-awaited shoulder touch, then reports J.D. to Sibby for insurance fraud. J.D. and Cox reaffirm their mutually annoying bond, and J.D. tries to admit Cox anyway, saying he's returning the protection Cox once gave him ("you have passed the baton"). The episode ends with a group hug-rejection scene ("How about a hug? Drop dead").

Subplots: Turk and Dashana clash over gloating; Turk fears he's at his professional peak with no hobbies or life outside the OR, until J.D. reminds him the real reward is molding interns into doctors. Tosh chooses endocrinology over pediatrics despite recruitment from Dr. Gould and warnings from Dr. Reid, capped by a dirty "glands" joke. Grant tries to mentor J.D. and gets friend-zoned. Asher suffers a throat-closing allergic reaction whose seriousness is left ambiguous.

- Turk: spars with Dashana over gloating, has a peak-skills midlife panic, accepts J.D.'s advice, and offers to teach J.D. the Aberdeen knot.

- Tosh: picks endocrinology over pediatrics after Dr. Gould's recruitment pitch.
- Asher: has an apparent allergic reaction (throat closing) initially dismissed as whimsy.

## S02E01

## Episode Plot

J.D. has started sleeping with his ex-wife Charlie again and is falling for her, even booking a Joey Fatone Cameo and planning a road trip to the house she was born in. At Sacred Heart, Dr. Cox is now a patient — an autoimmune illness has left him with dangerously low GFRs — and Elliot is treating him against Jordan's wishes. Jordan, Cox's wife and former chief of medicine, gives the staff 24 hours to diagnose him or she'll move him elsewhere. Tosh navigates the hospital's new consensual-relationship agreement paperwork around roommate Sibby while trying to shed her "Old Tosh" image and be taken seriously as "New Tosh." Elliot stops second-guessing herself, confronts Jordan, and trusts her instincts, switching Cox back to his original treatment and adjusting his antibiotics. The episode ends on the roof with J.D., Charlie, Turk, and an unplanned fireworks display. (っ˘з(˘⌣˘c)

## Important Character Actions

- J.D.: Sleeps with Charlie twice plus a "sleepy rub," hires a Joey Fatone Cameo for her, plans a road trip and "one last time," and pushes neurology to fast-track Cox's MRI.
- Elliot: Treats Cox, endures Jordan's ultimatum, verbally dresses down Jordan, admits she pushed the steroids, and revises Cox's treatment by instinct instead of more tests.
- Cox: Patient with low GFRs; refuses a kidney biopsy; goads Elliot; tells Elliot he taught her everything he knows and that he trusts her.
- Jordan: Threatens to transfer Cox, clashes with Elliot, and admits she loves Cox and fears losing him.
- Turk: Teases J.D., ribs Tosh about the relationship form, advises signing it ("Big up, monogamy"), and shares rooftop banter with J.D.
- Tosh: Debates signing Sibby's consensual-relationship form, insists Sibby "is not my type," and wants to be seen as a serious doctor, not "Tosher" or "TikTok Doc."
- Carla: Supports Elliot and Turk and nurses Turk through his test anxiety.
