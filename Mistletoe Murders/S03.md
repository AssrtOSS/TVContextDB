## S03E01

# Mistletoe Murders S03E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The season opens with a prologue: **Grace** (presumed dead) confronts **Aaron** about **Chimera** being compromised—someone else is using the name. Shauna and Roland are dead; everyone else is on the run.

Main storyline: **Richard Smiley**, the town's "Cheerman of the Board" (elected chair of the Christmas event planning committee), falls from his roof while hanging lights. He's found clutching a red mitten. **Detective Sam Wilner** investigates. **Helen Smiley** (Richard's wife of <1 year, formerly Helen Bishop/Marjorie) behaves oddly—she tells Emily "I never should have married him" and later confesses "I killed them. Every single one" (referring to her three husbands).

**Wes Hillman** appears at the station claiming Helen is a serial "black widow" killer: his father died in a staged car crash (pills in system), another husband died of CO poisoning while Helen was "away." Wes tracked her via a taunting Christmas card and a text warning Ruth. He's Helen's former stepson.

**Emily Lane** returns after a year away (told Sam it'd be a month). She reunites with Sam, her dad, Violet, June. **Brooke Carmichael** launches an aggressive campaign for Cheerman; June and Violet secretly nominate Emily too.

Forensics find a partial boot print on the roof that *doesn't match Richard's boots*—someone else was up there. The case becomes a murder investigation. Sam and Emily head to question Helen but get diverted to a 911 call: possible home invasion next door.

## Important Character Actions
- **Grace/Aaron**: Confrontation reveals Chimera compromised; Grace warns Aaron she has "precautions."
- **Sam**: Investigates Richard's death; resists mayor pressure to close it as accident; partners with Emily.
- **Emily**: Returns to town; reconnects with Sam (romantic tension unresolved); considers Cheerman run; visits Helen, hears confession.
- **Helen**: Acts grieving but makes damning admissions to Emily; sent Christmas card to Wes.
- **Wes**: Publicly accuses Helen at station; reveals he texted Ruth; journalist background.
- **Ruth**: Had explosive argument with Richard before his fall; received anonymous warning text (from Wes).
- **Brooke**: Blankets town with gift bags/swag for Cheerman votes; treats Emily as rival.
- **Mayor Ray Pemberton**: Pressures Sam to rule it an accident; friendly with Richard.

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Cheerman of the Board / Christmas Cheerman** (快乐主席/圣诞筹委会主席) — elected role planning all town December events.
- **Chimera** (奇美拉) — covert organization Grace belongs to; originally "exposing corruption and redistributing wealth," now compromised.
- **Grim Wreather** (死神花环手) — press nickname for Helen (from Wes's article).
- **Operations Officer Chase** (蔡斯行动主管) — Grace's handler; mother of **Thomas Chase** (special ops recruiter).
- **Shauna, Roland** — Chimera operatives, confirmed dead.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Emily was gone **1 year**, not the promised month; Sam feels betrayed.
- Grace is **alive** (was presumed dead); her cover identity is Emily Lane.
- Helen's three husbands: **Mason** (pills/car crash), **Emmett** (CO poisoning, detector expired), **Richard** (roof fall).
- Helen's former name: **Marjorie**.
- Wes is Helen's former stepson; journalist; tracked her via wedding licenses.
- Richard & Helen married **last spring** (<1 year).
- Ruth got warning text **~1 month ago** from Wes (she blocked it).
- Emily runs "Under the Mistletoe" year-round Christmas shop; Violet & June work there.
- Sam & Emily: romantic history, "not ready to give up," but Emily must leave for Chimera work.
- Mayor Ray: former furniture store owner, sold business for Town Hall job; ran unopposed.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "Bottomless hotcakes — unless you want more than four."
- **Cheerman** pun ("Chairman of the Board" → Christmas).
- Neighbor Albert: "Living next to a supernova" (Richard's light timer at 7 PM sharp).
- Brooke's campaign: spiced-eggnog scented candles as bribes; "spreading comfort and joy."
- "两个臭皮匠，顶个诸葛亮" (two heads better than one) — Sam to Wes.
- Emily's cats ("my boys") — she's a "lonely cat mama" this Christmas.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Who killed Richard?** Boot print ≠ Richard's boots; someone else on roof.
2. **Helen's confession** — "I killed them. Every single one." Guilt over inaction? Or literal?
3. **Grace/Emily's true identity** — Chimera operative; what's her mission?
4. **New Chimera** — Who hijacked the name? Aaron? Someone else?
5. **Aaron's fate** — Grace threatened him; he knows she's alive.
6. **911 call / home invasion** — At Albert's? Helen's? Intruder still inside.
7. **Emily vs. Brooke** — Cheerman race; Emily hasn't accepted nomination yet.
8. **Red mitten** — How did Helen's mitten end up in Richard's hand?
9. **Christmas card** — Helen's intent: apology or threat? Wes took it as taunt.
10. **Will Emily stay or leave?** Chimera work vs. Sam/town; "circumstances may have changed, but my feelings haven't."

## S03E02

## Mistletoe Murders S03E02 Summary

### Episode Plot
Richard Smiley, former Christmas Cheerman, is found dead on his roof clutching a red mitten. His wife Helen—who has a history of wealthy husbands dying under similar circumstances—becomes the prime suspect. Wes (Richard's son from a prior marriage) holds a press conference publicly accusing Helen of being a "black widow" killer. Detective Sam Wilner investigates while Emily Lane runs for Christmas Cheerman against longtime rival Brooke Carmichael.

### Important Character Actions
- **Helen Smiley**: Brought in for questioning on an unrelated matter (fish-feeding key mix-up), released. Reveals she insisted on a prenup excluding her from Richard's estate—no financial motive. Decides to leave town until Ruth convinces her to stay and become business partners.
- **Wes**: Orchestrates press conference to frame Helen. Actually murdered Richard: climbed roof via back ladder, took Helen's mitten (left off while using shears), wore it to avoid prints, pushed Richard off roof, then staged scene to implicate Helen. Confesses to Emily on roof before attempting to kill her; arrested by Sam.
- **Sam Wilner**: Investigates methodically. Discovers hidden camera in Albert's reindeer decoration (captures Wes on roof 11 min before 911 call). Learns Richard scheduled meeting to add Helen to will. Bluffs "state-of-the-art forensics return" to pressure suspects. Confesses feelings to Emily, wants a future together.
- **Emily Lane**: Runs for Cheerman, delivers impromptu speech defending Helen and community compassion over a Christmas market. Wins election (60+ votes) but declines—too much on her plate. Helps trap Wes on roof using reindeer lights timer (7 PM activation) as distraction.
- **Ruth**: Initially suspect (parked nearby night of murder, stood to lose inheritance if Helen added to will). Cleared. Reconciles with Helen, takes house off market, becomes Helen's wreath-making partner.
- **Albert Dobbs**: Neighbor feuded with Richard over Christmas lights. Admits moving ladder as prank, but terrified of heights—didn't go on roof. Cleared.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Christmas Cheerman**: Town's elected holiday ambassador (Emily wins, declines; Brooke runner-up)
- **GingerBrooke cookies**: Brooke's branded gingerbread cookies (running joke)
- **Chimera**: Hacker organization from Emily's past as "Grace"; Emily's former handler reactivates her to take it down permanently
- **Thomas**: Emily's former colleague/partner (deceased); handler references "this won't bring Thomas back"

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Helen's two previous husbands had substantial accidental death insurance payouts; investigations ruled insufficient evidence of foul play.
- Richard *was* planning to add Helen to his will (primary beneficiary) without her knowledge—meeting scheduled morning after his death.
- Helen's mitten found in Richard's hand because she removed it to use shears (safety snap); Wes took it, wore it, left it in Richard's grip.
- Emily's true identity: formerly "Grace," a hacker. Her past operation was exposed by Emily Cambridge (consultant recruited after teaching career derailed). Aaron (former partner) caused Cambridge's death via data breach to frame Grace.
- Sam's daughter Violet has outgrown Fletcher's Grove; Sam willing to leave for Emily.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "When you mess with the reindeer, you get the antlers!" — Brooke's campaign threat
- GingerBrooke cookies as metaphor for Brooke's competitiveness
- Emily's concession speech winning the election instead
- "Home isn't a place, it's a person" — Helen's line, echoed in Sam/Emily dynamic
- 60 voters in a small-town holiday election ("what do you expect?")

### Unresolved Threads
- **Emily's reactivation**: Handler (unnamed agency) demands Emily return to active duty to permanently bring down Chimera. She agreed months prior in exchange for new identities for her old crew. Must not tell anyone—including Sam. Deadline: after Christmas.
- **Aaron**: Still at large, "continues to seek [Emily] out." Handler believes Emily is best chance to capture him.
- **Sam/Emily relationship**: Sam declares "I wanna be where you are," but Emily cannot disclose her impending departure/secret work.
- **Helen/Ruth partnership**: Newly formed; potential for future friction or collaboration.

## S03E03

# Mistletoe Murders S03E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The "Christmas on Ice" show is rehearsing in Fletcher's Grove when its star, figure skater **Kimmy Baker**, vanishes. Blood is found on the rink ice, a rental skate blade tests positive for her blood, and her phone is left recording at the scene. **Detective Sam Wilner** treats it as an abduction; **Emily Lane** inserts herself unofficially until Sam formally asks her to be his partner.

Evidence points to a staged crime: Kimmy's rental car is found abandoned by the lake with an **ice auger** and a blood trail leading under the ice, so she is presumed dead. A white hoodie with two candy-cane hearts, a memory card of photos, and a hardware-store receipt in **White Rock** unravel the case. In the final confrontation Emily reveals the truth: **Kimmy faked her own abduction and murder, and Jon helped her every step of the way.** The episode ends mid-accusation.

Parallel to this, Emily's covert life resurfaces: a field operation ("Medusa") tracking a hacker cell planning a Christmas-morning power outage is scrubbed after she spots **Aaron's** bike. Her handler **Chase** demands she stop meddling locally and return in the new year.

## Important Character Actions
- **Sam Wilner**: Investigates the "abduction," remains skeptical of Jon, officially invites Emily to partner ("our methods complement each other"), applies for a warrant to search Derek's suite.
- **Emily Lane / Grace**: Goes solo to the crime scene, spots the blood-pattern and blind-spot anomalies, identifies the delivery driver, retrieves the hoodie via Brooke, and cracks the photo evidence. Confronts Jon at episode's end. Keeps her Chimera work secret from Sam.
- **Jon Kim**: Choreographer and Sue's nephew; argued with Derek, is clears of cheating, but secretly aided Kimmy's disappearance and offers KJ a replacement role.
- **Kimmy Baker / KJ**: Revealed alive and complicit in the fake murder; had also called off her engagement to Jon.
- **Derek Sutton**: Kimmy's boyfriend/co-star; hired PI Chuck, quits the show in protest when KJ is cast, "can't remember" where the memory card went — Sam suspects he is hiding something.
- **Chuck Sanducci**: Pizza driver/PI hired by Derek, followed Jon and Kimmy, gave Derek the memory card; thought Emily was from an old case.
- **Brooke Carmichael**: Launches the first Fletcher's Grove Christmas Market, hands Emily newsletters, unwittingly hands over the crucial hoodie from lost-and-found.
- **Ray (Mayor)**: Pressured about the market, warns the Zarkovs are dangerous, digs up intel on Pavel.
- **Violet & Kyle**: Kyle arrives early for Christmas break; only one week together, so Violet makes an ambitious schedule; snowboarding, board games, a private drive-in.
- **Sue**: Reconnecting with estranged nephew Jon; makes his favorite soup.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Kimmy Baker / Kimberly-Jade "KJ"** — Olympic-bound figure skater, injured out of competition; "one of a kind."
- **Christmas on Ice** — traveling skating show where the case unfolds; tour must go on despite the "murder."
- **Jon Kim** — choreographer, Sue's nephew, medical-school dropout.
- **Derek Sutton** — co-star, Kimmy's boyfriend; **Paula McKenzie** — Kimmy's, later KJ's, manager.
- **Chuck Sanducci** — private investigator / pizza delivery driver; rate "$500 plus expenses."
- **Zarkov family / Pavel Zarkov** — funded Kimmy since childhood, quasi-foster family; "not benevolent," "body count."
- **White Rock** — neighboring town with the hardware store in the photos.
- **Chase, Thomas, Medusa, Aaron, Chimera** — Emily's handler, field-team leader, her codename, her former partner, and the hacker cell/power-outage plot.
- **Reindeer Wrangle / Don't Melt the Snowman** — Violet and Kyle's childhood board games.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Emily is **Grace**, a reactivated operative; her new mission ties to the Christmas-morning blackout and Aaron. She expects to return in the new year and hides it from Sam.
- Sam and Emily are now **official partners**; her stated motive for the partnership is only "not entirely" about spending time together.
- Jon's estrangement from father and aunt Sue stems from quitting medical school; "life's too short not to do what you love."
- Jon and KJ **called off their engagement** after she admitted Derek hired the PI; KJ took Paula on as manager and joined the show as Kimmy's replacement.
- Derek quit the tour; if he flees town before the warrant, evidence may be lost.
- Kimmy's blood was on the skate and the ice; the **hoodie's blood** is untested (lab pending).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "I'm just a **basket case** for gift baskets."
- KJ joking that marriage would make her "**Kimberly Kim**."
- "I see myself more like a **fox** than a wolf."
- "You can't snap your fingers and a Christmas market appears"; "gas-guzzling eyesore."
- "He'll still be sad, but he won't be hungry" — Sue's soup logic.
- "**Bah humbug** to you, too" — Emily to Chase.
- "Super stealthy" — Emily tripping entering the rink.
- "The real Santa has magical reindeer... I'm driving a fridge."
- "**Don't Melt the Snowman**" has "a lot of strategy involved."
- "If it's meant to be, they'll find their way back to each other."

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Kimmy's motive and whereabouts** — fake abduction/murder with Jon's help; why, and where is she now? Confrontation cut off.
2. **The blood** — hoodie sleeve and staged ice scene; whose blood, lab results pending.
3. **Zarkovs / Pavel** — may send "representatives"; Emily asks Chase for a back channel; Ray's "body count" warning.
4. **Derek** — hiding the memory card, possible flight, warrant pending; his reason for hiring a PI beyond jealousy.
5. **Jon's complicity** — medical-school "blood skills" jab; extent of his role in the fake crime.
6. **Chimera field op** — scrubbed mission, Aaron on site, Emily's refusal to extract; secret from Sam.
7. **Sam/Emily** — partnership and romance uneasily combined; "not entirely" motive.
8. **Violet/Kyle** — one-week visit, packed schedule, future of the long-distance relationship.
9. **Christmas on Ice** — tour proceeds with KJ replacing Kimmy after Derek's exit.
