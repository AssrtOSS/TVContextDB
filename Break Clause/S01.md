## Break Clause S01E04 — November (Thanksgiving)

### Episode Plot
Ben and Lil host a Thanksgiving dinner party in their London flat, attempting to prove they can be "proper adult friends" while stuck together until the break clause activates. The evening spirals: the turkey comes from a supermarket bin, Lil's chaotic best friend Jos arrives freshly bereaved (grandmother died that morning), Lil's boss Owen shows up dressed as a "coloniser" pilgrim, and Alan (Ben's dad) eats an entire bar of mushroom chocolate and spends hours convinced he's a dog. Amid the chaos, Ben and Lil steal a quiet moment on the balcony where they mutually confess jealousy — Lil of Ben with Frances, Ben of Lil with Kas — and end up kissing as the credits roll to "I Fooled Around and Fell in Love."

### Important Character Actions
- **Ben**: Procures "bin turkey" (forgot to check sell-by date), tries to stage a mature dinner party, admits jealousy of Kas, initiates the kiss on the balcony.
- **Lil**: Pushes for the dinner party to prove adulthood, comforts grieving Jos, admits jealousy of Frances, reciprocates the kiss.
- **Alan**: Drives down from Leeds unannounced, parks in Thornton Heath, consumes mushroom chocolate, hallucinates being a dog (barking, purring, howling) for much of the episode.
- **Jos**: Arrives mid-grief ("my grammy just died"), oscillates between raw vulnerability and chaotic oversharing (hands-free orgasm at a bakery).
- **Owen**: Wears offensive pilgrim costume, flirts heavily with Jos despite mentioning a wife, implied to be on the verge of cheating.
- **Frances**: Brings plates, chairs, wine — saves the hosting logistics; Ben introduces her as "from upstairs."
- **Kas**: Lil's work colleague; Ben meets him for the first time, they bond; Lil later admits she thought Ben fancied Kas.

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Bin turkey** (垃圾桶火鸡) — turkey retrieved from a supermarket waste bin, becomes the episode's running gag and disaster catalyst.
- **Thornton Heath** (桑顿希思) — where Alan parks his camper van after failing to find a spot near the flat.
- **Rum Tum Tugger** — revealed as Ben's "sexual awakening" character from *Cats* (via the forehead-sticker game).
- **Mushroom chocolate** (蘑菇巧克力) — psilocybin edibles brought by Nic/Sean; Alan eats a whole bar.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Timeline: **November** (Episode 4 of 6; break clause activates in February).
- Lil's mother **Deb** knows Ben and Lil are "on a break" (not fully broken up) and defends Ben publicly ("not believing everything said about him just cos a woman said it").
- Ben's writing remains stalled; he survives on medical trials and market-research surveys.
- Lil is **youngest director in company history** at her architecture firm (Berlin gallery contract secured).
- Alan lives in a camper van; his marriage to Ben's mum is strained ("the more they work away, the more they stray").
- Jos's grandmother ("Grammy") was 96, "perfectly healthy," died suddenly that morning — Jos calls her "the only one who really saw me."
