## S17E01

# S17E01 — Cake Week

## Plot

Season 17 opens with Prue Leith's departure, played as a Doctor Who-style "regeneration" gag (TARDIS sound effects). **Nigella Lawson** is recruited as the new permanent judge alongside Paul Hollywood. The hosts are Noel Fielding and Alison Hammond.

The season's 12 amateur bakers enter the tent for Cake Week, which contains three challenges:

**Signature — Chocolate Stout Cake (2 h 15 min).** Bakers must include at least one filling or topping. Nigella's own iconic Guinness cake sets the benchmark. Gary's triple-alcohol cake (Guinness + whiskey ganache + Baileys buttercream, with a secret mayonnaise ingredient) earns Paul's remark: "That's probably the best cake here." Molly drops her bundt tin on the floor; she bakes replacement sponge from scratch, switches to sandwich-style, and finishes — judges praise the cardamom flavour despite the disaster. Shannon's cake melts because it wasn't cool enough; Clara's ambitious sugar dome collapses in the tent.

**Technical — Coffee and Walnut Cake (2 h, set by Nigella, blind-judged from an illustration only — no written recipe).** Key challenges: walnut-to-flour ratio, coffee intensity, and salt balance. **Tom wins 1st**, Gary 2nd, Gabe 3rd. Molly comes last.

**Showstopper — Self-Portrait Tiered Cake (4 h).** Bakers must create a minimum two-tier cake that depicts themselves or represents their identity. Mid-challenge, **Shannon leaves the tent unwell**, reducing the field to 11. Danni's vegan cake collapses on the table before judging; she breaks down in tears but is comforted by the group. Gabe's concept — a smashed isomalt mirror revealing his drag alter ego Blue Romantic painted behind it — moves Nigella to emotion. Moyin's 3D carved "Head of Heritage" celebrates his Nigerian roots with hibiscus curd (zobo-inspired) but suffers from an oven left off during baking. Yannis attempts a pointillist portrait on chiffon sponge and falls behind schedule.

## Star Baker and Elimination

**Mo** wins Star Baker (21-year-old law student, youngest in the tent; lemon-blueberry portrait cake of cat Simba drew enthusiastic praise for simplicity and balance). **No baker is eliminated** — the judges explain that since Shannon left for health reasons, it is fairer to let everyone continue into week two.

## S17E03

# S17E03 — Audience Choice Week

## Plot
First-ever "Audience Choice Week": thousands of public submissions set all three challenges.

- **Signature (3 hours):** bakers team up with a relative/partner to bake an "exquisite baked cheesecake" inspired by a family-favourite flavour combination.
- **Technical (2 hours):** a "Mystery Box" — no recipe given, bakers choose any bake from a limited pantry of staples (flour, raising agents, eggs, basics) after 5 minutes' planning. Judged blind.
- **Showstopper (4½ hours):** a "wearable bake" — an edible fashion piece actually modelled on the head/body.

Theme: families inside the tent for the Signature; Noel quips the judges look like they've been put in "little wardrobes." Alison Hammond arrives as guest host for the Showstopper segment, modelling the bakes.

## Character actions / key beats

### Signature — Baked Cheesecake (with relatives)
- **Tom + partner Alex**: New York-style, almond shortbread base, white chocolate filling, raspberry topping. Paul: "fabulously retro… handsome profile… love the tartness of the raspberries, it really makes it zing." Impression: very strong.
- **Shannon + mum Tammy**: "Naughty Bailey" (named after their late dog Bailey, who once licked a glass of Baileys). Ginger biscuit base, Irish cream + white chocolate Basque filling, dark chocolate ganache, Irish cream salted truffles. Paul: ganache is "very intense… might even put a bit too much salt. I can't even taste the Baileys."
- **Nikki + cousin Jen ("JenJen")**: Bakewell-inspired Basque, sour cherry gel, cherry filling, almond base. Cooling fails — Jen uses ice to shock the tin. Judges: 4 teaspoons almond extract is "overkill… starts burning the roof of your mouth. All you get is that and the cherries are lost."
- **Clara + fiancé Kieran**: "Honky-tonk cheesecake," bourbon + peach jam from Georgia (they're moving to Georgia, USA). Paul: bourbon flavour "spot-on," peach works well, but it looks like a "swimming pool" — too much peach jam; "a drizzle instead of a flood."
- **Connie + partner Myke**: Ginger + lemon Basque topped with two poached pears representing them both (Myke slices them thin). Paul: "florals of the pears go well… but I think it might have been better not adding more liquid."
- **Moyin + mum**: Mum stirs the orange curd. Basque, orange curd + candied decoration. Paul: balance, bake and look are "flawless" — **Moyin receives Paul's handshake** (noted as the first handshake of the series). "One of the best things I've eaten for some time."
- **Gary + sister Paula**: New York-style with sweet cherry compote "lake" and melted-sweet swan decorations. Judges: "swans are incredible… there's so much liquid… probably a little bit too much." Paul: "I want swans on everything I eat from now on."
- **Yannis + mum Ruhsar**: German-style with Quark (low-fat), fig jam topping, plus a little Philadelphia cream cheese to soften. Paul: "very delicate… I don't know that I like all the little pips of the fig."
- **Gabe + partner Ellis + Tyler**: "Throuple boyband" — coconut, lime + chocolate Basque, dark chocolate ganache, lime curd. Judges: "dancing shards of coconut… theatrical," but the lime curd is unnecessary — "bin the curd."
- **Mo + cousin Esrat**: Lemon + pistachio + cardamom + strawberry. (Judged separately.)

### Technical — Mystery Box (no recipe)
Bakers lean on familiar lemon/raspberry flavours. Judges note many sponges are overbaked and dense from raising-agent guesswork; Italian/Swiss meringue struggles (one Swiss meringue buttercream splits and is pivoted to American buttercream).
Final ranking (worst→first): Shannon 11th, Molly 10th, Yannis 9th, Gary 8th, Tom 7th, Connie 6th, Moyin 5th, Nikki 4th, Clara 3rd, Mo 2nd, **first place winner — left unnamed in subtitles (technical winner)**. Noel's recap calls out Yannis ninth, Gary eighth, etc.

### Showstopper — Wearable Bakes
- **Connie**: "Mother of the Bride" hat inspired by her mum's wedding-day hat. Caribbean rum + fruit + carrot cake. "Divine… superb… that is my kind of cake." Sister Myra and Connie emotional — dedicated "to all mothers." Standout.
- **Tom**: Dolly Parton wig topper (he's a hairstylist), banana sponge + caramel rum banana jam. "Intoxication by banana… I quite enjoy the banana… design-wise it's great." Keeps eating it.
- **Mo**: Victoria's Secret-style angel wings, almond sponge + cherry compote, wafer-paper feathers. Light and delicious, but "just looks like a cake… needed to look more like a hat than a cake." Wonky on the head.
- **Gary**: Knitted bobble hat (he's a knitwear technologist), chocolate + orange. "Looks like it's been knitted… really sensational… just a bit untidy, but it kind of works… elegant."
- **Gabe**: Turkish Delight biscuit + choux crown (he's a costumer). Design fantastic, choux a little dense, biscuit "overworked quite dramatically," rose flavour praised. "Has your signature ebullience."
- **Nikki**: Sombrero, gingerbread + lime, mojito-filled choux buns with craquelin. "Joyful… biscuit delicious… choux delicate… I want to mix the two." Favourite flavour combo of judges.
- **Yannis**: Bengali Hindu groom's wedding crown, sable pastry + choux with saffron-cardamom diplomat, mango lassi flavour. "I'd love to wear that… symmetry works… mango works really well." Pastry a bit hard, scale small.
- **Molly**: Daniel Ricciardo (Japanese GP) F1 cap, Earl Grey cake with Earl Grey buttercream + orange. Judges like look and citrus, but Earl Grey too subtle and sponge dense/heavy.
- **Clara**: "Pina couture" designer handbag, coconut liqueur + pineapple + coconut sponge (pina colada). Crocodile-skin fondant effect. "Exceptional… moist, buttery… looks dense then so light inside." Modelled by Alison on the catwalk.
- **Shannon**: Gingerbread fairy wings with blackberry purée + royal icing. Paul earlier: "so it's just biscuit decorated?" Judge: "4½ hours for a biscuit with royal icing… charming though it was, you've got to do something that's a bit of a Showstopper." Star Baker candidate undermined.

## S17E04

# Episode S17E04 — Bread Week

## Recap & Setup
- The week opens with a recap of S17E03 (the first Audience Choice week): Moyin and his mum won this season's first Hollywood handshake, Clara's runway-ready showstopper earned her Star Baker, and Shannon left as the second baker out after her showstopper wings collapsed.
- It's now Bread Week. Noel calls Paul Hollywood "The Boss Breadman" / "Bread Father"; Paul quips "bread is his bread and butter."
- Bakers still in the tent (this episode): Tom, Gary, Connie, Yannis, Molly, Gabe, Clara, Mo, Moyin. Nikki is mentioned but absent — she's gone home "due to family reasons" (Noel hopes she'll return next week). Noel steps in to describe Nikki's unfinished showstopper for the judges.

## Signature Challenge — Ultimate Garlic Bread (3 hr, yeasted dough, garlic must be the hero)
- Tom — Chelsea buns with Black Bomber (sharp Cheddar) on garlic butter; cites "Mrs Burton's GCSE food tech" inspiration. Paul advised him to roll thinner for tighter swirls next time.
- Gary — Chelsea buns with mature Cheddar + garlic herb butter. Big joke: Gary's middle name is also "Paul" (Gary Paul, after his father). Bakers quip about a DNA test with Paul Hollywood; royal-connection gag from Paul's past Chelsea buns.
- Connie — Four-cheese garlic butter (mozzarella, mature, smoky, Parmesan) twisted into a star shape, yum-yum-style pulls. Paul tells her she's still short on garlic.
- Yannis — Bulb-shaped layered loaf with fresh garlic, garlic powder, and garlic confit butter (garlic-naan style). Too much butter between layers = raw, underbaked dough when judged. Judges: "Basically what you've got is raw dough."
- Molly — Single-bulb garlic butter on mini loaves with a braided top, stitched more than braided. Densely proved, missing garlic punch.
- Gabe — 12 bulbs of garlic + three Neapolitan pizza flavours. Paul notes his "generous personality went into the bake the wrong way" — too much filling/garlic, raw centre.
- Clara — Spiralled buns with mozzarella + Thai basil & garlic pesto (her mother-in-law's recipe). Judges: Thai basil overpowers the garlic.
- Mo — Bear-shaped loaf with Naga chilli pickle (Bangladeshi cuisine) in dough and garlic butter. Admits openly: "I've never made bread before," "bread is super boring." Paul visibly offended; Gary told to step in as substitute Paul. Judges say less heat, more garlic.
- Moyin — Bread rolls with raw + roasted garlic, pine nuts, basil, Parmesan pesto. Self-deprecating about his garlic-breath legacy.
- Nikki (described, later stepped in by Noel) — Tear-and-share with basil/mint/pine-nut pesto, mozzarella, roasted garlic, semolina crunch. Judges like the flavours but it was under-proved.

## Interlude — Hailstorm
- Mid-Signature a violent storm hits the tent: thunder, golf-ball-sized hailstones. Bakers panic; Noel pops in to give time calls. Sets up the "Tom, do the time call" running joke.

## Technical Challenge — Paul's Flatbreads with Roasted Grapes & Blue Cheese (1 hr, 8 flatbreads)
- First time this series bakers receive a written recipe. Most had never made or eaten a flatbread.
- Recipe steps: make dough → rest → weigh 8 × 100g balls → fill and shape into rounds → fry.
- The challenge was a disaster: every batch came out undercooked or wrongly stuffed. Phil/Nigel-style line: "Probably the worst technical I've ever seen in 17 years." Joke rewrite: "flatbreads" became "fatbreads" / "pizzas."
- Final blind ranking (worst to first): Gary (9th), [8th undisclosed], Molly (7th), Mo (6th), Connie (5th), Tom (4th), Moyin (3rd), [2nd], and 1st to the best-looking but still raw batch. Bakers ruefully said the win didn't really put them "back in the safe zone."

## Showstopper — Celebratory Enriched Bread Display (large centrepiece + smaller breads + celebratory theme; 4.5 hr today, plus overnight cold prove)
- Molly — Harvest Festival basket: mini pumpkin brioche (pumpkin purée + winter spices, 113 g butter) and mini chocolate babka knots. Judges say it's too dry, the spice mix is odd in bread, the pumpkin gives colour but no flavour; basket looks great to eat but reading is "austere."
