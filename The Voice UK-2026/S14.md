## S14E06

# The Voice UK S14E06 — Final Round of Blind Auditions

## Plot

This is the last day of Blind Auditions. Going in, the four coaches have eight total spots: will.i.am has two left and feels "fulfilled"; Tom & Danny each have two; Kelly has one. The episode runs eight auditions, and all teams are filled to 10 by the end, leaving 32 acts heading into the Battles.

## Coach Status (Before / After)

- will.i.am: 2 left → full (19 humans, including 4 groups after picking up Bella Popera). Confident, boasts "my team now is the bomb dot com."
- Tom Jones + Danny Jones (joint McFly panel): 2 each → full. Tom says he is "as full as an egg."
- Kelly Rowland: 1 left → full with her last pick, Jessica Beth.

## Contestants and Key Beats

**Regan Preece (18, Swansea / South Wales).** Mum "Shelly" was on The Voice in 2015 and made it to the Knockouts. Regan plays rugby, calls his mother "Mammy," and sings "Something in the Orange" (Zach Bryan). Tom and Danny duet "Tennessee Whiskey" with him on stage. will.i.am turns, pitches Regan on Team Will; Tom plays the Welsh card ("you're a Welsh singer… and so am I"). Regan picks Tom and Danny. Danny jokes the Welsh connection means Regan can never walk the streets of Wales again (mobster accent). Regan joins Team Tom & Danny. Family nickname "Reegs" used throughout.

**Nikko (33, Nottingham).** Filipino-British, moved to the UK at age 9; brother-in-law taught him to rap. Married the day before the audition. Performs an original called "Check the Temperature" with a Tagalog rap verse. No chair turns. will.i.am later says he considered "Filipino" the password that would have made him push, but he already has rappers and "timing is everything"; he name-drops apl.de.ap (Black Eyed Peas) as his Philippines connection. Eliminated.

**Turah Sadiq (21, Manchester).** Student and member of a gospel Christian worship collective; performs "When You Believe." Kelly (only) turns. will.i.am says he "almost turned" but felt Turah was "overcooking it"; Danny adds the nerves will be "easily fixed with Kelly." Turah joins Team Kelly Rowland.

**Bella Popera — trio of Elanor, Keira, Julia (together six months).** Pop/opera mashup of Verdi's "La donna è mobile" and Billie Eilish's "Birds of a Feather." Only will.i.am turns. will.i.am says he "always turn[s] for opera" and praises the fuse. The panel teases Danny, who also trained in opera, for not turning. Joins Team Will. Will's team fills up immediately after.

**Robbie McMinn (19, Carrickfergus, Northern Ireland).** Singer-songwriter who began in musical theatre; plays a Lowden guitar (made in Belfast). Has non-epileptic attack disorder, says songwriting is the one thing that helps. Sings an original addressed to his sister ("If she gives you her heart…"). Only Tom turns. Tom notes the Irish lilt in his voice. Joins Team Tom & Danny.

**Jessica Beth (English teacher, Watford).** Trained at the Royal Academy of Music. Watford hometown link with Kelly Rowland. Performs Chappell Roan's "Pink Pony Club" with operatic flourishes. Kelly turns; "You complete me… You complete, yes… I'm your last act?" — confirms Jessica fills Kelly's final spot. Joins Team Kelly.

**Jeanette Akua (33, Buckinghamshire).** Business development director. Auditioned for X Factor in 2013, was put in a girl band that reached the live shows, and was told she was "not good enough on your own." Performs "All Along It Was a Fever" / "The Reason I Hold On" medley (Paloma Faith). No chair turns. The coaches visibly agonize, saying they would have turned if they had two spots. Eliminated. After her, only one seat (Tom & Danny) is left.

**Adam Tucker (30, King's Lynn, Norfolk).** Revealed mid-audition to have been the singing voice of Robbie Williams in the film Better Man. Performs "Rock DJ" / "Let Me Entertain You" style Robbie Williams material. Tom & Danny turn using their very last spot. Adam says he wants "to be the voice of Adam Tucker," not an impersonator. Joins Team Tom & Danny. All four teams now full.

## S14E07

# The Voice UK — Battles Episode (S14)

## Plot
The Battles phase opens. All four coaches' teams of eight are full; each coach has split their team into four pairs, assigned a song to each pair, and with the help of a guest mentor must choose three of eight acts to send to the semi-final. This episode covers two teams in full: **Team will.i.am** and **Team Tom Jones**.

## Team will.i.am (guest mentor: Cheryl)
will.i.am has wanted Cheryl as a mentor for thirteen years; she is introduced as a multi-platinum solo artist and Girls Aloud superstar with nine UK number-one singles and four number-one albums. After the four pairings perform, will.i.am reveals a three of his own — then announces he'll "love you and leave you" so Cheryl can deliver the news.

Battles and outcomes:
- **Sonna vs Teodora** — "Bad Dreams" by Teddy Swims. Both are praised (Sonna compared to Leona Lewis); will.i.am admits the pairing left him more confused, not less. Verdict: **Sonna through**.
- **Diogo vs Triple Threat** — drum 'n' bass / soul mash-up of "A Bar Song (Tipsy)" by Shaboozey with Kwabs's "Walk". Diogo stepped outside his comfort zone; Triple Threat brings the verses. will.i.am calls them "Quadruple Threat" as a joke. Verdict: **Diogo through**.
- **BMG vs Nana** — "Suavemente" (Elvis Crespo). BMG represent Africa with dance-Afrobeats fusion; Nana is described as Afro-Latina and as having no equivalent anywhere in the show's history. Verdict: **Nana through** (will.i.am's "first choice").
- **Ardu vs Bella Popera** — "Home" by Edward Sharpe and the Magnetic Zeros. A cappella wall of sound meets opera trio. will.i.am calls it "a piece of magic together" but questions what each group can do separately. Verdict: **neither through**.

Team Will's semi-finalists: Sonna, Diogo, Nana.

## Team Tom Jones (guest mentor: Paloma Faith)
Paloma Faith is reintroduced as a former Voice coach with a platinum single, number-one album, and a BRIT Award. Tom confirms he got married a few weeks earlier and walked out to Chuck Berry's "You Never Can Tell."

Battles and outcomes:
- **Vade vs Harry** — "End of the Road" by Boyz II Men. Tom turned last-second for Vade in the Blinds; Harry's falsetto was so strong it "sounded like two people." Verdict: **neither through** — Tom says they were evenly matched despite five-versus-one.
- **Amalia vs Robbie** — "Good Luck, Babe!" by Chappell Roan. The youngest pair. Verdict: **Amalia through**.
- **Cate vs Eric & Jensen** — "You Never Can Tell" by Chuck Berry. Cate reveals she is registered blind (sees only outer edges). Verdict: **Cate through**.
- **Molly J. vs Dakari** — "Against All Odds" by Phil Collins. Tom's last slot. Verdict: **Molly J. through**.

Team Tom's semi-finalists: Amalia, Cate, Molly J.

## Personal details and character beats
- **Vicky** (of Bella Popera) had her baby and is cheering from home.
- **Cate** is registered blind.
- **Harry's** falsetto is repeatedly singled out.
- **Robbie** recalls his lips quivering when Tom introduced himself.
- **will.i.am** says, "I wanna do something I've never done before — YOU tell them," then immediately backtracks ("Just kidding").
- Promo voiceover from **Emma Willis** pushes applications; a £120,000 prize is teased as the show's biggest-ever.

## Continuity / new terms
- "Malice At The Palace" (vs. "Rumble In The Jungle") — will.i.am's framing of the round as a fight.
- Battle round is back after S11–S13's "Callbacks" format (per series context); Tom literally says "The Battles are back."
- New "either/or/both" rule referenced: "You don't have to pick one or the other."
- Guest mentors are a standard mechanic this series (used across both teams here).
- Cheryl's appearance as guest mentor previews her S15 coaching role; Paloma Faith returns in a mentor capacity rather than as a judge (she was S5 judge).
- will.i.am continues from S1; Sir Tom Jones from S1/S6 onward.

## Jokes / cultural references
- "Rumble In The Jungle" (1974 Ali vs Foreman) → "Malice At The Palace" (2004 NBA brawl).
- "Smashed it" / "Quadruple Threat" / "That's the new lit!" — studio patter.
- "Don't overdo it. And don't underbake it." — will.i.am to the a cappella/opera pairing.
- "If we get close enough, we might live another 20 years" — Tom joking about the youthful pair.
- "I would say his voice has the more star quality" — Paloma on Dakari, vs. Tom picking Molly.

## Unresolved threads / preview
- Eliminated in this episode: Teodora, Triple Threat, BMG, Ardu, Bella Popera, Vade, Harry, Robbie, Eric & Jensen, Dakari — final placements TBD.
- Vicky's newborn / Cate's visual impairment / Tom's recent marriage — running personal threads.
- Promo at episode close: "Next time, the Battles return. **Team Tom & Danny** and **Team Kelly**," with **superstar guest mentors** yet to be revealed. Danny Jones previews "two teams singing for survival"; Tom Fletcher previews "now the real competition begins."
