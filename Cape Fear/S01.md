## S01E10

- Episode plot
  - Max Cady escapes custody during Hurricane Wesley and returns to the Bowden home, taking Anna, Tom, Natalie, and Zack hostage.
  - He forces the family into a grotesque mock trial, casting Anna and Tom as defendants and the children as his “jury.”
  - Anna admits that she and Tom manipulated Max’s trial: they concealed a deadlocked jury and pushed him toward a harsh sentence because she feared he had killed Melissa and feared Natalie might be his daughter.
  - Max drugs Zack and threatens to make the family participate in his ritualized “trial.” Anna and Tom manage to break free when Max becomes overwhelmed by revelations about Luke.
  - Tom refuses to kill Max, choosing to leave him for the authorities. Police search the house but cannot find him.
  - In the closing sequence, Max appears to have opened the “Melissa & Adam” restaurant. Natalie works there and narrates that she once loved Max, suggesting either continued manipulation, Stockholm-like attachment, or a deliberately ambiguous new alliance.

- Important character actions
  - Anna confronts Max publicly with Nevaeh’s recording, exposing his plan to have Nevaeh kill her; Max dismisses the recording as fabricated.
  - Anna finally confesses her role in Max’s conviction and acknowledges that she betrayed him because she believed he was dangerous and wanted to protect Natalie.
  - Tom admits he loved Anna during Max’s trial but says they did not sleep together during the proceedings.
  - Nevaeh confirms that Max ordered her to break into the Bowden house, drug the tea, seduce Luke, mutilate Zack, and eventually kill Anna.
  - Luke apologizes to Nevaeh before being shot; Max later claims Luke is his son, though Luke’s fate and parentage remain uncertain.
  - Natalie tries to save Max and later helps free her family, but the final restaurant scene leaves her relationship with him unclear.
  - Tom rejects Anna’s suggestion that he kill Max, insisting that Max should face prison instead.

- Newly established names and terms
  - Luke: a young man connected to Nevaeh and Max; Max claims he is his son with Melissa.
  - Melissa & Adam: Max’s new restaurant, named for his dead wife and son.
  - Hurricane Wesley: the Category 5 storm that triggers Savannah’s evacuation and enables Max’s escape.
  - Amy Brancato: the woman Max allegedly manipulated into killing Melissa; she confessed, but Anna insists Max himself killed Melissa.
  - Judge Levine: the judge who reconsidered Tom’s bail motion.
  - Noa: the source of information about Luke’s birth certificate.
  - Shangó: an Orisha associated here with justice, thunder, and lightning; Max invokes him during the mock trial.
  - “The People versus Anna Deveraux and Tom Bowden”: Max’s imagined courtroom framing of the family’s reckoning.

- Continuity-relevant facts
  - Anna and Tom knowingly helped secure Max’s long imprisonment despite the possibility of an acquittal.
  - Anna’s central motive was protecting Natalie from discovering that Max might be her biological father.
  - Max claims Crystal could not have children because of injuries from a car accident.
  - A home-birth certificate lists Crystal as Luke’s mother, while Anna argues that Crystal may have stolen Luke after killing Melissa.
  - Max’s belief that Luke is his son destabilizes his account of Melissa, Adam, Crystal, and his own past.
  - Max is still at large at the end of the main action; the police find no trace of him in the Bowden house.
  - Tom and Anna’s family survives the hostage situation, but their future remains shaped by the exposed legal and marital betrayals.

- Jokes and references useful later
  - The mock trial parodies courtroom procedure: objections, hostile witnesses, jury duty, oaths, verdicts, and “directed verdict.”
  - Max repeatedly turns legal language into personal theater, including the line that the Bowdens need to remain alive because he needs his “jury.”
  - The police find only “a very angry possum” under the porch, briefly puncturing the otherwise severe search scene.
  - Tom’s hook joke about his injured hand and the family’s final cheeseburger assembly line provide rare moments of dark domestic humor.
  - The restaurant’s “no cheese, no lettuce, no bun” order echoes the episode’s broader theme of people stripping away appearances and confronting what remains.

- Unresolved threads
  - Is Max truly alive and operating the restaurant, or is the ending filtered through Natalie’s unreliable perspective?
  - What exactly happened to Luke, and is he genuinely Max and Melissa’s child?
  - Did Crystal kill Melissa and take Luke, or is Anna’s theory another incomplete reconstruction?
  - What happened to Robert, Crystal, and the bodies connected to the river and boat?
  - Will Max be recaptured, and what criminal charges will follow the hostage-taking, assault, escape, drugging, and attempted killings?
  - Is Natalie still under Max’s influence, willingly aligned with him, or planning another attempt to expose him?

## S01E01

## S01E01 Summary

### Plot

Anna Bowden is profiled at home for her work at the Savannah Justice League Project (SJLP), which helps wrongly convicted people get out of prison. She refuses to discuss the Max Cady case during the interview. That same week, Max Cady's mistress kills herself and leaves a confession admitting she murdered Max's pregnant wife — not Max. Max has served 17 years in prison and is released on parole, which the news covers as a massive scandal, accusing Anna (who was his lawyer) and Tom (the prosecutor who later became her husband) of conspiring to put an innocent man away.

Tom and Anna learn that Byron French, an exoneree SJLP is showcasing at a major museum fundraiser, has disappeared. Meanwhile, their son Zack is acting erratically — he sneaks out before dawn to play video games, is transferred to a new school (Glenhurst), and will not answer his phone. Their daughter Natalie is harassed by a true-crime podcaster asking whether her mother and stepfather had an affair during the Max Cady trial.

At the fundraiser, Max Cady shows up uninvited and takes the stage. He delivers a charismatic, menacing speech saying he blames no one for his imprisonment — not the judge, not the prosecutor, not his lawyer. He says he "died 6,222 times" in prison, references his unborn son Adam Cady, and talks about "death by a thousand cuts." Afterward, Tom confronts Max privately. Max shows off prison scars and a metal plate in his head, then claims he wants to help the organization. Anna accidentally cuts herself on something, and Max lingers near the family.

Later that night, Tom finds Max Cady standing in their backyard next to a dead skunk. Max says he saw their son Zack outside earlier and suggests Zack might be on drugs. Zack returns home severely disoriented, high, and bleeding. The episode ends with the discovery that one of Zack's toes has been cut off, and Anna screams for someone to call 911.

### Important Character Actions

- **Anna** — tries to control the interview narrative, refuses to discuss Cady, cuts herself at the fundraiser (accidentally or otherwise), reassures herself that the trial "brought her Tom and Natalie."
- **Tom** — former prosecutor turned defense lawyer; confronts Max at the fundraiser, tries to locate Zack all evening, finds Max in his backyard.
- **Max Cady** — calls the SJLP office to announce he'll attend the fundraiser, delivers a public speech framing himself as a symbol of injustice, visits the Bowden home uninvited, plants the dead skunk in the yard, hints that Zack is on drugs.
- **Natalie** — deflects the podcaster's invasive questions about her mother's affair, mentions Zack may be with an online girlfriend.
- **Zack** — sneaks out before dawn, spends the day away, returns severely impaired with a missing toe.

### Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Byron French** — exoneree featured at the museum fundraiser; disappears the day of the event.
- **Hope & Kyle** — SJLP interns; Hope answers the office phone and inadvertently tells Max Cady about the fundraiser.
- **Noa** — SJLP colleague who coordinates Byron and handles press outreach.
- **Louis Pilgrim** — host of the true-crime podcast *Murder-Happy Characters*; harasses Natalie with trial-era rumors.
- **Glenhurst** — the school Zack is being transferred to for a "fresh start."
- **Adam Cady** — Max's unborn son, who would be a teenager if he had been born; central to Max's grief and identity.
- **Angola Swamp** — location where Max's wife's body was dumped.
- **"Death by a thousand cuts"** — Max's metaphor for a life sentence, echoed in the episode's final line about Zack's missing toe.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Anna was Max Cady's defense lawyer when she was pregnant with Natalie (she didn't know yet). She went into labor on the day of Max's sentencing; Tom, the opposing counsel, ran across the courtroom to help her, and she fell in love with him. Paul (Natalie's biological father) was Anna's fiancé at the time.
- Max pleaded guilty on Anna's advice to avoid the death penalty, but Judge Jackson sentenced him to life anyway.
- Max was in a coma for six weeks in prison and claims to have seen his wife and son in the darkness.
- The security system in the Bowden home malfunctions repeatedly, triggering false alarms.
- Max's mistress's suicide note and the knife are key pieces of evidence that got him released.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- Max's speech about "death by a thousand cuts" — fingers and toes, one by one — directly foreshadows the mutilation of Zack.
- "If I got royalties off being hated, I'd be as rich as sin."
- The podcaster's question about whether Anna and Tom "fucking during the trial" — likely a recurring source of public humiliation for the family.
- Natalie's line: "You used to put poor people in jail and now you just help rich people stay out" — hints at Tom's moral evolution and possible hypocrisy.

### Unresolved Threads

- Who drugged and mutilated Zack? Max implies he was "on something," but it's unclear whether Max administered the drug or if it was someone else (the online girlfriend Natalie mentioned?).
- Where is Byron French? Did Max have something to do with his disappearance?
- What did Max mean by "I saw him out front and assumed that was your son"? Was he inside the Bowden property?
- The dead skunk in the pool at the start and the dead skunk in the yard at the end — coincidence or Max's signature?
- Will Anna be able to prove Max is dangerous, or has his public speech made him untouchable as a symbol of injustice?
- What is the true nature of Natalie's biological father Paul, and how will that secret resurface?

## S01E03 Summary

### Plot

The episode opens with a pool trespassing incident: Trish and a friend enter the Bowden property, claiming "Tommy" invited them. Tom confronts and ejects them, noting the danger in a "Stand Your Ground" state. Meanwhile, Anna is obsessively monitoring Zack's phone via a mirror device, convinced the "AngelX" account catfishing him is Max Cady.

Anna consults a colleague at SJLP about Max. Though warned that targeting an exonerated man violates the organization's mission, Anna insists her family is at stake. The colleague advises her to "keep your friends close and your enemies closer." Anna then confronts Nevaeh Valentine about Zack's missing toe. Nevaeh claims Zack mutilated himself after returning home. Anna forces Nevaeh to text Zack a breakup and threatens to have her charged as an adult for drugging a minor.

A documentary crew led by Noa and cameraman Dusty arrives to film Max, whose viral interview has generated massive traffic for a three-part series. Zack invites them inside while Anna is in the shower. Furious but unable to stop it, Anna agrees to let Max and the crew accompany her on the drive to Atlanta to settle the Centurion prison lawsuit — a tropical storm is approaching Jacksonville, so they road-trip together.

At the negotiation, Centurion (represented by Coburn) offers $1.1 million. Max refuses, demanding an internal investigation and public apology after prison staff watched a white supremacist gang split his skull. Anna threatens to litigate publicly with the documentary crew filming every moment, and the settlement closes at $4.2 million.

The storm forces them to stop at a budget hotel in Macon. Anna and Max share a drink. Max recalls their first meeting, when she believed in an innocent man. He asks what changed; she insists nothing did. He quotes her own words back to her — "It's never too late to fix what we broke" — and she admits she might have given bad advice, saying that if she could do it again, she wouldn't have told him to take the plea deal.

Back home, Tom works late with Lexi; they almost cross a line but pull back. At a party, Natalie wears a vintage '90s dress she found in Anna's closet. A drone photo of Natalie is sent to everyone present; Nevaeh notes the violation, comparing it to when Zack circulated her sister's pictures.

The episode closes with Max singing a menacing, repetitive song — "What do they know about love, my dear?" punctuated by the "jingle of a dog's collar" — as Anna calls Zack to check in, her voice tight with unease.

- **Max Cady** — inserts himself into the Bowden home with a documentary crew, negotiates aggressively for the settlement, sings a threatening song, reopens old emotional wounds with Anna
