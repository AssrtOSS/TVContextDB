## S18E03

## Episode Plot

Four pitches: **Pup Stack** (portable weighted dog tether), **Nutcase Milk** (cashew-based chocolate milk, with Ninja as co-founder), **ZALT** (electrolyte+B-vitamin oral pouch), and **The UnderWarmer** (disposable heated vest). Guest Shark **Steven Bartlett** debuts; **Kendra Scott** sits as a guest panelist alongside **Kevin O'Leary**, **Lori Greiner**, and **Daniel Lubetzky**.

**Pup Stack** — Brian Zibricky and his daughter Kayla ask **$150K for 10%**. 20-ft rubber-coated tether with 360° rotation, built-in storage, expandable up to 40 lb. D2C, ~100% Meta-driven. 2026 YTD ~$260K, projecting $700K (56% YoY). Base $124.99 / COGS ~$24 / ship $21; 61% gross, 41% all-up margin. Origin: dog Cooper after Brian's wife was diagnosed with MS in 2014; Kickstarter 2022. Kendra opens at $150K/25% ("cuckoo crazy valuation"). Steven counters **$200K for 20%**, citing Meta-dependence risk and global expansion potential. Lori and Kendra both lobby to join Steven; Steven defers to the entrepreneurs. **Deal: Steven Bartlett + Kendra Scott, $200K for 20%.**

**Nutcase Milk** — Jo (Las Vegas NV) asks **$250K for 5%** ($5M valuation). Cashew-based nostalgic chocolate milk, tagline "make milk dope again," hero SKU "Chocolate Chaos." 12-pack COGS $15 (dropping to $12 at scale), retail $35 + $5 ship; CAC $58, LTV $190. Last year $70K, YTD $65,070; raised $1.8M at $10M last round; YTD net loss $300K; $200K bank, $200K inventory. Ninja (Tyler Blevins) revealed as co-founder on stage. Sharks object to valuation, weak protein, confused Nesquik-vs-performance positioning. **All five Sharks out.** Steven's parting note: "they tried to do all, and therefore none."

**ZALT** — Sam Mitchell (San Diego) and Dominic (West Palm Beach) ask **$150K for 5%**. Electrolyte + B-vitamin oral pouch (nicotine-pouch format without nicotine), 372 mg actives per pouch (sea salt, K, B6, B12), up to 1-hr wear. Launched Jan 12 of this year; $400K lifetime sales; YTD loss $50K. COGS $1.90, retail $9.99; 80% gross / 55% landed. Meta + Google Ads heavy. Started as a nicotine-replacement play, pivoted to hydration. Kendra and Steven out on parental/warning-label concerns about kids; Steven also says the format "makes him look like a rabbit." Kevin offers **$150K/15%**, entrepreneurs counter 10%, Kevin settles at **$150K for 12.5%**. **Deal with Kevin O'Leary.**

**The UnderWarmer** — Bryan (Kansas City) and Paul (St. Louis), brothers, ask **$100K for 15%**. Disposable, compression-fitted, air-activated heated vest with 8 heat packs (4 front, 4 back), 12 hr warmth. Landed $4.25 / wholesale $6.75 / retail $13.99. Started 2017, $1.1M+ lifetime, $268K last year; intentional side hustle, no debt, day jobs kept. Bryan: 25-yr painter/graphic designer; Paul: 25+ yrs in sales. 60% repeat rate, $3 CAC. Kendra raises landfill concern (defenders cite diapers, coffee pods). Kevin calls it a "nothing burger" / "poo-poo, pee-pee," out. Steven out (hereditary cold issue, but sees more negatives than positives). Daniel out (not exciting enough). Kendra offers **$100K for 25% + $2/unit royalty** (her "pull a Mr. Wonderful" move); entrepreneurs push back; royalty dropped, equity lands at **20%. Deal with Kendra Scott.**

## S18E04

# Shark Tank S18E04 — Summary

## Plot
Four entrepreneurs pitch a six-Shark panel that includes newcomer Mindy Kaling (guest Shark alongside regulars Lori Greiner, Robert Herjavec, Kevin O'Leary, and Kendra Scott). Three of four pitches close with deals; one is a four-way bidding war; one ends in Mindy's first Tank deal.

## Pitches and outcomes

### 1. Polygons — Rahul Agarwal (New Jersey; from India)
- Product: flat 3-in-1 magnetic measuring spoons (tablespoon, teaspoon, ½ tsp, ¼ tsp); leakproof, lab-grade precise; extra prototypes for measuring cups, funnels, flattened power strips.
- Ask: $350,000 for 7%. Lifetime sales $4M; $1.25M last year. Landed cost ~$7; retails $25–$29. Blended CAC ~$7.50. Profit ~$210K after $40K reinvestment; founder pays himself ~$500/month.
- Retail traction: already in MoMA, in talks with Williams Sonoma. Originally from India; recently relocated.
- Sharks out: Kendra Scott (valuation education problem vs. $1 competitors); Robert Herjavec (won't beat Mindy+Lori).
- Bidding war: Kevin O'Leary opened at $350K/15%, dropped to 10% to match. Mindy Kaling + Lori Greiner countered $350K/10% + $500K line of credit.
- Deal closed: **Mindy + Lori, $350K for 10% (8% + 2% advisory shares) plus $500K line of credit.** Rahul's first-ever Shark Tank deal.

### 2. Sunday Club — Ira Blossom (NYC; full-time Google UX lead, nights-and-weekends project)
- Product: monthly newsprint puzzle newspaper (crosswords, mini, sudoku, word search, comics, cover search-and-find); analog "anti-doomscroll" play. Subscriptions annual or quarterly.
- Ask: $150,000 for 5%. Single issue $7.99 + $1.99 shipping. ~5,200 subscribers; $800K revenue in year one; 23.6% margin. Just-announced spin-off **Sunday Club Kids**.
- Sharks out: Robert (digital guy, no faith in declining newspaper market); Lori (admires it but won't be the one to sell it).
- Offers: Kendra Scott $150K/20%; Kevin O'Leary matched Kendra's $150K/20%.
- Deal closed: **Kendra Scott, $150K for 20%.** Ira's first Tank deal.

### 3. Bye Bye Button — Adam Barnes and Dan Lamb
- Product: Demo includes "honey is bee vomit" / "Susan 'Bee' Anthony" bad-date skit.
- Ask: **$25,000 for 10%** (low ask flagged as suspicious). MSRP $34.99; current cost $10.83 (off-the-shelf fob case), drops to ~$4.83 once new overseas partner ramps up. ~$20K lifetime sales since July launch.
- Sharks out: Robert (first, citing low ask + minimal traction); Kendra Scott (calls it "a plot point to one of my sitcoms, but not a viable business").
- Bidding war: Kevin offered $25K for 50% with first $25K profit kicked back; Mindy countered $25K for 25% plus $2.25/unit royalty until $100K recouped; Lori briefly jumped to 49%.
- Deal closed: **Kevin + Mindy, $25K for 25% with $2.25/unit royalty until recoup is halved to $50K.**

### 4. Loli-Med — Crystal (San Diego, from New Orleans) and Ekhlas Naoum (Denver, from Iraq)
- Product: patent-pending lollipop-shaped medication dispenser — parents load a standard oral syringe, lock it inside an edible lollipop head; child sucks the dose through. Replaceable heads (12-pack planned); extensions to ice cream, chocolate, gummy bear.
- Ask: $75,000 for 10%. Launched December 2025; $92K sales, $66K profit; channels: DTC website + independent pharmacies.
- Economics: landed cost 76¢; retail $12.99, wholesale $5.99. Both founders are licensed pharmacists; Ekhlas is selling her own pharmacy to go all-in on Loli-Med.
- Three-way bidding: Robert + Mindy + Lori offered $150K/15% (Mindy: "we have three kids" pitch for socials); Kendra matched at $150K/10%.
- Founders asked for all four Sharks, then kept hesitating; Kevin went out ("too many cooks"); Mindy self-departed; Lori restructured to solo.
- Deal closed: **Lori Greiner, $150K for 10%.**

## S18E07

# Shark Tank S18E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
Season 18, Episode 7 features four entrepreneur pitches with three deals closed. Guest Shark **Rashaun Williams** joins the panel (Kevin O'Leary, Daymond John, Barbara Corcoran, Lori Greiner). Lori Greiner marks her **300th Tank deal** with a commemorative crown.

---

## Pitch 1: Hoop Trailer ✅ **DEAL**
**Entrepreneurs:** Scott & Gabby (San Diego, CA)  
**Ask:** $250K for 5% ($5M valuation)  
**Business:** Mobile arcade basketball trailer franchise (Hoop Trailer) + new Touchdown Trailer (football). Licensing model: operators buy trailer ($60K) + $250/mo licensing fee for exclusive 50-mile territory. Patent-pending custom trailer design.

**Key Metrics:** 77 trailers sold in 15 months, $5M contracts, $1M profit (47% margin on trailer sales). Operators avg $3–5K/mo profit.

**Shark Dynamics:**
- **Kevin O'Leary:** Two offers — (A) $250K/20% equity, or (B) $250K/10% + profit distributions until $250K recouped in 3 yrs. Called entrepreneurs' counter "greedy."
- **Barbara Corcoran:** $250K/15% straight equity; emphasized building operator "family" like Cousins Maine Lobster.
- **Rashaun Williams:** $250K/10% + profit distributions until recoup over 3 yrs. Positioned as sports-industry insider who brings community.
- **Lori Greiner:** Out (not right business for her).

**Outcome:** Entrepreneurs countered Rashaun at 7.5% with payback; he held at 10%. **Deal closed with Rashaun at $250K/10% + profit distributions until recoup.**

**New Terms/Names:** "Hoop operator" network model; Touchdown Trailer (football variant); perpetuity/royalty distribution structure.

---

## Pitch 2: Real Men Apparel Company ❌ **NO DEAL**
**Entrepreneur:** Jared Mortensen (Haysville, KS)  
**Ask:** $700K for 7% ($10M valuation)  
**Product:** Men's underwear with **Ball Metric System** — four pouch sizes (A=pool ball, B=tennis ball, C=pickleball, D=softball; 15% of orders). External hammock-style pouch. Best Fit Quiz for sizing.

**Key Metrics:** $1M sales *last month*; $8.5M revenue '25, $450K net (6% margin); $5M YTD '26. CAC ~$11. Bootstrapped (founder + wife, $160K life savings). Aerospace finance background → pet toy co → learned Amazon.

**Shark Reactions:**
- **Kevin O'Leary:** Out — has Bombas investment; would cannibalize.
- **Daymond John:** Out — loves business but can't invest in underwear category.
- **Lori Greiner:** Out — valuation not exciting.
- **Rashaun Williams:** Out — valuation fair but $700K ask too high; would've done $350K.
- **Barbara Corcoran:** Out — same valuation concern.

**Outcome:** All out. Entrepreneur insists valuation justified; Sharks say ask scared them off.

**Jokes/References:** "Twig to berry ratio" (Lori); "Z size = talk to your doctor, probably a tumor"; Kevin's "I'm not a bank, I sell rocket fuel" callback.

---

## Pitch 3: DadNose ❌ **NO DEAL**
**Entrepreneur:** Tristan Murray (Louisville, KY) — pharmacist by day, dad of 4 by night  
**Ask:** $30K for 10% ($300K valuation)  
**Product:** Scented nose cover (capsule + foam nose) for diaper changes. Three scents: milled cedar, "cup of joe," wife's candle (cologne). Reusable ~100+ changes.

**Key Metrics:** Lifetime sales <$40K since '21; declining yearly ($16.5K → $8K → $6K). $68K invested. COGS $3.25, retail $19 (single) / $39 (box).

Cited wife's Birthday Girl company (mail list → free birthday gift → 20% convert to subscription) as model. "100X revenue possible."

**Outcome:** No offers. Entrepreneur plans to use Rashaun's advice.
