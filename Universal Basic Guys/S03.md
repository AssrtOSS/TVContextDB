## S03E01

# Universal Basic Guys S03E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Tammy discovers a passion for painting at a "Sip n' Paint" class, where the teacher praises her "untapped talent" (her flower paintings resemble vaginas). Meanwhile, Mark and Hank's mother **Rhoda** arrives unannounced after **5 years away**. Mark, dreading her criticism, lies that the guest room is being converted into Tammy's art studio — forcing Rhoda to stay with Hank. Rhoda immediately "improves" Hank's sparse apartment, discarding his belongings including his beloved plant **Grassie**.

Mark learns neighbor **David** has been creating disturbing paintings while blackout drunk. He schemes to have David paint a commissioned mural for Tammy at the **Glantontown Arts District** festival by dressing David as Tammy. Rhoda challenges Tammy to paint sober; Tammy accepts. At the live mural event, David (in drag) is exposed. Tammy learns the "drunk paintings" were David's, not hers. Furious, she channels raw emotion into a single flower painting labeled "I'm mad" — which critics hail as brilliant. Rhoda admits she lost money on *Family Feud* slots and was kicked out by her roommate Ruth. Mark asks Rhoda not to live with him; Tammy overrides him and invites Rhoda to stay indefinitely. David sells the remaining "Tammy" paintings; Hank receives a replacement plant, **Grassie 2**.

## Important Character Actions
- **Tammy**: Starts painting, gets gallery show, paints mural sober, creates authentic art from genuine anger
- **Mark**: Manages mother's visit via lies, orchestrates David-as-Tammy scheme, finally tells Tammy the truth
- **Rhoda**: Returns after 5 years, manipulative but insightful, pushes Tammy to paint sober, moves in with Mark/Tammy
- **Hank**: Loses Grassie and minimal possessions to Rhoda's makeover; gets Grassie 2
- **David**: Paints dark art while drunk, cross-dresses as Tammy for mural scheme ("method acting")
- **Andrea**: Dismisses David's work as "not real art"

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Sip n' Paint** — wine-and-painting class
- **Rhoda Hoagies** — Mark and Hank's mother (first appearance; calls Mark "Marcus," Hank "Henry")
- **Grassie / Grassie 2** — Hank's blade-of-grass plant
- **"Violent Femme: The Paintings of Tammy Hoagies"** — gallery exhibition title
- **Glantontown Arts District** — local arts district commissioning the mural
- **Ruth** — Rhoda's roommate who evicted her
- **Family Feud slots** — gambling machine Rhoda lost money on

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Rhoda absent for 5 years; Mark calls her "Mom," she calls him "Marcus"
- Tammy is a nurse; Andrea is a Humantis executive
- Mark spent a full UBI check ($3,000) on art supplies for the fake studio
- Hank's minimalist lifestyle: 3 forks, 1 knife, 2 spoons, 3 chairs, 1 plant
- UBI premise continues ($3,000/month per resident)
- Setting: Glantontown, South New Jersey

- David's buyer for the remaining paintings

## S03E03

# Universal Basic Guys S03E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
Tammy drops Darren off at Sleepway Camp for two months, smothering him with first-aid gear (backup EpiPens, bee suit, bear horn) and secretly stashing AirTags in all his shorts. Mark and Hank duck out to go magnet fishing off the camp pier, chasing clout on a magnet-fishing Subreddit where a rival user, DredgeLord420, keeps out-hauling them. The Jersey Devil — a rebranded "chill S'more Lord" and camp legend — dares Darren to abandon the pool for the lake, so Darren signs up for Junior Lake Lifeguard training under the brutal Head Lifeguard Chase Waters, who preaches ruthless threat elimination ("You see a threat, you eliminate it"). Mark hooks a gun; Tammy declares it crime evidence, but Mark drops it back into the lake to grab photos for Reddit and then spots bullet casings near the lake guard shed. Tammy's snooping blows up a rescue drill ("Get off my baby!"), humiliating Darren; Chase kicks her off the beach and Darren doubles down on Chase's creed, accepting an after-dark "big boy job" at the South Bank shed. There, Chase reveals he is the legendary "Bog Kid" of Summer of '92 and intends to kill the Jersey Devil; Mark refuses ("That's my friend"). Mark and Hank return with a commercial-grade 17,000-pound magnet ("Bruteboss") and end up driving their SUV into the lake; campers report the drowning, and Darren — after Tammy admits he made the adult decision by calling her — performs the rescue. Tammy lets Darren choose his own summer; he stays at camp but switches to the pool. Mark is hired as a lake lifeguard despite not being able to swim, his gun post is deleted for "violating the spirit of magnet fishing," and Hank's completed 2012 Chunky soup collection becomes the Subreddit's top post of all time.

## Important Character Actions
- Tammy: overpacks Darren, hides AirTags in his shorts, snoops around camp, attacks a "drowning" camper mid-drill, publicly humiliates Darren, then reconciles and lets him choose pool over lake
- Mark: prioritizes Reddit clout over a crime scene, loses the gun and his SUV to the lake, refuses to let Chase murder JD, gets hired as a lifeguard who cannot swim
- Hank: completes the 2012 Chunky soup set (Sirloin Burger finale), rides along on the magnet scheme
- Darren: defects from pool to lake, embraces Chase's ruthless creed, rejects JD's tubing invite ("A lifeguard never leaves his post"), then rescues Mark and chooses a chill pool summer
- Chase Waters: runs a sadistic lifeguard program, gifts Darren a knife, exposes himself as the Bog Kid and sets out to kill the Jersey Devil
- Jersey Devil (JD): rebranded as "S'more Lord," dares Darren, gets magnet-clobbered (loses peripheral vision), offers Mark a lifeguard job

## S03E07

# Universal Basic Guys S03E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
Mark and Hank visit **Smokin' Birds**, a new BBQ joint owned by former Eagles linebacker **Hugh "T-Bone" Truckner**. Mark becomes obsessed with becoming Hugh's step-brother by setting up his mother **Rhoda** with Hugh's father **Earl**. Meanwhile, the **Glantontown Public Library** faces closure due to budget cuts, prompting David to organize a fundraiser with Rhoda's help. The two plots collide at the library fundraiser when Mark falsely claims Rhoda is dead to avoid a sandwich-naming ceremony, only for Rhoda to show up alive. Earl saves the library with a massive donation "in honor of his father's dead girlfriend."

## Important Character Actions
- **Mark**: Orchestrates "Operation: Step Brothers" (fake medical scare, hospital hack, makeover) to pair Rhoda with Earl; lies to Hugh that Rhoda died
- **Hank**: Reluctant accomplice to Mark's scheme
- **Rhoda**: Bonds with Earl at urologist; helps David with library fundraiser; learns to enlarge phone font; reveals she's vegetarian
- **Hugh "T-Bone" Truckner**: Protective of his dad; caters library fundraiser; bonds with Mark over Madden
- **Earl Truckner**: Author of *A New Leaf: My Journey Into Sustainable Gardening*; hits it off with Rhoda; donates to save library
- **David**: Leads library fundraiser; connects with Rhoda over tech help; gives rousing "community gardens for the mind" speech
- **Andrea**: Forbids David from seeing Rhoda, calling her a "home-wrecker" exploiting David's "eroticized dependency issues"
- **Tammy**: Pushes Rhoda to date Earl for the Eagles family discount

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Smokin' Birds** — Hugh's BBQ restaurant in Glantontown
- **The Rhodabega** — Sandwich named after Rhoda (planned naming ceremony)
- **Earl Truckner** — Hugh's father, author, vegetarian
- **Boingo Bingo** — Online game Rhoda plays at library computers
- **Operation: Step Brothers** — Mark's six-step matchmaking scheme

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Rhoda's tech illiteracy (multiple remotes, can't use GrubDash, needs printed emails) persists
- David's book *A New Leaf: My Journey Into Sustainable Gardening* is cataloged at the (now-closing) Glantontown Public Library
- Andrea's controlling dynamic with David ("reverse-Handmaid's Tale marriage") escalates
- Hugh's backstory: "nastiest linebacker in Eagles history," allegedly fractured a skull outside a club, dad has kidney stones
- Mark's "mentally challenged" / "slow" characterization reinforced by Rhoda and Hugh
- Library serves as only "semi-clean public bathroom within walking distance of Weglers"
