## S02E01

# Made in Korea S02E01 — Summary

## Episode Plot
A nine-year time jump lands the series in **October 1979**. Baek Ki-tae is now **KCIA Deputy Director**. President Park grumbles about his twenty-year grip on power while his two top lieutenants openly clash in front of him: **KCIA Director Cho**, who wants a softer touch against the spreading **Busan/Masan protests**, and **Presidential Security Chief Gwak**, who wants to roll tanks in. Park tells Ki-tae to monitor Cho and report back; privately he introduces Ki-tae to **Madam Shin**, a Japanese-speaking woman who comments on his Osaka-honed Japanese, and hints that Ki-tae is being groomed as the **next KCIA director**.

Ki-tae flies to Busan, where protests flare as his driver **Kihun** quietly briefs him on a meth pipeline — Taiwanese precursors, a Seoul lab run by **\"Changbae\" (Park Changbae)**, exports only to Japan. When Kihun suggests selling domestically, Ki-tae refuses: pushing drugs in Korea would be unpatriotic. In Busan, however, the drug has leaked onto the streets. KCIA Busan branch chief **Pyo Hak-su** (newly toupee'd) helps Ki-tae extract a Zainichi Korean detainee, **Professor Jang Gyeong-sik** (arrested on a trumped-up espionage charge), as a favor to yakuza contact **Choi Yu-ji / Ikeda Yūji**. Pyo also warns that **Prosecutor Oh Ye-jin**, now stationed at the Busan District Office, is a relentless drug-hunter and Geon-yeong's ideological heir.

Ye-jin goes undercover as a buyer, beats up the dealer's thugs, and flips him: the local supply traces to Changbae's Seoul lab. Cut to Changbae's lab — Ki-tae corners him with a gun over the Busan leak and a lecture on **\"shame\" as the one essential virtue**. Changbae swears innocence; Ki-tae tells him to **\"clean this up\"**.

A subplot tracks Ki-tae's brother **Baek Ki-hyun**, now a **lieutenant colonel** embedded inside General **Hwang Dae-sik's** Military Security Command (MSC). Ki-hyun reports that Hwang is recruiting academy colonels (drinks, golf) — including Colonel **Ahn Jae-ho** as his right hand. Ki-tae orders Ki-hyun to infiltrate Hwang's circle while pretending distance from it.

The episode then jumps to **Osaka**, where aging yakuza boss **Ikeda Osamu** announces his retirement and promises a successor vote. Yu-ji quietly recruits **Harimoto** (Tokyo ops chief and brother of the detained professor) by offering to spring Jang Gyeong-sik through Ki-tae's KCIA contacts. Ikeda dies of cardiac arrest that very night. At the funeral, a kumicho vote splits **Kimura Yoichi 10, Yūji 9** — Kimura wins. He insults her as a \"Korean mongrel whore\" and orders her ejected; a hammer cocks, then Yu-ji is shown standing as kumicho demanding submission, implying a violent coup.

The final sequence cross-cuts to the **Blue House**, October 26, 1979. Chief of Staff **Cheon Seok-joong** now pushes Park toward martial law; Park rants about Cambodia-style mass killings. Director Cho snaps that Cheon will be the first to die if he keeps talking. Cheon retorts: \"The good of our people should come first.\" A phone call pulls Ki-tae back to Seoul — Yu-ji has seized the clan, and Cho may be \"about to pull something tonight.\" Ki-tae is ordered to **Gungjeong-dong**. Gunfire erupts, the lights die, Cho's gun jams. The closing narration: on October 26, 1979, \"the kumicho, the boss, the president of South Korea, died.\"

## Important Character Actions
- **Baek Ki-tae:** Plays the Blue House factions off one another, accepts the assignment to shadow Cho, coordinates the Jang Gyeong-sik release as a favor to Yu-ji, interrogates Changbae at gunpoint over the Busan leak, and races to Gungjeong-dong when he senses something is wrong with Cho.
- **Director Cho (KCIA):** Openly defies Park's hard-line instincts; warns Ki-tae to trust only him; ultimately confronts Park during the 10/26 dinner.
- **Cheon Seok-joong (now Chief of Staff):** Advocates martial law, talks back to Park about putting \"the good of our people\" first, and is cut off by a phone call before anything more can happen.
- **General Hwang Dae-sik (MSC):** Courting the presidency by recruiting academy colonels; pressures the president's inner circle.
- **Pyo Hak-su:** Supplies Ki-tae with the detained Jang Gyeong-sik and the intel that drugs are spilling into Busan despite Ki-tae's \"export-only\" rule.
- **Oh Ye-jin:** Single-handedly raids a Busan dealer, beats his crew, and extracts Changbae's name as the source.
- **Changbae (Park Changbae):** Runs Ki-tae's Seoul meth lab; denies the Busan leak on his life; told to \"clean this up.\"
- **Baek Ki-hyun:** Now a lieutenant colonel inside Hwang Dae-sik's MSC circle, passed over for closer ties; Ki-tae assigns him to spy on Hwang while keeping up appearances.
- **Choi Yu-ji / Ikeda Yūji:** Recruits Harimoto via the Jang Gyeong-sik trade; loses the vote 10–9 to Kimura; reappears as kumicho after a cut-to-black, implying she killed Kimura.
- **Ikeda Osamu:** Announces retirement, then dies of cardiac arrest before the vote.
- **Harimoto:** Backs Yu-ji's succession bid in exchange for his brother's release.
- **Kimura Yoichi:** Old-guard victor who insults Yu-ji racially and is removed; implied killed off-screen.
- **Colonel Ahn Jae-ho:** Korea Military Academy graduate, Hwang's right hand at the MSC.

- **Chief Gwak (郭室长)** — Presidential Security Chief, hard-line rival to Cho.
- **General Hwang Dae-sik (黄大植)** — MSC commander, Korea Military Academy classmate of Cheon Seok-joong, eyeing the top.

- **Kihun (基勋)** — Ki-tae's driver/subordinate on the Busan run (distinct from brother Ki-hyun).
- **Changbae / Park Chang-bae (朴长培)** — operator of Ki-tae's Seoul meth lab.
- **Professor Jang Gyeong-sik (张景植)** — Zainichi Korean academic framed for espionage; Harimoto's brother; sprung by Ki-tae as a favor.
- **Harimoto (针本)** — Ikeda-clan Tokyo ops boss, regional leader.
- **Kimura Yoichi (木村洋一)** — Ikeda-clan old-guard contender; wins the vote then is deposed.
- **Colonel Ahn Jae-ho (安宰浩)** — Korea Military Academy graduate, Hwang's right hand at the MSC.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The series is now set **1979**, nine years after S1's late-1960s/early-1970s opening.
- Ki-tae's promotion ladder: KCIA agent → Deputy Director → Park's hinted pick for **next KCIA director**.
- Cheon Seok-joong has shifted from \"Presidential Security Chief\" into **Chief of Staff to the President** (\"the second-most powerful man in the country\").
- The KCIA's domestic role now includes running anti-communist arrest quotas — Pyo admits he \"hires a novelist\" to fabricate justifications.
- A Zainichi Korean diaspora pipeline exists: arrests in Korea, exploitation as fake spies, ties to Ikeda yakuza.
- Ki-hyun's rank progression now reaches **lieutenant colonel**; his embedded position inside the MSC is the leverage Ki-tae uses against Hwang.
- The Osaka flashback establishes that Ki-tae's KCIA role finances and protects the **Ikeda clan's succession** via Yu-ji.
- The episode ends on the **historical assassination of Park Chung-hee at Gungjeong-dong on October 26, 1979**, with Director Cho implicated as one of the conspirators inside the safehouse.

## Jokes and References Useful for Later Episodes
- **\"Shame is the most essential virtue.\"** Ki-tae's loaded lecture to Changbae doubles as a recurring thematic tag for him; the gun cock immediately after signals his tolerance for \"shameless\" subordinates is zero.
- **\"Changbae\" echo:** the Busan dealer's casual name for the Seoul supplier is the same nickname Ki-tae uses in person — convenient shorthand that may trip up either side later.
- **\"Why push drugs in this beautiful country? For patriotism.\"** Ki-tae's moral framing of export-only smuggling is already contradicted by the Busan leak; expect the contradiction to come back.
- **\"First grade to second grade\" rank analogy** — Ki-hyun explaining his promotion to Ki-tae's young niece in kid-language; sets up a soft familial contrast to Ki-tae's coldness.
- **\"Pampered sons of bitches... lop off their heads one by one\"** — Hwang Dae-sik's cartoonishly genocidal rant about the Busan/Masan protestors; a character beat that foreshadows how he talks when he feels safe.
- **Yūji's \"Korean mongrel whore\" insult** — the first explicit racial invective she absorbs publicly; her silent response (gun hammer) is the episode's most quoted visual.
- **Pyo's toupee banter** — running gag that the KCIA is going bald under stress, with Ki-tae's hair held up as a model of genetic good fortune.
- **\"Life's a long journey...\"** — Cho's parable to Ki-tae about walking one's own road, paired with his warning \"trust only me\"; a Cheon/Ikeda echo in the closing Cheon/Park confrontation about whose road the country is on.
- **Ki-hyun must report on Hwang's circle while pretending not to be Ki-tae's brother** — a classic mole tension set up explicitly and likely to recur.

## Unresolved Threads
- **Yu-ji's coup method** — the cut from Kimura's insults to Yu-ji as kumicho implies a violent takeover, but the actual seizure is not shown.
- **Changbae's fate** — Ki-tae's \"clean this up\" leaves the meth-lab operator alive but on notice; the Busan leak source remains unconfirmed.
- **Who is actually pushing drugs in Busan** — Changbae denies it; the dealer named him; someone inside the network is skimming or freelancing.
- **Director Cho's role in the 10/26 assassination** — he is in Gungjeong-dong with a jammed gun; was he conspirator, victim, or both?
- **What Cheon Seok-joong was \"about to pull tonight\"** — the warning call hints at a coordinated move that intersects with the assassination.
- **Ki-hyun's double game inside the MSC** — must spy on Hwang while remaining publicly loyal; his conversation with Ahn Jae-ho plants a future allegiance test.
- **Madam Shin's role and Ki-tae's Osaka connection** — she is positioned as a Park intimate who speaks Japanese; her function beyond this episode is unclear.
- **Park's promised \"next KCIA director\" handoff to Ki-tae** — now moot with Park's death; Ki-tae inherits a power vacuum instead of a planned promotion.
- **The memorial service** — Ki-tae sends Soyeong and Ki-hyun off to a memorial (presumably for their father) and never attends; an unresolved family beat.
- **Cho vs. Ki-tae** — Cho told Ki-tae \"trust only me… one day I'll end all of this.\" What \"all of this\" meant, and whether Ki-tae believed him, is left dangling into 10/26.

## S02E04

# Made in Korea S02E04 — "In the Name of the Father" Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode centers on the army headquarters confrontation where **General Jang** (Geonyoung's father) publicly asserts authority over the Joint Investigation Headquarters (JIH), while parallel threads advance: Cheon Seokjung's arrest and interrogation, Kitae's plot to eliminate Hwang Daesik, and the Baek brothers' explosive confrontation.

## Important Character Actions

- **General Jang (Geonyoung's father)** — Summons the JIH chief to army HQ, rebukes him for overstepping (arresting Cheon without permission, assuming vice-ministers' chair), denounces political maneuvering in the military, yet endorses the slush-fund investigation.
- **Jang Geonyoung** — Leads Cheon's arrest; personally interrogates him with waterboarding; introduced at JIH gathering as "our Zhuge Liang" (advisor/strategist).
- **Baek Kitae** — Orders Kihyun to assassinate Hwang Daesik at Heaven Cloud Pavilion using C-4 with a timer (red wire stops countdown, blue wire triggers instant detonation); bids emotional farewell to Choi Yuji, promising to reunite in Ikaino.
- **Baek Kihyun** — Confronts Kitae about their shared past (the stolen gun, the killing 15 years ago), accuses Kitae of using family as cover for ambition; later introduced at JIH as new lieutenant colonel.
- **Cheon Seokjung** — Arrested, held in punitive custody, waterboarded; refuses to talk but implicated by Choi Yuji's testimony and the 70B won ledger.
- **Choi Yuji** — Testifies against Cheon; released, placed at Tower Hotel Room 925 under armed guard; departs for Japan next morning.
- **Hwang Daesik** — Targeted for assassination at Heaven Cloud Pavilion (monthly visitor, stays ~3 hours).
- **Baek Soyeong** — Suffers severe psychotic break (hallucinates Daeil), hospitalized; Kihyun realizes he neglected her.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Heaven Cloud Pavilion (천운각 / 天云阁)** — Old courtesan house where Hwang meets contacts discreetly; ~100m from Kitae's ops base.
- **C-4 timer bomb** — Lethal radius 50m, ≈6 hand grenades; red wire = stop, blue wire = immediate detonation.
- **Punitive custody (징벌적 구금 / 惩戒拘留)** — Used to hold Cheon without standard warrant.
- **Ikaino (猪饲野 / 이카노)** — Osaka Koreatown; Kitae & Yuji's promised reunion spot (rice soup).

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- **70B won slush fund** — Entirely drug proceeds (meth), moved to Japan via Ikeda clan; ledger spans years.
- **Kang Daeil's death** — Confirmed murder by Kitae (not suicide); Kihyun learns this from Geonyoung's side.
- **Park Changbae** — Soyeong's meth dealer; vanished; Geonyoung pursuing him.
- **Kihyun's 9-year wait** — Geonyoung has waited 9 years to bring down Kitae.
- **Kitae/Kihyun backstory** — 15 years ago in Busan: Kihyun killed a Japanese man, Kitae disposed of the body; unspoken pact formed.
- **General Jang's authority** — Commands loyalty oath ("We are one"); can override JIH operations.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **"Our Zhuge Liang"** — Geonyoung introduced as master strategist at JIH drinking session.
- **Madam Shin's counsel** — "Let him enjoy his little victories" (restraint as strategy).
- **Shaman's dream reading** — Claims to see Kitae's breakup in a vision; "someone new is waiting."
- **"We are one" chant** — Military loyalty ritual co-opted by JIH; Kihyun forced to lead it.

## Unresolved Threads

- **Bomb at Heaven Cloud Pavilion** — Timer started; Kihyun present; detonation imminent.
- **Park Changbae's whereabouts** — Dead or fled? Both sides hunting.
- **Soyeong's condition** — Hospitalized; trauma rooted in Daeil's death.
- **Kihyu's allegiance** — Now inside JIH but loyal to Kitae? Double-play unclear.
- **Yuji's safety in Japan** — Kitae promised to join her; can he escape Korea?
- **Cheon's silence** — Will waterboarding break him? What does he know about Kitae?
- **General Jang's endgame** — Backs investigation but slapped down JIH overreach; true motives opaque.

## S02E03


# Made in Korea S02E03 — Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with a "two months earlier" flashback: a former convict-turned-construction worker meets a military contact over pork soup and beer. He hands over dossiers on Baek Kitae and Cheon Seokjung's secret slush fund of approximately 70 billion won — intel he spent seven years gathering. In exchange he wants a pardon and reinstatement once Cheon and Baek fall.

In the present, Jang Geonyoung is now Special Advisor at the Military Security Command (FCI / 保安司令部). Baek Ki-hyun reports for duty at the FCI on Colonel Ahn's recommendation, assigned to support Geonyoung. Their first lunch is cut short when Geonyoung receives a tip that a yakuza figure, Choi Yuji (Ikeda Yuji), is arriving via Baekho Enterprise with 70 billion won in cash. Geonyoung mobilizes the FCI and intercepts the handoff, arresting Choi Yuji and seizing the money — directly confronting Baek Kitae at the scene.

Kitae scrambles: he suspects a KCIA mole tipped off Geonyoung, orders Pyo Hak-su tailed, and dispatches a man to find Takahashi Kazuo — Yuji's longtime associate who entered separately carrying the ledger (account book) of Cheon's slush fund. Kitae knows that if the FCI obtains the ledger, it's over.

General Hwang (黄大植) blocks Geonyoung from pursuing the meth angle, insisting they wait out the president's 49-day mourning period. He orders Geonyoung to bury the drug charges and focus solely on the slush fund — revealing that exposing presidential men as drug dealers would damage the regime's legitimacy.

Choi Yuji is interrogated at the FCI. She stonewalls, citing Japanese citizenship and demanding embassy contact. Oh Ye-jin and Geonyoung escalate threats, invoking martial law, but Yuji refuses to reveal the ledger's location. Meanwhile, Japan files a formal protest through the Foreign Ministry and the Army HQ demands Yuji's release.

Geonyoung visits Baek Soyeong at her shop, reveals that Baekho Enterprise is a meth lab, and pressures her to cooperate or face prison alongside her brother. Soyeong has a young daughter, Minju, and is visibly a drug user herself.

Madam Shin meets with Cheon Seokjung and Kitae, offering to help navigate the crisis. Kitae warns Ki-hyun by phone that Geonyoung's transfer was designed to manipulate him. Over drinks, Geonyoung pressures Ki-hyun directly: choose between falling with his brother or surviving with the new order — invoking Soyeong and her daughter as reasons Ki-hyun needs to protect himself.

Kitae's men locate Takahashi Kazuo at Busan Hotel room 308. A violent confrontation erupts over the ledger; Takahashi initially resists, skeptical that Kitae's people could have spoken to the detained Yuji. The ledger changes hands.

The episode closes with Geonyoung declaring he'll lock up everyone involved. A senior figure who has held his position for 18 years — passed over in favor of "that bald Hwang" — meets with Kitae. Kitae requests and receives approval to personally eliminate Hwang Daesik.

## Important Character Actions

- **Jang Geonyoung** — Orchestrates the FCI raid on Choi Yuji and the 70 billion won seizure; confronts Kitae directly; interrogates Yuji; visits Baek Soyeong to apply pressure; pressures Ki-hyun to defect from his brother; pushes General Hwang to prosecute the drug angle.
- **Baek Kitae** — Loses Choi Yuji and the money to the FCI; scrambles to recover the ledger via Takahashi Kazuo; suspects a KCIA mole; warns Ki-hyun about Geonyoung; seeks approval to assassinate Hwang Daesik.
- **Baek Ki-hyun** — Transfers to the FCI; caught between his brother's orders and Geonyoung's recruitment pressure; his loyalty is the episode's central tension.
- **Choi Yuji / Ikeda Yuji** — Arrested, interrogated, refuses to talk; invokes Japanese citizenship; endures escalating threats without cracking.
- **Oh Ye-jin** — Leads the Yuji interrogation; pushes hard on martial-law threats.
- **General Hwang Daesik** — Blocks the meth prosecution to protect the regime's image; insists on waiting out the mourning period.
- **Pyo Hak-su** — Reports the seizure to Kitae; becomes a surveillance target when Kitae suspects an internal leak.
- **Madam Shin** — Meets with Cheon Seokjung and Kitae; offers political assistance with the Japanese detainee and ledger crisis.
- **Cheon Seokjung** — Strategizes with Kitae and Madam Shin; assigns Kitae to handle the ledger.
- **Baek Soyeong** — Confronted by Geonyoung about the meth lab; revealed as a drug user with a young daughter, Minju.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Takahashi Kazuo (高桥和夫)** — Yuji's associate of ten years; entered Korea separately carrying the slush-fund ledger; found at Busan Hotel room 308.
- **Colonel Ahn (安上校)** — Recommended Geonyoung's appointment as FCI special advisor; a high-school connection.
- **Baekho Enterprise (白虎企业)** — Kitae's front company; doubles as a meth-laboratory base.
- **FCI / Military Security Command (保安司令部)** — The military intelligence body where Geonyoung and now Ki-hyun operate; distinct from the KCIA.
- **49-day mourning period (四十九天国丧)** — The ritual observance after the president's death; used by Hwang as a pretext to delay prosecution.
- **Minju (敏珠)** — Baek Soyeong's young daughter; Soyeong is a drug user, putting the child at risk.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The "two months earlier" flashback establishes how Geonyoung obtained his initial intelligence on the Kitae–Cheon slush fund — from an ex-convict who spent seven years compiling it.
- The 70 billion won is entirely drug proceeds (meth), laundered through Cheon's slush fund and moved via the Ikeda clan to Japan; the ledger spans years.
- General Hwang's priority is protecting the regime's image, not justice — he will not allow a drug scandal to surface while the mourning period is active.
- The FCI and KCIA are openly at war: the FCI intercepts KCIA operations, and Kitae suspects moles inside his own organization.
- Baek Soyeong is both a drug user and a potential witness; her daughter Minju is a dependent who makes her vulnerable to pressure.
- Choi Yuji's Japanese citizenship creates a diplomatic flashpoint — Japan formally protests and the Army HQ demands her release, giving the FCI a limited window to extract information.
- Kitae's plan to assassinate Hwang Daesik is now authorized by a senior insider, directly setting up the Heaven Cloud Pavilion bomb plot seen in E04.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **"Enemy of my enemy is my friend"** — The opening narration frames the entire episode's shifting alliances; a thematic tag for the Geonyoung–Ki-hyun recruitment.
- **Pork soup and beer** — The ex-convict's meeting echoes Ki-hyun and Geonyoung's later drinking session; the same dish recurs as a Korean working-class bonding ritual.
- **"That bald Hwang"** — A recurring epithet (also in E04's notes) that signals how Hwang is privately蔑视 by the KCIA old guard despite his military power.
- **"Gangster" taunt** — Oh Ye-jin repeatedly calling Choi Yuji "gangster" during interrogation; Yuji's contemptuous silence becomes a character signature.
- **"You've changed"** — The ex-convict notes Geonyoung has changed; Geonyoung later echoes this to Ki-hyun. The question of who has truly changed — and who hasn't — recurs across the season.
- **Soyeong's salt-throwing** — After Geonyoung leaves, Soyeong throws salt outside (a Korean superstition to ward off bad luck); a darkly comic beat given her actual situation.

## Unresolved Threads

- **The ex-convict's identity and fate** — He gave Geonyoung the initial dossier but his pardon/reinstatement depends on actually bringing down Cheon and Baek; will he resurface?
- **The KCIA mole** — Kitae knows someone leaked the handoff details; he's tailing Pyo Hak-su but the real source is unconfirmed.
- **Choi Yuji's endurance** — She hasn't talked yet, but diplomatic pressure is mounting; the FCI's window to extract the ledger location is closing.
- **Baek Soyeong's cooperation** — Geonyoung left his number; will she call, or will she warn Kitae?
- **Ki-hyun's choice** — Geonyoung has made the recruitment explicit; Ki-hyun has not committed either way. His sister and niece are leverage.
- **Hwang Daesik's assassination** — Kitae has approval; the Heaven Cloud Pavilion plot is now in motion.
- **Who held the position for 18 years** — The senior figure who approved the hit on Hwang and was passed over for promotion is not yet named; his role in the broader power structure remains opaque.
- **Madam Shin's full agenda** — She is positioned as a political fixer with Japanese-language skills and ties to both Cheon and Kitae; her endgame is unclear.

## S02E06

# S02E06 "Made in Korea" — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
The season finale centers on a convergence of assassination plots and power struggles. Baek Ki-tae (KCIA) attempts to kill General Hwang Daesik (Presidential Security Service chief) during a gathering at Hwang's residence, but his younger brother, Lieutenant Colonel Baek Ki-hyeon, unexpectedly appears and disrupts the plan. Meanwhile, Prosecutor Jang Geon-yeong pressures Ki-hyeon to turn on his brother, revealing the KCIA has been credibly accused of conspiring to assassinate the President. Ki-tae coerces Captain Min Jong-hwan (MSC, with a defector uncle) into planting a briefcase bomb at the next day's Joint Investigation Headquarters (JIH) regular meeting where Hwang will attend. Cheon Seok-joong (Presidential Security Service chief) is in custody; his case is deemed hopeless. The episode closes with military alumni singing a marching song as the JIH meeting approaches — signaling the assassination operation is a go.

## Important Character Actions
- **Baek Ki-tae**: Orchestrates the hit on Hwang Daesik; recruits Min Jong-hwan as the bomber via blackmail (uncle defected to North); demands more explosives after the previous batch went missing; visits Cheon Seok-joong in detention under pretense of family concern.
- **Jang Geon-yeong**: Confronts Ki-hyeon, revealing the presidential assassination accusation against KCIA; warns Ki-hyeon his family will be destroyed if Ki-tae kills Hwang; interrogates Pyo Hak-su (KCIA deputy director) who supplies JIH personnel dossiers; presses Cheon Seok-joong for the "reason he's holding out" (waiting for Hwang to die).
- **Baek Ki-hyeon**: Attends Hwang's dinner unaware of the assassination attempt; later confronted by Geon-yeong who reveals Ki-tae's plan; admits he still hesitates ("playing Hamlet").
- **Pyo Hak-su**: Compiles JIH officer dossiers for Geon-yeong; reports Ki-hyeon will skip the JIH meeting (attending Martial Law Command meeting instead) and will leave 30 minutes early.
- **Captain Min Jong-hwan**: Coerced by Ki-tae to deliver the briefcase bomb to the JIH meeting; uncle Park Jeong-pil defected to North — Ki-tae uses this as leverage.
- **Hwang Daesik**: Hosts the military alumni dinner; targeted for assassination at the JIH meeting next morning.
- **Cheon Seok-joong**: Detained; Geon-yeong deduces he's waiting for Hwang's death to cut a deal.
- **Oh Ye-jin**: Notes the suspicious coded conversation between Ki-tae and Cheon about "cleaning the father's grave" (likely a signal for the bomb op).

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Joint Investigation Headquarters (JIH / 합동수사본부)** — the multi-agency body where the climax meeting occurs.
- **Martial Law Command (계엄사령부)** — Ki-hyeon attends their meeting instead of JIH, removing him from the blast zone.
- **Park Jeong-pil (박정필)** — Min Jong-hwan's uncle, defected to North Korea; used as kompromat.
- **"Cleaning the father's grave" / "pulling weeds"** — coded language between Ki-tae and Cheon Seok-joong for the bombing operation timing.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The KCIA as an institution has been **credibly accused of conspiring to assassinate the President** — existential threat to the organization.
- Ki-tae's drug operation cleanup continues: Park Chang-bae's body found dumped in the hills, killed by Ki-tae to erase traces.
- Cheon Seok-joong's slush fund investigation is the lever Geon-yeong uses; Cheon's wife begged Ki-tae for help.
- Ki-hyeon's sister Baek So-young and daughter Min-ju remain Ki-tae's vulnerability (Geon-yeong threatens them).
- The "red wire / blue wire" bomb motif from earlier episodes pays off — Ki-tae coaches Min on which wire to cut (red stops countdown, blue triggers immediate detonation).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **"We are one!" (우리는 하나!)** — the military alumni chant, now ironically echoing as a coup slogan.
- **The military marching song ("Through lofty mountains...")** — sung at Hwang's dinner and reprised at episode end; becomes the auditory signature of the conspiratorial officer clique.
- **Ki-tae's alcohol tolerance** — "never gets drunk" callback; Hwang pours Chivas Regal as a power move.
- **Min Jong-hwan's sinus sniffle** — recurring tell; Ki-tae notices it during the coercion scene.
- **Gaettong (개똥)** — Cheon's dog; "walk Gaettong" becomes a coded check-in phrase between Cheon and his aide.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Will Min Jong-hwan actually plant the briefcase?** He agrees under duress but is visibly shaken; may sabotage or be caught.
2. **Will Ki-hyeon ultimately side with Geon-yeong or Ki-tae?** He hesitates to the end; his attendance at the Martial Law Command meeting (not JIH) saves him but leaves his allegiance ambiguous.
