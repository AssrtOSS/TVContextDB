## S01E01

## S.W.A.T. Exiles S01E01 "Clean Entry" — Episode Summary

### Plot
Two years after a high-profile mission went wrong, forcing Hondo into retirement, Commander Hicks recruits him back to lead **44 Squad** — an experimental "last-chance" unit of marginalized young officers (Gen Z) — as LAPD brass moves to dismantle SWAT. Hondo clashes with the recruits during brutal selection exercises, then deploys them on their first live op: intercepting **Los Emperadores** cartel sicario **Carlos Vaminez** (a ghost from 2016) at a South Gate construction site. The arrest uncovers a kidnapping — Carlos's daughter **Celine (6)** — and the team raids an abandoned theater to rescue her. The op succeeds but leaves casualties; Hondo realizes the entire unit was set up to fail by someone powerful. He and Hicks agree to investigate the conspiracy. The episode ends with a mysterious figure receiving a report on Hondo, vowing to destroy him.

### Important Character Actions
- **Hondo**: Returns reluctantly; runs punitive PT/field exercises; calls out the setup during the theater op; rallies the team with a "show up when no one else will" speech; partners with Hicks to hunt the puppet master.
- **Jude Callan**: Rebels at every turn; quits mid-episode but returns for the raid; challenges Hondo's authority openly.
- **Ethan Cole**: Uses gait/posture analysis to ID Carlos from border cam footage; runs drone overwatch; freezes during the hostage-center sim but performs in the real op.
- **Sammy Bishop**: Livestreams the locker room; pushes for negotiation access; talks Carlos down in interrogation using cartel psychology.
- **Cassidy Han**: Refuses to stop filming; misses a hostage in sim; redeems herself covering Carlos's extraction.
- **Malik Henderson (né Anderson)**: Marine vet (Echo Co, 2/5); only one to hold the line in sim; confronts Hondo about the rigged scenario; earns Hondo's respect.
- **Deacon Kay**: Guest cameo — counsels Hondo over beers; reminds him "teaching them how to meet halfway" is now the job.
- **Hicks**: Reveals he was shoved out the moment Hondo stepped in; personnel files were doctored; agrees to dig for names.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **44 Squad / "The Four-Four"** — experimental unit designation
- **Los Emperadores** — cartel; "emperors" in Spanish
- **Carlos Vaminez** — ex-sicario, disappeared 2016, living undercover in South Gate
- **Celine Vaminez (6)** — Carlos's daughter, kidnapped as leverage
- **Lazario** — cartel boss referenced in Carlos's Chihuahua story
- **Commander Clint Myers** — section commander, stretched thin covering Red + Gold teams
- **Deputy Chief Ramon Carrero** — reform-unit backer, "straight shooter"
- **PFQ** — Physical Fitness Qualifier
- **Clean Entry** — tactical doctrine & episode title double entendre
- **Gold Team / Red Team** — existing SWAT squads (Gold grounded post-raid)

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Timeline: ~2 years after *S.W.A.T.* S08 finale; Hondo was forced out after leaking body-cam footage (never proven) of a kid dying on a raid.
- Hondo sold his mother's house; still reaches for the phone to call her.
- Deacon references Hondo's divorce from Michelle and his mother's death.
- Hicks implies the same brass burned him before; he still has downtown contacts.
- The "mole inside the new squad" arc is seeded — someone is reporting to an unseen superior at episode's end.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes

### Unresolved Threads

2. **The unseen puppet master** — final scene shows a shadowy figure ordering Hondo's destruction.

## S01E02

# S.W.A.T. Exiles S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
The newly formed 44 Squad faces their first real case: a mass shooting at a Koreatown dance club (Inchon Palace) leaves six dead. The victims were all Korean, but a non-Korean man — Kim Ji-Ho, a major drug operator — is identified among them. His death breaks a truce with rival Monte Barzarian, triggering a gang war. 44 Squad is tasked with intercepting a weapons shipment (FN P90s from Belgium) at Barzarian's textile warehouse, then at a secondary drop point. The operation devolves into a shootout; Monte is wounded, his son Andre attempts to kill him in the hospital, and 44 Squad stops the hit. Andre is killed by Malik (newly designated Scout). The episode ends with Hondo learning Commander Angela Pike sat on the hearing board that forced his retirement after Nico Reyes' death.

## Important Character Actions
- **Hondo**: Runs "auditions" for Scout role; clashes with new commander Pike; investigates Monte's shooter off-book with Deacon; realizes Pike was on his termination hearing board.
- **Jude**: Goes rogue during warehouse raid (nearly friendly-fires Hondo); reprimanded for breaking formation; later takes clean shot on Andre at hospital.
- **Malik**: Saves hostage + suspect alive during training (violating priority of life); leaves position during shootout to save Monte; earns Scout designation after team follows his lead at hospital.
- **Sammy**: Leads containment at drop point; freezes under fire; films content for social media; secures gun sponsorship deal without telling Hondo.
- **Ethan**: Finds shipping manifest via unauthorized database access (no warrant); discovers heavier container from Belgium; places cameras; spots Andre on hospital security feed; reveals wealthy family trust background.
- **Cassidy**: Refuses to read her personnel file; file reveals "rage issues" (not leg disability) as concern.
- **Deacon** (guest): Helps Hondo investigate; reveals he retired voluntarily for PI work (cheating spouses, missing heirs).

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **44 Squad** — experimental unit designation (replaces "Experimental Unit")
- **Commander Angela Pike** — new Metropolitan Division commander; "fixer" for broken departments; was on Hondo's termination hearing board
- **Monte Barzarian** — Armenian crime boss, Kim Ji-Ho's rival; truce broken by Kim's death
- **Andre Barzarian** — Monte's son, lawyer; orchestrates hit on own father
- **Kim Ji-Ho** — Korean drug kingpin; killed night before episode
- **June Barzarian** — Kim Ji-Ho's daughter; witnessed father's murder; father's last word: "Adeul" (Korean: son)
- **FN P90** — military-grade weapon appearing on streets (Belgian origin)
- **Scout** — formalized point/lead role for operations; Malik earns it

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Pike was one of three on the hearing board that cashiered Hondo

## S01E03

# S01E03 — Summary

## Plot

The episode opens with the freeway sniper case: Walter Kovic, a disgraced former Cassini mission launch specialist, opens fire on the 110, wounding eight. Hondo's experimental unit is called in. A brutal VR-augmented training scenario earlier that morning (armed robbery crew, missing 15-year-old, meth lab, baby-in-oven) doubles as targeted exposure therapy for Sammy, who has been vapor-locking since a previous stop. Pike watches the run, is impressed, and gives the team the Kovic op.

The team splits: Malik, Jude, and Ethan stake out Kovic's gutted house in northeast LA and learn from neighbor Nancy Foster that Kovic's wife Pam died 20 years ago, he lost his engineering job, a fire destroyed the rose garden and his house, his claim was denied, and he snapped. Shots ring out at LJT Insurance (Wilshire & 7th) — Kovic's insurer. Hondo, Sammy, and Cassidy lock down the parking garage and find claims VP Mark Miller dead, laptop missing, a rejection letter stuffed in his mouth. Sammy and Cassidy work Mark's assistant Maddie, who reveals the letter was actually signed by LJT CEO Sylvia Beckett, and that Beckett secretly met a "John Smith" then ordered every fire claim denied. Hondo's PI Tate is meanwhile still tailing Pike.

Ethan hijacks Mark Miller's stolen laptop remotely and finds a suppressed fire investigation: a decommissioned PWP transformer ignited the blaze. "John Smith" is on the distro list — he's the Head of Operations at Pascal Water and Power. The team races to the PWP plant as Walter begins his endgame. Ethan and Jude discover rocket-style electromagnetic "cells" bolted to every grid transformer; an overload would black out all of LA. The control room is destroyed, so the cells must be removed one by one. Ethan works out that heating the neodymium magnets past the Curie point will demagnetize them, like staging a rocket. Hondo talks Walter down from a dead-man's trigger that would detonate every tower. The grid holds; Sylvia Beckett is arrested for the LJT/PWP cover-up.

In the coda, Hondo confronts Pike at her pool hall and lays out his conspiracy: he believes the IA investigation into the Reyes cover-up, the transfers, Hicks bringing him back, and Pike being assigned over him are all connected — that someone wants to end him and SWAT. Pike admits she was IA, says Reyes crossed her desk, and insists she took the job to reform SWAT. Hondo later confesses to Pike that he falsified the warehouse-shootout after-action report to hide Sammy's freeze. The episode closes with Cassidy and Ethan both crashing at Malik's place, both admitting SWAT has been making them feel unlike themselves.

## Important character actions

- Hondo: leads the Kovic call, talks down the dead-man's-trigger standoff, confronts Pike with his conspiracy theory, and confesses to falsifying Sammy's AAR to protect her and the unit.
- Pike: observes the training and is won over, insists on mental-health priorities, takes personal time mid-manhunt (work-life balance stance), later arrested Sylvia Beckett, and told Hondo her side of the Reyes/IA history.
- Sammy: confided her anxiety and past freeze to Cassidy, was the implicit target of the sensory-overload VR drill, and co-led the Maddie interview at LJT.
- Cassidy: ran the Maddie interview, got the Sylvia Beckett / John Smith lead, and ended up crashing at Malik's.
- Malik: led the Kovic residence stakeout, evacuated the PWP north wing with Cassidy.
- Jude: scouted the PWP cell rig with Ethan and relayed radio updates from the field.
- Ethan: built the VR training program, hijacked the stolen laptop remotely, and devised the heat-demagnetize fix that saved the grid.
- Tate: Hondo's PI; reports Pike is unremarkable except a solo movie trip, and Hondo tells him to keep tailing her.

## S01E04

## S01E04 剧情摘要

### 剧集主线
本集以 **2026年6月9日的武力审查委员会（Force Review Board）** 听证会为框架，回溯6月6日 Clarita Cannabis Farms（克拉里塔大麻种植场）人质事件，审查 Hondo 枪杀 Rachel Richards 一案。

回溯部分：匿名911报警称种植场有人质劫持；特警到场却发现一队"ICE（移民与海关执法局）特工"抢先接管现场，拒绝LAPD进入并出示搜查令。Hondo 违规留在现场，靠一道侧门潜入；Ethan 的热成像因水培农场高温湿度失效，改用无人机侦察。Hondo 发现特工配枪是定制款 1911（ICE 标准配发为 Glock 19）、搜查令上签字的 Belmont 法官 2024年已退休——**所谓突袭是冒牌货，实为持械抢劫**。5名歹徒劫持8名人质，Hondo 在未封锁全部出口的情况下强行突入（"exigent circumstances"），追击逃跑嫌疑人时开枪击中 Richards。现场找不到那把枪，Hondo 的执法记录仪损坏，唯一能证明真相的无人机影像受损。

听证会部分：Pike 指挥官主导施压，Angela、Rodriguez、副局长 Correro 在场。Jude、Sammy、Malik、Ethan、Cassidy 依次作证。Cassidy 如实陈述"没看到枪"；Ethan 对委员会谎称影像已彻底损毁，实则按 Hondo 要求把无人机留在其办公室未修。团队据此推断 Hondo 在销毁证据，信任崩塌。

真相：Malik 潜入 Hondo 办公室偷出影像，交给 **米兰达（市长幕僚长）**，由其匿名提交。视频显示 Richards 拿起嫌疑人的枪、企图灭口蒙眼人质——她确系武装共犯。委员会认定 Hondo 行动正当，即刻复职。

结尾：Hondo 向 Ethan 保密交底"44小队里有人在监视我、向上头泄露情报"；最终场景有人向 Hondo 点破 **"Malik 就是内鬼"**，Hondo 表示不生气反而松了口气，"现在只需想出一个计划"。

### 重要角色行动
- **Hondo**：违令留守、强行突入、坚持开枪且坚称有枪；扣留受损无人机并让 Ethan 停修；复职后确认内鬼身份，开始布局反制。
- **Malik**：内鬼。作证时把水搅浑，偷窃证据交给米兰达，自称"不是为自己，比这更大"。
- **Ethan**：唯一无条件信任 Hondo（"问题在于信任，我信任你"）；对高层作伪证，事后不安。
- **Cassidy**：顶住压力如实作证"没有枪"，随后发现自己错了，陷入愧疚与困惑（"既然视频能洗清他，他为什么还要藏？"）。
- **Pike**：新任指挥官，公开针对 Hondo，想要"一个战利品"；败后仍称"我赢了我需要的东西"。
- **Correro**：主持审查的副局长，表面站 Hondo 一边，劝其辞职保全 SWAT。
- **米兰达**：市长幕僚长，接收赃物影像并匿名投放，承诺"等汉多走了有你一席之地"。
- **Sammy / Jude / Cassidy**：Sammy 负责解救人质；Jude 留守引导巡警；Jude 与 Cassidy 一夜情曝光。

## S01E06

# S.W.A.T. Exiles S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode resolves the mole reveal from E5: **Malik** admits he's been feeding intel to **Mayor Voss's chief of staff, Miranda Seitzmeier**. Hondo gives him an ultimatum — resign or stay and earn back trust. Malik chooses to stay.

**FBI Special Agent Elliot Gines** returns, reactivating **Jude's** two-year deep-cover identity **"Bobby"** inside the **Imperial Dukes**, a white-supremacist group led by **Ripley Collins** (now imprisoned). Ripley's son **Shane Collins** has surfaced in LA, robbed **Denton Security Services**, and stolen **master access to the city's entire security grid** (homes, offices, high-value collectibles across Bel Air to Rancho Palos Verdes).

Hondo and Gines trace a prison messenger (**Derrick Harris**, a trustee) passing orders from Ripley to Shane via Shane's sister **Shayla** (Jude's former lover, who once faked a pregnancy to test him). Hondo confronts Ripley in prison; Ripley refuses to save Shane, declaring "better a corpse in the dirt than breathing the same air as you people."

The team realizes the real target: **Amber Beck Davis**, wife of **Louis Beck** (the star witness against Ripley). Shane stages a home invasion as a "final exam" — ordering Jude/Bobby to execute Amber. Shayla reveals the pregnancy was a lie to test Bobby's loyalty. SWAT breaches; Hondo snipes Shane in a hostage standoff.

## Important Character Actions
