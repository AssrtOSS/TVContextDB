## S01E01

## Episode S01E01 — "Track of the Cat" (《猫踪》)

**Plot**

Cold open: Anna's voiceover — the park is a place to hide and feel safe ("Nature doesn't judge... It just is"). Sheila wasn't so lucky; someone else found her first.

Pre-dawn, Geoff calls: fellow ranger Sheila Drury never came home; her mom (Kelli) called — they do Wordle together every morning. Anna's sister Molly phones from New York (a psychiatrist, per her file); Anna deflects her concern ("Don't shrink me right now"), mentions Zach's birthday is this week — "I was married to him. I literally never forget."

Anna finds Sheila's body in the backcountry. It doesn't fit a mountain lion kill: deep wounds but no scratches, belly intact, no bite marks, no devil's-claw cuts, no evisceration (cats eat what they kill). Sheila was tracking cougars on a transect; her pack had no water — an experienced ranger would never go in dry, so someone took her there. "Something feels off." Anna stays with the body despite Geoff's storm warning, refusing to leave her to the vultures.

Geoff has already authorized trapping the cougar (dead goat in a cage, ranch hands, run by Harlan). Anna objects: the cat didn't kill Sheila, and relocating her this time of year would kill her and her cubs. She calls in FBI Special Agent Frederick Stanton — old acquaintance from her first year on the job, when someone tried to kill her and he investigated. Geoff: "You went over my head." Stanton initially sides with Geoff ("you don't have much to go on"), but Anna's read wins out.

Evidence points to Cory Nelson: his van holds Sheila's blood and wiped-clean water bottle; he posted heated rants against Sheila's RV-hookup project, got ticketed by her on a closed trail, sent death threats. Found dead in his red tent — diving beetles ate him post-mortem; cause of death: massive ketamine overdose, syringe present but nothing to tie off with. Official line: he killed Sheila, then OD'd. Anna: the neck wounds are three inches deep — "saber tooth tigers are extinct." Someone faked a cat kill, then framed Cory.

A dead pigeon appears on Anna's doorstep (Frieda jokes she has admirers; Anna: "Pigeons are a bad thing. Dead pigeons, Frieda."). A false call about golfers on Gunsight Ridge lures her out; she falls into a tiger trap (tarp over a pit), dislocates a shoulder, gets a concussion — someone lured her, left litter, and threw a rock at her head. She talks herself out of the pit ("Move, Anna... rock by rock"), calls Stanton, refuses a rescue: "Enjoy your various crimes."

Christina from the Visitor Center is in Anna's cabin with a casserole. She confesses: she and Sheila were secretly dating; she tidied Sheila's cabin as a last act of care. She's mid-divorce with custody issues; husband's on an oil rig.

Sheila's map holds a pin on Jeremiah D. Paulson, one of Montana's biggest landowners. Anna goes "hunting." Confrontation with Harlan, the ranger running the trap: he poached for Paulson ("sunsets are golden, but they ain't money"), killed Sheila when she discovered it (she trusted him), faked the cat wounds with a punch tool, framed Cory, left the pigeon, and tried to kill Anna. "Why?" — "You gotta go where the power is." Anna frees the cougar ("Go find your babies, mama"), takes Harlan down; when he begs not to be left alone to die: "Maybe she'll come back. Here, kitty, kitty."

Paulson arrives to "officially meet" her. He denies poaching, then: "I get what I want. Glacier has more resources than just cats... I want it all." Anna: nothing here is yours — "If you even touch one hair on the park's head, I'll stop you." Paulson: "You better be careful... some things you say you're gonna have to do."

Memorial radio call: "Ranger Sheila Drury, this is your final call. We'll see you on the other side." Anna adopts Sheila's cat Piedmont (no one else wants him; "What have I got to lose?") and tells Geoff she's staying (her Badlands transfer is cancelled; Geoff keeps it warm in case she changes her mind). At the bar, she tells young ranger Jesse that yesterday would have been her husband's 40th; she doesn't know Jesse's name or birthday — "Maybe one day you'll ask." Closing VO: "In this life, nobody really walks alone. Except the cat."

## S01E02

# Anna Pigeon S01E02 — Summary

## Plot
A family camping by the river reports 7-year-old Mandy missing during hide-and-seek. Anna finds her swept into the river, rescues her, and treats her for hypothermia — then gets suspicious when the father, "Michael White," can't produce a park registration and failed to mention Mandy's diabetes first. Through Stanton, FBI's ViCAP check reveals the family is really the Davies family from Utah — Mandy is Amanda Davies, brother Austin, dad Jake (rap sheet: B&E, scrap-metal theft, bar fights) — reported missing six months ago by the kids' grandmother. Anna finds their off-grid camp: kids' clothes, a grave, an empty insulin pen in Amanda's name, passport photos, and a goldenseal garden ("where the medicine grows"). Jake is caught on the eagle B-cam and shoots out the A-cam with a crossbow (his last bolt). When a pharmacy refuses him insulin without a prescription, he robs it with a knife and flees; the kids are taken into ranger custody, then spirited away during a staged distraction using mourning-dove "coo-coo" calls. Anna argues Jake isn't heading for the border but coming back for his kids — she's right. Grandmother Lori Grant (ex-military) insists on joining the chase. Anna corners Jake at the Kintla Lake Trailhead; Lori arrives with a gun, blaming Jake for Sarah's death. Austin talks his dad down ("Mandy doesn't even have her insulin"), and Jake surrenders. Anna tells Lori the truth: Sarah died of cervical cancer in the park — the grave was hers; she wanted to die in peace somewhere beautiful, not in a hospital. Sarah will be laid to rest near the family home.

## Key character actions
- Anna: saves Mandy from the river; distrusts the family's story from the start; finds the camp and grave; disarms and arrests Jake; shields the kids from the arrest by sending Mandy to pick flowers; reveals to Stanton that her parents vanished for three years when she was little and Molly became her mother overnight.
- Stanton: IDs the family via ViCAP; takes FBI jurisdiction; needles Anna ("Men don't come back for their kids," plus his dad's pack-of-smokes story) — and is wrong about Jake.
- Jake Davies: alias "Michael White"; genuinely loves his kids; took Sarah into the woods to cure her cancer with "tea and flowers" (goldenseal); keeps them fed by hunting and foraging.
- Austin: lies to protect the family, deflects with an eagle-parable about a dad who comes back, and ultimately talks his father into surrendering; asks what will happen to his mother.
- Lori Grant: filed the missing-person report; Desert Storm veteran who refuses to be left behind; nearly shoots Jake; never got to say goodbye to her daughter.
- Paulson: renews his threat to Anna ("That is a Paulson Promise"), then pressures a local landowner with 10 acres by the river to sell for $600,000 cash — or Paulson "gets it for free."
- Jesse: flirts with Anna, brings her a stuffed mouse (call-back to the eagle-cam story), and takes bets on whether the eagle dad returns.

## Newly established names & terms
- Davies family: Amanda "Mandy" Davies (initials A.D.), Austin Davies, Jake Davies, Sarah Davies (deceased), grandmother Lori Grant.
- Aliases: "Michael White" / "Mike Jr." (Jake and Austin).
- Leo — Mandy's stuffed lion (the "Lion" Anna fishes out of the river); Ace — the park's most difficult horse, which Anna always rides.
- Places: Kintla Lake Trailhead, Meadowridge (where Jake was last seen heading north).
- Terms: ViCAP (FBI database), insulin pen/cartridges, goldenseal (金印草), Blackfeet Reservation (Zoey's family), "Paulson Promise," Junior Ranger coloring pages, eagle cam A/B.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Anna is confirmed a widow ("rapidly aging widow" per Paulson); the cold open's voicemail ("Zach's birthday this week… I miss you") implies her late husband was named Zach, and she still has screaming nightmares (she asks a guest if they heard screaming; Molly's advice — write out your nightmares, "unless you don't want them to stop").
- Molly Pigeon is Anna's sister, not her mother — she raised Anna after their parents abandoned them for three years (Titanic on DVD, gone three years).
- Anna has decided to stay at the park (reaffirmed: "I think I'll stay"); she keeps the new eagle A-cam installed herself.
- FBI jurisdiction precedent set for park cases; Anna–Stanton banter continues (humming, bedbug check, "Don't let the bedbugs bite").
- Paulson's land-grabbing scheme is a new long arc: he wants the river property (owner's wife: Yasmina) and has openly vowed Anna won't last.
- The male eagle returns to the nest at episode's end — the "dad came back" beat mirrors Jake's love for his kids; the betting pool is settled.

## Jokes & references
- "Where there's a grave, there's usually a body, Geoff" — Anna's CSI-style line; Geoff's "regular pet cemetery" retort.
- "Bring you a mouse" eagle-cam flirtation, paid off by Jesse's stuffed-mouse gift.

## S01E03

# S01E03 "The Preacher" — Anna Pigeon

## Episode Plot
A series of bank robberies hits the area using a chilling MO: the robber shows tellers photos of their families and says, "God doesn't want anyone to die today." This matches a 20-year-old Detroit case — the uncaught "Preacher." When a controlled explosion in the park (to clear rotten trees) blows a buried toolbox of cash into the open, the serial numbers link to the current robberies. Manny, Anna's ranger colleague, is arrested after being framed — he found the money months earlier while illegally metal-detecting in the park to fund IVF for him and Bethany. Anna investigates, discovering the current pastor, Nelson, *is* the original Preacher. He buried his last score in the park decades ago, went straight, but was blackmailed into helping a new crew. The actual robber is Ken Thompson, a church member who handles explosives for the building expansion; his wife Lorraine (a teller from the Detroit robbery) is his accomplice. Pastor Nelson talks Ken down inside the bank; Ken surrenders after Lorraine appeals to their son. Anna calls in Guthrie (county/FBI) for the arrest.

- **Bethany**: Reveals IVF struggle; pleads for Manny; supports Anna.
- **Pastor Nelson**: Admits he was the original Preacher; buried the money; was blackmailed; enters bank to confront Ken; finds peace, suggests burning the photo of his last victim's family.
- **Ken Thompson**: Robs banks using Nelson's old MO; uses church explosives; holds up Copper Hill Bank; surrenders.
- **Lorraine**: Ken's wife; former teller at First Hancock Credit (Detroit); recognizes Nelson; tips Ken off; convinces him to surrender for their son.
- **Frederick Stanton (FBI)**: Appears in Montana; investigates Anna's husband Zach's murder; speaks with Molly (Anna's sister); learns of the red coat left at the party the night Zach died.

## S01E05

## S01E05

### Plot
Anna wakes trapped in an **oubliette** — a concealed pit with a single hatch exit — concussed, shoulder dislocated, no rope, no memory of how she got there. She’s been there at least a day. She rationally rules out a drunken fall or caving accident (wrong gear, no ropes) and realizes someone **put her there on purpose**. While dehydrated and hallucinating from a drugged canteen, she survives via **Third Man Syndrome**: her dead husband **Zach** and sister **Molly** appear, guiding her to use sensory triggers (sounds, smells, textures) to recover fragments of memory. She deduces the attacker doubled back and shoved her in after she witnessed something at a beach campsite.

Meanwhile, at the station, **Geoff** dismisses her absence — he thinks she fled to New York after their blowout fight (she’d booked a week off). **Jesse** and **Zoey** find her cabin emptied of clothes and hiking gear but the fridge still full; a box of her personal effects was mailed toward New York with $40 cash on top, ZIP smudged so it couldn’t be delivered. **Frieda** checks permits; **Zoey** defies orders and joins the search. The trail points to **Belly River Trail** (Anna’s favorite). A search party deploys with a dog.

Anna, near death, uses **dolomite rock (magnesium carbonate = climbing chalk)** to grip the wall and free-solos out. She’s found by **Kay**, an off-duty ranger camping nearby, who splints her ankle. The search team locates her abandoned camp — blood and hair in the tent — and the dog picks up a scent. The episode ends with Anna, rescued, having turned the tables: she lured her captor (“the monster”) into looking down at the empty pit, exploiting the psychology that “audiences are less interested in the seen than in the unseen.”

### Important Character Actions
- **Anna**: Survives 2+ days in the oubliette; diagnoses her own concussion/dislocation; rations drugged water (one capful/15 min); hallucinates Zach & Molly as survival mechanism; deduces intentional entrapment; climbs out using geology knowledge; identifies J.P. (the drunk beach clown from the bar fight) as likely perpetrator.
- **Zach (hallucination)**: Embodies Anna’s guilt — “You left me all alone… you deserve whatever happens.” Forces her to confront the night he died (she was drunk, didn’t lock the door, didn’t notice he was dead).
- **Molly (hallucination)**: Acts as therapist — explains Third Man Syndrome, trauma triggers, urges her to “do the next most necessary thing.”
- **Geoff**: Blames the “damn box,” assumes Anna quit; resists search until Zoey/Jesse force the evidence (empty cabin, staged departure).
- **Zoey**: Runs comms solo, checks backcountry permits, calls general store, rides out on search — “mentee becomes mentor” beat.
- **Jesse**: Finds the staged cabin, pushes for search, takes sat phone on rescue.
- **J.P. (beach guy)**: Drunk 23-year-old Anna humiliated at the bar (smashed his radio, refused to apologize). He and his crew are annual climbers at that beach — spatial behavior suggests he hid the crime in familiar terrain.
- **Kay**: Off-duty ranger; treats Anna’s ankle, gives her tensor bandage & trekking poles, becomes witness.

# S01E10 — Episode Summary

## Plot

End-of-season wind-down at the park. Seasonal rangers depart; Anna decides to stay through winter. A cold-open montage recaps season-one trauma: Zach's death, infidelity, Jeremiah Paulson, Anna prepping for a meeting with Molly (her sister, confirmed this episode), who coaches her to wear a blazer and makeup.

Geoff Miller calls: his dog Buddy is at the vet, sick from river contamination. Jeremiah Paulson poisoned the watershed with sulfuric acid to depress land prices, then bought the cheapened parcels. When Geoff refused to sell, JP vowed to take the land "for free" by ruining it. Geoff wants to sell; Anna pushes back ("it's not over 'til you fly into the sun and blow up").

Anna meets the county attorney about Kay's cave death. Bethany is now Bethany Paulson; the charges against her and JP are in separate trials. JP counter-offers: recant your testimony and I'll tell you about your husband's death — citing an NYPD clerk who says they have a suspect. Anna tells him to shove it.

She finds Dawson Wayze with a broken leg, patches him, and chats him up. Dawson name-drops an old guide named Harlan (in prison), asks after Anna's nephew (murder charges pending), and runs through local geology: his father mined copper at Butte; sapphires are mined locally; uranium is "the new big thing," leached with sulfuric acid. He then overpowers Anna, ties her with her own gear, steals her phone and pack, clips an ankle monitor on her, and admits "I'm a pretty bad person."

Cutaway: Bethany Paulson (née López) testifies that JP told her how he killed Kay and pushed her body into the cave, and that Jeremiah and JP threatened to kill her if she didn't help cover it up — directly contradicting Anna's testimony that Bethany acted alone. Bethany reveals Jeremiah is her father; he coerced her with the promise of acknowledgment.
