# Blodsoffer S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot

**Flashback (Two Years Earlier):** Peter Johansson (52, Hemfosa patrol officer) and partner Hugo Branković respond to a domestic disturbance in Järfälla. Hugo is pulled inside and stabbed. Peter radios for backup — dispatch says nearest unit is 20 minutes out. No one arrives. Hugo dies.

**Present Day:** A serial killer dubbed the "112 Murderer" (报警杀人案) is targeting police officers. Thomas Berg, rising homicide commissioner, leads the investigation. Peter Johansson is identified as the killer. During an arrest attempt, Thomas shoots Peter (non-fatally) but is pulled off the case — protocol forbids investigating a fellow officer you've shot. Rebecca Björklund takes over.

Thomas's colleague Max Abrahamsson discovers Johansson's dump site: a forest near Hemfosa where he displays severed heads, recreating Carl Larsson's painting *Midwinter Sacrifice* (仲冬祭献) — King Domalde of the Ynglings sacrificed after crop failures. The "king" is missing. Max goes alone, confronts Johansson, and is killed.

Alfred Berg, Thomas's estranged father and former high-ranking commander, visits the museum where his late wife worked. He meets Johansson there; they know each other. Alfred leaves with him — seemingly voluntarily, actually as the final "king" for the tableau.

Thomas realizes Alfred is the target. He tracks them to the forest in Järfälla. Peter forces a standoff: recreate the night Hugo died — total silence, no sirens, no backup. Thomas goes alone. Peter shoots Thomas (leg, punctured lung, 3 fractured ribs). Alfred is also badly wounded (2L blood loss, 3 fractured ribs). Thomas talks Peter down; Peter is arrested. Both Berg men survive, hospitalized.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Peter Johansson: 25-year veteran, Hemfosa, on sick leave (mental health); filed multiple complaints about missing backup call — all rejected
- **Dispatch records for Hugo's call were erased** — someone in management made them disappear
- Alfred Berg was the named dispatcher that night, but **not the final decision-maker**
- Thomas Berg: fast-tracked to homicide without patrol experience; resented by traditionalists
- Alfred commanded half the Stockholm force; job destroyed his marriage and relationship with Thomas
- Thomas and Charlotte have a young daughter; Charlotte relieved he resigned (he hasn't — suspended)
- **Max Abrahamsson is dead** — major loss for the team
