## S01E02

# The Body S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
The morning after the Halloween initiation at Devil's House, **Grace Franklin** lies in a **minimally conscious state** (CT shows no bleeding; toxicology returns **clean — no drugs in her system**). The Lions spiral into panic: Anya orchestrates a cover-up (delete photos, align stories: "studying at midnight"), while Elise increasingly fractures from the lie. **Leo Anderson** — Lisbeth's brother, who found Grace and called 911 — becomes the focus of police and Father Franklin's questioning; the Andersons' Pentecostal-cult backstory surfaces (mother Catherine has an autoimmune brain condition; family placed by Catholic Mission from West Virginia). Meanwhile, Lisbeth leverages the chaos to force a vote onto the Lions roster, then crashes a second, alcohol-only "initiation" at a club, gets drunk, and reveals she **kept photos** from the Halloween party. The episode ends with Elise blowing up the cover-up: she tells Maddie (and implicitly the group) that **Anya and Elise forced Grace to take ecstasy during her own initiation last year**, chanting **"Satan, take our souls. Take Grace as our sacrifice."** Grace's mother Theresa remains missing/unreachable; a lead named "Kumquat" vaguely recalls her.

## Important Character Actions
- **Anya**: Directs the cover-up; admits she supplied the E; uses college admissions as leverage; threatens to tell Leo if Elise talks.
- **Elise**: Initially complies, then rebels — reveals the truth about Grace's initiation to Maddie; confronts Anya ("You made her take the drugs").
- **Lisbeth**: Blackmails Anya into a team vote (wins 6/10); performs provocatively at the club; admits she didn't delete all photos; tells Anya she'll tell Leo if Elise is told.
- **Leo**: Questioned by Father Franklin and police; his mother Catherine defends him fiercely; takes the public blame ("He's the one taking the blame for it").
- **Father Franklin**: Manages crisis as priest/uncle; presses Leo on intentions; receives clean tox report from Dr. Rothholz.
- **Maddie**: Stress-eats donuts; terrified of expulsion/prison; learns the truth about Grace's initiation from Elise.
- **Olivia**: Vomits, shakes, admits she's on meds (Zoloft); begs Maddie not to tell Anya.

- **Minimally conscious state** (最低意识状态) — clinical designation for Grace's condition (not coma).
- **Kumquat** (金桔) — only lead on Theresa Franklin's whereabouts ("kinda remembers her").
- **Zoloft** (左洛复) — Grace was prescribed it; Elise/others speculate on depression/suicidality.
- **Springtime gala** (春季盛会) — fundraising event; Father Franklin invites Andersons as honorary co-chairs.
- **Initiation chant**: *"Satan, take our souls. Take Grace as our sacrifice."*
- **Lovely Bones'd** (verb) — slang for stalking/murdering like the novel/film.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Grace's **first initiation (year prior)**: Anya & Elise pressured her to take ecstasy; they chanted a mock-Satanic ritual.
- **Anya brought the drugs** to both parties.
- **Grace's phone is missing** — slept with it under her pillow; not at school, not at house.
- **Andersons' backstory**: Father died; mother Catherine has autoimmune encephalitis; placed by Catholic Mission (WV); Lisbeth dresses as Jesus for Halloween.
- **Father Franklin knew the family** before they arrived; tried to get Catherine to church.
- **Toxicology clean** — deepens mystery: not OD, not assault, not natural causes (aneurysm/stroke ruled out).

# S01E01 Summary — The Body

## Episode Plot
Halloween 2004 at St. Bartholomew's Catholic School (St. Barth's). Star Lions dancer **Maddie Delaney** breaks her ankle, triggering emergency tryouts. Newcomers **Grace Franklin** (Father Franklin's niece, gifted dancer) and **Lisbeth Anderson** (Pentecostal cult background, dresses as "Slutty God") audition. Grace outperforms, but co-captain **Anya Butler** invites Lisbeth to the Lions' secret initiation at the **Devil's House** (abandoned convent) anyway. The ritual — a half-joking Satanic "sacrifice" chant ("Prince of Darkness, take this most holy virgin sacrifice, and in exchange let us place at Regionals!") fueled by alcohol and ecstasy — ends with Grace seizing, stopping breathing, and lapsing into a coma. Only Anya and Lisbeth know the truth; they hide Grace in bed and swear silence.

- **Grace Franklin**: Earnest, devout, takes E at party, suffers seizure/respiratory arrest, becomes coma patient/visions focus.

## S01E05 — "The Exorcism at Devil's House"

### Episode Plot
Five days before Regionals, the Lions gather at Anya's house for intense rehearsal. Tensions fracture the team: **Elise confronts Anya** about Lisbeth's manipulation, revealing she stole Lisbeth's family cult videos; Anya deflects, accusing Elise of jealousy. Meanwhile, **Grace's condition baffles doctors** — she's breathing on her own with normal EEG, but reacts violently to the Lord's name. Catherine Anderson (Lisbeth's mother) claims Grace is possessed by Satan, telling Leo she "prayed the devil out of her" and nearly killed her.

At Anya's, the team spirals into a **drug-fueled crisis** (shrooms, alcohol). **Maddie breaks**, insisting the Halloween "sacrifice" was real — they invoked Satan, offered Grace, and now face demonic consequences. She demands they tell Father Franklin. **Lisbeth counters** with a ritual from her Pentecostal past: a "deliverance" ceremony to renounce the pact. The girls agree to perform it at **nightfall at the Devil's House** (the abandoned convent), when "the veil is thinnest."

The ritual becomes a **marathon confession-exorcism** led by Lisbeth (channeling her cult upbringing). Each Lion confesses sins — theft, infidelity, eating disorders, family trauma, sexual shame. **Maddie's "gluttony demon"** triggers a visceral possession-performance; the group screams "Get out!" until she collapses, "healed." Collective hysteria peaks: multiple girls claim to **see/feel Grace's spirit** in the room. Katie admits she faked her vision to mess with Anya. The energy breaks — "He's gone."

### Important Character Actions
- **Elise**: Confronts Anya with evidence (stolen cult videos); later refuses to participate in the ritual but stays; her eyes reportedly "turn black" during the snake incident.
- **Anya**: Hosts the practice/party; deflects Elise's warnings; agrees to the exorcism; has sex with Steven afterward (first time, emotional breakthrough).
- **Lisbeth**: Orchestrates the deliverance ritual using Pentecostal scripts; performs "snake rescue" at the end (kills a snake with an axe), cementing her hero status; earns the nickname **"Lizard"** and her Lions jacket (custom "L").
- **Maddie**: Catalyzes the exorcism; undergoes public "exorcism" of her eating disorder; believes Grace healed her.
- **Father Franklin**: Absent from the ritual but his authority looms; earlier at hospital, he shuts down Catherine's prayer over Grace.
- **Grace's parents (Michael, Theresa)**: Michael wants to move Grace home (fears Catherine); Theresa (nurse) witnesses inexplicable recovery; Father Franklin pushes for "trying everything" including prayer.
- **Steven**: Supports Anya through her sexual anxiety; they finally have sex post-ritual.

## S01E04 Summary

### Episode Plot
The mass hysteria spreads: a sixth Lion reports identical visions of Grace. Elise describes seeing Grace levitating in a red robe in the chapel; Bailey and Brit Bradley (twins) both convulse after "seeing" her. Father Franklin brings the Bishop in to contain the "foolishness," but the Bishop orders him to shut it down before it becomes a PR crisis. Meanwhile, Anya and Lisbeth scramble to control the narrative — Lisbeth convinces Anya to frame the visions as "spiritual" to divert attention from the Halloween ritual where they asked Satan to take Grace as a sacrifice. The girls' AIM group chat shows Grace briefly logged on, deepening the mystery.
