## Key Relationships

- **Lah Zhima → Lah Kara**: Father–daughter; master–apprentice in sabersmithing and Force basics
- **Margrave Juro ↔ Lah Zhima**: Former fellow Jedi Knights under Master Reno Tsutara; Juro commissions eight lightsabers from Zhima
- **Margrave Juro → Ethan / Homen / Kara**: Mentor / leader of the nascent Jedi restoration
- **Roden ↔ Niizo**: Sith acolytes; implied romantic bond (Roden grieves her death)
- **Sith Acolytes (Roden, Niizo, Hanbei, Hen Jin, Toguro, Homen initially)**: Infiltrate Juro's summons posing as masterless Jedi
- **99-99 → Lah Kara**: Loyal droid companion; emotional support
- **Gramps → Kara / Juro's group**: Reluctant transport; later joins rescue mission

## World Setting

- **Era**: Far future, long after *Episode IX – The Rise of Skywalker* (post-35 ABY); Jedi Order extinct, lightsabers lost to history
- **Planet Hy Izlan (ヒ・イズラン)**: Frozen Outer Rim world; kyber crystal deposits mined from orbit via pulley systems
- **Hy Izlan Aerial Temple (空中神殿)**: Orbital station; Juro's base, site of the summoning
- **Nawaam's Fortress**: Distant star system where Jedi Hunters take captured Zhima (referenced in *Child of Hope* / series)
- **Galaxy State**: "Era of War" (战乱时代); fragmented, war-torn, Sith operating from shadows

## Overall Plot Premise

Margrave Juro, ruler of Hy Izlan, secretly mines kyber crystals and commissions legendary sabersmith Lah Zhima to forge eight lightsabers. He broadcasts an encoded summons to Force-sensitives galaxy-wide, promising to restore the Jedi Order. Seven respondents gather at the Aerial Temple — but six are Sith acolytes sent to destroy the revival. Zhima is captured by Jedi Hunters; his daughter Kara flees with the lightsabers, pursued through snow forests. At the Temple, the Sith reveal themselves; Juro (hiding inside a steward droid) defeats them. Kara's blade ignites green — she is the "ninth Jedi." Juro, Ethan, redeemed Homen, Kara, and droids depart to rescue Zhima and rebuild the Order.

## Cultural Context & Adaptation Notes

- **Japanese Feudal Aesthetic**: Production I.G / Kenji Kamiyama infuse samurai/jidaigeki sensibility — Margrave = daimyō, sabersmith = swordsmith, Aerial Temple = mountain fortress. Chinese rendering should echo wuxia/xianxia register where fitting (e.g., "刀匠", "边境伯", "空中神殿").
- **Lightsaber Color Mechanics**: Blades are colorless until ignited by a Force-user; hue reflects wielder's connection (blue, green, purple, red). Translation must preserve "colorless/dark hue" → "亮起颜色" distinction.
- **"Ninth" Symbolism**: Nine = completion in Japanese culture (ku/kyū). Kara as ninth = hope fulfilled. Avoid numeric footnotes; let context carry weight.
- **Non-Canon / Alternate Timeline**: Explicitly non-canon (Lucasfilm "reimagined galaxy"). No need to harmonize with mainline SW continuity.
- **Manga & Sequel Continuity**: *The Ninth Jedi (manga)* and *Child of Hope* (Volume 3) expand lore; series *Visions Presents - The Ninth Jedi* (2026) continues directly. Terminology must stay consistent across all.
- **Disney+ Official Chinese Subtitles**: Use Disney+ Simplified Chinese (Disney+ 官方中字) as primary reference for approved terms (凯伯水晶, 光剑, 原力, 绝地, 西斯, 师父, 帕德旺, 边境伯).
