## S01E01

# Star Wars: Visions Presents - The Ninth Jedi — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The galaxy is in a "terrible age of conflict" after the Jedi and Sith have disappeared. The masked warlord **General Nawaam** rises with a massive fleet and **Nobuseri** fighters who swear allegiance to him. **Margrave Juro** of frozen **Hy Izlan** secretly mines **kyber crystals** and commissions legendary **sabersmith Lah Zhima** to forge eight lightsabers, broadcasting an encoded summons to Force-sensitives galaxy-wide to restore the Jedi Order.

The episode opens *in media res*: Juro's group (including Zhima's daughter **Kara**, droid **99-99 "Four-Nines"**, and pilot droid **Gramps**) flees Nawaam's commander **Gennoh** through a black hole escape. They seek aid from **Dunbai**, a former Jedi turned wealthy merchant ruling free city-state **Makoma Ko**. Dunbai refuses to join but gifts Kara a lightsaber forged by Zhima. Nawaam's forces attack Makoma Ko; Juro's team infiltrates Gennoh's ship, destroys the central droid control unit, and escapes. Meanwhile, Nawaam confronts captive **Zhima**, revealing Zhima is his "closest collaborator" — shattering Kara's faith. The episode ends with Nawaam igniting a blue blade, implying he is a Force-user.

## Important Character Actions
- **Kara**: Repairs ship systems under fire; distracts Gennoh in lightsaber duel to buy time for droid sabotage; learns her father may be collaborating with Nawaam.
- **Juro**: Leads infiltration of enemy ship; reveals he has "not forgotten the basic principles of the Jedi's teachings"; gives Dunbai a lightsaber to protect Makoma Ko.
- **Gramps**: Pilots through black hole gravity well; hides ship during battle; flies suicidal approach to enemy vessel.
- **Dunbai**: Refuses Juro's call but defends Makoma Ko's autonomy against Nawaam; keeps gifted lightsaber for planetary defense.
- **Gennoh**: Tracks Juro across jumps; commands Nawaam's ground assault; duels Kara, recognizes her as Zhima's daughter.
- **Nawaam**: Offers army command to anyone who kills him; captures Zhima; reveals he is Force-sensitive (blue blade).

- **Kara is Force-sensitive**; her blade ignites green (shown in context, not yet in this episode).

## S01E06

# Star Wars: Visions Presents - The Ninth Jedi — S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot

Kara flees through a frozen landscape pursued by bounty hunters **Bee-Toh** and **Eht**, who reveal her father **Zhima** single-handedly destroyed a Nawaam outpost without killing anyone. Kara experiences a Force vision of **Margrave Juro** counseling her: her lightsaber turned red when she lost emotional control, but Juro teaches that dark emotions don't make one Sith — only letting them control you does. The Zhima-forged blades reflect the wielder's inner state.

Kara engages the hunters in combat. Her blade shifts to **silver/white** when she achieves a "no-mind" state, allowing her to predict attacks. She defeats Eht but spares him, claiming his life in service ("from now on your life belongs to me"). Bee-Toh, impressed, switches sides after Kara offers him strong enemies to fight "on the side of justice." They depart to rescue Zhima and confront **Nawaam's** army.

## Important Character Actions

- **Kara**: Confronts her red-blade trauma; achieves silver blade through Force clarity; spares enemies; asserts leadership by claiming Bee-Toh and Eht as followers.
- **Juro (Force vision)**: Guides Kara past guilt; reframes Jedi teaching — "being a Jedi doesn't mean not having these feelings."
- **Bee-Toh**: Switches from antagonist to reluctant ally; motivated by combat thrill, not ideology.
- **Eht**: Defeated, pressed into Kara's service; pragmatic survivor.
- **Zhima (off-screen)**: Rampaged at Nawaam outpost; non-lethal destruction suggests retained Jedi restraint.

## S01E03

## Episode S01E03 Summary

### Plot
Bibin faces evacuation as a massive kyber-powered cannon on Yaago fires on the planet. Kwana coordinates the desperate exodus, unloading ore haulers to maximize refugee capacity. Juro, Tafflah, and Kara discover Master Reno's old ship in the palace maintenance deck, revealing a hidden hold large enough to save everyone. Meanwhile, the truth about the "incident" emerges: Zhima (Kara's father) killed Master Reno after discovering he'd corrupted the Jedi Order into an illegal ore-trading syndicate. The episode ends with the survivors departing for Yaago on a suicide mission—asteroid ships disguised as a belt to ram the enemy flagship before the cannon is repaired.

### Important Character Actions
- **Kwana** commands the evacuation, orders ore offloaded, summons Asteroid Diggers, and devises the Yaago infiltration plan
- **Juro** investigates the weapon with Ethan, confronts the truth about Reno's corruption, and accepts Zhima acted justifiably
- **Tafflah** reveals the full history of Reno, Zhima, and the Jedi Order's rise and fall on Bibin
- **Kara** learns her father Zhima was a war-obsessed Jedi who killed his master, then became a sabersmith
- **Nawaam** (Yaago's Force-sensitive general) holds Zhima's former apprentice(?), calls Zhima "Master," and prepares alternative attacks after the cannon fails
- **Gennoh** pressures Nawaam to attack Bibin directly

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Bibin** — mining planet under evacuation
- **Yaago** — enemy stronghold housing the kyber cannon
- **Nawaam** — Yaago's general, Force user, calls Zhima "Master"
- **Gennoh** — Nawaam's subordinate
- **Homen** — helps plan the asteroid-belt infiltration
- **Baro Do** — missing spaceport leader found dead in Reno's secret hold
- **Sabersmith** — Zhima's post-Jedi profession
- **The Incident** — Zhima killing Reno, triggering the Order's dissolution
- **Hy Izlan** — where Juro went, leaving Zhima as Reno's sole apprentice

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Only **50 Jedi Masters** survived galaxy-wide; Reno Tsutara was one
- Reno rebuilt the Order on Bibin with local support, expanded trade, and secretly replaced officials to run illegal ore operations
- Zhima was dispatched to eliminate Sith hired by rival systems for trade advantage
- Zhima's lightsaber did something inexplicable during the duel (color change noted); his eyes disturbed witnesses
- After the incident, Juro **disbanded the Jedi Order** entirely
- The cannon functions as a **giant lightsaber** powered by kyber crystals
- Nawaam's use of "Master" for Zhima implies Zhima may have taken an apprentice after leaving Bibin—unknown to Kara or Juro

### Jokes / References
- **"Dank farrik"** — Mandalorian-era profanity used by Juro
- **"May the Force be with you all"** — standard Jedi farewell

### Unresolved Threads
- Why did Zhima's lightsaber behave abnormally during the duel? What did his eyes show?
- Who is Nawaam's "Master" reference pointing to—did Zhima train an apprentice after Bibin?
- Where is Zhima now?
- Will the asteroid-belt ramming plan succeed against Yaago's heavy armor?
- What becomes of the Bibin refugees after Yaago?
- What is Nawaam's full connection to Zhima and the Force?

# S01E02 Summary — Star Wars: Visions Presents – The Ninth Jedi

## Episode Plot
Kara and Gramps travel to **Bipin (比宾)** per Juro's instructions. They discuss a red lightsaber spotted at **Makoma Ku (马科马库)** — possibly forged by Kara's father, Lah Zhima. Flashback reveals former Master **Reno Tsutara (雷诺·图塔拉)** attempted to revive the Jedi Order; darkness (the Sith) rose in response. **General Nawaam (诺姆将军)**, now allied with remnant Sith, has captured Zhima to exploit his lightsaber-forging tech for a galaxy-spanning **kyber crystal cannon (凯伯水晶炮)**.

Juro asks Kuanan's miners to join a resistance against Nawaam. Kuanan refuses, citing the tragedy where a Jedi master (Reno) was killed by his own padawan (implied: Zhima). Juro gives Kuanan a lightsaber forged by Zhima, declaring faith in him. Moved, Kuanan and the miners pledge allegiance.

Nawaam broadcasts a galaxy-wide surrender ultimatum and fires the kyber cannon, obliterating a planet that harbored Jedi. Juro, Kara, Ethan, Homen, Kuanan, and the miners unite to rescue Zhima from **Nawaam's Fortress**.

## S01E04

# S01E04 Summary — Star Wars: Visions Presents – The Ninth Jedi

## Episode Plot
Nawaam launches a surprise attack on a civilian refugee fleet using "meteor ships" to draw out Margrave Juro. Juro positions his forces as a shield for the evacuees. Meanwhile, Ethan infiltrates Nawaam's flagship to rescue Lah Zhima, revealing he trains under Juro. Nawaam confronts Kara, revealing he was Zhima's secret apprentice who witnessed Zhima kill his own master, Reno Tsutara. Nawaam seeks the "nothingness" state Zhima achieved in that moment—a void beyond Jedi or Sith he believes can bring galactic peace. At the bridge, Juro duels Nawaam but is distracted protecting Kara. Juro gives her his lightsaber and urges her to flee. Zhima ignites a captured saber; it glows silver, proving he has reattained nothingness. Nawaam orders retreat, destroys his own fleet, declares Juro dead, and dispatches Jedi Hunters to eliminate the remaining Jedi. The episode ends with Ethan calling desperately for Juro, Kara, and Homen.

## S01E07

## S01E07 Summary — Star Wars: Visions Presents – The Ninth Jedi

### Episode Plot
Nawaam's fleet arrives at Hy Izlan. The Bibin Defense Fleet under Lady Tafflah jumps in as reinforcements. Kara returns to the fortress via comms, reuniting with her captured father Zhima. Zhima reveals the kyber crystal at the fortress is of such extreme purity that firing it through the super-cannon would eradicate everything from Hy Izlan to the galactic rim. Zhima stays to guard the crystal while Kara, Ethan, Homen, Kwana, Bee-Toh, and the droids join the fleet assault. Kara senses Zhima in mortal danger and turns back; the others follow. Inside the fortress, Gennoh — a heavily upgraded cyborg seeking revenge against Zhima — duels Zhima and gains the upper hand. The crystal is seized by Nawaam's forces. Zhima orders Kara's group to destroy the cannon at all costs while he goes after Nawaam to end the conflict.

### Important Character Actions
- **Kara**: Re-establishes contact with Zhima; defies his order to leave and returns to rescue him; her green blade confirms her as the Ninth Jedi.
- **Zhima**: Reveals the crystal's uncontrollable power; stays behind to defend it; admits his past choice corrupted young Nawaam; sends Kara away to survive; departs to hunt Nawaam.
- **Gennoh**: Engages Zhima in a high-speed duel using cybernetic upgrades; seizes the crystal for Nawaam; declares revenge his only motive.
- **Bhozn**: Commands the fortress fleet defense; questions Gennoh's priorities.
- **Ethan / Homen / Kwana / Bee-Toh**: Join the fleet attack; follow Kara back into the fortress.
- **Lady Tafflah & Bibin Fleet**: Arrive as allied reinforcements; coordinate the external assault on the fortress power core.
- **Gramps**: Pilots the transport; detects incoming ships; ferries the group.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Nawaam (拉维)** — Warlord commanding the invading army; former youngling under Master Reno; twisted by Zhima's past error into a "monster."
- **Gennoh (格奴)** — Cyborg enforcer hunting Zhima; uses speed-augmented body and predictive tracking; wants revenge, not the crystal.
- **Bhozn (保赞)** — Fleet commander under Nawaam; blunt, loyal.
- **Lady Tafflah (塔夫拉夫人)** — Commander of the Bibin Defense Fleet; ally of Juro's cause.
- **Fort Dominion (统御堡垒)** — Nawaam's fortress housing the kyber cannon.
- **Kyber Cannon (凯伯水晶巨炮)** — Superweapon built around an ultra-pure kyber crystal; capable of galaxy-scale destruction.
- **Meteorite Fleet (陨石舰队)** — Allied fleet element referenced in battle orders.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The crystal's purity grade is unprecedented; its discharge would be uncontrollable and sterilize a line to the galactic edge.
- Nawaam was present when Zhima chose the "wrong path" during Master Reno's Jedi restoration attempt; Zhima accepts responsibility for creating him.

# Star Wars: Visions Presents - The Ninth Jedi — S01E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Time has passed since the battle on Bibin. Having escaped Juro's army, **Nawaam** has expanded his power and seized territories galaxy-wide; the Jedi — shattered by the loss of Juro — wage a guerrilla war, while Nawaam's probe droids intercept communications everywhere. **Lah Kara**, who witnessed her Master Juro cut down by Nawaam, is missing.

On a holographic call, Nawaam tells **Lord Gennoh** — who owes his new body to Nawaam — not to bother recapturing the escaped Zhima: he is "no longer necessary." The kyber crystal cannon is almost complete; the small-crystal resonator failed under sustained output, so it will now be powered by a single giant kyber crystal — which lies on **Hy Izlan**, Zhima's former hideout and Juro's former domain. Nawaam sets course for Hy Izlan. Addressing his army, he quotes Zhima's teaching ("to defeat a great darkness, one must utilize an even greater power"), declaring his army and cannon serve "justice" to end the era of war; pressed that this is Sith doctrine, he insists, "I am no Sith."

At the resistance camp, **Kwana**'s group learns **Dunbai** still won't answer, and Jedi Hunters force constant relocation; rumor spreads that Nawaam overthrew **General Shozan** single-handedly. **Homen** returns with good news — the frontier resistance (the **Delphidian leader**) will join the fight, won over by young **Ethan**'s coded message. Kwana rallies morale; the group's Force-sensitives sense Kara is alive.

Hiding on an uncharted, primitive world, Kara befriends native child **Eesha** (who no longer fears her), offering her a lightsaber if she becomes a Jedi to protect people. She mourns Juro, blaming her own "insolence" for his death, and recalls that he saved her long ago ("Save me, Father!"). Jedi Hunters arrive; the commander sees through her clever hiding, and a squad burns her village. Kara saves Eesha, sends the villagers to the river, kills the hunters aboard their ship and spares the passengers — only to discover the hunters hold Eesha and a lightsaber: "We'll use this one as bait. If you want your little one back, then you will bring us the Jedi girl." Kara: "You are a real piece of work, you know that?"

## Important Character Actions
- **Nawaam**: Continues his conquest; orders rebel suppression; declares Zhima expendable; reveals the cannon's new power source is Hy Izlan and sails for it; frames tyranny as "justice" and denies being Sith.
- **Gennoh**: Reports in from his gifted new body; begs to redeem his failure by recapturing Zhima; accepts Nawaam's orders.
- **Kara**: Survives alone on a primitive world; befriends Eesha; grieves Juro with self-blame; defends the village; kills the hunters; spares ship passengers; faces the bait ultimatum.
- **Kwana**: Holds the camp together ("Lose heart, and you'll play right into his hands"); insists they cannot run forever.
- **Homen**: Returns bearing the frontier resistance's acceptance.
- **Ethan**: Recruits the Delphidian leader via coded message ("rare to see a young Jedi like me in battle"); vows to find every fighter still willing to resist.
- **Jedi Hunter commander**: Dismisses the planet, then deduces Kara's evasion tactics; orders the village burned; deploys the "bait" scheme.
