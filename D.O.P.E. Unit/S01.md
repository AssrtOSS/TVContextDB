## S01E01

Note: the the series background file is a verification-failure log (it could not confirm the title against public sources), so this summary is drawn directly from the aligned EN/ZH subtitle file for the episode.

## Episode Plot

Cold open: in a Fishtown (Philadelphia) alley, Trey — son of Police Chief Terrance Watkins — is cornered by a street crew (including Skully) who accuse him of hoarding their money and product for weeks. They mock him with "This ain't the fuckin' '90s — Cash App, Apple Pay, Venmo exist." Trey says he came only out of respect for Blackwell, his financier, and leans on his father's rank. The crew leader stops Skully from killing a cop's son, then kills Trey himself. His body is found in an alley 20 feet from the Roths' S&G steakhouse and strip club.

At the funeral, Trey's mother Maria blames Terrance for pushing Trey toward the force ("That's where you lost him"); the chief vows at the casket that whoever is responsible will face justice. News coverage asks why the chief's son was in that alley.

The chief secretly forms a task force within the PPD Special Operations D.O.P.E. unit targeting the Roth crime family (money laundering, drugs, prostitution, run out of S&G, owned by Brianna "BB" Brown). It's personal. The Commissioner approves with one condition: the chief is too emotionally involved, so a lead agent must run it — the chief picks veteran undercover Special Agent Jimmy Sullivan. The chief also pulls a fresh Academy recruit, Officer King (cover story: she flunked out), with one mission: get close to Malcolm Carter and find out who killed Trey. He orders Parker and Reyes to keep the plan between the three of them.

Parallel: Zeke Williams returns after six months, orders the family to lay low and exit the drug business ("these new kids are stupid and sloppy"), tells them to use the club's girls for intel, and notes his cops on the payroll are walking on eggshells. He executes snitch Skully ("Snitches don't get passes") and puts the word out: whoever shot the cop's kid better turn themselves in — or else. At the evening debrief, Sullivan assigns Elijah Washington (find the family's weakest link) and JVon Dorsey (use his celebrity to open doors), and both must locate M.I.A. deep-cover Officer Natia Drummond. Officer King accepts her mission.

## S01E07

# D.O.P.E. Unit — S01E07 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with an extended "Previously on" recap covering Trinity Blackwell's alliance with Taft, Malcolm and Skully's $500K score plan, King and Wash discovering the hit on Trey came from inside law enforcement, and BB's 24-hour ultimatum to Malcolm and Zeke.

In the present, Malcolm and Wash respond to a neighborhood disturbance where a racist patrol officer is hassling local youth. Wash confronts the officer, who backs down after Malcolm flashes his detective badge. They turn body cameras back on and head out for steak and waffles.

King meets Chief Watkins on a rooftop. Watkins warns that Malcolm is looking to cut a deal and skip town, and that no one knows what loose ends he'll tie up on his way out. After King leaves, Bull — who has been watching — confronts Summer on the same rooftop. He's deduced she's a cop (never seen in lockup or the department, yet meeting Chief Watkins). He threatens to tell Malcolm unless she and Malcolm stop scheming against his client. Summer denies being law enforcement; Bull doesn't believe her.

Sullivan is revealed having an affair with an unnamed woman connected to the Roth organization. He discloses that Watkins has been sidelined and that Reyes and Parker now report to him — and he controls what flows upward. He warns her that undercover agents plan to raid her club looking for Zeke and Malcolm (no warrant yet), and delivers a bombshell: Carmen Serrano flipped, wore a wire, and recorded Blackwell agreeing to a huge exchange. The dope unit is actively setting up Blackwell's takedown. He promises to protect his lover by making her "off-limits" — marriage — and reveals the Roth family's real estate play: buy cheap gentrified buildings, flip them through crypto so the money can never be traced. She pushes back, questioning whether his love is real or just a means to keep eyes off the family business. He insists it's genuine, claims he's divorcing his wife, and drops another bomb: his girlfriend Tracey has been talking to cops — he has her file. This shifts the dynamic; she agrees to trust him.

Chief Watkins, despite being on mandatory leave, tells Reyes he met with King and is alarmed that Jayda King is losing herself inside her undercover persona "Summer." Reyes warns the Commissioner will take his badge if he keeps contacting her. Watkins is adamant: he put her there and won't lose another officer after Trey.

The episode's climax: someone (strongly implied to be Malcolm or an associate acting on BB's orders) ambushes the car carrying Chief Watkins, Reyes, and Yara. The shooter empties the entire clip, then calls someone to report he "fixed everything" for BB. Yara is killed (D.O.A.). Reyes and Watkins are rushed to the OR — Watkins flatlines but is revived. The Commissioner holds a defiant press conference vowing to crush the family syndicates. Parker and Reyes (the latter in a coma) are at the hospital; the Commissioner doesn't visit, busy with reporters.

Summer has a dissociative episode, repeatedly hearing "Remember who you are" before grounding herself: "You are Jayda King. Member of the dope unit." She visits a club looking for Nova and learns Nova hasn't been seen since Lou's death. Someone at the club recognizes Summer and calls it in. The episode ends with the caller reporting they have eyes on one of them.
