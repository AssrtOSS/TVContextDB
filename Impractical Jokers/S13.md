## S13E02

# Impractical Jokers S13E02 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode opens at an office where the Jokers play receptionists during fake focus groups. Murr pranks Sal by placing a hot plate on his chair before the challenge begins. Sal must sit through a focus group while the seat grows progressively hotter (reaching 106°F), enduring physical discomfort while performing scripted bits—including telling an attendee "our shitter is only good for pee-pee today." The prank escalates when Sal is told he has a sudden Zoom court appearance mid-session. "Judge Johnna Scrabis" puts Sal on trial for the "summary offense of dangerous burning" after allegedly setting fire to a kindergarten classroom and yelling "Sorry, nerds." Sal is sentenced to 14 days at the Percy Correction Facility and forced to repeat the word "fortnight" three times. When the judge adjourns court ("All rise!"), Sal must stand—breaking the hot seat rule and losing the challenge.

In subsequent receptionist rounds, the Jokers take turns behind the desk:
- One Joker measures a participant named Zeke's head circumference with a tape measure, then joins a Zoom funeral for "Arthur 'Artie' Delicata," excusing himself mid-eulogy to take a phone call, becoming emotional on his birthday, singing "Hallelujah" during the homily, and screaming "Get the [bleep] out!" at co-workers entering the room while smashing breakaway glasses.
- Another Joker greets Zeke with "What the fart, dude?" and claims they robbed a grave together.
- Q endures a frustrating round with a particularly difficult participant, John Szeluga ("Hard Jimmy"), who repeatedly walks out of the focus group. Q must keep coaxing him back in; Szeluga eventually leaves a third time, making Q lose.

The next major challenge takes place at Lume Studios, where crew members form a line for a fake pop-up event. Each Joker must convince passersby to wait in line using absurd premises:
- **Sal**: Claims the President of Papua New Guinea is inside, letting people sit on his lap and granting one wish each. He succeeds after delivering "good news and bad news" (lap access yes, wishes no).
- **Murr**: Claims the U.S. government is unveiling a brand-new color called "Dripple." He explains it using the Native Americans-not-understanding-ships analogy and declares only women can see it, successfully drawing in the crowd.
- **Q**: Claims former NYC Mayor David Dinkins' tomb is being opened for public viewing. Q gets too close to the "tomb" and is thrown out, failing the challenge.

Q is declared the night's big loser and faces punishment at the Carnegie Diner and Café.

## Punishment: The Time Machine

Q is placed inside a prop "time machine" while Sal and Murr (wearing tight wigs) pose as his friends from 23 months in the future, "calling back through time." They present Q with a newspaper from the future containing three horrific headlines, each requiring Q to complete an embarrassing task to "change the course of history":

1. **"Residential gas leak causes explosion—woman couldn't find house keys fast enough."** Q must dump a diner patron's purse contents onto the table to locate her keys. He complies; tampons spill out. The newspaper "updates"—she finds her keys in time.
2. **"Scientist on the brink of curing cat cancer fired because of marijuana drug-screening tests."** Q must approach a patron (a "scientist") and insist he accept a jar of clean urine for his drug test, saving all cats from cancer. Q reluctantly hands over the vial.
3. **"Top spies poisoned, U.S. Intelligence 'in tatters.'"** Q must stop two female patrons (American spies) from eating their food by swiping their entire table clean onto the floor. Q completes the task, saving the spies.

The episode closes with Q noting the wig has been cutting off circulation to his brain for three hours.

- **Q**: Struggles through the Szeluga receptionist round, loses the Dinkins tomb challenge, and endures all three phases of the time machine punishment.
- **Crew members**: Mike (running the pop-up event), Mark, and Dan play supporting roles in the line challenge.

## Plot

Three segments, one loser.

**Challenge 1 — "I Owe You One" (focus group waiting room).** Each joker sits next to a stranger, accepts a small favor (a cookie), and says "I owe you one." Producer Cole then dismisses the participant; the joker must storm into the moderator's office and flip out defending them — success requires the participant to choose to stay.
- Murr (target: Jeremy): "gorilla nuts" rant, tells Cole to put his "gorilla nuts in the mouth of goodness." Jeremy stays all day; Murr "took the bullet" and went home with full pay.
- Q (target: Dana): slippery-slope speech — "today they silence you on cookies, tomorrow cakes, then camps and the secret police, and soon the whole world forgets pie." Offers himself ("if you want me, take me"). Dana stays.
- Sal (target: Tara): "In 1993, when IBM fired 60,000 employees, I sat back and did nothing. Today is a new day." Murr storms in for him (locks the door; the moderator dials 911). Tara stays.
- All three succeed, so nobody hits the loser board.

**Challenge 2 — Cringiest Pickup Line (Tompkins Square Bagels).** Each joker hits on fake crush Kate (a producer), then gets a cringe rating from a customer; lowest rating loses.
- Sal sings "You're My Bagel Girl" — rated 7.92.
- Q uses the dead-sister/angel line plus "did you hurt your mom's vagina when she gave birth to a giant angel?" — rated "soft eight" (8.0).
- Murr's crush is his own wife; his line is literally "Hey, bitch" (she replies "No, bitch") — rated 8.5.
- Sal's 7.92 is lowest → Sal is the loser.

**Punishment — "Sal's House."** Sal's "dream comes true": his own sitcom pilot, but with no script and no direction, starting immediately. Set in his real childhood bedroom (circa 1987, rebuilt with original sheets, posters, and photos raided from his mom's house — "the Sal Vulcano Archives"). Alyssa Milano, his childhood crush, walks in. Sal must read his actual age-12 love letters, recite a poem ("Lyss, lyss, lyss… I breathe your air, I keep your hair… keep me as a pet"), then play Truth or Dare (truth: he itemizes his daily supplement pills; dare: Alyssa "autographs" his forehead — writing "dork"). The finale is Spin the Bottle with his "friends" (Donnie Baseball, George, and Benjamin Cat): the bottle lands on Donnie Baseball, Alyssa calls for "a pinch hitter," and Sal never gets the kiss. Ends with "See you next week" and the theme song.

**Challenge 1 — Fake Family / Disney Twist (Livoti's Old World Market).** The Jokers shop with "fake kid" Melana and ask a stranger to film them surprising her with Disney tickets — then rip the tickets away for a made-up misdeed. You're fine!"); ride a Segway continuously ("A Murr in motion has to stay in motion"); and ask couples "Quick question, Papa/Mama — how will I know which key is yours?" Final task: get a couple heading "across town" to give crew member Mike Cast a ride — "He's the neighborhood walkabout." Mike seals it with "You cool if I suck on this pot?" and "Do you guys party?"; "Right on. Across town!" — the punishment ends early. Murr: "I don't think I have the guts for this show anymore."
