## S02E03

# S02E03 摘要

## 主要剧情
- 维与苏菲在宿舍完成原创单曲《My Bad》的录影（苏菲担任导演），歌词宣泄维对以利亚的怨气。
- 暗线反派（旁白身份暂未公开）以"大脚怪的脚趾甲、小妖怪的鼻涕、龙疣"为材料（台词特别标注"伦理采集"）熬制爱情灵药，计划让以利亚喝下，使其爱上第一眼见到的人——布兰妮，从而把两人作秀恋情变成真的，并让以利亚彻底忘记维。反派把维与以利亚的组合称为 "Vee-lijah"，目标组合是 "Brit-lijah"。
- 餐厅里苏菲拿错了果昔——原本是给以利亚的特调"Elijah Special"（花生酱、巧克力、香蕉加可可碎），被苏菲喝下。海瑟接过苏菲多点的另一杯。
- 布兰妮与以利亚在社媒发布舞蹈影片，配乐《Maybe I'm Falling》是维与以利亚的"专属情歌"，且来自热门百老汇音乐剧。维心碎。
- 苏菲自封"挺女生城巿长（Mayor of Girl's Girl Town）"，引导维承认愤怒并把《My Bad》公开发布。
- 以利亚赶来解释：与布兰妮只是作秀恋情（PR relationship），目的是涨粉。维准备原谅，但《My Bad》已经外流。
- 反派在餐桌旁听到以利亚对维的表白后说出"Cupid, we have a problem"——目标没喝到灵药，喝下灵药的是苏菲。立即让语音助理把"做灵药解药"加入待办。
- 黛米在午餐时段借《Chef's Kiss》餐车 MC 桥段对苏菲唱出告白情歌，并当面说"I love you"。苏菲已被灵药影响/后来被人递了一杯"草莓香蕉+消除记忆解药"的果昔，告白被她遗忘。她以"我们只是朋友"回应，并提到有个"愚蠢的预言"。
- 维的影片被獠牙帮（Fang Gang）疯传，"to Elijah it"被当成动词（搞砸恋情）。布兰妮因丑闻拖累互动率，公开与以利亚切割并退追。
- 维在粉丝面前发布重制版《My Bad》——新结尾为和解段落，原谅以利亚与布兰妮；獠牙帮接受。以利亚把维的手机桌布身份拿出来，维正式和解，两人约定"别再让任何事把我们分开"。黛米在旁边幽幽地吐槽："了解了，预言，我们只是朋友。"

## 关键角色动作
- 维：把私人情绪转化为两版主打歌；先中伤、后公开为以利亚正名；最终确认以利亚为其手机桌布对象。
- 苏菲：担任导演、"挺女生城巿长"；误饮灵药；忘了黛米告白；明确划在"朋友线"。
- 以利亚：默认布兰妮用换歌版剪辑但不知情；为涨粉签 PR 恋情；被獠牙帮网暴。
- 布兰妮：为涨粉与以利亚做 PR；丑闻拖累数据后立刻切割；后来和以利亚一同被维在舞台上翻案。
- 黛米：写出告白歌、亲自演唱告白；搬出 600 岁"招牌动作——装室内电话吓人"自嘲；被苏菲一句话打发。
- 反派（未公开姓名）：制灵药、瞄准范·海辛血脉、临时改做解药。
- 海瑟：餐厅中接过苏菲的多余果昔，对巧克力让她亢奋有自觉。
- 托妮（Toni）：獠牙帮成员，对以利亚当面甩出"你牙上有谎言之网"的吐槽。

- 维的全名 Vampirina "Vee" Bubonica Hauntley、与以利亚的吸血鬼猎人家族范·海辛对立设定延续。
- 维的父亲指派黛米监护的安排仍生效；黛米能穿入物体。

- "Vee-lijah" 已是粉丝公认的 CP 称呼。

- 黛米暗恋苏菲的设定本集首次正面展开，被苏菲以"只是朋友"挡回。

## 对后续剧集有用的笑点 / 梗

- 苏菲 "草莓香蕉+解药" 的经历可与未来灵药/解药设定联动。
- Sor-Siri 作为反派的现代化助手，可继续承担反派独白桥段。

## 未解决的线索

- 苏菲吞下的爱情灵药是否仅产生短暂遗忘，还是有更深的咒语副作用，黛米并不知情。
- 那条"愚蠢的预言"内容、对谁生效仍未交代。

- 维接下来如何继续隐藏吸血鬼身份——反派已经在场并锁定以利亚的猎人血脉，暴露风险上升。

## S02E01

# S02E01 — Vampirina: Teenage Vampire

## Plot
Recap (Vee narrating): Season 1 — Wilson Hall year one, opened for Millie Eyelash, crush on Van Helsing Elijah, Lucien locked in the Mirror Prison then escaped and freed other monsters. The gang spent the summer hunting the remaining escapees. Just before Halloween, a "creature collector" demon warns that a vampire and a Van Helsing working together is forbidden by prophecy — then Demi accidentally disintegrates him before the full text is heard.

Day one of year two: Dean Merriweather opens with a song-and-baton number. Vee is publicly celebrated as "Vampirina" and as Millie's protégé (no response yet to the demos she sent). New transfer student Hazel arrives, a starstruck fan of both Vampirina and DJ PJ. Britney blocks Hazel's roommate placement by producing a signed contract — Delilah, the dorm bot, overrides her and Britney relents. Hazel is assigned the teacher's wing "penthouse" with a hot-tub delivery; Britney gets a designer-room reveal and Sophie gives Hazel backup designer PJs and DJ Mini Monster headphones.

Vee and Elijah agree they need to research the prophecy together, but try to stay apart in class. In Modern Movement (Ms. Olsen), the first assignment is contact improv — the teacher pairs Vee and Elijah. Hazel keeps wandering into their orbit. While practicing back-to-back (Demi's "science" suggestion), Demi finds the cause of the blasts: Elijah's Van Helsing ring. The ring falls off, vanishes, and the full prophecy surfaces from a talking head:

> "Natural enemies destined to fight. Van Helsings and vampires must never unite, or the world as we know it shall end on Halloween night."

A meteor slams through the attic the moment they touch. Their contact-improv showcase becomes "eye contact with contact lenses," which Ms. Olsen calls the epitome of contact improv. They reluctantly decide to take space. The episode closes on Millie Eyelash calling Vee with an offer to record an album for her label and livestream the first single on Halloween.

## Important Character Actions
- Vee: narrates the S1 wrap-up; publicly performs as Vampirina; agrees with Elijah to research the prophecy; dances with Elijah in class; accepts Millie's album/livestream offer.
- Elijah: reveals the Van Helsing ring is what's been blasting them apart; reluctantly agrees to "take space" from Vee after the meteor.
- Demi: accidentally disintegrates the prophecy-bearing demon with the creature collector's disintegration button; later locates the ring-as-cause in the family books.
- Sophie (DJ PJ): gives Hazel designer PJs and DJ Mini Monster headphones as a "pay it forward" gesture from her own first-year loneliness; has 60k followers vs Britney's 5k.
- Britney: blocks the new roommate with a single-room contract; reveals a designer dorm and hot tub; accepts Hazel only after Sophie agrees to a selfie.
- Dean Merriweather: introduces the year, name-drops Vampirina publicly, sends first-years to orientation.
- Hazel: posed as a starstruck transfer student; secretly reports to her "Sorcerer Queen"; first appears at a "penthouse" she claims to have moved into.
- Sorcerer Queen (off-screen, with cat Merlin/Marlin): assigns Hazel to keep Vee and Elijah apart; congratulates her, then reveals "mission just begun."
- Ms. Olsen: teaches Modern Movement, refuses to take the ring risk seriously, celebrates the contact-lens improv.

## New Names / Terms
- Hazel — new transfer student, sorcerer apprentice planted in the friend group.
- Sorcerer Queen — Hazel's mentor; has a cat named Merlin (called "Marlin" by Hazel, which meows in response).
- Delilah — the dorm / school AI; produces and enforces housing contracts, barges into Britney's room uninvited.
- Ms. Olsen — Modern Movement teacher; runs contact improv.
- Creature Collector — the demon carrying the prophecy, disintegrated by Demi before finishing.
- Mirror Prison — season-1-set holding cell for Lucien; now has multiple escapees.
- "Force of Nature" — Sophie's DJ PJ track heard as the school-day song.
- DJ Mini Monster — limited-release headphones brand Sophie gives Hazel.
- Earthworm ice cream — vampire self-care food.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Lucien (S1 villain) escaped the Mirror Prison and released additional monsters; the gang spent the summer recapturing them.
- Vee's stage name "Vampirina" is now public at school; she's accepted as Millie Eyelash's protégé.
- Britney is a vampire (turned in S1E11); the Hightower father's contract with the school is an active plot lever.
- Elijah still wears the Van Helsing ring with "V.H." inscription; its power protects Van Helsings from "full vampires" — and is the literal source of the blasts when they touch.
- The prophecy is tied to Halloween night specifically.
- Vee sent demos to Millie; Millie now replies with an album deal and a livestream date on Halloween.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

## Unresolved Threads

- Whether Vee's Millie Eyelash Halloween livestream will conflict with or trigger the prophecy (Halloween + Vee on stage = foreseeable collision).

- Demi mistranslating the prophecy order ("never interrupt a man who speaks in that deep of a voice") and his pattern of destroying key evidence.
- Hazel's promotion to "full sorcerer" — explicitly dangled as her incentive.
- The earthworm-ice-cream hot-tub meditation setup in Britney's room as a recurring location.

# S02E05 万圣节直播与预言

## 剧情
万圣节当日，两条主线交织：维与米莉·艾莱什（Millie Eyelash）的直播迫在眉睫；预言指明维（吸血鬼）与伊利亚德（范·海辛后裔）将于万圣节毁灭世界。维陷入焦虑，担心吸血鬼形象让网民厌倦。

黛米沿墙壁搜寻失而复得的魔法魔粉袋。维先向苏菲求安慰（"我们会一起度过难关"），再转向布兰妮求取"残酷诚实"的建议——布兰妮借机把维改造成粉色亮片"糖果流行乐风公主"（candy-pop princess），借试音环节提前曝光新形象。

高潮：同学海瑟（Hazel）突然将维和伊利亚德传送至"魔法师议院"（Sorcerer Senate）的牢房。海瑟为议会卧底学徒，伪装友好实为拆散二人组。魔法师女王现身，提出交易：服下可剥夺吸血鬼特质的魔药，即可解除预言、获得明星梦寐并与伊利亚德自由相守。维险些喝下，关键时刻想起黛米说过"魔法魔粉可抵御魔法药水，味道如小精灵头皮屑"（gremlin dandruff），事先服下解药而未被转化。女王误以为得逞，将二人释放。

直播环节，维以吸血鬼真面目登台，唱响主打歌《Beautiful Monsters》（核心歌词："Rebel creature, fearless vampire. Hands up, no games, we're all the same... Let the monster out, let her run wild"），宣告接受双重身份。直播末尾，女王现身吟唱 "From my staff, my minions shall rise. An army of undead will bring your demise!"，召唤不死军团。

## 角色动作要点
- 维：自我怀疑、险些喝下魔药，最终以吸血鬼身份登台，公开宣告"I'll never live my afterlife as someone else"。
- 苏菲：承诺陪伴、试音帮调试、热场DJ备选、剧本《The Big Stink!》修订版备用。
- 伊利亚德：牢房中以 "being two things makes you more, not less" 帮维坚定身份。
- 黛米：汇报魔法魔粉用途（抵御魔法药水的解药）；试音解围担纲暖场。
- 布兰妮：借建议之名强行改造维形象，声称等了"一年多"才迎来"大改造"。
- 海瑟：卧底身份揭晓；承认流星、乌鸦群、枯树、巨魔瘟疫等"恶兆"皆为她与同伙玛法达（Mafalda，与巨魔勾连）所为；游说维与伊利亚德投她竞选舞会皇后（prom queen）。
- 魔法师女王：威胁把维变成龙猫鼠（chinchillas）；扣押海瑟的魔法杖以胁迫服从。

# S02E04 摘要

## 剧情

万圣节前一周，威尔逊堂学院的秋季音乐剧《吸血鬼：尖牙、坏人与丑八怪》（Dracula: The Fanged, The Bad, and The Ugly）选角——这是一出将德古拉塑造成吃牛怪物的反吸血鬼音乐剧。维试镜了主打歌《Star》，并已为米莉·艾莱什录完歌曲，正与朋友享受校园时光。黛米一边抱怨餐厅里"长出"的死树是又一桩预言恶兆（流星、乌鸦、死树，只差瘟疫和火山），一边坚持他的"预言监视"任务。

幕后，海瑟（仍伪装成新转学生）被魔法师女王远程训斥"连结尚未切断"，并被威胁若不能成事将永远拿不到魔杖、还会落得和前任"梅林"一样去用猫砂盆。海瑟定了计——让维变成"被排挤的外人"以离开学校。

院长梅里韦瑟在受贿赂与多方压力下退位，把导演位让给了海瑟。海瑟上任第一件事就是把剧本改成反吸血鬼音乐剧，并把吸血鬼猎人"凡娜·凡赫辛"分给布兰妮、把"恐怖的双獠牙反派德古拉"分给维——还称维"演都不用演"。

排练中维不断试图替吸血鬼辩护（吸血鬼不偷牛、会用更人性化的台词），却成了布兰妮的"破坏者"。在决胜场口，维不自觉用念力让布兰妮险些摔断腿，又用念动救了她，自己陷入"吸血鬼是怪物、不属于人类世界"的自我怀疑。苏菲安慰她"一次改变一个人"，但维已萌生退意。

海瑟的捣乱副产品——真正的山妖（trolls）涌进学校，对人眼隐形。伊利亚德与黛米赶来用"集魔器"与"凡赫辛视力"赶走山妖。山妖被梅根的台词声音迷住，伊利亚德让梅根不停说话，黛米用布袋收妖。收妖结束后发现山妖身上有一袋魔法魔粉——只有魔法师才能使用。维、苏菲、伊利亚德意识到威尔逊堂内藏有秘密魔法师。

演出夜，布兰妮本该在高潮喊"我消灭你"，却临场即兴改了台词：拒绝处决德古拉、宣告吸血鬼也能和人类做朋友。海瑟在幕后气急败坏（"布兰妮不该接受她！"），又发现自己的魔法魔粉弄丢。她在维与伊利亚德的关系裂缝上勉强算是"奏效"，但这场音乐剧正朝着维一直希望的方向收尾。
