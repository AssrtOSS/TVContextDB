## Key Relationships
- **Fran + Nick**: Married; blended family (newborn + two stepchildren Violet & Jay); Nick lost first wife
- **Fran + Julie/Vanessa/Ros/Charlotte**: “The Not Alice Bell Five” — women wrongly accused, form uneasy alliance
- **Group dynamic**: Solidarity vs. suspicion; one may be the real Alice Bell
- **Graham Hunter**: External antagonist; weaponises online mob, true-crime audience
- **Dr Jason Nash**: Puppet-master; communicates from prison; promises livestream reveal
- **Online mob / “hive mind”**: Collective antagonist; incel culture, misogyny, vigilante violence

## World Setting
- **Contemporary UK** (filmed London / environs)
- **Real-world anchors**: Cameos by real broadcasters/politicians (Cathy Newman, Alastair Campbell, Rory Stewart) playing themselves
- **Tech layer**: TikTok, Reddit, podcasts, livestreams, doxxing, viral misinformation
- **Legal backdrop**: Alice Bell served time for falsifying records enabling Nash’s killings; released under new identity (witness-protection-style)

## Overall Plot Premise
Ordinary woman **Fran Da Silva** is suddenly accused online of being **Alice Bell** — former nurse, lover and accomplice of “Sandman” serial-killer **Dr Jason Nash** — now living under a new identity. Fran’s life implodes: vandalism, death threats, media siege, colleagues turn. She discovers **four other women** facing identical accusations. The five band together to clear their names, leveraging media (Vanessa’s PR skills) and tech (Ros’s skills). But **Dr Nash manipulates from prison**, and **Graham Hunter** fuels the mob. Central tension: **one of the five might actually be Alice Bell**. Themes: misogyny, mob justice, viral rumour, identity theft, female solidarity under suspicion.

## Speaking Styles & Tone Guide
- **Fran**: Measured, increasingly strained; internal monologue via reactions; British working-class warmth under pressure
- **Nick**: Steady, protective, pragmatic; dry humour; “husband trying to hold it together”
- **Julie**: Sharp, privileged but not oblivious; Abbington’s clipped delivery; occasional vulnerability cracks
- **Vanessa**: Fast, professional, media-savvy; PR-speak slipping into genuine fear; strategic profanity
- **Ros**: Tech-jargon fluent; gamer cadence; younger, more internet-native; sarcastic deflection
- **Charlotte**: Fragile, hesitant; addiction-recovery vocabulary; emotional volatility
- **Graham Hunter**: Performative, podcast-host rhythm; faux-investigative; manipulative charm → menace
- **Dr Jason Nash**: Calm, clinical, chilling; medical precision; sparse but weighted lines
- **Online comments / social media**: Abbreviated, misspelled, emoji-heavy, aggressive; keep raw flavour in subs
- **News anchors / Cathy Newman**: Broadcast register; neutral authoritative
- **Politicians (Campbell, Stewart)**: Recognisable public personas; measured, evasive, media-trained

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **Maxine Carr / Ian Huntley parallel**: Direct real-life inspiration (Carr = Huntley’s accomplice, released under new identity; women mistaken for her targeted by vigilantes). Chinese audiences may not know this — consider brief gloss or contextualise via dialogue.
- **UK true-crime boom**: Podcasts, TikTok “case analysis,” Reddit threads (r/TrueCrimeUK etc.) — familiar to UK viewers; may need footnote for “True Crime Buster” archetype.
- **Channel 4 tone**: Gritty, socially engaged, ensemble-led; not glossy US thriller — subs should reflect rawness.
- **British class / regional markers**: Fran’s leisure-centre job, Nick’s background, Julie’s “nouveau riche” vibe — subtle in dialogue; preserve where audible.
- **Misogyny & incel rhetoric**: Online abuse targets women specifically; slang (“foid,” “roastie,” etc.) may appear in background screens — translate meaning not literal.
- **Legal nuance**: “New identity” ≠ witness protection (UK has no formal WPP for released prisoners); clarify via context.
- **Real cameos**: Cathy Newman, Alastair Campbell, Rory Stewart playing themselves — Chinese viewers may not recognise; subs can retain names, context carries weight.
