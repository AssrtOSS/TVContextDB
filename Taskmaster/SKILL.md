# Taskmaster (UK) — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Identity
- **English Title**: Taskmaster (also Taskmaster UK / Taskmaster United Kingdom)
- **Chinese Title**: 任务大师 / 脑洞大师 / 挑战大师 (common fan translations; no official Mainland release)
- **Format**: British comedy panel game show
- **Creator**: Alex Horne
- **Presenters**: Greg Davies (Taskmaster), Alex Horne (assistant)
- **First Air Date**: 28 July 2015
- **Original Broadcaster**: Dave (Series 1–9), Channel 4 (Series 10–present)
- **Country of Origin**: United Kingdom
- **Language**: English
- **Series Count**: 22 (as of 2026), commissioned through Series 27 (2029)
- **Episodes**: 204 main episodes + 5 Champion of Champions specials + 7 New Year Treat specials
- **Running Time**: 44–47 minutes
- **Production Company**: Avalon Television

## Core Premise
Five celebrity contestants (primarily comedians) compete across a series of bizarre, open-ended challenges ("tasks") devised by Alex Horne. Tasks are pre-recorded at the Taskmaster House and outdoor locations; studio segments show the footage while Greg Davies awards points (usually 1–5) based on performance, creativity, lateral thinking, or whimsy. Each episode has a winner; the highest cumulative scorer wins the series trophy (a golden bust of Greg Davies). The show blends sitcom-style character dynamics with game-show structure.

**Recurring dynamic**: Greg = "dictator figure" (relaxed into playful tyrant); Alex = put-upon assistant who reads tasks, supervises, occasionally participates. Contestants address Greg as "Taskmaster" or "Greg"; Alex as "Alex" or "Little Alex Horne" (Greg's running nickname).

## World Setting
- **Taskmaster House**: Three-room former groundskeeper's cottage in Dukes Meadows, Chiswick, London. Redecorated each series with a theme (Japanese, Western, Art Deco, Victorian, Steampunk, etc.). Iconic portrait of Greg in living room changes per series.
- **Studio**: Clapham Grand (S1), Fountain Studios (S2–3), Pinewood Studios (S4–20), Television Centre (S21+). Studio set mimics Clapham Grand interior.
- **Outdoor Locations**: Vary per series (Dukes Meadows Bandstand, Buckinghamshire Railway Centre, Chesham United FC stadium, Gatwick Airport South Terminal, Frogmore Paper Mill, etc.).
- **Caravan**: Introduced Series 4; used for certain tasks, decorated to match house theme.

## Episode Structure
1. **Prize Task** (studio): Contestants bring an item fitting a theme (e.g., "most impressive thing you own made of metal"). Winner takes all.
2. **Pre-recorded Tasks** (3–5 per episode): Shown as VTs. Alex reads task from wax-sealed tri-fold card ("TM" branding). Contestants read aloud. Tasks range from physical ("eat watermelon in 1 minute") to creative ("stop-motion video with a potato") to lateral-thinking. Team tasks occasionally (pairs/trios share score).
3. **Live Task** (studio): Final challenge performed onstage.
4. **Scoring & Prizes**: Greg announces points, episode winner claims prize collection.

Special episodes:
- **New Year Treat**: One-off celebrity specials (non-comedians); trophy = golden Greg eyebrows statue.
- **Champion of Champions**: Series winners return; trophy = golden rest-of-Greg-body statue.

## Speaking Styles & Translation Notes
- **Greg Davies**: Authoritative baritone, sarcastic, hyperbolic insults ("You absolute weapon," "That is the most pathetic thing I've ever seen"), sudden warmth. Uses "Taskmaster" in third person. Rhetorical questions. Deliberate pauses for comic timing.
- **Alex Horne**: Fast, polite, slightly posh delivery. Literal readings of tasks. Self-deprecating. "Little Alex Horne" voice when mocked. Occasional deadpan asides to camera.
- **Contestants**: Varied — panel-show banter, self-deprecation, competitive boasting, genuine bafflement. Frequent interruptions, overlapping dialogue in studio. Task readings: must be verbatim (plot-critical).
- **Narration**: None; tasks conveyed via Alex reading + on-screen text overlays (often shown in VTs). Translate on-screen task text if burned in; otherwise rely on Alex's reading.

## Cultural Context & References
- **British Comedy Canon**: Contestants are UK comedy fixtures (panel shows, stand-up, sitcoms). References to *The Crystal Maze*, *Big Brother*, *Bake Off*, *Apprentice*, Edinburgh Fringe.
- **Class & Regional Accents**: Contestants span English regions, Scotland, Wales, Ireland; accents noted and joked about.
- **British Institutions**: GCSEs, NHS, M25, Waitrose, Tesco meal deals, pub culture, bank holidays, BBC, Ofcom.
- **Running Gags**: Greg's height (6'8"/203 cm), Alex's height/beard, "Little Alex Horne", Greg's "dictator" persona, the portrait, the caravan, "Your time starts now," wax seal, alphabetical seating.
- **Humour Style**: Absurdist, cringe, anti-humour, British understatement vs. Greg's bluster. "So bad it's good" attempts celebrated.
- **Audience**: Studio audience laughter/applause; no laugh track.

## Series-Specific Notes for Translators
- **Series 1–9 (Dave)**: Shorter series (5–10 eps), different trophy (generic S1, Greg-head S2+).
- **Series 10+ (Channel 4)**: 10 eps/series, consistent Greg-head trophy, higher production budget.
- **Contestant Rotation**: No recurring contestants in main series (except Champion of Champions). Each series = new cast.
- **Task Re-use**: Some tasks attempted across multiple series (e.g., "burst all bubbles on bubble wrap" — never aired).
- **On-screen Text**: Task instructions often displayed as graphics during VTs. Translate if burned in; note if soft-subbed separately.
