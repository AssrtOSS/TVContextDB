## S22E03 — Episode Summary

### Episode Plot

Series 22 (Greek theme) continues with its third episode. The show opens with Alex's "faux-toes" pun (fake toes / photos). Five tasks span the episode: a prize task about safety, a gleeking challenge, a before-and-after photo task, a noughts-and-crosses memory game (BOSNAC), and a live colour-sorting task while waving. Chloe Petts wins the episode, propelled by strong photo-task performance and the live-task victory.

---

| Contestant | Item | Points |
|---|---|---|

**Notes:** Diana Lucas in the audience, greeted warmly by Greg and Alex. Chloe's Greg model startled her repeatedly when she exited the bathroom. Richard claims to be a "public urinal sat nav" for all of London. Isy told a story about spilling blue paint in her car footwell. Nina demonstrated a ventriloquist drinking trick using slush powder.

---

### Task 1 — *Gleek*

- **Task:** Learn what "gleek" means and perform it. Fastest wins. May make two phone calls (yelling only, 30 seconds each). No internet.
- **Definition:** Involuntary projection of saliva from the submandibular glands under the tongue.
- **Results:**
  1. **Nina Conti** — 7:25, already knew the word, produced a genuine involuntary gleek. **5 pts**
  2. **Richard Ayoade** — 9:27, called Tim Key, who told him gleek was a 16th/17th-century card game; Richard played cards and claimed success. **4 pts**
  3. **Chloe Petts** — 12:25, called Ed Gamble (on his birthday), got the definition by text, used a large syringe to force liquid from under her tongue. **3 pts**
  4. **Matt Lucas** — called a friend, attempted to disguise spitting at Alex as gleeking. **2 pts**
  5. **Isy Suttie** — called husband Elis James and friend Nathan; Elis thought she was being attacked in the street; Isy simply spat at Alex. **1 pt**

**Key moment:** After scoring, Alex demonstrated a genuine gleek on camera, spraying saliva — Greg called it "one of the most distressing things I've ever seen."

---

### Task 2 — *Before and After Photos*

- **Task:** Take a pair of photos, one before and one after something happening. The pair that most makes Greg want to see what happened in-between wins. Two bonus points for the most unexpected in-between event.
- **Results:**
  1. **Nina Conti** — Before: cup of tea and sandwich. After: blue paint chaos with butterfly centurion wearing flippers. Alex was the target. **5 pts**
  2. **Chloe Petts** — Before: Alex smiling. After: Alex in tears. In between: fitted him with an onion-beard mask and read a series of devastating existential facts (dog doesn't understand why you left; one day you'll pick up your child for the last time; sheep fall over if not sheared; someone will say your name for the last time). **4 pts + 2 bonus pts**
  3. **Richard Ayoade** — Before: normal house exterior. After: cursed house with gargoyle. He put a curse on the house, appearing naked in the after photo wearing Spanx. **3 pts**
  4. **Isy Suttie** — Before: Alex in bath. After: bath filled with toy animals and barrels. Animated the animal attack with angry eyebrows on the creatures. **3 pts**
  5. **Matt Lucas** — Before: Matt on first step of ladder. After: same step. Planned "world's first-ever flight" but abandoned it due to severe vertigo. **1 pt**

---

### Task 3 — *Noughts and Crosses Against Your Old Self (BOSNAC)*

- **Mechanic:** Earlier in the day, each contestant filled in a BOSNAC (Big Or Small Noughts And Crosses) form: chose big or small (determines platform/chair height), wrote numbers 1–9 in a grid (order of cross placements), and answered category questions (food, bird, sound, adjective, colour, time, number, place). In the task, Alex places crosses according to the form; contestant drops noughts from height onto a grid. Must win the game, then correctly recall their own answers. Wrong answer = restart. Fastest winner wins.
- **Results:**
  1. **Nina Conti** — 45 seconds. Remembered "soup." **5 pts**
  2. **Isy Suttie** — 1:42. Used a "pass" strategy (if you say pass, ask another question — Alex accepted this). Remembered "crane" (bird). **4 pts**
  3. **Chloe Petts** — 2:18. Chose big (elevated platform); used sequential numbers 1–9, thinking it would be clever. **3 pts**
  4. **Matt Lucas** — 5:40. Forgot he had even filled in the form. Chose big. **2 pts**
  5. **Richard Ayoade** — 18:30. Struggled extensively. Answers included "vermilion" (colour) and "despair" (sound). **1 pt**

**Key moment:** Isy challenged Alex's ruling that a nought had missed (same situation happened to her and she accepted it), while Richard contested his and was allowed a replay. Isy called this "old-fashioned misogyny"; Alex agreed he was "an ally."

---

### Live Task — *Coloured Rolls on Poles*

- **Task:** Sort red, yellow, and green rolls onto matching poles (Tower of Hanoi style). Only one roll may leave the poles at any time. Must wave at the Taskmaster with one hand throughout. Fastest wins.
- **Results:**
  - **Chloe Petts** — winner. **5 pts**
  - **Matt Lucas** — slow but completed. **3 pts**
  - **Richard Ayoade, Isy Suttie, Nina Conti** — disqualified (multiple rolls off poles simultaneously). **0 pts**
- **Controversy:** A photo showed Nina with two rolls off at once. She claimed it was AI-generated and pointed out she appeared to have no hair in the image.

## S22E10

# S22E10 — Series Final (Grand Final)

## Plot
The series 22 finale with Chloe Petts, Isy Suttie, Matt Lucas, Nina Conti, and Richard Ayoade. Greg and Alex open by pulling Alex's invented "everyday cracker" (no bang, boring contents: contact lens solution, a cocktail sausage). Five segments decide the episode and the series trophy:

1. **Prize task**: bring an object that would look best on your Wikipedia page. Nina: a fridge of retired, decayed ventriloquist monkeys ("after years of fisting"); Matt: an eBay baseball signed by Liz Truss (won at £169.70 to undercut £170); Chloe: her head girl badge (genuinely elected at her all-girls school); Richard: a James Lloyd portrait of his character "Harauld Hughes," used to shamelessly plug his book *The Unfinished Harauld Hughes* (Everyman/Bollinger-shortlisted); Isy: proof her fourth cousin once removed is Lewis Hamilton (shared ancestor Henry William Larbalestier, b. 1804). Scores: Richard 1, Nina 2, Chloe 3, Isy 4, Matt 5.
2. **Mini/max model task**: make a tiny pleasant model in a matchbox in 10 minutes (first-ever "tiny envelope in tiny matches" task; Nina requests the show's first magnifying glass), then a hidden second task outside: recreate it 20× bigger in 10 minutes. Entries: Richard's simple but accurate one, Chloe's blue hairy dog (shaved Alex's/the crew's chest hair — "reef knot" aside), Matt's two versions of himself doing the NYT crossword, Isy knitting, Nina's elaborate bird. Scores: Richard 1, Isy 2, Chloe 3, Nina 4, Matt 5. Midway scoreboard: Nina/Isy/Chloe 6, Matt 10 despite never having won an episode.
3. **Feather task** (final visit to the London Museum of Water and Steam): get a feather into a hidden bucket, max 60 steps, fastest wins. Chloe uses a string-and-dog-poo-bag rig (notable "sick booty" studio bit; 44 steps incl. crawling); Isy ties feathers to her earring with reef knots but is slowest (24:15, lost an earring, 14:30 note confusion — actually Nina was 14:30 quickest-per-Greg's consolation line); Nina gets zero (lost the feather); Matt 9:50; Richard wins bouncing "kangaroo Ayoade" style, 8:55. Scores: Isy 2, Chloe 3, Matt 4, Nina 0, Richard 5.
4. **Final team task**: recreate a famous movie scene with no recognizable words in any language. Team of two (Isy, Nina, + guest Alex Horne) attempt *Titanic* (word-salad dialogue, "Een, dasht, tresh"); team of three (Matt, Richard, Chloe) do *E.T.* with trifle and a finger-wink, nailed Drew Barrymore. E.T. wins: 5 points to the trio, 4 to the pair.
5. **Live task**: ventriloquism — voice Alex while Greg guesses who's speaking; hand on Alex's back opens/closes his mouth. Round 1 "American ghost": Greg nails all five. Round 2 "extremely old person ordering food": Greg only gets Isy and Richard. Round 3 per-person voices (mystical mermaid, evil robot, WWE champion, French poet, shipwrecked parrot): only Isy's parrot fools him. Scoring: 5 points each to the four who deceived Greg once, 1 to Richard (never fooled him).

## Outcome
Episode winner: **Matt Lucas** (24 points) — his first episode win — prize items from the wiki task. Final series standings: Richard 131 (last), Matt 147, Isy Suttie 152, Nina Conti 158, and **series champion: Chloe Petts, 173 points**, lifting the titanium trophy.
