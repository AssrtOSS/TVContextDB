## S22E03

### 剧情

- 经过上一轮大规模淘汰，22名设计师只剩12人；被淘汰者与晋级者共同投票，最终救回比（Bi），丹妮、奥克塔维斯、宝、杨杨出局。
- 新挑战要求四支三人队伍创作三件套女装胶囊系列，以NFL球队的颜色、城市和标志为灵感。每队队长还将争夺超级碗门票及下一场挑战的免淘汰权；本轮实行双重淘汰。
- 分组：
  - 猎鹰队：布莱恩、比、伊丽莎白
  - 巨人队：杰弗里、安娜、克洛伊
  - 黑豹队：约瑟夫·麦克雷、纳海姆、亚伦
  - 老鹰队：安德烈、鲍比、裘德
- 黑豹队凭借夸张廓形、拼布和南卡罗来纳州古拉—吉奇文化的靛蓝灵感获胜。约瑟夫连续第二次获胜，并赢得两张第61届超级碗门票；黑豹队也取得下一轮队长免淘汰权。
- 猎鹰队安全过关。巨人队和老鹰队成为低分队，最终淘汰结果留到片尾之后公布。

### 重要人物行动

- 比从濒临出局到被救回，随后在布莱恩队中负责猎鹰翅膀灵感的褶裥连衣裙，证明了自己的技术能力。
- 布莱恩试图以队长身份主导猎鹰队，却因作品过大、过重且合身度不足受到批评。
- 安德烈与鲍比因审美判断和沟通方式爆发争执；安德烈认为鲍比过度摆出领导姿态，鲍比则认为安德烈表达过于居高临下。
- 鲍比在制作中把前片缝到前片、后片缝到后片，陷入崩溃；她的老鹰队最终因团队失控和完成度不足落入低分组。
- 克洛伊反复推翻方案，最后做出带蓝色丝带的失控造型；她承认自己过度思考，没能相信最初直觉。
- 杰弗里作为巨人队队长未能及时帮助克洛伊，评委认为他对队友负有领导责任。
- 裘德和安娜的作品相对安全，但评委分别指出裘德的造型缺乏超级碗关联，安娜的设计缺少突破。
- 克里斯蒂安在工作室中反复要求设计师把NFL、运动感、雕塑性与时装前瞻性结合起来。

- **胶囊系列**（capsule collection）：本轮每队制作三件可组合的女装系列。
- **House／队伍、Head of House／队长、蛇形选人**（snake draft）：本轮团队组建机制。
- **NFL球队**：亚特兰大猎鹰队、纽约巨人队、卡罗来纳黑豹队、费城老鹰队。

## S22E04

## S22E04 Episode Summary

**Elimination carryover from previous episode:** Team NFL capsule challenge concludes with double elimination. Chloe (worst look on her team) and Andriy (failed as team leader, did not take responsibility) are sent home. Jeffrey, though bottom-team leader, survives.

**New challenge — "Wild Inspiration":** Heidi announces a collaboration with Humane World for Animals. Five animals from five habitats serve as muses; all fabrics must be cruelty-free and animal-free. This week uses a head-to-head battle format: designers are paired, each pair works on the same animal, and the winner of each matchup is decided by judges' live vote. The overall top-two winner receives immunity plus a Humane Journeys expedition to the Great Bear Rainforest. Guest models come from Hulu's *Get Real* franchise, with one pair modeled by eliminated contestant Chloe Magleby (replacing Stassi Schroeder).

**Pairings and animal assignments:**
- Aaron vs. Anna → Lion (Aaron picked Anna)
- Jeffrey vs. Naheim → Great white shark (Jeffrey picked Naheim)
- Joseph McRae vs. Bryan → Scarlet macaw (Joseph picked Bryan, who has immunity)
- Bi vs. Jude → Arctic wolf (Bi picked Jude)
- Elizabeth vs. Bobby → Mustang (Elizabeth picked Bobby)

**Design highlights:**
- Joseph creates a wing-sleeve wrap dress inspired by his late mother, themed around freedom and beauty (scarlet macaw). Judges later vote him winner of his pair, though Law criticizes his arrogance and notes Joseph wore the same fabric as his model, which "lost points."
- Bryan makes a predictable fuchsia halter gown with a fishtail train. Christian warns it looks like "a thousand gowns that already exist." She later faces harsh critique.
- Jeffrey goes monochromatic shark-skin; Naheim does a more literal shark with red-lined gills suggesting "guts."
- Bi pivots overnight from a stained dress to a top-and-skirt combo after Sarah Jane Nader's body makeup ruins the original look.
- Anna redesigns her lion pantsuit on the fly when Chloe replaces Stassi as model, having to recut entirely. Aaron struggles more with the same model swap.
- Bobby's mustang-inspired flamenco look is disliked by model Ekin-Su, rattling Bobby's composure.

**Runway voting (head-to-head winners):**
- Anna defeats Aaron (unanimous)
- Jude defeats Bi (unanimous)
- Joseph defeats Bryan (unanimous)
- Bobby defeats Elizabeth (split decision)
- Naheim defeats Jeffrey (unanimous)

**Top two:** Anna and Jude. **Challenge winner:** Jude — earns immunity and the Great Bear Rainforest expedition.

**Bottom three:** Aaron, Bi, Bryan (all unanimously lost their pairs). Elizabeth, Bobby, Naheim, and Jeffrey are declared safe due to split-decision saves.

**Judges' panel:** Law Roach (CFDA award-winning stylist), Tyra Banks (supermodel/entrepreneur), Heidi Klum.

**Continuity-relevant facts:**
- Joseph McRae retains immunity from a prior win, which protects him despite Law's clear displeasure with his recurring arrogance and repeated silhouettes ("circle skirt" legacy).
- Bryan's immunity does not save her from elimination contention.
- Bi lands in the bottom for the second consecutive episode.
- Chloe Magleby (already eliminated) is brought back as substitute runway model, giving her screen time and emotional closure.
- Veejay Floresca (Season 21 winner) returns as guest model for the shark pair.
- Aaron's brand name is "A-Potts."
- Naheim, Bobby, and Jude are identified as a "Gen Z coalition"; Elizabeth and Bobby are roommates.

**Jokes and memorable references:**
- Tyra's note on Bi's look: "Mattress pad or maxi pad?" / "a pad for the heavier days" / "If my maxi pad is that chic I will buy ten."
- Heidi on Bryan's gown: "an airplane pillow," "the villain that's going to a ball," followed by "You still fly economy?"
- Elizabeth on Bobby's look: "Would I wear it for Halloween? Hell yeah. Pirates of the Caribbean's wife?"
- Joseph repeatedly mimics macaw squawking throughout the workroom.
- Christian to Bi's original ski-suit idea: "This is not Project Runway: Juniors."

**Unresolved threads:**
- The episode ends on a cliffhanger immediately before the elimination deliberation among Aaron, Bi, and Bryan. The eliminated designer for this episode is not revealed in the provided subtitles.
- Law Roach's building frustration with Joseph McRae is flagged as a recurring judging thread ("if you keep making the same mistakes you're gonna find yourself out").
- Bryan's confusion at losing to Joseph (whose look she called "safe, simple, bitter") sets up potential future conflict between them.
- Aaron receives a blunt warning from Law: "I cannot keep on being impressed by your potential — I need to see something on the runway."

# Project Runway S22E10 — Season Finale Summary

## Episode Plot
The season finale opens with a recap of the David's Bridal challenge (S22E09), where Anna won and secured the first finale spot, followed by Jude and Bi. The judges then surprised everyone by adding a **fourth finalist** — Joseph — after a head-to-head between him, Naheim, and Bryan. Naheim and Bryan were eliminated.

The final four (Anna, Bi, Jude, Joseph) received one week, $7,500, and mentorship from Christian Siriano to create five-look collections for New York Fashion Week. The episode covers their final workroom sprint, model fittings, the runway show, judges' critiques, and the winner announcement.
