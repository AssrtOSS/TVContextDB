## S03E01

### Episode plot

- Three years after their relationship ended, Ashley and Gordon unexpectedly reunite when Gordon books an insurance medical with Ashley, now a doctor.
- Gordon has returned to Sydney after six months in Warrnambool. He has sold the brewery, lost his late father’s $500,000 inheritance through bad investments, and now works for the brewery’s corporate buyer.
- Gordon asks Ashley to resume shared custody of Colin, but she refuses, saying her current circumstances make it impossible. Their meeting exposes how much unresolved feeling remains between them.
- Ashley lives with Megan, Rumi, and their 18-month-old child, Genre. David, the sperm donor and Ashley’s former partner, is also staying there, creating tension.
- Gordon’s business is unstable, Chiara is newly diagnosed with ADHD, and Brett is pursuing an online relationship with Carmen.
- Gordon and Ashley reconnect over Colin and eventually spend time together caring for him, ending the episode in an emotionally ambiguous but clearly intimate reunion.

### Important character actions

- Ashley insists on being addressed professionally as “Dr Molden,” but becomes flustered around Gordon.
- Gordon tries to appear casual, jokes through the medical examination, and offers Ashley access to Colin without demanding a relationship.
- Megan pushes back against David’s constant presence and sets firm boundaries with James, who was their sperm donor and keeps turning up uninvited.
- Rumi follows social-media parenting advice, including avoiding praise such as “good job,” which irritates Ashley.
- Chiara tells Gordon about her ADHD diagnosis and says she needs to slow down; Gordon initially trivializes it, then apologizes and agrees to take on more work.
- Brett insists Carmen is real despite Gordon’s suspicion that she is a scammer or a teenager pretending to be someone else.
- Gordon continues behaving impulsively, including approaching strangers to offer brewery facilities and food, despite Ashley warning him about personal safety.

### Newly established names and terms

- Dr Ashley Molden: Ashley is now working as a doctor.
- Brendan: Ashley’s intern, whose intrusive medical-questionnaire routine creates the episode’s central clinic gag.
- Genre: Megan and Rumi’s 18-month-old child.
- David: the sperm donor and Ashley’s former partner, who lives with Megan and Rumi.
- James: another former partner/donor figure who wants access to Genre but is not considered the child’s parent.
- Carmen: Brett’s online romantic interest, whose authenticity is uncertain.
- Echo Park: Gordon’s “little baby,” apparently a brewery or business project.
- Functional beer: Gordon’s proposed low-carbohydrate beer containing electrolytes, amino acids, antioxidants, and spirulina.
- HotDoc, NBN, pickleball, Swannies, and the Matildas are referenced as Australian digital, sporting, and cultural touchstones.

### Continuity-relevant facts

- Ashley and Gordon have been apart for roughly three years.
- Ashley returned from Warrnambool six months before the episode.
- Gordon has not contacted Ashley during that time, despite wanting news about Colin.
- Colin is alive and well but still has impaired legs; Gordon tells Ashley that Colin believes she is dead.
- Gordon’s brewery was bought by a larger company, and he is no longer financially secure.
- Lynelle and Professor Lee are now married; their earlier men’s-group/“man of the house” activities are remembered as bizarre.
- Megan and Rumi’s domestic life has changed significantly after Genre’s birth, and David’s presence threatens their established household routine.
- Chiara’s ADHD diagnosis may affect how Gordon’s business operates and how responsibilities are divided.

### Jokes and references useful later

- Gordon’s disastrous financial choices include combining alcohol with an axe-throwing venture.
- The clinic’s sexual-health questionnaire repeatedly confuses “insertive” and “receptive penile,” ending in the deliberately absurd “docking” discussion.
- Professor Lee’s men’s movement involved aggressively rehearsing “I am the man of the house,” only for the exercise to collapse into childish insults.
- James describes himself as a “fun uncle sperm friend,” prompting Ashley to threaten legal action if he keeps visiting without an invitation.
- Gordon and Brett repeatedly turn business names into “City” puns, including “Ethical City,” “Quarterly City,” “Idea City,” and “Tox City.”
- Brett’s online romance is mocked with references to Russian bots, mail-order brides, and “Hampton Wick,” a euphemism for a sex toy.
- Gordon is teased for gaining weight, gambling again, and growing a “fat Ned Kelly” beard.

### Unresolved threads

- Whether Ashley and Gordon will resume their relationship or establish a genuine shared-custody arrangement for Colin.
- What circumstances prevented Ashley from contacting Gordon and Colin after returning to Sydney.
- Whether Gordon can recover financially and make the brewery business viable under corporate ownership.
- Whether Chiara’s diagnosis will lead to meaningful changes or simply become another source of workplace conflict.
- Whether Brett’s relationship with Carmen is genuine.
- Whether David can find work and move out of Megan and Rumi’s home.
- Whether James will respect the boundaries around Genre.

## S03E03

# S03E03 Summary

## Plot

Ashley arranges a dinner with Gordon under the pretext of discussing Colin, but both know it's an attempt to "draw a line" under their failed relationship. Ashley is now living with David (a stable, steady partner), while Gordon's new partner Chelsea has just moved in and is rapidly imposing order on his life. Over a Spanish dinner, Ashley and Gordon dissect the wreckage of Gordon's drunken proposal at Megan's wedding — no ring, no parental consultation, Ashley feeling ambushed in public. Both admit they still think about each other; Gordon reveals he was parked outside the brewery watching Ashley. Neither resolves anything. Ashley goes home to David and says "I missed you."

## Important Character Actions

- **Chelsea** moves in early, brings a monstera, and institutes a color-coded shared calendar system. She cancels Gordon's dinner with Ashley and dismisses his autonomy.
- **Gordon** submits to Chelsea's systems, lies to her about the dinner (tells her it's a work thing with Jared), and admits to Ashley that his heart "fully broke" after she left — his father had just died and he gambled away most of his inheritance.
- **Ashley** tells Gordon she felt something irreparably break when he proposed; she went on placement in Warrnambool rather than regroup. She admits she was broken too but insists she's better now.
- **David** knows about the dinner but believes it's a "dog hang." He tells Ashley to shower first and "get the old dog off ya."
- **James** teases Ashley about "playing with fire" and reminds her David is a good guy.
- **Lynelle** and **Professor Lee** film a "if I've been murdered" video for their men's rights followers; Lee keeps veering into inappropriate pedophile jokes.

## New Names / Terms

- **Chelsea** — Gordon's new partner; works in Melbourne, has a shared calendar, is a "ring picker," micromanages.
- **"Hot Fish Man"** — Gordon's nickname for David (who apparently smells like fish).
- **Porron** — traditional Spanish wine pitcher the restaurant offers with a 10% discount.
- **WAM / WAWAWAM / KAWAM / PAPAP** — absurd nested activist group acronyms Lynelle claims are threatening her.
- **Warrnambool** — regional Victorian town where Ashley was on placement.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Gordon proposed to Ashley drunk at Megan's wedding; she neither said yes nor no.
- Ashley left for Warrnambool afterward; Gordon's father died around the same time.
- Colin is now Gordon's dog ("he is my dog now").
- Gordon has celiac disease and ongoing digestive health issues (his doctor reviews a detailed stool schedule with him).
- Gordon was parked outside the brewery watching Ashley the night she was there.
- Ashley and Gordon have not been in contact since she returned to Sydney.

## Jokes / References

- Colin's video: John suggests "the Four Dickheads," Colin counters with "the Beatles."
- Chelsea's color-coded calendar: red vs. blue for individual activities, purple for joint.
- Gordon's doctor appointment: a day-by-day bowel movement log ("Friday, dry. Plus blood. Saturday, loose.").
- The porron wine stream the server demonstrates.
- Lynelle's men's rights movement and the escalating acronym war (WAWAWAM, KAWAM, PAPAP).
- Professor Lee: "Poofters Against People Against Poofters" / "Or is it pedos?"
- David's "Jimbotron" / "Mr. Davidoff" banter with James.

## Unresolved Threads

- Ashley and Gordon's romantic status — neither commits to moving on nor rekindling.
- Gordon's health — the doctor visit suggests ongoing issues beyond celiac disease.
- Chelsea does not know about the dinner; David suspects it's more than a dog hang.
- Colin's long-term custody remains ambiguous.
- Gordon's claim that Ashley "ruined" him vs. his refusal to "wear that."

## S03E04 Summary

**Episode Plot**

David proposes to Ashley by driving her in the middle of the night to Camden for a hot-air balloon "New Beginnings Champagne Breakfast" and reaching for his dead nanna's engagement ring — the same ring Ashley found under the bed earlier that day. Ashley realizes what he is about to do before he asks and begs him not to. He accepts her answer, asks whether they can just pretend it never happened and go back to how they were, and Ashley tearfully says no. They go on the balloon ride anyway. Meanwhile, Gordon — who had kissed Ashley and slept with her at his own birthday party — confesses to Chiara and Brett, faces their fury, then breaks up with Chelsea mid-IVF appointment. Chelsea, enraged, reveals she already has high-quality embryos with an ex named Simon and tells Gordon he is "unfixable."

**Important Character Actions**

- **David** steals his dead nanna's engagement ring from under Ashley's bed and plans an elaborate hot-air-balance proposal at dawn in Camden. When Ashley says no, he asks to go back to "how we are" and she refuses.
- **Ashley** finds the ring and panics; with Megan and Rumi she builds a pros-and-cons list of David vs. Gordon and considers a plan to make herself vomit on cue to dodge a proposal. She ultimately cannot say yes to David and cannot say no to the relationship in plain terms.

- **Chelsea** pushes Gordon relentlessly toward IVF, arranges a late-cancellation clinic slot, and accompanies him into the collection room. After the breakup she delivers a devastating verdict — that Gordon's charm is a "red flag" at his age, that she was arrogant to think she could fix him, and that she has embryos with an ex that are higher quality.
- **Chiara** warns Gordon not to pursue Ashley and not to have a baby with Chelsea; she calls both "shit ideas." She also insults Gordon's body, calling his hips "bitch hips."
- **Brett** wears a custom T-shirt that mocks Gordon (calling him a "cockhead"). He offers to flea-bomb Gordon's house, gets overexposed to the chemicals, and is taken to hospital.
- **Megan and Rumi** help Ashley strategise how to dodge David's proposal. Rumi reveals she has been proposed to nine times and coaches Ashley to watch for the "pocket reach." Megan bluntly asks Ashley whether she actually loves David.
