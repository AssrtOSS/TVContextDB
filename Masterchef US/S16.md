## S16E10

### 剧情

- 前十五名选手迎来“融合转盘”挑战：各区域抽取一个陌生国家，将其料理与本土文化融合，限时一小时完成菜品。
- 抽签结果：欧洲×日本、亚太×法国、美洲×尼日利亚、非洲×希腊。拥有免淘汰权的朱莉娅改写规则，把非洲队的希腊料理换成澳大利亚料理，令非洲队措手不及。
- 最佳菜品分别是：爱舒的印度风味法式红酒炖鸡、阿加塔的西班牙墨鱼汁黑米饭配日式金枪鱼塔塔、彼得的尼日利亚澳大利亚融合炸鱼薯条、玛丽亚的尼日利亚巴西苏雅香料羊小腿配木薯泥。
- 玛丽亚凭羊小腿赢得本轮最佳菜品及免淘汰徽章，整支美洲队因此免于淘汰。
- 香帕、巴希娅和丽塔进入危险区。最终香帕与丽塔同时被淘汰，巴希娅幸存。

### 重要人物行动

- 朱莉娅首次获得免淘汰权，并将优势用于针对实力强劲的非洲队；她的策略成功制造了本轮最大变数。
- 玛丽亚冒险用高压锅炖羊小腿，在短时间内完成软嫩、浓郁的成品，成为美洲队的关键人物。
- 爱舒用印度香料、马萨拉和柠檬汁重新诠释法式红酒炖鸡，并借此纪念已故岳父。
- 阿加塔首次进入本季最佳菜品行列；她此前长期未进前列。
- 彼得原本担心澳大利亚料理，却做出评委称赞的炸鱼薯条。乔甚至表示此前没想到他能达到这样的水平。
- 香帕首次制作蛙腿，结果菜品缺乏香料冲击力，评委认为她从选材开始就判断失误。
- 丽塔的羊肉先没熟，回锅后又过熟且干燥；评委还指出她没有让肉静置。
- 巴希娅的鳕鱼熟度良好，但甜菜根与欧防风泥出水，导致整盘菜凌乱、味道失衡。

### 新确立的名字与术语

- **Wheel of Fusion**：融合转盘。
- **Immunity pin**：免淘汰徽章；本轮由玛丽亚获得。
- **Jollof rice**：乔洛夫饭。
- **Suya spice**：苏雅香料。
- **Vegemite**：维吉麦，澳大利亚浓缩酵母酱。
- **Wattleseed**：瓦特尔籽，彼得用于炸鱼面糊。
- **Ata dindin sauce**：阿塔丁丁酱。
- **Yaji spice**：亚吉香料。
- **Laksa**：拉克萨，马来西亚椰奶海鲜高汤。
- **Bhuna**：布纳，一种浓郁的香料烹调方式。
- **Arroz negro**：西班牙墨鱼汁黑米饭。
- **Coq au vin**：法式红酒炖鸡。

### 连贯性信息

- 本集结束后，玛丽亚拥有免淘汰徽章，美洲队暂时全员安全。
- 爱舒、阿加塔、彼得和玛丽亚成为本季目前最强的一组最佳菜品选手。
- 非洲队因朱莉娅的调换被迫处理澳大利亚料理，丽塔因此出局；朱莉娅的策略能力明显增强。
- 香帕离场时强调自己从小梦想在《厨艺大师》厨房烘焙；丽塔则表示会继续追求尼日利亚料理与自己的烹饪梦想。
- 淘汰后仍留在比赛中的非洲队选手包括彼得等人；巴希娅也成功留在欧洲队。

### 笑点与参考

- 选手一看到澳大利亚就联想到危险动物、袋鼠和海鲜；海蒂随后真的制作袋鼠肉薄片。
- 戈登借品尝菜品之机与巴希娅一起喝清酒，令她兴奋不已。
- 戈登对福开玩笑说，若他获胜，就带他去巴黎香榭丽舍大街；福因从未去过法国而激动回应。
- 乔评价彼得时说自己“震惊于他居然能做出这道菜”，形成带有意外认可意味的调侃。

### 未解决线索

- 下一集进入王室婚礼主题的团队挑战，每队负责一半婚礼宾客的餐点。
- 第十五季冠军双人组合杰西与杰西卡将回归。
- 预告显示出餐压力将引发争执、失火和混乱；海梅甚至被要求离场冷静两分钟，并称自己仿佛忘了如何做菜。

## S16E11

### 剧情概述
- 选手来到多伦多的卡萨罗马城堡，参加一场“厨艺大师皇室婚礼”外景团队挑战。
- 第15季冠军双人组杰西和杰西卡举行婚礼；他们要求菜单包含扇贝开胃菜，以及酥脆鸭皮、五分熟鸭肉的鸭肉主菜。
- 玛丽亚因上一轮获胜获得组队权，将美洲队与非洲队组成蓝队，欧洲队与亚太队组成红队。彼得担任蓝队队长，艾舒担任红队队长。
- 蓝队开场混乱，扇贝曾出现过熟、没熟和调味不足等问题，但随后逐渐恢复节奏。红队的扇贝出餐迟缓，玉米糊寡淡，鸭肉前期也经历严重失误。
- 蓝队最终凭借更稳定的整体表现获胜，红队进入淘汰评审。
- 海梅和阿加塔被认为是红队的两个薄弱环节。阿加塔因玉米糊失误、鸭肉划刀过深以及整体应对失控而被淘汰。

### 重要人物行动
- 玛丽亚选择与非洲队合作，延续此前合作获胜的判断。
- 彼得首次担任队长，努力重新组织蓝队，并在关键时刻调整人员：让特凯娅负责扇贝，玛丽亚转做沙拉。
- 艾舒担任红队队长，前期听取队员意见，但团队沟通失灵；主菜阶段她接管酱汁与摆盘，帮助队伍完成出餐。
- 海蒂因频繁表达不满被彼得提醒要有团队精神。
- 玛丽亚误解分量要求，一次煮掉过多扇贝，且成品先过熟后没熟，成为蓝队的主要风险之一。
- 朱莉娅和福负责扇贝时烧焦食材并引发锅具起火；戈登借此调侃“现在你们火力全开了”。
- 阿加塔第一次制作玉米糊，也缺乏鸭肉划刀经验；她把鸭皮划得过深，导致鸭肉可能变干，最终成为淘汰者。
- 海梅制作橙汁鸭酱时陷入慌乱，艾舒让她暂时离开厨房冷静，随后接手关键工作。

### 新出现的姓名与术语
- 卡萨罗马城堡（Casa Loma）：本集婚礼外景地点。
- 杰西（Jesse）与杰西卡（Jessica）：第15季冠军双人组，本集结婚。
- 皇室婚礼／“厨艺大师皇室”：对杰西与杰西卡婚礼的戏称。
- 橙汁鸭胸（duck à l’orange）。
- 柑橘白酒奶油汁（citrus beurre blanc）。
- 欧洲防风草泥、花椰菜泥、玉米糊、甜菜苦苣沙拉、红酒樱桃酱。
- 新郎新娘专属试菜与婚礼宾客反馈，成为本次团队胜负的重要依据。

### 连续性信息
- 玛丽亚拥有本轮免淘汰权，因此无法担任队长。
- 蓝队由美洲队和非洲队组成，并再次获胜；节目特别指出两队组合已形成良好战绩。
- 红队延续了上一场团队挑战后的沟通与协作问题。
- 本集确认第15季冠军杰西和杰西卡已从节目搭档关系发展为夫妻，并继续举办私人活动、快闪店、烹饪课及品牌合作。
- 蓝队获胜成员获得安全资格；红队至少有一人必须离场。

### 笑点与可供后续引用的内容
- 戈登看到冬季城堡婚礼后瑟瑟发抖，调侃下次“厨艺大师”婚礼应该改在海滩举行。
- 乔开玩笑说自己要去找普罗塞克起泡酒，戈登立即要求他也带一杯。
- 红队玉米糊被形容为像“直接扣在盘子里的麦片粥”。
- 戈登在锅具起火后说：“现在你们火力全开了。”
- 婚礼宾客称蓝队菜品“恰到好处”；戈登则称红队前期出餐像一场闹剧。
- “链条的强度取决于最薄弱的环节”成为本集解释团队失败的核心比喻。

### 未解决线索
- 下集将进行受社交媒体启发的神秘箱挑战。
- 尼克·迪乔瓦尼将作为特别嘉宾回归。

- “最具病毒传播力”的菜品主题会鼓励选手冒险，但预告明确暗示并非每次冒险都值得。

## S16E12

## S16E12

### Episode plot

- The episode opens with a recap of the royal wedding team challenge: Blue Team won and Aghata was eliminated. Tonight's challenge is a social-media-themed Mystery Box with special guest Nick DiGiovanni (Season 10 finalist turned viral creator with 50M+ followers and 20B views).
- The boxes contain phones and tablets; the brief is to cook a dish inspired by one's own culture with virality in mind — judged as hard on visuals as on flavor, 60 minutes on the clock.
- Two stakes are announced: starting tonight the immunity pin is gone, and the winner of the challenge gets a collaboration with Nick, while the best dish from each territory officially enters the top ten. One cook will be eliminated and miss the top ten.
- Nick's virality formula: visuals that stop people, flavor worth staying for, and accessibility so home viewers can recreate it.
- Top dishes per territory: Jaime (Asia-Pacific) — five-layer pandan surprise in a coconut cup served on dry ice; Julia (Europe) — Mount Etna raviolo, squid-ink pasta with ricotta, egg yolk, anchovy and tomato that "lavas" when cut; Dave (Africa) — Moroccan seffa medfouna, spiced chicken buried under sweetened steamed vermicelli in a tagine; Jake (Americas) — horchata crème brûlée in a churro bowl.
- Julia's raviolo wins the night; she gets the collaboration with Nick and takes Basia to the balcony with her.
- Bottom three: Foo (messy Vietnamese sizzling crepe taco), Peter (greasy, flat plantain funnel cake), and Maria (baby octopus rice with pressure-cooker-overtenderized octopus). Maria is eliminated.

### Important character actions

- Julia wins her first big prize of the season (she previously used her immunity pin in S16E10 to sabotage the Africa team); she admits she has little social media presence and dreams of opening a bakery, and Nick says the collab should change that.
- Jake finally gets recognition — top dish of Team Americas — but Gordon notes he still hasn't won a challenge outright; Jake calls the churro bowl his "win" dish and shares that he was adopted and not raised in a Mexican household, with horchata his connection to his culture.
- Dave gets his first top-dish placement for Team Africa and savors the validation as a content creator; he steams his vermicelli because Moroccans don't boil pasta.
- Jaime (who has 2M followers) nearly ruins his pandan dessert by swapping bowls — the dry ice ends up in the wrong vessel; judges call it a close call but love the dish's mystery.
- Basia gambles on a smash-burger-stuffed pierogi topped with a spicy cheese puff, combining a Ramsay recipe and a Nick recipe; judges say she "dodged a bullet" and didn't think it through, but she survives via Julia's balcony invite.
- Tkaiya forgets sugar in her hyper-realistic coconut mousse, patches it with powdered sugar at the last minute, and forgets the orange zest; her cake comes out slightly under — she survives.
- Maria (Brazilian, Bahia coast) pressure-cooks her octopus to tenderness but it comes out overcooked; the rice is praised but the dish never coheres. She is sent home, told "This is not the last of Maria."
- Foo, one of the oldest cooks and self-admittedly unfamiliar with social media, lands in the bottom but survives; his "only followers" are his two kids.

### Newly established names and terms

- Nick DiGiovanni: viral food creator, S10 finalist; collab prize is with him in Boston.
- Seffa medfouna: Moroccan dish; "medfouna" means buried — savory chicken under sweetened steamed vermicelli.
- Bobotie bomb: South African bobotie stuffed with rice, meat and egg in a "bomb" shape (dish mentioned, maker not named on screen).
- Horchata: cinnamon rice milk; churro bowl; crème brûlée.
- Pandan: described as a grounded, subtle, earthy vanilla.
- Mount Etna raviolo: squid-ink raviolo with a molten yolk "lava" effect over charcoal "volcanic ash" breadcrumbs.
- Bánh xèo: Vietnamese crepe, adapted into Foo's "sizzling taco."
- Tagine (Moroccan cooking vessel), flambé, puff puff (fried dough), chantilly cream, ganache, dacquoise.
- "The phone eats first" (手机先尝): the show's shorthand for social-media-era plating.
- Territory teams remain: Team Asia-Pacific, Team Europe, Team Africa, Team Americas.

### Continuity-relevant facts

- The immunity pin is retired starting this episode; even a winning dish no longer guarantees safety next round.
- The first four official top-ten members are named: Jaime, Julia, Dave, and Jake — the four territory top dishes.
- Maria, who won S16E10's immunity pin and helped Blue Team win S16E11, is eliminated — the season's early standout arc ends here.
- Julia's Boston collab with Nick DiGiovanni is now pending; Nick jokes it should fix her missing social presence.
- Jake's first-ever challenge win is still outstanding.
- Gordon and Nick previously made the world's largest beef Wellington and sushi roll together (50M+ views each); Nick recalls being yelled at by Gordon on this exact station when he had zero followers.

### Jokes and references useful for later episodes

- Gordon on Foo's plating: "The only chance this dish has got at going viral is if I was blind."
- Gordon offers to follow Foo on social media so he'd have three followers (his kids are the first two).
- "If it doesn't lava, it's not so much of a volcano" — the judges' test for Julia's raviolo.
- Nick: "I work a flambé into videos that don't even need a flambé"; Tiffany wishes Peter had flambéed tableside with cinnamon on the sparks.
- Joe's deadpan "you got a lot of brown balls there" to Heidi.
- Basia combining Ramsay's and Nick's recipes, and Ramsay's "you dodged a bullet."
- Maria's theory that "random stuff goes viral" — and Joe's dry "good luck."
- Heidi: "Is it hot in here or is it me?" — "All of the above."
- Jaime's dry-ice bowl swap becomes a "close call" gag; Tkaiya's repeated "I forgot the damn sugar."

### Unresolved threads

- Next episode opens the top ten with a MasterChef classic: the tag team challenge, cooking dishes based on the judges' fondest food memories, with self-chosen partners and a double elimination.
- The preview promises partner friction: "Switch!", a shepherd's pie, lemon-slicing confusion ("Just slice one!"), a burned dish, Aishu in the middle of it, and warnings that the pressure "could break them both."
- Only four top-ten spots are confirmed; the rest of the field must be trimmed, with the eliminated cook this week missing the cut.
- Maria's sendoff line ("This is not the last of Maria") leaves the door open for a return.

## S16E13

## S16E13

### Episode plot

- Recap: Julia won the social-media Mystery Box in S16E12, Maria was eliminated, and the top ten was set. Tonight opens in a "tropical MasterChef kitchen" — a jungle set built to promote DreamWorks' upcoming animated film "Forgotten Island" (a Filipino-folklore adventure about two best friends racing to save their most beloved memories).
- The challenge is the iconic tag team: partners cook together, one at the station while the other waits at the end, swapping on Gordon's "Switch!" call. They must replicate the judges' fondest food memories: Gordon's mother's shepherd's pie, Tiffany's striped bass over dirty rice with chermoula (from her farm upbringing, where nothing was wasted — gizzards and livers included), and Joe's veal milanese with sautéed rapini and oven-roasted tomatoes (his kids' favorite).
- Twist: contestants choose their own partners, from any territory, and it's a double elimination — the two worst cooks go home. 75 minutes. The winning team gets a huge advantage in the next challenge plus two tickets to the "Forgotten Island" red carpet premiere.
- Teams: Gray (Peter & Tkaiya), Pink (Dave & Jake), Blue (Heidi & Julia), Yellow (Basia & Foo), Red (Jaime & Aishu).
- The cook descends into chaos: Aishu burns her foot; Heidi forgets the egg yolks in the mash (so it won't gratinate); Jaime adds raw rice straight to the gizzards; Foo & Basia make chermoula first, rice undercooked potatoes, and nearly never get the shepherd's pie in the oven; the final minutes are frantic lemon-slicing ("Just slice one!") and last-second plating.
- Tasting: Gray Team is strong — shepherd's pie slightly overcaramelized but delicious, fish crispy, rice "just not dirty enough," veal a touch thick but tender. Pink Team is the standout — shepherd's pie with beautiful piped peaks, crispy-skin bass with a chermoula that "sings," and a veal milanese Joe calls a "dead ringer" that "almost tastes exactly like mine." Blue Team's shepherd's pie mash is a mess (but lamb delicious), their bass arrived without the chermoula — "totally incomplete" — though their veal was the best of their three. Yellow Team's shepherd's pie is "a pile of poop" with thyme stalks in the mash and lamb obliterated by herbs, the rice is undercooked, the veal crust burnt. Red Team's shepherd's pie is an "oil slick / giant zit" (undrained lamb fat), the fish is raw in the center with unblended chermoula and coriander seeds everywhere — "the worst dish we tasted."
- Pink Team (Dave & Jake) wins. Gray Team is also called forward as a standout but doesn't take the prize. Blue Team (Heidi & Julia) just barely survives — "did just enough." Red Team — Jaime and Aishu — are the double elimination. Basia and Foo survive.
- Sendoffs: Aishu is proud of representing India and says she's a much better cook than when she started. Jaime came to showcase her dad's Malaysian cooking, will keep making Malaysian food online and hopes for a food truck with her dad. Joe: the industry gains "two more strong women."

### Important character actions

- Dave & Jake get their first tag-team win — Jake's first outright challenge win of the season (resolving the running note that he'd never won); they joke about both wearing pink to the premiere.
- Peter & Tkaiya show the communication Gordon praised — clean handoffs, only minor deviations (rice "not dirty enough," veal a bit thick).
- Heidi & Julia's mother-daughter dynamic is called "wonderful but also dangerous" by the judges; Heidi's forgotten egg yolks and a rushed bass without sauce nearly cost them; Gordon tells them to stop rushing and be thorough.
- Foo & Basia are "the blind leading the blind" (Tiffany) — wrong priorities, undercooked potatoes riced, herb stalks in the mash; Foo's patience-first plan fails, but they survive.
- Jaime & Aishu fall apart on every dish — Aishu burns her foot, Jaime's gizzard-rice gambit misses the mark, the fish is raw — and become the eliminations; the judges stress it was lack of technique, not just bad time management.
- Judges' backstories surface: Gordon's mother's shepherd's pie, Tiffany's farm childhood (dirty rice from offal), Joe's kids' veal milanese.

### Newly established names/terms

- "Forgotten Island" (《遗忘之岛》): upcoming DreamWorks animated film inspired by Filipino folklore; theme of the challenge and source of the premiere-ticket prize.
- Shepherd's pie (牧羊人派): Gordon's mother's recipe — ground lamb ragu, egg-yolk mashed potatoes piped with butter, finished in the oven.
