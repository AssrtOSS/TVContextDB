## S02E01

## S02E01 Summary — *All's Fair*

### Episode Plot
Season 2 opens with the founding trio (Allura, Liberty, Emerald) navigating post-prison fallout: Carr Lane is free on a technicality (alibi at La Contenta ranch), the framing murder remains unsolved, and Dina Standish tries — and fails — to broker peace. Dina slaps Carr; Carr retaliates by confessing she planted the dementia drug and turned Dina’s maid. The firm takes on **Aphrodite Lovesong**, wife of media mogul **Ray Royale**, who never signed her prenup — her signature was written in **thymolphthalein** (invisible ink revealed by sodium hydroxide, then acid-fades). With both notary-witnesses dead, the mediation judge voids the prenup, awarding Aphrodite half of Ray’s estate. She announces *“Limp Dick Quarterly”* with Ray’s face on the cover.  

Allura throws a **Scorpio Ball** (homage to Grace Kelly’s 1940 party, Elizabeth Taylor’s Tiziani cape) on November 5 — Carr’s birthday — deliberately uninviting her. Dina insists on a truce invite. At the ball: **Liberty comes out as a lesbian** (“power lesbian”), introducing girlfriend **Jamie Stevenson**. Carr launches a vicious homophobic attack; Emerald and Milan physically confront her. Allura is furious at the chaos. The episode ends with a live scorpion released on the dance floor.

## Episode S02E03 — "Thanksgiving"

### Plot
Gloria Bloom, founder of the 25-location organic market/deli chain *Food! Gloria's Food!*, hires Grant, Ronson & Greene to divorce her husband Winston Bloom. Her one demand in the settlement is that Winston never receive the original recipe for her tuna noodle casserole — the dish she served at their first Thanksgiving. The firm (Allura, Liberty, Emerald, with Dina advising, and Carr Lane handling Gloria's side of the mediation) negotiates with a judge/mediator presiding over a settlement that has "dragged on since the dawn of time." The judge warns that if it is not resolved tomorrow — Thanksgiving Day — she vacates the settlement and they start over with a new mediator.

Around the case, the episode stages a full ensemble Thanksgiving: Dina's feast (her boyfriend Raul, Liberty and Dina's British-food argument), Emerald's three sons arriving to take the holiday hostage, a separate Ohio custody mediation for Milan and Chase, Carr's traumatic "Pretzel Jell-O Loaf Surprise", Gloria cooking a revenge menu for the firm, and Allura's weapon collection. It ends on an unexplained death threat against Allura.

### Important character actions
- Gloria (client): delivers the recipe monologue ("two high school sweethearts, one tomato, a heaping of success, and tossed cranberries"), made two Thanksgivings, cooked a signature heirloom turkey from her book *Mastering the Art of the Holiday*, refuses to yield the recipe ("I'd rather die"), explains it is her grandmother's gift — passed down through three generations, never written down, "It was love." At the very end, in Allura's office, she reveals the trick in her lemon chiffon is orange peel.
- The firm: interrogates Gloria, hires professional taste testers who reverse-engineer the casserole, and discovers the casserole is sold at the hot bar of Gloria's own stores — so Winston's demand is a power play, not a legal right. Proposed solution: give him a lifetime supply, not the recipe.
- Carr: represents Gloria, clashes with Gloria ("your stubborn-ass client"), brings the Jell-O loaf and its origin story, insists Dina Standish carve the turkey (Mayflower descendant), then has to talk Gloria down from the "outbreak of feminism" reaction.
- Dina: is "shook up" and reveals something in her past "I've never fully faced and can't run away from anymore"; discloses the plane scare; carves the turkey privately in the break room; cooks succotash she learned from "living with the tribe for two months."
- Brody (aspiring chef, working under Gloria): takes the carving gig, admits he wants to be a great chef, and stays after Dina's condition — he must learn "the Dina Standish method of turkey carving."
- Allura: shows off an M-9 Bayonet taken from an Albanian terrorist who held the Nespresso CEO hostage, offers food on it, tells a Jaws/folding-seat story, and is on a "peptide fat burner" so she won't eat enough to be poisoned. Ends the episode receiving a threat.
- Milan: pregnant ("the baby hasn't flown on a plane yet"), came to LA to support "my girls"; appears unchosen, "like I'm cursed."
- Chase: mediator scene with his ex over where his daughter Capricorn spends the holidays (coin toss → Chase gets Christmas); then shows up in LA on Thanksgiving Day anyway with Cap and a nanny, having chosen his new family.
- Emerald: gets her sons to leave for Boston/Linus Torvalds, Ava, and a camping trip, in exchange for all three back for Christmas.

## Episode Plot
The season premiere picks up after the Scorpion Ball cliffhanger. The firm travels to Martha's Vineyard to scatter Dina's late husband Doug's ashes, but the private flight becomes a near-death experience when both engines catch fire and electrical power fails. # Alls Fair — S02E05

## Episode plot
Dina Standish is incapacitated in a hospital (in a coma, though still speaking). Liberty visits her and the two bond over resentment of Allura ("Mommy Dearest"), agreeing to dig up Allura's secrets. Carr then drops the bombshell that she is Dina's daughter and, under the founder's agreement, Dina's executor/proxy while she is incapacitated.

Meanwhile the firm takes on Crystal Coats, whose 11-month marriage to Cody Coats imploded when his sister Orlanda threw paint at her at the wedding ("Eat paint, slut!") and then moved into their guest house; Cody chose Orlanda over Crystal. Liberty urges realistic expectations (roughly six months' alimony), while Carr pushes for the house and guest house. Crystal ultimately files for annulment on grounds of non-consummation, backed by a post-wedding PTSD diagnosis, though she admits to "hand stuff." Opposing counsel Jay Wilson (hearing impaired, with interpreter/lawyer George) outmaneuvers Liberty in mediation, but she is impressed rather than furious.

Carr engineers a vote to create an HR department, and the firm hires Reese Calloway (Cornell BA, Yale dual MBA/MS in HR dev, ex–Canterbury Katz & Crane). Reese immediately flags Allura's "business thong" and Liberty's décolletage ("a gaping, glistening ravine") as creating a sexually hostile work environment, and notes no employees received paid counseling after the Thanksgiving mass shooting in the building. Allura and Liberty want Reese gone and blame Carr.

Separately, Allura sits by Dina's bedside cooking a complex cake (Grand Marnier, Illy espresso, orange-liqueur soak, chocolate glaze) for the day Dina wakes. Bodyguard Raul delivers intel confirming stalker Marcus Weller bought "Lady of Fatima Bull Scorpions" on the black market, trespassed at Van Nuys Airport disguised as ground crew to sabotage Allura's plane, and was outside her house; Raul then resigns.

The episode ends on Liberty and Carr: at lunch, Allura and Emerald learn Liberty and Carr are sleeping together ("Damn right… more than an alliance… psychosexual"). Liberty proposes hiring Jay Wilson to neutralize him as a rival; Allura and Emerald object under the firm's all-female tenet, but Carr holds the swing vote if Dina dies, since she would permanently inherit Dina's position (2–2 deadlock). Liberty declares they will interview Jay anyway, then Carr files an HR complaint against Liberty herself.
