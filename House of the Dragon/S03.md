## S03E06

注：系列背景文件未找到；以下依据完整双语字幕整理。

### 剧情概述

- 河间地军队在腾石镇附近伏击海塔尔军，欧尔蒙·海塔尔的军队遭受重创。
- 君临陷入混乱：金袍军伤亡、辞职，街道治安恶化，海塔尔支持者煽动民众反对雷妮拉。
- 雷妮拉拒绝直接焚烧腾石镇，转而派刺客刺杀欧尔蒙；拜拉席恩使者抵达后，刺客伪装成使团发动袭击。
- 君临城中流言四起，民众公开支持“英勇的戴伦”。雷妮拉决定调遣瓦列利昂士兵进城恢复秩序。
- 伊耿与拉里斯计划乘船前往厄索斯；伊耿坚持寻找瓦格哈尔，拉里斯则建议前往凯岩城建立新宫廷。
- 伊蒙德与瓦格哈尔分离，藏身赫伦堡，并受到艾莉丝·河文的照料。艾莉丝透露赫伦堡曾有龙蛋遗留百年。
- 梅莎丽亚向雷妮拉透露伊蒙德可能在赫伦堡；雷妮拉以海伦娜的安全为条件，迫使梅莎丽亚协助寻找伊蒙德。
- 腾石镇的战局仍在继续，雷妮拉最终承认自己离开战场和君临太久，准备重新介入局势。

### 重要人物行动

- 雷妮拉：坚持避免屠杀自己的臣民；加强对阿莉森与海伦娜的看管；派人寻找伊蒙德；准备调兵守卫君临。
- 阿莉森：得知海伦娜怀孕后，曾试图带她逃离君临；因此被雷妮拉加倍监视。她开始意识到自己和海伦娜都已成为政治人质。
- 海伦娜：怀孕；继续表现出预知未来的能力，并指出伊蒙德所在的赫伦堡。
- 伊耿：因伤势和政治局势准备逃往厄索斯，但仍执着于寻找瓦格哈尔。
- 拉里斯：策划带伊耿离开维斯特洛，并主张前往凯岩城；继续利用伊耿的恐惧和依赖。
- 伊蒙德：脱离瓦格哈尔后躲入赫伦堡；在艾莉丝的影响下开始重新思考自己的孤独与命运。
- 艾莉丝·河文：收留伊蒙德，暗示赫伦堡拥有古老力量，并提出自己将成为“水之女领主”、伊蒙德成为身旁的龙王。
- 科利斯与阿利恩·瓦列利昂：决定调遣瓦列利昂士兵前往君临；阿利恩试图弥合自己与父亲之间的隔阂。
- 休：承认自己已成为沃米索尔的骑手并获封骑士，却发现妻子凯特并不认同他的选择。
- 欧尔蒙·海塔尔：继续以腾石镇为据点，试图拖延时间、争取大诸侯支持。
- 加尔温·海塔尔：对伊蒙德的统治感到恐惧，决定返回旧镇，为海塔尔家族可能的失败收拾残局。
- 梅莎丽亚：为保护自己和海伦娜而透露伊蒙德的下落，但要求雷妮拉保证二人最终获得自由。

### 新确立的名字与术语

- 腾石镇：欧尔蒙·海塔尔的军事据点，也是下一阶段战事的核心。
- 富特利堡：雷妮拉考虑袭击的地点，属于富特利家族。
- 秃鹰王：长期劫掠风暴地的多恩匪首。
- 戴伦·坦格利安：“戴伦一世”“英勇的戴伦”，海塔尔阵营正式拥立的新王。
- 阿里昂·拜拉席恩爵士：博罗斯·拜拉席恩的表亲，奉命前往腾石镇谈判。
- 水之女领主：艾莉丝对未来身份的自称。
- 梦卜者：海伦娜的预言能力，与丹妮丝·坦格利安预见末日的传统相联系。
- 梦火：海伦娜的龙。
- 雷妮亚·坦格利安：曾在女儿死后前往赫伦堡，并在此度过余生。
- 青铜之怒：沃米索尔的称号；休已成为其骑手。
- 托伦·曼德勒爵士：现任财政大臣，负责分配君临日益匮乏的资源。

### 连续性关键信息

- 海伦娜怀有伊耿的孩子；雷妮拉担心这个男孩日后成为王位争夺的工具。
- 阿莉森与海伦娜的逃亡计划失败，二人被严格限制行动。
- 伊蒙德与瓦格哈尔分离，雷妮拉尚未掌握瓦格哈尔的确切位置。
- 雷妮拉拥有六条龙，伊蒙德目前只有一条龙且处于失联状态。
- 赫伦堡可能藏有古老龙蛋，龙蛋已被遗弃约一百年。
- 海伦娜的预言能力得到明确确认，她关于伊蒙德位置的判断促成了雷妮拉的行动。
- 戴伦已经获得海塔尔与拜拉席恩阵营的公开拥立，王位争夺出现新的合法化方向。
- 瓦列利昂军队将从潮头岛调往君临，城中即将出现新的军事力量。
- 三女儿联盟的海上袭扰已经减少，瓦列利昂在海上的战果开始显现。

### 笑话与后续参考

- 雷妮拉讥讽龙焰能“凭魔法辨别”无辜者与罪人，反驳直接焚城的主张。
- 阿莉森回忆集市、水果摊、鹅叫和热馅饼，感叹战争前的日常生活已一去不返。
- 加尔温讥讽伊蒙德最大的战斗仿佛只是与消化不良作斗争。
- 伊蒙德以“男人总得有些简单的乐子”为自己离开山上的行为辩护，随后遭警告再踏入君临就会被挖掉眼睛。
- 拉里斯用“冬天的青蛙在冰下冬眠”劝伊耿隐藏伤势，让世人以为他已死。
- 艾莉丝关于“水之女领主”和“龙王”的话，可能预示她与伊蒙德将形成长期而特殊的同盟。

- 刺客袭击是否会成功，以及戴伦在海塔尔阵营中的实际影响力。
- 伊蒙德能否重新找到瓦格哈尔，雷妮拉是否会派人进入赫伦堡追捕他。
- 梅莎丽亚与雷妮拉的交易能否兑现，阿莉森和海伦娜是否最终获释。
- 戴伦、欧尔蒙与拜拉席恩军队将在腾石镇采取何种行动。
- 伊耿与拉里斯能否安全抵达厄索斯，或转而控制凯岩城。
- 休与凯特的婚姻是否破裂，以及沃米索尔的加入将如何改变龙战争的力量平衡。

## S03E01

### 剧情概述

- 伊耿二世重伤卧床后逃离君临，拉里斯·斯壮随行。途中二人被截获，身份险些暴露，最终前往暮谷镇并计划乘船前往龙石岛。
- 阿莉森秘密前往龙石岛，提出只要雷妮拉放过自己、海伦娜和杰赫拉，便可结束战争。雷妮拉接受了可能性，却仍决定夺取君临。
- 伊蒙德骑瓦格哈尔前往赫伦堡与克里斯顿·科尔会合。奥托则命令伊蒙德前往赫伦堡，以吸引戴蒙出战并避开雷妮拉的龙袭。
- 雷妮拉派科利斯率舰队夺取君临港口。三女儿王国舰队突然袭击咽喉湾，海战全面爆发。
- 瓦列利昂舰队在复杂水道中遭到伏击。雷妮丝、贝拉和杰卡里斯率龙参战，混战中偷羊贼失控，贝拉受伤，杰卡里斯与沃马克斯也陷入危险。

### 重要人物行动

- 雷妮拉试图以夺取君临结束战争，同时拒绝继续被保护，展现出强烈的王者意志。
- 杰卡里斯为保护雷妮拉，下令将她锁在房中；雷妮拉命令女王卫队解除禁闭，母子关系出现严重冲突。
- 贝拉支持雷妮拉参战，并担心克里斯顿和加尔温落入陷阱。
- 阿莉森承认自己曾推动伊耿登基，却没有教会他如何统治；她随后劝伊蒙德前往赫伦堡。
- 伊蒙德开始实际掌握绿党军政权力，并被阿莉森称为新的王权核心。
- 克里斯顿·科尔带领军队追击兰尼斯特军队，坚持处决强奸村民的士兵以维持军纪。
- 乌尔夫自称拥有坦格利安血统，是“龙种”，并以此解释自己能够驾驭龙。
- 洛哈尔率三女儿王国舰队发动攻击，目标包括海蛇本人及潮头堡。

### 新确立的人名与术语

- 欧尔蒙·海塔尔：率一万五千名海塔尔士兵北上，并带着戴伦和他的龙。
- 戴伦·坦格利安：阿莉森与韦赛里斯之子，已在河湾地随军行动。
- 乌尔夫：自称“龙种乌尔夫”，出身厄索斯底层，声称拥有王族血统。
- 洛哈尔：三女儿王国舰队指挥者，自称“屠龙者”。
- 半颚：三女儿王国舰队中的分队指挥者。
- 母拳号：洛哈尔的旗舰。
- 潮木王座：洛哈尔计划夺取潮头堡后坐上的象征性王座。
- 咽喉湾：连接黑水河与狭海的海域，有三个主要入海口。
- 龙牙礁：龙石岛水道中的浅滩和危险航道。
- 龙水：来自伊班的一种酒，名称带有明显的高等瓦雷利亚语或异域语音特色。

### 连续性关键信息

- 伊耿二世仍未被确认死亡，但已经离开君临；绿党内部开始围绕王位继承重新调整权力。
- 伊蒙德目前是绿党最具军事威胁的人物，拥有瓦格哈尔；阿莉森担心他最终被雷妮拉的多条龙围杀。
- 雷妮拉已拥有新的龙骑士与龙力量，但部分龙骑士尚未完全受控。
- 海蛇舰队原本封锁咽喉湾，却遭三女儿王国舰队伏击；潮头堡成为敌军的直接目标。
- 阿莉森与雷妮拉曾经的友谊仍是双方沟通的唯一私人纽带，但政治互信极其脆弱。
- 杰卡里斯因担心雷妮拉重蹈鸦栖堡的覆辙，开始以保护为名限制她的行动。

### 笑点与后续可用的引用

- 乌尔夫认为骑士只能得到一匹马，而自己已经有龙；他更在意城堡、酒和女人，体现其粗俗现实的性格。
- 乌尔夫说自己“认下”了龙种身份：王族血统可能来自传闻、骗局或真实家世，暂时仍不确定。
- 伊耿自嘲自己是“什么国王都不是”，还讥讽自己的王座和保护者，延续其自卑、逃避责任的状态。
- 洛哈尔把自己塑造成屠龙英雄，并宣称要坐上潮木王座、拿海蛇的耳朵下酒，显示其个人复仇远重于契约本身。
- “哪个国王当政不都一样”反映绿党军队对王位争夺的冷漠，也可用于表现军中对统治者更替的讥讽。

### 未解决线索

- 伊耿二世与拉里斯究竟逃往何处，是否会被雷妮拉一方找到。
- 阿莉森的和平提议是否真诚，雷妮拉是否会继续执行夺取君临的计划。
- 伊蒙德能否安全抵达赫伦堡，以及他是否会主动引诱戴蒙决战。
- 洛哈尔能否攻破潮头堡并俘获或杀死科利斯。
- 咽喉湾海战的最终伤亡与胜负尚未揭晓。
- 偷羊贼为何失控、是否仍受某位龙骑士控制，以及贝拉、杰卡里斯和各自龙只的生死状态，均待后续确认。

## S03E07

# S03E07 — "The Red Dragon and the Gold" (or equivalent title)

## Episode Plot

The episode weaves together multiple fronts of the Dance of the Dragons as the war reaches a critical inflection point. In King's Landing, Rhaenyra holds the Iron Throne but her rule is fraying — the streets are unsafe, petitioners go unheard, and her alliance with Mysaria fractures. In the Riverlands, Aegon II makes his final stand at Rook's Rest. Daemon's long concealment of Rhaena as Sheepstealer's rider is exposed, shaking Rhaenyra's trust. Aemond and Alicent reunite in hiding, while Corlys remains a Green hostage.

## Important Character Actions

- **Aegon II** refuses to flee Rook's Rest despite Ser Tyland Lannister's plea to surrender. He declares himself the true anointed king, invokes the Seven, and commands Sunfyre to burn the approaching Mooton forces — choosing a glorious death over ignominious flight.
- **Rhaenyra** rejects petitioners from the throne, dismisses Mysaria's counsel, and is confronted with the reality that her restraint has cost her the people's goodwill. She orders a strike on Harrenhal.
- **Daemon** is revealed to have hidden Rhaena as Sheepstealer's rider for months, presenting a head he claimed was her killer. His willingness to deceive Rhaenyra for his daughter's sake becomes an open wound between them.
- **Rhaena** confesses she cannot live cut off from those she loves and resolves to hunt Vhagar alone to redeem herself — or die absolved.
- **Aemond** reunites with Alicent, who reveals she tried to kill Aegon. He accepts her and declares he will father a child with her, continuing the Targaryen line in exile.
- **Mysaria**, spurned by Rhaenyra and called a whore, pivots to Alyn of Hull and offers him Driftmark — "Lord of the Tides" — if he turns against Rhaenyra.
- **Corlys** is held by the Greens, who believe his captivity will deter the Velaryons from attacking King's Landing.
- **Ser Tyland** chooses to die beside Aegon rather than abandon him. **Larys Strong** plans to immortalize Aegon's last stand in story and song.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Winter Wolves** — a military force (likely a sellsword or rebel company) operating in the field
- **Tom Tangletongue** — an informant reporting to the Vale lords
- **Janos** — a missing person sought by the same network
- **Rook's Rest** — the castle where Aegon makes his last stand
- **Fairmarket** — a Riverlands town near the Blue Fork where Sheepstealer was sighted
- **Mooton of Maidenpool** — house sworn to Rhaenyra, marching on Rook's Rest
- **Ser Adrian** — the knight who helped Rhaena escape the Red Keep via a secret gate
- **Honeyholt** — a minor castle Mysaria offers Alyn as a trifle before naming Driftmark
- **Lord of the Tides** — the title of the Driftmark ruler, now vacant with Corlys captive
- **Naathi** — a people who eat only vegetables (used as a dietary joke by the Hightower)
- **Daor** — a name called out during the dragon confrontation scene

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Rhaena is confirmed as Sheepstealer's rider — a major reveal with implications for dragon-rider balance
- Daemon has been actively deceiving Rhaenyra to protect Rhaena; his loyalty is now conditional
- Aegon II is presumed dead by Alicent; his actual death at Rook's Rest appears to occur on-screen
- Aemond and Alicent plan to produce a child — continuing the incestuous Targaryen tradition
- Corlys's captivity is a strategic asset for the Greens; without him, the Velaryon fleet may hesitate
- Rhaenyra's rule in King's Landing is unstable: jeering in the streets, foul matter smeared on her doorstep, people afraid to go out at night
- The river host is one day from Tumbleton; Velaryon forces will reach King's Landing the next day
- More Hightower loyalists are being discovered and handed to confessors (torturers)
- Helaena experiences prophetic "flashes" — a gray mare, references to the Winter Wolves and Tumbleton — but refuses to clarify them
- Alys Rivers is at Harrenhal with Daemon and does not trust Alicent

## Jokes and References

- "A hundred sharp little cocks all vying for attention" — a petitioner's description of the crowd around Rhaenyra's throne
- "Pigeon pie before the executioner's block?" — Corlys on his captivity meal
- "Just as the people of Naathi do" — the Hightower's vegetarian joke
- "A whore will always and ever remain a whore" — Rhaenyra's dismissal of Mysaria
- "Decent ale, that" — Alyn's understated reaction to news of Daemon striking him in the street

## Unresolved Threads

- Daemon's true allegiance and current whereabouts after flying out before dawn
- Whether Aegon actually dies at Rook's Rest or survives the encounter
- Rhaena's solo hunt for Vhagar — will she find the dragon, and at what cost?
- Alyn's decision: accept Mysaria's offer of Driftmark or remain loyal to Rhaenyra
- The approaching Velaryon fleet and river host — will they assault King's Landing or Tumbleton?
- Helaena's visions — what do the gray mare, Winter Wolves, and Tumbleton signify?
- Alys Rivers' influence over Daemon and her hostility toward Alicent
- The fate of Corlys as a hostage — will the Velaryons trade or attack?
- The skull Daemon brought Rhaenyra — whose was it, and what does it mean for his credibility?

## S03E08

House of the Dragon S03E08 — "The Queen's Peace" (or similar working title, per subtitles)

Synopsis
Episode 8 opens in the aftermath of the Gullet. Rhaenyra, devastated and enraged, lashes out at Jacaerys for disobeying her command not to fly, blaming him for the chaos that killed his own dragon Vermax and many Velaryon crew. In grief she lashes at everyone "who stood by," directing her wrath at Lord Corlys and the assembled court.

Key beats
- Sheepstealer rider Addam of Hull (the "dragonseed" bastard given safe passage to Pentos) defies orders, returns to the Vale, and begs Alys Rivers of Harrenhal for asylum. Alys refuses him entry but quietly shelters both Addam and Sheepstealer. She confesses to Corlys the same bargain: she will look the other way on the wild dragon in exchange for being left alone. Alys also demands Harrenhal as her price for helping Rhaenyra, and Corlys can only promise to speak of "some other reward."
- A Black army song celebrates the twin river victories at the Red Fork and lakeshore — 10,000 drowned, Daemon's "cunning trick" credited. Corlys receives a message from Rhaenyra: Jace is dead, the Black cause is now hers, and she orders the riverlords to march south, garrison Harrenhal, and meet her at King's Landing. She promises: "we will send dragons to scour the skies above you."
- In the Riverlands, Daemon returns from the Gullet and a run-in with Aemond (whose Vhagar escaped). He argues bitterly with Rhaenyra about her renewed trust in Alicent's word and about her methods; Mysaria's growing influence clearly rankles him. Alys's ominous prophecy lands: "your coming has been an omen of the end … Many things."
- Aegon II and Larys Strong, having fled the capital, drag the captive Alyn of Hull (Addam's brother) across the coast. Aegon is badly burned, dependent on milk of the poppy, furious at Alyn for revealing his identity to his captors, then forced to use him as a guide. Scouts report that Tyland Lannister's Triarchy fleet, sent against Rhaenyra, has been smashed at sea — Aegon's road to Dragonstone is now blocked.
- At Dragonstone, the small council reels. Baela reports the Sheepstealer attack: the wild dragon had a rider; it killed Jace. Rhaenyra orders Mysaria to find them, but news that Vhagar has flown for the Riverlands and the Gullet's defeat leave her gutted.
- The episode's centerpiece is the confrontation between Alicent Hightower and Larys Strong. Alicent sells her own Hand to the Gold Cloaks: she tells Commander Largent that Rhaenyra is returning and that Helaena has authorized her to let the queen take the throne. Larys, returning, confronts Alicent with thinly veiled threats about her past with Criston Cole and warns her that the Gold Cloaks meeting was treason. Alicent, cornered, screams for help.
- At court, Queen Dowager Alicent is arrested — by Ser Soren, at Rhaenyra's earlier written command. Grand Maester Orwyle exposes her treachery, citing the warning from her "sworn protector." Lord Jasper Wylde (Master of Laws) is also seized. He is later replaced, in the throne room, by Lord Bartimos Celtigar as Lord Commander of the City Watch contingent (rewritten title in subtitles: "Lord Commander of the …").

## S03E03

# House of the Dragon S03E03 Summary

## Plot
Following the fall of King's Landing, Rhaenyra Targaryen has taken the Iron Throne but must immediately confront a kingdom in ruins. The episode opens with Lord Ormund Hightower surrendering his army on the Roseroad after Daemon offers clemency; Ulf (one of Daemon's men) hints he has designs on the High Tower in Oldtown. Daemon also extracts Daeron Targaryen as a hostage to guarantee Ormund's good behavior.

In King's Landing, Rhaenyra interrogates the young captive she believes is Daeron, then learns from Grand Maester Orwyle that the crown treasury has been emptied — Tyland Lannister secretly shipped the royal gold out of the city before his presumed death in the Battle of the Gullet. Lord Corlys believes Tyland would have entrusted the gold only to Lord Jasper "Ironrod."

Facing famine (granaries empty, fields fallow, no ships to secure trade), the threat of Triarchy pirates raiding the Reach, and an empty treasury, Rhaenyra insists on a grand coronation to cement her legitimacy. Aemond has vanished after burning Daemon's garrison at Harrenhal and slaying its castellan; Vhagar and a mysterious new dragon, Sheepstealer, have not been seen for days. Mysaria reports a "maester of sorts" — a woman — at Harrenhal.

Rhaenyra confronts Alicent, who reveals she knew nothing of the gold's whereabouts and was kept in the dark by her own father Otto. Rhaenyra orders Mysaria to quietly purge the staff of Hightower loyalists. She then formally anoints herself by touching the Iron Throne — though bleeding in the process — after the High Septon refuses to perform the rite, having already anointed Aegon.

In small council, Corlys confesses that Alyn and Addam of Hull are his illegitimate sons by a shipwright. Rhaenyra knights Addam, Ulf (now styled "Ulf the White"), and Hugh (styled "Hugh the Hammer"), but delays legitimizing Corlys's bastards despite earlier agreement. She knights Ulf and Hugh with the Seven's vows; Addam's knighting ceremony is described more ambiguously.

Rhaenyra holds her first public audience, hearing petitions from a wool weaver (whose sheep were eaten by dragons), butchers, and Sylvi — a former Mysaria operative who demands justice, accusing wealthy lords of hoarding grain during the blockade. Rhaenyra then holds a banquet for nobles, serving them the meager rations the smallfolk endured, while her Gold Cloaks simultaneously raid their storerooms to redistribute hoarded grain.

In her chamber, Mysaria challenges Rhaenyra to also kill Daeron; Rhaenyra instead decides to send him to the Night's Watch (the Wall). When Alicent comes to visit her son before he departs, the captive is revealed to be an imposter — a common boy with bleached hair whose real mother Ormund threatened to hang. The real Daeron remains with Ormund in Oldtown.

The episode closes with Rhaenyra ordering all Hightower banners burned and a dragonkeeper arriving at the gate with news.
