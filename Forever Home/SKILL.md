# Forever Home (TV Series) — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Overview
- **Title**: Forever Home
- **Type**: British/Spanish murder mystery / crime drama television series
- **First air date**: 14 September 2026
- **Broadcaster**: Channel 5 (UK)
- **Season/episode structure**: 1 series, 6 episodes (6 × 60 minutes)
- **Production**: FilmNation Entertainment (UK) & Mediapro (Spain)
- **Based on**: *Forever Home* (2022 novel) by Graham Norton
- **Adapted by**: Clara Salaman (screenplay)
- **Directors**: Paul Gay, Claire Winyard

## Synopsis
Carol Crottie, newly married to Robert Barrie, discovers a long-dead body hidden in the basement of their Northumberland home. Robert, who is in the early stages of dementia, claimed his first wife Joan ran off with his brother 20 years ago. The corpse suggests otherwise. As Carol investigates with help from her mother Moira, dark family secrets unravel — involving Robert's children Kaden and Sally, their partners, and a neighbour known only as "Silent Dave." The series blends domestic thriller with dark comedy, shifting the novel's Irish setting to North-East England and changing the killer's identity from the book.

## World Setting
- **Primary location**: 7 Stable Row, an Edwardian house in Northumberland, North-East England (changed from Ireland in novel)
- **Time**: Contemporary (2026)
- **Atmosphere**: Autumnal, cosy-yet-shocking; "domestic thriller" tone à la Harlan Coben adaptations
- **Key locations**: The house (basement/cellar, faulty freezer), local leisure centre, police station, neighbourhood streets
- **Production note**: Filmed in Northumberland 2026; co-production UK/Spain

## Speaking Styles
- **Carol (Amy Nuttall)**: Measured, increasingly anxious but pragmatic; Northern English accent; internal monologue via reactions
- **Moira (Denise Welch)**: Loud, theatrical, comedic relief; broad Northern vowels; "Virgin Mother" exclamations; cerise elastane energy
- **Robert (Douglas Henshall)**: Fragmented, vague, occasionally lucid; dementia portrayed with restraint not caricature
- **Kaden (Lewis Cope)**: Defensive, spiralling; selfish but human
- **Sally (Jenny Walser)**: Sharp, selfish, secretive
- **Dan (Shane Zaza)**: Grounded counterpart to Kaden; equal weight in dysfunction
- **Silent Dave (Kevin Whately)**: Minimal dialogue; communicates through presence/looks
- **DCI Taylor (Ricky Shah)**: Procedural, sceptical

## Cultural Context
- **Channel 5 drama slate**: Part of the channel's push into original scripted drama (alongside *The Marlow Murder Club*, *The Feud*)
- **Graham Norton brand**: Bestselling novelist + chat-show host; previous adaptation *Holding* (ITV 2022)
- **Soap-star casting**: Nuttall (Emmerdale/Downton), Henshall (Shetland), Welch (Coronation Street/Waterloo Road), Whately (Inspector Morse/Lewis), Cope (Emmerdale) — familiar faces to UK audience
- **North-East England setting**: Specific regional flavour (accents, landscapes) replacing novel's Ireland
- **Dark comedy / domestic thriller hybrid**: "Indistinguishable Domestic Thriller Processing Complex™" (Guardian critique); tone closer to Harlan Coben Netflix than prestige HBO
- **Representation**: Interracial gay couple (Kaden/Dan) treated without angelising/villainising; dementia portrayed from family perspective
- **Autumnal aesthetic**: Cosy cardigans, fake coffees, garibaldis — deliberate visual language
