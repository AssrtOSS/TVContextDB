## S01E04

## S01E04 Summary — "Forever Home"

### Plot

Carol travels to Hawes to confront Joan Barrie, who she discovers is very much alive. Joan tells Carol her version of the past: Robert was emotionally neglectful after the children were born, his brother Neil came to stay, she and Neil fell in love, and Robert pressured her into leaving — making her feel like a selfish, damaged mother unworthy of her children. Joan claims Neil persuaded Robert to let her live in their mother's house. Carol finds Joan's account suspicious, particularly the detail about postcards sent under both their names.

Meanwhile, a decomposed male body wrapped in plastic is pulled from the river. Dan and his colleague Davina attend the scene; Newcastle CID arrives and DCI Isaac Taylor takes over, confirming it as a murder inquiry (blunt force trauma). Kaden is identified as a close relative DNA match — the victim is Neil Barrie. Robert is told at the care home; his dementia causes him to oscillate between recognising Neil and denying any knowledge of him.

Carol and Moira re-evaluate their assumptions: if Joan is alive, the body in the basement freezer was likely Neil all along, and Joan — not Robert — may be the killer who wrote the fake postcards. They agree to keep quiet, align their story, and stay close to Dan for intelligence on the investigation.

Joan makes her boldest move: she buys 7 Stable Row in cash, using a solicitor for a rapid completion. Kaden is furious when he discovers the buyer is his estranged mother. Sally, after meeting Joan privately, is emotionally overwhelmed and wants to believe her mother's return is genuine.

### Important Character Actions

- **Joan**: Reveals Robert and Moira were having an affair — a claim Carol has never heard. Threatens Carol directly: "This was never your home." Moves to reclaim Stable Row.
- **Carol**: Confronts Joan, then lies to police about her whereabouts. Defends Robert's character against Joan's accusations. Begins to suspect Joan killed Neil.
- **Moira**: Strategically keeps Dan close for inside information. Dismisses Joan's version of events and pushes Carol to protect themselves.
- **Kaden**: Meets Martha to deliver £2,000 of an owed £12,000; she threatens to cut off his access to the baby if he doesn't pay in full by week's end. Learns Joan bought the house and tries to stop the sale. Discovers Michelle was fired for the missing company money — but knows the ATM CCTV is non-functional, implying she may be scapegoated.
- **Dan**: Briefed on the body, provides local knowledge to DCI Taylor, but is formally taken off the case. Shares case details (gang theory) with the family, leaking information Carol later uses. Reveals to Kaden the ATM cameras don't work.
- **Sally**: Babysits for Patrick and Anna; Patrick's daughter Emily saw them together and asked why Sally was "fighting with Daddy." Sally and Patrick argue about the risk. She meets Joan alone and is emotionally drawn in.
- **Robert**: At the care home, momentarily remembers Neil ("He's in Stable Row"), then confabulates ("I don't know anybody called Neil"). Carol accompanies police to protect him during questioning.
- **Joan's confrontation with Carol**: Ends with Joan asserting she is moving back to Stable Row and wants Carol out.

## S01E05 — Summary

### Plot

Carol and Moira process the implications of Joan being alive: if the body in the basement freezer isn't Joan, it must be Neil. Carol has nowhere to live after Joan purchased 7 Stable Row in cash, and tensions escalate as Joan moves to reclaim the house, telling Carol to get out. Carol and Moira search the house for evidence proving Joan is a liar — Carol discovers Joan's old passport and confirms she never set foot in South America, contradicting Joan's cover story of fleeing to Peru, Chile, and Colombia with Neil.

A specialist policewoman interviews Robert at the care home with Carol as his "appropriate adult." She guides Robert back through his memories: Neil came to stay permanently ("Robert calls it his forever home"), and Robert recalls the day Neil died — he came home from the pharmacy and found Joan in the dark room with Neil's body. Robert's memory fragments into childhood trauma: Neil used to beat the children with a coiled telephone receiver on the head. The interviewer notes a small breakthrough and plans to return with sensory triggers (darkroom chemicals, music) to draw out more.

Dan and Davina examine the coroner's findings: Neil's body showed frostbite and had been frozen long before entering the water. The SCHAD enzyme indicates prolonged freezing, and bone analysis reveals Neil was in his 40s when he died, not 61 — meaning the murder happened decades ago. Dan spots a Fibonacci spiral pattern on the wound marks on Neil's skull, matching the wooden plank that killed him. The investigation team has been looking for suspects in the wrong decade.

Joan visits Robert at the care home and, when he lets slip that Neil was "in the freezer," she snaps and threatens him: "You keep your mouth shut, or I'll shut it for you." At the police station, Joan sits for an informal chat with DCI Taylor, claiming she and Neil lasted about two and a half years, split up, and she went travelling alone. She refuses a DNA swab, citing data-privacy concerns. She also drops the bombshell that Robert was having an affair with Moira behind her back.

Kaden finally confronts Joan face to face, telling her he doesn't love her and doesn't want her back. He recounts sitting at the window as a ten-year-old, waiting for a mother who never returned. His farewell: "I wish you'd fucking died. Stay away from my family."

Meanwhile, Sally confides in Moira that she has been having an affair with Patrick for six months. When confronted, Patrick denies everything and calls Sally delusional. At the local fundraiser, Sally retaliates by playing a recording of herself and Patrick having sex over the PA system, publicly humiliating Patrick and his wife Anna, who storms out with their daughter Emily.

Kaden picks up Martha to confront her about taking the baby and leaving. In the car, Martha goes into premature labour (she is only 27–28 weeks). Kaden panics, misfires a Siri voice command, and rushes her to the fundraiser venue where medical help is summoned.

The episode culminates with Dan arriving at the fundraiser and arresting Moira Crottie on suspicion of the murder of Neil Barrie, reading her the caution in front of everyone. Moira pleads with Silent Dave to intervene.

### Important Character Actions

- **Joan**: Visits Robert at the care home and threatens him to stay silent about Neil's body in the freezer. Under police questioning, she fabricates an alibi of travelling South America and refuses DNA. Reveals Robert and Moira's affair to the police. Shows a softer side consoling Sally after the sex tape debacle.
- **Robert**: In the care home interview, relives the day he found Neil dead — and his childhood abuse at Neil's hands. His memory flickers between past and present, providing crucial testimony before falling asleep.
- **Carol**: Searches the house with Moira, finds Joan's passport proving she never left the country. Presents this evidence to DCI Taylor. Confronts Moira about the affair with Robert, expressing shattered trust.
- **Moira**: Confesses to Carol that she and Robert had "a fling" during a rough patch, admitting Robert "loathed" Joan. Arrested at the fundraiser for Neil's murder.
- **Dan**: Makes a key forensic breakthrough (wrong decade, Fibonacci pattern, frostbite). Reinstated on the case. Arrests Moira at the fundraiser — a deeply personal moment given his relationship with Kaden.
- **Kaden**: Confronts Joan in a devastating scene, rejecting her completely. Drives Martha to hospital during her premature labour, revealing panic and vulnerability beneath his anger.
- **Sally**: Confesses the Patrick affair to Moira; plays the sex tape at the fundraiser in an act of self-destructive revenge. Joan consoles her afterward.
- **Silent Dave**: Greeted by Moira during her arrest ("Dave, do something"); his role remains enigmatic.

### Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Fibonacci pattern/code**: The mathematical spiral found on the wooden plank that killed Neil; Dan identifies it connecting the wound marks on Neil's skull.
- **SCHAD enzyme**: Forensic enzyme indicating Neil's body was frozen for an extended period before entering the water.
- **"Forever home"**: Robert's term for 7 Stable Row — the series title's literal origin. He tells the interviewer Neil stayed "forever," and he called the house his forever home.
- **The dark room**: Neil's photography darkroom in the Stable Row house; the site where Robert found Neil's body, and a locus of childhood trauma.
- **Mrs Grainger**: Long-time neighbour who remembers Neil fondly — "a lovely man" who did gardening and was great with kids. Notes the house was full of "non-stop rowing."
- **Nintendo Wii**: Used by the interviewer to anchor Robert's memory in the correct era.
- **Frostbite**: Physical evidence on Neil's body proving he was frozen before disposal in water.
- **"Lemon entry"**: Dan's mangled Sherlock-style deduction phrase, prompting Davina to ask if Neil "swam the last bit upstream."
- **Cagney and Lacey**: Moira's comparison of herself and Carol as a detective duo ("I'd be the glamorous one").
- **Scampi and chips**: Joan's nostalgic meal brought to Robert at the care home.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Neil was a photographer with a darkroom in the house. He was violent as a child — beating Robert and presumably others with a coiled telephone receiver. Robert eventually grew big enough to fight back.
- Robert worked at a pharmacy. The day Neil died, Robert came home early and found Joan with Neil's body in the dark room.
- Bone analysis places Neil's death in his 40s, meaning the murder occurred roughly 20 years ago, not recently. The team has been investigating the wrong time period.
- Joan's passport proves she never travelled to South America, Mexico, or anywhere abroad. She stayed in the UK the entire time, sending fake postcards to maintain the cover story.
- Robert and Moira had an affair during Robert's marriage to Joan. Moira admits it was "just a fling" but kept silent because she saw how in love Carol and Robert became.
- Kaden was 10 and Sally was 5 when Joan left. Kaden used to sit at the window waiting for her. Sally barely remembers her and used to tell people Joan died in a car crash.
- Martha is approximately 27–28 weeks pregnant with Kaden's child and goes into premature labour during a confrontation in Kaden's car.
- The fundraiser is a public community event — Sally plays the sex tape there, and Moira is arrested there.
- Emily (Patrick and Anna's daughter, about 5) was previously told by Patrick to keep quiet about seeing Sally and Patrick together.
- Dan aspires to become DCI; Davina mocks his ambitions. Dan was previously taken off the case but reinstates himself through his forensic findings.

### Unresolved Threads

1. **Moira's arrest**: Dan arrests Moira for Neil's murder, but the evidence against her is circumstantial. Is she truly guilty, or is this a misdirection?
2. **The wrong decade**: If Neil died 20 years ago, who were the suspects at that time, and what was the original context of the killing?
3. **Robert and Moira's affair**: Carol now knows. How will this affect her trust in Moira, her marriage, and the investigation?
4. **Joan's lies exposed**: Carol has photographic proof Joan never left the country. How will this evidence be used, and will DCI Taylor act on it?
5. **Martha's premature labour**: The baby is born (or arriving) months early. What are the medical implications, and how does this change the dynamic between Kaden, Martha, and the baby?

7. **Sally's self-destruction**: The sex tape gambit has alienated Anna and Patrick. Sally told Moira she's "sick of keeping secrets" — what else might she reveal?
8. **Robert's remaining memories**: The interviewer plans to return with sensory triggers. What else might Robert recall about the day of Neil's death?

12. **Dan's promotion ambitions**: He's back on the case and making real breakthroughs — will his findings conflict with DCI Taylor's theories?

### 剧情概要
本集为全季结局。莫伊拉被警方以涉嫌谋杀尼尔·巴里（Neil Barrie，尸体真实身份，即罗伯特的弟弟，“跟琼私奔”的说法被推翻）及妨碍司法公正逮捕。证据包括：裹尸围巾上的莫伊拉 DNA、VanWithoutAMan 租车公司 3 月 5 日收据（地址橡树园小径 11 号）、卡罗尔父亲大卫·克罗蒂渔具棚中与尸体一致的绳子和配重、货车内物证。莫伊拉坚称被陷害，只承认租车是“帮罗伯特的忙”。同时卡罗尔发现活的琼（Joan）就住在附近（护照显示她从未去过南美，明信片是伪造的，其中一张 2016 年马丘比丘明信片日期在尼尔死后十年，成为笑点兼线索）。卡罗尔在地窖箱子（尼尔的遗物）中发现影像证据：尼尔长期性侵五岁的萨莉（“Tina Ballerina”录像、暗房照片联页）。卡罗尔和父亲用 UV 灯查出遗物上全是血迹。真相层层揭开：凯登、萨莉、琼先后各自认罪，最终琼给出完整版本——她质问尼尔时被掐脖，罗伯特用楼梯柱头（pine cone 形、符合凶器在头骨留下的“斐波那契数列”花纹）砸晕尼尔，再用钉子钉入其眼睛；见照片后罗伯特崩溃，全家商定让琼假装与尼尔私奔出走，尸体由罗伯特藏进地窖夹墙密室（警方搜查时发现那面墙不合房屋结构）。莫伊拉获释，罗伯特因痴呆被认定 unfit to plead，将接受 trial of facts。
