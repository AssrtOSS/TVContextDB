## S01E04

## 剧情概述

- 帕特·墨菲在Hell’s Kitchen遇害。官方说法是他因毒品交易被几个波多黎各人杀死，但丹尼坚信格伦·基南与埃蒙·斯威尼下令有关。
- 约翰·戈蒂因文森特·扎卡罗失踪一事向斯威尼施压，并要求他杀掉米奇·弗拉纳根。斯威尼拒绝牺牲米奇，转而继续维持与意大利黑手党的脆弱关系。
- 吉米从被烧毁的Starship迪厅偷出六公斤可卡因，试图说服斯威尼让Westies进入毒品生意。斯威尼公开拒绝，却暗中把货卖给莱昂。
- 吉米和米奇发现斯威尼违背禁毒立场、私下出售毒品，还把所得十万美元捐给希伯尼安协会。两人开始怀疑斯威尼一直利用“保护街区”的形象为自己谋利。
- 斯威尼在希伯尼安协会晚宴上接受终身成就奖，并发表爱尔兰裔社区与Hell’s Kitchen归属感的演讲。与此同时，戈蒂安排费城杀手在晚宴上刺杀他。
- 格伦得知斯威尼准备杀掉丹尼，于是请求伯迪·波尔克利用联邦关系，把丹尼送到纽约以外、伪装成戒治诊所的安全屋。
- 布里奇特透露，她真正的身份是莎拉·麦肯纳，泰德·麦肯纳的女儿；她因伦敦地铁爆炸案被国际刑警组织通缉。她重新向IRA宣誓，并准备继续执行针对英国特工杰拉德·马卡姆上校的任务。

## 重要人物行动

- **斯威尼**：保护米奇；拒绝Westies公开贩毒；暗中出售六公斤可卡因；继续塑造社区领袖形象；威胁丹尼。
- **吉米**：偷走Starship的毒品；与斯威尼正面争论Westies的未来；发现斯威尼私卖毒品后动摇；向芬恩催收赌债，却因芬恩认识自己的母亲而暂时放过他。
- **米奇**：支持吉米扩大收入来源；逐渐看清斯威尼的虚伪，并试图说服吉米脱离对斯威尼的盲目忠诚。
- **格伦**：向丹尼道歉并解释自己离开家庭的原因；承认越战创伤、酗酒和前妻吸毒导致他抛下儿子；请求伯迪保护丹尼。
- **丹尼**：拒绝相信父亲，也拒绝离开Westies圈子；以掌握帕特命案疑点为条件，要求格伦让他回到原来的工作。
- **伯迪·波尔克**：同意尝试把丹尼转移到安全屋，并以斯威尼、卡斯特拉诺与Starship火灾之间的关系作为交换条件。
- **布里奇特**：承认自己仍在为IRA行动；她的任务目标是杀死曾杀害她父母的马卡姆上校。
- **戈蒂**：因毒品交易损失和向大保罗上贡的压力，决定绕过与斯威尼的协议，直接安排刺杀。

## 新确立的人名、组织与术语

- **文森特·扎卡罗（Vincent Zaccaro）**：失踪人物；戈蒂怀疑米奇与其失踪有关。
- **帕特·墨菲（Pat Murphy）**：Westies社区成员；被杀时即将成为父亲。
- **大保罗（Big Paul / Paul Castellano）**：对毒品交易采取双重标准，同时从相关生意中获利。
- **莱昂（Leon）**：毒品批发商，从斯威尼手中买下可卡因。
- **芬恩（Finn）**：经营杂货店、沉迷赛马并拖欠高利贷；认识吉米的母亲。
- **希伯尼安协会（Hibernian Society）**：以爱尔兰传统、天主教价值和公共服务为名举办表彰晚宴的社区组织。
- **莎拉·麦肯纳（Sarah McKenna）**：布里奇特·沃尔什的真实身份。
- **泰德·麦肯纳（Tadhg McKenna）**：布里奇特的父亲。
- **杰拉德·马卡姆上校（Colonel Gerald Markham）**：英国特工，被布里奇特认定为杀害她父母的人。
- **NORAID**：支持爱尔兰共和事业的组织；玛丽曾听过布里奇特在其活动上的演讲。

## 连续性要点

- 斯威尼公开反对毒品，实际却亲自卖货；他的社区慈善形象与黑帮经营者身份出现直接冲突。
- Jimmy与Sweeney之间的“门徒—养父”关系开始破裂；Jimmy仍感激斯威尼当年收留自己，却已无法忽视他的欺骗。
- Mickey与Jimmy形成更明确的政治与行动同盟，米奇开始推动Jimmy摆脱斯威尼的控制。
- Glenn与Danny的父子关系仍极度脆弱；丹尼已被卷入Westies内部冲突，斯威尼明确威胁要杀他。
- Starship迪厅火灾、被盗可卡因、斯威尼与卡斯特拉诺的关系，以及帕特的死亡可能属于同一条犯罪线索。
- 布里奇特对吉米隐瞒了更深的IRA背景；她的任务与马卡姆上校、伦敦地铁爆炸案和北爱尔兰冲突相连。
- 戈蒂已经决定杀死斯威尼，意大利黑手党与Westies的合作关系濒临崩溃。

## 玩笑与文化参考

- 斯威尼试穿礼服时抱怨腰围变大，埃迪讽刺他“背着自己喝啤酒”，体现老一代人物间的熟人式挖苦。
- 希伯尼安协会的“终身成就奖”被调侃为“捐十万美元即可获得”，暗示社区荣誉与黑帮金钱密切相连。
- 吉米以“布鲁克林有座桥在出售”讽刺他人仍相信斯威尼的清白。
- 芬恩拿“水渠赛马场35赔1”的内幕消息求情，延续其靠赌博翻身、却越陷越深的喜剧性形象。
- “Jacob’s Biscuits”是丹尼与母亲、姨妈艾琳之间的情感联系，后续可作为辨认丹尼情绪或家庭关系的细节。
- 葬礼上的“Slàinte”及希伯尼安晚宴的爱尔兰传统演讲，强化了Hell’s Kitchen作为爱尔兰裔社区的身份叙事。

## 未解决线索

- 戈蒂安排的刺杀能否在希伯尼安晚宴上成功？
- 斯威尼是否会被揭穿私卖可卡因，以及他从中获得的钱是否确实用于慈善？
- 帕特究竟因何被杀，斯威尼、格伦或毒品网络是否参与其中？
- 丹尼能否及时被送离Hell’s Kitchen？
- Jimmy会继续效忠斯威尼，还是与米奇一起反抗他？
- 布里奇特能否取回武器、完成针对马卡姆上校的任务，并平安回到吉米身边？

## S01E05

## S01E05 Summary

### Episode Plot

The episode weaves together three main threads: the IRA's assassination plot against Colonel Markham, Jimmy Roarke's entry into the cocaine trade, and Glenn Keenan's deteriorating position between the FBI and the Westies.

**IRA Assassination Plot.** Brendan Cahill reveals the rocket launcher meant for killing Colonel Gerald Markham has been lost. Bridget proposes using Semtex as a car bomb outside the British Consulate on 48th and Second Avenue, where Markham will receive an award on the 14th. She acknowledges civilian casualties are likely. To obtain the explosives, Bridget meets Randall, an ex-military arms dealer who charges $50,000 and quotes Pearse/Connolly poetry about blood and sacrifice. After the deal, Bridget executes Randall, claiming he was a drunk who might sell information about the cell to the highest bidder. This leaves Brendan without a clean source for future weapons.

**The Cocaine Deal.** Following a sit-down with Big Paul Castellano, Sweeney claims narcotics are forbidden — but secretly wants Jimmy to deal anyway, fronting $100,000 for the first load. Jimmy and Mickey meet Leon in Harlem, who teaches them to cut cocaine with baby laxative or inositol at a 1:1 ratio. At the exchange on 47th and 10th, Leon's associate Charlie Bricks (revealed to be on Gotti's payroll all along) pulls a gun and robs them. Jimmy and Mickey kill both Charlie and Leon, reclaiming the two kilos and the cash. Sweeney tells Jimmy to find a new supplier and warns that Gotti now knows he's dealing.

**Glenn's Downfall.** FBI Agent Birdie Polk pressures Glenn Keenan to wear a wire by threatening his son Danny's institutional placement. Glenn tries to plant doubt about Eddie Breen being an informant, but Sweeney refuses to believe it. Polk orders Glenn to arrest Breen to make Sweeney think Glenn is compromised and talk freely. The FBI interrogates Breen about Javits Center fraud (ghost names on Local 780 payrolls, no-show contracts at Pier 76), but Breen's lawyer gets him released. Breen confronts Glenn, and Sweeney has Glenn beaten, declaring him "off the pad" — no longer trusted or protected.

**Gotti's Offer.** John Gotti has Jimmy and Mickey abducted and taken into Italian territory. He offers a partnership: his cocaine supply, their street-level distribution, 50/50 split. He dismisses Castellano as a "dinosaur" and says Sweeney's Javits play is temporary. Jimmy refuses, saying he won't betray the man who brought him up. Gotti releases them, confident they'll return. They realize Gotti needs them badly or they'd already be dead.

### Important Character Actions

- **Bridget** executes arms dealer Randall to protect the IRA cell, demonstrating cold operational security and willingness to kill outside the immediate mission.
- **Jimmy** crosses a threshold: kills two men (Leon and Charlie Bricks) to protect the coke deal and money, establishing himself as a willing executioner in the drug trade.
- **Sweeney** plays both sides — publicly forbidding narcotics per Gotti's order while privately funding Jimmy's dealing, maintaining plausible deniability with Castellano.
- **Glenn** loses Sweeney's trust permanently by arresting Eddie Breen, leaving him isolated from both the Westies and unsure of his standing with the FBI.
- **Eddie Breen** holds firm under FBI interrogation, refusing to flip on Sweeney, and secures release via expensive counsel.

### Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Colonel Gerald Markham** — British officer responsible for killing Bridget's family (Tadhg and Maureen McKenna and their two sons); arrives at British Consulate on the 14th.
- **Randall** — ex-military arms dealer; sold Semtex from the Lexington armory; killed by Bridget.
- **Charlie Bricks** — Leon's associate, secretly Gotti's informant; caused the hitmen to be sent after Jimmy.
- **Leon** — Harlem-based cocaine supplier for Sweeney; killed in the botched deal.
- **Local 780** — union with ghost names on Javits Center payrolls (federal fraud evidence).
- **Pier 76** — site of no-show construction contracts tied to same contractors as Javits.
- **Sean, Connor, Declan** — low-level Westies crew members handling cocaine cutting operations.
- **"Off the pad"** — Sweeney's term for removing someone from protection/payroll; implies they are expendable or suspect.
- **Semtex** — plastic explosive obtained from Lexington armory via Randall.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The Javits Center project remains the primary money engine: ghost payroll names and no-show contracts are now documented federal evidence.
- Glenn is no longer trusted by Sweeney but remains embedded in the FBI investigation — his dual status is precarious.
- Danny (likely Glenn's son) is institutionalized with behavioral problems; Polk uses him as leverage.
- Bridget's cover identity as "Sarah Walsh" is intact with Jimmy, who remains unaware of her IRA mission.
- Charlie Bricks' betrayal explains why Gotti sent hitmen after Jimmy earlier — connecting back to prior episodes' violence.
- Gotti knows Jimmy is dealing but cannot act openly as long as Castellano believes the Westies are clean.

### Jokes and References Useful for Later Episodes

- **"The Irish, the only people who can start a bar fight over poetry"** — toast at an Irish social gathering, establishing the cultural self-awareness running through the series.
- **Pearse/Connolly poem** ("Where can we draw water... nothing but our own red blood to make a right rose tree") — Randall quotes this to sell the Semtex; the poem ties Irish revolutionary rhetoric to the characters' willingness to kill.
- **"Castellano's a dinosaur... peddling olive oil out of a Buick"** — Gotti's line positioning the old guard as outdated, foreshadowing internal Mafia power shifts.
- **"Breakfast of champions"** — Mickey's line about cocaine, used darkly when crew members are tempted to sample the product.
- **"Big money, low exposure"** — Jimmy's repeated mantra about the drug trade, already proven wrong by the bodies piling up.
- **"Slàinte"** — Irish toast used during the cocaine deal, contrasting the characters' heritage with their criminal enterprise.
- Leon's lesson on cutting cocaine with baby laxative becomes a recurring operational detail for the crew's drug business.

### Unresolved Threads

- **Semtex acquisition**: Bridget killed Randall before completing the full transaction; the Markham assassination plot still needs a delivery mechanism for the car bomb, and Bridget has no clean future contact for weapons.
- **Supplier problem**: Leon is dead and the Colombians are burned — Jimmy has no reliable cocaine re-up source despite Gotti's offer.
- **Gotti's patience**: He let Jimmy and Mickey walk away but expects them to return; his next move if they refuse is unclear.
- **Danny's fate**: Glenn's son remains in a troubled institution; Polk's leverage over Glenn still exists but Glenn's utility to the FBI is diminished.
- **Eddie Breen's knowledge**: He knows everything about Sweeney's operations (Javits skimming, Castellano deal); if he ever flips, the entire organization is exposed.
- **Sean's discipline**: The crew member is already snorting the product during cutting operations — a liability for the business and a potential source of internal conflict.
- **Glenn's isolation**: Off the pad, distrusted by Sweeney, not fully trusted by the FBI — his next move is unpredictable.
- **Civilian casualties**: The Markham bombing plan explicitly includes expected civilian deaths; when it happens, the political fallout could draw unwanted attention to Hell's Kitchen.

## S01E06

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with a tense confrontation: Jimmy is warned that Eamon Sweeney is not to be touched, nor anyone in the warning party's crew. Sweeney refuses Jimmy's suggestion to hit Gotti directly, instead choosing a better revenge — selling drugs, since Gotti is forbidden from dealing. Jimmy also reveals that their pal Leon is dead and that Charlie Bricks was in Gotti's pocket the whole time.

Meanwhile, Brendan's IRA cell prepares to assassinate British operative Colonel Gerald Markham with a car bomb (two kilos of Semtex, Czech blasting cap, garage-door-remote trigger on a VHF receiver) as he leaves the British Consulate. Brendan reveals that Bridget is Tadhg McKenna's daughter, wanted by Interpol for the London Tube bombings, and that Markham killed her parents — making the hit personal. Bridget is granted the trigger role but hesitates; at the climax, with "Mothers of Irish Freedom" protesters (including children) in the street, she cannot push the button.

Jimmy's drug business runs short of cash: he and Mickey can't afford the key for Tad Guggenheim ("Rad Tad"), a rich Yale dropout offering $100,000. Sweeney refuses to front money and sends them to loan shark Ruby Stein, who grudgingly lends $50,000 at double vig after Jimmy drops Sweeney's name. They buy one key from Dominican supplier Nelson, but the Tad deal is an ambush: Gotti steps out of the trunk, seizes the coke, and reveals he is now their only supplier — he told Nelson that selling to them means death. Gotti's deal: he supplies, they sell, 75/25, kept secret from both Sweeney and "Big Paul" Castellano, on condition Jimmy kills Tad.

In parallel, Keenan, recovering from three broken ribs, convinces Polk that Eddie Breen was the wrong mark and that Dukey Sullivan is the one who will flip. Keenan gets prostitute Alice Hamilton — raped and cut by Dukey — to sign an affidavit, wins a one-on-one beating of Dukey, and arrests him on the warrant.

## Important Character Actions

- Sweeney: forbids touching Gotti, launches the drug play as retaliation; refuses to front Jimmy cash, teaching him to run the business himself; confronts Keenan in the bar, threatens him, but lets him walk — "For now."
- Jimmy: takes the Gotti deal (75/25) after losing the coke and the borrowed money; resists killing Tad ("There's no need to kill him") but accepts the deal anyway; now owes Ruby Stein and works for Gotti behind Sweeney's back.
- Gotti: reveals Tad owes his bookie $250,000 and was his pawn; seizes the coke; forces the partnership; demands Tad's murder; notes Jimmy has "a good friend" (likely Mickey) who steered him right.
- Bridget: insists she's there for one reason only (avenging her parents); argues against civilian casualties and American press fallout; fails to detonate the bomb at the last second.
- Brendan: runs the op, grants Bridget the trigger, wants Markham dead regardless of civilian costs ("Then fuck America. I only care what the Irish think.").
- Keenan: confronts Sweeney about threatening his son, declares he'll put him away; coerces a junkie witness into signing an affidavit; arrests Dukey after a brutal fight.
- Ruby Stein: lends half the money at double vig, lectures the boys about the melting pot, advises them to take Gotti's offer, threatens to have them killed if unpaid.
- Polk: wary of Keenan's motives ("Not someone hell-bent on revenge"), skeptical that Dukey's arrest makes Sweeney flip.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- Tad "Rad Tad" Guggenheim: rich Yale dropout, stepfather is a Guggenheim, degenerate gambler, owes Gotti's bookie $250k.
- Ruby Stein: city's biggest loan shark, half Lithuanian/half Russian, "the bank," connected to all the bosses.
- Dukey Sullivan: Sweeney's other lieutenant; rapist who cuts his victims; brags about it at the 596; Keenan's flip target.
- Colonel Gerald Markham: British operative; killed Bridget's parents (Tadhg McKenna and family); interrogated Brendan at Maze Prison; the IRA target.
- Tadhg McKenna: Bridget's father, killed by Markham.
- Alice Hamilton: prostitute and junkie, Dukey's victim, signed the affidavit.
- Nelson: Dominican coke supplier ("I'm not your mami, I'm not your papi").
- Charlie Bricks: revealed as Gotti's man; Leon: dead pal.
- IRA cell members: Donal (bomb tech), Logan (scout), Mary (backup).
- Crew names: Connor, Sean, Declan (pool player), Ray (bartender).
- "Big Paul" Castellano: forbids Gotti from drug dealing; Gotti uses the Irish to avoid being whacked.
- The 596: bar where Dukey brags; Maze Prison; Mothers of Irish Freedom; Interpol warrant; London Tube bombings; Semtex; VHF garage-door trigger.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Sweeney and Breen no longer trust Keenan: he failed to tip them off that Eddie Breen would be grabbed. Breen knows something Keenan did ("You can ask him yourself").
- Keenan's backstory (not in his file): second child stillborn, wife relapsed and OD'd, he left, then stopped caring; he used to take Sweeney's bribes — "Those days are over." Sweeney earlier threatened Keenan's son.
- Gotti can't sell drugs himself (Castellano would whack him), so the Irish are his distributors; the deal must stay secret from both Sweeney and Big Paul. Ruby confirms everyone takes kick-ups (Castellano from Gotti, Sweeney from Jimmy).
- Gotti already offered "My supply. Your streets. Fifty-fifty" earlier; the crew swore they'd never sell for "that dago bastard" — now they're in at 75/25.
- Sweeney's decision to sell drugs is his retaliation against Gotti, and he must never learn Jimmy is now Gotti's partner.
- Bridget previously hid a weapon for Brendan — his pretext to bring her back into the cell; she's only there for revenge, and he grants her the trigger.
- Keenan's warrant is for rape/mutilation, but its real purpose is flipping Dukey onto Sweeney ("he'll sing in a minute"; "knows where the bodies are buried").

## Jokes / References

- Mickey suggests Tad escaped from Bellevue; Jimmy's crew calls him a retard/douchebag; Tad arrives with prostitutes as "witnesses" in case he's robbed, so he "could fuck 'em. Win-win."
- A crew member (likely Mickey) pops antacids "so I can talk to Italians without gettin' sick" — Gotti calls him a racist.
- Ruby: "I'm half Lithuanian and half Russian. Which half should I hate?" — his melting-pot speech against Irish/Italian tribalism.
- Ruby: "Dukey has a rap sheet as long as the Holland Tunnel."
- Keenan's piss motif: "they'll line up to piss on your grave, and I'll be first"; "You wanna piss on me?" during the Dukey fight.
- Soundtrack: New York Dolls' "Personality Crisis" (labeled); closing song "Keep Your Back Up Against the Wall" (unlabeled).

## Unresolved Threads

- Bridget fails to detonate: Markham survives; the IRA cell's fallout (Brendan, Mary) and whether the op is blown are unknown.
- Will Jimmy actually kill Tad, as Gotti demands? Gotti claims Tad "dies either way."
- Jimmy's debts: Ruby Stein's $50k plus double vig; the Gotti arrangement is a secret that could detonate with Sweeney and Castellano.
- Keenan's arrest of Dukey: will Dukey flip on Sweeney, and can Sweeney's lawyers make the case "not stick"?
- Gotti's pressure on Nelson (dead man if he sells to the Irish) — a latent conflict.
- Whether Sweeney discovers Jimmy's deal with Gotti, and whether the "Sweeney is not to be touched" protection holds.

## S01E02

# The Westies S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode opens with the fallout from Mickey Flanagan's kidnapping of Vincent Zaccaro, a made man in the Gambino family. Sweeney is furious — killing a made man brings catastrophic heat. Jimmy Roarke defends Mickey (his friend and Vietnam vet) and convinces Sweeney to "Houdini" the body. In a graphic sequence, the crew dismembers Zaccaro at the 596: grinding bone, melting the bullet, burning organs, and dumping long bones in the Hudson via the Circle Line. They sever Zaccaro's hand to plant fingerprints at Maiden Lane Jewelers (a Westies-protected store), staging a robbery to frame Zaccaro. The crew then drives toward Boston to dump Zaccaro's car at Liam O'Shaughnessy's Corktown Pub in Southie, making it look like he fled to Canada. En route, a state trooper pulls them over for a loose plate; Pat panics, sees the hand in the cooler, and shoots the trooper dead. They frame Zaccaro further — putting his prints on Pat's gun, leaving his wallet/license at the scene, torching the car (Kelly Malloy's, Jimmy's girlfriend). The FBI (Agent Polk) and NYPD (Glenn Keenan) investigate; Keenan's tip about Zaccaro's prints pays off, earning Polk's respect but deepening Keenan's compromise. Meanwhile, Bridget (living as "Sarah") is visited by IRA operative Brendan Cahill, who asks her to hold a weapons crate at the docks. She reveals this to Jimmy; he's livid she's re-engaging with the Cause. Brendan later tells her the weapons are for use in New York, not Ireland. The episode ends with police arresting Flanagan at a bar.

## Important Character Actions
- **Mickey Flanagan**: Kidnapped Zaccaro; off his meds (mental health/ECT history); vomits during body disposal; gives Erin a gun from a Sweeney job; arrested at episode's end.
- **Jimmy Roarke**: Shields Mickey from Sweeney; orchestrates cover-up; drives crew to Boston; frames Zaccaro; confronts Bridget about IRA crate.
- **Eamon Sweeney**: Orders body disposal; threatens to hand Jimmy/Mickey to Gambinos if plan fails; demands hand burned to dust, not dumped.
- **Pat**: Shoots state trooper after he sees severed hand; creates massive new problem.
- **Eddie Breen**: Coaches body disposal ("grind it, melt it"); assesses their chances as "a fart in a windstorm."
- **Glenn Keenan**: Tips Polk to Zaccaro's prints at jewelry store (heard from "drunks at Molly Maguire's"); on Westies' payroll; Polk suspects he's dirty.
- **Agent Birdie Polk**: Runs Gambino Task Force; secures warrants for Zaccaro's home and Bergin Hunt & Fish Club.
- **Bridget Walsh**: Reveals true name to Brendan; holding IRA weapons crate at docks; tells Jimmy "only I can do this."
- **Brendan Cahill**: IRA; asks Bridget to hold crate; admits weapons are for use in New York, not Ireland.
- **John Gotti**: Pressures Sweeney — "hell to pay" if Zaccaro harmed; suspicious of Westies.
- **Erin Malone**: Questioned by FBI about Zaccaro; has bracelet from "rich customer" received yesterday (likely Zaccaro).

# 剧情概述

Hell’s Kitchen内部帮派战争全面爆发。年轻一代吉米·罗克、米奇、康纳、瑞恩、凯文以及戴维的弟弟丹尼集体反叛老一代首领埃蒙·斯威尼。本集以多线叙事推进：吉米阵营主动出击，斯威尼阵营试图反制，Bridget完成对IRA目标的刺杀，FBI专案组Polk与Keenan试图利用内乱撬开局面，Gotti的甘比诺家族则暗中寻求新的合作对象。

**吉米·罗克**：发现西恩已被斯威尼下令处决（肖恩"消失很久"，吉米断言"Sean's dead"）。与米奇、康纳、瑞恩、凯文在酒吧密谋复仇，米奇当场要血洗斯威尼，吉米劝阻并提出"必须打倒Castellano"的长期策略。设下陷阱——主动打电话给斯威尼示弱，邀请其到修车行"男人对男人谈判"，实则伏击。结局用霰弹枪指斯威尼，但未杀死，而是安排Keenan用三公斤可卡因把斯威尼栽赃入狱。
