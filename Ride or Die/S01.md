## S01E02

# S01E02 — "The Yellow Wood"

**Episode plot**

The episode opens with Debbie processing the revelation that her best friend Judith is an international assassin — confirmed with a blunt "Yep." We learn Judith's codename is **Whiptail** and that her latest assignment was **Billy Donovan**. A title card tells us she was once the Agency's "gold standard," but now everything she touches "turns into chaos."

After the opening credits, Debbie is holed up at Judith's off-the-books safe house — not her cover home, but a secret property the Agency doesn't know about. She has a breakdown: fake house, fake life, fake friends. Her husband David is dead, and she wants to call her children. Judith says her real house is too dangerous and leaves Debbie there to shower while she goes to work.

Debbie escapes by guessing the door code (it's the same as her phone passcode) and calls 999 from her own home at **17 Rosemoor Lane**, reporting a break-in. On the call she tells the operator her husband was murdered the previous night at the **Water Relief gala** — then catches herself and claims a "mold company" took him instead.

Meanwhile, **The Director** (via handler Amanda) confronts Whiptail about her string of failures — Austria, Brussels, Beirut — and informs her that someone has placed a **Black Mark** on her. He delivers an ultimatum: "You're no agent of mine until you complete the job you were hired to do."

Judith calls the **Hotel Mont Claire** looking for David Claybourne's body. The hotel staff point her to the cover story — a "mold infestation" in Billy Donovan's room. She reaches her handler **Sam** and asks him to retrieve the body. Sam is aghast that an MP is involved; Judith insists David was already dead when she arrived.

A news bulletin reports the murder of **Martin Shaw**, a groundskeeper at **Milgate Cemetery**, from a single gunshot. Debbie accuses Judith; Judith denies killing him. Debbie notices guns hidden in the cooker and tells Judith she never wants to see her again.

Debbie then does something reckless: she walks into a meeting with **Billy Donovan** and the **Albanian mob**. She introduces herself as Debbie Claybourne, David's widow. **Ergon Kreshnik** — whose father he claims "is the Albanian mob" — reveals that David was running an operation for them and then **stole £24 million**. They need the money back.

Judith arrives just as the situation turns violent, puts a gun to Debbie's head as a ruse, and extracts her. In the car, Sam shows up with a plan to take Debbie to a safe house, but Debbie refuses — she needs to find Billy Donovan and learn how her husband stole £24 million. Sam protests, but Judith tells him: "She's the most important thing in the world to me. She's my best friend." Sam reluctantly agrees.

They regroup at **Queenie** and **Mrs. Williams' shop**, where Debbie gets new clothes and a hastily forged British passport under the name **Veronica Gillingsby**. Queenie explains she can't do American passports that fast. They coach Debbie on a British accent ("less is more"), and Sam confirms Billy Donovan's passport was flagged heading to **northern Spain**, where he has a safe house. He gives them a few hours to locate it.

The episode closes with Debbie and Judith sharing a drink. They set ground rules: "No more lying." "And no more killing." Final line: **"Eliminate the problem."**

---

**Important character actions**

- **Judith** leaves Debbie at her secret safe house, confronts The Director, retrieves David's body through Sam, rescues Debbie from the Albanian mob, and agrees to take Debbie on the mission — risking her entire career.
- **Debbie** escapes the safe house, calls the police on herself, confronts the Albanians face-to-face to learn the truth about her husband, and forces Judith to let her come along.
- **Sam** agrees to break Agency protocol, retrieves the MP's body, and brings Debbie to the shop despite knowing he could lose his job — ending with a veiled threat ("if you tell anyone about her, I will kill you").
- **The Director** officially puts Judith on notice: finish the Billy Donovan job or she's done.
- **Ergon Kreshnik** establishes himself as the Albanian side of the conspiracy — he wanted David alive.

---

**Newly established names and terms**

- **Whiptail** — Judith's assassin codename; she uses it with The Director and Sam
- **The Black Mark** — pirate code meaning "someone wants you dead"; left for Judith, signaling her system was compromised
- **Ergon Kreshnik** — son of the Albanian mob boss ("He is the Albanian mob")
- **Veronica Gillingsby** — Debbie's fake passport identity
- **Water Relief gala** — the charity event where David Claybourne died
- **Martin Shaw** — murdered Milgate Cemetery groundskeeper (apparent loose end)
- **Mold company** — recurring euphemism for the Agency's cleanup/cover-up crews
- **£24 million** — the sum David allegedly stole from the Albanians
- **17 Rosemoor Lane** — Debbie and David's home address
- **Northern Spain safe house** — Billy Donovan's apparent destination

---

**Continuity-relevant facts**

- Judith has a cover identity as a **forensic accountant**; her safe house is entirely off the Agency grid
- David Claybourne was not just an MP — he was **running an operation** for the Albanian mob and **stole £24 million** before he died
- The Agency uses a **"mold company"** as a front to remove bodies and sanitize crime scenes
- **Queenie and Mrs. Williams** run the document/forgery "shop"; can produce British passports in under an hour but not American ones on short notice
- Sam has significant latitude as a handler and is willing to bend rules for Judith — but only so far
- The Director's authority is absolute: Judith is suspended until she completes the Donovan hit
- The Black Mark suggests a **security breach** — someone knew Judith would be at the gala

---

**Jokes and references useful for later episodes**

- **"What's 200 years of Moroccan craftsmanship when you're in a hurry?"** — Judith casually destroying property while thinking
- **"A mold company took him" / "A mold company murdered your husband?"** — the cover story becomes a running gag that confuses everyone outside the Agency
- **"'Ello, guvnor... would you like a spot of tea?"** — Debbie's terrible British accent rehearsal
- **"Don't say it. Don't say it. I haven't said a word."** — Queenie and Debbie's exchange about clothing size
- **"Everything belonging to Debbie Claybourne stays here."** — handing over her watch (a wedding anniversary gift)
- **"Eliminate the problem."** — the episode's closing line undercuts the "no more killing" rule
- The title alludes to Robert Frost's **"The Road Not Taken"** — two roads diverging, a choice with no going back

---

**Unresolved threads**

- Who **killed Martin Shaw** at Milgate Cemetery? Judith denies it — is someone framing her or cleaning up loose ends?
- Who **placed the Black Mark** and compromised Judith's safe-house system?
- Who **hired the hit** on Billy Donovan, and why did they want David Claybourne dead?
- Will The Director **reinstate Judith** if she completes the job, or is there a deeper agenda?
- Can Debbie actually **pass as Veronica Gillingsby** without blowing her cover?
- Where exactly is Billy Donovan's **safe house in northern Spain**, and how long until Sam gets the location?
- Will the **Albanians** continue hunting Debbie for the £24 million?
- What exactly was David's **operation**, and where is the money now?

## S01E07

# Ride or Die — S01E07 "The Geneva Convention"

## Episode Plot

In Geneva, Debbie Claybourne is being interrogated at Interpol headquarters (Lyon). She negotiates a deal: she'll help Interpol catch the Albanian mob by withdrawing £24 million from the Private Bank of Geneva and handing it over to their boss, Ergon Kreshnik, at a plaza outside the bank. In exchange, Debbie wants immunity — for herself and for Judith.

Meanwhile, Judith, Sam, and Queenie are holed up in a Geneva safe house with their hostage, David (Debbie's husband). They plan to steal the money before Ana (Red Sparrow) can get it, using David as bait. Ana, however, gets the jump on everyone: she abducts Debbie in an ambulance during the smoke-bomb chaos at the bank.

In the ambulance, Ana reveals the truth to Debbie: 25 years ago in Turkey, a pregnant Debbie was picked up as a cover by Judith and Ana while they assassinated three men. Judith later shot Ana point-blank and left her for dead. Ana also reveals that Judith deleted Debbie's New York job acceptance email — keeping Debbie close as a useful "civilian asset" and cover identity.

Judith and the team pursue the ambulance, but Ana escapes with Debbie. Judith eventually finds Debbie, confirms Ana is an assassin, and says they should go home.

## Important Character Actions

- **Debbie** negotiates with Interpol, sets up a sting operation to catch Ergon Kreshnik, then is kidnapped by Ana
- **Judith** plans to kill Ana, protects Debbie, and ultimately finds her after the abduction
- **Sam** runs point on the bank operation, monitors cameras, loses visual on Debbie during the smoke bomb
- **Queenie** assists with tech/surveillance; mentions her mother's shop
- **David** is forced into the bank to withdraw the money as part of Judith's plan, then becomes collateral
- **Ana** (Red Sparrow) kidnaps Debbie, reveals Judith's betrayal, and escapes
- **The Director (Jack)** warns Interpol that Redback is a "time bomb" and demands cleanup

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Ergon Kreshnik** — head of the Albanian mob
- **Elijah** — set up the original bank account (mentioned in context)
- **Sanloem** — a place in Cambodia ("far away and hard to see"), where Judith dreams of retiring
- **Emilia** — a cleaner who works for Judith's organization across safe houses
- **Call signs confirmed**: Redback = Judith, Whiptail = Queenie, Red Sparrow = Ana

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Judith and Ana were partners in an assassination in Turkey 25 years ago
- Judith shot Ana after the Turkey job; Ana survived
- Judith has been using Debbie as a cover since they met (pregnant woman on the road)
- Judith deleted Debbie's New York job email to maintain Debbie as her "invaluable civilian asset"
- The Director acknowledges the organization exists because it stays hidden; now exposed by Redback
- Debbie finally learns Judith is an assassin and has been manipulating her for decades

## Jokes & References

- Debbie's "Australian" shower story (made up to hurt David)
- David's financial scheme: keep the interest either way ("failure is only a success that's run out of time")
- Queenie describes her role as "the agent behind the agent" and "multi-hyphenate"
- Debbie's terrible fake passport origin story: bought outside M&M World in Leicester Square
- "Kill of the song" — Debbie's absurd musical interlude

## Unresolved Threads

- Debbie now knows Judith used her and deleted her job — the friendship is shattered
- Ana is still alive and has escaped; she remains a threat
- The £24 million was not recovered; its location after the smoke bomb is unclear
- David is still with Judith and crew; his fate uncertain
- Interpol's investigation continues; the Director's organization is exposed
- Debbie and Judith's relationship is at a breaking point heading into the finale

## S01E03

# S01E03 — "Strangers on a Train"

## Episode plot

The episode opens with a flashback to Waterloo International Station in 1997 — a young Debbie, armed with an Interrail pass and a lawyer's bucket list, meets a charming stranger who offers to help her find platform D for her train to Barcelona. They talk for hours; this is how she met David. In the present, Debbie and Judith are driving through France toward Calais, hunting for Billy Donovan. Debbie refuses to call the children and says she wants to talk about David's death "but not with you." Judith tells her the Agency is trying to retire her — they called her a W.O.A.C.A. (Woman Of A Certain Age) and blame her recent failures on turning 50.

A truck has been following them since the tunnel. They take a backroad and blow a tire, only to find the tire jack has been stolen — someone sabotaged them at the petrol station. A large German man in a pickup pulls up to help; Judith interrogates him at gunpoint ("Who sent you?") but he's just a friendly whittler whose ice cream is melting. They steal his car, put him in the back seat with his groceries, and Judith calls Sam to dispose of the "body" (he's still alive — "Alive is worse").

News breaks that MP David Claybourne was absent from a Parliamentary vote on the Transport Bill. At the Hotel Mont Claire, Sam opens the body bags and discovers David is missing — "This is everybody. Oh my God." If The Director finds out they've lost an MP, "we are both fucked — and by fucked, I mean dead."

The Albanian boss confronts his son: a woman took down three of his men and stole £24 million. "No one steals from you." He orders Ergon to find Debbie and Judith.

At Calais station, the only train to Barcelona is the Royal Andalusian Express, leaving in 20 minutes — first-class sleeper only, eye-wateringly expensive. They board anyway. In the carriage, Debbie starts to crack — "Every time I look at you, I forget… you're the insane thing that happened to me" — but shuts down when she realizes she's talking about David's death. They toast to being "Veronica Gillingsby" and "no more lying." Judith explains the Biblical origin of her name (Judith, who beheaded Holofernes) and they discuss the Black Mark.

Sam brings his problem to Queenie and Mrs. Williams at the shop. Queenie agrees to help find the missing body in exchange for a favor — later revealed to be a meeting with The Director. Queenie tracks Debbie's passport to Calais. She and Sam investigate the hotel room; new carpet has been laid, but Queenie finds blood spots outside the door and luggage-cart indentations in the carpet. Someone moved a body before they arrived. She downloads an ATM camera while stress-eating Mini Cheddars, a Cherryade, a Wispa Duo, and Skittles.

On the train, Debbie and Judith drink peach Bellinis and invent extravagant fake travel stories for François, an amorous older gentleman. Debbie admits "I did this for a brief escape… but your whole life is made-up stories."

In the bar car, a stranger spikes Debbie's drink with hydroxybutyric acid (GHB). He confronts Judith: he's an assassin sent by The Director, and his target is Debbie — she called the police and exposed the organization. "You know the rules." A fight erupts. Debbie throws a bottle, breaks a glass door. When the assassin taunts that he'll kill Debbie slowly to hurt Judith, Debbie says "I don't even know who I'm looking at in the mirror anymore" — then kicks him. They realize more killers will be waiting at the station and jump from the moving train together.

After the jump, breathless and alive, they discover their compartment has been robbed — passports, money, everything gone. Walking along the road, they have a blistering fight. Debbie accuses Judith of living a wall of lies; Judith calls Debbie a coward who got off the train because she was too scared to make it on her own. Judith breaks down: "I'm terrified to lose you… I'm terrified I never even had you." Debbie: "You've always had me." They hug.

At the shop, Queenie reviews the ATM footage with Sam. "Can we get any wider?" "No, I can't get any wider, Martin Scorsese." She rewinds — there he is. David Claybourne. Alive. "Oh fuck… He's alive."

## Important character actions

- **Debbie** refuses to call her children, gets drunk on the train to escape grief, is drugged by the rival assassin, fights back, jumps from the train, and finally tells Judith she's always had her.
- **Judith** is paranoid about the German good-Samaritan (who turns out to be harmless), steals his car, calls Sam for cleanup, tells Debbie she's terrified of losing her, and jumps from the train.
- **Sam** discovers David's body is missing, enlists Queenie, traces the blood trail to a service elevator, and reviews the ATM footage revealing David is alive.
- **Queenie** trades her help for a future favor (a meeting with The Director), hacks the ATM download while eating her way through a junk-food picnic, and spots David on the footage.
- **The Director** is revealed to have placed the kill order on Debbie (not Judith) because she talked to police — confirming he'll eliminate anyone who threatens the organization.
- **Whiptail** — the assassin on the train — tells Judith he'd "love a shot at you" but Debbie's the job, establishing that Judith is still respected/feared in the assassin world.

## Newly established names and terms

- **W.O.A.C.A.** (Woman Of A Certain Age) — the Agency's derogatory term for Judith, used to justify forcing her out
- **Royal Andalusian Express** — luxury train to Barcelona, first-class sleeper only, absurdly expensive
- **François** — amorous older gentleman in the bar car who buys Debbie and Judith Bellinis
- **Hydroxybutyric acid** (GHB) — the drug slipped to Debbie in the bar car, "faster than a roofie and stays in your system longer"
- **Waterloo International, 1997** — where Debbie met David (Interrail pass, European bucket list)
- **Veronica Gillingsby** — Debbie's fake identity, now fully embodied ("I am Veronica Gillingsby. I'm a normal British woman with no problems.")
- **"Metaphors and that lovely hat"** — the excuse for why they got robbed on the train

## Continuity-relevant facts

- David Claybourne is alive — captured on ATM footage near the Hotel Mont Claire
- The Director has ordered Debbie's death independently of the Donovan hit; Judith is still technically on the Billy Donovan job
- Judith's codename "Whiptail" is known to other assassins; she has a fearsome reputation ("I'd love a shot at you")
- The Agency replaced the hotel carpet to hide evidence but missed blood outside the door
- Debbie and Judith are now stranded in France with no passports and no money
- Queenie's birth certificate is fake (made by her mother) — she wants The Director to explain why
- The Black Mark postcards were NOT sent by the assassin on the train ("That sounds fucking lame")
- The Albanian mob is actively hunting Debbie for £24 million; they wanted David alive
- Mrs. Williams always packs a tire jack — someone removed it and slashed the tire while they were at the petrol station

## Jokes and references useful for later episodes

- **"Who buys ice cream on their way to kill people?"** — Judith's deadpan observation about the German whittler
- **"He's a whittler! He makes these very poor renditions of wildlife. These weird little animals."** — Debbie trying to convince Judith the man is harmless
- **"Are you insane? Is this euro or yen or—?" / "Each?!"** — the ticket price for first class
- **"I'm an incredibly poorly paid clerk with the most boring job in the world, attempt to dazzle you?"** — David's 1997 chat-up line
- **"You got off the train because you were too scared to make it on your own."** — Judith's grenade in the roadside fight, and a direct echo of the 1997 flashback
- **"I've known you for 25 years and you haven't had a single proper romantic relationship."**
- **"Can we get any wider? No, I can't get any wider, Martin Scorsese."** — Queenie and Sam squinting at CCTV
- **"Veronica doesn't cry."** / "Debbie cries." — the moment Debbie breaks
- **"Everything belonging to Debbie Claybourne stays here."** — the wedding watch, left behind when they jump
- The title alludes to Hitchcock's *Strangers on a Train* — two strangers whose fates intertwine, a murder pact, and a fateful train journey

## Unresolved threads

- **David Claybourne is alive** — who's holding him, and why? Is he a prisoner, a collaborator, or on the run?
- **Who moved the body** from the hotel room before Sam arrived, and where is it now?
- **Who slashed the tire and stole the jack** — Albanians, the Agency, or a third party?
- **Who sent the Black Mark postcards** if not the assassin on the train? Someone else knows Judith's identity.
- **Queenie's real identity** — why did Mrs. Williams forge her birth certificate? What secret is The Director sitting on?
- **Debbie and Judith are stranded** with no documents or money in France — how do they continue the mission?
- **The Director's kill order on Debbie** — does Judith now have to choose between completing the Donovan job and protecting her friend?
- **Whiptail (the assassin)** survived the train confrontation and could return — who else has The Director sent?
- **The £24 million** — the Albanians still believe Debbie has it; will they close in while she's vulnerable?
- **Debbie still hasn't called her children** — how long can she keep them in the dark, and what happens when they learn David is alive?

## S01E01

# S01E01 — The Book of Judith

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with a botched assassination in an Austrian ski resort. Operating under the cover of a ski instructor, Judith (codename Whiptail) kills a wanted criminal with a steak knife in front of roughly a hundred eyewitnesses instead of taking the planned clean sniper shot. Her handler Sam is furious — this is only the latest in a string of chaotic jobs (Beirut, Brussels, Dubai). The Director has been reviewing Judith's file and questions her reliability. Sam gives her a Patek Philippe watch — heavily implied to be a retirement gift — and issues an ultimatum: one last chance. A crime syndicate client wants Billy Donovan poisoned at the Water Relief charity gala, death made to look like a heart attack. Judith resists (she "does war criminals, not crime syndicate jobs") but accepts.

Meanwhile, Debbie Claybourne is introduced at a political book club and later hunting for a Regency apothecary cabinet at an antique warehouse. She is an American Yale Law Review graduate who married British MP David Claybourne and has spent 25 years engineering his rise toward the prime ministership. At home she coaches David through his transport bill speech while he casually announces he wants a divorce, claiming they are "moving in different directions." Debbie is shattered and furious — "I made you, David."

Judith visits Queenie's cobbler-shop front to procure 220mg of aconite poison. Queenie is taking over the family's assassin-support "shop" from her mother. News of the Patek Philippe gift travels fast through the assassin grapevine. At the Water Relief gala, a drunken Debbie confronts a rival woman, exposing David's corruption (undeclared shares in a rival transport company, second-home tax fraud). The hit on Billy Donovan goes sideways: David Claybourne is found dead, three other men are down. Judith executes a "Code Red — full extraction," pulls Debbie into a van, and flees under gunfire.

After a chaotic chase and crash in a graveyard, Judith receives a message — "Miss me? D.C. XX" — which she deduces is a grave number (DC = 620 in Roman numerals, not Debbie's initials). Inside the grave: no body, just a page from the apocryphal Book of Judith. Judith identifies it as the Black Mark — pirate code meaning someone wants her dead. She then reveals to Debbie that she has been an assassin for nearly 29 years, that her real name is not even Judith, and that Debbie cannot go home. Sam reports to the Director that Billy Donovan is missing and Judith has vanished; the Director orders her found.

## Important Character Actions

- **Judith** botches the Austria job, accepts the Billy Donovan assignment as a last chance, procures aconite from Queenie, pulls Debbie out of the gala during a Code Red, and reveals her true profession and identity.
- **Debbie** discovers David's divorce plans, gets publicly drunk and exposes his political corruption at the gala, is extracted by Judith, and learns her best friend of 20 years is a contract killer.
- **Sam** pressures Judith on behalf of the Director, gives her the Patek Philippe, and later reports the gala disaster upward.
- **Queenie** sells Judith poison and is established as taking over her mother's assassin-support network.
- **David** asks for a divorce and is found dead at the gala.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Whiptail** — Judith's codename within the assassination organization.
- **The Black Mark** — a pirate-code signal meaning someone has marked Judith for death; delivered via a Bible/apocryphal page in a grave.
- **WOACA (Woman Of A Certain Age)** — Sam's jokey acronym for Judith, suggesting her age-related unreliability.
- **Water Relief** — the charity through which the Donovan crime syndicate launders money; site of the gala hit.
- **Queenie's shop** — a cobbler-front operation providing passports, weapons, and tech support to assassins; currently in "management transition" from Mrs. Williams to Queenie.
- **Aconite (乌头毒草)** — the poison Judith buys for the Donovan job, 220mg.
- **The Book of Judith** — not biblical canon but apocrypha; Judith was an assassin, establishing the episode's central archetype.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Judith has been an assassin for almost 29 years; she has lied to Debbie every single day of their 20-year friendship.
- Judith's real name is not Judith Burton — that identity is a forensic-accountant cover.
- Debbie has no idea about Judith's double life until the gala extraction.
- David is dead at the gala (Sam: "three men down, and David Claybourne"). This creates the Debbie/Judith rift and removes Debbie's political tether.
- The Patek Philippe watch is a recurring motif — a potential retirement marker that signals the organization is preparing to sever Judith.
- Debbie claims David stole £24 million, setting up a financial throughline.
- Sam is loyal to Judith but reports to the Director; he has put his neck on the line for her and will be under pressure after the gala.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- Debbie's Yale Law Review credential and her "I made you, David" refrain — recurring character beats.
- Sam: "News travels fast." / Queenie's mother: "Honour amongst thieves, gossip amongst assassins."
- Judith's dry distinction: "If I did it for free I'd be a serial killer."
- Debbie quotes *Little Women* to David; he accuses her of not having read it.
- The apothecary cabinet is a running Debbie obsession — she buys one at the warehouse and uses it as leverage to get out of reading the book club book.
- Debbie "stands in her power" post-divorce announcement and declares herself "a new woman."
- The codename "Blades" is floated and rejected; Sam says "Bye, Blades" anyway.
- Debbie on marriage: "That man hasn't known where his house keys are for 20 years."

## Unresolved Threads

- Who killed David Claybourne — and was Judith's poison hit on Billy Donovan interrupted, or was David the real target?
- Who placed the Black Mark on Judith, and why now?
- Where is Billy Donovan?
- Will the Director move to terminate Judith's contract — or eliminate her?
- Can Debbie return home, or is she now complicit/on the run?
- What is the significance of the apocryphal Book of Judith page — a warning, a message from a specific person, or a ritual?
- The £24 million David allegedly stole — where is it, and does Debbie actually want it, or is it a pretext to stay close to Judith?

## S01E06

# S01E06 — Moonlight on the Bosphorus

## Plot Summary

The episode picks up immediately after the Monaco heist. Judith has been shot and is in critical condition. Debbie, Judith, and Billy Donovan are hiding in Monaco, hunted by the Albanian mob. Debbie makes contact with Jacques Boucher, an Interpol agent who has been tracking the Albanian crime syndicate led by Ergon Kreshnik. Jacques offers Debbie protection in exchange for testimony against Kreshnik.

With Judith dying from her wound, the group needs medical supplies but has nowhere to turn. Mrs. Williams — Queenie's mother and their underground medical contact — has been killed. Queenie, consumed by grief and rage, steals antibiotics and surgical supplies from a hospital while Sam (Judith's handler) tries to talk her out of seeking revenge. The group holes up at Mrs. Williams's empty house outside the city, where Brian (Queenie's associate and a former army medic) stitches up Judith.

Debbie secretly arranges a dead-drop with Jacques: she trades the medical supplies box for intelligence on the Albanians, but plants a tracker on it first. Colette, Jacques's Interpol superior, discovers he has gone rogue and officially recalls him from the case, declaring the operation over.

In the episode's final movement, Debbie chooses to surrender to Interpol. She cuts a deal: she will testify against Ergon Kreshnik in exchange for Judith and Billy's safety. She reveals that her husband David stole £24 million from the Albanian mob — the reason they have been chasing her across Europe. Judith and Billy escape to Heathrow for a "girls' trip." The episode ends on a cliffhanger: The Director orders a kill team sent after Redback, who is revealed to be very much alive — and very dangerous.

## Important Character Actions

- **Debbie** takes decisive control: she negotiates with Interpol, trades intelligence for medicine, and ultimately turns herself in to protect Judith and Billy. She reveals David stole £24 million from the Albanians.
- **Judith** is incapacitated for most of the episode — shot, stitched up by Brian, and barely conscious. She whispers that Sam's people are following him.
- **Queenie** steals medical supplies from a hospital (forging the paperwork) and offers her dead mother's house as a safehouse. She confronts Sam and The Director over Mrs. Williams's death, vowing revenge.
- **Sam** tries to manage multiple crises: he warns Judith that Redback is alive, attempts to restrain Queenie from reckless vengeance, and faces The Director's cold pragmatism.
- **Billy Donovan** stays loyal — he returns after running off with the bag, helps get Judith to safety, and escapes with her at the end.
- **Jacques Boucher** goes rogue to pursue Debbie's case, defying Colette's direct orders. He is eventually pulled off the operation.
- **The Director** calls Sam by the name "Victoria" (Sam rejects this). He orders a kill team sent to eliminate Redback.

## S01E05

# S01E05 — "The Count of Monte Carlo"

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with Sam reporting to The Director that the hit on Billy Donovan was a setup by a former operative named Redback. The Director insists Redback is dead, but Sam says he saw her alive — her file claimed she died on a mission with Whiptail five years ago in Turkey, yet she survived. The Director forbids Sam from warning Whiptail, declares her no longer part of the organization, and threatens to kill Sam if he tells anyone.

Debbie, Judith, and Billy Donovan drive toward Monte Carlo, with Billy effectively their prisoner in the back seat. While driving stick shift and arguing over directions, Judith reveals she has quit being an assassin — she could no longer kill people and then look Debbie in the eye. She confesses she had sex with someone named "Bodhi" (Debbie is shocked; Judith refuses to elaborate). Their destination is a secure building where they plan to meet Elijah, a money mule who set up David Claybourne's bank account. To get in, they must pass a Lord of the Rings-themed password gate (the correct password is "Mellon" — "friend" in Elvish — which Billy repeatedly fumbles).

Inside, they meet Brian, Queenie's associate, who is impressed Debbie found him — she is the first wife ever to track him down. Brian confirms David was a "weaselly politician" who got involved with the Albanians; his wife knew nothing. Billy asks to access David's account to return the £24 million to the Albanians. Brian agrees to waive his usual fee, but when he checks, the account is empty — David has cashed out everything. Brian shrugs: David is probably on a beach in Costa Rica.

Meanwhile, The Director tells Sam that Redback is indeed alive. She escaped six months ago from an unsanctioned prison in the mountains above Ankara during a riot, killing half the guards. The Black Mark left for Judith is her work. Sam protests that he has known Ana (Redback) since she was 15 and that Whiptail's lies left Ana to rot. The Director coldly responds: "And Whiptail left. Nobody leaves." Sam replies, "I did." The Director says, "You were always an exception to the rule."

At Queenie's shop, Queenie confronts her mother, Mrs. Williams. She has broken into The Director's office, found her own file, and discovered her birth certificate is fake. Mrs. Williams says she was trying to protect her; they sell illegal passports for a living. Queenie demands to know who her father is — Mrs. Williams says he was nobody and left before she was born. Queenie declares she is going to warn Whiptail about the psycho assassin after her.

While leaving a restaurant, Judith is recognized by Arnand Vicente, an arms dealer, who calls her "Clara" — a former cover identity. He invites her, Debbie, and Billy to a party at his house that evening. Judith makes a hasty excuse and leaves without paying. Billy asks who Arnand was — Judith explains he was a friend of an old target; she killed his friend, who ran a human trafficking ring. Debbie learns Arnand collects stolen art and keeps it as collateral in his house. Judith sees an opportunity: they will rob him to get the £24 million to pay back the Albanians.

Sam is given an ultimatum: he has 24 hours to find Judith and make her talk, or The Director will trigger an Interpol Red Notice. Sam argues he does not think Judith is a killer, but The Director gives him no choice. Separately, Sam contacts the Albanian mob — he meets Besnik and tells them Debbie and Judith are in Monte Carlo. Besnik orders him to bring all the money.

Billy is captured by the Albanians and cuffed in a room. He offers them a deal — he is in cuffs, they are pacing, "that's our thing" — and says he can deliver Debbie and Judith for the money.

Debbie is determined to go through with the heist. Judith tries to talk her out of it, but Debbie insists: "I am doing this plan tonight." She struggles to say the word "fuck" (Judith teases her: "You can't even say 'fucking.'"). They shop for black-tie dresses; Debbie is rude to a sales clerk, then apologizes — she "recently learned to curse." The concierge brings a list of boutiques and writes down "Miss Gillingsby" (Debbie's fake passport name).

Ana (Redback) confronts Judith alone. She is alive and demands to know why Judith never looked for her. Judith says she believed Ana was dead — Whiptail lied. Ana says Judith broke the rules by having an emotional attachment (Debbie) and demands that Judith beg her to spare Whiptail. Judith refuses. Ana says she discovered Debbie's existence the day before she learned Judith was alive, and she plans to kill Debbie first — "your favorite girl."

At the party, Queenie provides a signal blocker — they will have two minutes and thirty seconds from the moment the safe opens. Judith flirts with Arnand, who shows her his private gallery: a Viking ivory chess set, a wooden head of Charles II (with a wig made from his lovers' pubic hair), a Rembrandt, a Vermeer, a Song dynasty bowl worth a million, and a Breguet watch (Number 160, made for Marie-Antoinette) worth £18 million. They crack the safe using the code 130697. Debbie instinctively breaks the signal blocker with her right hand. They must work without it.

Arnand catches them. He reveals he knows who Judith is — she is not "Clara" but an English assassin who poisoned his friend Jean-Paul. He has removed all weapons from the room and refused to eat or drink in her presence. He plans to trade her to the Albanians for weapons and total monopoly on Albanian territory, calling her his "most invaluable treasure." His men grab Judith.

Judith and Debbie fight their way toward the exit. A man intercepts them: Jacques Boucher, Interpol. He makes them keep dancing and reveals he knows everything — fake passport, jumping from a train, 25 years in London with no parking ticket, married but not wearing a ring, scared. He says he can protect her from Ergon Kreshnik and offers to help each other. Billy appears — "I know him. He's with the Albanians." Before Jacques can respond, the Albanian mob arrives and grab Debbie. They demand their money. Someone yells, "Shoot her!" — the episode ends on the standoff.
