## S01E01

## Episode plot
- At the East–West German border, British Press Attaché Mr. Anderson is stopped and searched by East German guards. A captain demonstrates close surveillance of Anderson’s household (daily habits, wife’s medical appointment) before letting him pass.
- Carolyn Martens is smuggled into East Berlin in Anderson’s car boot. Her handler briefs her on a deep-cover identity as Martha Schmitt, stressing that British authorities will deny her if exposed and will not rescue her; she is given hidden tools, including a cyanide capsule disguised in a tube/lipstick-like item.
- Operating as Martha, she uses a honeytrap/kompromat routine on Frank to obtain industrial information, while voicing frustration at doing lure work instead of being taken seriously as an operative.
- Martha lives in East Berlin with flatmate Stefan and has nosy neighbor Ilse Huber (and Ilse’s dog Moppel). Stefan is infatuated with an unnamed, kind-eyed border officer.
- MI6 briefs Martha on Lt. Col. Friedrich Baumann, a newly returned Stasi “spy hunter” from Moscow tasked with eliminating undercover British targets, and warns her to avoid his attention. She is assigned to shadow OG Brunstrom, a businessman who frequently travels West selling prefabricated furniture and may be trading information.
- Martha independently engages Brunstrom at a canteen, later following him and confronting Kurt Fischer at knifepoint over what she believes is a leak. Kurt reveals he is MI6 and that he is feeding Brunstrom fake intelligence to protect real sources while a leak is investigated; Brunstrom is their asset, unknown to the Stasi. Baumann’s presence during a tense moment forces them to improvise an escape.
- Martha’s handler reprimands her for insubordination but keeps her in place to avoid institutional embarrassment. She is paired with Kurt Fischer as his partner under a romantic-cover arrangement.
- Their new target is Hermann Metzl, a 28-year-old former neuroscience exchange student (University of Manchester) who commutes two hours daily to a heavily guarded facility instead of his official workplace; British intelligence does not know what happens there. The planned approach is through Metzl’s upcoming May wedding.
- At a wedding shop, Martha and Kurt observe Metzl and his fiancée. Stasi officers arrive searching for a girl shown in a photo; Martha identifies herself as the target, tells Kurt to stall them, flees, and is captured after a chaotic chase through secured gates.
- Baumann interrogates Martha as “Martha Schmitt,” probing her cover details and pressing her over a six-month affair with Press Attaché Anderson. He coerces her to become an informant reporting on Anderson and demands her underwear for a scent profile. Martha bargains for the release of Ilse Huber and her dog; Ilse is later freed and Moppel is returned.
- Back with Kurt, Martha reflects that she is now fully entangled. In a separate closing scene, Greta prepares jammy eggs for someone off-screen; he chokes, a timer rings, and she screams.

## Important character actions
- **Carolyn Martens / “Martha Schmitt”**: Enters East Berlin in deep cover; runs kompromat work; disobeys orders by engaging Brunstrom/Kurt; accepts a partner cover with Kurt; scouts Hermann Metzl; is detained by Stasi and turned into a pressured informant on Anderson while securing Ilse’s release.
- **Mr. Anderson (British Press Attaché)**: Crosses the border under visible Stasi scrutiny; revealed to be having an affair with Martha under her cover identity.
- **Kurt Fischer**: Appears first as Martha’s irreverent local contact/neighbor figure; revealed as MI6 running fake-intel drops through Brunstrom; becomes Martha’s assigned partner and supposed boyfriend.
- **Lt. Col. Friedrich Baumann (Stasi)**: Identified as a dangerous new counterintelligence hunter; personally interrogates and recruits/coerces Martha as an informant.
- **Martha’s MI6 handler**: Establishes deep-cover rules, briefs Baumann/Brunstrom context, reprimands Martha, then binds her to the Kurt partnership and Metzl mission.
- **OG Brunstrom**: Serves as the conduit in a controlled fake-intelligence operation (MI6 asset, not known as such to Stasi).
- **Hermann Metzl**: Observed wedding-shopping with his fiancée; established as a surveillance target with a suspicious daily routine.
- **Ilse Huber**: Taken by authorities after Martha’s defiance/reporting pressure context; later released following Martha’s deal.
- **Stefan**: Provides domestic cover/normalcy and reveals romantic fixation on a border officer.
- **Frank**: Target of Martha’s kompromat operation, pressured for dull industrial information.

## Newly established names/terms
- **People**: Carolyn Martens, Martha Schmitt (alias), Mr. Anderson, Mrs. Anderson, Winston (Anderson-associated dog, mentioned at border), Kurt Fischer, Stefan, Ilse Huber, Moppel (Ilse’s dog), Frank, Lt. Col. Friedrich Baumann, OG Brunstrom, Bill (mentioned as alternative handler for Brunstrom), Anne (handler’s wife), Hermann Metzl, Greta.
- **Organizations/roles**: British Embassy Press Attaché, MI6, CIA, Stasi, Security Forces, Council Member for Municipal Supply for the Neubrandenburg-Bezirk.
- **Places**: East Berlin, German Democratic Republic (GDR), Neubrandenburg-Bezirk, Ledenstadt / Ledenwalde (cover-history discrepancy), University of Manchester, Ackerman’s (shop mentioned).
- **Operations/terms**: Deep cover, alias, kompromat, honey trap, scent profile (for dogs), informant reporting.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Martha’s cover identity is fragile but operational: Baumann has formally recorded her as Martha Schmitt and leveraged her affair with Anderson to compel cooperation.
- British support is explicitly limited in deep cover (“you do not exist,” no rescue if compromised), yet MI6 keeps Martha in play after misconduct to avoid exposure/embarrassment.
- Baumann is positioned as an active, high-risk threat to undercover British operatives.
- There is an ongoing leak concern around nuclear documents involving CIA anxiety; Kurt claims his Brunstrom drops are disinformation to muddy what is real.
- Hermann Metzl’s unexplained work at a guarded remote building is the central forward intelligence mystery.
- Ilse’s detention and release show Martha can extract concessions from Baumann, but also that the Stasi can quickly retaliate against people around her.

## Jokes or references useful for later episodes
- Border/captain banter about Anderson’s breakfast, margarine/butter choice, and Mrs. Anderson’s smear test underscores invasive Stasi surveillance as dark humor.
- Martha’s cyanide “lipstick” moment and the “kiss of death” guess play as spy-gadget gallows humor.
- “Sexy little maggot at the end of the string… I want to be the fisherman” captures Martha’s frustration and ambition in a memorable image.
- Repeated competence/ego clashes: Martha mocking Kurt’s performance and tradecraft (“practically waddling,” “worse one-night stand”), and the handler refusing her a gun because of her driving.
- The awkward fake-couple backstory Kurt invents at the wedding shop (Martha rescuing him from teenage robbers, trousers falling down) is a running-tone example of their cover banter.
- “What’s three words no intelligence agency wants to hear?” / “We don’t know.” frames the Metzl mystery with dry espionage humor.

## Unresolved threads
- What is being done at the heavily guarded facility Hermann Metzl visits daily, and who he may be connected to from Manchester?
- How far will Martha’s forced informing on Anderson go, and can she manage/double-cross Baumann without exposing herself?
- Will Baumann’s scrutiny tighten around Martha, Kurt, or their Metzl operation?
- What is the source of the nuclear-document leak referenced around the CIA/Brunstrom disinformation effort?
- Who was the girl the Stasi were seeking at the wedding shop, and what was her significance?
- Why did Martha invite that wedding-shop girl over for cake, and what consequences will follow?
- What is happening in the final Greta/egg scene (who is choking, and is it accidental, medical, or foul play)?

## S01E02

# Honey S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Carolyn Martens (now operating as **Martha Schmitt**) is pulled from the fallout of the Brunstrom operation. After the Stasi inexplicably release her, she's partnered with **Kurt Fischer** and assigned by **Graham** to investigate the murder of **Hermann Metzl**, a state scientist working on classified neurological manipulation research. Metzl was killed in his home, allegedly by his fiancée **Greta**, but the circumstances suggest a cover-up. Martha and Kurt infiltrate the sausage factory where Metzl worked (a front for advanced brain experiments), discover Metzl's hidden notebook is now in Stasi custody, and barely escape after Martha's cover is blown. Meanwhile, Martha begins weekly Stasi debriefings as a double agent under **Friedrich Baumann** (head of counterespionage against British targets), feeding him false intel on her handler **OG Brunstrom** while cultivating a relationship with senior Stasi officers.

## Important Character Actions
- **Martha/Carolyn**: Assumes Martha Schmitt identity; punches a scientist in the throat when her cover fails; fakes Stasi cooperation to survive; leverages her "affair" with Brunstrom as cover.
- **Kurt Fischer**: New partner; provides CIA-grade disguises; clashes with Martha over methodology; helps extract her from the factory.
- **Graham**: Assigns the Metzl investigation; suspects Martha's Stasi release is suspicious; orders surveillance on her.
- **Friedrich Baumann**: Recruits Martha as Stasi informant; tasks her with weekly meetings and cultivating a senior officer; tests her with orgasm-simulation training tapes.
- **Greta (Metzl's fiancée)**: Arrested for murder; described as "desperately in love" but had a rough patch with Metzl (he was a slob, bad in bed, never took her out); suddenly "sunshine and rainbows" before the murder.
- **Achmedova**: Metzl's office neighbor; reveals Metzl worked on "manipulating neural pathways"; hints at a notebook with critical data.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Carolyn → Martha transition confirmed**: She is no longer Carolyn Martens; the identity switch is operational.
- **Brunstrom is dead**: Shot (not by Martha); his handler description was uselessly generic.
- **Stasi has Metzl's notebook**: Hidden behind his fridge; now in Stasi building — primary MacGuffin.
- **Metzl's work**: Neurological manipulation / neural pathways — advanced, classified, likely the reason for his murder.
- **Martha's Stasi arrangement**: Weekly visits; posing as German national having affair with Brunstrom; reporting to Baumann directly.
- **Graham suspects Martha**: "She's bullshitting" about Stasi release; orders investigation; "if she turns, you know what to do."
- **English vs. American tension**: Graham warns "don't let the English take over" — they're "dinosaurs," "stuffy," think "guns and money" solve everything.
- **Kurt's disguise tech**: CIA/Langley-supplied; "cutting-edge," lets them breeze past security.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **"Punching"**: Slang for dating out of one's league — used re: Metzl and Greta, and later Kurt's disguise makes him look like "Robert fucking Redford."
- **Pickles vs. snacks debate**: Kurt insists pickles are meal components; Martha eats them like apples.
- **Kamilla method**: Coffee agitation technique — becomes a running bit about scientific rigor applied to mundane tasks.
- **Zero K / Absolute zero**: Nickname for Achmedova (frigid/celibate before marriage) — "coldest thing in the universe."
- **Orgasm-simulation training**: Baumann makes Martha practice with a focus-group-optimized tape — "men are 14% more likely to divulge... they believe they've satisfied sexually."
- **Sofa intel**: Frau Anderson getting hair done + husband buying sofa = unhappy marriage + affair imminent — Martha's "I understand women" beat.
- **Red... Redhead!**: Martha's allergic reaction clue ("Reh" → Red → Redhead) during nut allergy crisis.
- **Munich beer keg**: Stolen as "evidence" during factory search — Kurt: "Well done."

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Metzl's notebook**: In Stasi hands; contains key to neural manipulation research; Martha must retrieve it during weekly visits.
2. **Greta's guilt**: Did she kill Metzl? Was she manipulated? The "rough patch → sunshine and rainbows" shift suggests external influence.
3. **Martha's loyalty**: Baumann tests her; Graham suspects she's turned; she's playing both sides — how long can she sustain it?
4. **Brunstrom's network**: He was "our guy" inside Stasi; his death leaves a gap; who replaces him?
5. **Neural manipulation program**: What exactly was Metzl building? Who benefits? Factory security suggests state-level investment.
6. **Frau Anderson**: Potential recruitment target — unhappy marriage, grooming for affair.
7. **Kurt/Martha dynamic**: Forced partnership; mutual distrust; Graham's "take it up with top brass" implies higher-level mandate.
8. **The "English"**: Graham's warning suggests MI6/UK involvement coming — jurisdictional conflict looming.

## S01E03

# Honey S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
Greta Abstreiter, fiancée of molecular biologist **Hermann Metzl**, escapes Stasi custody after killing two *Volkspolizei* officers with her bare hands. Metzl—ostensibly a sausage-factory worker with a molecular-biology diploma—was secretly researching **neurological manipulation** in an obscure state-funded lab. His notebook containing the research is now the prime target. Martha (Stasi) and Kurt (CIA, cover: garbage man) must find Greta before the Stasi does, using Metzl's colleague **Kamilla Akhmedova**—a neuroscientist who runs "Kamilla's Book Club"—as their lead. The episode intercuts their book-club infiltration, a disastrous honey-pot attempt on Stasi officer **Baumann** (who holds Metzl's notebook), and Greta's confession to Kamilla that she suffers blackouts and believes Metzl did something to her brain.

## Important Character Actions
- **Martha**: Leverages Stasi access to interrogate Greta's mother; blackmails a regional agricultural ombudsman (her asset) for grain data; clashes with Kurt over credit for the Akhmedova lead; goes to book club undercover.
- **Kurt**: Posing as Martha's boyfriend at book club; attempts to seduce Kamilla; reveals his career stalled because his partner (Deputy Director's son) took credit for Kurt's work; helps steal Baumann's dog *Moppel*.
- **Greta**: Tells Kamilla she loses time, never saw blood before the killings, and Metzl was always there when she "came to"—implying neurological conditioning.
- **Baumann**: Sees through Martha/Kurt's ruse at his apartment, dismisses them as out of their depth, retains Metzl's notebook.
- **Moppel**: Fails attack training repeatedly; stolen by Martha/Kurt as leverage/distraction.
