## Premise
Sixteen-year-old star rower **Teagan Tao** is uprooted to the US East Coast after her father’s misdeeds surface. Her new elite prep school has no girls’ rowing team, so she becomes **coxswain** of the dysfunctional boys’ varsity crew. The series follows her navigation of warring rivals, messy romantic entanglements, betrayed friendships, and a complicated mother-daughter relationship — all while trying to whip the team into championship shape.

## Speaking Styles
- **Teagan**: Direct, competitive, technically precise on water; vulnerable, guarded off water. Uses rowing jargon naturally. Teen vernacular with emotional restraint.
- **Josh**: Charismatic, slightly entitled prep-school ease; masks insecurity with charm. Banter-heavy.
- **Cam**: Intense, driven, sarcastic. Challenges authority (including Teagan’s). Short, clipped sentences.
- **Coach Hayden**: Authoritative, economy of language. Coaching cadence: imperative verbs, technical corrections, motivational bursts.
- **Ella**: Protective, weary, pragmatic. Maternal warmth laced with stress. Avoids confrontation; deflects with humor.
- **Team banter**: Fast, overlapping, slang-heavy (bro-culture, locker-room humor). Inside jokes, call-backs.

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **Canadian production, US setting**: Spelling/vocabulary follows Canadian English (our/our, -re/-er) but characters speak US prep-school idiom. Subtitles should target Chinese audience familiarity with US teen-drama conventions.
- **Rowing authenticity**: Cast trained with Olympic coxswain Terry Paul; terminology is accurate. Preserve technical terms with standard Chinese rowing equivalents (see table).
- **YA/Netflix tone**: “Frothy” — balances soapy romance, rivalry, friendship betrayal with sports underdog arc. Comparable to *Ginny & Georgia*, *XO Kitty*, *Friday Night Lights* on water.
- **Class/privilege**: Prep-school wealth is backdrop, not satire. Characters’ access to elite coaching, equipment, travel is normalized.
- **Mother-daughter core**: Emotional anchor; translation should preserve nuance of love/resentment/protection.
