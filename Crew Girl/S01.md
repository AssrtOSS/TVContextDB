## S01E07

# Crew Girl S01E07 — Summary

## Plot

The episode (titled via title card "Whip, Whiplash") opens with Teagan's catastrophic first practice: she confuses port and starboard, the boat runs lopsided, and she loses Baloo's rhythm entirely. The team jokes about returning her like a defective product.

At the bursar's office, Ella (now insisting on "Emerson") negotiates tuition after the family education trust was seized because of her husband's crimes. The school offers a $7,000/month installment plan plus the door opens to the "Chou Scholarship" for families representing Easton values. A flashback at Ella's diner job reveals she asked boss Jim for a paycheck advance.

Back at practice, Teagan hears the team is dreading "Easton Spirit Week," which culminates in the Iron Crucible, a 14-mile, seven-obstacle course that allegedly kills 0.07% of participants. The crew's main event is the alumni race: dads vs. sons. Rich Regis (Josh's father, a former Olympian and four-time Haverford Cup stroke) tours donors through the boathouse he rebuilt after a fire. Hayden walks Teagan through each rower's psychology. Cam privately vents to Teagan that being benched from stroke would kill his Olympic dream; Hayden takes her to a scenic overlook he used to share with a childhood friend from Easton.

At Parents' Night, Ella plays the scandal-wife role perfectly, dazzling the Easton moms, and bonds with Imogen, whose husband also "ripped everyone off." Gwendolyn Chou sizes Ella up. Rich pressures Hayden to put Josh back in stroke. Hayden privately promotes Cam to stroke, then reverses the call minutes before launch because Rich leveraged Principal Cabrera's niece for an LV internship. The boys' eight loses the alumni race after Josh ignores Teagan's "wait for my call" command. Teagan blames herself; Josh blames her. Cam snaps at Josh at the clambake, Josh insults Cam's mother, Ryder sides with Cam, and the two cousins brawl. Hayden cancels the party.

The next morning's Iron Crucible becomes the real stroke-seat trial. Josh and Ryder sabotage the cargo net climb. Teagan boosts Cam onto her shoulders to clear it. At the final wall, Josh hears his late father's voice coaching him through "the pain cave," and both Josh and Cam push through to finish — first time anyone has finished in six years. Ryder taps out. Hayden awards stroke to Ryder. Rich treats the finishers to burgers.

At home, Ella unveils her first event-planning gig: a James Bond "casino night" birthday for a teenage athlete, fee enough to cover Teagan's term. The client arrives at the door.

## Important Character Actions

- Teagan: disastrous first outing as cox; receives "conductor, not musician" advice; lobbies Hayden for stroke intel; misses her cox box (Damon Quaid prank — found in the women's change room); clashes with Josh mid-race; boosts Cam at the cargo net; becomes the first Crucible finisher in six years; admits "daddy issues and spite" is her motivational toolkit.
- Ella: insists on "Emerson"; secures $7K/month tuition plan; begs Jim for an advance; turns scandal into social currency at Parents' Night; lands her first paid event-planning client (the Bond party).
- Josh: campaigns aggressively to reclaim stroke; defies Teagan's race call; nearly quits at the Crucible wall, then finishes via his father's "pain cave" memory; fights Ryder at the clambake.
- Cam: learns he is stroke, then is bumped for Josh at the last moment; Hayden tells him Easton wants "someone the Easton crowd can follow," not a "townie"; storms off vowing to make the Olympics; encourages Teagan to stay; powers through the Crucible.
- Ryder: backs Cam for stroke; brawls with Josh at the clambake; finishes second in the Crucible and wins stroke.
- Hayden: privately picks Cam, then caves to Rich's pressure; announces stroke via the Crucible; praises Teagan for finding what motivates the boys.
- Rich Regis: donates the boathouse; pressures Hayden for Josh; uses the Cabrera/LV favor; tells Josh the anger is fuel after a loss.
- Baloo (Bronson): struggles with rate and slide; revealed as Britney's twin via different surrogates; their senator mother is up for election.
- Britney: confronts Bronson about a "thirsty" pull pic.

- Easton Spirit Week — culminating in the Iron Crucible.
- Iron Crucible — 14-mile school-wide obstacle course: seven stations separated by ~2-mile runs; signature obstacles include the Atlas carry, tire run, cargo net climb, and the literal final wall.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Family finances: education trust seized; tuition via $7K/month installments; Ella working double shifts at the diner (Jim is her boss).
- Ella is positioning herself as an event planner — clients liked her party ideas during her husband's boutique-hotel days.
- Rich Regis built/donated the new boathouse after the old one burned down; the Regis family has occupied stroke seat for four generations (Josh would have been the fifth).
- Hayden rowed stern pair with Rich (7-seat to Rich's 8-seat); Rich still calls him "Matt."
- Hayden and Ella have a shared past — he was "the rower she used to know," and they used to frequent the same diner; they explicitly deny being "more than friends," but the framing leaves it open.
- Rich's corruption of the stroke decision (Cabrera niece / LV internship) is now known to Hayden.
- Ryder is established in this episode as Josh's cousin (not brother), the product of Rich's affair with the dog walker.
- Cam's Olympic ambition is explicit; Hayden privately endorses it.
- Teagan's Crucible finish is a school milestone — first in six years; only the crew had any finishers at all (four of them).
