# S01E07 — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
The team travels to its **first away regatta of the season**, the **Head of the Laurence** — a 6K head race that doubles as a barometer for the rest of the season, including the **Haverford Cup**. Pre-race rituals include an EDM conditioning session choreographed by Teagan, carb-loading with the "free speed" pasta-water blessing (Cam and Teagan are christened "Crabby"), and Britney extending an olive-branch "girls' night" to Teagan. Britney's room turns into an ambush: she demands Teagan leave Easton and offers to pull strings for a tryout at **Gravenhurst Academy** (the East Coast's best girls' crew, who follow Teagan on socials because she was the **Southwest junior single-sculls champion**). Teagan secretly ergs at 6:30 a.m. the next morning — hitting a 7:32 2K — and is offered a mid-semester transfer. When Rider accidentally reveals the tryout before the race, the boys erupt, and Teagan confesses to Ella she no longer knows what she wants. A tense pre-launch sequence culminates in a near-crash at the bridge into the first turn; Teagan wrestles the shell through and the Eagles post a winning time, sparking chants of "Crabcatcher!" and a celebratory lake toss. At the after-party, Cam confesses he doesn't want to be "just friends," Teagan reciprocates with "tacos," and the episode closes on the boys' dorm chanting "Josh!" — a sequel hook. In B-plots, Ella learns the anniversary getaway was bankrolled by David's platinum membership, leaves him a voicemail declaring she's done, and later fends off advances from her longtime crush **Senator Liam Beecham** (mom to rider **Madison**) over a Mexican-taco dinner date. Josh takes a covert call from **Dr. Monroe**, who is treating him for **hypertrophic cardiomyopathy** and threatening to inform his parents.

## Important Character Actions
- **Teagan**: Designs the EDM erg playlist and runs boys' conditioning; coaches her "stop/start/rate up" drills; endures Britney's power play and accepts the Gravenhurst tryout in secret; ergs a 7:32 at Gravenhurst and is offered a transfer; is confronted by Rider before the launch; steers the team through the bridge crisis and wins the race; leads the post-race team huddle, distributes snacks, and brokers a near-mutiny-to-team-speech ("We're a team of nine"); tells Ella she's "confused" about rowing vs. coxing; confides in Cam that leaving "isn't personal"; calls her dad at the end.
- **Cam**: Shrugs off "drinkable" Cam-Dior banter with Britney; reveals to Teagan he feels treated as the "bare necessities" guy, that "reliable isn't exactly a sexy reputation," and that the team, teachers, mom, sister all assume he doesn't try; warns Teagan not to go to Gravenhurst because Easton will be left without a cox; later admits he doesn't want to be just friends.
- **Josh**: Crowned **Fall Formal King** alongside Britney (so the "form-posal" reference is now canon); broken up with by Britney on the dance floor; afterwards asks **Cam** to approach Teagan for him; takes a covert call about his heart condition; party-crashes the celebration under "Easton! Easton! Easton!" chant, identified by name as "Josh Regis" — teased as a fresh troublemaker figure for next episode.
- **Rider**: Discovers Teagan's Gravenhurst tryout; snaps that she was "always in it for herself" and tells her "don't let this deadweight hold you back any longer"; then leads the bridge approach on the water and hears "For Ryder!" from the engine room; at the party returns the spotlight to Teagan and tells Cam she always knew he'd make his move.
- **Britney**: Stages the "girls' night" with an agenda: she wants Teagan gone; weaponizes Teagan's single-sculls past and the Gravenhurst connection to dangle a "win-win" exit; receives the breakup text from Josh (his girlfriend) but stays calm; reappears as the post-race voice of "drink up" and the formal "King and Queen" announcer.
- **Ella**: Shops for a heritage-dinner at **The Crab Shack** (chowder week) with help from **Fran** and the handsome **Matt** (filling in for the injured **Jim**); is upgraded to a platinum suite using David's membership; fields a "you and me hang" room-service plan with Teagan; later goes to dinner with **Senator Liam Beecham**, deflects his Roman-Holiday date idea, leaves a tearful but firm goodbye voicemail to David; at the formal, asks Britney "are you mad at me" — Britney says "yes."
- **Senator Liam Beecham**: Ella's recurring date; widowed father of Madison; asks Ella to **Roman Holiday** at the rep cinema; admits Madison "always finds out" when he dates; tells her "it takes longer to let go than you expect."
- **Dr. Monroe**: Cardiologist phoning Josh to demand the follow-up test; warns **hypertrophic cardiomyopathy** left-ventricle thickening is serious; threatens to loop in Josh's parents under state minor-consent rules; Josh capitulates and agrees to schedule.

- Coach Hayden's stroke-seat choice going into Haverford Cup is now in flux — Josh is the de facto stroke per the race, with Rider still in the engine room.
