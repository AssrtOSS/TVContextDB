## S03E02

# Percy Jackson and the Olympians — S03E02

## Episode Plot
Dr. Thorn, a servant of "the General," tries to capture siblings Bianca and Nico di Angelo at a school event, but the Hunters of Artemis, led by Zoë Nightshade, drive him off. Annabeth has already been taken by Titan magic — magic that was actually after Percy and Nico, "Forbidden Children" prized by Kronos. Artemis appears and identifies the General as Atlas, the Titan who led Kronos's armies in the first war and was cursed to hold the sky forever. She sends the three demigods to Camp Half-Blood, grants Percy a quest, assigns Thalia to the hunt for Annabeth, and names Zoë as their escort. Apollo arrives in his sun chariot, offers them a ride, and warns not to influence the children or Zeus will hear. Percy reveals he dreamed Annabeth walking at camp with the Oracle, who is usually dead. At camp, Thalia clashes with other campers over Zeus, and the group finds Luke cursed, holding the sky — the Titan's curse is a test of will that only a willing substitute can end. Percy talks Luke down. The Oracle then speaks a prophecy in green mist: five shall go west to the goddess in chains; one shall be lost in the land without rain; the Bane of Olympus shows the trail; campers and Hunters will prevail together; one must withstand the Titan's curse; one shall perish by a parent's hand. Artemis is hunting the Bane of Olympus before the winter solstice — one week away — and the Hunters move to find Percy, son of Poseidon. Atlas appears as the episode ends.

## Important Character Actions
- Percy demands a quest from Artemis so they can consult the Oracle; argues with Thalia; talks Luke into releasing the sky's burden; loses Annabeth's dagger, which carries Manticore's blood, in the lake.
- Thalia rejects camp and Zeus, plans to hunt Luke alone, and clashes with Percy over leadership and trust.
- Zoë Nightshade leads the escort, answers to Artemis, and later reveals she witnessed Zeus strike Thalia down on Half-Blood Hill and hid the truth at Zeus's request.
- Artemis grants the quest, hunts alone, and swears to find the Bane before the winter solstice.
- Apollo ferries the group toward camp in his sun chariot, then bows out, citing fate.
- Luke, cursed and holding the sky, relives his failures (Golden Fleece, Golden Apple, Thalia) until Percy convinces him it is an illusion and he lets go.
- Grover reunites with Percy at camp; Chiron, afraid of the cold, runs the camp, which is always summer inside its boundary.
- Nico panics when the Oracle speaks — "I don't have a card for this" — and runs off.

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- Hunters of Artemis — elite hunters in an eternal hunt, led by Artemis; Zoë Nightshade is a lieutenant.
- Forbidden Children — children of the Big Three; prizes to Kronos; quick to attract monsters.
- The General = Atlas — Titan who led Kronos's armies against Olympus, cursed to hold the sky, called the terror of the gods.
- Titan magic — the force that took Annabeth.
- Camp Half-Blood — sanctuary for half-bloods, safe from monsters, always summer within its boundary; run by Chiron.
- Half-Blood Hill — where Zeus struck Thalia down.
- The Oracle — usually dead; delivers prophecies in green mist.
- The Great Prophecy — the overarching prophecy campers reference.
- Apollo — god of archery, prophecy, and poetry; drives the sun chariot; Artemis's twin brother.
- Winter solstice — the deadline, one week away.
- The Bane of Olympus — ancient monster returned to devour forbidden children and gain power to destroy the gods.
- Titan's curse — holding the sky; breaks the mind before the body; only a willing substitute can take it.
- Manticore — monster whose blood is on Annabeth's dagger.
- Long Island Sound — Percy is likely halfway across it by the end.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Percy has been on two quests and met nine gods; before today, Thalia had only met Zeus.
- Annabeth wanted Percy and Thalia to be friends because they are so alike.
- Luke tried to kill Percy and is believed to be with the side that took Annabeth, yet Percy believes Luke knows where she is.
- Annabeth's dagger carries Manticore's blood and was needed to jump-start the Oracle; it was lost in the lake.
- The Oracle speaking while alive is abnormal; Percy's demigod dreams can predict the future.
- Thalia is a daughter of Zeus and a Forbidden Child; Zeus's treatment of her is a major grievance.

## Jokes and References
- Travis and Connor Stoll ask Thalia for an autograph because her signed card's resale value would double.
- Percy asks Zoë whether she is "immortal or invulnerable — I always get those mixed up."
- Apollo lists his domains (archery, prophecy, poetry) and calls his sun chariot a "hot car."
- Percy jokes that he once dreamed of trying to kill Thalia ("Who won?"), then calls the lost dagger "the key to reviving the corpse."
- "Letting fate take the wheel" while driving the chariot; "Bollocks" when the dagger turns up missing.
- The prophecy's "goddess in chains" and "one shall perish by a parent's hand" foreshadow later confrontations.

## Unresolved Threads
- Where Atlas took Annabeth and who the "goddess in chains" is.
- The meaning of "the land without rain" and who will be lost there.
- Which demigod must withstand the Titan's curse and who will perish by a parent's hand.
- Luke's fate after releasing the sky.
- The Bane of Olympus still at large before the winter solstice.
- Nico's whereabouts after fleeing the prophecy, and the soldiers "following their orders without fail" at the close.
- Whether Thalia will stay at camp or join the quest; Percy's missing dagger.

## S03E01

## Episode Plot

Percy has a nightmare recapping the previous season: Luke poisoned Thalia's tree with Kronos's help, the Golden Fleece quest restored her, and Chiron revealed the Great Prophecy — a child of the eldest three, at sixteen, would become a weapon that could destroy Olympus. Percy believed he was that child until Luke told him "You were never the one. It was always Thalia." In the dream Luke demands Percy prove his loyalty by burning Camp Half-Blood and killing "the son of Poseidon."

In the present, Percy and Annabeth try to recruit Thalia to help rescue two demigod children at Westover Hall, a military school in Maine. Thalia greets Percy with "Where's Luke?" and attacks him with her Aegis shield; she declines the quest. During the drive (with Sally at the wheel), Percy admits he no longer wants Thalia along, revealing she has spent six years as a tree and remembers only dreams and nightmares about Luke — "for her, it was one day."

At Westover, undercover satyr Grover Underwood briefs them: the school is crawling with enemy forces, some students and staff work for Luke, and they are recruiting half-bloods for Kronos. During the winter dance, the team moves to extract siblings Bianca and Nico di Angelo. Annabeth reveals she has been invited to join the Hunters of Artemis — "Immortality and you," readable by moonlight — and Percy talks her out of it. Dr. Thorn, a shape-shifting monster serving "the General," attacks with paralytic-poison spikes, immobilizes the group with "Silence," and takes them prisoner, calling the di Angelo children bait. The Hunters of Artemis drive him off; Artemis appears and says the magic that seized Annabeth was Titan magic aimed at Percy and Nico. Apollo arrives in the sun chariot to ferry them away. Percy notes Luke knows where Annabeth is because he is allied with whoever took her.

## Important Character Actions

- Percy: recounts the nightmare; admits to Annabeth he distrusts Thalia; defends Nico from bullies; bonds with him over Mythomagic; argues Annabeth out of joining the Hunters; is identified by Artemis and Apollo as the son of Poseidon.
- Annabeth: co-leads the extraction; reveals the Hunters' invitation and that she wants Percy and Thalia to be friends because she may "go away forever"; is taken by Titan magic.
- Thalia: rebuffs the recruitment, asks "Where's Luke?", uses her Aegis shield in the fight against the monster.
- Grover: runs the Westover operation undercover as "Mr. Underwood"; suggests the goat kick.
- Bianca and Nico di Angelo: the extraction targets; Nico's Mythomagic cards are contraband; both dyslexic; Bianca defends Nico.
- Dr. Thorn: a teacher/monster who serves "the General," uses paralytic spikes and "Silence," takes the group alive; driven off by the Hunters.
- Artemis and Apollo: intervene; Artemis claims dominion over wild beasts; Apollo brings the sun chariot.

## Newly Established Names and Terms

- Thalia Grace: daughter of Zeus, "most powerful demigod on Earth," restored from a tree, saved Luke's life.
- The Great Prophecy: child of the eldest three, weapon at sixteen, may destroy Olympus; Chiron lied about it six years ago on Zeus's orders.
- The Golden Fleece: heals anything; Kronos wanted it to raise his champion, Luke.
- "We take care of our own": camp creed, invoked for Thalia.
- Westover Hall: military school in Maine, "training for Dracula's army," winter dance, "Excellence is a tradition."
- Dr. Thorn / "the General": Thorn's mysterious superior, who will "enlighten you as to your true purpose."
- Bianca and Nico di Angelo: siblings, di Angelo family, attracted a monster quickly.
- Mythomagic: card game with dice and miniatures; contraband at Westover.
- The Mist: hides monsters; "Don't trust anything you see, or hear or smell."
- The Hunters of Artemis: elite unit on an eternal hunt; immortality invitation, read by moonlight.
- Aegis: shield that "creates fear in monsters," "plus five to defense."
- Titan magic: the force that took Annabeth.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Luke now serves Kronos, recruits half-bloods for him, and was behind the poisoning of Thalia's tree.
- The prophecy child was revealed as Thalia, not Percy; Percy vowed "I will never be your weapon."
- Thalia's six years as a tree left her with only dreams; her loyalty to Luke persists because she still sees him as family.
- Annabeth's friendship push with Thalia was motivated by her own plan to leave forever with the Hunters.
- Demigods share dyslexia; Grover is a satyr (goat kick); Percy is son of Poseidon.
- Children of the Big Three attract monsters quickly; Kronos prizes them.

## Jokes and References

## S03E03

## Episode Summary: S03E03 — "The Titan's Curse" (Part 2)

### Plot
The quest to rescue Annabeth and Artemis continues westward. Zoë Nightshade leads Thalia, Grover, and Bianca di Angelo toward Washington, DC, following the prophecy clue "The Bane of Olympus shows the trail." Percy, not chosen by the Oracle, separately pursues them on Blackjack (gifted by Mr. D). At the National Air and Space Museum, the Nemean Lion — bait to lure Artemis — appears on a world globe, its claw pointing to Mount Othrys (San Francisco). Spartoi (undead skeletal warriors) ambush the group. Percy arrives, uses the Aegis, and helps kill the Lion by targeting its mouth. The Spartoi capture Nico; Bianca stays with the quest. Percy learns Annabeth is held at Mount Othrys, where Atlas bears the sky. Percy temporarily takes the sky burden so Artemis can fight Atlas. Annabeth tricks Atlas back under the sky. Zoë is fatally wounded. The prophecy's "five shall go west" is fulfilled with Percy as the fifth. Nico is sent back to camp with Clarisse; Thalia receives Zoë's Hunters coat provisionally.

### Important Character Actions
- **Percy**: Defies the Oracle, follows on Blackjack; takes the sky from Artemis/Atlas to save Annabeth; tricks Atlas into resuming the burden.
- **Thalia**: Argues to join the hunt; becomes provisional Hunter; resists taking the sky.
- **Zoë Nightshade**: Leads the quest; mortally wounded protecting Artemis; passes her coat to Thalia.
- **Bianca di Angelo**: Deciphers the Lion's globe clue; stays with the quest; promises to protect Nico.
- **Nico di Angelo**: Sneaks after the group; captured by Spartoi; sent back to camp.
- **Artemis**: Captured, used as bait; freed when Percy takes the sky; helps defeat Atlas.
- **Atlas**: Holds the sky at Mount Othrys; tries to force Artemis/Percy/Annabeth to take it; tricked back under.
- **Luke Castellan**: Revealed as working with Kronos; cares for Annabeth (giving her leverage).
- **Mr. D**: Gives Percy Blackjack; reveals winter solstice deadline (Olympus war vote).

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Mount Othrys** — Destroyed Titan fortress opposite Olympus; where sky and earth first met; Atlas's prison.
- **Spartoi (地生人)** — Undead warriors born from dragon teeth; follow orders without fail; "cannot be killed."
- **The Bane of Olympus (奥林帕斯祸害)** — Prophecy term for the monster (Nemean Lion) that can destroy Olympus after devouring a forbidden child.
- **Winter Solstice deadline** — Five days; only day Mr. D can leave camp; Olympus votes on war with Kronos.
- **Forbidden children (禁忌之子)** — Demigods of the Big Three (Zeus, Poseidon, Hades); the monster gains power by devouring one.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Prophecy lines confirmed: "Five shall go west to the goddess in chains," "The Titan's curse must one withstand," "One shall perish by a parent's hand," "The Bane of Olympus shows the trail."
- Annabeth held at Mount Othrys (San Francisco).
- Luke is the "other" destined to bear the sky curse (per Atlas/Kronos).
- Thalia is a forbidden child (daughter of Zeus); must be kept from the Bane.
- Nico and Bianca lived in Washington, DC before Las Vegas.
- Zoë was once a Hunter lieutenant; knew Hercules; scar from failing to replicate his feat.
- Atlas is Zoë's father (implied by "General" address and shared history).
- The Great Prophecy (终极预言) mentioned — separate from current quest prophecy.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "Nothing's Gonna Stop Us Now" playing in the van — Zoë hates it.
- Thalia: "There's always a 'You're gonna fail and die' part" about prophecies.
- Mr. D calls Percy "Peter" and says "I still hate you."
- Stoll brothers poisoned Phoebe with centaur blood (hives) — "technically not interfering."
- Blackjack can only carry one rider — Nico left behind.
- Percy's hunger for power after eating Atlas's apple — "it won't end your hunger, only make you crave the power."

### Unresolved Threads
- **Nico's fate**: Sent to camp with Clarisse; promised to protect Bianca; knows gods "can't be trusted."
- **Zoë's death**: Mortally wounded by Atlas; dies after quest succeeds.
- **Luke's allegiance**: Working with Kronos; cares for Annabeth; destiny to bear sky curse.
- **Olympus war vote**: Winter solstice (5 days); gods decide whether to fight Kronos.
- **Thalia as Hunter**: Provisional; coat passed; long-term status unclear.
- **Bianca's promise**: Must protect Nico on the quest.
- **Atlas**: Tricked back under sky; still imprisoned.
- **Kronos's plan**: Uses Luke, Spartoi, and the forbidden children to overthrow Olympus.

## S03E04

## S03E04 Summary

### Episode Plot
The quest group (Percy, Thalia, Zoë, Grover, Bianca) flees Washington, DC pursued by **Spartoi** (earth-born warriors). **Ares** intercepts them, cursing **Riptide** to fail when Percy needs it most, and reveals the gods will vote at the **Winter Solstice** (four days away) on whether to execute Percy and Thalia as threats to Olympus per the Great Prophecy. **Aphrodite** appears, offering a shortcut through the **Junkyard of the Gods** (Hephaestus's **Deepwater Forge**) via a Hephaestus-marked key. Inside the endless junkyard, Bianca takes a **Mythomagic** figure of Talos, inadvertently activating the prototype automaton **Talos 1.0**. The group is hunted by Talos; Thalia is badly injured. **Tyson** (Percy's cyclops half-brother) contacts Percy via scry box, revealing Talos's shutoff switch is in its head, accessed through its heel. Bianca sacrifices herself to reach the switch, shutting down Talos. Dying, she swears the **Hunter's Oath** to Artemis, becoming an immortal Huntress. Nico, back at camp, senses her loss.

### Important Character Actions
- **Ares**: Curses Riptide; reveals Olympian vote to kill Percy/Thalia; claims "Bane of Olympus" gains power by devouring a forbidden child.
- **Aphrodite**: Gives Hephaestus key for Junkyard shortcut; votes for Percy; frames love as the force to survive the Titan war.
- **Bianca**: Takes Mythomagic figure → activates Talos; reaches shutoff in head; swears Hunter's Oath; dies telling Percy "don't fear" for Nico.
- **Percy**: Contacts Tyson via scry box; coordinates Talos takedown; refuses power grab, insists goal is saving Annabeth.
- **Zoë**: Questions Artemis's wording ("encounter" not "devour"); realizes Artemis may have lied to her.
- **Thalia**: Wounded by Talos; urges Bianca to take Hunter's Oath; refuses to kill Talos for power.
- **Nico** (remote): Senses Bianca via mental link; guided to gas-station portal symbol.
