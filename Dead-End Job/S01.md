## S01E03

## Dead-End Job S01E03 — "Milkman Vlog" (送奶Vlog)

### Episode Plot
Hyeok-jun, having survived his first supernatural gig, tries to quit Spider Staffing Agency but is lured back by a "200× daily wage" milk-delivery job at Heukran Officetel (22:00–06:00, 11 bottles, 1-hour limit). Desperate to pay his friend Byeong-cheol’s hospital bills after Hanbit Logistics denies responsibility for Byeong-cheol’s rhabdomyolysis, Hyeok-jun accepts. The delivery turns into a nightmarish maze: floors don’t match, room numbers repeat (444), door handles vanish, and he’s pulled into a live TV studio set ("Challenge! A Father's Miracle"). With seconds left, his sister Ji-yoon appears and helps deliver the final bottle to 505. A game-like UI flashes "MILK DELIVERY: COMPLETED" and "BYEON HYEOK-JUN, AGE 30 CONVENIENCE STORE: CONTINUE," implying the world runs on game logic.

### Important Character Actions
- **Hyeok-jun (Byeon Hyeok-jun)**: Refuses Mr. S’s offer initially, gets scammed at a pyramid-scheme interview (Xenon HR / "High Seed Big Tech"), visits hospitalized Byeong-cheol, takes the milk run, films a vlog during delivery, barely escapes the building’s traps.
- **Eum Yeon-joo (Labor Accident Investigator)**: Reveals her identity, explains her unit handles "strange cases unfit for police or civil court" involving Spider Staffing Agency, gives Hyeok-jun her card and urges him to talk.
- **Kwak Byeong-cheol**: Hospitalized with rhabdomyolysis from Hanbit Logistics overwork; company denies liability; uninsured, faces possible kidney dialysis.
- **Ji-yoon (Byeon Ji-yoon)**: Gives Hyeok-jun a luxury bag from Carrot Market to "value himself more," notices his pull-up bar fell and rope in trash (suicide warning), later materializes inside the building to save him.
- **Mr. S (Spider Staffing head)**: Persists with the 200× offer, knows Hyeok-jun’s debt and Byeong-cheol’s bills.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Heukran Officetel (黑兰商住公寓)** — site of the milk-delivery gig.
- **Yeongsan Milk (灵山牛奶)** — brand delivered; only 11 bottles.
- **"Challenge! A Father's Miracle" (挑战！爸爸的奇迹)** — family TV show Hyeok-jun gets sucked into.
- **Game UI overlays**: "MILK DELIVERY: COMPLETED", "CONVENIENCE STORE: CONTINUE", "EVENT VENUE: CONTINUE" — first explicit gamification of reality.
- **Rhabdomyolysis (横纹肌溶解症)** — Byeong-cheol’s diagnosed condition.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Spider Staffing Agency targets debt-ridden workers with escalating pay multipliers (50× → 200×).
- Hanbit Logistics follows "2 hr work / 20 min break" per Ministry guidelines but denies injury claims.
- Yeon-joo’s investigation is official: Workers’ Compensation Union (劳动灾害协会) Accident Investigator.
- Hyeok-jun’s debt and family history (formerly wealthy, now poor) are known to Mr. S and Byeong-cheol.
- Ji-yoon’s note: "IF YOU CAN'T REACH ME, GO TO SPIDER STAFFING AGENCY" — she knows about the agency.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- "They say you'll die if you do something out of character" — meta comment on the genre.
- AB blood-type cup-sharing excuse — recurring gag about blood-type superstition.
- "神仙兼职" (god-tier gig) — Mr. S’s pitch phrase, likely to return.
- Carrot Market (당근마켓) luxury bag — cheap/knockoff humor.
- Building floor-number chaos (444 = death omen in Korean culture) — supernatural numerology.
- Vlog format — Hyeok-jun narrates to camera, breaking fourth wall.

### Unresolved Threads
- What is the true nature of the building / the "game" overlay? Who controls it?
- Will Yeon-joo and Hyeok-jun formally team up? She has his contact; he hasn’t called.
- Byeong-cheol’s fate: dialysis, compensation, survival.
- Ji-yoon’s knowledge of Spider Staffing — how deep is she involved?
- Mr. S’s endgame: why escalate pay? What happens after "completion"?
- The "CONVENIENCE STORE: CONTINUE" tag — is the convenience store (Ep 1–2 setting) the hub/lobby?

## S01E07

## S01E07 — Neo World After Dark

### Episode Plot
Hyeok-jun (Hyuk-jun) receives his massive payout (₩128,384,000) from the construction-site gig, pays off his debts, and celebrates with his mother and sister Ji-yoon. He reveals to Ji-yoon that he crossed over from the other world through a door at **Kyungil Neo World** (庆一新世界), an amusement park built over the former **Kyungil Textile** (庆一纺织) factory. Flashbacks show Yeon-joo was a union activist at that factory 40 years ago; she escaped through the door when the factory was demolished and the park built atop it, emerging into a world where no one remembered her.

Hyeok-jun returns to Spider Staffing Agency. Mr. S assigns him the **Kyungil Neo World Parade Part-Time Job** (10 PM–4 AM) with a **Full-Time Transition Program** (转正流程) — the only gig that lets him reach the door again. At the park, Hyeok-jun is cast as the **Wolf** in a “Three Little Pigs” parade rehearsal. The rules: never speak human language, never remove the mask. During the live parade the fable inverts — the **pigs hunt the wolf**. Hyeok-jun barely survives the rehearsal.

Meanwhile, Ji-yoon confronts Mr. S for sending her brother back into danger. Mr. S reveals **Yeon-joo is a monster from the other side** — a Workers' Compensation Service (劳动灾害保护院) investigator, an organization disbanded in the 1980s. He claims she’s using Hyeok-jun and vows to “handle” her.

At the parade’s climax, Hyeok-jun’s mask is knocked off. The system flags him as a **“defective character” (残次品)** and dispatches a **sanitation crew (清洁工作)** to eliminate him. He flees into the **Fantasy Haunted House (幻想鬼屋)**, where a twisted attraction — **“Challenge! A Father’s Miracle” (挑战！爸爸的奇迹)** — begins.

---

### Important Character Actions
- **Hyeok-jun**: Pays off debt, confesses other-world origin to Ji-yoon, accepts the parade gig to reach the door, survives reversed fable, mask removed → marked for termination.
- **Ji-yoon**: Learns brother’s secret, confronts Mr. S at the park, discovers Yeon-joo’s true nature.
- **Yeon-joo** (flashback): Led union strike at Kyungil Textile; escaped through the door when factory became amusement park; 40 years later, posing as investigator.
- **Mr. S**: Assigns Hyeok-jun the parade job, exposes Yeon-joo as a monster, claims he’ll “handle” her.

## S01E08

# Dead-End Job S01E08 — "Last Carnival"

## Episode Plot
The finale opens on a twisted variety show, **“Challenge! A Father’s Miracle,”** where Byeon **Min-su** (Hyeok-jun’s father) performs a lethal stunt—juggling on a unicycle—for a cash prize. Hyeok-jun interrupts, begging him to stop; Min-su breaks down, repeating “I should just die.” The scene shifts to the **“Fantasy Haunted House”** (the doorway to the other world). Yeon-joo stands before the door; Mr. S (Spider Staffing Agency manager) pressures her not to open it, arguing that releasing the other world will destroy everything. Yeon-joo counters that everything in the other world originated here. Hyeok-jun arrives, fights Mr. S, and rejects his utilitarian creed—“In order to save more people, we need to make more sacrifices”—with “They just wanted to live.” Mr. S strangles Hyeok-jun, but Min-su appears, apologizes for deceiving him, and urges Hyeok-jun to leave. Min-su stays behind, handing Hyeok-jun a final message for the family. As a fire alarm blares, Hyeok-jun and Yeon-joo escape. The world “didn’t end, but it didn’t change either.”

Epilogue: Hyeok-jun, Min-su, and Ji-yoon share tteokbokki in their cramped home—ordinary life resumed. A news report details **mysterious nationwide fires**; the National Forensic Service found an **unknown substance and unidentified DNA** at every site, matching the Kyungil Neo World explosion. Hyeok-jun receives a **Spider Staffing Agency manager offer** via “predecessor’s referral,” followed by a **Kyungil Group 2025 H2 recruitment acceptance**—the cycle renews.

## Important Character Actions
- **Hyeok-jun**: Confronts his father on live TV; physically fights Mr. S; rejects the “sacrifice” logic; escapes with Yeon-joo; implicitly accepts the Agency manager role.
- **Yeon-joo**: Stands at the threshold of the other world; refuses to be intimidated by Mr. S; escapes with Hyeok-jun.
- **Min-su (Father)**: Performs the deadly stunt; breaks down; ultimately sacrifices his return to let Hyeok-jun and Yeon-joo go, passing a final letter/money.
- **Mr. S**: Reveals 40 years of “balancing” the world through sacrificed temp workers; tries to stop the door opening; defeated but leaves a recorded succession message.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **“Challenge! A Father’s Miracle”** (도전! 아빠의 기적) — exploitative variety show framing the deadly gigs.
- **Fantasy Haunted House** (판타지 귀신집) — the physical gate to the other world.
- **“The other world” / “Another world”** (다른 세계) — explicitly named as the source of monsters and the destination of the sacrificed.
- **Kyungil Group** (경일 그룹) — corporate entity linked to the Neo World explosion and Hyeok-jun’s new job offer.
- **National Forensic Service** (국립과학수사연구원) — investigative body identifying the unknown substance/DNA.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Mr. S has run the Agency for **40 years**; the manager role passes by **“predecessor’s referral.”**
- The **balance is broken**—the door was opened, monsters entered, fires spread nationwide.
- Hyeok-jun is now the **new Spider Staffing Agency manager** (final shot: acceptance notification).
- The **Kyungil Group recruitment** mirrors the series’ opening premise (high-paying, suspicious offer).
- Min-su’s line “You sound just like your dad” confirms Hyeok-jun inherited his father’s values.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- Variety-show parody format (“Challenge! A Father’s Miracle”) — could recur as in-universe media.
- Track-and-field “warning” callback (Hyeok-jun’s past).
- Tteokbokki / sundae / fritters meal — the series’ comfort-food motif, signaling fragile normalcy.
- “I’m confident” job-interview photo pose — bookends the premiere’s resume montage.

## Unresolved Threads
- Will Hyeok-jun **perpetuate or dismantle** the Agency’s sacrificial system?
- **Nationwide mystery fires** continue; the **unknown substance/DNA** remains unexplained.
- Yeon-joo’s investigative path—does she pursue the Agency or the fires?
- Ji-yoon’s role in the new cycle.
- Whether the **other world’s incursion** is truly contained or expanding.

## S01E06

# Dead-End Job S01E06 Summary — "The Walls Don't Talk" / "沉默的墙"

## Episode Plot
Hyeok-jun flashes back to his childhood: his family appeared on a TV show *A Father's Miracle* (爸爸的奇迹) where his father failed a unicycle-juggling challenge. After cosigning a loan bankrupted them, his father vanished, claiming he'd found a "god-tier job" (神仙差事). In the present, the Spider Staffing Agency boss (Mr. S) confronts Hyeok-jun, noting his unusual survival talent across prior gigs (milk delivery, Yangcheon Bio Clinic). Hyeok-jun asks about **Byeon Min-su** (卞敏洙) — the boss doesn't know the name. Hyeok-jun accepts a 200×-pay construction night shift ("the last one").

On site, crews clash over removing supports installed the previous day. Rookie Hyeok-jun works alongside veteran foreman and "Old Kim." Kim falls from height removing an illegal banner; the foreman **sends the ambulance away**, claiming orders not to let it in. Workers revolt and quit; only Hyeok-jun stays. In the basement, the foreman reveals the building's secret: a shaman demanded a **"tower of human bones"** — 100 live sacrifices for generational wealth. He admits doing it for the money. He mentions a prior worker, "Old Byeon," missing fingers, bad at the job but stubborn — "met a bad end on-site." Hyeok-jun presses: **Byeon Min-su** — his father. The foreman clarifies: Byeon isn't dead. **He walked through a door while working and never came back.** Episode ends with a lullaby about a sewing machine, flowers, and butterflies.

## Important Character Actions
- **Hyeok-jun**: confronts Mr. S about his father; takes the lethal construction job despite wanting out; stays when everyone flees; identifies Byeon Min-su as his father.
- **Mr. S (Spider Staffing boss)**: reveals he's tracked Hyeok-jun's prior jobs; calls him a "natural"; deflects on Byeon Min-su.
- **Foreman**: runs the sacrificial site; blocks medical help; confesses the 100-sacrifice ritual; reveals Byeon disappeared through a door.
- **Old Kim**: falls to his injury/death; catalyst for the workers' walkout.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **A Father's Miracle** (爸爸的奇迹) — TV variety show Hyeok-jun's family appeared on.
- **Byeon Min-su** (卞敏洙) — Hyeok-jun's father's full name (previously only "dad" / "Old Byeon").
- **Yangcheon Bio Clinic** (阳川医院) — referenced as a prior Spider gig (ep 4–5).
- **Human-bone tower / 100 sacrifices** (人骨塔 / 献祭一百个活人) — the building's supernatural foundation ritual.
- **The door** (一扇门) — unexplained portal/disappearance mechanism on site.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Hyeok-jun's debt ledger shown: Uncle Cheol-i 150k, Sang-woo 190k, Sang-min 180k, Mi-gyeong 320k (all by specific months).
- Job progression confirmed: Tunnel 50× → Quarry 100× → Construction 200× ("the last").
- Spider Staffing boss has monitored Hyeok-jun across multiple assignments.
- The construction site is **where Hyeok-jun's father worked and vanished**.
- Foreman knows the supernatural terms but participates for pay — "Is making a living a crime?"
- Yeon-joo (investigator) absent this episode; her probe continues off-screen.

## Jokes / References for Later
- *A Father's Miracle* unicycle failure — ironic mirror of father's real "miracle" job hunt.
- Foreman's line: "Buildings aren't built from nothing. They're built on blood and bones."
- Closing lullaby (sewing machine, red/yellow flowers, white butterflies) — eerie domestic contrast to the bone tower.

2. **Is Byeon Min-su alive in another space/dimension?**
3. **How many of the 100 sacrifices are complete?**
4. **Mr. S's endgame for Hyeok-jun — recruitment or sacrifice?**
5. **Will Hyeok-jun return to the site or investigate the door?**
