# S01E02 Summary — *It Gets Worse*

## Episode Plot
Abi secures a promotion to the newly invented "people team" at Greta's startup while navigating a tense dinner party with boyfriend Tom. Meanwhile, Ethan and Sam settle into their new flat, meeting neighbor Aaron who takes an interest in Ethan. The evening implodes when Abi discovers Tom's unused Fleshlight, triggering a raw argument about their dead sex life and emotional distance. The trio reconnects amid the wreckage, floating the idea of flat-hunting together again.

- **Abi**: Advocates for herself in review meeting (per Greta's advice), gets promoted to "people team," hosts dinner to prove she hasn't abandoned Ethan/Sam, confronts Tom about the sex toy and their intimacy vacuum.

- **Greta**: Promotes Abi, invents "people team" concept (HR without training), asks inappropriate question about analingus.
- **Aaron**: Flirts with Ethan, invites himself to dinner, asks Ethan out (or Ethan believes so).

# S01E01 Summary — *It Gets Worse*

## Episode Plot
Ethan (Leo Reich) opens in therapy, narrating his "easy life" but crushing hopelessness — triggered by their landlord selling the flat. The trio (Ethan, Abi, Sam) must vacate by tonight; the new place isn't ready until the 27th (~3–4 weeks), so they plan to crash with Jesse, Ethan's hot-but-awful casual partner. Ethan drilled a hole in the wall trying to hang a photo; they need it fixed to get their deposit back. Abi reveals she's moving in with her boyfriend Tom, blowing up the new flat contract (needs three incomes). A disastrous night at club "Slut Pig" ends in a screaming row: Abi feels pushed around; Ethan and Sam feel blindsided. Ethan goes to Jesse's, has humiliating sex, gets kicked out. The episode closes with the trio back in the old flat, Ethan asking to stay with one of them, and Sam producing proper repair materials from her warehouse job.
