- Directors: Jamie Childs, Helen Shaver, Stephen Dunn
- Broadcaster: Netflix (worldwide; produced via Pacesetter Productions)

- Season / episode structure: 1 season, 6 episodes (S01E01–S01E06); episode titles had not been publicly announced at the time of writing and remain listed as TBA on EpGuides and TheTVDB
- Genre: suspense / thriller; also tagged drama, horror, mystery

## Synopsis (full-series premise, not single-episode)

The remote Newfoundland fishing town of Snook's Arm is terrorized by an unidentified sea creature. Calvin Penney, a stubborn, big-hearted local fisherman still haunted by his father's mysterious death decades earlier, must protect his fractured family and his close-knit community from the creature and from forces that threaten their vanishing way of life. He allies with Fonda Howander, a visiting marine researcher, while trying to keep his adult son Wade and the rest of his family safe. The series blends local Newfoundland supernatural folklore, family drama, suspense, and dark humor, and explores themes of community, resilience, legacy, and inevitable change.

## Character relationships and roles

- Calvin Penney: lead; big-hearted but burdened bayman / fisherman of Snook's Arm; single father of three; haunted by his father's long-ago mysterious death; feels "cursed" and out of step with his own community.
- Wade Penney: Calvin's son; works alongside his father in the fight against the creature.
- Jay, Ruth, Mary Penney: other Penney children / family members; together they form the fractured family Calvin is trying to hold together.
- Fonda Howander: visiting marine researcher who partners with Calvin to identify and confront the sea creature; key outsider / expert role.
- Jan: local figure in Calvin's circle.
- Officer Jaxon Noseworthy: local law enforcement; community anchor during the crisis.
- Noah: younger local connected to the central group.
- Linda: town resident played by Newfoundland comedy icon Mary Walsh.

## World setting and cultural context

- Location: Snook's Arm (also rendered Snooks Arm), a fictional small Newfoundland fishing town on the rugged Atlantic coast of Canada. Series was filmed on location in Newfoundland and Labrador.
- Cultural frame: Newfoundland has a deep, distinct identity — historically a separate dominion before joining Canada in 1949, with its own dialect (Newfoundland English), Irish and West-Country English ancestry, fishing-village ("outport") traditions, and a long history of maritime disasters, killer storms, the collapse of the cod fishery, and supernatural local folklore. Creator Jesse McKeown deliberately drew on these legends when inventing the sea-creature threat.
- Community texture: tight-knit, eccentric, warm but fatalistic; outsiders (like Fonda) are conspicuous.
- Economy and way of life: small-scale inshore fishing; the Penney family and townsfolk embody a "vanishing way of life" threatened by both the creature and larger forces of change.

## Speaking styles and register notes for translation

- Dialogue carries a strong Newfoundland English flavor: clipped vowels, archaic phrasing, dropped consonants, local idioms, and dark, dry humor. Subtitles should not literally Newfoundland-ize the Chinese, but should preserve the rough, laconic, slightly weary tone. Avoid overly literary Mandarin.
- Calvin's voice: gruff, fatalistic, self-deprecating; lines like feeling "cursed" should land as 自认倒霉 / 觉得自己被诅咒 rather than melodrama.
- Fonda's voice: more clinical and outsider; scientific register when discussing the creature.
- Older locals (Jan, Linda, Officer Noseworthy): community-gossip and wary-elder register; often speak in folk-wisdom cadence.
- Humor is deadpan and embedded in crisis scenes; preserve dry wit rather than translating literally.
