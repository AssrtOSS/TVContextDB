## S01E01

## Episode S01E01 — "The Dispatcher" Summary

### Plot Overview
The episode opens with Ian Ridley, a former detective now working as a police dispatcher, navigating the 11-year anniversary of his daughter Maggie's disappearance. A vigil is organized by his ex-wife Debbie for their "lost" daughter, but Ian resists closure. The body of a young girl is discovered—initially thought to be Maggie due to matching pajamas—but DNA results come back negative. Meanwhile, Ian's son Dean (who was babysitting the night Maggie vanished) is pressed to re-examine his memory of a man knocking at the door. At the vigil, Ian attempts to use Dean to identify a potential local suspect. The episode ends with Ian receiving a distress call from a teenage girl at a phone booth in Port McKenna who may be Maggie—she gives the name "Sarah," then "Mags," then "Maggie." Ian recognizes the voice and races to find her.

### Important Character Actions
- **Ian Ridley**: Pushes to reopen the cold case; confronts DNA results showing the found body isn't Maggie; tries to use the vigil as a trap for the killer; receives the climactic emergency call from a girl claiming to be Maggie.
- **Debbie (Deb)**: Organizes the vigil as a "fresh start" / goodbye; furious when she learns Ian withheld the DNA results; accuses him of 11 years of false hope.
- **Dean (Ian's son)**: Reluctantly agrees to scan faces at the vigil; snaps at Ian when the task fails; leaves with his boyfriend Sam.
- **Jenna Reid**: Appears shaken by news of the found body; takes medication for migraines; refuses to attend the vigil per her father Henry's strict religious isolation rules.
- **Henry Reid**: Jenna's controlling father; forbids face makeup, outside contact; tells Jenna "the outside world is a bad place, and a godless place."
- **Sarah / "Maggie"**: Teenage girl living in isolation on a farm; sneaks out to a party; gets drunk/lost; calls Triple Zero from a phone booth; gives conflicting names (Sarah → Mags → Maggie).
- **Tommy Brenton**: Jack Brenton's younger brother; brings Sarah to the party; agrees to take her to the map coordinates she found.
- **Jack Brenton**: New neighbor; introduces himself to Ian's family; non-drinker, early riser.
- **Donald "Donnie" Reid**: Ian's supportive brother; tries to mediate between Ian and Debbie; visits the farm.
- **Bill McCracken**: Police colleague; guards the crime scene; lets Ian look briefly.
- **Isabel Pena**: Mentioned as overseeing the investigation; Ian questions her handling of the crime scene perimeter.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Triple Zero (000)**: Australian emergency number; Ian works as a dispatcher for this service.
- **"Possum" / "负鼠宝宝"**: Ian's affectionate nickname for his daughter (used in flashback/family scenes).
- **Map coordinates**: Numbers Sarah shows Tommy—lead to a location 40 minutes away; significance unknown.
- **Vulpes vulpes**: Scientific name for red fox; Sarah shows Tommy a fox figurine/toy.
- **The vigil**: Community gathering for Maggie; Ian views it as performative; Debbie sees it as closure.
- **Jane Doe**: Unidentified body; the found girl remains unnamed.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Maggie disappeared **11 years ago**; Ian was a detective, now a dispatcher.
- Dean was babysitting the night of the kidnapping; a man knocked, claimed wrong house, left; Dean was high on weed and didn't get a good look.
- The road to where the body was found **did not exist 11 years ago**—killer had to carry the body on foot, suggesting a local.
- The found girl wore **Maggie's pajamas**—implies connection between cases.
- Ian has **37 hours sober** (AA/recovery); Donnie presses him to work the steps.
- Jenna's family (Reids) live on an isolated farm; **Henry controls all outside contact**; they are "people of faith" who fear government removal of children.
- Sarah has **never left the farm**; believes the government would force her into school and take her away if discovered.
- The Brentons (Jack & Tommy) are **new neighbors**; Jack bought the block next door.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **"Bridge over the River Kwai is a classic" / "Boring"**: Family movie-night banter; establishes Ian's attempts at "culture."
- **Radish gardening**: Ian's hobby; "murdered it as a baby" — dark humor foreshadowing.
- **"You're the family favorite"**: Sibling dynamic between Tommy and his brother (unnamed).
- **Whiskey & cherry cola**: Sarah's first drink; Tommy's "legend" status for getting her there.
- **"Chook"**: Australian term of endearment Donnie uses for Sarah.

### Unresolved Threads
1. **Is the caller actually Maggie?** Voice recognition suggests yes, but she's disoriented, drunk, and gives multiple names.
2. **Who is the Jane Doe?** Pajamas match Maggie's; same perpetrator suspected; more bodies may be found in the forest.
3. **What are the map coordinates?** Sarah wants to go there urgently; Tommy agrees to take her after the party.
4. **The man at the door (11 years ago)**: Dean may recognize him; Ian believes he's local and attended the vigil.
5. **Jenna's reaction to the body discovery**: She nearly collapses; knows something? Her father Henry isolates her aggressively.
6. **The Reid/Brenton connection**: Jack Brenton moved in next door; Henry warns Sarah to stay away from "that end of the property."
7. **Ian's relapse risk**: He admits if he goes home he'll drink for three days; stays at the dispatch center instead.
8. **Sarah's identity**: "Sarah" vs "Mags/Maggie" — amnesia, trauma, or deliberate deception?
9. **The party assault**: Sarah is cornered by a boy (Charlie/Damo?); she flees, calls 000 from a phone booth.
10. **Henry's "medicine"**: Jenna finds old medication; Henry rejects it ("I don't believe in that stuff"); hints at mental health/control dynamic.

## S01E03

## S01E03 — "The Lie"

### Plot

Sarah — the girl living with Henry and Jenna Reid — sneaks out to a teenage party in town, gets drunk, and calls Triple Zero from a pay phone on Piper Street. Dispatcher Ian Ridley takes the call. During it, Sarah says "Maybe my name is Mags," triggering Ian's belief that the caller is his long-missing daughter Maggie. Sarah is picked up from the phone booth by a van and returned home. The next day, Ian canvasses the skate park, gets a description of events from witness Damian Tully (who saw Sarah get into a van voluntarily), and traces his ex-wife Deborah Ridley to a gift shop in Port McKenna. He tells his AA sponsor Donnie that Maggie is alive, that the abductor has been local for eleven years, and that he nearly drank again. Donnie relays the "Mags" connection to Henry, who angrily forbids him from going to the police or telling Ian anything. Meanwhile Sarah, increasingly troubled, asks Tommy to bring her the missing-person flyer so she can see Maggie's face. Ian has the Triple Zero call audio enhanced and hears something on it that devastates him. The episode closes at Ian's AA meeting, where he publicly accuses someone he trusts and depends on of lying to him.

### Character Actions

- **Ian Ridley**: Investigates the call independently (canvasses witnesses, checks CCTV, requests fingerprint dusting of the phone booth). Nearly relapses on alcohol. Gets the call audio enhanced. Publicly confronts a trusted person at AA.
- **Henry Reid**: Physically locks Sarah in her room as punishment. Pressures Donnie into silence, claiming losing Sarah would destroy the family. Lies to Sarah that "Mags" was a local girl who was murdered. Threatens Tommy via Donnie at the tree house.
- **Jenna Reid**: Appears increasingly fragile; Henry describes her as "unwell." She references the dead girl on a flyer, expresses fear "we'll lose her," and says "it all went wrong."
- **Donnie (Donald Reid)**: Acts as intermediary. Raises the possibility that Sarah could be Maggie based on age and photo resemblance. Confronted by Henry, he backs down and agrees to stay silent. Later hosts the AA meeting where Ian makes his accusation.
- **Sarah**: Questions her own identity — asks Henry who "Mags" is, asks Tommy whether drunken memories can be false, and requests the flyer to look at Maggie's face. Secretly meets Tommy despite being grounded.
- **Tommy Brenton**: Helps Sarah escape her locked room (via kitchen window and a key with a ribbon). Reports to Sarah that an "old guy" (Ian) was at the skate park asking about her and "Mags." Is warned off by Donnie at Henry's direction.

### New Names / Terms

- **Damian "Damo" Tully** — partygoer; last person known to have seen Sarah before she left for the pay phone. Has a visible injury Sarah may have caused.
- **Charlie** — host of the party; hostile when Ian questions him.
- **Deborah Ridley** — Ian's ex-wife; runs "Ridley's Flowers, Homewares and Gifts" in Port McKenna. No listed phone number.
- **Piper Street** — location of the phone booth used for the Triple Zero call.
- **Tree house** — Sarah and Tommy's regular meeting spot; located on the Reid property.
- **Constable Lewis** — officer who dusted the phone booth for fingerprints (found nothing) at Ian's insistence.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Sarah told the Triple Zero operator her name might be "Mags" and that she "can't remember anything."
- CCTV does not cover the phone booth; fingerprints yielded no results.
- Sarah was returned home in a van she entered voluntarily — no abduction was observed.
- Henry claims Maggie was "murdered," contradicting the open missing-person case.
- Henry was not present at Sarah's birth; Donnie did not meet Sarah until she was about nine.
- Donnie is Henry's brother, making him Sarah's uncle by the Reid family connection.
- Ian's ex-wife Deborah is alive and runs a shop — the Ridley family is fractured but not destroyed.
- Call logs at the dispatch centre have gone missing after a "software update" (Polly's explanation).
- The episode title "The Lie" refers to the central deception: Henry has raised Sarah as his own daughter while the Ridleys believe Maggie was taken.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- **Tulip bulbs**: Ian suggests to Maggie (in a memory/hallucination) that they plant tulip bulbs they have been saving — a domestic detail that may recur as an emotional motif.
- **Mouse bones**: Tommy brings Sarah a gift of mouse bones, and she calls him the "weirdo" — their bond is built on mutual outsider status and odd shared interests.
- **"King Shit"**: Lewis's dressing-down of Ian for pulling rank as a former station chief establishes institutional resentment that could limit Ian's access to official resources going forward.

### Unresolved Threads

- What exactly Ian heard on the enhanced Triple Zero audio that made him swear and go silent.
- Who Ian is accusing of lying at the AA meeting — the framing points toward Donnie (who knows the truth and stayed silent), but it could be someone else entirely.
- The missing call logs at dispatch — accidental or deliberate cover-up?
- Who was driving the van that picked up Sarah, and where it took her before she was returned home.
- What "Mags" means to Sarah on a subconscious level — she has fragmentary memories (a favorite song, "I like to sing") but no coherent identity recall.
- Jenna's mental state and her reference to "the dead girl" on a flyer — does she know or suspect Sarah's true identity?
- Tommy's next move after being warned off by Donnie; whether he will bring Sarah the flyer as promised.
- The sermon/preacher audio fragments woven through the episode suggest a religious framework underpinning Henry's justifications — the nature of that belief system and its community remains unexplored.

## S01E04

## Episode S01E04 — "The Truth" Summary

### Plot
Henry visits Donald ("Donnie") after Ian's confrontation, learning Ian "knows." Henry argues Ian has no proof, pleads family loyalty, and arranges an afternoon meeting at the farm. Ian recounts to police that after confronting Donald he spotted Henry — the station's window cleaner of many years — tailed him, and was run off the road. Meanwhile, Damian Tully confirms he saw a teenage girl get into Donald's van on Piper Street the night of the Triple O call, so police bring Donald in; he refuses to answer and the interview is suspended at 12:42 p.m. Henry walks into the station to accuse Ian of following him, damaging his car, and trying to kill him — but Pena pivots: she and Ben Sizemore will visit his residence over the kidnapping allegation, with a warrant if he refuses. Henry, saying he has nothing to hide, drives them out. Pena permits Ian to come only if he stays in the car outside the property.

The farm visit is deceptively benign: a colorful house full of Sarah's artwork, tea served by a polite 14-year-old, and a birth certificate — Sarah Elizabeth Reid, born 6 September 2012, parents Henry Matthew Reid and Jennifer Elaine Reid. Sarah denies attending the party or making the 000 call. Bill later verifies the certificate number (568695) with Births, Deaths and Marriages: the record is real. Ian is devastated; Debbie declares she's done with his certainties.

Parallel thread: Jennifer Reid, presenting as "Jen," visits Debbie's flower shop, talks about her own "miracle" pregnancy and asks whether Debbie has another child. The encounter leaves Debbie hospitalized overnight (elevated heart rate; the baby is fine). Ian realizes Jenna was probing her, and pushes Bill to confront the Reids before they flee. Henry preps Sarah for departure ("bad men... policemen are coming"; feed the animals — "we're going away"), and Sarah says goodbye to Tommy at the tree house, leaving him a letter and her belongings ("it's only a loan"; "thanks for being my friend — I've never had one before"). Donald confronts Henry at the fish works; Henry says they're leaving for somewhere they won't be found, and Donald warns against going back to their father — "the man's a fanatic," with his compound. Donald urges Jenna to stay with him; she refuses — "I've gotta face this. For Sarah" — admitting she's been hiding and came to tell him what really happened.

At the farm, Bill and Ian (against orders) confront the family as the car is being loaded. Jenna approaches Ian to ask about Deborah. Ian confronts Sarah with a music box from her infancy: "I'm your dad, Maggie." Henry screams "Leave our daughter alone!"; Ian retorts "She's not our daughter." Henry draws a gun, Bill orders him to drop it, shots are fired — Sarah is hit. Henry loads Sarah and Jenna into the car and flees. The episode ends with Ian rushing to Bill, who appears to have been shot.

### Character Actions
- Ian: gives police his account of the chase; begs to be present at any rescue (allowed only in the car, under threat of reckless-driving charges); verifies the birth record with Debbie; persuades Bill to visit the farm; crosses the line physically to confront Sarah with the music box.
- Henry: manages Donnie's panic; counter-reports Ian to police; stage-manages the farm inspection (claims to speak for his absent wife); prepares the family to flee; pulls a gun in the standoff and escapes with Sarah and Jenna.
- Jenna: visits Debbie's shop under the name "Jen"; confesses to Donald she's been hiding and wants to face the truth "for Sarah"; challenges Henry's plan ("that girl didn't have a choice in any of this"); is caught in the shootout.
- Donald: nearly breaks under interrogation silence; confronts Henry about the police and the flight plan; fails to talk Jenna into staying.
- Debbie: confronts Ian over the birth record ("I'm done"); hospitalized after Jenna's visit; her account confirms Jenna was gathering information about Maggie.
- Pena/Ben: conduct the interview and farm visit; refuse Ian's DNA-test/court-order demands for a minor.
- Sarah: performs the role of daughter under Henry's coaching; denies the 000 call; says goodbye to Tommy.

### New Names / Terms
- Henry Matthew Reid — full name; window cleaner subcontractor; car registered to a business-park office in Geelong; prepaid burner phone.
- Jennifer Elaine Reid — Jenna's full legal name on the certificate.
- Sarah Elizabeth Reid — DOB 6 September 2012; birth certificate no. 568695, verified as genuine.
- Births, Deaths and Marriages (registry) — used to check the certificate.
- McKenna Seafoods — Donald's employer as a fisherman.
- The compound / "Dad" — Henry and Donald's father, called "a fanatic"; destination Henry apparently intends to flee to.
- Music box — Ian's toy for baby Maggie, used in the confrontation.
- "Triple O" call — recurring reference to the emergency call.

### Continuity Facts
- Sarah's verified birth record (2012) appears to exclude her from being Maggie, who vanished 11 years ago — yet the certificate's legitimacy is now the crux (registered fraudulently after the abduction?).
- Donald's record: 2015 DUI causing serious injury, 24-month suspended sentence; otherwise clean; in town ~5 years.
- Henry has cleaned the station windows for years without anyone knowing him — deliberate placement.
- Damian Tully's witness statement ties Donald's van to Piper Street.
- Ian is on bail; Pena warns custody if he steps out of line.
- Debbie is pregnant; baby is fine, she's kept overnight.
- The farm belonged to Jenna's late aunt; Henry restored it.
- Jenna's "miracle" baby line mirrors the radio preacher's sermon framing the episode.

### Jokes / References
- Radio preacher: "a little miracle will do" — ironic counterpoint to Jenna's shop line about pregnancy as a miracle.
- "We don't inject chemicals into us" — the Reids' anti-medicine worldview on full display when asked for vaccination records.
- "Would I lie to you?" — Henry's chilling coaching line to Sarah.
- "You can call me Izzy" — Pena's rapport technique with minors.
- The tree-house letter and "it's only a loan" — Sarah/Tommy token likely to matter later.

### Unresolved Threads
1. The shootout: Sarah is shot; Jenna's condition; Bill appears gravely wounded — all fates unconfirmed.
2. Henry's escape with Sarah (and Jenna) — destination presumably the father's compound (setup for E05, "The Prophet").
3. How the fraudulent birth registration was created, and whether the registry record can be exposed.
4. Whether Jenna will now defect and reveal "what really happened," as she told Donald.
5. Whether Donald finally goes to the police.
6. Ian's legal exposure after defying Pena at the farm.
7. The fanatic father and the compound — the belief system behind Henry's justification remains unexplored.

## S01E06


## S01E06 — "The Choice"

### Plot

The episode opens with a family on the run: a father ("Da") travels with his young daughter and his gravely injured wife ("Ma"), refusing hospital care and insisting he is taking them to his father's property, where "Grandpa" — a prophet and healer — will cure her. The daughter begs her mother to insist on a hospital.

Police investigate a church hall connected to recent gunfire. The hall's key-holder list includes Donald Reid as the contact for a local AA branch. The hall is cleared with no one inside.

Ben Sizemore receives a call: Ian Ridley has been to Debbie's house, taken Bill McCracken's gun, and is driving a vehicle belonging to his son Dean. An APB goes out on the vehicle. Dean tells police his father took the gun and didn't want him involved.

Ian is bleeding from a shoulder wound and holding Donald at gunpoint in a car. Ian explains that Henry (Donald's brother) has killed a police officer — Bill McCracken — and that Henry's wife was also shot. Donald suggests going to the police; Ian rebukes him for refusing to help earlier when Ian begged.

The family on the road stops to refuel. The father tells the girl to stay hidden in the car. She sees Donald and another man (Ian) outside and runs to them, recognizing "Uncle Donald." Ian calls out "Mags! Maggie!" and begs her to unlock the car door; she panics and drives off. The father reassures her that "Uncle Donald" is a weak man and that they came without police, so their destination is still secret. He takes a shortcut to reach "Grandpa."

A service station near Naracoorte reports a disturbance: two men, one with a heavily injured shoulder. Police mobilize.

Ian and Donald stop so Donald can treat Ian's shoulder wound. Donald reveals: Jenna Reid visited him yesterday to say goodbye. She and Ian were once lovers. Jenna told Donald why they took Maggie — the abductors had a daughter named Sarah, who died when her neck was caught between two fence posts while playing outside. Ian erupts: even if that's true, Maggie was his perfect child, and he has grieved for eleven years, blown up his life, and can never get the lost baby back. He demands Donald tell him where they're going. Donald reveals their father's compound — about 20K further, just past a big gully on the left.

Police (Sizemore briefing) cross-reference known associates and identify Gareth Reid — Henry's father — linked to a religious group called the Church of the Chosen Apostles, which faced 2017 charges for depriving children of education and medical care before disbanding. Their old base was Three Mile Hill, about 40K away. Tactical units and a perimeter are dispatched.

Henry arrives at the compound with an injured Jenna and introduces "Sarah" as his daughter. The compound is empty. Gareth explains the government raided years ago, took the children, and the congregation deserted him. He refuses to help Jenna — and reveals "your wife's dead." Henry collapses in grief. Sarah asks why the pursuing man claims to be her father; Henry calls him "an addict, sick in the head."

Sarah confronts Henry: he taught her that lying was a sin, but he's lied her whole life. Henry claims God led him to her and protecting her was his life's purpose. He finally confesses: the "girl in the forest" he told her about — Sarah — was their own daughter, who died in an accident, and he buried her away from the farm to spare her mother. When Sarah asks about her real family, Henry insists he is her father and that she was happy.

Gareth mobilizes his remaining men (Eli, John, Rebecca) to repel Ian. Sarah is moved inside by Rebecca.

Ian arrives. A shootout erupts. Sarah hides while Ian and Gareth's people shoot at each other. Ian finds Sarah and shows her photos — a toy/music box, her "real" mother, a brother, family photos. He promises they'll be a family again. Sarah admits her "ma" is gone but refuses to leave Henry.

Henry grabs Sarah and uses her as a human shield, threatening to break her neck to flush Ian out. Sarah cries for help. Henry is shot and killed — by his own father Gareth, who says "you made me do it." Ian finds Sarah and asks her to come out; he apologizes, says they can start again somewhere new. A tactical officer demands Ian drop his weapon. Ian hesitates ("I've been dreaming about this moment for so fucking long") then complies. Sarah tells him: "My name is Sarah. Stop calling me Mags."

In the final scene, Sarah is welcomed into Ian's home by Debbie. A social worker has set up her room upstairs with her easel and piano. Debbie introduces Sarah to the baby. Sarah holds her at first, then just looks. She asks if she was a good sleeper; Debbie says no — she kept her up all night wanting to be fed. Debbie says Sarah had determination and character and she always knew Sarah would be "a world beater." Sarah asks if she can attend her "husband's" (Henry's) memorial. She meets two girls at the gathering; Debbie introduces her. Sam and Dean embrace; Sam and Dean toast each other.

### Important Character Actions

- **Ian Ridley** — Drives Dean's vehicle; holds Donald at gunpoint, bleeding from the shoulder; forces Donald to reveal the compound's location; exchanges fire with Gareth's men at Three Mile Hill; confronts Henry; is shot at by Gareth; refuses to drop his weapon at first, eventually complies, walks out of the compound with Sarah.
- **Donald Reid** — Bound by Ian at gunpoint; tends Ian's wound; reveals Jenna's visit, the abduction motive (Sarah's death), and the compound's location; begs Ian not to shoot up the compound.
- **Henry Reid** — Arrives at Gareth's compound with the fatally wounded Jenna and "Sarah"/Maggie; begs his father for help and is denied; confesses to Sarah that her name Sarah belonged to their deceased daughter and that he is her father; uses Sarah as a human shield; is shot and killed by his own father.
- **Sarah / "Maggie"** — Disobeys her father at the service station and runs to Ian; panics and drives away; confronts Henry about his lies; refuses to abandon Henry even after his death ("I don't care. I loved her, and now she's dead. I can't lose him too"); insists her name is Sarah; eventually leaves with Ian and integrates into his family.
- **Gareth Reid** — Head of the Church of the Chosen Apostles cult; abandoned by his congregation after the 2017 government raid; refuses to help Jenna; deploys Eli, John, and Rebecca into defensive positions; shoots and kills his own son Henry to "finish" the trouble.
- **Jenna Reid** — Mortally wounded; dies at the compound.
- **Bill McCracken** — Killed by Henry in the previous episode; his gun is the one Ian is using.
- **Ben Sizemore** — Coordinates the police response; receives the alert on Ian's vehicle; later briefs the team and identifies Gareth Reid as the cult connection.
- **Debbie** — Hosts Sarah at the Ridley home; the baby is hers; tells Sarah she always knew she'd be "a world beater."
- **Dean (Deano) Ridley** — Ian's son; tells police his father took Bill's gun and didn't want him involved; embraces Sam and toasts him at the memorial.

### New Names / Terms Established

- **Gareth Reid** — Donald's father; leader of the Church of the Chosen Apostles; based at Three Mile Hill.
- **Church of the Chosen Apostles** — religious cult implicated in 2017 for depriving children of education and medical care; disbanded by authorities.
- **Three Mile Hill** — the cult's compound, ~40K from the current police position, ~20K past a big gully.
- **Naracoorte** — service-station town where the disturbance involving two men (one wounded) is reported.
- **Sarah** — (a) the deceased original daughter of Henry and Jenna, who died when her neck was caught between two fence posts; (b) the name given to Maggie by her captors.
- **Eli, John, Rebecca** — remaining followers of Gareth at the compound; Rebecca is tasked with taking Sarah into hiding.
- **Sam** — appears at the memorial; embraced by Dean.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Ian and Jenna were once lovers; Jenna visited Donald to say goodbye and to explain the kidnapping motive.
- The abduction of Maggie was driven by the Brentons' own daughter's death (Sarah, fence-post accident).
- Bill McCracken is dead; his gun is the one Ian is using.
- Henry killed Bill McCracken and shot Jenna; both are dead by the episode's end.
- Ian's family fractured by Maggie's loss 11 years ago; his wife (Debbie) is alive with a new baby.
- Dean's car is the vehicle Ian is driving.
- The Church of the Chosen Apostles was a 2017 case; the compound was supposed to have been vacated and clearly was not.
- "Sarah" tells Ian she does not want to be called "Mags" — her identity in the new family is Sarah.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- "Possum" — Henry's affectionate name for Sarah.
- The toy/music box Sarah remembers from before; the tune is one she asked Ian to play repeatedly ("Again, again"). Strong anchor for any future memory/identity work.
- "World beater" — Debbie's phrase for what she always predicted of her daughter.
- "Great man… prophet and healer" — Gareth's self-positioning, thoroughly discredited in this episode; the term "the prophet" remains in the S01E05 episode title and as a thematic label.
- The "girl in the forest" is a story Henry told Sarah to cover up his biological daughter's death.

### Unresolved Threads

- The disposition of the remaining cult members (Eli, John, Rebecca, Gareth) after the police perimeter arrives is not depicted — arrest, flight, or casualties are unspecified.
- Sarah's psychological integration — she has agreed to live as the Ridley's child but insists on the name Sarah and is still grieving both Jenna and Henry. Her long-term identity and relationship with Ian's actual family (the baby, Dean) are unresolved.
- Sarah has asked to attend Henry's memorial and floated returning to a "normal school" or even back to the farm as gestures of compromise; whether she revisits the farm is unresolved.
- Police accountability and consequences for Ian's armed rampage (shooting, abducting Donald, breaching the compound) are not addressed.
- The public/legal resolution of the Reid/Brenton kidnapping case — charges, custody, media — is not shown.
