## Episode Plot

TLC revival episode hosted by Dara Ó Briain with panellists Harriet Kemsley, Rhys James, Ahir Shah, Janine Harouni, Ed Byrne, and Glenn Moore. The format follows the post-watershed TLC structure: topical stand-up, improvisation, and a running gag where the stand-up round name changes every episode (this week: "Abu Dhabi-doo!").

Five rounds plus closing:

1. If This Is the Answer, What Is the Question? — Sport category. Answer is 99.1%, revealed as the proportion of charges against Manchester City reportedly upheld by the Premier League (initially 114 of 115, later amended to 115 of 115).
2. Scenes We'd Like to See + news riffs — "Brilliant Things the Sheikh Has Done," then the Gloucestershire terror plot (five men arrested near an RAF base, spotted by a passerby/farmer who texted the village WhatsApp group). Bill Gates's warning that unchecked AI could cause a billion deaths.
3. Between the Lines — Rhys performs as Meghan Markle returning to the UK; Janine translates the subtext.
4. You Think That's Bad? — Panellists outdo each other with personal misery stories.
5. Abu Dhabi-doo! (Wheel of News stand-up challenge) — Harriet on Body Confidence, Ed on Friendship.
6. Picture of the Week — Andy Burnham on a Mersey Ferry at the Labour Party Conference in Liverpool.
7. Scenes We'd Like to See — "Things You Wouldn't Hear Over a Tannoy System," followed by "Unlikely Things to Hear on a Gardening Programme."

Final winners: Harriet Kemsley, Rhys James, and Ahir Shah. Commiserations to Janine Harouni, Ed Byrne, and Glenn Moore.
