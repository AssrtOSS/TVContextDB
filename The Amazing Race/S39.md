## S39E03

# S39E03 — Morocco (Marrakech & Agafay Desert)

## Plot

Leg 3 begins with all teams on the same equalizer flight from Madrid to Marrakech. The route is Razani Pottery (Roadblock) → Agafay Desert (Detour) → Jemaa el-Fna Square (Route Info) → Koutoubia Gardens (Pit Stop).

The Roadblock is "Who feels like they're going to pieces?": one team member smashes rejected clay jugs until they find a clue; 10 say "Try again." Eleven jugs sit at the task.

The Detour offers Walk (roll a massive tire to a hitching post, fill it with alfalfa, fetch two camels, lead them back to eat) or Talk (drive a desert buggy to three signposts, memorize the Amazigh tea name spelled out, then recite it correctly to an elder at a tented camp for tea and the next clue).

The Route Info task takes teams to a juice cart at Jemaa el-Fna Square, where they must replicate the gravity-defying fruit pyramid on Cart #30 to the satisfaction of a local juicer.

Phil awaits at Koutoubia Gardens, behind the 12th-century Koutoubia Mosque.

## Character Actions

- Ann-Marie & Riley (mother/daughter, Riley is her son): arrive first to the pottery, complete the Roadblock efficiently, choose Walk, dominate the tire, and execute the fruit stand with Riley coaching Ann-Marie from below. Use their Express Pass at the fruit stand task after their display keeps collapsing. Riley earlier quipped that the smart time to use it is "when it's advantageous for us."
- Ali & Joanna (soccer players, dating): finish third despite collapsing their display multiple times. Joanna's confessional repeatedly lauds Ali's World Cup / Olympic-caliber strength.
- Conner & Garrett (best friends): finish fourth after Garrett finds the Roadside Assistance jug. Conner leads the tire-rolling with Garrett on moral support. Choose Walk.
- Anuar & Andrea (father/daughter, Andrea is a "soccer girl"): finish second. Burn their Express Pass at the Detour when they see Matt & Michelle arriving and prefer the matchup. Andrea is a "puzzle person" and reasons about fruit sizing. Phil's exposition states Andrea is a "soccer girl," though she is in fact Anuar's daughter.
- Doug & Dylan (grandfather/grandson): struggle badly at the fruit stand (Dylan "what's wrong with us? why are we so bad at stacking fruit?"), come perilously close to last place. Doug is 56 ("we're athletic, we're not professionals").
- Daisha & Dalton (dating): attempt Walk, Daisha physically cannot move the tire, switch to Talk. Memorize the tea name with help from Jaime & Cody. Then get stuck on the fruit stand and are rescued when Jaime & Cody double back to help.
- Jaime & Cody (siblings): the alliance of the leg. After Dalton helps Daisha memorize the tea syllables, Cody and Jaime turn around at the fruit stand to help Daisha & Dalton stack fruit. Jaime is a dietitian who recognized the produce.
- Matt & Michelle (married, Michelle dominant in puzzles): race the fruit stand efficiently. Michelle takes control and locks it in.
- Jody & Jenn (best friends, married, "we like working with them"): last to the Roadblock, Jenn smashes dozens of jugs before finding her clue on the 47th try, as Daisha & Dalton depart. They go Talk. Their fruit-stand sequence runs as a parallel race to Doug & Dylan through the call to prayer at dusk; the episode cuts away before either team reaches Phil.

- Detour: Walk vs Talk.
- Amazigh tea name (transliterated as something like "Yan, ya, yaw, ya" or "Yal, yi, yaw, yi, yan" depending on team pronunciation).
- Arset El Bilk garden: meeting point referenced in the Route Info.
- Jemaa el-Fna Square: where the fruit-stand task lives.
- Koutoubia Gardens / Koutoubia Mosque: Pit Stop.
- OneKeyCash from Expedia: leg prize — a five-night Singapore trip (Pulau Ubin cycling, Singapore's "last authentic kampong," market/cooking class, Gardens by the Bay night, Tioman Islands snorkeling).
- "G" — the nickname Saran and Dafina use for each other ("we're gangsters in this thing called life").

## S39E04

# Episode S39E04 Summary — "Moroccan Scramble"

## Plot

Ten remaining teams begin Leg 4 in Marrakech via a new format: teams depart every two minutes in a "Moroccan Scramble." Route Info sends them by taxi around the old city walls to Bab Debbagh (the "Gate of the Tanners"), then into the Medina to complete three challenges, each yielding one-third of a clue. Final Pit Stop is Madrasa Ben Youssef, revealed once the three clue pieces are combined. Expedia-branded "experience" framing throughout.

## Important character actions

- Anuar & Andrea and Ann-Marie & Riley — first parent-child pair to a clue box. Despite agreeing to wait, Andrea & Anuar abandon Ann-Marie & Riley once they finish the slipper task; Riley is upset ("if we didn't help get here?").
- Dafina & Saran — use the first-ever Roadside Assistance at Slipper Search, splitting two different slipper hunts; first history-of-the-show team to deploy it.

# The Amazing Race S39E07 — Summary

## Episode Plot
The eight remaining teams fly 4,000+ miles from Albania to **Sri Lanka**, landing at Kuttiduwa Beach for a **live Double U-Turn vote**. Doug & Dylan, as last-place finishers in the previous non-elimination leg, receive an **automatic U-Turn**. The other teams vote: **Matt & Michelle (6 votes)**, **Daisha & Dalton (5 votes)**, and **Anuar & Andrea (3 votes)**. The three U-Turned teams must complete both sides of the Detour.

**Detour** ("Dry Out or Sell Out?"): Teams either dry fish on coconut-fiber mats or sell 5,000 rupees' worth of fish at auction. Most choose drying; Matt & Michelle and Doug & Dylan opt for selling.

**Route Info**: Take a tuk-tuk to Negombo Bus Terminal, bus to Colombo, walk to Mada Park.

**Roadblock** at Apsaras Saree Centre: One teammate loads 30 boxes onto a handcart and delivers them through chaotic Colombo traffic.

**Route Info**: Tuk-tuk to **Henry Pedris Grounds** for **tuk-tuk polo** — one drives, the other tries to score a goal with a mallet in 60 seconds.

**Final Pit Stop**: Taxi to **Independence Square**, Colombo. Last team to check in is eliminated.

## S39E08

# S39E08 — Vientiane, Laos (Leg 8)

## Episode plot
- All teams fly from Sri Lanka to Vientiane, Laos, meeting at Patuxai Monument (modeled on Paris' Arc de Triomphe, honoring Lao soldiers who fought for independence from France).
- Detour: "Something to Taste" — shop for 11 ingredients and cook larb (Lao's unofficial national dish) to a local chef's satisfaction; or "Nothing to Waste" — haul 15 crates of empty Beerlao bottles through the streets and stack a 75-crate load onto a recycling truck. Most teams pick the beer side; Anuar & Andrea, and later Michelle & Matt, take the cooking side.
- A second consecutive Double U-Turn board appears after the Detour. Erin & Javi U-Turn Michelle & Matt; Ann-Marie & Riley U-Turn Daisha & Dalton — the same two targets as the previous leg's vote.
- Route Info takes teams to Wat Thong Khankham, then the Lao National Circus for a Muay Lao (Laotian kickboxing) performance task judged by Master Koy, then Wat Si Saket for the Roadblock: "Who's down for the count?" — count 582 Buddha statues on a wall, submitting guesses by presenting a flower to the judge.
- Pit Stop: Buddha Park (Wat Xieng Khuan). Ann-Marie & Riley check in first, Erin & Javi second, Conner & Garrett third, Ali & Joanna fourth. Matt & Michelle arrive last and are eliminated.

## Important character actions
- Erin & Javi reach the Buddha-counting Roadblock first; Riley gives Javi the answer (582) after Javi promises to let Ann-Marie & Riley hit the mat first — so Erin & Javi deliberately settle for second.
- Anuar & Andrea write out an English version of the larb shopping list and share it with Daisha & Dalton, saving them significant time; Andrea openly helps because Matt & Michelle are in last and she wants the top team out.
- At Wat Si Saket, Andrea, Joanna, Conner, Daisha and others pool their counts ("we all worked together... divide and conquer"); Daisha joins the group late and is briefly suspected of just farming answers.
- Ann-Marie & Riley's teamwork and a fast finish earn them the leg win; Daisha & Dalton sprint the final stretch to beat Conner & Garrett to the mat but lose out (Conner & Garrett 3rd).
- Matt & Michelle, hit by the U-Turn after a lost30-minute navigation stretch, recover through the beer Detour but never erase the deficit; Phil eliminates them.

## Newly established names / terms
- Patuxai Monument (凯旋门), larb (肉末沙拉), Beerlao (老挝啤酒), Wat Thong Khankham (通坎寺), Lao National Circus (老挝国家马戏团), Muay Lao (老挝拳, distinct from Muay Thai), Master Koy (科伊师傅), Wat Si Saket (西沙萨寺), Buddha Park / Wat Xieng Khuan (佛像公园/香昆寺).
- Recipe terms: padaek dip, mak pet pong, toasted rice flour, culantro.
- Local phrases: "khob chai" (谢谢), tuk-tuk (嘟嘟车).
- Sponsor terms: OneKeyCash from Expedia; next destination Manchester, England (Lake District tour, street art walk, music heritage tour).
