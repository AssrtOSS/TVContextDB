## S01E01

# Slo Pitch S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Brovaries begin a new season in the Mississauga beer-league slo-pitch league. Coach Joanne reveals her 10-year partner Leanne (team co-founder) dumped her three months ago and joined the rival **Tech-ass Rangers**. The team has finished last six years straight. Joanne demands they "play to win" this year. The squad drones Leanne's first game, discovering her new fiancé **Roy Dabir** (Tech Town CEO). Joanne's office job forces her to miss the game, coaching via Zoom from a parking lot. Sponsor **Barb Barberetti** (Public Lass Bar & Grill owner, city council candidate) introduces **Mel**, a former pro player living in a van, paid $200/game. The team unionizes demanding equal pay; Joanne covers everyone from her grandmother's birthday money. At the field, Leanne arrives with Roy and texts Joanne about their engagement. The Brovaries rally, fabricating Joanne's success (CEO, helicopter, happy with Sasha) to save face. They lose the game, but Joanne keeps her job (boss praises her spreadsheets) — with eight more accounts and no raise.

## Important Character Actions

- **Boris**: Runs drone surveillance; aggressively confronts Leanne ("I draw excellent dicks"); threatens to vandalize her car wrap.
- **Ann**: Digs Instagram/Pinterest intel (finds Roy, "Roy's man cave" board); reveals Leanne's fiancé; helps fabricate Joanne's success lies.
- **Zari**: Objects to spying; leads "Norma Rae" unionization; straight-woman commentary throughout.
- **Lee**: Pilots drone; chaotic Gen-Z energy; "fight or make out?" speculation.
- **Mel**: Demands game fee upfront; triggers labor dispute; "rock bottom" pro-to-beer-league arc begins.
- **Barb**: Sponsor intro; sign typo ("Are you fucking deaf"); recruits Mel; city council campaign; pretends not to know Ann.
- **Leanne**: Shows up at game with Roy; attempts "mature" closure; gets shut down by Joanne's fabricated wins.
- **Sasha**: Brief supportive presence; "came over you" per Zari.

## S01E02

# Slo Pitch S01E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Joanne spirals after her breakup with rival coach Leanne, posting thirst traps at 3 AM. Team sponsor **Barb Barberetti** (conservative City Councillor candidate) catches them and threatens to pull funding. Joanne retaliates by drafting **Non-Fraternization Agreements (NFAs)** banning all inter-team dating/sex — including "prolonged hugs" — citing her and Leanne's past at space camp. The team (Ann, Boris, Zari, Mel, Lee) hatches a **"Seduce Joanne" campaign** ("Horny Lane") to manipulate her into burning the contracts, recruiting Sasha to play "mean" (Joanne's type). Joanne catches on and flips the script: she forces a **Spin the Bottle / Greek bathhouse "team building" session** to exhaust everyone's libido. The team shares traumatic romantic histories (Mel's pro career ended by ACL tear during relationship drama; now $30k in debt living in a van). Joanne declares victory — "horn dogs" transformed into "focused players." Barb returns, hates the glam makeovers ("sad stripper moms"), demands "relatable" looks for her campaign ad. Joanne discovers Sasha liked her thirst trap; keeps the photos up. Episode ends filming Barb's commercial.
