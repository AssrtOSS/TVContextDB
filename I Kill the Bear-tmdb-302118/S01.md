## S01E01

# I Kill the Bear — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Thibault siblings — fourth-generation Franco-Ontarian bear wranglers for film — announce their retirement after 100 years in the business. Andrée introduces the family: eldest Cédric, middle Gabby (her favorite), and youngest Olivier, who lives by the mantra "the bear needs calm." They've worked on *Grizzly Adams*, *The Edge*, *The Great Outdoors*, *The Revenant*, consulted on *Ted*, and count *Legends of the Fall* as their crowning achievement. Their final film will be shot under "New Hollywood" conditions — corporate HR culture, sensitivity readers, and animal-welfare scrutiny — which they find hostile to bears. After this movie, they plan to release their bear into the wild.

## Important Character Actions
- **Andrée** delivers the framing narration, establishing family history and the retirement decision.
- **Olivier** lives in the bush building tree forts; embodies the "bear needs calm" philosophy.
- **Gabby** is positioned as the conflicted lead — his "problems began when it became unacceptable to disagree with someone."
- **Cédric** (off-screen) referenced as eccentric, comic relief.
- **Bob** appears briefly as a Hollywood fixer type ("Relax. It's under control").

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Family base: Sudbury, Ontario (Northern Ontario bush).
- Four Thibault siblings: Cédric (eldest), Gabby (middle), Andrée (youngest sister), Olivier (youngest brother).
- They do not watch TV, read news, or use the internet — insulated from modern discourse.
- Gabby's central conflict: inability to operate in a culture where disagreement = silencing/ruin.
- The bear they work with is unnamed in this episode; release planned post-finale.
- Hollywood production side (Timothy, Rose, Edward, Kathy, Bob) represents the clash machine.
