**Production Companies:** Anomaly Pictures, Barnstorm, ESPN, Omaha Productions, 20th Television  

## Premise
Arrogant star quarterback **Russ Holliday** disgraces himself in a championship game — fumbling before the end zone, then shoving a fan into a boy in a wheelchair. His reputation ruined, he spends eight years adrift. With help from his makeup-artist father, he creates a prosthetic disguise and assumes the identity of **Chad Powers**, a walk-on quarterback at tiny **South Georgia College** (the Catfish). The series follows his double life, the team's improbable rise, and the personal reckonings forced by the deception.

## Key Relationships
- **Russ ↔ Danny:** Only ally who knows the secret; provides IDs, glue, moral support.
- **Russ/Ricky:** Romantic tension; she discovers his identity in S1E6.
- **Ricky ↔ Jake:** Strained father-daughter dynamic; she resents being "the coach's daughter."
- **Jake ↔ Wendy:** Marriage fracturing under football obsession; her affair with Russ (unknowingly) is a season-1 climax.
- **Russ ↔ Mike:** Distant but supportive father-son; Mike admires the craft of the disguise.
- **Chad ↔ Gerry:** Rivalry turning grudging respect; Gerry is the "honest" foil to Chad's deception.

## World Setting
- **Primary location:** Fictional **South Georgia College** (rural Georgia), home of the **Catfish**.
- **Football context:** Lower-tier FBS/FCS program; boosters (Tricia), NIL adjacent, *College GameDay* appearance in S1E5.
- **Timeline:** Season 1 spans one fall season (Sep–Oct 2025 diegetically); Season 2 begins immediately after.
- **Tone:** Absurdist comedy grounded in character drama; *Ted Lasso* comparisons frequent in criticism.

## Speaking Styles
- **Russ/Chad:** Fast, self-deprecating wit masking insecurity; shifts to vulnerable sincerity when mask slips. As Chad, adopts a softer, "aw-shucks" Southern-cadence.
- **Danny:** Rapid-fire, pop-culture-literate, loyal but not afraid to call out Russ.
- **Ricky:** Sharp, guarded, professionally competent; drops armor only with Chad.
- **Jake:** Folksy coach-speak ("We're fixin' to…", "ball control"), conceals panic about job security.
- **Mike:** Industry jargon (FX/makeup terms), dry humor, proud of craft.
- **Gerry:** Earnest, polite, slightly naïve; speaks in clean, unironic sentences.
- **Tricia:** Corporate-booster polish; transactional language ("investment," "brand").

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **College football culture:** Walk-on mythos, booster influence, *GameDay* prestige, NIL era references.
- **Southern setting:** Casual "y'all," church references, lake-house gatherings, high-school-football-is-religion vibe without caricature.
- **Disguise trope:** *Mrs. Doubtfire* / *Tootsie* lineage; prosthetic application scenes are technical (spirit gum, stipple, hair-laying) — keep terminology precise.
- **Manning family presence:** Eli & Peyton as EPs; Eli cameo adds authenticity for US viewers; may need footnote for Chinese audiences unfamiliar with NFL legacy.
- **Hulu/Disney+ streaming model:** Weekly drops after two-episode premiere; "Quarter" episode titles mirror football structure.
- **Season 2 note:** Premiered Sep 3, 2026; described by creators as "a different show in the best way" — expect shifted dynamics (identity exposed? new setting?).
