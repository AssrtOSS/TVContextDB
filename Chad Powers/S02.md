## S02E05

## Chad Powers S02E05 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode opens with Danny (the Catfish mascot) being fired by campus administrator Mr. Brent after Gerry accuses him of deliberately injuring the backup QB to get Chad on the field. Meanwhile, Ricky receives a job offer from Syracuse as QB coach — Terry Cooke from Syracuse Athletics wants her to start immediately, before the playoff push. Gerry has vanished after hearing Russ use his real voice in the bathroom. Russ and Danny conspire to discredit him by planting evidence in his locker; Danny improvises a 3D-printed gun instead of the planned steroids. The frame-up escalates: police get involved, ESPN breaks the story, and Gerry goes rogue, calling Chad to threaten Ricky ("you took what I love; I'll take who you love"). Gerry shows up at the Hudson house. In a parallel confrontation, Coach Hudson (Jake) confesses to Ricky that his heart condition forbids him from coaching — he lied about being medically cleared. The episode ends with Danny texting "Gotcha" after apparently capturing Gerry.

### Important Character Actions
- **Danny**: Fired as mascot, loses scholarship; plants 3D-printed gun in Gerry's locker (off-plan); captures Gerry at episode's end.
- **Russ**: Drives the frame-up to protect the Chad identity; clashes with Danny over Ricky's safety vs. self-preservation.
- **Ricky**: Offered Syracuse QB coach job; wrestles with leaving; learns her father's heart condition makes coaching life-threatening.
- **Coach Hudson (Jake)**: Reveals he cannot coach again per doctor's orders; has been lying to Ricky and the staff.
- **Gerry**: Discovers Chad's voice secret; goes off-grid, acquires 3D-printed gun (planted); threatens Ricky to hurt Chad; appears at Hudson house armed/unstable.
- **Coach Byrd**: Discovers the planted evidence; participates in the Gerry welfare check.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Terry Cooke** — Syracuse Athletics recruiter who offers Ricky the QB coach position.
- **"Zodiac manifesto"** — Coach Dobbs's description of the notebook found in Gerry's locker.
- **3D-printed gun** — The escalation device Danny substitutes for steroids; triggers police/ESPN involvement.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Ricky has a concrete offer to leave South Georgia for Syracuse (QB coach, immediate start).
- Coach Hudson's heart condition is real and disqualifying; he has been coaching against medical advice.
- Gerry knows Chad = Russ (heard the voice switch in the bathroom).
- Police + ESPN are now investigating Gerry; the gun frame-up is a felony-level complication.
- Danny is no longer the mascot ("Whiskers") and has lost his scholarship.
- Chad was planning to stay another year at South Georgia before NFL entry.
- Texas game next week: win-or-die for playoff berth.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **"Butt brother" / "屁股兄弟"** — Danny's term for Chad (Sasha the mascot intern uses it; Danny told not to).
- **"New York Times branzino"** — Danny's mocking nickname for Russ via text.
- **"Deer-like panic" / "小鹿般的惊恐"** — Danny's self-description while circling the block.
- **"Permanent full moon over our lives"** — Coach Hudson's metaphor for the cascading chaos.
- **Van Gogh "tee-tee" joke** — Tricia asks if Gerry cut off his ear/penis like "that painter" and put it in a lunch sack.
- **Beauty and the Beast** — Episode opens with the Angela Lansbury version playing diegetically.

### Unresolved Threads
- Gerry is captured by Danny — but is he arrested, released, or does he still hold the secret?
- Will Ricky accept the Syracuse job and leave before the Texas playoff game?
- Can Coach Hudson actually step away, or will he coach the playoff game despite his heart?
- The 3D-printed gun is a crime; police involvement threatens Russ, Danny, and Ricky by association.
- Russ's Cybertruck and public Russ Holliday sightings (Dobbs saw him) keep the identity risk high.
- The Texas game (playoff decider) looms next episode with QB uncertainty and coaching chaos.

## S02E02

## Chad Powers S02E02 Summary

### Episode Plot
Bye week at South Georgia College. Coach Hudson (Jake) plans to "ambush" Chad at his house to understand him better. Ricky warns Chad, who scrambles with Danny to fabricate excuses (fumigation, scorpion bite). Instead, Jake and Chad bond at the program's original 1950s field house—where Jake once sought counsel from Coach Muter during his first divorce. Jake opens up about failing his kids (Ricky and Chad); Chad reveals his mother was his football parent and died of cancer, doubting she'd be proud of him. Jake calls off the ambush, declaring Chad "a real person." Chad attends the senior cheerleaders' bye-week party, where a drunk Gerry confronts him with a photo of Ricky leaving Chad's house, accusing them of an affair. Officer Tom and Coach Hudson intervene; Jake covers by saying he sent Ricky to quiz Chad on new schemes. Gerry is forced to apologize and walk home. Ricky later tells Chad she can't be his friend anymore.

### Important Character Actions
- **Jake Hudson**: Initiates the house visit, pivots to genuine connection at the field house, shuts down the coaching staff's suspicion, protects Ricky publicly.
- **Ricky Hudson**: Warns Chad about her father, later ends their friendship after the party fallout.
- **Chad/Russ**: Vulnerable with Jake about his mother and self-worth; maintains disguise at party; slips into normal voice (Russ) on phone with Ricky.
- **Danny**: Manages disguise logistics (noses, wigs, mustaches), pushes Chad toward social integration.
- **Gerry**: Drunk, vengeful, "knows the secret" (misinterprets Ricky/Chad dynamic), photographs Ricky at Chad's house, humiliated by Jake.

### Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Batcave** — Chad's house (disguise workshop).
- **I Am Sam** — Dobbs's mocking nickname for Chad.
- **Bye-week party** — Annual senior-cheerleader tradition; "the reason people go to college."
- **Dry cleaner (upstairs)** — Gerry's residence; former owners "shot each other."

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Jake was OC at South Georgia earlier; Coach Muter used the old field house for heart-to-hearts.
- Jake brought there during first divorce; Ricky overheard adult arguments.
- Chad's mother drove him to practice; died of cancer (not asbestos).
