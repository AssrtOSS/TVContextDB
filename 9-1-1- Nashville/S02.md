## S02E01

Episode summary of 9-1-1: Nashville S02E01, based on the aligned subtitles and series context file:

## Plot

The cold open finds Cammie Raleigh dispatching a mass emergency at the Bennet Museum, where architect Henry Babel is unveiling his "Tower of Babel" at the Solomon Prize of Design ceremony. Giant murder hornets erupt from a museum display, the security doors slam shut, and dozens of guests are trapped. Station 113 lands on scene: Captain Don Hart kills the lights to lure the hornets toward the glow like a bug zapper, and the team extracts the survivors (three fatalities, black-tagged). Roxie, who confesses a crippling entomophobia, refuses to abandon a seven-months-pregnant guest, Onia, who suffers a toxin-induced seizure until the baby's kick confirms both survived. Don himself is stung — the stinger lodges in his jugular — and is only saved when a stranger directs the crew to his collarbone; they scrape the stinger out with a credit card and push epinephrine.

The stranger is Rogue Kelly, a new Metro PD homicide detective from New York. He treats the attack as deliberate: the museum's HVAC was cranked to the mid-80s (far above the display-safe range) to agitate the hornets, a display case was dragged into the escape path to trap the crowd, and the nest is stuffed with paper wads made of eucalyptus fiber — Bible paper — bearing the quote "I will send swarms upon you and upon your people and all your houses."

Parallel domestic threads: Blythe and Dixie clash in court-ordered anger management (Judge Lowe), where the therapist diagnoses their feud as displaced rage at Don; at home Blythe has moved Don into the guest room — a "bed divorce" — still wounded by his old "all in" devil-emoji text to Dixie. At the Hart family brunch, Ryan's wife Sam hides two secrets: she is pregnant and has cancer. Meanwhile Cammie talks Judy, a wheelchair-using new arrival, through a blood flood rising through the drains of 406 Calder Street. 113 forces the barricaded basement door and revives Judy after CPR and defibrillation. The blood turns out to be non-human — pig/cow/sheep blood drained from a neighboring butcher shop's blood-sausage tanks via a rerouted sewage line — and a second plague quote is found: "He turned their rivers into blood." Rogue declares it is only the beginning.

## Character actions

- Don Hart: runs the museum rescue, gets stung, survives via Rogue's observation; undergoes an "ambush physical" (A1C 6.1, elevated BP, possible sleep apnea); refuses GLP-1s; accepts the bed divorce, pledging loyalty to Blythe.
- Ryan Hart: leads radio/USAR calls, invites Rogue to brunch as "Harts hospitality," helps keep Sam's secret.
- Blue Bennings: full team member now — forces the stuck door, cooks the brunch steak after all-night tutorials.
- Roxie Alba: reveals entomophobia yet stays with the seizing pregnant patient under swarm attack.
- Taylor Thompson: brings diamond-tipped jaws, runs CPR with Roxie on Judy.
- Cammie Raleigh: coaches Judy through breaking the basement window with her wheelchair footrest; keeps dispatch calm throughout.
- Blythe Hart: files the bed divorce; faces Dixie in group therapy; still owns Dixie's recording contract (bought back by Dixie for $4M out of spite).
- Dixie Bennings: taunts Blythe in session, reasserting she had Don first ("girl code").
- Rogue Kelly: deduces the hornet release was staged; exposes the family's bed divorce by reading body language; takes over the blood scene; warns of more plagues.
- Sam: diagnosed pregnant with cancer, begs Ryan to delay announcing at brunch.

Rogue shares the Bible page in confidence; ninhydrin processing yields dozens of overlapping unidentifiable prints. Ryan later reassures Cammie by phone, but the Pharaoh then calls her directly, thanks her for saving the looksmaxxer survivors, and promises a bigger plague.
