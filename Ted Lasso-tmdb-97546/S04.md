## S04E09

# Ted Lasso S04E09 — Episode Summary

## Plot
- Richmond Women lose 0–1 at home to Southampton, a "toothless display" that drops them from title contenders to mere promotion hopefuls. Two games remain; a win in the next match clinches the final promotion spot.
- Star striker Gemma Jay is still out with a knee injury (6 weeks); Ted refuses to play her despite her being on the bench, using her as a decoy.
- Ted learns from the post-match press conference (where he fainted last episode) that his high blood pressure and possible pregnancy... wait, that's Keeley: at the doctor's urging she checks her period app and realizes her last period was 8 weeks ago.
- Keeley lunches with Rebecca and reveals she's pregnant (accidental — she'd been lax with her pill due to acne side effects). Rebecca is the first person she tells; she has NOT told Roy yet.
- Rebecca's takeover worries: Rupert's lawyers deny buying shares, but buying has paused ("he's plotting his next move"). Control hinges on the mysterious "Milk sisters," who've sold to Rupert before and can't be reached.
- Coaching staff devise a 4-4-2 diamond formation with the telepathic Harrigan twins (Siobhan and Niamh) stacked in midfield to compensate for missing Gemma.
- Keeley plans to skip the Bristol City match for her blood pressure; Roy stays behind to keep her from watching. Ted invites Mae Green to ride the team bus to Bristol.
- At Ashton Gate, Richmond fall 0–2 to Bristol City. Gemma Jay comes on, wins a penalty and converts (2–1), then feels a twinge and is subbed at halftime. Ted starts Shannon Stone, an over-thinking player he's been mentoring, for her season debut.
- Quinn (who insists on playing gloveless) makes key saves — including wearing gloves for the first time. The Harrigan twins combine for the equalizer (2–2), then captain Lizzie Davis heads Richmond ahead 3–2. In stoppage time, Shannon Stone completes a remarkable run from Quinn's long clearance to score the winner: Bristol City 2–4 Richmond. Richmond win promotion to the Women's Super League.
- Mid-match, Rebecca gets a call: her man Hugo has found where the Milk sisters live ("the Château de Milk"); she orders him to get their shares "whatever it takes."
- The episode ends in celebration with an ominous note: a security guard interrupts Rebecca — "there's been an incident... It's Ms. Green."

## Important Character Actions
- Ted: benches an impatient Gemma Jay, refuses to clear her; gives Shannon a pep talk ("you're too in here, bring it down here") and starts her in the biggest match; takes Henry to the pub and laments missing his games; handles the babysitting/handoff with Mae.
- Keeley: discovers her pregnancy, tells Rebecca first; secretly searches "will I be a good mum" via Siri; nearly cracks under Roy's embargo on watching the match.
- Roy: managers Keeley at home (keeps her calm, watches via absurd makeup nicknames for players); also negotiates his own coaching contract situation off-screen.
- Rebecca: hosts the Milk sisters' rep lunch (declines raw fish — hinting?); rejects sushi and orders the pursuit of their shares at any cost to block Rupert's 49%+ play.
- Gemma Jay: returns from injury ahead of schedule, scores the penalty on her comeback despite the knee twinge.
- Shannon Stone: comes off the bench, scores the dramatic winner, "returning home a hero."
- Mae: joins the team bus, gives a rousing pre-match speech about women once being barred from playing; requests to sit in the supporters' section (swapped with a fan named Suzi Campbell).

## S04E10

## Plot

Opens at Mae's wake inside The Crown & Anchor. Mae Green, longtime Richmond supporter and pub landlady, has died; the team mourns over drinks. Roy's agent phones in: Roy has been offered the Chelsea head coaching job. Keeley privately tells Roy she's pregnant (two months, not telling anyone yet).

The women's team has sold out Nelson Road for their final match vs. Crystal Palace. Ted gives a pre-match talk referencing Mae. Nate Shelley drops in, now coaching Poland's national team, with his baby daughter Athena. Captain Lizzie Davis arrives late with her kids dumped on her by her ex; teammate June helps watch them while the team runs a towel-folding race. Shannon Stone gets her first start for her childhood club.

At the shareholders meeting, Zelda Southport (women's sports financier) drops a dramatic reveal: she's been buying up Richmond shares through Hugo Riddles and now claims a controlling interest. Rebecca counters in three moves — Hugo Riddles defected and sold their block to Rebecca, Mae's will bequeaths her 2% stake to Rebecca, and the three pub regulars inherit the Crown & Anchor. Rebecca crosses 50% and keeps the club. Rebecca forgives Zelda with a revised scorpion fable — "It's in the scorpion's nature to sting; just as it's in my nature to save."

Richmond Women win in front of a packed Nelson Road. Roy confirms he's leaving for Chelsea. Ted tells Rebecca he wants to apply for the now-vacant Richmond manager job. Trent Crimm distributes copies of his book "The Richmond Way" to the team. Matthijs (nickname "Thijs"), Rebecca's longtime Dutch partner, proposes in her office — "Fuck it. Marry me." — with roses. Rebecca says yes. Henry presses Ted about how his grandfather died; Ted shares what Nana told him (fell down stairs, hit his head, around Ted's age) and promises Henry he'll re-prioritize next season.

A montage closes the season set to Mary Oliver's "When Death Comes," ending on an off-season huddle, "112 days until first match."
