## S03E01

## Plot

The episode opens with a cold open setting up the season arc: Congresswoman Hernandez has been kidnapped on American soil, her family murdered at the behest of China’s Ministry of State Security (MSS). CIA leadership discusses deploying a Lioness. A montage of fragmented dialogue references an ongoing mission to show Iran and China that the U.S. is not the only nation willing to operate across open borders, while Josie Carrillo’s father, Pablo, remains central to an active operation. Cruz is told she is “the part” of the mission, and someone warns they will soon “see how well you fly.”

The main storyline follows Joe McNamara as she runs a high-risk snatch operation in Ukraine. A FISU double agent who penetrated Russia’s SVR in 2018 approaches Kyle McManus at a bar called The Net, offering up Aleksi Yurimov—the head of SSD and “Putin’s hammer”—who is operating from a farmhouse accessed through a gas pipeline used to move Russian troops and equipment. Joe dispatches Cruz, now a Delta Force operator, to capture Yurimov alive as a potential bargaining chip. The ground team includes Delta operator Cody Spears (Taylor Sheridan) and Grady, a K9 handler, supported by Joe’s CIA QRF team (Bobby, Tucker, et al.).

The operation unfolds with a drone swarm and artillery prep (105s, 155s, 120mm mortars) before Delta operators and attack dogs breach the farmhouse through trenches, neutralize North Korean perimeter guards, and extract Yurimov while burning the remaining hostiles. During exfil, the team is chased by an unspecified high-tech threat—likely drone swarms or robotic systems—through smoke and artillery cover. Joe confirms positive identification (PID) of the target, and the team exfiltrates the country.

The episode closes on Joe’s home. Two individuals posing as state police arrive at her door late at night. Joe recognizes they are not law enforcement, demands badge numbers and a warrant, and confronts them outside. She calls the FBI, reporting, “I’m blown, and I have two down,” before the screen cuts to black.

## Important Character Actions

- **Joe** runs the Yurimov capture operation from the ground, confirms PID, and neutralizes two operatives who come to her home, indicating her cover has been compromised.
- **Cruz** operates fully integrated with Delta Force, breaching the target farmhouse alongside Cody and Grady, proving she has retained everything from her Force Recon training.
- **Cody Spears** leads the Delta assault, coordinating the drone strike, trench advance, and K9 deployment, and vouches for Cruz’s competence to the skeptical QRF.
- **Bobby and the QRF** provide overwatch and exfil defense but are largely sidelined by Delta operators who view them as amateurs untrained for this scale of warfare.
- **Grady**, a new Delta K9 handler, deploys dogs during both the breach and the exfil pursuit.
- **Byron Westfield and the CIA chain** set the strategic objective of capturing Yurimov for leverage rather than killing him, to avoid triggering Putin’s severe retaliation.

## Newly Established Names and Terms

- **Aleksi Yurimov** — head of SSD, described as “Putin’s hammer,” operating a frontline cell from a Ukrainian farmhouse.
- **SSD** — Russian special services department; Yurimov’s organization.
- **FISU** — foreign intelligence service unit; the double agent offering Yurimov belongs to it.
- **46** — an individual Kyle McManus was allegedly seen with in Kiev and at the Ukrainian embassy; identity and significance unclear.
- **North Korean perimeter guards** — provide outer security around the pipeline position; motivated but tactically poor, with failing comms integration.
- **Gas pipeline** — Russian forces have tunneled into it to ferry troops and equipment for a planned offensive.
- **PID** — positive identification; Joe confirms Yurimov’s identity post-capture.
- **SBU Alpha** — Ukrainian Security Service special unit providing parallel overwatch for the operation.
- **“Your father’s the mission now”** — Pablo Carrillo’s status as a target or asset continues into S03.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Cruz has fully transitioned from Force Recon and the Lioness program to Delta Force, operating on equal footing with Cody.
- The QRF team remains Joe’s direct-action and extraction unit but is increasingly outgunned by the scale of near-peer warfare in Ukraine.
- Josie Carrillo’s father, Pablo, remains a live thread; his capture or value as an asset is described as how he survives.
- Chinese MSS is directly implicated in the killing of U.S. officials on American soil, escalating the cartel-diplomatic nexus from S02.
- Joe and Neal’s domestic life continues as a counterpoint to her operational world; their daughters Kate and Charlie appear briefly at breakfast.
- Bobby and a handler (possibly his ex Tracer, implied by the intimate exchange about getting shot) maintain a personal relationship across agency lines.

## Jokes and References

- “There’s Tier One and there’s these fuckers” — Delta operator dismissing CIA QRF’s relevance in Ukraine.
- “It’s like World War I and Star Wars slammed into each other” — description of drone-and-trench warfare in Ukraine.
- “Love their macho bullshit” / “I fucking love it” — Cody on the Delta team’s own performative masculinity.
- “You never get nervous about me getting shot, Bobbo.” / “’Cause you always get fucking shot.” — exchange between Bobby and a handler, implying a long personal history.
- “Ain’t the border, babies, this some big boy shit right here” — Delta operator on the scale of the Ukraine operation.
- “We may miss the fight tomorrow, but we ain’t missing the fight” — QRF accepting their support role.
- The fake cops scene ends with Joe’s threat: “You’re not the police, but you’re two minutes from meeting them.”

## Unresolved Threads

- **Joe is blown.** Two operatives (likely SVR, MSS, or cartel-affiliated) have located her home. She neutralizes them, but the sender and their intelligence source remain unknown.
- **Yurimov’s value.** Capturing him is described as removing Putin’s queen from the chessboard, but no explicit trade or interrogation outcome is shown.
- **Congresswoman Hernandez.** The kidnapping that opens the episode is not resolved; a Lioness deployment is ordered but not shown.
- **Kyle McManus in Kiev.** His connection to “46” and the Ukrainian embassy is mentioned by the FISU double agent but not explored.
- **Pablo Carrillo.** His role as “the mission” continues, with no resolution on whether he is captive, cooperating, or being targeted.
- **The high-tech threat on exfil.** The team is chased by an unspecified system—drones, robots, or autonomous weapons—that is never fully identified, suggesting a new battlefield reality that will recur.

## S03E02

### Plot

The episode opens on a recap of the Yurimov pitch ("You'd be taking Putin's queen off the chessboard") and drops straight into the fallout of E01's cliffhanger. Two men posing as Virginia state police come to Joe's home; she refuses to step outside, they kick in the door and rush her, and she kills both. Before the fight, they strip-search her for an implanted locator — she insists the CIA doesn't implant its people ("Airport scanners pick them up"). In the aftermath the only item recovered from the attackers is a locator, which Kaitlyn reads as a deliberate message: "Maybe it's their way of saying we overplayed ours."

Joe's tactical response team (SRT) and the FBI sweep the neighborhood and roll up the Russian observation team: a driver and two spotters on a rooftop. The spotter is taken down, Bobby draws out the driver with a hat and he's killed ("Driver's down"), and two spotters are captured as PUCs and held at Langley. Kaitlyn orders the "thinkers" — the team from Camp Peary — flown in ("You shake that tree until you find her") and tells Byron that captured spies always break: "When she breaks, who knows what spills out?"

Neal confronts Kaitlyn on the driveway ("What happened to my wife?!"), gets 90 seconds, and is told to tell the kids nothing. The family is moved to a house on Camp Peary — "Last resident was a four-star." Kate and Charlie are traumatized ("I'm pretty fucking not okay"), guarded by a QRF operator. A flashback shows Joe's first night home: Neal's birthday-suit joke, her monologue about $90 Amazon drones making their work obsolete, and their sex interrupted by a duty call.

At Langley, Byron lays out the timeline: March 4, a Ukrainian double agent pitched Yurimov's location; Friday March 14, the snatch succeeded. Yurimov sits in a Jordan facility awaiting transport to Gitmo, with war-crimes prosecution (plus the Wagner Group attacks in Syria) as legal options. The working theory on how Joe was made: a visual at Boryspil airport, AI-cross-referenced against Russian data hauls from the Shadow Brokers (2017) and SolarWinds (2020) attacks. Joe pushes back with Occam's razor: the Ukrainian double agent "hooked us" — her guess is Yurimov is still on the Russian front and they're holding "some actor from the fucking Mossovet Theatre." Kaitlyn says she's investigating that possibility.

Byron confronts Russian official Maxim at a dinner (Kaitlyn present, on her fourth champagne). Byron refuses any trade for the captured spotters, threatens embassy deportations and feeding them to "the feral hogs at Gitmo," and offers Russia a face-saving way out: pull troops out of Eastern Ukraine. Maxim counters that it's "a two-way street." Meanwhile a new strategic track surfaces: Venezuela, holder of the world's largest proven oil reserves, where U.S. resources are skeletal after 12 years of inactivity — "fish on the hook is the goal."

The QRF splits: Cruz and a partner are dispatched to Kyle McManus ("I need you and Cruz to head to Kyle"), the rest stay with Joe's family. At a Washington gala (the Secretary of Energy among the guests), someone who changed phone numbers to avoid contact is cornered and dragged outside by a persistent figure demanding they talk. Joe arrives at Camp Peary to find Neal drinking at the bar; the episode ends with her apologizing — "I'm so sorry" — as the family breaks down together.

### Important Character Actions

- **Joe** kills the two intruders in front of her children, then fights the system's read on her exposure: she argues the Ukrainian double agent ran a provocation and that the captured "Yurimov" may be a decoy. She apologizes to Neal at Camp Peary.
- **Kaitlyn** orders the Camp Peary team in, delivers the "everybody breaks" argument, leads the Langley debrief on champagne, and quietly commits to checking the double-agent theory.
- **Byron** presents the operation timeline and plays hardball with Maxim: no trade, deportation threats, Gitmo threats, and a demand to pull Russian troops out of Eastern Ukraine.
- **Maxim** (Russian SSD/SVR representative) claims to know what the U.S. took and "who took it with her" — implicating Joe — and promises retaliation: "You tried to touch us here? It's a two-way street, buddy."
- **Neal** confronts Kaitlyn, grills a team member ("You work with my wife?"), and ends the episode drinking alone at the Camp Peary bar before the reconciliation.
- **Bobby and the QRF** capture the observation team, run the takedown while posing as FBI ("Badges out, boys. The whole world's coming"), and guard the family — "Welcome to black side."
- **Cruz** is sent with a partner to rendezvous with Kyle McManus, signalling a new tasking.
- **A four-star general at Camp Peary** tells Joe her time is up in the field: "Someday, you're gonna move to a desk. Station chief, maybe. It's time."

## S03E03

**S03E03 — Special Ops: Lioness**

## Plot

The cold open recaps the fallout of the home attack: the only thing recovered from the dead Russians is a locator, Neal's "What happened to my wife?!", and Meade's warning to Westfield that "when she breaks, who knows what spills out?" The team then snatches a woman off a train — "Olga Balakin?" — who answers in Ukrainian and is terrified. At the farm safehouse, Joe interrogates her, convinced she leaked Joe's identity to the SVR: two Russian contract agents with restraints hit Joe's front door 48 hours after the Yurimov extraction. The grilling is brutal (family threats, "Boys... get a finger"), and Oleksandra explodes with genuine anti-Russia fury, even attacking Tucker. It was a vetting: her father's recognition ("My Oleksandra?") is the proof — "We needed proof she is who she says she is, and you were the proof." Joe lays out the play: the SVR still thinks Yurimov is in Ukraine, so Oleksandra stays under and tells them she has him — "all the hornets come out."

At home, Neal and the girls ask Joe to quit; she refuses with a soldier's-oath speech ("a soldier's job is to fight until the soldier is unable to fight"). She offers Neal something framed as "an insurance policy on the car"; he won't take it. "I'm sorry this followed me home... It means finding the rest of them."

At Langley, Joe argues the attack was timing, not a leak: the SVR learned the package was gone and ran two-man teams at every known case officer, "shaking the trees." Yurimov is confirmed at Gitmo — "a big prize, too big to use as bait." Meade: "We'll make her give us another one."

Kate and Charlie get Randy and Two Cups as babysitters; Kate tries to skip school for Fair Oaks Mall; a staged-looking road incident forces evasive driving (the girls beg to slow down), and Kate grills the operators in an elevator: "Never lie to strangers. Only lie to people close to you."

At the farm briefing, Meade reveals Oleksandra is "the Lioness who befriended al-Zarqawi's third wife" and fed his location to Special Forces for the airstrike. Oleksa gives the wolf-pack/tiger/bear lecture ("disease kills the bear") and explains honeypots; a new analyst, Amber, confirms and lectures on the pyramid of clandestine ops against the U.S. Joe: "We're big-game hunting" — NSA analysts, senators, governors, news anchors. Kyle's nods confirm Oleksandra's asset list: NSA, JSOC, a Senator, plus two more. Joe wants to out them all; Kyle warns "the sky falls" but hints at another way. Oleksandra goes into unofficial custody.

That evening, Joe pitches Meade and Westfield: announce the catch via the press secretary — no names, a posture of knowing everything ("What they imagine we know will be much more than what we have actually ascertained"). Meade adds the trap: release Oleksandra and stay close — "hunting mice by setting the barn on fire." Joe: "At the end of that, you have no barn." She still won't accept the leak theory — "Either she did it or she knows who did it... Let me sleep on it." Meade to Kyle: "He's already decided. You don't sleep on a 'no.'" The episode ends with the press secretary announcing the FBI counterintelligence arrest of an SVR agent.

## Important character actions

- **Oleksandra ("Oleksa")** survives the interrogation without breaking, claws at Tucker when called a Russian, briefs the team on SVR tradecraft, and is tasked with baiting her own service.
- **Meade** delivers the al-Zarqawi reveal, the "barn on fire" plan, and the cold calculus: "We let her use us. We used her right back."
- **Westfield** resists reaching out to Russia first (it concedes leverage), then clears the statement.
- **Kyle** confirms the asset list by counting his own nods (five) and proposes "running the rats out of the outhouse" as an alternative to public exposure.
- **Neal** asks Joe to quit and refuses her money.
- **Randy and Two Cups** run the girls' detail, handle the road incident, and trade banter with Kate.
- **Amber** (new) vouches for Oleksandra's claims and lectures on foreign-influence methodology.
- **Press secretary** delivers the no-names SVR arrest statement.

## Episode Plot
The episode intercuts two timelines: **present day** (Joe McNamara kidnapped by Russian SVR, 5 days into the search) and **six months earlier** (Joe's home life and an operation targeting Russian assets in the US).

**Present:** Russian operatives abduct Joe in Baltimore. The team (Kaitlyn, Byron, Kyle, Bobby, QRF) tracks the kidnappers to a hangar at a regional airfield. Kyle executes a captured Russian spy to extract the tail number of the getaway plane: a Challenger 300 (N-565-KJ) that departed Bahía de los Ángeles, Mexico, bound for an unknown destination with transponder off. Range: 3,400 nm — puts Cuba, Mexico, Venezuela in play. The SVR intends to trade Joe for Yurimov, a Russian officer captured by the US in Ukraine, but the US fears Russia will hand Joe to Iran for a propaganda war-crimes trial.

**Six months earlier:** Joe prepares Neal and their daughters for the reality of her work, forcing Neal to firearms training. The FBI publicly announces the arrest of a "Russian spy" — a deliberate ploy to destabilize SVR networks. Joe briefs her team on **Operation Trésor**: use the publicity to flush Russian assets, insert a Lioness agent near compromised targets, and flip them for their SVR handler identities. Joe makes contact with **Charles Robbins** (Northrop Grumman sales) at a bar — "CONTACT ESTABLISHED." Meanwhile, Byron meets Russian deputy ambassador **Maxim**, who reveals Russia wants US help recovering something valuable stolen from the Russian president.
