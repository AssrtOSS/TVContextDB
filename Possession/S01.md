# S01E01 — Possession

## Episode plot
The cold open establishes the stakes at Hope Hill, the old sugar-plantation estate in St. Ann parish, Jamaica: the late tycoon Alfred Connaught disinherited his son Oliver and left everything (a £50 million inheritance) to a Jamaican national, Cudjoe East. Housekeeper Angelo warns Claudia about a mysterious trunk ("Juju. Protection… if you see it, don't touch it… you're not safe"), then is found dead — officially a suicide, though Cudjoe insists he was lynched the day Claudia arrived.

Cudjoe storms into the lawyers' office (George Delal's firm) and refuses to give the inheritance back "ever." Claudia tries to trace him — a police contact (Patsy Dawkins) refuses to run his plate; a phone tip places him selling at the Jonkunnoo Festival. There she confronts him: he claims he was born enslaved on Hope Hill in 1733, is 292 years old, that Alfred wanted to "right the wrongs of the past" as reparations, and that he intends to redistribute the money to his people. He warns her: don't play with things you don't understand; stay away from the Connaught family and the house.

Claudia and Oliver investigate Alfred's past and find an 1838 British government letter — addressed to Claudia's seventh great-grandfather, "formerly property of Charlotte Connaught," valued at £30 each (£10,000 total) — documenting that slave owners, not slaves, were compensated after emancipation (£20M total, repaid by taxpayers only in 2015). Alfred kept the letter locked in a drawer with handling gloves: he cared about it.

At night Claudia and Oliver are trapped in Hope Hill House: power keeps failing, doors won't open, the landline is dead, and a figure (Cudjoe?) watches from outside. They smash a window; Claudia badly cuts her hand. Oliver recounts the old housekeeper Sabrine, who claimed the family was evil and that Oliver "had the devil in him" (Obeah), and performed salt-and-rum rituals against "bad energy." Oliver half-jokingly proposes marriage. The episode ends with them trapped inside, as a radio report confirms Angelo Corey's death was ruled suicide.

## Important character actions
- Claudia McKenzie: mixed-race British lawyer (white British / Black Caribbean); investigates Cudjoe, confronts him, finds the 1838 letter, dismisses him as "a chancer" — then ends the episode trapped and bleeding in the house.
- Cudjoe East: the mysterious heir; claims immortality and enslavement at Hope Hill; accuses Claudia over Angelo's death; refuses to surrender the inheritance; warns her off; later appears watching the house.
- Oliver Connaught: disinherited son; wants to fight the will; fears failing his son Reggie; bonds with Claudia over drinks; jokes about marrying her.
- Angelo Corey: beloved community leader and reparations activist; found dead at Hope Hill (suicide ruling contested by Cudjoe); had warned Claudia about the trunk.
- Mercy Stevens: Claudia's aunt (her mother's sister), a priestess with a balm yard in Port Royal; tracks Claudia down and says their ancestors are reaching out to her.
- Historical timeline (1733 onward): the dying planter wants the enslaved Matthew Desfriene to run Hope Hill as a freeman (£100/year); overseer O'Connell and the planter's daughter ("the miss") oppose him; Flora's tongue is cut out ("a liar has no need of a tongue") yet she lives; Sarah is accused of witchcraft/Obeah, her daughter Abi is sold, and she is shot; Matthew is arrested under a new anti-Obeah edict. The miss is later courted by a Bristol man who urges her to abandon the island after the rebellion ("Tacky may be dead, but he inspired revolt all across the island").

## Newly established names and terms
- Hope Hill (House), St. Ann, Jamaica — the plantation estate; the Connaught family seat.
- Alfred Connaught (deceased tycoon) and Charlotte Connaught (18th-century owner of Claudia's ancestor).
- Cudjoe East; Claudia McKenzie; Oliver Connaught; Reggie (Oliver's son); Tom (Claudia's father); Mercy Stevens; George Delal; Patsy Dawkins; Angelo Corey.
- Historical figures: Matthew Desfriene, Flora, Sarah, Abi, Mr O'Connell, the "miss" (planter's daughter), Sabrine (former housekeeper).
- Terms: Obeah; zombi (Cudjoe's definition: a puppet that can't rest or die until the white massa allows it, working long after its body shrivels); balm yard; Nine Night; Jonkunnoo Festival; juju; the trunk (cursed object).
- The 1838 compensation letter — slavery-reparations history (owners compensated £20M; repaid by taxpayers until 2015).

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Cudjoe: born enslaved at Hope Hill, 1733; 292 years old; will hand the money to his people; claims Angelo was murdered.
- Flora (grandmother figure): "He may be your father, but I am your mother's mother. And you're just as black as me" — implying Cudjoe may be Claudia's father and tying her lineage to Hope Hill.
- Claudia's ancestor was enslaved by Charlotte Connaught; Alfred and Cudjoe met to discuss "your family history" (per Angelo), and Alfred's will reads as family-specific reparations.
- The zombi speech frames the show's mythology: the enslaved dead of Hope Hill are bound until released — a lens for Cudjoe, Flora, and the "witch" figures who survive mutilation and death.
- Tongue motif: the planter dies of tongue cancer ("a week, at best"); Flora's tongue is cut yet she lives in good health; she is rumored to have cursed the father — the "nag whose tongue was cut… she lives."
- Mercy's claim: nothing happens without the ancestors' knowledge or authority.

## Jokes and references
- Mercy deflates her own mysticism: "You left your location tag on" — Claudia: "You're sick." "It's a joke, man! Lighten up."
- Oliver's failed proposal: "Shall we get married?" — "Wasn't that funny. OK. Sorry."
- Claudia: "Do you think I'm a pussy?" / Oliver: "Absolutely not! … Maybe a teeny-weeny bit."
- Salt and rum rituals: "That woman got through a lot of salt. And rum, funnily enough."
- Needle drops: "You Make Me Feel So Young," Diana King's "Shy Guy," "9 Lives," and the folk song "Me Donkey Want Water" ("Hold him Joe… don't you let him go") — the refrain foreshadowing the "don't let it go" dynamic of the curse.
- George's "You're not going to believe this. Cudjoe East just walked into my office."

## Unresolved threads
- The trunk: what it is, what's inside, and why opening it means "you're not safe."
- Angelo's death: suicide vs. lynching — Cudjoe insists murder.
- Cudjoe's true nature: immortal former slave, ghost, or con man? Will he keep the £50M? Oliver vows he won't get it back "ever."
- Who or what trapped Claudia and Oliver in the house (Cudjoe, or a spirit?) and what Claudia's cut hand sets in motion — the series title suggests possession is coming.
- Claudia's parentage (is Cudjoe her father?), Flora's present-day role, and why Alfred cared about Claudia's family history.
- Mercy's warnings and the role of Claudia's mother in summoning her; Sabrine's old claims about the family's evil.
- The fates of Matthew Desfriene, Sarah, and Abi, and how the planter's daughter connects to Charlotte Connaught.

## Possession — S01E05 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode interweaves two timelines: **1800s Jamaica** (Hope Hill plantation) and **modern-day Britain**, connected by a generational curse of possession and a reparations legacy.

**Historical timeline (1800s):**
- **Matthew / Cudjoe (Kojo)** — an enslaved Fante man on Hope Hill plantation, literate (taught by his father), not an obeah-man despite accusations. His grandmother called him Kojo.
- **Flora** — an obeah woman who can shapeshift (turns into a mosquito). She teaches that spirits remain nine nights in a body before the *duppy* (ghost) departs.
- **Charlotte** — the "white witch" who governs Hope Hill. She works slaves to death, then continues enslaving them as *zombi* (eternal slaves). She possesses **Oliver** (a white man of the plantation class).
- **Mercy** — an enslaved woman connected to Cudjoe; she invokes Saint Michael for protection.
- To end Charlotte's possession of Oliver, they need **Cudjoe's tooth** to destroy the binding. Cudjoe is ultimately killed/betrayed ("buried in the dirt where he belongs").

**Modern timeline:**
- **Oliver** — a British man, father of **Reggie** (8), married to **Claudia**. He appears to be **possessed by Charlotte** (the historical witch). The possessed Oliver is calculating, ruthless, and admits to being a "willing host" — he wants Reggie and his inheritance, and doesn't fight Charlotte because "we want the same things."
- **Claudia McKenzie** — Oliver's wife, a solicitor. She goes missing (arrested for attacking her client), then reappears at Oliver's funeral. She is also entangled with the possession (Charlotte speaks through her at times).
- **Tom McKenzie** — Claudia's father, desperate to find his daughter. He pressures the British embassy (£1.6M reparations payment from Oliver's father's will is revealed).
- **Sarah** — a detective investigating Claudia's disappearance.
- **Reggie** — Oliver and Claudia's son, traumatized after Oliver pointed a gun at him.
- **Oliver dies** (ruled "accidental death by fire"). At his funeral, the possessed Oliver/Claudia dynamic plays out: Charlotte (via Oliver) tries to take Reggie and the inheritance; Mercy/Claudia resists.
- The episode ends with **Flora's spirit** (as a mosquito) flying east over Port Maria, across the ocean, back to Africa — "I'll wait for you there."

## S01E03

## Possession S01E03 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode interweaves **historical (1733-era) and modern timelines**. In the past, **Charlotte Connaught**—unhappy in a contractual marriage to a gambler husband—studies European ritual magic (a Latin/German/Greek grimoire) with **Mr. Waite**, a British Museum board member. She learns of Obeah's power, including a woman transforming into mosquitoes to attack white people. Charlotte later curses **Cudjoe** (born enslaved at Hope Hill, 1733), rendering him immortal.

In the present, **Oliver** is violently possessed at Hope Hill. He vomits, attacks Claudia, and speaks of a "zombie" encounter—repeating a conversation from minutes prior. Claudia believes **Cudjoe** is an Obeah-man punishing them for ancestral crimes. A community elder clarifies: Oliver is possessed by **Charlotte**, not Obeah. The spirit was **contained in a sealed trunk/box in the bedroom**; someone opened it and released her. **Angelo** (who bore the same finger scar as Oliver) died—ostensibly suicide, but Charlotte compelled it.

Claudia is arrested back in Bristol. Oliver gives a statement accusing her of hitting him with a tin can and claiming Cudjoe beat him with a poker. Claudia calls her father, warning him to contact the Jamaican embassy if she doesn't reach him in 24 hours. In custody, she hears a woman whispering *"It burns."* Meanwhile, Cudjoe's vehicle is located; police consider him armed and dangerous. Oliver's father (**Mr. Connaught**) appears at the station to secure Claudia's release. During a tense meeting, Oliver—confused, memory-fragmented—calls Claudia **"Matthew,"** then denies it, suggesting ongoing possession or identity fracture.

---

### Important Character Actions
- **Charlotte (historical):** Studies grimoire, demands a child, curses Cudjoe with immortality.
- **Cudjoe:** Claims 292 years of life; hunted by police; tells Oliver "zombie" means loss of self, outliving everyone you love.
- **Oliver:** Possessed by Charlotte; attacks Claudia; gives false police statement; calls Claudia "Matthew"; doesn't recall the assault.
- **Claudia:** Defends herself and Oliver; arrested for aiding/abetting + GBH; invokes embassy failsafe.
- **Mr. Connaught (Oliver's father):** Intervenes legally to free Claudia; motives ambiguous.
- **Angelo (deceased):** Community leader; knew the trunk's secret; died by Charlotte-induced suicide.

## S01E04


## S01E04 — Episode Summary

### Episode Plot

Claudia's situation escalates: she phones her father Tom, instructing him to call the Jamaican embassy if she goes silent for 24 hours. She is arrested on suspicion of aiding and abetting a criminal but released after the DPP approves it. George Delal phones to say Cudjoe has walked into his office to return the entire £50 million estate — and Claudia races to stop him from signing, arguing he lacks autonomy.

Tom arrives in Jamaica. Claudia seeks out a local spiritual healer who identifies Cudjoe's condition as genuine possession — "duppy wid skin" — not Obeah, and not a con. The healer proposes a spirit journey: Claudia must cross over, meet the ancestors, and ask for guidance. The journey is dangerous ("I see it happen once… it's enough"), and the healer charges escalating fees (J$500,000, then J$1 million) while preparing Claudia by braiding her hair with castor oil and an ancestor's tooth.

During the spirit journey, Claudia enters a historical scene: her mother **Stacy-Ann** (Stace) has just given birth. The baby is named Claudia (the father liked "Esi," an African name, but Claudia's mother insists). Stacy-Ann develops postpartum complications — stitches painfully inflamed, breasts bruised purple — but doctors dismiss her concerns and leave. She hemorrhages and dies, repeatedly whispering "It burns" — the same phrase Claudia heard whispered in her cell in E03. The ancestors present Claudia with the answer she sought: she needs Cudjoe's tooth — the one around the duppy's neck — to destroy the possession.

Claudia and Mercy travel to Hope Hill at night to confront Cudjoe. Claudia sends Mercy to keep watch while she enters the house. Inside she finds Cudjoe in distress; she tries to reach him ("It's me. Look at me."). Oliver then appears — possessed — and attacks. The episode ends in chaos: Mercy screams, Oliver lunges, and someone cries for help repeatedly.

### Important Character Actions

- **Claudia**: Arrested and released; races to stop Cudjoe signing away the inheritance; consults the spiritual healer; undergoes the spirit journey; resolves to take Cudjoe's tooth to end the possession; enters Hope Hill alone.
- **Mercy**: Accompanies Claudia to the healer; participates in the ancestor-calling ritual; agrees to help ("If a dis Stacy want, wi ah gon do it… but wi ah gon do it my way"); present at Hope Hill during the confrontation.
- **Tom McKenzie**: Flies to Jamaica to find Claudia; skeptical of the supernatural ("There's no such thing as ghosts") but accepts Cudjoe saved her life.
- **Oliver (possessed)**: Appears at Hope Hill near the end, attacks, and warns Claudia: "You really shouldn't have been drawn into this. He's not the man you think he is."
- **Cudjoe**: In possession-related distress at Hope Hill; Claudia tries to reach him before Oliver intervenes.
- **George Delal**: Contacts Claudia about Cudjoe's decision to surrender the inheritance.

### Newly Established Names and Terms

- **Stacy-Ann (Stace)** — Claudia's mother, who died of postpartum medical neglect in a Bristol hospital.
- **"It burns"** — Stacy-Ann's dying phrase; echoes the whisper Claudia heard in her E03 cell; now tied to Claudia's mother.
- **Spirit journey** — a ritual crossing-over to meet the ancestors for guidance; can be fatal.
- **"Duppy wid skin"** — the healer's term for a possessed person (a duppy wearing a living body).
- **"Ibaye, ibaye"** — the calling phrase used to invoke ancestors.
- **Mama's toolbox** — a container holding ancestor teeth used in rituals; one of the teeth is used in the preparation for the spirit journey.
- **"Know what burns"** — the ancestors' phrase during the spirit journey, suggesting they understand Claudia's pain.
- **"You meet your ancestors clean… and you meet them neat"** — Stacy-Ann's saying, motivating the ritual preparation.
- **George Delal** — the Jamaican lawyer handling Cudjoe's inheritance paperwork.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Claudia's mother died because medical staff ignored her postpartum symptoms — the "It burns" whisper from E03 is her mother's voice, not Charlotte's.
- The ancestors' dreams began for Claudia when she took the inheritance job, not after arriving in Jamaica — the healer notes this crisis is "longer than you think."
- **Cudjoe's tooth** is the physical anchor of Charlotte's possession; destroying it will end the possession.
- The healer knew about Claudia's dreams before being told — her grandmother was also a priestess.
- Claudia's father Tom is skeptical but supportive; he flew to Jamaica despite his doubts.
- The £50 million inheritance is again at stake — Cudjoe was about to surrender it all back.
- Oliver's possession is worsening: he appears at Hope Hill and warns Claudia away from Cudjoe.

### Jokes and References Useful for Later Episodes

- **"Esi" vs. "Claudia"** — the naming scene: Tom wanted an African name (Esi); Stacy-Ann rejected it ("I'm not calling my daughter 'Easy'"). This establishes the tension between African heritage and British assimilation.
- **Mercy's escalating fees** — she starts at J$500,000 ("That's like two grand!"), then demands J$1 million when Claudia asks a question during the ritual ("You rushed me. Put me at risk. Pay double time").
- **"My own mother was a priestess"** — the healer's revelation that Claudia's grandmother was a priestess who crossed over many times, establishing a matrilineal spiritual lineage.
- **"You left your location tag on"** — Mercy's running joke about technology undermining mysticism.
- **The rice-in-hair legend** — Mercy braids Claudia's hair while telling how enslaved women wove rice into their hair to survive the Middle Passage.

### Unresolved Threads

1. **What happens to Cudjoe's tooth?** — Claudia went to take it; the episode ends mid-confrontation.
2. **Oliver's warning about Cudjoe** — "He's not the man you think he is" — what does the possessed Oliver know or want Claudia to believe?
3. **Mercy's fate** — she screams as Oliver attacks at Hope Hill; her condition is unknown.
4. **Cudjoe's condition** — is he still possessed, or was the spirit journey beginning to help before Oliver intervened?
5. **The DPP's release** — why was Claudia released? Who pulled strings, and why?
6. **The ancestors' full message** — "We have had council. Know what burns" — what else did the ancestors decide?
7. **Tom McKenzie's role** — he's in Jamaica but absent from the Hope Hill confrontation; what is he doing?
8. **The healer's past** — "I see it happen once" — whose spirit journey killed them?
