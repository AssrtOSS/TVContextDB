# Possession translation context

## Identity

- Exact work: *Possession*, a 2026 British Sky Original supernatural thriller limited series.
- TMDB TV: 279089.
- First aired: 6 August 2026; Season 1 has 5 episodes.
- This is the Sky series set between Bristol and Jamaica. Do not confuse it with the 2025 Japanese film *Stigmatized Properties: Possession*, the 1981 film *Possession*, or any other work whose title contains “Possession”.
- Official sources: https://www.sky.com/watch/possession and https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/279089-possession

## Premise and setting

The story follows Claudia McKenzie, a mixed-race British lawyer who travels to Jamaica to challenge an inheritance claim filed by Cudjoe East against the wealthy Connaught family. The central location is Hope Hill, a former plantation in Jamaica. The series moves between contemporary Bristol/Jamaica and historical plantation-era Jamaica, combining inheritance, slavery reparations, family history, Obeah, spirits, and possession.

## Established names and terms

- Claudia McKenzie — British solicitor/lawyer investigating the inheritance and Hope Hill.
- Cudjoe East — the mysterious Jamaican claimant and alleged heir; he says he was born enslaved at Hope Hill in 1733 and is connected to the estate’s hidden history.
- Oliver Connaught — the disinherited Connaught heir and Claudia’s partner/husband; father of Reggie.
- Reggie — Oliver and Claudia’s child.
- Tom McKenzie — Claudia’s father.
- Mercy Stevens — Claudia’s aunt, a spiritual/priestess figure linked to the family’s ancestors.
- Angelo Corey — a Hope Hill community leader whose death is disputed.
- Flora — an important historical and spiritual figure.
- Charlotte Connaught — the historical antagonist associated with Hope Hill and possession.
- Hope Hill — the Connaught plantation estate in Jamaica.
- Obeah, duppy, zombi, juju, balm yard, and Nine Night — retain Caribbean cultural meaning and do not flatten them into generic horror terms.
- The inheritance and reparations history are central themes; historical references to slavery and compensation require precise, serious wording.

## Translation guidance

Keep the distinction between the modern British/Jamaican storyline and the historical plantation timeline. Preserve names consistently across episodes. Claudia’s legal register, Cudjoe’s Jamaican cultural and historical register, family dialogue, and supernatural/ritual speech should remain distinct. Do not import characters, plot, or terminology from the Japanese film *Stigmatized Properties: Possession*.
