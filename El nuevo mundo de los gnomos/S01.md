## Plot
The mayor of Nompton, Lachlan Morrison, has mortgaged the entire council budget to import a museum-piece "Gnome Queen" statue from Germany as the centrepiece of an upcoming Gnome Festival that Channel Six's Brant Sanders is covering live. Overnight, the Gnome Queen disappears along with dozens of ordinary garden gnomes from across town, lights explode inside one local house, and pets and livestock (Barb's favourite chicken Melba; Arnold's daughter's gnome Mr Fluffy) are attacked. Police officer Arnold Kipps, his colleagues Josie and Devon, and assigned observer Evelyn Lopossie (Ellie) McKay investigate, while the mayor pressures Arnold to recover the statue before the broadcast. A town-hall meeting of parents deflects blame onto the local teens (Jenny, Dylan, Zayn Falkner). Arnold's evening at the town hall ends with an apparent blackout/earthquake and alcohol on his breath; he wakes unable to remember the night but insists the Gnome Queen was still there when he left. The theft appears connected to Mr Henrickson, whose car is parked near the rental store VideOzone.

## Important character actions
- Arnold Kipps lies to the mayor about where he was, hides evidence (overdue DVDs), and blacks out at the hall the night of the crime.
- Mayor Lachlan Morrison confesses to Arnold that she embezzled the entire council budget to buy the Gnome Queen, and threatens Arnold's career if the statue is not recovered before Brant Sanders' live broadcast.

- Josie pushes back against prioritising the Gnome Queen over the wider rash of vandalism, then agrees to door-knock for leads.

- Mr Henrickson admits he has "been hanging around somewhere" while the Gnome Queen went missing; Arnold and Ellie decide to confront him.
