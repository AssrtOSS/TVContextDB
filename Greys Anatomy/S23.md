## S23E04

# Love on the Brain — S23E04 剧情总结

## 主线剧情

**Meredith / Bailey 线（VIP 病房与 MEN1 诊断）**：Bailey 的助教（TA）在她的研究提案笔记上只写了 "mid"（平庸），打击了她。她的病人 Lillian Kemp（莉莲·坎普）因反复严重上腹痛、顽固性恶心呕吐再次入院，内科全面检查无果；其子女（Khadijah、Amari 等）态度强势施压。家人透露他们并非有钱人，是法律助理和电工，凑积蓄住进 VIP 病房。Meredith 不愿放弃，追问家族史后（姐姐 Cici 肾结石+胃溃疡、哥哥视力下降+低血糖、表弟 Bobby 慢性腹泻）怀疑多发性内分泌腺瘤（MEN），安排基因检测和 PET 扫描，最终在十二指肠发现肿瘤，符合 MEN1，安排手术。Bailey 由此获得研究选题：为什么 VIP 病房能查出六个月未确诊的病因，想用它改善所有人的医疗。

**Amelia 线（寄生虫囊肿）**：八岁男孩 Migue（米格尔·贝拉斯科，Miguel Velasco）从 Parsons General 转院（该院神经外科等部门接连关闭、疑似全院关停），足球训练头部落地后癫痫发作，Parsons 误诊为脑震荡。CT 显示顶叶巨大囊肿，Amelia 术中发现囊肿内有"子囊"——实为寄生虫（包虫/棘球蚴，美国极罕见，可能在意大利感染，孩子几周前随家人去意大利看过尤文图斯比赛）。因包虫液含毒素、破裂可致过敏性休克，必须完整取出，采用罕见的水动力分离术（hydrodissection，用盐水完整剥离囊肿）成功；术后需监护一两周并服抗寄生虫药。母亲自责带他去意大利。

**Jo 线（生育创伤互助）**：Jo 参加分娩创伤互助小组（birth trauma support group），起初回避分享，借口对核桃过敏逃避，被好心的孕妇 Tracie 当真并送急诊。Jo 坦白真相后两人在急诊深聊：Tracie 回忆灾难性分娩（催产、产科医生手术时手机铃声一直是 Britney Spears 的 "...Baby One More Time"、剖腹产由心脏外科医生完成、孩子发育里程碑迟缓），产后 PTSD、已辞职；Jo 也坦言自己"经历了一些事"，怕开口就回不去。两人交换电话号码。

**Plastic surgery 线（Ben Warren 证明自己）**：整形外科 attending Wright 的 fellow 患者 Grant de Vera（格兰特·德·维拉，州议员 Mallard 的幕僚长）右腓骨骨皮游离皮瓣术后。Ben 因送孩子+红鲑洄游封路迟到被 Wright 批评；他擅自去接 Wright fellow 的女儿挨了埋怨，后独立发现皮瓣下血肿致灌注不足/筋膜室综合征，果断用卸订书钉放血+水蛭疗法抢救，争取到 Wright 认可，Wright 想推荐他竞争华盛顿整形外科医师协会理事会的 fellow 职位。但结尾他对同事把功劳说成自己"兜底换来的"，被揭穿他曾下错肝素剂量、忘换敷料等旧事，暴露其邀功心态。

**Sockeye Salmon Run 创伤线（Tracie 之死）**：Tracie Pearson 陪妹妹参加红鲑洄游 10K 跑被踩伤：左胫骨节段性粉碎性骨折、胸骨骨折、胸壁创伤。新来的骨科医生 Millin 提出先外固定架（ex-fix）再 ORIF。她 EKG T 波改变、肌钙蛋白轻度升高被观察处理；随后术中血压下跌、血氧降至 80、室速、心跳骤停——事故中心脏冠状动脉已受损，导管室发现前降支（LAD）近段夹层合并血栓，放支架后夹层延伸至其他血管，抢救无效死亡。Ndugu（恩杜古）主刀导管室，新医生在 IVUS/导丝操作中表现出色获好评。Ry（Tracie 妹妹）与 Millin 结下缘分（Tracie 生前一直给妹妹撮合单身医生）。

**Cormac / Amelia 感情线**：Cormac 未经商量清空 Amelia 日程订了惠德比岛（Whidbey Island）周末，又订了 90 分钟上门双人按摩，Amelia 不满他先斩后奏（她不喜欢双人按摩、不愿非紧急取消手术），也牵出她术后焦虑。Cormac 坦言"保持清醒（sober）靠能提前计划"，道歉并要取消酒店，Amelia 反说既然不算惊喜了就去，但要求提前发行程——"我们永远不出房间"。

**Simone / Benezro 线**：Simone 的旧伴侣（新住院医主任 Benezro）持续越过界限帮她（向 Beckman 推荐她的实验室等），她挑明困惑：分不清这是职责还是旧情；对方承认"我也想你""我不好"。Simone 的 TA 认为她的研究想法"mid"（结肠癌大都市疗效课题太宽泛、做烂了），她受 Lillian 病例启发确定了自己的研究方向。

**Nick / 其他**：Bailey 与 Meredith 谈到医院新高管 Norton 正拆毁她毕生建立的体系，但因 Catherine 信任他无法解雇；Bailey 主动约 Norton 共进晚餐拿数据。

## S23E06

## Episode plot

Grey Sloan is collapsing under a flood of transfers: Parsons General is shutting down and Seattle Pres's PACU has a gas leak, so the hospital is out of supplies (pantoprazole, ondansetron), vending machines are empty, and residents are working without breaks. Leadership (Catherine and the new executive Norton) wants to protect the VIP "Olympic" wing and its revenue, even proposing to deny transfers and close to trauma; Webber refuses ("I'm not turning away sick patients to protect your marketing tool... if that's a problem, fire me"), and the VIP floor gets used for overflow anyway.

Four patient threads run through the chaos:
- Keith Martin (82, post-TURBT) is bounced from the Olympic floor back to the waiting room because he didn't pay the surcharge. Hours later he is in perforated-bladder crisis, codes, and dies after 40 minutes of CPR (time of death 15:47). Webber tells the resident to "call it, son."
- Oscar Sanchez (22, Parsons transfer with cerebral edema) deteriorates; with no ICU bed, no neurosurgeon available, and a "holding pattern," residents perform a bedside craniectomy, push mannitol three times, find a subdermal hematoma, and save him.
- Joanne Vũ (18, elite figure skater) has a C6 disc herniation. She rejects disc-replacement surgery for physical therapy, then acutely loses sensation and needs emergency cervical surgery. Link is impaired during the operation and is removed from his own case by Dr. Adams.
- Two diagnostic errors drive stakes: Millin runs a contrast study on Leah Chen's bowel obstruction without personally ruling out a closed-loop obstruction, causing a crisis and a stern lecture from Bailey; Shadee Ebrahim, a violinist with an ovarian cyst, goes missing after Kwan lies to her overbearing mother to give her space, and is found stealing macarons on the VIP floor.

Simone is dumped by Bryant, who says he needs to focus on training; she later admits she still loves him but decides she also wants to focus.

## Important character actions

- Webber defies Catherine and Norton over the VIP wing, chooses patient care over hospital marketing, and personally delivers the bad news on Keith Martin.
- Bailey pushes residents to step up ("your attendings are bailing water out of this sinking ship with teaspoons") and later tears into Millin over the contrast-study error; she and Webber are aligned against Norton.
- Norton and Catherine treat the Olympic wing as a "marketing tool," citing tens of millions in VIP-clinic revenue.
- Adams takes over Joanne's surgery and throws Link out of the OR, then checks on a shaken Amelia afterward.
- Amelia reveals she saw Link pick up tramadol a month ago, admits she looked at his chart (multiple refills, daily use), and confronts him; he denies a problem and calls it a HIPAA violation.
- Millin is given Leah Chen's care as a "battlefield promotion" and falters; Kwan hides Shadee to escape her mother's pressure.
- Bryant ends things with Simone; an advice conversation about "fighting for him" runs through the episode.

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Parsons General's closure and Seattle Pres's gas leak are the ongoing overflow engine for Grey Sloan; supplies and staff are stretched to breaking.
- The VIP-wing fight escalates from S23E04: Bailey's "health equity in VIP" research mission now collides directly with Norton and Catherine's plan to protect VIP revenue and deny transfers, with Webber openly defying Catherine.
- Link's tramadol thread becomes an immediate patient-safety crisis: he is impaired in surgery, removed from his case, and Amelia has both witnessed it and breached his records to confirm daily use — a major relationship and accountability arc.
- Keith Martin's death is a direct consequence of the overcrowding/VIP triage failure; Oscar's survival is the "fight" counterpoint.
- Simone's breakup (and mention of a month lost to measles) continues her personal/research-focus arc; Benezro is not featured here.
