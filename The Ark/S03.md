## S03E01

### 剧情概述

- 方舟一号收到罗斯星发来的警告：“别来罗斯星。”加内特舰长仍决定前往救援，因为方舟四号可能有幸存者。
- 罗斯星的太阳爆发出极强的太阳风暴。阿丽西亚改装人工重力系统，制造太阳防护罩，为搜救争取时间。
- 方舟四号已在轨道上严重损毁，但部分尾部舱段和中心舱段成功着陆。加内特、布莱斯、基米、莫罗佐夫与方舟四号幸存者本·哈蒙德上将会合。
- 他们发现方舟四号船员遭到大型原生生物袭击。这些生物惧怕阳光，只在夜间活动，并持续摧毁幸存者的避难所。
- 防护罩即将失效，而日出尚有数小时。伊恩提出利用方舟的太阳帆将阳光反射到地面，为救援小队开辟通道。
- 计划成功，小队冲向穿梭机；但布莱斯为掩护加内特撤离，被生物拖走。加内特亲眼见到他死亡。
- 返回一号大本营后，加内特将布莱斯的死讯告知伊娃，并转达他最后的遗言：“我爱你。”
- 与此同时，大本营开始面对殖民地内部的治安和身份冲突。彼得罗维奇被任命为安保主管，凯莉·福勒获释并成为他的助理。
- 安格斯与凯莉在探索区采集样本时发现一只外形酷似欧亚黑鹂的死鸟；附近随后传来可疑声响。

### 重要角色行动

- 莎伦·加内特：坚持前往罗斯星救援；在危机中协调舰桥、地面小队和撤离行动；失去布莱斯后向伊娃传达死讯。
- 詹姆斯·布莱斯：参加罗斯星搜救；在最后撤离时主动吸引生物，确认死亡。
- 阿丽西亚·内文斯：设计并维持太阳防护罩；后与伊恩调整太阳帆方案，为地面小队制造光照通道。
- 伊恩：因“昏迷梦境”获得的知识并不可靠，关键时刻仍利用太阳帆提出有效救援方案。
- 基米·乔马：参与地面搜救和防御，协助小队撤离。
- 奥尔加·莫罗佐夫：以战地医务兵身份参与任务；本集明确她并非正式医生，但大家仍称她为“医生”。
- 本·哈蒙德：方舟四号指挥官；说明幸存者长期遭受原生生物袭击。
- 彼得罗维奇：被任命为一号大本营安保主管，并获得“治安官”这一边境殖民地式称号。
- 凯莉·福勒：获释后拒绝因仇恨冲突进行反击，显示出改变；接受担任彼得罗维奇的助理。
- 安格斯：邀请凯莉进入探索区协助采样，继续调查罗斯星生态环境。
- 伊娃·马尔科维奇：得知布莱斯死亡，坚持索要穿梭机和方舟的记录，以亲自确认事实。

### 新出现的名字与术语

- 罗斯星（Ross）：方舟四号的目的地，拥有类似地球的大气与水资源，但存在剧烈太阳风暴和危险原生生物。
- 方舟四号（Ark 4）：在罗斯星遭太阳风暴与生物袭击，绝大多数人员死亡。
- 本·哈蒙德（Ben Hammond）：方舟四号指挥官，军衔为海军上将。
- 奥尔加·莫罗佐夫（Olga Morozov）：方舟一号搜救队成员，战地医务兵。
- 米兰·彼得罗维奇（Milan Petrovic）：原方舟十五号安保负责人，曾在地球担任警察；现任一号大本营治安官。
- EF1：东部联邦的一艘飞船，仍未回应呼叫；彼得罗维奇的儿子在船上。
- 一号大本营（Homebase One）：殖民者集中生活、交易和处理治安事务的基地。
- 探索区（exploration zone）：大本营外围的采样与勘探区域。
- 太阳帆（solar sails）：高度反光，可被用来把阳光投射到地面。
- 原生生物：罗斯星上的大型夜行生物，惧怕阳光，子弹无法有效阻止它们。
- X10级耀斑：严重削弱方舟一号太阳防护罩的太阳爆发。
- 格拉维拉克森（Gravelaxacon）：伊恩在梦境中似乎学到的技术名词；他尝试应用时发现相关知识完全不可靠。

### 连续性要点

- 方舟一号已抵达并参与一号大本营的殖民建设，任务重点从寻找家园转为扩大殖民地、寻找其他方舟与维持内部秩序。
- 前美国太空总署（GSA）和东部联邦（EF）人员刚结束敌对状态，双方仍存在歧视、斗殴和政治不信任。
- 加内特与伊恩在梦境中经历过长期恋爱关系，但现实中加内特仍拒绝公开承认两人的感情，以维护自己对两派船员的领导公信力。
- 伊恩与阿丽西亚的技术能力依旧关键，但伊恩必须面对梦境知识并不可信这一事实。
- 布莱斯死亡，伊娃失去伴侣；这将改变方舟一号的指挥与人际关系。
- 罗斯星的空气成分并不稳定：距离大本营越远，氧气迅速下降，二氧化碳、氮气和硫酸成分上升。安全探索范围暂时只能扩大到约十五公里。
- 大本营发现一块带有未知文字的高强度合金石板。其材质含有无法识别的元素，可能来自地球以外的先进文明。
- 罗斯星出现与地球生物高度相似的鸟类，暗示该星球生态系统可能存在更深层的来源或异常。

### 笑点与可延续的参考

- 伊娃提醒布莱斯两人的“一周年纪念日”，布莱斯却只记得“差不多一年前”。
- 伊恩称布莱斯为“卷毛”，声称两人在昏迷梦境中相恋多年；布莱斯完全不记得这段经历。
- 阿丽西亚准备改装人工重力系统时，布莱斯预判她会说“但是”；阿丽西亚随后只说“能持续三个小时”，形成笑点。
- 伊恩差点关闭人工重力，阿丽西亚指出这样只会让所有人飘到天花板上。
- 布莱斯调侃伊恩总想着把外星生物的消息告诉伊娃，称他“陷得太深”；随后又透露自己住在隔壁，早就听见了伊恩和伊娃的亲密通话。
- 伊恩试图用梦境中的技术知识解决太阳风暴问题，边说边意识到自己讲的全是胡话。
- 彼得罗维奇被建议称为“治安官”，随后以西部片式口吻向拘留者自我介绍，凯莉讽刺地评价：“情况越来越好了。”

### 未解决线索

- 罗斯星的原生生物究竟是什么，是否具备更高智能，以及它们为何会攻击殖民者。
- 罗斯星的空气恶化是否会向一号大本营扩散，殖民地能否长期维持。
- 未知文字石板来自哪个文明，石板上的信息能否破译。
- 罗斯星上出现地球相似鸟类的原因。
- 东部联邦飞船 EF1 的下落，以及彼得罗维奇儿子的命运。
- 安格斯和凯莉在探索区听到的声音来自什么生物或其他未知存在。
- 凯莉获释后能否真正获得殖民地居民的信任。

# The Ark S03E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Admiral Ben Hammond (Ark 4) arrives on Ark 1 and attempts to assert command over Captain Garnet, citing his higher rank. Garnet challenges him using the GSA charter—since the GSA no longer exists and the crew chose to follow her, Ark 1 is now a Homebase 1 ship. Hammond backs down but stays aboard as Garnet investigates the Tyson system, where Evelyn Maddox set a course before fleeing.

Ark 1 drops from FTL behind Tyson's moon and encounters **Ark 22**, commanded by **Kurtis Drake**. Drake reveals he's married to **Jill**, a clone of Garnet, and that Ark 22 is a sanctuary populated entirely by **Gen-2 clones**. Drake captures Garnet and Ian, bringing them to Ark 22 where they learn:
- **Kurtis Drake Prime** invented cloning science
- Clones were created for exploitation (labor, organs, experimentation)
- **Gen-3 clones** (cloned from Gen-2 DNA) develop psychosis
- Drake is searching for a specific **G-44 model**—Maddox was helping him find one

Meanwhile on Ark 1, Hammond refuses to risk the ship to rescue Garnet and Ian ("the math doesn't work"). Alicia develops a **weaponized EM pulse** based on William Trust's design. The crew stages **Plan B**—a mutiny to rescue their captain despite Hammond's orders.

On **Homebase 1**, Angus Medford and Kelly Fowler are missing after entering the "expanded zone." Felix organizes a search party while Sanji confronts her ex-husband **Milan Petrovic**, newly appointed sheriff. Kelly and Angus are trapped in an artificial structure with strange crystals emitting a piercing ultrasonic sound that affects Kelly's implants.

The episode ends with Ark 1 firing the EM pulse—but **Ark 22 has already jumped away**. On the planet, Felix and the search party realize something far worse happened to Angus and Kelly.

## S03E03

## The Ark S03E03 Summary

### Episode Plot
Garnet and Ian are trapped on **Ark 22** (Drake's ship) after the previous episode's cliffhanger. They discover a room full of **Gen 3 clones** who have "lost their minds." Attempting to escape via shuttle, they learn a shuttle cannot detach from an Ark during FTL travel—it would break apart. **Ian** devises a risky workaround: rigging the shuttle's impulse coils to draw power from Ark 22's FTL retrofit, creating a temporary FTL bubble (a technique he once used on **Ark 8**). The maneuver succeeds; they eject, send an encrypted distress ping, and **Alicia** triangulates their position. **Ark 1** rescues them just as Drake's ship closes in. Drake holds fire because his doctor identified Garnet as "the one"—a special Gen 3 he's sought since launch.

Back at **Homebase**, a generator explosion kills/injures crew. **Eva** determines it was deliberate sabotage using **trillium**, an explosive compound too volatile for Ark transport—meaning someone brought it aboard. Tensions flare between former GSA and Eastern Federation personnel. **Admiral Hammond** attempts to seize command, but the crew refuses her orders. Garnet returns, reprimands Alicia and Eva for insubordination, then thanks them for saving her. A wounded "John Doe" wearing **Angus's clothes (inside out)** is brought to medbay—Angus himself remains missing. The episode ends with Drake raging at his crew to find **Homebase 1** (a self-named colony not in any database) now that Garnet has escaped.

### Important Character Actions
- **Garnet & Ian**: Escape Ark 22 via improvised FTL bubble maneuver.
- **Mrs. Drake (Kurtis Drake's wife)**: Betrays her husband, helps Garnet/Ian reach the shuttle. She's in a relationship with **Clark (a J6-82 clone)** and wants to flee Drake.
- **Alicia**: Triangulates the distress ping under pressure from Hammond.
- **Eva**: Rigs the impulse coils for the FTL bubble; analyzes trillium residue proving sabotage.
- **Hammond**: Tries to assume command via GSA override codes; crew refuses. Garnet later confirms she won't face court-martial (no GSA = no authority).
- **Drake**: Revealed to have hunted a specific Gen 3 ("the one") since launch. Orders Homebase 1 located by brute-force search of all habitable planets.
- **Kelly**: Suspected by Strickland and others of harming Angus; Garnet refuses to accept this.

## S03E04

**S03E04 – The Ark (Season 3, Episode 4)**

---

### Episode Plot
The Ark One crew splits across two fronts: on the planet **Ross**, search parties hunt for missing crew (Angus, Kelly) while confronting a mysterious saboteur; aboard Ark One, **Sharon Garnet learns she is a Gen-3 clone** and reconciles with Sanji (Dr. Kabir). On Ross, the saboteur—**John Doe**, a man absent from all Ark manifests—is found to possess **three cochleas per ear and altered vocal cords**, a congenital condition that makes generator noise agonizing; his "sabotage" was self-defense. The team discovers a **hidden underground complex** opened by a **musical-scale touch-code on stone slabs** (C major, missing three notes), where Angus and Kelly are held by **human-like cave dwellers** who share John Doe's ear anatomy. Alicia uses her WD to reproduce a pain-frequency and drives the locals off, rescuing the pair. Meanwhile, **Brice is alive**—rescued by Dr. Morozov days earlier in a collapsing ruin, stalked by "raiders." Brice and Morozov jury-rig comms and fire a signal to Ark One just before the structure fails; **Morozov appears to die** in the attempt. Back on Ark One, **Admiral** (Ark 15) probes **Lieutenant Commander Ilic**, whose military records are heavily redacted and tied to a covert program called **Black Thorn**.

---

## S03E05

**Episode Plot**  
A distress signal on a secure GSA frequency—embedded with Brice’s ID—draws Ark One back to Ross. Garnet leads an away team (Eva, Kimi, Ian, Sanji, Kabir, Brice) to the surface. They discover an underground complex **identical** to the one on “Homebase” (32 ly away), down to the tunnel geometry and near-impenetrable walls. Inside, they find **Brice alive** in a chamber, his body wrapped in living “webbing” with tubes behind his ear. He insists Dr. Morozov nursed him back from fatal wounds, but the crew saw Morozov die. The walls abruptly “swallow” the women (Garnet, Eva, Kimi, Kabir), transporting them into a web-filled cavity; the men are left behind. Ian and Brice track the women via movement inside the walls, pry open a seam, and pull them free seconds before sunset forces the shuttle to leave.  

Back on Ark One: **Admiral Hammond has been murdered**—stabbed with Strickland’s sword, only Strickland’s prints on the hilt. Strickland is detained but protests innocence; Kelly Fowler vouches for him and offers to shelter his daughter Katheryn. Alicia’s genomic sequencing returns a bombshell: Ross flora/fauna are **genetically identical to Earth species** (Bermuda grass, Eurasian blackbird, European black poplar). Brice’s medical exam shows **zero trauma, no scars—and a regrown appendix** (removed in college). The webbing healed him completely; the others only have a tiny post-auricular scar from the tube. Brice’s “Raiders” story is dismissed as tube-induced hallucination, yet the physical evidence defies explanation.

---

**Important Character Actions**  
- Garnet authorizes the Ross mission over Ava’s objection (she watched Brice die).  
- Brice refuses to leave without “Doc,” forcing the team to confront the reality of Morozov’s death.  
- Ian and Brice coordinate the rescue of the abducted women, using wall-seam tactics learned on Homebase.

# S03E11 — The Ark

## Plot

Several storylines converge as Ark One presses forward with the Gen-3 cure. Garnet travels to the station for a tense rendezvous with Drake aboard Ark 22, where Alexandria (the cook Drake once sold to Anton) serves as living proof the cure works. Drake is arrested by Lt. Reed — his own second-in-command — for trafficking, kidnapping, and human experimentation. The cure is administered across Ark 22's Gen-3 population, including Dr. Morgan (who confesses she cut open Gen-3s under Drake's coercion, though her "husband" threat is exposed as a lie — she has been Drake's lover since Jill left). Strickland and Kimi investigate Drake's death in his cell and find Drake's body was moved post-mortem.

Down on the surface, Brice and Eva descend into the underground chamber. Using infrared goggles tuned to a phase-shift (reverse-engineered from the high-speed camera data that captured the control panel during the shuttle's earlier contact), they locate a control interface covered in lines and symbols that only those out-of-phase can see. Eva deciactivates the force field using circuit analogy logic, revealing a massive alien artifact. Sanji's autopsy of Drake shows an aneurysm, but lividity confirms the body was dragged into the cell after death. Alicia discovers a Neural Correlates Transfer Device (NCTD) in Drake's quarters — it can move a mind between identical brains; Drake's plan was immortality by leaping between his own Gen-3 clones.

The revealed structure is identified as a terraforming / terraform-maintenance machine extending 2 km underground, regulating the planet's regolith and magma and converting CO₂/acids into breathable air. It took a direct hit from EF-1's pulse-locked weapon, leaving damage on its top edge, and is now glitching — explaining the lava, earthquakes, and oxygen anomalies. It also emits Subspace Transmissions (SSTs), suggesting it is networked with an identical machine on Ross that previously generated the solar flares that destroyed Admiral Hammond's Ark.

In an attempt to avoid re-implanting the "Doc" program in Brice, Alicia proposes using an air-gapped terminal aboard Ark 16 (empty but with life support still running). Ana Vujik, a former EF pilot, ferries her up under Garnet's orders. The plan goes wrong: signals jam, an unknown shuttle attempts to dock, and Ark 16 jumps — ending up 23 light-years from its destination with Alicia and Ana aboard.

Meanwhile, the Gen-3 cure is rolled out to Ark 22's Gen-3 G44 population, including C-1 — all direct Gen-3 clones of Drake himself, kept in a luxurious suite and groomed to view themselves as "superior" and "most important." The cure confirms the C-1s will not turn psychotic.

Back at Homebase, a stone tablet chimes and produces new readings; the data suggests not enough time remains to save the planet. Alexandria, digging through Ark 22 logs, locates a video file of DNA-donor testimonials that may explain the "destined" attraction Brice has felt toward Eva — donors who couldn't conceive gave their DNA so their clones would be given to other childless couples, implying two people in the crew are genetically linked through the same donor pair. Brice muses on whether love is "preprogrammed" and concludes it doesn't matter.
