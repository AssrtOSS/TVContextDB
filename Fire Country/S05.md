## S05E01

## Plot
Stations 42 and Three Rock are pulled into the 175th Edgewater Founders' Day Festival under severe strain: Pineville's station was destroyed in a prior flood, so 42 is covering two towns, and Battalion Chief Sharon Leone has been MIA "for weeks" on a motorcycle trip. Manny Perez is acting BC, with Eve Edwards running Three Rock and Jake Crawford holding down 42's engine. At the festival Bill Gottlieb (chair of the Edgewater Board of Supervisors) announces an emergency budget to hire more hands; local Hayden confronts Manny over three homes lost last week while 42 was in Pineville (Hayden's mother's house was one of them).

Mid-festival a massive sinkhole opens under Main Street, trapping civilians. Bode Leone and Jake descend into the cavity with help from a passing Philadelphia ex-firefighter named CJ; volunteers from the crowd help clear rubble. Triage is set up at Smokey's Tavern under Dr. Camille Thurston. Mrs. Ritter, the old Music Appreciation teacher who taught both Jake and Eve, is pinned under a half-buried car; Bill Gottlieb suffers a near-fatal bleed (suspected femoral artery); multiple patients present.

Bode's grandfather Walter "Nonno" Leone, who has dementia, smells sewer gas, walks Bode through a gas check on the radio, and warns that an exposed sewer line is leaking flammable gas just as a car battery starts sparking inside the hole. A newly arrived Three Rock inmate named Angel pushes Eve clear. Three Rock inmates build a highline; Mrs. Ritter is pulled out in a basket and handed to medics. CJ's car is wrecked by a sinkhole-ejected manhole cover, stranding her in town.

After-action, Sharon returns and reveals the emergency funding has been pulled — every dollar is going to fix Main Street. Bode proposes reviving Edgewater's old volunteer fire department (VFD, last led by Donny Wolfe, formally disbanded a few years ago). Manny is skeptical but visibly entertains it. The episode cuts to black on Manny calling dispatch: "shots fired, firefighters down" at the still-open scene.

## Important character actions
- Sharon Leone: voicemail to Bode claims she is fine; returns from her trip only to cancel the funding.
- Manny Perez: has been acting BC since Sharon's departure; publicly thanks Gottlieb for the funding, privately pulls Bode aside about Walter wanting to help; later warns Eve that Angel is dangerous and is on the fence about the VFD at the AAR.
- Bode Leone: handles the morning PINEVILLE/Alder-Road grass-fire call; meets and recruits CJ at a campfire warning; pilots the Merrow Towing rotator trick as a high-point; in-sinkhole with Jake and CJ; identifies and ventilates the sewer-gas hazard; sells the VFD idea to Manny at the AAR.
- Jake Crawford: recruits crowd volunteers, coordinates Three Rock for the highline, and distracts Mrs. Ritter with jokes while she is still trapped.
- Eve Edwards: welcomes the new Three Rock crew, runs the highline pull, and spars with Manny about keeping Angel — quoting the show's line "no one's the worst thing they've ever done."
- CJ (last name undisclosed): passing-through ex-firefighter, Pennsylvania ID, pulled into the incident by chance; becomes the backup who saves Gottlieb and stabilizes Mrs. Ritter; her wrecked car ties her to Edgewater for the moment.
- Angel: new Three Rock inmate with two alleged stabbings at Folsom (per inmate Tex); Manny's brother's son; saves Eve from the sparking battery; dislocated right shoulder (recurring childhood injury); volunteers for every task.
- Walter "Nonno" Leone: at the festival, refuses to be sidelined, then walks Bode through the gas-detector warning that prevents a larger detonation.
- Camille Thurston: runs the Smokey's triage, treats Mrs. Ritter, Gottlieb, and ultimately Angel's shoulder.
- Mrs. Ritter: former Edgewater Music Appreciation teacher; trapped, suspected lacerated spleen; redirects Jake to talk about Violet to keep calm.
- Bill Gottlieb: surviving Board of Supervisors chair; leg wound + heavy blood loss.
- Hayden: local whose mother lost her home; voices community anger at 42.

## S05E02

# S05E02 Fire Country — 剧情摘要

## 主线剧情
接续 S05E01 的枪击事件。Jake Crawford 头盔中弹，子弹贯穿，留下玻璃纤维碎屑，所幸无生命危险；同一波袭击也击中正在三岩就医的囚犯 Ben（腿部贯穿伤，按惩教署 CDCR 政策戴手铐）。三岩与42 号的调查把枪击源头指向当地人 Hayden Moffitt——一位在 Founders' Day 期间家中房屋被烧、母亲 Shelly 在医院因吸入性烟雾死亡的退伍军人。Sharon 与 Mickey（警长）部署全城搜捕 Hayden，灰白色卡车为关键线索。

火灾警报响：2501 Edge Cliff Valley Road 发生植被火灾。ECSO 防弹衣不足，Sharon 命令待命。Angel 指出40 mph东北阵风可能将火吹入镇内，Eve 决定不穿防弹衣也带队上山。Station 42 + 三岩联合上山砍防火带。Manny 作为新晋 Battalion Chief 在前线指挥，但他的注意力被外甥 Angel 分散，导致他对局势判断迟缓。

到达火场后，Jake 与 Manny 发现多处起火点（人为纵火），地上留有纸条："你们烧了我们的房子，还害死了我妈妈。我算这笔账是天经地义。" Hayden 的卡车被锁定。Angel 在前线被蜂群严重叮咬（童年时曾因颈部叮伤休克），陷入过敏性休克，需要 EpiPen。三岩的 EpiPen 在客车上，无法取回。Bode 穿越火线将随身急救包中的 EpiPen 送到 Angel 手中。

Hayden 被 Sheriff 部门抓获后声明"我不是枪手——是我弟弟 Bryce"。Hayden 是上山来阻止他的。Bryce 在西北山脊高处射击，故意打偏，想让 Hayden 还击将其击毙灭口（具体灭口动机未解）。

Sharon 违抗 Mickey 的命令上山，与 Hank 一同对峙 Bryce。Hank 与 Bryce 曾一起练柔术，以此为切入口劝降。Jake 提出奇招：用引火信号棒（fusees）点燃三处火灾之间的空地，借上升气流将火势引向北方耗尽燃料自行熄灭——成功。Tanker 37 空投与点火配合，Bryce 被捕并被宣读米兰达权利。

## 关键人物行动
- Bode Leone：在酒吧遇到 CJ（费城消防员、现流浪），劝她随车同行；枪战中护住 Manny；穿越火线为 Angel 送 EpiPen；逐步挖出 CJ 的真实故事。

- Sharon Leone：违抗 Mickey 命令上山，亲自与 Bryce 对峙；事后向 Luke 承认对 Founders' Day 期间的 Moffitt 火灾等事件有负罪感。

- CJ（金发女郎 / 费城山羊 / Superman）：费城前消防员，因拒绝加入营队长掩盖建筑违规回扣而被威胁；营队长故意派她进入已被火灾损坏的楼梯间想让她"因公殉职"；她用斧头砸墙逃生；漂泊到 Edgewater，车祸后本想离开，被 Bode 说服留下来协助组建志愿消防队。

# Fire Country S05E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
Three intertwined storylines converge: (A) Bode leads the VFD recruits on their pack test; they detour to rescue a goat (Pepper) and its owner Janice, then discover Ken Bauer pinned under an overturned tractor at Bauer Farm. A crush-injury emergency unfolds — Ken can't be extricated without releasing fatal toxins, so the team waits 15+ minutes for Station 42 and an air ambulance while Ken says goodbye to his wife Gloria. Ken dies during extrication. (B) A viral video of Jake's "winners and losers" structure-triage comment triggers Cal Fire legal complaints; Violet (Cal Fire lawyer) threatens a gross-misconduct investigation. Sharon pushes Jake to apologize; he instead meets the Fishers, shares specific memories of their lost belongings, and they drop the complaints. (C) Eve confesses to Violet she falsified the Three Rock buggy repair paperwork — the radiator came from inmate Angel via a contraband phone. Eve protects Angel; Manny warns her it'll backfire. Three Rock inmates do punitive "housekeeping" while Eve faces disciplinary review.

# Fire Country S05E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
A prison transport van crashes on Route 9, freeing three inmates: **Crystal Wallace** (life without parole), **Paige Dalton** (arsonist), and **Kendra**. They carjack Cal Fire Engine 1591, taking **Manny Perez** and **Sheriff Mickey Fox** hostage, and head for **Cribb Valley** (Deadwater area) to retrieve a stolen **1836 Colt Paterson revolver** worth $1.5M from an abandoned salvage yard. A wildfire burns through the valley concurrently. Bode, Boone, Jake, Eve, and Three Rock crews mount a rescue while battling the fire. A jackknifed semi hauling **ammonium nitrate** blocks Route 6, creating an explosive hazard. The team clears a path through the trailer, positions snipers (Hank, Chang) on **Knowles Ridge**, and ultimately rescues Manny and Mickey. Crystal is killed by Mickey; Paige and Kendra are captured. ## Episode Plot
A cascade of electrical fires erupts across Edgewater when a new data center at the renovated paper mill overloads the town's aging power grid.
