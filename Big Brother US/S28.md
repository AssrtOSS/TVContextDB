# Big Brother US — S28E32

## Episode Plot
Day 66, live eviction night. The episode opens on the wreckage of the "Icons" trio (Angela, Dee, Devens) being placed together on the block by HOH Barrett, with Yash's Golden Power of Veto ensuring no one can be saved. The recap frames it as Barrett's "biggest shot of the game" and Yash "ensuring one of them is heading to the jury house."

Inside the house, the fallout of the veto meeting dominates. Devens publicly accuses Barrett of betraying a Day One alliance, Dee launches a "savage" personal attack ("you will regret this for the rest of your life"), and the Crossover side rallies around Barrett and Drew. Drew is widely mocked for being too afraid to speak up, with Angela demanding he "grow a backbone." Barrett doesn't deny the backstab ("it doesn't surprise me, I did backstab them") but admits Devens is the bigger threat he wants gone. The Icons try to lock in votes by recasting Barrett and Drew as the real dangers; Dee pitches to every voter that the two men are bigger targets and asks the house to "keep me over Devens."

The competing plans: Melody is angry that Devens wants to keep Dee over Angela and tells him flat-out her issue is with him; Yash presses Dee on whether she can be trusted ("devil you know"); Angela clings to her showmances with Yash/Drew and publicly plays matchmaker, which Dee privately notes is reckless (it draws attention to her); Dee and Yash trade promises about future loyalty.

The BB Blockbuster ("Reinvent the Wheel" — assemble a double-sided wheel puzzle and hit the button) is won by Devens for the second straight week, saving himself. Angela and Dee must face the live vote. Devens immediately tells Yash and Taylor he's 100% with them. Julie then runs the live eviction: Drew (only one vote to evict Dee), Melody (votes Dee, not happy with Angela), Devens (votes Angela, his "partner in crime"), and Taylor (votes Angela). Yash casts the deciding vote to evict Angela. By 3-2, Angela leaves the house at age 53 after eight weeks inside, telling the Icons she loves them and that "we are just like you."

In the exit interview Julie reveals Angela's husband Chuk took out all the Christmas decorations. Angela calls Barrett "not my favorite person," says she doesn't fully respect his HOH play (could have put up one or two instead of three), and confirms she would have stayed loyal to Dee over Devens to the end, citing jury management concerns about Dee already being a Survivor winner. She's convinced about a Melody–Drew showmance ("everybody knows") but stops short of confirming. Her goodbye messages are warm: Dee calls her "the cutest and most wonderful"; one houseguest tells her "we need somebody who can win"; another insists putting her up was "purely game, nothing personal." Angela heads to the jury house to reunite with Lala ("Lala and I are going to be poolside").

The episode closes with Julie teasing three weeks of relentless gameplay ahead: Sunday's HOH, Wednesday's "infamous Pressure Cooker" veto, and a double eviction one week from tonight — followed by the final veto competition and "the most shocking three-part HOH competition ever" before finale night on October 1st when a 7-person jury votes for the winner.
