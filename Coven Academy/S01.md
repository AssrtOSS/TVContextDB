## S01E01

## S01E01 — "A Hex Education"

### Plot
Cold open in the French Quarter: three girls are out past curfew; locals warn them to "stay safe," then a voice says they'll be "someone's lunch" within minutes.

Briar Roberts arrives in New Orleans from New York to investigate her mother's death. A man drives her to "Crescent Veil Academy" — the cover name for Coven Academy. In headmistress Ms. Graves's office, Graves needles her about her mother; Briar flares up and accidentally blows up the office, activating her powers. Outside, trainees Sasha and Tegan intercept her and explain: a newly activated witch broadcasts like a "blinking red sign." Sasha is an empath who reads feelings; Tegan is a time-shifter (only in moments of need).

They give Briar a 20-minute tour — she concedes in 10. Highlights: self-defense class (vampires have been active; three students didn't come home last night), the warlocks, and the vault — oldest scrolls, first potions, original armor. Sasha tries and fails to read Briar's emotions. Graves tells Briar to train first, promises protection, and dangles answers about her mom later.

The driver, Jake — a warlock posing as a foreign "exchange student" with a fake accent — asks Briar on a Garden District ghost-tour date. His stepsister McKenna, the head witch student, is worried: Jake was with the three missing girls the night they vanished. Jake is secretly running an advanced locator spell with relics from the missing girls' rooms to find out if they're alive. Briar settles into her dorm; Sasha checks on her. Cold close: a mysterious woman orders "Alexander" to find someone and "leave her for Ollie."

### Important character actions
- Briar: snaps at Graves over her mother → accidentally blows up the office (powers activate). Refuses to stay, then bargains for 20 minutes. Deflects Sasha's emotional probing with a Pisces joke.
- Ms. Graves: deliberately provokes Briar about Mary to trigger her powers; refuses to answer questions until Briar has trained.
- Sasha: scouts Briar outside, tours her, tries and fails to read her, then brings her to the dorm room with the bunny stickers on the door.
- Tegan: time-shifts to assess how attractive Jake is; warns that magic exhausts beginner witches.
- Jake: infiltrates under cover as the cabbie/exchange student; asks Briar out; secretly attempts an advanced locator spell with McKenna; admits he was with the missing girls the night they vanished and left them.
- McKenna: supplies relic items from the missing girls' rooms for Jake's spell; functions as both head witch and worried stepsister.
- Mysterious woman / Alexander / Ollie: closing beat establishes Ollie (vampire) as Briar's designated pursuer.

### Newly established names and terms
- Briar Roberts — sarcastic NYC teen, daughter of Mary Roberts
- Ms. Graves — headmistress, alumna, withholding info on Mary
- Sasha — empath / emotion reader
- Tegan — time-shifter
- Jake — warlock, McKenna's step-brother, fake-accent cover
- McKenna — head witch, Jake's stepsister
- Mr. Cole — teaches the "soft stuff" (Jake's locator spell is beyond his curriculum)
- Alexander — student receiving antagonist's orders
- Ollie — vampire, positioned to "find" Briar
- Coven Academy / cover name Crescent Veil Academy
- Vault — sacred archive
- Locator spell, time-shifting, "dark forces," the trainee coven

### Continuity-relevant facts
- Three witch students vanished the previous night; vampires unusually active.
- Mary Roberts is dead; she was a Pisces, "the kindest person" Briar knew. Briar keeps her boots and a pink backpack.
- Briar has a juvie record: she broke into a NY police station to do the cops' detective work for them (flagged as "anger issues").
- Graves triggered the power activation on purpose and knows more about Mary than she'll say.
- Sasha explicitly cannot read Briar's emotions — flagged as unusual.
- Jake was with the missing girls the night they vanished; he left them with a "buddy."
- Jake's "exchange student" persona and accent are a cover.
- Briar's telekinesis is tied to anger ("when I'm mad I can move things").
- McKenna has access to relics from the missing girls' dorms.

### Jokes and references useful for later episodes
- "Keep the meter running. I don't plan on staying long." — running gag about Briar treating everything like a NYC cab.
- "Give me 20 minutes. It'll only take 10." — inverts Tegan's power; sets up time-bending as both plot device and teen-comedy beat (Tegan uses it to scope Jake's hotness).
- Pisces deflection — Briar hides behind astrology whenever Sasha probes.
- "Head witch McKenna or concerned stepsister McKenna?" — locks in the McKenna/Jake sibling dynamic.
- "Some of our classmates didn't make it home last night" / vampires-active line — plants the missing-girls arc.
- "How many urban legends are actually real" + the werewolf joke — teases later supernatural reveals.
- Briar's signature NYC-vs-NOLA grumble ("a billion degrees, I hate it here") recurs throughout.

### Unresolved threads

- What are the exact scope and limits of Briar's anger-tied telekinesis?
- Jake's "buddy" that night — who is he, and is Jake implicated in the disappearance?
- Why does Jake need a fake cover to approach the new girl?
- Briar's pink backpack (still in the car) and Tegan's promise to bend time back 30 minutes to retrieve it — pending.

## S01E02

Plot
- Ms. Graves opens magic class by telling Briar, Sasha and Tegan that individually they are only formidable "in doses," but as a trinity / coven they are unstoppable. Briar is on her phone looking up an article about her late mother (a Pisces) instead of participating, and Graves pulls rank.
- McKenna, the self-styled "head witch," crashes the lesson, mocks Briar's coven as D-list and calls them "charity cases." A vault down the hall then explodes.
- Briar helps at the vault (a few old potions are lost, but everyone is "human" and accounted for). McKenna turns up with a cut arm and Briar stops to thank her for calling Briar a "more powerful witch" — the line that triggered the burst.
- Tegan time-warps Graves so Sasha can buy Briar cover to sneak out on a date with Ollie; Sasha gives Briar a charm-stone that "won't protect you, but it'll glow if you're in danger."
- Jake (a warlock) flags the west exit as the only gate that unlocks from the inside, then probes whether Briar's outing is about her mom's Brooklyn murder. Briar insists nothing is linked and goes alone.
- Briar meets Ollie on a New Orleans ghost tour (Ollie shows up with roses he admits he paid for via "light burglary"). The "guide" who books her seems to know her by name. While walking the haunted house, Ollie and Briar bond over both having recently lost family; Ollie says he's doing school online while crashing with relatives.
- Mid-tour Ollie's heart races, he tells Briar to jump out the second-floor window, and the guide drops the act: "I never should have sent a boy to do a woman's job." The episode ends on Briar realizing the tour is a setup.

Character actions
- Briar: stays sarcastic and defensive in class ("I make jokes when I'm nervous"), pushes back on Graves, defends Sasha and Tegan against McKenna, refuses Jake's help on the murder, sneaks out to her date, and emotionally opens up to Ollie about her mom.
- Sasha: acts as the coven's empath (catches Briar's crush on Ollie in class, senses her heartbeat at the haunted house, hands her the warning charm, covers for her with Graves).
- Tegan: demonstrates time-warping by briefly warping Graves out of her office so the coven can clean the vault unmonitored and Briar can escape — but warns it "bought time," it can't erase memory.
- Ms. Graves: enforces discipline ("Drop the jokes, pull it together"), threatens Sasha with suspension for lying, and gets time-warped by Tegan.
- McKenna: head witch, Briar's stepsister (their parents married each other), openly antagonistic; her "more powerful witch" remark appears to catalyze the vault surge.
- Jake: persistent warlock who flirts, offers tips (west exit, why warlocks are Graves' favorite, north wing location), and tries to insert himself into Briar's murder investigation.
- Ollie: magnetic, gentle on the date, but clearly more than human — senses Briar's racing heart from across the room and reads danger before any threat is visible.
- The ghost tour guide: presents as a chirpy host, then drops the mask and addresses Briar by name; frames Ollie as a tool she "sent," implying she orchestrated the date as a trap.

- "Head witch": McKenna's self-claimed title.
- Vault: a school storeroom holding old potions; target of the explosion.

## S01E03


Episode 3 — Coven Academy (Season 1)

Plot

- Open: Briar, Sasha and Tegan break the rules again and Graves slaps them with a week's detention plus a stripped oath that paralyzes any spell or charm they try for seven days ("blood to ash and ash to wind..."). The punishment: they must reorganize the entire vault. Punishment echoes the cold-open threat, hinting Graves knows what is out there and is bracing for it.
- Mr. Cole's class: Warlock boys are stuck teleporting objects (not themselves). He roasts Jake for dozing off, teases shape-shifting as the "hard stuff" they have not earned yet, and dismisses the students' rumor that they're planning to track the missing girls themselves: "there's a kernel of truth to every rumor," but only trained adults should handle vampires. He assigns a week's detention.
- Graves drops the bombshell that the missing girls are still alive — "only advanced magic could tell you that" — and warns Briar that her powers are flash over control. Briar fires back: "maybe you should stop underestimating me." Graves punishes the coven by locking their powers for a week ("seven suns shall see you bare, no spell shall spark, no charm shall dare") and adds that she has "a date with the devil."
- Detention in the vault: Briar and the girls bicker about whether Graves is hiding things and whether McKenna is friend or foe. They argue: Briar defended them to McKenna but also got their powers revoked. Briar's parents' divorce trauma resurfaces — "I'm not gonna let anything sneak up on me again" — and she is visibly shaking.
- The discovery: Tegan finds the 1998 Crescent Veil yearbook. Briar's mother Mary is in a photo with Ms. Graves and a vampire woman — labelled "Most Likely to Succeed (Magic)." This is the first concrete link showing Mary was a student here.
- Cut to1998 — Emma Graves's flashback: A vampire greets Emma in a house requiring an invitation; the vampire has just been "turned" (woke up) by being bitten and is now a hybrid, declaring "I'm the best of both worlds now, Emma. Hybrids are unstoppable. And beyond evil." The vampire tests Emma by inviting her to attack "your oldest friend," and forces her to choose between Briar and the coven: "Bring me Briar, Emma. This is the last time I'm going to ask nicely."
- Reveal drop: Graves and Mary were sisters in a coven. Mary left the coven, and the vampire (Mary's sister) accuses Emma of killing her out of resentment. "Tell me you're not evil enough to take out your own sister." "Sisters don't kill sisters." So Mary's "sister" — a hybrid vampire-witch — is the one who hunts the new generation and may be the dark force closing in on Briar.
- Back in present: Tegan and Sasha find an archivist (not a snitch — Beverly archetype) willing to break the "no talking in the vault" rule and help with a ritual. Tegan offers a summoning: Briar can talk to Mary if she admits something she's never told anyone.
- The sacrifice / confession: Briar reveals her deepest secret — her last words to Mary were "I wish she would just disappear" before a fight, Mary tried to call her about "something super urgent," and Briar ignored the calls. Mary went out looking for her after Briar broke curfew, was killed on the subway by a random attacker who has never been found. Briar's conclusion: "I'm the reason my mom is dead. I've never said that before." She asks the coven to help her find Mary's killer because she cannot do it alone.
- Ritual line: "Bound by womb and laid in clay, I call the dead to speak this day."
- The summoning fails at first ("It didn't work… I'm sorry, Briar"), then cuts to Mary speaking through the ritual circle: "Briar, honey, is that you?" The episode closes on that reveal.

Important character actions

- Briar: clashes with Graves in class, pushes back about being underestimated, admits her deepest guilt about Mary, and recruits the coven to help solve her mother's murder. The pink backpack / "Pisces" running bits are sidelined this episode; her anger-driven power is now tied to grief and self-blame rather than righteous sarcasm.
- Ms. Graves: escalates discipline (one-week detention, powers stripped, vault reorganization) but immediately pivots into caretaker mode, granting the coven access to a summoning because she cared about Mary. She tells Briar, "you were born with powers for a reason."
- Sasha: cements her role as the coven's empath — senses Briar's shaking, gently coaxes the real confession ("it has to be something we can't already tell"), and comforts her after the apparent ritual failure.
- Tegan: time-warper — locates the 1998 yearbook in the vault and broker contacts ("I'm an archivist, not a snitch"). Her willingness to defy Graves is now overt, not covert.
- Mr. Cole: warlock instructor; gates the warlocks' curriculum (teleporting objects first, shape-shifting later), confirms vampires are real and only trained adults should hunt them, and assigns detention for the warlock boys' planning.
- The 1998 vampire / Mary's sister: a witch-turned-hybrid who survived being bitten and now views hybrids as "unstoppable... and beyond evil." She insists Mary left the coven and accuses Graves of killing her; she demands Briar be brought to her. She is positioned as the central antagonist — the "ancient dark force" lore now has a face and a family tie.
- The vault archivist: introduces a new ally-adjacent role; emphasizes that talking to the dead costs a truth, not blood.

Newly established names and terms

- Crescent Veil Academy (1998 yearbook) — the historical name for Coven Academy, useful as an alias.
- "A date with the devil" — Graves' cryptic line when leaving; in context refers to confronting the hybrid / vampire antagonist.
- Hybrid (witch-turned-vampire) — a new classification distinct from a pure vampire; "unstoppable and beyond evil."
- The vault as both punishment location and sacred archive (yearbooks, oldest scrolls, first potions, original armor).
- "Sisters don't kill sisters" / "bring me Briar" — explicit framing of coven as family and Graves/Mary/the vampire as a sister-trio.
- The summoning ritual's sacrifice rule: the mourner must confess something never spoken aloud; the incantation "bound by womb and laid in clay."
- Charm-stone from S01E02 is not referenced here — Sasha's role has shifted from warning trinkets to active ritual support.

Continuity-relevant facts

- The 1998 chapter is now confirmed to be the same flashback era Graves keeps hinting at; the vampire in that era is Mary's coven-sister, not Mary herself.
- Mary Roberts attended Coven Academy under the name Crescent Veil Academy, was part of Graves' original coven, and left it. Her death in Brooklyn and the Coven threat are not coincidence — Briar's arrival is "because of my mom."
- Graves is confirmed as an original coven leader who has held back Briar's questions deliberately; her willingness to bend her own rules for the summoning is a major shift.
- The "missing girls" arc gains a new beat: Graves admits they are still alive and that only advanced magic could have confirmed it — i.e., she has been scrying or otherwise tracking them.
- Briar's mother was murdered on a New York subway by an unidentified attacker who has never been caught — the murder mystery now has a ritual-confirmed witness (Mary herself).
- "Bring me Briar, Emma" establishes that the dark force is targeting Briar specifically, not just any witch-in-training.
- The vault as plot hub: S01E01 introduced it as the school's sacred archive, S01E02 blew it up, S01E03 uses it as detention and the site of the year's most important discoveries (yearbook, summoning).

Jokes and references useful for later episodes

- "D for 'Didn't try'... 'Done with the basics'" — the warlock grading gag returns when Mr. Cole roasts Jake; pairs with the earlier "charity cases" / "wild card" McKenna bit to mark Briar as academically unproven despite raw power.
- "She got our powers revoked. She's… She's…" — the in-group gag pattern where Tegan and Sasha keep trying and failing to summarize Briar.
- "I always resented her for leaving the coven" — Graves' old wound; foreshadows a later confrontation in which the coven that abandoned Mary is judged by the one she chose.
- Briar's Pisces deflection returns: "She's like my best friend, and… she didn't want me to go on this date back at home" — the line doubles as both astrology banter and the literal last conversation with Mary.
- "Only advanced magic could tell you that" — Graves finally concedes she is running advanced scrying on the missing girls, re-contextualizing her earlier "no, no, that's a prediction" line.
- The 1998 flashback tone matches the SKILL guidance: period-appropriate, more formal, ritualized cadence from Graves and the vampire, contrasting the teen slang of present-day scenes.

Unresolved threads

- Is the 1998 vampire (Mary's sister / hybrid) alive in the present day, and is she the one who killed Mary in Brooklyn — or did she order it? The subway "random attacker" line is now suspicious.
- The "date with the devil" Graves mentioned — does it mean she is going to confront Mary's sister directly this same night, or is it a figure of speech?
- Mary's voice reached Briar during the summoning, but only one line is heard. What does Mary need to tell her about the "something super urgent" she tried to convey the night she was murdered?
- The hybrid claims Mary "left the coven." Why did Mary leave, and why did Graves let her?
- Briar's powers are locked for a week (seven suns) — will the coven be able to fight the hybrid without magic during that window?
- The archivist knows about advanced magic and is willing to break rules. Is she neutral, allied to Graves, or secretly connected to the antagonist?
- McKenna's "more powerful witch" line in S01E02 unlocked Briar's surge; the vault still needs reorganizing, and McKenna is unaccounted for in this episode's detention lineup.
- Jake's locator spell from S01E01 and the warlock boys' plan to find the missing girls both remain unresolved; Mr. Cole's warning that "only trained adults should handle it" suggests he is more involved than he lets on.
- "Bring me Briar, Emma" — the antagonist's specific demand for Briar implies Briar is the hybrid's endgame, not just another trainee; the reason is still unknown.

# Coven Academy — S01E06 Summary

## Episode plot

Opening flash: in a dark basement, three witches call for help and a figure named Jake is called by name — a callback to the missing girls from earlier episodes.

Cut to present. Jake is out with Ollie (newly turned vampire), feeding on a human victim. Ollie hesitates to bite ("I can't do it"). Jake tells him his "first human catch is always the best" and warns that when real hunger hits, "you'll feed… whoever's standing next to you." Ollie cannot bring himself to do it.

The next morning, Ms. Graves announces at assembly that the three missing classmates have been found alive, rescued from a witch–vampire hybrid that has now been destroyed. Lockdown is officially lifted and the school returns to magic lessons. Students cheer and look forward to Hex Fest, a clandestine party before the autumn exams.

In private, Agnes warns Graves that "the killer is still out there, Emma… the threat is just beginning." Graves dismisses her as an alarmist.

In the West Corridor (Cosmo Atrium), Ms. Graves scolds the trio for sneaking out and recovering their powers, then deflects when Briar points out that Tamora and her mother Mary had identical neck marks. She reveals the girls are in a "trial period" until the junior-year winter solstice formal, when covens are officially named during the Covening ceremony.

Briar meets Jake in the boys' wing. Jake is benched for the day by Mr. Cole as punishment for finding the missing girls first. Jake notes that Graves reacted with "wax doll level blankness" to the murder connection. He offers to help Briar investigate.

Jake teleports Briar back to the Tamora crime scene, which has been wiped clean. He casts a vision spell: "Through dust and time, let sight confide, unmask the face that they try to hide." He sees Tamora — but the killer's face is magically blocked. Jake concludes the killer's identity is being obscured by serious magic, and they flee.

In the girls' study area on Baronne Street, the coven studies for next week's Autumn Coven exam (Level 1 casting, protection charms, advanced potions). Briar confesses she and Jake snuck back to the crime scene; Sasha is more intrigued than scared. They debate whether Mary's death, the hybrid's death, and the kidnapped girls are connected, and Briar insists she has to know how the story ends.

To find Ollie, Sasha uses a rose petal Briar kept from their ghost-tour date. The three chant "Map it, mark it, draw it clear, show the path that leads from here." The spell yields the address 1395 St. Charles Avenue, but the building is listed as "address no longer in use." The girls insist on going together.

Meanwhile, Graves meets a man named Peter (father figure from her past, possibly1998 timeline). He tells her he feels watched and followed lately; she tells him to keep a lookout. Graves says nothing more is happening.

Briar, Sasha and Tegan arrive at the abandoned building on St. Charles Avenue. Briar asks Sasha about the warning crystal Sasha loaned her during the ghost tour — she has not seen it since then. They ring the bell; a doorman answers and they ask for Ollie. The episode ends on Briar seeing Ollie and gasping "Oh, my God."

## S01E07

# S01E07 Summary

## Plot
Briar leads Sasha and Tegan into a vampire neighborhood to confront Ollie after learning he's a vampire. Ollie reveals he was turned against his will; Tamora (his aunt) lured the trio onto the ghost tour as bait and exiled him to America. He believes Tamora is targeting Briar as part of a larger plan and is herself scared of something bigger coming. Before Briar can press further on her mother, Jake tracks them via a tracking spell. A fight erupts in which Jake warns Briar she'll "end up like your mom" — a remark that cuts deep and drives the wedge between them.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Ley lines / 靈脈** — global magical conduits that open at the witching hour of Winter Solstice; can be "poisoned" with a poured-in dose of dark magic.
- **Witching hour (靈夜)** — the midnight window on Winter Solstice when ley lines are open.
- **"Fluctus fuscos tenebrarum vos verte"** — William's dark-magic spell (Latin; rendered 幽暗之流，轉化爾等).
- **"Moon so high, with sun down low…"** — opening invocation of the ley-line ritual.
- **"Bind what moves, deny it flight. Seal it fast in golden light."** — the counter-seal used by Emma/Mary/Tamora.
- **Hex Fest** — past event referenced as the source of the ley-line spell idea; trick or genuine? Stays ambiguous.
- **Demon-blooded creatures** — William's umbrella term for vampires and werewolves; his stated rationale for the 1998 plan.
- **Council** — the magical governing body that stripped William's powers in 1998. Hasn't been localized yet but functions as the season's tribunal.
- **Sentry / 哨兵** — guard on duty the night of the 1998 ritual; killed in the confrontation.
- **Crescent Veil / 新月紗** — William's current school, location of the emergency-vault moonstone; heavily warded against vampires (who can only enter via a standing invitation).
- **Moonstone / 月長石** — rare restorative crystal; the only known way to restore powers stripped by dark magic.
- **Standing invitation** — Coven Academy's loophole that lets vampires in if a vampire like Ollie has been formally invited; the vampire clan plans to weaponize it.
- **Elixir of life / 長生不老藥** — the secret Sasha is chasing; located at Florence.
- **Giglio d'Ombra / 幽影百合學院** ("Florence") — the sister academy Sasha applied to; home of the deepest, most-guarded magical secrets in the world.
