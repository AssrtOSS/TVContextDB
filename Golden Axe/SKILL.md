# Golden Axe — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Overview

**Title:** Golden Axe
**Type:** American adult animated fantasy sitcom
**Creators:** Mike McMahan, Joe Chandler
**Premiere:** September 16, 2026 (Paramount+)
**Episodes:** Season 1, 10 episodes (all released simultaneously)
**Based on:** Sega's *Golden Axe* video game franchise (1989–)
**Animation:** Titmouse, Inc.
**Production:** CBS Eye Animation Productions, Sony Pictures Television, Original Film, Sega Sammy Group, Important Science

## World Setting

The series is set in **Yuria**, a medieval fantasy world inspired by the game's original setting. This is a land of magic, mythical creatures, and ancient prophecies. The world features:
- Enchanted forests and volcanic regions
- Magical potions and spell-casting
- Dwarven mines and barbarian tribes
- Shape-shifting and elemental sorcery
- A blend of high fantasy and comedic anachronism (the show is a sitcom with modern humor)

## Character Relationships & Backstory

- **Gilius, Ax, and Tyris** are the classic trio from the original 1989 game, united by their shared vendetta against **Death Adder** (死神艾德).
- **Gilius** — A dwarf from the mines of Wolud (沃鲁德矿山). His twin brother was killed by Death Adder's soldiers. He wields a battle axe and casts lightning magic.
- **Ax** — A barbarian whose mother was murdered by Death Adder's forces. He uses a claymore (two-handed broadsword) and commands earth/volcanic magic.
- **Tyris** — An amazon warrior whose parents were killed by Death Adder. She wields a longsword and commands devastating fire magic.
- **Hampton Squib** — A new character created for the TV series. An inexperienced, underprepared adventurer who joins the trio, providing fish-out-of-water comedy.
- **Chronos "Evil" Lait** — A beastman (humanoid panther) from *Golden Axe III*. In the show, he serves as an additional companion.

## Overall Plot Premise

The trio of warriors — Gilius Thunderhead, Ax Battler, and Tyris Flare — must save Yuria from **Death Adder**, an evil giant who has seized control of the land. Along the way, they are joined by the inexperienced Hampton Squib and the panther-warrior Chronos. The series plays the classic fantasy quest premise for comedy, with the heroes facing absurd challenges and bumbling their way through epic adventures.

## Speaking Style Notes

- **Gilius** — Gruff, short-tempered, uses dwarven exclamations. Speaks in clipped sentences.
- **Ax** — Earnest, straightforward, sometimes slow on the uptake. Barbarian bluster.
- **Tyris** — Confident, sarcastic, magical jargon. The most composed of the trio.
- **Hampton** — Nervous, verbose, modern-sounding (the comedy relief). His dialogue should feel contemporary and slightly out of place in the fantasy setting.
- **Chronos** — Cool, measured, occasional feline mannerisms.

## Cultural Context

- The original *Golden Axe* (1989) is a Sega arcade classic; the show targets both nostalgic gamers and new audiences.
- The TV series is a **comedy/adult animation** — it parodies fantasy tropes rather than playing them straight.
- Created by **Mike McMahan** (known for *Star Trek: Lower Decks*), so expect similar irreverent, referential humor.
- The show originally was ordered by **Comedy Central** before moving to **Paramount+** for premiere.
