## S22E12

# American Dad S22E12 — "The Book of Roger" / "The Last Supper" / "Downstairs"

## Episode Plot
Three unconnected storylines converge thematically around false hope, protocol, and salvation:

**A. Mars — Father Roger**  
Roger poses as a priest on a failed Mars colony, burying the only congregant (Daryl/Carol). He prays for a flock; a wanderer arrives with expensive boots and a heavy sack. Roger's conscience (or God) convinces him to rob the man. The sack contains only shoe polish and a book Roger's already read. The wanderer dies; Roger buries him too, then prays again for a soul to save — and a spaceship full of suicidals appears overhead.

**B. Earth Fragment 151822 — Jeff & Al**  
Jeff (Jeffrey) and Al are debt-bond miners on a corporate asteroid. They find an antique space blaster, escape onto the collection train, but the blaster's recoil launches Al into space. Al refuses rescue, telling Jeff to live — and to *not* start "TuttleJeff Shrimp." Jeff escapes alone.

**C. S.S. Deucalion (Generational Ship) — The Smiths**  
Stan, Francine, Steve, Hayley live in "Downstairs," a rigid protocol-bound kitchen serving the "Upstairs" crew on a 500-year colonization mission (year 284). The corn cutter breaks; Stan invokes Article 14 §3-22-P to go upstairs for a replacement. He discovers the mission is doomed: New Earth is a superheated gas sphere, fuel insufficient for alternatives. The Upstairs crew is holding a cyanide-fueled suicide orgy. They thank Downstairs for "non-monotonous" meals, forbid future contact, and gift a music player.

## Important Character Actions
- **Roger**: Murders/lets die a wanderer for money; fails; ends up with a ship full of souls (the Deucalion crew).
- **Jeff**: Escapes certain death; loses best friend Al; inherits a dream (shrimp business) he's told to abandon.
- **Stan**: Breaks generations of protocol to go upstairs; learns the truth; returns with only a radio.
- **Francine**: Supports Stan informally; her corn-heavy menu is validated by Upstairs.
- **Al**: Sacrifices himself so Jeff lives; dies in space.

- **Jeff's backstory**: Born into company debt on Fragment 151822; never knew Earth.
- **The Smiths' situation**: They've been Downstairs for generations (Stan's father, grandfather never went up). This is either far-future or an alt-timeline — not main canon continuity.
- **Bullock**: Exists as ship captain "Jean Luc Bullock Memari" — a *Star Trek* + Bullock amalgam.

## S22E08 — Francine's Head Injury & the John Mayer Radio Delusion

## Episode Plot
Six months after Stan accidentally dropped Francine 300 feet, shattering her skull, the couple is in therapy with Dr. Fay. Francine has recovered physically but harbors a delusion that John Mayer created a satellite radio station to communicate with her. Stan refuses to call it an accident — framing it as "an opportunity for growth" — and dismisses Dr. Fay's concerns about psychosis. When John Mayer invites Francine to meet him at the Win Hotel in Las Vegas, the couple drives from Virginia (Stan won't fly; he dislikes "paper trails"). At the hotel, Francine discovers other women with the same delusion and is embarrassed *for them* — not herself. Stan buys a hyperbaric chamber from Roger's merch-guy persona and traps himself in it; Francine overhears him calling her "crazy" and bolts. Stan has an emotional meltdown backstage with Wayne Newton, confessing guilt through a "donkey shame" metaphor (mishearing "Danke Schoen"). Wayne helps him deduce John Mayer is broadcasting from the penthouse. There, Dr. Fay reveals *she* was the one John Mayer was actually reaching — she broke in and tied him up. John Mayer confirms he met Francine years ago at a Bed Bath & Beyond and started the station for her, but admits he was "casting too wide a net." Francine tells Stan she loves and chooses him; Stan transforms from "donkey shame" to "horse proud." Dr. Fay claims the entire ordeal was her therapeutic plan.

## Important Character Actions
- **Stan**: Causes Francine's skull fracture, refuses responsibility for 6 months, drives to Vegas sleep-deprived (18 hours straight), breaks down crying with Wayne Newton about being a "trapped donkey," finally admits guilt, reconciles with Francine
- **Francine**: Packs zip ties, snacks, and a hospital catheter for the road trip; listens to John Mayer's broadcast the entire time without Stan noticing; escapes Stan's custody through the casino; ultimately tells Stan she chooses him and likes "weird [bleep]"
- **Dr. Fay**: Gives Stan her private cell number; flies to Vegas independently; discovers she was John Mayer's true intended recipient; infiltrates the penthouse via sewer pipes; ties up John Mayer; claims credit for orchestrating the Smiths' reconciliation
- **John Mayer**: Broadcasts "Life with John Mayer" satellite radio; plays songs as coded messages; invites Francine to Vegas; admits to the Bed Bath & Beyond meeting; concedes his "net was too wide"
- **Wayne Newton**: Performs "Danke Schoen" at the Win Hotel; provides Stan emotional counsel; identifies that John Mayer is in the penthouse; physically helps Stan get upstairs (attributes his agility to sleeping in a hyperbaric chamber)
- **Roger (as Leoncio Bedoncio)**: Poses as Wayne Newton's official merch guy; sells Stan a hyperbaric chamber (price escalates from $53K to $65K to $80K across the episode); Wayne later says he doesn't work for him

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Dr. Fay** — Smith family couples therapist/counselor; has Stan's private cell number
- **Leoncio Bedoncio** — Roger's persona; Wayne Newton's "official merch guy"
- **Win Hotel** — Las Vegas hotel where John Mayer broadcasts and Wayne Newton performs
- **"Life with John Mayer"** — the satellite radio show name
- **Hyperbaric chamber** — Wayne Newton's sleep device; Roger sells a scam version to Stan
- **"Donkey shame"** — Stan's malapropism of "Danke Schoen"; becomes his metaphor for guilt over the accident
- **"Horse proud"** — Stan's counterpart metaphor for feeling secure in his marriage after the reconciliation
- **"Catacomb-worthy skull"** — Stan's bizarre compliment for Francine's reconstructed skull

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Stan dropped Francine 300 feet, causing a catastrophic skull fracture (doctors "think they've found almost all" of the bone fragments)
- Francine was hospitalized for months; her catheter dates to that stay
- The accident is a known event to the family — Steve and Hayley reference it openly
- Francine and John Mayer met briefly years ago at a Bed Bath & Beyond while both looking at shower radios
- Roger runs merchandise scams at Las Vegas entertainment venues under a persona
- Stan and Francine are in ongoing couples therapy (Dr. Fay has their file)

- Francine still believes John Mayer is sending her messages at the end of the episode (quotes Seal's "Crazy" as a message for her), suggesting the delusion persists at least partially
