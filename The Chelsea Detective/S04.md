# S04E03 — The Chelsea Detective

## Plot

Max and the Chelsea CID investigate the murder of **Sonia Blessing**, 52, owner of "Blessings Holistic," a beauty salon where she also lived in the flat above. She is found dead on a Saturday morning by staff arriving for work. Ashley's post-mortem confirms death by strangulation (fractured hyoid bone, petechiae, dislocated right shoulder, mouth covered from behind); the killer wore gloves. No CCTV covers the flat or salon, but the intercom suggests Sonia buzzed her killer up voluntarily. She was dressed down in loungewear, with a bottle of Prosecco out and a bath running — waiting for a guest she was comfortable with, not a date. Her phone is missing; her laptop was damaged in the struggle.

A subplot runs through the episode: **Layla** reveals that the IOPC is investigating Max's arrest of **Charlie Weir**, a mugger of his aunt Olivia; Charlie has complained Max used "undue force" twisting his arm. Max is interviewed that afternoon but ultimately cleared thanks to witness statements and CCTV (he admits in confidence to Layla the episode made him doubt his own conduct and question his work/life balance).

Investigation progresses through three suspect tracks:

1. **Mark Hart** — Sonia's most recent ex, a martial arts instructor (karate and tai chi) at the leisure centre. They met six months earlier when Sonia took self-defence classes after being followed at night. He broke it off because she wanted something more physical, he wanted commitment; he asked her out again a few weeks before her death and she refused. He has a tenuous alibi (caring for his dementia-suffering father at home) and is later caught on Battersea Park CCTV near the cafe, but only as a coincidence — he runs a tai chi class at the Old English Garden and saw Sonia leave the cafe on her own.

2. **Johnny Leonard** — a DJ at the Pig's Ear, barred from the salon after becoming aroused during three massages. He has a solid alibi (working a "Naughty Noughties" DJ set until 1:30 am) confirmed by his manager Ed and bar CCTV. He is found to have planted a motion-activated hidden camera in the ladies' loos at the pub and is sacked. He was also seen lingering near the salon — and is revealed to have hidden a similar covert camera at Sonia's flat four weeks before the murder; the footage captures the attack (face not clearly visible, but a palm print on the massage table later matches the killer).

3. **Reece Gilmarsh** — the Masons' dog walker and brother of **Nancy Gilmarsh**, an 18-year-old trainee who worked with Sonia (then "Linda") 15 years earlier at "Hair by Moira" in Croydon. Linda (Sonia) had dislocated her shoulder the day before a bride's hen-party hot-stone massage, so she let Nancy do it; Nancy burned the bride's back, the local papers crucified Nancy ("Heartless. Trainee parties as bride-to-be mops her tears"), and Linda skipped town, leaving Nancy to take the blame. Reece sent the anonymous blackmail letter ("Linda, I know what you did.") to Sonia at the Battersea Park cafe a week before the murder, demanding money for his sister. Sonia agreed to pay and gave him her number. Reece is arrested fleeing into the Clarence Bailey estate with class As (coke and oxy) he'd hidden by the bins; he offers a vague Ladbroke Grove drug-dealing alibi for the night of the murder. He is not the killer.

A fourth suspect emerges: **Robbie Campbell**, Linda's documented abusive ex (five domestic-violigence reports over eight years, 2012; breach of the peace 2013). Records show he lived with her in Hastings 2007–2012 — the same period as the 2009 stair fall and surgery at Conquest Hospital. The "white, bald, older man" Nancy saw at Linda's old flat is identified as him. He is tracked to a Tottenham address, but his sister **Tracy Campbell** reveals he has been dead for a year — directly cremated at Enfield Crematorium on 3 October last year, after Tracy collected his benefits. He is not the killer either.

A separate thread then reveals that **Sonia Blessing does not exist**: her NI number fails to resolve. A DVLA error is traced to a 2015 deed-poll name change. Ten years ago she was **Linda Norris**, struck off the General Council of Massage Therapists in 2013 for failing in her duty of care. The burner phone that had been ringing her for three weeks — last text "On my way," 23:03 the night of the murder — pings off a cell tower near the Masons' house. A camera installed by Johnny Leonard in the salon captured a pre-booked massage session with **Tara Mason** (the only known overcharged client) where, instead of undressing, the two simply talk; three weeks ago Sonia hands Tara a small object.

The penny drops: Sonia recognised another domestic-abuse victim and was helping her escape. The packed capsule wardrobe, the £3,000 cash, the Loch Lomond Airbnb chalet booking starting the day after the murder, and the burner phone were all for **Tara** — funded by Sonia's overcharging. The killer is **Piers Mason**, Tara's husband, a controlling banker. He discovered the burner phone hidden in the mattress, set a trap by cancelling his golf weekend, drugged his wife (honey instead of sugar in tea), went to Sonia's flat in her place and strangled her, then took the drugged Tara to A&E just after 1:00 am to fabricate a caring-husband alibi. He is arrested at home after a palm print on the massage table is matched to his 2021 DUI arrest file.

## The Chelsea Detective S04E04 Summary

### Episode Plot
Architect **Rowan Jameson** receives a lifetime achievement award at the Royal Chelsea Hotel. During the ceremony, he falls from the penthouse balcony and dies. DI **Max Arnold** and DS **Layla Walsh** investigate. Initial suspect **Victor Budimir** (Rowan's former Cambridge colleague and business partner, with a history of plagiarism accusations) claims he was attacked by a man in a baseball cap fleeing Rowan's suite. No hotel CCTV exists due to privacy policy.

Rowan had been opposing the **Petrov Foundation**'s controversial modern extension at **St Cuthbert's College, Cambridge**, funded by Belarusian-born philanthropist **Sir Francis Petrov**. The investigation shifts to Cambridge, where Rowan's Cambridge contemporaries — **Charlotte Kendall** (College Principal), **Victor Budimir**, and **India Vanstone** (Rowan's first wife) — all have motives.

Two days later, **June Goldman** (Charlotte's PA of 10 years) is found strangled in the Principal's house. **Cole McIlroy**, a troubled ex-serviceman June hired at the college, becomes prime suspect when his DNA is found on the murder weapon (a scarf). A hearing aid discovered near where Rowan's body landed yields DNA matching skin fragments under Rowan's fingernails — it belongs to **Simon Kendall**, Charlotte's husband.

### Key Character Actions
- **Max Arnold**: Leads investigation; confronts Petrov at his houseboat; deduces Simon Kendall's involvement via hearing aid DNA; arrests the Kendalls.
- **Layla Walsh**: Stays in Cambridge to work with cousin **DI Martha McGuire**; interviews suspects; uncovers Petrov's CCTV lie (his car left *after* the murder); discovers the Kendalls' financial motive.
- **Martha McGuire** (Cambridge DI, Layla's cousin): Provides local support; hosts Layla; coordinates joint investigation.
- **Charlotte & Simon Kendall**: Orchestrate both murders. Simon pushes Rowan off the balcony (flying to Chelsea, not stranded in Scotland). They frame Victor Budimir, then strangle June when she discovers Petrov's bribe letter (a Portuguese retirement home + cash for supporting the extension) and tells Rowan, who threatens exposure in his speech. They plant Cole's DNA on the scarf (Simon obtained it during sex with Cole) and stage a break-in at Cole's room.
- **Francis Petrov**: Lies about his movements; his driver hits India Vanstone; now under investigation for bribery/corruption.
- **India Vanstone**: Reveals Rowan stole the Chaloner Award-winning design from Charlotte; working with a Sunday paper to expose Petrov.
- **Ashley Wilton**: Forensics — matches hearing aid DNA to Simon; confirms no Victor Budimir DNA on Rowan.
