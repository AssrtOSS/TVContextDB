## S02E01

# Marshals S02E01 — "All Hat No Cattle" 概要

## 剧情
电视网系列《法警小队》第二季首播。凯斯·达顿在开车途中走神，开场画面是一段闪回/前情：东营前一次遭扫射，袭击者从一辆路过的皮卡里开枪。凯斯事后意识到"父亲应当保护孩子，而非不断伤害他"，于是把儿子泰特·达顿送到汤姆·韦弗那里去钓鱼散心，希望汤姆能帮泰特平复心境。这一决定成为整季的导火索。

与此同时，团队内部正经历重组：安德里亚·克鲁斯原本准备调回华盛顿特区，团队成员私下惋惜"要找个得力的人顶上"。但雷恩沃特在国会山就迁移矿场一事作证后，安德里亚被召回蒙大拿报到，安德莉亚在雨中重返办公桌，自嘲"LZ 偏了好几千英里"。

回到庄园时，律师斯坦·科布找上门，递交汤姆的"正式报价"——愿以更高价收购东营，并撂下威胁："你儿子的未来可全押在这儿了"。凯斯强硬回绝，但当调查深入后，团队发现汤姆早已布局：汤姆的牧场工头杰布·巴勒斯正是东营袭击者的头目，杰布曾在加拿大第二联合特遣部队服役，十年前因多次受审被不名誉退伍、且有蓄意伤人前科，被安德莉亚和贝尔标注为"可能是负责这支小队的人，但不太可能是幕后主脑"。贝利·米尔斯与贝尔在尼斯勒简易机场附近遭伏击，车是向部族警长借的，导致安德莉亚和贝尔都受损。安德莉亚由此确信韦弗就是幕后主脑。

## 重要角色动作

凯斯·达顿
独自驾车前往怀俄明州大角镇韦弗的私人牧场赴约。在路上，他让多莉·韦弗拼车同行，把她当作情报来源和情感缓冲。多莉先是被凯斯恐吓："如果敢有任何企图，我会让你女儿的血溅满墙壁"，随后反过来用情感筹码稳住他："我见过我爸为家人放弃复仇"，并透露韦弗在德州福克湖附近有处房产，"周围布满监控"。多莉还说出了凯斯做梦都没想到的关键情报："如果我爸真打算挑起战争，他会去一个自己有军队的地方……怀俄明州的一个牧场"。随后，安德莉亚通过调查发现，韦弗表面声称拥有八万头牛、分散在俄克拉荷马和德州的饲养场，但发票数据精确到磅、每周一致，明显是"幽灵牛"——汤姆实际上在运营一场价值两亿美元的庞氏骗局。

皮特·"卡尔"·卡尔文
闪回中与凯斯并肩作战，警戒"他在包抄我们"，随后两人撤入一处据点反击，撤退时回头看："他们正在撤退，带走我们需要的答案"。结尾在医疗室里，他一边包扎伤口，一边透露自己要去看肿瘤科医生："本周我要陪你去盐湖城看肿瘤科医生……那儿人少一些"。这一段说明卡尔确实身患重病，未来几集将面对生死抉择。

伊莎贝尔·"贝尔"·图雷克-斯金纳
和米尔斯·基特尔一同前往尼斯勒简易机场附近的拖车公园询问线索时遭伏击，靠强行冲撞才脱身。回到团队后，她冷静指出"我们得顺藤摸瓜查清这场阴谋"。

安德里亚·克鲁斯
在返岗的第一天就陷入感情纠结。她对凯斯依依不舍："听了你那番道别之后，哪还走得了"。面对这场危机，她在旁听着贝尔的自嘲"我们是个威胁"，随即开始分析："要是韦弗知道我们盯上他了，泰特就不再是筹码，而是弃子了"。结尾时，她决定留下来，"留下来会成为蒙大拿人"。这条线暗示她留在蒙大拿后，将更深入地参与凯斯和多莉的私人事务。

多莉·韦弗
对凯斯有真实的感情，这让她陷入痛苦的道德困境——她主动帮凯斯打听父亲的计划，甚至陪他同车赶赴父亲的据点，却在路上说出"我会让你女儿的血溅满墙壁"，旋即崩溃大哭。凯斯没有完全放下戒备："你不是我的搭档，你是我的筹码"，但多莉反过来用情感筹码稳住他："你越生气，思维就越混乱……如果我爸迟迟收不到科布的消息会怎样？"。在最后摊牌时，汤姆强迫多莉在场，试图用"亲情斩不断"来压制凯斯，但多莉说出"我也有过自己的希望和梦想，但他总是设法让我留下来……把他的基业变成了我的使命"，最后平静地对凯斯说："我认识的他绝不会去敲诈勒索"。最终多莉被汤姆的手下杀死，悲壮地倒下："我所做的一切全是为了他"，成为本季最具情感冲击力的死亡。

汤姆·韦弗
开场时他是看似关心孩子的"长辈"，让凯斯把泰特送来钓鱼。但镜头一转，揭示韦弗早已指挥袭击凯斯家。结尾处他与凯斯对峙，撕掉伪装："我所有努力的成果都受到威胁"。当凯斯拿出武器时，韦弗冷笑"凡事都可以谈判"。最后被凯斯逼至死角时，他没有选择反击，而是说出最具威胁性的话："这是你应得的复仇。带着多莉死去的痛苦活下去，才是你应得的惩罚"。这一句彻底揭示了韦弗的行事哲学：不求胜利，只求让敌人余生都活在阴影里。结尾安德里亚带来的情报是："韦弗两年前在怀俄明州被永久禁止发售和销售证券"，揭示了他的欺诈前科。

凯斯家牛仔
死在部族警长借出的车里，被杀手灭口前泄露了雷恩沃特的行踪，成为本季的第一具尸体。

怀俄明州地方警长
在多莉护送凯斯进韦弗牧场的途中拦住他们，搜走凯斯的步枪。汤姆解释"这件事必须光明正大"。这是"地方势力被金钱收买"主题的延续。

Dolly
死在了试图保护凯斯、却被父亲利用的时刻。她的遗言是"我做错了什么？除了投胎当了我的儿子？"——揭示韦弗的残酷逻辑：连自己的女儿都只是棋局的一部分。

泰特·达顿
被汤姆扣押作人质。在被困期间，他保持冷静，没有崩溃。凯斯救出他时，泰特说："你来救我了，爸。就像你一直做的那样"。这句台词与开场"父亲应当保护孩子"的主题形成闭环。

Jimmy Hurdstrom（吉米·赫德斯特罗姆）
通过德州福克湖的线索引出——他在德州的6666牧场工作，本季将正式加入法警团队。

## S02E02

# S02E02 "End of Watch" — Marshals

## Plot
Picking up after Tate's rescue from Tom Weaver, Kayce and the Marshals team juggle two arcs: Tate's slow recovery at East Camp and a manhunt for Wayne Plummer — a fired Gallatin County Sheriff's deputy turned suppressor manufacturer now killing current and former officers who disciplined him. The team serves a no-knock warrant on Plummer's garage, where Belle steps on a pressure-plate IED (defused by Kayce) and they uncover a cottage industry of homemade suppressors plus a 300-page grievance manifesto. Plummer ambushes Gallatin deputies with his own truck, kills Kyle Husted, wounds Cheryl Lundrigen, then mails the manifesto to a TV station. The team traces his kill list to a "dignified transfer" ceremony for Husted at the coroner's office. Cal and Belle get pinned by a sniper; Kayce and Andrea clear the perimeter and confront Boone Plummer — Wayne's son — whom Kayce talks down. Wayne dies in the raid; Boone surrenders. In a parallel thread, Cal privately tells Kayce and Andrea he has a Pancoast lung tumor and faces chemo, radiation, then surgery. Maddie reconnects with Cal and introduces her anxious young son Braxton.

## Important character actions
- **Kayce Dutton** defuses the pressure-plate IED at the Plummer garage; leads the breach at the dignified-transfer ceremony; talks Boone Plummer off the rooftop ("There's no shame in being loyal to family. Just 'cause he marched you through hell doesn't got to become the devil. Let today be his legacy, not yours."); shields Tate from Mo/Rainwater's "muscle" at East Camp; visits John Dutton's grave with Tate.
- **Pete "Cal" Calvin** privately discloses his Pancoast tumor diagnosis to Kayce and Andrea; insists on no special treatment; reveals his Salt Lake City trip the day before was the cancer specialist visit; hides the news from Maddie.
- **Isabel "Belle" Turek-Skinner** stands on the IED without flinching, later pinned with Cal at the ceremony.
- **Andrea "Montana/Dre" Cruz** partners with Miles on the car-LLC lead; covers the dignified-transfer site with Kayce; Harry visits just to tell her he is glad she is sticking around.
- **Miles Kittle** still on desk duty after the S1 finale injury; uncovers Plummer's dormant LLC and three "bug-out" cars; clashes with Cal over keeping the cancer secret from Maddie (his girlfriend).
- **Harry Gifford** quietly admits to Cal that he was Flathead County Sheriff 18 years ago and signed Plummer's dismissal order — making him Plummer's real target at the warrant house; later admits he personally dinged Boone's academy application.
- **Maddie** explains her Bozeman move as "followed a guy, didn't work out"; introduces Braxton (her son) and his stuffed moose "Moosey"; agrees to a slumber party after Braxton overhears "cop-killer" news.
- **Tate Dutton** still anxious: recurring stomachaches, makes Kayce pinky-promise he won't get hurt again; tells Kayce "It's the Dutton way" at John's grave.
- **Thomas Rainwater** brings sage and his nephew Troy to East Camp to help rebuild; later phones Kayce to offer Tate sanctuary at Broken Rock for "peace."
- **Mo** delivers fry bread and wojapi (his mother made them); reassigns the plate to Maddie.
- **Boone Plummer** arrives as second shooter at the dignified transfer; surrenders after Kayce's appeal and repeatedly apologizes.
- **Wayne Plummer** killed during the raid.
- **Corrine Irons** (new) leads a reservation protest at Broken Rock; her husband Nathan and Cameron Standing Bear died during the mine fight; threatens to rally votes against Rainwater if he does not deliver.

## S02E06

# S02E06 Summary — Marshals

## Episode Plot
Cal is in Salt Lake City for a mid-treatment oncology appointment at the VA, leaving Kayce as acting team lead. Mo’s nephew **Troy Brings Plenty** goes missing after hiking at **River Run State Park** (Loy Trailhead) looking for agates for his sister’s baby-shower rattle. Kayce and Mo find Troy’s body at the base of a cliff. **Sheriff Briggs** (Broadwater County) rules it a suicide and blocks their investigation. Kayce and Mo discover **poached elk carcasses** (heads sawed off, backstraps removed) nearby and track boot prints to a poacher’s hide. The poacher witnessed Troy arguing with another man at the overlook, saw the man strike Troy with a flashlight and push him off the cliff, and hands over a cracked flashlight lens as evidence. Meanwhile, **Andrea** accompanies **Reed** (a veteran she met at the bar) to the VA; Reed suffers a PTSD crisis, pulls a gun, and Andrea talks him down.
