## S05E01

# Animal Control S05E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Emily is suspended (hearing in 4 weeks) and **Templeton Dudge** serves as interim precinct boss. He imposes new utility belts with his self-written "origin story" Employee Handbook. Frank leads a mutiny, but Templeton counters by assigning the career-making call — **Taima**, the Seattle Seahawks' live mascot (an augur hawk) escaped at Pocket Beach — to Daisy. Frank goes rogue anyway. Meanwhile, **Roman Park** returns with a flashy private animal-control startup (drones, driverless trucks) and announces he's secured a city pilot contract to run **Precinct 17**, threatening to absorb all of Seattle Animal Control. The episode ends with Frank and Templeton forming **"The Coalition"** to fight Roman.

## S05E02

# Animal Control S05E02 — Episode Summary

## Plot

The precinct is under corporate and bureaucratic pressure: customer-service scores have dropped, rival outfit Pax Romana is winning public favor with free purple water bottles, and precinct boss Templeton is being grilled at City Hall by "the Commandant" (Reggie, the mayor's cousin). Emily, recently demoted, has been rebranded as "Citizen Czar" — a fake title Templeton invented after watching Anastasia. To hit perfect tens across the board, the team must fix Truck 12's lone bad review from a customer named Julie S.

Two parallel storylines drive the episode. In the A-plot, Frank and Shred's response to Julie S.'s complaint spirals into a public confession, a sketchy apology visit, and ultimately Frank going on a date with Julie to "save their jobs." In the B-plot, Patel recruits Emily and Shred to scare off Addy's new boyfriend Walt, a tall animal-loving dork, so Addy will get back on her flight to India and Patel can reclaim his "me time."

The B-plot backfires when Patel accidentally tranqs Walt, who surprisingly enjoys the experience; Emily accidentally encourages Addy to sleep with Walt; and Addy then announces she's breaking up with him anyway. The A-plot resolves with Frank's date leading to a 10/10 review — but it's a "pity ten" after Julie alludes to his performance issues. The episode closes on a recurring crow Frank insulted earlier, clearly coming back for revenge.

## Important Character Actions

- Frank: Complains about crawl spaces and Halloween props while rescuing a French bulldog named Barkley. Refuses accountability for bad reviews, then publicly takes the blame for Shred's offense (air-hankying into Julie's bird spa). Visits Julie to apologize, charms her, gets her number, and ends up on a date. Tells a crow to "take a hike" — it returns at the end.
- Shred (Frederick Taylor): Discovers the bad review was his fault — he was caught on Julie's Ring camera blowing his nose into her bird fountain. Publicly confesses, offers his resignation, calls himself "a repugnant menace," and stands on a desk to take accountability. Later supports Frank via group FaceTime during the date.
- Emily: Reveals Templeton demoted her to "Citizen Czar." Coaches Frank on apologies, sets up the review-fix, and tries to break up Addy and Walt. While doing so, accidentally validates Addy's relationship and nudges her toward losing her virginity. Flirts with Frank throughout ("babe" slips out; he calls her "Czar").
- Patel: Wants Addy shipped off to India so he can walk around his house naked again. Oversees the breakup plot, accidentally shoots Walt with a tranq gun, forges paperwork to cover it up, and admits his grandfather "knows a guy" for blue pills.
- Victoria: On the periphery; sarcastically backs Frank, comments on his performance anxiety, and shares in the team's gleeful group FaceTime to Frank mid-date.
- Templeton: Off-site all episode; Emily mentions he's at City Hall getting reamed out by the Commandant over the scores.

## S05E03

# Episode S05E03 — Plot Summary

## Cold Open / Setup
Precinct 17 has been shut down by **Roman Park's** private company **Pax Romana**, and Roman is closing municipal precincts in numerical order — Frank's unit is almost next. Officers from Precinct 17 released the animals from Truck 46 ("Noah's Ark") as the precinct closed. Temple referencing it as "the purple nightmare" coming for them.

## A-Plot — Frank & Templeton's Alliance vs. Roman
- **Coalition of unpleasant necessity**: Frank and Templeton (Emily's wording) have allied their precincts to compete with Pax Romana, who is beating them to every call.
- Emily stands behind the coalition publicly but in private says "this is a marriage of convenience."
- Emily has a **brass-marmot lapel pin** — gift from the "commandant," representing Washington's state animal (Olympic Marmot). She wears it through the episode.
- Problem: Roman is reaching every dispatch first. Emily brings in IT guy **Alex** to upgrade the precinct system; Frank and Temple deduce the IT visit itself is how Roman eavesdropped.
- Temple tells Emily to project strength in front of officers; she cries off-screen; Victoria tells Emily to "go across the street, order a matcha, and then cry in the ladies room like a big boy."

## B-Plot — Undercover Mission (Emily as Tabitha Buttons / "Tabs")
- Emily Price is still suspended (in-city service suspension — referenced as "sexual predator / suspended for boinking Shred on the office couch," a callback).
- To investigate Roman, Frank and Temple recruit Emily to interview at Pax Romana under the alias **Tabitha Buttons** ("Tabs" per her nephew).
- Practice run: Emily fails ("I cannot, I cannot"); Frank outfits her with a concealed mic ("double-bag the mic 'cause she sweats").
- Emily enters Pax Romana; Roman sees through the "Tabitha" cover immediately, pushes her to admit she's Emily Price, the disgraced supervisor.
- Roman's "loyalty test": shave Emily's armpit with a straight razor. Emily weathers it and covertly extracts the key intel: **Roman has a hidden microphone in the precinct** — but he boasted about it just to test her.
- Reveal: there is no truck bug. **The bug is in Emily's Olympic Marmot lapel pin.** Temple figures this out off-screen; Frank keeps wearing the pin through to the end of act.
- Roman releases a petting-zoo camelid (callamé family) two blocks from the precinct while Emily is still mid-interview, then humiliates Frank and Temple by dropping the Waikiki puzzle piece he stole from Frank's apartment — exposing that Roman has had access to Frank's home ("did you break into my home, you maniac?").

## C-Plot — Patel & Victoria Babysit the Pig
- A stray pig ("this little non-kosher nightmare") arrived the previous night; Frank is babysitting it because it won't sleep in the kennel.
- The pig is affectionately called **"Grumpus"** by Frank during the interview setup.
- While Emily is undercover, Patel and Victoria handle the "loose dog" call. They form a **band** ("Slash and Axl" — GNR reference) using Patel's office drums and Victoria's guitar pickup; Roman's car audio system plays during the wire feed. Alex (IT guy) joins on guitar briefly before being outed as Roman's mole.
- The pig escapes Victoria's scrunchie sedation and is found later in the parking lot.

## Act 3 — Rabid Dog Response (Pin-Bug Payoff)
- Dispatch: rabid dog at **1515 Alonzo Boulevard**.
- The precinct uses the pin to monitor Roman's radio; they arrive first.
- Roman's Pax Romana team arrives with **military-style response**: helicopters, mini-birds ("fire up the mini birds"), thermal vision, smoke grenades.
- The "rabid dog" turns out to be **Schwarzkopf**, the therapy dog at the **Veterans Administration Long Term Care** facility. Frank de-escalates with "the human touch" — sits and comforts the dog in front of veteran residents.
- Smoke grenade at the VA triggers combat flashbacks in the veterans; news reporter **Tracey Palladino** covers the scene on camera.
- Roman is forced on-camera to apologize; Emily is publicly absent during the climax ("I wanted to let you guys have your moment").

## Resolution
- **Emily is reinstated.** Emily lifts her "temporary ban," role TBD ("not in a position of authority" + "can't have [her] around unaccompanied men"). She negotiates conditions (her couch steam-cleaned and saged twice).
- **Emily's couch**: still on the precinct roof ("I figured it'd seen enough action") — implicit callback to Frank/Emily hookup on the office couch.
- **Patel / Victoria swap**: Victoria admits she set Patel up with Alex; Alex bonds with Patel (bass / catch / beer); the band falls apart.
- **The pig** remains with Victoria; renamed **"Piggy Stardust"** (Bowie reference) at the end. Victoria declares she'll still eat pork but loves the pig.

## S05E06

# Animal Control S05E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Frank's cop brother Patrick (Officer Shaw) gave away their mother's cast iron pan — a family heirloom steeped in decades of chicken-dinner memories. Frank recruits Patel to break into Patrick's condo and steal it back during Emily and Shred's housewarming/"Couple's Launch Party." The heist goes sideways when Patrick catches Patel; Frank negotiates a "prisoner swap" (pan for Patel) but ultimately relents and lets Patrick keep it. Meanwhile, Emily and Shred navigate co-hosting tensions (bar cart placement, gift etiquette), Victoria calls out Daisy's fabricated grandmother story, and the group ends the night eating Mom's chicken recipe together — until Shred scrubs the pan's seasoning clean with industrial cleaner, resetting 40–50 years of flavor.
