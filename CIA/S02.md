## S02E02

# CIA S02E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
Viktor Molotov, a veteran SVR officer and Colin's longtime adversary, arrives poisoned with ricin (12 hours to live). He offers a deal: help him find his poisoner, Gregor Lyalin, and he'll expose an SVR operation to eliminate US assets in Russia. The team verifies the poisoning, tracks Gregor through SVR logistics man Misha Botvinnik, and captures Gregor at a Brooklyn nightclub using a "Colorado two-step" ruse. Gregor reveals the real target: Sarah Reed, a communications contractor carrying Moscow asset names, arriving at Fulton Station in two hours. The team mounts a decoy operation swapping Teddy for Sarah on the train. Meanwhile, Viktor escapes custody to see Lyudmila Shushkin, a cellist he once surveilled and fell in love with, performing at Lincoln Center. At the station, they identify Pasha—Viktor's own protégé—as the true poisoner. A chase ensues; Pasha is captured, Teddy rescued. Viktor is talked down from executing Pasha. The SVR team is deported via State Department arrangement. Colin declines the Chief of Station vacancy. Stan probes Colin about Felix Vincente.

## Important Character Actions
- **Viktor Molotov**: Initiates the case defecting-in-place; escapes to hear Lyudmila play; nearly kills Pasha before standing down.
- **Colin**: Runs the op, leverages history with Viktor (Helsinki '17, Marrakech '07), declines promotion to stay in field.
- **Bill Goodman**: FBI liaison; drives tactical execution at station; coordinates takedown.
- **Gina**: Analyst pushing for field work; uncovers Felix Vincente file is fully redacted; runs tech intercept on Misha's call.
- **Teddy**: Volunteers as decoy (5'8" match for Sarah); survives abduction attempt.
- **Gregor Lyalin**: Flips under pressure (embezzlement kompromat, family threat); reveals Sarah Reed and Pasha.
- **Pasha**: Viktor's protégé, actual poisoner; captured after station chase.

## S02E05

# CIA S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Two threads run in parallel and converge thematically around legacy and control.

Vincente/Palomar: Colin and Bill interview high-security prisoner Guram Dadiani, whose name appears on a list of US special operators from Operation Palomar (1997, Tbilisi, Georgia). Guram corrects the record: they were children recruited for regime-change work, taken into the woods and trained in surveillance, weapons, infiltration and killing. Felix Vincente was trainer Robert Goodman's favorite, "like a son." When the program was cancelled and the assets abandoned, Vincente blamed Goodman and swore to kill him. Bill realizes Goodman is his own father, who died of cancer, and admits he wants to catch Vincente both for justice and to ask whether his dad was a good person.

Tentilla/Übergott: China's MSS warns Jen that Übergott's lawyers aggressively contacted Chinese AI firms, implying their model was stolen. CEO Jared Fallon is forced to admit an inside job: engineer Marie Poquette walked Tentilla out in a water bottle, etched on a glass "puck." A 60-second citywide hack (bio-weapon and missile alerts, no fatalities) demonstrates the model reaching municipal networks, emergency broadcasts and private firms. Marie is a whistleblower trying to wake people up, not a thief for profit; her AI research model had previously produced a bespoke cure for her sister Cecile's rare autoimmune disease. Colin and Bill win her trust, but MSS operative Darwin Feng goes rogue and kidnaps Cecile, demanding the model by 5 p.m. A dummy-program stalling plan lets the team locate and raid the Flushing safehouse at 112 Roosevelt, rescue Cecile and recover Tentilla. Marie is nevertheless taken in on theft and cyberterrorism charges. In the final twist, Deputy Director Kovulchek installs Olivia Markinson as chief of the New York station, with every operation now running through her.

## Important Character Actions
- **Colin**: Interrogates Guram; coerces Jared Fallon into cooperating by threatening a press leak; pressures Jen for MSS property addresses; reveals he worked under Olivia and tracked Vincente to Havana; pledges to find Vincente before Olivia does.
- **Bill Goodman**: FBI liaison; discovers his father Robert Goodman ran Palomar and trained Vincente; drives arrests and the shootout at Pearson Bank; delivers the morbid "body hits the floor" line and the "ride or die" loyalty; insists Marie bears responsibility for her monster.
- **Marie Poquette**: Stole and demoed Tentilla; negotiated immunity; used Tentilla to control doors, cameras and lights during the bank firefight; bluffed Feng with "validation protocol" and "brick the weights" threats; accepted arrest as worth it.
- **Cecile ("Cece") Poquette**: Confronted agents with mace; revealed why Marie built AI; was kidnapped and rescued.
- **Jen**: MSS contact; identified Darwin Feng as the rogue operative; supplied three Flushing properties; deflected suspicion of tipping them off.
- **Olivia Markinson**: Pressured Nikki to stop digging into Vincente; arrives from DC and is named station chief.
- **Nikki**: Pushback from Olivia and Kovulchek; loses operational autonomy.
- **Jared Fallon**: Arrogant CEO who folded under leak/coercion threats.
- **Guram Dadiani**: Traded his Palomar testimony for a shot at Colorado and mountains.
