## S02E10

## S02E10 Summary

### Episode Plot
The Hunt Ball at the Hampshire estate serves as the season finale backdrop. Multiple storylines converge: Gerald's wedding, the Venturer franchise bid awaiting IBA decision, Declan and Maud's marriage imploding after her affair with Tony is exposed, Charles Fairburn revealed as Tony's mole (blackmailed over his sexuality), and Rupert and Taggie's romantic tension culminating. The episode ends with Maud leaving Declan for London, Lizzie demanding divorce from James, and Rupert and Taggie together.

### Important Character Actions
- **Rupert Campbell-Black**: Attends ball solo but flirts with Annabel Kemble-Taylor; confronts Bas over Taggie; ends night with Taggie romantically.
- **Declan O'Hara**: Furious at Maud's betrayal; publicly attacks Charles when mole revealed; daughters refuse to leave with him.
- **Maud O'Hara**: Admits affair with Tony; tells Declan "It's over" and leaves for London with daughters staying at Venturer house.
- **Tony Baddingham**: Crashes ball, gloating about Venturer's impending loss; reveals Charles as his spy; threatens to destroy Declan and Rupert.
- **Charles Fairburn**: Exposed as Tony's mole — blackmailed with recordings of his homosexuality; begs Declan's forgiveness, refused.
- **Taggie O'Hara**: Arrives with Bas; Rupert warns her off him; shares intimate moment with Rupert at episode's end.
- **Gerald**: Gets married; undergoes "blooding" ritual before ceremony.
- **Sarah Stratton**: Shows up uninvited; Paul refuses to speak except through solicitor.
- **Lizzie Vereker**: Confronts James about his contempt for her writing and person; demands divorce.
- **Perdita**: Ambitious female polo player; pitches documentary to Declan; flirts with Rupert; leaves with cricketer.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Annabel Kemble-Taylor** — aristocratic woman Rupert brings to ball (reputation: "Half Leicestershire's been up her")
- **"Sin bin"** — euphemism for a room/vehicle for sexual assignations at hunt balls
- **Larkshire Gold Cup** — polo trophy Rupert won years prior
- **"Friar Fuck"** — nickname for Reverend Baines (corruptible IBA vote)
- **Orion the Hunter** — constellation Rupert points out to Taggie

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Venturer pitch went well; Ladbrokes odds at 2-1
- Maud sold her novel to Venturer (betraying Tony/Corinium)
- Tony has phone taps on all Corinium lines — knew everything about Venturer *and* Charles's sexuality
- Charles was Tony's mole, not Maud (Tony's final reveal)
- Declan's daughters (Taggie, Caitlin, Beatrice) side with Maud, refuse to go home with Declan
- Lizzie and James's marriage ends — she wants divorce, he values career partnership over intimacy
- Freddie's "Jones Jets" polo team recruiting Perdita
- IBA franchise decision imminent ("realm of the gods now")

## S02E11

## S02E11 Summary

### Episode Plot
The IBA announces the Central South West franchise winner: Corinium (Tony Baddingham) beats Venturer (Declan O'Hara). Tony tells reporters "the best man won"; Declan says "we were robbed," but the panel's decision is final and no appeal is possible. Declan apologizes to his team and begins winding Venturer down, able to pay staff only until Christmas. Rupert reveals he mortgaged Penscombe Court to finance the bid and may have to sell it, find a job, or return to London. Tony, meanwhile, celebrates with a "quicker and cheaper" ethos, announces fictitious new programmes on James Vereker's show, and throws a no-expense-spared Christmas party. The episode closes with Declan and Cameron staging a live takeover of Corinium's Uncensored, launching an on-air investigation into bribery, corruption, bullying, and sexual harassment at Corinium under Tony — cut off mid-accusation.

### Important Character Actions
- **Declan O'Hara**: Loses the franchise, winds down Venturer, and later hijacks Uncensored live to expose Tony. Calls the school matron asking to speak to daughters Beatrice and Camilla.
- **Tony Baddingham**: Wins the franchise; plans a development surrounding Declan's pied-à-terre ("to the victor the spoils"); blackmails an ally over a tape and threatens a "Poof MP in Secret, Sordid Double Life" exposé on Gerald Middleton; tells Reverend Fergus Penney "I own you, lock, stock and barrel"; declares love for Taggie to Helen.
- **Rupert Campbell-Black**: Leaves for Buenos Aires with no return date; rejects Helen's offer of a US Olympic coaching job in California; tames the difficult pony Mystic in Argentina; rebuffs Perdita's advances; punched by Dommie for his behaviour.
- **Cameron Cook**: Rejects Tony's fake line-up ("he's full of shit"); co-hosts the Uncensored takeover.
- **Freddie Jones**: Attempts to reconnect with wife Val and children Ellie and Sebastian by cooking dinner; appears to confide he must tell Val something.
- **Lizzie Vereker**: Confirms James discovered her affair with Freddie; she asked for a divorce, was sacked from How To Stay Married for selling her novel to Venturer, and remains conflicted.
- **Charles Fairburn**: Now at Uncensored; pursues Daysee, who demands the truth about his betrayal of Venturer.
- **Taggie O'Hara**: Brings Christmas presents to Rupert's children Tabitha ("Tab") and Marcus; rebuffs a sexual advance; is pressured by the children over Rupert's absence.
- **Bas Baddingham**: Arranges the Argentina polo trip to buy ponies for Freddie's Jones Jets and attend Lorenzo's wedding.
