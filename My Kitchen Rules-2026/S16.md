# My Kitchen Rules S16E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
The second instant restaurant of
The second instant restaurant of the season takes place in Adelaide, South Australia, where best mates **Angus** (funeral director) and **Simon** (teacher) host their pub-themed instant restaurant **"Last Orders"** — named as a nod to both pub culture and Angus's funeral profession. The episode follows their three-course service, kitchen challenges, and a major table conflict involving Alisha and Deanne.

## Important Character Actions

**Angus & Simon (Hosts)**
- Theme: "gastro pub vibe" with beer garden feel, skulls on table, headstone-shaped cheese boards
- **Entrée**: Wagyu sausage roll with caramelised onion chutney and kimchi (rough puff pastry made from scratch)
- **Main**: Gochujang chicken parmi with enoki fries and green apple slaw
- **Dessert**: Coconut panna cotta with sesame snap and butterscotch sauce (panna cottas set with coconut milk)
- Major kitchen crisis: enoki mushrooms waterlogged, wouldn't fry crisp; chicken risk of overcooking while waiting
- Angus makes heart-shaped coconut garnish for Deanne (flirting subplot)
- Final score: **70/100** (6+6+7 from judges, 33/50 from guest teams) — 2 points behind leaders Krysten & Lucy (72)

**Alisha & Deanne ("Classy Bogans")**
- Alisha explodes at table over "acid" terminology debate with Colin/Manu/Jane
- Feels targeted and condescended to for not knowing fine-dining terms ("gongany," "Napoleon sauce," "acid" as culinary term)
- **First walkout of the season** — storms out screaming, returns later
- Deanne supports her; both vow to prove they're "real cooks" despite being homestyle
- Conflict escalates: "I've been made to look like an absolute idiot" / "Australia, I'm a real cook"

**Jane (Food Stylist)**
- Sparks "acid" debate by using term "acid" for salad balance
- Defends terminology as standard culinary language
- Admits never having eaten a chicken parmi (shocks table)
- Scores: gives 6 for entrée, 6 for main, 6 for dessert

**Colin Fassnidge & Manu Feildel (Judges)**
- Colin steps in to mediate Alisha conflict
- Scores: entrée 6, main 6, dessert 7
- Praise panna cotta texture; criticize dry Wagyu beef, insufficient gochujang punch, soggy enoki fries, sloppy slaw

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Last Orders** — Angus & Simon's instant restaurant name
- **Gochujang** — Korean red pepper paste (described as "Korean version of tomato sauce")
- **Enoki fries** — battered/fried enoki mushrooms as chip substitute
- **"Considered" cuisine** — Shane & Jane's term for their thoughtful plating approach (vs "fine dining")
- **Wombok** — Chinese cabbage used for kimchi
