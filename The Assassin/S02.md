# The Assassin — S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Edward tracks down his birth mother **Louise Hendrikx** (also known as **Marguerite Gilles**) in Berlin. She lives there with her five sons, including **Titus**. Julie, deeply suspicious, has Luka run a background check and discovers Louise has a hidden compartment containing cash and coded numbers. A tattooed man and woman visit Louise with a "life-changing" offer — recruiting her as a drug mule. Julie investigates further, finding Louise's sister, who reveals the operation: the same recruiters got her sister **Ela Patarinka** to swallow cocaine packets; they burst in her stomach and she died before landing.

Louise proceeds with the mule run. At a handoff near **Bar Eden**, Kayla is revealed as the operation's enforcer, forcing mules to swallow packets at gunpoint. A confrontation erupts; **Maxime** is shot. Kayla shoots Louise (whether accidentally or intentionally is ambiguous). Julie arrives moments later and helps cover it up. Titus witnesses everything. He later tells Edward: "Your wife shot our mother. And Julie covered it up." Edward confronts them, disbelieving Kayla's denial, and orders Julie out of his life.

## Important Character Actions
- **Edward**: Finds his birth mother; initially embraces the reunion; turns on Julie and Kayla when the truth emerges.
- **Julie**: Runs surveillance on Louise; investigates the drug operation; covers up Louise's death to protect Edward; admits jealousy of Louise; agrees to lie to Edward at Kayla's urging.
- **Kayla**: Runs the Berlin drug-mule pipeline; forces packets into mules at gunpoint; shoots Louise; manipulates Julie into silence ("it will destroy him").
- **Luka**: Traces the phone number; advises Julie to compartmentalize; provides grounded perspective.
- **Titus**: Witnesses his mother's death; becomes the truth-teller who shatters Edward's trust.
- **Louise**: Accepts the mule offer despite sister's fate; dies in the handoff.
