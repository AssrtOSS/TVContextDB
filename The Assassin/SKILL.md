## Key Relationships
- **Julie ↔ Edward:** Mother–son, estranged ~30 years; Julie gave him up as a baby to protect him.
- **Julie ↔ Luka:** Friends; later romantic partners (by finale).
- **Julie ↔ Damien:** Former handler–operative; Damien betrays her under duress.
- **Julie ↔ Sean:** Former lovers; both assassins; Sean sent to kill her.
- **Edward ↔ Kayla:** Engaged; complicated by Edward’s secret investigation of CGM.
- **Kayla ↔ Ezra:** Siblings; rival agendas within CGM.
- **Aaron ↔ Kayla/Ezra:** Father; manipulative, protects “Chantaines” secret at all costs.
- **Marie ↔ Edward:** Claims to be aunt / biological mother; actually surrogate mother; spent 29 years planning revenge on Julie.
- **Jasper ↔ CGM:** Former IT head; blackmailed Aaron; knows “Chantaines” codename (bluffed details).

## World Setting
- **Primary locations (Season 1):** Greek island (filmed Porto Germeno, Salamis, Athens), Bulgaria (1994 prologue), Albania (Tirana), Libya (Tripoli), France, London, Spain (finale).
- **Timeframe:** 1994 flashback (Julie’s last hit, pregnancy revealed) → present day (2025), 31-year gap.
- **Tone:** Stylish, darkly comic thriller; “preposterous but tightly paced” (critics). Mix of espionage, family drama, corporate conspiracy.
- **Technology notes:** AI voice impersonation used to mimic Damien; what3words geocode; encrypted laptop; modern surveillance vs. analog tradecraft.

## Overall Plot Premise
Retired assassin Julie Green lives quietly on a Greek island. Her estranged son Edward, an investigative journalist, tracks her down seeking answers about his father and a mysterious trust fund. Julie’s past violently resurfaces: a hit is placed on her (and Kayla Cross) using AI to impersonate her handler Damien. A wedding massacre forces Julie, Edward, and Luka to flee onto the yacht of billionaire Aaron Cross. The conspiracy centers on “Chantaines” — a codename in a suicide note from Kayla and Ezra’s mother, implicating Aaron and CGM in the 1994 murder of an arms-dealer investor. Julie discovers she was the assassin hired for that hit but spared the family. Edward’s true parentage (surrogate Marie, father killed by Julie) is revealed in a final confrontation where Marie poisons Edward and demands he kill Julie; he kills Marie instead. Season 1 ends with Julie and Luka together, Edward recovering, Ezra burning the suicide note, and a three-month jump teasing Season 2.

## Speaking Styles
- **Julie:** Laconic, dry wit, professional jargon (“handler,” “contract,” “clean”), rarely explains; British accent (Keeley Hawes).
- **Edward:** Inquisitive, verbose, journalist’s precision; emotional vulnerability beneath professional façade.
- **Luka:** Gruff, loyal, few words; Greek-accented English (Gerald Kyd).
- **Aaron Cross:** Measured, corporate menace; power-player cadence (Alan Dale).
- **Kayla:** Poised, strategic, shifts between corporate and personal registers.
- **Ezra:** Slick, entitled, cocaine-fueled volatility (Ep. 4–5); later cold CEO.
- **Marie:** Calm, vengeful, maternal manipulation; French-accented English (Gina Gershon).
- **Damien:** Weary, pragmatic, betrayer under pressure.
- **Sean:** Cocky, flirtatious killer; banter with Julie.

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **British TV thriller conventions:** Understatement, dark humour, class awareness (Edward’s journalism vs. Julie’s working-class soldier background).
- **Greek setting:** Local colour (wedding massacre, butcher Luka, island geography); Greek-accented English for locals.
- **Corporate conspiracy:** CGM as archetypal “evil multinational”; mining, arms dealing, charitable foundation as cover.
- **Family secrets / identity:** Surrogacy, adoption, hidden parentage — treat sensitively; avoid overly melodramatic Chinese phrasing.
- **Violence:** Graphic but stylised; subtitle tone should match — clinical for Julie, shocked for Edward.
- **Anachronism note:** 1994 flip-phone is a known error; if referenced in dialogue, translate literally (do not “fix”).
