## S11E01

## S11E01 — Episode Summary

### Plot
A council crew dredges a car from the sea at **Roar Point**. Inside the boot: skeletal remains of an adult male, submerged for years. The car is registered to **Raymond Linden**, a respected Lerwick GP who vanished in March 2017 — nine years ago. Linden had spiralled into alcohol and painkiller addiction, stole £20,000 from the practice account (never reported), and disappeared the same night he took the Aberdeen ferry. The original investigation treated it as a voluntary missing-person case (MISSPER) with no foul play suspected.

DI **Ruth Calder** and DI **Alison "Tosh" McIntosh** reopen the case. A parallel thread emerges: **Ivan Dudka**, a Ukrainian contract worker at **Ross Waste Management**, was also reported missing in April 2017. Ivan had an emergency home visit appointment with Dr Linden on 3 March 2017 — the day Linden fled — at an address in **Isbister** (not Ivan’s known Lerwick hostel). Tosh and Sandy trace both threads; Cora (pathologist) eventually identifies the remains as **Raymond Linden** via a past **high tibial osteotomy** (knee surgery). Ivan Dudka’s fate remains unknown.

### Important Character Actions
- **Calder & Tosh**: Lead the joint investigation; Calder interviews the Linden family, Tosh pursues the Dudka/Ross Waste angle.
- **Sandy Wilson**: Runs the DVLA check, traces Linden’s digital footprint (no UK exit record, no GP registration since 2017), talks to croft-dweller **Donald Pierce** near Roar Point.
- **Eric Linden** (Raymond’s brother): Reveals Raymond called him the night he left — “in trouble,” asked Eric to look after son **Jonah**. Admits he cancelled the private investigator (McFarlane) to protect their mother **Martha** from learning the truth.
- **Emily Linden** (ex-wife): Confirms Raymond’s addiction; says she was “glad he wasn’t here.”
- **Murray Ross** (Ross Waste owner): Claims not to remember Ivan Dudka; likely lying — Ivan had complained about unsafe working conditions.
- **Donald Pierce**: Eccentric hoarder living at the croft by Roar Pier. Witnesses a figure in a **black/blue work van (one tail light)** watching the car recovery; later attacked at his home by an intruder.
- **Ruth Calder**: Personal subplot — medical check-up (“picture of health”), domestic life with partner **Alan**, tension over his pending move back to the manse.

2. **What happened to Ivan Dudka?** (Not the body in the boot; 5'5" vs 6'+ skeleton.)
3. **Why was Ivan in Isbister** that afternoon — not at his Lerwick hostel?
4. **What “trouble”** forced Linden to flee and steal £20k? (Drug debt? Medical error? Blackmail?)
5. **Identity of the van driver** (black/blue work van, one tail light) who watched the recovery and attacked Donald Pierce.
6. **Murray Ross’s involvement** — why lie about Ivan? Unsafe conditions? Something worse?
7. **Eric Linden’s full motive** — protecting Martha, or concealing his own knowledge?
8. **Root cause of Raymond’s collapse** — “something kicked it all off” but never named.

## S11E02

## S11E02 — Shetland（基于对齐字幕）

### Episode plot
A car boot full of bones is found; the car is registered to Raymond Linden, a Lerwick GP reported missing in March 2017. The remains are confirmed as Linden via a surgical plate on his knee (matched by pathologist Cora), and he likely died the day he disappeared. The case reopens two 2017 disappearances: Linden and Ivan Dudka, a Ukrainian contract worker for Ross Waste Management who had an appointment with Linden in Isbister the afternoon Linden vanished.

DI Ruth Calder and DS/DC Tosh McIntosh investigate. Suspicion lands on Emily Linden (Raymond's widow) and Graeme Gould (surgery partner, now Emily's lover), whom Emily's mother denounces as a Logan — "cheats and liars." Gould paid his stepfather's £19,000 care-home arrears in cash in April 2017 and was on the Scottish mainland when £20,000 was withdrawn from the surgery account in Aberdeen; he claims he was visiting a patient in North Roe, corroborated by a road landslip that delayed him.

Tosh goes missing after visiting Murray Ross about the Dudka–Linden link. She leaves a voicemail for partner Donnie and daughter Louise: trapped in a flooding tank/chamber at an Isbister industrial unit, she believes it was deliberate and records a farewell. Billy locates her car and the tank; Calder races to the scene. Ross is caught trying to flee by yacht from Voe Marina (he hates flying) and confesses: Dudka had an asthma attack at the tank, Ross left him for two minutes and he died, Ross concealed the body for nine years and lied to Dr Linden when he arrived, then deliberately locked Tosh in the flooding tank. Ross admits Dudka's death but denies killing Linden; Calder doubts he is Linden's killer. Ross is charged with attempted murder of a police officer, failure to report a death, concealing human remains, and perverting the course of justice.

A mystery woman (mid-30s, slim, not Emily) was seen waiting in a car with Linden on his last day — never identified. The episode ends with Emily and Gould planning to sell the practice, leave Shetland and marry; Emily hesitates, then a woman claiming to be her cousin arrives at her door: "I know exactly what you did."

### Important character actions
- Tosh: follows up the Dudka call-out with surgery receptionist Nora Shand; trapped in the tank; records a farewell voicemail to Donnie and Louise; rescued by Billy and Calder.
- Ruth Calder: leads the inquiry, interviews Gould and Ross, coordinates the search, arrests Ross at Voe Marina and reads him his charges.
- Sandy Wilson: traces Ross's former Isbister premises and his 2015 health-and-safety fine; follows up Donald Pierce's report of a suspicious van with a broken tail light at the crime scene and SOCO evidence bags found on the beach.
- Billy: finds Tosh's car, locates the tank, rescues Tosh, and supports her afterward.
- Murray Ross: attempts flight by yacht; confesses to Dudka's death and trapping Tosh; denies Linden's murder.
- Graeme Gould: cash payment of care-home debt; mainland alibi; North Roe patient visit confirmed via Emily's call and the landslip.
- Emily Linden: widow now in a relationship with Gould, initially concealed from police; visited at the close by a blackmailing cousin.
- Raymond's brother: admits he withheld Raymond's pre-disappearance phone call ("in trouble") out of misplaced loyalty — "being a good brother."

### Newly established names/terms
Raymond Linden, Emily Linden, Graeme Gould, Ivan Dudka, Murray Ross, Nora Shand (surgery receptionist), Donald Pierce (croft holder at Roer Point), Roer Point, Voe Marina, Ross Waste Management, SOCOs (Scene of Crime Officers), the Isbister tank/industrial unit, Donnie and Louise (Tosh's partner and daughter), Kirsty (Environmental Health contact), Sandra and Annie (Ross's wife and employee). "The mainland" is used for travel to Scotland proper and features in alibi checks.

### Continuity-relevant facts
- Ruth Calder is now DI in charge; Jimmy Perez is absent (post-Perez era under Ashley Jensen's lead).
- Tosh's family unit: partner Donnie and daughter Louise; she was due to meet them at the airport the morning she disappeared.
- Scottish jurisdiction is maintained (Procurator Fiscal/court context; no American coroner/DA terminology).

### Jokes or references useful for later episodes
- Donald Pierce's hoarding and his cousin Kirsty in Environmental Health — an established local contact for Sandy.
- "SOCOs" is explained to Donald, marking him as outside police procedure.
- Tosh's tank ordeal and farewell voicemail — likely trauma carried into later episodes.
- Emily's cousin's blackmail ("I know exactly what you did") — a dangling threat over Emily and Gould.
- Emily's mother's "Logan family — cheats and liars" outburst — establishes the Linden family's poor local reputation.

### Unresolved threads
- Who killed Raymond Linden: Ross denies it and Calder agrees he is probably not the killer (different disposal method); Gould has motive (money, Emily, the practice) but an alibi for the disappearance afternoon; the £20,000 withdrawal appears to have been made by someone impersonating Linden while Gould was on the mainland — all open.
- The mystery woman in the car with Linden on his last day — unidentified; possibly an affair.
- Emily's cousin: what she knows about Emily and what she intends to do with it.
- Tosh's recovery after the trapping; Donnie and Louise's response to her ordeal.
- What Raymond said in the withheld call from his brother ("he was... in trouble").
