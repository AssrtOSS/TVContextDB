## S11E01 — Episode Summary

### Plot
A council crew dredges a car from the sea at **Roar Point**. Inside the boot: skeletal remains of an adult male, submerged for years. The car is registered to **Raymond Linden**, a respected Lerwick GP who vanished in March 2017 — nine years ago. Linden had spiralled into alcohol and painkiller addiction, stole £20,000 from the practice account (never reported), and disappeared the same night he took the Aberdeen ferry. The original investigation treated it as a voluntary missing-person case (MISSPER) with no foul play suspected.

DI **Ruth Calder** and DI **Alison "Tosh" McIntosh** reopen the case. A parallel thread emerges: **Ivan Dudka**, a Ukrainian contract worker at **Ross Waste Management**, was also reported missing in April 2017. Ivan had an emergency home visit appointment with Dr Linden on 3 March 2017 — the day Linden fled — at an address in **Isbister** (not Ivan’s known Lerwick hostel). Tosh and Sandy trace both threads; Cora (pathologist) eventually identifies the remains as **Raymond Linden** via a past **high tibial osteotomy** (knee surgery). Ivan Dudka’s fate remains unknown.

### Important Character Actions
- **Calder & Tosh**: Lead the joint investigation; Calder interviews the Linden family, Tosh pursues the Dudka/Ross Waste angle.
- **Sandy Wilson**: Runs the DVLA check, traces Linden’s digital footprint (no UK exit record, no GP registration since 2017), talks to croft-dweller **Donald Pierce** near Roar Point.
- **Eric Linden** (Raymond’s brother): Reveals Raymond called him the night he left — “in trouble,” asked Eric to look after son **Jonah**. Admits he cancelled the private investigator (McFarlane) to protect their mother **Martha** from learning the truth.
- **Emily Linden** (ex-wife): Confirms Raymond’s addiction; says she was “glad he wasn’t here.”
- **Murray Ross** (Ross Waste owner): Claims not to remember Ivan Dudka; likely lying — Ivan had complained about unsafe working conditions.
- **Donald Pierce**: Eccentric hoarder living at the croft by Roar Pier. Witnesses a figure in a **black/blue work van (one tail light)** watching the car recovery; later attacked at his home by an intruder.
- **Ruth Calder**: Personal subplot — medical check-up (“picture of health”), domestic life with partner **Alan**, tension over his pending move back to the manse.

### Unresolved Threads
1. **Who killed Raymond Linden** and dumped the car at Roar Point?
2. **What happened to Ivan Dudka?** (Not the body in the boot; 5'5" vs 6'+ skeleton.)
3. **Why was Ivan in Isbister** that afternoon — not at his Lerwick hostel?
4. **What “trouble”** forced Linden to flee and steal £20k? (Drug debt? Medical error? Blackmail?)
5. **Identity of the van driver** (black/blue work van, one tail light) who watched the recovery and attacked Donald Pierce.
6. **Murray Ross’s involvement** — why lie about Ivan? Unsafe conditions? Something worse?
7. **Eric Linden’s full motive** — protecting Martha, or concealing his own knowledge?
8. **Root cause of Raymond’s collapse** — “something kicked it all off” but never named.
