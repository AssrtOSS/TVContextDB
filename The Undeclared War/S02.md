## S02E05

# The Undeclared War — S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot

A sophisticated worm has infiltrated UK government systems, currently contained within Trade and Immigration but threatening to spread to the Home Office, NCA, MOD, and through GCHQ into American networks. The worm appears to be searching for something specific, though its exact target remains unknown.

Simultaneously, GCHQ mole Calvin Song (凯文·宋) is exposed after accessing a MAC address at malware analyst Phil Draper's terminal under the guise of translation work. Before he can be properly interrogated, Calvin is found hanged in his cell, eliminating a critical intelligence source.

To trap the worm, Lieutenant Williams (威廉姆斯上尉) deploys a "tar pit" (焦油坑) — a virtual deception network that mimics target systems, luring the worm into a hall of mirrors where it loops endlessly. The system is successfully deployed across gov.uk startup services, and the worm is contained.

Meanwhile, Danny Patrick (丹尼·帕特里克) and Saara (萨拉) coordinate the extraction of Vadim Trusov (瓦迪姆·特鲁索夫), a Russian asset hiding in China who possesses a long alphanumeric ciphertext believed to be the key to the worm's true purpose. After MSS agents raid Vadim's safehouse, Danny, Roz Cavendish (罗斯·卡文迪什), Saara, and Williams guide Vadim and his Chinese associate Hai (海) to escape by overloading the building's power system to blind CCTV, then fleeing through a fire escape to an alley where Hai's delivery-driver friend picks them up.

## Important Character Actions

- **Danny** leads the extractions of Vadim while managing the worm crisis; later approaches a contact named Michaela (knows her 15 years) to get Vadim extracted from China, bypassing official MI6 channels because the PM won't risk the trade deal.
- **Barbara Napier (芭芭拉)** interrogates Charlie about Phil's terminal, confirms Calvin as the mole, and later refuses to open sealed MI5 recordings that might implicate the PM in Chinese influence — drawing a firm line that GCHQ does security and technology, never politics.
- **Roz** oversees the technical deployment of the tar pit and joins the extraction operation.
- **Saara** maintains the comms link with Vadim via burner phone, guiding him through the escape.
- **The Foreign Secretary** pressures GCHQ to restore systems, prioritizing the ongoing UK-China trade talks at Admiralty over security concerns, and reminds Danny of "political pragmatism."
- **Hai** helps Vadim escape but reveals she texted her sister, potentially compromising them; her family has been arrested by the CCP.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The worm is **contained but not defeated** — its objective is still unknown but linked to Vadim's ciphertext.
- Vadim and Hai are **on the run in China** with MSS hunting them; Danny is pursuing an unofficial extraction route because the PM will not authorize action that jeopardizes the trade deal.
- **Sealed MI5 recordings** from the China nuclear energy deal negotiations exist in national security holdings, redacted on the day Makinde (麦金德) became Prime Minister. They have never been opened.
- Calvin's **death in custody** removes the only direct witness to how deeply Chinese infiltration reached inside GCHQ.
- The worm is described as **adaptive**, designed to evade any anti-malware deployed against it.

## Jokes and References

- **"You keep your girlfriend up-to-date"** — Williams teases Danny about Saara; Danny immediately denies she's his girlfriend.
- **"Use their inner resources"** — Vadim's bleak joke about life under China's social credit system, saying his mother would say that.
- **"Try being Russian"** — Vadim deflects Hai's complaint about Chinese authoritarianism.
- **"Political pragmatism, Danny"** — the Foreign Secretary's recurring refrain whenever he pressures GCHQ.
- **"We do security and technology, never politics"** — Barbara's line in the sand when refusing to open the sealed recordings.

## S02E04

## Episode Plot

A sophisticated worm infiltrates the UK Department of Immigration, crashing systems and causing physical damage (printers catching fire). Saara traces it to an FSB “breaking and entering tool” modified for Chinese APT10. Meanwhile, GCHQ identifies malware analyst Phil Draper as the suspected mole behind a leaked MAC address.

Danny Patrick and his wife Barbara Napier clash over work-life balance as she departs for UK–China trade talks at Admiralty House. MI6’s Roz Cavendish arrives to independently audit Phil’s questioning. The worm proves to be self-replicating, jumping to the Department of Trade during the talks. Reverse engineering fails because the worm uses environment keying.

In Shenzhen, Russian asset Vadim Trusov—aided by a Chinese woman named Hai—contacts Danny via secure line. He confirms APT10 involvement and possesses a stolen alphanumeric ciphertext that may reveal the worm’s target, but refuses to share it until extracted.

Danny seeks emergency approval to shut down government systems. Foreign Secretary Marston and the PM’s office refuse, prioritizing the trade talks over security. Charlie performs memory forensics on a RAM dump, recovering a timestamped log showing the MAC address was accessed in October 2022 at 10:51. Phil realizes he left Barbara Napier alone at his terminal for 10–15 minutes while fetching coffee. The episode ends with Phil making the explosive allegation that Barbara—Danny’s wife and his boss—was the one who accessed the terminal.

## Important Character Actions

- **Danny** manages the worm crisis, obtains emergency ANPR/surveillance access from Legal Clearance (Fran), confronts Marston’s refusal to shut down systems, and coordinates with Vadim.
- **Barbara** attends the China trade talks, meets Nicky Chan in St James’s Park, and is revealed to have been left unattended at Phil’s terminal during the critical access window.
- **Roz** announces MI6’s audit role and delivers the technical assessment on environment keying.
- **Saara** identifies the malware origin and maintains the comms link with Vadim.
- **Phil** is formally interrogated, denies taking Chinese money, and ultimately tells investigators Barbara was alone at his terminal.
- **Vadim** confirms the APT10 link, demands safe extraction before handing over the ciphertext, and prepares to flee with Hai.
- **Hai** helps Vadim evade MSS and police, yelling during the confrontation at their safehouse.
- **Charlie** recovers the deleted MAC access logs via RAM dump memory forensics.
- **Marston** blocks the system shutdown, demanding “political pragmatism” to protect the trade talks.
- **Nicky Chan** (a.k.a. Chan Chi Lam) appears first seeking someone at an MSS front disguised as a Chinese takeaway, then meets Barbara in the park.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Hai (海)**: Chinese woman sheltering Vadim; MSS is hunting both of them.
- **Nicky Chan / Chan Chi Lam (尼基·陈 / 陈志林)**: Chinese operative linked to the Admiralty trade delegation and an overseas MSS office.
- **XXN Ventures / Xiangxin Corp (祥信集团)**: Chinese tech giant with CCP ties; provided venture capital for Phil’s friend’s gaming company, forming the motive thread in the mole case.
- **Environment keying**: Malware technique that decrypts only on a target system’s specific configuration, defeating sandbox reverse engineering.
- **Memory forensics / RAM dump**: Method used by Charlie to recover deleted MAC address access records.
- **ANPR**: Automatic Number Plate Recognition system; Danny gains emergency access for the investigation.
- **Surge mode (“Laughing Panda”)**: Two-week intensified operational period for the Malware team targeting Chinese APT10.
- **Fran**: GCHQ Legal Clearance officer who grants Danny emergency surveillance access.
- **David Neal**: Senior official Danny attempts to reach for ministerial sign-off.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The worm is designed to evade quarantine and is actively searching for a specific target; it has already jumped from Immigration to Trade and could spread further.
- Vadim’s ciphertext is described as the key to the worm’s true purpose, linking the Russian data scrape to the current UK government infiltration.
- Phil’s login was used for the MAC theft, but the timestamp points to a window when Barbara Napier was alone at his terminal.
- An MSS overseas office operates covertly under the cover of a Chinese takeaway in the UK.
- The UK–China trade talks at Admiralty are politically sacrosanct; the PM’s office will not authorize actions that risk them.
- Danny and Barbara’s marriage is under severe strain due to conflicting work crises and her sudden trips to London.

## Jokes and References

- **Vadim to Hai**: “Thank me in bitcoin, not flower boy eyes.” / “Insult.” — establishes his transactional worldview and their banter.
- **Danny**: “I won’t blow up any shit, unless I have to.” — dry offer to lead the worm response.
- **Vadim**: “You’re learning, Russki.” / “Nothing’s ever lost in this world for free.” — cynical lesson to Hai.
- **Roz to MI6 chief**: “Marston’s girl.” / “And no longer a girl.” — asserting her rank.
- **Danny on Saara**: “She’s not my girlfriend.” / “Magic.” — deflecting Williams’ tease about Saara not letting him down.
- **Barbara’s phone message**: “67! I got a 2:1!” — misheard exam result during the trade-talk waiting room.
- **Marston’s refrain**: “Political pragmatism, Danny.” — recurring justification for blocking security actions.

## Unresolved Threads

1. Is Barbara Napier the mole, or is Phil deflecting blame? Who actually accessed the MAC address during those 10–15 minutes?
2. What is the worm’s true target, and how does Vadim’s ciphertext unlock it?
3. Can Vadim and Hai escape Shenzhen before MSS intercepts them?
4. What is Nicky Chan’s role in the Chinese delegation, and what does he want with Barbara?
5. Will Danny eventually override Marston and shut down the systems unilaterally?
6. How far does the Xiangxin Corp/XXN Ventures financial trail go inside GCHQ?

## S02E01

## Episode Plot

Following the devastating Russian cyber attack on UK political institutions and telecoms, the NSA has suspended all intelligence sharing with Britain. RAF squadrons scramble to intercept Russian aircraft entering UK airspace, with the Defence Secretary preparing to order a retaliatory strike on Kaliningrad. Danny Patrick and his team recognize the air incursion as a Russian provocation—a fake attack designed to goad Britain into firing first and becoming the aggressor. Using material leaked by FSB coder Vadim Trusov, Danny trades a Russian malware exploit targeting the President's personal security (White House CCTV, etc.) back to the NSA via Michaela Jones. This restores the US alliance, and President Putin backs down after a direct warning invoking NATO Article 5.

With the immediate crisis resolved, Saara reveals the mechanism of the original Russian intrusion: the malware escaped a sandbox computer by exploiting its MAC address, which had to be known before it arrived at GCHQ. This confirms a mole inside the organization. Richard Marston, now appointed Foreign Secretary, asserts direct authority over GCHQ and MI6, embedding the 14th Signal Regiment at GCHQ under Lieutenant Colonel Cavendish as "Director of Rapid Reaction." Cavendish is tasked with a parallel offensive operation to mine and blow up the FSB system—a move Danny fears will expose Vadim and trigger war. The episode closes with the mole hunt narrowed to the Malware department, Vadim shown on CCTV being led from FSB headquarters in St Petersburg, and Saara joining the offensive cyber team to strike back.

## Important Character Actions

- **Danny** brokers the NSA restoration by handing over the Russian exploit; resists Marston's parallel offensive operation that endangers Vadim; oversees the mole hunt.
- **Saara** identifies the MAC-address leak mechanism, implicating an insider; requests transfer to offensive cyber operations to hurt the Russians after Vadim's arrest.
- **Marston** (now Foreign Secretary) centralizes control over GCHQ and MI6, appoints Cavendish, and greenlights an independent offensive cyber campaign.
- **Cavendish** arrives with the 14th Signal Regiment, sets up a separate offensive operation under Marston's authority, and refuses to answer to Danny.
- **Charlie Francis** (Head of Security) conducts routine interviews, probing sandbox access policies.

## S02E02

## Episode Plot

The episode opens in the immediate aftermath of the mole revelation. Saara undergoes a rigorous security review where she discloses her communications with Vadim Trusov. Through a Discord server routed through the event management centre, Saara makes contact with Vadim, who reveals he is alive and hiding in China. He tells her that Stage 3 of the cyber attack—the data scrape—was actually carried out by China, not Russia.

In Shenzhen, Russian coders including Vadim are welcomed by Yin Technologies, a Chinese cyber-development enterprise. They are told they are there to redesign their "breaking and entering tool" for Chinese operations. The coders are essentially prisoners—forced to work, their phones confiscated, their families told only that they are engaged in important national work.

Back at GCHQ, the mole hunt intensifies. Phil Draper's gaming company comes under scrutiny after investigators discover his friend Jules's start-up received venture capital with possible Russian connections. Charlie begins examining who had access to the sandbox computer during the critical period in 2022.

Danny and Saara travel to London to brief Foreign Secretary Richard Marston on the evidence of Chinese involvement. Marston is skeptical but acknowledges the weight of evidence. He warns them to keep the information tightly contained due to the ongoing UK-China trade talks, which are politically sacrosanct.

The FSB back door that Vadim left for GCHQ is discovered and closed, meaning the FSB knows about the leak. Danny warns Saara to tell Vadim to go off-grid immediately—his life is in danger. Hai, a Chinese woman who is part of the underground democracy movement, helps Vadim escape by providing anti-facial recognition clothing and disabling the service elevator's CCTV and alarms. Vadim flees the building as alarms blare.

## Important Character Actions

- **Saara** undergoes a security review, discloses her contact with Vadim, and maintains the communications link that reveals the Chinese involvement in Stage 3.
- **Danny** manages the mole hunt, briefs the Foreign Secretary, and coordinates with Saara to warn Vadim when the FSB back door is closed.
- **Charlie** investigates Phil Draper's gaming company and begins examining sandbox access logs to narrow down the mole.
- **Vadim** confirms Chinese involvement in Stage 3 and escapes from Yin Technologies with Hai's help.
- **Hai** risks her life to help Vadim escape, revealing she is part of the democracy movement and that she loves her country but not the CCP.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Yin Technologies**: Chinese cyber-development enterprise in Shenzhen, described as one of the most sophisticated and sustained cyber attackers in the world.
- **Ming Liu**: Translator for the Russian coders at Yin Technologies.
- **Hai**: Chinese woman sheltering Vadim; part of the underground democracy movement; opposes the CCP.
- **APT10**: Chinese Ministry of State Security-backed advanced persistent threat group.
- **Stage 3**: The data scrape phase of the attack, revealed to be Chinese in origin.
- **Breaking and entering tool**: The malware used in the attack on the UK, which the Russian coders are forced to redesign for China.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The mole is inside GCHQ's Malware department and had access to the sandbox computer before the malware arrived.
- Vadim is in China, working for Yin Technologies under duress.
- The FSB knows about the back door and has closed it, putting Vadim's life in immediate danger.
- The UK is negotiating a major trade deal with China, which the government prioritizes over responding to the cyber attack.
- Hai is part of the Chinese democracy movement; her family is at risk from the CCP.

## Jokes and References

- "Chicken tikka masala for sir." / "Jalfrezi for the lady." — Taj Mahal takeaway order, a rare domestic moment.

- What is China's true objective in the cyber attack?

## S02E03

# The Undeclared War — S02E03 Summary

## Episode Plot

This episode is structured around Danny and Barbara's 15th wedding anniversary dinner at the same restaurant where they first met, with the narrative unfolding primarily through flashbacks that reveal Barbara's devastating hidden history with Nicky Chan in Hong Kong.

In 1997, a young Barbara—studying Mandarin at Oxford—went to Hong Kong for the handover. There she fell in love with Nicky Chan, a law postgraduate and pro-democracy student leader. He disappeared without explanation; she later learned he had abandoned her, likely in revenge for Britain's "historical betrayal" of Hong Kong.

Years later, MI6 sent Barbara back to Hong Kong to find a leak who was feeding information about British-supported pro-democracy groups to the CCP. Nicky resurfaced as an Occupy Central leader and helped her identify the source: Rupert Wright, a diplomat at the British embassy. Together they set a trap—planting false leads to see whose flat the police raided. Rupert was exposed. MI6 shut down its Hong Kong operation, abandoning its local assets. Nicky was arrested by the People's Police.

The narrative then jumps forward: Nicky has escaped to London and taken a guest lectureship at SOAS (School of Oriental and African Studies). Barbara, now China Team Leader at GCHQ, encounters him again. Nicky faces harassment from radicalized Hong Kong students who call him a "CCP stooge" and throw eggs at him—part of what he claims is a covert CCP campaign to discredit him. Barbara discovers his name in GCHQ's intercepted communications as a suspected CCP agent and deletes the file to protect him, believing in the man she once loved.

At a private meeting, Nicky finally reveals the truth: he is an MSS agent. He orchestrated the entire Hong Kong operation, turning Rupert Wright through his "middle-class guilt" and Chinese boyfriend. He has been playing Barbara since her return to Hong Kong. Now he blackmails her—threatening to expose her deletion of classified files, which would destroy Danny's new career as GCHQ Director of Operations and end their marriage—unless she cooperates.

Back at the anniversary dinner, Barbara has just discarded the "Bi Ba Ya" pendant Nicky gave her in Hong Kong, symbolically severing that past.

## Important Character Actions

- **Barbara** deletes Nicky Chan's file from GCHQ systems, compromising her security clearance and career. When Nicky reveals he is MSS and blackmails her, she chooses to push Danny away by demanding a divorce rather than let him be destroyed by association.
- **Danny** is offered the Deputy Director of Operations post at GCHQ but initially refuses because it requires 24/7 availability and he has two young children. He later accepts and becomes Director of Operations. When Barbara suddenly demands a divorce, he offers to resign immediately; she refuses.
- **Nicky Chan** reveals he is a long-term MSS agent planted inside Hong Kong's pro-democracy movement. He framed Rupert Wright to protect himself and now blackmails Barbara into becoming his asset inside GCHQ.
- **Rupert Wright** is exposed as the Hong Kong leak—turned by the MSS through exploitation of his personal life—and takes the fall for Nicky.
- **Calvin Song** is introduced as a GCHQ linguist with cousins in Beijing who can translate Chinese malware text.

- Nicky demands Barbara obtain the MAC address of a malware sandbox at GCHQ for an upcoming Chinese ransomware attack on the NHS—directly setting up the worm plot of S02E04.

## S02E06

# The Undeclared War — S02E06 Summary

## Episode Plot

Barbara Napier confesses to Danny that she is the mole, explaining how Nicky Chan—her former lover from Hong Kong and a long-term MSS agent—blackmailed her. Nicky discovered Barbara had deleted his surveillance file from GCHQ systems, a criminal offense that would destroy Danny's career. He manipulated her sense of historical guilt over Britain's treatment of China and demanded the MAC address of a malware sandbox. Barbara provided it, believing it was a one-time act to protect Danny. Nicky passed it to the FSB, who stole an encrypted key from GCHQ and gave it to the MSS.

Rather than let Barbara take the fall alone, Danny resolves to expose Prime Minister Andrew Makinde, whom he believes is the true architect of a corrupt China trade deal. Using David Neal's login credentials—obtained by distracting David at the door—Danny accesses sealed MI5 recordings from the 2022 nuclear deal negotiations. He finds audio of Makinde demanding a £20 million investment into his father-in-law's Shanghai hedge fund and suggesting Richard Marston would need to be "looked after." Danny embeds the recording in a flood of mundane emails to smuggle it out before Charlie Francis arrives to relieve him of duties.

Simultaneously, the worm breaks out of the tar pit. Roz Cavendish takes command and deploys an AI replica of the worm, run at double speed, to predict its path. The replica reveals the worm was never targeting UK systems—it was looking for a way to jump the Atlantic into US military networks. Its ultimate target is AUKUS, the Australia-UK-US satellite system controlling the South China Sea missile defence. The real objective is Taiwan, and the stolen Russian-held key was the Chinese component designed to unlock it. The NSA is alerted to reset encryption codes.

Vadim Trusov is extracted by boat to American territory, but Hai refuses to leave, choosing to stay and fight for her country despite her parents being arrested by the CCP.

The Makinde recording goes public. The Prime Minister and Foreign Secretary stand accused of grave conflict of interest and accepting bribes. Downing Street claims the recordings are fake and cancels the Chinese delegation banquet. Barbara's charges are mysteriously dropped, but Danny is detained for breaches of the Official Secrets Act involving STRAP 3 material—though he is ultimately released without charge. The episode ends with Alex Sheldon offering Barbara a chance to work as a double agent.

## Important Character Actions

- **Barbara** confesses fully to Danny, explains Nicky's blackmail, and accepts she must turn herself in to protect her family
- **Danny** refuses to let Barbara fall alone; steals David's login, finds the Makinde recording, and smuggles it out via email before being caught
- **Charlie Francis** relieves Danny of duties and detains him for STRAP 3 breaches, but ultimately lets him walk out
- **Roz** takes command of the worm response, builds the AI replica, identifies AUKUS as the target, and confronts Alex Sheldon when he dismisses the recording as a deepfake
- **Saara** guides Vadim's extraction via NSA boat
- **Vadim** escapes to America; **Hai** stays behind to fight
- **Alex Sheldon** offers Barbara a double agent role, revealing he knows about Nicky and the deleted files
- **PM Makinde** goes into hiding as the recording goes public
