## S03E04

### 剧情概述

- **18号筒仓**：朱丽叶在矿井中幸存，却因吸入毒气昏迷。市长办公室计划让她“意外”死亡；司法部还试图杀死护士，以掩盖她被救出的事实。护士带朱丽叶逃离医疗部，并暗示有人曾替换她的失忆药物。
- 雪莉、诺克斯和机械部协助朱丽叶转移。朱丽叶逐渐恢复记忆，认出诺克斯，并坚持寻找卢卡斯。她和雪莉最终进入封锁的“挖掘机空洞”，在那里发现一扇隐藏入口。
- 保罗·比林斯调查矿井中发现的尸体，确认死者死于后脑遭钢筋重击，且死前并未在矿井内；尸体原本应当永远不会被发现。他还追查失踪的卡拉·麦克莱恩及其学徒职位。
- 伯纳德与罗伯特·西姆斯确认朱丽叶仍活着，并命令突击队低调搜捕她。卡米尔主动介入，试图亲自处理朱丽叶。
- **现实世界线**：丹尼尔·基恩和海伦追查夏洛特参与的伊朗行动。丹尼尔的线人史蒂文·普莱斯失踪，通讯录音也被拿走。山姆·“山羊”·拉姆齐起初拒绝帮忙，后来通过一次性手机联系丹尼尔，声称发现了重大线索。结尾，丹尼尔与海伦抵达朱丽叶所在地点，朱丽叶向他们打招呼。

### 重要人物行动

- **朱丽叶**：从昏迷中苏醒，恢复部分记忆；拒绝躲藏，继续寻找卢卡斯并追查筒仓深处的秘密。
- **雪莉**：陪朱丽叶逃亡，并在医疗部外与司法部对峙。
- **诺克斯**：利用机械部通道和升降设备帮助转移朱丽叶；与朱丽叶的旧日矛盾开始松动。
- **护士**：违抗西姆斯夫妇，救走朱丽叶；被突击队抓获，生死未明。
- **保罗·比林斯**：发现尸体死因与官方说法不符，并开始独立调查。
- **伯纳德、罗伯特与卡米尔**：试图控制朱丽叶事件，避免引发更大范围的恐慌。
- **卢卡斯**：确认仍然活着；他相信外面的环境确实有毒，筒仓屏幕显示的荒凉景象是真实的。
- **丹尼尔**：继续调查妹妹夏洛特的任务异常，拒绝接受威胁。
- **山姆·拉姆齐**：重新投入调查，并发现了尚未公开的关键资料。

### 新出现的名字与术语

- **Emerson**：医疗部中与朱丽叶相关的人员。
- **Carla McLain**：供应部人员；她的学徒职位与失踪案有关。

- **Digger Void（挖掘机空洞）**：朱丽叶与乔治过去常去的深层区域，表面封锁但仍有隐藏通道。
- **第76层泵房**：护士安排朱丽叶之后前往的藏身地点。
- **Sam “the Ram” Ramsey**：山姆·拉姆齐，丹尼尔过去服役及乐队时期的朋友。
- **Helen**：与丹尼尔接触的神秘助手。
- **Radford Group**：网络安全智库；史蒂文·普莱斯目前任职于此。
- **“Big Foot Lives”**：网络国际象棋用户名的残余部分，可能是寻找神秘对手的线索。

### 连续性关键信息

- 有人提前替换了朱丽叶的药物；幕后身份暂未揭示。
- 卢卡斯没有死亡，且继续坚持“外面有毒”的官方版本。
- 医疗部摄像头曾被关闭，说明有人具备系统权限并在掩盖行动。

- 司法部掌握突击队和线人网络，能够迅速封锁楼层、监控通讯并抹除证据。
- 朱丽叶与乔治曾到过挖掘机空洞；该区域可能隐藏更大的筒仓秘密。

## S03E03

## 剧情概述

- 朱丽叶在记忆受损后独自走遍18号筒仓，执着追查卢卡斯·凯尔的下落。她相信卢卡斯可能利用矿井横向挖掘，试图联系其他筒仓。
- 卡米尔·西姆斯因奥拉·肯特失踪而调查朱丽叶和卢卡斯。她发现卢卡斯可能并未死于叛乱后的矿井清理。
- 为逼出卢卡斯，卡米尔下令向矿井释放毒气，且未提前疏散人员。朱丽叶与机械部成员展开营救，在矿井中找到卢卡斯和一具身份不明的尸体；奥拉也被发现重伤。
- 伯纳德向卡米尔施压，要求她利用朱丽叶在矿井中的英勇行为制造团结叙事，并除掉朱丽叶这个“存亡级别的威胁”。
- 丹尼尔·基恩追查妹妹夏洛特参与的伊朗任务。他与海伦前往暗网中间人处，确认存在一段未加密的任务通讯录音。录音显示四架飞机及其飞行员同时失去控制，事件背后疑似存在人为操纵。
- 罗伯特·西姆斯与卡米尔讨论维生素D+。这种药物能逐步影响记忆，罗伯特认为必要时可将其投入供水，以换取筒仓整体存活，并让儿子安东尼有机会将来离开筒仓。

## 重要角色行动

- 朱丽叶：拒绝停留在安全区域，前往裂口和矿井调查卢卡斯；在毒气袭击中救出卢卡斯，并确认奥拉身份。
- 卡米尔：追查卢卡斯和朱丽叶，批准矿井熏蒸；接受伯纳德提出的政治处置方案，决定杀死朱丽叶。
- 伯纳德：将朱丽叶定义为威胁，要求卡米尔用她的死亡避免动用维生素D+；明确强调信息部主管的核心能力是欺骗。
- 罗伯特：努力维持与卡米尔及儿子安东尼的家庭联系，同时准备执行维生素D+计划。
- 诺克斯、雪莉和机械部：协助朱丽叶调查矿井，参与毒气撤离与救援。
- 丹尼尔：调查夏洛特任务的通讯异常，并决定继续取得完整录音。
- 海伦：因追查夏洛特任务而被解雇，仍陪丹尼尔寻找录音。
- 卢卡斯：在矿井中现身，证明此前关于其死亡的判断可能是假的。
- 奥拉·肯特：在矿井毒气事件中被发现重伤，生死未明。

## 新建立的名字与术语

- 夏洛特·基恩：丹尼尔的妹妹，伊朗任务中的高级飞行员。
- 胡安／华尼托：在“沙漠风暴”及后续军事任务中阵亡的士兵。
- 帕特里克·肯尼迪：18号筒仓头号通缉犯，掌握地下隐蔽地点。
- 奥拉·肯特：供应部主管麦克莱恩的影子，近期失踪。
- 迈克·戴维森：奥拉的新男友，曾是格伦达·哈伍德的伴侣。
- 格伦达·哈伍德：麦克莱恩的前任影子。
- 马克·钱伯斯：工具锻造部门主管，曾与卢卡斯或矿井相关人员接触。
- 维生素D+：可影响记忆的药物；大规模投入供水可能成为筒仓级别的控制或生存方案。
- 暗网中间人“史蒂夫”：持有伊朗任务录音的联系人，真实身份不明。
- Digger Void／挖掘机空洞：矿井中的封闭区域，玛莎已在三个月前封锁。
- 熏蒸／灭鼠行动：司法部以灭鼠为名向矿井释放毒气，实际目标是逼出卢卡斯。

## 连续性关键信息

- 卢卡斯的“死亡”尚未成立；他至少在本集毒气行动时仍活着。
- 卢卡斯作为伯纳德的影子，知道其他筒仓的存在；他在信息部、矿井和伯纳德身边获得的知识可能相互关联。
- 筒仓法律严格限制隧道挖掘深度，原因可能与防止筒仓之间建立联系有关。
- 任何筒仓之间的接触都会立即触发保障程序。
- 卡米尔曾在朱丽叶被送去清洁前放她离开，原因并非单纯害怕突袭队；她承认自己当时希望看到朱丽叶能查出什么。
- 伯纳德承认信息部主管的工作核心是欺骗，且卡米尔在叛乱期间操控罗伯特、两面周旋的能力正是她被选中的原因。
- 朱丽叶因记忆药物失去关于父亲的重要情感记忆，但她的判断力、技术能力和追查真相的本能仍在。
- 奥拉的失踪可能与供应部、影子制度和麦克莱恩有关。
- 伊朗任务通讯使用了过时且未加密的设备，四架飞机同时失控的原因仍未解释。

## 笑点与后续可用引用

- 丹尼尔和海伦前往暗网找录音时，海伦说地点可疑；丹尼尔回应“暗网的特点就是可疑”。

- 朱丽叶对雪莉说，只要自己还记得，就会一直记诺克斯这笔账。
- 罗伯特试图安排家庭日和公园活动，却被卡米尔指出他们长期因筒仓公务缺席家庭生活。

- 毒气行动造成的具体伤亡，以及奥拉能否存活。

## S03E05

### Episode plot

- Juliette and Bernard survive the fire outside Silo 18. Mechanical workers break protocol and open the airlock before the required 48-hour delay, rescuing Juliette while Bernard remains critically burned.
- Juliette is unconscious in hospital and is sworn in as mayor from her bed. Camille, now head of IT, plans to administer forgetting drugs so Juliette will not remember what she saw in Silo 17.
- Robert Sims secretly turns against Camille’s orders. He asks Mechanical to hide Bernard’s apparent death and helps protect Juliette.
- Juliette, Bernard, Lukas, and Mechanical begin coordinating against the Safeguard. They learn that there are 50 silos, each with its own radio frequency, while Silo 1 monitors all channels.
- Juliette’s recovered memories reveal that the Safeguard is a poison-filled pipe capable of killing all 10,000 people in a silo. Silo 17 capped its pipe, but the group still does not know where the system enters or how it is controlled.
- Mechanical discovers that Critical Supply’s light-bulb reserves have been systematically stolen. Only about two years of usable bulbs remain, threatening the farms and the silo’s food supply.
- Orla, the Supply head’s shadow, was murdered while investigating the thefts. Bernard’s former shadow and other highly informed people are hidden in the lower levels.

### Important character actions

- Juliette survives the exterior fire and becomes mayor, but remains vulnerable to memory suppression.
- Bernard admits that the voice in the vault ordered him to act and that it may be able to kill everyone in the silo.
- Robert chooses to betray Camille’s faction, helps fake Bernard’s death, and recruits Knox and Shirley.
- Camille takes control of IT and orders Juliette’s death, while Robert hides the extent of his opposition from her.
- Lukas works with Juliette to understand the Safeguard and the radio network.
- Knox and Shirley help move Bernard and protect the group, despite still resenting his role in the rebellion.
- Juliette’s friends use relics to stimulate her memories. George’s watch finally reaches her and helps trigger an important recollection.
- Regina is ordered to obtain Juliette’s sheriff badge and other personal items, which may help restore Juliette’s memory.

### Newly established names and terms

- The Order: a secret rulebook for the head of IT and their shadow, covering emergency actions and memory-drug procedures.
- Safeguard: a silo-wide extermination system involving poison in a pipe.
- Silo 1: the silo that monitors the radio frequencies of the other silos.
- Critical Supply: a restricted store of irreplaceable items loaded into the silo at its founding.
- Amy Hansen: the nurse caring for Juliette.
- Orla: the murdered Supply shadow investigating Critical Supply thefts.
- Glenda: a capable but difficult Supply employee.
- Salvador Quinn and Mary Meadows: two of the only three people previously known to have reached the deep door; George Wilkins was the third.
- “Channels”: the separate radio frequencies assigned to individual silos.

### Continuity-relevant facts

- Forgetting drugs can erase a person’s memories entirely if used too long, but relics from the person’s past may trigger recovery.
- The Order explicitly instructs IT leaders to keep drugged people away from personal artifacts.
- Juliette’s dream of being with Bernard in the airlock was actually a suppressed memory. She had intended to die outside before meeting Juliette changed the situation.
- Juliette remembers that George found a door and that the Safeguard pipe was capped in Silo 17.
- George’s watch was passed from Juliette to her father, then to another person, and is now returned to Juliette.
- Critical Supply reserves are much lower than official expectations: approximately two years of light bulbs remain, putting agricultural production at risk.
- The group must communicate in code because Silo 1 monitors active frequencies.
- Bernard claims Juliette knows how to stop the people or system capable of killing the silo, though she cannot yet remember how.

### Jokes and references useful later

- Mechanical mocks Bernard’s concern about the 48-hour airlock rule with “Fuck 48 hours.”
- Knox and Shirley threaten to throw Bernard into the incinerator for real if Juliette is harmed.
- Robert jokes that his “sheriff card” is actually a badge.
- Bernard’s burned condition prompts jokes about everything smelling or tasting burnt.
- Juliette and Bernard’s discussion of fear in the mines reinforces the Mechanical department’s blunt, practical humor.

### Unresolved threads

- How does Silo 1 activate the Safeguard, and why was the poison pipe installed?
- Can Juliette recover her memories before Camille’s drugs erase them?
- Who is stealing irreplaceable supplies, and was Orla killed because of the investigation?
- What is behind the deep door and what remains of the machine that dug the silos?
- Why does George’s research connect the door, the relics, and the Safeguard?
- Will Robert’s betrayal be discovered by Camille?
- Can the 50 silos communicate without Silo 1 detecting them?

## S03E01

# Silo S03E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Three months after the rebellion, **Juliette Nichols** serves as Mayor of Silo 18. The episode opens with her morning routine—vitamins, mushroom soup delivery from Tony—establishing her reluctant public role. The **first Silo Council meeting** convenes in the Mids cafeteria: level representatives and department heads now govern jointly. **Robert Sims** delivers a speech framing Bernard Holland as a usurper whose authority "wasn't in the Pact," positioning himself and Camille as reluctant converts to transparency. He introduces Juliette as "the only person sent out to clean ever to return."

Department reports follow: **Orla Kent** (26, Supply shadow) reports Critical Supply items missing, possibly stolen via construction; **Ed Harwood** (Mines) reveals a B-92 tunnel collapse and that the Pact forbids digging more than 200 feet outward—300 years of tunnels double back on themselves; **Knox** (Mechanical) credits Juliette's fix for the generator; **Paul Billings** (Sheriff) notes crime is down but Kennedy's masked "Outsiders" remain at large, and Lukas Kyle is still missing.

Intercut with the Silo timeline: **Daniel Keene** (Congressman, Georgia) presses to join the **Iran Committee** after a dirty bomb hits DC. His sister **Charlotte** (F-35 pilot, *Nimitz*) flies a retaliatory strike—but her squadron hits a **mountain facility**, not the stated targets. She's shot down, rescued by a Turkmenistan team, and survives with **TBI**. Daniel learns she's being sent to the **Heidi Stensen Clinic** (Fairfax), run by **Thurman's mother**.

Back in the Silo, **Juliette experiences fragmented memories/dreams** of being outside the airlock with Bernard, feeling an overwhelming compulsion to "do something." **Camille Sims** guides her through breathing exercises, reinforcing the "refuge hut" narrative (insulated suit, helmet, waited until strong enough to return). But Juliette recalls a **crowbar**—not the official story. **Sims** confronts her with the truth: he watched Bernard burn; the suit was insulated, Bernard's wasn't.

The episode ends with **Camille and "The Algorithm"** discussing Juliette's memory suppression. The Algorithm orders: **"Double the dosage."**

# Silo S03E10 — Inward and Upward

## Plot
The episode intercuts three timelines that finally converge.

17号筒仓 (the present inciting incident). After Solo (索洛) hides Jimmy (吉米), Russell Conroy's (罗素·康罗伊) young son, inside the Forward Vault (前部地库), the silo descends into open rebellion. The sheriff leads a charge across a blown bridge, seizes Conroy, and uses him as a human shield to force Jimmy to open the Vault. Jimmy refuses. A Safeguard is attempted but fails: no pressure drop, the compound pipe is suspected disabled. Susan, the senior drone commander Susan, working with Troy (特洛伊), a cryo specialist who has been rapid-sequenced after seven years on ice, scrambles six drone groups (30 L of compound plus 2,000 rounds of ordnance). When the sheriff reaches the berm with 9,832 fugitives, Troy orders a full external attack; Susan refuses to fire on women and children and is dismissed. Compound is released, the airlock is gassed, all six groups engage, thermal sweeps finish the runners, and 9,835 fugitives are killed. Internal survivors number under one hundred. Russell's body is delivered to Jimmy in IT, communications to Jimmy are cut, and armed drone surveillance over the ruins is set for five years. Susan is demoted and put back to cryo for "the end of The Project."

The Project's aftermath (the "present" of the wider arc). Troy is debriefed and given a certificate of merit by the Director. Susan is discussed and the Directive (《指令》) is cited as the binding authority; the Senator (参议员) is named as having forbidden inquiries into past records. Troy confesses to Victor (维克多) that he keeps seeing a woman when he is sequenced; Victor promises to find a pretext to research her.

18号筒仓 (the climactic thread). Sims (西姆斯, i.e. Robert Sims 罗伯特·西姆斯) pleads with the Director to stop a Safeguard of Silo 18, claiming raiders (突击队员) are launching a counteroffensive. He is given five minutes. Down in the mines, Juliette (朱丽叶), Knox (诺克斯), Shirley (雪莉), Walker (玛莎·沃克), Teddy (泰迪), Nash (纳什), and Hoover (胡佛) fight the raiders while the Safeguard pressurizes. The Safeguard appears to fire; pressure fails to drop. The line was capped: the Safeguard failed, the rebels survived. Sims privately orders the raiders to stand down. No one is sent to clean. Juliette is reunited with her son Anthony (安东尼). Bernard (伯纳德) and Chloe (克洛伊) confirm Troy has been woken. Juliette radios Lukas (卢卡斯), who is healing in another silo and is "so good to hear" her. She promises to bring everyone home but must first revisit the door Lukas found in the Digger Void (挖掘机空洞).

The closing turn (a parallel cryo shift, 35 years later). A different Phil rapid-sequences a different Troy with the same thermal flush ritual; greeter banter echoes the opening beat. Henry (亨利) of Internal Security explains that Silo 18 destabilized about three months ago after Juliette was sent out to clean, that she made it across to Silo 17 and back on good tape, and that Conroy defected to the rebels long enough for them to cap the pipe. A new Victor is reportedly missing; another Founder has killed himself and left no note; the last order on his desk was to wake Troy. Troy is briefed on the failed 18 Safeguard and told armed surveillance is up. He asks Operations to pull up activity near 18's Forward Vault. On screen, Juliette and her group stand at the door Lukas found. A voice orders them not to investigate. Juliette negotiates: no contact with another silo, no Safeguard, no investigation. She accepts. Walking back, the group realizes a Safeguard cannot reach them from the surface but could come via the door, via underground drilling, or via a follow-up attack. They decide the only durable answer is to preempt the people who run The Project: "We need to take out Silo 1" (1号筒仓).

The episode ends with Troy receiving a pre-recorded message from Victor, who has "taken himself off shift, permanently." Victor confesses relief that the Safeguard of 18 failed and finally keeps his earlier promise: the woman Troy keeps seeing is named Helen Drew (海伦·德鲁).
