# S01E01 剧情摘要

## 整体剧情
本集采用双线结构。开场是 1914 年的德国:一个反派的队伍为召唤"地精女王"(Die Gartenzwergkoenigin)举行仪式,在一个谷仓中用至日与"处女之血"试图完成咒语,结果被反抗的女孩与年长男人(Kurt Hayward 库尔特·海沃德)破坏——咒语未生效,斧头(地精女王的力量之源)被夺走;追逐中敌方头目 Stotzenberg 试图以咒语反扑但被击退,Stotzenberg 中弹倒下;Kurt 在临终前把斧头托付给一个人(Lydia 莉迪亚),嘱咐"绝不能让她落人坏人之手,她永不见天日"。旁白式闪回结束。

跳到当代:澳大利亚小镇 Nompton 正紧锣密鼓筹备首届 "Gnome-A-Palooza"(格诺姆狂欢节)。警局的高个子长官 Arnold Kipps(阿诺德·基普斯,高级警长,被同事们叫 Arnie / Lucky)与留守同事 Devon(戴文)一起忙前忙后。本集主轴是 Arnold 当天的几件糟心事逐步叠加,小镇处于脆弱状态,最后在夜里被地精女王"惊醒",在 Arnold 家中与他正面对话。

# Gnomes S01E02 — Summary

## Plot
The episode picks up in the wreckage of the town hall after the previous episode's rampage. Arnold Kipps, hungover and having spent the night hiding in Mrs. Dunkly's house, is in trouble on multiple fronts: his ex Ellie has returned to Nompton on a closure/evaluation assignment, his station is under scrutiny, and the Gnome Queen (the ceramic antique the mayor is using as the festival's centerpiece) has been stolen along with dozens of ordinary garden gnomes across town.

The mayor, Lachlan Morrison, confesses to Arnold that he stole the entire council budget to buy the Gnome Queen from a German museum, so recovering her isn't optional — "no Gnome Queen, no Nompton." He's also terrified because Channel Six reporter Brant Sanders is coming to do a live opening-ceremony spot and the festival hinges on a tidy ticket-sale profit.

Arnold pulls the local teens — Zayn Falkner, Jenny, and Dylan — in for questioning, and their parents show up in force to defend them. Zayn insists he was home all night and, more tellingly, says every lightbulb in his house exploded, which Arnold notes is suspicious. The Falkner father, Michael, is hostile and trying to shift blame onto neighbour Apostoli.

Parallel threads develop:
- Arnold and Ellie drive to old Berty Henrickson's place (the caretaker whose car is at the hall) to track the lead. Ellie finally gets Arnold to admit, on the record, that she was right about the wider case.
- Josie (Devon's mum) is panicking about being transferred to the city by rail. Devon keeps trying to dunk biscuits in her camomile tea.
- Arnold's overdue DVDs at VideOzone (a Blockbuster analogue) have piled up 14 missed messages; the manager Evan is missing when Arnold returns them.
- The mayor escorts two visiting journalists from "Better Gnomes And Gardens" magazine (Gillian and Dana) around town, trying to redirect them from the missing queen. At the local pub, he introduces master gnome-craftsman Tobias Black, who shows off pop-culture pieces ("Game Of Gnomes" / "You know nothing, Jon Snow," "50 Shades Of Clay," plus a Harry gnome).
- Arnold is told his daughter's pony Mr Fluffy has gone missing, and that a chicken called Melba was killed.
- Berty Henrickson takes Rosie (the dog) out against Arnold's instructions, determined to clear his name by proving he's not the thief.
- Devon radios the station to report an "escalation with Barb" (her chickens were mutilated). The team decides the vandalism and gnome thefts must be connected and retools the investigation: Josie searches the hall, Arnold and Ellie go after Berty, Devon handles the chicken scene.
- A mysterious voice at the empty VideOzone store is talking to Arnold about his "DVD pyramid," and the episode ends mid-threat: a gnome growls "I know you're the one stealing the gnomes" at someone in the dark.

## S01E03

# Episode S01E03 — Gnomes

## Plot
The episode picks up in Nompton the morning after the Gnome Queen statue is stolen. Arnold Kipps, partnered with the returning Ellie McKay, tries to manage two parallel disasters: a forensic nightmare in the back of an ice-cream truck and the public-relations catastrophe of the inaugural Gnome-a-Palooza festival going live. Meanwhile, the teens Zayn, Rosie, Jenny and Dylan have captured real footage of a murderous garden gnome on Zayn's camcorder but can't get past the town's "weird cop" (they eventually seek out Police Officer Lucky, who sends them to Arnold). A mysterious new sheriff pulls into town just in time for the festival reveal, forcing the killer-gnome secret into the open — and ending on a whiplash cliffhanger.

## Important Character Actions
- Arnold: Tells Ellie he passed the hall last night and the Queen "was definitely still there." Attempts to cancel Gnome-a-Palooza after the mutilated body turns up with no physical evidence, no suspects, no leads. Falls for Mayor Lachlan Morrison's wink-bribe ("no Gnome Queen, no Nompton") and reverses the cancellation. Assigns Devon to keep trying Forensics, Josie to stash the corpse in the truck's freezers, and himself to track the missing Mr Henrickson. Faints while playing Wingardium Leviosa ("Berty Henrickson!") in a stunt gone wrong and is out cold in the patrol car at episode's end.
- Ellie: Pushes for the festival shutdown, openly clashes with Arnold over his bribe deal and his double-wink ("you just winked…/No, I didn't"). Concedes to the "complicated" teenage Zayn-Dylan-Jenny-Rosie group. Moments after Arnold collapses she realises the keys are in his pocket and the doors are locked.
- Mayor Lachlan Morrison ("plum chuffed," over-ambitious, winking bribe-offerer): Pulls strings to keep the festival open because "no Gnome Queen, no Nompton." Late in the episode he pulls back a velvet cover on stage to "introduce the oldest gnome on Earth" — a stand-in Gnome Queen ("She'll have to do until we find the real one / Or until she finds you") — and that stand-in kills a man in front of the crowd ("Kill me! / I want my money back!").
- Devon (Dev): The officer who keeps getting his mouth open near the body (the corpse hisses vent gas in his face twice: "Oh, God. / Devon, mouth shut! / That one got in my eyes"). Hands out Gnome-a-Palooza flyers, is mocked on live radio ("why there's so many cops at the video store"), and is tapped for his partner who pulls a stolen-camera stunt at festival.
- Josie: Tasked with putting the body "somewhere cold" — they stash the corpse inside the ice-cream freezer. Repeatedly asked to eat through 50 L of ice cream to keep it frozen ("I've eaten as much as I can… Too much ice cream - I think I'm gonna…"). Becomes the dispatch voice when Arnold radios for backup.
- Bev (Barbara): Front-desk operator; keeps the radio, takes Josie's plea to eat ice cream, and apologises to "Jennifer" that Lucky must be off his radio.
- Lucky (Officer Kipps): The "weird cop" the kids finally seek out; redirects them to Arnold.
- Teen foursome: Zayn, Rosie, Jenny and Dylan. Zayn is the self-aware delinquent ("I'm an arsehole… sporadic acts of petty violence and low to mid-level property damage") whose mum is off-frame and whose son studied at school with a new counsellor. Rosie is the target of all three boys' crushes and gets the film-evidence moment ("Rosie! / I think we killed it"). Jenny is the sceptical one ("He's making this shit up / he's just gonna say the same thing that that weird cop said"). Dylan is the violent Zayn foil who ends up in a fistfight with the Mayor's son during the festival ("Get your hands off my son! / Your son is a criminal").
- Brant Sanders: Sleazy media / influencer personality (rumour: "had two ribs removed so he could…"), on his "last warning," openly leers at Ellie ("a lady cop… the first time I wouldn't mind getting screwed by the police"). Demands a "hero shot" of the Gnome Queen at festival time, then films himself ("Trying to send a photo… Photo of what? / My… / — Brant? / Gross.").
- Superintendent Harvey Poll: Rides in on a motorbike ("getting my Ks in… keep this rig tight"), admits he dismissed the kids' footage ("What an idiot — a handsome idiot — I've been"), finds the sidearms stolen by gnomes, pulls out nunchucks, and rallies Arnold and Ellie as "Spear Nompston's dynamic duo" with a dojo gesture ("One mind, one body"). Gets captured ("knocked off his bike") by the Mayor as a hostage, shouts "Not so ready!" when he realises the Mayor's weapon is aimed at him. After his capture, he delivers a sincere deathbed-style confession to Eloise ("You're the best damn policewoman… police person… police… Shit.") just before the villain tosses his badge off the bridge ("No, I need that to live!").
- Gnome Queen (villain): Previously missing, returns as the stand-in statue that kills a stagehand mid-reveal. By the end of the episode she is driving the patrol car herself with the unconscious Arnold inside ("Who's driving the car? / No-one?"), and a snotty vent ("Bring me my hatchet. / Wake up.") implies she is using Arnold's body or voice.
- Stefanie: Perky local who sidetracks Arnold with a pool-party invite ("swimsuit optional"); quickly rebuffed by Ellie.
- "Jim" / "Jan": Mayor: Trash jokes ("Shtum!") at the teen scuffle, calls Zayn's dad at the festival ("Officer, arrest this man!" / "Your son is a criminal"), and appears to take Harvey hostage late in the episode.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The Gnome Queen statue was confirmed stolen overnight; Arnold saw it intact the night before, but cannot account for the missing window.
- Mr Henrickson was the last person to see the Gnome Queen and is now missing; a torch with his blood on it was found.
- The mutilated body has zero physical evidence — "not so much as a bloody fingerprint" — suggesting non-human agency (i.e., a gnome did it, not a person).
- No mobile reception in the hall; Forensics cannot be reached. This isolation will recur.
- The body is stashed in the ice-cream truck, with Josie eating her way through 50 L of ice cream to keep it cold. This is a permanent Chekhov's gun for the rest of the run.
- Arnold's station is "being shut down"; the Gnome Queen is explicitly framed as the town's economic anchor ("no Gnome Queen, no Nompton").
- The Mayor and Brant are ceding territory to each other — Brant wants a "hero shot," the Mayor cedes; the Mayor offers Harvey a "good word" with Brant's super.
- The kids' camcorder footage is now in police hands (Harvey pockets it after Arnold collapses).
- Arnold was on his "last warning" before this incident — that status is going to matter when Brent's "super" arrives.
