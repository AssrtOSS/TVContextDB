# Gnomes (2026) — Background

## Basic facts
- Title: Gnomes (Chinese working title: 《地精浩劫》 — also seen as 《花园怪物》in trade press; use 《地精浩劫》for consistency with the "Gnome-ageddon" marketing framing).
- Format: TV series, live-action comedy-horror; 1 season, 6 episodes, ~30 min each.
- Premiere: 8 October 2026 (all episodes dropped on the Stan platform that day; first linear-style slot listed as Thursday 11:59 pm AEST).
- Broadcaster / distributor: Stan (Australia, streaming; backed by Nine). International sales by Happy Accidents; German distribution by Network Movie.
- Production: Total Fiction and Screen Invaders; major investment from Screen Australia, in association with Stan, with support from Scribble Films and VicScreen; post/VFX support from Screen Queensland.
- Creators / showrunner: Joel Kohn (creator; also director and co-producer; previously The Mirror). Writers: Tegan Higginbotham and Paul F. Verhoeven. Set-up director / EP: Matthias Hoene (Theodosia, Cockneys vs Zombies). Other directors: Sian Davies. Producers: John Molloy, Richard Kelly; Stan EPs Donna Chang and Andy Ryan.
- Genre positioning: pulpy splatter-gore horror-comedy, explicitly pitched as a 1980s monster-mashup in the vein of Gremlins and Edgar Wright's Hot Fuzz, with a "small-town besieged" structure.

## Setting
- Nompton — a fading Australian country town. The name is a deliberate pun on "gnome" + "hampton" (suggesting bourgeois suburbia); render as 诺普顿 or keep as Nompton in subtitles since it is a fictional, not real-world, place.
- The town is hosting its inaugural "Gnome-a-Palooza" festival on the eve of the outbreak — a celebration of garden-gnome kitsch that becomes the trigger for the horror.
- Suburban / cul-de-sac aesthetic: tidy front lawns, terracotta ornaments, pastel houses. Visually pitched as ordinary Australia overrun by the uncanny.
- The Gnome Queen is a regal, ancient antagonist who awakens an "ancient evil" and animates a horde of ceramic garden gnomes into killers.

## Relationships and team structure
- Arnold Kipps and Ellie McKay are exes on the force: ex-partner in policing and ex-girlfriend in life. Their reunion under crisis is the central two-hander.
- Arnold's police station is being shut down; Ellie returns to Nompton specifically to deliver that closure, which sets up the emotional friction at the start of the outbreak.
- The "ragtag team" the duo pulls together across the season is described in the official synopsis as misfit cops, rebellious teens, and an overly ambitious mayor — treat this trio as the recurring supporting archetypes.
- The Gnome Queen is the season antagonist; the killer ceramic gnomes are her foot-soldier horde ("a hellish horde," "a nightmare horde of killer ceramic gnomes").
- Tone of relationships: banter-heavy, ex-lovers-slash-partners dynamic, Australian small-town police-procedural sarcasm, with sudden horror pivots.

## Recurring terminology and Chinese renderings
- Gnome — 地精 (lit. "earth-spirit") in fantasy/folklore usage; for the garden-ornament sense used here, 《地精》is standard in Chinese-language press coverage of similar horror properties. If a more vernacular register is needed, 花园小矮人 / 陶瓷小矮人 is acceptable for the physical ornaments.
- Gnome Queen — 地精女王
- Nompton — 诺普顿 (fictional town name; do not transliterate as "诺普顿镇" every time — vary with 小镇诺普顿 / 诺普顿镇).
- Senior Sergeant / Senior Constable — 高级警长 / 高级警员; Australian police ranks, not US ones; "Senior Sergeant" in Victoria Police rank is roughly equivalent to 警督级别.
- Police station — 警局 / 派出所 (avoid "警署" which is HK usage).
- Gnome-a-Palooza (festival) — 格诺姆狂欢节 / 地精音乐节; first official town festival, recurring set-piece.
- Gnome-ageddon (marketing term) — 地精末日 / 格诺姆大灾变.
- "Cuddleous garden gnomes" (pogdesign synopsis) — 看似可爱的花园地精 (foreground the "看似可爱" to preserve the ironic-horror tone).
- Splatter-gore — 血浆恐怖 / 喷洒式恐怖片.
- "Smash faces and take names" — colloquial marketing line; render as 把脸揍扁再记仇 (or keep the English idiom with a footnote the first time).

## Speaking style
- Australian vernacular throughout: "sub-par day," "totally sub-par," "ragtag," "misfit," "having a grudge," colloquial cop banter. For Chinese subtitles, use 澳式口语 equivalents (口语化、缩略句、"伙计""老兄"等), not stiff Mainland-mandarin or HK-Cantonese.
- Horror-comedy tonal contrast: deadpan sarcasm while bodies drop. The series "acknowledges its own absurdity" per ScreenHub — keep one-liners dry rather than panicked.
- Character voices: Arnold — under-pressure, "phoney bravado" everyman cop (Guardian review); Ellie — sharper, return-from-the-city professional; the Gnome Queen — regal, menacing, theatrical.
- In the press material, gnome-related puns are used ("gnome-one here is getting out alive," "Gnome-ageddon," "Gnome-a-Palooza") — Chinese subtitles should keep the wordplay feel by either a direct pun (格诺姆 / 诺 / 精) or a footnote, not a literal dump.

## Cultural context
- Garden gnomes are a long-running European kitsch export — gnomes in 1980s suburban horror, Gremlins, and Troll dolls are the obvious touchstones, and the show's marketing openly invokes them. Chinese viewers may not have the lawn-gnome folk history; a one-line gloss ("欧美常见的花园装饰陶瓷小矮人") helps.
- The "small-town besieged by monsters" framing is a Hot Fuzz-style homage; if the audience is assumed unfamiliar, a brief subtitle gloss is appropriate.
- Australian police-rank vocabulary is rank-specific and not interchangeable with US ranks; the show leans on that local texture.

## Premise (season-spanning, not single-episode)
A fading Australian country town called Nompton is on the eve of its first Gnome-a-Palooza festival when an ancient evil is awakened by the Gnome Queen. The town's ceramic garden gnomes come to murderous life. Senior Sergeant Arnold Kipps, already dealing with the impending shutdown of his beloved police station, is forced to team up with his ex-partner and ex-girlfriend Senior Constable Ellie McKay, who has just returned to Nompton to deliver that shutdown. Over the six-episode season, Arnold and Ellie rally a ragtag crew of misfit cops, rebellious teens, and an over-ambitious mayor to fight the Gnome-apocalypse and save the town.
