## S01E02

```
S01E02 — Summary
```

The friends continue the key-drawing "screw-each-other" game proposed by Samu Meyer. Bella draws first; Anna draws and is paired with "Comrade Leo" (Leonardo Albert Pohlkamp / Valentin), a wealthy, self-described "white, male, wealthy, rather good-looking" German. A separate driver scene shows someone complaining about a taxi detour through Holzbach Street that pulls over for a burger.

Paul's pair (with Bella) opts out of sex and spends the evening on nostalgia: vinyl records making a comeback, a high school graduation song on a dock, Scrabble and pizza. Valentin treats Siena to a luxury champagne-and-oyster dinner at a high-rise venue ("ants in the colony of capitalism"), confesses his "principles" (pre-warmed porcelain cups, heirloom watches, daily-polished bathtub) and gets a Cinderella moment when Siena injures her ankle.

Anna and Valentin play Scrabble; he boasts he's never lost and slips a compliment ("beauty before age" instead of "age before beauty"). Anna confesses she's never slept with anyone but Paul, and worries Paul would never go through with it. Valentin tells her Paul is currently with someone else.

Valentin and Siena have sex; Paul and Siena separately have sex on a construction site where a security guard catches them. The "Sabine" name surfaces negatively (Valentin's comment). Anna calls Valentin's phone; he answers drunk, identifying himself as Leonardo Albert Pohlkamp, "victim of police brutality," and claims he knows the "best lawyer in the world," Christian Pratt.

At the police station: the officer lays out the situation — "the nerd" (Paul) "slept with the doll" (Siena), though his actual partner is "the pretty, angry woman" (Anna); Anna did not sleep with Leo/Valentin. Valentin declares the game isn't over and gets arrested. Paul defends Anna to Siena ("this is my wife"), but Anna still explodes when she realizes Paul went through with it. Paul tries to apologize in a side conversation but keeps stammering.

The next morning, Anna and Paul return home to their daughter Mica. Anna lies ("the car was parked in the driveway all night — what your mom says"). She confesses jealousy and then says Paul slept with someone while she didn't, and demands: "I want to play again." Paul: "What?"

## S01E01

# Episode S01E01 — Friends Sex and Games

## Plot
The episode opens mid-argument: Anna asks Paul to slap her during sex at a lakeside wedding reception; Paul refuses, calling himself "old-fashioned," while Anna calls him out of touch with "sexual liberation." Anna is roped into working the reception as a wedding planner with Bella. On a private dock she spots **Samu** — her high school crush — making out with **Siena**. Samu dropped out of school without explanation years ago and was rumored to be producing for Ariana Grande in LA; he's now only in town for a few weeks. He invites the whole friend group to dinner at his place the following evening.

At Samu's vast, borrowed Côte d'Azur apartment (huge, "rent looks like a phone number"), the friends play an escalating "Never Have I Ever." Leo admits spray-painting "Free Knut" on the Berlin Zoo; Samu alludes to Bangkok; Anna admits she's never been to a sex party (Julie mentions a tantra retreat that "got out of hand"). Siena proposes the **keys game**: men drop their keys on the table, women draw, the key owner is who you spend the night with. Anna pitches it to Paul; he resists but admits to "theoretical" curiosity. In the group chat, Julie & Valentin opt in, Leo in (only if he finds a babysitter), Bella in enthusiastically. Paul wavers, Anna presses, he relents. They agree on four rules — safe sex, "only yes means yes," no sex outside the game, no falling in love — and hand over the keys.

## Character actions
- **Anna** — drives the sexual-liberation theme; reconnects with Samu; spearheads convincing Paul.
- **Paul** — refuses the slap, refuses the game initially, then capitulates; nervously accepts on condition "it won't change anything."
- **Samu** — hosts, drops mysterious hints about Bangkok and a rumored pregnancy with high school math teacher Mrs. John.
- **Siena** — boldest of the group, proposes the keys game, has a side bet with Samu over Coachella, is promoting Ski Aggu.
- **Julie & Valentin** — married couple on the opposite shore; claim intimacy-not-sex; first to opt in via group chat.
- **Leo** — vandalizer of Berlin Zoo; conditioned on finding a babysitter for his kids.
- **Bella** — co-planner with Anna, enthusiastic adopter of the game.

## New names / terms
- **The keys game** — central premise: random key draw determines partner for the night.
- **Knut** — Berlin Zoo polar bear Leo references.
- **Ski Aggu** — artist Siena is promoting.
- **Commando X** — old school computer game; Leo calls it military-industrial indoctrination, Anna defends it as a puzzle game.
- **Tantra retreat** — Julie's offhand admission of last summer's "out of hand" event.
- **Mrs. John** — high school math teacher rumored to have gotten pregnant by Samu.
- **Coachella bet** — Siena wagers that if she doesn't sleep with one of them within a week, she pays for Samu's spring flights.
- **Mondrian Street site** — work project Anna mentions taking over.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Setting: lakeside locale with Mediterranean / Côte d'Azur feel; multiple couples have lake houses.
- Samu's residence is a borrowed apartment, not his own.
- Paul and Anna have two kids: **Karla** and **Che**; they bribe them into computer time, contradicting Anna's "no bribery" stance.
- Leo also needs a babysitter for his own kids — establishes him as a father.
- Julie is preparing an exhibition; Paul/Annas are juggling the Mondrian Street project and storyboard deadlines.
- Samu told Anna he was producing for Ariana Grande in LA; confirmed by Julie.
- Anna's high-school crush on Samu: dyed hair orange, drew freckles to imitate Lindsay Lohan (Samu's celebrity crush).
- Siena and Samu already play the keys game as a couple — established norm for them.

## Jokes / running gags
- "What century are you living in, Paul?" — Anna's recurring jab.
- "Mr. Guevara" — Siena's mocking nickname for Paul.
- Theory-vs-practice riff: communism, stuffed-crust pizza, jetpacks (better in theory).
- "Free Knut" graffitied on the Berlin Zoo — Leo's defiant, absurd activist moment.
- Samu mishears "rowboat" as "towboat."
- Paul's "sacred knight's oath" about the kids' video game vs. real-life duties.
- Coachella bet as a stakes device.

## Unresolved threads
- Who draws whose key, and what happens that night.
- Whether Paul will freeze or follow through.
- The Mrs. John pregnancy rumor — never confirmed or denied.
- Siena's Coachella bet outcome.
- Leo's babysitter hunt.
- Siena's promotion of Ski Aggu — likely to recur.
- Anna's work stress (storyboard, shake tasting poorly) and Julie's exhibition — peripheral but planted.

## S01E05

# S01E05 Summary

## Plot
A couple's key-drawing party organized by Siena. The women toss their keys into a bowl and the men draw to be randomly paired for the night. Paul, whose wife Julie goes home early because she is not in the mood, draws Bella's keys and they end up having sex. Paul drops Bella at her place rather than bringing her home. Julie returns home feeling inexplicably "inspired/electrified" and "more like herself." In a parallel subplot, wedding planners Anna and Leo scramble to land a high-profile celebrity wedding for "Bina Delara," while Leo wrestles with his discomfort over the price tag. The episode ends on a cliffhanger setup for a surprise.

## Important character actions
- **Bella** — Has just been told by her ob-gyn that her pregnancy chances are minimal and that she needs a hormone specialist. She attends Siena's party after a friend convinces her and gets drawn by Paul. She later serves Paul pancakes and thanks him for a "lovely evening."
- **Paul** — Lets Julie go home without him, stays at the party, draws Bella, has sex with her, drops her at her house (not his), and the next morning texts Julie "Can't stop thinking about you." He also confesses to a vague, unnamed longing — later revealed to be for **Mila**.
- **Julie** — Leaves the party early because she is not in the mood; doesn't want Paul to feel obligated to stay home with her. Comes home later feeling "inspired" and "electrified" by an unnamed experience she cannot describe.
- **Siena** — Host of the party; her invitation included more people than Bella expected. She runs the key-drawing ritual.
- **Valentin** — Draws keys that turn out to be his own.
- **Tom** — First man called on to draw.
- **"Comrade Leo"** — Partner in the wedding-planning business with Anna. Bitter about Delara's last-minute demands but pushes through because of the career opportunity.
- **Anna** — Wedding planner; texts with Samu and is stuck in her office on the Delara job.
- **Samu** — Texts Anna asking "Can we see each other?"
- **Sebastian** — Texts "I thought you'd never ask!" in response to an apparent dinner invite.
- **The mother/daughter breakfast scene** — Mother married at 18, retrospectively wonders if she missed out by marrying young; still considers herself lucky with Dad. Daughter jokes about wanting a wedding "like that."

- **Mila** — A woman Paul met while traveling; the unnamed "him" he is drawn to in spite of loving Julie.
- **Comrade Leo** — Leo's nickname/affectionate jab.
- **Bina Delara / Delara wedding** — A celebrity-tier client driving Leo and Anna crazy with last-minute changes; the wedding is a recurring status-symbol motif in the episode (also referenced by the daughter wanting a similar wedding).
- **The Key Game** — Siena's signature party format: women deposit keys, men draw. Recurring phrase: *"The game is the game."*

## S01E04

# S01E04 — Friends Sex and Games

## Plot Summary

The episode intercuts four overlapping threads: Samu's sudden entry into advertising work, Paul being pushed out of the same gig, a couples' "game" being relaunched with new participants, and Valentin/Mica's fertility arc.

Samu, freshly invited to an Ibiza DJ residency, decides to stay put after Paul and Anna ask him to take a crack at a chocolate-shake commercial for the client "Max Shroom." His demo wins over the young marketing team instantly, sidelining Paul (36, "too old," per the client). At home, Samu and Leonardo struggle to have sex because Leo's mother has taken over the apartment — they even establish the safe word "haute couture" after Leo squeezes himself into expensive underwear.

Meanwhile Paul and Anna announce they want to play "the game" again, but Valentin and Mica bow out in order to try for a baby. Anna proposes expanding the circle to keep numbers viable; the group toasts a new round. Paul, humbled by losing the client, ends up bonding with Samu over indie music (Donny Slade) and concedes he had underestimated Samu. Anna, dealing with a "sex crisis," heads alone to Jon's bar, runs into Samu, then leaves abruptly after grabbing the wrong bag.
