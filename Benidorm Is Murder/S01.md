## S01E02 "Die Laughing" — Episode Summary

### Plot
Vince Starky — a faded 1990s game-show host (*Quiz Bang*, *Luck Be A Lady*) attempting a comeback with five nights at the Benidorm Palace — is found dead on the roof of the Laughter Lounge comedy club. A fire extinguisher (used as a doorstop) is the apparent murder weapon. Dennis and Rosa investigate alongside DS Maria Hernandez and PC Jesús Ballesteros.

### Key Character Actions
- **Dennis & Rosa**: Re-form their reluctant partnership; Dennis spots the fire-extinguisher doorstop, deduces the locked-room mechanic, and ultimately solves the case via the "dead people don't sunburn" insight.
- **Frank Mann**: Veteran comic, Chris's presumed father. Reveals he and Vince were the double act "Half Rice Half Chips" 30 years ago; both slept with the same Redcoat (Linda). Frank is infertile (childhood mumps), so Chris is Vince's biological son. Frank argued with Vince on the roof the night of the murder — Vince wanted to tell Chris the truth; Frank wanted the secret kept.
- **Chris Mann**: Aspiring comic. After learning Vince is his real father, he pushes Vince in a rage; Vince falls, hits his head on the fire extinguisher, and lies unconscious. Chris flees, panics, and later confesses to the "murder."
- **Vienna**: Vince's new wife (married weeks). Lied about going to bed — actually went to Cheeky's Foam Disco (stamp on wrist). Motive: inheritance, but she didn't kill him.
- **Ziggy**: Comedy promoter, heavily in debt, sleeping in her office. Took out event insurance on Vince's 5-night run (which had sold <25% of tickets). **Actual killer**: Found Vince alive but unconscious on the roof at sunrise, finished him off with the fire extinguisher to claim the insurance, tossed the weapon off the roof (locking herself out), then staged an "air-con engineer" call to regain entry.
- **Paco**: Doorman; found the body, gave a poetic Spanish testimony.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Half Rice Half Chips** — Frank & Vince's old double-act name
- **Laughter Lounge** — the comedy club (crime scene)
- **Benidorm Palace** — theater venue for Vince's residency
- **Cheeky's Foam Disco** — nightclub Vienna visited
- **Event insurance** — Ziggy's motive (pays out if headline act dies)
- **"Dead people don't sunburn"** — Dennis's forensic key

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Dennis's estranged daughter Carla (birthday call rejected by her boyfriend Sam).
- Frank's infertility (mumps) → Chris is Vince's son, raised by Frank.
- Ziggy's debt → sleeps in office, smokes on roof (black marks on wall).
- The Tavern (Dennis's bar) toilets still need fixing (Maria's jab).

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **"Dead people don't sunburn."** (Dennis's catch-phrase deduction)
- **"Invasion of the Boddingtons Snatchers"** — Chris's zombie-tourist bit

- **Bobby Davro** sending flowers (running gag)

### Unresolved Threads

- Ziggy's full fallout (insurance fraud + murder charges)
- Whether Frank faces charges for withholding the initial push

## S01E03 "Henidorm" — Episode Summary

### Plot
A hen weekend in Benidorm. At a bar on Carrer Guevara, maid of honour Megan Heaney announces she's hired a luxury villa ("Love Island amazing," pin dropped in the WhatsApp group) as a surprise. Bride-to-be Leona is furious — she organised the weekend and it's "her thing"; Megan snaps that Leona's mam Tania is a "jailbird" and gets called a "wagon." Megan storms off alone at midnight, saying she's heading to the villa.

Back at the pub, Rosa reveals Dennis's alcohol licence expired two years and five months ago and blackmails him into taking the case ("Maria can protect you — make yourself indispensable"). At the villa, Megan Heaney, 35, is dead, strangled with a sash. Dennis reads it as a crime of passion: no forced entry, two champagne glasses, killer escaped via the patio doors, knocking over an ornament; a partial shoe print by the front door isn't Megan's (she wore heels).

The hens are interviewed at the pub, but all their alibis collapse: Chloe and Leona claimed they left at 2am (the bar closes at 1); Cora claimed a 1am beach walk (the beach closes at midnight); Tania remembers nothing, is missing €200 and has a bad back. Boyfriend Paul Murphy dishes the dirt — Leona lived in Megan's shadow, Tania has a temper, and ex Chloe went "looper" after he left her for Megan two years ago (cut up his trousers, sent threatening messages). Chloe confesses to Rosa that she still hates Megan, but Jesús confirms her alibi: she was getting a four-hour tattoo from just after 1am. Informant Jackie (scoot-mobile, Rulox watches) reports Megan and Cora arguing, with Cora swinging at her. Staked out, Cora admits to a drunken fling with stripper Bernardo (Tania paid for the private dance); Megan saw them, laughed, and threatened to tell Sean, Rock Choir and Father Brian. "I might be a wanton harlot, but I'm no murderer."

The shoe print matches Leona, who is missing. Dennis finds her first: at ~1:30am she went to the villa to confront Megan, found the door open, heard a smash, saw Megan already dead, saw a figure flash past the window, and ran — not calling anyone because "Mam said not to trust the police." Dennis re-reads the villa: heart-shaped balloons, champagne, "not a penis in sight" — romantic, not fun. Megan had hired it so Paul could propose: pop the balloon, take the ring, kneel. When the hens refused to come back, she called it off. Paul had actually landed 24 hours before Jesús "picked him up" at the airport: he was waiting in the villa, strangled her when she cancelled the proposal for lack of an audience, deleted her messages, fled out the patio doors when Leona arrived (the smash she heard), then returned, packed, cabbed to the airport and waited for the call. He's arrested as Chloe lunges at him ("She bullied me too! That doesn't give you the right to kill her, you stupid prick!").

Coda: Rosa is made pub manager (in name only, no authority — "Shut up or I'll fire you"), and a teaser: the "Bingo Queen" begs Dennis for help — "They're gonna kill me" — hinting at Italians, a "Valentina De Rossi," and the golden rule: "It's the ticket that wins, not the player. Always pay for your own ticket."

### Important Character Actions

- Rosa: blackmails Dennis into the case, interviews Chloe alone ("entre mujeres"), runs the stakeout, and negotiates being made bar manager/licensee, having read his bank statements and demanding he hire an accountant (her father is one).
- Maria: matches the shoe print to Leona, tries to bring her in; signs off "Nice work."
- Jesús: sent to meet the grieving boyfriend (he's "not good with sad people"), finds the tattoo parlour, and breaks up Chloe's attack on Paul.
- Tania: bullies Leona into silence ("They WILL blame you... The kind full of Hairy Marys"), then rails at Paul: "You're a piece of shite!"
- Chloe: admits hating Megan and loving Paul, but has a tattoo-parlour alibi; attacks Paul at the arrest.
- Cora: confesses her Bernardo fling as "penance"; unknowingly clears herself.
- Paul Murphy: the killer — snapped when Megan cancelled the proposal; deleted her messages and staged the airport arrival.

## S01E04

## Benidorm Is Murder — S01E04 Summary

### Episode Plot
The "Bingo Queen" Barbara "Babs" Fredericks, who won €1 million five years earlier, is found dead in her caravan with a two-euro coin lodged in her throat. Her dying act: dabbing six numbers (22, 4, 83, 71, 74, 90) on a bingo ticket. Dennis and Rosa investigate alongside DS Maria Hernandez and PC Jesús Ballesteros. The numbers—initially read as bingo calls—are revealed to be a *Countdown* numbers-round sum (totaling 344), pointing to a locker at the caravan park where Babs hid the cash to avoid inheritance tax for her daughters Ronnie and Connie. The killer is Sandra Shenton, Babs' lifelong friend, who paid for the winning ticket but was never acknowledged; egged on by her secret lover Javier (the bingo caller), she confronted Babs, shoved the coin down her throat in a rage, and fled. Javier framed Valentina De Rossi (Babs' Italian rival) by planting Babs' bracelet at her property, then tried to flee to London Luton—where Spanish police arrested him.

### Important Character Actions
- **Dennis & Rosa**: Decode the "bingo code" as a *Countdown* sum (344 → locker 344); Dennis confronts Sandra and Javier at the caravan park.
- **Sandra Shenton**: Confesses she paid for the winning ticket; kills Babs in a struggle over the money; later framed by Javier.
- **Javier Gomez**: Bingo caller; Sandra's secret lover; manipulates her, steals the bracelet to frame Valentina, searches Babs' van during the football match, arrested at airport.
- **Ronnie & Connie**: Babs' daughters; unaware of the hidden cash; Ronnie obsessively searches storage lockers; Connie solves the *Countdown* sum.
- **Valentina De Rossi**: Arrested on planted evidence; released after Javier's capture.
- **Maria & Jesús**: Guard the crime scene; María issues warrant for Valentina; Jesús injured his back moving evidence.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **BingoDome** – the bingo hall where Babs was "face of" (called "Bingo Queen")
- **Bingo calls** – traditional rhyming nicknames (e.g., 22 = "two little ducks", 4 = "knock at the door", 83 = "time for tea", 71 = "bang on the drum"/"Jamaican rum", 74 = "hit the floor", 90 = "end of the line")
- **Countdown numbers round** – Babs' daily ritual; the six numbers sum to 344 (locker number)
- **Early-onset dementia** – Babs' self-diagnosed condition (curcumin, ginkgo, ginseng, huperzine A supplements)
- **Caravan park** – Babs' "church" and safe place; where the €1M was hidden in locker 344
- **"The ticket wins, not the player"** – bingo maxim (Valentina's line, later echoed by Dennis)

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Babs won €1M **five years ago** (shown in cold open); never spent it ("stash it away, live off the interest").
- She withdrew the full million in **100 × €10,000 cash withdrawals** over six months (12 months ago) to dodge inheritance tax.
- No will exists—Babs avoided probate tax so daughters get every cent.
- Dennis's bar is confirmed as **"The Tavern"** (referenced in E6 preview in skill doc).

- Dennis's birthday passes uncelebrated (he refuses cake/party); Rosa gives him tealights.

## S01E05

## S01E05 — Episode Summary

### Plot
Wealthy tourist Mick is found dead on a Levante Beach lounger. Early post-mortem suggests poison, but toxicology finds nothing in his stomach contents, belongings, sun cream, or vape. Dennis and Rosa investigate alongside DS Maria Hernandez and PC Jesús Ballesteros. The breakthrough comes when Dennis notices a dead mouse by the pub bins — poison has already been laid. He realises the killer used phosphine gas from water-activated rat poison, concealed inside a duplicate Union Jack hat (flags upside-down vs. right-way-up) that Mick wore over his face for his afternoon nap. The culprits are Mick's stepdaughter Lauren and her partner Travis: Lauren stole cash from Mick's "bag for life" for nail appointments; they panicked about their impending baby and lack of housing, and decided to accelerate Mick's inevitable death (quadruple bypass, poor lifestyle) to inherit £200k. Travis sourced rat poison from his waste-plant job; Lauren swapped Mick's steak order for chicken Kiev so the faint garlic smell of phosphine would be blamed on the food. They staged a jellyfish sting on Lauren as a diversion while Travis swapped the hats. Colin (Lauren's dad, ex-con) tries to take the fall to protect his daughter and grandchild, but Dennis refuses, letting the young family stay together — though Maria will inevitably solve it.

### Important Character Actions
- **Dennis**: Spots the dead mouse → deduces phosphine gas; compares hat flags in photos to prove the switch; reconstructs the full mechanism (hat swap, jellyfish diversion, garlic misdirection); refuses to let Colin confess, choosing "justice over law" (Miss Marple quote).
- **Rosa**: Interviews the family; presses Dennis on the divorce papers from Tiff; supports the investigation while managing the pub.
- **Maria & Jesús**: Run official forensics (stomach contents, hat, belongings all clean); Maria texts Dennis the results; will eventually close the case.
- **Lauren & Travis**: Confess the full plot — motive (baby, housing, Mick's cruelty/control, £200k inheritance), method (rat poison → phosphine in hat, chicken Kiev garlic cover, jellyfish diversion, hat swap).
- **Colin**: Reveals Mick's abuse of Caz (control, violence); admits own criminal past (armed robbery, 8 years); tries to take the rap; Dennis rejects it.
- **Caz**: Learns the truth from Lauren; devastated but supportive of her daughter.
- **Tiff**: Serves divorce papers; reveals the settlement gives Eddie (her new husband) 50% of The Tavern — Dennis loses half his bar.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Phosphine gas** — released when aluminium phosphide rat poison gets wet; smells of garlic at low concentrations; fatal to heart-compromised victims.
- **Union Jack orientation** — subtle difference (wider white stripe above/below red diagonal) used to spot the swapped hat.
- **"Bag for life"** — reusable shopping bag where Mick kept £200k cash in the airing cupboard.
- **Levante Beach (莱万特海滩)** — confirmed as the crime scene location.
- **Evoque** — Mick's car (Range Rover Evoque), cited as evidence of wealth.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Dennis's divorce finalised** — Tiff and Eddie now co-own half The Tavern; Eddie appears at the pub with "gangster" associates, claiming it's "just businessmen."
- **Dennis's heart** — Tiff asks after it; he says "Grand."
- **Rosa's leverage** — Still holding Dennis's expired alcohol licence over him (from E3).
- **Dennis–Maria tension** — Continues; she calls him in, they work together smoothly.
- **Colin's backstory** — Ex-con (armed robbery, car theft, assault, 8 years); reformed for his son Travis; ashamed of his past.
- **Mick's abuse** — Coercive control (no phone, no money, no going out alone), physical violence (hit Caz multiple times); explains family motive.
- **Lauren's pregnancy** — Revealed mid-episode (morning sickness); the baby is the catalyst for the murder.

- **Miss Marple quote** — "The law is not always justice, and justice is not always the law" (corrected by Rosa: "Justice is not always the law, and the law's not always justice").

## S01E06

# Benidorm Is Murder — S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Eddie books a private "leaving party" at The Tavern for Stan Grainger, a crime boss who served 10 years for armed robbery — and whom Dennis Crown put away 30 years ago as the key witness. Dennis hides his identity using the alias "Denzel" (claiming it's like Denzel Washington). The lock-in proceeds with Stan's associates: Ray (ex-security), Gav "the Car Boot King" O'Grady, Ricky (beauty parlour chain owner), and Stan himself. During a karaoke session, the power cuts out. In the darkness, Eddie is stabbed to death. Dennis reconstructs the murder: Ray tripped the main breaker with his walking stick, Ricky retrieved the knife using a pocket square to avoid prints, passed it to Stan who donned a golf glove, and Gav kept a laser pen trained on Eddie so Stan could locate him in the dark. All four participated. Stan reveals the party was always a setup — a "goodbye party" for Dennis. The gangsters have 50 alibi witnesses in Marbella, left no DNA or fingerprints, took everything they touched, and drugged Dennis's drink to impair his memory. Only Dennis's prints are on the knife. Maria arrests Dennis for murder despite Rosa's insistence he's innocent.

## Important Character Actions
- **Dennis**: Solves the locked-room murder in real time using sensory clues (sound distance, aftershave scent, laser position); realizes too late the entire evening was a frame-up; arrested at episode's end.
- **Stan Grainger**: Orchestrates the frame-up as revenge for his 10-year sentence; reveals he knew Dennis's identity all along; walks free with his crew.
- **Eddie**: Books the party for "ten grand"; reveals Dennis's past to Stan; murdered by the group.
- **Rosa**: Tries to stop the arrest; insists Dennis didn't do it; Maria reminds her they must follow evidence.
- **Maria Hernandez**: Arrests Dennis based on the physical evidence (prints on knife, witness placing him at scene, motive/threats).
- **Jesús Ballesteros**: Supports Dennis; tells Maria he doesn't believe Dennis killed Eddie but acknowledges the procedural obligation.
- **Tiff**: Warns Rosa the gangsters are "dodgy"; reveals Eddie didn't always move in good circles; suggests calling Maria.
