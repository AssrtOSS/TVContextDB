# Benidorm Is Murder — Subtitle Translation Background (英译中背景资料)

## 1. Title verification (标题核实)

"Benidorm Is Murder" is a genuine British television series: a six-part "cosy crime" detective drama created by Ian Jarvis, starring John Hannah. It is NOT a film, and NOT the ITV sitcom "Benidorm" (2007–2018) — do not confuse the two. Note that the same production was announced in 2025 under the working title "Death in Benidorm" (w/t) and was retitled "Benidorm Is Murder" before broadcast; both names refer to this series. No other TV series, film, or subtitle using this exact title was found.

- Type: TV series (crime drama / cosy crime), United Kingdom
- First broadcast: 30 July 2026 on Channel 5 (network brand "5", UK free-to-air, part of Paramount)
- Premiere: world premiere at the Italian Global Series Festival, July 2026
- Production: Clapperboard + Blackbox Multimedia; ZDF Studios co-producer and international distributor
- Crew: created by Ian Jarvis; directed by Simon Delaney (Death in Paradise, The Young Offenders); writers Tom Parry, Claire Downes, Yasmine Akram; commissioned by Greg Barnett (5)
- Runtime: 6 x ~47 min (Wikipedia) / billed as 6 x 60 min (ZDF Studios; incl. ad slots)
- Filming: on location in Benidorm, Spain, late 2025 (extras casting at Meliá Benidorm hotel, Oct 2025)

## 3. Overall premise (剧情总览)

Dennis Crown, a retired British detective who swapped UK policing for a quiet life running a bar in Benidorm, is reluctantly dragged back into detective work when tourists start turning up dead. He is egged on by his Spanish barmaid Rosa, a crime-drama superfan: Dennis supplies real-world experience, Rosa supplies encyclopaedic TV knowledge. Each episode sees the duo tackle a new murder in paradise while staying on the right side of the local Spanish police. Tone: warm scenery, lighter-than-procedural "cosy crime" in the vein of Death in Paradise, The Mallorca Files, The Good Ship Murder (the latter also a Clapperboard show). Episode vignettes (per ZDF Studios): Take That tribute band at adults-only Hotel Ahora; a comedy night at the Laughter Lounge; a hen-weekend death in a villa; a bingo queen found dead with six numbers dabbed; a wealthy tourist dead on a Levante Beach lounger; and a lock-in with crime boss Stan Grainger.

## 5. World setting (世界设定)

Real-world resort town Benidorm on Spain's Costa Blanca (Alicante province, Valencian Community) — one of Britain's most popular package-holiday destinations since the 1960s. The action centers on Dennis's bar (referred to in E6 as "The Tavern"), the seafront, hotels, and Benidorm's nightlife economy (Irish bars, comedy clubs, bingo halls, hen/stag parties, adults-only hotels). Local Spanish police (Maria, Jesús) use British-style ranks (DS, PC) in the series. Key geography: Levante Beach (莱万特海滩) appears in E5.

## 7. Speaking styles and translation notes (语言风格与翻译提示)

- Dennis: sharp intellect, dry wit, world-weary, understated British deadpan — translate with 冷幽默、克制、不煽情.
- Rosa: enthusiastic crime-drama superfan with encyclopaedic TV knowledge; her dialogue leans on detective-show tropes and she speaks Spanish-accented English. Keep her energy high in Chinese; render show references naturally (e.g., 侦探剧套路), no need to name-check real shows unless the line requires it.
- Maria and Jesús: local Spanish police; Maria professional (often exasperated by the amateur duo), Jesús more easygoing. Use natural spoken Mandarin; keep rank terms consistent (探长/警官 above).
- Guest characters: British holidaymakers with broad regional/working-class registers, plus entertainers and gangsters — match register in Chinese (市井气 vs 江湖气), avoid overly literary phrasing.
- Episode titles are puns (see section 2): prefer creative Chinese puns over literal translation; if a pun can't survive, keep meaning + a subtitle note.
- British informal/bar slang (lock-in, hard men, lager-lout comedy registers) should be rendered idiomatically, not literally.

## 8. Cultural context (文化背景)

- Benidorm = the archetypal British "sun, sea, sangria" package-holiday resort: high-rise hotels, karaoke bars, Irish pubs, full-English breakfasts, sun-lounger wars, stag/hen parties, and a large British expat community. British viewers know the town intimately, which is the show's comedic shorthand — the joke is "murder in the middle of British holiday culture."
- The show sits in Channel 5's cosy-crime strand (alongside The Good Ship Murder) and is pitched as escapist: warm scenery, suspicious deaths, light tone.
- Not to be confused with: ITV's long-running sitcom "Benidorm" (2007–2018, about British holidaymakers) — the ITV show is a comedy with no murder plots; if a translation memory item references "Benidorm" alone, verify which show it belongs to.
- "Death in Benidorm" is this series' earlier working title, not a different series; a Chinese distributor may have used either name in early materials.
- Spanish names (Bécquer, Hernández, Ballesteros, Jesús, Cruz) are Spanish/Catalan — transliterate from Spanish pronunciation, not English spelling.
