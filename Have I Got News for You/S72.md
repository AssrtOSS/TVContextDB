## Episode Plot

The episode covers the **US midterm elections** (Democrats' surprise overperformance, Trump's unprecedented concession), **King Charles & Camilla's Caribbean tour** (reparations demands, Andy Burnham's travel controversy, Ed Miliband's steel-drum cameo), and a run of lighter domestic stories: a 28-year global walker returning to Hull, a Brazilian U20 football brawl record, an Armenian parliamentary punch-up, an Australian pizza chef fired for refusing pineapple, post-Brexit ferret passports, EU ketchup-sachet bans, and the Collins Dictionary "word of the year" stunt.

---

## Important Character Actions

- **Steph McGovern** opens with a Russell Brand gag ("Don't mention Russell Brand") and runs the autocue meta-joke ("the trick is to simply read whatever is on the autocue").
- **Ian Hislop** deadpans through the slavery-reparations segment, nearly getting himself "cancelled" with a pro-slavery quip, then apologises for an eye infection ("looks like I'm crying").
- **Paul Merton** delivers absurdist riffs: Trump as "bitchy fashion podcaster," Middlesbrough chat-up lines, and the bongo/steel-drum distinction ("a bongo is made out of a bong").
- **John Tothill** (introduced as "camp as a bank holiday weekend in Croydon") gets compared to Trump's campness, notes Karl Bushby "looks a bit like my dad."
- **Helen Lewis** lands the Kuenssberg threesome callback ("you've got to queue up for those two") and the Missing Words "aerosols" save.
- **Andy Burnham** (off-screen) detours the official RAF plane via Manchester to Antigua — defended by Paul as a PM perk, mocked by Ian as "HS3."
- **Ed Miliband** plays steel drums (not bongos) on the royal tour; clip shown of his 2015 Lib Dem conference "Ain't No Mountain High Enough" cover.
- **Karl Bushby** (archive) finishes 28-year walk, looks around Hull, immediately sets off again.
- **Roger Stone** rebrands menswear site *Stone Swank* → URL renders as *stoneswank*; panel watches his promo video (HELEN: "You don't see breeches like that since the Third Reich").
- **Zack Polanski** (Green Party) changed name from David Paulden, ex-Lib Dem; 2015 conference singing clip aired.
- **Keith Thripp** (Dorking Wanderers fan) deed-polled name to "Dorking Wanderers 0."
- **Racehorse "Merrylegs"** renamed by US Jockey Club over Hitler reference; owner's son called "Goebbels Gestapo Herbertson."
