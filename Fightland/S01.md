## S01E01

# Fightland — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with a flashback to Las Vegas: Maduka "Duke" Kilroy wins the WBC heavyweight championship. That night, at the celebration, attackers strike; Duke's younger brother Calvin is killed by a hammer blow to the neck. Duke beats the killer to death with his bare hands. He is sentenced to eight years in prison.

In prison, Duke is visited by Amado Rodriguez, a cartel figure. Amado reveals the attack was ordered because Duke was sleeping with Kingsley Marshall's wife, Joy. He offers a deal: he will help Duke find Kingsley and get revenge, and in return Duke will help the cartel take over London's drug market. Duke accepts.

Eight years later, Duke is released. He returns to London and reconnects with his estranged father Gazzer (who runs a community gym in Streatham) and his best friend Jay (now a freelance debt collector). Duke learns Kingsley has vanished months ago — rumors place him anywhere from Turkey (hair transplants) to hiding from the police.

Duke insinuates himself back into Marshall Promotions, meeting Kingsley's children — CeCe (cold, calculating, insists on being called Cebella, runs the gambling side) and Zeek (hot-headed, runs the criminal operations). CeCe makes clear she hasn't forgiven Duke for the media storm that nearly destroyed the business.

Duke discovers his former lover Joy is now a property developer. He also meets her young son Langston (nicknamed Aiden), who is Duke's child — conceived before Duke's imprisonment. Joy reveals she tried to reach Duke 214 times during his sentence; he never responded.

Meanwhile, a £3.5 million drug shipment is stolen from the Marshalls. Duke and his cartel contact Lazaro plant drugs at a Russian-owned warehouse and prepare to frame the Albanians (who run London's stolen car market via shipping containers) to ignite a war between the Marshalls and their rivals — a conflict meant to flush Kingsley out of hiding.

The episode ends in a standoff: Jay has followed Duke and stumbled onto the drug-planting operation. Zeek's people grab Jay and find a recording device on him. Zeek tells Duke that if he doesn't get answers, both Jay and Duke are dead.

## Important Character Actions

- **Duke** — Wins title, kills Calvin's murderer, accepts cartel deal in prison, returns to London, lies his way back into Marshall Promotions, discovers he has a son, initiates the false-flag drug operation
- **Amado Rodriguez** — Approaches Duke in prison, brokers the deal that sets the season in motion
- **Jay** — Picks Duke up from prison, works as a freelance enforcer, follows Duke at the wrong moment and gets caught with a wire
- **Joy** — Has moved on as a property developer, reveals Duke's son exists, is building an independent life apart from Kingsley's shadow
- **CeCe** — Coldly rebuffs Duke's attempt to rejoin the family business but assigns him a fighter to test him; holds her ground against the network during the crisis meeting
- **Zeek** — More welcoming than CeCe but volatile; threatens a reporter and later kidnaps Jay when he suspects Duke's betrayal
- **Gazzer** — Reluctantly lets Duke stay but warns him he's changed; suspicious of packages from Mexico

## S01E03

## Episode Plot

Picking up from the botched Albanian frame-up: Duke and Lazaro decide the way to hurt Kingsley is through his money, not his children ("Kingsley only cares about money"). They plan to bomb a closed-off area of the Marshall casino — an IED sourced by Jay, planted by Lazaro's ex-military contact. Meanwhile, Kim Harper is revealed as an undercover officer who wants to "flip" Duke to get to Kingsley; her partner Lewis pulls her from the operation after their CI, Simeon Marshall, is found stabbed and dumped in the Thames (South Bank, 6 a.m., killed the day after the shipment was hit). Kim refuses to stand down — her missing sister Rina is the personal stake.

Duke reconnoiters the casino when Zeek invites him, gets talked into attending Aiden's birthday party there, and coaches a young fighter (Rocks) at the gym — which triggers a violent confrontation with Gazzer, who accuses Duke of poaching his fighter and kicks him out ("you're just a reminder of bad times"). Jay delivers the bomb device but, on learning the target is the casino rather than the warehouse, refuses to be part of it ("This certainly ain't Calvin").

At the casino, the ex-military bomber balks at the security guard ("No innocents"), so Lazaro handles the guard himself ("I'm sorry, pendejo") and the device detonates. The Marshalls blame Nikolai — Duke's false flag works. Zeek wants war; Cebella wants proof. Zeek then admits to Duke that he and Cebella have no idea where Kingsley is — the "in contact" story was a lie, and "he could be dead for all we know." Declaring he doesn't need his father, Zeek drives Duke to hit the Russians with no backup ("Back up's with Cebella. It's just me and you.") — the episode ends as the two go in alone.

## S01E06

# S01E06 — "There Is a War Coming"

## Episode Plot
Kingsley returns from hiding after a cartel hit, reclaiming control of the Marshall empire. He confronts Zeek and Cece for their failures, offers Joy a controlling stake (a trap blending empowerment with incrimination), and eliminates the Russian distribution network by executing Nikolai using newly built private military contractors. Meanwhile, Duke's father Gazzer nearly dies in hospital; Kim reveals she's an undercover NCA officer whose partner Lewis and sister Rina were killed by Kingsley. The episode ends with Kingsley staging a press conference at Tottenham announcing Duke's boxing comeback — declaring himself the chess player, Duke the piece.

## Important Character Actions
