## S01E11

## Episode S01E11

### Plot
- On a train, Mukuru Izumi rages at the one who forced his sister Shiika through Transfloration, demanding revenge and refusing to forgive Yomiko for simply being alive. A companion argues Yomiko regrets it and once saved their life, and threatens to kill Mukuru if he kills Yomiko; Mukuru insists they are not alike.
- The companion dives into "Ivy's memories, Mukuru Izumi's memories," returning to Paupertown. Young Mukuru and Shiika scavenge scrap metal and candy wrappers; Shiika shapes snow into fake candy because the real thing is unaffordable.
- Shiika falls ill with a cold, mild malnutrition and diarrhea. A doctor waives his fee and gives three days of fever reducers and antibiotics. Mukuru finds a discarded *Introduction to Architectural Design* and stumbles over "STRUCTURE."
- Mukuru collapses: no Spiriflora nearby means no oxygen, and he is diagnosed with hypoxic encephalopathy, brain damage causing limb paralysis. The doctor explains oxygen and tells him knowledge and imagination keep misfortune away; Mukuru asks how everyone else acquired them.
- Strangers abduct Shiika. Dr. Kudai offers Mukuru an experiment, surgery to turn him into a plant that can move again, in exchange for finding Shiika. Mukuru becomes "subject number five." Dr. Kudai later confirms Shiika underwent Transfloration and was harvested.
- The memory-reader reveals Paupertown's snow is poison (century-old bacteria and trash), so Shiika ate poison all along, and confesses to having Shiika undergo Transfloration to protect themselves. Mukuru cannot forgive them.
- Present day: Hikasa intends to kill Dr. Kudai with a bomb; Transflora Medical staff want to stop Hikasa and bring Dr. Kudai to court over Ivy/Mukuru's Late Stage Transfloration and Glory Days Party involvement. He refuses. Mukuru reads an assailant's intent and dodges gunfire; Toshiro is shot, dies, and sprouts roots. A consciousness wakes in a field of flowers where all memories and emotions live, able to read everyone's thoughts, and wants to "make something."

### Important character actions
- Mukuru: swears revenge, rejects forgiveness, begs Dr. Kudai to find Shiika, accepts the experiment, later dodges bullets by reading intent.
- Shiika: scavenges, makes snow candy, falls ill, is abducted, Transflorated, harvested.
- Yomiko: remorseful; credited with saving the companion's life.
- Dr. Kudai (九大博士): human experimentation on mobile plants, takes Mukuru as subject #5, refuses to testify.
- Hikasa (日笠): decides to kill Dr. Kudai with a bomb.
- Transflora Medical employees: try to stop Hikasa, want Dr. Kudai on the stand.
- Toshiro (十四郎): leads someone somewhere, is killed, grows roots.
- The memory-reader: reads Mukuru's memories, shares his pain, confesses causing Shiika's Transfloration, asks to be friends.

### New names and terms
- Transfloration (转花手术); Late Stage Transfloration (重度转花)
- Paupertown (贫困街); Spiriflora (灵花)
- hypoxic encephalopathy (缺氧性脑病)
- Dr. Kudai (九大博士), Yomiko (咏子), Shiika Izumi (泉椎香), Mukuru Izumi (泉梦来), Ivy, Toshiro (十四郎), Hikasa (日笠)
- Transflora Medical (转花院); Glory Days Party (荣日党)
- "subject number five" (五号样本); "irregularity" (异类)
- The "field of flowers" where memories and emotions of all things are alive
- The maxim "knowledge and imagination keep misfortune away"

### Continuity-relevant facts
- Shiika is Mukuru's younger sister; he is her provider. Mukuru is her provider even after his paralysis, which is untreatable with current technology.
- Shiika's fate: Transfloration, then harvested (采伐).
- Paupertown's snow is poisonous; Mukuru blames himself for never stopping her.
- Mukuru is Ivy, subject #5 of Late Stage Transfloration, now able to read memories, emotions, and shooting intent.
- The Glory Days Party is tied to the research; a court case is being prepared.
- Translation note: the Chinese renders Mukuru as 哥哥 ("older brother") when addressed by a younger character, while the English alternates between "Mukuru" and "Mister." "Ivy" (IVY) is kept as a proper noun.

### Jokes and references useful later
- "Jump off this train right now and die" as the blunt counter to "atone."
- Snow "candy" as a running motif, recontextualized as poison.
- Dr. Kudai's glib "It'll be easy as pie!" about finding Shiika.
- "Knowledge and imagination" bookends the episode, from the doctor's lecture to the flower field.

### Unresolved threads
- The memory-reader is called both "Ivy" and "Mister/brother," and confesses to causing Shiika's Transfloration; its relationship to Yomiko and Mukuru stays ambiguous.
- Whether that confession contradicts the earlier claim that Yomiko forced Shiika's surgery.
- Whether Hikasa's bomb detonates and who actually kills Dr. Kudai.
- Toshiro's fate: shot and apparently dead, roots growing, while a consciousness wakes elsewhere.
- Who says "I didn't get to say goodbye to Yomiko or to Mom," and whose family that is.
- Whether Mukuru can ever be happy or forgive; the reader says no because he killed many people.
- Outcome of the court case and the exposure of Transflora Medical and the Glory Days Party.

# Fool Night S01E09 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode centers on a multi-layered operation to capture **Dr. Kudai** (九大博士), creator of the mobile Spiriflor **Ivy**. A team including **Takami** (鹰见), **Asada** (浅田), and **Yorutake** (夜竹) has forged a document claiming Transflora Medical and Dr. Kudai jointly developed Ivy 40 years ago. Their plan: plant the fake document at Transflora Medical Number 4, let the Anti-Transfloration Movement "steal" it during an Archives inspection, then force the Movement to call Dr. Kudai as a witness in court/Diet — allowing capture.

Simultaneously, the **Archives (图书室)** locks down Transflora Medical Number 4 for 20 hours under the pretext of inspection. **Inspectors (监察官)** swarm the facility, but the Anti-Transfloration Movement has infiltrated their ranks. **Chief Hiragishi** (平岸阵课长) of the **Unsection (无课)** is detained for allegedly stealing Archives documents. **Asada** is assassinated with a silenced weapon. **Akira Yatsuka** (八束晶), Deputy Chief, hides in a locker. **Kanaeno** (叶野) reveals she has undergone Transfloration and uses plant abilities to protect someone, sustaining critical injuries. The episode ends with inspections ongoing and the 20-hour countdown not yet complete.

## S01E03

# Fool Night S01E03 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode centers on **Yomiko Horai** (蓬莱咏子), a Locator Division (搜索课) worker with two years left before she undergoes Transfloration (转花) and becomes a Spiriflora (灵花). She assists police investigating a serial murder case where victims are wealthy individuals and children, with only candy left at scenes. The killer is suspected to be someone mid-Transfloration—devoid of humanity yet capable of murder.

Yomiko tries recruiting her brother **Toshiro (十四郎)**—who also has two years left and possesses a "superpower" (hearing Spiriflora voices)—to help both the investigation and a personal request: finding a woman from a photo in her locker. Toshiro initially refuses both.

Parallel flashbacks reveal Yomiko's childhood: an academically pressured girl who befriends a poorer classmate, trades textbooks, and learns to value the present over a rigid future. The classmate warns her: "You'll die young!"

In the present, Yomiko and Toshiro reconcile over food. While leaving, a **mobile, aggressive Spiriflora** attacks them—screaming "Die!" and "I'll kill you." It throws a sonic projectile, damaging Toshiro's hearing. Yomiko pushes her ability to the limit, perceiving the creature's killing intent without ears, and they barely escape. The episode ends with Toshiro injured, calling for an ambulance.

## S01E05


## Episode S01E05

### Plot
A flame-covered creature corners Yomiko while a bystander begs "Akira" to shoot it. The creature, revealed to be a child by its speech and skull size, explains its motive: "that woman took my sister from me." A flashback shows a hidden room of Human-Sourced Furniture labeled REINA, KENTA, AIKA and SHIIKA; the speaker's sister Shiika was chosen for the Parade, and Yomiko was the only one who could stop it but still performed Shiika's Transfloration anyway. The creature recounts a life of cold, hunger, no school and caring for the dying, concludes that everyone should suffer as it did, and thanks the listener for hearing it out before being consumed by fire.

A news bulletin reports that at 8:18 p.m. an unidentified burning creature appeared on 5th Street in Spora Square, leaving 31 dead and 8 injured, and experts classify it as a Late-Stage Transfloration case. The tone then cuts to a hospital: light conversation over a "Western-style, quasi-hamburger steak," a Transflora Medical researcher furious that a valuable research subject was burned to ash, and Akira recovering among visitors including Toshiro, who is on a soup-only diet after his own Transfloration.

A heated disciplinary meeting follows. A senior officer berates the group for stealing an officer's gun and using incendiary bullets, threatens arrest under the Firearms Control Act with a three-year sentence, then compounds the blame onto Transflora Medical. The group collectively negotiates the punishment down by dividing one suspension year among two, three, four and finally five people (73 days each), settling on a pay cut and three months' suspension for Toshiro. In a hospital board game, a "genetic modification that solves world hunger" earns only 30,000 while buying lunar land costs 500,000, prompting "the worst game ever" and "even in a room full of losers, you're losing."

An investigator questions Yomiko: four years ago, shortly after joining Transflora Medical, she performed Transfloration on Shiika Izumi, a Human-Sourced Furniture victim whose Spiriflor was made into furniture for a collector. Shiika is believed connected to Ivy, and Yomiko remembers her. Shiika's furniture was stolen by Ivy and remains missing. Yomiko is discharged and heads for Transflora Medical 4, but anti-Transfloration protests block the roads, with chants against the Oxygen Tax and human experimentation, a clash in which a father loses sight of a girl named Meg, and police declaring the protest over. Two Transflora Medical staff reviewing the case find the attacker used Uncaria, a Rubiaceae vine they hold no Seeds for, concluding Ivy was not their creation even though the child was forced through Transfloration. The episode ends with a decision to travel to Paupertown tomorrow to learn about the Parade.

- Akira: was urged to shoot Ivy, stole a gun and used incendiary bullets, is threatened with arrest, is defended by colleagues, and can talk to Spiriflora.
- Toshiro: recently Transflorated, defends Akira ("it was the right thing to do"), draws three months' suspension, and remarks "I'll never get promoted with you around."
- Investigator (Himejima): presses Yomiko on her personal history and Shiika's case.
- Transflora Medical staff: review the case file, identify Uncaria, and conclude Ivy was made elsewhere but still Transflorated.
- Akira and a companion: resolve to go to Paupertown the next day.

## S01E07

# Fool Night — S01E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode weaves three main threads: the core investigation team hunting for Dr. Kudai's whereabouts, a harrowing Paupertown flashback showing a father's desperate choice to undergo Transfloration for his daughter's escape, and a philosophical confrontation at Transflora Medical about complicity in the system. The investigation hits dead ends at the Archives and in the field, but a breakthrough comes when Yomiko discovers Dr. Kudai has a younger brother, Mitsuru. Visiting him reveals deep family trauma and yields no location for the doctor, but confirms Mitsuru's hatred for his brother and the Transfloration system. Meanwhile, the Anti-Transfloration Movement reorganizes as the Glory Days Party, with a major policy address scheduled in five days that could shift public opinion irreversibly.

## Important Character Actions
- **Akira Yatsuka**: Childhood flashback shows he was frightened of bathrooms; his former caretaker Kanaeno reveals she worked for the Yatsuka family.
- **Toshiro (Jūshirō/Kaminari)**: Plays "strikeout" with windows; hears a Spiriflora say "I won't forgive you" — confirming his unique perception ability. Argues the "train" metaphor: getting off means losing your seat/livelihood.
- **Kanaeno**: Former Yatsuka family employee; recognizes Akira as "young sir."
- **Yomiko (Hōrai)**: Searches the Archives (rebuffed); finds record of Kudai's younger brother; visits Mitsuru with Toshiro. Admits she only works at Transflora Medical for money, memorized noble rhetoric for interviews.
- **Meg Okumura**: Paupertown resident; sells antibiotics; confronts her father about his Transfloration decision; later attacks someone after learning her father was caught, gets fired.
- **Meg's Father**: Decides to undergo Transfloration for the 10M yen subsidy to escape Paupertown with Meg; gives her his grandmother's watch (12-hour dial); caught two weeks later.
- **Mitsuru Kudai**: Dr. Kudai's younger brother; reveals Kudai cut off two of his fingers as a child "to compare with an anatomy book"; hates his brother, wants him jailed, but genuinely doesn't know his location. Votes for Glory Days Party. Accuses Transflora Medical staff of being no better than Kudai.
- **Saito**: Plans to attend Glory Days Party policy address and protest; motivated by Meg's ruined life.

- **Mitsuru Kudai (九大满)**: Dr. Otaro Kudai's younger brother; lives off-grid, no communication with outside world.

- **Ivy's Cultivation Report (IVY育苗报告)**: Document written by Dr. Kudai himself; key evidence to prove Kudai and Ken Hikasa created Ivy/Mukuru Izumi.
- **Ken Hikasa (日笠健)**: Anti-Transfloration Movement member; has 5–6 man military-grade security detail.
