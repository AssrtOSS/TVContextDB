- **Title**: Outer Banks (also abbreviated OBX)

- **Production**: Rock Fish, Red Canoe Productions

## Core Relationships

### Pogues (Core Friend Group)
- **John B + JJ**: Best friends since 3rd grade; ride-or-die loyalty
- **John B + Sarah**: Romantic partners (on/off); cross-class romance (Pogue + Kook)
- **JJ + Kiara**: Best friends → romantic partners (slow burn, confirmed S3)
- **Pope + Cleo**: Close bond → romantic partners (S3+)
- **Pope + Kiara**: Brief romantic tension (S1–S2); remain close friends
- **John B + Pope**: Leader + strategist; occasional friction over secrecy

### Cameron Family
- **Ward ↔ Sarah**: Father-daughter; manipulation vs. rebellion
- **Ward ↔ Rafe**: Toxic enablement; Ward covers Rafe's crimes
- **Rafe ↔ Sarah**: Sibling rivalry; Rafe resents Sarah's "favorite" status
- **Wheezie ↔ Sarah**: Younger sister; loyal to Sarah, often leveraged

### Antagonist Dynamics
- **Ward vs. John B**: Father-figure betrayal; Ward killed Big John
- **Rafe vs. Pogues**: Personal vendetta; escalates to murder attempts
- **Groff vs. JJ**: Biological father abandoned him; S4 culminates in JJ's murder
- **Singh/Limbrey vs. Pogues**: Treasure hunters using Pogues as pawns

## World Setting

### Geography & Class Divide
- **Outer Banks, North Carolina** — barrier islands; fictionalized **Kildare County**
- **The Cut** (僻地/贫民区): Working-class "Pogue" territory; John B, JJ, Pope, Kiara live here
- **Figure Eight** (八字岛/富人区): Wealthy seasonal residents ("Kooks"); Cameron family estate (Tannyhill)
- **Kildare Island** (基尔代尔岛): Main setting; marshes, waterways, lighthouse, shipwrecks
- **Poguelandia** (波格兰迪亚): Deserted island where Pogues stranded S2 finale–S3 premiere
- **International**: Nassau (Bahamas), Charleston (SC), Orinoco (Venezuela), Solana, Morocco (Essaouira, Agapenta)

## Overall Plot Premise
A group of working-class teenagers (**Pogues**) in the Outer Banks of North Carolina hunt for legendary treasure tied to the **Royal Merchant** (1829 shipwreck) and the missing father of their leader, **John B**. Their quest pits them against wealthy **Kooks** (led by the Cameron family), corrupt law enforcement, and ruthless treasure hunters across four seasons spanning the Caribbean, South America, and North Africa. Themes: found family, class warfare, loyalty vs. betrayal, generational trauma, and the corrupting power of gold.

### Season Arcs
- **S1 (2020)**: Royal Merchant gold; John B framed for sheriff's murder; ends with John B/Sarah presumed dead at sea
- **S2 (2021)**: Pogues in Nassau; Cross of Santo Domingo; Ward's fake death; Pogues stranded on "Poguelandia"
- **S3 (2023)**: El Dorado (City of Gold) in Venezuela; Big John alive; Ward & Big John die; 18-month time jump
- **S4 (2024)**: Blackbeard's treasure (Blue Crown amulet); JJ's true parentage (Groff); JJ murdered in finale
- **S5 (2026)**: Final season; revenge for JJ; "bring everything home"

## Speaking Styles & Voice Guide

### John B (Chase Stokes)
- **Tone**: Earnest, determined, natural leader; narrates via voiceover (internal monologue)
- **Vocabulary**: Colloquial OBX slang ("bro," "man," "dude"); poetic when describing treasure/history
- **Pattern**: "We're Pogues. We don't quit." / "My dad didn't die for nothing."
- **Chinese Voice**: 朴实真挚，带旁白感；口语化但关键时刻升华为誓言口吻

### JJ (Rudy Pankow)
- **Tone**: Chaotic, impulsive, fiercely loyal; masks trauma with humor/recklessness
- **Vocabulary**: Profanity-heavy (fuck, shit, damn); surf/dirt-bike slang; nicknames for everyone
- **Pattern**: "Let's get weird." / "Pogues for life, baby." / Deflects emotion with jokes
- **Chinese Voice**: 语速快、碎句多、脏话保留或转为"他妈的/操/靠"；情绪爆发时碎裂感强

### Pope (Jonathan Daviss)
- **Tone**: Analytical, cautious, morally grounded; intellectual anchor
- **Vocabulary**: Precise, explanatory; "The thing is…" / "Logically…" / historical references
- **Pattern**: Plans aloud; stresses consequences; "I'm not built for this"
- **Chinese Voice**: 逻辑清晰、句式完整、少用俚语；情绪压抑时短句化

### Kiara (Madison Bailey)
- **Tone**: Fiery, principled, environmentalist; bridges Pogue/Kook worlds
- **Vocabulary**: Activist language ("ecosystem," "privilege"); calls out hypocrisy directly
- **Pattern**: "That's not how this works." / "You're being a Kook right now."
- **Chinese Voice**: 犀利直接、带道德审视；对JJ柔和、对Topper/Rafe尖酸

### Sarah (Madelyn Cline)
- **Tone**: Conflict-torn; polished Kook exterior, raw Pogue loyalty underneath
- **Vocabulary**: Code-switches: proper with parents, slang with Pogues; "I don't know who I am"
- **Pattern**: Apologizes often; "I'm sorry, I'm trying"
- **Chinese Voice**: 双重语域切换明显；对John B柔软、对Ward冷硬、对Wheezie护短

### Ward (Charles Esten)
- **Tone**: Calculated charm masking sociopathy; Southern gentleman veneer
- **Vocabulary**: Business/political rhetoric ("interests," "legacy," "family"); gaslighting language
- **Pattern**: "I did what I had to do." / "You're my daughter, I'll always protect you" (while endangering her)
- **Chinese Voice**: 斯文败类感；敬语套话掩盖威胁；关键词"家族/利益/保护"反复强调

### Rafe (Drew Starkey)
- **Tone**: Unstable, volatile, desperate for paternal approval
- **Vocabulary**: Aggressive, slurred when high; "You don't get it" / "I'm not crazy"
- **Pattern**: Escalates from threats to violence; childlike need for "Good job, son"
- **Chinese Voice**: 语气忽高忽低、打断重启；脏话密集；脆弱时音量骤降

### Groff (J. Anthony Crane) — S4 Villain
- **Tone**: Chillingly calm, manipulative, theatrical; "father" performance
- **Vocabulary**: Mythic/fated language ("destiny," "bloodline," "curse"); literary references
- **Pattern**: "We're the same." / "I gave you life." / Monologues before violence
- **Chinese Voice**: 戏剧性腔调、文雅掩饰恶意；称JJ为"儿子"时阴森

## Cultural Context for Translation

### Regional Specificity (Outer Banks, NC)
- **Dialect markers**: "Y'all," "fixin' to," "bless your heart" (sarcastic), "OBX" as identity
- **Class signifiers**: Boat types (formula vs. yacht), addresses (The Cut vs. Figure Eight), schools (public vs. private/boarding)
- **Economy**: Tourism seasonal; fishing/charter work (Pogues); real estate/development (Kooks)
- **Law enforcement**: Sheriff elected; deputies loyal to local power (Ward); SBI = outside intervention

### Gen-Z / Teen Culture (2020–2024)
- **Social media**: Instagram aesthetics (Sarah's "Kook princess" curation), TikTok trends referenced
- **Music**: Soundtrack features contemporary indie/hip-hop (Khalid, Lil Baby, Jungle, Remi Wolf); diegetic at parties
- **Substance use**: Casual weed, underage drinking, Rafe's opioid/cocaine addiction portrayed unglamorously
- **Sexuality**: Kiara/Pope/JJ fluidity; Sarah/John B explicit but emotional; no "very special episode" framing

### Historical & Mythological Layers
- **Denmark Tanny**: Based on real historical figure (Denmark Vesey adjacent); freedman who built Freedman's Church
- **Royal Merchant**: Fictionalized 1829 wreck; draws on real NC shipwreck lore (e.g., *El Salvador*, *Queen Anne's Revenge*)
- **Blackbeard (Edward Teach)**: Real pirate; *The Adventure* grounded at Ocracoke 1718; "wife's curse" invented for show
- **El Dorado**: Muisca legend relocated to Orinoco basin; show treats as historical fact

### Sensitive Content Flags
- **Domestic/child abuse**: JJ-Luke (graphic S1E4, S2E5); Rafe-Ward (psychological)
- **Drug addiction**: Rafe (ongoing); Luke (alcohol/meth); Barry's operation
- **Violence**: Shootings, stabbings, drownings, explosions; on-screen deaths (Peterkin, Gavin, Ward, Big John, JJ)
- **Suicide ideation**: JJ S1E4, S4E10; Ward's boat explosion (ambiguous)
- **Sexual content**: Teen sex (John B/Sarah, Pope/Kiara, JJ/Kiara implied); non-graphic

## Translation Decision Log (For Consistency)

| Decision Point | Choice | Rationale |
|----------------|--------|-----------|
| "Pogues" | 波格 (transliteration + 族群感) | Not "波格人" (clunky); "波格" functions as noun/adjective |
| "Kooks" | 库克 (transliteration) | Preserves slang feel; "古怪" loses insider/outsider charge |
| "The Cut" | 僻地 | Captures geographic + socioeconomic marginalization |
| "Figure Eight" | 八字岛 | Visual + proper noun; "八字形" too descriptive |
| "OBX" | OBX / 欧比克斯 | Keep abbreviation; expand once for context |
| "Royal Merchant" | 皇家商船号 | Standard ship naming convention |
| "Cross of Santo Domingo" | 圣多明各十字架 | Historical artifact naming |
| "Blue Crown" | 蓝冠 | Concise; "蓝色王冠" too literal for amulet |
| "Poguelandia" | 波格兰迪亚 | -landia → 兰迪亚; established fan term |
| Profanity (fuck/shit/damn) | 他妈的/操/靠/见鬼 | Match intensity; JJ gets most leeway |
| "Bro/Man/Dude" | 兄弟/哥们/老兄 | Context-dependent; JJ↔John B = 兄弟 |
| Voiceover (John B) | 旁白 / 内心独白 | Distinguish from dialogue with brackets or italics |
