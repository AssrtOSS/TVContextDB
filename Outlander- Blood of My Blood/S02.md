## S02E01

## S02E01 Summary — Outlander: Blood of My Blood

### Episode Plot

The Season 2 premiere picks up directly after the S1 finale, split across three locations:

**Castle Leathers (Grant territory):** Young Malcolm Grant is found dead in his chambers — stabbed with his own dirk. His uncle (Uncle Mac / "Grant uncle") arrives to collect the body and immediately suspects MacKenzie foul play, blaming Ellen for jilting Malcolm and driving him to his death. Maura (Malcolm's sister) is distraught; windows haven't been opened and clocks haven't been stopped — the proper funeral rites are being ignored. The Grant uncle takes Malcolm home for burial and assumes temporary lairdship, which gives him the power to reverse the clan's Jacobite declaration and side with the loyalists. War crosses have been lit — conflict is imminent.

**Castle Leathers (Lovat's seat):** Julia and baby William are returned safely by Arch Bug, who captured them during their failed escape to Craigh na Dun. Lovat is drunk and raging — the crosses are lit, his wife and heir were abducted, and Brian is missing. Julia plays the dutiful wife, offering to help prepare him for war while planning secretly to contact Maisri the seer. She confides fully in Davina, revealing she is from another time (1923), that Henry also traveled through the stones, and that they were separated when Henry went through without her at Craigh na Dun. She desperately wants to find a way back to her daughter Claire (age 5, turning 6 on October 20th).

**Inverness Lunatic Asylum (1924):** Henry wakes in a straightjacket, having been found on the moors near Craigh na Dun with a head wound and shoulder injury. Dr. Perryman diagnoses shell shock and neurosis. Henry is disoriented — he answers "1715" for the year, then corrects to "1915," and can't reliably name the current prime minister (it's 1924, Ramsay MacDonald). He's subjected to electroconvulsive therapy, which triggers vivid WWI memories and visions of Julia and Claire. He fixates on recurring mental fragments: the letter "C," the date October 20th, and names — Fraser, Leathers, Bug. Reverend Wakefield (the chaplain) takes a dictated letter for Henry; Henry insists on recording specific details (S-W-A-K, "C," Oct 20, Julia, Fraser, Leathers, Bug, "the prophecy about the king — he thinks my son is next in line") but knows the letter can never reach Julia because "she's not there."

**Leoch (MacKenzie stronghold):** Ellen has surrendered herself to MacKenzie men and returns home. Colum slaps her and demands to know why she ran; she tells him he'll want to hear what she has to say. She's locked in the chapel.

**Meanwhile:** Brian returns to Castle Leathers and confronts Lord Lovat, pledging to fight out of loyalty to his oath as a Fraser, not out of any love for his father. Lovat tasks Brian with recruiting Fraser men for the Jacobite cause, with the threat of dishonor if he fails.

In a brief flash-forward, Henry and Julia prepare to attend a Remembrance Day service with young Claire. Claire's stuffed toy "Floppy" is lost; Henry quips they should buy ten of them. Julia reflects that when she's with Henry, "everything else goes quiet."

## S02E02 Summary — *Outlander: Blood of My Blood*

### Episode Plot

**18th-Century Highlands**  
Ellen MacKenzie returns to Leoch after fleeing. Colum and Dougal confront her for jilting Malcolm Grant; Ellen counters that Colum hired Gallowglass mercenaries to kill Brian Fraser. **Young Claire**  
Brief flashback: young Claire Beauchamp readies for an archaeological trip to the Middle East with Uncle Lamb, clutching her stuffed rabbit Floppy.

## S02E03

## Outlander: Blood of My Blood — S02E03 Summary

### Plot
While the clan's warriors ride off to the Jacobite Rising, James MacKinney stages a violent coup at Castle Leoch, murdering guards and seizing the hall. He claims Red Jacob always intended him as laird and demands the keys and treasury. Colum stalls him by revealing that the estate was legally transferred to Ellen ("every acre, every stone, in her name"), forcing MacKinney to hunt Ellen alive — to make her sign the deed over or marry her by force. Meanwhile Ellen, Maura, and the household women turn the tables: using their father's lesson ("rabbits don't swim" — predators only look where they expect prey), they pick off MacKinney's men one by one from the castle's hidden passages. Ellen ultimately kills MacKinney himself with her father's bow ("Face a MacKenzie for them. Rabbits do swim."). In the fallout, Janet dies of wounds sustained defending the household, and it is revealed that Lovat has secretly betrayed the Jacobites — he made a pact with Argyll and the Crown, and the MacKenzie/Fraser war party is riding into a trap. On the road, Lovat splits the forces (loch road vs. Drovers' road), positioning himself to control Brian.

### Important Character Actions
- Colum: bought time by invoking the deed and Ellen's claim; later leads a defense, regrets misjudging Brian ("he's no traitor"), and gives Ellen his father's arrows.
- Ellen: leads the resistance inside Leoch, echoing Red Jacob's hide-and-seek lesson in a flashback; kills MacKinney; vows to ride out to find Brian and Dougal and bring them home.
- Maura (Dougal's Grant-born wife): initially suggests surrendering, is arrested after a servant dies helping them, then stands down MacKinney using the Grant name; accepts her MacKenzie identity ("A Grant learns to survive" / "ye have that wrong then… MacKenzie").
- Janet: secretly pregnant; mortally wounded, names her unborn daughter Ellen if a girl; dies praying with Ellen.
- Glenna: tends Ned's wound; volunteers to ride with Ellen; vouches for Grace, a London/Whitechapel-born maid skilled with knives.
- Ned Gowan: refuses to serve MacKinney ("I serve the rightful laird"), is wounded, survives.
- Lovat: reveals his Crown pact to Colum to break him; splits the war column under the pretext of avoiding Redcoat pursuit; manipulates Brian, warning that love for Ellen makes him "unpredictable"; orders Brian to ride with him.
- Brian: chooses to ride with Murtagh instead of Lovat; insists he is not his father.
- Seema (the Grant "gift" scullery maid): Lovat's planted spy and bed-companion; confirms to Julia-overlap lore that Henry (the "bladier") went half-mad after the Grants told him his wife was dead, carrying a St. Anthony pendant to "bring back his Julia and his little girl, Claire"; Maura asks Lovat to send her back, and Lovat instead orders Seema to watch and keep Julia "safe and faithful" while he's away.

## S02E04

# S02E04 — Episode Summary

## Plot
Three storylines converge as the Jacobite trap springs shut. Brian and Ellen's camp is shattered when Fraser banners arrive bearing news that Lovat has defected to the Crown, taken Inverness, and left the Jacobite army—including Dougal—exposed. Brian rides out alone to intercept his father. Henry, who followed Julia through the stones, surfaces at Castle Leathers with severe head scarring; he reveals he was confined in a 1920s Inverness asylum and electroshocked into forgetting he ever had a daughter. Brian returns with proof that MacKinney's men—roughly twenty—have infiltrated Dougal's host. Dougal, persuaded by Brian and the loss of his sister Janet, diverts south to join Mar at Perth. Ellen prepares to flee Leoch the next morning; in the small hours she and Brian confess their love, name their unborn child, and part at dawn.

## Character Actions
- **Arch Bug (so-called "Sassenach"/English captive)**: dragged before the new Grant laird, claiming Fair Folk branded him; assigned twenty lashes, then released after Murdina insists the Fair Folk marks make him untouchable. His loyalty to the MacKenzies surfaces when he admits the late laird ordered him to lie about the miscarried child.
- **Ellen**: confides her pregnancy to Brian; takes Brian's boyhood *sgian-dubh* as a token; arranges Seema's absence the next morning before her own flight south; dyes a scrap of purple from her underskirt into a flower and tucks it into Brian's plaid as he departs.
- **Brian**: rides out to confront Lovat, returns with intelligence on MacKinney's hidden saboteurs; openly defies his father, renounces his Fraser name and titles, and rides off with Ellen's brother Dougal.
- **Lovat / Simon Fraser**: publicly disowns Brian, orders his ambush of Dougal, scoops up Argyll's offer (Beaufort, restoration of titles), pockets Inverness uncontested, and marches to link with Argyll.
- **Young Colum MacKenzie**: browbeats Arch Bug into service as his *bladier* (spokesman) for the laird succession and the Inverness push; jokes that beating a Sassenach "should be adequate."
- **Dougal MacKenzie**: initially dismissive of Brian's counsel; reverses course on learning of Lovat's betrayal and Janet's death; swears Ellen a MacKinney-free passage at first light via Dalwhinnie.
- **Davina Porter (Mrs. Graham)**: receives word Henry is at Bowmont, immediately plots Julia's escape from Leathers; later identified as the woman who helped Henry flee the Inverness asylum.
- **Henry Beauchamp**: limps into Leathers with stitched scalp wounds, haltingly reclaims his marriage; holds his son William at sword-point by accident; confesses the asylum and the electroshock; prepares to desert with Julia to retrieve three gemstones (seer-blessed brooch, wedding ring, watch) for a crossing home to Claire.
- **Julia Moriston**: arranges Henry's covert stay at Leathers; seduces/recovers him in a tender reconciliation; engineers their dawn escape; writes one final letter to Claire framed as "come home yourself, whole," not "stay alive."
- **Seema**: a former Grant scullery maid working Leathers kitchens; in a confrontation with Julia claims Henry slept with her believing she was Julia, after a grief-induced crossing back to 1924 London woke him in her bed.

## New Names & Terms
- **Balloch**: Brian's loyal Fraser officer, present at the tent confrontation with Lovat.
- **Murtagh**: Brian's man who readies the horses and is sent as a secondary messenger.
- **Mr. Cameron**: Davina/Julia's escort officer at Leathers, sleeping off his whisky.
- **Hugh (cousin)**: a turncoat Fraser who warns of Mar's doomed rising and plans to fly a Union flag over Lovat's lands.
- **The needfire / Na daoine sithe**: Highland folklore terms Murdina and Ellen invoke — purificatory fire, the "Fair Folk" who hunt wanderers and mark men's faces with un-mortally stitched wounds.
- **The Marquis of Tullibardine / Mar's army at Perth**: the Jacobite rally point Dougal now heads for instead of Inverness.
- **Beaufort Castle / the pretender Fraserdale**: Lovat's lost seat, promised back by the Duke of Argyll.
- **Bowmont**: the estate where Henry is billeted.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Ellen is pregnant with Brian's child — the line that becomes Jamie Fraser. The pregnancy is now an explicit dramatic stakes point the lovers speak aloud.
- Janet MacKenzie (Colum, Dougal, and Jocasta's sister) was killed defending Leoch; Maura survives and is helping Jocasta with the wake.
- Young Malcolm Grant died (reportedly of a broken heart) after Ellen jilted him; this is the official Grant line Colum uses to justify punishing Arch.
- Lovat's betrayal is now public knowledge to the MacKenzies; archers can identify Fraser colors, and Lovat's route deliberately funnels enemies toward Inverness via the loch road.
- Henry has personally travelled the stones twice (once to 18th century, once back to 1924); he remembers Julia, William, and Passchendaele, but electroshock therapy erased his conscious memory of daughter Claire. The seizures/brain lesions visible on his head are the inert remnants of the "lightning the Fair Folk put in him."
- Mrs. Graham is established as Henry's asylum-rescuer and Julia's wartime contact, and now operates as the pair's time-travel strategist.
- Davina Porter and "Mrs. Graham" are the same person (Julia asks after her at Henry's arrival; Henry later names her as the woman who got him out).

## Jokes, Callbacks & References
- Arch's cover story — "*the Fair Folk* put *lightning in his head*" — is a comic inversion of the show's literal electroshock trauma: villagers attribute modern psychiatric damage to fairy malice.
- Colum's threat — "Twenty lashes should be adequate, Mr. Bug" — plays the violent-punishment card for political theater while secretly channelling Arch's already-public "fairy-mark" immunity.
- Julia's line to Henry: "It's to bring you home. … You come back yourself, whole" deliberately inverts the earlier epistolary promise ("when this war is over, we shall meet at last"), reframing reunion as embodied presence rather than survival.
- The Gaelic love-song that opens the episode ("Long the way… for my love that's lost") foreshadows Brian's defection and the lovers' enforced parting.
- The 18th-century march song ("Soldiers with swords… he that would honor win must not fear dying") bookends the Jacobite mobilization, with the lyric "Trumpets on turrets high" rhyming visually with Lovat's cannons atop Inverness's walls.
- Ellen's purple-fabric flower (her mother's "color of dreams" motif) mirrors the St. Anthony pendant and wedding-ring-as-gem callbacks — talismans cluster around the time-crossing couples.

## Unresolved Threads
- Brian's open rebellion: he has renounced Fraser titles and ridden south; Lovat has sworn to strip him of "name, clan, protection" — the next time father and son meet will be on opposite sides of the battlefield.
- Ellen's dawn flight from Leoch via Dalwhinnie, with Brian's small blade and twenty-plus MacKinney saboteurs still hidden in the Jacobite rear guard.
- The unborn child's fate and the question of whether Ellen reaches Brian before Inverness falls or Mar's rising collapses (the show has both Henry and Colum independently predict the rising will fail).
- Henry's mental damage: even with Julia's love, his memory of Claire is incomplete; whether the stones can restore what electroshock erased is the central mystery for the Henry-Julia line.
- The three required gemstones (Mrs. Graham's brooch, Julia's wedding ring, Henry's watch) — none yet retrieved; whether Henry can "slip away" during the fighting and reach stones with all three intact.
- Seema's claim against Henry: she has now told Julia directly and framed herself as the lover Henry "didn't know he was loving." Her presence at Leathers and a brewing confrontation with Davina sets up a potential exposure of Henry's wound to Lovat.
- "Where is my son?" — repeated twice in Seema's monologue: William's location (with Julia at Leathers vs. moved out) is unsettled from her vantage, foreshadowing a custody-style threat.
- Lovat holds Inverness with cannon; the Jacobite army is bleeding north while Mar sits at Perth. The strategic shape of the rising — and whether Henry/Julia/Brian all reach the stones before its collapse — now drives every remaining episode.

## S02E06

## Outlander: Blood of My Blood — S02E06 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode intertwines two timelines. In 1740s Highlands: Ellen is brought to Castle Leathers (Lovat's seat) after a stranger killed Robert MacKinney and abducted her. Lovat has seized Inverness bloodlessly in the Crown's name. Brian is missing after refusing to fight for Lovat, remaining loyal to the Jacobite cause. At a tense feast, Lovat's wife reveals he drugged the wine with a potion to destroy Ellen's womb — ensuring their son Simon remains the last Lovat heir per a prophecy. Ellen, pregnant with Brian's child, miscarries and is returned to Leoch, spirit broken. Dougal and Old Angus are imprisoned at Fort William after a failed raid; two Fraser of Lovat men ride to rescue them. Brian and Murtagh infiltrate the fort but Murtagh dies in the escape. In WWI England: Julia and Henry reunite secretly at Castle Leathers, establishing a "postbox" (a loose stone) for correspondence while Henry searches for gemstones to enable time travel.

### Important Character Actions
- **Lovat (Simon Fraser)**: Seizes Inverness for the Crown; poisons Ellen to prevent further heirs; frames his cruelty as protecting the prophecy.
- **Ellen MacKenzie**: Confronts Lovat, calling him a traitor; discovers pregnancy; suffers forced miscarriage; refuses to stay at Leathers.
- **Brian Fraser**: Absent — holding to Jacobite oath, causing Lovat to denounce him as a fool rotting in a ditch.
- **Dougal MacKenzie**: Leads 20 men in suicidal attack on retreating Redcoats; captured with Angus; only Alec MacMahon returns.
- **Captain Gerard Grey (Earl of Melton)**: Interrogates Jacobite prisoners at Fort William; restrains Sergeant Randall's brutality.
- **Julia Moriston & Henry Beauchamp**: Reunite; create covert letter drop; commit to finding each other across time.
