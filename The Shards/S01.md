## S01E01 — "Pilot" (The Shards)

### Episode Plot

The episode opens in May 1980, with seventeen-year-old Bret Easton Ellis narrating his experience seeing *The Shining* at a Westwood theater. While waiting for the film to begin, he spots a stunningly handsome boy — tall, tanned, GQ-model gorgeous — who briefly meets his gaze. Bret is instantly fixated. He searches for the boy after the screening but can't find him. The episode then jumps to September 1981, the start of senior year at Buckley School, a prep school in Sherman Oaks. Bret, his girlfriend Debbie Schaffer, and his best friends Thom Wright and Susan Reynolds spend the last days of summer riding horses in Malibu, doing cocaine, and planning an extravagant senior year. Their idyllic routine shatters when Robert Mallory — the exact boy from the theater — arrives as a mysterious new transfer student on the first day of school. He tells a polished backstory (divorced parents, dead mother, previous expulsion, now living with an aunt in Century City), but Bret immediately clocks him as a liar. Robert shows pointed interest in Susan Reynolds, asks Bret about Matt Kellner, and follows Bret in his Porsche after school, culminating in a tense confrontation at the Galleria. That evening, Bret watches a news report revealing that a teenage girl's body has been found on a Woodland Hills tennis court, linked to two earlier killings. The serial killer — dubbed "The Trawler" by police — is hunting young women across LA, using satanic rituals involving missing pets. The episode ends with a terrified girl pleading for help over a phone line.

### Important Character Actions

- **Bret** obsessively identifies Robert as the boy from *The Shining*, confronts him about it twice, and is met with denials both times. He maintains a secret sexual relationship with classmate Matt Kellner while publicly dating Debbie. He is writing a novel (which will become *Less Than Zero*) and tells Terry Schaffer he wants him to option it.
- **Robert Mallory** arrives with a rehearsed, too-smooth backstory. He asks pointed questions about Thom and Susan's relationship ("How long have they been dating?"), inquires about Matt Kellner specifically, and tells Bret a cryptic observation: "When you talk to me, you're really talking to yourself."
- **Thom Wright** is protective of Susan and dismissive of Robert's arrival. He shares that Debbie's father, Terry, once invited him to a Jacuzzi, making him deeply uncomfortable.
- **Susan Reynolds** is the newly elected student body president, reluctant homecoming queen candidate, and the object of both Bret's secret love and Robert's apparent fascination.
- **Debbie Schaffer** is Bret's wealthy, rule-breaking girlfriend (platinum hair, hot pink lipstick). She throws the last big party of summer.
- **Terry Schaffer** makes a predatory grooming approach toward Bret under the guise of career mentorship, inviting him to lunch at Le Dome to discuss his writing. He also propositions Thom.
- **Liz Schaffer**, drunk, publicly accuses her husband of soliciting young men.
- **Matt Kellner** has a secret sexual relationship with Bret, is the class stoner, and — critically — notices his fish are gone and his cat has gone missing.
