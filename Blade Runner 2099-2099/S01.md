## S01E03

## Blade Runner 2099 — S01E03 Summary

### Episode Plot
Marian, a Blade Runner, investigates Marek's disappearance while partnering with Olwen. A memory-recording mask Marek accessed is empty — someone wants the memory it held. Joseph Muller is revealed to be helping replicants "disappear." Meanwhile, Marian tracks Muller to the cooling flats near the ocean, where surveillance loses him. A raid at Lomita Towers uncovers Tess Stanley (a runner from a swept depot) and her brother Elias, who couldn't walk but suddenly can after receiving new meds from a buyer named Ray. Marian and Olwen trace the trail to a motel where Ray meets Blue — the original Joi template — revealing Ray as a self-described "time-traveler" from the future who knows Blue's mother Helena. The episode ends with Marian crossing the border into Mexico, tracking Marek four miles ahead.

### Important Character Actions
- **Marian**: Interrogates a Voight-Kampff tester about Marek's off-record conversations; threatens a witness with off-world deportation; commandeers Janusz's vehicle after his death; crosses border solo to pursue Marek.
- **Olwen**: Hosts Marian; conceals her connection to Marek; lies about her whereabouts; later coordinates with Blue at the bar.
- **Marek** (absent): Visited a printing stack off Sepulveda (carbon ash on shoes); watched a Tyrell Corp off-world recruitment vid repeatedly; accessed a memory mask.
- **Ray (buyer)**: Supplies Elias's nerve-regeneration meds; meets Blue, calls himself a "time-traveler"; knows Helena and Blue's past; implies he needs Tyrell's memory before an off-world assignment to Yabai.
- **Elias**: Paralyzed replicant who walks after new meds; used as bait to flush Marek.
- **Muller**: Helps replicants vanish; last seen entering a building at the cooling flats that leads to the ocean.
- **Blue**: Performs as "the real Joi"; recognizes Ray's Joi ad from the Fisher building; agrees to help Ray for her brother's sake.

# Blade Runner 2099 — S01E01 summary

## Episode plot
A Tyrell Corporation salesman pitches Nexus-4 Replicants to managers of a Shimata carbon-tube factory, touting the "more human than human" slogan: one Replicant does the work of 20 humans, tolerates radiation and pressure changes, eats and sleeps little, needs no pay, and carries a built-in backstop — limited lifespans, manufactured at age 21, with no parents, no children, no past, no future. At a depot shift change, LAPD officers run ID sweeps. A worker named Tess flees during the checkpoint and is captured. Cora, an ID/name broker with a sick brother (Elias) and a flat registered under Tess's name, needs a fresh identity before she is found. She visits Hedi, a blind veteran who lost his eyes in a phosphorus attack during the war; Hedi reveals Cora is wearing the boots of Marek, a Replicant Blade Runner who went rogue, shot off his tracker, and dumped his vehicle near San Diego. Hedi gives Cora the serial number of Marian Jessup, a female Replicant transferring in from Off-World (Ibeji), so Cora can pose as her and enter Property Division to retrieve an item Blade Runners seized from someone at the Rosewood. Hedi dies (eight days left). Cora passes the voice-print checkpoint as Marian Jessup, meets officer Yahya, and undergoes the Index — an empathy evaluation — while a detained man, Isaac Brandt (who bought the name "Caelan Bagby" from a dealer in Downey and worked as a loader at the Richfield Plaza bombing), is processed nearby.
