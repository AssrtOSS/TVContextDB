## S01E01

# S01E01 — Friday: Arrivals

## Plot

The episode intercuts between past and present: a flashback shows Hollis filming a Christmas episode of her food blog *Hungry with Hollis* just before police arrive to inform her that her husband Matthew has died in a car accident (struck a deer on Midland Road). In the present, Hollis is on a morning show promoting her new cookbook *Everyday Special* when she breaks down crying on camera. After the humiliating interview, a friend suggests she reclaim control by hosting a weekend with one friend from each era of her life. Hollis reluctantly calls her publicist Chelsea and commits to doing it this weekend at her Nantucket home.

Parallel introductions follow the four invitees:
- **Dru-Ann Jones**, a sports agent, is in crisis: a clip of her telling soccer star Posey Woodford to "suck it up and play" has gone viral. "Cancel Dru-Ann Jones" is trending; she's being labeled a "mental health denier." Her boss JB tells her to take the week and lay low.
- **Tatum McKenzie**, owner of a dry-cleaning shop, is at a doctor's office awaiting biopsy results. She's terrified—her mother died nine weeks after the same procedure. The doctor reassures her, "You are not your mother."
- **Brooke Kirtley** packs for the trip while her husband Charlie, a lawyer, tries to talk her out of going because of a Tuesday deposition. She runs into her former friend Electra Undergrove, who is staying in a Sconset rental.
- **Gigi Ling**, a British-born Delta pilot, is in the cockpit when Hollis calls to invite her. She agrees to come.

Hollis calls her daughter Caroline, who claims she's too busy with school and a biology final. Hollis notices Caroline's voicemail is still full of saved messages from Matthew. Caroline then surprises Hollis by showing up at the Nantucket house anyway, having rearranged her schedule.

Guests begin arriving. Caroline asks them to sign consent forms for her documentary photos. Dru-Ann arrives first; she and Caroline trade jokes about being "canceled," and Dru-Ann offers to leave, but Hollis insists she stay. Brooke arrives in an enormous hat and makes awkward jokes about orgies and vibrators. Gigi arrives—reserved, precise, bringing a candle as a gift. Tatum arrives in heels with an expensive bottle of wine and reveals she's brought **Jack Finnigan**, Hollis's first teenage boyfriend. The reunion is charged; Jack tells Hollis she "smells the same" and admits he's only on the island for the weekend to check on his aunt's house.

At dinner, the group dynamics crackle: Dru-Ann and Tatum bicker about the dry-cleaning shop; Brooke overshares about her breast augmentation and her daughter Hazel (community college, OnlyFans, interior landscaping); Tatum asks what porn everyone watches, leading to Brooke's admission that she and Charlie don't communicate about what she likes. Hollis explains she and Gigi bonded online after Matthew's death because it was easier to talk to a stranger than to old friends she'd shut out. Caroline confronts Hollis, accusing her of caring more about her followers than about anything real, and Hollis tells her to put the camera away.

Later, in the kitchen, Hollis admits to Tatum that she and Matthew "weren't in a great place" when he died, and that how they left things is all she can think about. She doesn't want Caroline to know. The episode ends with a montage of Matthew's saved voicemails playing on repeat: *"Hi, my love. It's me, Matthew. You're probably already at the hotel. I'm heading to the airport."*

## Key Character Actions

- **Hollis** orchestrates the weekend as a curated "five-star" recovery project, performs composure, burns the tart, and privately confesses the troubled state of her marriage to Tatum.
- **Caroline** inserts herself uninvited, films the weekend, and challenges Hollis's authenticity.
- **Dru-Ann** arrives under a PR cloud, uses humor to deflect, and is told to lay low.
- **Tatum** brings Jack as a surprise, masks her medical fear with irreverent humor.
- **Brooke** performs friendliness, overshares, and reveals her marriage is strained.
- **Gigi** observes quietly, bringing a small gift, revealing little.
- **Jack** re-enters Hollis's life, creating romantic tension and Caroline's amusement ("a Nicholas Sparks movie").

## S01E02

## The Five Star Weekend — S01E02 "Friday Night: Pajama Dance Party"

### Episode Plot
The first full evening of Hollis's curated weekend unfolds. After the arrivals and dinner in Episode 1, the five women settle into the house. Hollis tries to maintain her hostess composure while grieving Matthew. Tensions rise as the friends clash over how to support her — Gigi advocates simply *being present*, while Tatum and Dru-Ann push for confrontation. A "Never Have I Ever" game escalates into raw revelations. The evening culminates in a blackout, a noise-complaint visit from a childhood acquaintance turned police officer (Teddy Cooper), and Hollis persuading Gigi to stay by admitting she needs a "fresh start" with someone who doesn't know her past failures.

### Important Character Actions
- **Hollis**: Breaks down describing her grief — NyQuil dependency, food tasting like ash, inability to open the "Matthew post-death to-do" folder. Insists on the pajama dance party as distraction. Later confesses to Gigi she invited her for a "clean slate."
- **Tatum**: Provokes Hollis with a brutal *Never Have I Ever* prompt ("lost my husband in a car accident and threw a three-day slumber party"). Reveals a breast-cancer scare: found a lump, biopsy results pending until Monday. Asks Hollis not to tell the others.
- **Dru-Ann**: Fields a crisis call from her boss (JB) about client Posey Woodford. Drafts a non-apology statement refusing to admit fault for accusing a golfer of faking a mental-health crisis. Caroline coaches her on genuine apology structure (admit wrong → express regret → give context).
- **Brooke**: Admits a college fling with a woman during *Never Have I Ever*. Confides in Sunny (Nantucket local) about husband Charlie's lawsuit — an intern accused him of inappropriate behavior; Brooke dismisses it as the intern wanting a promotion.
- **Gigi**: Bonds with Caroline over shared loss (Gigi's mother). Connects with Dylan (bartender, former boogie-board student) at the bar. Nearly leaves feeling out of place; stays after Hollis's plea.
- **Caroline**: Documents the weekend. Mediates Dru-Ann's apology draft. Reconnects with Dylan, who caddied for her father.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **"Hollibabes"** — Hollis's term for her online followers.
- **"Matthew post-death to do"** — Hollis's avoided computer folder.
- **Teddy Cooper** — Nantucket police officer; the women babysat him as a toddler ("Teddy Bear").
- **Posey Woodford** — Dru-Ann's client (Angel City FC soccer player), central to Dru-Ann's cancellation scandal.
- **NAMI** — National Alliance on Mental Illness; Dru-Ann cites her donation in the draft statement.
- **Dylan** — Bartender at the Nantucket bar; taught Caroline to boogie-board at Cisco Beach; caddied for Matthew.
- **Eddie** — Mentioned as likely contractor who redid the bathroom (Hollis doesn't remember the brass fixtures).

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Tatum's mother died of cancer; Tatum has now outlived her by 12 years.
- Hollis and Jack (teenage boyfriend) broke up because she wanted to leave Nantucket, he wanted to stay. She regretted it for two years.
- Gigi's mother died recently (she initially says "last year," then corrects herself — timeline ambiguity).
- Brooke has been with Charlie since age 16; never been with a woman since college.
- Hollis's wedding featured Tatum and Dru-Ann performing a childhood dance routine ("the ocean").
- The house has no generator; breaker box location unknown to Hollis (Matthew handled "house stuff").
