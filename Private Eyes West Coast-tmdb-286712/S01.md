## S01E07 Summary — Private Eyes West Coast

### Episode Plot
Beloved cheerleading coach **Lucia Amante** is found bludgeoned to death in her gym office. The prime suspect is **Cassie Paalberg**, a top flyer who threatened Lucia on video hours before the murder after being kicked off the team for drinking. Cassie's father **Neil** hires Shade and Angie to clear her name. The investigation uncovers that Lucia was running a **college admissions scandal** — forging Ivy League recommendation letters and collecting weekly cash deposits. The Buzz Snap account **CheerNoEvil669** that framed Cassie was created by **Sasha**, Lucia's rival coach, who was being blackmailed by Lucia (along with cheer judge **Ashwin**) over their affair. Sasha killed Lucia with a second-place trophy, hid the blood-soaked money in the gym mats (on loan from her own gym, Spectrum), and planted the Buzz Snap to frame Cassie. Cassie is exonerated and rejoins the team.

### Important Character Actions
- **Angie** goes undercover as a "cheer mom" at Island Elite; bonds with Darlene ("PomPomMom") and extracts intel on the threat video's chain of custody.
- **Shade** golfs with **Piers Tremblay** (Genevieve's father) to access **Ashwin**, the cheer judge making cash drops; exposes the admissions scam and Ashwin's affair with Sasha.
- **Raine** breaks Partridge-encrypted Buzz Snap account, confirming **Sasha = CheerNoEvil669**.
- **Will** watches Dex, spars with Shade over parenting ("no shoulders" rule, homemade vs. pouch food), and provides the "Snowplow" dad insight.
- **Detective Xiao** questions Cassie but holds back the murder-weapon detail (trophy); later tacitly acknowledges Shade/Angie's work.
- **Ty** (assistant coach) provides alibi, reveals Lucia's paid contract offer and cash deposits.
- **Darlene** recorded the Cassie–Lucia argument; **Morgan** (her daughter) forwarded it to Sasha.
