## S01E04 Summary — *The Split Up*

### Episode Plot
Multiple crises converge: Uncle Rhik faces eviction from Bickerton Lane Farm after missing court dates; Maya discovers Michael has drained the children's education trust fund; Dhruv's £200k payment to Zoya Bassu sparks affair suspicions; Aria and Luke are sent to the farm to persuade Rhik, where they find the missing will that secures his inheritance. The episode culminates at the joint "fox party" (hen/stag do), where Zoya reveals to Aria that **Dhruv is her father** — making Luke and Aria half-siblings.

---

### Important Character Actions
- **Aria & Luke** drive to the farm, search Rhik's chaotic property, locate the will John left him, and prevent immediate repossession.
- **Maya** confronts Michael over the emptied trust fund (Danny & Charvi's education money); threatens to "crucify" him.
- **Dhruv** offers Zoya £200k to leave; she refuses, demanding "what I'm owed" and later drops the paternity bombshell.
- **Neal** admits to Dhruv he's been lying to Aria about the co-leadership plan; Dhruv extracts a promise Neal will stand by Aria whatever comes.
- **Rhik** accepts the farm must be sold; moves in with the Kishans temporarily.
- **Zoya** reveals to Aria at the fox party: *"He's… my father"* (Dhruv).

## S01E01

# S01E01 — The Split Up

## Plot
Aria Kishan, heir apparent of Manchester family-law firm Kishan Law, balances three colliding pressures: closing major divorce cases, restarting her decade-long relationship with fiancé Neal Sharma, and absorbing the shock of Dhruv (her father and firm founder) hiring Luke Christos as senior associate without consulting her. The episode moves from a morning property viewing through a workplace ambush celebrating her engagement, the collapse of the Alex & Tash celebrity-marriage consultation, and culminates in the chunni (Punjabi engagement party) where Dhruv publicly announces Aria and Neal as joint managing partners — stripping her of sole succession. Aria suffers a panic attack; Luke finds her and confronts their past.

## Important character actions
- Aria settles the Prasad divorce at £10.5M (including the Saint-Tropez apartment) and closes a Chancery case at £26M, proving her case-won credentials while Dhruv keeps Luke on a leash.
- Aria takes on the Alex Edwards consultation solo because Dhruv is "backed up"; she steers Alex and Tash Edwards toward honesty over brand preservation, ending their contest.
- Dhruv ambushes Aria with a champagne "affectionate intervention" to force the chunni that very evening, using Kav's stolen wedding folder.
- Dhruv independently headhunts Luke Christos (financial forensics background, almost poached by Fearnleys), bypassing Aria and giving Luke the prenup folder she authored — Aria publicly reminds him he is her boss "not for long."
- Dhruv announces at the chunni that Aria AND Neal will be joint managing partners; Aria confronts him privately, accusing him of ambushing her in public to prevent challenge.
- Luke tells Aria he didn't know she worked at Kishan Law until after being offered the job, asks her out (she declines), then arrives at the chunni uninvited and reveals he came to find out if their weekend meant more.
- Neal initiates the engagement ambush with Dhruv and Harshika, defends the "puppy-dog eyes" defence when Aria is furious.
- Maya (Aria's sister) flirts openly about Luke, teases Aria about her prom dress and "emo phase," and is single again ("her son is single. Again").
- Kav (brother) arrives at temple still in yesterday's clothes ("walk of shame to God's house"), carries out the logistical setup of the intervention and chunni.
- Alex Edwards initially agrees to Tash's offer to dump Tilly and save the brand, then reverses course — he wants divorce, and to tell listeners the truth.
- Tash Edwards (via Zoya) contests the divorce and proposes a reconciliation deal tied to the sponsor clause.
- Michael Lanigan (Maya's husband) is caught by Aria in a compromising moment with Becca in Maya's house; Aria warns Maya, who initially dismisses it.
- Zoya Bassu of Halletts (London) arrives as Tash's lawyer — Dhruv already briefed on her celebrity-case reputation.

## S01E06

# 剧情梗概

Aria与Neal的婚礼前二十四小时，Aria向Neal坦白她和别人（Luke）有过一夜情。Neal愤怒但拒绝追问对方身份，强调自己"不是被视作理所当然的备选"，并表态"在弄清楚之前不确定还想不想结婚"。婚礼当天Aria选择照常举行。Luke独自在律所向Aria表白"无论这是什么、可能变成什么——我入局了"，并难听地唱了Atomic Kitten的《Whole Again》。Neal在婚礼后留下辞呈，返回伦敦。

## Maya线

Maya与丈夫Michael的离婚案在FDR（Financial Dispute Resolution，财务纠纷调解）阶段完成签署。Maya在调解室情绪崩溃离开，Michael追出请求复合、回归家庭。Maya最终拒绝："我不再相信我们了。"

## Zoya身世与基山律所继承权

Dhruv向全家人坦白：Zoya Bassu是他的亲生女儿，来自他来英国前在德里的包办婚姻——她与Aria、Maya、Kav是同父异母的兄妹。

Zoya索要基山律所25%估值现金。Dhruv曾安排把她的份额卖给伞形公司Cartwells（Cartwells希望他留任MD），后改为£200,000现金+写入遗嘱，被Zoya拒绝。

## Zoya的监护权案

Zoya与前夫Vivek争夺女儿Indy（Indira）。心理评估对Zoya有利。Vivek提出若Zoya同往德里，他将在当地安顿她、偿还英国债务、让她每日见Indy。Zoya拒绝，但处境恶化：Vivek已在全国所有律所将她拉黑，她濒临破产，付不起诉讼费。Aria承诺"这事交给我，我会跟爸爸谈"。

## Aria的职业决定

Aria宣布将基山律所一部分股份卖给Cartwells，把现金给Zoya用于争夺监护权；Aria本人离开Kishan Law，独立建所。这一决定事先未与Neal商议。

Aria同时向Dhruv表态希望专攻**调解（mediation）**而非诉讼，理由是"帮人们与所爱的人正确沟通，减少痛苦和创伤"。Dhruv仍倾向诉讼路线。

## 重婚疑云（核心揭晓）

婚礼上，一位名叫Arun的男子找到Dhruv，自称是Zoya的生父。他手持一份结婚证书作为筹码，指控Dhruv当年在德里与Zoya母亲（从未与Arun离婚）的包办婚姻为重婚——Dhruv在此婚姻未解除的状态下"再婚"了Aria的生母。Arun威胁：停止支持Zoya，否则曝光该证书。Dhruv表面安抚。

## S01E03

## 剧情主线

第三集围绕两条并行冲突推进。Aria 瞒着父亲 Dhruv 签下了曼城市中心新写字楼（Needleworks）的租约，准备将 Kishan Law 搬离 Dhruv 与亡妻 Raina 共同购置的旧址；与此同时，Maya 在丈夫 Michael 因出轨分居七个月后正式回归律所，却立刻遭到 Michael 以"parental alienation"为由的反击，而推手是他带来的新伴侣 Becca。
