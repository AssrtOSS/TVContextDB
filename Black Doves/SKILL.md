## Key Relationships
- **Helen ↔ Wallace**: Marriage of 10+ years, two children; he is unaware of her spy life.
- **Helen ↔ Jason**: Secret affair; he was MI5 investigating her (unknown to her until finale).
- **Helen ↔ Sam**: Deep platonic bond; met 2014, she saved him & Michael when pregnant; he calls her “Daisy” (her 2014 alias).
- **Helen ↔ Reed**: Handler/recruiter; complex loyalty — Reed manipulates but also protects.
- **Sam ↔ Michael**: Former lovers; Sam hid real job (insurance cover); tension over honesty.
- **Sam ↔ Lenny Lines**: Coercive boss–subordinate; Lenny uses emotional blackmail (threatens Michael & daughter).
- **Reed ↔ Marie**: Mother/daughter (revealed ep 6); Marie is Webb nanny — long-term placement.
- **Dani ↔ Helen**: Fellow Black Dove; groomed as Helen’s replacement with Wallace.
- **Wallace ↔ Dani**: Professional; she flirts to maintain cover; he is oblivious.
- **Jason ↔ Maggie ↔ Phillip ↔ Yarrick**: Intelligence chain — Maggie fed gossip to Phillip, who leaked to Yarrick, triggering murders.

## World Setting
- **Time**: Contemporary, set over Christmas 2024 (diegetic dates match release).
- **Place**: London — Westminster, South Bank, Highgate/Highbury, Borough Market, Somerset House, Liberty London, National Theatre, Crystal Palace (Helen’s house).
- **Political Landscape**: Conservative government; Defence Secretary (Wallace) ambitions for No. 10; PM Richard Eaves implicated in cover-up; MI5 vs. CIA vs. Chinese MSS tension.
- **Organizations**:
  - **Black Doves** (黑鸽 / 黑鴿): Private “spies-for-hire” — steal industrial, political, diplomatic secrets for highest bidder. Not a state agency. Name from creator’s local Brighton pub.
  - **MI5** (军情五处 / 軍情五處): Domestic intelligence; ran Jason’s operation.
  - **CIA** (中情局 / 中央情报局): London station under Mitch Porter; Cole Atwood operative.
  - **Clark Crime Family**: London underworld; Alex (matriarch), Trent (son), drug & arms network.
- **Tone**: “Love Actually but we kill everyone” (Knightley) — Christmas backdrop, British wit, graphic violence, emotional stakes.

## Overall Plot Premise
Helen Webb, wife of the UK Defence Secretary, has spent a decade passing his secrets to the Black Doves. When her secret lover Jason is assassinated, her handler Reed reactivates Sam Young — a burnt-out assassin and Helen’s closest friend — to protect her. Their investigation pulls in the Chinese ambassador’s death, a missing video, the Clark crime family, and a cover-up reaching the Prime Minister. The conspiracy forces Helen and Sam to confront their past choices, the cost of their double lives, and whether people like them can ever have a “happy ending.”

## Cultural Context for Translators
- **British Christmas**: Not religious — cultural default. Crackers, pudding, “Bleak Midwinter” carol (Rossetti/Holst), office parties, “Die Hard” debate.
- **Class & Politics**: Wallace = Tory rising star (Skipton & Ripon = real constituency); Highgate = media/political set; “Red Wall” references possible.
- **London Geography**: Dialogue name-drops real places (Westminster, South Bank, Borough Market, Crystal Palace, Liberty, Somerset House) — keep names, don’t localise.
- **Spy Tradition Subversion**: Not MI6/James Bond; private mercenary org; no gadgets, just tradecraft, knives, guns, burner phones. “The firm” = criminal, not Service.
- **Geopolitics**: UK–China–US triangle; ambassador death = diplomatic crisis; “third world war” hyperbole in dialogue.
- **Representation**: Sam (gay, mixed-race actor), Helen (woman lead in genre), Reed (older woman authority), diverse supporting cast — avoid stereotyped renderings.
- **Humour**: Very British — understatement, irony, swearing as punctuation. “Knife fight at a jeweller’s? Tuesday.” Preserve deadpan.
