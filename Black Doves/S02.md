## S02E03

"The Coming Night" / 《黑夜将至》** (the series background only documents Season 1; Season 2 premieres on the conversation date).

---

## Episode Plot

**Cold open:** Dani (Black Dove trainee, now desk-bound after demotion) breaks into Helen's flat seeking help to return to field work. Helen rebuffs her but lets her stay the night after Dani admits she "can't be alone."

**Morning after:** Helen and Wallace maintain a fragile domestic façade. Wallace has a meeting with the U.S. President; Helen learns from Reed that Downing Street is moving to make Wallace PM by September — which would protect Helen (no one divorces in No. 10). Reed warns: the organization will "put us in the fucking wood chipper" if they become useless.

**Sam's spiral:** Sam is unravelling after killing Jerry (an innocent civilian) to protect Helen's secret. Helen confronts him; he lashes out. Reed's replacement (Conteh) pressures Sam, arguing his "soul" makes him valuable — but Sam is visibly breaking.

**Archive infiltration:** Helen sends Dani (with a camera contact lens) into the MI5 archive to find "Wildflower" files — the codename Reed was hunting when she disappeared. Files are missing; Dani barely escapes detection.

**Battersea Park dead drop:** From a captured Black Dove bagman, Sam learns Reed used a lockbox under a bench near the Peace Pagoda. Conteh will send someone to check it; Sam must intercept and extract Reed's location.

**The Chernov dinner:** Wallace dinners with Russian plutocrat Alexi Chernov and wife Katia. Helen uses the bathroom break to confront Katia privately: the Paris "robbery" (8 bodies) was a cover-up; their housekeeper Ida was a spy tortured and killed. Helen demands Alexi's KGB past in Berlin and the "Wildflower" codename.

**Poison soup attempt:** Eleanor (assassin) and Sam try to poison Alexi via soup at the dinner. Chaos in the kitchen — wrong bowl nearly served to the PM. Alexi survives.

**Closing:** Sam gets the address where Reed is held. Helen and Sam agree: tomorrow morning, they break Reed out and finish this.

## S02E05

# Black Doves S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Helen, Sam, Williams, and Eleanor assault a Black Doves holding facility to rescue Reed. Parallel storyline: the Prime Minister manages the fallout from Russian oligarch Alexi Chernov's poisoning death hours before a peace summit. The team breaches the site, fights through mercenary security, and extracts Reed. During escape, Reed reveals her Berlin past. Meanwhile, MI5's Perryman informs the PM that Helen's identity has been erased from all records — her real name may be Lily Morris, with a stepsister (Bonnie Weir) claiming Helen framed her for patricide. Episode ends with Lenny Lines calling Sam: a bounty is on his head, and the "package" is secured.

## Important Character Actions
- **Helen**: Leads the rescue op; chooses to finish the fight rather than run; rejects Olly Murs playlist.
- **Sam**: Fights beside Helen; admits he has "nothing left" and his debt to Helen is paid; receives Helen's affirmation that *his strength* makes her strong.
- **Williams & Eleanor**: Provide covering fire; Williams confesses love to Eleanor ("I love you too") mid-gunfight; both survive.
- **Reed (Fionnuala)**: Reveals Berlin codename "Wildflower"; had daughter Vita with Franz; was set up by turned agent Ida Povetkin; Franz survived Berlin, blackmailed Chernov, rebuilt Kestrel for Vita.
- **Prime Minister**: Refuses to cancel summit after Chernov's death; agrees to let Chernov's widow deliver his peace speech; confronted with Helen's erased identity.
- **Perryman (MI5)**: Presents evidence Helen = Lily Morris; all records scrubbed; Bonnie Weir's prison claim now corroborated.

## S02E06


---

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with the aftermath of the Black Doves HQ explosion. **Kestrel** (红隼), a rival private intelligence organization, has attacked the Black Doves headquarters, killing key leadership including **Cecile Mason** (塞茜尔·梅森) and about a dozen staff. **Vita** (薇塔) — revealed to be **Elizabeth Reed's daughter**, abandoned in a Soviet orphanage — leads the Kestrel assault. She confronts Helen at gunpoint, forcing her to choose: assassinate Prime Minister **Richard Eaves** (理查德·艾夫斯) and publicly confess to being a Black Dove, or watch her children die (Marie has taken them to London Zoo under Kestrel control).

Simultaneously, a peace summit in London descends into chaos when armed men abduct the PM. Wallace and Helen escape to a stairwell; Wallace believes Helen simply hid in fear. Sam, Williams, and Eleanor mount a rescue at the zoo, eliminating Kestrel operatives. Marie is confronted by Helen and Sam — she admits Kestrel placed her as nanny years ago but claims she'd never harm the children.

In the aftermath: Kestrel's leadership is decimated but not destroyed (Katia Chernov dead, remnants remain). Reed and Helen negotiate a new partnership — Helen demands transparency and agency; Reed concedes. Sam departs London, telling Helen he has no reason to stay. Wallace, shattered by Helen's confession, is left in shock — their marriage's future uncertain. The episode closes on a quiet Christmas street encounter between Helen and a stranger (mirroring the series' opening), suggesting the cycle continues.

## S02E01

# Black Doves S02E01 Summary

## Episode Plot

**Wallace becomes Prime Minister** after winning the Tory leadership contest. The Webbs move into Downing Street; a multinational defense summit is scheduled for next week. Helen attends a diplomatic party where she converses fluently in Russian with the wife of Alexi Chernov and meets **Agent Laila Joshi**.

In parallel, **Helen breaks into a secure location with a safecracker** to retrieve files from a Bolt & Tasker safe using keyword **"Wildflower"** — the same MacGuffin from Season 1. She calls **Sam** mid-heist for technical guidance (he taught her), revealing their ongoing rapport.

**Sam is arrested for murder** (a setup). In custody, **Patrick** recruits him for a private entity: four targets, fee +20%, no negotiations. First target: **Erik Klein, alias "Zeus."** Sam accepts to secure release.

Helen waits for **Reed** at their usual bench (Wednesday 9:30) — Reed never appears. Summoned to a clandestine Black Doves facility, Helen faces the **Executive Management Committee**: Mrs. Burkinshaw, Ms. Conduct, Mr. Rodgers, and **Mr. Robert Conteh**. They inform her Reed ran an **off-book operation in Paris** (armed assault on Chernov's penthouse, hunting "Wildflower" files with Laila Joshi) and has vanished. **Robert becomes Helen's new handler**, demanding Wallace's defense summit security briefings.

Helen covertly clones Robert's laptop. In the final moments, she realizes **Sam's target "Zeus" is a Black Dove** and calls to abort.
