## Premise
Mark and Hank Hoagies, two South Jersey factory workers, lose their jobs when AI-powered robots replace them. Enrolled in a universal basic income pilot that pays them $3,000 a month, they suddenly have abundant free time — and most episodes follow their misadventures, schemes, and small-town troublemaking with friends, family, neighbors, and rivals. The satire is built around the question of what people actually do with their lives once basic needs are decoupled from labor.

## Setting
South Jersey (New Jersey Pine Barrens-adjacent suburban/working-class milieu). Local flavor — Wawa, boardwalks, the Jersey Devil legend, shore culture, Italian-American / Eastern European family names, and the accent and idioms of the region — is a recurring backdrop rather than mere scenery.

## Main Characters
- Mark Hoagies — proud, carefree, idiotic middle-aged man with a frat-boy persona; constantly starts trouble when slighted, stubbornly ignores good advice
- Hank Hoagies — Mark's simple-minded but likable younger brother, often his partner-in-crime; loves hot dogs
- Mernft Man — Mark's underworld-connected friend; real name Scott Myfrynski
- Artie Muldonich — laid-off co-worker with a vast junk-metal / spare-parts collection, recruited for Mark's ill-conceived projects
- Tammy — Mark's down-to-earth wife, works as a nurse; was previously married
- David — neurotic neighbor and wannabe author; gets pulled into Mark and Hank's antics
- Rhoda Hoagies (Season 3+) — Mark and Hank's mother

## Recurring Characters
- Andrea — David's stern, domineering Asian-American wife; frequent unnecessary dramatic confrontations with David
- Darren — Tammy's emotionally withdrawn half-Filipino 14-year-old son (plays video games); Mark's step-son
- Steve DelVecchio — Mark's obnoxious, more successful rival who seizes every chance to gloat
- Jersey Devil — legendary mythical winged creature befriended by Mark and Darren; later plays on Mark's hockey team "The Goofballs"
- Mr. Bouchard — Mark's childhood friend, now Darren's high school history teacher
- Craig Malamut — plays various minor characters

- Generic New Jersey vernacular: dropped r's, "youse", shore talk, casual regional slang; the show leans into these for color## Cultural Context
- Universal basic income (UBI) — the policy idea that every adult citizen receives a regular unconditional cash payment from the government. The show uses UBI as a launching pad for working-class satire about leisure, boredom, and post-work identity.
- AI / automation replacing factory labor — riffs on contemporary anxieties about technological displacement.
- South Jersey culture — distinct from North Jersey; Italian-American, Polish-American, and Filipino-American families; suburban sprawl, beach culture, the Pine Barrens, the Jersey Devil legend, Wawa as civic institution.
- Fox's Animation Domination — the Sunday-night adult-animated block (Simpsons, Family Guy, Bob's Burgers, etc.) where the show airs.
- Hot dog as a character signifier — Hank's love of hot dogs is a recurring visual and verbal motif; the surname "Hoagies" (a type of sandwich) is part of the family-name humor.
