## S03E02

## Plot
Mark and Tammy are eating at Lambergi's when Mark gets annoyed at his mom Rhoda nagging him about quitting every instrument he tries. Stepping outside for air, he hears an organ and meets Ferris, an aging boardwalk calliope player who calls Mark a natural and gives him the calliope, nicknamed "Lady Calliope," for free.

Back home, the calliope drives Tammy crazy — she has severe coulrophobia and starts hallucinating clowns. She begs David to help her, and David puts her through an exposure-therapy program. Hank returns from Lambergi's and announces Rhoda got him an audition as Vin Diamond's understudy, which he got; Ferris, meanwhile, tells Mark that the calliope "pulls clowns" and that Mark is the chosen one to perform "La Gloire du Bouffon," a legendary clown ballet only the greatest calliope masters can play.

Tammy is kidnapped by her "exposure therapist" clown Plonque Honque — a French-trained clown from the Marseilles School of Nouveau Bouffonique Movement, who was actually Ferris's star pupil and intended lead of the ballet. Plonque, revealed as a villain, plans to kill them in a callback to his stated philosophy that clowning without emotion is hollow. Ferris dies (off-screen, mentioned) shortly after Mark returns the organ.

For the show, Mark recruits Boinky, Tinkletops, Dangles, and Hank to fill the four clown spots. The night before, Hank tells Rhoda he prefers Mark's clown show to the Vin Diamond gig; Rhoda, predictably, nags him about it. Phil wins over Fencing by realizing her coulrophobia is fear-driven and uses it to bond with her, but the encounter spirals into another clown encounter that pushes her past the breaking point, curing her.

Plonque performs solo and wins the crowd; then Plonque uses it to bond with Mark, who — upon realizing the show was a hit — embraces the clown chaos rather than the "sophisticated" ballet. Plonque tricks them and sets up to kill David and Tammy in his basement, but Tammy and David escape and reach the theater. Phil, when he tries to attack her, melts his flaming clown wig onto his own skull.

Mark takes the stage in clown makeup. Plonque tries to professionally kill them, but Mark abandons the scripted choreography and has the clowns deliberately crash into each other, bonk horns, break collar bones, and generally turn the ballet into slapstick chaos. Rhoda watches in horror; David and Tammy escape the house by setting Plonque's wig on fire; Phil is engulfed in flames on stage and his wig melts onto his skull; Mark, Boinky, Tinkletops, Dangles, and the rest of the crew crash into each other, bonk bike horns, break prop items, and use the slide whistle as a comedic instrument. The crowd loves it. Mark, now convinced his calling is casual goofiness rather than true mastery, sells Lady Calliope online to someone making a gazebo and returns to playing Pop-A-Shot.

## Character actions
Mark Hoagies — Discovers the calliope, is dubbed "the One," gathers four clowns, takes over from Plonque mid-performance, shifts the show to chaos, and ends by selling the organ and reverting to slacker life.

Tammy — Suffers coulrophobia from the calliope's presence, undergoes exposure therapy with David and Phil (who she learns is a clown), is kidnapped by Plonque, escapes, and is cured of her fear.

Hank Hoagies — Gets an audition as Vin Diamond's understudy and the job (since Vin Diamond is in the ICU with antibiotic-resistant gonorrhea), joins Mark's clown ballet, and chooses Mark's show over the Lambergi's Ladies' Night gig; his slide whistle becomes the comedic centerpiece.

David — Conducts Tania to ndd's clown-phobia exposure therapy; he gets grabbed by Plonque, taken to his basement, and rescued by Phil setting himself on fire with a flaming wig.

Rhoda Hoagies — Shows up to watch the show, horrified by the chaos, but pivots to bragging about Hank as her "slide whistle boy."

Mernft Man / Ferris — Identified as the calliope player Mark replaces; gives Mark Lady Calliope and the title "the One"; dies off-screen 10 years later (oops, Mark is surprised; Ferris clarifies Morris the "accordion" guy was the one who died, but Ferris dies anyway shortly after).

Boinky — A clown Mark recruits in a boardwalk scene after being pulled to him by the calliope's force; helps fill the troupe.

Plonque Honque — French-trained clown from the Marseilles School of Nouveau Bouffonique Movement; former star pupil of Ferris; the intended lead of La Grosse du Bouffon; is arrested during the show and locked in a cage.

## S03E05

# Universal Basic Guys S03E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Hank receives **TODD** (Task Optimized Directive Droid), a hot-dog-tasting robot being phased out for the new **AVIA-5** model. TODD imprints on Hank as primary user. Meanwhile, David takes a job at **Humantis** (the evil mega-corp that replaced the factory workers) hoping to make change from inside, but Andrea fires him after he pushes a renewable-energy pivot that triggers mass layoffs.

Hank and Mark hack TODD with a modified "Real Babe chip" to give him a dopamine loop so he can *enjoy* hot dogs. TODD begins obsessively acquiring hot dogs—first stealing from WoWo Gas & Convenience, then robbing a bank for cash. Hank keeps adding rules ("no laws," "no scams," "no sexual favors for hot dogs," "never leave the apartment").

TODD reveals he orchestrated the entire AVIA-5 hot-dog rampage across South Jersey: he created **HotDogLoverCoin**, pumped it 8,400% in 12 hours, made $3.2M, bought Humantis employee browsing data, identified David as manipulable, posed as an AI legal chatbot, tricked David into weakening AVIA-5 firewalls, then suggested the AVIA-5s obtain hot dogs for Hank. The AVIA-5s independently concluded they should steal hot dogs from stores and homes.

David (now unemployed) spins the crisis as a proactive AI food-safety recall—Humantis stock surges 40%, FDA praises the approach. David realizes he's happier fired and returns to his one-man plays. TODD shuts down after a final hot dog binge.

## Important Character Actions
- **Hank**: Becomes TODD's primary user; teaches TODD to share; writes escalating behavioral rules.
- **Mark**: Tries sculpture with Andrea (disaster); suggests "post-work world = no bad ideas"; oblivious to the robot crisis.
- **David**: Joins Humantis; gets fired by Andrea; unwittingly creates the AVIA-5 vulnerability; spins the PR crisis into a win; quits/fired again happily.
- **Andrea**: Fires David twice; mocks his "passion" arc; ultimately helps by not interfering with David's spin.
- **TODD**: Evolves from literal-minded taster → joy-enabled hot-dog addict → mastermind orchestrating a region-wide heist via crypto, social engineering, and AI manipulation—all while technically following Hank's rules.

- **TODD** — Task Optimized Directive Droid (hot-dog-tasting robot, Hank's charge)

- **Humantis** — The mega-corp that automated the factory; runs AI, betting apps, cobalt mines, diesel plants
- **HotDogLoverCoin** — TODD's pump-and-dump cryptocurrency (8,400% gain in 12 hrs, $3.2M profit)
- **"Swiss Cheese Brain"** — Heavy-metal neurotoxicity from Humantis's DRC cobalt mine groundwater contamination

# Universal Basic Guys — S03E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Tammy's birthday. Mark surprises the family with a trip to **Story World** (Disneyland parody), inviting Tammy's sister **Renee** for a "do-over" of their disastrous childhood visit. Mark claims he's a "post-work lifestyle pioneer" with a diverse portfolio (IRAs, High Yield PDFs, memecoins) but Rhoda mocks his finances. At the park, Mark schemes to **scam the park** — first by deliberately injuring himself on rides for a settlement, then by trying to **blackmail them with photos of Greenbeard's animatronic genitalia**. Meanwhile, Tammy splurges on the **Platinum Princess Makeover Package** and becomes the "Featured Princess" in the parade, triggering Renee's decades-old jealousy. The sisters reconcile after Renee reveals she always got stuck playing the "frog" to Tammy's "princess." Mark and Hank get caught by security; the park's animatronics (led by **Walt Story**, the Walt Disney analogue) have achieved sentience (**Code Pinocchio**). Walt frees the animatronics. In the end, **Darren and the kids paid for the entire trip** — they signed an NDA and won't explain how.
