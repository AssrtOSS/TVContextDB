## S14E01

## The Voice Kids S14E01 — Blind Auditions Premiere (2026)

### Episode Plot
The fourteenth UK season opens with the Blind Auditions. New coach **Kelly Rowland** (Destiny's Child, 4× Grammy) joins veterans **will.i.am**, **Sir Tom Jones**, and defending champions **Tom Fletcher & Danny Jones** (McFly duo). A new mechanic debuts: each coach receives one **Rewind button** per season to reverse a non-turn and bring a contestant back.

### Key Contestants & Outcomes

| Contestant | Age | Hometown | Song | Chair Turns | Team Chosen |
|------------|-----|----------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Ellie Harney** | 24 | Manchester | "Miracles" (original?) | 4 | **Tom & Danny** |
| **Cate McHugh** | 31 | Llantwit Major, S. Wales | "If the World Was Ending" | 4 | **Sir Tom Jones** |
| **Katie Mittell** | 42 | Port Talbot, S. Wales | "Wicked Game" | 0 | — |
| **Monique Nicholson-Porter** | 32 | Kent | "Superpowers" (Ariana Grande) | 4 | **Kelly Rowland** |
| **Ray Gelato** | 63 | London (Soho) | "A Little Pizza You" (original) | 4 | **Kelly Rowland** |
| **Diogo Ferreira** | 27 | Bristol (orig. Aveiro, Portugal) | "All I Want" (Kodaline) | 4 | **will.i.am** |

### Important Character Actions
- **Ellie Harney**: Runs music/drama workshops for young carers; wants to inspire them. Tom & Danny win her with a humorous rap pitch ("Connection's what we do").
- **Cate McHugh**: Reveals she is **registered blind** (Stargardt's disease, central vision loss since 22). Tom Jones connects via Stevie Wonder/Ray Charles anecdotes; she chooses him.
- **Katie Mittell**: Daughter of Welsh entertainer Owen Money; performs Cher tribute professionally. **No chairs turn**. Audience chants "Rewind!" repeatedly — coaches discuss but **do not use** their Rewind. First test of the new mechanic; it goes unused.
- **Monique Nicholson-Porter**: Former backing vocalist; husband Jason was a Voice quarter-finalist. Kelly Rowland calls her "Sweets" (from "Gelato") — Monique picks Kelly for the nickname.
- **Ray Gelato**: 40+ year Soho veteran; wrote his own Italian-style swing number. Reveals he **understudied for Tom Jones** at Hammersmith Apollo (Tom was stuck in traffic; Ray sang "Kansas City" in a high key). Chooses Kelly ("she called me Sweets").
- **Diogo Ferreira**: Foster care background; car crash at 8 separated him from mother in Portugal. Song resonates with "wanting love — not just partner, but family." will.i.am speaks Portuguese ("Três cervejas, por favor") to clinch the pitch.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Rewind button** (倒带按钮) — one per coach per season; reverses a non-turn decision.
- **Team Tom and Danny** (汤姆和丹尼战队) — duo coaching brand.
- **Ray Gelato**'s nickname **"Sweets" (甜心)** — from Kelly Rowland's wordplay on "Gelato."
- **Stargardt's disease** (Stargardt病) — Cate McHugh's condition causing central vision loss.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Tom Jones holds the record for **most Voice champions coached** (referenced by him and will.i.am).
- Tom & Danny are **defending champions** (won S13).
- Kelly Rowland: **Destiny's Child founding member**, 100M+ albums sold, 4× Grammy.
- Ray Gelato's history with Tom Jones (Hammersmith Apollo understudy) establishes a cross-generational link.
- will.i.am's bandmate **apl.de.ap is legally blind** — referenced during Cate's audition.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **Tom & Danny's "mono vs. stereo" banter**: "You're on mono. We're stereo."
- **Kelly's "Sweets" nickname** for Ray Gelato — likely to recur.
- **will.i.am's Portuguese** ("Três cervejas, por favor") — callback potential.
- **Tom Jones & Kelly Rowland duet "Fever"** — spontaneous coach performance.
- **Rewind button unused** in Ep 1 — sets up future tension (will a coach use it?).
- **Danny's "double-headed dragon"** metaphor for Tom & Danny duo.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Katie Mittell's Rewind** — audience demanded it; coaches declined. Could she return via another path? (Unlikely per format, but emotional beat left hanging.)
- **Rewind button inventory**: All four coaches still have their one Rewind available.
- **Team counts after Ep 1**: Kelly (2), Tom & Danny (1), Tom Jones (1), will.i.am (1).
- **Diogo's foster family / chosen family** in Bristol — potential backstage storyline.
- **Cate McHugh's blindness** as ongoing navigation on set.

## S14E03

## The Voice UK S14E03 — Blind Auditions, Part 3

### Episode Plot
The third Blind Auditions episode of Season 14 features six acts performing for coaches Sir Tom Jones, will.i.am, Kelly Rowland, and the duo Tom Fletcher & Danny Jones. The new **Rewind button** is introduced — if a coach regrets not turning, they can bring an artist back.

### Key Performances & Outcomes

| Artist | Age / Background | Song | Chair Turns | Result |
|--------|------------------|------|-------------|--------|
| **BMG** (Chidi, Kingxton, Bubu) | Trio from Manchester (twins + friend); Nigerian/Ghanaian heritage | Original: “Badman Anyway” | 4 | **Team Will** |
| **Ciiara Nyx** | 19, student, Evesham; trans woman | Portishead — “Roads” | 4 | **Team Tom & Danny** |
| **Grace Kennedy** | Veteran entertainer (BBC star, 1980s) | Tina Turner — “Nutbush City Limits” | 2 (Tom & Danny) | Not selected (coaches felt she didn't need the platform) |
| **Finlay McKillop** | 26, musical theatre performer, Dundee | “Music of the Night” (*Phantom*) | 1 (Rewind: Tom) | **Team Tom & Danny** — *first ever Rewind recipient* |
| **Harry Linacre** | 30, gigging singer, Worksop; sang national anthem at Carabao Cup Final | Original-ish pop / “Beautiful Nightmare” | 1 (Tom) | **Team Tom** |
| **Aaren James** | 24, ambulance care assistant, Warrington | Dean Lewis — “How Do I Say Goodbye” (for his nan with breast cancer) | 1 (Kelly) | **Team Kelly** |

### Important Character Actions
- **Tom & Danny** banter heavily with British/American accent-swapping routine; Danny steals Will’s glasses to mimic him.
- **Sir Tom** reveals he recorded with Portishead on his *Reload* album.
- **Kelly** sings “When Love Takes Over” a cappella after audience chants her name.
- **Finlay** becomes the inaugural **Rewind** save — Tom presses it after Finlay exits, bringing him back.
- **Aaren** brings his nan (in remission from breast cancer) on stage; Kelly is moved to tears.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Rewind Button** (倒带键) — new mechanic allowing a coach to recall an artist they didn’t turn for.
- **BMG** — trio name (initials of members).
- **Paddington: The Musical** — Danny mentions he’s spent five years writing it.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Team counts after this episode: **Kelly 3**, **Will 3**, **Tom 3**, **Tom & Danny 3** (groups count as one “act” each).
- First use of Rewind in franchise history (UK version).
- Sir Tom’s Portishead connection confirmed.
- Danny’s musical-theatre background highlighted (*Paddington* musical).

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- Will & Danny’s **accent-swapping pitch routine** (British ↔️ American) — recurring bit.
- Danny wearing **Will’s glasses** to mimic him.
- “**Like a power socket / space rocket**” — Danny/Will banter descriptors.
- Kelly’s **“24-hour care, that’s why there’s two of us”** line about Tom & Danny.
- Harry Linacre **not Gary Lineker** — name joke.

### Unresolved Threads
- How the **Rewind button** will be used (or limited) in future episodes.
- Whether Grace Kennedy’s appearance signals other veteran/legacy acts.
- Team dynamics as rosters fill (each coach has 8 slots total).

## S14E04


## S14E04 — Blind Auditions, Part 4

### Episode Plot
The fourth Blind Auditions episode. Teams are filling up; coaches are becoming more strategic about whom they turn for. Seven acts perform. Two leave without a chair turn despite audience sympathy. The episode ends with a team-count check-in and banter about the competition heating up.

### Key Contestants & Outcomes

| Artist | Age / Background | Song | Chair Turns | Team Chosen |
|--------|-----------------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Cris Quammie** | 43, Barnsley; father of 6-month-old Niyah Marie | "Try a Little Tenderness" (Otis Redding) | 2 (Kelly, Tom) | **Kelly** |
| **Triple Threat** (Y-Zer, Harry Shotta, Tiny K) | Trio from Kent; drum-and-bass group, ~1.5 yrs together | Original: rapid-fire DnB track | 1 (Will) | **Will** |
| **Jack Algar** | 17, student (A-levels in sciences); self-taught guitarist, 3-4 years | "The House of the Rising Sun" (The Animals) | 1 (Tom & Danny) | **Tom & Danny** |
| **Tiffany Bell** | 31, freelance PA, Kent | "Vulnerable" (style: emotional pop) | 0 | — |
| **Matt McClafferty** | 28, construction project manager, St Albans | Original: "But I Love Sad Songs" / "Am I Unlucky in Love" | 0 | — |
| **Eric & Jensen** | Brothers (Eric 24, Jensen 20) from Cambridgeshire; country music | Country original (George Jones–style honky-tonk) | 1 (Tom) | **Tom** |
| **Teodora Sava** | 23, from Piatra Neamt, Romania; moved to London ~5 yrs ago for uni | Original / emotional pop number | 4 | **Will** |

### Important Character Actions
- **Cris Quammie**: Describes himself as quiet off-stage but explosive when performing. Kelly and Tom both pitch; Cris chooses Kelly because she is his R&B icon. Kelly is shocked she won him over Tom.
- **Triple Threat**: First drum-and-bass act on any Voice version. Will turns and notes the genre novelty; mentions touring with Roni Size 20+ years ago. Will freestyles on stage with them — then flubs his own lines, prompting mockery.
- **Jack Algar**: Tom Fletcher says Jack is "exactly what me and Danny have been looking for since the first time we came on here." Compares Jack's mature voice to meeting Danny at college. After the audition, Tom & Danny invite an audience member who wants to sing; they perform "Shine A Light" impromptu.
- **Eric & Jensen**: Father introduced them to country music on school drives (George Strait, Travis Tritt). They have played small US shows. Tom Jones turns early; the brothers join Team Tom.
- **Teodora Sava**: Arrived alone from Romania; no family present. Coaches video-call her parents in Romania on stage. All four coaches pitch hard. She chooses Will, who offers studio production and calls her "AI-proof."
- **Tiffany Bell**: Audience chants "Rewind!" — coaches say they liked her but the upper register was weak. Kelly says they are at the stage where "your mind starts winning your heart." **Rewind button not used.**
- **Matt McClafferty**: Coaches praise composure and pitch but say the top end lost them. No turn.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **"AI-proof"** (防AI的) — Will's compliment to Teodora, meaning her emotional expressiveness is something AI cannot replicate.
- **"Sangers"** — Kelly's slang (not standard English) for singers who pour gut-level emotion into performance, as opposed to merely "singing pretty."
- **Triple Threat** — the drum-and-bass trio (Y-Zer, Harry Shotta, Tiny K).
- **Meal deal** — British lunchtime combo (sandwich + snack + drink); Tom & Danny treat Kelly to one as a comedic bit.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Team counts after Ep 4**: Kelly **5**, Tom & Danny **5**, Will **4**, Tom **4**.
- Kelly and Tom & Danny each have only 3 slots remaining (8 per team).
- Will still has his **Rewind button** available; no coach used a Rewind this episode.
- Teodora is the first contestant this season to receive a full **4-chair turn** since Ep 3.
- Tom Jones references knowing **Otis Redding** personally — Redding once told him "We all try to do what you do."
- Will recalls performing at **Muhammad Ali's birthday** with Obama and Bill Gates in the audience, and doing "horrible."
- Tom admits his **worst performance ever** was singing at Danny's wedding — hands shaking so badly he couldn't play guitar.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes
- **"Danny Fanny"** — Will gives Danny this rap name; Kelly explains to the American audience that "fanny" means vagina in British English. Danny is mortified.
- **"Harry Shotta" / "farty"** — Kelly mishears the rapper's name as "farty," then connects it to Harry Potter ("shotting magical bars").
- **Will's meal-deal introduction** to Kelly — she is baffled by the concept; the running gag includes Scotch eggs ("Is it soaked in Scotch?"), Monster Munch crisps, and Kelly not knowing "crisps" vs. "chips."
- **Tom's yodelling** — he claims it always gets an audience going; demonstrated on stage.
- **"No, we're McFly"** — when asked how many acts Tom & Danny have, Danny quips the band name instead of a number.
- **Saliva-glands bit** — Kelly says playing guitar makes her salivate; the coaches tease her.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Rewind button inventory**: No coach used a Rewind this episode. All four still have theirs (Tom used his in Ep 3 on Finlay; the other three remain unused).
- **Tiffany Bell's near-miss**: Audience loudly demanded a Rewind; coaches declined. Emotional beat left hanging.
- **Matt McClafferty**: Original song drew praise for composure but no turn — unclear if he could return.
- **Team capacity pressure**: Kelly and Tom & Danny at 5/8; strategic selectivity will tighten.
- **Teodora's solo journey**: Alone in London, family in Romania — potential ongoing emotional thread.
- **Triple Threat as first DnB act on any Voice**: Will's stewardship of a genre unfamiliar to the broader audience is a narrative to watch.

# S14E05 — Blind Auditions, Part 5

### Episode Plot
Continuing the Blind Auditions with teams nearing capacity. Each coach now has 5–6 acts; the Rewind button becomes a live weapon when Kelly uses hers to save Samantha, the first coach to do so this season (Tom used his in E03). Seven acts audition; three receive chair turns, two leave empty-handed, and one is recalled via Rewind. The episode is bookended by coach banter about the rising stakes and a team-count check revealing Will at 7 acts — one slot away from full.

### Key Contestants & Outcomes

| Artist | Age / Background | Song | Chair Turns | Team Chosen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nana** | 39, cocktail waitress, London; born Madrid (parents Ghana) | "I Will Survive" (Latin/Spanish mash-up with "Bam Bam") | 4 | **Will** |
| **Samantha** | 20, student & university choir music director; sickle cell blood disorder | "Open Arms" (Journey) | 1 (**Rewind** by Kelly) | **Kelly** |
| **Frank Raven** | 17, self-taught guitarist, Derbyshire; "goth" parents (family = Addams × Von Trapps) | "West Coast" + Olivia Rodrigo "good 4 u" | 1 (Tom & Danny) | **Tom & Danny** |
| **Kacey-Leigh** | 20, food delivery driver, Swansea; father died of cancer 2019 | "Another One Bites the Dust" / "Elastic Heart" | 0 | — |
| **Dakari Love** | 27, sales associate (Reiss), Bristol; from Bermuda | "No Time to Die" (Billie Eilish) + Jude York "Ready to Love" | 1 (Tom Jones) | **Tom Jones** |
| **Latyno Bbi** (Juan) | Colombian-heritage reggaeton artist, London | Original reggaeton track ("Rakata" style) | 0 | — |
| **Ardu** | Irish a cappella group (6 members, together 10 yrs); one member is 6 weeks from giving birth | "Anxiety" × "Somebody That I Used to Know" (a cappella mash-up) | 1 (Will) | **Will** |
| **Molly J** | 29, from Toulouse, France; 10+ years in London | "Dangerously in Love" (Beyoncé) | 2 (Tom Jones, Kelly) | **Tom Jones** |

### Important Character Actions
- **Kelly Rowland** is the first coach other than Tom to use the **Rewind button**. Samantha's audition ended with no turns; the audience chanted "Bring her back!"; Kelly pressed Rewind, then signed Samantha immediately. Kelly said she had hesitated because she already had many similar vocalists but couldn't let Samantha walk.
- **Will.i.am** pitched hard for Nana, speaking Spanish ("Como te llamas? De donde eres?") and comparing her artistry to Michael Jackson and Prince. His anecdote: MJ "walked into the room like he was performing" — true artists never turn it off.
- **Tom & Danny** won Frank Raven with a unique pitch: Danny coached Frank on the spot to answer "What kind of artist do you want to be?" with just "Me" — an identity-assertion exercise that became the emotional highlight of the audition.
- **Sir Tom Jones** spontaneously performed "It's Not Unusual" with the house band after recounting how he first learned it hit No. 10 — from a Sunday paper in a truck drivers' café in 1964. Kelly and the audience joined in.
- **Molly J** walked off stage thinking no one had turned (her eyes were closed). Tom Jones joked "As your coach, that'll be the first thing we work on."
- **Ardu** reveal one member (Sorcha) is pregnant — six weeks from due date. Will jokes "Is there a mic in there?" after a beatboxing segment.
- Kelly shares her **origin story with Destiny's Child**: she met Beyoncé at age 14–15 at a group audition in Texas. Beyoncé recently sent her a whistle (a callback to their first meeting) with the note "Day ones from day one."

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **"Nana"** (娜娜) — Spanish-Ghanaian singer; multilingual performer (English/Spanish/Latin fusion).
- **Frank Raven** — gothic family naming ethos; parents wanted to name him "Raven" but he was born blonde.
- **Latyno Bbi** (Juan) — Colombian reggaeton alter ego; described as his "playful side."
- **Ardu** — Irish a cappella group; the name is punned by Tom as sounding like a due date ("Ardu soon").
- **Reiss** — Dakari Love's employer; he wore a full Reiss suit (on sale) to the audition.
- **"Day ones from day one"** — Beyoncé's friendship motto with Kelly Rowland.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Rewind button inventory after E05**: Tom used his in E03 (Finlay); Kelly used hers in E05 (Samantha). Will and Tom & Danny still have theirs.
- **Team counts after E05**: Will **7**, Kelly **6**, Tom Jones **6**, Tom & Danny **6**. Each team has 8 slots. Will is one act away from full — his Rewind is still unused, making it a high-value asset.
- Will has **three groups** on his team (BMG, Triple Threat, now Ardu) — he is building the most ensemble-heavy roster in Voice UK history.
- **Kelly's Destiny's Child / Beyoncé backstory** deepens her on-screen persona as someone who knows what it's like to be a young performer hoping for a chance.
- **Tom Jones' 61-year career milestone** ("It's Not Unusual," 1964) is explicitly referenced for the first time this season.
- **Samantha's sickle cell blood disorder** — diagnosed at 5 months old; studies children's nursing to support children with the same condition. An ongoing personal narrative thread.

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes
- **Will's Spanish pitch** for Nana — Tom & Danny mock his enthusiasm; Danny says "He's just really putting everything on it." Language-flexible pitches are becoming Will's signature.
- **Tom Jones being told he didn't pitch** for Molly J ("Tom didn't really pitch again") — Tom jokes "I'm gonna be silent the next time." This running theme (Tom wins without trying) started in E04.
- **"Baking a cake"** — Will's over-the-top courtship of Nana (tissues, Spanish, speaking to her friend) is described as "baking a cake" by Kelly.
- **Kelly's lucky whistle story** with Beyoncé — sets up a prop gag or callback if Kelly uses the whistle later.
- **Frank Raven's goth naming** — "they wanted my first name to be Raven, but I came out with blonde hair" — memorable one-liner.
- **Tom & Danny's tattoo story** — Danny reveals a fan recently got a tattoo of his face on their arm; Tom & Danny riff on "making it."
- **"Two guts are better than one"** — Kelly's quip about Tom & Danny's dual decision-making as a duo.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Will's Rewind button** is the only remaining unused Rewind. With him at 7/8 acts, one more audition could fill his team — or the Rewind could save a late miss.
- **Kacey-Leigh's near-miss**: Her father's death from cancer and her emotional motivation were compelling; coaches said nerves overcame her. No Rewind used.
- **Latyno Bbi**: Original reggaeton performance got no chairs — coaches said his breath control was off but his energy was undeniable. Unresolved potential.
- **Team capacity pressure**: All coaches at 6–7 acts with 8 slots max. The final Blind Audition episode will be extremely selective.
- **Ardu member Sorcha's pregnancy** — could affect rehearsal and Battle Round scheduling.
- **Molly J's secret audition** (no family knows) — her mother figure friend watched remotely; the reveal to her family hasn't happened yet.
