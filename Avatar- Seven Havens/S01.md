## S01E02

# S01E02 — Into the Storm

## Plot
The episode opens with Pavi's voiceover framing the world: humanity overthrew the Avatar's "cruel reign," and the survivors built the Seven Havens. The action then jumps to Nisha secretly training Brigade cadets in earthbending; she slips up and bends in public. Captain Karthik scolds her and bars her from the festival. Frustrated, Nisha vents to Ruhi that "no one understands us."

Stowing away at the festival grounds, Nisha stumbles into Pavi, who is also bending in secret. The two realize they look identical — twins. They demonstrate bending for each other and play catch and tag. Nisha notices Pavi's missing lower leg (amputee from birth). They confess that neither remembers anything before three years ago and that Captain Karthik has been training Nisha in secret.

Jae, the Airbender, arrives and reveals that the Brigade's senior staff already know Nisha's secret; Jae's cover inside the Brigade is "bending-less assistant" to Karthik. Jae demonstrates airbending and asks Pavi to show her abilities, then declares, "Two Avatars? … Never before you two." Jae explains that the Seven Havens' public memory blames Korra for the Cataclysm and treats the Avatar as the world's enemy, but Jae still believes in the reincarnation cycle's protective purpose.

Pavi, self-deprecating ("I'm a nobody… just a fluke… collecting rocks and introducing myself to bugs"), tries to flee back to her cave. An alarm interrupts them: a major storm is striking Ellora. Karthik mobilizes the Brigade, including Jae, and forbids Nisha from joining the front — "Ellora's not ready for you." Pavi initially refuses to help, citing a past trauma involving a friend named Sang that made her life "fall apart." Nisha persuades her.

During the storm, citizens shelter; a strange energy-based creature ("scary zappy thing… made of energy") attacks. Pavi and Nisha combine earthbending to destroy it. Karthik credits them both. The pair finds Commander Bountan wandering, eyes altered, muttering "It is coming." The episode ends with Nisha asking Pavi to be sisters; they reconcile and race back to the Compound with Geet and Ruhi.

## Important character actions
- Nisha bends publicly against Karthik's orders, training cadets in secret.
- Pavi demonstrates bending (including all four elements, per Nisha's claim) and joins the fight against the energy monster.
- Jae reveals airbending publicly and confirms knowledge of Nisha's Avatar status; Karthik deploys Jae to a frontline squad.
- Karthik shields both twins from public reveal, forbids Nisha from the front line, and later praises the girls' joint victory.
- Commander Bountan is found post-battle in an altered, raving state.

## Newly established names and terms
- The Bending Brigade / Brigade (internal enforcement body within a Haven) — covers "registered" vs "unregistered" benders.
- The Compound — the Brigade's walled headquarters/compound inside Ellora.
- Captain Karthik — leader who has trained Nisha in secret for three years.
- Commander Bountan — senior commander, found catatonic/mutating post-attack.
- Yuy, Chung — Brigade soldiers mobilized during the storm.
- Ulan — name called out during the storm (likely a cat-monkey or companion).
- Sang — Pavi's former friend; an unresolved incident around her broke Pavi's life apart.
- "Energy monster" / "scary zappy thing" — new threat type, distinct from spirits seen earlier.
- The Cataclysm — official term for the apocalyptic destruction attributed to Korra; treated as recent cultural trauma.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Pavi is a congenital amputee (born missing part of one leg).
- Both twins have no memory before roughly three years ago; their origins are a mystery.
- The Seven Havens were founded after the fall of the previous Avatar's "cruel reign."
- The Avatar reincarnation cycle is not common knowledge; most people believe the Avatar is humanity's destroyer.
- Jae hides as Karthik's bending-less assistant inside the Brigade.
- Nisha has secretly trained Brigade cadets, including children who play "hunt the Avatar" at festivals.
- The twins can combine bending effectively in combat; Pavi's strongest element is earth.

## Jokes and references useful for later episodes
- "Hunt the Avatar" — children's tag game where the Avatar is the hunted target, mirroring public sentiment.
- "Kulfi Pop" — festival prize; flavor/cultural texture for later festival scenes.
- "Teacher-dad-boss" — Nisha's affectionate jab at Karthik's triple role.
- "Cat-cousins" — Geet and Ruhi's kinship debate after the sisters reunite.
- "Winner gets the first Kulfi Pop!" / "You're it!" — recurring play-frame that doubles as Avatar-world metaphor.

## Unresolved threads
- Identity and origin of the energy monster, and whether it will return ("if that thing comes back").
- Commander Bountan's altered eyes and prophecy "It is coming" — is he possessed, transformed, or warning of something larger?
- The twins' shared pre-three-years-ago memory gap and what made them separate.
- What happened to Pavi's friend Sang.
- How/whether the twins' existence will be revealed to Ellora and the wider Havens.
- The cataclysmic history of Korra, lost records, and the Order of the White Lotus (still off-screen).
- Whether Pavi will formally accept her Avatar role and train with Nisha and Jae.

## S01E03

# Episode S01E03 — "The Order"

## Plot
After the storm, Pavi and Nisha discover each other's existence and recognize their identical faces. A voiceover establishes the world: an ancient Avatar brought the Cataclysm; civilization rebuilt around the Seven Havens; the Avatar has returned — but for the first time, there are two. Most fear destruction, some (a narrator) believe they will save the world.

At the underground hideout, the twins meet Nisha's Order handler and are told that, as Avatars, they must master all four elements. Nisha demonstrates excellent earthbending form while Pavi — who refuses to fight — panics through her first sparring drill and gets kicked in the eye for her trouble. After Pavi declares fighting pointless, the sisters trade insults: Nisha mocks Pavi's love of ground-sleeping, bugs, and "musk"; Pavi fires back about Nisha's rock "friends." Nisha storms off, then panics when Pavi disappears through a window with Geet.

Jae is given 7.85 minutes by Commander Karthik to retrieve Pavi. He finds her in the slum outskirts with a frightened spirit-creature whose eyes have gone wrong. Pavi calms it by playing the kalimba song stuck in her head — "Oh little one, where'd you take my heart?" Both girls suddenly remember it as their mother's lullaby. The creature calms. Pavi takes Nisha back to her secret place, where Nisha realizes Pavi's "friends" are two semi-sentient stones named Luster and Mica. The twins reconcile; Nisha invites Pavi to meet her (Nisha's) friends, then the reverse; the sisters commit to staying together and being "the kind of Avatars we want to be."

Pavi returns to Karthik and asks two favors: help find their parents (still alive, she hopes), and let Jae teach them airbending. Karthik agrees to both. An Order member formally greets them: "Welcome to the Order." Nisha badgers Jae to let her try his air skimmer, calling him "wind boy." Jae warns them his sweets fund is gone — "always bet on airbenders doing the right thing." Closing narration: "Ellora now has two Avatars. We can take care of whatever comes our way."

## Character Actions
- Pavi: learns about being an Avatar; refuses to fight on principle; flees with Geet after their spat; calms the spirit with a song; reconnects with Nisha over shared memory; asks Karthik to help find their parents and make Jae their teacher.
- Nisha: proves capable in earthbending drills; attacks Pavi during sparring; mocks Pavi's homemaking preferences; panics when Pavi runs; reveals her own "rock friends" Luster and Mica; apologizes; commits to staying paired with Pavi.
- Jae: trains them; admits he isn't cut out for the responsibility; tracks down Pavi; wins back the twins' trust; agrees to teach them airbending ("wind boy").
- Commander Karthik (newly promoted): leads the Bending Brigade; trusts Jae with a 7.85-minute window; agrees to help find the twins' parents.
- Shilpa (new): Jae's coworker who notices his distraction and tails him to the secret basement; reluctantly agrees to "observe" for the Order for now.

- The Order of the White Lotus — described in-story as a centuries-old secret order that protects and trains the Avatar. After the Cataclysm their numbers dwindled to almost nothing; now they operate from shadows because the whole world is against the Avatar.

- Hamka — a senior Order member; "don't get in Hamka's way."
- Luster and Mica — Pavi's pair of semi-sentient rock companions at her old hideout.

- Harshlands — the dangerous territory where exiled criminals (and, per Jae, the girls if exposed) are left to fend for themselves.

## S01E01

# S01E01 — "On the Horizon" (premiere)

## Plot

The episode opens in Ellora (the haven of wind and sand), where 9-year-old Pavi and her cat-monkey Geet are trying to reunite a pet beetle named Sang with his relatives. Sang's family has been captured, so Pavi drags Geet toward the city's walls to search.

Pavi is the squad leader of a children's Brigade patrol unit. She and her friends — Daemin, Uki, Lan — chase Sang through a tunnel system beneath the festival grounds. Above them, Commander Bountan and Captain Karthik of the Brigade stage a public conscription: a mother named Malika is told every bender must join the Brigade's shrinking ranks; her young son Ulan is the "gift" she gives to Ellora. Karthik warns his troops that the Brigade is on high alert today — the anniversary of Avatar Korra's defeat and the Cataclysm — though he privately tells Jae (Pavi's Airbender mentor and friend) the Avatar is "not a threat today."

At the festival, the children watch an anti-Avatar street play that dramatizes Korra as a tyrant. Vendors sell Anti-Avatar charms and beetle-shell jewelry. Pavi defends a sack of beetles ("Sang's brothers and sisters") from a vendor, exposes her Earthbending in the scuffle, and is identified by onlookers as "an unregistered bender." Jae pursues her out into the Harshlands — the wasteland surrounding Ellora.

A sandstorm drives them into Old World ruins. A tornado touches down; Pavi unconsciously airbends to shield them both. Jae realizes she has just seen multi-bending (Earth + Air) and recognizes Pavi as the new Avatar. Exhausted, they make their way home.

Jae reports the sighting directly to Captain Karthik. He questions her closely, then drops the cliffhanger: "You can't have found the Avatar… because I already have."

## Important Character Actions

- Pavi — squad-leader routine; rescues Sang and his relatives; picks a fight with a beetle vendor; accidentally reveals Earthbending publicly; airbends (unconsciously) during a tornado to protect Jae and Geet; collapses from exhaustion.
- Jae — runs safety checks (wall integrity, storm gutters); reassures Pavi after the sandstorm; reports a multi-bender sighting straight to Karthik.
- Captain Karthik — conscripts Ulan from Malika using Ellora's motto as leverage; privately waves Jae off Avatar fears; reacts to Jae's news with controlled interest, then reveals he already holds "the Avatar."
- Commander Bountan — executes the door-to-door bender conscription.
- Malika and Ulan — mother-son pair representing the cost of the conscription law.
- Daemin — squad member, obsessed with surprise hugs (birthday-only) and lotus fritters; naively suggests Korra "just needed her morning chai."
- Uki, Lan — squad/festival children; Lan is the one upset by the play ("already giving me nightmares").

## S01E06

# Episode S01E06 — Night of the Afflicted

## Plot

A cold open intercuts the twins' lingering questions about why their parents hid each from the other with the four-elements narration established earlier, reaffirming that **two Avatars** now exist for the first time and that most fear them as destroyers.

The main story runs in two halves:

1. **Doctor visit and the "phantom leg" revelation.** Pavi's prosthetic keeps hurting her; Jae insists she see **Dr. Jia**. Pavi reveals she has never seen a doctor (Geet refuses to accompany her for fear). At the hospital Dr. Jia explains her pain is *phantom limb pain* and that **she lost the lower part of her leg within the past few years** — contradicting Pavi's belief she was "born like this." Pavi's memory is suppressing the trauma.
2. **The Affliction outbreak at the hospital.** Tala, the bender accidentally injured earlier by Lan while sealing a growing breach, is hospitalized. The Brigade is exhausted; benders have been disappearing to the **Faceless**. A new condition — **the Affliction** — is spreading: fear, hallucinations, red eyes. The first wave was hit directly by **The Anomaly**; Ellorans are now spontaneously Afflicted.
3. **Pavi blown cover in the Afflicted Ward.** Wandering in, she is recognized as the Avatar by an Afflicted man. She defuses him by joking "if I were the Avatar, I'd have two legs — I only have one." Nisha then earthbends the terrified man *into the wall* to calm him. Dr. Jia observes that **fear is both symptom and cause** of the Affliction.
4. **Afflicted riot + Commander Karthik falls.** An alarm sounds; a "demon beast" (a red-eyed creature the Afflicted call a *camodillo*) breaches containment. Karthik, already exhausted and unable to stop the growing storm, is hit by Affliction himself — haunted by hallucinations of old comrades (Bountan, his mother, Pai Sho games) and past campaigns.
5. **The energybending reveal.** Pavi and Nisha combine earthbending with an instinctive ability — **energybending** — to defeat the Afflicted. Karthik snaps out of it when the twins call his name.
6. **Political fallout.** The Brigade chants *"Keep benders safe!"* and confronts Chancellor **Kayoko**, who still refuses to call other Havens for reinforcements.
7. **Parents flashback + Ellora's distress call.** A flashback shows Mom and Dad fleeing through **the Harshlands** with the twin infants two weeks earlier: *"I don't know if we're doing the right thing."* In the present, the twins hug and promise to ask each other for help. Pavi operates Ellora's radio to broadcast: *"This is Ellora… we need you."* No Haven answers.

## Important Character Actions

- **Pavi** reveals phantom-limb pain she has been hiding; performs earthbending instinctively on the Afflicted; inadvertently discovers she can **energybend**; broadcasts Ellora's distress call.
- **Nisha** physically earth-walls an Afflicted to "calm" him; engages The Anomaly alongside Pavi; later asks Pavi not to pity her after seeing her cry about her leg.
- **Jae** insists Pavi see a doctor, later publicly sides with the Brigade in front of Kayoko and presses her to seek outside help.
- **Dr. Jia (new)** — hospital physician treating Pavi and the Afflicted; key voice arguing fear causes the Affliction.
- **Karthik (Commander of the Brigade)** — drained, fails to stop the growing breach, becomes Afflicted mid-episode, is broken out of it by the twins, then confronts Kayoko.
- **Kayoko (Chancellor)** — doubles down on refusing inter-Haven aid.
- **Tala** — hospitalized after Lan's accident, later Afflicted alongside Karthik.
- **Lan** — blames herself for injuring Tala; seals the breach with another bender; encourages Brigade grievance action.
- **Daemin** — escorts Tala to the hospital (line preserved from prior ep).
- **Bountan** — Afflicted patient Karthik hallucinates as an "old friend"; calls Karthik by his name and reminds him of *firestorms in the Tong Desert*, the *siege at Crescent Bay*, and his mother's awful cooking.
- **Geet** — refuses doctor's office out of cowardice, retorts *"I'm not a scaredy cat-monkey!"*

## S01E11

# Avatar: Seven Havens — S01E11 "Battle of the Ruins" Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode interweaves three converging threads: **Pavi and Nisha** with the Faceless in the Old World ruins, **Jae** navigating his double-agent position within Ellora's leadership, and **Chancellor Datu/Kayoko** weaponizing spirit energy against both the Avatar and the Faceless.

Pavi's bending remains blocked; Agam (Faceless elder) diagnoses a blocked Root Chakra tied to fear. Nisha confesses she never lost her bending—she suppressed it from fear of losing control and losing Pavi. The twins reconcile via their "twin promise." Meanwhile, Jae (now confirmed as Commander) plays both sides: he reports to Chancellor Datu that he captured the Faceless Queen (Zi), but secretly warns Pavi/Nisha of Kayoko's plan to channel spirit energy from the reservoir into the ruins—"one arrow, two rattle-ravens" (strike both the Avatar and the Faceless). The spirit energy will breach the reservoir within a week; fear makes it more volatile.

At the ruins, Elloran guards ambush the escaping Faceless. Pavi and Nisha's parents appear as spirits/ghosts in the ruins. Jae arrives, helps them fight, and urges them to flee. The episode ends with a massive spirit-energy blast from Ellora's direction—Chancellor Datu has fired the weapon, and the Avatar (Pavi) is presumed to have retaliated.

---

## Important Character Actions
- **Pavi**: Confronts her fear of being "the Avatar"; admits she's scared of everything; bending remains blocked but Root Chakra work begins.
- **Nisha**: Reveals she lied about losing bending; fear is losing control and hurting Pavi; commits to twin promise.
