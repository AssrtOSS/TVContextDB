# Avatar: Seven Havens — Translation Background

## Series Identification

- English title: Avatar: Seven Havens
- Chinese title (mainstream zh-TW/HK CN renderings): 《降世神通：七處庇護所》/ 《降世神通：七淨土》/ 《降世神通：七大避風港》
- Format: American animated fantasy / post-apocalyptic television series (2D-animated, half-hour episodes)
- Created by: Michael Dante DiMartino & Bryan Konietzko (co-chief creative officers of Avatar Studios)
- Production: Avatar Studios + Nickelodeon Animation Studio; animation outsourced to La Chouette Compagnie (France)
- Composer: Jeremy Zuckerman
- First air: 2026-10-09 on Paramount+ (first three episodes same day; remaining Book 1 weekly in batches of three, through 2026-10-30)
- Season/episode structure: 2 "books" (seasons) × 13 half-hour episodes = 26 total
  - Book 1 (S1) subtitle: "Survival"
  - Book 2 (S2) subtitle and exact premiere TBA
- Network/Platform: Paramount+ (exclusive streaming)
- Book 1 episode titles: "On the Horizon", "Into the Storm", "The Order", "The Faceless", "The One Left Behind", "Night of the Afflicted", "What Lies Beneath", "The Envoys", (ep 9 TBA), "Ghosts and Spirits", "Battle of the Ruins", "The Dome", "The Root Chakra"

Note on franchise position: Sequel to Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005-2008) and The Legend of Korra (2012-2014). Set 225 years after the end of The Legend of Korra and 299 years after the end of The Last Airbender. Carries forward all prior franchise terminology.

## Relationships

- Pavi ↔ Nisha: twin sisters separated at birth / in childhood (long-lost twin), working together to uncover their mysterious shared origins.
- Pavi ↔ Jae: mentor/mentee and friend; Jae is an Airbender who lives with her in the city.
- Pavi ↔ Geet: pet cat-monkey bond (companion role mirrors Appa to Aang).
- Nisha ↔ Ruhi: pet cat-monkey bond (companion role mirrors Appa to Aang; mirrors Naga to Korra).
- Pavi ← Avatar lineage: reincarnation of Korra → Aang → … (prior Avatar cycle continues).

## World Setting

- Genre: fantasy + post-apocalyptic. The world was shattered by a cataclysmic event (the official logline attributes the destruction to the previous Avatar era / Korra). Two centuries of broken civilization later, the survivors have rallied around seven remaining strongholds.
- The Seven Havens: civilization's last pillars / safe zones, risen from the post-cataclysm ruins. They are at risk of collapse; saving them is Pavi's central mission.
- Human-vs-spirit dynamic: the world is overrun by spirits; portals between the human and spirit worlds remain open, and Korra's lost Avatar State means past Avatars no longer provide direct guidance.
- Cultural inversion of the Avatar role: past Avatars were seen as saviors; in this era, the title "Avatar" marks Pavi as humanity's destroyer in the public imagination. She is hunted by both human factions and spirit enemies.
- Faction hinted in trailer: "the Order of the White Lotus" — a "freaky underground cult that worships the Avatar" (per trailer dialogue); should not be conflated with the heroic White Lotus of earlier series.
- City: Ellora / Alora — Pavi's home city; spelling variation exists across English-language sources (Paramount+ page uses "Alora"; Wikipedia and Spanish wiki use "Ellora"); subtitles should follow the on-screen title once seen.

## Plot Premise

A 9-year-old Earthbender, Pavi, discovers she is the new Avatar succeeding Korra in a broken, spirit-haunted world. Unlike her predecessors, she is publicly feared as the bringer of destruction. Pursued by both human and spirit enemies, she joins forces with her long-lost twin sister Nisha, Airbender mentor Jae, and her cat-monkey Geet (with Nisha's own cat-monkey Ruhi) to unravel their shared mysterious origins and save the Seven Havens — the final pillars of civilization — before they collapse.

- Cataclysm / apocalyptic event → 大災難 / 末世浩劫
- Seven Havens → 七處庇護所 / 七大避風港 / 七淨土 (three competing zh renderings currently in use; pick one and stay consistent — 庇護所 is most literal; 避風港 is colloquial; 淨土 is from amo.watch catalog entry)

New series-specific terms:

- The Order of the White Lotus (per trailer) — renderings used elsewhere in the franchise include 白蓮宗 / 白蓮教; do not collapse to the heroic White Lotus of prior series
- "Order" (episode title) → 教團 / 組織 (do not pre-commit to a definitive rendering until episode 3 dialogue confirms)

## Speaking Style & Cultural Context

- The series keeps the franchise's blend of mythic-Asian (Water Tribe, Earth Kingdom, Fire Nation, Air Nomads) and Western serialized storytelling, but the visual style shifts toward French surrealist (Mœbius) and 1990s anime influence (per DiMartino/Konietzko).
- Tone is more post-apocalyptic and morally gray than prior Avatar shows: characters openly discuss fear, prejudice, and dehumanization of the Avatar. Hunted-child narrative beats (mirroring Korra/Aang's early arcs but inverted in public perception).
- Character voice: Pavi reads as a 9-year-old — earnest, scared, occasionally whiny; not a chosen-one stoic. Jae as mentor uses warmer, sage-like phrasing. Antagonists (per trailer) speak in menacing, ritualistic tones ("I will severely punish whoever is hiding the Avatar").
- Notable slang/idiolect from trailer: "freaky underground cult" (referring to the Order); speakers mix colloquial English with formal/monumental pronouncements — translators may mirror with 俚俗對白 vs. 莊嚴宣告 的對比.
