## S02E02 — Plot

The Hawkins Investigators Club splits into two threads: a nighttime "goblin hunt" in the woods that turns out to be a raccoon, and an investigation into a break-in at the Dandridge family arcade where a thief seemingly vanished into thin air. On security footage the kids spot glowing "ghost lights" (will-o'-wisps) and track Charlie Dandridge to a campsite off Cornwallis, where they encounter a supernatural presence haunting the woods.

## Important Character Actions

- **Dustin** leads the HIC, insists on investigating the arcade break-in despite police dismissal, confiscates the security tape as "evidence of supernatural phenomena."
- **Lucas** navigates the woods during the goblin hunt, later helps confront Charlie's associates at the arcade.
- **Mike** films for the HIC's Samuel Hawkins movie project; gives El a glowing flower from a roadside patch.
- **Max** breaks up with Lucas (returned her walkie), rejects Valentine's Day as a commercial scam rooted in her parents' failed marriage.
- **El** receives the glowing flower; it activates in her presence.
- **Charlie Dandridge** holds the new Centipede high score (stolen from Max), has been hiding in the Cornwallis woods since his uncle Ernie's release.
- **Uncle Ernie** (Charlie's "creep" uncle, recently paroled) takes Charlie hunting in those woods.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Hawkins Investigators Club (HIC)** — formalized name for the kids' group, self-described as "first line of defense against paranormal threats."
- **Operation Goblin** — HIC codename for their night patrol.
- **Bloodaxe Bill / The Mad Miner of Hawkins** — local legend who blew up the old silver mine; could sense ore veins through rock with a silver axe.
- **Ghost lights / will-o'-wisps** — the glowing orbs seen on the arcade security tape before the thief vanishes.
- **Cornwallis woods** — hunting grounds Charlie knows "like the back of his hand."

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Timeline: Winter 1985, between ST S2 and S3; no active gates to the Upside Down.
- Hopper is out of town (giving the kids more freedom).
- The Dandridge arcade break-in: ~$30 in quarters stolen, side-door exit, timecode skips on tape, lights affect the intruder.
- Samuel Hawkins town-founder movie project assigned in history class (Mr. M / Mr. Henderson).
- Max's parents divorced; father lives in another state.
- El's flower glows in her hand — possible residual Upside Down connection or new phenomenon.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- "HIC already got us in trouble once today" — running gag of failed cryptid hunts (Mothman = hairy camper, werewolf = Mrs. Kingsley's Pekingese).
- "Flat Eggos" = "sell like hotcakes" (Dustin's Eggos callback).

- *The Breakfast Club* new release — Lucas/Max date movie reference.
- "Wretched hive of scum and bullying" — *Star Wars* Mos Eisley quote from Dustin.
- Charlie's uncle Ernie = "creep uncle" just out of jail.
