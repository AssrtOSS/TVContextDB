## City of God: The Fight Rages On — S02E02 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode opens with Buscapé (Rocket) narrating from his community radio booth: a transformer has been shot out in the Old Block, power is out, and vans are avoiding the area. He warns residents to stay indoors and walk the Gabinal Road to the gas station if they must move.

Buscapé's narration then maps the current territorial layout of Cidade de Deus:
- **Cranium (脑壳)** runs **Karatê** — the coolest head among drug lords, sharp with on-the-spot math
- **Ocimar** formerly ruled **Treze**; after his death, his associate **Matarrindo (马塔林多)** took over — "the devil left a stray little demon behind"
- **Caçula / Grafitti (老幺/涂鸦)** — youngest of the old runners, surprisingly still alive
- **The Apts (公寓区)** — chaotic, violent, cursed since Li'l Zé; no lord survives there (Li'l Zé, Curió, Bradock all dead)

**Carrot (胡萝卜 / Cenoura)** returns from prison. He once refused Curió's offer to divide the Apts, warning the area was cursed. Now back, he intervenes in a shootout between Genny's and Matarrindo's men, ordering a ceasefire: "Let's talk business." He commands respect — even Curió made him godfather to his eldest son. Carrot mediates a sit-down between **Genny (杰尼尼奥)**, **Matarrindo**, **Cranium**, and others, insisting on the favela's code of honor: respect territory, the enemy is outside (militia/politicians), not inside.

Parallel storyline: **Reginaldo / Cabeção (雷吉纳尔多/卡贝考)** and militia enforcer **Touro (陶罗)** plan the **"Good Neighbor" (好邻居) project** — a front for militia expansion. They'll start in **Freguesia (弗雷格西亚)**, then **Quintanilha (金塔尼利亚)**, **Inácio Dias (伊纳西奥·迪亚斯)**, **Tirol Hills (蒂罗尔山坡)**. They'll use police ("earthworks / 土方活儿") to soften targets, then move in with a "legit" security firm (**Matilha / 马蒂利亚**) to extract cheap property sales. **Jerusa (杰鲁萨)** advises them; **Melonhead / 瓜头** (arms trafficker) is back in the West Zone.

**Berenice (贝雷尼ス)**, now elected City Councilor, launches her **mobile office (流动办公室)** in Freguesia — neutral ground where refugees from Curió/Bradock's war settled, and where most of her electors live. She faces party pressure to support **Israel (伊斯拉埃尔)**'s housing regularization project (which would benefit militia land-grabbing). Berenice resists but is warned: oppose it and explain to voters why you blocked housing for 200 families.

**Cinthia (辛西娅)** and **Delano (德拉诺)** (her son) visit a lawyer (Rejane) to fight Delano's case. Delano rejects "justice" — he only believes in his own justice now.

**PQD (Cleiton, ex-45th Battalion)** appears — kicked out after being scapegoated. He meets a former colleague; "that battalion's full of rats."

Militia (Matilha) violently evicts residents from **Sítio (锡蒂奥)** at gunpoint, giving them five minutes to leave or pay. "Peace has a price." Residents' belongings thrown out.

**Li'l Foot (小脚仔 / Pézinho)**, Bradock's old friend, returns seeking refuge with Genny. Genny wants him dead ("he was with Bradock"), but **Carrot** intervenes: "Friends are friends, enemies are enemies." Carrot orders Matarrindo to take Li'l Foot into his squad. They arm up — Mineira's militia is coming.

Final scenes: Berenice's mobile office operates in the community; residents report Matilha has brought calm ("more like forced calm"). Berenice discovers **Jerusa** is the contact on Matilha's pamphlets — the lawyer is working with the militia front. Buscapé spots Delano in a car with Jerusa.

# S02E03 Summary — City of God: The Fight Rages On

## Episode Plot

**Berenice's political fight takes center stage.** As a newly elected city councilor, she runs a "mobile office" in the community, listening to residents' complaints about militia-backed evictions and shoddy construction. At a City Council session, she delivers a blistering speech exposing a "regularization" bill as a land-grab scheme benefiting big developers (backed by Israel Cavani/Reginaldo and the militia). Her testimony swings the vote: what started 22–19 in favor ends 23–24 against. Reginaldo orders her assassination.

**Leka revives the funk parties.** Struggling to find sponsors, she discovers MC Chiado (a shy but talented young rapper) via Cerol. The party at Owl's Bar is a hit, but Leka clashes with her girlfriend Genny over money and loyalty. A barter deal with Danone's Hair Lounge (haircuts for promotion) gets the ball rolling.

**Delano's betrayal surfaces.** Cinthia learns her son Delano is working for **Mineira** (a militia faction). She cuts ties with him, devastated.

**Militia evictions turn violent.** "Bull" (Touro) and plainclothes militia men demolish homes in Sítio under the guise of a "security firm." Residents call Jerusa for help; she watches with unsettling calm. Zilda escapes and alerts her.

**Trafficker civil war brews.** Geninho (Curió's heir) confronts **Pézinho** ("Little Foot"), blaming Jerusa for poisoning Bradock against them. Geninho reveals **Cenoura** (Carrot/Sandro, returning from the film) backs their plan to wait and strike back. They vow to reunite the "family" and eliminate Jerusa's faction.

**Cliffhanger:** Gunmen open fire on Berenice's community celebration in the park. Screams, chaos, "Run!" — fate of Berenice, Cidão, and attendees unknown.
