## S05E05

Episode plot
Jean White and Judith Lloyd James visit the collector John Marsden at his home to browse his "phantasmagoria" collection. Marsden shows them Chinese fingernail guards, daggers once owned by the swordswoman and opera singer Julie de Bernieres, and a 19th-century Tsar-era Art Nouveau personal seal bearing the initials JLJ — Judith Lloyd James. Moments after the gifts are exchanged, two gunshots ring out. Marsden's two bodyguards, Greg Salvine and Aris Tobert, have been shot outside the house; Greg dies, and Aris is rushed to hospital with a superficial wound.

Meanwhile, Jean's son Dom is being questioned by police for punching Marco, Claudie's estranged partner, who threatened Claudie and pushed her to the ground. Jean pressures Dom to strike a deal with Marco, then secretly records Marco admitting he pushed the pregnant Claudie, using the video to blackmail him into dropping the charges.

The shooting investigation turns on Paulette Salvine, Greg's four-months-pregnant widow. Inspector Caron interviews Aris, who admits to an affair with Paulette and describes Greg as violent and controlling. Paulette eventually confesses: she followed Greg's delivery driver to a lay-by, hid in the back of his van, cut Marsden's CCTV cables at the property, and shot both men — killing Greg and only wounding Aris. She says Greg isolated and abused her, assumed the baby was his, and that she saw no other way out. Aris walked away from their affair, which she cites as her motive. Paulette is arrested for Greg's murder and the attempted murder of Aris.

Important character actions
- Jean: investigates alongside Caron, consoles Paulette, records Marco's confession on video and uses it to force him to drop the case against Dom, and remains drawn to John Marsden despite Caron's repeated warnings that he is dangerous.
- Dom: admits punching Marco; is pushed by Jean and Geraldine to publicly apologize; avoids prosecution after the blackmail.
- Judith: attends the visit to Marsden's; receives the JLJ seal; later helps run the pub lunch buffet with Jean.
- Caron: leads the shooting investigation, interviews Aris and Paulette, and secures Paulette's arrest.
- Paulette: confesses to the shooting, reveals Greg's abuse, her affair with Aris, and that the baby's paternity is uncertain.
- Aris: admits the affair; survives his wound but is initially treated as the prime suspect.
- John Marsden: uncooperative with police, claims every criminal up to the Basque region has a motive against him, and continues to cultivate Jean.

Newly established names/terms
- John Marsden — antique/death-memorabilia collector with a "phantasmagoria room" and criminal connections.
- Greg Salvine — Marsden bodyguard, murdered; married to Paulette, expecting a son.
- Aris Tobert — Marsden bodyguard, wounded but survives; Paulette's former lover.
- Paulette Salvine — Greg's widow; confesses to the shooting; arrested.
- Julie de Bernieres — historical swordswoman and opera singer; her daggers are in Marsden's collection.
- Dr Crippen — murderer whose spectacles are in Marsden's collection.
- JLJ — initials on the seal, standing for Judith Lloyd James.
- Zenith — family nickname for Jean and Dom's unborn grandchild.
- Portaloo — the portable toilet the delivery driver used, key to establishing how Paulette reached Marsden's estate.

Continuity-relevant facts
- Jean first met Marsden when he held a gun to her head; she has since remained involved with him despite the danger.
- Marsden's collection includes Dr Crippen's spectacles and Julie de Bernieres's daggers.
- Claudie is pregnant, engaged, and planning a post-wedding gender reveal party; her great-uncle Patrick is helping with wedding plans.
- Dom is expecting a child with Geraldine; "Zenith" is the unborn grandchild's nickname.
- Inspector Caron and DI Richard are the investigating officers; Luca is Marsden's usual delivery driver.

Jokes/references useful for later episodes

- Jean compares her relief to "Steps reuniting."
- Dom tells Jean their life together is "better than Dynasty."

- "Swap Shop" (BBC show) is referenced when Jean recalls playing it with Jeremy.
- The gender reveal party and wedding car double-booking set up upcoming wedding events.

Unresolved threads

- Paulette's legal fate and Aris's position with Marsden remain open.
- Jean's continuing entanglement with the dangerous Marsden, against Caron's warnings, is an ongoing risk.
- Claudie and Dom's wedding and the planned gender reveal party are upcoming events not yet resolved.
