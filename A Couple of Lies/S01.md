# A Couple of Lies — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Two affluent, marriage-strained couples — **Carl & Helene** (Stockholm) and **Adam & Scarlett** (Sicily) — begin a premium home swap via "House Swap." Each hopes the change of scenery will repair their relationship. Instead, both discover the other's home doesn't match its listing, triggering parallel spirals of suspicion, surveillance, and gaslighting.

**Stockholm archipelago (Adam & Scarlett's POV):**  
Adam and Scarlett arrive at Carl & Helene's modern island home. They meet nosy neighbors **Silvia Regner** (西尔维娅·雷格纳) and her retired ex-investigator husband **Bernt** (贝恩特). Silvia claims Helene borrowed a scarf — a pretext to enter the house. Scarlett discovers **Helene's dress** in the closet. Police arrive asking questions. Helene (alone in Sicily) hears unexplained sounds in the Stockholm house that no one else hears.

**Sicily (Carl & Helene's POV):**  
Carl and Helene settle into Adam & Scarlett's villa — nicer than photos. Carl finds **lip stains on glasses**, photographs them to send to the owners. He receives a crisis call from business partner **Ragnar** (拉格纳): a damaging video has leaked; hotel seller **Rosita Perotti** (罗西塔·佩罗蒂) has seen it and gotten cold feet; rival buyer **Henry Johnston** (亨利·约翰斯顿) is negotiating at 1:00 PM. Carl admits to Helene he let **Tom** stay in their Stockholm house before departure; Tom "must have brought someone" — explaining the lip stains. Carl suffers a **memory blackout episode** ("It happened again… total blank") linked to a prior incident "at the office." He promised Helene to stop working and prioritize her and their **unborn baby**.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
1. **Carl's condition**: Recurring memory blackouts ("It happened again… total blank"); triggered by stress; Ragnar knows and uses it as leverage.
2. **"What happened at the office"**: Prior incident that forced Carl to promise Helene he'd stop overworking; humiliating for Helene.
3. **Company crisis**: Carl's company survives only if Rosita Perotti deal closes; Ragnar says "we need this to survive."
4. **Pregnancy**: Helene is pregnant ("last holiday where it's just the two of us"); baby is a major pressure point.
5. **Home-swap symmetry**: Both couples simultaneously occupy each other's homes — enabling parallel surveillance.
6. **Locked walk-in closet**: Established in series background as recurring mystery; not yet opened in E01.
7. **Police visit**: Stockholm police question Adam & Scarlett at Carl & Helene's house — reason unknown.
