## S01E08

# Episode Plot

King Walton III of the Western Kingdom dies after confessing to his brother Heinrey that he took Krista from Heinrey in their youth and asking Heinrey to preserve the alliance with the queen's family (Duke Zemensia's Council of Elders), possibly by wedding Krista. Heinrey ascends the throne. The Eastern Empire sends condolences and begins selecting delegates for Heinrey's coronation; Sovieshu asks Navier if she will attend Rashta's pregnancy celebration, and she declines.

Navier's household discovers Viscount Lotteshu, Rashta's former master, has been visiting and extorting Rashta. Navier sends her brother Kosair to investigate. Kosair confronts Lotteshu at a pawn shop over the Crimson Ring — an imperial healing heirloom Sovieshu gave Rashta — and learns Lotteshu knows Rashta's deepest secret: she already has a newborn child (about five to six months old) fathered by Lotteshu's son Alan Rimwell. Kosair captures and tortures Lotteshu for more.

In the Western Kingdom, Heinrey debates marrying Krista with McKenna, who pledges loyalty "as long as it's not Navier," and Heinrey privately admits he misses Navier terribly.

Navier's maid Eliza reveals that the previous empress made all of Sovieshu's mistresses infertile via cursed infertility cookies (no antidote; causes miscarriage if taken while pregnant), and that Navier herself is the only one affected. Royal law review confirms no child born to a mistress can be imperial heir — only the Empress's child can inherit.

Artina returns injured and tells Navier that Kosair is in peril because Lotteshu exposed Rashta's secret child. Navier resolves to tell Sovieshu the truth to save Kosair. At dinner, Sovieshu declares he will divorce Navier so Rashta can become empress. Navier confronts him with knowledge of Kosair's confinement and the infertility scheme, declaring she will protect her unborn child at all costs. Sovieshu banishes Kosair, and Navier decides she will personally attend the Western Kingdom coronation as the Eastern Empire's delegate.

# Important Character Actions

- **Walton III**: On his deathbed, apologizes to Heinrey for taking Krista, urges him to keep the queen's family alliance, then dies.
- **Heinrey**: Ascends as King of the Western Kingdom; considers wedding Krista for political stability but remains emotionally tied to Navier.
- **Sovieshu**: Refuses to lift Kosair's banishment; plans to divorce Navier and install Rashta as empress; hosts a tense dinner with Navier.
- **Navier**: Declines Rashta's baby celebration; dispatches Kosair to investigate Lotteshu; learns of her infertility and Rashta's secret child; chooses to reveal the truth to Sovieshu to protect Kosair; decides to attend the coronation herself.
- **Kosair**: Confronts Lotteshu at the pawn shop, recovers the Crimson Ring, captures and tortures Lotteshu; is later confined by Sovieshu and banished.
- **Lotteshu**: Extorts Rashta over her slave past and secret child; reveals the child's existence to Kosair; is captured.
- **Eliza**: Reveals the infertility cookie conspiracy to Sovieshu, having kept it secret due to a promise to the previous empress.
- **McKenna**: Swears loyalty to Heinrey with the condition that Heinrey not marry Navier.

# Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Crimson Ring / Red Flame Ring (红焰之戒)**: Imperial healing heirloom; Sovieshu gave it to Rashta; Rashta allegedly sold it at a pawn shop.
- **Viscount Lotteshu**: Rashta's former master and ongoing blackmailer; father of Alan Rimwell.
- **Alan Rimwell**: Lotteshu's son; Rashta's former forced lover; father of her secret newborn.
- **Krista**: Walton III's queen/consort; Heinrey's former love interest; potential political bride for Heinrey.
- **Walton III**: Late King of the Western Kingdom; Heinrey's older brother.
- **Duke Zemensia / Council of Elders**: Controls the Western Kingdom's elder council and shared power with the late king.
- **Baltin Duchy**: Preparing a coronation delegation including wealthy merchants seeking cheap Western minerals.
- **Infertility cookies (不孕饼干)**: Previous empress's cursed cookies that made all imperial mistresses infertile, with no antidote.
- **Charles' farmhouse**: Location where Kosair was held before Navier's intervention.

# Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Navier is infertile due to the previous empress's cookies; Rashta's pregnancy is therefore politically and magically suspect.
- Rashta has a hidden newborn (approx. 5–6 months old) with Alan Rimwell, unknown to the public and potentially to Sovieshu.
- Under Eastern Empire royal law, only the Empress's child can inherit the throne; a mistress's child has no claim.
- Kosair is Navier's biological brother and a general; his banishment removes a key protector from court.
- Heinrey is now King of the Western Kingdom and will hold a coronation attended by foreign delegates.
- McKenna explicitly rules out supporting a Heinrey–Navier marriage.
- Sovieshu intends to divorce Navier and crown Rashta; Navier intends to resist.

# Jokes or References Useful for Later Episodes

- Walton III's deathbed vow never to ride in a carriage until the harbor is reclaimed from the Eastern Empire — a recurring nationalist talking point.
- Kosair is known as "the iron-blooded" (铁血城主), establishing his fearsome reputation.
- McKenna's deadpan line about being "chased off with you" if Heinrey opposes everyone — establishes his unconditional but conditional loyalty.
- The infertility cookie revelation frames Rashta's pregnancy as a potential fraud or magical workaround, a likely future flashpoint.

# Unresolved Threads

- Whether Navier is actually pregnant given her established infertility, or whether her "unborn child" claim is a bluff to pressure Sovieshu.
- The fate and legitimacy of Rashta's secret child with Alan Rimwell.
- Whether Sovieshu will actually divorce Navier, and how the Troby family will react.
- Kosair's fate after banishment.
- Heinrey's coronation and whether Navier's attendance will alter Western–Eastern relations.
- Duke Zemensia and the Council of Elders' demands on the new king; the Baltin mineral trade negotiations.
- Whether Rashta's slave past or her secret child will be exposed publicly, and what Sovieshu will do if he learns the full truth.

## S01E07

# S01E07 — The Remarried Empress

## Episode Plot

Navier’s brother, General Kosair (Koshar), returns to the capital after defeating the Springwater Bandits and is awarded a medal by Emperor Sovieshu. At the victory banquet, Kosair publicly reminds Sovieshu of his oath to cherish and protect Navier, which the emperor has clearly broken. Meanwhile, Rashta reveals her pregnancy, and Sovieshu dotes on her. At the same banquet, Heinrey’s cousin McKenna is introduced with an absurdly long title and the nickname “Leopard Cat,” and he and Kosair engage in a comic push-up contest.

Heinrey tells Navier he must return to the Western Kingdom because his father, King Walton III, is critically ill. Rashta uses the occasion to weaponize her pregnancy: she tells Navier that Lady Albani has spread a rumor that Navier is infertile and will likely become the adoptive mother of Rashta’s child. When Kosair furiously defends Navier and threatens Rashta, Rashta stages a collapse, claims Kosair pushed her, and raises a miscarriage scare. Sovieshu banishes Kosair from the palace pending an investigation, refuses Navier’s defense of him, and accuses Navier of cold hostility toward Rashta’s unborn child. Rashta then openly threatens Navier: if anything happens to her child, neither Kosair nor Navier will be forgiven.

The episode ends with Heinrey’s departure for the Western Kingdom. Navier declines to see him off publicly. They share a private farewell over their secret letter correspondence (“my queen”), and a confidential exchange about an infertility potion that can make a man’s “seed dry up” or leave a woman infertile for life — a secret to be kept “until it is all over.”

## Important Character Actions

- **Kosair/Koshar**: Returns as undefeated Commander of the North; submits victory report; scowls at Sovieshu; publicly demands the emperor honor his oath; threatens to rip Rashta’s tongue out; is banished from the palace and dragged away by knights.
- **Navier**: Earnestly defends Kosair to Sovieshu; refuses to bid Heinrey farewell publicly but shares an intimate private parting; is wounded by rumors that she is infertile and will adopt Rashta’s child.
- **Sovieshu**: Awards Kosair the medal but turns on him over the oath; sides entirely with Rashta; orders Kosair’s banishment; scolds Navier; claims his leniency toward her is “consideration.”
- **Rashta**: Announces pregnancy; cites apple pie (the emperor’s favorite food) as the only thing she can eat during morning sickness; spreads/implies the infertility rumor about Navier via Lady Albani; fakes or exaggerates being pushed and a stomach ache; threatens Navier over her unborn child.
- **Heinrey**: Tells Navier of King Walton III’s critical condition; prepares to return to the Western Kingdom; says his friendship has healed Navier’s wounds; asks if she loves her husband and suggests taking a lover for revenge (she declines).
- **McKenna**: Introduced as Heinrey’s cousin and First Vanguard/Storm Assault Captain; blue bird shifter; the butt of a running pronunciation gag (“McKenna” vs. “MenKenna”).
- **Lady Albani**: Named as the source of the infertility rumor told to Rashta.
- **Eliza**: Navier’s lady-in-waiting; tasked with ensuring Heinrey’s safe return; present at the banquet.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Kosair / Koshar (克沙勒)** — Navier’s brother, Commander of the North, undefeated general, “General Kosair.”
- **Springwater Bandits (常时泉)** — bandit group Kosair defeated; he established a village for surrendered enemies.
- **McKenna (麦肯纳)** — Heinrey’s cousin, chief aide, blue bird shifter; also ridiculed as “Leopard Cat” / “lonely, spotted, colorful scarlet leopard cat of the Himalayan mountains.”
- **Lady Albani (晏伯溟夫人)** — source of the rumor that Navier is infertile.
- **Ian (安)** — the name in a letter enclosing a lock of hair, allegedly Rashta’s unborn son.
- **King Walton III (威登三世)** — Western Kingdom king, in critical condition.
- **Infertility potion** — folk remedy: makes a man’s seed dry up; for a woman, her “field” becomes unfruitful; sterility for life.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Rashta’s baby’s paternity is questionable: she previously had a lover (Alan Rimwell) and a child; the letter names the current fetus “Ian,” and a lock of hair is enclosed as “proof.”
- Rashta’s past (slave origin, former lover, prior child) is held over her by the Roteschus/Alan Rimwell; she is insecure and desperate to protect this pregnancy.
- Kosair is now banned from the palace; his investigation/punishment is pending; Navier’s standing with Sovieshu has fallen sharply.
- Navier and Sovieshu’s marriage is effectively over in practice, though not yet legally dissolved; she has not yet exercised her remarriage right with Heinrey at this point in the file.
- Heinrey’s succession to the Western throne is considered secure, but King Walton III’s death will require his return and final words.
- Navier and Heinrey maintain secret correspondence; she calls him implicitly “my queen” in his letters to her — a notable intimacy.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- McKenna’s name pronunciation gag (“Mc” vs. “Men”) and his overblown “scarlet leopard cat” title — comic relief that also establishes his loud, earnest personality.
- “Navier’s first time calling anyone a friend” — McKenna’s wager at the drinking toast, marking her emotional opening toward the Western court.
- The push-up contest between Kosair and McKenna — establishes physical rivalry/camaraderie between North and West.
- Apple pie as the emperor’s favorite food — Rashta uses it to manipulate Sovieshu’s sympathy during morning sickness.
- “My queen” letters — the private channel between Navier and Heinrey that will matter once she remarries.
- The infertility potion — a Chekhov’s gun for future sabotage of either Rashta’s or Navier’s fertility claims.

## Unresolved Threads

- Whether Rashta’s unborn child “Ian” is actually Sovieshu’s or Alan Rimwell’s.
- Outcome of Kosair’s banishment and the assault investigation; whether witnesses (Eliza) will recant or confirm.
- Whether the infertility rumor about Navier is true, and who benefits from it (Lady Albani, Rashta, or others).
- King Walton III’s fate and the timing of Heinrey’s ascension/return.
- Whether Navier will exercise her remarriage privilege with Heinrey and when the divorce becomes final.
- Who will use the infertility potion and against whom.
- Rashta’s threat against Navier and Kosair — whether she will act on it if she miscarries or loses the child.

## S01E09

S01E09 — The Remarried Empress

### Episode plot
Right after his coronation, King Heinrey awaits word from the Eastern Empire while Navier stays at his side. A secret visitor confirms Sovieshu's plan: divorce Navier and crown Rashta empress before her child is born so the heir is legitimate. Rashta's circle also moves to silence Viscount Lotteshu (Roteschu), keeper of her slave-past secrets — "He has to die" — and to wipe out loose ends tied to her son and the child's father "just as if they never existed in this world."

Sovieshu files for divorce through Marquess Lante on three charges: (1) Navier's brother Koshar pushed pregnant Rashta, attempting to kill the imperial heir; (2) Navier had Koshar imprison and torture Lotteshu to extract false statements about Rashta's secrets; (3) she showed no remorse and was openly hostile toward the unborn child. Lante urges caution, but Sovieshu insists everything finish before the birth.

At the coronation celebration, Navier represents the Eastern Empire, publicly backing Heinrey and the new trade agreements. The crowd chants "Long live King Heinrey!" as the Western Kingdom reconstitutes itself as an empire under the "winds of change" and the ideal that all people are born equal. That night Heinrey proposes — "A king needs a queen" — and Navier accepts.

Five days later at the divorce trial, Sovieshu's counsel is Marquess Lante; Navier appoints no counsel and does not contest the divorce. She then shocks the court by requesting approval of her remarriage — to King Heinrey, who steps forward to ask for her hand. The magistrate grants the divorce and the remarriage in the same session; Navier ceases to be Empress of the Eastern Empire. Sovieshu's objection ("I forbid this") is overruled.

Bookending the episode: Rashta has lost Navier's Crimson Ring (her maid lost it) and is feeling her unborn child kick with Sovieshu; meanwhile Navier signs her bequests, giving all her assets to her loyal household in a tearful farewell.

### Important character actions
- Navier: calmly consents to divorce in court, immediately invokes her remarriage privilege, accepts Heinrey's proposal, signs away all her assets to her staff, and coldly rejects Sovieshu's apology as "empty."
- Sovieshu: rushes the divorce before Rashta's baby is born, presents Lante's charges, convenes an emergency council to make the divorce public, and futilely forbids the remarriage.
- Heinrey: crowned king, delivers the empire-building coronation speech, proposes to Navier and is accepted.
- Rashta: learns of the divorce and coronation plan, demands Lotteshu be eliminated, lost the Crimson Ring, and appears pregnant and affectionate with Sovieshu.
- Marquess Lante: presents the divorce application and evidence against Navier as Sovieshu's counsel.
- Court Magistrate: approves both the divorce and Navier's remarriage.

### Newly established names/terms
- Crimson Ring (红焰之戒): Navier's ring, lost via Rashta's maid; previously said to be held by Koshar/Kosair under tight imperial-knight guard.
- Marquess Lante: Sovieshu's divorce prosecutor.
- Duke Riverty (Libert Empire), Duke Zemensia (Western Council of Elders), Krista (late king's widow), King Walton III (Heinrey's late brother).
- "Winds of change": coronation slogan; Western Kingdom becomes an empire; the equality ideal.

### Continuity-relevant facts
- Rashta is pregnant with Sovieshu's child; legitimacy via birth in wedlock drives the rushed timeline.
- Koshar (Navier's brother, Troby heir) is accused of pushing pregnant Rashta and is under tight guard by imperial knights.
- Navier finalizes budgets for schools, hospitals, orphanages, and war widows' pensions before leaving court.
- Navier uses the same-court-session remarriage prerogative to turn her divorce into an immediate remarriage to a foreign king.
- Heinrey is now King of the West, moving toward emperorship; Western royal bloodline includes Bird Shifter heritage per series context.

### Jokes or references useful for later episodes
- Rashta's helpless third-person mask ("Goodness, you're adorable") versus her bloodthirsty planning ("He has to die… wipe them out") — the gap is her fatal flaw if exposed.
- Young Navier's childhood prayer ("light… even the coldest and darkest places," "strongest pillars," "ever-loving heart") flashed back at the wedding, contrasted by an elder's lament: "And… now a divorce. I feel that maybe I let them down."
- "I will not wear the crown" — Navier relinquishing the Eastern crown.
- Sovieshu's kindness over the lost ring and the baby-kick scene show his full investment in Rashta and the unborn heir.

### Unresolved threads
- Sovieshu's refusal to accept the remarriage — likely Eastern/Western rupture or conflict.
- Rashta's coronation as empress and the birth of Sovieshu's heir; legitimacy challenges ahead.
- The Crimson Ring's true location; Koshar's fate and the torture/false-statement charge against Lotteshu.
- Lotteshu remains locked up ("in the outhouse"); Rashta wants him dead.
- Rashta's son by Alan Rimwell and the "child's father" elimination thread.
- Navier–Heinrey wedding timing and her status (ex-Empress of the East, Queen of the West).
- Whether the Eastern Empire accepts the remarriage or treats it as treason; the wider magic-decline crisis remains untouched.

## S01E10

The Remarried Empress — S01E10 Summary

Episode plot
The divorce hearing ends with Navier publicly agreeing to end her marriage to Sovieshu, then immediately invoking her imperial privilege to remarry — accepting King Heinrey of the Western Kingdom and becoming the first remarried empress in Eastern Empire history. Sovieshu furiously forbids the remarriage, has Heinrey thrown in prison, orders palace windows closed and all message-carrying birds shot down, and places Navier under confinement. McKenna arranges an escape through a secret palace passageway; Heinrey breaks out and spirits Navier out on the night of the full moon, while her banished brother General Kosair (Koshar) awaits in the West. Sovieshu demands armor to lead the pursuit himself, but ministers restrain him, warning an armored emperor would look like a declaration of war. Rashta is crowned Empress — "Rashta Ishka Botulin" — and her first order is to erase every trace of Navier, with the search concentrated at the Blood Ridge route to the Western Kingdom. Navier and Heinrey cross into the Western Kingdom, where loyalists swear allegiance to her as queen. A post-credits scene shows Heinrey confessing to McKenna that he still has not told Navier his bird-shifter secret identity ("Queen").

Important character actions
- Navier: accepts divorce, immediately remarries Heinrey, rejects Sovieshu's ultimatum to stay in exchange for freeing Heinrey, declares her heart no longer belongs to him, and escapes via the secret passage.
- Sovieshu: bans the remarriage, imprisons Heinrey, orders archers to shoot message birds, demands armor for a personal pursuit, and ends the episode vowing to ruin everything Navier loves.
- Heinrey: proposes and remarries Navier, breaks out of prison with McKenna's help, escorts Navier to the Western Kingdom, and keeps his identity as the golden bird "Queen" hidden from her.
- Rashta: realizes she was only a one-year placeholder empress, allies with Ergi, demands King Heinrey be killed, is crowned Empress, and orders Navier erased from the palace.
- Ergi: coaches Rashta, frames the removal of "tiresome obstacles," and backs her coronation.
- McKenna: engineers the secret-passage escape and pressures Heinrey to confess the bird-shifter secret to Navier before it blows up their marriage.
- Marquess Lante: urges trust in the pursuit party and talks Sovieshu out of appearing in armor before the public.

Newly established names/terms
- Rashta Ishka Botulin — Rashta's full name as crowned Empress.
- "Queen" — Navier's pet name for Heinrey's golden bird form; his shifter identity remains unrevealed to her.
- Blood Ridge (血之棱线) — the border route to the Western Kingdom where Eastern forces are massed.
- Springwater Bandits (常时泉) — northern bandits crushed by Kosair before the episode.

Continuity-relevant facts
- Navier and Heinrey were secret pen pals who exchanged love letters by bird before the remarriage; Rashta impersonated the pen pal to manipulate both of them.
- Koshar/Kosair, Navier's brother and former general, was stripped of rank and permanently banished for allegedly threatening the Emperor's unborn child; he is now in the Western Kingdom.
- Heinrey has ascended to King of the Western Kingdom; core royals are Bird Shifters (Heinrey: golden bird; McKenna: blue bird).
- Sovieshu's plan was always to make Rashta empress for one year, establish her child as heir, then bring Navier back — Rashta learns this and turns on him.
- No Eastern Empire empress had ever remarried before Navier; the divorce court's legality is settled ("the judge went bang, bang, bang").

Jokes or references useful for later episodes
- McKenna's comic teasing: Heinrey should confess "on your knees, or do a handstand," and "Are you going to kneel to her naked?" — tone relief amid the escape.
- Rashta calling Sovieshu "老公" (old man/honey) while openly plotting his downfall — ironic pet name.
- Sovieshu's line "Wouldn't you say our meeting was fate?" — his recurring fate/obsession motif.
- The "Queen" secret: if Navier learns Heinrey is the bird she corresponded with and loved by letter, McKenna warns she might break the marriage — a clear fuse for later episodes.

Unresolved threads
- Navier still does not know Heinrey is "Queen" / a Bird Shifter; the secret threatens the new marriage.
- Rashta, now Empress, is hunting Navier and has ordered all traces of her erased; the Border Ridge dragnet is active.
- Sovieshu has vowed vengeance ("Everything you love will be turned to ruins") and may still pursue her personally, risking East–West war if Heinrey dies in an Eastern prison.
- Northern troop deployment and the broader Magical Decline/mana-loss crisis remain unresolved world arcs.
- Kaufman's love-potion subplot and Ergi's long game against Rashta are still dangling. (≧▽≦)
