## S09E06

## Episode Plot

A retrospective clip-show built around a framing device: Sally Lindsay's cold open totes up the family's lifetime stats (40,000 bedtime stories, 53,500 sausages, 20,000 tins of baked beans, 30,000 toilet rolls, 100,000 nappies, 41,016 fibs) and announces that Noel and Sue will review the 22 things raising Britain's biggest family has taught them. The episode walks through 19 lessons before saving the "top three" for the finale: never planning the 22 kids; sibling fall-outs; birthday parties; weaning Heidie; the kids running the weekly shop; discipline; Luke coming out; grandkids; losing children on days out; driving lessons; the birds-and-bees; smelly boys; chore games; "Mum knows best"; one-on-one time with Josh; holiday arguments; and facing your fears. The closing third covers the 2014 loss of Alfie and ends with Noel telling the children he is proud of every one of them, followed by a house-wide game of hide and seek.

## Important Character Actions

- Sue: tries the pickle and tape weaning tricks on Heidie, then throws a "big girl" party with a "No more booby ever" cake, finally ending her breastfeeding years. Buys a camper van without consulting Noel. Aborts Katie's driving lesson in panic.
- Noel: takes Josh on a father-son kayak trip to pull him away from a bad crowd; conquers his fear of heights on the "leap of faith" despite terror; falls off an unsteadied ladder while painting, horrifying Sue ("single mum... with all of the kids").
- Luke: reveals he knew he was bisexual at 14 and hid it for nearly six years; reconciles with Chloe at Christmas after their 18-month fall-out.
- Chloe: hosts the 25th birthday party where the older kids wreck the brand-new hot tub; the group pays for it.
- Archie: confirmed by the whole family as the naughtiest Radford; mid-meltdown antics throughout.
- Max: stays home alone during a family holiday after playing Noel and Sue off against each other.
- Hallie and Phoebe: vanish at Alton Towers, turning up safe on the grass verge.
- Katie and Luke: both fail their parents' driving lessons and agree a professional instructor is needed.
- Flashback (2012): Sophie gives birth to Daisy, the first grandchild, while Sue is pregnant with Casper.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- "Mum and Dad Driving Academy" — the family's in-house driving school; both learners "fail their probation" (爸妈驾校).
- "The leap of faith" — team-building activity: wobbly ladder, platform, high-five a hanging ball (信仰之跃).
- "Haymaker challenge" — Oscar's Florida mega-burger challenge, which he loses (超级海麦克汉堡挑战).
- "Boys v girls" chore board; the odd-sock hunt (prize: an ice lolly); the dishwasher game.
- The pickle/tape weaning tricks; the "big girl" weaning party.
- The running stat gags: 40,000 bedtime stories / 41,016 fibs.
- Grandchild-title confusion: nana vs granny vs uncle/auntie.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The family now owns a camper van (Sue's impulse purchase); Max hates camper-van holidays and opted to stay home.
- Luke is bisexual and out; the first Lancaster Pride was 2017; the family is fully supportive, and Luke's story prompted messages from others.
- Chloe and Luke's 18-month fall-out is resolved; Chloe is now 25; Ellie has turned 18.
- The hot tub from Chloe's party was wrecked and paid for by the older kids; Noel and Daniel have sworn off parties.
- Alfie Thomas, the 17th child, was stillborn in 2014; he was named and is never to be forgotten — the family's hardest moment.
- Heidie is fully weaned, ending Sue's breastfeeding era for good.
- Noel has a serious fear of heights (Blackpool Tower glass platform, balconies, multi-storey car parks).
- Privacy rituals: the landing door squeak is deliberately unoiled, windows get shut, and Jack's 11pm tooth-brushing is a running intrusion gag.
- Josh had a school/wrong-crowd patch about two years prior, fixed by the kayak trip.
- By the time Heidie is 18, Noel and Sue will have cooked nearly half a million meals.
