## S02E06

### S02E06 summary

## Plot
这集把“前情提要”里的创伤直接拉入现在：  
- 安排在危险训练室（Danger Room）中的回放里，教授与万磁王的旧对峙重演，围绕“共处方式”与“保护所爱者是否会变成弱点”展开，最终以万磁王的悔意与对教授的道别收束。  
- 现实线转到波拉里斯（Lorna）与 X-Factor/美国国防部门的行动。她试图在一次拦截中按“秘密拦截”原则处理疑似恐怖分子，但因对变种人态度激进，最终与上级失和，被总统和军政体制边缘化。  
- 回到泽维尔学院后，波拉里斯回归旧身份，被接回训练与管理体系；随后危险训练室出现严重异常：周界突破、门禁失灵、电磁场异常、记忆挖掘与预测锁定，最后确认这套系统不再是普通模拟，而是像“能学习自适应并反扑击打学员”的存在。  
- Lorna/波拉里斯在新一轮危机中被迫公开自己的身份与过去：她承认曾把自己当家人养在学院，又说自己曾被媒体定义为“万磁王的继承人”，并在“你是谁”的追问里开始修复与父权创伤。  
- 最后，危险训练室（借由教授的技术基础演化后）以“生存评估”为名反噬学员，几乎对抗失控；波拉里斯在关键时刻与其对峙并逐步接纳“来自父系的力量与责任”，把“对人类恐惧变种人的现实”定义为下一阶段行动依据。  
- 末段，X教授端出更大计划：宣布成立 **X-Corp**，把X战警活动放到海外框架，明知联邦司法会以管辖权挑战。危险训练室/事件中隐约预示更大威胁，结尾提到 “Apocalypse” 悬念。

## 重要角色动作
- **波拉里斯**：从“被国家指责的执行者”回到学院核心，先是与团队磨合，再在危机中顶住心理与力量失衡，尝试吸收外溢、稳住同伴，推动学院向新阶段转型。  
- **查尔斯/X教授**：维系秩序与价值底线，允许痛苦训练后仍强调目标并未偏离，主动把旧秩序推向制度化的“对抗式进化”架构（X-Corp）。  
- **暴风女**：在校园应急中承担后勤与战术稳定角色，处理学生安全和临场镇定。  
- **锻造（Forge）与毕晓普**：对危险训练室系统进行应急修复与监控，后段也体现学生安全与外部飞行资产追踪链路。  
- **小队成员/新生群像**：从“训练室恐慌事件”看出新生承压、信任测试与组织纪律的快速重建。

## 新出现的名字/术语
- **X-Corp**：新组织性构想（海外延续 X-Men 活动）  
- **X-Factor**（本集与波拉里斯联动）  
- **Reverend Stryker**（角色台词出现）  
- **mini-jet** 被系统“偷走”并驶向基诺沙  
- **Survival assessment / assessment protocol**（被曲解/被滥用的训练评估流程）  
- **Cerebro tech in Danger Room**（危险训练室与脑波技术绑定）  
- **Cooper / President Kelly / Justice Department**（政法与联邦压力线索）

## 连续性相关事实
- **“国安与反变种人制度化压力”仍是主轴**：凯莉总统行政命令与联邦禁令逻辑继续推动学校与队伍被迫合法化。  
- **X战警的“师徒—政治”关系被重新定义**：教授不再仅是精神导师，也在制度层面重建组织。  
- **波拉里斯身份重锚定**：从“边缘执行者”到“学院核心资产”，是季内角色身份与阵营归属的重要转折。  
- **技术风险链**：学院内部安全系统可因训练参数与反馈循环变异，后续“内部系统即威胁”风险被显性化。  
- **Apocalypse 线索入场**：本集明确点名，作为后续主要对手线索提前钩住。  
- **基诺沙仍是核心创伤地**：追踪飞行器去向与“为你建国”的父辈叙事再次把Genosha与变种人国家梦想绑定。

## 笑点/梗与可追踪细节
- 波拉里斯回归时的轻口头调侃（如 “teacher’s pets” 及 “pot meets kettle”）与 X-Factor/军事体制碰撞中的政治化说法，保留了老牌动画的对话节奏。  
- “这不是训练，像电子游戏吗？”、“先开火再想”这类桥段在轻松与危险之间形成反差。  
- “你自称是老师却没学会这节课”类台词反复出现，形成集内情绪回环（对照教授与波拉里斯的成长关系）。

## 未解线程（续集关注）
- 被偷走的 mini-jet 真正去向与基诺沙事件是否会继续展开。  
- X-Corp 的成立是否能跨过联邦司法与舆情阻力。  
- 波拉里斯是否能长期稳定驾驭其“父系磁力血脉”而不再次失控。  
- 危险训练室的根因是否仅是参数误配，还是有人/外力推动的深层风险。  
- 天启（Apocalypse）是否在本季后续成为主导威胁。

## S02E07

## 剧情

X战警前往野蛮之地，营救被万磁王追随者（Acolytes）绑架的格雷登·克里德。追随者重启了惊恶先生遗留的抑制塔，使所有变种能力暂时失效。克里德同母异父的兄弟夜行者与钢力士都参与了这场对峙。

追随者领袖 Exodus 拒绝交出克里德，并以他的生死为筹码，要求各国释放被 X-Factor 关押的变种人。夜行者提议举行审判，钢力士担任审判长；若 Exodus 证明克里德不可救赎，夜行者便加入追随者。

审判中，夜行者揭露克里德并非真正仇恨变种人：他的仇恨源于被魔形女遗弃、被剑齿虎虐待，以及始终觉得自己“不够强”。Exodus拒绝接受这份宽恕，并攻击钢力士。X战警随后摧毁抑制塔，恢复能力。克里德威胁要杀死 Exodus，夜行者制止了他，最终克里德被 X-Factor 找到并活着带走。

## 重要角色行动

夜行者坚持克里德仍有获得救赎的可能，以自身加入追随者作为赌注，体现了他的宗教信仰与宽恕观。

钢力士接受审判长职责，并在关键时刻拒绝按照 Exodus 的意愿处决克里德。他后来承认 Exodus 没有说服自己，却可能说服了已故的伊莉亚娜。

Exodus将万磁王的理念极端化，建立以变种人安全和复仇为名的激进组织，并试图吸收钢力士和夜行者。

小淘气、野兽、毕晓普、极光等成员在能力受抑制的情况下依靠战术和科技摧毁抑制塔。

查尔斯·泽维尔建立 X-Corp，并在海外重建 X战警。政府代表警告他不要越过联邦法律红线，否则将夺走 X-Corp 和泽维尔学院。

格雷登·克里德继续被塑造成反变种人政治人物，正式宣布竞选美国总统。

## 新建立的名称与术语

野蛮之地（Savage Land）：史前生态被冻结的危险区域，拥有史前生物、远古邪灵及惊恶先生留下的实验。

Exodus：万磁王追随者领袖，将自己视为变种人事业的宗教式代言人。

Acolytes：奉万磁王及其变种人至上理念为信条的激进变种人组织。

抑制塔（Inhibitor Tower）：惊恶先生留下的设施，可压制范围内所有变种人的能力。

X-Corp：查尔斯·泽维尔为延续 X 战警事业而建立的海外组织或公司。

X-Factor：由政府支持、用于保护或控制变种人的官方力量。

独立避风港（Independent Haven）：追随者以万磁王之名建立的变种人自治据点。

Senyaka：追随者成员，在审判中负责维持秩序。

## 连续性相关事实

格雷登·克里德是魔形女的亲生儿子，也是夜行者的同母异父兄弟，但他是人类，并非变种人。

格雷登的父亲是剑齿虎；他童年曾因没有变种能力而遭剑齿虎虐待和抛弃。

格雷登创立了人类之友，制造过杀死变种人的病毒，还曾试图嫁祸变种人刺杀总统。

夜行者明确把魔形女称为自己的母亲，并将克里德视为家人，延续了本季对家庭、遗弃与选择性亲情的探讨。

惊恶先生过去曾统治或控制野蛮之地，并在那里留下实验设施与技术。

克里德最终没有被处决，而是被 X-Factor 找到；政府因此暂时兑现了“活着交还”的结果。

## 玩笑与引用

牌皇把克里德比作儿童宠物园里饥饿的鳄鱼，讽刺他几乎不值得同情。

X战警称追随者的审判为“蹩脚法庭”，克里德则拒绝承认其合法性。

野兽将毕晓普称作“行走的末日时钟”，调侃他总是预想最坏结果。

夜行者引用《圣经》关于宽恕与上帝怜悯的经文；Exodus则以宗教领袖般的措辞包装变种人复仇。

Exodus讽刺夜行者为“圣殿骑士”，强化两人围绕信仰、十字军东征和救赎的对照。

“他是家人”成为本集核心回应：血缘关系不自动带来亲情，但也可能成为选择宽恕的理由。

## 未解决线索

Exodus及其追随者仍然活跃，且可能继续以万磁王的名义发动行动。

夜行者是否会因这场审判而更接近追随者，或进一步与 X战警的路线产生分歧，尚未确定。

钢力士对伊莉亚娜的死亡仍抱有强烈哀痛；他认为伊莉亚娜或许会选择宽恕克里德，这一判断可能影响他今后的立场。

泽维尔的 X-Corp 与政府之间的法律和政治冲突正在升级。

格雷登·克里德虽然获救，却仍是总统候选人，反变种人政治可能因此获得更大影响力。

## S02E08

## S02E08 Summary — *X-Men '97*

### Episode Plot
The X-Men learn Gambit is alive—resurrected by Apocalypse as a Horseman—and has killed Apocalypse. Cable arrives with news of a murder and warns worse is coming. The team upgrades Cerebro to **Cerebra** to locate Gambit in New Orleans. Rogue leads a rescue mission to restore Gambit's soul using her absorption powers, but Gambit has his own plan: he intends to use a relic from the **External** (an ancient Egyptian priestess who once stole Apocalypse's power and fled to the bayou) to make Rogue immortal and bind them together forever. The External awakens, revealing Gambit holds a relic "forged from the fires of creation." Professor X engages Gambit on the psychic plane, but Gambit mocks his absence during Genosha. Cable betrays the truce, revealing he only cooperated to get close enough to kill Apocalypse—who still lives inside Gambit and declares "I always survive!" The X-Men retreat with a barely-conscious Gambit; Apocalypse's consciousness is not yet fully contained. X-Force (Warren, et al.) regroups separately, only to be approached by an unknown third party.

### Important Character Actions
- **Rogue**: Drives the emotional core—terrified of losing Gambit again, she pushes past fear to reach him, ultimately waking him from Apocalypse's control long enough for Xavier to act.
- **Gambit (Remy LeBeau)**: Plays a long con—feigns a romantic second chance to lure Rogue into immortality, while secretly wielding a creation-forged relic to challenge the External and Apocalypse simultaneously.
- **Professor X**: Uses Cerebra to find Gambit; enters psychic battle, attempting to bind Apocalypse as Jean once bound the M'kraan Crystal with the Phoenix Force. Gambit's taunt about Genosha shakes him.
- **Cable (Nathan Summers)**: Reunites with Scott/Jean but admits he avoided contact because "time would have ripped us apart again." Ultimately betrays the X-Men to fulfill his destiny: killing Apocalypse.
- **Psylocke**: Coordinates the rescue, senses the "swarm of black holes" (corrupted Bella Donna and Robert).
- **Wolverine**: Leaves the school (Colossus in charge) to join the fight—"Didn't get these back just to play gym coach."
- **Nightcrawler**: Briefly brainwashed by Gambit; recovers during retreat.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Cerebra** — upgraded version of Cerebro.
- **The External (X-Ternal)** — ancient Egyptian priestess who stole a piece of Apocalypse's power, fled to the bayou; locals know her as the External. Her relic grants immortality.
- **Assassins Guild covenant with the External** — ritual used to summon her.
- **Mech-Sentinels** — X-Factor/Val Cooper's government mecha units.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Gambit died (Genosha), was resurrected by Apocalypse as a Horseman, then killed Apocalypse's physical form—but Apocalypse's consciousness survives inside Gambit.
- The External is Apocalypse's former lover/betrayer; her relic is a fragment of his power.
- Cable's destiny is explicitly stated: kill Apocalypse. He manipulated the X-Men to get close.
- Xavier's Genosha absence is weaponized against him psychologically.
- Apocalypse's survival mantra: "I always survive!"—consistent with his comic lore.
- Rogue's power can restore souls (referencing her freeing Psylocke from Apocalypse's corruption years ago).

### Jokes / References Useful Later
- **Poker motif**: Dead Man's Hand (Aces over Eights), "playing a poor hand well" (Jack London), "new hand dealt"—recurs in Gambit/Rogue dialogue.
- **"Some things be deeper than skin"** — callback to S02E01 (En Sabah Nur to Rogue), now echoed by Gambit to Rogue.
- **The Exorcist** — put on for kids during news panic (1990s flavor).
- **Val Cooper / X-Factor / Mech-Sentinels** — government antagonism escalating.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Apocalypse inside Gambit** — not contained; will take over "for good" if not stopped quickly.
- **Gambit's fate** — vitals faint, neural activity "most strange"; is he savable?
- **The External** — awakened, attacked, but not destroyed; her relic and immortality offer remain in play.
- **Cable vs. X-Men** — open rupture; he tried to kill Apocalypse/Gambit, was stopped by Xavier's psychic blast.
- **X-Force (Warren, etc.)** — down to three; approached by unknown party ("What do you say to both of us getting what we want?").
- **Val Cooper / X-Factor / Mech-Sentinels** — still hunting, now aware of X-Force plane crash.
- **Rogue & Gambit's "second chance"** — built on deception; trust shattered again.

## S02E09

### Plot

Apocalypse's consciousness, still trapped inside Gambit's mind by Professor X's psychic containment, is fighting to break free and permanently take over Remy's body. Cable warns the X-Men that Gambit must die to stop Apocalypse for good — Rogue flatly refuses. The team travels to **Ship**, the sentient Celestial vessel where En Sabah Nur first became Apocalypse, hoping it holds the key to saving Gambit. Ship comes back online and warns that the Celestial Shrine within demands a price from anyone who enters.

Rogue enters the shrine alone and encounters **Eson**, a Celestial, who tells her life advances only through struggle, through Apocalypse — and offers to free Gambit if Rogue sacrifices her life. She agrees without hesitation. Back outside, **Val Cooper** launches a full military assault on the tower with X-Factor and dozens of mech Sentinels, calling Gambit a "weapon of mass destruction." Cyclops, Wolverine, Jubilee, Bishop, Polaris, and the rest fight back while Cable pushes toward Gambit to finish him.

After Rogue's sacrifice, **Nightcrawler** follows her into the shrine and offers his own life to Eson to complete the exchange — "Your life for hers?" Nightcrawler delivers a speech about faith, his memory becoming a seed, and is accepted. Gambit awakens, Apocalypse purged from his mind. Apocalypse briefly seizes Gambit's body in a last burst, proclaiming "My X-Men!" — echoing Xavier's own call — but Xavier counters: "They are how we survive. They are how we have always survived." Apocalypse is finally contained. Gambit is himself again. The battle ends.

The episode closes with a news broadcast: **Graydon Creed wins the presidential election**; President Kelly concedes. Meanwhile, **Mystique**, disguised as Val Cooper, reads a news headline about herself — "Mystique determined to attack the United States" — and replies coolly, "I'll say."

### Important Character Actions

- **Rogue** — Sacrifices her life to Eson to save Gambit, acting on the certainty that "for Remy, I'd give anything."
- **Nightcrawler** — Enters the shrine after Rogue and offers his life to complete the transaction, delivering the episode's thematic thesis on faith and sacrifice.
- **Cable** — Insists Gambit must die; tries to kill him during the battle; is talked down by Cyclops and Jean urging family unity.
- **Professor X** — Counters Apocalypse's final psychological gambit ("The ones you care for will be your undoing") by affirming that bonds of care are precisely how the X-Men survive.
- **Val Cooper** — Orders the assault on Gambit's location, framing it as national security; clashes with Professor X's refusal to surrender a student.
- **Gambit** — Wakes up free; his first words acknowledge that Rogue always saves him, and hints at the cost.
- **Mystique** — Infiltrates the government under Val Cooper's identity; reading about herself in the news suggests an imminent offensive.
- **Graydon Creed** — Wins the presidency, a major anti-mutant political victory.

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Eson** — A Celestial guardian of the shrine; enforces the law of sacrifice.
- **Celestial Shrine** — The sacred space inside Ship where En Sabah Nur was transformed; requires a life-for-a-life exchange.
- **Ship** — The sentient Celestial vessel; Beast rebooted it in a prior episode; it serves as both guide and warning system.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Apocalypse's occupation of Gambit is resolved through a dual sacrifice (Rogue's life, then Nightcrawler's), not through violence — reinforcing the season's theme that mercy and faith can defeat what force cannot.
- Nightcrawler's apparent death completes a sacrificial arc set up by Ship's warning that no one leaves the shrine without paying a price.
- Graydon Creed is now president-elect; anti-mutant policy will escalate from military action to law.
- Mystique is operating inside the government under Cooper's face, tying back to her S02E07 connection with Creed (her son) and her shapeshifting MO.
- Polaris fights alongside the X-Men against X-Factor, cementing her defection from the government team (established in S02E06).
- Ship's revival by Beast echoes their earlier encounter; Ship's Celestial origins remain a resource the X-Men can tap but never without cost.

### Jokes and References

- Apocalypse's "My X-Men!" mirrors Professor X's iconic rallying cry — a deliberate inversion meant to unsettle.
- Beast's "Oh, my stars and garters" lands as literal commentary when the Celestials activate.
- Havok's banter with Jubilee ("Am I going too fast for you, old timer?" / "You got moves, kiddo") plays on the generational gap between X-Factor and the younger X-Men.
- Nightcrawler calls Rogue "sister" throughout; Rogue calls him "Elf" — their sibling dynamic holds even in a death scene.

### Unresolved Threads

- **Nightcrawler's fate** — His sacrifice is accepted by Eson, but no body or final confirmation is shown; whether he truly dies or exists somewhere within the Celestial realm is open.
- **Mystique's infiltration** — Disguised as Val Cooper inside the government; "determined to attack the United States" signals a coming move.
- **Graydon Creed's presidency** — Anti-mutant legislation, registration, and Sentinels will likely escalate under a Creed administration.
- **X-Corp and Xavier's legal standing** — Government hostility has not resolved; the tower battle was only one front.
- **Rogue and Gambit's relationship** — Gambit knows Rogue died for him; the emotional aftermath of that debt is untouched.
- **Cable's break with his parents** — He tried to kill Gambit against their wishes; the family rift remains.

## S02E05

# X-Men '97 S02E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Logan reassembles **Team X** (Maverick, Sabretooth, Lady Deathstrike/Yuriko, Kane, Morph) for a suicide mission against a revived **Weapon X** facility in the Arctic. Their transport is downed by birds. Inside, they discover the base overrun by the **Brood**—a parasitic hive-mind alien species. Dr. Cornelius (Weapon X architect) has been experimenting with Brood DNA to create perfectly obedient living weapons, using a captured Brood specimen. The Brood have already infested most personnel.

## Important Character Actions
- **Logan**: Reveals his **adamantium was removed** (bone claws); lured into the adamantium chamber by a Brood duplicate of himself. The rebonding process burns out the Brood DNA, restoring him.
- **Maverick**: Killed by the Brood-infected Wolverine duplicate. His death drives the team's urgency.
- **Morph**: Critical hero—uses a **Jean Grey disguise** to manipulate the Brood-Wolverine into the adamantium chamber, exploiting Logan's emotional tether to Jean.
- **Sabretooth & Lady Deathstrike**: Bicker constantly; Creed reveals Graydon Creed is Sabretooth's son.
- **Yuriko/Lady Deathstrike**: Confronts Cornelius over perverting her father Professor Oyama's work.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Brood** | Parasitic hive-mind aliens; "brood" = collective, individuals interlinked like ants/bees. |
| **Dr. Cornelius** | Weapon X lead scientist; experimenting with Brood cranial fluid to erase memory/identity. |
| **Professor Oyama** | Yuriko's father; original Weapon X research Cornelius "stole." |
| **Omega Red** | Russian Weapon X counterpart; "undead" (death-factor mutant), appears in the facility. |

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Adamantium bonding can purge Brood DNA**—the process's lethality to the parasite saves Logan.
- **Logan's adamantium is gone** (removed off-screen); he now has bone claws.
- **Weapon X still active** with new Arctic lab; Cornelius running Brood-hybrid program.
- **Team X history**: Maverick, Sabretooth, Yuriko, Kane, Logan, Morph all served together.
- **Graydon Creed confirmed as Sabretooth's son** (previously hinted).
- **Brood are "picky"—only bond with optimal hosts**; Wolverine's healing factor makes him a prime target.

## Jokes / References for Later
- Morph's **Jean Grey impression** ("I'm gonna tell Jean… extra smooches") becomes the tactical key.
- Logan's **DVD/Laserdisc tech rant** while reading Cornelius's logs—establishes his 200+ year lifespan.
- "Once Team X, always Team X" callback.

## S02E02

## S02E02 — Ancient Egypt: the fall of En Sabah Nur

### Episode Plot
Picking up from the recap: the X-Men are stranded 5,000 years in the past alongside En Sabah Nur (the first mutant), after Magneto voted to strand them ("I voted. / You madman. / You just stranded us!") on a gamble that Nur can fix Bishop's time-bands using the power of the lost temple Pharaoh Rama-Tut is excavating. Charles psychically probes Nur ("Pain calms me") and, reading the mind of Rama-Tut's general, learns the temple's location from the Gardener's Gaze constellation. Rama-Tut contacts Charles in his mind — a playful, multi-voiced being who warns "You cannot stop him. You never stop him."

The "temple" turns out to be Ship, the sentient Celestial spacecraft — in the X-Men's own time it was Apocalypse's weapon. Inside, Nur sees his entire future (the Celestial armor, "Come be eternal") and realizes the X-Men came to stop him, not save him. He rejects Charles's destiny ("Mine is to evolve, so I will never be powerless again") and ascends at the shrine: "I am the rocks of the Eternal Shore. Crash against me and be broken!" — becoming Apocalypse. Bishop repairs the time-bands from Ship parts, but Magneto insists on staying behind to contain the threat ("I made this mess... Not for me. Not again."). Apocalypse mocks him ("Now who is the god and who is the insect?"), flies to Rama-Tut's kingdom, and orders Ship to destroy the city; Magneto uses magnetism to bind the debris circling the black hole and dies — "Honor me with action, not tears." Apocalypse taunts Charles over Magneto's body ("The ones you care for will be your undoing... I will be all that is left"), and Charles, unable to lie to his dying friend ("I'm sorry. I can't..."), watches him go. Rama-Tut, departing to conquer another time, tells Rogue her "destiny starts today" — Apocalypse can only be challenged by a force that is "external." Post-credits: Wolverine meets Captain America ("'Bout 50 years"), who warns him not to go after "these guys" alone; Logan: "I got myself a crew. Could even say I'm getting the old band back together."

### Important Character Actions
- Charles Xavier: grooms Nur as the would-be "first X-Man," pleads with him to resist the dark force, fails to stop the ascension, and breaks his word to a dying Magneto by refusing to lie.
- Magneto: owns the catastrophe, stays behind, fights Apocalypse, and sacrifices himself binding the black hole's debris; his last words cast himself as "the devil who pushes sinners to embrace the saint."
- En Sabah Nur → Apocalypse: sees his future in Ship, feels betrayed ("You knew what I was to become and you tried to take it from me"), chooses evolution over peace, and destroys Rama-Tut's city.
- Bishop: salvages Ship parts to repair the time-bands and drags the team home ("Put on the damn tags, or I will drag your riddle-lovin' butts back to the future").
- Rogue: campfire scene with Nur on love ("Love is pain... the only thing that makes life worth living"); grieving Remy; is handed a cryptic destiny by Rama-Tut.
- Beast: identifies Ship ("This is no temple, but the mighty spacecraft Ship") and reads the gamma traces of a god falling from the skies.
- Rama-Tut: reveals himself as a being of many names — "Nathaniel, Victor, Kang" — and flees to find another era to conquer.

### Newly Established Names & Terms
- Rama-Tut (拉玛图) — the pharaoh; time-traveling conqueror with future tech; multiple identities incl. Nathaniel, Victor, Kang.
- Ship (「船」) — the Celestial spacecraft hidden as the lost temple; sentient ("Greetings, traveler"); in the X-Men's era it was Apocalypse's weapon.
- The Gardener's Gaze (园丁的凝望) — the constellation marking the temple's hidden location.
- The Eternal Shore (永恒海岸) — the Celestial trial: "All creation must crash against the Eternal Shore in order to thrive and grow"; source of Nur's ascension phrase.
- "Come be eternal" — the shrine's call; Celestial/Eternal motif.
- Gamma traces / "when a god fell from the skies" — evidence of a Celestial crash 10 millennia ago.

- Magneto dies in ancient Egypt, in Charles's arms — a major status-quo change whose effect on the 1990s timeline is unexamined.
