## S02E08

# The Hunting Wives — S02E08 Summary

## Episode Plot
Nadia Callahan dies in a single-car crash on Old Mill Road the night after recording Margo's confessional at the studio; Margo was the last person to see her alive. Nadia's laptop — holding Sophie's evidence against Margo — vanishes from the crash scene; Margo took it. Jia-Yi Moffitt is found strangled in her apartment, with Trout suspected. Sophie O'Neil returns after her abduction by Zelda Moffitt as Greggton police prepare to close the kidnapping case.

Sophie tells Cal, then Graham, that Margo killed Abby Jackson: Margo had an affair with Brad, got pregnant, and aborted; when Brad realized Margo killed Abby and threatened to expose her, Margo killed him too. Sophie had handed all her evidence to Nadia; it was on the missing laptop. Wanda and Flynn review cleaned-up audio from the lake-house 911 call, in which a voice accuses "Zelda" of taking Margo hostage. Graham files for full custody of Jack and plans to move back to Boston.

At Jed's press conference announcing the president's endorsement, Margo improvises a public confession — she cheated on Jed, the affair caused a pregnancy, and she had an abortion ("I am a murderer"), framed as redemption through Jesus. It plays as a "game changer" power play rather than campaign sabotage. A time jump follows: news reports Governor Jed Banks signing the "Turn Away Gay bill," allowing healthcare providers to deny LGBTQ services on religious grounds, with Margo at his side. Sophie watches at a bar and calls it hypocrisy.

Jonny is named head of the Texas Rangers, shuts down the Kyle Burkett probe, and seals all related files — he is in the Bankses' pocket. Sophie confronts Margo and secures a recorded confession to Abby's murder. Margo offers a "me for you" deal with Jed (Margo takes the fall so Sophie walks free), then demands $10 million when rejected; Sophie reveals she has copies of the recording. The episode ends with an unanswered mysterious phone call.

## S02E01 Summary

### Episode Plot
A cold open shows a sheriff's department surrounding a location where Margo Banks is reportedly held hostage ("Release the hostage!"), intercut with intimate encounters — a teaser of the season's abduction storyline.

Margo campaigns with husband Jed at a rally in Mineola, giving a speech about her drug-addict mother and "Texas is the land of opportunity" before endorsing Jed for governor; afterward the talk turns to donor calls. Deputies Salazar and Flynn investigate a stolen blue Ford pickup left in the road with blood nearby (Fort Worth PD wants it returned; Salazar insists it's evidence), then answer a 10-67 in Blanco Woods: a buried teenage girl, 15–16, dead three to four weeks, found with a purse but no ID — a Doe with a tattoo.

Sophie is in therapy with Graham over her separation (rental, giving him space, missing Graham and Jack, three weeks sober, Margo blocked, nightmares). She describes contorting herself into the "perfect wife and mother" box, which Margo "opened," releasing her worst self; she wants the pre-Margo life back. Graham is skeptical ("surprised she didn't say coffin") and asks what changed.

Margo arrives at the reality show's "fake house" to film *Married Justice*. EP Nadia Kelly pitches it as "Real Housewives in law enforcement," really about the wives. The cast buses in drinking; Stacy Marshall is back from Phoenix; Nico is the cinematographer, and everyone is "always on camera." At home, a parent promises Jack PTA cookies while querying a $1,500 Castillo Auto Body charge blamed on hitting an armadillo, then asks for the Tesla dashcam USB for insurance. Jed gives Margo a foot rub in the limo and the wives gossip about their husbands' quirks.

Nadia pushes the cast to discuss Abby Jackson for the "law enforcement angle." On camera they react to the new body ("just like Abby") and call Jill a double murderer (Abby and Starr), while Callie doubts it ("wore florals and didn't own a vibrator"). Off camera Margo is rattled; Callie teases that she's "having feelings" over Sophie and Brad, and asks whether Sophie is spreading rumors or would go to Jonny ("she's bananas"). Jed and Margo get intimate; he floats her as "America's sheriff" or Texas attorney general, citing Nancy Grace and his own unused law degree. Margo leaves Kyle a voicemail ("Try recovering data from this now, Elon"), complaining Jed hates her, she lives in the pool house, and asking how Mexico is.

At a D1 basketball game, Brad — a freshman — is bullied by Marcus and beaten in a fight. His father Clint calls about Brad being kicked off the team; they argue over their mother's death and Clint's religious framing ("let the devil inside," "mercy that the Lord took her"). Jed confronts Graham at the construction site over the $3 million hole and "our wives bumped brisket" (Margo and Sophie's affair), threatens to replace him, and proposes screwing Sophie while Graham watches ("Good talk"). Graham tells Sophie Jed threatened to fire him; Sophie suggests mind games, Graham retorts "before or after she took off her clothes," and Sophie warns that without his job nothing keeps them in Maple Brook, reminding him about the cookies.

At a donor meeting, Lincoln Trout — a 12-year-old 4chan/incel political prodigy — pitches himself with "Peter Thiel money" and dirt on Jed and Margo gathered by a hired one-eyed stalker; Jed takes him on as new campaign advisor. Trout pitches Margo a viral "woman of the people" photo op at the Giant Penny; Margo demands Jia-Yi's removal and a spot on the reality show, and they deal. Deputies interview Elly's foster mom, who hasn't seen her in a month, blames pills/meth and Elly "changing a few months back," and laments nobody noticed the girl was dead.

Zelda and Gentle John abduct Margo ("Mandy") over a debt Kyle owes, giving her a week and taunting old grudges ("your brother," "a pound of flesh") — the source of the cold-open standoff. At the Giant Penny, Margo is mobbed by fans; Sophie confronts her in the car: Kyle came to her house and threatened to kill her if she went to the cops. Margo says Jed kicked her out, she's in the pool house, Alba people are after her, and pleads that what they had was real. Sophie calls it all lies and orders her out. Final voicemails: Margo urgently tells Kyle that Zelda is looking for him and to call, not text.
