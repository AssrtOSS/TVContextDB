## S01E04

## S01E04 · The Only Who Knows You（唯一懂你的人）

### Plot
The episode opens with a young hacker nicknamed Mictlán（小路易斯）being beaten in a men's restroom by men who want to know who he works for. Intercut: a voice reads the obsessive love letter of Plutarco Gómez to Pamela Dosantos — half confession, half threat.

Amelia is summoned before party higher-up Guillermo, who accuses her of disloyalty for refusing to back militarization, mocks her as a gender-quota seat-filler, and warns she will be expelled if she does not toe the line. She resolves to prove her loyalty another way.

Tomás visits Plutarco Gómez at home. Plutarco keeps a shrine of Pamela photos (a "strange obsession with death") and flies into a rage when Tomás brings up Carmen, Pamela's stylist. Plutarco pulls a .38 revolver, fires a shot, and ejects him. Plutarco's apologetic son stays behind to hand Tomás Carmen's contact, calling his father just an obsessed old man.

Jaime finds Mario at the university and pulls him into the investigation. Mario is handed the encrypted bank statement from Pamela's inbox; he plans a brute-force crack at home. Meanwhile, the teen hackers Nico and Viridiana are caught in the school computer lab and warned they will be reported.

Tomás is being prepped for a live TV segment on the real-estate clan. His producer Rosendo forbids him from mentioning Pamela Dosantos or Salazar ("Let the dead bury the dead"). On air, the host pivots to ask why he is avoiding the Pamela case; Tomás uses the moment to break a new story — Veracruz Governor Enrique Granado is financing his billboard campaign with laundered cartel money. Rosendo is furious, but the segment immediately triggers a call from Granado's office requesting a meeting.

Mario interviews Carmen in person. Carmen, who designed Pamela's most iconic looks and calls herself Pamela's best friend, hints Pamela knew about powerful secrets and had a premonition "they were coming for her."

The torturers release Mictlán with his ID, family address, and a warning to stop playing detective. Amelia dines with Urtegui, brokered by Carlos, and trades political support in exchange for replacing Tamaulipas's candidate with Urtegui's daughter Alondra. Velasco later confirms Urtegui is "seriously considering" backing the party line — Amelia is now a key vote-broker.

Tomás and Amelia, after sleeping together, agree to a no-expectations, secret-only arrangement. Amelia blows off Granado to stay with Tomás.

Jaime confronts a Gulf-region official with documented evidence of a fuel-theft ring, demanding the Port of Tuxpan security contract as his cut. A deal is struck.

Jaime and Mario break into Pamela's apartment using a credit-card bypass and lock-picking tools. They spot miniature dolls Carmen made for Pamela — one that Carmen never received. They then move to La Guacamaya restaurant, where wall photos show Salazar dining with Pamela and the owner. A man enters, asks suspicious questions, offers free shrimp soup, and they decide to leave.

### Important Character Actions
- Tomás: pushes into Plutarco's house, gets shot at, survives; pivots live TV away from real-estate clan onto Governor Granado's cartel funding, putting himself directly in the crosshairs of a sitting governor.
- Amelia: defies the party whip, brokers Urtegui's alignment in exchange for installing Alondra in Tamaulipas, sleeps with Tomás, and skips the Granado meeting for him.
- Jaime: recruits Mario in person; blackmails a fuel-theft kingpin into handing him the Port of Tuxpan security contract; breaks into Pamela's apartment with Mario.
- Mario: decrypts part of Pamela's inbox, conducts the Carmen interview, and is visibly unnerved by what she reveals about Pamela's premonition.
- Carmen: becomes a witness; intimates that Pamela knew powerful secrets and sensed she would die.
- Mictlán (Luisito): tortured and warned off the case, but not silenced.
- Nico and Viridiana: nearly exposed at school; forced to be more careful.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- Plutarco Gómez and his son — Pamela's obsessive stalker-fan; lives with a shrine; armed and unstable
- Carmen — Pamela's stylist and self-described best friend; owns Pamela "miniature" couture dolls
- Guillermo — Amelia's party inquisitor; enforces the militarization line
- Urtegui — power broker; trade is Tamaulipas candidate swap for his daughter Alondra
- Carlos — Amelia's handler who arranged the Urtegui meeting
- Velasco — Amelia's party contact who delivers post-meeting results
- Governor Enrique Granado of Veracruz — cartel-funded billboard campaign; now requesting a sit-down with Tomás
- Rosendo — Tomás's TV producer; sets the no-Pamela, no-Salazar rule
- La Guacamaya — restaurant linking Salazar to Pamela via wall photographs
- Port of Tuxpan — Jaime's prize from the fuel-theft blackmail
- "Let the dead bury the dead" — Rosendo's on-air gag order
- Mariguanol — cannabis ointment joke passed between Amelia and her partner

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Amelia's rebellion over militarization is now public inside the party; she is one misstep from expulsion, but Urtegui's dozen votes keep her essential.
- Tomás is no longer protected by "only the real-estate clan" — by going on air about Granado, he has put a cartel-connected governor in his debt and on his trail.
- Jaime now holds leverage over a Gulf fuel-theft ring and has secured the Tuxpan security contract, recasting him from security expert to insider-player.
- Carmen states Pamela "knew things" about influential leaders and felt they were coming for her — directly tying the murder to insider political secrets, not just a stalker.
- A miniature Carmen-doll Pamela never delivered sits in Pamela's apartment; that specific unfinished piece is now a clue tying Carmen into Pamela's last days.
- The "Carmen knows where to find Salazar" thread implied by Plutarco's rant is unresolved; Carmen herself has only hinted, not confirmed, any deeper involvement.

### Jokes and References Useful Later
- "Tell me what spam you get, I'll tell you who you are" — Mario's running gag about spam folders as personality tests.
- "A dickasaurus" — Amelia's flirty insult to her partner, reused as an affection marker.
- "The camera adds ten pounds" / "double chin" banter between Jaime and Mario — establishes their ease with each other.
- "Hey, Luisito, Mictlán or whatever the fuck you call yourself" — gives Mictlán's real first name as Luis, and codifies that Mictlán is a chosen alias.
- "No business like show business" — Mario's line on entering Pamela's apartment, doubling as a tonal wink.
- Carmen's dolls are a recurring visual motif — Pamela gave Carmen a miniature of every piece Carmen designed for her; the missing one is the next breadcrumb.
- The "look up for me" / "freeze and warm up" Mariguanol scene cements Amelia's domestic partner as someone who treats cannabis ointment casually — useful flavor for later scenes.

### Unresolved Threads
- Who tortured Mictlán and who ordered it? Their warning implies a second party is tracking the investigation independently of Salazar.
- What exactly did Pamela know about "influential leaders," and to whom is Carmen prepared to reveal it?
- Did Plutarco act alone, or was he a useful prop for someone else's obsession? His gun is on the table; his son still hosts visitors.
- What is in Pamela's apartment beyond the dolls? Tomás and Mario left La Guacamaya without retrieving a Salazar link, only a Carmen-curio.
- Will Tomás survive the Granado meeting, or is the cartel-financed governor the next threat vector?
- What is Jaime's long game with the Tuxpan contract — protection for his family, or entry into the same circles he warns Tomás about?
- Amelia's "no expectations, no one finds out" arrangement with Tomás is bound to collide with her rising party profile and Velasco's surveillance instincts.
- Nico and Viridiana have been flagged by a teacher; the school channel can now be used to identify or pressure them.

# Inside The Circle S01E03 — The Official Story

## Plot summary

The episode opens in media res on the restaurant attack from the end of S01E02: Jaime is shot through-and-through shielding Tomás, the gunmen are repelled, and Amelia is hustled into an armored SUV driven by Peña. A title card leads into a flashback narrated by Amelia — she recalls how she, Jaime and Tomás grew up together, that Jaime and Tomás both developed feelings for her but Jaime never acted on his, framed around a Rocío Santana party where the bottle supposedly decided whether Amelia and Jaime kissed in the storage room. Jaime tells her he has wanted to be the one to kiss her for years; she pulls back, calling it "weird" because "we're like siblings."

Back in the present, Jaime is patched up at Mario's house (the attackers were "dumb" — they grabbed Viridiana's iPad but left Jaime's watch). Tomás calls Jimena to reassure her without revealing what happened; Jaime stays overnight at Amelia's because he's scared. Jaime is convinced the hit was ordered by Salazar; Amelia is less sure and raises Pamela Dosantos's death as a possible link.

Mario tells Nico, Viridiana and Jimena to drop the Pamela investigation, but Nico reveals his father actually wants them to keep digging. Jaime is warned by a contact that the bullet was traced to the Northern Cartel and is told to flee the country or have his gallery-owner friend put in a word. Jaime tells Tomás the attack was aimed at him, not Salazar — the gunmen were "enemies of some of my clients" — and covers for the group.

Tomás, Amelia and Mario coordinate damage control with Jefferson handling ballistics; a fixer named Manuel is told to make sure nothing ties back to Amelia, Tomás or "Mr. Mario." The official story pushed to the press is a "protection racket," masking 11 massacre victims and a "State Prosecutor" count.

Nico skips school with Jimena and Viridiana to show them Mexico City "the right way," buying corn dogs; Jimena feels like the third wheel between Nico and Viridiana. Meanwhile the cell-phone video of Amelia's SUV under fire goes viral (#UnharmedRepresentative / "Rep and bodyguard escape attack"), and a third massacre is broadcast — an armed group storming a restaurant, 11 dead.

Guillermo, Amelia's political handler, tries to use the attack to push the party's militarization bill and a security-budget increase; Salazar is the main beneficiary and Alondra Urtegui (the father) is being leaned on to flip his vote. At a joint press event Amelia publicly breaks with the party line, declaring militarization "not the way," calling instead to fight impunity and corruption — a move that puts her directly in Salazar's crosshairs.

Tomás digs through Pamela's belongings and finds ongoing, untraceable BTC transactions on the blockchain, hinting at a payoff trail possibly linked to Salazar. He is fired from pursuing that angle by his editor Rosendo but pivots to Representative Octavio Loreto's real estate fraud network (illegal land buys, extra penthouses, illegal evictions, fabricated lawsuits) — which Rosendo accepts. Amelia collapses emotionally at her apartment; Salazar has threatened her, the party set her up, and she resents Tomás for not listening. Mario calls with something he needs to show the group. The episode closes on the antagonists: a general (implied Salazar) is told "someone is getting information from somewhere they shouldn't," and an unidentified man is seized by armed men and dragged into a vehicle, screaming.

- Amelia defies her party at the press conference, publicly opposing militarization. She later vents at Tomás that the party used her, Salazar threatened her, and nobody in her life actually listens.
- Tomás takes over damage control, coordinates with Jefferson on ballistics and Manuel on cover-up; pivots from the Pamela autopsy angle (rejected by Rosendo) to a Loreto real-estate fraud story; discovers Pamela's bitcoin payments.
- Mario hosts the wounded Jaime, patches him up, calls off the Pamela investigation for the kids — but then calls with a new discovery to share.
- Nico, Viridiana and Jimena form the junior investigator trio; Nico defies Mario's order and keeps going because his father wants them to.
- Rosendo (editor) kills the Pamela autopsy story and threatens to fire Tomás, but accepts the Loreto real-estate story.

- A general (Salazar or aligned) orders "someone is getting information from somewhere they shouldn't" handled; an unidentified man is abducted at episode's end.

## S01E02

# 账单要付 (S01E02) 概述

## 剧情概要

Salazar 一方闯入记者 Tomás 的住所，把 Pamela Dosantos 案的所有材料搜走；他的女儿 Jimena 暂被藏到 Mario 家与 Viridiana 同住，被告知"只是抢劫未遂"。Jaime 送来备用笔电并逼 Tomás 暂离 Pamela 案。Amelia 一边安顿家属，一边筹划找 PRI 出身、与"将军"有旧的 Carlos 探口风。

Tomás 被 Salazar 的人追到太平间外，却拿到了 Pamela 的验尸报告。Nico 与 Viridiana 在网上深挖，发现案发现场视频里倒影出一辆车身印有"特诺伏 / Tecnovolt"（并不存在的电工公司）的灰色厢型车，且当晚整条街的 C5 监控被人为关停。Amelia 通过家族律师 Remus 拿到 Salazar 的精神科处方。Mario 查到洛雷托（Loreto）以违法加盖批文换顶层公寓的腐败线索；四人加 Mario 终于在高档餐厅 Peña 重聚。验尸显示 Pamela 是先被窒息、后被"专业式处决"伪装成激情犯罪；Mario 准备再爆 Loreto，Amelia 提 Salazar 服药后可能幻听。

正要点题时，武装人员闯入餐厅。Jaime 指挥众人撤向厨房与员工通道；枪声密集，Mario 一度与大家走散，Jaime 身中一枪。

## S01E05

# 之前与之后 / The Before and After — Episode Recap

## Plot
The episode opens mid-escape: Tomás and Mario flee together, Mario injured (missing part of his femur, a callback to earlier violence). Mario insists on retrieving a doll from "Carmen's" place as a pretext to investigate Pamela's apartment — once inside, he tells Tomás a stranger, not a neighbor, was ransacking the unit with a gun. Tomás and Amelia separately learn two new threats: Amelia's political ally Carlos (revealed as Jaime's father and rival) warns that the Northern Cartel is hunting Jaime, while Tomás confirms Salazar and the Secretary of Defense remain active pursuers. Amelia visits Jaime at his mother Lucita's home and confronts him about the cartel hit; Jaime admits a botched raid "stepped on" narco toes. Carmen, the designer holding Pamela's recorded archive, summons Amelia and ultimately hands over a suitcase of cassettes and password-protected USB drives she had been guarding as Pamela's "life insurance." Parallel hacking work by Viridiana and Nico surfaces Joaquín Plascencia, a businessman linked to several of Pamela's shell companies. Carmen, increasingly frightened, decides to share the archive with Amelia rather than destroy it. The episode closes with Tomás and Amelia finally alone — and both confessing feelings for each other.

## Important character actions
- Tomás and Mario flee a chaotic scene; Mario limps badly but refuses to leave behind a doll that becomes their cover.
