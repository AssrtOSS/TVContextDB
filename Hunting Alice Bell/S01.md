## S01E03

## Hunting Alice Bell — S01E03 "One of Us" Summary

### Episode Plot
The five accused women travel to Dublin for a global women's conference, intending to present a united front. The trip fractures under pressure: Fran suffers a panic attack; Charlotte spirals with alcohol and reveals an abusive ex (Liam) threatening revenge porn; a heckler disrupts their panel, leading to Jason Nash appearing via smuggled prison video. Nash announces **one of the five is Alice Bell**, that he'll reveal her identity via livestream, and warns she'll frame another to save herself. The women return to London shaken, turning on each other. Fran discovers Vanessa secretly met Graham Hunter in Dublin. Nick urges Fran to investigate the other women to clear her own name; Fran resists. Fran visits Darius Assadi (the blinded boy from Nash's past) hoping he can identify Alice — he cannot. The episode ends with Nash in prison raging at his appeal's collapse, vowing to expose Alice Bell publicly regardless of legal restrictions, and a tracker placed on Vanessa's car.

### Important Character Actions
- **Fran**: Leads panel Q&A; freezes when asked what she wants; delivers viral "total cocking bollocks" line; has panic attack in toilets; visits Darius; refuses to suspect her allies until Nick forces the issue.
- **Vanessa**: Runs PR strategy ("we were victims, now we're owning it"); secures biggest suite; later seen covertly speaking with **Graham Hunter**; denies it when confronted.
- **Charlotte**: Drinks heavily; confides in Ros about Liam's revenge-porn blackmail; flees party, is found intoxicated; 79% of online poll votes her as Alice Bell.
- **Ros**: Supports Charlotte; calls out Julie's speculation; bonds with guy at party (reveals she's 17, he's decent).
- **Julie**: Speculates wildly on Alice Bell's preferences; pushes "maybe it's Charlotte" narrative; clashes with Vanessa.
- **Nick**: Anchors Fran at home; suggests investigating the other women to pre-empt being framed; places tracker on Vanessa's car.
- **Jason Nash**: Smuggles video into Dublin Q&A; declares one of five is Alice; promises livestream reveal; loses appeal; threatens to ignore court order and name her.
- **Graham Hunter**: Runs cat-prediction stunt (Dorothy the Cat) on his show; fuels speculation; meets Vanessa privately.
- **Darius Assadi**: Blind former patient; remembers Alice Bell kindly (brought warm orange squash); cannot identify her now.
- **Violet & Jay**: Suffer school-gate harassment; Violet lashes out at Fran, blames her for family chaos.

## S01E02

# S01E02 — Stronger Together

## Episode Plot
Dr Jason Nash (in prison) tells his lawyer Alice Bell is still at large, dangerous, and has killed 14 victims. Fran meets the four other women falsely accused of being Alice Bell: Julie, Vanessa, Ros, Charlotte. They compare origin stories — Vanessa’s assistant doxxed her; Ros’s game *Doctor Death* drew gamergate-style abuse; Julie’s lottery publicity sparked envy; Charlotte’s affair with her manager triggered rumours from his wife. They realise police only hand out crime numbers. In a pub, Graham Hunter’s followers attack them; Fran has a panic attack. Nick traces Graham’s address via Ros’s tech skills and confronts him — discovering Graham **is a police officer**. Fran’s house suffers a smoke-alarm incident (possible arson). Vanessa books them on Cathy Newman’s live show. On air, Fran exposes Graham as a serving officer and the mob’s ringleader; he is suspended. The women get police protection (patrol cars), #TotalCockingBollocks trends, and media offers flood in (Loose Women, BBC Breakfast, Best of Britain Awards, Dublin Women’s Conference). Final scene: Graham visits Nash in prison — they are collaborating. Nash tells him “we will find a way to make her pay” and hints one of the five **is** Alice Bell.

- **Graham Hunter / True Crime Buster**: Exposed as serving police officer; suspended; secretly colluding with Nash from prison.
- **Dr Jason Nash**: Puppet-master from cell; promises livestream reveal; directs Graham to identify which woman is Alice Bell.

## S01E04

# Hunting Alice Bell — S01E04 “Truth & Lies” Summary

## Episode Plot
The alliance fractures when Nick, convinced Vanessa is the real Alice Bell, places a tracker on her car and breaks into her home with Fran, finding prison-postmarked letters from Dr Jason Nash (“The Sandman”) in her desk. At a tense group meeting, they confront Vanessa with the evidence. She reveals her true identity: **Vanessa Patton**, daughter of **Vincent Patton**, a notorious organized criminal imprisoned in Newbourne — the same prison as Nash. She visited her estranged father, not Nash, and kept his letters. Graham Hunter had tried to recruit her as a mole to spy on the group; she refused. The group reconciles, but the planned 5 pm Nash livestream reveal collapses when Nash fails to appear. Next morning, news breaks: **Nash has been violently attacked in prison and lies in a coma**. Meanwhile, Charlotte’s abusive ex stalks her; Violet and Jay snoop Fran’s iPad and discover medical records showing Fran was **detained under Section 2 of the Mental Health Act** with a **morphine addiction** — matching Alice Bell’s nurse profile. Violet confronts Jay: the timeline fits (Fran got with Nick ~9 years ago, after Alice’s release). The episode ends with Violet and Jay searching Fran’s empty house during the family dinner.

## S01E01

# Hunting Alice Bell — S01E01 Summary

## Episode Plot

**Cold open:** Humberside Crown Court, Lincoln. Former nurse **Alice Bell** sentenced to 18 months for falsifying operating-theatre records to provide an alibi for her boyfriend, anaesthetist/serial killer **Dr Jason Nash** (“The Sandman,” 14 murders). She will be released under a new identity. **Graham Hunter**, true-crime influencer (“True Crime Buster”), whips up an online mob to hunt her down.

**Main timeline:** **Fran Da Silva**, leisure-centre manager in Woodgate, Northamptonshire, lives with husband **Nick**, stepchildren **Violet** (teen) and **Jay** (teen), and baby **Macie**. A brick smashes their window. Fran’s TV interview about the centre’s wave machine goes viral; **True Crime Buster** runs facial-recognition on a still, claims a **74% match** to Alice Bell, and doxxes Fran’s address.

Cascade of consequences:
- Colleagues turn; boss **Jo** suspends Fran (“staff feel unsafe”).
- House sale collapses — buyer withdraws after “checking with Fran.”
- Jay beaten at school by peers chanting “Alice Bell’s kid.”
- Mob surrounds the house; Hunter livestreams. Fran brandishes passport, birth certificate, GCSEs, marriage cert — mob refuses to believe.
- **Social services (Stephanie Marshbrook)** arrives on a school referral: baby Macie “at risk.” Fran, cornered, lets Nick take Macie to his mother.
- Nick floats moving to Plymouth; Fran refuses to flee.
- Final scene: **Julie Brent** (from Filey, Scarborough) approaches Fran in a quiet lane — shows she’s been receiving identical harassment for months. “I am not Alice Bell” chant together.

## S01E06

# S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Charlotte Haleford's funeral at St Benedict's Church is disrupted by Graham Hunter livestreaming as TrueCrimeBuster. The four surviving women (Fran, Julie, Vanessa, Ros) attend, then gather at the village hall for a final meeting. Tensions explode: Julie insists Charlotte *was* Alice Bell; Vanessa and Ros resist certainty. Jay blames the group for driving Charlotte to suicide. Liam (Charlotte's childhood boyfriend) appears, intensifying the confrontation. 

At home, Nick confronts Fran with proof he investigated: he visited her solicitor Adaba Ukwani and learned the full Alice Bell statement ("I too deserve a home. I too deserve some peace. I too deserve to live my life without fear") was never publicly released in full—yet Fran quoted it verbatim at the village hall. Fran confesses: she *is* Alice Bell. She falsified operating-theatre logs for Dr Jason Nash (The Sandman) under coercion—he abused and controlled her. She never killed anyone. Charlotte's suicide note ("I'll be asleep by six"—Nash's catchphrase) was a deliberate gift to give the other women innocence and freedom. Nick cannot accept this; he calls the authorities. Fran prepares to flee but cannot leave her family. The episode ends as police arrive.

## Important Character Actions
- **Graham Hunter**: Livestreams funeral, doxxes location, fuels mob.
- **Julie**: Aggressively pushes "Charlotte = Alice Bell" narrative; refuses doubt.
- **Vanessa & Ros**: Challenge Julie's certainty; Vanessa mocks "closure" rhetoric.
- **Jay**: Confronts group, accuses them of murdering Charlotte by casting her out.
- **Liam**: Reveals he knew Charlotte from childhood; calls women murderers.
- **Nick**: Independently investigates Fran, confronts her with solicitor evidence, ultimately calls police.
- **Fran**: Confesses full truth—identity, coercion, log falsification; tries to leave but stays for family.
