## Episode S31E98 — The Daily Show with Michael Kosta

### Episode Plot

Michael Kosta hosts this episode covering three main news topics: Trump's midterm convention in Dallas losing attendance, the 25th anniversary of 9/11 and Islamophobia targeting New York Mayor Mamdani, and the 2026 NFL season kickoff. The show features two segments and a guest interview.

### Important Character Actions

- **Michael Kosta (host)** opens with "Hell, yeah" energy, leads the monologue and interview, jokes about his own receding hairline.
- **Jordan Klepper (correspondent)** appears live at City Hall in a recurring role as a parody of a Fox News–style commentator. His bit: no matter what Mamdani does (attend, skip, stay silent, leave town), it's a "slap in the face." He escalates to absurdity until claiming George W. Bush "did 9/11."
- **Josh Johnson (correspondent)** hosts the "In Too Deep" segment, doing deep-dive investigation comedy on fraudster Daejon Love.
- **Jia Tolentino (guest)**, New Yorker staff writer, discusses her article "Our Plastic-Surgery Nightmare" and the concept of "Instagram face."

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Daejon Love** — man who posed as a San Francisco 49ers wide receiver to scam two dozen women out of over $1 million; also claimed to be a wealthy Swiss real estate developer. Arrested by federal authorities.
- **Mamdani** — New York City Mayor (Muslim), facing an online petition to skip the 9/11 memorial ceremony. Signed a proclamation declaring September 11 a citywide day of remembrance.
- **Ramzi Kassem** — Mamdani's chief counsel; previously represented a convicted al-Qaeda member whose brother-in-law hijacked the Pentagon plane. Fox News criticized Mamdani for handing him a ceremonial pen.
- **Tom Cole** — House Appropriations Chair, cited a "can't-miss dove-hunting event" as excuse to skip Trump's convention.
- **"Instagram face"** — coined by Tolentino in 2019; describes the homogeneous facial look promoted by algorithm-driven social media (Kim Kardashian / Bella Hadid / Kendall Jenner hybrid).
- **"Mar-a-Lago face"** — the exaggeratedly artificial plastic surgery look common among women at Mar-a-Lago; Tolentino notes it's an ironic example of "gender-affirming care" in an administration opposed to it.
- **"Looksmaxxing"** — men's appearance-obsession culture born from right-wing / incel communities; frames beauty as dominance display rather than pleasure.
- **"In Too Deep"** — Josh Johnson's recurring deep-dive segment.
- **Cara** — one of Daejon Love's victims; knew he wasn't on the roster but stayed "because I live for the plot."

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- This episode aired on or near the 25th anniversary of 9/11 (September 11, 2026). The 2026 NFL season also kicked off the same night.
- Trump held a "midterm convention" in Dallas; many GOP members conspicuously avoided it with various excuses (district events, dove hunting, son's wedding in Greece, son's high school football game). Kosta jokes about "Wednesday Night Lights" / "Clear eyes, full hearts, can't go."
- JD Vance is shown awkwardly saying "9/11, we're about to celebrate the 25th year," prompting Kosta to correct him.
- The show references Bush's post-9/11 speech defending Islam ("Islam is peace") as a contrast to current anti-Muslim sentiment.
- Kosta reveals he has children (relevant to the parenting-while-on-phone exchange with Tolentino).

### Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- "Wednesday Night Lights — Clear eyes, full hearts, can't go" (parody of Friday Night Lights catchphrase, used to mock politicians dodging Trump).
- "And then that guy was in a movie with Kevin Bacon" — six-degrees-style joke extending the guilt-by-association logic used against Mamdani.
- "Eat lead, symbol of peace" — darkly comic line about dove hunting as excuse to skip the convention.
- "Pen boy" — Klepper's derisive nickname for Mamdani, used to show the absurdity of the pen-scandal.
- "Slap in the face" becomes Klepper's escalating running gag through the whole correspondent segment.
- Josh Johnson's "I stayed awake for the first eight minutes of the Paul Giamatti miniseries" — self-deprecating joke about referencing the John Adams / Boston Massacre history.
- "Built like an equipment manager" — Johnson's self-deprecating physical comparison to Daejon Love.
- "Swiss Miss" joke — the only thing Johnson knows that's "Black and Swiss."
- Tolentino's "land acknowledgment" analogy for celebrity plastic-surgery disclosure — acknowledging an unjust system while perpetuating it.

### Unresolved Threads

- Kosta promises more coverage of Trump's Dallas midterm convention "tomorrow" — setup for next episode.
- The segment tease about Fox News "uncorking a 25-year-old bottle of Islamophobia" is announced but the deeper Fox News angle is only partially covered through the Mamdani/Klepper segments; there may be follow-up.
- Daejon Love's trial outcome is not mentioned (the case appears to be ongoing).
- The broader "looksmaxxing" and male beauty-culture topic is raised but only briefly touched in the interview — potential for future coverage.
- The Moment of Zen features a lighthearted debate about men carrying bags ("murses"), ending on a speaker saying "Sorry" — no resolution, just a gentle sign-off.
