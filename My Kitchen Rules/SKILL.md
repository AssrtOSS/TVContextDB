# My Kitchen Rules — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Identity
- **English title**: My Kitchen Rules (abbreviated MKR)
- **Chinese titles**: 我的厨房规则 (Mainland China) / 我的廚房我作主 (Taiwan/Hong Kong)
- **Genre**: Australian competitive cooking game show / reality TV
- **Country of origin**: Australia
- **Original language**: English
- **First air date**: 1 February 2010
- **Broadcaster**: Seven Network (Australia)
- **Production companies**: Seven Productions (2010–2020), ITV Studios Australia (since 2022)
- **Seasons/series**: 15+ (as of 2025; season 16 slated for 2026)
- **Episodes**: 423+ (as of season 15)
- **Running time**: 60–120 minutes per episode
- **Narrator**: Septimus Caton (consistent across seasons)

## Core Premise
Teams of two home cooks with pre-existing relationships (partners, siblings, friends, parent/child) represent Australian states/territories. They transform ordinary homes into "instant restaurants" for one night, cooking a three-course dinner (entrée, main, dessert) for rival teams and celebrity chef judges. Teams score each other; judges score each course. Lowest-scoring teams face elimination cook-offs. The last team standing wins a cash prize (A$100,000 in early and recent seasons; A$250,000 in seasons 3–10).

## Format Structure (Pre-2020 / Classic Format)

1. **Instant Restaurant Rounds** — Teams split into groups (initially 2 groups of 5; later 3 groups with gatecrashers). Each team hosts once. Lowest scorers eliminated or sent to cook-offs.
2. **Pre-Finals Challenges** (at Kitchen HQ or off-site):
   - People's Choice Challenge (public vote)
   - Food Truck (season 5 only)
   - Rapid Cook-off (30 min, key ingredient)
   - Showdown (60 min, core ingredient)
   - Sudden Death (3-course, 3 hours, blind tasting from season 4)
3. **Finals** — Semifinals (2 matches) → Grand Final (winner crowned).

*Post-2020 revival (season 12+) streamlined format; fewer episodes, modified challenge lineup.*

## Speaking Styles & Tone Guidance

- **Narrator (Septimus Caton)**: Dramatic, deep, present-tense storytelling ("Tonight, one team will be eliminated…"). Chinese: 深沉、戏剧性的旁白口吻，用现在时叙述。
- **Manu Feildel**: Warm, encouraging, French-accented English; culinary authority with humor. Catchphrases: "My kitchen, my rules" (title drop), "Cook with heart." Chinese: 温暖鼓励，法式口音痕迹，权威中带幽默。
- **Pete Evans (seasons 1–11)**: Direct, occasionally blunt, "mate" camaraderie. Chinese: 直率、兄弟感、偶尔严厉。
- **Colin Fassnidge**: Irish lilt, sharp critique, "no-nonsense" but fair. Chinese: 爱尔兰口音痕迹，犀利公正，不留情面。
- **Contestants**: Everyday Australians — colloquial, regional slang (e.g., "mate", "heaps", "reckon", "brekkie"), emotional under pressure. Chinese: 口语化、接地气、保留澳式俚语味道（可加注或意译）。
- **Judges' critiques**: Technical culinary terms (mise en place, emulsion, reduction, crumb, balance, seasoning, texture) + sensory language. Chinese: 专业烹饪术语准确对应；感官描写细腻。

## Cultural Context for Translation

- **Australian states/territories rivalry**: NSW, QLD, VIC, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NZ (season 3 only). Teams proudly represent home state; banter plays on interstate stereotypes.
- **Aussie slang & humor**: Self-deprecation, larrikinism, "tall poppy" awareness. Don't over-polish; keep flavor.
- **Food culture**: Multicultural influences (Asian, Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, Indigenous bush tucker). Dish names often hybrid; transliterate or describe.
- **Cash prize**: Tax-free in Australia; life-changing for ordinary families.
- **Broadcast rhythm**: Aired 2–3 nights/week in prime time; "event television" with live-tweeting culture.
- **Sensitivity**: Pete Evans' post-show controversies (anti-vaccine, conspiracy theories) — if referenced in later seasons, handle neutrally. No political commentary in show itself.

# My Kitchen Rules（我的厨房规则）翻译背景资料

## 作品核验

My Kitchen Rules（缩写 MKR）是澳大利亚竞技烹饪真人秀，2010年2月1日于 Seven Network（七频道）首播，至今已播出15季、共423集（单集60–120分钟）。制作方为 Seven Productions（2010–2020）及 ITV Studios Australia（2022年起）。本资料所指为澳大利亚原版，非英国版、新西兰版、美国版或南非版改编，亦非同名电影。

## 中文片名

- 通行译名：《我的厨房规则》（My Kitchen Rules 南非版等中文报道沿用此译名）
- 可保留英文原名 My Kitchen Rules，缩写 MKR

## 节目赛制与术语（英文 → 建议中文）

- Instant Restaurant → 快闪餐厅 / 即时餐厅：各队将自家住宅改造成一晚主题餐厅，招待对手队伍与评委
- three-course meal → 三道式晚餐（前菜 entrée、主菜 main、甜点 dessert）
- Group 1 / Group 2 → 第一组 / 第二组
- leaderboard → 排行榜 / 积分榜
- Sudden Death cook-off → 突然死亡淘汰赛 / 生死对决烹饪赛
- Blind Tasting Panel → 盲品评审团（不知出赛队伍身份打分）
- People's Choice → 观众选择奖 / 大众评分
- Kitchen HQ → 厨房总部（第12季起的核心厨房场地）
- Last Chance Cook-Off → 最后机会对决
- Semi-final / Grand Final → 半决赛 / 总决赛
- Gatecrashers → 空降队伍 / 不速之客队（第4季中途加入的三支队伍）
- Comeback Kitchen → 复活厨房
- grand prize: A$100,000 → 十万澳元大奖
- Team names：各队常以两人名字合体命名其餐厅（如 Janelle + Monzir 的 "Monzelle"），保留英文名不译

## 参赛者关系与世界设定

每季约9–18支两人队伍，按州组队：新南威尔士（NSW，悉尼）、维多利亚（VIC，墨尔本）、昆士兰（QLD，布里斯班）、南澳（SA，阿德莱德）、西澳（WA，珀斯）、塔斯马尼亚（TAS）、首都领地（ACT），个别季有新西兰队。队伍关系为现实中的夫妻、母女、姐妹、闺蜜、父子等（如 father and daughter team 父女档、married couple 夫妻档、Italian sisters 意大利姐妹）。比赛在真实的民宅与 Kitchen HQ 之间轮转，涵盖澳大利亚城市与乡镇（如 Margaret River 玛格丽特河、Albany 奥尔巴尼）。

## 整体剧情前提

素人二人组以自家为舞台轮流开办“快闪餐厅”，由对手队伍和名厨评委按前菜、主菜、甜点打分；排名末位淘汰或进入突然死亡对决。各组决出后经复活赛、半决赛层层晋级，最终两队在总决赛现场制作百人份宴席，争夺十万澳元冠军奖金。节目核心看点是烹饪实力、餐桌礼仪冲突与队伍间的戏剧性人际摩擦。

## 语言与说话风格

- 评委点评直率、多用烹饪术语（reduced 收汁、seasoned 调味、undercooked / overcooked 火候不足/过火、presentation 摆盘、balance of flavours 风味平衡）
- Manu 常夹法语词（bon, magnifique, entrée），译文中法语词可保留或音译加注
- 参赛者口语化、情绪化，常见澳洲俚语（mate、arvo、brekkie、fair dinkum）与方言称呼（不强制直译，按语境处理）
- 旁白（第1–15季 Septimus Caton）为轻快叙述体，介绍队伍与赛程

## 文化背景

- 澳大利亚家庭料理多元：地中海（意、希腊）、东南亚（泰、越）、中东与英式传统菜并存，评委常评“向祖母致敬”（tribute to nan/gran）
- " Bring a plate" 与 backyard BBQ 等澳式聚餐文化是队伍菜单灵感的常见来源
- MasterChef Australia 的成功直接催生本节目，两节目评委圈高度重叠，字幕中提到 MasterChef 时沿用通行译名《厨艺大师》
- 悉尼、墨尔本等州首府认同感强，州际对抗（state rivalry）是节目叙事主线之一

## 译名使用原则

人名采用“常见中文译名 + 首次出现附英文原名”的方式；无通行译名的参赛者名字直接保留英文。队名、餐厅名保留英文。赛制术语全片保持一致（如 Instant Restaurant 一律译“快闪餐厅”）。
