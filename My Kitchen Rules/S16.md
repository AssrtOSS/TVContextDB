# My Kitchen Rules — S16E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
Season 16 premiere (2026). First instant restaurant hosted by **Krysten & Lucy** (mother-daughter, Gold Coast hinterland, Queensland) at their property "Puggles" — named for baby platypus in their creek. Three-course dinner for five rival teams and judges **Manu Feildel** & **Colin Fassnidge**.

| Team | Relationship | State | Notable Details |
|------|--------------|-------|-----------------|
| **Krysten & Lucy** | Mother/daughter | QLD | Lucy: self-taught baker, sells croissant pastries at markets; Krysten proud of daughter's 3-year dedication |
| **Mary & Golden** | Friends (nursing school, Philippines) | — | Want to showcase Filipino food; dream: food truck for night-shift nurses |

| **Tiffany & Giancarlo** | Partners (18 yrs, not married) | — | Entrepreneurs; Italian/Greek fusion; dream: buy a *palazzo* in Sicily |

## Important Character Actions
- **Jane/Colin connection revealed** at table: worked together in food styling. Jane didn't disclose — called a "bombshell," accused of hiding it. Jane defends: "fair is fair," accepts low scores if warranted.
- **Champagne cork incident**: cork hits a guest in the eye during welcome; Angus jokes "if it went worse, you're in good hands" (funeral director).
- **Jane's "taster not eater" stance**: leaves food uneaten; called disrespectful by other teams. She argues tasting ≠ eating; industry habit.
- **Colin visits kitchen during paella cook**: challenges Lucy's method (prawns in too early); they pull seafood out and re-add later. No *socarrat* achieved.
- **Dessert triumph**: Lucy's croissant puff — beautiful layers, "bakery quality." Both judges give **8/10**; Lucy: "my reputation is on the line."

## S16E06

## Episode Plot

The final instant restaurant of round one: "stylish foodies" **Jane & Shane** (married, Blue Mountains NSW) host **Lyst Haus** — their house is passed over for dinner, and guests eat in the neighbouring church's hall, which the parish lent them for the night. Jane (professional food stylist, has worked with Colin) built the whole night around plating. They must beat **Alisha & Deanne's 68** (bottom of the ladder) or go home.

- **Entree — duck egg chawanmushi, king crab, caviar**: served in the duck-egg shells on a bed of pink rock salt. Rave looks ("this presentation alone is 10"), but the custard set on top and stayed runny at the bottom — crab moisture leaked in. Colin/Manu: "the technique was wrong," style over substance; Manu's demo of the sloppiness drew his baby-shoulder joke. Judges: **5 / 4**.
- **Main — master stock pigeon, chilli, cucumber, samphire, shallot pancake**: intended homage to Peking duck pancakes, heads on the plate for drama. The confit overcooked the birds ("mummified," "like boots," "in the crematorium"); a pressure-cooker pivot failed completely. They shredded the meat, dropped the heads, kept one leg "for the sculptural element." Guests: "pigeon dry, dry, dry." Judges: **4 / 3** — Manu: pigeon must be served pink; pink breast with braised leg "would've been a 10."
- **Dessert — "tomato sour"** (their saving grace): tomato-mascarpone gelato, peppercorn meringue, strawberry-amaretto jam, finger lime, tomato powder, served in a coupe glass as a riff on an amaretto sour cocktail. Widely liked — "best dish of the night." Judges: **7 / 7**.

Guest teams total **20/50**; grand total **50** — well under 68. **Jane & Shane are eliminated; Alisha & Deanne survive.** With round one closed, **Tiffany & Giancarlo (1st) and Mary & Golden (2nd) are fast-tracked to Kitchen HQ**, while Alisha & Deanne, Angus & Simon and Krysten & Lucy must tour Australia again against **three new gatecrasher teams** (teased: a Perth mum-and-son pair).

## Important Character Actions

- **Jane & Shane's bravado before the cook**: target 90, "top of the ladder by the end of the night," prize money for an Italian palazzo, "we're not here to cook a chicken schnitzel." Pre-existing dig: Jane had said no instant restaurant before Tiffany & Giancarlo's brought flavour.
- **Jane**: ran plating exclusively; set the judges' portions ("this one's for Colin, this one's for Manu"); publicly rejected Manu's critique ("The technique was right... He was wrong"), then broke down mid-service — "I feel like an absolute failure," needed a couple of minutes.
- **Shane**: pushed the "be better" mantra and the pressure-cooker pivot; "I am a duck — gliding on the top, but my legs are frantic underneath."
- **Alisha & Deanne**: openly rooted for a flop ("I would love for Jane and Shane to really just flop"), hoped the pigeon was overcooked, joked about buying the palazzo "just to rub it in their face"; Alisha turned nervous once the dessert landed — "I'm starting to get a little nervous now."
- **Angus** (funeral director) held forth on doves at the table; **Golden** admitted confusing pigeon with dove.
- **Judges**: Colin turned guests away at the door ("We cannot let you in"), praised the menu ("it bloody reads well"), liked master stock, samphire and pancake; Manu led the tough critiques and signed off "Au revoir, and good luck."

- **Church hall venue** — dinner held next door in a borrowed church, not the home.

- **Gatecrashers (S16)** — first look: "mum and son from Perth," best mates, "zesty, fresh flavours," "Welcome to hell" (names not yet given).

## S16E15

## Episode plot
- Woolworth’s Fresh World Flavours Challenge at Kitchen HQ: six teams cook mains from assigned world cuisines across two rounds. In each round, all teams taste and critique, but Manu and Colin choose the weakest dish to send that team to elimination.
- Mary and Golden, having won dish of the day in their previous Kitchen HQ cook, gain the advantage to assign teams to rounds, set pick order, and choose their own cuisine.
- Round 1 (Mexican, Indian, French): Sheridan and Ted pick Indian and cook Goan prawn curry with swordfish and vegetable samosa; Tiffany and Giancarlo pick Mexican and cook cochinita pibil on salbute; Angus and Simon are left with French and cook duck magret with apple and cider jus. Tiffany and Giancarlo’s pork fails to build pressure properly and is blitzed in a blender, leaving it dry; they are sent to the elimination cook-off.
- Round 2 (Italian, Chinese, Irish): Mary and Golden choose Chinese and cook yum cha (pork shumai, prawn wontons, Chinese broccoli, sauces); Zac and Zoe choose Italian and cook zucchini ricotta rolls with mushroom arancini and Italian salad; Harrison and Oliver are left with Irish and cook beef rissoles with colcannon and Guinness gravy. Mary and Golden’s shumai steam poorly and plating falls apart, while Zac and Zoe’s zucchini is undercooked/watery and elements lack refinement; Zac and Zoe are sent to the elimination cook-off.

## Important character actions
- Mary and Golden: Use their advantage strategically, aiming to push Angus and Simon into French and elimination; later pick Chinese for themselves and try to steer Italian away from Harrison and Oliver. They become overwhelmed in round 2, and Golden becomes emotional at judging over the pressure and family sacrifices.
- Angus and Simon: Accept French despite being targeted, ignore Manu’s suggestion to keep the dish simple without added vegetables and later add asparagus; deliver a well-cooked duck that keeps them safe. They then give notably positive/strategic critiques in round 2, which Mary and Golden interpret as payback.
- Tiffany and Giancarlo: Struggle with timing and pressure-cooker issues; Giancarlo pulses the pork in a blender as a rescue attempt, but texture suffers.
- Sheridan and Ted: Work through time stress and disagreement over finishing samosas; Ted drives flavour while Sheridan manages components and cleanup.
- Zac and Zoe: Choose Italian to feel more comfortable and to avoid giving Harrison and Oliver their stronger cuisine; their zucchini rolls suffer from thick, unseared slices and excess moisture.
- Harrison and Oliver: Treat the Irish cook as meaningful (Oliver references his nan’s rissoles/Irish heritage), follow Colin’s suggestion to finish rissoles in gravy, and produce a dish Colin largely approves of.
- Manu and Colin: Judge the key technical failures and successes (duck cook vs apple treatment/laziness, blended pork dryness, raw/thick dumpling wrappers, uncharred watery zucchini, colcannon texture).

## Newly established names/terms
- Challenge: Woolworth’s Fresh World Flavours Challenge.
- Round 1 dishes: Cochinita pibil on salbute (Tiffany and Giancarlo), Goan prawn curry with swordfish and vegetable samosa (Sheridan and Ted), Duck magret with apple and cider jus (Angus and Simon).
- Round 2 dishes: Yum cha spread of pork shumai, crispy prawn wontons, Chinese broccoli and sauces (Mary and Golden), Zucchini ricotta rolls with mushroom arancini and Italian salad (Zac and Zoe), Beef rissoles with colcannon and Guinness gravy (Harrison and Oliver).

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Recap context: Harrison and Oliver previously survived an epic elimination by defeating Krysten and Lucy; Simon and Angus’s accusation that others were “dishonest” in critiques remains an active tension, especially with Mary and Golden.
- Teams active this episode: Angus and Simon, Tiffany and Giancarlo, Sheridan and Ted, Mary and Golden, Zac and Zoe, Harrison and Oliver (presented as three gatecrasher teams and three OG teams still in the competition).
- Advantage source: Mary and Golden earned the round-assignment/cuisine-pick advantage by getting dish of the day in their last Kitchen HQ cook.
- Harrison and Oliver state they have already been in one elimination cook-off before this episode and are desperate to avoid another.

## Jokes or references useful later
- Angus and Simon lean into being outsiders with no friends in the competition; Simon is jokingly reduced to “Angus’s friend” by Mary.
- Manu’s French greeting/playfulness (“Bonsoir, bonsoir”) and the “ooh la la” French-duck banter.
- Colin arrives for the Irish round with “Top of the morning to ya” and breaks into a jig.
- Running gags/lines: Ted’s “frying high,” Giancarlo’s emergency “reverse mince” after blending pork, Harrison and Oliver’s “rock and rissole roll,” Zac and Zoe’s “risotto king for hostels.”
- Angus and Simon theatrically pretend to be upset when assigned French, then claim they will have the last laugh.

## Unresolved threads
- Elimination cook-off matchup is set: Tiffany and Giancarlo vs Zoe and Zac (Manu asks for “dishes from your heart”).
- Mary and Golden are safe but rattled after wasting their advantage and clashing publicly with Angus and Simon over critique “dishonesty.”
- The feud/strategic tension between Angus and Simon and the rest of the table (especially Mary and Golden) remains active, with Angus and Simon showing they may use critiques tactically.

## S16E17

**My Kitchen Rules S16E17 — Ultimate Instant Restaurants: Harrison & Oliver (Sydney)**

**Episode Plot**  
The first *Ultimate Instant Restaurant* round begins in Sydney’s inner west. Best mates **Harrison and Oliver** (“hipster mates”) launch **Bloody Bellissimo**, cooking double the usual load — two entrées, two mains, two desserts — for the four remaining rival teams plus judges **Manu Feildel** and **Colin Fassnidge**. The lowest combined score (guest teams + judges) at round’s end will be eliminated.

**Menu — Bloody Bellissimo**  
- **Entrée 1:** Kangaroo tartare, taro & wattleseed crisps  
- **Entrée 2:** Prawn risotto (Oliver’s signature; “breaking the MKR risotto curse”)  
- **Main 1:** Rabbit cacciatore with polenta  
- **Main 2:** Milk-braised pork belly, cavolo nero, roast potatoes (targeting Mary & Golden’s “pork queen” title)  
- **Dessert 1:** Nonno’s *mele al forno* (baked apples stuffed with prunes, zabaglione) — Oliver’s grandfather’s recipe  
- **Dessert 2:** Ricotta-cream cannoli, pistachios (Harrison’s domain)

**Important Character Actions**  
- **Harrison & Oliver**: lean on friendship (“secret ingredient”), split Italian vs. modern dishes, support each other through cannoli dough crisis (cracking shells → fixed by rolling thinner first).  
- **Oliver**: maintains “poker face” all night; finally cracks a smile on receiving a **10** for cannoli.  
- **Angus & Simon**: accused of **strategic scoring** in prior elimination (gave Tiffany & Giancarlo 3, Zoe & Zac 8). They deny but later award a surprising **9** — Oliver suspects strategy.  
- **Mary & Golden**: defend honesty (“we’re mums, nurses”), give first **9**.  
- **Sheridan & Ted**: only team to order tartare + baked apple; give a **6**, citing overpowering shallots and undercooked apples.  
- **Judges (Manu & Colin)**: introduce **open critiquing & open scoring** (“honesty has been a hot topic”). Colin calls prawn risotto “best risotto ever on MKR”; Manu awards first **10** of season for cannoli.

**Newly Established Names / Terms**  
- **Bloody Bellissimo** — H&O’s restaurant name (“when you eat it you go ‘oh, that’s bloody beautiful’”).  
- **Ultimate Instant Restaurants** — new round branding (double dishes, instant elimination).  
- **Open critiquing / open scoring** — teams critique & score face-to-face at table.  
- **“Risotto curse”** — MKR history of failed risottos; declared broken by Colin.  
- **“Pork queens”** — Mary & Golden’s self-title; H&O aim to dethrone / become “pork kings”.  
- **Nonno’s mele al forno** — Oliver’s grandfather’s baked-apple dessert (emotional anchor).

**Continuity-Relevant Facts**  
- Top-five teams remain: **Harrison & Oliver, Angus & Simon, Mary & Golden, Sheridan & Ted, Zoe & Zac**.  
- Previous elimination (Tiffany & Giancarlo) decided by **team scores** overriding judges’ 1-point margin — fuels strategic-scoring accusations.  
- **Guest-team total for H&O: 33/40** (9, 9, 9, 6).  
- **Judges’ total: 48/60** (Colin: 9+9+4=22; Manu: 7+9+10=26).  
- **Grand total: 81/100** — benchmark for remaining teams.  
- Next instant restaurant: **Sheridan & Ted in Perth** (WA).

**Jokes / References for Later Episodes**  
- **“Poker face”** — Oliver’s stone-cold expression becomes running gag; breaks only at 10.  
- **“Bloody Bellissimo”** catchphrase — reused by judges/teams.  
- **Dream talk** — Angus dreamed of Sheridan (“trying to convince her I’m a good guy”); Zoe dreamed of Manu & Colin served “slop” by Zac.  
- **“Competent”** — Angus’s backhanded compliment for risotto becomes table meme (“positive word!” / “it’s not!”).  
- **“Crackling echo”** — pork belly skin crunch heard “across the room / road”.  
- **“One rotten apple”** — Colin’s pun on undercooked baked apples.

**Unresolved Threads**
