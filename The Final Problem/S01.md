## S01E02

# S01E02 Summary — *The Final Problem* (《最后一案》)

## Plot

The storm continues to trap the hotel guests on Santa Águeda. After Elisa Mander's death is initially ruled a suicide (hanging in the pavilion), retired Sherlock Holmes actor Benjamin Basil and aspiring thriller writer Foxá begin pushing the investigation toward murder. The episode escalates from suspicion to confirmed homicide when the group realizes Elisa was drugged, struck on the head, and hanged with a rope that was tampered with using a knife — someone staged the suicide.

The investigation intensifies through dinner and the evening. Dr. Seruya presents the forensic findings (asphyxiation by hanging, rigor mortis already set in, indicating death occurred earlier than thought). Basil methodically dissects the crime scene — the missing rope, the double floor marks from a moved table used to hoist the body, the missing knife — while the hotel manager Mrs. Ausländer grows increasingly alarmed that Basil is alarming guests.

Toni (the waiter) and Catalina (the maid) become entangled when a ring Elisa gave Toni is found missing; Toni claims Elisa gave it to him after an intimate encounter, then gave it to Catalina. Basil and Foxá deduce Toni is not the murderer (he's ruled out as a thief) but his testimony and the ring create further mystery around Elisa's relationships.

The episode ends with Basil climbing to the terrace to peek into Dr. Seruya's room (where Seruya summoned him privately), discovering the door is locked from inside and the doctor apparently missing. A cliffhanger: Basil calls out, the doctor doesn't answer, and the key is stuck in the lock.

## Important Character Actions

- **Benjamin Basil**: Plays detective throughout — deduces Elisa was drugged and murdered, not suicidal; identifies the missing rope and knife as planted evidence; examines the crime scene; deduces Toni's innocence; manipulates the situation to get closer to Seruya's room; climbs to the terrace at night.
- **Foxá**: Assists Basil with literary-genre analysis; confronts Mrs. Ausländer about the murder allegations; is mocked by Mrs. Ausländer for "scaring guests" with murder speculations; gives Basil a copy of *The Vanished Dagger* by Frank Finnegan (one of his pen names).
- **Adela Figueroa**: Reveals she traveled with Elisa (not sisters but lookalikes); shares that Elisa's husband (Eduardo) died during the war; she met Elisa in Paris; is interviewed by Basil about Elisa's romantic life.
- **Mrs. Ausländer**: Defends the suicide ruling; tries to manage guest panic; discovers Toni and Catalina's involvement with the ring; orders Gerard to escort Toni to his room; is frustrated by Basil's interference.
- **Dr. Seruya**: Conducts the autopsy; presents findings suggesting suicide (later contradicted); summons Basil privately to the pavilion; is caught in his room with the door locked at episode's end — suspicious behavior.
- **Toni (waiter)**: Questioned about the ring; admits kissing Elisa and receiving the ring; claims Elisa gave it to him willingly; is escorted to his room by Gerard under suspicion.
- **Fonseca**: Confronts Mrs. Ausländer; claims she was in her office at night; is accused by Mrs. Ausländer of stealing her key (tobacco residue found in her office).

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Santa Águeda** (圣阿格达) — The remote islet where the hotel sits.
- **Ausländer Hotel** (奥斯兰德酒店) — The isolated luxury hotel managed by Mrs. Ausländer.
- **Frank Finnegan** (弗兰克·芬尼根) — A pen name used by Foxá; author of *The Vanished Dagger* (*The Vanished Dagger* / *消失的匕首*).
- **Eduardo** (爱德华多) — Adela Figueroa's late husband; died during the war; Elisa's former lover in Paris.
- **Dr. Seruya** (塞鲁亚医生) — The hotel's doctor conducting the autopsy.
- **The Vanished Dagger** (*消失的匕首*) — A book by Foxá (under pen name Frank Finnegan); later referenced as a knife potentially used to cut Elisa's rope.
- **"Checkmate" / "将军" / "将死"** — Chess metaphor used in the dialogue (Basil and another character playing chess); "Checkmate" signals the investigative game beginning.
- **"The game is afoot"** — Holmesian phrase invoked by the group; marks the official start of the murder investigation.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The **rope used to hang Elisa disappeared** — Dr. Seruya is suspected of taking it to examine the neck wound.
- The **knife** used to tamper with the rope also disappeared; Basil suspects it may be the same dagger from Foxá's novel.
- **Elisa's jewelry (ring)** was given to Toni, then passed to Catalina; the ring is later found hidden. The ring has sentimental value — it was from her deceased husband's proposal.
- **Basil's hands** show a fresh ring mark — one ring is newer than the other, suggesting recent involvement with the victim.
- **Mrs. Ausländer's office key was stolen** — evidence of tobacco residue suggests someone was there at night.
- **Generator and radio are down** — the storm damaged the radio, cutting off contact with Palma and the outside world; Gerard is trying to repair the antenna.
- There are now **12 suspects trapped** on the island (previously thought 10).
- **Elisa Mander's body was moved to the hotel** before the doctor's examination, at the request of her friend Adela.
- **Mrs. Ausländer confirms Elisa and Adela had a room with two beds** and slept separately — ruling out romantic partnership between the two travelers.

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes

- **Sherlock Holmes meta-jokes**: Multiple characters joke about Basil being Sherlock Holmes ("You're Sherlock Holmes"; "The detective par excellence"). The English actor comparison and the "better than the English counterpart" line establish cultural context.
- **"Irene Adler was special to him"** — Basil's discussion of his character's only romantic attachment provides character depth and a potential parallel for his own interactions with Adela.
- **"The curious case of the ex-pretend sleuth, divorced and with no children"** — Basil's self-deprecating humor about his real life vs. Holmes.
- **Chess metaphors** ("Checkmate," "I sacrificed my rook") — mirror the detective game and hint at strategic moves in later episodes.
- **"Playing detectives"** — Foxá's phrase from the opening, now the group's unofficial motto.
- **"The usual?"** — Basil's drink order (double whiskey neat) is a recurring character tic.
- **"The game is afoot"** — Classic Holmes phrase, now the group's operational slogan.

## Unresolved Threads

- **Dr. Seruya's suspicious behavior**: Summoned Basil to the pavilion alone; locked himself in his room; the key is stuck in his door lock. His role in the murder remains unclear — is he the killer, or is he being framed/held hostage?
- **The missing rope and knife**: Where are they? Who took them? Seruya is suspected of taking the rope; the knife's connection to Foxá's novel is teased.
- **Fonseca's alibi and the office key theft**: Did she steal the key herself or have an accomplice? What was she doing in the office at night?
- **Toni's involvement with the ring**: How deep is his relationship with Elisa? He claims he received the ring after an intimate encounter — is this true or a cover?
- **Catalina's role**: She received the ring from Toni; she's the one who found the ring; she also appears to know more than she lets on.
- **Mrs. Ausländer's full involvement**: She controls information flow, has a master key, and has her own agenda in suppressing the murder theory.
- **Vicky Montes (the producer)**: Her connection to Basil and the yacht situation is still unclear — is she part of the conspiracy?
- **The radio repair**: When will Gerard fix it? Who will arrive from Palma?
- **Who killed Elisa Mander**: The group now knows it was murder, but the identity of the killer remains unknown — Suspects include Seruya, Mrs. Ausländer, Fonseca, Toni, or someone else entirely.
- **"The dagger" from Foxá's novel**: Is the fictional weapon becoming real, or is someone using the novel as a blueprint?
