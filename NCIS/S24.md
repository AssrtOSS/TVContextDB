# S24E01 摘要：NCIS 局长更替 + SECNAV 女儿绑架案

## 剧情主线麦基的少年儿子 Mateo 被神秘人胁迫，从 NCIS 系统内窃取了海军部部长（SECNAV）安保小组的完整档案，并被指派到一处"死信箱"（dead drop）进行交接。托瑞斯事先察觉异样，独自跟踪 Mateo，却在交接现场被走火的枪弹擦伤；麦基随后赶到的塔尼目睹事件，对儿子涉入的规模深感震惊。

绑架者利用盗来的安保档案，对 SECNAV 女儿 Catherine（17 岁）实施绑架；他们身上携带的正是 Mateo 在死信箱留下的那把枪——负责 Catherine 的两名特工当场遇害。绑匪向 SECNAV 提出要挟：在 20 小时内交出某份旧档号对应文件，否则撕票。SECNAV 决定临阵换将，任命北太平洋（Northwest Connectionist——本来与帕克背景有关的一位新任局长）加布里埃尔·拉罗什（Gabriel Laroche）为 NCIS 新任局长。

拉罗什上任后作风强硬，立即将麦基和托瑞斯逐出大楼、暂停两人职务，并命令 Knight 等人按 REACT 标准流程推进。但麦基偷出大楼，与同样被赶出的托瑞斯会合，潜回大楼地下借助塔尼（托尼）的办公室继续暗中调查；Knight 与 Parker 则在楼内应 Laroche 之命处理案件。托瑞斯违抗"禁止任何探员进入"的命令，由帕萨（Polk——指派）Parker 批准从 Tony 办公室地下把塔尼（托尼）放进一家餐厅（diner），化解了与 Mateo 的僵局，化解僵局，避免流血。

## 关键角色行动

- **麦基 / McGee**：因儿子被牵连而心绪崩溃，但仍坚持前往现场与儿子对峙；在被停职后仍与托瑞拉合作，冒险进入餐厅说服 Mateo 投降。
- **托瑞斯 / Torres**：未告知同事便独自跟踪 Mateo；中弹后仍坚持追查，违抗命令放塔尼（托尼）进餐厅协助麦基，体现出对麦基家庭的关怀。
- **塔尼 / Tony DiNozzo**：出场自称"Very Special Non-Agent Anthony DiNozzo"，揭示他已离开 NCIS 十年，正为法国安保公司 Salus Mondiale 在华盛顿特区开设办事处（satellite office）。他通过黑客 Boris 听到关于 NCIS 的"坏蛋闲聊"，主动跟进死信箱才被 Mateo 走火击中。后续以"非探员"身份灵活进出大楼，多次提供关键线索（暗网论坛 Elusive Dream、I2P 加密隧道、Goonies 类比）。
- **奈特 / Knight**：负责拉罗比跟下的现场处置与汇报，违抗拉罗比指令、与 Parker 联手让塔尼进入餐厅，事后遭拉罗比严厉训斥。
- **帕克 / Parker**：合理利用规则漏洞（"你禁的是探员，他不是"）批准塔尼潜入餐厅；事后被拉罗比当场解除职务、收回徽章与证件，剧情设计为被解雇但仍继续行动。
- **马什 / SECNAV Marsh**：要求交出绑匪索要的旧档文件，并当面开除帕克；但事后亲自到 Salus Mondiale 办公室向塔尼道谢，承认拉罗比不是合适的局长人选，转而邀请塔尼出任新 NCIS 局长。
- **凯西 / Kasie**：在鉴识实验室追踪 I2P 流量元数据，定位 Mateo 所取内容为 SECNAV 安保小组档案；后又从绑匪索要的视频中剥离背景音（码头浮标摩擦船体的声音）协助定位巴尔的摩港（要强调"音学特征"）。
- **达奇 / Ducky**：以远程口吻提醒团队"没有调查参与律师参与律师的问题"。
- **达菲 / Ducky（？） / 与 Ducky 类似的资深人士……实际对白中**：提示 Tony 是助理法医层面的老熟人，包括 Jimmy Palmer 在内均被要求协助但拒绝。
- **拉罗比 / Laroche（Gabriel Laroche）**：作风强硬，强调"按规矩办事"、"box breathing"，下令 REACT 在场时不得有探员擅自行动，否则一律暂停职务；拒绝立刻用未确认内容进行交换，最后释放 Sword & Tiger。

## S24E02

## Plot

Tony DiNozzo is in his first week as NCIS Director, delivering his first all-hands speech when a biohazard alarm (which he secretly triggered) clears the building. During the evacuation, the team learns that a Naval Intelligence officer, DeWitt (a.k.a. "Mallory"), was gassed at a dinner party in suburban Virginia. The hostess, Linda Fenwick, hid in the pantry and survived; the rest of the guests, plus her husband Greg, were knocked out. Four gas-masked gunmen swept the house for 96 seconds and left empty-handed.

McGee and Torres interview the Fenwicks (Greg owns a shipping store, Linda hosts a party-planning podcast called "Dining with Dazzle"). Knight, meanwhile, notices three guests — Frank Russo (butcher), Dawn Spurlock (estate attorney), and Evan Jenks (shampoo-company research tech) — each held a 2 a.m. bonfire that night. Kasie digs up the ashes: paper, hair, and fingernail fragments. DNA-destruction attempts.

Greg disappears. His car is later found at the bottom of a ravine with his body inside; the coroner calls it a heart attack, but Jimmy (with Tony leaning in) finds a sophisticated poison. Linda gives McGee a lead: a small brass key on Greg's keychain he called "work stuff." Tony finds it hidden on Greg's person, and it opens a storage unit packed with roughly 100 jars of pig eyeballs. Kasie deduces Greg was extracting a retinal enzyme from the vitreous fluid — a low-tech way to make designer opioids, sidestepping DEA surveillance on standard chemical purchases.

Knight and Torres run surveillance on a man who had been tailing Greg for weeks — a wig-wearing suspect named Carl Moody, PhD chemist with a Florida designer-drug record. Tony hijacks the interrogation; Moody turns out to work as a "corporate fixer" for a drug company. A research tech (Jenks) had stolen a new formula, and Greg, Russo, and Spurlock formed a black-market ring around it. The formula, however, is not an opioid — it's a hair-loss cure promising full regrowth in six months in a $30 billion market. Moody had already recovered the stolen formula two weeks before the dinner party and can prove it.

Kasie, "leaning into the weird," disassembles the Fenwicks' robot vacuum and triggers a fail-safe that streams a massive encrypted file. McGee traces it to Mervin Dorf, a.k.a. "Dead Drop," a hacker who brags about exploiting smart-device ToS loopholes. He was hired to covertly record every camera-equipped smart device within a two-mile radius, sending footage back passively every three months. McGee pulls two-day-old footage from the Fenwick vacuum: it shows the gas attack and the four commandos, one of whom is matchable through facial recognition.

The trail leads to the CIA, which has been using the hacker to sweep for deep-cover foreign operatives in that zip code. The toupee-wearing Greg Fenwick was one of them — vain enough that an oddity in his appearance tipped the Agency off. The CIA gassed the dinner party because a manufacturer firmware update was about to wipe their surveillance cache. But the Agency insists Greg's poisoning was not their work. Parker pulls additional footage from the Fenwicks' smart pet feeder and catches the moment Linda spikes Greg's toothpaste (he asks about his coffee; she corrects him). "Lovely Linda" was a fellow deep-cover agent, and Agent Cross confirms the Fenwicks were never legally married. Linda was about to abort the mission but decided to "go done done" on her husband.

The CIA wants Linda kept in the field as the only lead on a much larger, imminent operation run by very worried senior officials. Tony cancels the arrest, lets Linda believe the cover story that the drug company staged the break-in and her husband died of a heart attack, and lets her walk.

## Important character actions

- Tony DiNozzo (now Director): secretly pulled the biohazard alarm as a "personnel inventory" to see who runs from problems vs. toward them; hijacked Knight's interrogation of Moody; ordered McGee to "get a probie"; reveals to McGee he plans to keep doing fieldwork as a pressure-release valve ("bust some bad guys with you").
- McGee: running point on the case, interviewing Linda, tracing the hacker's signal, getting nudged by Torres and Parker to tell Tony to "stay in his lane," and being told at episode's end that SECNAV has a different job lined up for him.
- Knight: re-interviewing dinner guests, spotting the bonfire pattern via neighborhood chats, running solo surveillance on Moody (with wig, in a café), and pulling the pet-feeder footage that nails Linda.
- Torres: partner to McGee on the host interview and to Knight on the tail, and openly grumbling about Tony's directorial behavior.
- Parker: running a parallel "Ed Gein / black-market cornea" eye-collection case in his office; supplies Tony with a satellite look at Moody; trading notes with Tony in the director's office in a flirtatious, slightly weird register.
- Kasie: identifies the retinal-enzyme angle, finds the vacuum fail-safe, sprinted up two flights of stairs in loafers to preserve the transmission, and races to trace it before the upload completes.
- Jimmy (Palmer): performs Greg's autopsy, locates the hidden brass key on the body, and trades domestic grievances with Tony (Tony is crashing on his couch; thermostat wars, expired eggs, "favorite autopsy gremlin" insults).
- Ziva (off-screen, by phone): has reportedly deputized McGee to keep Tony on a doughnut-free diet, since "he only listens to strong women."

## S24E05

## NCIS S24E05 Summary

### Episode Plot
A Marine sergeant, Michael Sullivan, is found waterboarded and dumped at a Silver Spring bus stop. The victim's phone leads to Monica Sims, a former Marine Staff Sergeant and Sullivan's Afghanistan buddy, who's gone missing with her 7-year-old daughter Chloe. Chloe witnessed two Chinese men arguing at a "clown store" (convenience store) and speaks Mandarin—taught by her late father. Security footage shows store owner Troy Tepikian luring Sullivan into the back room. Chloe identifies one attacker from a photo: retired D.C. detective Thomas Lau, who has a fresh "Ghost Dragons" (鬼龙) tattoo. Lau is soon found stabbed with a Marine-issue Mark 2 knife, Marine-style.

Investigation reveals Troy, Lau, and Lau's cousin Fred Yong (recently paroled ex-con) were running a fake "Ghost Dragons" protection racket, shaking down local businesses. Fred went rogue, expanded with tattoos and muscle, and killed Sullivan when he investigated. Monica went vigilante to avenge Sully and her husband (killed years earlier by gangbangers fleeing a drive-by). She tracks the operation to Brockway Mill Lumber Company, where the *real* Ghost Dragons (Baltimore-based Triad branch) are sending a crew to reclaim their name. NCIS arrives with Baltimore PD backup, arrests everyone; real Ghost Dragons flee.

Parallel B-plot: McGee prepares a speech for Mateo's sentencing hearing. After teammates critique his formal draft, he abandons it and speaks from the heart as a father. The judge—who also overcame a rough childhood—grants Mateo a one-time alternative to jail: enlist in the Navy.

- **Monica Sims**: Goes rogue vigilante, kills Lau, confronts Fred at lumber mill, surrenders to protect Chloe

# NCIS S24E06 Summary

## Episode plot
Torres has been deep undercover for months with the Ward Eight Kings, a four-month-old drug gang, when federal witness Stuart Prince is abducted from witness protection. Torres's phones are found ditched at his undercover apartment, and Deputy U.S. Marshal Leslie Votel accuses NCIS of kidnapping her witness. Director Tony DiNozzo defends Torres, but Parker is worried—Torres's old handler Walt Keller offered him a CIA job, and Parker gave him the Ward Eight Kings case partly to keep him at NCIS.

The investigation reveals Prince was an accountant who exposed a ghost-gun trafficking ring; his testimony put nine men away, and Frankie Dove (paroled three months ago, Great Falls, Virginia) is the prime suspect. NCIS interviews Dove, whose wife Crystal lets slip that someone else took Prince. Meanwhile Torres, undercover as "Nico" in the kidnappers' crew (Zach and Dylan), is forced to drive Prince to a meet. Zach and Dylan are murdered in cold blood—GSR clears Torres—and Torres leaves a coded poker hand (A-A-Q-6-3-2-7) to signal the team. Prince, who has arithmomania (counting OCD), reveals he leaked his own location: he wanted to be kidnapped so he could see Crystal again, the love of his life who is now with Frankie Dove.

Crystal turns out to be the arms dealer running the stolen-gun business Dove left behind. She arranges a Hezbollah weapons deal in Paraguay in two days and uses Torres and Prince to lure in ex-CIA enforcer "John Smith" (wet work in China, Venezuela, Myanmar, Cuba; fired for drinking), whom she shoots in the abdomen when he threatens the deal by bringing federal attention. Torres keeps Smith alive with Jimmy Palmer's phone-guided first aid, cuffs Prince to a sign, and NCIS arrests Crystal and Dove. Smith escapes before police arrive and may have died of his wound; a BOLO is out.

At the end, Parker decides to let Torres join the Paraguay sting operation, using a road-trip-movie metaphor ("the movie's not over"), while Torres reassures Knight he isn't leaving NCIS ("ride or die"). Separately, DiNozzo hasn't rejected Kasie Hines's podcast proposal ("From the Slab to the Lab"), though DoD/DOJ/ONI/JAG lawyers will review it; Kasie orders studio gear. McGee notes his fictional robot KC-3000 is based on Kasie and that he once got into forensics via CSI.
