# Hell's Kitchen (US) — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Verification
- **Title**: Hell's Kitchen (American TV series) / Hell's Kitchen US
- **Format**: Reality competition cooking show
- **First air date**: May 30, 2005
- **Broadcaster**: Fox (US)
- **Seasons**: 24 (as of 2025)
- **Episodes**: 378+ (ongoing)
- **Runtime**: 41–44 minutes
- **Production**: A. Smith & Co. Productions / ITV Studios

## Synopsis
Celebrity chef Gordon Ramsay hosts a high-pressure culinary boot camp where 12–20 aspiring chefs compete for a head chef position at a Ramsay restaurant plus a $250,000 salary. Each season follows a progressive elimination format: contestants are split into two teams (traditionally women on **Red Team**, men on **Blue Team**), face weekly challenges and dinner services, and nominate peers for elimination. Ramsay makes the final dismissal decision. At the final 5–6 chefs, teams merge into **Black Jackets** for individual competition until two finalists remain. The winner earns the executive chef role.

## Relationships & Hierarchy

```
Gordon Ramsay (Head Chef / Host)
├── Red Team Sous-Chef  ← mentors women's team
├── Blue Team Sous-Chef ← mentors men's team
├── Maître d'           ← front-of-house, guest management
└── Contestants
    ├── Red Team (red jackets)
    ├── Blue Team (blue jackets)
    └── Black Jackets (merged, individual competition)
```

- **Sous-chefs** enforce standards, assist in challenges, run pass during services.
- **Maître d'** manages dining room, relays tickets, handles VIPs.
- **Team dynamic**: collaboration during service; conflict during nominations.
- **Ramsay's catchphrase dismissals**: "Shut it down," "Get out," "You're done."

## World Setting

- **Primary location**: Purpose-built studio restaurant "Hell's Kitchen" with dual identical kitchens, dining room, dormitory, and pantry.
- **Locations by era**:
  - Seasons 1–18, 21–22: Los Angeles, California (warehouse studio)
  - Seasons 19–20: Las Vegas, Nevada (Caesars properties)
  - Seasons 23–present: Ledyard, Connecticut (Foxwoods Resort Casino)
- **Restaurant façade**: Simulates fine-dining service; real diners (not actors) seated for dinner services.
- **Prizes**: Head chef position at a Ramsay restaurant (varies by season) + $250,000 salary.

## Speaking Styles

### Gordon Ramsay
- **Explosive, profane (bleeped), direct**: "You donkey!", "Panicked mess!", "Raw!", "Shut it down!"
- **Vocabulary**: donkey, muppet, plank, pillock, clown, disaster, embarrassment, joke
- **Patterns**: Rhetorical questions ("What are you?"), repetition for emphasis ("Raw. Raw. RAW."), second-person confrontation
- **Softer moments**: Quiet encouragement at black-jacket stage; fatherly pride at finale
- **Translation tone**: Sharp, authoritative, colloquial; retain "Chef" as "主厨"; profanity → bleeped or "该死/见鬼" equivalents

### Contestants
- **Confessionals**: Reflective, strategic, emotional; "I need to step up," "This is my dream"
- **In-kitchen**: "Yes, Chef," "Heard, Chef," "Behind," "Walking in [protein]"
- **Nominations**: Blame-shifting or ownership; "I put myself forward," "They messed up"

### Sous-Chefs
- **Calm, technical**: "Walk with me," "Taste this," "Fix the seasoning"
- **Occasional sternness**: "Eyes up," "Communicate"

### Maître d'
- **Polished, professional**: "Table 4, two minutes," "Right away, Chef"
- **Guest-facing**: Warm, apologetic for delays

### Narrator (Jason Thompson)
- **Dramatic, present-tense**: "As the red team crumbles…", "With minutes remaining…"
- **Episode bookends**: "Previously on Hell's Kitchen…", "Next time…"

## Cultural Context for Translation

1. **US Reality-TV Conventions**: Confessionals ("talking heads"), dramatic music stings, "coming up" teasers, recap montages — preserve pacing cues.
2. **Culinary French Terms**: *mise en place*, *à la minute*, *mise en bouche*, *amuse-bouche*, *sous-vide*, *flambé*, *chiffonade*, *brunoise* — keep English or transliterate (e.g., "米苏安普拉斯") per audience familiarity.
3. **Measurement Units**: Fahrenheit oven temps, ounces/pounds, cups — add metric in parentheses if needed for CN audience.
4. **Censorship**: Profanity bleeped in US broadcast; Chinese platforms may require full sanitization ("混蛋" → "家伙", "狗屎" → "垃圾").
5. **Cultural References**: Pink's Hot Dogs, Vegas casinos, WWE cameos (S24), US geography (state-themed challenges) — annotate or localize.
6. **Gendered Team Split**: Traditional M/F split (broken in S1, S18, S21) — note when subverted.
7. **Ramsay's UK vs US Persona**: US edit amplifies rage; UK version more mentorship. Translate for US-edit tone.
8. **Product Placement**: HexClad, Spiceology, specific proteins — retain brand names.
