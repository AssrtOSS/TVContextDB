## S01E03

### S01E03 剧情梗概（Lucky）

- 前半段从犯罪网的两条线并行推进：一边是**John–Cary**早期运作残留的业务线，另一边是**Priscilla–Dutch**对嫌疑人 **Noah** 的施压。Noah 坚称不认识 **Cary**，被问到是否伪造新 ID 时否认；Priscilla 方面开始讨论若 Cary 抛弃妻子（或被迫做出选择）会有什么后果。  
- 这时 **John Armstrong（监狱来电）** 报警告 Lucky：局势在恶化，强调“先去安全处所”，并要求她能复述“下一步要在更安全地方接听”。  
- FBI 侧（**Rand / Gates**）追踪到一家拉斯维加斯租车记录：预订挂 **Priscilla** 名，疑似 Cary 留下，随后出现“车并非终点线索、很可能是掩护”的反向判断；Rand 与 Gates 因策略出现分歧（程序 vs 直觉）。
- Priscilla 与 Rand 的对峙升级：Rand 直指是 **“about you and me”**，提及她有一次联邦失误；Priscilla 还在场反杀信息对调试（“你要的是这条线”）  
- Lucky 在一场小孩生日会执行“隐身”式入场（孩子最难识别）拿礼物卡、钱包等现金线索，随后和同伴（John）有一段“魔术/伪装”式配合。  
- Lucky 转入“新身份”分支：找服务者改名造身份，表示自己要脱离现状（不想再和 Cary/旧身份绑定），并在对方解释下给出**可操作身份素材**（错误样本/样印件+真实地址用于绑定信用卡）。  
- John 在另一个关键来电中确认：Cary 已改名并办新身份证，还拿到地址，赌约是“你要拿回他偷走的所有钱”，并直接教她“进退有度，像 Zorro 一样来回一刀”，强调不做情绪化“对账”。  
- 夜间，Lucky 在酒吧与一名陌生人聊天，从“陌生认可比熟人更客观”“living well is best revenge”到后续“借口喝醉+FaceTime妻子”的敲诈性对峙，显现她在高压下仍能扭转局势。  
- 结尾处出现关键硬信息：**Wayne 介入**，确认 Cary 全款买下蓝色 Heritage Bronco（$10M 级别损失压力），并掌握其新身份资料；Rand 与 Wayne/Priscilla 侧向“谁能先拿下 Cary”博弈。

---

### 重要人物动作（本集重点）

- **Lucky**：继续使用“场景即身份”方法生存；先在社交场合低位渗透再转向高风险身份重建；同时保持行动—撤离节奏。  
- **John**：从监狱继续指挥，给出复盘式行动原则（先安全、后取证）；明确背叛后收款目标。  
- **Priscilla**：从“母性—威胁”双轨继续出场，逼问与甄别，默认自己的网络比警方更快更狠。  
- **Dutch**：执行者身份收紧，配合对 Noah 的身体逼问与威胁。  
- **Rand**：程序意识与直觉都在试错，既追车辆线索也被 Priscilla 反制；在情绪上被 John/Wayne压制。  
- **Wayne**：强调“零容忍”“不是钱的问题”，把追捕升级成清算级别。

### 连贯性要点（后续参考）

- Cary 的新身份已被确认存在，但是否“活人可控”尚未定论；他仍是多方追逐目标。  
- Priscilla 的网络能将“死胡同线索”快速转为其他判断，说明警方线索存在可被反向规避。  
- Lucky 已在公开场景建立“伪装社交+新身份筹备”双轨，后续可作为其反击基础，且 John 的“取回 stolen money”指令继续压实。  
- 法律与犯罪两端都已明确进入“个人仇恨+资源清算”叠加阶段，不再只是单纯追凶。

### 笑点 / 细节 / 参考彩蛋

- “**I see the room / not invited yet still invisible**”式语言把社交场景转成操盘空间。  
- “**I can read the room**”与“**你很漂亮**”的反差段落，既有性张力也有潜在威胁。

- “我不是律师，但要永久改变”一段，强化 Lucky 情绪线从“被害型嫁接”转向“主动重置”。

## S01E01

## 剧情概述

- Lucky 与丈夫 Cary 在拉斯维加斯完成一宗涉及数百万美元赃款的行动，原计划搭乘货机离开美国，开始新生活。
- 当晚两人庆祝并重宣婚誓；Lucky 隐约察觉计划有异，却被 Cary 安抚。
- 次日清晨，Lucky 醒来后发现 Cary、全部现金及他的个人物品都已消失，自己被单独留下。
- FBI 已追查这笔两年前从西海岸燃油骗局中消失的资金，并在赌场展开全国通缉行动。Lucky 伪装成清洁人员，利用装卸区卡车逃离。
- 她搭乘前往大峡谷的巴士，途中接到狱中父亲 John 的电话。FBI 很快锁定她的位置，但 Priscilla 及其手下 Dutch 抢先赶到。
- Priscilla 质问 Lucky 赃款和 Cary 的下落，Dutch 将她带回 Priscilla 的宅子。

## 重要角色行动

- **Lucky**：迅速伪装、观察出口，借陌生卡车司机脱身；没有现金和稳定身份，仍试图继续逃亡。
- **Cary**：带走全部资金并独自离开，留下 Lucky 承受 FBI 与犯罪集团的双重追捕。
- **Billie Rand**：负责赌场搜查和现场指挥，迅速调取监控、封锁出口，并判断 Lucky 的行动逻辑。
- **Eli Gates、Diego Cruz、Tom Mason**：参与赌场监控、交通路线和现场追捕。
- **John Armstrong**：人在隆波克联邦监狱，通过旧电话线路联系 Lucky，继续以“导师”身份指导她。
- **Priscilla Matheson**：从 FBI 手中截走 Lucky，要求她交代 Cary 和赃款的位置。
- **Dutch**：Priscilla 的老牌执行者，也是 Whittaker 的手下；负责抓捕 Lucky并将她带回宅子。

## 新建立的名字与术语

- Lucky 的正式姓名：**Luciana Armstrong**
- Lucky 的丈夫：**Cary Masterson**
- Lucky 的父亲：**John Armstrong**
- Cary 的母亲、犯罪集团首领：**Priscilla Matheson**
- FBI 探员：**Billie Rand、Eli Gates、Diego Cruz、Tom Mason**
- 犯罪网络人物：**Wayne Whittaker、Dutch**
- 关键地点：**拉斯维加斯、凯撒宫、隆波克联邦监狱、亚利桑那州、大峡谷**
- 关键案件：与黑帮有关的生物柴油骗局、西海岸燃油骗局；涉案资金可能超过一千万美元，原骗局据称骗取政府超过两亿美元。

## 连续性要点

- Cary 与 Lucky 的婚姻同时也是犯罪合作关系；两人原本计划靠这次行动彻底退出犯罪生活。
- Cary 离开时带走钱，却没有带走 Lucky，暗示他可能早有独立计划。
- Lucky 的衣物和物品仍在房间，Cary 的东西已消失，FBI 因此判断两人可能策略性分开或发生背叛。
- John 的犯罪训练深刻影响 Lucky：她擅长察言观色、操控谈话和临场脱身，却也难以摆脱父亲的声音。
- John 留给 Lucky 的核心准则是：**看清形势、谁都别信、不走捷径**。
- Priscilla 明确表示钱属于更高层的人；若资金无法追回，Lucky 与 John 都可能遭到杀害。
- Billie 判断 Lucky 回到赌场与 Dutch 有关，并指出她必须在 Dutch 找到她前被 FBI 找到。

## 笑点与参考

- Cary 把逃亡计划说成只剩六小时的“旧生活”，两人随后举杯庆祝并重宣婚誓，浪漫仪式与犯罪撤离形成反差。
- Lucky 对卡车司机谎称自己有一个“喝醉后会施暴的男友”；司机看穿这只是掩饰，却仍给她钱并帮助她。
- John 自称“没有人比你更会掌控全局”，Lucky 回应“今天可不是”，讽刺两人此刻一个在监狱、一个被追捕。
- Lucky 用“牢里的人都这么说”回应 John 的自信，John 以 “Touché” 接受反击。
- 片中多次出现“旧生活”“追着你最害怕的东西不放”等歌词，与 Lucky 的逃亡、背叛和身份创伤相呼应。

## 未解决线索

- Cary 带着钱去了哪里？他是否从一开始就打算抛弃 Lucky？
- 赃款真正属于谁，Wayne Whittaker、Priscilla 与 Cary 各自掌握多少信息？
- Cary 是否出卖了 Lucky 与 John，或只是独自逃跑？
- Lucky 能否从 Priscilla 的宅子逃脱？
- FBI 能否在 Dutch 之前找到她？
- John 是否知道更多关于这笔钱、Priscilla 及 Lucky 当前处境的内幕？

## S01E04

## Episode Plot

Lucky meets FBI Special Agent Billie Rand, who reveals that John is in danger and asks Lucky to help bring down Priscilla and Wayne Whittaker. In exchange, Billie offers protection and possible immunity for John and Cary.

Lucky finds Cary at his La Jolla beach house. He admits that he drugged her, stole the money, and abandoned her because he believed Priscilla would kill them both. Cary explains that he had cooperated with the FBI and that John’s theft from Wayne created the crisis. Lucky refuses to give Cary the cryptocurrency seed phrase.

The FBI raids the house. Lucky and Cary escape together in a stolen vehicle, evade a helicopter and police perimeter, and temporarily disappear on foot. They are intercepted by Priscilla and Dutch. During the confrontation, Cary is shot. He dies in Lucky’s arms—or appears to die—while the FBI closes in.

## Important Character Actions

- Lucky negotiates with Billie rather than accepting a deal immediately; she demands full immunity for both John and Cary.
- Cary admits he planned to take the money from Lucky before their escape and claims he acted to protect her.
- Cary reveals that Lucky was never charged or interrogated after the earlier federal raid, increasing suspicion around her cooperation with law enforcement.
- Priscilla is released from prison after two years, apparently through Wayne’s lawyers.
- Priscilla pressures Cary to recover Wayne’s missing money, claiming John had been skimming from Wayne for years.
- Lucky uses her improvisational skills to evade the FBI, steal another car, manipulate a bystander, and escape the initial perimeter.
- Priscilla and Dutch locate the fugitives; Cary is shot during the final pursuit.
- Billie appears to shoot or confront Priscilla amid the chaos, but the aftermath is unclear.

## Newly Established Names and Terms

- Special Agent Billie Rand: FBI investigator pursuing Priscilla and the fugitives.
- Eli Gates: FBI agent involved in the La Jolla operation.
- Wayne Whittaker: the higher-level crime figure whose money John stole and whose organization threatens Cary.
- La Jolla: location of Cary’s beach house and the FBI raid.
- Seed phrase: the cryptocurrency recovery phrase controlling the digitized stolen money.
- Temple City: a past criminal operation Lucky and John survived.
- “Into the Black”: song used over the closing sequence.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Priscilla’s gasoline scheme involves buying fuel wholesale without state taxes, then reselling it through shell companies at full price.
- Priscilla routinely arranges for others to take the blame; Cary and John are currently exposed as fall guys.
- John has allegedly been skimming money from Wayne for years.
- Cary says he cooperated with the FBI because he could not find another way to protect himself and Lucky.
- Lucky was arrested during the earlier operation but was never charged or interrogated.
- Cary knew about Lucky’s escape plan before they left Las Vegas and intended to take the money from her.
- The money was converted into digital form, and the seed phrase is stored with Lucky; without it, the funds may be inaccessible.
- Lucky and Cary still have strong feelings for each other despite the betrayal, but trust between them is severely damaged.
- Lucky’s deal with Billie may have been based on incomplete or misleading assurances: Billie later says Cary was too deeply involved to protect.
- Cary’s apparent death leaves the money, the seed phrase, and Lucky’s legal status unresolved.

## Jokes and Useful References

- Cary jokes that Lucky is still the better driver, despite her chaotic escape driving.
- Lucky mocks Cary’s attempt to act charming after betraying her and jokes that he should choose a softer place if he hits her again.
- Cary says he still needs Lucky to kill spiders for him, underlining that he is less hardened than Lucky and John.
- Priscilla sarcastically suggests Cary and Lucky could move to Fresno and work at Starbucks, using an ordinary life as an insult and a fantasy of escape.
- Lucky’s “fresh deck” metaphor echoes the episode’s central question: can she start over, or is she condemned to keep playing the hand dealt by John and the criminal world?

## S01E05

# Lucky S01E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
The episode picks up immediately after the heist gone wrong. Lucky, posing as "Amanda Turner," plays the devoted granddaughter at a hospital where her "grandmother" Ruth Turner is critical after a staged incident. FBI Agent Billie Rand visits, and her boss Kershaw reveals **Cary Masterson is dead** (gunshot wound) while Lucky remains at large. Lucky flees to a motel, spins a cover story to a sympathetic stranger at a bar (claiming her husband died in a car wreck), then faces **Priscilla Matheson** at the beach house. Priscilla blames Lucky for Cary's death, reveals Cary stole from her, and gives Lucky an ultimatum: recover the money or face lethal consequences. Meanwhile, Billie and Gates review hospital footage placing Lucky at the scene washing blood off her arm. In a calculated move, **Billie arranges John Armstrong's early prison release** (8 years before parole) to use him as bait. Lucky calls John, confirms she has the money, and arranges a meet at their old elevated spot ("the high one") — where the FBI arrests John.

## Important Character Actions
- **Lucky**: Maintains "Amanda Turner" alias at hospital; faints from adrenaline crash; confronts Priscilla defiantly, admitting she can't keep running; reveals she has the stolen money; lures John to the meet knowing FBI watches.
- **Priscilla**: Coldly orders Cary's cremation; confronts Lucky with "you fucked me" / "we were pawns" exchange; threatens next meeting won't be cordial; asserts Cary was "dead the minute he stole that money."
- **Billie Rand**: Visits Lucky at hospital; orchestrates John's release via DOJ favor ("between me, you, and the wall"); uses John as bait despite Gates' objections.
- **John Armstrong**: Released unexpectedly; suspicious of Billie's motive ("she's using you to smoke me out"); meets Lucky at the high spot; arrested on arrival.
- **Gates**: Cynical partner; pushes Billie to rest; makes the DOJ call under protest.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Peter Kershaw** — Billie's FBI supervisor (first appearance).
- **"The high one" / "old place"** — Lucky and John's elevated meetup spot (continuity reference).
- **Dana** — Billie's daughter (referenced in hospital footage review).
- **Muscle relaxers / "groggy"** — Lucky's hospital medication (plot device for memory-recall scene).

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Cary Masterson confirmed dead** (gunshot wound, DOA).
- **Money location**: Cary was the only one who knew where it was hidden; Lucky now claims she has it.
- **John's prison sentence**: 8 years remaining until parole; release was highly irregular ("never" happens).
- **Priscilla's leverage over John**: She implies she knew what would happen "if he stole from me" — suggesting John previously crossed her.
- **Billie's personal stake**: Divorced, missed daughter's birthday, fears "fucking her up" — parallels Lucky/John dynamic.
- **Lucky's mnemonic**: "Apple, hamster, karma, snow, holiday, sunset, pledge, turkey, identity, purpose, fossil, mushroom" — a memory anchor (possibly for aliases or safe locations).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **Bar stranger's "Yoda" jab** — Lucky's philosophical "cold is absence of heat / evil is distance from good" monologue gets a pop-culture callback.
- **"Cowork-relax"** — Billie and Gates' banter about working while injured becomes a running dynamic.
- **"Fuck that guy" toast** — Shared venting ritual between Lucky and the bar stranger; echoes Lucky/Cary intimacy.
- **Priscilla's "nickel-and-dime schemes"** — Dismissive callback to John's low-level cons; reinforces class hierarchy in the crime world.

## Unresolved Threads
- **Where is the money really?** Lucky says she has it; Priscilla doesn't believe her; John may know.
- **Who actually killed Cary?** Priscilla implies she ordered it ("he was dead the minute he stole"), but the shooter is unseen.
- **John's arrest**: Was it Billie's plan all along, or did Lucky anticipate it and use John anyway?
- **Lucky's next move**: She's now exposed (Priscilla knows she's local; FBI has John; no clear escape route).
- **Ruth Turner's fate**: The "grandmother" in surgery — was she a real victim or part of Lucky's con?
- **Dana's testimony**: She saw Lucky washing blood in the hospital bathroom; could identify Lucky's injury/alias.

## S01E02


Episode S01E02 — "Lucky" (Apple TV+)

Plot
The episode opens in flashback with a younger Lucky and her father John belting David Bowie's "Modern Love" in a car, establishing their close, performative bond. The story then splits into three braided tracks:

- John in prison receives an unscheduled visit from Priscilla, who has just been released. She wants the missing $10M (Cary's heist money) returned so she can pay off Wayne Whittaker and "save both their kids." She implies Lucky will die if she doesn't "play ball." John, smug and unrepentant, suggests Lucky is already playing a game Priscilla hasn't figured out. He admits "now and again" he misses her.

- Lucky, on foot after the previous episode's car crash, is found in the desert by a grandmother, Sylvia, who lives off-grid with her two granddaughters. Lucky uses her standard cover: abusive-husband story, car pushed her out, he's a cop, she can't go back. Sylvia, whose own daughter died in a similar abusive relationship, takes her in, gives her a shower, food, and offers to drive her toward Las Vegas.

- The FBI investigation. Rand and Gates examine the burned car from S01E01: two dead men, a shell-company plate, and the scene is ~30 miles from where Agent Tom Mason was hit by a car (Mason is now out of surgery; his field career is over). Assistant Director Peter confronts Rand: she ran a Vegas op without authorization, came up empty, and the dead bodies keep stacking up around Priscilla. Rand's theory: John skimmed a "nest egg" from Whittaker's dirty money during the original fossil-fuel scam, stashed it, and is now (with Priscilla out) trying to move it — calling Lucky to retrieve it. Whittaker has dispatched Priscilla to chase the same money. The AD warns Rand: "Don't make her your white whale again."

- Priscilla and Dutch's hunt. Dutch tracks a K-9 trail that goes dead at a coyote carcass. Priscilla personally visits a man ("Noah") at a Long Beach address found in Cary's rental car (a Subaru Forester parked at the Pike's Outlet mall for a day); when he claims ignorance, Dutch beats and kills him. Priscilla then phones Wayne Whittaker for more time, is rebuffed, and Whittaker explicitly threatens Cary.

- The FBI catches up. Agent Rand, driving out from Vegas, knocks on Sylvia's door. Sylvia lies; Lucky hides in the girls' bedroom, wins the kids' trust with a coin-trick ("Can you guys keep a secret?"), and they keep quiet. Rand, suspicious of a fourth dish on the drying rack, presses Sylvia — then softens and asks to come in for water. She shows Lucky's photo, warns Sylvia she's dangerous (killed two men, burned their car), and instructs Sylvia to lure the girls out with ice cream. Lucky overhears, locks the bedroom door, and ultimately escapes in Sylvia's truck — Rand fires her weapon at the fleeing vehicle.

- The episode ends in flashback to a gas station/convenience store. Young Lucky steals a brown F-150, burning a scratcher ticket. In the present, a desperate Lucky flees a roadblock and ends up at a gas station, where Dutch corners her, demanding the money and Cary. She stalls; a 911 call and a convenience-store fire break out; Dutch is shot by police and the stolen F-150 is rammed. Lucky drives off wounded. Final voiceover: John teaching her, "You're only in danger if you're not in control. We're in control," over "Goodbye Sunshine" playing as she escapes.

Key character actions
- Lucky: deploys the classic "abused wife pushed out of a car" con on Sylvia; later improvises a coin-trick bond with the granddaughters; steals Sylvia's truck to escape Rand; eludes Dutch at the gas station.
- John: taunts Priscilla in prison, refuses to give up Lucky's location, hints he warned Lucky first.
- Priscilla: visits John uninvited, beats/kills Noah in Long Beach, begs Wayne Whittaker for more time, struggles with Dutch's insubordination (he half-chokes her, mocking her for still thinking "sex enters into the equation").
- Dutch: tracks Lucky via bloodhound until a dead coyote; kills Noah; corners Lucky at the gas station; is shot by responding officers.
- Agent Billie Rand: deduces the John-Armstrong-nest-egg theory; gets dressed-down by the AD but is given "some rope" to keep going.
- Sylvia: rescues Lucky, lies to Rand, then is manipulated into calling the girls out with ice cream; her fourth plate gives the game away.

Newly established names/terms
- Sylvia — the desert grandmother (full name not yet given; the Lita to granddaughters Elie and a younger sister).
- Elie (older granddaughter) and "Nenas" (collective pet name) — children of Sylvia's late daughter.
- Toby the bunny — household pet / grandmother's "wild-goose-chase" decoy.
- Agent Tom Mason — the FBI agent hit by a car in S01E01; now hospitalized, career over.
- Assistant Director Peter — Rand's boss at the FBI.
- Subaru Forester — Cary's rental, GPS-located by Priscilla to the Pike's Outlet mall in Long Beach.
- Wayne Whittaker — confirmed as the higher-level crime boss bankrolling the original "fossil fuel scam" (worth a half-billion dollars); now threatening Cary directly.
- The "nest egg" / Whittaker money — the MacGuffin: $10M (or more) that John skimmed and stashed, which Priscilla, Dutch, Whittaker, Rand, and Lucky are all hunting.
- Mohave County / Kingman, AZ (~40 miles south) — confirmed desert setting.
- "Lita" — Spanish term the granddaughters use for their grandmother.
- The fossil-fuel scam — new exposition: the original $500M laundering scheme from which John diverted a cut.
- "Lucky 10" scratchers — a small but pointed prop gag (Lucky playing a lottery game called Lucky while on the run).

Continuity-relevant facts
- Priscilla is newly released from prison; this is her first scene interacting with John since his incarceration.
- The burned car scene from S01E01 is confirmed: two dead male attackers, fake ID / Delaware shell-company plates — Rand attributes it to Lucky.
- Cary's whereabouts: still unknown. His rental (the Subaru) is in Long Beach at a mall; Dutch is en route there.
- The $10M figure is now the explicit target; previously it was "the money."

## S01E07

# S01E07 Summary — Lucky

## Episode Plot

The episode opens with a flashback of young Lucky being coached by John through a tense scenario — he insists the plan will work, she pushes back saying he can't know that, and he tells her "the field changes, but the game is the same."

In the present, Lucky is wired up and positioned at a remote gas terminal to meet Wayne Whittaker, working as an FBI informant. Agents Rand and Kershaw brief her: she must get Whittaker to verbally confirm awareness of the gas scam and that he profited from it. Lucky pushes back on the safe word concept, noting it "probably means it's already too late."

At the meet, Lucky faces Whittaker directly for the first time. He is calm and menacing — he calls her a "two-bit hustler," threatens to have his men cut her open for the crypto seed phrase, and reveals he had a son with someone but rejected fatherhood ("That was your choice, not mine"). Lucky learns from Whittaker that Priscilla killed her partner (Billie's partner, implying an FBI agent death) and that Whittaker has been operating through corruption within the FBI itself.

The critical turn: Lucky deduces that Kershaw is a mole working for Whittaker. She reveals this mid-meeting — Kershaw has been feeding Whittaker information, including the FBI's surveillance position. She blows the operation open, forcing a three-way standoff: Lucky had Rand listening in, Whittaker had Kershaw, and both sides must now negotiate in the open. Gates disarms Kershaw at gunpoint.

Whittaker then reveals he has captured John as leverage. He holds John hostage and demands the seed phrase, threatening to kill Lucky in front of her father. John defiantly tells Whittaker to shoot him, saying "I'm the one who stole from you." Lucky refuses to hand over the seed phrase. Wayne orders Dutch to beat John. Priscilla then arrives unexpectedly, escalating the standoff. She is furious — she tells John this is his mess to clean up, then orders Dutch to hit John again.

Priscilla ultimately kills Whittaker (stabbing/shooting him in the chaos). In the aftermath, Priscilla confronts John, blaming him for everything. Lucky pleads for their lives: "Give me a chance. Or you can kill us, but I'm done running." Priscilla — in an unexpected gesture — lets them go, gives them the car, and says "I didn't do it for you." She keeps the money. Lucky tells Priscilla "I hope you use it wisely."

The episode's emotional core follows: Lucky and John sit together at a diner eating burgers. John shares a recurring prison dream where he wraps newborn Lucky in his sheets and walks out of prison together into the sun. Lucky shares her own recurring dream — she's underwater and can't breathe. John says it means "not having enough time to do the things you want to do."

The final confrontation: Lucky confronts John with a devastating memory from their past — a swimming pool incident where young Lucky nearly drowned. John had intentionally put her at risk to run a con (suing the hotel for free rooms). He didn't care that she couldn't swim. Lucky tells him: "The best cons are 90% truth, right, Dad? And the truth is I was drowning." She realizes John has always been working an angle, even with her. She tells him she loves him but won't see him again, and walks away. The episode closes with Lucky leaving John behind, and Billie Rand confronting Lucky one final time — Rand admits she'll keep pursuing her, but lets her go for now.
