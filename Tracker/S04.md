## S04E01

Episode plot

Colter Shaw takes a case from 15-year-old Charlie Wooledge, whose father Sean — a San Pedro construction worker — left home after suspicious work texts and never returned. Sean's phone is off, and he was secretly texting Bern Maddox, a paroled ex-con (extortion/assault), who admits selling Sean a gun to protect "his girl," Stephanie Rodriguez, a port supervisor. Sean's younger son Hank is home alone; Charlie kept quiet partly because a prior CPS report (when Sean left sick Hank home alone) threatened to split the brothers up.

The trail leads to a house where port gate clerk Brent Russo — Stephanie's ex — lies dead after a struggle. Blood spatter indicates Sean was dragged away, and Stephanie is also missing. Detective Kao takes the homicide, which raises the stakes: if Sean's children are found without a guardian, they could enter protective custody. Reenie Green's federal check ties Russo to Russian organized-crime figure Archer Litvak, who is trafficking people in "ghost containers" — unrecorded shipping containers. Russo needed Stephanie's supervisor access to delete port records so a container could disappear.

Colter finds Sean tied up at a container yard; Stephanie has been taken. At Yard 4, Colter rescues Stephanie as federal agents close in on Litvak's crew. Sean reunites with Charlie and Hank and introduces Stephanie as his girlfriend. Colter waives his fee, telling Charlie to stay in school and "settle up" after graduation.

Important character actions

- Charlie Wooledge: contacts Colter via Reddit, downplays his age and role, follows Colter to the welding shop, hides Hank's situation to avoid CPS.
- Colter Shaw: takes the case, keeps police out initially, runs the Maddox lead, interprets the murder scene as involving a fourth party, rescues Stephanie at Yard 4, reunites Sean with his sons.
- Sean Wooledge: left after suspecting texts, bought a gun from Maddox to protect Stephanie, tried to confront the traffickers, was captured; Stephanie was taken after him.
- Stephanie Rodriguez: pressured by ex Russo to delete container records for Litvak's smuggling operation, kidnapped, rescued.
- Bern Maddox: sells Sean a gun, on parole, fearful of blowback.
- Reenie Green: checks federal files on Russo, links him to Litvak, coordinates police/federal response.
- Randy: provides phone, vehicle, and partial-plate lookups; calls Maddox a "bad dude."
- Detective Kao: works the homicide scene, shares information with Colter.

Continuity-relevant facts

- Colter works as a reward-seeking tracker who finds people; cases arrive via word-of-mouth and Reddit.
- Colter's father died when he was young; he helped raise a younger sister, which he uses to bond with Charlie.
- Colter is divorced; he and Reenie joke about hit men and shovels regarding his ex.
- Colter's office was sold by the landlord; he is in a temporary space and wants his own place once settlement money arrives.
- Reenie references the Praiter-Rockwell file: a wealthy family (millions in assets, company voting stake) playing dirty — an ongoing complicated case.
- Reenie tries to set Colter up with Adam Shevlin, an old school acquaintance; Colter declines, calling it a "very long story."
- Sean's children face CPS/protective-custody risk without a guardian — a recurring lever.
- Federal agents move on Litvak's crew by episode end; rescued container victims are being returned to families.
