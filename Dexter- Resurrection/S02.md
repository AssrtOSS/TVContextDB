## S02E01

## Episode Summary: Dexter: Resurrection S02E01

### Plot
Dexter Morgan, now driving for UrCar in New York, tracks **Al Walker (a.k.a. "Rapunzel")** — a serial killer who cuts off victims' ponytails and travels the country with his family in an RV. Dexter kills Al in an abandoned auto body shop in Pennsylvania, missing Harrison's police academy graduation. Meanwhile, a new threat emerges: the **Five Borough Killer** calls in a threat to kill at a Mets game; a woman is shot dead when the game is postponed. **Joey Quinn** arrives at NYPD Homicide asking about Angel Batista.

### Important Character Actions
- **Dexter**: Kills Al Walker (Rapunzel); misses Harrison's graduation; wrestles with desire for recognition ("midlife crisis" per Harry).
- **Harrison**: Graduates police academy, joins NYPD Homicide; shares location with Dexter; struck out comically at a bar.
- **Angel Batista (via phone)**: Calls Dexter with a lead on the New York Ripper in Five Points.
- **Sam McCormick**: Receives another taunting call from the NY Ripper about his daughter Rachel (killed 15 years ago).
- **Don Framt**: Interrogated for the 9th time as NY Ripper suspect; lawyer shuts it down — no hard evidence, file from Prater's vault may be planted (0.83% error probability).
- **Joey Quinn**: Shows up at NYPD asking about former Miami Metro colleague Angel Batista.

## S02E04

# S02E04 Summary

## Episode Plot

The episode centers on two parallel threads: Dexter vetting the new **JawSplitter** (John Thomas Jr., "JJ") at a Prater gathering, and the NYPD hunting the **Five Borough Killer (FBK)**.

**JawSplitter thread:** The original JawSplitter (John Thomas Sr.) has been dead two years. His 21-year-old son JJ appears at Prater's townhouse claiming the mantle, boasting four kills since his father's death. Dexter spends the day with JJ — pizza, a boat ride on the *Slice of Life Too*, conversation — to determine if he fits the Code. JJ reveals his father groomed him from age 10 (blood draws, forced to assist kills at 14). JJ shows Dexter his father's preserved jaw with silicone implants, admitting he killed him because Sr. was becoming a liability (memory lapses, weakness). Dexter realizes JJ killed to *replace* his father, not escape him. Dexter reveals he is the Bay Harbor Butcher, then executes JJ (M99, then kill).

**FBK thread:** The killer calls 911, announces a subway murder. NYPD floods every train car (44.75% of force). No kill occurs on the subway, but a homeless addict dies in the Bronx — the borough FBK hadn't hit yet. Toxicology reveals **thallium** ("the poisoner's poison"), a homicide mimicking natural death. FBK's methods are now inconsistent: shooting (victim 1), roof push (victim 2), poisoning (victim 3). Claudette Wallace is pulled off the Ripper case.

## Important Character Actions

- **Dexter**: Vets JJ all day; accesses police database (via Harrison) to check JJ's sealed juvenile record; reveals true identity to JJ before killing him; debates Harry (hallucination) about Harrison's path.
- **Harrison**: Runs database check for Dexter; makes first arrest (draws weapon); partners with Claudette; buys drinks with coworkers.
- **Quinn**: Develops theory that Prater met killers directly, bought trophies via shell company ($500k annual withdrawals stopped when Prater vanished, restarted when Sleepy-Eyed Stranger appeared).
- **Charlotte Brown** (Prater's ex-security chief): Warns Dexter to walk away; reveals Angel Batista died pushing through her security.
- **JJ**: Confesses four kills; shows father's jaw; admits patricide for succession; tries to manipulate Dexter with "victim" narrative.
- **Claudette**: Orders full tox panel on Bronx victim; confirms FBK struck again; removed from Ripper case.
