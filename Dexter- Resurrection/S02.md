## Episode Summary: Dexter: Resurrection S02E01

### Plot
Dexter Morgan, now driving for UrCar in New York, tracks **Al Walker (a.k.a. "Rapunzel")** — a serial killer who cuts off victims' ponytails and travels the country with his family in an RV. Dexter kills Al in an abandoned auto body shop in Pennsylvania, missing Harrison's police academy graduation. Meanwhile, a new threat emerges: the **Five Borough Killer** calls in a threat to kill at a Mets game; a woman is shot dead when the game is postponed. **Joey Quinn** arrives at NYPD Homicide asking about Angel Batista.

### Important Character Actions
- **Dexter**: Kills Al Walker (Rapunzel); misses Harrison's graduation; wrestles with desire for recognition ("midlife crisis" per Harry).
- **Harrison**: Graduates police academy, joins NYPD Homicide; shares location with Dexter; struck out comically at a bar.
- **Angel Batista (via phone)**: Calls Dexter with a lead on the New York Ripper in Five Points.
- **Sam McCormick**: Receives another taunting call from the NY Ripper about his daughter Rachel (killed 15 years ago).
- **Don Framt**: Interrogated for the 9th time as NY Ripper suspect; lawyer shuts it down — no hard evidence, file from Prater's vault may be planted (0.83% error probability).
- **Joey Quinn**: Shows up at NYPD asking about former Miami Metro colleague Angel Batista.
