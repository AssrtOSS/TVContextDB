## S01E05 — Christmas Eve at Chénoise

### Episode Plot
Christmas Eve at the prison. Corinne volunteers on her night off to give inmates a "normal" Christmas despite budget cuts (empty generator, power strip overload). A subplot involves missing inmate **Lopez** ("the Legionnaire"), who turns out to be hiding in the workshop dressed as Santa, helping run the party. The episode ends with Corinne's estranged son **Jonathan** appearing at the gate, and inmate **Samir Fayed** ordered released due to a procedural error.

### Important Character Actions
- **Corinne**: Insists on keeping Christmas running despite power failure; gets confronted by Sister Valentine that her presence reminds inmates of oppression/racism; takes advice from a volunteer (who turns out to be a mother who catfished her own son) to cut ties with that woman — unknowingly describing her own situation with Jonathan.
- **Jeff**: Promoted to Senior Corrections Officer; cries uncontrollably; left in charge for the night; plugs all decorations into one strip causing blackout.
- **JP**: Bonds with Lopez over spilled coffee, recruits him as Santa; raps poorly; leads search with dog **Croquette**.
- **Stéphanie**: Gets drunk on smuggled vodka (from oranges), melts down during family visits, accuses an inmate of being at fault for his own imprisonment.
- **Lopez**: Found in workshop upset about spending Christmas alone; becomes Santa, saves the party mood.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Croquette** — drug-sniffing/search dog (Philou's, borrowed for the night)
- **Sister Valentine** — head of Catholic volunteer group
- **Sabryina** — inmate's online "girlfriend" (almost certainly a catfish)
- **Samir Fayed** — inmate with procedural error in file, ordered released
- **Jonathan** — Corinne's son (first on-screen appearance at gate)
- **"The Corinne method"** — shorthand for her idealistic, hands-on management style

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Jeff's promotion is now official (Senior Officer).
- Generator has no fuel — warden says too expensive; power company not coming until morning.
- Corinne's son Jonathan has been estranged; he shows up at the gate saying "my girlfriend helped me realize you've only got one mom" (Sabryina name matches the catfish).
- Samir Fayed's release order creates immediate paperwork urgency.

### Jokes / References for Later
- **Vodka-in-oranges** smuggling trick (syringes) — Michel tastes it.
- **JP's rap** ("Yo, fellas, I'm losin' my mind") — becomes a recurring bit.
- **Jeff's 15-year decoration** labeled "FOR MOM JONATHAN" — foreshadows son.
- **Truth or Dare** game in break room — Stéphanie's dare (eat something gross) interrupted.
- **Lioness metaphor** from drunk Stéphanie ("I was raised by my mom and grandma… I'm also a lioness").
- Michel's "Jewish thief" line on non-Jewish inmate — "Can't say anything anymore."

### Unresolved Threads
- **Jonathan at the gate** — Corinne hasn't seen him in years; he mentions Sabryina (the catfish).
- **Sabryina catfish** — the volunteer Corinne talked to *is* the catfish mother; Corinne doesn't realize it's about her.
- **Samir Fayed release** — procedural error found; will he walk tonight?
- **Generator/power** — still out, fuel still unfunded.
- **Stéphanie's meltdown** — filmed by inmate; potential disciplinary fallout.
