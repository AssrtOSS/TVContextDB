## S06E03

## Episode Plot
The episode covers the night of Jesus’s arrest and the illegal Sanhedrin trial that follows. It opens with a flashback to Mary and Joseph bringing infant Jesus to Jerusalem for His dedication, where they reunite with Rachel (mother of John the Baptist) and meet her son Samuel (“God has heard”). The main timeline begins after Jesus is seized in Gethsemane: His mother Mary and the women disciples search for Him while the Eleven hide in Jerusalem. Caiaphas convenes a nighttime proceeding — illegal under Torah law — stacking hostile witnesses to secure a charge that will force Pilate’s hand. Witnesses testify about Jesus’s Sabbath healings, His Nazareth synagogue claim to fulfill Isaiah 61, and the temple cleansing three days prior. Nicodemus and Joseph of Arimathea (Yussif) challenge the trial’s legality and prepare a formal defense for the dawn assembly. Meanwhile Peter denies Jesus three times as the rooster crows; Judas is confronted by the other disciples and insists he was obeying Jesus’s command (“do what you’re going to do quickly”). Caiaphas adjourns the session with a verdict: Jesus will be delivered to Pilate, and His followers and their families are to be rounded up.

## Important Character Actions
- **Mary (Jesus’s mother)** confronts temple guards, refuses to be silenced, and searches the city with other women.
- **Caiaphas** orchestrates a rigged night hearing, reserves sole questioning rights, and pushes for a political charge (temple destruction = sedition) to compel Roman execution.
- **Nicodemus (Saba)** exposes procedural illegality, discredits inconsistent witnesses, and tasks Yussif with bringing the scribe Eli bar Gideon to the morning assembly.
- **Joseph of Arimathea (Yussif)** rehearses legal arguments with Nicodemus, citing Torah precedent to prove the trial unlawful.
- **Peter** denies knowing Jesus three times; the rooster crows; he is later accused by the disciples of betrayal.
- **Judas** claims Jesus told him to “do it quickly” at the Last Supper; he believes he was forcing Jesus’s hand to reveal power, then realizes a third option: Jesus is the Son of God and will let it happen. He heads to the temple to “set things right.”
- **Disciples** agree to meet at the Court of Women at first cockcrow; Thomas is missing.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The trial is held **at night**, violating Torah procedure (capital cases must be tried by day; verdict cannot be same day).
- Charge shifted from blasphemy to **political sedition**: “threatening to destroy the temple” → delivered to Pilate as a threat to Roman order.
- Jesus’s earlier claims referenced: “Lord of the Sabbath” (Capernaum), “fulfillment of Isaiah 61” (Nazareth, two years prior), “destroy this temple and in three days raise it up” (temple cleansing, three days ago).
- **Judas’s betrayal** occurred at the Last Supper (“do what you’re going to do quickly”).
- **Peter’s denial** fulfilled Jesus’s prediction; rooster crows at dawn.
- **Nicodemus and Joseph of Arimathea** are secretly disciples, now mounting a legal defense at the formal morning assembly.
- **Followers and families** targeted for arrest; women hiding at Lazarus’s house in Bethany.
- **Thomas** is unaccounted for; left earlier but has not arrived.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- Joseph of Arimathea falling asleep during trial prep; Nicodemus: “Even the sleeping can see [the trial is illegal].”
- Yussif: “Everything in my life has been leading to this moment.” (Dramatic lawyer energy)
- Judas’s “third option” realization — foreshadows the crucifixion’s theological meaning.

## Unresolved Threads

- Can the disciples rescue the women at Bethany before the temple guard arrives?
- What will Judas do at the temple? (Return the money? Confront the priests?)
- Will Peter rejoin the group after his denial?
- Where is Thomas, and will he return?
- The formal dawn assembly: will any Sanhedrin members dissent publicly?
- Jesus’s silence throughout the trial — will He speak before Pilate?

## S06E04

# The Chosen S06E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Sanhedrin conducts a pre-dawn, procedurally illegitimate trial of Jesus. Nicodemus attempts a legal defense — challenging jurisdiction, demanding witnesses, quoting Psalm 2 and Proverbs 30:4 — but Caiaphas steamrolls the proceeding. Under oath before the living God, Jesus answers "I am" to being the Christ, the Son of God, and cites Daniel 7:13–14 ("Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power, coming with the clouds of heaven"). The council cries blasphemy and votes unanimously for death. Since only Rome can execute, Jesus is bound for Pilate. Meanwhile, Judas tries to return the blood money; the priests refuse it ("What is that to us?"). The disciples scatter in hiding — warrants now extend to all followers and their families. Saba suffers a heart attack after Matthias confirms he witnessed Lazarus raised. Nicodemus files a formal appeal (mistrial, prosecutorial error, gross misconduct) but the clerk notes the Sanhedrin won't reconvene for a week due to Passover.

## Important Character Actions
- **Nicodemus**: sole dissenting voice in the Sanhedrin; quotes Scripture to expose the trial as fulfillment of prophecy; files appeal afterward.
- **Caiaphas**: invokes "one man must die to save the nation" (John 11:50) as divine mandate; silences Nicodemus by force.
- **Jesus**: silent until adjured by the living God; then declares "I am" and the Daniel 7 vision.
- **Judas**: returns 30 pieces of silver, confessing "I have sinned by betraying innocent blood"; priests mock his late piety; he flees in despair.
- **Matthias**: testifies to Saba (and Zohara) that he saw Lazarus dead four days, then walk out of the tomb at Jesus' call.
- **Saba**: collapses with heart failure after realizing he spent years seeking disproof while his heart believed.
- **Zohara**: confronts Nicodemus about the missing Capernaum money (he gave it to Jesus), his hidden belief, and his exclusion of her from the journey.
- **Peter & Eden**: reunite; Peter argues restraint ("He told us to let Him be taken… someone has to stay alive so His message can spread"); Eden wants action.
- **Mary Magdalene (Zee)**: tries to leave to protect the women; physically restrained by James and the others.
- **Yussif (Joseph of Arimathea)**: supports Nicodemus; relays the verdict to the disciples.
- **Centurion Yanni**: dismisses Nicodemus's plea for Roman intervention ("rumors and fanciful stories").

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Agunah** (עגונה) — "chained woman" unable to remarry without husband's death proof; legislative subplot (Shimon vs. Shammai).
- **Mashiach** (משיח) — Hebrew "Messiah / Anointed One"; used in Psalm 2 quotation.
- **Cohen HaGadol** (כהן גדול) — High Priest title used formally in chamber.
- **Adonai El Roi** (אדני אל ראי) — "The God Who Sees Me" (Gen 16:13); invoked by Zohara to Saba.
- **Shiva** (שבעה) — seven-day mourning period; referenced for Lazarus (now ended because he's alive).
- **Song of David** — Psalm 23 ("The Lord is my Shepherd"); callback to Saba's earlier lesson to Matthias.
- **Agur son of Jakeh** — Proverbs 30:4 quoted in full by Nicodemus ("Who has ascended to heaven… what is His name, and His Son's name?").

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Lazarus resurrection** confirmed by multiple witnesses (followers + mourners); "word will travel fast."
- **Jesus walked on water** — cited by Yanni as rumor; confirmed as known miracle.
- **Disciples sent out two-by-two** — referenced in Peter/Jesus breakfast scene; Peter still adjusting to new name.
- **Passover timing** — Sanhedrin adjourns for a week; Jesus must go to Pilate immediately.
- **Sanhedrin factions** — Shammai infiltrating Herodians/Sadducees; Hillel's bloc losing ground.
- **Dathan (Batya's niece's husband)** — lost at sea three years prior; agunah case driver.
- **Missing Capernaum money** — Nicodemus gave a "significant sum" to Jesus; Zohara discovers via banker Hersh.
- **Warrants for disciples & families** — declared by Sanhedrin; Peter, Eden, Zee, James, John, Philip, Nathanael, Little James, Zebedee, Veronica, Tamar all at risk.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **"Walk on the Water" song** — full lyrical motif ("Oh child, come on in / Jump in the water / Got no trouble with the mess you been").
- **Mezuzah materials** — wooden (childhood reminder), silver/gold (tradition), Aleppo glass ("the times we live in").
- **"Just your average, everyday, middle-of-the-night trial"** — centurion's sarcasm.
- **Hagar in Beersheba wilderness** — Saba's grandmother's favorite story; Zohara throws it back at him.
- **Nicodemus quoting Proverbs 30:4** — "Who has gathered the wind in His fists? Who has wrapped up the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is His name, and what is His Son's name? Surely You know."
- **"I almost called you Simon"** — Peter/Judas morning scene; name-change friction.
- **"We slept on grass… no bag… usually no apples like these"** — nostalgia for early ministry simplicity.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Judas's fate** — fled the temple courtyard with returned money rejected; "He was right about everything."
2. **Saba's survival** — heart attack on screen; Reuben sent for doctor; no resolution shown.
3. **Nicodemus's appeal** — filed but clerk says "A holiday is a holiday… we won't reconvene for a week." Too late for Jesus.
4. **Jesus before Pilate** — handoff imminent; Roman neutrality message already received.
5. **Disciples' safety** — warrants active; Peter/Eden heading to temple; Zee restrained but determined; Zebedee/Veronica/Tamar seeking Jairus.
6. **Zohara & Nicodemus marriage** — raw confrontation; she feels betrayed by omission (Lazarus), he by her pressure to return to old life; "I'll die an unhappy man" vs. "I see you, Nico… I still love you."
7. **Jairus's role** — synagogue ruler; may have Sanhedrin access; Zebedee party en route to find him.
8. **Agunah legislation** — Shimon's bill pending; Shammai blocking; unresolved by episode end.
