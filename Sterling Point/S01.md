## S01E02

# S01E02 — "She's Giving Single & Bi"

## Episode Plot

The cold open recaps episode 1: Annie and Connor learn they are beneficiaries of Gordon's trust (a parcel of land in Canada), Steven hid Gordon's letters in a safe, Gordon's last letter to Annie said "Find her," Ellis flew her in by seaplane, she saw a photo and whispered "Hey, Mom," ran into Rory (NYC connection), and Denise told her Goldie filed for divorce from Steven. Annie then crashed into Ramona's cottage claiming it was her Airbnb; Ramona let her stay one night on the couch ("I'll feel bad if a bear eats you") and Annie noticed Ramona looks exactly like the woman in the wall photos — her mom.

Next morning Ramona evicts her. Stranded, nearly run over by a boat, Annie is rescued by Rory and ends up at a party at Rory's family's mega-mansion, wearing one of Denise's dresses. Denise confirms Goldie filed for divorce — everyone in the city knew, because Steven came back from Muskoka with two children and no wife; Goldie had brought the twins there for a summer and never went home. This triggers a panic attack; Denise calms her (cold water on the feet, vagus nerve).

Meanwhile the friend group (Oona, Sully, Ellis, Maple) reveals they hung out with Annie all Canada Day, assuming Ramona had rented her the cottage — which Ramona never did. Ramona, convinced Annie is a con artist who stole a photo of her dead mother, chases her down by boat. Annie confesses: "Goldie is my mom — my adoptive mom… she's my mother too. I think we might be sisters." Ramona's first reaction is rejection: since Annie was adopted, "we're not related — you're not my sister," and orders her back to New York. Ellis talks Annie into staying ("Maybe that's your answer" — she's barely scratched the surface). In the final scene, Oona urges Ramona to finally open the last unopened letter from Gordon (found in his briefcase after his death); after Oona reads it, Ramona reads it herself and concludes: "Annie is not lying."

## Important Character Actions

- Annie: lies to Steven (he thinks she's in Thailand, via Connor's cover story), digs into the divorce story, confesses the sisters theory to Ramona, decides to stay.
- Ramona: evicts Annie, chases her down, rejects the sister idea on blood-relation grounds, then reads Gordon's letter and changes her mind about Annie's honesty.
- Oona: returns to the island after caring for a dying old man; flirts with Ramona ("Come in, baby"); gets fired from catering for nearly stabbing someone with a knife she forgot she was holding.
- Denise: drops the divorce bombshell, mentors Annie through a panic attack (cold water on feet / vagus nerve trick).
- Connor: back in NYC, freshly dumped; covers for Annie, refuses to tell Steven; doubts the sister theory.
- Ellis: openly smitten with Annie ("Have you seen her side profile?"), jealous she's at Rory's party, urges her to stay.
- Joe: checks on Ramona at the marina ("if you need anything…"), which she deflects.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- Goldie: Annie's adoptive mother (died of cancer when the twins were two); now revealed as Ramona's biological mother — the shared mother at the center of the mystery.
- Capucine and Oliver: Connor's recent ex and his boyfriend, who came out publicly (Pride flag in his Insta bio) before coming out to Connor and is now in Thailand with a new boyfriend.
- Port Carling: the nearby town where rentals actually exist (there are no Airbnbs on the island).
- Marina Crew: the gang's marina job shirts; Starlink is the cottage's internet.
- Ramona's father: per island lore, "a random tourist Goldie had a fling with" while married to Steven — never in the picture. Annie finds this story implausible.
- Gordon's briefcase letter: Ramona's unopened letter from Gordon, read this episode, contents unseen by the viewer.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Annie and Connor were adopted as newborns by Goldie and Steven; Steven is "a sterile king" — they couldn't have bio kids.
- Goldie brought the twins to Muskoka for a summer; she filed for divorce from Steven and "never went home." This is a possible second layer to her death.
- Gordon wrote Annie and Connor letters their entire lives; Steven hid them in a safe. Everyone loved Gordon; Steven alone called him an asshole (reason unknown).
- Gordon's trust names Connor and Annie as beneficiaries of land in Canada — a latent property conflict with Ramona, who was set up by Gordon with the cottage and island.
- Annie apparently took a photo of Goldie from the cottage (the "stolen photo" Ramona accuses her of).
- Ramona has been completely alone since Gordon died; Oona was away caring for a dying old man; the group is her only family.
- Connor must keep the Thailand cover story and not tell Steven anything, or Steven will fly up and drag Annie home.

- Annie's texts turning green (iMessage failing in Canada); "I didn't even download Love Island."
- Oona's read on Annie — "She's giving single and bi" (the title line); the "teenage real estate agent trying to sell the island to the Four Seasons" and "she's 40 with an incredible skin-care routine" (75 bottles in the bathroom) theories.

- Oona never initiating make-outs with married women ("occasionally I initiate").

## Unresolved Threads

- Contents of Gordon's letter to Ramona (what convinced her Annie wasn't lying).
- Why everyone — especially Steven — kept Annie and Ramona apart, and Steven's real history with Gordon, Goldie, and the divorce.
- Whether Ramona's father is really an anonymous tourist; Annie's doubt hints the story is a cover.
- Whether Annie and Ramona will reconcile as sisters, and who legally owns the island.

- Ellis's feelings for Annie vs. Rory's presence — the love triangle is now live.

## S01E03 — "My Lifelong Debilitating Wolf Phobia" (我对狼的终身顽疾恐惧症)

### Plot
Recap of eps 1–2 (Annie arriving at the cottage, the "secret biological daughter" accusation, the stolen photo, Ramona's "you're not my sister" rejection) leads into the morning after: Ramona is "winterizing" the cottage so she can live there year-round and wants Gordon's hoarded furniture gone. She hands Annie a box of junk to take to the thrift store on the mainland. Inside, Annie finds her mother's things — a collie lamp and a cassette mixtape. Ellis gives her a ride (his family runs the boat rental, cash only), and at the thrift store they bond over sunglasses and buy a peacock-blue Huber Scoop chair for Ramona; Rory helps carry it and gets invited to the "thing" Annie is planning. Back at the cottage, the mixtape plays: it's a teenaged Goldie recording a love message to "Joe." Ramona flees and swims away; Annie tracks her to Joe's place and learns Joe and Goldie dated in high school. Annie plans a surprise party for Ramona (Sully strong-arms Ellis into getting Annie a gift). The party goes sideways when Rory's friend Lincoln crashes with his crew. Oona shows up with Stacey Mallory, a camp counselor, and leaves with her, gutting Ramona. Ellis kisses Annie on the porch; Ramona catches them. Night: Ramona invites Annie to sleep in her room — first real sister-bonding. Cliffhanger: Connor bursts in, announces he flew up (overcoming his "lifelong, debilitating wolf phobia"), and reveals he "zillowed" the place: the island is worth $22.7 million, Gordon left it to all three of them, and he wants to sell it. Annie and Ramona are stunned — Ramona didn't know she co-owns it with the twins.

## S01E04 — "Take Me to the Cliffs" (带我去悬崖)

### Episode Plot

Recap of eps 1–3 (Goldie's mixtape to Joe, Ellis at Ramona's party, the "vibe" question, Rory kissing Annie, Steven learning Annie fled to Canada and Connor volunteering to fetch her) rolls into Connor's arrival: Gordon left the island to all three of them — "now it's a threesome." Connor accepts a one-week trial: if he still hates it, they'll seriously discuss selling. Meanwhile, Gordon's estate lawyer Marjorie Lewis phones Joe's house asking for "Joseph Anderson" and Ramona Campbell's contact number; Ellis takes the message and doesn't pass it on.

The group goes cliff jumping (the spot Goldie used to jump from). ## S01E06 — "I Still Bleed Through My Pants all the Time"

### Episode Plot
Annie's 18th birthday spirals into a chaotic night of drinking, impulsive sex with Rory, and her adoptive father Steven arriving unexpectedly on the island. The next morning, hungover and spiraling, Annie confides in Ramona about losing her virginity "on a whim" and fears she's ruined things with Ellis. Meanwhile, Maple's final camp showcase becomes a backdrop for the group to celebrate her first period with a raucous "period crown" ceremony and a collective howl at Jupiter. Tensions peak when Steven and Joe (Ellis's father) have a violent confrontation at the marina, exposing two decades of buried history: Steven took the twins to New York after their mother's affair with Joe; Joe raised Ramona as his own. The episode ends with Denise (Rory's mother) publicly revealing that **Joe is Ramona's biological father**, shattering Ramona's world and prompting her to flee.

- **Annie**: Turns 18, gets blackout drunk, sleeps with Rory, lies about prior experience, spirals into identity panic, bonds with Ramona over shared vulnerability, tries to connect with Ellis but is seen leaving Rory's house.

## S01E01 — "But I Just Took an Ambien"

### Plot

Seventeen-year-old Annie Jacobson is rejected from a competitive Wharton summer business program, the culmination of her overachieving gap summer. While her twin Connor consoles her (and dodges an awkward near-breakup with his closeted boyfriend Oliver), a lawyer named Marjorie Lewis informs the family that their estranged maternal grandfather Gordon has died and left Annie and Connor a trust: property on a parcel of land in Muskoka, Canada. Steven (the twins' widowed adoptive father) immediately confiscates Gordon's letters and orders all further communication routed through him.

Annie reads her letter privately. Gordon apologizes for not fighting harder to see her, says the island was her mother's favorite place, and tells her to "find her" — a cryptic instruction that haunts the rest of the episode. Gordon gave Steven nothing in the will. Connor, indifferent, agrees to cover for Annie if she goes to Canada investigating, then leaves for Ibiza with Oliver.

Annie flies to Toronto, takes a connecting seaplane piloted by Ellis — a local teenager who immediately sparks with her — and arrives at Sterling Point, a secluded island on Lake Muskoka. The cottage is ramshogany, stuffed with nuts and old furniture. She finds a framed photo of her late mother on the wall, whispers "Hey, Mom," and decides to sleep elsewhere. At the Sterling Point Marina, she rents a boat lesson from Joe (Ellis's father), meets the brash, British-accented Oona (who offers to teach her and nearly sinks them both), and learns from Oona's younger sister Maple that the island's social world is divided between working-class locals and wealthy "mega-mansion" tourists.

Ellis brings Annie and Oona to a Canada Day party at the mega-mansion of Denise Ballantine, where Annie reunites with Rory, a soft-spoken NYC acquaintance from middle school. Denise lets slip that Annie's late mother used to spend summers in Muskoka and eventually filed for divorce from Steven — a detail Annie did not know. The revelation rattles her. Ellis drives her home afterward and tells her the island could be worth millions and might be sold to a developer — giving Annie a reason to care about its fate.

Back at the cottage, Annie calls Connor and Steven without success. She enters the cabin and finds Ramona, a guarded local girl her own age, already living there. Ramona demands to know who she is. Annie blurts out her discovery: she thinks their mother had a secret biological daughter. Ramona, who claims the cottage as her late grandfather's, reacts with hostility.
