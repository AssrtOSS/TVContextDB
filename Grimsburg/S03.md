## S03E01

# Grimsburg S03E01 Summary

## Episode Plot
A *Last of Us* parody cold-open frames Season 3 as the point where animated shows desperately parody pop culture. The "Zaddy virus" sweeps Grimsburg, transforming men into attractive older specimens with salt-and-pepper hair, manicured beards, excessive jewelry, and obsessive "performative masculinity" hobbies (woodworking, vintage audio, street photography, terrible guitar playing). Marvin Flute discovers he's immune. Harmony, Lt. Kang, and Summers (briefly infected) investigate, aided by "The Stars" — a rebel faction of celebrity handsome men (Brad Pitt, Michael B. Jordan, Leo DiCaprio, Ryan Reynolds, Adrien Brody) threatened by the democratization of hotness. The cure: the beards are the vector; shaving reverts men to normal. Marvin's "unreasonable confidence" is revealed as the source of his immunity.

## Important Character Actions
- **Marvin**: Immune due to pathological self-confidence; leads the resistance; kisses (jokingly) to test fairytale-cure logic; publicly embraces self-love at episode's end.
- **Harmony**: Partners with Marvin; reveals her dead husband Martino was hideously ugly but radiated "crazy confidence"; shows his photo to prove the point; admits she needed a special permit to marry him.
- **Summers**: Infected early; develops extreme photography obsession (Zeiss Otus lenses, tilt-shift); cured by beard removal.
- **Lt. Kang**: Living with male partner; relationship hinted in S1, "confirmed during this pandemic" (meta-joke); hosts dinner with Marvin/Harmony.
- **The Stars**: Celebrity faction fighting the pandemic to preserve their exclusivity; escort Marvin's group to CostLand in G-Wagons.
- **Stan**: Brief meta cameo — notes he "didn't map onto any characters for the parody"; editor marks his scenes for deletion.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Zaddy** — attractive, fashionable, charismatic older man (Pocket Urban Dictionary definition)
- **Zaddy apocalypse / Zaddy pandemic** — the outbreak
- **The Stars** — rebel faction of professional handsome celebrities
- **CostLand** — warehouse club (Costco parody); safe zone for non-Zaddies
- **Mutated Gene** — payroll employee with a mutated gene (literal name joke)
- **Goldblum (verb)** — to become sexy-older-man-coded like Jeff Goldblum
- **Hottie or Nottie** — "scientific" attractiveness scale
- **Cuddle Crew** — emotional-trigger method to reach pre-Zaddy identity
- **The Muffinist** — fake Adrien Brody film

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Harmony's late husband **Martino** was grotesquely ugly ("pile of moldy jockstraps") but his unearned confidence made him hot; he died running by an indoor hotel pool despite a "No Running" sign.
- Marvin's broken commemorative watch marks Martino's time of death: **10:32 a.m.** (Courtyard hotel gift).
- Marvin's defining trait: "unreasonable amount of confidence, considering..." — now canonically his immunity source.
- Kang's same-sex relationship: hinted in S1, jokingly "found each other during this pandemic."
- Episode explicitly self-declares **non-canon**: "it's a parody episode, so I don't think any of this stuff is, like, canon."

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- S3 meta-premise: animated shows parody hits in S3 (*Family Guy*→*Star Wars*, *Simpsons*→*Cape Fear*, *Great North*→*Bob's Burgers* in winter jackets).
- *Last of Us* structure: cold open talk show, horse travel, "first pandemic for the ladies," Joel-death audience-loss gag.
- "Don't M3GAN me! We already did that parody." — AI garden gnome.
- Wes Anderson boxed set, Father John Misty, RFK Jr./Kyrie Irving vaccine jokes, Ted Cruz cameo, Ryan Reynolds/Minute Loans/gin-phone-self-awareness hustle.
- "Immune-dingus" insult; "For once?" callback on Marvin's self-love declaration.

## Unresolved Threads
- None intended — episode frames itself as a standalone parody. However, Harmony's public acceptance of Martino's memory and Marvin's "confidence as immunity" concept could resurface if the show chooses to canonize emotional beats.

## S03E02

# Episode Plot
Grimsburg Middle's "Pre-Cogs" (psychic predictors in a tank) announce yearbook superlatives, and Stan Flute is named "Most Likely to Commit Patricide." Marvin doesn't know what the word means until Martinez spells it out, after which he panics. To bond with Stan — and to neutralize the prophecy — Marvin finally attends Stan's hobby horse competitions, turning into an obsessive "Sports Dad" who tracks Stan's diet via Google Sheets and demands winning above integrity, democracy, or clean water. Stan wins a string of meets using a banned salve called "Clippity Clop," and Marvin keeps sourcing it for him. In the championship against Pembrooke, Marvin secretly withholds the drug and fakes applying it via mime. Stan loses, and Marvin finally gives him the back-pat he's been craving for years. The episode closes with a new prophecy: Stan is now "Most Likely to Commit Matricide."

# Important Character Actions
- **Marvin Flute**: Goes from ignoring Stan's games to becoming a tyrannical conditioning coach; blackmails the Clippity Clop dealer; lies about applying the drug; ultimately chooses fatherhood over winning. Dr. Pentos lectures him that he's "addicted to pride."
- **Stan Flute**: Rides "Cinnamon Rainbow Starship" through playoff rounds; becomes dependent on Clippity Clop; loses the championship cleanly; receives the long-awaited pat from his dad.
- **Mr. Flesh** (visible only to Stan): Builds a popsicle-stick replica of the Menendez Brothers' mansion and tells Stan he's "free" to kill his parents.
- **Wynona**: A "severance" patient at Grimsburg Cybernetics (parody of Apple TV+'s Severance). Her work-focused innie plans to "unsever," merging with her many outies (Off The Clock Wynona, Car Wynona, Elevator Wynona, Grocery Produce-Bag Wynona, etc.). Off The Clock Wynona and Car Wynona run off together in a car rather than disappear; the innie ultimately completes the unsevering anyway.
- **Dr. Rufis Pentos**: Coaches rival rider Pepper; delivers the pride-addiction sermon to Marvin.
- **Harmony Flute**: Bookends the episode via the matricide punchline.
- **Lt. Kang**: Referenced as "legally dead"; mentioned in a flashback to being buried in an open grave at Wynona's birthday party.
- **Greg Summers**: No direct role; offscreen only.

## S03E03

## Plot

A new candy called **Brain Rot** has hooked his daughter, so the unnamed **Mayor** leans on **Chief Martinez**, who gives **Officer Otis Volcanowitz**—an eight-year-old cop—one last chance to take down the distributor. Martinez pairs Otis with **Marvin Flute**, who is reluctantly assigned as his handler.

Marvin and Otis infiltrate the middle school: Otis poses undercover while Marvin dresses in Stan's clothes and pretends to be a dorky student. They soon learn the candy is being produced by **Dr. Rufis Pentos**, who has turned a sentient **Smart Board** into his meth-lab-style cook. **Stan Flute** is already in on the operation—he's been selling Brain Rot for Pentos as his side hustle.

Stan engineers the bust to fail so he can have Marvin to himself: he baits Otis (who has a germ phobia) into a nurse's-office detour by pushing a blind kid, then arranges for Pentos to slip past the SWAT team. Pentos escapes into the desert (Breaking Bad callback) and disappears into a mysterious cloud. Martinez fires Marvin.

The episode closes with Marvin and Otis reconciling as fellow "kids," Otis getting reinstated for a downtown robbery, and a hanging reference to Pentos calling out "Grandma" toward the escaping cloud.

## Important character actions

- **Dr. Pentos** — opens with a self-recorded, Walter-White-style confession; revealed as Brain Rot's maker; collaborates with his sentient Smart Board; attempts desert escape when SWAT raids.
- **Officer Otis Volcanowitz** (new) — eight-year-old cop with a higher-pitched affectation; struggles through the stakeout, the playground slide, and getting kicked in the groin; reconciles with Marvin.
- **Marvin Flute** — paired with Otis as his mentor/protégé; goes undercover in Stan's clothes; reflects on his past undercover as "Candy"; gets fired after the botched raid.
- **Stan Flute** — secretly selling Brain Rot for Pentos; engineers Marvin's firing to remove Otis as competition for Marvin's attention; calls an anonymous tip on himself; demonstrates unexpected cunning.
- **Chief Martinez** (new) — Otis's commanding officer; fires Marvin; reinstates Otis within minutes.
- **Lt. Kang / Det. Summers / Harmony** — Absent in this episode.

- **Officer Otis Volcanowitz** — eight-year-old cop, voice permanently altered

## S03E05

# Grimsburg S03E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Grimsburg PD goes on a team-building retreat at a **CrimeShare** — an Airbnb-style rental where a crime occurred. Kang books a house that turns out to be the site of mere **tax evasion** (the "calculator he fudged his Schedule E on"). The team is trapped by **Puzzle Paul**, a *Saw*-parody villain who forces them to solve riddles or face death traps (bear traps, drowning in "poo-age," crushing walls). The retreat devolves into a *Saw* pastiche where the only way out is through the control room on the other side of a lethal trap gauntlet.

## Important Character Actions
- **Kang** reveals he is **illiterate** (cannot read words or numbers); he dictates emails, orders daily specials, texts only in emojis (hotel = home, two sneakers = out). He also cannot read analog clocks ("half past" makes no sense).
- **Marvin & Wynona** engage in a **chili cook-off** ("chill chili cook-off chill-off"), bonding over grandparent-taught recipes (Marvin's grandpa's wooden spoon; Wynona's "bitch of a grandma"). Wynona's secret ingredient: **chocolate**; Marvin's: **chili powder**.
- **Summers** (cyborg) transforms into a **boat (SS Summers)** to ferry the team, then enters the control room to hit the kill switch — but Kang can't read which button is "kill switch" vs. "kill swift."
- The team uses a **"Hot To Go" / YMCA body-spelling method** to communicate letters to Kang so he can press the right button.
- **Puzzle Paul** is unmasked as the **tax evader** who rented his own house on CrimeShare, then built baroque deathtraps to murder guests because "tax evasion isn't sexy enough for true crime junkies."
- **Wynona** films a **six-part video series** about teaching Kang to process emotions and learn the alphabet.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **CrimeShare** — true-crime-themed vacation rental platform (Airbnb meets murder houses).
- **Puzzle Paul** — *Saw* parody villain; claims never to have seen the "clumsy ten-picture franchise"; traps are "elaborate combo of reading and murder."
- **Poo-age** — Puzzle Paul's pun on sewage.
- **Hot To Go / YMCA body-spelling** — cross-generational communication method for spelling words physically.
- **Kill swift** — misread button that accelerates the trap (Puzzle Paul installed it "in case it was taking too long and I had somewhere to be").

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Kang's **illiteracy** is a centuries-old secret (he's immortal); his emoji code (hotel = home, two sneakers = away) is now known to the team.
- Kang **cannot read analog clocks** — new vulnerability.
- Summers' **boat transformation** ("booze cruise time") confirmed as a functional cyborg mode.
- Wynona's **grandmother** ("bitch of a grandma, but hell of a cook") and Marvin's **abusive grandpa** (wooden spoon) established as backstory.
- The team's **trust fall** metaphor becomes literal ("trust fall but with actual dying").
- Marvin's **award-winning chili** with Goldfish crackers canonized.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- *Saw* franchise: "clumsy ten-picture franchise," "kindergarten riddles from bubblegum wrappers," "Pez dispensers," the control room always on the other side of a gruesome trap.
- *The Bear* (FX): "premium HBO… at the very least FX's *The Bear*," "before the brother killed himself."
- Lea Michele reference: "What the Lea Michelle?" as a minced oath.
- "Generational connector" — YMCA / Hot To Go as cross-age communication.

## Unresolved Threads
- **Kang's literacy arc** — Wynona's six-part series implies ongoing lessons; whether he actually learns to read remains open.
- **Puzzle Paul's fate** — arrested? escaped? The house is still a CrimeShare listing ("pull all the sheets off the beds").
- **Wynona's video series** — may resurface as in-universe content or blackmail material.
- **Team dynamics** — the "vacation mode" bet (no work talk) collapsed instantly; the chili cook-off tie ("chill chili cook-off chill-off") has no declared winner.

## S03E07

# Grimsburg S03E07 — "Evidence Locker Part 2: Full Frontal Evidence"

## Episode Plot
A tornado traps Marvin, Harmony, Stan, Mr. Flesh, Kang, Summers, and Pentos in the evidence locker/tornado shelter. To pass the time, they share origin stories in a *Treehouse of Horror*-style anthology framed as "Evidence Locker Part 2."

---

## Three Origin Stories

### Harmony Flute — "Raised by Bears, Exploited by Media"
- Abandoned in woods as infant, raised by bears.
- Discovered by **Ms. Clayton**, a news reporter who "raised" her to be a news story — exploiting her as a "wild child" for ratings.
- Orphanage deliberately abandoned babies in woods to make them "interesting" for rich adoptive parents (wolves, beavers, worms).
- At the **Grimsburg Gala** for "interesting adoptees," Harmony met young Marvin. Exposed Clayton's scheme; Clayton stole the story anyway.
- **Key revelation**: Harmony was *manufactured* as news, not raised to be a newswoman.

### Dr. Rufis Pentos — "Evil LLC to Music Teacher"
- Parole-board one-man show: born with heart murmur he interprets as "body demanding more evil."
- Climbed corporate ladder at **Evil LLC** (mailroom → CEO via murder), lost his "evil drive" when thrill of being caught vanished.
- Reignited by meeting **young Marvin Flute** at the **Fennell Emerald** heist (emerald makes everyone look white through it).
- Framed Marvin's teacher to take her job at Grimsburg Middle, planning to mold Marvin into "greatest detective" and "worthy adversary" — the Hannibal to his Clarice.
- **Key revelation**: Pentos *chose* teaching to engineer Marvin as his nemesis; 26-act play cut short by parole denial + teacher shortage.

### Lt. John Kang — "Immortal First Sheriff of Cursed Grimsburg"
- Immortal, centuries old: witch hunter, cobbler, Cleopatra's gigolo. Nothing fulfilled him.
- Arrived in **Grimsburg** — cursed since a bungled virgin sacrifice.
- Partnered with **Bartholomew Flute** (town drunk, Marvin's ancestor?) to investigate murder of Vic Pianoguy.
- Used **deductive reasoning** (shoe prints, poison in moonshine) to catch killer: a sex worker whose heels were stolen by a cross-dressing john.
- Bartholomew became sacrifice; Kang became sheriff with drunk as mentor.
- **Key revelation**: Kang founded Grimsburg PD; Bartholomew Flute and **Solomon Summers** (Greg's ancestor) were the first case.

## Grimsburg S03E08 Summary

### Episode Plot
Grimsburg Day — the town's cherished holiday and second-leading cause of death — prompts Lt. Kang to run a disaster drill. When Flute botches it chasing ice cream, Kang brings in the **Fur Force** (毛茸特攻队): four militarized dogs (Bossy, Octane, Diggz, and Crotchey) originally designed for a kids' show, repurposed by the military. Each officer gets a canine partner; Flute receives Crotchey, a crotch-sniffer absent from the theme song. After Crotchey provokes him, Flute punches the dog and faces suspension unless he completes an obedience-training course run by the Fur Force themselves.

The "training" is a thinly veiled re-education camp where humans are conditioned like dogs (sit, roll over, stickers, treats). Stan — appointed **Number One Pup Boy** — eagerly enforces it. Flute initially breaks and becomes obedient ("Ruff. Ruff."), but Summers' self-deprecating spiral ("I'm stupid, wrong, dumb, shorter than average") snaps him back. They discover the Fur Force plans to use Grimsburg Day fireworks to **neuter the entire town into total obedience** (guillotines for genitals: "Guillopeens," "Vaginotines"), waiting until Otis turns 13 so he hits puberty first.

Flute and Martinez (who finally admits she's a rookie boss unsure of herself) escape by weaponizing the dogs' own obedience drills ("roll over" inches them toward the fence). Fireworks — the dogs' phobia — break the Fur Force's control. The town is saved; the dogs revert to chasing mailmen. Flute and Martinez reconcile over ice cream.
