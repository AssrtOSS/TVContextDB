## S24E33

## Episode plot
- Wednesday live episode of *It Takes Two*, presented as a midpoint-of-series, midpoint-of-week edition.
- Opens with training-room diary footage from roving reporter Abi, featuring couples reflecting on recent judges’ comments and rehearsing upcoming routines (including paso doble work set to “Everybody Wants To Rule The World” and discussion of a risky entrance/lift that had only been tried shortly before the live show).
- Specialist choreographer Jenny Thomas joins with Aljaz to discuss her long-running role creating charleston and salsa routines for *Strictly*, the technical challenges of those styles, and the couples she is choreographing this week.
- A behind-the-scenes feature follows floor managers Frosty, Adrian, Paul, and Alan, showing how they coordinate presenters, judges, couples, and last-minute problems during a live Saturday show.
- Abi visits Delta and Kai in training as they prepare an Argentine tango to “Missing You,” focusing on spins, spotting, and a more elegant, slower interpretation.
- Head judge Shirley Ballas appears for an extended interview about the previous weekend, her deciding dance-off vote, her scoring priorities, feedback for multiple couples, and a tribute marking her tenth year as head judge.
- The episode closes with a preview of the next day’s guests: Jaime & Carlos, Lacey & Vito, Tabby & Mark, and costume designer Vicky Gill.

## Important character actions
- **Abi (roving reporter):** Delivers training-room diaries; practices hold/steps with dancers; checks in with Delta and Kai on Argentine tango technique and styling.
- **Jenny Thomas:** Explains how she and her ex-husband introduced charleston to the show (from 2009, after earlier group-number work), outlines why salsa/charleston need specialists, and describes tailoring choreography to each celebrity’s strengths.
- **Aljaz:** Reflects on working with Jenny, credits her as crucial to making professionals look authentic in non-ballroom/Latin styles, and recalls standout routines.
- **Floor managers (Frosty, Adrian, Paul, Alan):** Manage pre-show movement, cue presenters/judges/couples, handle live-show logistics, and react to incidents (e.g. sweeping up beads after Dianne’s dress shed them).
- **Delta and Kai:** Train Argentine tango; Kai emphasizes elegant leg/foot quality and slower control; Delta works on spins/spotting and plans a slicked-back look for week eight.
- **Shirley Ballas:** Reviews the closest dance-off of the series so far, explains why she saved Cach and Maddie, comments on technique and performance quality across several couples, and responds emotionally to a tenth-anniversary tribute package.

## Newly established names/terms
- **People:** Jenny Thomas (specialist choreographer), Frosty/Adrian/Paul/Alan (floor managers), Dave (named by host off-camera).
- **Routines/songs referenced this week:** Paso doble to “Everybody Wants To Rule The World”; Argentine tango to “Missing You.”
- **This week’s charleston assignments (per Jenny):** Cach & Maddie (more modern music) and Jaime & Carlos (traditional charleston feel).
- **Shirley’s judging priorities/language:** “opposite arm to hip/fruit” coordination cue, emphasis on posture, musicality, flexibility, arm styling, and coordinated upper/lower body.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Jenny Thomas has been part of *Strictly* for 20 years and is credited with 70 charlestons and 68 salsas; charleston was introduced to the show in 2009.
- Aljaz’s first *Strictly* dance with Jenny was a charleston with Alison Hammond (recalled as a favourite despite elimination that week).
- Aljaz highlights his charleston with Tasha as especially intricate; Jenny cites George and Alexis’s “The Inbetweeners”-themed charleston from last year as a favourite.
- Dance-off context: Shirley cast the deciding vote between Cach & Maddie and Bethany & Nancy, saving Cach & Maddie for improved footwork, swing/sway, floor coverage, posture, and body shaping.
- Recent performance notes from Shirley: Dani and Mykyta earned her highest score of the night (including a slide down the banister moment); Tabby & Mark’s paso doble earned an 8; Lacey & Vito praised for rumba quality/pivots/connection; Will & Dianne’s quickstep earned a 7 with noted improvement; John & Katya’s salsa earned a 7 with feedback to add rotation, fuller hip action, and clearer footwork.
- Delta and Kai are in week eight and preparing an Argentine tango with a slicked-back styling choice.
- Shirley marks ten years as head judge; tribute includes her son and messages from colleagues/contestants.
- Next episode guests announced: Jaime & Carlos, Lacey & Vito, Tabby & Mark, and Vicky Gill.
