# Strictly Come Dancing: It Takes Two — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Identity
- **Official English title**: Strictly Come Dancing: It Takes Two  
- **Alternate titles**: Strictly: It Takes Two, It Takes Two  
- **Type**: British daily companion magazine programme (spin-off)  
- **Parent show**: Strictly Come Dancing (BBC One, Saturday prime-time)  
- **First broadcast**: 25 October 2004  
- **Broadcaster**: BBC Two (weeknights 18:30 during main show run)  
- **Country / language**: United Kingdom / English  
- **Series count**: 23 (2004–2025)  
- **Episode count**: 1,062+ (as of 2025)  
- **Running time**: 30 min (Mon–Thu), 60 min with studio audience (Fri)  
- **Production**: BBC Studios; multi-camera; various London/Elstree studios  

## World Setting & Premise
A daytime magazine show aired every weekday alongside each autumn series of *Strictly Come Dancing*. Each episode reviews the previous Saturday’s live performances, shows training-room footage of the celebrity–professional couples preparing for the next show, and features interviews, expert analysis, costume previews, and light-hearted games. The tone is warm, conversational, and celebratory — extending the “Strictly” brand into daily appointment viewing.

## Key Relationships
- **Host ↔ Couples**: supportive interviewer, never adversarial; “How are you feeling?” “What was the hardest part?”
- **Host ↔ Experts (Waite, Clifton, Gill)**: defer to technical authority; banter rooted in mutual respect
- **Host ↔ Judges (Horwood, Ballas, Mabuse, Du Beke)**: clips shown; occasional studio visits; respectful but playful
- **Pro dancers ↔ Celebrities**: mentor/mentee; “journey” narrative central
- **Friday Panel (celebrity fans) ↔ Couples**: speculative, affectionate, elimination predictions

## Speaking Styles & Translation Guidance
- **Presenters**: warm, inclusive, British daytime TV register — “lovely,” “gorgeous,” “brilliant,” “nerves,” “journey.” Avoid overly formal Chinese; use natural spoken Mandarin (e.g., “太棒了”“紧张吗”“这段旅程”).
- **Ian Waite**: precise, technical, encouraging — “frame,” “footwork,” “timing,” “connection.” Render with dance terminology: “舞框”“脚步”“音乐性”“默契”.
- **Vicky Gill**: descriptive, fabric-focused — “chiffon,” “rhinestones,” “structure,” “movement.” Keep tactile: “雪纱”“钻片”“版型”“动起来”.
- **Joanne / Kevin Clifton**: expert but accessible — “rotation,” “hip action,” “rise and fall.” Use standard ballroom CN terms: “转体”“臀部动作”“起伏”.
- **Friday Panel / Celebrity guests**: colloquial, speculative, humorous — “I reckon,” “dark horse,” “safe bet.” Translate idioms functionally: “我觉得”“黑马”“稳了”.
- **Rylan / Ellie Taylor**: camp / dry wit — lean into personality; preserve humour over literalism.
- **Catchphrases**: “Keep dancing!” → “继续跳舞吧！” / “Keep on dancing!”; “It takes two!” → “这就要两个人！” / “双人共舞！”.

## Cultural Context for Translators
- **British institution**: *Strictly* is a national event (12–13 M viewers); It Takes Two is its daily “water-cooler” extension. References to BBC, licence fee, Radio 2, *The One Show*, Blackpool Tower, *Blue Peter* are common.
- **Class & regional accents**: presenters/contributors span RP, Estuary, Welsh, Northern, Scottish — subtitles should not flatten to standard Mandarin unless dubbing; note accent humour (e.g., Kevin’s “Grimsby” pride).
- **Disability representation**: Rose Ayling-Ellis (deaf, S19 winner), Ellie Simmonds (dwarfism), Tasha Ghouri (deaf) — respectful terminology: “听障”“侏儒症” not derogatory terms.
- **LGBTQ+ visibility**: same-sex pairings (Nicola Adams/Katya Jones S18; Richie Anderson/Giovanni Pernice S20; Layton Williams/Nikita Kuzmin S21) — neutral language “同性组合”“双男/双女组合”.
- **Charity / Children in Need**: annual special; Pudsey Bear references.
- **Scheduling**: airs Mon–Fri 18:30 on BBC Two; Friday hour-long with audience — “studio audience” laughter/applause cues.
- **Social media integration**: #Strictly, #ItTakesTwo, viewer tweets read on air — retain hashtags or translate as “话题标签”.
