## S01E08

# Episode S01E08 — All or Nothing!

## PlotMorning-after fallout from the Key Game and the trip to Stella's house. Anna admits to Paul that she slept with Samu; Paul has already read Samu's texts saying he "can't stop thinking about you." Anna justifies it: "I needed it. For me." Siena walks in on the confrontation ("How fitting."). Paul tells Anna to do whatever she wants; Anna collapses: "My life is a total mess."

Anna confides in a friend, who pushes her to go to tonight's game and talk to Samu to get clarity before deciding anything.

Siena's birthday doubles as the next Key Game venue. She greets the group with "be free!" and hands out keys — "Let the games begin." A fight between Leo and Paul spills out — "Keep your hands off my wife!" / "You really think Anna belongs to you?" — the men brawl.

At the party: Anna confronts Samu ("We were happy until you came along with your shitty game." Samu replies: "More than you think."). Leo tracks Bella down, apologizes for not respecting her work, proposes ("I'm ready to endure one [wedding], if it ends with you saying 'I do'"), and they settle on an open marriage. A new couple is introduced when Valentin draws key 17 and meets Sebastian.

Comic beat at the end of the night: two characters try to confess at the same time — "I'm pregnant." / "I'm gay." — and immediately say bye.

Closing scene: Anna finds Paul and declares she is done chasing other lives. "Every time in my life when I didn't dare do something, said no to something, really I was saying yes to us." Anna chooses Paul: "I love you. I want our life." Final beat — "Does Paul know yet?"

## Important Character Actions

- Anna: confesses affair, leaves Paul, talks with Samu, then returns to Paul; ends the episode choosing her marriage.
- Paul: reads the texts, fights Leo, reconciles with Anna.
- Samu: texts Paul about Anna; at the party, hints he knows more about Paul's marriage than expected.
- Siena: hosts the birthday / game; walks in on Anna–Paul at the worst moment; runs the key draw.
- Leo: crashes the party, gets ejected, fights Paul, apologizes to Bella, proposes, agrees to open marriage.
- Bella: rejects Leo, then accepts his apology and proposal; leaves the party to "go home."
- Valentin: draws key 17, meets Sebastian; pulled between the group's chaos.
- Julie: advises Anna on clarity; appears in the closing pregnancy/gay punchline.
