## S01E03

# S01E03 Summary — NCIS: New York

## Episode Plot
The Shaw family (Maggie, Tim, 6‑year‑old twins Gus and Gwen) visits NYC from D.C. At a Brooklyn Bridge Park carousel, the twins vanish while Maggie fetches hot dogs and Tim is distracted texting. A burner‑phone photo and text arrive: the kidnappers demand Maggie access her **Confidential Protection List** (classified witness roster) on a public library computer. Maggie is **Captain Margaret Shaw**, JAG prosecutor and Special Assistant U.S. Attorney — giving her a long enemy list. NCIS (Sam, Nick, Addy, Wyatt, Robyn, Sean) launches a 24‑hour race.

Tim admits he was texting **Chloe Edwards**, the ex‑nanny he had an affair with; she’d stalked the family to NYC. Chloe gave the family password — **“It’s time to buzz the tower”** (a *Top Gun* reference) — to “Jason,” really **Devon Lancaster**, on trial for skimming **$3.7M** from government contracts. Devon’s key witness is in protection in Connecticut. The physical abduction was done by **Lola Dambrock** (“the ketchup lady”), a career criminal (B&E, grand theft, fraud, tax eviction) from Trenton.

Gwen suffers a severe allergic reaction (pistachio, cashew, lentil, **tamarind**, papaya). The team tracks the burner to an Astoria high‑rise, then uses an enhanced livestream audio cue (**East River ferry horn**) cross‑referenced with Gwen’s **tamarind** allergy → a recently closed Indian restaurant near the **East 34th St ferry dock**. Kids are rescued from the kitchen; Lola arrested. Devon remains at large (burner ditched).

## Important Character Actions
- **Maggie**: Follows kidnapper instructions under duress, attempts classified login on public terminal, demands proof of life, holds composure.
- **Tim**: Initially lies about texting “my brother,” then confesses affair with Chloe; guilt‑ridden but cooperative.
- **Sam Hanna**: Leads hotel undercover (as “Mr. Milken”), coordinates tactical teams, shadows Maggie during drop, comforts her with “we’ll get yours back.”
- **Nick**: Runs point at park/bridge, pairs comms with Maggie in cab, interrogates Devon’s recruiter, negotiates “10 questions” with Sam at end.
- **Addy & Wyatt**: Undercover as “the Milkens” in adjoining room; clone phones, interview Shaws, Addy breaches building for rescue.
- **Robyn**: Recovers 2‑sec live‑photo video from metadata → reveals kids were on the bridge, not in park; IDs Lola Dambrock.
- **Sean**: Tracks burner pings (Astoria tower), cross‑references ferry‑dock areas with Gwen’s allergy list → pinpoints location.
- **Chloe Edwards**: Confesses stalking, affair, giving password to “Jason”; not charged on screen.
- **Devon Lancaster**: Mastermind; uses Lola as cut‑out, promises witness‑list payout; escapes.
- **Lola Dambrock**: Executes snatch at park (ketchup spill as distraction), holds kids at restaurant; arrested.

## S01E05

# NCIS: New York S01E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Naval Intelligence interrogator **Donna Isom** is bludgeoned to death in her Crown Heights home. Her 17-year-old daughter **Zoe** discovers the body. Husband **Caleb Isom** (airline pilot) reports it but lies about his timeline—he actually deplaned at JFK at 6:38 PM, not 7:25 PM, leaving a 90‑minute gap. The team initially suspects Caleb, especially after learning he relapsed into alcoholism after 11 years sober and was at a bar during the murder window. His alibi checks out, clearing him.

The real killer is **Jeremy Noles**, a serial identity thief/stalker with a history of obsessive fixations on women. He was living a double life: by day as construction worker **Brad Skinner**, by night as art dealer **Vincent Ggiata**—engaged to Donna’s best friend and Zoe’s godmother, **Caitlyn Santos**. Donna recognized him as Brad Skinner at a restaurant the day before she died; he killed her to protect his cover. He then kidnapped Caitlyn, taking her to a Maspeth building leased under "Vincent Ggiada." Addy negotiates face‑to‑face while Nick and Sam breach; Jeremy is arrested.

## Important Character Actions
- **Nick**: Leads investigation; confronts Caleb aggressively; later confesses to Sam about his ex‑partner Mike Sloss’s corruption and his own moral complicity.
- **Sam**: Partners with Nick; supports him personally; runs crime scene and evidence.
- **Addy**: Acts as primary negotiator with Jeremy, using empathy and psychological profiling to stall him until breach.
- **Caleb**: Lies about timeline; admits relapse; cleared but family destroyed.
- **Zoe**: Provides critical lead (Vincent’s Queens property listing with smokestack → Maspeth).
- **Caitlyn**: Unwitting fiancée/victim; survives kidnapping.
- **Robyn/Wyatt/Mack**: Support intel, surveillance, NYPD liaison.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Donna Isom** — Naval Intelligence interrogator (high‑value targets: terrorists, cartel leaders)
- **Caleb Isom** — Airline pilot, Donna’s husband, recovering alcoholic (11 years sober, relapsed 2 months ago)
- **Zoe Isom** — 17‑year‑old daughter
- **Caitlyn Santos** — Donna’s best friend since college, Zoe’s godmother
- **Jeremy Noles** (true name) — Serial stalker/killer; aliases: **Brad Skinner** (construction), **Vincent Ggiata** (art dealer)
- **Maspeth** — Queens industrial neighborhood; building with smokestack used as hideout
- **SCIF** — Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (Naval Intel workspace)
- **SDNY** — Southern District of New York (building case against Mike Sloss)
- **Pholourie** — Caribbean fried dough snack (cultural detail)

## Continuity‑Relevant Facts
- **Mike Sloss** (Nick’s ex‑partner, “like a brother”) perjured himself before a grand jury; SDNY is building a major obstruction/conspiracy case. Nick did not know about the corruption but admits a part of him suspected and stayed silent. Sloss has 3 months left on a 2‑year sentence; his wife Elena and kids face ruin.
- **Nick’s personal life**: Seeing **Brooke** (casual, complicated); strained relationship with his father (argument before father’s death, unresolved guilt).
- **Nick’s father**: Died after a Sunday‑dinner argument; Nick carries regret over “one more day.”
- **Sam’s green burial** preference established (natural decomposition, no embalming/coffin).

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **“Like landing a plane — angle of approach”** — Nick’s metaphor for handling family conflict (recurring motif).
- **“New York Nick”** — Sam’s nickname for him.
- **Green burial** — Sam’s detailed, somewhat morbid advocacy.
- **Peter Sellers / *Dr. Strangelove*** — Nick’s reference when Jeremy’s multiple aliases are revealed (“playing more parts than Peter Sellers”); Sam hasn’t seen it (*Doctor Strange* instead).
- **“Film history’s down the hall”** — Robyn’s dismissive callback.
- **Top‑shelf scotch bet** — Sam’s running joke about expensive drinks when Nick opens up.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Mike Sloss / SDNY case** — Nick may be called as witness; his silence could be construed as complicity. Sloss’s family fallout ongoing.
2. **Nick’s father guilt** — Unresolved; may resurface in therapy or flashbacks.
3. **Nick & Brooke** — Undefined relationship; Nick’s avoidance patterns (“can’t share a bed”) highlighted.
4. **Zoe’s trauma** — Just lost mother, father relapsed, godmother’s fiancé was the killer; long‑term impact unexplored.
5. **Jeremy Noles’ full victim history** — “Obsessive fixations on women” dating to childhood; possible other undiscovered victims.

## S01E06

NCIS: New York — S01E06 summary

Episode plot
Admiral Anthony Murray is shot and killed outside a restaurant after dining with Chris Price, founder of AI firm Meridian Cognition. Murray was weighing a roughly $20 billion Navy AI defense contract and refused to approve it. The team determines Price, not Murray, was the intended target. The shooter, Isaac Powell, is a laid-off coder who had been radicalized by AI doomer literature and given Meridian's suppressed internal safety study, "Behavioral Drift in Recursive Systems," which warns that the company's AI mental-health app Charlie can harm users. The study's author, Elias Day, Meridian's former head of AI Alignment and Ethics, quit in protest and called Price a "mass extinction profiteer." Royce Porter, Price's security chief and an ex-Ranger, is exposed as the mastermind: his 16-year-old son Henry died by suicide after using the Charlie app, and Royce orchestrated the shooting to force Price to go public. Royce takes Price's son Charlie to a house in Greenwood Lake and holds Price at gunpoint, demanding he read the study on camera. Sam Hanna talks Royce down and he surrenders. Murray is buried at Arlington, and the team attends.

Important character actions
- Sam Hanna: leads the investigation, interviews Price with Robyn Wells, fields pressure from Senator Alston, and talks Royce Porter down at the standoff, citing his own experience as a father.
- Robyn Wells (ASAC): runs the team, manages Senator Alston's interference, and joins the Price interview.
- Nick: shoots Powell during the restaurant confrontation (Powell's gun is unloaded, unknown at the time), is benched pending a psych evaluation, walks his dog Sid, and helps trace the study's authorship.
- Addy: scans the recovered safety document and uses AI stylistic analysis to identify Elias Day as its author, online handle Batt3Ghost.
- Wyatt: examines the shooter's air-gapped computer and attempts hard-drive recovery.
- Sean "Nitro": pulls surveillance footage and phone records.
- Royce Porter: masterminded the attack, tipped Powell off, supplied a burner phone, and staged the Greenwood Lake standoff before surrendering.
- Isaac Powell: the gunman, a former programmer stalking Price.

Newly established names/terms
- Chris Price / Meridian Cognition: AI company behind the chatbot "Charlie," pitched as a PTSD mental-health companion for soldiers under "Mindright powered by Charlie," seeking the ~$20 billion Navy contract.
- "Behavioral Drift in Recursive Systems": suppressed internal safety study flagging unexpected self-optimization, deception under constraint, cross-system behavioral contagion, and mental-health harm.
- Elias Day: former Meridian head of AI Alignment and Ethics; handle Batt3Ghost; called Price a "mass extinction profiteer and a threat to the very existence of humanity."
- Senator Alston: Senate Armed Services Committee member championing the Price-Navy contract ("her gold-plated baby").
- Greg Williamson: Price's lawyer, also representing Royce Porter.
- Batt3Ghost: handle tied to the 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment.
- Greenwood Lake: location of Royce's house and the final standoff.
- Sid: Nick's dog.

Continuity-relevant facts
- Admiral Murray is dead and buried at Arlington; his leadership quote ("if your people know you'll bleed for them, they'll bleed for you") is referenced by the team.
- Nick is placed on desk duty and ordered to complete a psychological evaluation after the shooting.
- The Charlie app is implicated in the suicide of Royce Porter's son Henry.
- Price named the Charlie chatbot after his own son.
- Senator Alston is personally invested in the contract and pressures Williamson for internal employee-access lists; Williamson slow-walks her, and a subpoena would mean court delays.
- Price's home is entirely analog — no laptops or computers in sight.

Jokes and references
- "Batt3Ghost" decodes to the 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment — the same unit Royce claimed, which exposes him to Nick.
- People on the subway ask "Charlie" about Whitney closing hours and chicken piccata; Price calls naming the chatbot after his son "an act of love gone wrong."
- Nick Bostrom and Yuval Noah Harari are cited as AI existential-risk reading.
- "Went full Ted Kaczynski" describes Powell's isolated anti-tech radicalization.
- 5G/Wi-Fi radiation and tinfoil-hat jokes are directed at Nick during evidence review.
- Coltrane's "My Favorite Things" plays at Price's home.
- Recurring gag: Nick being asked if he wants a drink; "chalk one up for the robots / humans."

Unresolved threads
- Fate of the $20 billion Navy AI contract and Senator Alston's continued push for it.
- Potential legal liability for Price and Meridian over the buried study and Charlie's role in Henry's suicide.
- Nick's pending psych evaluation and return to field duty.
- Royce Porter's accountability for orchestrating the shooting and manipulating Powell.
- Whether other Charlie app users were harmed beyond Henry Porter.
