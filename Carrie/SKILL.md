# Carrie translation context

# Carrie — Background for Subtitle Translation

A reusable reference for translating subtitles of the Prime Video series *Carrie*. Verified facts come from Amazon Studios press materials, the Prime Video listing, the Carrie Universe Wiki, the Wikipedia miniseries article, Entertainment Weekly, and NYT / Variety / Deadline / TV Insider coverage. No prior ASSRT subtitles below ID 800000 were readable, so established Chinese renderings follow the long-standing conventions of licensed Chinese editions of Stephen King's novels and earlier Carrie adaptations.

## 1. Title and series facts

- Title: Carrie (mini-series; eight episodes, single season announced). Use 《嘉莉》 as the standard Chinese title, matching established King translations. Do not append "电视版" unless space allows 《剧集：嘉莉》.
- Format: American supernatural horror / teen-drama miniseries.
- Developed and showrun by Mike Flanagan; Stephen King executive produces. Production: Amazon MGM Studios, Intrepid Pictures, Red Room Pictures. Music: The Newton Brothers. Cinematography: Michael Fimognari.
- Premiere: October 7, 2026, exclusively on Amazon Prime Video, in over 240 countries and territories.
- Production dates: principal photography in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, June 16 – October 17, 2025.
- Season/episode structure: Season 1, eight episodes, all released October 7, 2026. Episode titles double as high-school subjects:
  1. First Period — 《第一节课》
  2. Foreign Language — 《外语》
  3. Social Studies — 《社会研究》 / 《社会学》
  4. Dramatic Arts — 《戏剧》 / 《表演艺术》
  5. Physical Education — 《体育》
  6. Home Economics — 《家政》
  7. Romantic Literature — 《浪漫文学》
  8. AP Physics — 《AP 物理》 / 《大学预科物理》
- Episode unit:集. The show itself is 限定剧 / 迷你剧. Refer to Flanagan as 主创/showrunner, not only 导演.

## 2. World and premise

- Time and place: contemporary United States, social-media era. The town is modeled on King's fictional Chamberlain, Maine. The school is Thomas Ewen Consolidated High School ("Ewen High"), carried forward from earlier adaptations.
- Tone: grounded teen drama with supernatural horror. Modernization hinges on viral bullying, phone-recorded incidents, and online pile-ons — translate literally ("viral bullying scandal" → 网络霸凌丑闻," "goes viral" → 疯传/病毒式传播," "social-media age" → 社交媒体时代).
- Core premise: Carrie White has been raised in near-seclusion with her protective mother Margaret. After her father Ralph dies suddenly, the household comes to the attention of authorities and Carrie enters public school for the first time. She faces bullying led by Chris Hargensen and her clique, the divided loyalties of Sue Snell and Tommy Ross, gym teacher Miss Desjardin who defends her, and the gradual awakening of telekinetic ("念动力" / "心灵遥感") powers. Across the season, small choices build toward a single, shattering night centered on the senior prom ("毕业舞会"), exploring kindness vs. cruelty and whether she ends up hero, monster, or something in between.
- Adaptation note for subtitles: unlike the 1976 film, Margaret is portrayed as fiercely loving and protective rather than purely tyrannical, and Carrie enters the world wide-open, eager and trusting. This nuance shapes lines on "protecting" / "sheltering" Carrie.

## 4. Recurring terminology and Chinese renderings

- telekinesis / telekinetic powers → 念动力 / 心灵遥感; the show pairs this with "the awakening of mysterious powers" ("神秘力量的觉醒"). Avoid 超能力 / 特异功能.
- prom → 毕业舞会 (preferred over期末返校节 for consistency with the 1976 film rendering).
- high school senior prom → 高中毕业舞会.
- public high school (first time) → 公立高中（首次入学 / 第一次走进公立高中）.
- viral (post / incident) → 疯传 / 病毒式传播 / 走红网络 — pick one and stay consistent.
- social-media age → 社交媒体时代.
- prom prank / senior prank → 毕业舞会恶作剧.
- "kindness vs. cruelty" → 善意与残忍 / 善良与恶意 — the show's tagline; render identically whenever voiced.
- Christian fundamentalism → 宗教狂热 / 基要派信仰; Margaret's faith is intense, protective Christian fundamentalism.
- telekinetic ability to move objects → 念力移物 / 隔空移物.
- telekinetic episode (medical / overuse) → 念动力发作 / 能力发作; never translate as "剧集" (that means episode of the show).
- bleeding (nosebleed, bodily backlash) → 流鼻血 / 身体反噬 — keep medical tone literal.
- Principal Grayle → 格雷尔校长 / 校长格雷尔.
- Ewen High / Thomas Ewen Consolidated High School → 尤恩高中 / 托马斯·尤恩联合高中.
- Chamberlain, Maine → 缅因州张伯伦镇 (interchangeable with 钱伯伦镇 — pick one and lock it).
- Mrs. White / the Whites → 怀特太太 / 怀特一家.
- "make a hero, a monster, or something more complicated" → 造就一个英雄、一个怪物、或者更复杂的存在 — verbatim when Carrie's arc is discussed.
- pig's blood → 猪血; for the bucket incident, the established King novel idiom is "猪血淋头".
- prom court (king/queen) → 舞会国王 /舞会王后.
- limo → 豪华轿车 / 加长礼车.
- after-party → 续摊.
- viral clip / short-form video → 短视频.
- DM →私信.
- trending → 上热搜 / 冲上热搜.

## 5. Speaking styles

- Margaret White: melodically soft but zealously certain; mixes scriptural cadence with home-schooling vocabulary. References to sin (罪), repentance (忏悔), and protection (庇护 / 守护) carry heavy religious weight.
- Carrie White: tentative, wide-eyed, earnest; simple syntax, frequent "I'm sorry" / "I didn't mean to", many ellipses. When her power awakens, voice flattens, declarative, eerily calm.
- Chris Hargensen: sharp, contemptuous, social-media fluent; leans on Gen Z / Alpha slang, mocking tone, often breaks tension with a phone screen.
- Sue Snell: earnest, anxious, prone to self-justification; the recurring motif "Is it too late?" → 还来得及吗？
- Tommy Ross: kind, plainspoken, athletic; brief, warm.
- Miss Desjardin: firm, protective, no-nonsense teacher; literal disciplinary vocabulary (请假 / 处分 / 停课).
- Billy Nolan: slacker-cadence, slang-heavy; soften slang just enough for comprehension without losing the delinquent feel.
- Henry Grayle: bureaucratic, sonorous; titles matter (校长 before surname).
- John Hargensen: lawyerly, clipped; "my client" → 我的当事人 carries weight in key scenes.

## 6. Cultural context to keep consistent

- The setting updates King's 1974 novel into the social-media era: viral clips, group chats, anonymous accounts, online pile-ons, school-board press cycles. Subtitles must keep these literal — "snaps" → 短视频, "DM" → 私信, "trending" → 上热搜.
- The bullying pivots on a filmed gym-shower scene and its viral fallout; Carrie's menarche in the gym is a flashpoint mirroring the novel and the 1976 film. Subtitles must remain non-euphemistic; do not soften "first period" or menstruation references. The episode title "First Period" plays on school-class and menarche; translate the school-class sense and let context carry the double meaning.
- Margaret's religiosity draws on American Evangelical / charismatic-Protestant imagery; render "prayer" / "the Lord" / "amen" without importing Catholic imagery not in source. Do not substitute "修女" for "祷告".
- The prom is treated with American high-school ritual weight (crown, court, limo, after-party); translate each as the standard Chinese equivalents (舞会国王 / 王后, 豪华轿车, 续摊).
- Carrie's telekinesis is presented as adrenaline-triggered, especially under threat or humiliation; moving objects, shattering glass, and starting fires should remain matter-of-fact, not magical-girl-coded.

## 7. Translation do / do-not

Do:
- Keep Christian religious vocabulary literal and consistent; respect Margaret's fundamentalism as belief, not mockery.
- Render "telekinesis" / "telekinetic" as 念动力 or 心灵遥感, never as 超能力 / 特异功能.
- Keep proper nouns stable across episodes: 怀特, 哈根森, 斯内尔, 德雅尔丹, 格雷尔 — three lines, three surnames.
- Keep school-subject episode titles literal so the pattern 《第一节课/外语/社会研究/戏剧/体育/家政/浪漫文学/AP 物理》 is recognizable.

Do not:
- Do not localize character names phonetically into novel graphics (e.g., 嘉莉 is locked; do not change to 卡莉 / 凯莉 mid-series).
- Do not use honorific 你 excessively — keep school-formality in teacher/student lines (e.g., 老师 rather than repeated 你).
- Do not refer to the show as a 电影; it is 剧集 / 迷你剧.
- Do not append subtitle after 《嘉莉》 unless space clearly demands 《嘉莉 第一季》 or 《嘉莉（剧集）》.
- Do not soften bullying or menstruation references; the show does not.
