## S22E02

### 剧情梗概
本集是 Only Connect 扩展季（全季 38 期）开局阶段，Two Hearts 与 Ad Astra 两队首次正名上场，前五十分钟主要是 **Lion / Eye of Horus / Water / Twisted Flax / Horned Viper** 轮替进行连接题。  
- Two Hearts 以“老虎”为题率先得分，靠 Malt Extract→Tony the Tiger→Katy Perry 等误导后又抓回“Tigger”与“被老虎吞掉”线。  
- Ad Astra 在 Eye of Horus 中用 “Eagles/Crystal Palace + Super…” 解出一套 **“normal/super”** 变体题。  
- 两队在水题、Twisted Flax（音乐）与 Horned Viper（图像）里互有失手与挽回，最终 Round 1 仍偏向 Ad Astra。  
- Round 2 是序列题，Two Hearts 在 **棋手称谓（king/bishop/knight/rook）与法国面包 fougasse** 上有关键突破；Ad Astra 在超市高端牌系序列上拿分；Two Hearts 末尾题目“真鸭子”卡壳未得分。  
- Round 3 连接墙 Two Hearts 与 Ad Astra 各解满 10 分。  
- Round 4 缺失元音题非常接近，最终 One team 25 分、另一队 24 分，Two Hearts 以一分优势赢局，Ad Astra 虽惜败但获得本等级复活机会。

### 重要角色动作
- **Eden（Two Hearts 队长）**：带队首次公开作答，保持稳健思路，关键时刻抓到老虎题。  
- **Ben（Two Hearts）**：Water 题与后续序列里多次参与关键拆解。  
- **Olivia（Two Hearts）**：与 Ben 的长期搭档关系强化了团队默契与信息输出。  
- **Louise（Ad Astra 队长）**：保持对“super”与图像序列题的高速度反应，Round 4 进入最后阶段仍执行到位。  
- **David / Paul（Ad Astra）**：父子/夫妻身份说明队伍结构后续可持续利用；在 round 中多次给出核心联想关键词。  
- **主持人**：频繁提供 bonus chance 与元音重排线索，并在关键处点明答题逻辑（如“looks-like/sounds-like”鸭子谚语）。

### 新增人名与术语
- 人物/名词：**Bertie Bassett（Liquorice Allsorts 角色）**、**Karl Lagerfeld**、**Claudia Winkleman**、**Fagin**、**Jean Rook**、**Beverley Knight**、**John Bishop**、**The Kinks**、**Demis Roussos**、**Robbie Williams**、**Lemar**。  
- 题材关键词：**“Super”扩展组合法**（Crystal Palace/Eagles 与 Super ...）、**缺失元音（Missing Vowels）**。  
- 其他：**Sainsbury’s Specially Selected / Tesco Finest**、**fougasse**（加百里香的法式面包）、**gallinule**（误导中的“像鸭但非鸭”桥段）。

### 连续性相关要点
- 剧集明确宣告本季是 **38 集扩展季**（非 32 集）。  
- 两队都可视为“新鲜配置”的公开首次参赛，后续可继续追踪其对抗风格。  
- Two Hearts 以 One-Point 胜局领先；Ad Astra 仅差一分且仍有复活机会，形成可追分延续叙事。  
- 队伍设定（David 为父亲、Paul 为丈夫）和 Louise 的婚戒小轶事是可复用的连续背景信息。

### 彩蛋与梗（可供后续剧集引用）
- 主持人强调“**看起来像鸭、叫起来像、游起来像=像鸭**”的典型类比，形成典型节目式教学型笑点。  
- Two Hearts 提到“你们太玩电子游戏”，并在末段被反向利用为得分优势来源。  
- 题目里连续出现的游戏语言：**Game over / Insert coin / Ready player one / Next level**。  
- 提到 Owen Paul 公开说这档节目是“最喜欢的浪费时间节目”——可复用成观众互动/节目口碑梗。  

### 未解线索 / 续集关注点
- 本集无重剧情悬念，主要是对决结果导向；唯一可延续的是：  
  - **Ad Astra 的复活机会/补偿局**（本等级可再博一局争取晋级）。  
  - 两队下一阶段是否能再现 Round 4 的末段高压表现。

## S22E03

### 节目进展

- 本集是新一季加长版的开场，规则强调第四轮可能带来较大的分差。
- “默西塞德之眼队”对阵“左撇子队”。首轮结束时，默西塞德之眼队以 2–3 落后。
- 第二轮后比分为 7–8，左撇子队继续保持领先。
- 两队的连接墙都拿到满分 10 分，总分变为默西塞德之眼队 17 分、左撇子队 18 分。
- 最终“元音缺失”环节中，左撇子队表现突出，以 28–20 获胜，直接晋级下一轮；默西塞德之眼队仍未淘汰，还需再赢一场才能晋级。

### 重要人物与队伍行动

- 左撇子队由米勒·麦格罗蒂领队，成员还有米娅·伊格尔瑟姆和吉娜·贝尔德；三人以左撇子身份为队名与队伍特色。
- 默西塞德之眼队由埃利奥特·奥蒂领队，成员为托卢·阿贾伊和 AJ·道勒，三人均来自绍斯波特。
- 米勒提到队伍从第 15 季左右开始观看节目，近期补看旧档案。
- 埃利奥特因曾被鹅追赶而反复做噩梦，主持人将其与《小丑回魂》中会变形成恐惧对象的生物联系起来。
- AJ 曾为 Boney M 担任暖场嘉宾；托卢曾入围“年度眼镜佩戴者”决赛。

### 新建立的名称与术语

- 队伍：Sinistrals＝左撇子队；Merseyside Eyes＝默西塞德之眼队。
- 题目图标：Eye of Horus、Horned Viper、Twisted Flax、Lion、Water、Two Reeds。
- 连接墙与题目术语：Connecting Wall、Missing Vowels、MRS GREN。
- MRS GREN 代表生物的生命特征：Movement、Respiration、Sensitivity、Growth、Reproduction、Excretion、Nutrition。
- 节目说明采用总分制，第四轮的分值变化可能显著影响最终排名。

### 连续性相关事实

- 左撇子队晋级下一轮，默西塞德之眼队进入下一场级别赛。
- 本集进一步确立主持人擅长把选手背景、答题失误和文化知识转化为即兴笑料。
- 两队都展现出较强的连接墙能力；左撇子队在“元音缺失”环节尤其强势。
- 左撇子队的队员年龄偏轻，主持人曾预期他们认不出帕特里夏·劳特利奇，结果米娅最终辨认出来。

### 笑点与可延续参考

- 节目以“总分大约 3250 至 3675 分”调侃体育直播的宣传口吻。
- 选手把数字线索、足球队和体育图片误认为运动类题目，主持人反复打趣“今晚不是体育之夜”。
- 主持人声称自己做过一个给《连线》参赛者评定“性感与否”的网站，并称本集排名很高。
- 关于 Facebook、YouTube、eBay 和 Netflix 起源的题目被说明可能只是创业民间传说。
- 鹅、窗帘和“跳出来吓醒沙发上的伴侣”构成片尾笑料，或可作为主持人口头风格的延续参考。

### 未解决线索

- 默西塞德之眼队虽落败，仍保留晋级机会，后续比赛结果未定。
- 节目没有交代两队在下一场比赛中的对手或具体安排。
- 若后续继续引用本集，创业网站起源故事应视为趣闻，不能当作完全可靠的历史事实。

## S22E04

# Only Connect – S22E04 Summary

## Episode Overview
A first-round heat featuring two teams of three competing across Connections, Sequences, Connecting Wall, and Missing Vowels.

## Teams
- **Frenemies (亦敌亦友队)** – Captain Nia Roberts (former civil servant, Basque football chants), Jason Butler (warehouse team leader, former Darts League chairman), Karen Wooding (former CSR, heritage railway enthusiast). They met on rival quiz teams.
- **Marvins (马文队)** – Captain Ben Jewell (public health registrar, Northumbrian pipes player), Dan Walsh (project manager, played football at 9,000 ft), Kate Mac (policy officer, knitter). United by admiration for Douglas Adams; they are colleagues from an unnamed UK council.

| Category | Winning Team | Solution |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Stage names with titles (Mr Beast, Mrs Hinch, Ms Dynamite, Master Chief) | Frenemies (bonus) | People whose public name includes a title |

## Outcome
- **Frenemies win** and advance directly to the next round.
- **Marvins** are not eliminated; they receive another chance later in the competition.

## Continuity & Reference Notes
- **Echo chamber**: Producer Jase (question setter) noted he and five male colleagues all knew "Mr Beast" but not "Mrs Hinch", illustrating generational/cultural knowledge gaps.
- **Grandfather's axe / Trigger's broom / Sugababes**: Referenced when discussing the Ship of Theseus paradox — identity persistence despite component replacement.
- **Only Fools and Horses**: "Trigger's broom" explicitly cited as the modern cultural touchstone for the paradox.
- **Sugababes**: All three original members eventually replaced; the name was used by both the reformed originals and the new lineup simultaneously.
- **2:22 A Ghost Story**: West End horror play; described by Jase as "absolutely terrifying."
- **Bad *Just a Minute* players**: Reference to the long-running BBC Radio 4 panel show — contestants who repeat themselves.
- **"What would Rick do?"**: Dan's line before a bold gamble — likely *Rick and Morty* reference.
- **Letter from Ron in Carlisle**: A viewer records episodes and plays last week's to fool his wife into thinking he knows all the answers — a self-defeating conceit since posting the letter exposes the trick.

## New Names/Terms Established
- **Mrs Hinch** (Sophie Hinchliffe): Cleaning influencer; used as cultural litmus test.
- **John-117**: Master Chief from *Halo* video game series.
- **Sol-fa scale**: Do, Re, Mi, Fa with double meanings.
- **Desmond Tutu / Douglas Hurd**: Rhyming slang for "3rd" (Third class degree).
- **Magnus Norman**: Former world No. 2 Swedish tennis player.
- **Cypriot cities**: Larnaca, Limassol, Paphos, Nicosia (by population).

## Unresolved Threads
- Marvins remain in the competition as a "highest-scoring loser" and will return in a later play-off round.
- The Sugababes naming paradox is mentioned but not fully explored as a potential future question source.

## S22E05

# Only Connect — Series 22 Episode 5

## Plot summary
A new "extended, longer series run" opens with host Victoria Coren Mitchell joking that the change might finally let her finish Paradise Lost. Two teams compete across four rounds. The Colonial Geese stumble badly through round one but mount a strong comeback, ultimately winning 16–14 and advancing; the Double Features finish on 14 and are told they will get "another chance to win through later on."

## Teams and characters
- The Double Features (right side): Mia Lewis (captain, comedy graduate who performed stand-up for an Australian cricket team), Katie Rae Taylor (trainee hairstylist and amateur sculptor), Daisy Jeavons (comedy writing/performance graduate who paints friends as cats). All fans of The Rocky Horror Picture Show. Only do "a few pub quizzes," not structured.
- The Colonial Geese (left side): Henry Schofield (captain, anaesthetic resident, makes tiramisu), Reuben Bennett (paediatric resident, first job selling fruit), Sharon Lam (architectural designer who designed her own loo roll holder). All from New Zealand. Self-described weak on British culture; revised British monarchs, planned to revise motorways. Henry says he's looking forward to the Missing Vowels round — and the team groans.

## Round 1 — Questions (Geese 2, Double Features 3)
- Twisted Flax (music, Colonial Geese): all songs about underground transport systems — Chappell Roan's "Subway," The Jam's "Down In The Tube Station At Midnight" (the host needles them for confusing it with their own "Going Underground"), "Deeper Underground," "Metro." Geese guess "Systems" but can't specify; Double Features answer.
- Eye of Horus (Double Features): clues relate to exceptions in Paralympic sport rules — judogi/judo (vision-impaired players start by gripping each other), bench press (performed lying down), "a second bounce is permitted" (wheelchair tennis), visually impaired football (ball filled with bells; crowd told to stay silent except on goals).
- Water (Geese): picture clues link to explorers — duck = Drake (Sir Francis Drake), chopping vegetables = Cook (Captain Cook), chopper bicycle = Raleigh (Sir Walter Raleigh), polo = Polo (Marco Polo).
- Horned Viper (Geese): all known as "Sir" without being knighted — "Sir George Stubbs" (self-styled in an Agatha Christie novel), footballer Les Ferdinand ("Sir Les"), Stevie Wonder's "Sir Duke" (about Duke Ellington), Robot Wars' "Sir Killalot." Neither team spots it; host reveals.
- Two Reeds (Geese): pairs that are perpendicular to each other, including warp/weft (cricket stumps) and x-axis/y-axis. Geese answer after two clues for 2 points.
- Lion (Double Features): each first word means the second if you add "up" — back up = save, leg up = help, face up = accept reality, knees up = party. Double Features answer after three clues for 2 points.

## Round 2 — Sequences (Geese 4, Double Features 8)
- Twisted Flax (Geese): Sesame Street Muppets in rainbow colour order — Elmo (red), Ernie (orange), Big Bird (yellow), then green (Kermit the Frog is the best answer; Oscar the Grouch also accepted). The Snuffleupagus trap is brown. Double Features grab the bonus point.
- Eye of Horus (Double Features): clock-face positions 12, 3, 6, 9 with the hour-hand angle — answer 9 o'clock = 270°. Double Features work it out but say "45°" and lose it; host confirms.
- Horned Viper (Geese): "Big ___" sets needing a "Big Three" — Arthur Ransome's Big Six (Swallows & Amazons Norfolk book), African Big Five (prized/difficult game animals), tennis Big Four (Federer, Nadal, Djokovic, Murray). Geese guess "TP"; Double Features guess "Big Two" then are told the answer is "the Big Three" (e.g. Roosevelt, Stalin, Churchill).
- Water (Double Features): things you do after getting up — wake up, get up, then "What's Going On" (screaming at the top of your lungs, from 4 Non Blondes). Double Features answer correctly.
- Lion (Geese): pinnipeds (flipper-footed sea mammals) in increasing size — Steller sea lion, walrus, Northern elephant seal, Southern elephant seal. Geese answer correctly; host notes it's "in your part of the world" and asks if they've been to Antarctica (they haven't; Victoria mentions a direct Christchurch flight).
- Two Reeds (Double Features): TA, TB, TC, TD — Teaching Assistant, TB (tuberculosis, the nodule-growing bacterial infection the Geese claim as their wheelhouse), TC (Top Cat), and an acceptable fourth is "TD" as an abbreviation for today. Double Features supply TD.

## Round 3 — Connecting Walls (Geese 9, Double Features 11)
- Water wall (Double Features). Four groups: things that come in sticks (Dynamite, Rock, Celery, Chalk); female rappers (Ms Dynamite, Doja Cat, Meghan Thee Stallion, Princess Nokia); brands (Volvo, Lego, Spotify, Carlsberg) — actually Scandinavian brands, but "brands" accepted on opening heat leniency; first-person singular Latin verbs (Credo "I believe," Lego "I read/play," Audio "I hear," Veto "I forbid"). Double Features find three connections = 3 points.
- Lion wall (Colonial Geese). Groups: ___ scheme (Colour, Pension, Ponzi, Rhyme); Egyptian artefacts (Ankh, Cartouche, Pyramid, Sistrum — plus Obelisk left over); Glastonbury festival stages (Acoustic, Avalon, Pyramid, The Other); surnames "Nick ___" (Nolte, Cave, Park, Faldo). Geese find the scheme and Egyptian groups, lose all three lives, but supply three connections = 5 points. Reuben pulls the "Nicks" group from "deep in the subconscious."

## Round 4 — Missing Vowels (Geese win 16–14)
Victoria gives the Geese "a bit of leeway" on vowel pronunciation. Three categories:
- Expressions featuring bathroom items: "throw in the towel" (Double Features), "wash your mouth out with soap" (Geese), "sink or swim" (Geese), "throw the baby out with the bathwater" (Geese).
- People living in Vienna in 1908: Sigmund Freud (Geese), Adolf Hitler (Double Features), Gustav Klimt (Double Features), Leon Trotsky (Geese).
- Items in a first aid kit: thermometer (Geese), antiseptic wipes (Geese), triangular bandage (Double Features — Geese lost the chance by missing the "R" in "triangle"), micropore tape (Geese).

The Geese sweep the round. Final score: Colonial Geese 16, Double Features 14. Victoria notes it is "a great recovery after a shaky start." The Double Features are told they will get another chance to win through later in the series.

## Jokes and references useful for later episodes
- Running meta-joke about Victoria finally finishing Paradise Lost thanks to the new longer series run.
- Henry Schofield's enthusiasm for the Missing Vowels round is established early and pays off in round four.
- Reuben Bennett's "deep in the subconscious" intuition moment on the Lion wall.
- Closing gag: viewers can now dispose of old Only Connect episodes at the local household recycling centre, either in the "rubble and ceramics" skip or by distracting a council operative with a softly spoken question about where to put a broken toaster and slipping the disc into their jacket pocket.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- The Double Features are explicitly told they are "not out of the competition" and will get "another chance to win through later on," so they are scheduled to return in a later series-22 episode.
- Final scores to carry forward if referenced: Colonial Geese 16, Double Features 14.
- Round-by-round running scores: R1 Geese 2 / Features 3; R2 Geese 4 / Features 8; R3 Geese 9 / Features 11; R4 Geese 16 / Features 14.

## Unresolved threads
- The Double Features' promised return appearance in a later series-22 heat.
- Whether Victoria will actually finish Paradise Lost under the new extended format.

## S22E06

S22E06 — Only Connect

Plot
- Episode pits Accrual World (the finance/tax team on Victoria's right) against the Knitwits (the knitwear enthusiasts on her left). Both teams are still in the competition, so there is no elimination this episode — the closing line "who stays in the competition and who also stays in the competition" lands as the running joke. Knitwits win 26–22.

Important character actions
- Victoria opens with a tax-deductibility bit, asking the Accrual World trio why a face-lift or veneers aren't allowable expenses. David Evans says it is a question of dual use; Reggie Chamberlain-Wilde chips in about cases so ridiculous they can only be for commercial gain. Victoria lands the punchline "I am two-faced."
- Victoria asks the Knitwits if they made their own jumpers. Charlotte Stokes says they only consume knitwear, but they have promised to knit jumpers for the final — possibly for each other. Victoria recounts a "last year" team of vegetable enthusiasts who liked other people's vegetables but had never grown one, telling them "don't quote that out of context."
- Knitwits recognise only Andy Flower and Robert Plant on the flora wall; everything else is guessed.
- Accrual World nail the music wall only after losing the lead clue; David Evans identifies Anthem/Fanfare For The Common Man and the Kaiser Chiefs' Everything Is Average Nowadays.
- Sam Hosegood (Knitwits captain) admits he "did Seamus Heaney for GCSE" and loved it, but says "I'm more of a mathematician."
- David Evans drives college minibuses around Durham on a properly insured basis, despite not holding the entitlement on his licence.
- The Knitwits' Connecting Wall solves in full (perfect 10), making the scoreboard tight going into Missing Vowels.
- Victoria closes by reading a letter from Ted in Godalming about his Surrey Sunday League cricket match, referencing Brian Johnston's "bowler's Holding, the batsman's Willey" gag, with local players Chris Cupping, Oliver Balls, Peter Slap and Alan Tickle.

Newly established names/terms
- Accrual World members: Reggie Chamberlain-Wilde (finance officer, regional fencing champion), Niccola Mullen (finance officer, Morris-danced in Milton Keynes), captain David Evans (VAT specialist, darts nickname "The Gentleman").
- Knitwits members: Dom Hewett (librarian, favourite tree silver birch), Charlotte Stokes (quality manager, yoga enthusiast), captain Sam Hosegood (machine learning engineer, failed Grade 4 clarinet).
- Wall/question icons used: Twisted Flax, Two Reeds, Eye of Horus, Water, Lion, Horned Viper.
- Picnic-style quiz references introduced as answers: Tina Turner "Typical Male"; Kaiser Chiefs "Everything Is Average Nowadays"; ELP "Fanfare For The Common Man"; "Anthem For The Common Man"; Pikachu; David Bowie's Aladdin Sane lightning bolt; Kim Betts as Lightning in the original Gladiators; DC's The Flash; Arthur Miller's Death of a Salesman; Seamus Heaney's Death of a Naturalist; Dave Davies of The Kinks' Death of a Clown; the film Death of a Unicorn; Gunter Grass; Barbara Bush; Robert Plant; Andy Flower; PEGI (Pan European Games Information) and BBFC; Linus Pauling (only laureate besides Marie Curie to win Nobel Prizes in two different fields — chemistry and peace); UK driving licence categories (D = bus/minibus, C = HGV, F = tractor); Spanish numbers cero/uno/dos/tres and the shampoo brand Tresemme; wartime PMs Asquith, Lloyd George, Neville Chamberlain, Winston Churchill (3,160 days = 64.49 × Liz Truss's 49).

Continuity-relevant facts
- Victoria's asides position this as a mid-Series 22 episode: she references "last year" vegetable enthusiasts and an earlier Best Exotic Marigold Hotel round from "many years ago" before Joe Exotic was a celebrity.
- Score arc: Round 1 Knitwits 2, Accrual World 7; Round 2 Knitwits 8, Accrual World 13; after Connecting Wall Knitwits 18, Accrual World 19; final Missing Vowels Knitwits 26, Accrual World 22.
- Both teams are described as "still in the competition" — no one is knocked out here.

## Episode PlotSeries22 (a 38-part run including four specials; the host laments the channel rejected his "Specials special" idea). Two new teams compete:

- **Two Hearts** (captain Eden Cook-Watson, with Ben Baker and Olivia Grimes) — united by distaste for Daleks
- **Makeweights** (captain Eleanor Stiles, with Omer Elchanan and Isaac Barden) — united by an obsession for oars

Eden reveals he would be John Simm's The Master; Eleanor mentions running a Barcelona marathon (~4 hours) between episodes. Two Hearts dominate early, lead 7–3 after Round 1, extend to 10–3 after Round 2, and complete a tough Connecting Wall to finish 13–5. They win the Missing Vowels round 18–12 and advance.
