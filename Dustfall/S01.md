## S01E01

# Episode 1

## Plot

Opens the same Saturday night on two parallel threads. At the Royal Arms Hotel, 18‑year‑old Edie Kelly turns down her friend Finn Morrison ("I like you as a friend… I don't want to muck it up"), Finn leaves upset. Meanwhile at her late grandmother's Queenslander, Detective Sergeant Antigone "Tig" Pollard is mid‑date with her boyfriend Mitch, a snake‑catcher, when the call‑out comes: a young woman found naked, injured, in a cane field. Tig goes to the scene and finds Edie, blanketed and barely verbal, with abrasions across her back consistent with being dragged. A farmer who found her is still discharging a firearm around pigs at the scene; Tig confiscates it. Edie's mother Fiona and father Ross are contacted from Brisbane.

The next morning at the hospital, SANE nurse Shayna Griffin performs a forensic exam (internal exam confirms sexual assault); Tig secures the toxicology and DNA kits and orders CCTV from The Royal. A chat with Shayna flags a prior "case on the beach" — a German tourist drugged and raped about seven months earlier whose statement was coerced into retraction after Detective Quinn threatened to charge her with wasting police time; the woman left town. The pattern is now repeating.

That night, Tig's mother Olivia — a gender‑studies academic on sabbatical — turns up unannounced at the house to talk about selling it. Tig refuses; Olivia plans to write and clear the place out. Olivia crashes Tig and Mitch's dinner by inviting herself, then invites Tig's old flame Andy D'Silva, a local musician who once gave Tig guitar lessons. Olivia provocatively defends older‑man/younger‑woman attraction as "an anthropological norm." Andy mentions his 15‑year‑old daughter Krystal. By the end of the night Tig is exhausted, Olivia has the run of the house, and the case is far from closed.

## Important character actions

- Edie Kelly: refuses Finn at the pub, calls a ride that never shows, gets cornered by "Davo," is approached by a stranger called "Jack" who buys her a drink, then loses memory until waking alone and naked in the cane field. Speaks in fragments during the forensic exam; in a closing flashback asks a man to stop questioning her and panics.
- Remi: runs errands for Tig — pulls CCTV, traces Edie's phone, contacts Fiona, flags that Finn Morrison's phone is out of range and he didn't show for school.
- Shayna Griffin: conducts the SANE exam, discloses the earlier "German girl" case, confirms the clinical findings, and notes Edie was almost certainly dragged through the field.
- Tig: handles the field scene, sits with Edie, drives the case forward, spars with her new boss over calling forensics without sign‑off, and confronts her mother over the house and her past with Andy.
- Olivia Pollard: arrives uninvited, takes over the house, invites herself to dinner, invites Andy, deploys academic put‑downs, drops "patriarchal incarcerative state" on Tig's career choice, and tries to charm Mitch while undermining Tig.
- Andy D'Silva: shows up at the pub and at the house with condolences for Nan and flowers; reveals his history with Tig and Olivia, mentions daughter Krystal (15), and sings a song containing the line "Sent me back home to our September song."
- Mitch: chops wood, fixes Tig's loo (and walks in unannounced while she's showering), absorbs Olivia's provocations, leaves before bed.
- Fiona Kelly: arrives from Brisbane with Ross, resists Tig's request to leave the room during the statement, eventually concedes; later shows up uninvited at the police station and is sent home.

- Finn Morrison: rejected by Edie, leaves the pub before her, hasn't been contactable since; his name surfaces on social media in connection with the assault.

## S01E05

## Episode S01E05 — Dustfall

### Plot
The investigation escalates after Simon Prendergast is arrested — his DNA matches one of Edie Kelly's rapists — only for him to be found dead in his car outside the gym (GHB in his system, same drug used on victims). A second suspect, Davo Bradley, is also dead. Tig connects Edie's case to Jade Dawson's assault from a year earlier: identical MO (GHB, multiple attackers, white van). Jade recalls a distinctive smell — eucalyptus/spicy/clove, stronger than aftershave. Tig discovers Edie and Prendergast both had Daintree ulcers (mosquito-borne Buruli ulcer) treated with rifampicin + clarithromycin, and seeks pharmacy records to identify other perpetrators. A Polaroid of a young girl (likely Edie) is found in Andy D'Silva's studio; Andy is questioned, his friend Lachie (who has a studio key and is an avid cyclist) brought in. CCTV shows a white van with a bike rack near both crime scenes. Prendergast's gym locker yields a burner phone, GHB, needles, and "souvenir" photos of unconscious women — one likely Edie, others possibly Jade Dawson and Hannah Mueller. Two men are taken into custody at episode's end. Meanwhile, Maya meets Noah (her alleged rapist from the "beach case"); he admits the assault but demands she drop the defamation suit. Tig confronts her mother Olivia about Andy grooming her at 16 (he was 32).

- **Andy**: Questioned over Polaroid in his studio; claims it's "bullshit"; names Lachie as only other key holder.
- **Lachie**: Andy's friend, strong cyclist (300–400 km/week), brought in for questioning.
- **Ronnie**: Prendergast's terrified partner, reveals his cycling obsession and gym locker habits.

## S01E06

# Dustfall S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
The investigation into Edie Kelly's drug-facilitated gang rape converges on a cycling group connected to Simon Prendergast. Tig and Remi identify Dean Ogilvy and Greg Filmore as two of the three perpetrators via DNA, cycling app (Spinner) records, and the distinctive muscle-balm smell (eucalyptus/clove) from the crime scene. Dean is arrested at North Gap High; Greg is picked up at his pharmacy. However, CCTV proves neither could have murdered Simon Prendergast (both were at the Royal until after 11 pm). Edie, traumatised and disillusioned by the prospect of a years-long trial, refuses to testify. Shayna Griffin, burnt out by systemic re-victimisation, quits to scatter her late partner Alex's ashes. The episode ends with Tig walking into a police station to report her own historical sexual assault.

## Important Character Actions
- **Tig**: Interrogates Andy D'Silva (cleared via alibi/DNA); pushes Dean Ogilvy interview; confronts Shayna over Edie's withdrawal; ultimately reports her own past assault.
- **Remi**: Runs CCTV/Spinner analysis; identifies the cycling group link; verifies Dean/Greg alibis for Simon's murder night.
- **Andy D'Silva**: Provides DNA, alibi (Myrtle Bay with Dana Roebuck); denies knowledge of photo/studio intruders.
- **Dean Ogilvy**: Arrested at work; his wife Cat Greenslade (Andy's property manager) gave him studio access for cleaning.
- **Lachie Barrett**: Reveals Dean caused his crash during the June 23 Bindi ride and didn't stop; identifies Greg Filmore as support van driver.
- **Greg Filmore**: Pharmacist; drove support van; DNA matches Edie's assault.
- **Edie Kelly**: Refuses to proceed with prosecution despite strong DNA evidence; chooses self-preservation over a 2-year trial.
- **Shayna Griffin**: Supports Edie's withdrawal; clashes with Tig over victim welfare vs. conviction; leaves town.
- **Olivia Pollard**: Dropped defamation case after meeting Noah; Tig acknowledges their fractured history.

- **Cat Greenslade** – Property manager, Dean's wife, gave him studio access.
