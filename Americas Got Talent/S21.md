## S21E08

## S21E08 《America’s Got Talent》内容摘要

本集是节目本季海选收官日（**last day of auditions / 现场赛还有 44 个名额**），前段 Judges’ panel 先回顾“很多好表演、金色按钮用尽、今晚必须精挑细选”，并明确最终会进入“需再看一遍的争议区”讨论。海选现场节奏从高能舞台秀到情绪化才艺再到投票复盘，整体结构为：

- **开场与压力说明**：多次强调 “golden buzzers 用完/没有剩余” 与“还有很多通过者”；强调今天是最后一天，入场评审需从约 100 个候选中筛掉大量。
- **演出推进**：先后出现《The Sound》家庭乡村乐队（高票通过）、“Cat Williams”喜剧开场与反复被否的笑点段落、“Los Servidores”玛丽亚奇改编组、Sarah Hardwig（盲人歌手）与其情绪共鸣突破、“Maria”柔术连贯挑战、以及“Pynk Beard”创作歌手团。
- **舞台氛围转换点**：多次出现“观众即兴嘘声/掌声交替”，笑点段落与高质量表演交错，形成明显“观众情绪拉锯”。
- **收官前后**：Sarah 和 Pynk Beard 都拿到四票通过；主持台与评委在节目尾段正式进入裁决模式，强调“很多必须再看一次的项目”（re-see shortlist），随后启动分组复看机制。

## 重要角色动作与决策要点

### 已确认明确过线（明显高亮）
- **The Sound（洛密欧，密歇根）**：家人组，乡村歌手，获得多位评委认可并通过。
- **Los Servidores（南加州社区合唱/玛丽亚奇组合）**：节目尾段提及“惊喜/新颖改编”，评委明确正向评价，最终也倾向保留。
- **Sarah Hardwig（田纳西州纳什维尔，18 岁）**：盲人背景、以 Lauren Daigle《You Say》情绪化独唱见长，评委强调她的“声音与感染力”，且表达“观众应被你鼓舞”。

## S21E09

Live Acts Revealed**:

---

## Episode Plot

This is the special "Live Acts Revealed" episode where the judges (Simon Cowell, Howie Mandel, Mel B, Sofía Vergara) and host Terry Crews reveal which audition acts have secured one of the 44 spots in the live shows. The episode opens by recapping the 10 Golden Buzzer acts who already have locked in their places, then transitions into the judges' deliberations to fill the remaining 34 spots. The central conflict emerges: there is far more talent than available spots, forcing the judges to make difficult choices. Simon introduces a new solution — "Judges' Callbacks" — where each judge will personally invite a small group of borderline acts to perform again at their homes for a final chance at the live shows.

## Important Character Actions

- **Simon Cowell**: Proposes the callback system where judges split up and review acts a second time in person. Selects his callback group: Nyjah & Zyah, Akira, Elektro Dads, Travis Garland, Sando, and Elsie. Also had history with Mackenzie (who auditioned for him on *The X Factor* at age 4, 21 years ago).
- **Howie Mandel**: Competes with other judges for Golden Buzzers (jumps on Yu Ho-Jin's buzzer "like his life depended on it"). Selects callback acts: Nene, Naimana & Daniele, Cris Sosa, Rynia, Daniel Alvarez, Bird & Byron.
- **Mel B**: Presses Golden Buzzer for Isaac Atkins without hesitation ("wasn't taking any chances"). Selects callback acts: Butter & Grit, Joel (magician), Lachlan Patterson, Bety, Madelyn & Malachi.
- **Sofía Vergara**: Presses Golden Buzzer for Lara D after missing Lai Noelle (which Simon "stole"). Selects callback acts: Olivia & dogs, Brook Lynn, Darrell, Kameron Marshall, Vahtang.
- **Terry Crews**: Hosts, maintains high energy, delivers news to acts in their hometowns.

## Newly Established Names/Terms

- **Judges' Callbacks / Callbacks**: New mechanism this season where each judge invites ~6 acts to their home for a second performance to decide final live show spots
- **44 live show spots total** (10 Golden Buzzers + 34 deliberated spots)
- **Gallery of Talent**: Room where all yes-act photos are displayed for judges' review
- **Golden Buzzer acts (Season 21)**: Luke, Acro Canine Crew, Lai Noelle, Lara D, Yu Ho-Jin/Hundred Fingers, Isaac Atkins, Rubén Roldán, Mackenzie, Veronika, Royal Lasers

- **Mackenzie**: Auditioned for Simon on *The X Factor* at age 4 (21 years ago), predicted he'd return to AGT stage — now gets Simon's Golden Buzzer on aerial pole

## S21E14

# America's Got Talent — Season 21, Episode 14 (Quarterfinal 2) Summary

## Plot / Show Structure

- This is the **second live quarterfinal**. Eleven acts perform; only **three** advance to the semifinal based on America's vote. Voting runs on the AGT app, NBC.com/AGT, and TikTok (comment the act's name on the official post). Each viewer may cast up to 10 votes per act; window closes at **7:00 a.m. Eastern the next day**.
- **Simon Cowell is absent**, undergoing an operation that cannot be postponed (not an emergency). Terry Crews sends best wishes on air; Howie later pleads "Come back soon, Simon." The panel is reduced to **Howie Mandel, Mel B, and Sofía Vergara**.
- **Mel B holds this week's Golden Buzzer**, with power to send an act straight to the **final** (bypassing the semifinal).
- Backstage TikTok co-hosts: **Kaylor Martin** (Love Island) and **Dan Gheesling** (Big Brother winner).
- Terry opens with a tribute to **Dolly Parton**, who passed away earlier that day.
- Tomorrow night's results show will feature performances by **Steve Ray Ladson** and **The War and Treaty**.

## Acts and Outcomes

1. **LaRUSSELL** (rapper, Vallejo, CA) — performs his original "Dreams / Tell Me What You Living For" with a large crew of Bay Area kids. Backstory: launched community "Back Yard" shows after working at UPS. Judges found it chaotic but full of energy; Howie compared it to a high-school recital rather than a professional stage.
