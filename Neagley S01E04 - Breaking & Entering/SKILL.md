## Series Overview

- **Creators/Showrunners:** Nick Santora & Nicholas Wootton
- **Based on:** Character created by Lee Child; spin-off from *Reacher*

## Premise
Frances Neagley (Maria Sten), former 110th Special Investigations Unit Army investigator and Jack Reacher’s closest former colleague, now works as a private investigator in Chicago. When a beloved friend from her past dies in a suspicious accident, she launches a relentless pursuit of justice that uncovers a conspiracy centered on **Echo Canyon** — a clandestine facility conducting human experimentation under the guise of a "project." The investigation pulls in police corruption, military secrets, and Neagley’s own buried childhood trauma.

## Key Relationships
- **Neagley ↔ Reacher:** Former Army 110th Special Investigations Unit partners; deep trust, shorthand communication.
- **Neagley ↔ Calvin (father):** Strained; he knows secrets about her childhood (aversion to touch, first-grade report card) but refuses to speak.
- **Neagley ↔ Hudson:** Reluctant allies; he bends rules for her, she pushes legal boundaries.
- **Neagley ↔ Renee:** Close working partnership; Renee provides intel, tech, emotional grounding.
- **Keno ↔ Mandy:** Protective; Keno risks extraction to save her from "the project."
- **Woodrow ↔ Cole:** Co-leads of Echo Canyon; Woodrow handles operations/finance, Cole runs the science.
- **Plemel ↔ Murphy ↔ CPD corruption:** Police hierarchy compromised by Echo Canyon money.

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **US military/MP culture:** Rank, unit pride, "battle buddy" bonds inform Neagley/Reacher dynamic.
- **Chicago PD hierarchy:** Deputy Chief > Commander > Lieutenant > Sergeant > Detective > Officer. Corruption arcs reflect real CPD history (Jon Burge era, consent decree).
- **Private investigator license:** Neagley operates legally but bends/breaks laws; "PI" status lets her access scenes police can't without warrant.
- **Pharma/experimentation ethics:** Echo Canyon echoes MKUltra, Tuskegee, Edgewood Arsenal — American historical touchstones for non-consensual human testing.
- **Subtitle constraints:** 2 lines max, ~38 chars/line (CPS ~17). Prioritize meaning over literalism; preserve Neagley’s clipped rhythm.
