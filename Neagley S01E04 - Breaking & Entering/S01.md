## Neagley S01E04 — "Breaking & Entering"

**Plot Summary**

Neagley, Hudson, and Renée launch a direct assault on Echo Canyon after deciding they can't trust local cops or wait for federal bureaucracy. Neagley bluffs her way in by posing as a Rawlings operative sent to warn Cole of an imminent FBI raid — a ruse that successfully lures Cole and the community underground into a silo. In reality, Rawlings' mercenary team (led by Balega) arrives to destroy all evidence of the project, planting three bombs to kill everyone and make it look like a Jonestown-style mass suicide orchestrated by Cole.

The team splits: Hudson and Renée enter through the front to draw fire, while Neagley infiltrates through the vent system. Keno, held captive in the silo, rallies the trapped community members to break out by brute-forcing the elevator doors. Neagley locates the bombs and, using a shoelace to jam a walkie-talkie's PTT button, creates broad-spectrum radio interference to prevent remote detonation — sacrificing all comms in the process.

Cole is shot during the mercenary assault. Neagley confronts him; he blames Woodrow and Rawlings. She leaves him with a shattered leg. The team evacuates all civilians topside and collects every computer drive and laptop as evidence. Mandy is confirmed dead.

Rawlings, watching news coverage of the explosion from a distance, calmly books a flight to Stockholm, confident in his nine non-extradition citizenships. Neagley intercepts him; he taunts her, certain no arrest can stick. She kills him with a suppressed weapon — "Enjoy your trip."

The next morning, Neagley delivers damning evidence to the FBI, leading to the arrest of Deputy Chief Plemel.

**Key Character Actions**

- Neagley: orchestrates the entire assault, disables the bombs via IED-jamming technique learned in Lebanon, kills Rawlings personally, delivers evidence to FBI
- Hudson: provides armed assault support, fights mercenary guards
- Renée: navigates the vent/silo system, relays intel on bomb locations and civilian positions
- Keno: breaks out of captivity in the silo, rallies community members to force the elevator, survives
- Cole: shot by mercenaries; left alive but injured by Neagley
- Balega: leads Rawlings' mercenary team; all neutralized
- Reacher: arrives at the hospital after the case; Neagley thanks him for always accepting her as she is
- Hudson's confession: tells Neagley he has feelings for her; she acknowledges but says she can't reciprocate physically yet — the cemetery handshake was "a first step"

- Calvin has died (passed the previous night); left Frances a recorded letter and a childhood photo
- Tommy Crane was drawn to Echo Canyon because he was "trying to heal" from the Percy trauma
