## Character relationships
- Hal Jordan — veteran Green Lantern, USAF test pilot, near retirement, training his replacement. Dry-witted, grizzled, "right-stuff" pilot archetype (modeled on Chuck Yeager).
- John Stewart — first Lantern ever directly chosen by the Guardians instead of by a ring. Former Marine and architect; reluctant, principled, intense.
- Hal ↔ John: mentor / mentee, then reluctant detective partners. Tonal dynamic is odd-couple / buddy-cop (Hal blunt and abrasive; John measured and reserved).
- Hal ↔ Sinestro: former mentor / father-figure relationship, now adversarial; Sinestro was expelled after killing a Guardian during a failed coup.
- Hal ↔ Kerry Kane: romantic interest in the series.
- Kerry Kane — sheriff of Rushville, originally from Gotham City, divorced from Billy Macon by 2026 (married in 2016). Suspicious of the arriving "big-shot" Lanterns.
- William "Will" Macon — Kerry's father-in-law, powerful local rancher, paranoid about alien threats.
- Billy Macon — Kerry's ex-husband, Will's son, lawyer, does his father's bidding.
- Zoe Macon — Will's wife; secretly a Manhunter infiltrator who seduces John.
- John Stewart Sr. — John's military father.
- Bernadette Stewart — John's mother.
- Noah Macon — Kerry and Billy's son (child in 2016, teenager in 2026).
- Antaan — alien antagonist with self-detonation ability, avenging the genocide of his people.
- Lianna — Guardian of the Universe who recruits John.

## World setting
- Earth-present + interstellar backdrop. Primary Earth location is Rushville, Nebraska (the rural American heartland / plains), with flashbacks to 2016 and present-day 2026.
- Hal's flashbacks depict his early life and test-pilot career; John's flashbacks show his childhood and adolescence before recruitment.
- Cosmic setting: the Guardians of the Universe rule Oa and govern the Green Lantern Corps across many sectors; Sector 2814 is Earth.
- The Manhunters are ancient predecessor robots to the Green Lantern Corps; some survived and continue covert operations on Earth.
- Sinestro is imprisoned in space as of 2016 after killing a Guardian during his coup; he has escaped by 2026.

## Overall plot premise
A veteran, semi-retired Green Lantern (Hal Jordan) is given the job of training his replacement, John Stewart — a Marine and architect who is the first Lantern ever hand-picked by the Guardians. While John completes his qualification interviews, the two are sent to Rushville, Nebraska to investigate a fatal shooting that Hal believes is extraterrestrial. What looks like a small-town murder opens into a wider conspiracy involving a local rancher family, a Manhunter posing as a human wife, an alien avenger (Antaan), and the still-looming threat of Sinestro, who escaped his prison during the decade between Hal's and John's flashbacks. The series is structured as a slow-burn Earth-based detective mystery — explicitly modeled on True Detective and Slow Horses — rather than a space opera.

## Speaking styles and cultural context
- Hal: grizzled, dry-witted, blunt, old-school American test-pilot register. Short, clipped, often sardonic. Often references his past and his mentor Sinestro.
- John: measured, military-precise, restrained. Speaks like an ex-Marine who is also an introspective architect. Carries weight and regret.
- Sinestro: theatrical, charming, philosophical menace — Hannibal Lecter register — calmly menacing, formally eloquent.
- Kerry: small-town sheriff cadence, terse, territorial, pragmatic.
- Will Macon: rancher patriarch — patriarchal, conspiratorial, rural-American vernacular, paranoid about "outsiders" and "aliens."
- Antaan: grief-driven, ritualistic, alien formality — formal/elevated speech patterns.
- Lianna: serene, oracular Guardian cadence.
- Cultural context: rural Great Plains / American heartland tone for Earth scenes (Rushville, Nebraska) — ranch culture, sheriff politics, family loyalties, anti-outsider suspicion. Cosmic scenes carry formal quasi-religious / mythic weight from the Guardians and Corps tradition. The series blends neo-Western atmosphere with superhero procedural.
- Background music features "State Trooper" by Bruce Springsteen (teaser trailer) and "Beside You in Time" by Nine Inch Nails (final trailer) — American roots / industrial textures reinforcing the mood.
