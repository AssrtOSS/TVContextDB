## S01E01

## S01E01 — Plot

The episode intercuts a 2016 training flashback with the 2026 present-day case that opens the series.

**2016 (flashback).** Hal Jordan is a veteran Lantern being shadowed by his handpicked trainee John Stewart. Hal has just returned from brokering peace on Pharon 4 (drunk, late). John chafes at the slow pace: he has not been allowed to wear the ring, was recruited "the old-fashioned way" by the Guardians, and—two months in—only had his ring activate today in panic, when he used it to manifest "bubble wrap" and survive a HAL-initiated flying-pedagogy ambush. A follow-up "kid-in-a-cloud" backup-consciousness of Hal materializes inside John's ring, confirms the year (2016), answers John's homework questions ("Do I trust you?" → "We get along great"), and tells him: deliver to Hal the line "There's hail in Rushville, Nebraska." Hal walks off to investigate his own future.

**2026 (present).** On a routine taco-stand stop in Rushville, Hal is recognized by fans (he wears the suit "only on special occasions"—ties-as-pain-in-the-ass speech). John catches up; both have been dispatched to a four-dead mass shooting at a Rushville/Kimball rivalry high-school football game. Sheriff Kerry Kane shuts them out on jurisdiction ("extraterrestrial events only"), but Hal insists his gut says otherwise. The two quietly spot a sixth-suspect still on the scene—an outlier whose warm-coat ditching flagged him.

Hal stages his own arrest by starting a bar brawl so he can be booked next to the suspect; charges are eight counts of aggravated assault. Bail ($200K) is posted at the courthouse by William "Will" Macon, a.k.a. the defendant "William Macon for the defense," Kerry's ex-father-in-law and patriarch of the local ranch. Will invites them to supper. Billy Macon (Will's son, Kerry's ex-husband, the lawyer who just retained Hal) doubles down on the dinner pitch.

**At the Macon ranch dinner.** Will lectures on colonization and reveals the household is a self-styled prepper outfit, with a fiery "they shoot an AR-15 like a duck" militia streak. Zoe Macon (Will's wife) handles the kitchen and silently arranges tofu steaks for vegan John. Noah (Billy/Kerry's son, starting quarterback, All-State) practices archery by shooting arrows off someone's head. Kerry arrives separately, furious at Hal, and extracts a promise that Hal will only interrogate with her consent. Hal privately admits to John: "I definitely slept with his wife."

**Morning interrogation.** Kerry allows five minutes; Hal takes the room with Waylon Sanders, a Kimball-based commercial truck driver who somehow made it through the shooting. Hal unmasks him via cultural-quiz gaps: 1970 Huskers championship under Bob Devaney ✓, Heisman winner Johnny Rodgers ✓, Harry Potter's Hogwarts ✗, Barack Obama limited to "president, controversy over his birth certificate." Waylon hedges, then delivers a carefully hypothetical confession: an alien on a war mission with a thought-activated biometric neutron device woven into his skeleton, capable of leveling the town. Hal starts reciting the Green Lantern Corps Oath. Waylon finishes it and says "Vengeance." Beep/bleep sounds warn the room.

**Coda.** A separate conversation is implied between Waylon and an unnamed ally he knows from before ("Look at you. You've barely aged."), who can supply real-time updates on Noah's life and whose only other co-conspirator is "the groundskeeper."

## Important character actions

- **Hal Jordan** — runs an intimidation playbook (fake arrest, bribe-accepting defense counsel) to get to the suspect; calls himself a "regular person" in the 2016 interview; recites the full Corps Oath at Waylon; befriends fans, sweet-talks a child for a selfie; admits in private to sleeping with a suspect's wife.
- **John Stewart** — eats abuse from Hal without retort, keeps radio silence in the bar-room, supplies the "14 species can pass as human" stat, later delivers a layered "fear" monologue arguing Hal is lonely, not fearless.
- **Kerry Kane** — asserts jurisdiction, refuses the Lanterns scene access, negotiates a five-minute window, ultimately allows the morning interrogation; visibly loathes Hal.
- **William "Will" Macon** — posts bail, hosts dinner, frames himself as an "isolationist student of history" preparing against an alien advanced team; coordinates through a black-NDA employer.
- **Billy Macon** — defense counsel who plays dumb in court and recruits Hal via dinner invitation.
- **Zoe Macon** — learns John's name first, prepares a tailored vegan meal, says little; Hal has previously slept with her.
- **Noah Macon** — starting QB / All-State; archery prodigy; child of a divorced couple; sits out most adult conversation.
- **Waylon Sanders** — the survivor/suspect; alien infiltrator posing as a Kimball trucker; self-destruct-equipped; motivated by vengeance.

## Newly established names / terms

- **Pharon 4** — civil-war planet Hal recently mediated.
- **Abin Sur** — Hal's predecessor as Lantern of Sector 2814; died ~20 miles from present-day Rushville; introduced Hal's ring with the line "Are you afraid?"; last words may have been "Good luck" or "Ah, fuck."
- **Victoria Falls** — site of Hal's first public save (a meteor), precipitating the 2016 "60 Minutes" reveal.
- **The Halogram** — Hal's holographic projection, used as a stationary lookout (he uses it to monitor Rushville from his taco stop).
- **"Hail in Rushville"** — code phrase future-Hal sent backup-Hal to convey: aliens are in Rushville. Halemonic because "hail" homophonally encodes the alien threat.
- **Rushville vs. Kimball** football rivalry — Kimball is a 2–2.5-hour-drive rival town; the four dead are Kimball fans.
- **The Corps Oath** — quoted in full ("In brightest day and blackest night…").
- **Biometric neutron device** — skeleton-integrated self-destruct speculated in the interrogation.
- **"Backup quarterback"** — John's own self-description; one-Lantern-per-Earth policy.

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Lanterns are uploaded weekly to the ring's cloud; ten years separates Hal's last backup from the present.
- Earth has historically been a "galactic backwater" the Guardians preferred to ignore—John's handpick is treated as exceptional.
- The ring "chooses" the wearer, irrespective of interview / merit, which Hal resents and John wrestles with.
- 14 alien species currently possess technology capable of passing human medical screening (X-ray, MRI, blood) undetected on Earth.
- Kerry is divorced from Billy Macon; Noah is their child; Zoe is Will's wife; Will is Billy's father.
- Will's household is militarized (NDA-covered employer, ranch-as-compound, anti-government paranoia, anti-colonizer rhetoric); Noah's archery drills are family-typical.
- Hal's interpersonal weak point: he cared about no one until the ring chose him, and treats the Corps as a surrogate family—John's read.
- Antaan (per world bible) self-detonates to avenge his species' genocide; Waylon's skeleton neutron device matches that profile and his stated motive is "Vengeance."

## Jokes and references useful for later episodes

- **Harry Potter / Hogwarts** — used as a pop-culture tell to unmask aliens; Waylon misses it; Hal winches with "you ignorant alien fuck."
- **antennae plural** — John corrects Hal in interrogation; tells you John's discipline when Hal postulates "hidden antennas."
- **The bubble-wrap save** — John used the ring to manifest bubble wrap under duress; Hal grudgingly approves ("you're a genius"); a Hal-old-school reference point for evaluating John's instincts.
- **Counterfeit money** — John teases Hal for using the ring to manifest small change at the taco stand; Hal insists it's "the power of my will."
- **"Are you afraid?"** — recurring Lantern selection prompt; first heard from Abin Sur, then echoed later.
- **"Ties" / "Special occasions"** — Hal's anti-uniform philosophy, tied to immaturity vs. earned gravitas.
- **Trucker / "hang your hat"** — Waylon's cover dialogue; the human-vernacular phrase Hal flags for the impersonation.
- **"Ass-face" / diner Halogram** — Hal's hologram-aware diner regular; will presumably recur as Hal's human anchor in Rushville.
- **Bruce-Springsteen/NIN soundtrack motif** (per series bible) — implied in the slow-burn heartland tone.

## Unresolved threads

- **Waylon's real name, species, and backstory.** "Vengeance" plus skeleton-bomb points to Antaan, but the species wiped out, and by whom, is not stated.
- **Zoe Macon's role.** The closing "you've barely aged" exchange links Waylon to a familiar who tracks Noah; "the groundskeeper" is the only other person in on it. Zoe (Will's wife, secret Manhunter per world bible) is the likely conversation partner; her stake and John-targeting come later.
- **Will's NDA employer.** He won't disclose; framed as "the government," but John reads that as unlikely. He is the local leader of an anti-alien militia-covert and possibly the operation's on-Earth coordinator.
- **The "advanced team."** Will claims the suspect is part of one; scope and other members unspecified.
- **The Hale–Zoe affair.** Hal slept with Zoe and is being hosted by her husband; Kerry knows or senses enough to warn Hal; fallout pending.
- **Hal's self-message.** He walked out on his backup-Hal at the taco stand without reading more; "There's hail" is now decoded, but the full import (why *now*, why Nebraska) is still his private calc.
- **Sinestro.** Mentioned only in world bible context; no name-drop yet in this episode—his escape and threat to Hal still looming.
- **John's fear monologue.** The "Hal is lonely, not fearless" thesis is offered in the car; Hal has not responded.

## S01E02

# Lanterns S01E02 — "Trust Fall"

## Plot

After John again fails to fly, Hal sends him on a Coast City errand to fetch the power lantern from Hal's house, since Hal's ring is at 5% charge. Hal insists John take his projected bird along as a "training exercise" — concentrate or it dies. Hal tells John to feed Earl, and gives directions to the spare key hidden under a "potato rock" beside Hal's door.

In Rushville, Hal drags four alien corpses through the football-stadium metal detectors to prove they are actually alien detectors. Kerry reveals the detectors were installed by her father-in-law, Will Macon, on land funded by an unnamed "benefactor" protected by an NDA. Despite Kerry's plea to let her handle the conversation, Hal drives to the Macon compound with the bodies and confronts Will directly. Will refuses to name the benefactor and warns of a shoot-on-sight order from the guard tower.

While Hal is at the compound, John — on his way back from Coast City with the lantern — is intercepted on the plane and kidnapped by Waylon's alien companions, who reveal themselves as survivors of a genocide. They show John a vision of what a Manhunter did to their people, tie him up, and offer an ultimatum: identify the Manhunter hiding in Rushville within 48 hours, or they will detonate themselves in the town square and "destroy half of western Nebraska." They want John — not Hal — because they don't trust Jordan, and they believe the Guardians may have hidden the Manhunter on Earth.

John escapes (or is released), is shot by a Macon-compound sniper, and is patched up by Kerry. Recovering, he runs into Zoe Macon (Will's wife) at a diner; they have slept together, and she explains her "unconventional" marriage to the deeply weird, eccentric rancher Will. John finally delivers the lantern — covered in alien goo from his kidnapping — to Hal at the motel and reports the 48-hour deadline. Hal deduces that Abin Sur brought the Manhunter to Earth, the Manhunter killed him, and that is how Hal got his ring. Hal then rings up Sinestro through a back-channel ("Authorize Hal Jordan. Access granted.") and asks what he knows about Manhunters. Sinestro tells him a surviving Manhunter would be "the second greatest threat" he is facing, then pivots to asking about John Stewart. The episode closes on a song sung from Zoe's perspective about addiction, submission, and "wanting to feel something again."

## Important character actions

- Hal: tests John on flying, sends him to Coast City, feeds him the bird task, deliberately provokes Will Macon at the compound, gets warned about the tower sniper, deduces the Abin Sur / Manhunter connection, contacts Sinestro through his ring.
- John: fails to fly again, finds the lantern in Hal's voice-activated vault (pocket dimension), gets kidnapped by Antaan's allies, survives a vision of their massacre, accepts the 48-hour ultimatum, is shot by a Macon sniper, sleeps with Zoe, delivers the lantern.
- Kerry: patches John up, agrees to a decaf, opens up about being from Gotham, warns Hal not to keep storming the compound, admits her jurisdiction fight was because she didn't know the detectors were alien tech.
- Will Macon: refuses to name his "benefactor," cites an NDA, threatens trespassers with the tower sniper, displays his sophisticated anti-alien technology with rancher swagger.
- Zoe: introduced as John's affair partner; defends her marriage to Will; sings the closing song.
- Sinestro: reached remotely via Hal's ring, doles out one cryptic line about Manhunter threat level, immediately tries to pivot to interrogating Hal about John.

## Newly established names / terms

- Earl — Hal's pet, food in the icebox, startled John when he finally appeared.
- "Potato rock" — Hal's smiling/frowning decorative rock used to hide his spare house key.
- Pocket dimension / voice-activated vault — Hal stores the lantern in a black, voice-gated box ("Greetings, Hal Jordan. To access the vault, please confirm identity."); the greeting adds "Mogo doesn't socialize."
- Alien detectors / stadium metal detectors — installed by Will, secretly rigged to flag extraterrestrials.
- The "benefactor" — anonymous funder of the Macon compound, NDA-protected, possibly "Keyser Söze."
- "Authorize Hal Jordan" / "Access granted" — back-channel handshake Hal uses to reach Sinestro.
- Mogo — name-checked in the vault greeting (the living-planet Lantern).
- Abin Sur — name dropped by Hal as the previous Lantern for Sector 2814, who brought the Manhunter to Earth and was killed by it.

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Hal's house has been his for eight years; nearly empty (one bed), given to him by a grateful rich man whose child he saved — confirms Hal's reluctance to put down roots.
- Hal's Coast City address and key location are now known to John.
- The Coast City lantern lives in a voice-activated pocket-dimension vault, not in the open.
- Will Macon's compound is funded by a hidden benefactor, runs advanced alien-detection tech, and has a shoot-to-kill tower guard.
- Kerry is divorced from Billy Macon and was originally from Gotham ("moved here on a whim").
- Zoe is Will's wife, not Kerry's contemporary; she is the Manhunter-recruited-into-the-household suspect from the pilot's setup.
- The aliens killed at the stadium had a surviving team led by Waylon's associates; they entered Earth by following John to Rushville.
- The aliens can self-detonate at sufficient scale to wipe out half of western Nebraska.
- John's bird construct is now established as proof he can sustain a living construct without wearing his ring — something Hal labels "practically impossible."
- Hal can reach Sinestro remotely via his ring without the Guardians' permission.
- Sinestro ranks a surviving Manhunter as Hal's "second greatest threat," implying a larger threat (Sinestro himself) is also active.

## Jokes / references useful for later episodes

- Hal calls the mystery funder a "Keyser Söze situation" — villain never seen, possibly fictional.
- Hal describes Will as "the Marlboro Man thing."
- Hal lobs the Corps oath at John ("In brightest day, in blackest night…") while teasing him about singing into a hairbrush.
- Tom Osborne is invoked as a Nebraska swear-word substitute twice ("In the name of Tom Osborne…," "I swear on Tom Osborne's life").
- Hal mocks John's bird as "just a bird," then later privately concedes the feat with "Hoorah."
- "Mogo doesn't socialize" — voice-recognition Easter egg for the living-planet Lantern.
- The bird = subconscious motif is flagged by Hal as something to revisit.

## Unresolved threads

- The 48-hour clock to identify the Manhunter in Rushville.
- The identity of the Manhunter — could be any human in town; Zoe is the prime suspect (married into the Macon household, opening seduction of John).
- The identity and intent of Will's "benefactor."
- What other technology is stored at the Macon compound beyond the stadium detectors.
- Who shot John — Will's men, Billy's men, or someone else inside the compound.
- Why the aliens distrust Hal, and whether Hal's arrival "an hour later" after their team's exposure implicates him.
- Sinestro's whereabouts and his reference to a "second greatest threat" (implying a first, likely himself).
- Hal's nightmares about Sinestro ("You kept saying his name in your sleep") and what they reveal about his past.
- The closing song's speaker and significance — Zoe's POV, thematically tying addiction, submission, and "erasing the human condition" to her Manhunter nature.

## S01E03

# Episode 3 — OutKast

## Plot
A three-timeline deep dive into John Stewart's origins. In 2026, a Guardian interviews John in a bare room for the role of Green Lantern — but the real story lies in two flashbacks: Bernadette and John Sr. raising young John Jr. as a designed-to-be-Lantern from age five, and John Sr.'s catastrophic 2016-era Kamchatka sniper mission that ended his military career and quietly set up his son as the Guardians' chosen candidate.

## Important Character Actions
- The Guardian conducts John's interview overnight, confirms one of the twelve Guardians has recently died, and notes Earth has never produced a Lantern before; the ring's selection process is unusually traumatic for humans. John doesn't ask "why me" because he already knows.
- John Stewart Sr. is woken in the night, floated out of bed in a green bubble by the ring. He tells his wife, who thinks he's going insane; he nearly drinks himself to death, waves a gun at the kids, and is told to leave the house.
- Bernadette Stewart receives the Guardian at home while John Sr. is away, demands proof of alien origin (the Guardian levitates her coffee), and is told that John Sr.'s rejection broke him. She then pivots the family's entire future onto their youngest son, John Jr.
- Bernadette and John Sr. condition John Jr. from age five: separate bedroom, no shared school, firearms drills culminating in a William Tell apple shot off his father's head. The "birds" that supposedly report his progress to the Guardians are revealed to be Bernadette's fabrication.
- Young John Jr. successfully shoots the apple off his father's head, admits he was scared while doing it, hates the cheeseburgers his father uses as reward, and secretly loves drawing.
- Adult John Sr. is recruited at an Outback Steakhouse for a solo nine-month mission to take out "Gray Wolf" at his Kamchatka retreat. He refuses the panic button. The mission strands him alone in the wilderness for nine months eating bugs and berries.
- Hal Jordan, unbriefed and on his own, overhears at a strip club that Gray Wolf is heading to his retreat. He flies to Kamchatka, destroys the compound, captures Gray Wolf, and drops him at Guantanamo — burning 300 acres of Russian sovereign soil in the process.
- To hide that US foreign policy relied on a drunk superhero at a titty bar, John Sr. accepts a dishonorable discharge in exchange for keeping Hal's involvement secret. Hal had been repeatedly warned not to intervene in terrestrial affairs.
- Hal shows up unannounced at the Stewart home after John Jr. gets the job, calls him "Junior," compares the parents to the "Midwest Huxtables," spoils the surprise, and privately tells John the only way he'll get the ring is "over my dead fucking body."

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- "OutKast" — episode title with three layers: the Outback Steakhouse meeting, a parody of OutKast's "Hey Ya!" the brothers sing, and John's outsider status.
- Gray Wolf — code name for a wanted terrorist/launderer with a Kamchatka retreat.
- Kamchatka Peninsula — site of John Sr.'s mission and Hal's unsanctioned strike.
- William Tell shot — the childhood conditioning ritual, shot off the father's head.
- "The birds" — Bernadette's fabricated Guardian surveillance agents; later admitted to be a lie.
- "The path" / destiny — the Stewart family's phrase for John's preordained role.
- "Courage is fear that's said its prayers" — John Sr.'s line of doctrine.
- "Sometimes a Black man has to raise his own hand" / "Choose yourself" — Bernadette and John Sr.'s core motto, likely to echo throughout the series.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- There were twelve Guardians; one is now dead (Lianna acknowledges it obliquely). This finally accounts in present-day terms for the killing tied to Sinestro's old coup.
- The ring's selection process destabilizes human candidates; many experience "emotional turmoil." Earth has never had a Lantern before.
- John Sr. was chosen by the ring but rejected because he was afraid; Bernadette describes that night as "the beginning of everything falling apart."
- John Sr.'s Marine background is what the Guardian cites as the genetic reason John Jr. may carry Lantern potential.
- Three operatives were lost on Kamchatka before John Sr.; the previous team lost contact months ago.
- Hal Jordan's unsanctioned Kamchatka intervention is the direct cause of John Sr.'s dishonorable discharge — and therefore of the resentment John Jr. carries into his interview.
- John Jr. claims no ill will toward Hal, despite acknowledging Hal ruined his father's career; Lianna notes this is "human."
- John's orders, if he gets the job: replace Hal Jordan, but Hal will be told John is merely his "backup."
- Lianna confirms John Jr. is the first Lantern ever chosen directly by the Guardians, not by a ring.
- Bernadette's compensation from the Guardian was a diamond, which caused police trouble when she tried to cash it; the Guardian later apologizes.
- John has two older brothers, Marcus and Aaron. Marcus teases him about Mama forcing him to listen to "piano music"; the brothers debate censored rap lyrics together.
- Drawing is established as John's true love and a Lantern tool — his adult interview test requires him to manifest his father's watch from memory with full inner engineering.
- Bernadette secretly applied John to art school as a Plan B; John is accepted. The "path," she insists, hasn't changed — only detoured.
- Lianna's parting promise to Bernadette: John's offspring "could one day" be a candidate — long-term franchise setup.

## Jokes / References Useful Later
- "Hey Ya!" parody (OutKast reference) sung by Marcus and Aaron around John's art-vs-training conflict — useful callback for sibling dynamics and John's suppressed artistic side.
- Hal calling John "Junior" repeatedly — almost certainly a running gag for the rest of the season.
- Hal comparing the Stewarts to "Midwest Huxtables" — signals Hal over-performing charm; tonal mismatch with the family's wariness.
- "Drunk superhero at a strip bar" as the actual origin of a major US foreign-policy incident — establishes Hal's rogue, anti-authority operating mode.
- "Are you afraid?" — the canonical Lantern question, here asked by John Sr. of his five-year-old son, mirroring the ring's test of John Sr.
- The "birds" fabrication — high-value thread if/when John discovers the lie.
- Cheeseburgers as John's reward and his hatred of them — small character beat that may recur.

## Unresolved Threads
- Which Guardian died, and how does it connect to Sinestro's old coup and his 2026 escape? Lianna dodges the question.
- Sinestro himself: Lianna flags that Hal "still harbors deep affection for his mentor" as "troubling," but no Sinestro action this episode.
- Hal's open hostility — "over my dead fucking body" — guarantees a future confrontation between mentor and replacement.
- Will John learn the "birds" were fabricated? He has not been told in present day.
- John's true feelings about replacing Hal versus being installed as his "backup."
- The fate of the three lost Kamchatka operatives.
- Art school: does John still attend, or has the Guardians' call superseded Bernadette's detour?
- Bernadette's diamond aftermath and the lingering resentment toward the Guardians for it.
- The Manhunter subplot is dormant this episode — no Zoe, no surveillance subtext — but Lianna's "first Guardian-chosen" framing keeps the inheritance thread alive.
- John's offspring as future Lantern — long-tail setup, not addressed again here.

## S01E04 — The Weenie

### 官方简介
约翰与哈尔按照新计划，通过警长凯莉调查 Manhunter 的过去；哈尔探访塞尼斯托之后开始承受后果。

### 剧情与连续性
- 哈尔访问监狱中的塞尼斯托，试图确认 Manhunter 的来源、弱点和守护者责任。
- 约翰与哈尔调查梅肯庄园，发现一座具有诱饵性质的建筑和超出地方背景的武器技术。
- `weenie` 在本集是建筑学和主题公园语境中的视觉锚点，不是食物。应译为“诱饵建筑”“视觉锚点建筑”等自然表达。
- 调查把两人引向 Atlas Inc.、Hector Hammond/Hector Gutierrez 相关身份线和卫星技术。
- 晶体心脏线索为识别 Manhunter 提供了阶段性方法：Manhunter 可以模拟人类外形，但在特定死亡反应上存在差异。
- Will Macon 与 Manhunter 的关联进一步浮现；Zoe 在爆炸后存活，使约翰开始怀疑她的真实身份。
- 哈尔违反与守护者有关的承诺，私下接触塞尼斯托。这个决定会影响哈尔的组织关系，也加剧他与约翰之间的信任问题。

### 固定译名
- The Weenie：诱饵建筑 / 视觉锚点建筑。
- Atlas Inc.：阿特拉斯公司。
- Hector Hammond：赫克托·哈蒙德。
- Hector Gutierrez：赫克托·古铁雷斯。
- crystal heart：晶体心脏。
- satellite dead-man’s switch：卫星死亡触发开关。

### 翻译提示
- “Manhunter”是身份和物种名称，不要在不同对白中随意改成“猎杀者”“猎人”或普通机器人。
- 约翰在本集更多承担调查和推理功能；哈尔的威胁、讽刺和冲动仍需保留。

## S01E05

# Lights Out (S01E05)

## Plot

Diner: Kerry is cornered by two hitmen sent by Will Macon (her ex-father-in-law; her father was a "snitch"). Zoe (Manhunter) intervenes, breaks one hitman's clavicle, forces the other to cut off his own pinky as proof, then regenerates Kerry's missing finger. She extracts a promise: when dangerous people come for Zoe, Kerry must protect her.

Hal reveals to a restrained John (in a car) his real scheme: let Macon's militia and Antaan's incoming alien team fight each other (Antaan as smokescreen), while Hal goes after Zoe and escapes to Oa, where the Guardians will "kiss his ring." He abandons John.

Rushville militia goes on alert ("this is not a drill"). Zoe warns Noah (Kerry and Billy's son) that many people — including people he loves — will die today. She makes him promise to remember her training: "see without being seen."

Kerry confronts Billy, confessing she "owes" Zoe, who gave her "a miracle." Kerry calls Zoe a war criminal — "not even human," "not even a she" — but refuses to leave her home because of Noah.

Sinestro reports via comms that they captured the wrong alien ("the trainee" — John, not Antaan). Hal meets Sinestro in person at the motel; they form a temporary alliance ("enemy of my enemy"). Sinestro identifies the Manhunter's current male body as William Macon, holed up at a heavily-guarded ranch nearby. Sinestro admits he had sex with Macon and explains that Manhunters seduce "the alpha" of a community — Sinestro himself was the actual target, not John.

Zoe heals John's burns (inflicted by Hal). She confesses she is the last surviving Manhunter: the Guardians created the Manhunters to enforce justice, then "flipped a switch" and exterminated them as too brutal. She plans to drain Hal's ring and kill him, then have John tell the Guardians Hal died a hero saving Rushville from Antaan. In exchange, she offers John the ring.

John phones his father John Stewart Sr. to be patched through to Lianna. On speakerphone with his mother Bernadette, he describes being ambushed by Hal, his rental car totaled, and nearly burned alive. He hasn't taken the ring yet, but plans to.

Antaan confronts Sinestro at the motel and exposes him as "Human" (Sinestro is in disguise). Hal calls out to the Manhunter.

Hal vs Manhunter. Hal's ring drops to 5%. The Manhunter: "You don't deserve this ring." Hal: "I don't deserve it. It fucking chose me." Hal realizes Zoe left him as bait — John was meant to find him. The Manhunter confronts Hal: "Was it worth it? They replaced me. They'll replace you!"

Kerry calls stand down, reports Hal alive at the motel. Lianna arrives with John, who now wears the ring. She calls the day a "shitstorm," brings a bag ("your sins and consequences"), and confesses she imagined wearing the ring would feel like pride or power — but it just felt empty. "Hal betrayed everything to keep this ring."
