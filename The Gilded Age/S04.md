## S04E02

## Episode Summary: *The Gilded Age* S04E02

### Episode Plot
The episode opens with the aftermath of the shooting from the S3 finale: **Will Morris** (son of Anne Morris) attempted to assassinate **George Russell**. Mrs. Morris confronts George at his home, pleading for her son's life. George has taken Will into Pinkerton custody rather than involving police, and hasn't decided his fate.

**Bertha Russell** returns from Newport to a household in flux. She manages the Heritage Society's upcoming dinner for **President Cleveland** at the van Rhijn house, navigates the Metropolitan Opera season (the Academy having canceled), and mediates between George and his escalating ruthlessness.

**Marian Brook** deepens her commitment to settlement house work on the Lower East Side, defying Aunt Ada's safety concerns and Agnes's disapproval. She declares this work—food, medical care, education, legal aid—is her life's calling.

**Dr. William Kirkland** faces institutional racism at Bellevue Hospital when a white physician bars him from treating his own patient, declaring "colored doctors are not qualified to practice at Bellevue."

**Larry Russell** clashes with George over business autonomy during the Rockefeller negotiation, then explores a Gilbert & Sullivan venture at the Met (exploiting lack of international copyright).

**Peggy Scott** confronts **Mrs. Kirkland** at a Daughters of Friendship tea after being introduced as a "widow"—Peggy corrects the record: her marriage was annulled, she has a child, and she refuses to be ashamed.

The episode culminates in George confronting Will at gunpoint, forcing him to feel the bullet wound, then ultimately showing mercy—banishing Will from New York forever. George returns home adrift, confessing to Bertha he has "lost his sense of direction." She demands they be true husband and wife again.

### Unresolved Threads

2. **Will Morris banished** — "Out of New York." Will he truly never return? Anne Morris promised they'd leave forever.
3. **Dr. Kirkland vs. Bellevue** — Institutional racism blockade; will he fight, circumvent, or accept?
4. **Larry's Gilbert & Sullivan venture** — He said he's "overextended" but was tempted. Will he invest behind George's back?
5. **Ada's household rebellion** — Curtains, telephone, footman hired over Agnes's objections. Escalating power struggle.
6. **Marian vs. Ada/Agnes on settlement work** — Marian calls it her life; Ada fears for safety; Agnes disapproves. No resolution.
7. **Peggy vs. Mrs. Kirkland** — Confrontation was sharp but public; future Daughters of Friendship interactions will be tense.
8. **Heritage Society dinner for President Cleveland** — Logistics, guest list, Agnes vs. Bertha influence — upcoming event.
9. **Oscar & Carrie's marriage** — Newly established; Carrie's family dynamics (Mrs. Kirkland) may complicate.
10. **George & Bertha's marriage** — She demanded "let us be husband and wife again"; he stayed the night but crisis unresolved.
