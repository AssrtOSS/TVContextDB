# War S01E01 — plotMorgan Henderson, founder of social-media platform Zephyr, accepts the Pioneer of the Year Award and gives a speech calling for online accountability. After the ceremony he walks past his driver, enters an elevator, and has a brief, awkward "good night" exchange with Flora Monroe, a young woman in the same hotel. He then phones his wife Carla (a Hollywood star) to say the award is "done."

In London, lawyers Nick Taylor and Serena Byrne (Taylor & Byrne, Carla's new legal team) meet Carla and receive a compromising photo of Morgan with Flora. Carla admits she set up the photo using Flora as a "honey trap" at the recent awards. Nick and Serena plan to break Morgan's prenup, build a paper trail showing they disapproved of the trap, and assemble a war-room team led by barrister Budgie. They decide to serve divorce papers by email after Carla's Friday film premiere; Carla refuses to serve in person.

Morgan consults Cathcarts (partner David Schill), his long-retained firm. Cathcarts tells him he is "bulletproof" under the drop-hands prenup. Morgan then confesses to sleeping with Flora — a mid-twenties actress — and does not know she was Carla's trap. Aide Eric flags that Michael Ancker has messaged Morgan directly on Zephyr, signalling an acquisition bid. Morgan refuses to engage.

Johnny Warren — South London (Peckham / Ledbury Estate), University of Michigan law 2020, three years at Wyatt-Wren in Manhattan — is back in London working as a waiter at an upscale bar with colleagues Demi and Vonnte. He is rejected at his Cathcarts interview with Schill after his self-pitch (CV fits on one page, second in class, Wyatt-Wren training, threats to "walk across the street" to Pret a Manger). At a dinner party hosted by Sophie and her husband Angus (a City finance type), Angus humiliates him in front of Bella's parents over his stalled career. Johnny punches Angus ("punching up") and is fired from the bar.

Johnny then interviews at Taylor & Byrne. Holding a Cathcarts offer, he leverages it. Serena and Nick hire him as junior associate on the divorce. Closing twist: Cathcarts reveals it planted Johnny inside Taylor & Byrne as a mole, orchestrated through a manipulated reference from Elliot Wyatt at Wyatt-Wren. "He works for them now. Except he really works for us."

The episode ends at Carla's film premiere. Her movie is about wartime spies — "double-crossing, secrets and betrayal." At midnight, after the red carpet, she and her lawyers will hit send on the divorce filing.
