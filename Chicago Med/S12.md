## S12E01

## Plot

The S12 premiere braids flash-forwards with the main story. Cold opens tease: Will Halstead confesses his love to Hannah Asher; someone tests positive for prion disease; a HIPAA leak of Dr. Rabari's files leads to a resignation demand. Cut to present: Mallory Sloan has been installed as the interim "Ms. Goodwin" while Sharon Goodwin is sidelined for the leak investigation, and Sloan's aide Miranda is gutting nursing shifts to cut costs. A mass shooting erupts at a wedding in a church — roughly twelve dead, two dozen wounded — and the ED swings into mass-casualty mode.

## Main Cases

Tamar Pollard, 32 (the bride). GSW to the left chest, bullet lodged in her uterus. She is about 23 weeks pregnant and didn't know it. Dr. Hannah Asher operates; the plan starts as an early C-section, but Asher pivots mid-procedure, removes the bullet, and keeps the baby in utero. Mother and fetus stabilize.

Alyssa Garcia, 22. Trampled while fleeing the gunman. Left hemothorax is released; she bleeds out. Time of death 13:17.

Dakota Cross, 8. Her mother Edith shoved her under a pew; Dakota took a head wound, then went functionally blind. Eyes work normally — pure traumatic conversion. Dr. Frost orders a CT, consults neurosurgeon Dr. Abrams, and attempts transcranial magnetic stimulation. The first session triggers a flashback and stops; a second attempt is pending. Mom Edith ends up at East Mercy, initially confused with another patient named Cross, then reached via FaceTime.

Dexter Sutherland, the groom. Turns up uninjured after three hours missing. Dr. Charles coaxes out that he had cold feet, never drove to the church, and turned off his phone. His mother "Birdie" had convinced him Tamar was after the family fortune from his father's plastics company.

Henry Cyrus Murphy, the shooter. Tamar's possessive ex-boyfriend from a six-year college relationship. Crashes his car into the ED waiting room to "finish it," killing Bruno, the wedding guitarist who had just been discharged after carrying Dakota to safety. Ripley treats Henry in Trauma 1 for broken ribs, clavicle, and tension pneumothorax with a chest tube and ketamine. Henry is alive and taken into custody.

## Important Character Actions

- Hannah Asher: operates on Tamar solo after Dr. Lovell is unavailable; pivots successfully to save both mother and fetus. Co-parenting baby Mabel with Dean Archer at her condo, no romantic involvement.
