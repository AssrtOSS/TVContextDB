## S01E02

## S01E02 — “Crosby, Stills, and Nash”

### Plot
Picking up after the crash (Sonny was out about a week), Sonny narrates that before things went insane he had a great life — just him and his mom. Now convinced the woman at home is a swap-out, he spends the weekend hiding in his room; only Weldon takes his texts seriously. Monday is Career Day at school. Ellen 2 drives Sonny in the drivers' ed car (with a student "second brake") and refuses to move until he talks to her. Sonny hopes the townspeople at Career Day will spot a difference, and he decides today the situation "has to go down," wanting real police help.

He's summoned to the counseling office — the counselor, Jason Chang, knows about the mom theory (Vogel told him), so Sonny backpedals that it was "just a joke." Terrified Jason will report to Ellen, Sonny pushes for the cops, but Weldon routes him instead to a private investigator he knows from Front Porch Forum. Meanwhile Chloe reads Sonny a handwritten letter — she feels guilty because he was driving to her house before the crash — but then Ellen 2 texts Chloe asking where Sonny is, and Chloe tells her, alarming Sonny.

Talking to Officer Buckley at Career Day, Sonny learns his house was hit by a break-in while he was in the hospital; Buckley asks him to keep it from Ellen ("just kids screwing around"). Sonny and friends video-call P.I. Rick Davis, who silently exits the chat when Sonny mentions someone impersonating his mom. Ellen 2 confronts counselor Jason (claiming worry, promising to Snapchat him) and clashes with vice principal Mark, who wants Sonny suspended for pushing Vogel at the dance; Ellen 2 asserts discipline is her purview and refuses Mark's coffee before serving it to others.

Mallory, stuck at her pastor father Alan's booth (prayer cards instead of napkins), is embarrassed; later a blurred photo printout of Ellen goes to the main office printer and Ellen finds it — Sonny claims it was a Costco card copy, but Ellen surprisingly says she also sees a difference, philosophizing that kids mistake parental safety for a promise and then blame moms for the world. Jason advises Sonny the only way forward is reconnecting with his mom. Sonny walks out of Ellen 2's emotional Career Day speech; Weldon drives him to the "alien lady," the forum PI at her rural property. She's "sometimes" a PI, counts the break-in as kids messing around, and initially refuses the case — until Sonny's raw monologue ("I know something... something's deeply fucked up with her, not with me") lands. She agrees to help, needing time to find something concrete. Sonny closes plotting the questions: her motive, why she's doing this, whether she has an accomplice — because "somebody saw something."

### Character actions
- Sonny: refuses to accept the "joke" framing, seeks cops/PI, lies to Ellen 2 (Costco card), delivers the believing-one-person speech.
- Ellen 2: monitors Sonny via Chloe's texts, disciplines via her authority, shows eerie insight (hair question, "I also see a difference"), yet performs normalcy publicly.
- Weldon: the only believer; drives Sonny to the PI; apologizes for leaking to Jason.
- Vogel: leaked the theory to Jason; genuinely can't see any change in Ellen.
- Chloe: supportive privately, but reports Sonny's location to Ellen 2.
- Mallory: humiliated by her dad Pastor Alan's booth.
- Jason Chang: keeps counselor confidentiality awkwardly, advises Sonny to talk to his mom.

### New names and terms
- Jason Chang — school guidance counselor; Mrs. Minard — co-counselor.
- Mark — vice principal (student discipline; wrote the zero-tolerance policy with Ellen).
- Officer Buckley — cop at Career Day; later heard on PA about shoplifters.
- Pastor Alan — Mallory's father, church Career Day booth.
- Mrs. Bergeron — flower shop owner (her employees smell like beer).
- Rick Davis, P.I. and notary public — "24/7 free consultation," silent-exit quitter.
- Unlikely/unnamed PI woman (likely Sharla, per cast) — runs trackers Crosby, Stills, and Nash, loaned to Fish and Wildlife; Sonny's forum handle is MooseMan340.
- Front Porch Forum — town public forum (compost sales, conspiracy chatter: cryptids, One World Order, face peelers).
- Open Case Services — PI directory site; "cone of silence"; drivers' ed dual-brake car.

### Continuity-relevant facts
- Timeline: week-long coma; break-in at the house during Sonny's hospitalization — Ellen 2 concealed it and blamed kids.
- Subtitles label her "[Ellen 2]," treating the impostor as a distinct entity.
- Prior incident: Sonny pushed Vogel at the dance, apologized afterward; Mark is using it to push suspension.
- Ellen 2 actively tracks Sonny through Chloe and Jason; Jason may report back.
- The blurred photo printout reached the main office printer — others may have seen it.
- The PI will help only once Sonny finds "something concrete."

### Jokes and references
- Episode title = the PI's three tracking dogs (Crosby, Stills & Nash folk-rock trio).
- "Private dick" / "Front Porch Forum compost" / "silent exit from the chat" running gags; Ellen telling counselor "I'll Snapchat you."
- Wire's "I Am the Fly" over the PI reveal; closing Molly Drake "I Remember" with a creepy "Sew faster" voice; hymn "Praise His Name" at Alan's booth.
- Sonny's "enhance the photo" bit making the blurry photo worse; "Yo Shovel, bro!" at the donut booth.

### Unresolved threads
- What actually happened to the real Ellen, and the impostor's motive/accomplices.
- Who burgled the house and what they took.
- Whether Jason, Chloe, or the main-office printout will leak more of Sonny's theory to Ellen 2.

- Ellen 2's odd remark about hair, and her apparent agreement that something is different.

## Phony S01E04 — "Vibes Are Rancid"

### Episode Plot
The episode centers on a **"Dead Body Party"** at Bucket's Pond in the woods. Shovel has spread a rumor he saw a corpse there; Sonny becomes convinced the body might be his "real" mother. The party turns out to be a teenage blowout with a potato cannon launching a scarecrow-like dummy. Shovel eventually admits he was "tripping balls" when he saw the body, describing the spot as having **"rancid vibes"** (episode title drop). The group locates the area but finds nothing — until someone appears floating in the water. It's revealed as a prank. Police raid the party for underage drinking. Ellen (Principal Cooke) arrives, having been tipped off, and confronts Sonny publicly. He refuses to leave with her, declaring he'll stay at Vogel's. The episode ends with a mysterious figure digging with a shovel in the woods.

### Important Character Actions
- **Sonny**: Relentlessly hunts for the body; confronts Shovel aggressively; publicly defies Ellen at the police raid; chooses Vogel's house over home.
- **Ellen**: Tracks Sonny to the party (via a deal with an unnamed caller); uses principal authority to disperse kids; attempts to force Sonny into the car; humiliated when he refuses.
- **Shovel**: Admits the body sighting was drug-fueled; describes "rancid vibes" at the location; shows Sonny the spot (nothing there); later claims he was building a "kayak out of mushrooms."
- **Mallory**: Convinces Shovel to show them the location; acts as bridge between Sonny and Shovel.
- **Vogel**: Wears a protective boot (injury referenced); called "fart mummy" by friends; Sonny praises him as "sensitive soul" to impress Sofia.
- **Weldon**: Escaped home because his mom cut her hand and went to urgent care; asks Shovel detailed forensic questions (skin slippage, bloat).

# Phony S01E05 — "House of Dog" Summary

## Episode Plot
Sonny refuses to go home with the woman claiming to be his mother ("Ellen 2"), holing up at Vogel's instead. Meanwhile, PI Sharla shares at AA that her case involving Sonny has escalated — a woman (presumably Ellen) met a man behind a bar and handed him a large cash payment; the getaway car is a rental registered to **Tracy Marks**, who hasn't lived at the listed address in over a year. Sharla followed the man but lost him because she was drinking (gin, zero days sober). At school, Principal Ellen ("Ellen 1") mentions her birthday, Sonny's optimism about their runaway dog Tucker returning, and reveals her father died today — and that **Tracy**, her estranged sister, reached the ashes first. Sonny and Mallory visit the **House of Dog**, a roadside memorial for dead dogs, where they find a plaque resembling Tucker. They trace the burner phone found on the suspect to **Claude Rains** (a fake name, referencing *The Invisible Man* actor) and a likely bogus address. The episode ends with Sonny reading his mother's journal: she suspected Tracy of breaking into their house, called her "erratic and scary" after their father's funeral — confirming Ellen has a sister named Tracy Marks.

# S01E06 Summary

## Episode Plot
Sonny and his friends (Vogel, Weldon, Chloe, Mallory) drive to Tracy's house in Green Valley after discovering Ellen has a secret sister, Tracy Marks/Walsh. They find the address through a property search—registered to David Walsh, Ellen's father (Sonny's grandfather). Meanwhile, Ellen at school faces an HR visit (Debbie from the district) and a confrontation with Mr. Carbone over a past grievance. At Tracy's house, the group encounters Eric, the shady man from the bar. Sonny breaks in and confronts Tracy, who reveals she's his aunt. Ellen arrives, and the sisters argue bitterly: Ellen claims Tracy was cut off for drug use and endangering Sonny as a baby (hitting a mailbox with him in the car seat); Tracy claims Ellen erased her from the family and stole their father's inheritance money. ## S01E07

# Phony S01E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
Sonny, now diagnosed with **Capgras Syndrome**, begins EMDR therapy and auditory bypass exercises (talking to his mother without looking at her). He tells his friends the impostor theory was all in his head, but physical evidence keeps undermining that conclusion: a real van following him, a second dolphin keychain from the crash night, and — most critically — **two distinct versions of Ellen** appearing simultaneously (labeled "Ellen 1" and "Ellen 2" in the subtitles). The episode culminates at Mallory's baptism, where Sonny's father unexpectedly arrives.

## Important Character Actions
- **Sonny**: Accepts Capgras diagnosis; starts EMDR; tells Vogel the investigation is over; breaks up with Chloe after she returns his necklace (a different one than he gave her); discovers the second dolphin; confronts Ellen about their lost hand-squeeze ritual.
- **Ellen 1**: During auditory bypass exercise, reveals Sonny's father's backstory — grandfather (David Walsh) owned a grocery chain; father felt inadequate, got a construction job, then got depressed and left town. Confirms father *did* visit the hospital after the accident (Vogel saw them talking).
- **Ellen 2**: Present during the same exercise; responds warmly to the therapy's progress. Later at home, doesn't recognize the "I love you" hand-squeeze ritual (3 squeezes / 4 return).
- **Both Ellens appear together** at lines 361–371, speaking in tandem about "letting people in even when we feel scared."
- **Mallory**: Gets baptized; delivers a sincere speech about faith as a path, not a destination, and doubts being part of faith. Invites Sonny.
- **Chloe**: Returns Sonny's handmade necklace (a different one); breaks up with him, saying she doesn't need his protection.
- **Weldon**: At the pond with a metal detector and drone; proposes Mandela Effect / simulation theory ("glitch in the matrix") to explain Sonny's memory discrepancies. Finds a second dolphin keychain — Sonny only had one.
- **Sharla**: Asks for a tracking dog to search the pond for a dead body; tells Sonny "what you guys get into is your business."
- **Vogel**: Relieved Sonny dropped the investigation; got the lead in the school play.
