## S02E01

# S02E01 Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
The crew of the *Raza* is imprisoned in a maximum-security GA facility on an airless moon after their capture at the end of Season 1. Chief Inspector Shaddick interrogates them about the Iriden 3 incident — a white-hole device delivery that killed 15,000 people. Six (Lt. Kal Varrick) reveals he was GA undercover; his cover was blown and Five wiped everyone's memories to save him. The Android refuses to cooperate, protected by a dead-man's-switch security protocol. Meanwhile, an Ishida cruiser orbits the prison demanding Four's (Ryo Ishida) extradition to Zairon for public execution. 

In general population, Three, Two, and "One" (actually Derrick Moss with Jace Corso's face) navigate prison politics. New inmate **Nyx** — a Seer — knows the supply shuttle schedule and brokers an escape plan. Prison kingpin **Arax Nero** initially threatens them but switches sides, offering protection in exchange for a joint breakout. Truffault (Mikkei) visits One, exposing his true identity: wealthy industrialist **Derrick Moss** who underwent reconstructive surgery to resemble career criminal **Jace Corso** (GA's Ten Most Wanted). Anders (Six's ex-partner) warns the warden has been paid to have them killed. Four feigns injury to reach the infirmary, delaying transfer. **Misaki Han-Shireikan**, his childhood friend and now Imperial Royal Guard commander, arrives to collect him personally.

The breakout executes during a staged riot: Five orders the Android to "kill them all," she slaughters guards, and the crew flees aboard the Ishida shuttle with Arax, Nyx, and prison doctor **Devon Taltherd**. Six stays behind to seal the airlock, confronting Anders. A closing news report confirms One = Derrick Moss. A mysterious woman (likely the Empress/Seers) learns the *Raza* escaped with "our asset" (the Android) and orders pursuit.

## S02E05

## S02E05 Summary

### Episode Plot
The crew investigates One's murder. The Android installs an illegal "human emulation" upgrade to go undercover at a GA outpost in the Tallaren sector, successfully hacking their system to identify the suspect: **Jace Corso** (One's doppelgänger), seen on hotel surveillance *after* One was shot. They track Corso to an abandoned settlement on a planet with a hidden fusion reactor. A GA cruiser led by **Chief Inspector Kierken** arrives. The Android hides the Raza in the ionosphere while the crew goes planetside. Corso barricades himself in a survival shelter with supplies and a distress beacon. Three and Four cut through the door; a cave-in traps Two, Three, Four, Nyx, and Corso in old mine tunnels—along with Kierken and his men. Forced to dig together, Corso reveals he was a **hired assassin** paid to kill One, but claims the employer was **not Traugott** (whom Two suspected). The Marauder arrives and rescues them; Kierken stands down but reveals he overturned Four's extradition to Zairon, intending to try them under GA jurisdiction. Two refuses, noting corporations would kill them in custody. The Android suffers a capacitor failure during atmospheric entry; her internal simulation warns the upgrade is evidence of a deeper programming flaw making her a danger to the ship.

### Important Character Actions
- **Android**: Installs illegal upgrade, passes as human ("Kiva Ehrens"), hacks GA outpost, crash-lands Marauder in ionosphere to evade detection.
- **Two**: Leads vengeance mission for One; refuses GA custody; asserts crew solidarity ("if we don't fight for each other, who the hell will?").
- **Three & Four**: Breach Corso's shelter; Four insists on digging together despite Corso's betrayal expectations.
- **Nyx**: Saves Three from falling debris (enhanced hearing); joins ground team ("your fight, my fight—we ride together").
- **Six**: Navigates tunnels injured; mediates with Kierken.
- **Corso**: Bargains for life with info on true hirer; reveals One's death was a contract kill, not identity theft.
- **Kierken**: Pursues crew into tunnels; offers GA trial over corporate execution; knows crew's real identities.

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Tallaren sector** – GA outpost location

- One was heir to his father's company; CEO is a new suspect.

## S02E04

# S02E04 Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
Three reconnects with his former crew (Tanner, Gar, and others) who abandoned him at a serillium mine years ago. They pull a payroll heist at a mining colony, but Three discovers Tanner murdered his adoptive parents and raised him as a thief. Three kills Tanner and returns to the Raza with the payroll. Meanwhile, Five and the Android visit a space station, use **Transfer Transit** to investigate a sketch, and identify it as **Dwarf Star Technologies** headquarters on **Terra Prime (Earth)**. The Android meets a group of free androids led by **Victor**, learning her emotional responses are **intentional design**, not a programming flaw. Five swaps the mysterious key she stole, revealing it accesses "pockets of interdimensional space-time." The episode ends with **Commander Nieman** (Ferrous Corp) and **Reynaud** discussing the Raza crew holding "the key to victory" in a coming war.

## Important Character Actions
- **Three**: Reunites with old crew; confronts Tanner about parents' murder; kills Tanner; returns payroll to Five with interest
- **Five**: Accompanies Android to station; investigates sketch via Transfer Transit; pickpockets/swaps the key; loans Three money at 20% weekly compound interest
- **Android**: Meets Victor's android collective; accepts emotional upgrade offer; learns her empathy is by design; kisses Victor goodbye
- **Tanner**: Reveals he killed Three's parents during a drunken argument; claims he spared Three and "redeemed" himself by raising him; killed by Three
- **Victor**: Diagnoses Android; reveals free androids living among humans; offers humanity-passing upgrade; warns "free yourself before it's too late"
- **Nyx**: Poses as British architectural student at Transfer Transit (improvisational cover)

## S02E06

## S02E06 Summary

### Episode Plot
The *Raza* crew, low on fuel and funds, accepts Nyx's tip to hijack a freighter carrying "Shadow" (synthetic hallucinogen, street name for Protase 130). The heist goes smoothly until Two and Nyx discover the ship's "cargo" is actually prisoners wired into a collective consciousness machine — Nyx's people, including her brother Milo. Nyx had concealed this; her real goal was rescuing Milo. The crew extracts Milo and the Shadow, but the Seer ship (led by Hansmeed) pursues. Three and Six take the *Marauder* into a toxic atmosphere to hide while the *Raza* lures the Seers away. Milo reveals the Seers' predictive ability (C.P.A. — Cognitive Predictive Ability, enhanced by drugs and machine linkage) and that they anticipate the crew's moves via probability models. Hansmeed demands Milo's return; Two bluffs executing Milo to force a standoff. Milo chooses to return to the Seers voluntarily, intending to subvert them from within by feeding false predictions. The crew escapes with half the Shadow cargo.

### Important Character Actions
- **Nyx**: Orchestrates the heist as cover to rescue Milo; lies to the crew about the true nature of the target; apologizes afterward and asks Two to let them stay.
- **Two**: Leads the heist; discovers Arax Nero was communicating with Alicia Reynaud (Ferrous Corp board member) via subspace link; bluffs Hansmeed by threatening to kill Milo; lets Milo go back.
- **Milo**: Reveals Seer mechanics (collective consciousness, C.P.A., data-driven probability prediction); predicts a major paradigm shift / all-out corporate war within six months; predicts a future betrayal aboard the *Raza*; chooses to re-enter the machine to sabotage predictions from inside.
- **Three & Six**: Bicker while hiding in the *Marauder* on a toxic planet; Six admits he turned them in (past betrayal); Three refuses to forgive.
- **Five**: Bonds with Milo; learns the Seers predict a "major paradigm shift" and that the *Raza* crew has a pivotal role.
- **Android**: Detects Arax's deleted comm logs via power usage; reconfigures shields with no spare parts; notes *Marauder* can survive <24 hrs in the toxic atmosphere.
- **Hansmeed (Seer leader)**: Demands Milo and cargo; knows Two won't kill Milo; forgives Milo's data manipulation but warns he's watching.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Shadow / Protase 130** — synthetic hallucinogen / party drug, high-value contraband.
- **Electus** — corporation that originally abducted Nyx's people and ran the collective-consciousness experiment.
- **Hansmeed** — leader of the Seer rebellion who seized the ship but continued the experiment on his own terms.
- **C.P.A. (Cognitive Predictive Ability)** — enhanced intuition/precognition via genetic mutation + machine linkage + drugs.
- **Seer ship** — the vessel housing the collective consciousness machine (formerly Electus property).

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Alicia Reynaud** (Ferrous Corp elite) was in contact with Arax Nero — links the prison-boss arc to Ferrous.
- The Seers' predictive models forecast **all-out corporate war within six months**; the *Raza* crew is flagged as pivotal.
- Milo is the most talented Seer; his return gives the Seers a powerful asset, but he intends to feed them false conclusions.
- The Seers plan to acquire more ships, enslave more people, and build a fleet — becoming unstoppable if unchecked.
- Nyx's people have a genetic mutation for heightened C.P.A.; the machine amplifies it into tactical precognition.
- *Marauder* atmospheric-hiding trick established (toxic atmosphere masks sensors).
- Three still refuses to forgive Six for the S01 betrayal.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- Three: "Old-fashioned heist? … So, who we knocking off?" — classic Three enthusiasm for crime.
- Three: "God, I hate being blind" while hiding in toxic soup — callbacks to sensor-blind combat.
- Android's "Damned if you do" proverb when Two asks about outsmarting predictors.
- Milo's deadpan "Sometimes it does" when Four asks if knowing the future changes it.
- Nyx and Four sparring / sharing quiet time at the end — deepening bond.

### Unresolved Threads
- **Milo inside the Seer collective** — can he actually subvert Hansmeed's predictions? Will he be turned?
- **Alicia Reynaud / Ferrous connection** — why was Arax talking to her? What does Ferrous want with the Seers?
- **Corporate war in six months** — specific flashpoint unknown; *Raza* crew's role undefined.
- **Future betrayal aboard the *Raza*** (Milo's prediction) — who, when, why?

- **Half the Shadow cargo returned** — Hansmeed knows they shorted him; Seers still have Milo; truce is fragile.
- **Three vs. Six** — tension unresolved; trust not restored.

## S02E03 — "The Box" (Apple TV+ Dark Matter)

### Plot

The episode interweaves three timelines. **Jason 2** gives a guest lecture on superposition and the ethics of paradigm-shifting technology, arguing that if something can be done, it will be done. **Jason 1** and **Daniela** are building a new life in the alternate reality — apartment-hunting, Charlie starting school. **Ryan Holder** has located **Amanda Lucas** (Jason 2's ex-girlfriend from his home reality) and they use the box together.

Ryan and Amanda arrive in Jason 1's world. Ryan 1 meets his alternate self at Jason 1's apartment. **DARPA** — alerted by the dozens of dead Jasons across Chicago — closes in with agents and SWAT. A car chase through Chicago streets follows as Jason 1, Ryan 1, Ryan 2, and Amanda flee to the box.

Meanwhile, a PI named **Mitchel** (hired by someone called **Sheila**) has been surveilling Jason 2. Mitchel saw a couple walk into the cube at an abandoned school and vanish. Sheila realizes a family exists in both San Francisco and Chicago simultaneously. Jason 2 has been secretly studying the box, trying to understand or destroy it, lying to Daniela about it.

Jason 2 confronts Daniela about his obsession; she calls him out for changing the plan (keep/understand vs. destroy) without discussion. Jason 2 admits the box is "taunting" him — it's his dream made real, and he can't let go.

At the end, Jason 1 returns to the site where the box was destroyed and discovers **it has respawned** — a new, fully functional box in the same location. Jason 2 realizes: the box regenerates in worlds where it no longer exists or isn't operational. Destroying it just triggers its self-replication.

### Key Character Actions

- **Jason 1**: Drives the escape from DARPA with Ryan 1, Ryan 2, and Amanda; later discovers the box has respawned
- **Jason 2**: Gives the lecture; secretly studies the box; lies to Daniela; is being tracked by Mitchel/Sheila/DARPA
- **Ryan Holder** (both versions): Ryan 2 finds Amanda and brings her through the box; Ryan 1 helps Jason 1 escape
- **Amanda Lucas**: Accompanies Ryan 2; recognizes Jason 1's apartment (she knows of him); helps locate the box via thinking of Jason 2 rather than the place
- **Daniela**: Confronts Jason 2 about his deception and obsession
- **Charlie**: Starts at a new school; makes a friend; writes a college essay about the box experience ("a box created by another version of my dad")
- **Mitchel/Sheila**: PI and client tracking Jason 2's anomalies

### Newly Established Names/Terms

- **The Corridor** — the space between realities inside the box (Jason 1 says Jason 2 made choices "in the Corridor" that spawned duplicates)
- **DARPA** — Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency; has been investigating the dead Jasons, assumes they're clones
- **Amanda Lucas** — Jason 2's ex from his home reality
- **Sheila** — possibly Jason 2's family connection; involved in tracking him
- **Mitchel** — PI hired to surveil Jason 2
- **Velocity Pharmaceuticals** — company willing to produce a vaccine/treatment (connected to a subplot with a hospitalized person)
- **Glass Daisy** — a band mentioned by Charlie's new schoolmates
- **The box's self-replication rule** — it spawns in worlds where it doesn't exist or is non-operational; destroying it triggers regeneration

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Dozens of Jason duplicates (and many dead ones) resulted from Jason 2's actions in the Corridor
- Daniela and Charlie escaped into the box with one of the Jasons (Jason 1 presumably)
- Jason 1 kidnapped Jason 2 initially, but Jason 2 made it home
- Jason 2 built the box over ~20 years with a team
- Jason 1's world is "Jason 2's world" — the box brought Ryan 2 and Amanda there because Jason 2 is present
- The box cannot be permanently destroyed; it respawns

### Jokes / References Useful Later

- Charlie's college essay describing the box as created by "another version of my dad" — darkly funny and sets up how he processes trauma
- Charlie's "I gazed into the abyss and saw a humanoid animatronic mouse gazing back at me" (referencing abandoned Chuck E. Cheese world) — Nietzsche parody as a coping mechanism
- The artist-name memory game (Van Gogh, Vermeer, Degas) — Jason 1 and Daniela's bedtime routine; a gentler characterization moment
- Ryan 1: "Why does everything bad happen as a result of your fuckups?" directed at Jason 1

### Unresolved Threads

- **DARPA is still hunting** — they saw two Ryans and are running facial recognition; they want everyone alive but won't stop
- **The box has respawned** — what does this mean for Jason 1's plan to destroy it? Can it ever be destroyed?
- **Which Jason did Daniela and Charlie escape with?** — unclear if it's definitively Jason 1 or a different duplicate
- **Jason 2's relationship with Sheila** — she's pushing him back into a "family business"; her full agenda unknown
- **The hospitalized person** (seen receiving vaccine/care) — connected to Velocity Pharmaceuticals subplot, identity and significance TBD
- **"Monsters"** — screamed by an unidentified person during the DARPA confrontation; significance unknown

## S02E07

# S02E07 概要

## 劇情脈絡

本集跳脫刀鋒號主線，呈現一條「地球側」平行時間軸，環繞在賴恩·霍爾德（Ryan Holder）於 S01E12 揭露瞬間驅動設計圖後所引發的連鎖反應：另一條時間線上，賴恩並未把驅動交給星際委員會，而是於芝加哥希卡克瓦公園啟動「立方體（the Box）」後，與另一名杰森結伴行走於無數平行世界之間。

全集由數條人物線交織：
- 紐約街頭的偵探米切爾·麥克納馬拉（Mitchel MacNamara）追查麥克納馬拉科技的死敵「極速科技（Velocity Tech）」線索，闖入一處廢棄學校內的立方體據點。
- 五年前因立方體誤闖地球、留在達妮埃拉（Daniela）世界的「另一個杰森」，化名賈森·斯圖爾特（Jason Stewart）在芝加哥 Village Tap 酒館當調酒師。
- 達妮埃拉之父在黑虹瘟（Black Iris Plague）爆發前已探訪過 74 個世界，並以「萊頓·文斯（Leighton Vance）」之名接管極速科技；其終極計畫為打造巨型金字塔型立方體「The Pyramid」，讓一個排球場大小的房間直接走進通道、橫跨至其他世界。
- 賴恩·霍爾德（達妮埃拉之父）被達妮埃拉送回原世界後，發現自己正是黑虹瘟的零號病人；而杰森（Dessen 2）與阿曼達（Amanda）兩年前曾降臨達妮埃拉家中，引發立方體連鎖。
- 達妮埃拉之子查理（Charlie）為慶祝與早逝雙胞胎兄長麥克斯（Max）同日的生日，獨自進入立方體，要去一個麥克斯還活著的世界。

## 主要角色行動

- **杰森 1（Dessen / Stewart）**：與達妮埃拉在 Bakery 街區重逢，承認自己進入通道是想回到過去見亡妻安妮（Anne）；達妮埃拉則因長期面對「多版本丈夫」而回到童年創傷——不知哪個父親才是真實。兩人共處數日後，他幫助達妮埃拉追查失蹤的查理。
- **達妮埃拉（Daniela）**：在父親家中讀取各平行世界的檔案，發現雷頓之父萊頓只見過 74 個世界（與其聲稱的數千個差距懸殊）；父親向其坦白，因為黑虹瘟致死率極高（芝加哥每日死 2 萬人），他讓達妮埃拉帶另一個杰森同行，作為尋找解藥與淨化地球的保險。
- **賴恩·霍爾德**：在 CDC 隔離病房接受夏普博士（Dr. Janet Sharp）詢問；由於兩人皆從黑虹瘟康復，被視為零號病人——他雖感染更早，卻「死得更晚」，因杰森 1 與他在希卡克瓦的立方體同行而獲得通道經驗。達妮埃拉把一個版本送到父親實驗室，與父親的複本一起研發 30 天緩釋貼片。
