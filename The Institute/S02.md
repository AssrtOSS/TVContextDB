## S02E01

## The Institute — S02E01 Summary

### Episode Plot

**Cold open / "Previously on" montage** recaps the Season 1 climax: the Institute breakout, Avery's sacrifice on the "Big Phone," the building collapsing, and the children fleeing with Tim Jamieson.

**Three primary storylines intercut:**

1. **The Fugitives (Maine woods → Waterville → safe house)**  
   Luke, Kalisha, Nick, George, Iris, Helen, and an injured Tim flee on a freight train. Tim took a bullet; they stop at a diner to stitch him up (no hospitals — gunshot wounds get reported). Luke uses TK to empty an ATM ("vending machine principle"). They scatter the group to divide pursuers. Tim wants to stay hidden; Luke insists they need evidence (Maureen's flash drive) to expose the Institute. Tensions spike — Nick calls Luke a "selfish prick" for risking them all. George sneaks off to a Best Buy/GameStop to play Xbox; the safe house is compromised. Wendy is brutally killed by intruders while the others are out.

2. **Sigsby's Reckoning (Massachusetts)**  
   Julia Sigsby attends her father Joseph's military funeral. The "Man on the Phone" (her unseen superior) sends a watcher; she confronts him at the graveside. He offers a path back into the organization — she's the only supervisor to lose an Institute, and her blackmail video (copies distributed, dead-man's switch) keeps her alive but not trusted. She wants back in; he says she needs leverage. "What do you get the men who have everything?"

3. **The New Operation / Pre-Cog Program (Undisclosed location, high-altitude flight)**  
   Tech billionaire **Nolan Reeves** (Dassault Falcon, Saudi-royal tier) visits a black-site facility run by **Dr. Sylvia Mansell**, head of **Prevision** (precognition program). They're down to 9 aging pre-cogs (two in 90s, one in 80s). Reeves funds an AI that "dreams" its own pre-cognitive visions — still buggy. He recruits **Dr. Daniel Hendricks** (co-author of the definitive study on pre-cognitive quantum manipulation) away from the Institute's director, hinting the director's days are numbered. The pre-cogs ("Lynne Ledoux" is the center/strongest) drift in a shared timeline, scouting for apocalypse events; their merged forecasts feed the AI. **Hinges** identified — people whose removal during a specific "time keyhole" averts catastrophe. Elimination is a separate, siloed division ("separation of church and state").

## S02E02

## S02E02 Summary — *The Institute*

### Episode Plot
The episode opens with a catastrophic blow to the Institute’s precognition network: **eight of the world’s nine functioning Pre‑Cogs (PCs) are assassinated** in a coordinated hit on a PC Station. Only **Lynne LeDoux** survives. The Director (Victor’s superior) panics—without at least two PCs the “mission” collapses—and orders an AI‑based replacement project accelerated.

Meanwhile, **Tim Jamieson** is arrested by local police who believe he murdered his partner **Wendy Gullickson** (and two other officers). During interrogation, Tim calmly predicts the FBI will take custody within 30 minutes, wipe all recordings, and he will disappear. A fake FBI team arrives and extracts him—revealing Institute interception.

On the run, **Luke, Kalisha, Nick, George, Iris, and Helen** hole up in a safe house. Nick reveals he brought **“someone else”**—a woman (credited as **Nicky**) who can **shield her thoughts from Kalisha’s telepathy**. Kalisha recognizes her as a “scorpion” (traitor) from the Institute; Nick argues they need her to rescue Tim. Luke agrees, over Kalisha’s fury.

**Dr. Hendricks** (alive after the PC Station attack) confronts Luke, revealing Luke has **precognitive ability** and offering him “royalty” treatment as a PC if he returns voluntarily. Luke refuses.

Kalisha uses her TP to track the fake FBI convoy, **rescues Tim** in a brutal roadside ambush, but is left drained—“strong as Avery ever was.”

**Nolan Reeves** (tech billionaire) meets with Institute leadership, negotiating his AI system’s integration and demanding his security chief **Joshua** be briefed on the PC Station investigation. The Institute agrees but insists he remain in “protective custody.”

The episode closes with Luke rallying the group: **the only way to destroy the Institute is from the inside**. He proposes re‑entering the facility, using the **“Big Phone”** (the direct line to the Man on the Phone / Committee) to tear it down. Nick and Kalisha hate the plan but concede they have no alternative. George commits “for Avery.” Tim and George stay outside as the advance team.
