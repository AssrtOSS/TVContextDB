## Episode Plot (S03E05 focus)

### **Rhûn & Sauron's Mobilization**
- The Dark Lord summons the armies of Rhûn to Middle-earth; they chant "Hail Sauron!" and prepare to march at the next blood moon.
- Sauron's forces plan to canvass **Eryn Galen (Greenwood)** from here to **Erebor** searching for a lone quarry on foot — someone whose secret "will shape the fortunes of all."

### **Adar & the Orcs**
- Adar interrogates an Orc named **Marnûkh**, who has five children (now dead). Adar refuses to kill him, keeping him alive to serve when the Master Ring is on Adar's hand.
- Adar sends messengers by foot and wing to hunt the quarry; the target is revealed to be **Celeborn**.

### **Elrond & Elros Flashback (First Age)**
- A flashback to the choice of the Half-elven: **Elros** chooses mortality and a crown/scepter in Númenor; **Elrond** chooses immortality.
- Elros argues Númenor will be a "beacon of hope ever shining in the West"; Elrond fears Men fall too easily.
- Elros swears: "In one year or a thousand years... I'll be there."

### **Galadriel & Celeborn Reunion**
- Galadriel finds **Celeborn** alive in the woods (he survived Thangorodrim/Ancalagon's fire by being kicked into a ditch, then endured imprisonment in **Thangorodrim**).
- Celeborn befriended a torture-master **Häzgarak** and his line, told tales of Galadriel to Orcs (her singing, dancing, golden hair), kept hope alive by imagining her happiness.
- Emotional reconciliation: both believed the other dead for ages. Celeborn admits he pushed her away because seeing her in every face was too painful.
- Celeborn reveals he was searching for a lieutenant of Morgoth at the Black Keep when Galadriel passed by — he didn't call out, consumed by his task.

### **Elrond & Gil-galad in Númenor**
- **Gil-galad** and **Elrond** arrive in Númenor; only three attack ships escort them — Pharazôn establishing leverage.
- **Pharazôn** (now King) mocks Sauron as a "blacksmith" with a tower/throne but no noble blood; boasts of spearing a "sea-worm" deemed undefeatable.
- Gil-galad warns: Rhûn has entered the war; left alone, Elves will fall. Urges Pharazôn to act now — "Why wait and fight with one fist an enemy we can defeat now with two?"
- Pharazôn demands Gil-galad order **Míriel** to surrender her claim; Gil-galad refuses — "Ordering Míriel to surrender to Pharazôn is not what he [Elros] would want."
- Elrond seeks **Elros' counsel** (via vision/hallucination at the Hall of Kings?).

### **The Stranger / Gandalf & Pallando (Blue Wizard)**
- The Stranger (now identified as **Gandalf**) meets **Pallando the Blue** — one of the Five Istar.
- Pallando reveals the Secret Fire showed him their destruction: **Gandalf dies at the hands of a Ring-bearer; Pallando dies at Sauron's hand**.
- Pallando urges kneeling to Sauron as the only way; Gandalf refuses: "I would sooner embrace death."
- Pallando is captured by Orcs serving Sauron ("Sauron wants him alive... Let us bring the Lord of Gifts a wizard").

## S03E06

# S03E06 Summary — The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power

## Episode Plot

**Númenor — The Parley and a Royal Marriage**
Elrond arrives in Armenelos to broker peace between Queen Regent Míriel (Faithful) and King Ar-Pharazôn (King's Men). Pharazôn's forces surround the city; civil war looms. Elrond warns Míriel that striking first would brand her a butcher and prove Númenor fragile to Sauron. Míriel sends a parley request; Pharazôn accepts with written terms. At the meeting in Kingsmen Square, Míriel shocks everyone by proposing marriage to Pharazôn — one army, one Númenor, sailing together to Middle-earth to defeat Sauron. In return: freedom of worship for the Faithful and release of her son. Pharazôn agrees, but the alliance remains deeply distrusted on both sides.

**Khazad-dûm — Brother Against Brother**
King Durin III (Thráin) obsesses over finding the Seven Dwarf Rings hidden by his father. He imprisons Durin IV, threatens Disa (who refuses to sing for him), and eventually casts Durin into a deep pit. Disa and Durin IV reconcile; she admits she should have heeded his warnings about Thráin. The Rings are buried in the Elder Mines — Disa knows the location but will only reveal it to protect their children, held hostage by Thráin's guards.

**Eregion / Barad-dûr — The Secret of the Three**
Galadriel infiltrates Barad-dûr and finds Celeborn alive, tortured by Sauron for the secret of the Elven Rings. Sauron (as Annatar) simultaneously tortures Celebrimbor's former apprentice in Eregion. Both learn the same truth: the Three were forged with a hammer made by Fëanor himself — unbreakable, unmeltable by any forge, even dragon-fire. The gold and silver came from Valinor (Finrod's dagger). The only heat sufficient to work such metal exists in Mount Doom (Orodruin). Sauron now knows he must go there to forge a Master Ring capable of commanding the Three.

**The Stranger & Nori**
Brief interlude: the Stranger and Nori shelter in an old ruin (once Galadriel and Celeborn's dreamed home). He sings a lament for what is lost. Galadriel later visits the same place, confronting her grief and admitting she hunted Sauron to avoid facing her loss.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Ar-Pharazôn** — Pharazôn's regnal name as King of Númenor.
- **Fëanorian Hammer** — The unbreakable hammer forged by Fëanor (maker of the Silmarils and palantíri), used to forge the Three Elven Rings. No forge can melt it; only the fires of Mount Doom suffice.
- **Valinor Gold & Silver** — The metals of the Three Rings, sourced from Finrod's dagger (brought from Valinor).
- **Elder Mines** — Ancient dwarf delvings where the Seven Rings are hidden.
- **Hall of Lore** — Númenórean archive burned by Pharazôn's men; Loremaster died inside.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- **Celeborn is alive** — previously believed dead for an Age; Galadriel's hunt for Sauron was partly driven by grief over his loss.
- **The Seven Dwarf Rings** — still hidden in Khazad-dûm; Thráin desperate to find them.
- **The Three Elven Rings' secret** — now known to Sauron: Fëanorian hammer + Valinor metals + Mount Doom heat = Master Ring potential.
- **Isildur's fate** — still unknown; his belongings recovered, but he is missing (last seen in the Southlands/Mordor).
- **Númenor's civil war** — temporarily averted by marriage alliance, but both sides expect betrayal.
- **Mount Doom (Orodruin)** — established as the only forge capable of working the Fëanorian hammer; Sauron's next destination.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **Disa's singing** — "She'd sooner eat a bowl of broken glass than sing for you." (Thráin) / "She sung for me before, Brother." (Durin)

## S03E08

# The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power — S03E08 Summary

## Episode Plot
The season 3 finale converges multiple storylines as Sauron's full force marches on the Númenórean camp. Pharazôn, now calling himself Ar-Pharazôn, challenges Sauron to single combat for the title "King of Men." Meanwhile, Gandalf and the Blue Wizard Pallando escape the Dark Wizard's tower after a philosophical confrontation about the Istari's true purpose. Galadriel and Elrond arrive in Númenor with warnings of Sauron's overwhelming numbers (100,000 Rhûn men + 50,000+ Orcs) and the One Ring on his hand. The episode ends with both armies poised for battle as Pharazôn's defiant challenge echoes across the field.

## Important Character Actions

| Character | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Ar-Pharazôn** | Issues written challenge to Sauron: single combat for the title "King of Men"; rallies Númenórean troops with speech invoking Númenor's legacy; zero desertions overnight |
| **Gandalf (The Stranger)** | Rejects Sauron's offer of dominion over the West; declares "I am a servant of the Secret Fire"; breaks Pallando free from Dark Wizard's control through moral argument |
| **Pallando (Blue Wizard)** | Recovers identity; turns against Dark Wizard; joins Gandalf to lead Dark Wizard's former followers against Sauron; departs West to face Manwë's judgment |
| **Dark Wizard** | Attempts to corrupt Gandalf with offer of Middle-earth's West; reveals Sauron spared Gandalf because killing an Istar invites their return in stronger form; commands Khamûl |
| **Galadriel** | Arrives in Númenor with Elrond and Celeborn; delivers intelligence on Sauron's forces; reunites emotionally with Celeborn after an Age apart |
| **Elrond** | Counsels Pharazôn on Sauron's psychology; invokes Elros's teaching "Death is the Gift of Men" to steel Númenórean resolve |
| **Celeborn** | Reunites with Galadriel; chooses to remain/commit to their bond "year after year, century after century" despite the pain of time's passage |
| **Khamûl (Nazgûl lieutenant)** | Named as commander of Dark Wizard's human followers (Gaudrim); receives a Ring of Power, becoming "greatest of the Nine" — the Witch-king |
| **Tom Bombadil** | Appears briefly; sings his song; walks with Gandalf/Pallando partway toward Elven lands; references Goldberry |

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Pallando** — Blue Wizard (Istar), one of the Five Wizards sent to Middle-earth
- **Khamûl** — Named Nazgûl lieutenant, becomes the Witch-king ("A Witch-king no man can slay")
- **Nazgnagôl** — Dark Wizard's term for Pallando (possibly Black Speech for "Ring-servant" or similar)
- **Gaudrim** — Human followers of the Dark Wizard from Rhûn, loyal to Khamûl
- **Withywindle** — River in Tom Bombadil's domain (Old Forest)
- **Manwë** — Vala invoked as Pallando's judge upon returning West

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

1. **Five Wizards confirmed**: "Five there were. I am one... you are another." Only two remain (Gandalf, Pallando); three unaccounted for (Saruman, Radagast, two Blue Wizards — though Pallando is one Blue).
2. **Sauron's strategy**: He *spared* Gandalf deliberately — killing an Istar invites their spirit to return "in a form more potent."
3. **The Nine Rings**: Khamûl receives his Ring on-screen, becoming the Witch-king of Angmar (future Lord of the Nazgûl).
4. **Rhûn + Mordor alliance complete**: 100,000 Men of Rhûn + ~50,000 Orcs + Dark Wizard's former forces (now defecting).
5. **Naval & land blockade**: North Men blockade the sea; South Men blockade land — Sauron has cut off all escape/aid.
6. **Elven Rings powerless**: "Our Rings can no longer protect them" — the Three cannot shield against Sauron while he wears the One.
7. **Celeborn alive**: Confirmed present; his and Galadriel's relationship strained by an Age of separation and the Rings' making.
8. **Númenor's armada**: "Most powerful armada the age has known" assembled over five years, now committed to battle.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- **Tom Bombadil's song** ("Ho, Tom Bombadil / Harken now and hear me") — establishes his presence in show canon; Goldberry referenced.
- **"Death is the Gift of Men" / "Curse, more like"** — Elros/Elrond brother dynamic echoed in Isildur/Anárion lineage.
- **Lilies everywhere** — Dark Wizard's followers cast down arms; lilies bloom where they fell (symbol of liberation).
- **"Half-Elf" / "Half-Elf" exchange** — Soldier calls Elrond "Elf," he corrects "Half-Elf," soldier retorts "Or maybe just you... Elf."
- **Pallando's staff vanishing** — "The will of Sauron is not so easily undone" — Ring corruption persists on artifacts.
- **Celeborn's "Always" vs "Never"** — "Not nearly so long as... never" — echoes Elvish perspective on time/mortality.

## Unresolved Threads

1. **Battle of the Númenórean camp** — Pharazôn vs. Sauron single combat pending; full engagement imminent.
2. **Fate of the Three Elven Rings** — Galadriel wears Nenya; Vilya (Elrond) and Narya (Círdan/Gil-galad) unaddressed this episode.
3. **Other two Blue Wizards** — Only Pallando accounted for; Alatar's fate unknown.
4. **Dark Wizard's ultimate fate** — Escaped? Defeated? His tower abandoned but he departs on a mount.
5. **Stranger's staff** — Still missing; "The will of Sauron is not so easily undone" implies recovery needed.
6. **Celeborn's choice** — Whether he stays with Galadriel in Middle-earth or seeks the West remains open.
7. **Khamûl's transformation** — Just received his Ring; full Nazgûl metamorphosis not yet shown.
8. **Mordor's consolidation** — Sauron marching with One Ring; Barad-dûr construction, Mount Doom activity unresolved.
9. **Annatar persona** — Sauron still operating as Dark Lord openly; "Annatar" deception of Celebrimbor/Eregion appears concluded.
10. **Tom Bombadil's role** — Walks West with Gandalf/Pallando; future involvement unclear.
