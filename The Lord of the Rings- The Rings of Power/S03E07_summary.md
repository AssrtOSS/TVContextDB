## S03E07 Summary — The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power

### Episode Plot

**Eregion / Barad-dûr — Celeborn's Fall and Sauron's Triumph**  
Galadriel finds Celeborn alive but broken in Barad-dûr. Sauron visited him disguised as Galadriel, extracting the secret of the Three Rings: they were forged with a Fëanorian hammer (unmeltable by any forge) using gold and silver from Valinor (Finrod's dagger). The only heat sufficient is Mount Doom. Sauron now has everything needed for a Master Ring. Celeborn, dying, reconciles with Galadriel — both believed the other dead for an Age. He warns Sauron has other traps in motion, drawing Elven allies into a snare. They part with a promise: "Our story is not what we dreamt… but leave this place with me now, and I promise from this day on, it will be ours again."

**Khazad-dûm — Thráin's Betrayal and the Banishment**  
King Durin III (Thráin) has bent the knee to Mordor. He banishes Narvi and his family from Khazad-dûm, striking their names from the stones. Narvi refuses the Rings ("Don't let any Dwarf wear a Ring in our mountain"). Thráin declares: "It's not your home anymore." Disa and Durin IV (in exile) vow to retake the kingdom. Thráin's guards now enforce Sauron's will; the Seven Rings remain hidden in the Elder Mines.

**Númenor — The Marriage and the Fleet**  
Míriel weds Ar-Pharazôn to unite King's Men and Faithful. She rejects Elendil's plea to flee: "You're asking me to trade the happiness of two people for the suffering of millions." The marriage is a political lie — "by the laws of Númenor, my hand will belong to Pharazôn, but in the eyes of the Valar, my heart will always belong to another." Míriel commands the combined fleet to sail for Middle-earth: "Together, with the armies of Ar-Pharazôn, march into battle again as one people… and triumph over The Dark Lord Sauron in victory!"

**The Deeps — Sauron and the Balrog**  
Sauron descends to the Balrog (Durin's Bane), offering the Iron Crown and the world below: "I will loose you from this prison of stone… to reign eternal over all Khazad-dûm." The Balrog rejects him — "Even Sauron cannot move a mountain. But the Dwarves can." It demands the secret of surviving endless fire. Sauron bargains: the Rings in exchange for the answer.

**Rhûn / The Stranger's Path — Gandalf and Pallando**  
Gandalf (The Stranger) and Pallando (Blue Wizard) debate the Secret Fire. Pallando reveals their prophetic dooms: Gandalf dies by a Ring-bearer; Pallando dies by Sauron's hand. Pallando urges submission to Sauron as the only path; Gandalf refuses: "I would sooner embrace death." Pallando is captured by Orcs ("Sauron wants him alive… a wizard for the Lord of Gifts"). Gandalf continues toward Mordor alone: "Soon we reach Mordor. Sauron awaits."

**Mount Doom — Sauron's Ascension**  
Sauron performs the ritual of Udûn's dark fire in the Sammath Naur. He submits to the flame: "Thou must become one with it… reduced in infinite pain to purest being, until thy will shall become the only will that is." He emerges transformed — "Thou shalt not burn. Thou shalt not die. Thou shalt be emptied… Cast off the last light of The One. And become only thyself. Thou and thou forever." The episode ends with the Ring Verse chanted in Black Speech and Quenya.

### Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Fëanorian Hammer** — The unbreakable hammer forged by Fëanor (maker of Silmarils and palantíri), used to forge the Three Elven Rings. No forge can melt it; only Mount Doom's fire suffices.
- **Valinor Gold & Silver** — Metals of the Three Rings, sourced from Finrod's dagger brought from the Undying Lands.
- **Udûn (Black Speech)** — The dark fire of the underworld; the primordial flame Sauron submits to at Mount Doom.
- **Sammath Naur** — The Chambers of Fire inside Mount Doom (named implicitly by the ritual setting).
- **Lord of Gifts** — Orc title for Sauron (Annatar reference).
- **Kibil-nâla** — Dwarven river/spring where Narvi arranges a boat for his family's escape.
- **Iron Crown** — Morgoth's crown, now claimed by the Balrog; Sauron offers it as tribute.
- **Ancalagon & Ungoliant** — Invoked in Sauron's ascension chant: the mightiest dragon and the primordial spider of the First Age.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts

1. **The Three Rings' secret is fully compromised** — Sauron knows: Fëanorian hammer + Valinor metals + Mount Doom heat = Master Ring capable of commanding the Three.
2. **Sauron's physical transformation** — He voluntarily enters Udûn's fire, shedding the last light of Eru to become a pure will. This is the show's depiction of his "fair form" annihilation and rebirth as the Dark Lord.
3. **Thráin (Durin III) is now a vassal of Mordor** — Khazad-dûm is effectively occupied; the Seven Rings are the prize.
4. **Númenor's fleet sails united** — Faithful and King's Men together, commanded by Míriel, with Pharazôn's armies. Civil war averted by political marriage.
5. **Pallando captured** — One of the Five Istar now in Sauron's hands; Gandalf alone.
6. **Celeborn's fate** — He dies (or fades) after the reunion; Galadriel now carries the warning alone.
7. **The Balrog knows Dwarves can move mountains** — Foreshadows the War of the Dwarves and Orcs / Durin's Bane's eventual release.
8. **Mount Doom is now active as Sauron's forge** — The Master Ring will be made there.
