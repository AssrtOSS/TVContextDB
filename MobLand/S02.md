## S02E01

# MobLand — S02E01 Summary

## Plot
Months after S1, the Harrigans are in decline: Brendan and Archie dead, O'Hara dead, Eddie AWOL, Seraphina alienated, fentanyl gone and heroin exposed. Conrad takes a meeting with Turkish partner Gakbu Ruzgar at a greyhound track (Green Park Dogs) to close an eight-figure "brown" deal, only to learn the first shipment was already bought and paid for behind his back — Gakbu secretly went to Kevin. Enraged, Conrad, with Paul and the newly hired Fergus, guns down Luca Ruzgar and half his family in broad daylight, announcing "We are the Harrigans!" Gakbu (absent from the meeting) declares war on the family. Harry scrambles to contain the fallout: scrubbing CCTV via his contact Finch, relying on cloned plates on Paul's Defender and burner-phone alibis at the Princess of Shoreditch pub, then abducting the witness Dean with Zosia's help and forcing him to retract his statement to police and instead name "Gakbu Ruzgar" as his coercer, on pain of death by midnight.

## Important Character Actions
- Conrad: massacres the Ruzgars; orders Harry to "take care of Gakbu"; curses Kevin ("bucket of pig spunk son").
- Maeve: engineered the fixed greyhound race (ringer dog Ballycare Moon, £1M via 200 tame punters); admits she kept Harry unbriefed on the Gakbu meeting; now mentoring Eddie ("My plans for you are big"), demanding £50k and Gina out of his life; also running Gina in a protection racket shaking down YouTuber Jason (10% of £300k/month, "protected" by Roger and Rodway).
- Harry: crisis-manages the massacre aftermath; counsels Kevin to reconcile with Conrad; warns Kevin that Kat McAllister is dismantling the family; Jan tells him she wants a divorce and throws him out.
- Bella: secretly visits Kat, selling out Conrad — "His time is over." Negotiates: Kat gets half of all Harrigan revenue for 4 years in exchange for putting (Bella's chosen horse) on the throne; must report any Harrigan contact word-for-word.
- Seraphina: also secretly meets Kat — "I'm the smartest" Harrigan; her pitch is that Conrad's era is over; Kat tests her ruthlessness and sets the same reporting condition.
- Tattersall: introduces himself to now-US-Ambassador Kat, positioning himself as a "facilitator" who'll find her a "paladin" — a young criminal who'll run London in five years while the Harrigans become footnotes. Immediately afterward he is grabbed by unknown captors: "Hello, Frankie... Start talking, copper."
- Jan: attends a couples session with Harry (therapist Emily), recounts the stabbing and Gina's runaway note, then serves Harry divorce ultimatum via WhatsApp voice message ("You, Harry, are not enough").

## MobLand S02E02 Summary

## S02E03

# MobLand S02E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
The Harrigan family reels from a coordinated attack: Fergus is executed at the Falkland Arms pub, while Roger Hylton and Rodway Johnson are gunned down outside Kevin and Bella's home. The shooters—professional, masked, carrying AK variants—leave no trace; CCTV cuts out at the critical moment. Harry is abducted, shown a photo of Bella tied to a chair, and delivered a message: Frankie Cooper wants a sit-down at midnight at Harrigan's yard, with Harry, Kevin, and Conrad present.

Frankie Cooper arrives in a loud suit, lays out a detailed restructuring plan for North London (four sectors, route captains, Tuesday settle, standardized protection/gambling/debt/sex-work/supply/comms), and declares his price: he runs everything, Harry stays as fixer, Seraphina handles books/routes, Kevin is front-of-house, Bella goes home alive—and Conrad and Maeve get bullets in the head. The Harrigans refuse. Frankie reveals he's spent the day raping Bella to provoke Kevin; Kevin nearly shoots him but is talked down. Bella is released unharmed. The episode ends with the family fractured—Conrad and Maeve have announced their divorce to Seraphina, Harry's loyalty is tested, and Frankie Cooper walks away alive, promising to return.

## S02E04

## Episode Plot

Harry is managing an escalating war inside the Harrigan family, with Conrad now demanding Harry choose between his side and Kevin's side. A new antagonist, **Frankie Cooper**, has surfaced as Kat McAllister's enforcer after Harry refused Kat's deal. The episode tracks parallel threats:

- Harry stages the family "yard" as a gas-leak scene to cover a Semtex-in-bike-helmet bombing, then dismantles his corrupt police contact **DI Finch** after a police detective named **Kavanagh** starts tracing tampered ballistics from the earlier dog track shooting back to him.
- **Seraphina** confesses to Harry that during the Amsterdam ordeal with Brendan, she decided the family ship was sinking and called Kat herself — she is now working with Kat, not the family.
- **Maeve**, separately, lures **Eddie** and **Gina Da Souza** onto a flight to **Kerry, Ireland** (destination **Sneem**), using Eddie's need for his passport. Maeve's motives are opaque but sinister; she frames it as a "quest."
- Harry strings Finch up on a ladder with a noose to extract a final confirmation that no Harrigan file exists with his wife **Lisa**, then hangs him. Harry tells Conrad he's now "in with Kat" and will use the position.
- Episode ends with Harry arriving at a location with Brendan's old grey BMW, yelling **"You want a war, Cooper? I'll give you a fucking war!"** — a direct confrontation with Frankie Cooper.

Speaks Irish Gaelic ("Fág an bealach") when angry. Hints he has finally "seen the head and tail of the snake" about Maeve.

Lets slip to Gina that Harry never told her about the Harrigans' split — hinting at a wider schism inside the family.
- **Seraphina Harrigan:** Confesses the Amsterdam pivot to Harry; lets slip she has already seen Conrad that morning; appears resigned to betraying her father to Kat.
- **Kevin Harrigan:** Furious that Bella was held tied to a chair overnight; defends pulling a gun on the kidnapper against Harry's advice; demands Harry meet him at the Cotswolds at 8 a.m. or he's out. Phone call at end — "now or never."

- **Gina Da Souza:** Receives Harry's call to go home and stay off the phone; travels to Kerry with Eddie as Maeve's plan.
