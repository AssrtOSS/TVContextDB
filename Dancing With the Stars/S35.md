## S35E01

## S35E01 — Season Premiere, Night One (Male Stars)

### Episode Plot

The Season 35 premiere is a two-night event. Night 1 features all eight male celebrity contestants performing one dance each, with one couple eliminated by the end of the episode. Each star chose a Billboard #1 hit from their birth year as their song. An ABC.com voting outage forced the show to count only text votes, a twist announced mid-episode. Voting was US/Canada only, ten votes per couple per method.

### Important Character Actions

- **Harry Shum Jr.** topped the leaderboard with 21/30, leveraging his professional dance background (started as a backup dancer, starred in *Glee* and *Crazy Rich Asians*). He expressed fear of letting a partner down but committed to overcoming it.
- **Ezra Frech**, a double Paralympic gold medallist (100m and high jump, Paris 2024), delivered the night's most emotional performance. Born with one finger on his left hand and a curved left leg, he had a 15-hour surgery at age 3 where his leg was amputated below the knee and his big toe was transplanted to his left hand. He explained the critical difference between above-knee and below-knee amputees: he has zero control over the lower part of his prosthetic, meaning it can buckle. The choreography was adapted with diagonal steps instead of forward walks to accommodate his running prosthetic.
- **Guillermo Rodriguez**, the Jimmy Kimmel Live sidekick and self-described "first ever parking lot security guard" on DWTS, received the lowest score (10/30). Bruno called his performance a "crazy sexy grasshopper" move unrelated to cha cha.
- **Taylor Hanson** performed without his Hanson bandmates for the first time. He has seven children with his wife. Derek compared his saved-mistake recovery to great showmanship.
- **Tyler Cameron** came in second on *The Bachelorette* Season 15 and stated he refuses to come in second here. Derek compared his aura to Patrick Swayze. Carrie Ann docked him for a lift violation.
- **Conner Leavitt** (from *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives*) noted his wife Whitney Leavitt competed on Season 34. His sister-in-law is Whitney Carson (also a pro on this season, partnered with Guillermo — the two Whitneys are different people).
- **Witney Carson** revealed she is currently pregnant while dancing.
- **Jackson Olson**, a second baseman for the Savannah Bananas (an entertainment baseball team), said his teammates sent him "a million texts" on debut night.

## Important Character Actions
- **Tatyana Ali**: Emotional reunion with host Alfonso Ribeiro (played her TV brother on *Fresh Prince*); chose ambitious jive despite timing issues; son's 10th birthday during show.
- **Amber Glenn**: Olympic figure skater (2026 team gold) adjusting to ballroom technique (hip placement, "digging into floor" vs gliding); Bruno notes skating habits (front of leg vs back).
- **Ciara Miller**: Reality star (*Love Island*, *Summer House*) processing on-screen betrayal; Carrie Ann moved to tears by her vulnerability and growth mid-dance.
- **Giada De Laurentiis**: Competed at 56 for daughter Jade (college-bound); no prior dance experience; judges praised elegance and emotional intent.
- **Maura Higgins & Mark Ballas**: Reunited after *The Traitors* (where she "trusted all the wrong people"); earned night's highest score (21) with sultry, continuous-flow foxtrot.
- **Sarah Jane Nader & Hailey Bills**: First same-sex female couple this season; Hailey's pro debut; Sarah Jane redeeming sister's early exit; eliminated despite "bounty vibe" and representation impact.
- **Julia Stiles**: Superfan of the show; Dolly Parton personally approved "9 to 5" weeks before her passing; character-driven quickstep with nerves showing.
- **Jenna Dewan**: Former backup dancer (*Step Up* star) returning to "first love"; paired with bucket-list partner Val; judges want less solo-quality, more partnership detail.
