## S02E04


---

### Episode Plot
One month before present day, Pasin and Jin-man coordinate a rescue mission for Pasin's son **Atti** (kidnapped by Babylon due to his leukemia). They use a secure 12-hour rotating email channel with their mole inside Babylon. Jin-man travels alone to Thailand on a forged passport (Choi Myung-gyu) while Pasin stays to defend the Murthehelp mall. In Korea, Pasin is ambushed by Babylon operatives led by **Jo Seong-gu** (Team Leader Jo), a former colleague transferred to a "hot as hell" post after Jin-man dismantled his unit. The mole's emails stop arriving a week ago — Babylon intercepted the channel. Pasin is captured and turned (or coerced) into a double agent. He returns to the mall, hacks the security system with Babylon's black-hat team, and opens the doors for a full assault. Ji-an barely escapes with Jin-woo and Cheol-seung. Babylon's leader **Q** is manipulated into believing Jin-man killed her lover **J** (actually killed by Bale), driving her to hunt Ji-an. The episode ends with a new character, **Ahn Seong-beom**, introduced at a Korean BBQ restaurant.

- **Jin-man**: Goes solo to Thailand on forged passport to meet mole; leaves Pasin/Ji-an to hold mall
- **Pasin**: Ambushed in Korea; captured by Babylon; returns as apparent double agent; hacks mall security; enables full Babylon invasion
- **Ji-an**: Discovers mole's emails stopped (marker missing); realizes Babylon intercepted comms; flees mall when Pasin betrays them
- **Min-hye**: Volunteers to accompany Pasin to verify Atti's location; fate uncertain (Pasin claims "I took care of Minhye")
- **Jo Seong-gu**: Leads Babylon Korea ops; taunts Jin-man; orchestrates Pasin's turn; manipulates Q via false revenge narrative
- **Brother/Jin-woo/Cheol-seung**: Hold mall defense; evacuate Ji-an when breach occurs
- **Q**: Babylon commander; believes Jin-man killed J; sent to capture Ji-an as "queen" to draw out "king" Jin-man

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Mole compromised**: Only Jin-man, Pasin, Brother, Min-hye, and the mole knew the 12-hour rotating email system. Leak implies internal betrayal.
- **Pasin's status**: Ambiguous — either turned coercively (to save Atti) or genuinely flipped. Babylon orders Q to "eliminate Pasin once Jeong Jian is captured."
- **Mall security breached**: Motion-sensor cameras and alarm system fully hacked; Babylon now has unrestricted access.
- **Ji-an's escape**: She, Jin-woo, and Cheol-seung flee the mall; Brother's status unknown.
- **Babylon's strategy**: "Trojan Horse" chess metaphor — turn a knight (Pasin), capture the queen (Ji-an), force the king (Jin-man) into endgame.
- **Bale's role confirmed**: Killed J; bus bombing was cover-up (consistent with S1).
