## Overall Plot Premise
College student **Jeong Ji-an** was raised by her enigmatic uncle **Jeong Jin-man** after her parents' violent deaths. Jin-man, posing as an eccentric agricultural-equipment warehouse owner, secretly trained her in combat, marksmanship, and survival. When Ji-an receives news of Jin-man's apparent suicide, she returns home only to be besieged by elite assassins. She discovers **Murthehelp** is a sophisticated dark-web marketplace supplying weapons and gear to a global criminal network — and Jin-man was its legendary operator, a former top mercenary for the PMC **Babylon**.  

Flashbacks reveal Jin-man's past: his feud with the psychopathic **Bale** led to his family's massacre, forcing him into hiding to protect Ji-an. In the present, Ji-an must master the mall's codes and rally Jin-man's former allies — Muay Thai master **Pasin**, S-class killer **So Min-hye**, and the mysterious underground manager **Brother** — to defend the warehouse. She ultimately assumes leadership of Murthehelp, defeats the primary attackers, and learns Jin-man faked his death to draw out his enemies.  

**Season 2** escalates: Babylon's East Asia Division, led by **Kusanagi**, renews the hunt. Jin-man and Ji-an launch a full counter-offensive against Babylon itself.

## Cultural Context & Translation Considerations

1. **Honorifics & Family Terms**  
   - 삼촌 (samchon) = uncle (paternal/maternal) → 叔叔  
   - 조카 (joka) = niece/nephew → 侄女 / 侄子  
   - 할머니 (halmeoni) = grandmother → 奶奶 / 外婆 (paternal/maternal distinction if needed)  
   - 오빠 (oppa) / 형 (hyung) / 누나 (nuna) / 언니 (eonni) — retain or map to 哥/姐/哥哥/姐姐 per speaker gender and intimacy.

2. **Military / PMC Jargon**  
   - 작전 (operation/mission) → 行动 / 任务  
   - 타겟 (target) → 目标  
   - 안전가옥 (safe house) → 安全屋  
   - 철수 (withdrawal/extraction) → 撤退 / 撤离  
   - 교전 (engagement/firefight) → 交火

3. **Dark-Web / Tech Terms**  
   - 다크웹 (dark web) → 暗网  
   - 암호화 (encryption) → 加密  
   - 서버 (server) → 服务器  
   - 아이디 / 비번 (ID/password) → 账号 / 密码  
   - 접속 (access/login) → 登录 / 接入

4. **Action / Weaponry**  
   - 저격수 (sniper) → 狙击手  
   - 소음기 (silencer) → 消音器  
   - 탄창 (magazine) → 弹匣  
   - 근접전 (close-quarters combat) → 近身格斗 / CQB  
   - 엄폐물 (cover) → 掩体

5. **Korean-Specific Cultural Beats**  
   - Funeral rites (장례식) — bowing, incense, mourning clothes → 守灵 / 烧香 / 素服  
   - University entrance / campus life — soju gatherings, group projects → 大学生活细节  
   - Convenience-store meals (편의점 도시락) → 便利店便当  
   - Subway / last-train urgency → 末班车

6. **Tone Consistency**  
   - Series blends gritty action thriller with dry gallows humor. Preserve the contrast: Jin-man's deadpan one-liners ("농업용 장비야" — "It's agricultural equipment") vs. visceral violence.  
   - Flashbacks (past) vs. present: differentiate via tense/aspect markers (了/过 vs. 正在/着) where Chinese grammar allows.
