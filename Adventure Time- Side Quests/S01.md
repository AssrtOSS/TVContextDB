# Adventure Time: Side Quests — S01E16 Summary

## Episode Plot
Finn and Jake prepare a party at the Tree House. Jake reveals he found a **teleportation crystal** while dumpster-diving. When they test it by trying to teleport ice cream, the crystal instead summons the **Ice King**, who claims he locked himself out of his castle and hid a spare key somewhere in the Tree House. Finn and Jake reluctantly help him search, only to discover the key hunt is an elaborate multi-stage riddle gauntlet orchestrated by Ice King ("Ice King's Widdle Riddle Jamboree"). After solving numerous cryptic clues — including "the only knot in the tree fort that can never be untied" (revealed to be Jake's belly button area) and a "back to start" reset — they finally locate the spare key **under their own doormat**. Ice King immediately produces a "spare spare key," declares the gathering a "nerd fest" (they're playing a board game), and leaves. The episode ends with Finn, Jake, BMO, Ice King (briefly), and a Banana Guard playing **Tower of Stinko**, a surreal dice-and-card board game involving smell goblins and "Princess Toots."

## Important Character Actions
- **Jake**: Dumpster-dives for party supplies; finds teleportation crystal; performs "hup hyah" searching chant; solves the "unknotable knot" clue by recognizing his own belly.
- **Finn**: Attempts to speedrun the riddle chain; briefly questions whether they should "get along" with Ice King; rips into Ice King verbally when the "back to start" riddle appears.
- **Ice King**: Teleports in accidentally; manipulates Finn and Jake into a night-long riddle game; reveals he hid the key under the doormat "where else would I put it, under my own doormat?"; carries a backup spare key; abandons the party for not being "cool" enough.
- **BMO**: Appears briefly, naked "just like Ice King," then joins the board game.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Teleportation crystal** — found in a dumpster; summons Ice King when targeting ice cream.
- **Ice King's Widdle Riddle Jamboree** — Ice King's self-given name for the multi-stage key hunt.
- **Tower of Stinko** — board game with chrome dice, "smell goblins," "Princess Toots," "boinky" and "gleebo" cards, and a Wednesday-only pink/blue swap rule (fifth-edition variant).
- **Spare spare key** — Ice King's backup to the backup key.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Ice King acknowledges he and Finn/Jake are "enemies for the most part" who "fight, bicker, try to kill each other occasionally," but reframes it as "great passion" and suggests they're "kind of... chums." Finn and Jake reject this.
- The Tree House has a doormat (used as the canonical hiding spot).
- Ice King's castle has a lock he can be locked out of.
- BMO's established "naked like Ice King" gag recurs.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **Teleportation crystal** — reusable plot device; remains in Finn and Jake's possession.
- **Tower of Stinko** — fully detailed fake RPG/board game with its own terminology (boinky/gleebo, edition wars joke); ripe for callbacks.
- **Ice King's "spare spare key"** habit — implies he routinely hides keys in others' homes.
- **Wednesday rule / fifth-edition player** — meta TTRPG humor consistent with the show's RPG-parody vein.
- **Banana Guard** casually showing up at the Tree House party — normalizes Candy Kingdom citizens visiting.

## Unresolved Threads
- The teleportation crystal's origin and full capabilities are unexplored.
- Ice King's "spare spare key" suggests a pattern of breaking into / hiding things in the Tree House that could recur.
- No consequence for Ice King's manipulation; he leaves unbothered.
