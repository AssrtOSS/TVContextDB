## Episode S01E05 — Marcos, Revisited

### PlotChelsea flies to northern Spain (a vineyard/castle estate outside Bilbao) to revisit her third ex, Marcos Vera, the billionaire owner of the vineyard where she spent part of her gap-year program. The episode cuts between three timelines: present-day Chelsea and Jason back in Boston, present-day Chelsea in Spain with Marcos, and flashback Chelsea during the original Spanish summer ten years earlier.

In flashback, Marcos first approaches Chelsea on a bike ride and tries to give her a ride. She coldly rejects him, lecturing him about carbon footprints, his family's wealth, and Page Six. He persists and even auctions his car for an environmental charity without telling her; she eventually relents. They fall deeply in love. Just as Marcos asks Chelsea to abandon her planned move to Germany and stay the summer with him, her father calls: her mother is dying. Chelsea leaves Spain without telling Marcos and never contacts him again.

In present-day Spain, Chelsea tells Marcos she is now seeing someone. He offers a tour of the renovated estate, revealing he has sold the rest of his family's business portfolio, converted the vineyard to fully organic farming, and is sitting on roughly $48 billion with no heirs. He proposes she run a new Spanish foundation he is founding, with full autonomy, focused on cancer research and the environment — "no red tape." Chelsea says she needs to sleep on it.

In present-day Boston, Jason agonizes about Chelsea being alone with Marcos, calls his friend for advice about whether to call her, and confesses (to himself and his sister) that he loves her but hasn't said it. His fantasy-football buddies notice the tension.

Chelsea calls Jason that night. She tells him she is sure of her feelings and wants him. She also tells him about the foundation offer. Jason, self-aware about Chelsea's "no handouts" pride and his own inability to leave Boston until his sister is through college, agrees they should try long-distance — but Chelsea, realizing the impossibility, decides quietly not to accept the job. She declines on her own.

Back in Boston, Chelsea walks into a meeting expecting the board-approved layoffs she was warned about earlier in the season. Her boss Aidan instead tells her she has been selected as the one to be let go — Knightly is the survivor. Chelsea immediately quits, knowing Jason cannot afford to lose the job: his sister's club-soccer fees ($10K), the school tuition he already pays, his parents' debt-driven collapse, and his refusal to accept money from Chelsea all mean he needs this paycheck more than she does. She does not tell Jason the real reason; from his perspective, she has chosen Spain.

Jason believes she chose the foundation and tries to be graceful ("We could try long distance"). Chelsea breaks up with him, saying it is too much too soon and walks out.

Time jump to one year later. Chelsea narrates that she has returned to Boston. Glenn has proposed to Sherri, who says yes. Annabelle and Chelsea are bridesmaids. Chelsea reflects on her father getting to have great love twice. She confesses directly to Jason — present, watching her — that she has spent the last year regretting letting him go and wants only him. The episode ends mid-song with Jason still in the room.

### Important character actions

- Chelsea, flashback: shames Marcos over his wealth; leaves Spain without a word when her mother is dying; never calls to explain.
- Marcos, flashback: auctions his car for an environmental charity; converts vineyard to organic; asks Chelsea to stay.
- Marcos, present: tours Chelsea through renovated estate; reveals he has sold the family portfolio and has ~$48B; offers her the foundation.
- Chelsea, present Spain: tells Marcos she is seeing someone; later declines the foundation offer on her own.
- Chelsea, present Boston: learns Jason is on the layoff shortlist; quits her ACC job to protect him.
- Jason, present: confides to his sister and friend that he loves Chelsea; says he cannot leave Boston until his sister is in college; agrees to try long distance.
- Chelsea, one-year-later: confronts Jason; confesses she never wanted anyone else.

### New names, terms, and facts established

- Marcos's full surname: Marcos Vera.
- Vineyard location: outside Bilbao, Spain. Architecture dated to the 1800s.
- Wine featured: Garnacha / Grenache ("full bodied, bright, notes of cherry and raspberry, hint of pepper, pairs with grilled meats and spicy flavors").
- Marcos's father: deceased; Marcos has inherited the estate, sold the other companies in the portfolio, sits on $48 billion.
- Marcos's new initiative: a Spanish foundation, no red tape, full autonomy to Chelsea, focus on cancer research and the environment.
- Chelsea's Boston employer: the ACC (the Aidan-or-similar-supervisor's nonprofit). Boston Children's Hospital new wing was an ACC project.
- Aidan: Chelsea's mentor and supervisor at the ACC; broke the layoff news to her.
- Sophie Arnault: the donor whose gift is "literally keeping us afloat" — the Paris donor from earlier episodes is now identified by name.
- Jason's sister: ~junior-high age; tried out for a club team; dues roughly $10,000; Jason already pays her school tuition and refuses Chelsea's offer of help.
- Jason's friend / fantasy-football crew member "Aidan," and his wife Melissa; another friend with a one-month-old baby.
- Glenn + Sherri: Glenn proposes; Sherri accepts; Chelsea and Annabelle are asked to be bridesmaids.
- Boston neighborhoods referenced: Dorchester (Jason's hometown).

### Continuity-relevant facts

- "Paris is Always a Good Idea" was the late mother's bucket-list directive, sent via Annabelle — recurring frame.
- The third-exe = Marcos, the gap-year Spain chapter.
- Chelsea's pattern of misjudging men and then leaving without explanation ("a pattern with me").
- Jason's "breaking point" / long-distance rule: he keeps choosing women with bigger dreams than Boston, and he can't leave until his sister finishes college.
- Aidan earlier told Chelsea "it's your account" regarding Sophie Arnault's Paris donation — confirmed here as the deal that saved the ACC.
- The board is pressuring layoffs across the organization, not just Chelsea's team.
- Chelsea's mother remains deceased; her father is alive and is the one who originally called her in Spain.

### Jokes, callbacks, and useful references for later episodes

- "I'm not falling — I've fallen. I'm flat on my face for you" — Jason's self-aware confession, used for comedic effect with his fantasy-football league.
- Jason's "Dorchester nobody vs. castle billionaire" self-deprecating bit, undercut by Chelsea: "Hey, don't sell the Dorchester guy short. He's a good kisser."
- "Not many guys I know would send the woman they're falling for after a man who owns a castle" — Annabelle to Jason about Chelsea's trip.
- Annabelle's recurring line that Chelsea is "following her heart, putting work second" — she calls herself Chelsea's therapist and gets told "you still aren't my therapist."
- "It's a pattern with me" — Chelsea's twice-stated self-diagnosis about choosing women with bigger dreams than Boston (Jason) and leaving Marcos without explanation.
- Ending song cues a Hallmark-style romantic ballad; visual gag of Annabelle watering plants when Chelsea arrives back unexpectedly.

### Unresolved threads for the finale

- Jason still does not know Chelsea quit to save his job; he thinks she chose Spain/the foundation.
- Chelsea has just confessed to Jason that she wants only him; the finale needs to show whether he accepts.
- Marcos remains in Spain with the foundation unstaffed — could reappear if Chelsea does not take the role.
- Glenn and Sherri's wedding is in motion — bridesmaid duties for Chelsea and Annabelle.
- Jason's sister's club fees and the family debt are ongoing financial pressures.
- The line "maybe we all deserve more than one chance at a great love" is now a thematic thesis statement for the finale.
- Chelsea's narration notes "it's been a year" — implies a time-skip finale wrapping up the Spain revisit and the Knightly revisit simultaneously.

## S01E06

**Episode S01E06 – Knightly, Revisited**

**Episode plot**  
The season finale opens with Chelsea at her father Glenn’s wedding to Sherrie in Spain. Flashbacks and present-day scenes weave together: Chelsea reflects on the year since she left Boston (and Jason), admitting she still misses him daily. Annabelle, now reconciled with her ex Rhyland (a Real Madrid player), encourages Chelsea to reach out to Jason. Chelsea finally calls Jason—but the call goes to Aidan, the ACC’s new director, who flies in personally to accept Chelsea’s $2 M/year donation. The meeting is cut short; Chelsea realizes she came hoping to see Jason. Marcos urges her to call Jason; she sends a voice-note “pitch” laying out why they belong together. Jason shows up at the wedding, hears the pitch, and counters with his own. They kiss, reconcile, and dance at the reception.

**Important character actions**  
- Chelsea: delivers a raw, drunken Kelly Clarkson karaoke (“Since U Been Gone”) confessing she blew her chance with Jason; later records a vulnerable voice memo “pitching” their relationship.  
- Jason: flies to Spain unannounced; reveals he saved Chelsea’s job a year earlier (she never knew); listens to her pitch and responds with his own, confirming he loves her.  
- Annabelle: pushes Chelsea to contact Jason; texts her own ex Rhyland; shares hard-won wisdom that grief “never gets easier, you just think about it less.”  
- Glenn: marries Sherrie; shares a tender father-daughter moment the night before, giving Chelsea his mother’s digitized wedding video and telling her “it’s never too late if he’s the one.”  
- Marcos: hosts the wedding at his castle/vineyard; acts as Chelsea’s supportive friend and wingman, teasing her about Jason.  
- Sherrie: honored in Glenn’s vows as the woman who “shaped Glenn into the man he is” and “raised two amazing women.”

**Newly established names/terms**  
- **Aidan** – new senior director at ACC (Children’s Cancer Coalition), replaces Jason’s old role.  
- **Rhyland** – Annabelle’s ex, now playing for Real Madrid (first on-screen mention of his team).  
- **“Work dude” / “Ex-work dude”** – running nickname Chelsea/Annabelle use for Jason; retired by the end (“you can call me Jason”).  
- **“Pitch”** – metaphor for a romantic declaration, echoing their nonprofit fundraising language.

**Continuity-relevant facts**  
- Chelsea’s mother Christine died ~1 year ago; her hidden illness and decision not to tell Chelsea so Chelsea would go to Europe is now fully understood and forgiven.  
- Glenn & Sherrie’s wedding fulfills Christine’s dying wish (“she wants the three of us to go to Paris together” – now reinterpreted as “go to Spain together”).  
- Jason’s job was saved by Chelsea’s behind-the-scenes intervention (she threatened to quit if he was fired); he didn’t know until Aidan told him.  
- The ACC donation ($2 M annually) is finalized, securing the nonprofit’s future.  
- Chelsea has lived in Spain for a year working with Marcos’s vineyard/nonprofit venture.

**Jokes / references for later episodes**  
- “Work dude” nickname permanently retired; Chelsea refuses to call him “Jason” and vice-versa (“It’s weird”).  
- Annabelle’s “tough-love sister” persona vs. Chelsea’s “coddling.”  
- Kelly Clarkson “Since U Been Gone” karaoke becomes Chelsea’s emotional breakthrough moment.  
- “Heaven Is a Place on Earth” plays during first dance – bookends the series’ musical motif.

**Unresolved threads**  
- Long-term living arrangement: Chelsea (Spain-based) vs. Jason (Boston-based) – they acknowledge “details can come later.”  
- Annabelle/Rhyland dynamic – she texted him, no reply yet; left open.  
- Chelsea’s vineyard/nonprofit role in Spain – whether she stays or returns to Boston is undefined.  
- No explicit “what happens next” for the ACC now that both leads are romantically entangled.
