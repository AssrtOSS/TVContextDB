## S03E01 — India

### Episode Plot
Conan begins Season 3 in Mumbai, taking the legendary commuter train (7M+ daily riders), playing street cricket with locals, meeting fan Nikhil Kini in Bangalore, crashing Nikhil's mock talk show at Toit Brewpub, visiting a dhobi ghat (open-air laundry), meeting superfan Rustom "Rusty" Irani and his parents, and culminating in a Bollywood fantasy sequence with stars Ali Fazal and Richa Chadha.

### Important Character Actions
- **Conan**: Rides Mumbai train (gets back scratched by stranger, meets local "impersonator"), fails at cricket ("Transformer from Princeton"), teaches Nikhil American talk-show tropes (forced laughter, soul-free ambition, Ted Bundy comparisons), stages monkey AT&T store bit, visits dhobi ghat, meets Rusty & parents, attempts Bollywood stardom.
- **Nikhil Kini (fan)**: Bangalore video creator/aspiring talk-show host; roommates Jeet ("serial rascal"), Anis (crypto, at gym), Vishal; hosts "The Nikhil Kini Show" at Toit with Bro-Tones band.
- **Rustom "Rusty" Irani (fan)**: Lifelong wheelchair user (spinal muscular atrophy); credits Conan as gateway to Stooges, Marx Brothers, Monty Python, SNL; father is aggressive wingman/cockblocker who announces Rusty's erections and fertility to dates.
- **Rusty's parents**: Shehraj (father, "King of Bombay City") flexes bicep, blows conch shell, honks horn, brandishes unloaded shotgun; mother tries to change subject during sex-talk.
- **Aditi**: Mumbai cultural ambassador; teaches namaste, foot-touching respect, Shah Rukh Khan pose.
- **Ali Fazal & Richa Chadha**: Bollywood power couple; shut down Conan's star aspirations ("not Bollywood material"), duet on "Namaste in Your Lane."

# S03E02 — The Netherlands

## Episode Plot
Conan arrives in Amsterdam on **King's Day (Koningsdag)**, where the city wears orange for the king's birthday — not him. He explores the **vrijmarkt** (citywide flea market), meets fan **Maaike** (a 6-foot high-fashion model), and attempts a modeling career with male model **Nilo** and designer **Diek**, resulting in brutal honesty about his "beady eyes," "creepy demeanor," and prominent eye vein. He visits tulip fields, harasses mascot **Tilly the Tulip** (accusing her of sociopathy and future tulip uprising), then tours the **Fietsdepot** where thousands of canal-fished bikes are impounded. In Amsterdam, he meets fan **Lucas**, a former pro basketball player, and teaches Dutch-style trash talk ("Van Gogh fuck yourself," "You must be Edam 'cause you're no Gouda"). He visits clog makers **Roel & Tim**, inventing **Crogs** (clog + Croc) and the pun **shoe-venir**. The episode culminates in The Hague at the **International Court of Justice**, where Conan faces a mock trial: "Every country of the world versus Conan O'Brien," featuring footage from earlier segments. He sings his defense ("Lock me up and throw away the key"), and the episode closes with a resolution to the **Hannah** relationship thread and a **Kathy Griffin** cameo.

## Important Character Actions
- **Maaike**: 6-foot model fan; critiques Conan's hair ("jumpy"), suggests hand modeling for scabies cream
- **Nilo**: Male model; jealous of Conan's hair; declares him "most beautiful man I've ever seen" under duress
- **Diek**: Designer; puts Conan in outfits ranging from *Miami Vice* to "stolen first-class pajamas"
- **Tilly the Tulip**: Mascot; unbothered by Conan's escalating fake confessions (murder, hit-and-run)
- **Romy**: Tilly's handler; shares tulip-eating diarrhea story Conan cuts
- **Roger & Patrick**: KLM Delft Blue house collectors (106 houses); Roger "lives in storage facility," eats rodents
- **Martin & Jaya**: Fietsdepot staff; impound bikes 4 weeks, wrecked bikes 2 weeks; deadpan through Conan's bits
- **Lucas**: Basketball fan; learns trash talk; plays 1-on-1 against coach **Dave**; uses "Van Gogh fuck yourself" on teammates
- **Roel & Tim**: 800-year clog tradition; Roel: "If they buy, happy. If not, happy." Conan pitches Crogs
- **Jonathan**: Swedish cyclist; ditches girlfriend for Conan; "I can do it again" (meet on app)
- **ICJ Prosecutor**: Presents Conan's "crimes" (bike bits, trash talk, singing); uses real episode footage

## S03E03

# Conan O'Brien Must Go — S03E03 Morocco

## Episode Plot
Conan and Sona travel to Morocco to source argan oil for Sona's hair. The episode splits between the Sahara Desert (East Morocco) and Marrakesh. In the desert, they visit Yassir (a fan from Casablanca) and his family, then stay with Aicha of the Amazigh tribe at a nomad camp. They ride camels, endure a "therapeutic" sand burial, and drive a vintage non-AC car 9 hours to Marrakesh. In the city, Conan does a photoshoot with artist Hassan Hajjaj, shops in the souk (used for *Sex and the City 2*), buys a leather tuxedo from tailor Monsieur Le Lu, and finally secures Sona's argan oil at *Le Sens de Marrakech*.

## Important Character Actions
- **Conan**: Gifts Yassir's family items from their own hallway; rides "Coca-Cola Zero" (the aggressive camel); suffers through sand burial while Sona enjoys it; drives dangerously in vintage car while video-calling his mom; tries on absurd outfits for Hajjaj photoshoot; purchases leather tuxedo, rug jacket, leather duffel, and "boner tee" shirts.
- **Sona**: Gets buried in sand and finds it "nurturing"; hijacks Conan's voiceover narration; receives massive argan oil bottle (lasts "two days"); calls shopkeeper "creep of the century."
- **Yassir**: Casablanca project manager who hasn't seen *Casablanca*; watches *Avengers: Endgame* and *Off Campus* hockey show.
- **Aicha**: Amazigh nomad who traveled 250 km by camel to settle near water; hosts them with tea and medfouna (Berber pizza).
- **Hassan Hajjaj**: Celebrity photographer (Billie Eilish, Riz Ahmed, Cardi B) who stages outdoor photoshoot with musician Maroune Lbahja on guembri.
- **Monsieur Le Lu**: Leather tailor who fits Conan for tuxedo (ready in one week — after they leave).

## S03E04


## Episode Plot

Conan flies to Manila to help fan **Mustafa** become an action star. He contacts the #1 Philippine cop show *Sanggang-Dikit*, which casts Mustafa (and gives Conan a villain role as the Sator Medallion–obsessed antagonist). He explores Intramuros with local fixers **Inky** and **Girlie**, rides bamboo bikes through Fort Santiago with guide **Josh**, and films a recurring "Ah, the Philippines" infomercial bit (each factual claim corrected by a crew member). He meets fan **Trisha**, who runs the **Bravo Tanauan Hotel** (22 rooms), and works the front desk—renaming it the "Four Seasons" and playing bellhop ("Jerry Lewis 1960 *The Bellboy*" tribute). He samples **balut** (duck-egg embryo street food) with vendor **Jose Magayes**, accompanied by a hired actor **Dr. Arroyo** (a "quack" doctor who recommends balut). He tours the **MacArthur Suite** at the **Manila Hotel**, delivers an extended "I shall return" comedy riff, and meets fan **Charles**, who works at his uncle's candy company **Philip Sweets** (nickname "Phisman"), makers of **Tarzan** and **Texas** bubble gums. Conan agrees to celebrity endorsement, reveals a giant billboard (someone else's face, his body), and proposes a **candy balut** (white-chocolate shell, gooey center) as a new product. The episode closes with Conan proposing to buy a Philippine island to hunt guests (a riff on *The Most Dangerous Game*), learning he needs a Filipino spouse to own land—then declaring Jose the balut vendor his fiancé.

## Important Character Actions

- **Conan**: Films a villain scene for *Sanggang-Dikit* (fake mustache falls off); wears an ube-colored (purple yam) barong Tagalog; rides a bamboo bike with a bamboo helmet; works hotel front desk demanding 3-night stays and tips; eats balut after consulting "Dr. Arroyo"; sleeps on MacArthur's bed; proposes island hunting grounds; creates candy balut for Philip Sweets.
- **Mustafa**: Aspiring Philippine action star from the podcast video call; shows off his instant strike; films his first cop-show scene alongside Conan.
- **Inky & Girlie**: Local fixers/helpers; Girlie gets caught eating competitor candy from her desk, nearly fired, then reinstated; they prefer Conan to other celebrity travel-show hosts ("those look like wax figures").
- **Trisha**: Hotel owner; starts Christmas in September; lets Conan play bellhog and front-desk clerk.
- **Josh**: Bamboo-bike tour guide at Fort Santiago; deflates Conan's attempt to fabricate ancient history ("started in 2009, by the CEO").
- **Dr. Arroyo**: Fake physician hired for the balut bit; delivers a "quack" pun and enumerates duck species.
- **Jose Magayes**: Balut street vendor (80/day); Conan proposes to him for island-ownership purposes.
- **Charles**: Fan and candy-factory nephew; eagerly accepts Conan's (unwanted) handshake deal; describes products Tarzan and Texas.
- **Asher**: Manila Hotel guide; endures Conan's MacArthur Suite comedy ("I don't want to see that dump" prank twice).
