## S01E03

# Episode plot

S01E03 follows FTX's chaotic early days in Hong Kong. The episode opens with a Bloomberg reporter, Hannah Miller, and an editor named Valerie debating whether to publish a story about FTX and Alameda covering something up; they settle on implying a hidden relationship between Sam and Caroline's two companies without accusing anyone of crime. Meanwhile Sam and Caroline prepare to move from New York to Hong Kong to launch FTX, a crypto exchange that would donate all profits to effective-altruism charities, because an SEC license would be too slow to obtain in the U.S. Their personal status is awkward: Caroline was Sam's first intimate partner, but they insist they are "not together together," just friends and co-founders, even as they relocate together.

At the new office, the team hires friends — Gary Wang, Nishad Singh, Ryan Salame — and holds an all-hands led by counselor/ADHD specialist George Lerner. Sam reveals that Alameda's arbitrage profits are drying up and that FTX is days from folding; all the hired coders have no-showed, having been poached by CZ in Malta, and every VC has passed. In a desperate move, Sam and Caroline gatecrash a fake crypto soirée in Central, where CZ (Changpeng Zhao of Binance) reveals he orchestrated the VC rejections and the coder exodus to crush FTX before it launches, demanding Sam delete the code and retreat to Berkeley. Sam has an on-floor breakdown ("I failed! I'm a fucking failure"), Caroline reaffirms they are partners, and CZ then pivots to offering $100 million for a 20% stake, becoming FTX's lead investor. The episode ends with a flash-forward to the Bahamas in 2022: Nishad has called Sam's father, Joe Bankman, asking about criminal liability; Sam and Caroline try to buy Nishad's silence with money, an NDA, and a severance package for Claire, insisting Claire must never learn the truth; and Hannah Miller tells a colleague she is ready to run her story after Sam's evasive Bloomberg interview.

# Important character actions

- Sam pitches FTX to his parents on a call from Hong Kong, frames the move as "earn to give" effective altruism, gives a cringe "MasterClass" on company culture, breaks down in front of CZ, then accepts CZ's investment while hiding the $8 billion hole from nearly everyone.
- Caroline coaches Sam through the breakup-party flirtation disaster, defines their relationship as "friends," comforts Sam after the CZ meeting ("wherever we go… I'll always be your friend"), and later negotiates Nishad's silence while demanding Claire never learn the truth.

- CZ engineers the no-show coders and VC rejections, then offers the rescue investment with strings attached ("raid us for spare parts," suppress newcomers).
- Hannah Miller pushes Sam on Alameda/FTX ties and his living situation, then decides to publish her exposé.

## Episode plot
The episode opens with a flash-forward to the FTX collapse: news reports that Alameda Research and FTX have filed for Chapter 11, and police arrive with an arrest warrant for Samuel Bankman-Fried at his parents' home; his mother insists the legal strategy is "not to explain anything," while Caroline is present and Sam promises he can "fix it."

It then flashes back to 2016. Sam, a junior trader at a New York hedge fund, builds an election-prediction algorithm that beats CNN by seconds, netting the firm $150 million in one night before losing $300 million on a doubled-down bet. His boss keeps him because Sam is an effective altruist who gives most of his salary away ("earn to give"). During new-hire training, Sam meets Caroline Ellison; they bond over brainteasers, math, Harry Potter, and One Direction. After drinks, Caroline directly propositions Sam, and they sleep together; she discloses she is polyamorous, and Sam agrees.

Sam quits the fund to launch a crypto effective-altruism fund ("give away 99% of the profits"), and Trabucco joins him as co-founder. The story jumps ahead: FTX is now a $36 billion empire headquartered in the Bahamas, with Caroline running Alameda Research. When the Terra stablecoin depegs 2% and Voyager calls in its loan, Caroline proposes secretly buying Voyager to bury records of loans between Alameda and FTX; Sam agrees to go "all in." At a celebrity party (Katy Perry's Diet Coke rider, Tony Blair, Bill Clinton, Liam Payne), Sam meets Hot Steve, a former intern now running his own crypto company. Meanwhile, new hire Rachel discovers on her second day that Alameda secretly took $8 billion in FTX customer money and covered it with Voyager loans; Caroline dismisses it as a "mislabeled file" that has been fixed. The episode ends with Trabucco warning Sam on a yacht that risking everything for Caroline may be a mistake, and Sam defending her.
