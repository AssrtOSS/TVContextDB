## S01E02

Episode: S01E02 "Shroom with a View"（《菌门之下》）

Plot Summary

Raine（雷恩）登门，把她室友 Lauren（劳伦）的求助案交到 Shade 与 Angie 手里：Mayette Reyes（玛耶特·雷耶斯），维多利亚大学新生，18 岁，熬夜去铜溪公园（Copperstream Park）徒步后失踪，缺考早 10 点的统计学考试，并缺席当晚同一宿舍 Lauren 的关心。门缝下塞进匿名纸条「敢说出去你就死定了」（"Talk and you're dead."）。Shade 联想到女儿 Jules 失踪时的处境，两人接下案子，开始 P.I. 事务所复工。

夫妇俩同时还在面试住家保姆——因为他们决定把「重启侦探业务」和「雇人照看 Declan（迪克兰）」同时进行。

Investigation Path

- 前往铜溪公园找公园管理员，管理员告知亲眼看到 Mayette 从树林里走出来，被一个「穿格子衬衫、工装裤、Blundstones 布伦斯顿工装靴」的男子带上车开走。
- 向重大案件组组长肖紫（Detective Violet Xiao）汇报；肖紫态度冷淡，要求失踪 24 小时后再通知，否则请夫妇俩带着「马戏团（circus）」离开她的办公室。
- 探访 Mayette 与 Lauren 的宿舍：两人曾亲密，Lauren 误发短信「我的室友是乐趣消亡的地方」给 Mayette 后关系破裂；Mayette 哲学 101 课上周被退；Mayette 桌上有萨特、伯纳德·威廉斯、列维纳斯等人文教材。
- 夫妇俩伪装奖学金委员会人员进入哲学 101 教室，被教授识破赶出（课上提及萨特戏剧「这里是有出口的」）。
- 课后学生透露 Mayette 与哲学助教 Isaac（艾萨克）有冲突，因为 Isaac 把她的论文未细读就上交，被发现是用 ChatGPT 生成后 Isaac 反过来责骂她。
- 笔迹比对显示匿名威胁纸条并非 Isaac 所写。
- 在宿舍内发现 PL 3000 型窃听器（有效距离约 200 米）已失效一周。在间谍用品店确认几周前现金卖给一名 20 多岁、穿格子衬衫+Blundstones 的大学篮球运动员，包上有印号（球员专属）。
- 潜入大学体育馆更衣室堵人，锁定篮球队员 Connor Carmine（康纳·卡迈恩），他当场被女队友（Ozzy 女友）当场戳穿假借探望「纳奈莫生病阿姨」的外出谎言并追逐。
- 康纳在审讯中承认他用窃听器监视 Mayette，自称属于「灵魂伴侣协会」（Soul Double Society）的单身学生约会俱乐部，领袖是 Grace（格蕾丝）；他坚称只按 Grace 指示「不惜一切代价」行事，从未想过伤害 Mayette，本人行踪由球队上午团建 alibi 核实（开 PL 3000 接收端的号码背包证实）。后续确认 Grace 把 Mayette 与康纳配对后，Mayette 拒绝康纳，因为她已有男朋友 Ethan（伊森，姓不详）。
- Ethan 是维多利亚市某唱片店员工，迷上「采菇」（mushroom foraging），网名「ImAFunGi」，最近连旷两班、手机不通；账号最后一张动态是和 Mayette 在铜溪公园停车场的自拍，正是二人最后被目击地点。
- Raine 被派卧底假扮希腊留学生「Minerva Perapolski」参加俱乐部举办的单身泳池派对。Grace 在台上发表「灵魂伴侣」「眼睛是灵魂之窗」「眼神交流即灵魂交流」之类说辞；Raine 与忠实会员 Guy（盖伊）接触，得知 Mayette 当众指控 Grace 是骗子、并打算向学校举报她以孤独学生敛财后，被俱乐部屏蔽；Grace 还拒绝放人，认定 Mayette 不能拒绝「她的灵魂伴侣」。Raine 在派对上同时被 Grace 配对给一位女生 Isobel（伊莎贝尔）。
- Will（威尔）用挪威吊床（Norwegian sling）哄 Declan 午睡，趁机对候选人 Celeste 实战观察——她是法国互惠生背景的幼教学位者，会乐器、会可食用彩泥配方，让 Raine 后续调侃「挖墙脚一个潜在的冯·特拉普家族成员」。
- 夫妇俩向肖紫再次汇报，提到失踪学生已增至两人、校园疑似邪教。肖紫主动升级态度：她会通知家属并向值班警长（staff sergeant）申请搜救队。
- 二人前往铜溪公园采菇露营区搜索（省立公园内采菇违法，但大家都是「on the DL」偷采）。Shade 分享自己曾为采松茸离网六周、单日一千刀现金收益，但「抢别人的存货比采新菇容易」；同时警告剧毒「death cap 毒伞鹅膏」可致肝肾衰竭（R.I.P., Mort）。
- 林中找到弹壳、血迹拖拽痕迹，定位一处藏身处，发现 Mayette 与受伤的 Ethan。持枪绑匪穿着袜子套靴以避脚印，命二人带他们进入林深处——意图让他们吃下 death caps 伪装成采菇人误食而死。
- 绑匪交代：欠恶人债，本想靠抢 Mayette/Ethan 的松茸存货赚钱还债，结果开了一枪后恶人接管他的「生意」，如果他停手就死。
- Shade 故意抢过毒蘑菇假装吞下，并偷偷调包换上 Ethan 包里的可食菇，骗过绑匪后将其制服。Angie 在听到毒蘑菇前已经濒临崩溃，一度逼问 Shade「求你别丢下我」、「我等了一辈子才等到你」、「我是你的灵魂伴侣吗？」；事后 Shade 透露他在 Ethan 包里调包，Angie 威胁「要和你离婚，还要拿走一切」。

Aftermath

- Mayette 与 Ethan 被救出送医；案件解决。Angie 与 Lauren 修复关系，Mayette 才知 Lauren 当初很担心她。
- Raine 正式加入团队并开价：每周 20 小时、期中/期末 1.5 倍薪、要零食；夫妇俩以「freelance、按需接单+零食」成交。
- 回家后发现 Will 制作的「保姆手册」（拍嗝顺时针、三文鱼泥加梨、备用《十个小脚趾》等条目），夫妇俩顺势正式邀请 Will 担任 Declan 的全职 manny（男保姆）。Will 泪目接下，说「能帮你们抚养这只小山雀（chickadee）是我的荣幸」，并把赢来的 nanny Celeste 退掉。
- 结尾暗示 Shade 对 Celtic/Blundstones/羊毛风造型重新有好感。

## S01E05

# Private Eyes West Coast — S01E05 Summary

## Episode Plot
Shade and Angie take on a new client, **Sona**, whose mother **Ida** died six years ago in what police ruled carbon-monoxide poisoning. Sona is certain her mother's boyfriend **Mike Marantz** murdered her for her $3.1M estate. The investigation leads the pair to **Tofino**, where Mike now operates under the alias **Mike Mason** as a surf instructor at the **Wildsurf Inn**. Shade and Angie go undercover — Shade as "Patrick Reeves" (a *Point Break* riff) and Angie as "Dolores" — to infiltrate Mike's circle, earn his trust, and extract a confession. Complications arise when a second private investigator, **Vincent Jabeur** (from Seattle), is also tracking Mike for a different client; Vincent is later found dead on the beach. The true killer is revealed to be **Odessa** (a.k.a. Adrian), Mike's long-time accomplice and romantic partner, who murdered Ida out of jealousy when Mike genuinely fell for her, and killed Vincent when he confronted her. Mike is arrested as an accessory; Odessa is charged with both murders. Sona gets closure and adopts Mike's Bernese Mountain dog, **Elvis**.

## Important Character Actions
- **Shade & Angie**: Run a three-phase op — locate Mike, befriend him, extract confession. They split up temporarily (Shade surfs with Mike; Angie bonds with Odessa) — a first for the married duo.
- **Shade**: Bonds with Mike over hockey (recognized as "The Shadow"), shares a fabricated "smear campaign" story to mirror Mike's criminal past, and ultimately goes "bad cop" in the sauna to pressure a confession.
- **Angie**: Builds rapport with Odessa, spots the tarot "Wheel of Fortune" card missing from Odessa's deck (matching Ida's reading), and deduces Odessa's guilt. Physically subdues Odessa at the climax.
- **Will**: Babysits Declan; debates "kind lies" vs. brutal honesty with Raine. Misses Declan's first steps but later witnesses the "seconds."
- **Raine**: Runs facial recognition across exotic retreat photos, identifies Mike's aliases (Marantz → Mason → Martin), finds vet records linking Mike to the Wildsurf Inn, and uncovers Odessa at every Endless Summer location — confirming her as accomplice.
- **Vincent Jabeur**: Seattle P.I. hired by another of Mike's victims; confronts Odessa, gets killed (pushed, hit head on rock).
- **Mike Marantz / Mason / Martin**: Serial romance scammer (5+ victims). Confesses his MO in the sauna — worked with Odessa; fell for a mark (Ida), triggering Odessa's jealousy. Shows genuine remorse for Ida.
- **Odessa / Adrian**: Mike's partner-in-crime and lover. Killed Ida by turning on gas while Mike was at a Blue Rodeo concert (alibi). Killed Vincent to cover tracks. Arrested.

## S01E06

# S01E06 Summary — Private Eyes West Coast

## Episode Plot
George Xiao plans to propose to girlfriend Colette using a Ming/Qing Dynasty heirloom ring (insured $10k) hidden inside a nested box: velvet box → red-wax-sealed gift box (custom “双喜” stamp) → chocolate egg. He delivers the sealed egg to Marée Argentée restaurant at noon Saturday; by 8 PM dessert service, the ring is gone. Matt and Angie take the case to earn Violet’s trust as a police contact. The theft window narrows to 4–6 PM when pastry chef Denise encased the egg. Fingerprint from the wax seal (via Raine’s 80%-accuracy app) matches Ernie Dodd — Colette’s father, the “Mattress King” (Dodd Furniture empire, cheesy space commercials). But George reveals he *already has the ring*; he postponed the proposal when Colette got sick. The real theft occurred earlier, from Ernie’s house — someone opened and resealed the box with the one-of-a-kind stamp.
