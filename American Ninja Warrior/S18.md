## S18E08

### 剧情概述

- 中部赛区资格赛第二晚在拉斯维加斯举行。六道障碍为收缩台阶、滑油闪电、多米诺效应、旋转咖啡杯和立方阵，前二十名晋级下周地区决赛。
- 本晚共有15人完成赛道。萨姆·福尔瑟姆以56.15秒取得最快成绩；前五名将在地区决赛首轮轮空。
- 巨型斜墙本晚无人征服，因此没有人赢得一万美元奖金。

### 重要人物与行动

- 玛吉·欧文在第六个参赛赛季首次按响蜂鸣器。她克服了曾在新秀赛季淘汰自己的旋转咖啡杯，并在最后一次机会登上斜墙。
- KK·安德鲁斯以15岁之龄完成赛道。她是全国顶尖撑杆跳高选手，曾因过度训练背部两处骨折，如今以“KK the Kourse Krusher”之名崭露头角。
- 海斯·戴尔完成个人首秀并按响蜂鸣器，延续因伤缺席的哥哥欧文·戴尔（“Flamingo Kid”）的家族传统。
- 斯潘塞·约翰逊在离开多年后终于取得职业生涯首个蜂鸣器。他创办“Change Course”项目，为曾入狱、无家可归或受毒瘾困扰的人提供重新开始的机会。
- 杰伊·“Lache”·刘易斯取得连续第六个资格赛蜂鸣器，并以领先15秒的成绩暂居最快。之后再次挑战巨型斜墙，却仍未成功。
- 萨姆·福尔瑟姆击败杰伊的成绩，赢得主持人马特的赛季预测竞赛；他与妻子伊莎贝拉曾获得最近一届情侣锦标赛冠军。
- 梅根·马丁离开五年、组建家庭后回归。她闯到第六道障碍并晋级地区决赛，但在最后冲击斜墙时未能按响蜂鸣器。
- NBC队长杰米·拉恩宣布参赛生涯告终。他带着儿子齐吉完成告别挑战，在旋转咖啡杯落水；这是他连续参赛16季后可能的最后一次出场。
- 艾登·霍利只训练了一个月便闯到立方阵，凭借社交媒体特技经验完成强势首秀，但因握力耗尽落水。
- 伊莱贾·“Boss”·布朗宁在立方阵失误，未能延续去年资格赛最快成绩；弟弟朱利安则在第二道障碍出局。
- 加比·斯蒂芬和梅雷迪思·金代表女性新秀表现突出。梅雷迪思抵达斜墙并确保晋级；加比在斜墙前失误。

- “Tripleheader”：三重赛，下周地区决赛使用的三赛道竞速形式。
- “KK the Kourse Krusher”：KK·安德鲁斯的昵称，意为“KK赛道碾压者”。
- “Main Stage Michael”：迈克尔·瓦西列维奇的昵称。他是音乐剧演员，闯到第五道障碍立方阵。
- “Sky Ninja”：杰斯·旺布尔斯的昵称；她热爱主题公园并希望成为主题公园设计师。
- “Big Cat”：卡斯滕·威廉姆斯的昵称。他完成第十二个蜂鸣器，并第八次进入全国总决赛。
- “Change Course”：斯潘塞·约翰逊创办的公益项目，核心口号是“One team, one dream”（一个团队，一个梦想）。
- “Dyer Situation”：戴尔兄弟家族的戏称，海斯用个人蜂鸣器证明自己能延续哥哥欧文的成绩。
- “Merica”：梅雷迪思·金的昵称，因她出生于美国独立日7月4日。

### 连续性与后续参考

- 本晚晋级者包括梅根·马丁、伊莱贾·布朗宁和艾登·霍利等未完成全程的选手。

Episode S18E10 Summary

Plot Overview

This is the opening night of the American Ninja Warrior National Finals in Las Vegas. All 60 finalists (20 per region) are seeded from a Tripleheader race and placed into groups of four. Each group runs a round-robin: three races per finalist, top two per group advance, and the fastest third-place finisher earns a wild card. The top two finishers face do-or-die races in a 32-person bracket until a champion is crowned. The top four women by obstacles completed all season will later race for a Women's Champion title. Racers compete side-by-side on one of two twin-lane courses, with the second course introducing two new obstacles: Sky Hooks (a longer safer route or a risky reverse-hook shortcut) and Beehive (sideways laches with a 270-degree spin). The shared final obstacle is the iconic Hourglass Drop, a notorious test that has defeated roughly 70% of competitors historically, and is described as a "do or die" test of precision and grip.

Key Competitor Arcs

Group 1 (Isaiah Thomas, Daniel Gil, RJ Roman, Grace Gordon): The "deepest foursome" loaded with superstars.
- Daniel Gil (11-year veteran, 33 career buzzers entering the night) opens with a comeback win over RJ Roman on Hourglass Drop to earn his 34th career buzzer, moving one behind Joe Moravsky's all-time record.
- Isaiah "Flyboy" Thomas (6'2" Florida favorite, last year's final four) cruises past Grace Gordon, then crushes Gil on Cane Lane with a buzzer in 43 seconds, the night's fastest time, to go 2-0.
- RJ Roman (last year's wild-card act) loses his opener like in season 17, then survives a coaching vs. student duel against his former Florida student Grace Gordon with a buzzer. In the final race, RJ runs a sub-51 second time that beats Gil's time and temporarily keeps his season alive.
- Gil's season hangs by a thread: he needs to beat Gordon, gets the win on Cane Lane, but his dismount on Hourglass Drop fails. Gil survives only because Gordon also misses the Hourglass Drop, making his survival feel like "salvation in Sin City."

Group 2 (Jackson Erdos, Ashton Myler, Isabella Folsom, Daniella Blanchard):
- Myler (Ninja Kidz member, 29th seed) shocks number-two seed Erdos in round two, falling on Hourglass Drop but securing the win, then breaks the buzzer literally in round three.
- Isabella Folsom (last year's final 32, trained in the family barn with husband Sam and brother-in-law Phil) stages a comeback by beating Blanchard in round two and surviving her Hourglass Drop fear.
- Erdos, after two shocking falls, needs a super fast finish in round three against Blanchard and delivers with a buzzer, beating RJ's 50.75-second time by about one second. His wild-card hopes depend on the remaining two weeks of group competition.
- Folsom makes history as the first woman ever to go undefeated in her group, finishing with 32 obstacles completed.

Group 3 (Ryan Cooper, Nathanael Honvou, Nam Phan, Anna McArthur):
- Cooper ("Ninja Coop," Texas high schooler, former "Ninja Junior" competitor) and Honvou (Ohio teen, former "Ninja Junior" champion) are best friends living 1,000+ miles apart. Honvou is 7 inches taller and 60 pounds heavier.
- Cooper wins their round-one race by less than half a second in a photo finish (iron sharpens iron), then dominates Phan in round two.
- Honvou wins his opener against McArthur with barely making the grab, then dominates McArthur again in round three to finish 2-1. McArthur becomes the first woman to conquer Hourglass Drop.
- Cooper and Honvou both advance 3-0 from this group.

Group 4 (Noah Meunier, Anabella Heinrichs, Nick Warren, Simeon Serrano):
- Meunier (last year's runner-up, holds record for most consecutive obstacles) shocks the group by taking the safe route on Sky Hooks in round one and falling on Hourglass Drop, giving Heinrichs an opening. He then loses in round two when his risky shortcut attempt on Sky Hooks backfires.
- Heinrichs (Colorado hairstylist, third straight finals, back-to-back qualifying buzzers, only woman in her group) earns a buzzer win over Serrano, learning to skip the swing on Hourglass Drop. She later falls to Nick Warren. Meunier and Warren advance.
- Nick Warren (high school football linebacker rookie) stays perfect all night with two buzzer-beating wins.
- Meunier survives round three, getting his first buzzer of the night to finish 2-1.

Group 5 (Phil Folsom, Kaden Lebsack, Bob Reese, Colt Elder):
- Phil Folsom (number-one seed, rookie, Folsom family dynasty) takes the risky Sky Hooks route to edge two-time Last Ninja Standing Kaden Lebsack in a dead heat on Hourglass Drop in round one. Folsom goes 3-0 on the night.
- Bob Reese (South Carolina social media star with millions of followers, has a backyard extreme sports park, Instagram: @bobreesecookiemonster) pulls off a front flip onto the Slide Surfer in round one, loses his lead, but wins when Elder falls on Cane Lane. In his second race, he attempts a Beehive flip, and in round three, he goes for a back flip, but his season ends.
- Colt Elder (Virginia chicken farmer, "Colt the Bolt") runs the fastest time of his life in the final race, even jumping ahead of Lebsack on Sky Hooks, but his stumble on the dismount gives Lebsack a 0.14-second win.

Group 6 (Enzo DeFerrari Wilson, Max Feinberg, Nacssa Garemore, Zay Keaton):
- Florida training partners Wilson and Garemore (met at age 9) put on the two fastest times on the course in their round-two race, where Wilson hits his head on the wall, giving Garemore the win.
- Garemore goes 3-0 and tops the group. Wilson finishes 2-1, using his time tiebreaker to advance over Feinberg.
- Max Feinberg (Colorado, University of Denver grad, AI engineer) and Zay Keaton (LA stuntman) are both eliminated after 0-2 and 0-3 nights respectively.
- Feinberg finishes as the top third-place finisher on this course with a sub-minute time, boosting his wild-card hopes.

## S18E11

# American Ninja Warrior S18E11 — Night Two of the National Finals

## Episode Plot

Hosts Matt Iseman and Akbar Gbajabiamila host night two of the all-racing National Finals in Las Vegas. Sixty competitors are seeded into four-person round-robin groups; top two from each group advance to a final-32 bracket starting next week. Groups 7–9 race on Course 1, Groups 10–12 on Course 2. The Hourglass Drop trampoline transfer is the recurring decision-maker.

Course 1 obstacles: Slide Surfer, Catch and Release, Swinging Peg Boards, Salmon Ladder, Cane Lane, Hourglass Drop.
Course 2 obstacles: Slide Surfer, Sky Hooks (shorter safe route vs. risky shortcut with reverse hook), Beehive (sideways laches with mid-spin), Swinging Peg Boards, Salmon Ladder, Cane Lane, Hourglass Drop.

## Important Character Actions

Group 7 (Course 1)
- Addy Herman beats Luke Beckstrand after Luke falls on Cane Lane; she later beats a hurt Nick Hanson and her own first coach Lucas Reale, going 3-0.
- Luke Beckstrand rebounds with a 48-second win over Hanson, securing his spot; Lucas Reale is eliminated.
- Nick Hanson pulls an abdominal muscle on Swinging Peg Boards in race one, plays through all three races, and finishes the course in race three for a feel-good moment.

Group 8 (Course 1)
- Jay "Lache" Lewis beats rookie Akshara Pappu ("Snack-shara") and Olivia Lopez; takes a fall on Hourglass in one race then secures his spot with a fast finish.
- Elijah "Boss" Browning (Tennessee, 6'2") beats Olivia Lopez via reach advantage, then clinches the group with a buzzer against Pappu.
- Olivia Lopez finally nails Hourglass Drop in her third try.

Group 9 (Course 1)
