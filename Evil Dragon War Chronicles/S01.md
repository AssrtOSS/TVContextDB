## S01E02

## Episode S01E02 Summary

Episode Plot

The episode opens on backstory narration: a monk named Hoi Hung (会雄) perfected dark magic at Goh-Yeh mountain (御岳山), reached the 46th level of power, became a paragon of the black arts, and passed his knowledge to disciples as a guarded secret. The master ritual — the Rising Dragon Ceremony (升龙仪式) — is known only to the abbots of Goh-Yeh. Someone pledges to the ancestor to perfect the ritual using "the stone."

In the present, the enemy has set up a front company in Shinjuku (新宿). Mukori is placed in command of the Night Stalkers (夜行者) and orders a spell to lure all the great beasts out of hiding; capturing the Chee-Shek alone is enough, since the other beasts will come to rescue her. The abbot's side — an old abbot (住持) plus disciples Ura (浦) and Kinshu (金秋) — learn that Hue/Koitchi is still alive and his presence is at the Double Towers (双塔). Shiho (诗穗), still recovering, can sense the White Tiger's presence and leads them there.

Ura and Kinshu arrive (comedic "long time no see" entrance), reveal they were sent by their teacher to help disrupt the spell, and dispatch enemies. The abbot and Ura exchange history: over eighteen years ago the Night Stalkers tried to kill Ura, and Ura brought the ten-year-old Koitchi to the abbot. Hue/Koitchi is found but is not in control — apparently manipulated (Green Dragon is feared to be behind it). Mukori subdues him, reveals herself as a woman and the Night Stalkers' leader, and challenges the White Tiger to a duel. Shiho is captured. Ura and Kinshu kill a suffering woman who might have been a lead to Shiho, drawing the abbot's anger. Koitchi recovers, calls the abbot "Grandfather," fights one-on-one, and extracts the ritual details: tonight, in Shinjuku. The episode ends with the group departing — Koitchi taking the abbot's place in the car.

Important Character Actions

- The abbot (住持): guardian of Koitchi, repeatedly corrected from "Brother"; uses pressure points (穴位) on the possessed Hue/Koitchi; complains of arthritis; calls the dead woman's rite.
- Koitchi / Hue-san (恭一 / 会雄): the White Tiger, an orphan raised by the abbot since childhood; found alive but controlled and subdued; recovers, urges rescue of Shiho, interrogates a captive, learns the ritual time and place.
- Shiho / Shihu (诗穗): recovering; senses the White Tiger; captured by the enemy.
- Mukori (千代伎美鬼): newly appointed leader of the Night Stalkers, revealed to be a woman; manipulates Hue/Koitchi and challenges the White Tiger; escapes.
- Ura (浦) and Kinshu (金秋): comic-relief disciples sent by their teacher to disrupt the spell; kill a woman who could have located Shiho.

Newly Established Names and Terms

- Goh-Yeh mountain (御岳山): seat where Hoi Hung mastered dark magic and home of the abbots.
- The Rising Dragon Ceremony (升龙仪式): the master ritual, abbot-only knowledge.
- Shinjuku (新宿): enemy front company and ritual site.
- Double Towers (双塔): where the White Tiger's presence is sensed.
- Chee-Shek (契石): designation applied to Shiho; the subtitle ties it to sensing the White Tiger, while the series context identifies Shiho as the Vermilion Bird holder — treat this label as uncertain.
- Night Stalkers (夜行者): enemy organization, now led by Mukori.
- The stone: item central to completing the ritual.
- Green Dragon (青龙): suspected of controlling Hue/Koitchi.

Continuity-Relevant Facts

- The White Tiger (Koitchi/Hue) is an orphan, short-tempered by nature, raised by the abbot (guardian) for 15+ years; Ura brought him to the abbot at age ten, eighteen years ago.
- The enemy goal is to lure all four great beasts out of hiding; capturing the Chee-Shek alone suffices as bait.
- The ritual is set for tonight in Shinjuku — the likely focus of the next episode.
- Hue/Koitchi was alive but not in control, with "no trace of Koitchi left" while subdued; Green Dragon manipulation is feared.
- Front-company operations in Shinjuku are the enemy's cover.

Jokes and References Useful for Later Episodes

- Recurring correction gag: calling the abbot "Brother" instead of "Abbot" ("That's abbot, remember").
- The abbot's old-man gags: arthritis acting up; "don't get so worked up, at your age."
- Ura and Kinshu's "comedy act" / "long time no see" entrance and their "we'll finish our game some other day" banter with Mukori.
- Running complaint: "I never get the easy stuff, I always end up doing the dirty work."
- "Don't get in another accident" / "Don't be stupid" — and "Don't dawdle, old man."

Unresolved Threads

- Shiho's capture and rescue — the central cliffhanger.
- Who was the suffering woman Ura and Kinshu killed, and was she truly the lead to Shiho?
- The Rising Dragon Ceremony in Shinjuku tonight and the role of "the stone" / the ancestor's plan.
- Whether Green Dragon is controlling Koitchi, and the state of his consciousness.
- Mukori's identity as a woman and her connection to the Night Stalkers/ancestor.
- The cry "Brother… save me! Save me!" from a dying figure — whose brother, and what it implies.

Translation caveat: the subtitles render the central male figure inconsistently as "Hue-san," "Hue Koitchi," and "Koitchi" (会雄 / 会恭一 / 恭一), and label Shiho as "Chee-Shek" despite the context identifying her as the Vermilion Bird holder. These should be verified against the original before being treated as canon.

## S01E03

Episode Plot
The villain's servant Mokuri reports success, and the villain (Green Dragon) prepares a ritual to fuse himself with Shiho, the reincarnation of the woman he loved 1,300 years ago, when magic ruled the country. Koichi (White Tiger), his grandfather/the abbot, and the monk Ura infiltrate the compound during the ritual preparations, planning to attack once the ceremony begins. Inside, the villain declares Shiho his wife and claims her soul has already been absorbed by him. Koichi awakens as the White Tiger and names Green Dragon his mortal enemy, sworn rivals across seven lifetimes in which the White Tiger always lost and failed to protect her. Ura stays behind to hold off enemies and urges Kinshu to save Shiho, but Kinshu is killed. Ura arrives seeking "the stone," only to learn it was destroyed 18 years ago to escape its power's influence; the villain settles for killing him. During the fight, Koichi refuses to give up for Shiho's sake. Shiho intervenes, pleading for them to stop, then tells Ura, "Thank you, Hue-san. I wanted to be with you. Farewell," before dying. Koichi vows they will meet in the next life, and the episode ends on a blank screen.

Important Character Actions
- Koichi: insists on rescuing Shiho over the mission, awakens as the White Tiger, fights Green Dragon, vows to change destiny.
- Grandpa/abbot: joins the infiltration, shouts during the battle, is told to stay put "until the show's over."
- Ura: scouts ahead, covers Koichi's entry, is beaten by the villain's servant, arrives late seeking the stone, and is present when Shiho dies.
- Kinshu: the villain's servant (or a captive); told to flee and save Shiho, is killed during the battle ("Kill her, Ura!… I'm finished!").
- Shiho: resists the villain's claim that she is his wife, has her soul absorbed, briefly breaks free to stop the fight, bids Ura farewell, and dies.
- Green Dragon: completes the ritual fusion with Shiho, fights Koichi, dismisses "brute force," and waits 18 years for a rematch.

Newly Established Names/Terms
- Green Dragon (青龙): the villain, Koichi's mortal enemy; "Chee Shek" in the English subs is Shiho (诗穗/詩穂).
- Mokuri (木利): the villain's servant.
- Kinshu (金秀): killed in the episode.
- Ura / Hue-san (浦): the monk who aids Koichi.
- White Tiger (白虎): Koichi's spirit beast; Shiho is the Vermilion Bird (朱雀) holder per the context skill; grandpa is tied to Black Tortoise (玄武).
- Seven lifetimes: the length of the White Tiger–Green Dragon rivalry.
- "The stone": an object destroyed 18 years ago; Ura hoped to recover it.

Continuity-Relevant Facts
- 1,300 years ago, magic controlled the nation's fate and innocents were powerless pawns; the villains seek to re-establish that power.
- The ritual would make the villain "completely inhuman."
- The stone was destroyed 18 years ago because its power could not be used / to escape its influence; the villain has waited 18 years for Koichi to reappear.
- Shiho dies at the end of the episode; Koichi says they will meet in the next life.
- English-subtitle spellings vary (Koitchi, Chee Shek, Shihu, Hue-san); per the context skill, do not merge names based on appearance alone.

Jokes / References Useful Later
- Ura's "What a pain! That guy's so stubborn" and Koichi's "You monks only know liturgy and ritual; how could you understand the love of man and woman?" establish the monks' comic-reluctant dynamic.
- Grandpa's "Be a good boy and stay there until the show's over!" is an oddly modern, fourth-wall-adjacent line.
- "See ya." is a casual sign-off before the final fight.
- Shiho's "we'll be together in the next life" sets up possible reincarnation payoff, though this is the final OVA episode.

Unresolved Threads
- Whether Green Dragon is actually defeated — the episode ends mid-aftermath with Shiho's death and a blank screen.
- The fate of Shiho's soul after being absorbed and then "going together" with the villain.
- The destroyed stone — with it gone, Ura's objective is unreachable, leaving any seal or power tied to it unresolved.

## S01E01

## Episode Plot

**S01E01** opens at the **Shrine of the Spirit Isle (灵岛圣祠)** where the 70th keeper receives a report: a monk has escaped, and the search for **Chee Shek (朱雀, Suzaku/朱雀)** — a girl bearing the southern sacred beast's power — continues. The keeper identifies **Hue Koitchi (比勇恭一 / Hiiragi Kyōichi)**, a rising ceramic artist exhibiting in Shinjuku, as the **White Tiger (白虎, Byakko/白虎)** of the west.

Hue meets **Shiho (诗穗 / Shihō)** at his exhibition; she feels a calming resonance with his work. They arrange dinner at her home in the **Double Towers (双子塔)** near an old shrine. Hue's grandfather — the **abbot (住持)** of a temple — intercepts him, revealing their shared lineage: Hue is White Tiger, the abbot is **Phoenix (凤凰, Hōō/鳳凰)** of the east, Shiho must be Suzaku of the south, and the north belongs to the **Spirit Isle cult (灵岛邪教)** / **Night Stalkers (夜行者)** led by **Green Dragon (青龙, Seiryū/青龍)** — identified as **Sakaki (榊)**.

That night, Sakaki attacks the Double Towers, kidnapping Shiho for a ritual. Hue rushes to save her, battles Sakaki, and realizes both are "spawn of the great beasts." Sakaki unveils Shiho's true form as Suzaku. Hue shields her with his life; the episode ends with Hue mortally wounded, Shiho screaming his name, and the abbot arriving too late.

---

## Important Character Actions

- **Hue Koitchi (比勇恭一)**: Ceramic artist; learns he is White Tiger; rushes alone into enemy territory to rescue Shiho; sacrifices himself to protect her.
- **Shiho (诗穗)**: Girl drawn to Hue's art; unaware she is Suzaku; her awakening triggers the climax.
- **Abbot / Grandfather (住持 / 爷爷)**: 70th keeper of the Phoenix line; Hue's grandfather; reveals the Four Gods framework and the 18-year conflict; arrives at the end.
- **Sakaki (榊)**: Green Dragon / cult enforcer; fought the abbot 18 years ago; kidnaps Shiho for the ritual; kills Hue.
- **Spirit Isle Cult Servants**: Execute the keeper's orders; track the White Tiger and Suzaku.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- **18-year gap**: The abbot and Sakaki clashed 18 years ago; the cult has been "recovering strength" since.
- **Lineage**: Hue is the abbot's grandson; both are practitioners of the light arts (玄道).
- **Ritual goal**: The cult seeks to gather all four sacred beast bearers for a resurrection ceremony (ancestor revival).
- **Power manifestation**: Hue's White Tiger power surfaces under duress; Shiho's Suzaku awakening is forced by Sakaki.
- **Location anchor**: Double Towers + old shrine = operational base for the final confrontation.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- Hue's self-deprecating "artist's suffering" when Shiho says she came for his sculptures, not him.
- The abbot insisting on "abbot" not "grandfather," then immediately hitting Hue — a recurring dynamic.
- "Stars are beautiful" quiet moment between grandfather and grandson before the battle.
- Sakaki's line: "It won't be like 18 years ago" — callbacks guaranteed.

## Unresolved Threads

1. **Hue's fate**: Mortally wounded; unclear if death is permanent or if White Tiger power can revive him.
2. **Shiho's captivity**: Taken by Sakaki for the ritual; her Suzaku power just awakened.
3. **The ritual**: Timing, location, and full purpose (ancestor resurrection) not yet shown.
4. **Black Tortoise (玄武, North/Water)**: The fourth beast bearer is unaccounted for — possibly the escaped monk or another hidden character.
5. **Abbot's next move**: Arrived alone; no backup shown; must rescue Shiho and confront Sakaki.
6. **Cult hierarchy**: Only the 70th keeper and Sakaki seen; deeper leadership unknown.
