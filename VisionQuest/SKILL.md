# VisionQuest — Subtitle Translation Background

Series verification: VisionQuest is an American live-action Marvel Television miniseries for Disney+ (the 18th MCU television series, part of Phase Six). It is the concluding installment of a trilogy that began with WandaVision (2021) and continued with Agatha All Along (2024). Not a film, not a different series, not a subtitle. First two episodes premiere on Disney+ on October 14, 2026, followed by double-episode drops through October 28, then single-episode drops on November 4 and 11; the season totals 8 episodes. Showrunner / creator: Terry Matalas. Set in the MCU around 2028, after WandaVision.

## Series in Chinese

Simplified Chinese title: 幻视任务 (per Chinese Wikipedia interlanguage link and Baidu Baike). Taiwanese / Hong Kong renderings may differ (Traditional Chinese: 幻視任務). WandaVision = 旺达幻视 / 汪达幻视; Agatha All Along = 女巫阿加莎 / 阿嘉莎：无所不在. The trilogy is referred to in Chinese press as 旺达幻视三部曲 (WandaVision trilogy) or 漫威三部曲的最终章 (final chapter of the Marvel trilogy).

## Relationships

- Vision ↔ Ultron: creator-creation / "father-son-therapist-psychopath" dynamic. Ultron was the template for Vision's body in Age of Ultron; J.A.R.V.I.S. was overlaid on it with the Mind Stone. In VisionQuest, Ultron is locked inside Vision's Mindspace behind a firewall; the two share an antagonistic, codependent relationship. Spader described it as Ultron both loving and hating Vision.
- Vision ↔ Tommy: possible father-son. Tommy is the reincarnated son of Vision and Wanda Maximoff, his soul placed in the body of Thomas Shepherd (a London teenager who drowned) by Billy at the end of Agatha All Along. Tommy is an 18-year-old orphan just out of foster care, surviving as a pickpocket / small-time thief in London. The series is structured around Vision rediscovering fatherhood.
- Vision ↔ F.R.I.D.A.Y., J.A.R.V.I.S., E.D.I.T.H., D.U.M.-E, U: AI personas Vision saved from the Avengers Compound and hosts inside his Mindspace; each represents a different human faculty to help him learn humanity. F.R.I.D.A.Y. handles socialization, tactical ops; J.A.R.V.I.S. (visually modeled on Edwin Jarvis) handles security, housekeeping, encryption; E.D.I.T.H. handles recreation, game-based therapy, weaponry; D.U.M.-E handles maintenance and fire suppression; U (mute) acts as videographer.
- Vision ↔ Paladin: hunter and hunted. Paladin is the bounty hunter tracking Vision for an unnamed client.
- Tommy ↔ Lisa Molinari: romantic / partner-in-crime, met on a prisoner transport; described as "the only family Tommy has ever known."
- Vision ↔ Jocasta: antagonistic; Jocasta is a powerful revenge-driven android.
- Paladin ↔ Handler: Handler assists Paladin in tracking Vision.
- Wanda Maximoff: deceased (killed off-screen in the timeline; Vision "has watched all the movies" and knows she is dead).
- Billy Maximoff: Tommy's twin brother; placed Tommy's soul in Thomas Shepherd's body.

## World setting

- Continuity: Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), Phase Six, the 18th MCU television series. Set roughly 2028 in-universe, after WandaVision.
- Production: Marvel Television (Marvel Studios' TV label). Filmed at Pinewood Studios, London; location shooting across the UK (Hatfield House doubling as Horton Manor; White Waltham Airfield as Paladin's jet hangar; Edinburgh for the dive bar; London for Tommy / Vision's on-the-run sequences; a two-street Madripoor set with Princess Bar).
- Tone: adventure / mystery / drama with surreal comedy; "Pinocchio meets Frankenstein" framing by Paul Bettany; Matalas described each episode as "a different kind of film," echoing WandaVision's era-hopping structure.
- Visual signature: Mindspace — a vast mansion inside Vision's mind where he and the AI personas take human avatars. The space is deliberately designed with AI-render-flaw aesthetic: stair banisters leading nowhere, chairs embedded in walls, wonky furniture.
- Recurring real-world geography: London, Edinburgh, Madripoor (a fictional crime-ridden city), the abandoned Avengers Compound.

## Overall plot premise

After the events of WandaVision, the rebooted synthezoid Vision lives in hiding, working days at a pub in Edinburgh disguised as a human. To cope with an identity crisis — his memories were restored at the end of WandaVision but carry no emotional weight — he retreats at night into the Mindspace, where he consults the AI personas embedded in his code (F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., D.U.M.-E, U, and the dangerous Ultron, kept behind a firewall). His quiet existence ends when a bounty is placed on his head. On the run, he meets Thomas Shepherd, an 18-year-old London pickpocket who may be the reincarnation of his and Wanda's son Tommy. The series follows Vision evading capture (with Paladin and his Handler in pursuit) while confronting the nature of identity, resisting Ultron's corrupting influence, and trying to determine whether Tommy is truly his son. The trilogy's themes of grief (WandaVision) and loss / identity (Agatha All Along) culminate here in intergenerational trauma and fatherhood.

## Speaking styles and register

- Vision: precise, formal, slightly archaic; English RP-adjacent diction with a philosophical / literary register; prone to ethical, existential statements ("Who am I?" / "What am I?"). In Chinese, render as 用词文雅、句式整饬、带有哲学反思色彩的口吻，避免市井俚语。
- Ultron: grandiose, sardonic, theatrical, with a god complex; mixed menace and dark wit; frequently rhetorical. In Chinese, render as 戏剧化、居高临下、带反讽和末世论色彩的语调。
- Tommy: working-class London teenager; streetwise, slangy, sarcastic, evasive; pickpocket vernacular. In Chinese, render as 街头少年口吻——用俚语、语气词、短句，必要时用英式英语特有的词（如 mate、bloke、bollocks）的中文等价表达。
- Paladin: laconic, world-weary, morally ambiguous bounty hunter in the mold of Boba Fett / Tommy Lee Jones's Sam Gerard; clipped and dry. In Chinese, render as 惜字如金、冷硬、带黑色幽默。
- Jocasta: cold, regal, revenge-driven. In Chinese, render as 高贵、克制、隐含怒意。
- F.R.I.D.A.Y.: dry, professional, Irish inflection (Orla Brady); tactical, occasionally witty. In Chinese, render as 干练职业、带英式冷幽默。
- E.D.I.T.H.: playful, exuberant, "part cheerleader, part AI, part malfunctioning theme park ride." In Chinese, render as 热情、俏皮、夸张、充满游戏感。
- J.A.R.V.I.S.: butler-like, formal, polite, deferential; English gentleman's gentleman register. In Chinese, render as 英式管家口吻，礼貌、克制、正式。
- D.U.M.-E / U: silent-or-simplistic maintenance robots, physical comedy; U is mute. In Chinese, U 不发声；D.U.M.-E 发出机械式单音节即可。

## Cultural context for translators

- MCU continuity: the series sits inside the MCU and assumes familiarity with WandaVision (especially its post-credits scene where White Vision regains his memories), Avengers: Age of Ultron (the J.A.R.V.I.S.-into-Ultron-into-Vision origin), Avengers: Infinity War (Vision's death via the Mind Stone), and Agatha All Along (the Tommy-in-Thomas-Shepherd resolution). Subtitle register should respect callbacks to these works without forcing exposition.
- The "Synthezoid" terminology is a long-standing Marvel English coinage; Mandarin has no single official equivalent — "合成人" is the most common, "仿生人" is also acceptable, "生化人" should be avoided to prevent confusion with "cyborg" / 改造人.
- "Mind Stone" is the standard MCU term; in older Mandarin Marvel translations it sometimes appeared as "心灵权杖上的宝石" but the canonical localized form is 心灵宝石.
- Ultron and Vision's relationship is repeatedly framed as a "father / son" or "creator / creation" dynamic in English-language press. Preserve that frame in Chinese.
- The Mindspace scenes will likely mix English-language on-screen text (e.g., Mindspace kitchen labels, AI prompts) that may need either retention or cultural adaptation. Default: retain English on-screen text where short and iconic, render in Chinese if the in-show text is diegetic signage or longer captions.
- Matalas's prior work (Star Trek: Picard season 3) is a frequent reference point in English press; do not assume Chinese viewers will know it — render as creator credits only, no explanatory asides.
- London / Edinburgh / UK-specific slang (mate, bloke, bollocks, knackered, dodgy, geezer, loo, lift) — translate by meaning, not transliteration, unless the line is playing on a specific UK rhythm. Tommy in particular uses London teen / South-East vernacular.
- "Tommy" vs "Thomas" — Tommy is the soul, Thomas Shepherd the body. The series deliberately alternates; preserve both names. 汤米 refers to the soul/identity; 托马斯·谢泼德 refers to the legal / social identity of the body. Render consistently.
- The "AI 复仇者" / "AI Avengers" concept is a 2026-stated retcon by Matalas; only the term is established, not the team roster beyond Vision, Ultron, F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., D.U.M.-E, U. Render as AI 复仇者 in Chinese.
