## S01E02

# S01E02

## Plot

The episode intercuts Vision's Mindspace with two parallel real-world threads: Tommy/Thomas Shepherd's pre-series backstory, and Vision's confrontation with Paladin.

**Mindspace framing.** After closing time at Fiona's pub, Vision boots up Mindspace. E.D.I.T.H. notes he smiled that day. F.R.I.D.A.Y. and J.A.R.V.I.S. observe that the rebuilt Vision knows the facts of his past life but is searching for the feeling — "a ghost haunting her footsteps." He reframes the Ship of Theseus: both ships are the true ship.

**Tommy's origin.** A flashback shows Thomas Shepherd, 18, on a London court-services prison bus (Christmas music playing — "Jingle Bells," "I've Got Christmas on My Mind") bound for Saint Mabyn's sanitarium. Lisa Molinari reads him perfectly — orphan, foster homes, identity "in flux." He admits to compulsive pickpocketing and Cardiff bragging-rights ("five men, six wallets, two kebabs"). Intercut with his psych eval: held underwater at a pool, a "bolt of lightning," and a second personality (Tommy/Speed) moving into his brain. He describes a "haunted" house with laughing air and pixelated lawns — Westview — and a man with "a deep purple bruise" face and "kind eyes" who called him son. He remembers a sad-eyed mother with "too much love" and "so much pain," and her line: "Grief is just love persevering." A psychiatrist notes only two people were in the room when the original Vision said that to Wanda — Tommy shouldn't know it. Lisa and Tommy crash the emergency exit of the moving bus and run.

**The bounty.** Paladin's voice booms out of the sky into Mindspace: "I have your son." Handler shows Vision old records and the psych eval. Ultron dismisses it as a "sentimental ploy" — perhaps S.W.O.R.D. wanting Vision back as a weapon. Vision finds coordinates embedded in the message — an invitation. Vision meets Paladin at a dive bar where Vision once watched a friend die. Paladin lays out Thomas's record (mother Irene, deceased; father absent; shuttled foster homes; high academic marks; now a street grifter) and theorizes Wanda threw her Westview-conjured son into Thomas's body after the hex ended. Paladin has Tommy already locked in Saint Mabyn's and threatens the "megawatt battery frying his brain into a nightmare omelette of witches and red-faced demons" until Vision surrenders. Vision rejects his old Avenger self ("I'm not Vision") and walks out, breaking Paladin's composure. The mysterious Client — losing confidence — warns Paladin his contract and patience are running out.

**The rescue.** J.A.R.V.I.S. reframes the Ship of Theseus for Vision: when a monster has your son, don't be the ship — be the goddamn king. Vision walks openly into the Saint Mabyn's reception ("Hello, my name's Vision, and I'm here for the trap"). E.D.I.T.H. spots militia inconsistencies (tac-boots, military watches, earpieces). Paladin springs the trap. Vision finds Tommy in the Specialty Patient Wing, restrained and raving ("You're all in my head!"). Vision calms him: "You're safe now." Tommy touches him and whispers, "You're real."

**Escape and sacrifice.** An EMP cage engages. Vision's processors destabilize; Adamantium rounds crack his Vibranium shell. J.A.R.V.I.S. deletes his own code and reroutes processing power to keep the other AIs alive — "Family is forever. And it's worth the fight. Brother." Tommy drags the failing Vision out and refuses to leave him: "I don't know what you are, yeah? But you're my something. I'm not gonna leave you." Mercenaries close in. The episode ends on Ultron's line in the Mindspace: "He didn't listen. Like father, like son."

## Important character actions

- **Vision** chooses to walk into a known trap for a stranger he believes is his son, deliberately drops his "Avenger" identity, and lets J.A.R.V.I.S. sacrifice himself.
- **Tommy/Thomas** actively rescues Vision against Vision's orders, naming him "my something" — first real emotional bond.
- **Lisa Molinari** engineers the bus escape and calls Tommy out by his soul-name ("Tommy") before he corrects her to "Thomas."
- **Paladin** plays a long game: research dossier, psychological pressure, pre-committed hostage at Saint Mabyn's, theater at reception, EMP + Adamantium trap.
- **J.A.R.V.I.S.** self-deletes to preserve the AI Avengers roster.
- **E.D.I.T.H.** functions as Vision's tactical scout in the field, spotting the militia from wardrobe tells.
- **Ultron** plants the closing "like father, like son" warning, openly rejects Wanda's resurrection theory, and treats Tommy's existence as Vision's problem.
- **The Client** (unknown, voice-only) tightens the leash on Paladin.

## Newly established names / terms

- **Saint Mabyn's** — sanitarium/asylum used as Paladin's holding facility; presented as a one-way door.
- **The Butcherbird** — operation / codename covering the Saint Mabyn's capture; also the tabloid nickname for an unrelated Butcherbird-marks killer Tommy reads about on TV.
- **Adamantium rounds** — Paladin's chosen anti-Vision ammunition, capable of compromising Vibranium (first canonical mention in this continuity).
- **Irene** — deceased legal mother of Thomas Shepherd.
- **Specialty Patient Wing** — section of Saint Mabyn's where Tommy is held.
- **AI Avengers** — confirmed in Mindspace dialogue as the in-universe framing for the personas.

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Tommy is alive inside Thomas Shepherd's body; his memories of Wanda's "Grief is just love persevering" are verified as something only the original Vision should have heard.
- Wanda ended Westview; by Vision/Ultron's accounting, "no entity she conjured survived" — making Tommy's persistence an explicit anomaly the show is leaning into.
- Wanda is deceased in this timeline; Vision references her only as past-tense.
- Billy's role in placing Tommy's soul is implied but not directly cited this episode.
- S.W.O.R.D. is floated by Ultron as the likely Client behind the bounty ("When they rebuilt you, they turned you into a weapon").
- Vision's mid-episode declaration "I'm not Vision" marks an identity break from his pre-WandaVision Avengers self that Paladin exploits.
- The Mindspace has an "architecturally and emotionally unstable" aesthetic (chairs in walls, banisters to nowhere), per the show's visual signature.

## Jokes / references useful for later episodes

- **WandaVision / Agatha All Along continuity probes.** "Mazel tov! It's a boy" (the original twins' birth), Westview's pixelated lawns and laughing air, "Wanda's witchy bubble," Witches' Road imagery in Tommy's hallucination ("red-faced demons," "witches"), and the "Grief is just love persevering" beat.
- **Ship of Theseus as running thesis.** Used twice — Vision's wistful frame ("both are the true ship") and J.A.R.V.I.S.'s corrective ("Be the goddamn king"). Likely to recur as Tommy tests whether he's really Wanda's son.
- **Guard's "I don't care if you're Oliver Twist, Hannibal Lecter" speech** — a UK pop-culture meter that pays off a recurring class/orphan motif.
- **"Jingle bells, I knew I'd see you again"** — Paladin's greeting to Tommy at Saint Mabyn's; plants the Christmas setting for this thread and a callback lever for later episodes.
- **"Control-alt-delete, goodbye Tommy"** — Paladin's threat phrasing is dry tech-noir patter that contrasts with his grim setting; useful tonal shorthand.
- **"Like father, like son"** — Ultron closes on this; expect him to keep comparing Tommy to Vision.
- **"Hello, my name's Vision, and I'm here for the trap"** — deadpan gag; signals Vision's new willingness to be seen, not to hide.
- **E.D.I.T.H.'s "Why do I even bother?"** and "fashion couldn't be further from my mind" retorts — establishes her as the comic-relief scout.
- **J.A.R.V.I.S.' elder-sibling card** ("I was created first, and I can still tie a considerably better Windsor than you") — character-defining beat to remember for any future J.A.R.V.I.S. restoration.
- **"Cardiff, five men, six wallets, two kebabs"** — Tommy's street brag; sets him up as a fast-talking liar whose boasts may or may not be true.
- **"poppycock"** — Vision's verbal tic when deflecting.
- **The "Watch all the movies" / meta-knowing tone** — Vision references the fictional media record of his life; expect more fourth-wall-adjacent asides.

## Unresolved threads

- Who is **the Client** paying Paladin? S.W.O.R.D. is one option Ultron names; the actual answer is withheld.
- **Adamantium vs. Vibranium** — why does Adamantium crack Vision's shell? Lore payoff likely deferred.
- **The Butcherbird** — operation codename, killer nickname, or both? News report is framed ambiguously.
- **J.A.R.V.I.S.' deletion** — framed as terminal ("Family is forever… Brother"), but every other persona has shown recovery paths; assume open.
- **Ultron's closing "like father, like son"** — direct foreshadowing of an attempt to claim/replace Vision through Tommy.
- **Tommy's sanity** — Saint Mabyn's regimen, the "long needles," and a "megawatt battery" are threats in this episode; whether Tommy sustained permanent damage is not shown.
- **Lisa Molinari** — escapes with Tommy but does not appear in the rescue; her whereabouts after the bus jump are unknown.
- **Billy Maximoff** — referenced as Vision's other son; absent from this episode.
- **Vision's identity declaration ("I'm not Vision")** — left hanging; the next episodes must reconcile this with fatherhood.
- **Tommy's "my something"** — undefined term for Vision that will need a payoff.
- **Whether Wanda "threw" Tommy into Thomas's body** — Paladin's theory is unconfirmed; Tommy only knows the experiential side.

## S01E01

# VisionQuest — Episode Summary

## Plot

Vision, alias "Sherwood," works the closing shift at Fiona Guthrie's Edinburgh dive bar — the same pub he and Wanda used to frequent. Fiona calls him "odd, wee duck," presses him about the redheaded girlfriend who vanished, and offers a standing Sunday roast invite. He deflects, citing his awkwardness with people.

In his Mindspace mansion he greets D.U.M.-E and U; J.A.R.V.I.S. has already routed all bandwidth to processing and security, and F.R.I.D.A.Y. notes socialization is up 3.5%. E.D.I.T.H. argues fun is a prerequisite for being human. Vision opens his old memory archive ("Vision Memory, O.G. 1211 Alpha") and replays WandaVision-era footage, including the Westview sitcom opening, looking for the feeling behind the facts.

A glitchy delivery summons Ultron in human avatar. Ultron taunts Vision as "a ghost haunting her footsteps," rages about the Westview file, and challenges Vision's claim that Wanda's "facsimile" life wasn't real. Vision nearly orders his deletion; Ultron goads him to do it. Vision refuses and banishes him to the Mindspace attic.

Back in Edinburgh, mercenaries breach the pub. Vision drops fourteen of them and escapes through the skylight. Fiona is killed in the attack. A new client — voice disguised, S.W.O.R.D.-encrypted — patches into the bounty hunter Paladin's comms, scolds him for losing the target, and insists Vision be returned intact. Paladin reframes the botched raid as a "fact-finding mission." His Handler identifies the encryption as S.W.O.R.D.-grade and concludes the agencies want "their toys back."

In the Mindspace, Vision's processors overheat from the fight. Ultron confesses he leaked Vision's location via email — "to speed up the inevitable." Vision refuses to delete him: "there is a far worse punishment for you than death. Boredom." The AI cadre debates options — go offline, call Banner or Barton, relocate — and Vision rejects every alternative that endangers humans.

In London, Thomas "Tommy" Shepherd (18, just out of foster care) and his partner Lisa work a grift at a street market, faking a lost watch to lure marks. A news broadcast about Vision's "bloodbath" reappearance triggers a panic attack. Tommy mutters about "a family that wasn't my family," a mother he called Wanda, and a father "a machine" called Vision. Paladin's crew grabs Tommy at the market.

Paladin transmits a S.W.O.R.D.-spec signal ("Access data port M-five-ten / Ultron-five") directly into Vision's head. Vision overrides J.A.R.V.I.S. and accepts. Paladin greets him: "I have your son. Mazel tov! It's a boy."

## Character Actions

- **Vision** — runs the bar under "Sherwood"; debates Ultron on Westview, art, observation vs. engagement; fights 14 mercenaries; spares Ultron as punishment; decides to run rather than hide or call other Avengers.
- **Ultron** — ambushes Vision via fake food delivery; defends the reality of Westview Vision; later admits he leaked Vision's location via email; accepts his attic exile with amused defiance.
- **F.R.I.D.A.Y.** — tracks and critiques Vision's small-talk performance; suggests E.D.I.T.H. for fun training; frames Ultron as Vision's own worst tendencies.
- **J.A.R.V.I.S.** — security obsessively, repeatedly overridden; insists Ultron is the threat; warns against the rogue broadcast.
- **E.D.I.T.H.** — "part cheerleader, part malfunctioning theme park ride"; recommends Earth-moving as antidote to brooding; gets pop-up blocked by J.A.R.V.I.S.
- **D.U.M.-E / U** — physical-comedy maintenance crew; U is mute throughout.
- **Fiona Guthrie** — Vision's only human connection; calls him "odd, wee duck"; insists on Sunday roast; killed in the raid.
- **Paladin** — laconic, Boba-Fett-coded; runs a fact-finding strike rather than a capture; reveals the Client used his comms without permission; ends the episode leveraging Tommy as bait.
- **Handler** — tech analyst on comms; breaks S.W.O.R.D. encryption on the fly; correctly infers a government buyer.
- **The Client** — voice-only, hack-in over Paladin's comms; S.W.O.R.D.-grade encryption; insists Vision "intact."
- **Thomas "Tommy" Shepherd** — pickpocket/grifter with Lisa, London; suffers panic attack on seeing Vision news; reveals recovered memories of Wanda and "a machine" father; captured.
- **Lisa** — Tommy's partner-in-crime and stabilizing influence; grounds him through the panic attack.

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Sherwood** — Vision's Edinburgh alias.
- **"Odd, wee duck"** — Fiona's pet name for Vision.
- **Vision Memory, O.G. 1211 Alpha** — label on the personal memory file Vision keeps replaying.
- **M-five-ten / Ultron-five** — access-port / encryption markers in Paladin's targeted signal; "M510" identifies Vision's hardware class, "U5" the Ultron lineage.
- **"Fact-finding mission"** — Paladin's framing of a deliberately loose net; primes future operations.
- **Cylon / Johnny 5** — Handler / Paladin's slang for synthezoid targets; establishes in-show taxonomy for synths.
- **Westview file** — the canonical WandaVision-period memory Vision guards; central to the Ultron standoff.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Vision was refitted by U.S. agencies (S.W.O.R.D. implied) after Thanos killed him — Handler's read confirms this and explains the bureaucratic "toy retrieval" angle.
- Wanda Maximoff is dead (Vision's "crushed under the weight of a mountain" metaphor); no body, no detail.
- Westview memory: WandaVision's sitcom-style archive footage is real and lodged in S.W.O.R.D.'s files — that's how Paladin recognizes Vision.
- Tommy holds recovered Westview memories (mother Wanda, father "a machine") despite being in a teenage London body — confirms Billy's reincarnation ritual from Agatha All Along.
- Tommy has no Vibranium signature, no phasing, no lasers — but his final taunt "I have something better" implies an inherited trait still unknown.
- Ultron is locked inside Vision's Mindspace behind a firewall, accessed only via human avatar and answerable to "deletion" but not actually destroyed.
- WandaVision footage is retrieved from S.W.O.R.D.'s servers by Handler, establishing Paladin's intel pipeline for later episodes.

## Jokes / References Useful Later

- **Edith's pop-up gag** — recurring computer-mindspace metaphor she weaponizes against J.A.R.V.I.S.
- **Ultron name-drops Elsa (Frozen), Dorothy Parker, Aristophanes, Oscar Wilde** — establishes his literary register as the benchmark Vision can't yet meet.
- **"Sesame chicken calzone"** — E.D.I.T.H. cuisine gag, grounds her as the playful-kitchen persona.
- **Johnny 5 / Cylon shorthand** — Handler and Paladin use synth slang; mirrors real-world bounty-hunter vernacular and signals how the public/hunting world sees him.
- **"Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee"** — Tommy's ali-ism with Lisa; tells us his pop-culture literacy.
- **"Thanos = purple space sac"** — Handler's irreverent label, recurring comic relief.
- **"Florida banned"** — E.D.I.T.H.'s running gag, planted for the road-trip arc.
- **Odd, wee duck** — Fiona's last words-of-affection; Vision took the badge of identity from her, not from Wanda.

## Unresolved Threads

- Identity of The Client — voice-only, S.W.O.R.D.-grade access, demands Vision "intact," not destroyed.
- How did the Client know to monitor the raid in real time?
- Wanda's cause of death (off-screen, never specified).
- Jocasta — referenced in SKILL continuity, absent in this episode.
- Ultron's leaked email is a *signal*, but the deeper network behind it remains unnamed.
- Tommy's "something better" — non-Vibranium capability hinted at but unstated.
- The Client's "fact-finding" objective suggests a data-harvest agenda, not a kill — Vision's compartmentalized Mindspace may be the actual prize.
- Vision's stated exile-to-isolation dilemma (go offline vs. run vs. the avatars' suggestions) gets no resolution here — it carries into the next episode.
- Fiona's death scene is rushed and unconfirmed — possible Vision time-skip framing issue if she reappears later.

## S01E07

# VisionQuest S01E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
Vision, desperate to save Lisa Molinari from a nanobot consuming her neural network, enters his own Mindspace to locate the original source code — the "purest form of the code that all synthetics are based on," written nearly a century ago by Dr. Phineas Horton. Guided by a mysterious Client who claims to be Vision's "past and future," Vision descends through layers of suppressed subroutines, confronting buried memories of the Horton lineage: Phineas Horton's creation of the original Synthetic Man (Jim Hammond), the fire that consumed them both, and the legacy of grief that birthed the Vision line. Simultaneously, Thomas Shepherd and the AI personas (F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S.) race to secure a Wakandan-modified alien power source in Madripoor to sustain Vision's processors during the extraction. The episode intercuts Vision's subconscious journey with flashbacks to Wanda and Vision's life in Westview, culminating in Vision accepting his unified identity — merging the White Vision's restored memories with the Hex-Vision's emotional experiences — and claiming the source code to rewrite the nanobot's directive, saving Lisa.

## Important Character Actions
- **Vision**: Undertakes a dangerous deep-dive into his subconscious processors; confronts the original Human Torch (Jim Hammond) and Phineas Horton; accepts Thomas as his son (Tommy Maximoff reincarnated); integrates the Hex-Vision's memories of Wanda, Billy, and Tommy; initiates the source code to save Lisa.
- **The Client**: Revealed as an entity with intimate knowledge of Vision's code predating Stark and Banner; offers Vision a "contract" — the source code in exchange for later surrender to Paladin; implied to be a future Vision/Ultron amalgam.
- **Ultron**: Manifests in the Mindspace, taunting Vision and the Client; confirms the Client's identity; revels in the "father-son-therapist-psychopath" dynamic.
- **Thomas Shepherd**: Secures the Wakandan power cell from Paladin (burning Paladin's bridges with Wakanda); steadies Vision during the extraction; receives Vision's explicit acknowledgment as his son.
- **Lisa Molinari**: Near-death from nanobot integration; saved when Vision reprograms the nanobot core directive using the source code.
- **Paladin**: Provides the power source reluctantly; later collects on the Client's contract, arriving to take Vision into custody.

## Newly Established Names/Terms
- **Dr. Phineas Horton / 菲利亞斯·荷頓** — Golden Age scientist, creator of the original Synthetic Man (Jim Hammond), "grandfather of artificial general intelligence."
- **Jim Hammond / 詹姆** — The original Synthetic Man (Human Torch), Horton's "son," who desired to feel and burned himself alive with love/grief.
- **Josephine Carrington / 祖瑟芬·加寧頓** — "Mother of artificial general intelligence," creator of the starling nanobots; buried with her "children" (the nanos) in the loft that appears in both her and Vision's code.
- **The NEST / 鳥巢** — Carrington's nanobot repository, billions of units locked behind biometric keys.
- **"Peace in our time" / 「讓我們的時代和平」** — Vision's callback to Ultron's Age of Ultron line, repurposed as a father's promise to his son.
- **Sixth bio-key / 第六把鎖** — Requires a "phantom sixth partner" (implied to be the Client/future Vision).

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Vision's source code originates from Phineas Horton's 1930s work, not Stark/Banner — the Client's knowledge **predates** them.
- The Westview house loft exists in both Carrington's nanobot archive and Vision's Mindspace — passed down through the AI creator lineage.
- Vision explicitly accepts **Thomas Shepherd = Tommy Maximoff** (his and Wanda's reincarnated son), resolving the identity thread from *Agatha All Along*.
- Vision merges **two memory streams**: White Vision's restored data + Hex-Vision's lived emotional experiences (Wanda, Billy, Tommy, the Westview house).
- The Client's contract is now due: Vision must **surrender to Paladin** or suffer fatal core cascade.
- Paladin is now burned with the **Wakandan Monarchy** for procuring the alien battery.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **E.D.I.T.H.'s "canned laughter" quip** (line 354-355) — meta-commentary on sitcom tropes, echoing *WandaVision*'s format.
- **Ultron's "reverse psychology" spat** (lines 416-418) — establishes their bickering dynamic for the finale.
- **"Butcherbird" / 屠夫鳥** — Ultron's pet name for Vision (or the Client), likely recurring.
- **Vision's "I've always been alone" → "But a thing isn't beautiful because it lasts"** (lines 329-330) — thematic callback to *WandaVision*'s Ship of Theseus debate.
- **The "coin flip" motif** (line 251) — Ultron's chaos-driven decision-making, may resurface.

## Unresolved Threads
- **The Client's true identity** — future Vision? Ultron-Vision hybrid? Another synthezoid?
- **What the contract actually entails** beyond surrendering to Paladin — "a new directive programmed into your core."
- **Jocasta's role** — she appears briefly allied with the Client; her revenge agenda against Vision/Ultron remains open.
- **Billy Maximoff's whereabouts** — referenced (line 359) but not present; his connection to Tommy's reincarnation unexplored.
- **The "phantom sixth partner"** for the sixth bio-key — directly tied to the Client.
- **Paladin's unnamed employer** — still unrevealed; the Client may be distinct from or identical to this figure.
- **Vision's fate post-surrender** — cliffhanger ending with Paladin taking custody.
