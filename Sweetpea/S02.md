## S02E05

# Sweetpea S02E05 Summary

## Episode plot
Rhiannon Lewis prepares to receive a bravery award at the Pride of Carnsham Awards while DCI St John's team prepares a raid on her home to find evidence tying her to a string of murders (including DS Sagar, killed in an RTA after meeting her, and AJ Pierce, whose car was dredged from the reservoir but whose body was never found). Craig, conflicted after telling Rhiannon he's done lying, is pressured by St John to stay undercover and act normal. Craig's renovation crew demolishes Rhiannon's kitchen and garage, ruining the raid. The police pivot to a sting: Craig will wear a wire, guide Rhiannon backstage, and use a "lingering hug" referencing his late father Tommy to get her to confess about AJ. Liv Pierce, AJ's mother, has been befriended by Rhiannon and is urged to appeal for information during Rhiannon's speech. Rhiannon arrives at the awards with Dr. Gabriel Saxena; Liv confronts her, and Rhiannon gives a speech naming AJ as missing and giving the police tip line. Backstage, Liv approaches Rhiannon and Rhiannon begins to confess about the day AJ came over, but journalist Freya blows the sting, shouting that Liv is working with the police. Chaos erupts, the alarm sounds, and Rhiannon flees Carnsham with Craig in a car, declaring they are on the run. Rhiannon dismisses Craig's concern about Liv, saying they can "take care of her later."

## Important character actions
- Rhiannon: writes her award speech, accepts the bravery award, publicly appeals for information on AJ, begins a backstage confession to Liv, then flees Carnsham with Craig.
- Craig: agrees to wear a wire and run the sting, calls in the renovation that demolishes the search site, helps Rhiannon escape.
- DCI St John: orders the raid, then pivots to using Liv to elicit a confession; frustrated when Craig compromises evidence.
- Liv Pierce: confronts police at the car wash over her missing son, is picked up/comforted by Rhiannon, wears/agrees to wear a wire as the police's key.
- Freya: journalist who pushes the two-killer theory (Rhiannon + Gabriel), tries to publish, is stopped by Craig, then sabotages the sting at the awards.
- Gabriel Saxena: Rhiannon's new boyfriend, "the doctor," suspected by Freya and Marina of involvement in the murders.
- Detective Marina: surveils Rhiannon, is present at the awards as part of the police operation.

## S02E01

## Episode plot

Season 2 premiere opens with a "Previously on Sweetpea" recap of Rhiannon Lewis's kill list, the Julia Blenkingsopp case, and AJ's murders. Rhiannon wakes at her late father's house in Carnsham with a heavy nosebleed. Her sister Seren arrives (flight delayed) and demands she go to hospital; Rhiannon refuses, lies that she tripped up the stairs, and is discharged with suspected concussion after being seen by Dr Gabriel Saxena — she refuses to let her name enter any hospital database.

Rhiannon is now a junior reporter at The Gazette. She meets Daisy at a cafe, attends an anger management class where she crosses paths with Detective Marina Farrar, and cuts ties with Julia Blenkingsopp ("I don't need anyone"). She later confronts Daisy's boss, Paul Attwood, over his treatment of Daisy; the encounter implies Rhiannon kills him ("You're the monster! I'm doing a good thing").

Freya Kirk from Gazette Head Office visits, pitches a "People's Paper" rebrand, and nominates Rhiannon for a Pride of Carnsham Award for her role in the Julia Blenkingsopp rescue. Fellow junior reporter AJ is away chasing a lead; former staffer Jeff died in a "terrible accident."

Freya summons Rhiannon to the Little Spoon Cafe: manager Paul Attwood has been found stabbed to death (multiple wounds, fatal neck injury) with "monster" written beside the body. Rhiannon reports from the scene, pushing an angle that the killer was protecting the public (Paul allegedly sexually assaulted staff). She flirts with Marina at the crime scene, and the episode ends with her drafting an article framing the killer as someone taking the law into their own hands to protect the innocent, over Moby's "Extreme Ways."

## Important character actions

- Rhiannon Lewis — refuses hospital treatment and lies about her injury; attends anger management; severs ties with Julia; attacks/kills Paul Attwood; reports on his murder and pitches a vigilante-killer narrative.
- Seren — drives to Carnsham, tries to get Rhiannon to hospital, backs off after being shut out.
- Daisy — cafe worker, befriends Rhiannon, declines a Gazette admin job because Paul is her landlord.
- Paul Attwood — steak-blog pub manager, Daisy's boss and landlord, sexually assaults staff (alleged), is murdered.
- Freya Kirk — Head Office representative; pushes award nomination and new editorial direction.
- Marina Farrar — on restricted police admin duties (DI St John's order, three months) because Rhiannon triggered her unprofessional behavior; attends anger management; flirts with Rhiannon at the crime scene.
- Bella — Head of Police Admin Services; Tilly — her eager colleague.

## Newly established names/terms

The Gazette; Freya Kirk; Daisy; Paul Attwood; "Risk It For A Brisket" (Paul's steak blog); Little Spoon Cafe; "Sadmin" (police Administration nickname); Pride of Carnsham Awards; Tommy's Transformations; Dr Gabriel Saxena; DI St John; "monster" message at the crime scene; "People's Paper" rebrand pitch.

## Continuity-relevant facts

- Julia Blenkingsopp is missing (Season 1); Rhiannon slashed her tyres (CCTV footage exists, on a USB stick) and took credit for her "rescue."
- Marcus Rebac case closed with no DNA evidence; Rhiannon's reporting on it boosted Gazette sales.

## S02E03

Based on the subtitles, here is the summary for **Sweetpea S02E03**:

## Episode Plot

AJ Pierce's car is discovered in the reservoir, prompting a police search for his body. Rhiannon (junior reporter at the *Gazette*) is questioned by police and initially lies, claiming AJ never showed up for their meeting. Under pressure from AJ's mother and a school counsellor who spots her deception, Rhiannon admits AJ came to her house, stayed the night, then left without explanation after she "let him see the real me." The police treat it as likely suicide; Rhiannon insists he wasn't suicidal.

Meanwhile, Marina (Rhiannon's friend/colleague) grows suspicious after receiving a USB with CCTV footage and tips from a parent at the school where she works. DS Rory Sagar (the "cop with the dog") leaves Rhiannon an off-record voicemail asking to meet at The Bell pub to discuss the "horrific murders." Marina meets with Rory and becomes convinced Rhiannon is a serial killer connected to Paul Attwood, Arthur Goodyear, Marcus Rebac, and now AJ Pierce. Marina and Rory agree to team up and track Rhiannon "as friends."

Rhiannon asks Daisy to investigate Dr. Gabriel Saxena (A&E doctor AJ mentioned seeing). Daisy obtains Gabriel's address. Craig (Rhiannon's ex/builder) shows up to inspect the house for renovation work; Rhiannon refuses him garage access. The fuse box trips—Rhiannon can't reach it because Craig is barred from the garage. Marina helps fix it. The episode ends with Rhiannon apparently attacking a man who identifies himself as a police officer at her door.

## S02E04


## Episode plot

The episode opens in the aftermath of DS Rory Sagar's death. Dr Gabriel Saxena has told police it was a single-vehicle crash on the A417 outside Carnsham ending in cardiac arrest, with himself as the upstanding witness. Rhiannon cleaned the scene, including her own nosebleed. Over drinks, the two bond over their shared secret: Rhiannon chased the detective who was onto her to his death, while Saxena's specialty is a clean injection-induced cardiac arrest. Rhiannon deflects his interest ("things are complicated with my ex") and heads to work.

Liv Pierce, AJ's mother, turns up at Rhiannon's flat with a dog named Percy and news that the reservoir search found nothing, police now suspect foul play, and AJ's body is being hunted. She presses Rhiannon, who claims the police have her statement (given to DI Diana St John). Marina, a journalist fixated on the case, badgers detectives for not interviewing Rhiannon, confronts Rhiannon, and recruits Craig (Rhiannon's other half) to break into the locked garage on the excuse of smelling burning. She and an ally then go through Rhiannon's computer, find searches for "Gabriel Saxena," and conclude he may be the next victim.
