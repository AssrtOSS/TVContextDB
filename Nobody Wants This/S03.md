## Episode Summary: S03E05

### Plot
The episode centers on Joanne and Noah navigating post-engagement dynamics while their friends and family insert themselves into wedding planning. Parallel storylines follow Morgan's writing struggles and Sasha's disastrous third date.

**Main arc**: Joanne recounts the elaborate ruse behind their engagement (a fake "Brian's wedding" invitation Noah kept for two weeks). The couple envisions a "Julia Roberts wedding" — evening, outdoors, barefoot, ~50 guests, white candles on a dirt path in a remote mountain clearing. Bina ambushes them with a dinner inviting Noah's parents (Ilan, mom, dad), where Noah's father reveals recent blepharoplasty complications. Wedding planning tensions erupt: parents want a grand affair; Joanne and Noah want small. Noah finally sets boundaries, declaring the wedding is theirs alone.

**Morgan subplot**: Her agent pressures her for book proposal pages. She attends a writers group but flees, calling attendees "frumpy." A tarot reading yields the **Two of Cups** ("most powerful love card") and a card confirming she's on the right path as a writer. She leaves inspired, realizing how to fix her proposal.

**Sasha subplot**: Third date with Poppy (who she dislikes and who seems to dislike her). The "Band-Aid" metaphor frames it as rebound sex to get over Esther. Sasha bails mid-date. At the house, she meets Julian (real estate agent showing a celeb-owned property). They connect; Julian offers to be her "Band-Aid." They leave together.

- **Joanne**: Recounts engagement backstory; pushes back on parental wedding takeover; admits she hates most weddings ("corporate").
