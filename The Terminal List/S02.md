## S02E08 Summary

### Episode Plot
President Drozdov (Russia) departs Kherson airfield in an armored convoy bound for Moscow, accompanied by Yana. The convoy is ambushed by an unknown helicopter. During the chaos, Yana vanishes. Drozdov's security chief Yuri concludes she betrayed them — leaking their position to either Ukrainian or American forces — and orders her hunted down and killed along with any American collaborators.

### Important Character Actions
- **Drozdov**: Insists on traveling to Moscow to negotiate an end to the conflict, rejecting safety in D.C.
- **Yana**: Disappears during the ambush; accused of treachery by Yuri.
- **Yuri**: Takes tactical command after the ambush; orders QRF extraction, then issues kill order on Yana.
- **Reece & Strain** (referenced): Previously warned Drozdov's team about compromised safety.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **VoltStreem** — Media outlet acquired to "share the truth"; framing the attack as Ukrainian-perpetrated using U.S. rifles destined for the front.
- **Kherson airfield** — Departure point for the convoy.
- **QRF (Quick Reaction Force)** — Russian rapid response unit; 9-minute ETA to ambush site.
- **Mill warehouse (no roof)** — Designated extraction point, 400m southwest of ambush.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Russian delegation already departed before the convoy left.
- American rifles bound for Ukraine were used by the snipers (per VoltStreem narrative).
- Drozdov believes going to Moscow is the only path to ending the war.
- Offices likely bugged by CIA (per team's assumption).

### Unresolved Threads
- **Attacker identity**: Ukrainian patrol, American operators, or third party?
- **Yana's fate**: Captured, turned, or killed? Did she actually betray them?
- **VoltStreem's true agenda**: Propaganda arm or genuine truth-teller?
- **Reece/Strain connection**: How do they factor into the Moscow play?
- **CIA surveillance extent**: Offices compromised?
