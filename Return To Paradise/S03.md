## S03E05

## Plot
- Mackenzie and Leo visit Wild Ones wildlife park, where keeper Joe “Uncle Joe” Gratton is found murdered inside the cassowary enclosure after a radio distress call. The cage is electronically locked, with Joe’s pass logged in at 1:30pm and Rupert Flint’s at 1:53pm when the body is discovered.
- The initial evidence suggests Joe was killed inside a sealed enclosure with the cassowary claw from his own prop belt, creating an apparent locked-room puzzle.
- The investigation uncovers multiple people with grudges or secrets: entertainer Willow Lightfoot (actually activist Caroline Benson) who cloned Joe’s pass and fought with him over harassment; Judith Coulston, mother of crash victim Melanie Coulston, who had been hounding Joe before claiming forgiveness; and park owners Rupert and Gracie Flint, who were entangled in illegal animal smuggling, financial fraud, and a $10,000 hush payment to Joe.
- A fire opal ring found at the scene is identified as the engagement ring Joe’s crash victim Sofia Paymer wore. Mackenzie realizes Sofia was Leo Drake’s fiancée, linking Leo personally to Joe.
- Mackenzie deduces Leo confronted Joe near the enclosure, killed him in a sudden attack, faked Joe’s radio distress call to create an alibi, and later used the emergency response to re-enter the cage, return the radio, and conceal forensic details. Leo confesses to killing Joe in revenge and is arrested.

## Important character actions
- Mackenzie Clarke: investigates the locked-cage murder, connects the ring to the earlier fatal crash, reconstructs the timeline from purchases and forensic inconsistencies, and arrests Leo.
- Leo Drake: accompanies Mackenzie to the park, conducts the autopsy, omits/separates key findings, fakes the distress call, tampers with the scene, and ultimately confesses to murdering Joe.
- Joe Gratton: victim; prior drunk driver who killed Sofia Paymer and Melanie Coulston, later drank on the job, released a dingo while intoxicated, and blackmailed/exploited the Flints.
- Gracie Flint: runs park operations under financial strain; admits sourcing cheap feed while claiming premium deductions (tax fraud) and paying Joe $10,000 to stay quiet.
- Rupert Flint: travels internationally to build the Wild Ones brand; admits involvement in illegal breeding/smuggling plans tied to Joe.
- Willow Lightfoot / Caroline Benson: children’s entertainer persona used by an animal-rights activist; assaulted Joe for inappropriate behavior, cloned his pass, and is caught attempting further action in the park.
- Judith Coulston: Melanie’s mother; harassed Joe via calls (using a second phone registered to Mabel Schmidt), slashed his tyres, sent a funeral wreath after his death, and expresses belief he deserved worse.
- Colin Cartwright: assists the investigation, learns from Dolphin Cove Diners contacts that the ring’s stone is a distinctive fire opal, and objects to Reggie leaking case-related material.
- Glenn Strong: apologizes for earlier inappropriate behavior, tells Mackenzie Leo seems decent and that her happiness matters most, and helps Mackenzie with an off-the-books task to obtain answers.
- Reggie Rocco: deals with damaging campaign coverage, tries to reclaim the narrative, and benefits from an “anonymous police source” undercover story appearing in the Echo.

## Newly established names/terms
- Wild Ones: wildlife park in Dolphin Cove where the murder occurs.
- Cassie: the cassowary in whose enclosure Joe is killed.
- Joe “Uncle Joe” Gratton: park general duties manager and murder victim.
- Gracie Flint and Rupert Flint: sibling owners/operators of Wild Ones.
- Willow Lightfoot: park entertainer identity; real name Caroline Benson, an activist with prior protest convictions.
- Judith Coulston: Melanie Coulston’s mother; connected to Divine Harvest volunteering and Reverend Tyler’s church.
- Mabel Schmidt: Divine Harvest client whose name was used to register a second phone for harassing Joe.
- Sofia Paymer: second victim in Joe’s earlier fatal crash; Leo Drake’s fiancée.
- Melanie Coulston: first named crash victim; Judith’s daughter.
- Dolphin Cove Diners: local group whose members help identify the opal as a rare fire opal.
- Flint Force: social media/content branding referenced by the Flints.

## Continuity-relevant facts
- Joe’s fatal crash was 11 years earlier near Perth: he drove drunk (far over the legal limit, at very high speed), killing Sofia Paymer and Melanie Coulston; he served two years and received a lifetime driving ban, with sentencing affected by mishandled lab blood results.
- Leo was engaged to Sofia and kept her fire opal engagement ring on a chain; the same ring is found in the cassowary enclosure and bears Leo’s DNA.
- Leo became a pathologist in response to the lab failure that weakened the original case against Joe.
- Forensic reconstruction: Joe’s aorta and inferior vena cava were severed, meaning he would have died within seconds and could not have made the radio distress call attributed to him.
- Leo’s movements are tied to two ice-cream purchases ten minutes apart and a trip “to the bathroom,” which provided opportunity to stage the call and manage the scene.
- Wild Ones is financially precarious: Gracie committed feed-claim/tax fraud and paid Joe hush money; Rupert pursued illegal animal smuggling/breeding to keep the park afloat.

## Jokes or references useful for later episodes
- Mackenzie’s love of wildlife parks is framed as affection for animals “neatly categorised and located on a map,” reinforcing her orderly mindset.
- The cassowary/koala show banter and Joe’s grim “only the strong survive” storyteller persona recur as darkly ironic echoes of the case.
- Colin’s enthusiasm for the Dolphin Cove Diners (and their oddly specific expertise/social rituals) becomes a recurring source of useful information and comic friction.
- Reggie’s campaign mishaps and eagerness to spin events for publicity continue as political comic relief, including the leaked undercover photo credited to an “anonymous police source.”

## Unresolved threads
- Mackenzie is personally shaken by Leo’s betrayal and arrest, especially after trusting him and beginning a relationship that Leo insists was real.
- Glenn’s feelings remain complicated: he apologizes and affirms Mackenzie’s happiness, but underlying tension around her relationship with Leo is not fully resolved.
- Leo and Mackenzie had planned to attend a forensic symposium in Melbourne; the arrest abruptly ends that future.
- Reggie’s campaign benefits from leaked case publicity despite Colin’s warning, leaving potential fallout from the “anonymous police source” breach open.
- Closing episode cues suggest Mackenzie is off her game in subsequent events (including mention of an incident involving poisoned cake and doubts about her ability to solve cases), pointing to emotional and professional repercussions ahead.

# Return To Paradise S03E02 Summary

## Episode Plot
A construction-site "accident" at Lucas Denetto's Dolphin Cove townhouse development proves to be murder. Young tradie **Saxon Rouse** is found dead at the base of a building, apparently having fallen through a safety barrier from the roof. New pathologist **Dr Leo Drake** (first day) notes the hard hat came off before impact and earmuffs are missing. Mackenzie determines Saxon was killed *before* the team meeting — lured downstairs by his ex-lover **Bee Mumbi** (site manager), bludgeoned with a sledgehammer by **Dale Clempton** (mentor tradie), then staged. The "fall" sound during the meeting was created by **ice bolts** in the barrier that melted later, and a recorded saw played over speakers. Saxon had discovered an **ancient stone axe head** proving the site sits on culturally significant ancestral land — a find that would shut down the build. He tried to blackmail Lucas; Bee (who bought a unit off-plan with her grandmother's money) and Dale (desperate to keep his job amid divorce/family-court costs) conspired to silence him. Lucas runs a fraud scheme (fake workers, union bribes) and profits more if the project fails. **Bee and Dale are arrested for murder; Lucas faces fraud/bribery charges.**

## S03E06

## Episode plot
- Dolphin Cove holds its council election on a Saturday, complete with polling at the school and a "democracy sausage" stall. Candidate and Shank Meats owner Bob Shank collapses and dies after campaigning, voting, eating part of a sausage, and taking a slice of his ex-wife Linda Halfacre’s passion fruit sponge.
- Early signs suggest poisoning: a sulphur-crested cockatoo dies after eating cake, and testing finds sodium hydroxide (drain cleaner) crystals in the icing. However, video shows no one tampered with Bob’s slice after he took it, and other customers who ate cake are unaffected, making the poisoning timeline seem impossible.
- The investigation uncovers multiple motives around the election and Bob’s personal life: Linda’s bitter divorce, Josh Waterman’s opposition to Bob’s proposed abattoir (which would demolish Sea Spray Retirement Village where Josh’s parents live), Cynthia Shank’s affair with Josh and pregnancy with Josh’s baby, and Dr Ellen Novak’s secret relapse and prescription fraud discovered by Bob.
- Mackenzie realizes the key clue is what Bob could not eat. A tick bite from a past Shank Meats Christmas pig-shooting party left Bob with alpha-gal syndrome, a fatal red-meat allergy he hid because of his meat-industry image. Ellen, who knew his condition, switched his vegetarian sausage for beef at the barbecue and masked it with extra sauce. Bob died from the allergic reaction.
- After Bob collapsed, Cynthia panicked and sprinkled drain cleaner on the cake to stage a poisoning, believing no one would accept that his death was an accident. Mackenzie exposes Ellen as the deliberate killer; Ellen is arrested for Bob’s murder. Reggie Rocco wins the council election.

## Important character actions
- Mackenzie Clarke: leads the investigation while emotionally shaken by the Leo Drake case, identifies the sausage stain as significant, connects Frankie’s tick bite/autopsy clues to Bob’s alpha-gal syndrome, solves how and why Bob was killed, and has the Leo brief of evidence ready to send.

- Glenn Strong: handles lab/testing work, confirms sodium hydroxide in the cake icing and that the sausage sample tested clean of poison, and supports Mackenzie personally during her crisis of confidence.
- Felix Wilkinson: helps canvass/campaign for Reggie, investigates leads on the ground, misses voting because of the case, and later reconciles with Reggie after her win.
- Dr Ellen Novak: council candidate and Bob’s GP; secretly relapses into opioid addiction, writes prescriptions in patients’ names and collects them, is confronted by Bob, then murders him by serving beef instead of his required vegetarian sausage.
- Cynthia Shank: Bob’s pregnant wife; is having an affair with Josh and carrying his child, tries to protect Bob’s vegetarian-sausage cover during the event, and attempts to disguise the allergic death as cake poisoning after he collapses.
- Josh Waterman: incumbent councillor and candidate; opposes the abattoir to protect Sea Spray Retirement Village and his parents, and is revealed as Cynthia’s lover and the baby’s father.
- Linda Halfacre: Bob’s ex-wife and Dolphin Bakeries owner; bakes and serves the passion fruit sponge, resents Bob after a bruising divorce and financial loss, but is not the killer.
- Reggie Rocco: runs for council, acknowledges the barriers of campaigning without money or party backing, wins the election, and delivers a community-focused victory speech.
- Frankie: Glenn and Mackenzie’s dog; a tick bite prompts the chain of reasoning that leads Mackenzie to alpha-gal syndrome.

## S03E04

# Return To Paradise S03E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
Human remains are discovered in concrete during ocean pool renovations by Clayton Concreting. The victim is **Davey Clayton**, who vanished 43 years earlier (March 1983). Davey was last seen at a beach bonfire on March 5, 1983, supposedly leaving town to surf. The concrete was poured by his father Rod at 6am on March 6. Photos from the bonfire show Davey driving away at 5:30am — only 30 minutes before the pour. Autopsy reveals blunt force trauma to the back of the skull and a boxer's fracture (he punched someone). The breakthrough: Daylight Savings ended that night; the surf club clock wasn't wound back until morning, so 5:30am on the clock was actually 4:30am real time — giving a 1.5-hour window. The killer used this "extra hour" to murder Davey after he returned for his surfboard and hide him in the wet concrete. The killer is **Laurie Rogers**, Phil's photography partner, who couldn't bear Davey leaving with surf manager Mike "The Shark" Richards. Laurie forged postcards, hid Davey's van at his parents' coastal property, and exploited the clock discrepancy. Laurie is arrested.

## Important Character Actions
- **Mackenzie Clarke** leads the cold case investigation; identifies the Daylight Savings timeline trick
- **Phil Strong** admits he encouraged Davey to "follow your dreams, with or without me"
- **Mars** confesses he stole $1,000 from the business to buy Yvonne's engagement ring and framed Davey
- **Rod Clayton** admits hitting Davey and telling him "you're no longer part of this family" but denies killing him
- **Yvonne** was at the bonfire all night with Mars (alibi confirmed)
- **Laurie Rogers** killed Davey in a rage over abandonment; forged handwriting on postcards; hid the panel van
- **Glenn** and the pathologist brawl over methodology; Glenn later comforts Mackenzie
