## S04E01

# Reacher S04E01 — Summary

## Episode Plot

Picking up from the S3 finale ("Previously on Reacher"), Jack Reacher is on the run in Philadelphia with a small team: his right-hand soldier Tamara, Philadelphia cop Jacob, and Russell Plum, the freelance journalist brother of Anna Merrick — the Pentagon clerk who shot herself on the Broad Street Line while trying to hand off a jump drive of classified military records. The CIA kidnapped them at a black site, treated them as enemy combatants, and they escaped; now they're holed up in a loft while both the CIA and a separate mercenary crew ("the Big Red Machine," so nicknamed for the 1975 Cincinnati Reds) are hunting them.

The team needs two things: the missing jump drive (last seen on the train), and a fourth witness the police never interviewed — Nolan Cahill, chief of staff to potential presidential candidate Congressman John Sampson. After surviving a two-pronged assault on the loft (one Reacher thwarts by using wasp spray as a makeshift weapon; Jacob nearly kills a prisoner to find his missing nephew Ben), Reacher swaps their compromised van with a car thief named Cyrus (trading a Windstar for a stolen Highlander plus plates from Cyrus's sister Rayona's Jetta, and Russell's cat "Pancake" as a goodwill gift to cousin Ray Ray). He then meets journalist Lila alone at Playtime Bowl, a bowling alley on 2nd Street, where she comes clean: she's not Anna's relative, her bruises were self-inflicted, and her "mother" Amisha is actually a survivor of Indonesian atrocities who hired bodyguards. The real story is that in 1998 Delta Force, led by Sampson, trained General Putra's soldiers, who then massacred dissidents including Amisha's husband. Anna Merrick agreed to help Lila expose it and was killed before she could deliver the drive. Reacher promises to find the drive but warns he decides what to do once he sees it.

Mercs find the bowling alley. Amisha and the bodyguards escape, but Reacher and Lila are separated and Reacher is injured. At a Philly impound, Jacob and partner Docherty find Anna's abandoned car — glove box forced, seats shredded — and discover a winter jacket, a Bear Creek ski-resort pass, and a SECOND Pentagon work badge Jacob didn't know about. Tipped that it grants Level 8 clearance to Building 28, Station J12, Reacher drives overnight to Baltimore to confront Sampson directly. Sampson admits he was in Indonesia with Delta Force in 1998 and met Putra, but denies any atrocities. When Reacher presses about Cahill being the fourth witness, Sampson insists Cahill wasn't on the train. They confront Cahill together. Cahill drops a "things never stay hidden" line citing Watergate, Iran-Contra, and Hunter Biden's laptop, then shoots himself before answering.

## Important Character Actions

- Reacher: stops Jacob from killing the captive mercenary so they can extract intel; warns Lila to leave the Rittenhouse Hotel; meets Lila alone and warns he decides the drive's fate; drives alone to Baltimore; uses the code name "Springfield" (Reacher's entry code at Sampson's building).
- Jacob: emotionally volatile; nearly executes the prisoner to find his missing nephew Ben; at the impound, breaks down over a canceled Bear Creek trip with Anna and Ben; later pockets the second work badge from the car, contaminating chain of custody.
- Tamara: tactical counterpart to Reacher; argues he needs backup; spots the bruises on the merc from Lila's hotel and traces the threat.
- Russell Plum: the leak that drew both teams to the loft (posted about the drive on 4Chan with a VPN); provides an evidence-tampering fake ID pulled from a raided fraud ring.
- Lila: lied about Sampson being her father (and the bruises); reveals she is a journalist whose mother was killed in Indonesia; steals the bracelet to Reacher and refuses to leave despite the danger.
- Sampson: meets Reacher in Baltimore, admits Delta Force training of Putra, denies war crimes, claims ignorance of Cahill on the train.
- Cahill: confronted alongside Sampson, kills himself before explaining his presence on the train or the drive.

## S04E04

# Reacher S04E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
Reacher's team (Jacob, Russell, Plum/Tamara) investigates the death of government worker Anna Merrick and the kidnapping of her son Ben in Philadelphia. Anna stole classified documents on a USB drive from a federal document destruction facility to ransom for Ben's life, but the kidnappers (mercenary group "Big Red Machine") killed Ben anyway. Anna, realizing her son was dead, took the subway to hunt his killers but committed suicide when a police officer identified himself—destroying her last hope of vengeance. Reacher deduces Anna mailed the drive to journalist Lila before dying. The team races to Lila's hotel (Liberty Gardens) but finds she fled after an attack; the drive isn't there. Ben's body is discovered in a dumpster at 9th & Callowhill—throat cut with a karambit, the same Indonesian curved knife that killed Mary Ellen Daniels (the OnlyFans woman from earlier episodes). The episode ends with mercenaries converging on Lila's new location ("Princess' tower") and a medical examiner confirming the karambit link to Indonesia.

His chief of staff Nolan Cahill (Delta Force, John Sampson's trainee) jumped off a roof after following Anna onto the subway.

## S04E05


Episode S04E05 Summary

# Plot

Lila and Amisha weren't kidnapped — they returned to find their bodyguards dead (Reds already slaughtered), then carved up the Reds with karambit knives and fled. Reacher realizes he misread the situation.

The thumb drive Anna "mailed" was never sent to Lila — Anna deliberately hid it and mailed it to her brother, the person she trusted most. Jacob already has the drive.

The Lila alias only surfaced weeks ago: a Platinum Amex with a six-figure limit, used to buy first-class Indonesia→U.S. tickets for the pair and two bodyguards, then a fifth ticket from Djibouti for an unknown male accomplice.

Tamara Green calls 911 from a stolen unmarked Ford Taurus, giving a running commentary on Parsons Avenue. Jacob takes a hairpin turn at speed — his high-school "rite of passage" — and crashes the SUV. Jacob notes Lila never made that turn in high school.

- DeRosa (a Lt. Reacher calls "Uncle Monty" in Hackensack) arrives in Mayfield. Jacob tells him Ben was murdered and that the two women who jumped are behind everything. Merle Harkavey — a local man found on Pleasant Avenue with a slashed jugular — was carjacked by the wet, fleeing women. They survived the fall.

Reacher privately reveals that the Feds/CIA may be covering the case up. DeRosa agrees to call, then orders White Manna sliders (15 cheese, 15 no onions, 3 fries, 6-pack of Cokes) on the PD account.
- With the FBI an hour out, Reacher, Neagley and Jacob slip out. DeRosa ditches Jacob's stolen cruiser at a chop shop run by Cyrus (whose sister wants a new BarcaLounger after Cyrus's cat destroyed the old one); Reacher threatens to seize anything without a bill of sale. They take a different car — "nice ride," from "an old friend" — and head to Philly. (Unclear "old friend" — possibly a callback to an established ally or a one-off contact.)
- In Philly they hole up in a hotel room whose housekeeper only speaks Spanish ("No-no hablo"). The drive is now powered on and readable.
- The drive contains a single image: a photo of three men, identified as Sampson, Cahill, and General Putra. It is password-locked but accessible. No other files. They cannot understand why anyone would kill over a single photo.
- Anna's text history with Lila (a blocked number) is found on her phone. The night Anna died, Lila sent her a Philly address — 190 East Moyamensing Avenue — first floor an abandoned restaurant under renovation. Before that, Lila sent a video of Ben being tortured: "Tell your mother… Mom, they're cutting her up. They say I'm next if you don't do what they say."
- Jacob breaks down and swears he'll take the women's knives "and dig them deep into their guts… Slaughter them like the animals they are." Neagley tells him that's not who he is, and that the Anna and Ben he loved are "as dead as they are" — he won't let Neagley let him spiral.
- Reacher arranges a meeting with the presidential candidate — whose personal cell number he had in his wallet ("Giant Boxcar Willie" — Reacher's card). The candidate shows the photo to an old contact who confirms the other men are Sampson, Cahill, and General Putra. Reacher presses: a fourth person is in the photo — a scientist doing malaria research, not Delta Force. The candidate says he doesn't know the scientist's name; that was Cahill's assignment; his focus was Putra's men. Reacher mocks the candidate: "You're in the perfect line of work, Congressman."
- News breaks: Reacher is wanted citywide, every channel. A witness from the Liberty Gardens massacre (who saw Reacher, Lila, Amisha, and the Reds enter together) is being interviewed live. The group splits: Jacob takes Plum to the library to identify the scientist via public internet; Reacher, Neagley and Tamara go to the restaurant.
- Reacher deliberately gets "arrested" by Tamara on camera to throw off the manhunt — but the witness on the live broadcast spots that Tamara and Reacher are together ("the big guy and their two friends went in first! She followed them in. They all know each other!"). The manhunt pivots to Tamara.
- Pursued by Philly PD, Reacher, Neagley and Tamara rappel down the hotel exterior from a window using one Army-issue harness for two people. Neagley slips, the glass cracks; Reacher pulls her to safety and they drop to an awning. They flee on a city bus; a passenger recognizes Reacher from the news and shouts. They exit.
- At 190 East Moyamensing Avenue: the abandoned restaurant is a torture site. Reacher reads the room — Ben's fully desiccated blood (days old), broken restraints, a slit throat. Ben, a Division I athlete, broke free in a fear-induced adrenaline burst and nearly escaped, but was caught and killed at the door.
- Lila is there, alive and waiting, holding a gun. "Give me the drive, Reacher." Reacher refuses: "It's her gun against your knife. My knife. And leverage." Lila apologizes — "I'm sorry. I should've listened" — then demands the drive "Now." Episode ends on the standoff.

The project created aerosolized chemical weapons in an Indonesian rainforest lab (with General Putra's permission) and attempted to reverse-engineer antidotes, but never succeeded. Putra discovered the operation, stole the weapon, slaughtered a village, and the U.S. covered it up. The formula was accidentally captured in the background of a photo with Putra; the whiteboard was out of focus but modern AI image deconvolution can now recover it.
