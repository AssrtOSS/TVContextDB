## S02E05

## Episode plot

Cold-open recap of Season 2: Pearce ("the Frenchman") is loose; Paris doctors are being coerced into weaponising MERS into a more contagious, more deadly strain; Karen has been turned and is now working for Voltov; Pearce is heading back to Benghazi, where Mishkin is running the operation, while Voltov's cover story frames Madrid as the planned outbreak's source.

Present-day opens on a Paris playground. Alice introduces her children Samuel and Maya on the swings while a neighbour dad introduces his son Diego. Armed men burst in demanding "the Frenchman"; they seize Alice and the kids at gunpoint, force her to silence them during a game of hide-and-seek, and bundle them into a van. Alice manages to urge a passer-by to call the police. A flashback lore beat: a researcher "passed away recently" in a plane crash — the staged death of a scientist to silence her research.

In a long road-trip monologue, Vincent/Taleb recounts an Iraq contractor job protecting power-line engineers. A panicked Toyota ran the roadblock; both the driver and his labouring pregnant wife — the hospital was minutes away — were killed. Vincent tells his partner this current mission "feels like a good thing," and that he wants his last mission to be something better than that.

Along the Libyan coast, Vincent pushes on to Hassan's house. Hassan — Vincent's longtime interpreter, thought dead after the helicopter extraction in Benghazi — is alive, married to Eisha. He was held by Voltov but escaped. Vincent heads back to bring in Zara, Hannah, Max, Keane and Oscar.

Out at sea, Zara Taylor is on a boat with "Bob" (real name Luca), an arms middleman; Benghazi is ~450 nautical miles away. In a cut, a Voltov doctor is shown her family held hostage with a brutal "This is what happens when you fuck with me" threat, then coerced into finishing the MERS modification. One more test is required; her family is promised release afterward, but she knows she will be killed.

Voltov upgrades the Frenchman bounty to $50,000 after two of its men go missing. The Madrid female operative's plan is laid out: the outbreak will start in Benghazi; once it begins, no one will look there — everyone's eyes will turn to Madrid. The dead scientist's body must never be found.

At a Libyan coast rendezvous with the dealer Malek, sound carries in the desert; Malek has removed the firing pins from all pistols as insurance. The deal turns violent; a female operative single-shots the ambush and the weapons are recovered. Bob asks if he still gets paid.

A side subplot runs in Madrid: the same operative reflects on her "date" with the scientist Andrew. She has cloned his phone; Moscow will send emails from his account making it look like he was worried about lab safety, turning him into the leak's scapegoat. Her handler reflects on her late mother — who sacrificed everything for her — and promises they will be together once "this is over."

Back on the road, Voltov roadblocks force the team to find a guide. Vincent and Zara drive to Hassan's house; the team parks nearby. Wild dogs alert on approaching Voltov heavy-calibre troops; the team is given the choice of dying or surrendering. They come out, playing for time — Zara asks "Is she related to James Taylor, the American singer?" — and are marched into trucks. In the confusion Vincent shouts "Go, Hassan!" and Hassan slips away. Voltov specifically asks for "Zara Taylor."

Max, loaded into one truck, throws himself out the back. Vincent phones him; Max is near the Atiq Mosque. Vincent tells him to make for the docks; the team will meet him there at first light.

## S02E06

### Plot

Voltov (new main antagonist) is engineering a weaponised MERS virus through a compromised Madrid scientist, Karen, whose family is held hostage. Cover story: a leaked Madrid lab accident. Parallel action: Vincent Taleb and Zara Taylor infiltrate Mishkin's Libyan coastal base — barracks, vehicle workshop, and a stockpile of Gaddafi's gold bars. To get firepower, they cut a deal with local warlord Malik: attack Mishkin's base in exchange for all the gold. The assault overruns the base; Vincent frees captured allies (Hannah and others) and kills the traitor Max, who had sold the team's exfil routes and rendezvous points to Mishkin. Vincent reaches a containment chamber where Spanish scientist Rachel is infected with the modified strain. She extracts a deathbed promise to save her children Samuel and Maya, held at a farm in La Mancha. Vincent then interrogates a guard, confirms the family location, and learns Mishkin has already escaped. A separate subplot has Zara on a dating-app date with Alice in Madrid while Voltov accelerates the release. The episode closes with Voltov and Mishkin reunited, Karen held in Benghazi, and the release timeline moved forward.
