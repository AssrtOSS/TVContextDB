## S05E06

### 剧情概述

- Lou-Lou 死后，Marvin 追问 Kanan 真相。Kanan承认自己在枪战中误杀 Lou，Raq随后掩盖了事实；Marvin因此陷入悲痛、愤怒和自责。
- Kanan向 Jarita 坦白：自己是 Raq 的儿子，也承认最初接近她是为了套取情报。Jarita因被利用而震怒，但两人仍承认彼此有真实感情，并约定今后不能再撒谎。
- Raq试图维持毒品网络，却因 Joaquin 被杀而失去可卡因货源。她与 Lou 的治疗师谈话，想知道 Lou 对自己的真实看法，却被迫面对自身的愧疚与自我评价。
- Kanan借助新的交易关系扩大供货能力，向 Hafiz 提供比 Raq 更高的需求量和更好的价格，逐步动摇 Raq 原有的供应链。
- Famous 遭到杀害。警方线索显示，Snaps 和 Pop 可能利用腐败警察除掉他；Kanan得知后决定追查并复仇。
- Raq、Kanan、Marvin及各自手下的冲突持续升级。Marvin主张谨慎布局，Kanan则越来越倾向于直接报复。

### 重要人物行动

- **Kanan**：承认误杀 Lou；向 Jarita 透露真实身份；拓展自己的供货网络；准备追究 Famous 之死，并考虑对 Raq展开反击。
- **Raq**：隐瞒 Lou 的死因；试图修复与上游供应商的关系；借心理治疗寻找 Lou 留下的评价；继续多线应对外部敌人。
- **Marvin**：得知真相后几乎杀死 Kanan，最终被 Jukebox 阻止；在悲痛中重新将注意力放到生意和女儿身上。
- **Jukebox**：阻止 Marvin 当场杀死 Kanan，并继续保护家人。
- **Jarita**：识破 Kanan与 Raq的关系；退出与 Kanan的合作，同时答应帮他查一件与警方有关的事。
- **Garcia**：追查 Shawn Figueroa 被杀案，得知 Snaps和Pop可能涉案；确认第117分局受到联邦调查，随后继续寻找更多证据。
- **Captain Baptiste**：被迫退休，其经手的 Figueroa 案因此受到污染质疑。
- **Hafiz**：在更高利润和更大需求的诱惑下，转向与 Kanan合作，背离 Raq。

### 新出现或被明确的名字与术语

- **Captain Baptiste**：第117分局警监，已被迫退休。
- **Preston Tanner**：联邦调查局探员，负责调查警局内部腐败。
- **Ishmael Henry / Stephanie Henry**：Snaps和Pop的本名。
- **Hafiz**：重要货源或进口商，原本与 Raq合作。
- **Pino**：与 Hafiz一方有关、可能反对换供应商的人。
- **Joaquin**：原有可卡因货源联系人，已被杀。
- **Habib**：受到严密保护的目标人物。
- **“pocket aces” / “silver bullet”**：Lou的治疗师用来形容 Jukebox，意思是 Marvin手中最强的王牌。
- **“L’chaim”**：意第绪语祝酒词，意为“为生命干杯”。
- **Onyx《Bacdafucup》**：片中出现的90年代嘻哈音乐引用，强化时代背景。

### 连续性关键信息

- Lou的真正死因已向 Marvin、Kanan和观众明确：Kanan开枪导致 Lou死亡，Raq负责善后和隐瞒。
- Kanan与 Jarita的关系建立在欺骗之上，但感情确实存在；两人暂时维持联系，信任已经严重受损。
- Famous之死与 Snaps、Pop及腐败警察网络相连，可能成为后续报复和警方调查的交汇点。
- Raq的供应链正在崩解；Kanan开始拥有独立于母亲的货源和交易能力。
- 第117分局、Howard案、Burke案与 Figueroa案已被联邦调查串联起来，Garcia成为少数仍被视为清廉的警察。
- Marvin逐渐把 Jukebox视为自己反击敌人的核心力量，也开始重新承担父亲责任。

### 笑话与文化引用

- 警局里流传 Donaldson 把嫌犯忘在汽车后备箱直到周一的荒诞故事；Billy Martin 的拦查故事被称为“经典”。
- “不要查看礼物马的牙齿”被改造成关于供应合同的讽刺说法。
- Marvin和Lou-Lou治疗师以“扯平等于炼狱”讨论复仇：单纯报复无法消除失去亲人的痛苦。
- Kanan获赠新车时调侃母亲曾想给他买车，自己却退回，因为“没有什么东西是免费的”；这一点凸显他对 Raq控制方式的反感。

### 未解决的线索

- 谁具体指使了 Famous 的谋杀？Snaps和Pop究竟承担了多大责任？
- 哪些纽约警察参与了 Figueroa 案及 Famous 之死？
- Garcia最后答应 Jarita调查的具体事项是什么？
- Kanan是否会真正对 Raq动手，还是只打击她的供应链和声望？
- Hafiz背离 Raq后，Stefano及其盟友会如何回应？
- Marvin能否借助 Jukebox反击，同时避免把女儿再次拖入暴力冲突？

## S05E07

Episode Plot

Raq’s position collapses after Flossie Siegal cuts ties with Marvin and informs Pino Bernardi that Raq knew about the drug switch connected to the judge’s death. Stefano, Snaps, Pop, Breeze, and their allies now treat the Thomases as exposed targets. Federal agents also pressure Raq over a property sale allegedly tied to a money-laundering scheme.

Kanan joins Snaps and Pop’s operation but learns they ordered Famous’s murder because they suspected him of cooperating with police. He confronts them, conceals his anger, and accepts their demand that he prove his loyalty by killing someone close to home. Snaps and Pop then send him after Raq, while Marvin is assigned to handle the operation.

Kanan’s attempt to intimidate Flossie backfires: she abandons Raq, though she promises not to harm him. Jarita ends her relationship with Kanan after realizing he manipulated her and used her against Flossie.

Marvin and Raq recognize that their organization is nearly destroyed. Marvin admits that money and street power cannot repair the family’s emotional damage, especially Raq’s estrangement from Kanan. Raq searches for Unique through Pernessa, only to learn that Unique and Kadeem have left New York.

Important Character Actions

Kanan embraces his new affiliation while insisting that he works for himself, not to satisfy Snaps and Pop. He confronts them about Famous but does not openly retaliate.

Marvin protects Kanan from being immediately targeted, yet accepts that he may have to fight him. He prepares to attack a pawnshop and later confronts Kanan at a church, warning that the psychological damage of this life will follow him.

Raq refuses the federal agents’ proposed cooperation deal and denies knowingly laundering money through the property sale. She begins preparing emergency contingencies, including a case meant to reach her daughter if she dies.

Jukebox worries about Marvin’s federal exposure and openly challenges his denial that everything is going wrong. She also rejects Iesha’s attempt to resume their relationship, partly to protect her.

Snaps and Pop manipulate Kanan by denying responsibility for Famous’s death and framing his doubts as disloyalty. They require him to kill Raq as proof that he belongs to them.

New Names and Terms

Flossie Siegal: Marvin’s former contact and Raq’s business associate; she cuts ties with Raq after learning that Kanan knew about the drug substitution.

Jarita: Kanan’s former romantic connection, whom he used in his attempt to pressure Flossie.

Hafiz: A major supplier or criminal partner who discusses eliminating Raq.

Pino Bernardi: Stefano’s superior or business partner; Raq’s value depends on whether she can continue generating money for him.

Tramont: A hostile figure who refuses to restore Raq’s access to work and declares her isolated.

Agent Tanner: Federal investigator connected to the money-laundering inquiry.

Joint Terrorism Task Force: The federal unit investigating the buyer of Raq’s property.

Pernessa and Kadeem: People connected to Unique; Pernessa says Unique and Kadeem have left New York permanently.

Alize and pruno: Alize is cheap flavored liquor; pruno is improvised prison alcohol, used in Snaps’s mockery of a disloyal associate.

Continuity-Relevant Facts

Famous’s death is attributed to Snaps and Pop, who suspected he was talking to police. Kanan now knows the truth but has not yet acted against them.

Raq has lost Flossie, much of her product and distribution network, and most of her political protection. Stefano’s faction considers her and Marvin fair game.

Federal authorities possess surveillance of Raq meeting Stefano and Pino, evidence from the property transaction, and a potential money trail involving an inflated sale price and an alleged broker kickback.

Marvin remains emotionally attached to Kanan despite Kanan’s betrayal and violence. Their conflict is now both a family crisis and a direct criminal threat.

Breeze is emerging as a dangerous rival. Marvin and Raq compare him with former threats such as DefCon, High Post, and Unique, but admit that their old control of the streets is fading.

Jukebox discovers that Iesha possesses a gun, reinforcing the danger surrounding their relationship and Jukebox’s family.

Jokes and References

Marvin claims his argument with Captain Baptiste at the retirement party was only about a Jets bet and dinner at Peter Luger, an intentionally weak explanation during the investigation into Baptiste’s death.

Marvin calls himself “Carl Moses,” comparing his ability to evade police to the biblical Moses.

Snaps uses “Fool me once…” before threatening an associate who secretly ran a side business.

The episode’s music reinforces its themes: “The Good Life” contrasts polished criminal success with concealed sadness, while “Little Ghetto Boy” frames Kanan’s future as a child being pushed toward adult responsibility and violence.

Unresolved Threads

Will Kanan actually attempt to kill Raq, or turn on Snaps and Pop after confirming their role in Famous’s murder?

Will Marvin protect Kanan or obey the order to eliminate him?

Can Raq evade the federal money-laundering investigation and the criminal organizations now targeting her?

What is inside the case Raq gives the pastor, and will it reach her daughter?

Will Jukebox protect Iesha, end the relationship permanently, or become involved in the gun-related danger?

How will Raq and Marvin respond to Breeze’s growing power and the loss of their former territory?

## S05E08

# Power Book III: Raising Kanan — S05E08 Summary

## Episode Plot
The season finale centers on **Raq's desperate endgame**: cornered by Stefano Marchetti and the FBI, she attempts to trade intelligence on the Italian mob (Stefano and Pino Bernardi) for full immunity and witness protection for herself **and Kanan**. The Feds offer a standard "Queen for a Day" proffer deal but require verified intel first. Meanwhile, **Pino Bernardi cuts his losses** — he knows Kanan killed Marvin — and hands Raq over to Stefano, who now controls her fate. **Kanan rejects the FBI overture** (Agent Tanner visits his apartment; Kanan kicks him out), refusing to violate the code he was raised with. **Jukebox reveals her long game**: she orchestrated the murders of the Bellotti brothers (cooperating witnesses in the Hempstead pill-ring case) and tells Raq she wants her to *live* — alone, stripped of everyone she loves — which Jukebox declares "way worse than bein' dead." The episode closes with Raq urging Kanan to hear Stefano out, a flashback to Kanan's infancy (Marvin and Lou-Lou debating how tough he needs to be), and a voice-over rap framing the entire saga as a **Southside Jamaica Queens story** that is now Kanan's to carry.

## Important Character Actions
- **Raq**: Negotiates with Bernardi → FBI; demands dual immunity; admits she "put Kanan in this shit" and takes full blame; begs Kanan to talk to Stefano.
- **Kanan**: Refuses FBI deal ("I ain't going out like that"); tells Unique "Fuck the Feds"; later accepts Raq's plea to meet Stefano.
- **Jukebox**: Confronts Raq; confesses she killed the Bellotti brothers; declares psychological warfare — "I want you to have nothin'… just like me."
- **Unique**: Caught between Stefano and Raq; relays Stefano's offer (Raq lives if she does one job, then leaves NYC forever); warns Kanan the Feds will come for him.
- **Stefano**: Accepts Raq from Pino; sets terms — one task, 24-hour exit, never return; implies body disposal via smelting factories.
- **Pino Bernardi**: Betrays Raq ("She's done to my business what pantyhose did to finger fuckin'"); gives Stefano permission to kill her.
- **Marvin / Lou-Lou (flashback)**: Marvin insists infant Kanan must "toughen the fuck up"; Lou-Lou protects him ("I ain't never gonna let nothin' bad ever happen to you").

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Queen for a Day agreement** — standard proffer/immunity offer used by prosecutors.
- **Early Tyler** — Lump's young associate, murdered; Lump notes his killer was recently "taken out himself."
- **Glen & Shane Bellotti** — cooperating suspects in Hempstead pill-ring investigation, murdered (by Jukebox's crew).
- **Doris DeFazio** — Stefano's childhood neighbor/crush; the "first and best kiss" that fueled his lifelong rivalry with Pino.
- **187 / 188 Fourth Avenue** — childhood addresses of Pino and Stefano.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- **Marvin's death** is now common street knowledge ("current scuttlebutt"); Bernardi uses it to pressure Raq.
- **Kanan killed Famous** (confirmed by Unique to Raq in prior ep, referenced here).
- **Judge & Colombian connect** both dead due to Raq's bad product — Stefano cites this as why she's "done."
- **Snaps & Pop** are dead (killed by Stefano's order per Unique); Lump eulogizes them at the wake.
- **Raq's supply line (Flossie)** is gone; she has no product, no plug, no territory.
- **Kanan's pill-ring link** (Hempstead) is real — FBI has him on wire/meetings; he denies it but Burke has photos.
- **Thomas family fracture**: Marvin gone, Lou-Lou sidelined, Jukebox turned enemy, Kanan drifting toward Stefano.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **Richard Gere / gerbil urban legend** — banter between Unique and his guys (classic 90s pop-culture myth).
- **"Parmesan-ass nigga"** — Unique's slur for Stefano (Italian heritage).
- **Captains Courageous (1937)** — Pino's "greatest moment" watching it at the Savoy with Doris.
- **"I'ma fall for this flag before I fuck with these feds"** — Kanan's patriotic refusal (ironic given his criminal life).
- **Southside origin rap** — closing track name-checks Prince, Preme, E-Z Wider, 50-on-500, Cat, Pap — lore for the *Power* universe pre-history.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Will Kanan actually meet Stefano, and what "one thing" will Stefano demand?** (Implied: a hit or betrayal.)
2. **Can Raq survive Stefano's 24-hour clock?** She has no crew, no product, no protection.
3. **Jukebox's endgame** — she's alive, armed, and explicitly targeting everyone Raq loves (Kanan, Lou-Lou, etc.).
4. **Lou-Lou's role** — he's at the wake but silent; does he step up or fold?
5. **FBI's next move** — Raq's proffer is pending; Kanan rejected his side deal; Burke/Hickson still have the Kanan surveillance.
6. **Unique's loyalty** — he delivered Stefano's terms but warned Kanan; whose side is he on when shooting starts?
7. **The Thomas legacy** — flashbookends frame Kanan as the heir to "this Southside story"; Season 6 (if any) inherits his choice.

## S05E03

## Power Book III: Raising Kanan — S05E03 Summary

### Episode Plot
The episode opens with a Hundun (chaos demon) myth framing, paralleling Raq's unseen monstrosity. Lou-Lou is found dead, dumped by Unique's crew. The Thomas family reels: Marvin wants immediate vengeance on Unique's leverage (Pernessa and son Jerome), while Raq restrains him, knowing war with Unique + Stefano Marchetti means no turning back. Kanan, manipulated by Raq, confronts her about Fame/Krystal/Dad murders — she denies, he doesn't believe her. Their relationship fractures completely; Kanan walks away. Meanwhile, Unique courts Snaps & Pop (old-guardconnectors to Italian supply) proposing a Southside corporation with Kanan as partner. Kanan meets Unique, says he's only about business now — uncle's death didn't come up. Marvin visits Unique in church, swears vengeance ("he'll meet me before he meets God") but Unique denies killing Lou. Police side: Burke's captain retires, tells Garcia to solve Howard's murder for commissioner track; Garcia presses Amber (witness to Lovett killings) who lies about a white tattooed shooter — Garcia sees through it. Breeze (Kanan's uncle-by-street) arrives, schools younger crew on operations, warns "shit will get perilous." Episode ends with Marvin and Raq mourning Lou at his funeral; Marvin breaks down, admits he can't carry it. Famous's ghost haunts Kanan via Wu-Tang "Shame on a Nigga" needle drop.

### Important Character Actions
- **Raq**: Contains Marvin's rage, lies to Kanan about murders, positions herself as protector while enabling the violence.
- **Kanan**: Confronts Raq with accumulated evidence, severs ties ("You and me, we don't mean nothin' to each other no more"), aligns with Unique/Snaps/Pop for pure business.
- **Marvin**: Demands Pernessa/Jerome locations, threatens war, breaks at Lou's funeral, visits Unique in church with death threat.
- **Unique**: Denies Lou's murder to Marvin, recruits Kanan + Snaps/Pop for Southside corporation, sends Pernessa/Jerome away for safety.
- **Snaps & Pop**: Broker Unique–Kanan alliance, control Italian supply line, stay above the fray.
- **Breeze**: Returns as elder statesman, sets up pawn-shop operation on Guy Brewer, warns Kanan/Unique about peril.
- **Garcia**: Leans on Amber (threatens foster placement) to flip testimony on Lovett murders.
- **Burke's captain**: Retires, passes Howard case to Garcia as career-maker.

### Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Hundun** — Chinese chaos demon metaphor for Raq (blind, faceless, corrupts without knowing it's a monster).
- **Breeze** — Kanan's street uncle, veteran operator, "hustler DNA," runs pawn-shop front on Guy Brewer.
- **Pernessa** — Unique's partner, mother of Jerome, leverage point.
- **Jerome** — Unique & Pernessa's son, motivation for Pernessa to demand exit.
- **Guy Brewer** — Boulevard location of old pawn shop, new Breeze/Unique/Kanan ops base.
- **Archer** — Avenue where supply chain failed (Taz blamed).
- **DefCon** — Referenced by Snaps/Pop as legacy connection.
- **Taz** — Street lieutenant managing Archer supply.
- **Kadeem Mathis** — Flamboyant jeweler ("dookie chains"), comic relief.

### Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Lou-Lou confirmed dead (dumped in stairwell at Edgemere); Jane Doe ID'd as Black woman leaving scene.
- Raq hired dirty cops to kill Famous (Kanan knows).
- Kanan knows Raq killed Krystal, Fame, and their father.
- Unique has soldiers on bikes monitoring streets ("wired").
- Snaps & Pop control Italian supply (Stefano Marchetti / Phil Russo Jersey connection).
- Phil Russo attacked Snaps/Pop's people; Stefano declares "righteous war" on Russo.
- Burke's old CI is the "Thomas girl" (Jukebox).
- Marvin & Raq are siblings; Jukebox is Marvin's daughter, Kanan's cousin.
- Kanan & Famous were "brothers"; Famous's death still drives Kanan.

### Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- Breeze's "feng shit" vs. Chinese feng shui.
- Stefano's "my cock saved my liver" knife-wound joke.
- Kadeem Mathis "stealing style" banter with Taz/Breeze.
- "Blow Pop nigga / I ain't never been a sucker" battle-rap line.
- Wu-Tang "Shame on a Nigga" closing needle drop — Method Man, ODB, RZA, Rae verses.
- "Southside ain't no different from East New York. Blocks change, work stay the same."

### Unresolved Threads
- Who actually killed Lou? (Unique denies; Marvin suspects; Raq noncommittal; Stefano/Russo war looms.)
- Will Pernessa/Jerome stay hidden or become casualties?
- Can Kanan/Unique/Snaps/Pop alliance hold without Raq/Marvin war erupting?
- Will Amber flip on Lovett murders? (Garcia gave herself a week.)
- Garcia's commissioner track depends on solving Howard — will she cross lines?
- Marvin's mental state — he said "this shit too much for me" and walked out of own brother's funeral.
- Kanan's total break from Raq — is it permanent or tactical?
- Breeze's "no side hustles" warning — will someone break it?
- Stefano vs. Phil Russo war — will it pull Southside crews in?
