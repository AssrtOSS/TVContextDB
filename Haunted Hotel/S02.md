## S02E01

# Haunted Hotel S02E01 — Summary

## Plot
The Freeling family celebrates their 100th guest without a supernatural incident, but magical balloons start chasing people and need popping. Katherine announces the hotel is finally "in the black" and decides the next step toward legitimacy is hiring a real employee. While reviewing applicants, the family discovers that the woman they've been calling "ghost" — the one with a knife in her back who calls Ben "Champ" — has actually been alive the whole time. She's Shayna, granddaughter of the bloodline guardian who has lived in the attic for a year. She reveals her family must always have someone on-site to suppress an "ancient evil" whose name starts with "P" (she guesses "Pete"; Nathan insists that's wrong).

The family hires Catherine Withakay (C/K spelling confusion), a 30-year luxury hotel veteran seeking a "challenge." Overqualified, grounded, and totally normal, Catherine becomes catnip for every supernatural oddity in the building: the Honeymoon Suite flirts with her, Abaddon gets weird holding her hand, Jessica the vent-ghost wants to drag her away. Catherine insists on staying overnight to test the full guest experience.

Purple ooze begins leaking through the vents. Esther identifies it as hellfire and sets her mattress ablaze. Catherine disappears overnight and is found outside in a trance; Nathan diverts guests while Ben and Esther pull her out. The ooze is a mind-controlling agent forcing people to dig up something underground. Catherine saw a binding symbol in her trance — she needs to get close to stop it. The family convinces Shayna to return, offering her a real room with a bathroom this time.

They race through a hotel now half-consumed by ooze with the Callahan wedding four hours out. They reach a banquet hall where a giant eye — the ancient evil — is surfacing. Catherine stabs it. The god is put back to sleep; reburying the eye buys roughly three years.

Afterward, Catherine has no memory of the night beyond "a wonderful stay" and is let go as "not a good fit." Abaddon confesses his crush and begs her to stay (offering roof-sleeping and rock-throwing at dogs); she leaves calling him "little boy." Shayna — chased someone, pepper-sprayed her way out of a gas station — is hired on the spot.

## Key Character Actions
- Katherine: drives the hire; admits the hotel "destroys everything good or turns it to evil"; fires Catherine; entrusts Shayna with HR approval from Ben.
- Nathan: distracts guests during the trance rescue; rallies the team against the ooze; delivers the bad news that the god's name isn't Pete.
- Ben: made Head of HR; tracks Shayna down, then ultimately calls her for backup; notes the ooze resistance as an HR issue.
- Esther: torches her own mattress with hellfire; provides the occult vocabulary ("everything's a ritual").
- Abaddon: crushes on Catherine hard (flower delivery, "you intrigue me"); goes catatonic with heartbreak; gets briefly distracted by a mouse mid-grief.
- Shayna: exposes the family's bloodline duty; serves as anti-ooze (the ooze falls off when she approaches); becomes the new hire.

## New Names / Terms
- Shayna — bloodline guardian, replaces her grandfather, former attic squatter, new employee
- Catherine Withakay — "C/K" spelling gag, Swiss Alps / Cairo pedigree, fired
- Pete — running red-herring name for the ancient evil (explicitly wrong)
- Bog boys — referenced as Abaddon's past captors; tied to the banana shortage
- Callahan wedding — four-hour deadline that gets displaced to another hotel
- The binding symbol — Catherine saw it in trance; still needs deciphering
- The ancient evil / cosmic god — manifested as a giant eye, now re-buried

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- The family hit 100 incident-free guests; the hotel turned profitable for the first time.
- The Freeling hotel now has a formal-ish HR structure (Ben = Head of HR).
- Shayna's bloodline guardian role is now public within the family; she is promised a proper room.
- The Undervale has a re-buried cosmic eye giving roughly three years of peace before the next dig cycle.
- Catherine "Withakay with a K" exists in-universe as a fired former employee — a ghost-of-a-coworker that could recur.
- The unseen "bog boys" are now part of Abaddon's backstory vocabulary.

## Jokes / References Worth Carrying Forward
- Abaddon's "in the black" → "eternal darkness, finally!" bit.
- "Everything's a ritual" — brushing teeth, changing clothes, sacrificing goats to a sea monster.
- Nathan's skin-condition gag for refusing handshakes (ghost).
- "Doors are traps!" — Abaddon's travel philosophy.
- Mothman's cave — established off-site location; Abaddon wanted to egg it.
- Honeymoon Suite hitting on a new hire, then pivoting to Abaddon after Catherine leaves.
- The Hamlet "Laertes" speech Nathan quotes to flirt.
- Shayna's arsenal — banshee talon, melted-toothbrush knife, pepper spray origin story.
- "Champ" as Shayna's name for Ben.
- "Catherine with a C / Withakay with a K" as a recurring spelling gag.
- Purple ooze as a recurring ooze/mess motif (toasters, vents, mattresses).
- "House settling" — euphemism Katherine uses for supernatural noises.
- The hotel ruining a wedding and quietly relocating it — running gag.

## Unresolved Threads
- The cosmic god's true name (starts with P, definitely not Pete).
- The binding symbol Catherine glimpsed — not yet deciphered.
- The three-year countdown on the buried eye.
- Shayna's full pre-attic backstory: who she was running from and whom she was chasing.
- Bog boys — identity, location, whether they'll return for Abaddon.
- Abaddon's first heartbreak, still raw; may resurface.
- The ghosts' collective comment that they "don't deserve a qualified employee" — possible arc of self-reflection.
- Why Catherine became supernatural catnip; the vents still remember her.

## S02E06

# S02E06 — Death Comes to the Undervale

## Plot
A heavily armed stranger, Dante Crowe, arrives at the Undervale claiming an apocalypse is imminent. He locks Katherine, Ben, and Esther in the women's bathroom while Nathan slips through the wall. Crowe reveals he can physically touch ghosts because a childhood curse has kept him between the worlds of the living and the dead since age ten.

Crowe explains his mission: three beings called the Omens — messengers of Death — are converging on the hotel. The prophecy: *"Banished to the three corners of the earth, the Omens shall reunite. And from death, Death shall arise."* Katherine initially wants to flee, then changes her mind and stays to keep an unpredictable stranger out of her hotel.

Ben impresses Crowe with his perceptiveness (noticing the gun on entry) and his willingness to listen, and is named Crowe's sidekick — callsign "Shorts." Katherine becomes "Eyes." Crowe gives Ben weapons training, fastens a binding-spell anklet on him, and orders Katherine to evacuate every ghost in the building.

The family splits up to fight two of the Omens:

- **Leaf Omen** — a walking tree. Ben and Nathan lure it into the gardening shed, then Esther uses a grenade to blow it into the well behind the hotel.
- **Fire Omen** — Esther and Katherine trigger a backdraft in a wallpapered room to incinerate it.

After the fights, the evacuated ghosts return, demanding to touch Crowe. Their touch overloads him; the three Omens' hearts are destroyed, and Death herself manifests. She greets Crowe familiarly: *"Dante. Good to see you again."*

Death has wired the hotel with explosives while the family was locked in the bathroom. Crowe and Death apparently have a long personal history — Ben flatly states they dated. Death's stated plan: bring the hotel down on top of her.

Katherine refuses to be a supporting character in Crowe's story. She orders her family to make their own plan: she will collect the bombs, Esther and Ben will drag Death outside. Nathan bear-hugs Death out of the building. Esther quotes Dickinson outside the door: *"Because I could not stop for Death, she kindly stopped for me."*

Outside, Crowe asks Esther to help finish the binding spell so Death cannot escape. She refuses. Crowe drops a personal revelation: a prophecy says Esther will become the savior of all humanity. Esther's response: *good to know, still not helping.* Crowe retrieves his weapons from his car trunk. The bombs, now in Katherine's arms in the yard, begin beeping faster.

Abaddon returns to the hotel, injured, to rebuild his nest — declaring his subplot of escaping Katherine's control complete.

## Important Character Actions
- **Abaddon** — opens the episode trying to leave with a chip clip and "shiny smellies," declares the women's bathroom "Abaddonia," goes into the forest, fights something, returns wounded and ready to nest.
- **Katherine** — changes from wanting to flee the Omens to staying; later refuses to be a side character in Crowe's story and takes charge of bomb disposal.
- **Nathan** — slips through the wall to find help, accidentally confronts Crowe alone first, helps Ben with the Leaf Omen, bear-hugs Death out of the building.
- **Ben** — becomes Crowe's right-hand man "Shorts"; trained with a weapon harness; fights the Leaf Omen; states the obvious about Crowe and Death dating; refuses Crowe's request to bind Death.
- **Esther** — tries Babylonian offensive magic ("Perdere invasorem. Kuathe gish."); fights the Fire Omen via backdraft; quotes Dickinson; refuses Crowe's binding-spell help despite learning she is prophesied to be humanity's savior.
- **Crowe** — arrives armed to "save the world," recruits Ben, deploys binding anklets, dispatches the Omens one by one ("Rest in pieces, asshole"), reveals long personal history with Death.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Dante Crowe** — the gun-toting guest; can touch ghosts because of an orphanage curse from age ten; has a personal history with Death.
- **The Omens** — three messengers of Death. Two are fought on-screen:
  - **Leaf Omen** — ambulatory tree defeated by grenade blast into a well.
  - **Fire Omen** — defeated by a backdraft.
  - A third Omen is referenced but not fought this episode.
- **Death** — personified female entity, romantically entangled with Crowe, plans to collapse the hotel on herself as part of a larger scheme with a hinted "step two."
- **Abaddonia** — Abaddon's self-declared kingdom in the women's bathroom.
- **Binding spell** — ankle-ward magic that prevents Omens from using the wearer as a conduit.
- **Silver stake** — weapon effective against Omens (Ben instinctively uses one).
- **"Shorts" / "Eyes"** — Crowe's callsigns for Ben and Katherine.
- **"Effleurage"** — Crowe's proposed all-clear code word; cut off mid-explanation.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Crowe can interact physically with ghosts because of a childhood curse that has kept him between living and dead since age ten at an orphanage.
- Ghost census has not been taken since the 1950s; the hotel holds somewhere between 80 and 340 ghosts (including headless ones, excluding multi-headed ones).
- Death rigged the hotel walls with explosives while the family was locked in the women's bathroom — the wall-drilling noise they heard was bomb placement, not renovation.
- A prophecy exists naming Esther as "the savior of all humanity."
- Crowe recognizes Esther's magic as Babylonian on sight.
- Crowe states Ben has "instincts you can't teach" — first explicit prophecy-style beat about Ben's destiny as a spirit conduit with combat potential.
- Katherine was about to announce a hotel-wide scavenger hunt at the top of the episode; cancelled by the Crowe intrusion.

## Jokes and References Useful for Later Episodes
- **Catchphrase:** Crowe says *"Rest in pieces, asshole"* after slaying both Omens — Ben deadpans *"he just says that every time."*
- **Nicknames:** "Shorts" (Ben) and "Eyes" (Katherine) are Crowe's shorthand and may recur.
- **Nathan's Chevy Impala:** opens his chitchat with Crowe — college-era crash into a tree. Confirms Nathan attended college and owned a beat-up car.
- **Annabelle's reaction** to Ben's touch-power is sexual/possessive; useful for future ghost-girlfriend beats.
- **Esther's chair army** pitch to Crowe — *"I could curse these chairs so they could fight for us"* — signals she wants to weaponize household objects, a likely future gag.
- **Esther quoting Dickinson** to Death establishes her as literary as well as occult.
- **"They dated" / "Tie down like marriage"** — Nathan and Esther reading Crowe and Death's dynamic; the romantic subtext is now canon for future episodes.
- **"My chip clip" / "shiny smellies"** — running Abaddon possessions bit.
- **"Effleurage"** — code word gag aborted mid-setup; the lighter "massage" innuendo is on the table for later.
- **"I'm single, by the way"** — Katherine flirts awkwardly with Crowe in passing; establish a beat the show may reuse.
- **Nathan's scavenger hunt** was set up at episode start and never happened — potential thread for a later family-morale episode.

## Unresolved Threads
- **Death's "step two"** — explicitly described as *"messier"* than collapsing the hotel; the larger apocalyptic plan is intact.
- **Crowe and Death's relationship** — confirmed romantic history ("seen the beginning, the end, and everything in between") with no full exposition; motivations of both sides unclear.
- **Third Omen** — Crowe says three Omens, only two are dispatched on-screen; the fate of the third is not addressed.
- **Binding spell incomplete** — Death escaped before the spell finished; remains unbound and free to act.
- **Bombs beeping faster** — placed in Katherine's arms in the yard; immediate detonation threat unresolved at episode end.
- **Prophecy about Esther** — newly revealed but unexamined; her refusal to help Crowe despite knowing it positions her against Death's plan, not for it.
- **Ben's destiny** — Crowe's "instincts you can't teach" line marks him as more than a conduit; future combat/training payoff implied.
- **Abaddon's forest encounter** — he left injured but never explains what attacked him or what happened in the woods; likely tied to the third Omen or the cult teased in Season 1's finale.
- **Crowe's weapons** — retrieved from car trunk at episode end, deployment deferred.
- **The well behind the hotel** — site of the Leaf Omen's defeat; structural damage and possible supernatural contamination not addressed.
- **Katherine's scavenger hunt** — cancelled by the incursion; left on the table for family-bonding payoff later.

## S02E02

# Haunted Hotel — S02E02 概要

## 剧情主线
Ben 为纪念与鬼魂女友 Annabelle 1937 年被勒死前的最后一顿晚宴，重新举办了同样的晚宴，并声称准备了一份"改变人生的惊喜"。Katherine 担心 Ben 会向 Annabelle 求婚，刻意避开自己曾约会过的 Joel（由 Ben 偷偷代发了邀请）。与此同时，Esther 因 Basilisk Eye 的猎杀矛盾假装和 Abaddon 绝交，将其引到酒店地下的旧矿井；Abaddon 误把一处深坑当成陷害手段，结果两人一同掉入坑底，遇上一头对魔法免疫的巨兽 **无尽之喉（The Ceaseless Maw）**。晚宴上菜式接连失控，Annabelle 与 Ben 因"乌龟"和"求婚"误会当场分手；Joel 帮 Katherine 做清汤时两人破冰，但 Joel 离开前目睹了 Maw 的爆发。最终 Maw 冲出地面被 Heather 一瓶防狼喷雾喷退，众人解除危机，晚宴以尴尬而略温馨的方式收尾。

## 关键角色动作
- **Ben**：筹办晚宴，自以为惊喜是一只象征"陪伴到永远"的乌龟（其实是 Ben 因担心异地而想留给 Annabelle 的信物）；误把求婚的说法闹得满城皆知，最后当众"求婚式送龟"，反被 Annabelle 甩。
- **Annabelle**：全程高压控制男神，频繁扇 Ben、穿墙消失、再折返追加怒吼；自曝原计划是给 Ben 灌毒药让她变鬼永远在一起，因看见 Maw 差点杀死 Ben 才主动提分手。
- **Katherine**：全剧核心操盘手，既要拆散 Ben 的求婚企图，又要应付 Joel 的旧情复燃，还要管理失控的晚宴；最终对 Joel 承认"对感情的看法是错的"，但 Joel 已决定回家哭一场。
- **Nathan**：被迫与灌木里的鬼魂 **Gert** 相亲，全程尴尬发挥"bro-speak"；最后评价"pretty good party"，并点出 Randy 存在能让 Maw 不再出没。
- **Joel**：Katherine 的约会对象，2024 年那场被杀人犯袭击后的旧识；随身带防狼喷雾出场，主动帮做"plain soup"，最后评价"pretty good party"后离开，留下一句"call me"。
- **Esther / Abaddon**：二人因 basilisk 眼睛反目闹绝交，在矿井里一路追逐掉进坑底；对峙中 Esther教 Abaddon 道歉/原谅，Maw 出现后又共用 basilisk eye 活下来。Abaddon 学到的核心教训：**朋友吵架不一定要互相残杀，可以道歉并原谅**。
- **Randy**：Ben 的鬼魂好朋友（首次正式登场），负责娱乐环节，做了一堆关于 Katherine 的尴尬模仿；后来被 Nathan 视作抑制 Maw 的关键。
- **Stabby Paul**：对 Katherine表现出执念，被 Joel视为威胁；最后 Shayna 抱怨 Paul 太黏人而甩了他。

## 新登场 / 新确立的名字与术语
- **The Ceaseless Maw（无尽之喉）**：地底古老巨兽，对魔法免疫，"贪得无厌、死活不肯让人骑"。被 Heather 的防狼喷雾暂时击退，似乎会被 Randy 牵制。
- **Gert**：住在灌木丛里的最安静、最古老鬼魂朋友，Undervale 当年作精神病院时就在，曾被丈夫诊断为"女性疯狂病"；会用意念做"plain soup"。
- **Randy**：Ben 的鬼魂好朋友，常做过于具体的模仿秀，自称很忙但实际很闲；本集首次正面登场。
- **Shayna**：曾被 Stabby Paul 拿来当相亲对象参考的女性鬼魂；本集与 Paul 短暂约会后甩掉 Paul，理由是"太黏人"。
- **Heather**：Esther 唯一的人类朋友，本集喷退 Maw 立大功，结尾被 Abaddon 当作"纯真恶魔之子"来赞美。
- **Friedrich**：到场心理学家幽灵。
- **Emily Ghost**：1930 年代上流社会女鬼。
- **Basilisk Eye**：Basilisk 死后可分到的稀有素材，Abaddon 抢着独占。
- **The Attic Strangler**：Annabelle 当年谋杀她的凶手，本集透露他藏了一瓶毒药。
- **The Tommy Dorsey**：Annabelle 1937 年派对曾邀请的嘉宾（历史背景）。
- **Pantry（储藏室）**：Ben 求助时当作会回应他的对象，本身带一点拟人属性。

## 连续性相关事实
- 鬼魂可在"同一天死亡"的同伴之间交换死亡时所穿的服装（Annabelle 与 Grady、Emily 之间的"换装"机制）。
- Annabelle 的死亡细节补完：1937 年在自家晚宴上被 Attic Strangler 勒死，时间是甜点之前。
- Katherine 与 Joel 的过去：曾因一场约会被连环杀手袭击，之后 Joel 在复印店、Katherine 在处理 ooze 中渐渐失联。
- Ben 仍是 spirit conduit，但本集未强调能力，仅在穿越墙时被 Nathan 一句"Right. I'm a ghost"点出身份反差。
- "Katherine 没办好酒店，也没搞定男人"由 Randy 公开吐槽，呼应她离婚、独自经营酒店的处境。
- Nathan透露自己曾瞒着家人买摩托车、出事故后骗家人说是被自动扶梯夹断腿，进一步补完其荒诞青年史。
- Abaddon 与 Esther 的"结伴猎杀 + 平分战利品"契约被打破又修复：眼珠在二人重归于好后再次共享。
- Randy 被设定为可压制 Maw 的存在，为后续剧情埋下伏笔。

## 笑点 / 梗与可复用的桥段
- **Ben 的"求婚"假象**：晚宴请柬写"a life-changing surprise"，Katherine、Nathan、Heather 全部脑补成订婚，最终 Ben 拿出的是一只乌龟，Katherine 只能苦笑"死乌龟 + 担心儿子向鬼求婚 = 我的人生"。
- **Wink 梗**：Ben 自称不会眨眼，只能用"wink, wink"形容表情；后面反复被 Heather、Annabelle 当面模仿/吐槽。
- **Nathan 与灌木约会**：把"branch out（拓展交际）"双关用在字面树枝上，再夸 Gert 的名字"真短"。
- **Randy 的极具体模仿**：模仿 Katherine 睡着时的脸、假装挠嘴唇抠鼻子，被 Katherine 当场叫停。
- **Annabelle 的"两次 storm out"**：第一次冲出去又折返追加怒吼，结尾再来一次"我恐怕又得冲出去了"。
- **Basilisk 矛盾**：Abaddon 抢走本该分给 Esther 的眼睛，还振振有词"Yes, but then I got bored"，凸显其"小孩式残忍"。
- **"Plain soup"贯穿**：Gert 的招牌清汤是心电感应；Joel 帮 Katherine 现场熬了一锅"温水加一点胡萝卜"，被 Ben 当场点评——这是 Joel 与 Katherine 重归于好的契机。
- **Joel 的防狼喷雾两次立功**：对 Maw、对 Esther 抱怨都用上了"this mace is good"。
- **Pizza 外送小哥**：水/披萨双职业、自称单身（"Not legally, but my wife kicked me out"），吐槽网站小费他一分钱拿不到——可作为人物在后续出现的固定笑点。
- **Honeymoon Suite 与灵魂象征意**：Ben 说出"A gift to live as long as our love"时直接致敬 Honeymoon Suite 的"陪到永远"人设。

## 未解悬念 / 后续线索
- **Ceaseless Maw**：被击退但未消灭，Nathan 强调"Randy 在它就不会回来"——如果 Randy 离开酒店会怎样？
- **Annabelle 的毒药瓶**：仍在 Attic Strangler 藏匿处，Annabelle 的极端占有欲未消，下一集可能再被利用。
- **Annabelle 与 Ben 的关系**：已分手（"Maybe in another death"），但 Annabelle 显然仍在酒店，二人的羁绊远未结束。
- **Joel 与 Katherine**：电话联系说定但 Joel 暂时退缩，二人会再次约会还是彻底翻篇未定。
- **Stabby Paul 与 Katherine**：Paul 被 Shayna 甩，其对 Katherine 的执念很可能在后续集数继续发作。
- **Basilisk Eye**：目前由 Esther 与 Abaddon 共有，这件魔法道具可能成为后续冒险主线。
- **Basilisk 本身**：被杀死并取眼，是否还有其他同伙或更大 Basilisk 种群未交代。
- **Heather**：作为唯一的人类外援，是否会逐渐加入 Esther 的黑魔法圈子。
- **Abaddon 学到的"原谅"**：本集为他打开了新技能树，后续可能用于对付 Acolytes of Abaddon 等更严肃的威胁。
- **Katherine 与 Nathan 关于"是否该让 Ben 做傻事"的育儿哲学碰撞**：Nathan 援引父母当年禁摩托车反致自己偷买的旧事，暗示 Katherine 的"越禁越想做"理论可能反噬到她自己身上。

## S02E03

# Haunted Hotel — S02E03 概要

## 剧情主线
Esther 和 Ben 受 Katherine 之命去树林小屋里，向被偷走草药的女巫道歉。兄妹俩声称只摘了"花园附近的草药"，女巫 Courtney Vandenberg 当场识破：她煮了一锅诅咒汤，三人被迫灌下。Katherine 因担心儿子而喊"别强迫我儿子喝"，被诅咒"什么都攥不住"；Esther 因撒谎被诅咒"显出内心的恶魔"，背上长出角和尾巴；Ben 则因过敏反应昏倒，被送进医院。

诅咒发作：Ben 的过敏让他无法呼吸，幸好及时用抗组胺药脱险；Esther 长出红色犄角和尾巴，本人却颇以为傲；Katherine 双手无力，连钱包都拿不起来。Ben 主动要求回医院，"专注让他感觉更好"，甚至不愿诅咒消失——因为他的诅咒"让他能看清一切"（背上长满眼球）。

## S02E07

# Haunted Hotel S02E07 — Summary

## Plot
The Undervale's former "ghost mayor" has finished his unfinished business, triggering a jazz funeral at the hotel. Inspired by the event and desperate for a break from endless chores, Nathan announces he is running for the new ghost mayor. The winner gets to wear the special "ghost hat" (which Nathan has wanted since dying). Meanwhile, Katherine plans a rare night off, Ben is given towel-theft-skip task with guest 316, and Abaddon sharpens a long stake.

Katherine meets Miriam Baxtail, the hotel's previous (living) owner from the lobby portrait, who now haunts a woodshed decorated with deer skulls. Miriam ran the Undervale for 45 years using "discipline" and reveals a **concierge** artifact (bellboy/groundskeeper/concierge — three small supernatural helpers in one) hidden on a shelf by the front desk. Katherine is uncomfortable but desperate, and dings the bell. The concierge solves every chore instantly — folding towels, restocking amenities, even producing coffee — and grows extra arms whenever Katherine mentally adds to her workload. She loves it.

The election candidates assemble: Billy the Urchin, Big Monster, The Mist, Shy Katie, Stabby Paul, and a terrifying newcomer called **Dr. Slashface** — a headless ghost who pledges to drive all living people out of the hotel so it belongs only to ghosts. Miriam admits she used the concierge too and was consumed by it over years; her whole plan was to pass it on to Katherine so she could take over once Katherine is gone.

Nathan drafts Esther as campaign manager. She produces an attack ad (edited by her friend Heather) arguing Slashface died outside and is therefore "a weird lake ghost," not a hotel ghost, plus a charge of being "a doctor of kissing butts." The ad airs on the hotel channel, earning boos for Slashface.

Katherine's grip on the concierge slips — it slaps Ben without her intending to, and visibly has back-arms controlling her. She tearfully admits the concierge is the only thing making her feel in control. She agrees to stop using it.

At the eternal-fire election ring, Nathan publicly confesses the attack ad was a lie and demands an honest vote. Voters turn to Slashface anyway. Esther then enters as a fourth candidate. Pressed on whether a living girl can be mayor, she points out there is no rule against it. She then escalates: she openly "death-shames" Slashface by saying he is not alive and cannot flutter a curtain, reframes ghost-vs-living governments as absurd, and convinces the crowd to abandon the leader concept entirely in favor of a council of elders — and then, seizing momentum, they chant "Anarchy! Anarchy!" Slashface tries to crown himself, but the freed electorate declares Dr. Slashface mayor by acclamation, with the hat placed on his head.

Abaddon tries to stop the concierge from reasserting control over Katherine on the roof but is repelled. Ben talks Katherine down by relating to her feelings of powerlessness and volunteering his own hands. She releases the concierge. Back at the hotel, the family jokes about the day's events, including, Nathan notes Abaddon once ran a bar in Hell when Jake (its owner) was away.
