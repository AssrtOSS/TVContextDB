## Relationships Quick-Reference

- **Katherine ↔ Nathan**: Siblings; Katherine younger. Unresolved grief, Nathan’s death circumstances undisclosed to him.
- **Katherine ↔ Ben/Esther**: Mother ↔ children; stressed but loving.
- **Nathan ↔ Ben/Esther**: Uncle ↔ niece/nephew; Nathan becomes Esther’s de facto father figure.
- **Ben ↔ Esther**: Sibling bickering, underlying loyalty.
- **Abaddon ↔ Esther**: Closest bond; demon + child dynamic, mutual weirdness.
- **Abaddon ↔ Family**: Reluctant ally/protector; crawls vents, offers dubious help.
- **Freeling family ↔ Undervale ghosts**: Co-owners/tenants; Katherine wants paying human guests, ghosts want reign of hotel.

## World Setting & Supernatural Rules

- **Location:** Undervale Hotel, upstate New York (on a hill).
- **History:** Former asylum → prison → asylum; won by Nathan in a card game with a hooded stranger (2024).
- **Supernatural hierarchy (rock-paper-scissors):** Ghosts > Demons > Humans > Ghosts.  
  *Humans can banish ghosts; ghosts can overpower demons; demons overpower humans.*
- **Permanent residents:** Dozens of quirky ghosts (Candle Head, Chain Ghost, Dog Catcher Ghost, etc.), the Honeymoon Suite, Abaddon.
- **Netherworld realm (幽冥界/虚空领域):** Where banished ghosts are sent; accessible via spirit conduit.
- **Bloody Mirror (血镜):** Artifact that can summon high-level demons (Mortoth).
- **Death-day loopers (忌日循环者/死亡日轮回者):** Ghosts forced to relive their death repeatedly (e.g., Tony).
- **Conduit (媒介/通灵者):** Living person who can bridge the living/dead; Ben is one — ghosts touch him → shared psychedelic euphoria.

## Overall Plot Premise (Series-Wide)

Katherine Freeling inherits the Undervale Hotel from her late brother Nathan. She moves in with her two kids, Ben and Esther, intending to run a normal business. The hotel turns out to be infested with permanent ghost residents — including Nathan himself, who stays on as a well-meaning but chaotic ghost co-owner. An ancient demon, Abaddon, trapped in a boy’s body since the 1700s, also resides there. Each episode blends **hotel-management sitcom plots** (guests, reviews, rivals, finances) with **supernatural threats** (demons, cults, curses, reality-show ghost hunters). The family’s personal drama — Katherine’s grief, Ben’s adolescence, Esther’s isolation and magic — intertwines with the hotel’s chaos. Season 1 ends with the family accepting the ghosts as partners; Season 2 teases a world-ending cult (Acolytes of Abaddon).

## Speaking Styles (for dialogue tone)

- **Katherine:** Crisp, pragmatic, business jargon mixed with exhausted mom sighs. Sarcastic one-liners. Avoids emotional vulnerability — deflects with tasks.  
  *Subtitle tip:* Use formal/standard register for business scenes; sharper, clipped sentences when stressed.
- **Nathan:** Upbeat, colloquial, 2010s bro-speak (“dude,” “bro,” “super stoked”). Rambles. Deadpan absurdity. As a ghost, no filter.  
  *Subtitle tip:* Keep casual, slightly dated slang; don’t over-polish.
- **Ben:** Teen hesitation — “uh,” “I guess,” “maybe.” Earnest but awkward. Creative tangents (writing, art).  
  *Subtitle tip:* Preserve filler words; slightly softer tone than Katherine.
- **Esther:** Deadpan, macabre vocabulary (“necromancy,” “vessel,” “ritual”). Childish excitement over dark things. Occasional genuine vulnerability.  
  *Subtitle tip:* Mix clinical occult terms with kid-speak; don’t make her sound too adult.
- **Abaddon:** Archaic, grandiose (“I, Abaddon, shall—”), then instantly childish (“that’s not fair!”). Misuses modern tech terms (“the glowing rectangle,” “the face-book”). Refers to self in third person sometimes.  
  *Subtitle tip:* Contrast formal/archaic phrasing with petty complaints; keep third-person self-ref when used.

## Cultural Context & Translation Notes

- **Tone model:** *Bob’s Burgers* meets *Ghosts (US/UK)* meets *Rick and Morty* — family sitcom skeleton, supernatural absurdity flesh, adult-animation gore/edge.
- **Humor sources:** Juxtaposition of mundane hotel ops (Yelp reviews, health inspections) with horror tropes; character-driven dysfunction; Abaddon’s fish-out-of-water modern world confusion.
- **Running gags:** Nathan’s unfinished projects (hedge maze, Forbidden Wing); Abaddon’s vent travel + tech fails; Esther’s zombie dad attempts; Ben’s ghost-conduit euphoria; Honeymoon Suite’s innuendo.
- **Serialization:** Episodic “monster/guest of the week” with season arcs (Katherine-Nathan grief, Ben’s conduit discovery, Esther-Nathan bond, Abaddon’s loyalty test).
- **Visual gags matter:** Subtitles may need to accommodate on-screen text (hotel signs, ghost chalkboards, Esther’s spell books) — check for forced narratives.
- **Profanity/Adult content:** TV-14; strong language (bleeped or not), gore, sexual innuendo. Translate intent, not just literal words.
- **Cultural references:** US pop culture (reality TV, Yelp, TikTok trends, 1700s vs. now). Localize for target audience where obscure; keep flavor where recognizable.
