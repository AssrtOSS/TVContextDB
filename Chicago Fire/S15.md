## S15E02

## S15E02 剧情梗概

51 局迎来新见习消防员（probies）的高强度训练季，同时两条主线并行：米尔斯（Mills）因伤瘫痪后接受理疗、承认再也回不了消防岗位；61 号救护车的维奥莱特（Violet）与莉兹·诺瓦克（Lizzie Novak）救治一名被警方强制收治的精神科患者安妮塔·希金斯（Anita Higgins），随后追查她被违规关押的真相。赫尔曼（Herrmann）的支线是秘密情色小说家身份暴露——出版商逼他出席朗读会，最终他亲自上阵顶替放鸽子的演员。

## 重要角色行动

- 西弗莱德（Severide，车长）与基德（Kidd，中尉）共事引发总部议论；洛文（Lowen）分队长来访考察见习生，私下警告西弗莱德"基德是为你工作，不是与你共事"，且他尚未升分队长却执掌 51 局招人眼红。
- 训练场上巴斯克斯（Vasquez）与见习生伯克（Burke）起冲突，被现场目睹（米尔斯在场外劝说无果），西弗莱德事后令巴斯克斯停班回家；基德认为应上报总部，两人爆发指挥权争执，最终西弗莱德出于"观感"让步让巴斯克斯归队，但巴斯克斯私下向伯克承认责任在自己。
- 钻机救援事故：伯克在钻机下坡侧垫木险些被压，巴斯克斯将其拽出；伤者疑似颅脑损伤送芝加哥医疗中心。
- 维奥莱特与莉兹认定安妮塔非自愿被关，通过 Gaffney 的查尔斯医生和帕克（Parker）获取线索，援引《伊利诺伊州精神卫生法》第 3-804 条（患者对收治提出正式异议后有权到中立机构复检），带警察上门把安妮塔从 Valley Crest 接出送 Chicago Med。维奥莱特由此联想到自己母亲。
- 道森不在场；莉兹的记者追求者雅各布（Jacob）送报纸当"线人"，她最后主动表示愿做他的爆料消息源。
- 米尔斯坐轮椅，神经科检查有进展但自认无法复职；与女友（未具名亲吻对象）互动。
- 赫尔曼的情色小说《Burning Desire》（笔名赛勒斯·梅伯里 Cyrus Mayberry）：穆奇（Mouch）找"拉里叔叔"审合同，发现合同未规定必须本人出席，雇了《泽西男孩》候补演员伊森（Ethan，辛迪表亲家的孩子）顶替；演员开场前两分钟被叫去演弗兰基·瓦利，赫尔曼只好自己上台朗读，大获成功。

# Chicago Fire S15E03 Summary

## Episode Plot
**Main Call**: Anhydrous ammonia leak at a cold-storage facility (35th & Ashland). Squad 3, Truck 81, and Ambo 61 respond. 10–12 workers trapped in the freezer room. Hazmat ETA 14 minutes. During ventilation efforts, a pressure spike blasts Tony (Squad 3) with ammonia when his hood is torn off — he's hospitalized overnight but cleared for next shift.

**Valve Investigation**: Kidd and Severide discover someone manually turned a distribution valve, doubling/tripling pressure. Rust shavings prove deliberate action. The four probies on perimeter duty are suspects. McCaffrey eventually confesses: he thought he was shutting off gas, panicked, and lied. Worse, he went over Kidd's head and used family connections (father = state senator Allen McCaffrey; grandfather = CFD legend Bill McCaffrey) to pressure the Deputy Commissioner. Kidd, with Boden's backing, recommends termination from CFD entirely. McCaffrey is cut loose; he threatens "this isn't over."

**Cruz Arc**: Cruz has spiraled post-rehab — hasn't left house in a week, skipping PT, severe headaches, depressive "buried in sand" mindset. Herrmann and Severide visit; Cruz rejects Herrmann's gift (Otis's cane) and snaps at their concern. Later appears briefly at Molly's for breakfast but leaves early. Unresolved: whether he'll return to PT/duty.

**Novak/Vasquez/Romance**: Bystander "Bryan with a Y" gives Novak his number at the ammonia scene. Vasquez runs library-card database (285 matches) to ID him. Novak texts; they meet at a karaoke bar. Vasquez admits he missed his chance with Novak (she confessed at Herrmann's vow renewal). Novak and Bryan hit it off.

**Karaoke Bar Electrocution**: Ambo 61 catches a second call — karaoke singer electrocuted by faulty mic; second victim shocked trying to help. Novak and Violet save both. Manager comps drinks for life → team plans return.

**Boden Mentors Kidd**: Now First Deputy Commissioner, Boden visits 51, advises Kidd on probies: "different generation, need extra guidance and patience, but we can still make great firefighters." Reveals he knows McCaffrey's father (Pensions Committee ranking member).

- **Tony**: Survives ammonia blast; dark humor about unfixed compartment door becoming his epitaph.
- **Boden**: Advises Kidd as senior leadership, confirms McCaffrey family clout.

## S15E04

# Chicago Fire S15E04 Summary

## Episode Plot
ATF agents Evans and Warren pull Captain Severide into an arson investigation at Cook County Judge Scanlon's home. The judge's 5-year-old daughter **Kaley** is missing; the babysitter died in the fire. ATF Special Agent **Calloway**—Severide's old colleague from Alabama—takes jurisdiction. The fire shows no clear origin point or V-pattern, the gas valve was warped but locked, and the babysitter's body was posed calmly in a chair, suggesting she was incapacitated before the fire.

A white Honda Civic fleeing the scene leads to a BOLO. The car is later found torched with a chlorine powder/diethyl ether accelerant. The "scales of justice" symbol left at the scene points to someone Scanlon prosecuted. Severide and Calloway trace the MO to **Anton Liddell** (2008 serial arsonist who paralyzed victims before burning them). Liddell, now in Pontiac Correctional, admits he had an accomplice: **David Palmer**, a body shop owner who supplied chemicals. Palmer's shop appears on the diethyl ether purchase list.

Palmer flees his shop, leading police to the **Elk Motel** (where Liddell was caught in 2008). Severide and Stella find Kaley alive in a motel room. Calloway confronts Palmer; a fire breaks out. Calloway dies pulling Severide out. Autopsies reveal both Palmer and the babysitter died of smoke inhalation with no drugs, no wounds, no struggle signs—meaning Palmer was incapacitated *before* the motel fire. Severide realizes Palmer was a fall guy; the real arsonist framed him to lure Calloway (the "worthy adversary" Liddell spoke of) and now has Severide in their sights. The symbol **"Nemesis"** (adversary) confirms the target was Calloway all along.

## Important Character Actions
- **Severide**: Leads the arson investigation alongside ATF; spots the posed body anomaly; connects the MO to Liddell; recruits Van Meter; rescues Kaley with Stella; survives the motel fire; deduces Palmer was framed and the real killer is still free.
- **Stella Kidd**: Finds and comforts Kaley at the Elk Motel; brings her to safety.
- **Calloway (ATF)**: Runs point on the case; brings Severide in; visits Liddell in prison; goes rogue to the Elk Motel to "finish it"; dies saving Severide.
- **Van Meter (ex-OFI chief)**: Provides the Liddell case details; identifies the paralytic MO; accompanies Severide to interview Liddell.
- **Liddell**: Admits an accomplice existed (Palmer) in exchange for a deal; recants when deal falls through.
- **Palmer**: Flees, destroys evidence at his shop; found dead at Elk Motel, posed like the babysitter.
- **Chief Pascal / Firehouse 51**: Support the investigation; Mouch handles SOP paperwork; Cruz's past injury referenced as reason Severide stayed at 51.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Nemesis** — the symbol/name left at the judge's house; Greek for "adversary," signaling the arsonist views Calloway/Severide as a worthy opponent.
- **Elk Motel** — where Liddell was arrested in 2008; where Palmer dies and Kaley is found.
- **Anton Liddell** — 2008 serial arsonist (paralytic + fire MO), imprisoned at Pontiac Correctional.
- **David Palmer** — Liddell's accomplice; body shop owner who supplied solvents; framed as the current arsonist.
- **Diethyl ether + chlorine powder** — chemical accelerant used in the getaway car fire.
- **Pontiac Correctional** — Illinois prison where Liddell is held.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Calloway and Severide worked together in **Alabama**; Van Meter was Severide's OFI boss and groomed him for leadership.
- Calloway was up for a **commendation/award** (most arson convictions nationally).
- The Liddell case (2008) was prosecuted by **Scanlon as ADA**, not judged by him—key investigative pivot.
- Stella and Severide have **48 hours off** at episode start, interrupted by the case.
- Mouch complains about **digital apparatus inspection logs** replacing paper.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes

- Calloway: "I didn't save my marriage" vs. Severide saving his — dark humor about their divergent paths.

## Unresolved Threads

- **"Nemesis" symbolism** implies an ongoing cat-and-mouse game; the arsonist seeks a "worthy adversary."

- **Liddell's true role** — he recanted the accomplice claim; unclear if he knows the current killer or was manipulated.

## S15E07

# Chicago Fire S15E07 Summary

## Episode Plot
Three intersecting storylines: (1) Violet's estranged mother arrives unannounced, revealing terminal illness (~1 year to live) and asking Violet to spend her remaining time in Boston; their confrontation exposes years of maternal pressure toward "respectable" careers and Violet's anger at being made to feel small. (2) An RV fire injures a child; investigation reveals the propane tank was deliberately tampered with (flathead screwdriver marks on pressure cap). A second explosion at a food truck (Windy City Pig) shows identical tampering. Four defective tanks were sold by a hardware store; the owner's nephew Robby "refurbished" empty tanks by jamming pressure caps, creating BLEVE risk. Two tanks remain unaccounted for—one tracked to a residential address, the other to Win-Pig LLC (the food truck's company card). (3) Novak's Prada boots are stolen; a stakeout using a baby monitor fails (monitor doesn't record). The "thief" turns out to be neighbor Grayson, who intercepted packages for safekeeping after another resident reported thefts—he's also persistently interested in Novak romantically.

- **Violet**: Avoids mother's calls; confronts mother about lifelong undermining; asserts she's happy as a paramedic; tends to mother's fall/medical episode at episode end; pressured to move to Boston.
- **Vasquez**: Loses temper with RV neighbor (dog guy) accusing Edgar of arson; reveals he lived in his car after father's sentencing; accepts Herrmann's coaching on controlling triggers.
- **Novak**: Organizes porch-pirate stakeout; discovers Grayson had her packages; learns Grayson has door-cam footage.
- **Mouch**: Runs stakeout with Novak; introduces her to *Columbo*; retrieves faulty tank from 1912 East Racine.
- **Carver**: Counsels Violet using Kelly/Benny parallel—hardest part is not making peace while parent is alive.
- **Severide/Burke**: At NIPSTA for certifications; due back in one shift.
