# Last Seen S01E05 "The Prophet" — Summary

## Episode Plot
Dual timeline. **15 years ago**: Henry Reid establishes his fundamentalist community on a farm near Port McKenna. His prodigal son Donald returns after hitting bottom (gambling, drinking, drugs, living in a car). Henry stages a public "return" ritual, forcing Donald to recommit. Jenna arrives—pregnant, not yet married to Donald—and Henry accepts them conditionally. The community runs on isolation, biblical literalism ("nature as classroom, Bible as only book"), and Henry's absolute authority.  
**Present day**: After Bill McCracken is murdered and Maggie is abducted (end of Ep 4), Ian and Debbie's marriage implodes. Debbie blames Ian entirely—his obsession, his drinking, his pushing Dean away. She declares she never wants to see him again. Ian, desperate, assaults Donald and forces him at gunpoint to drive to Henry's farm, believing Henry has Maggie.

## Important Character Actions
- **Henry**: Delivers "prodigal son" sermon reframed as daily choice to submit; humiliates Donald over shoddy workmanship; accepts Jenna but keeps control.
- **Donald**: Returns broken, tries to buy a fishing boat to escape; later relapses (drunk at feast), attacks Henry, is banished. In present, refuses to help Ian until forced.
- **Jenna**: Enters community pregnant; wins Henry's tentative approval by echoing his teachings; shows early signs of postpartum collapse (later timeline).
- **Ian**: Confronts Debbie with raw honesty—admits he blamed her for pushing Dean away, for his year of blindness drunk. She dismantles him: "There's no good place in you. Just bitterness and rage."
- **Debbie**: Final break. "I gave you so many chances… I blame you now. I never want to see you again."

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Port McKenna farm** — Henry's compound, inherited via Jenna's aunt.
- **"The Prophet"** — Episode title; refers to Henry's self-positioning.
- **Prodigal Son ritual** — Public re-indoctrination ceremony Henry uses to enforce loyalty.
- **Radishes / parsley confusion** — Light moment showing outsider ignorance of farm life.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Maggie vanished **11 years ago** (present day); the flashback is **15 years ago** (pre-Maggie, Sarah is a child).
- Donald's addiction cycle: left → hit bottom → returned → relapsed at feast → banished.
- Henry's rule: "No chemicals" (crops, medicine, life)—extends to rejecting modern interventions.
- Ian was a detective **before** becoming a 000 dispatcher; Bill was his friend/partner.
- Debbie and Ian have a son **Dean** (teenager) who was neglected during Ian's Maggie obsession.

## Jokes / References for Later Episodes
- **Playboy magazine** — "Except for the Playboy we found" (Donald/Henry teenage rebellion).
- **Fishing boat** — Donald's recurring escape fantasy; resurfaces as symbol of freedom he never takes.
- **"Nature as classroom, Bible as only book"** — Henry's catchphrase; ironic given his manipulative control.
- **Hand-tracing drawing** — Ian does this with Maggie (Ep 1 callback); later becomes evidence/connection.
- **000 dispatcher procedure** — Ian's professional instincts vs. fatherly panic.

## Unresolved Threads
1. **Where are Henry and Maggie?** Ian and Donald en route to farm; unknown if Maggie is there.
2. **Jenna's postpartum state** — Flash-forward shows her catatonic, refusing to surrender baby's body; cause/timing unclear.
3. **Donald's sobriety** — He's drunk during feast; Ian smells it. Can he stay clean under pressure?
4. **Debbie and baby** — Left alone; Dean tasked with watching them. Ian's final request: "Look after them."
5. **Henry's endgame** — Why take Maggie? Ideological "saving"? Leverage over Ian? Revenge on Donald?
6. **Sarah** — Henry and Jenna's daughter; last seen feeding chickens. Her role in abduction unknown.
