## Relationships
- **Ian ↔ Debbie**: Separated couple; share missing daughter Maggie (disappeared 11 years prior, age ~5–6 at time)
- **Ian ↔ Donald**: Friends; Donald is Henry's brother
- **Henry ↔ Jenna**: Married; fundamentalist/ideological household; daughter Sarah
- **Henry ↔ Donald**: Brothers
- **Ian → Maggie**: Obsessive refusal to accept her death; drives all action

## World Setting
- **Location**: Victoria, Australia (fictional town **Port McKenna** and surrounding bushland)
- **Time**: Present day (2026); Maggie vanished 11 years earlier (~2015)
- **Institutions**: **000** emergency service (Australia's 911/999 equivalent); Victoria Police
- **Atmosphere**: Bleak coastal/bush landscape; tight-knit community with buried secrets; prestige-drama gloss (Bonnie Elliott cinematography)

## Overall Plot Premise
Ian Ridley, a former detective turned 000 dispatcher, has spent 11 years refusing to accept his young daughter Maggie's disappearance as a closed case. When a child's remains are found in nearby bushland and, shortly after, a distress call comes in from a teenage girl he is convinced is Maggie, Ian forces his way into the investigation—jeopardizing his sobriety, his relationships, and the fragile lives of the Reid family next door. The series balances procedural mystery with raw psychological study of grief, guilt, and the stories families tell themselves to survive.

## Speaking Styles
- **Ian**: Terse, frayed, self-lacerating; oscillates between procedural clipped speech and raw outbursts; Australian vernacular ("shitter", "mate", "reckon") used naturally, not performatively.
- **Henry**: Measured, slow, cadenced; biblical undertones; silences weighted with threat.
- **Jenna**: Restrained, precise; emotion leaks through minimal words.
- **Donald**: Laconic, loyal, wary; working-class Aussie.
- **Debbie**: Sharp, exhausted, protective; walks line between anger and grief.
- **General**: Naturalistic Australian English—contractions, elisions, slang (bush, ute, servo, arvo) appear organically. Avoid over-translating colloquialisms; preserve rhythm.

## Cultural Context for Translation
- **First Apple TV+ series fully produced & filmed in Australia** (Victoria). Authentic locations, not "doubling".
- **000 vs 911**: Do not localize to 911; retain 000 with optional first-use note.
- **Indigenous presence**: Not a central theme in available sources; if referenced, use respectful terminology (Traditional Owners, Country).
- **Class/regional markers**: Accents reflect Victorian coastal/working-class milieu; subtitles should not flatten into generic "standard Chinese" but convey register differences (Ian/Donald vs Henry/Jenna).
- **Grief culture**: Australian "tall poppy" restraint—men especially suppress emotion until it fractures. Translation should mirror this repression, not amplify melodrama.
