# Slow Horses — Subtitle Translation Background

## Series Overview
- **Title**: Slow Horses (2022– )
- **Chinese Titles**: Mainland: 《流人》 | Taiwan: 《外放特務組》 | Hong Kong: 《翻盤特工隊》
- **Format**: Television series, British spy thriller / dark comedy
- **First Air**: 1 April 2022 on Apple TV+
- **Broadcaster**: Apple TV+ (global)
- **Structure**: 6 seasons to date (2022–2026), 6 episodes per season, ~40–55 min each
- **Source**: Mick Herron’s *Slough House* novel series (1 season ≈ 1 novel)
  - S1: *Slow Horses* | S2: *Dead Lions* | S3: *Real Tigers* | S4: *Spook Street* | S5: *London Rules* | S6: *Joe Country* + *Slough House*
- **Premise**: Disgraced MI5 agents are exiled to Slough House, a dingy London backwater department run by the vile but brilliant Jackson Lamb. Called “Slow Horses” (a pun on “Slough House”), they do mind-numbing paperwork — until real operations pull them back into the shadows.

## Key Relationships
- **Lamb ↔ River**: Antagonistic mentorship. Lamb mocks River’s pedigree but shields him; River resents then respects Lamb.
- **Lamb ↔ Standish**: Long-suffering loyalty. She runs the office; he pretends not to need her.
- **Lamb ↔ Taverner**: Cold war. Former colleagues; mutual loathing, forced cooperation. Lamb knows Taverner’s secrets (Charles Partner).
- **River ↔ David Cartwright**: Grandson/grandfather. David (“OB”) is River’s moral anchor; his dementia (S4+) drives emotional arc.
- **River ↔ Sid**: S1 romance/comradeship; her “death” haunts him; her return (S6) upends everything.
- **Louisa ↔ Min**: Ex-lovers, professional partners, bickering trust.
- **Roddy ↔ Everyone**: Parasitic brilliance. Mocked by all, indispensable to all.
- **The Dogs (MI5 Security) ↔ Slow Horses**: Institutional enemies. Webb/Duffy hunt them; Lamb outmanoeuvres them.

## Cultural Context for Translators
- **British class system**: Accents signal background (River’s posh vs. Shirley’s Estuary). Preserve via register, not phonetic spelling.
- **MI5 vs. MI6**: MI5 = domestic (The Park); MI6 = foreign (The River House). Never conflate.
- **“Slow Horse” pun**: Only works in English. In Chinese, 《流人》 (“drifters/exiles”) captures the *status*; add translator note if “slow horse” dialogue appears.
- **Historical references**: Charles Partner (Lamb’s dead colleague), 1970s/80s Cold War ops — keep names, footnote if obscure.
- **London geography**: Regent’s Park, Islington, Aldersgate, Slough (Berkshire) — verify locations; don’t auto-translate place names.
- **Season-long arcs**: Each season adapts one novel; plot threads (Partner, David’s dementia, Taverner’s ambition) span seasons. Maintain consistency.
