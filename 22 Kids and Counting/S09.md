## S09E02

## Episode S09E02 Summary

### Plot

The episode weaves together three parallel storylines about stepping outside one's comfort zone. Sue discovers holiday photos that reveal she has gained significant weight — peaking at 10 stone 13 after 22 pregnancies — and decides to go public on social media about her weight-loss journey. Six months of treadmill, swimming, and gym work later, she loses three stone and hits her target, but faces a wave of online accusations claiming she used weight-loss jabs (Mounjaro). She ultimately records a video response refusing to disclose her methods, choosing privacy and setting an example for her daughters instead.

Sophie, separated from husband Joe for about a year, is raising three children (Daisy, April, Leo) alone while working 10–12 hour shifts. Feeling like a failure, she takes up endurance training under coach Stephen for an 8K multi-station competition in Glasgow. Running proves her Achilles heel — heart rate hitting 179, knee pain, and near-quit episodes — but her kids rally for a night training session and she turns a corner (5K in 25 min). At the Glasgow event against 20,000 athletes, the whole family secretly makes the three-hour trip. Despite nausea and nerves, she completes the challenge.

Aimee, the homebody daughter who is the oldest girl still living at home, is compared throughout to her slightly-older sister Ellie, who has moved out with boyfriend Steven and travels constantly (Norway, Poland). Noel and Chloe orchestrate a blindfold pin-on-a-map exercise that lands Aimee on York. She takes the TransPennine Express alone, befriends strangers on a ghost tour, sings karaoke, and returns transformed. At her 20th birthday "pretty in pink" party, she introduces mystery boyfriend John, stunning the family.

### Important Character Actions

- **Sue** publicly posts her weight-loss goal on social media, loses 3 stone over six months, then records a defiant video response to online critics.
- **Sophie** commits to a Glasgow endurance competition as a post-separation redemption arc; her children join a wet evening training session; Noel secretly brings the family to Glasgow.
- **Aimee** accepts a blindfold pin challenge, travels solo to York for the first time ever, and reveals boyfriend John at her birthday party.
- **Noel** drives the "secret support" logistics for Sophie's competition and coaches Aimee to "start small."
- **Chloe** suggests Sophie try physical challenges; also proposes dating apps (rejected) and a hairstyle change.

## 剧情概要

三条主线交织：遗产、阅读、和解。Sue 刚满 50 岁，决定为全家“把账理顺”：请房产中介 Callum 评估房子、请人对馅饼厂估值，并首次立遗嘱。Noel 一贯回避这个话题——最难的是馅饼厂归属：Chloe 和 Luke 正在经营，卖了他们会失业，分给谁又怕引发手足之争。与此同时，大扫除暴露出全家几乎没人再读书，Tilly 借“世界读书日”发起挑战：孩子们每人在节前读完一本书，Sue 则必须扮成孩子们选的角色并全天穿戏服——他们选了蚱蜢。最大的情感线来自 Noel：他的母亲 Val 的二婚丈夫 Clive（Noel 中学英语老师）去世，Val 的骨灰六年来一直被 Clive 留在自家沙发后面，如今终于可以处理后事——但遗嘱揭开一个残酷真相。

## S09E04

## Episode Plot

Two storylines run in parallel. Noel travels to the Isle of Mull with his best friend Jamie Walker so Jamie can climb Ben More (3,169 ft, the highest peak in the Inner Hebrides) in memory of his daughter Sophie, who died of a rare kidney cancer at 17 ahead of her second anniversary. Back in Morecambe, Sue — suddenly the family's only chauffeur while Noel is away — secretly buys a small learner car and tries to teach Max to drive, with predictably chaotic results.

## Important Character Actions

- Jamie and Rebecca Walker are the Radfords' long-time friends; their daughter Sophie Walker died aged 17, and daughter Ella inherited Sophie's Beatles love after Sophie played her "Hey Jude." The families first meet in Liverpool (Mersey barge trip, girls shopping, Noel and Jamie taking kids to the Beatles Museum) before Noel drives 250+ miles to Oban and ferries over to Mull.
- On Mull, practitioner Zoe ropes Noel and Jamie into forest bathing, tree-hugging, and meditation — a setting where Jamie opens up about still feeling Sophie "in another dimension / spirit world."
- They visit a naturist sauna run by Donna; Jamie and Noel jump into freezing water, endure a recorded mindfulness track voiced by "Guru Sue," and finish with a Scottish water fight.

Boggy ground, constant steepness, and a black rain/sleet cloud roll in; with darkness only two hours away, Jamie finally accepts they must turn back short of the summit. They console themselves that Sophie "would laugh at the pair of failures" and christen themselves "bad-weather friends."
- Sue juggles drops for Aimee, Max, and Tillie, secretly haggles a 1.2-litre car from £2,995 to £2,650 (sweetened with three Radford's pies), has it customised in neon blue with a fire stripe, and tries to teach Max. Max floors the throttle ("I think it's on fire"), swings into the wrong lane, and Sue's nerves snap — she declares he needs a proper instructor.
- The episode closes on Chloe crying while holding a wailing Heidie, admitting she "may be struggling with post-natal depression."
