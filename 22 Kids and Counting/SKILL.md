## 2. Premise

Cameras follow Britain's largest family, the Radfords of Morecambe, Lancashire, as they navigate life with 22 children (ranging from newborn Heidie to adult Chris) and, in later seasons, a growing number of grandchildren. From Series 1 onward, Sue and Noel have stated they are not having more children of their own; the "22" in the title has remained fixed even as older children begin their own families, with grandkids covered under the "…and Counting" portion of the title. Episodes track milestones: birthdays, school runs, the family bakery, home renovations, holidays, weddings, new babies, bereavements, and the everyday logistics of a 10-bedroom former care home packed with people and pets.

## 4. Relationships

- Noel and Sue — married couple, the central parents. Address each other by first name; children call them Dad/Mum (爸爸妈妈) or by first name on camera.
- Sue's mother / Noel's mother — occasionally referenced as "Nan" / "Grandma" (奶奶/外婆).
- The married-in spouses (Nicole, Joe Broadley, Jacob Wallace, Harley Passmore, Connor Carter) are referred to as 儿媳/女婿 or by first name.
- Older siblings act as second-parents to younger ones — recurring framing as "big brother"/"big sister" (哥哥/姐姐).
- "Auntie"/"Uncle" (阿姨/叔叔) is used for an older sibling-of-parent figure when grandchildren arrive.

## 5. World Setting

- Home: a converted 10-bedroom former care home in Morecambe, Lancashire (兰开夏郡莫克姆镇).
- Business: Radford's Pie Company, also known as Faraday's (拉德福德馅饼公司 / 法拉第馅饼店), a bakery in nearby Heysham (希舍姆), acquired 1999. Episodes frequently feature pie-making, deliveries, and the shop front.
- Locale cues: northern English working-class seaside setting; the family are practising Christians and attend a local church; children attend a mix of state schools plus home education; family holidays often feature UK caravan parks ("caravan holiday" 房车度假) and European trips.
- Cultural context: frequently compared in UK press to "Britain's Duggars" (the US 19 Kids and Counting family). Do not translate that comparison literally — keep it as "英国的杜加尔家" only with a brief gloss on first mention, otherwise prefer the show's own framing of "Britain's biggest family" (英国最大的家庭).

## 7. Speaking Style and Register

- Noel's voice: warm, broad northern English (Lancashire) accent, working-class register, frequent use of "love" / "lass" as endearments. Render with colloquial Mandarin that preserves warmth — e.g. 亲爱的、姑娘.
- Sue's voice: pragmatic, organising, joking — direct and warm; calls Noel "dad" when the kids are speaking and addresses him by first name otherwise.
- Children: distinct age-graded voices from toddler babble to teen slang; older kids adopt YouTuber/social-media register ("lit", "savage", "OMG") — keep modern Mandarin equivalents (超棒、笑死、我的天) without over-translating.
- Northern English dialect: "mam", "nowt", "summat", "ta", "aye" — keep as standard Mandarin (妈妈、没什么、什么、谢谢、对) unless quoting; do not anglicise dialect words into Mandarin slang.
- Narrator: Sally Lindsay delivers warm, observational voiceover in standard received pronunciation — translate as standard Mandarin narrative voice.
- Recurring emotional beats: "it's overwhelming but we love it" (累并快乐着), "another mouth to feed" (又多一张嘴), "the kids are having kids" (孩子们也开始生娃了), "it's a lot but we make it work" (事情很多，但我们撑得住).

## 8. Cultural Context

- The show is a successor to the Channel 4 strand 14 Kids and Counting → 15/16/17/18/19/20 Kids and Counting. After 20 Kids, the family moved to Channel 5 and the format changed in Series 1 of 22 Kids and Counting (no new Radford babies of Noel's and Sue's planned, but Sue still appears in many episode storylines).
- The number 22 in the title refers strictly to Sue's biological children; grandchildren are added under the "…and Counting" rubric.
- UK reality-TV convention: observational, light touch, no dramatisation. On-screen text is in British English; measurements in metric (pounds sterling £, miles, °C); dates as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Religion: family is Christian; church attendance is shown; prayers at mealtimes are referenced ("grace" 饭前祷告). Do not editorialize.
- The family has been the subject of UK press scrutiny (large family, state benefits, social-media income). On-screen, however, the tone stays positive and family-focused. Subtitles should match that on-screen tone.

## 9. Translation Notes

- On-screen names: keep the on-screen English spelling in subtitles (e.g. "Heidie" not "Heidi") and add Chinese in parentheses only on first appearance per episode.
- "Counting" in the title: if forced to translate the series title for marketing, "22个娃还在生" is the established British press translation; otherwise keep English title verbatim per Channel 5 branding.
- Babies/children: use 直系关系 + 名字 (e.g. "苏的女儿克洛伊") on first mention to disambiguate among 22 children.
- Death of Alfie: handle with "夭折" or "早逝" depending on tone; do not say "stillborn" in dialogue unless spoken.
- English currency: £ → 英镑; sums over £1,000 may be rendered as "上千英镑".
- Do not anglicise place names in dialogue (Morecambe 莫克姆, Heysham 希舍姆, Lancashire 兰开夏); but on-screen location captions may keep English.
- Numbers in dialogue: spell out small numbers (一、二、三…) in running dialogue; keep digits in addresses, dates, phone numbers.

## 家庭背景

Noel和Sue均在婴儿时期被收养，童年相识。Sue 14岁时怀上第一个孩子，两人在Sue 17岁、Noel 21岁时结婚。全家住在莫克姆一栋改建的前护理院中。

家庭经营"Radford's Pie Company"（拉德福德馅饼公司），位于海舍姆 (Heysham)，1999年收购。

## 节目沿革

该家庭最早出现在2012年Channel 4纪录片《15 Kids and Counting》中，随后逐年拍摄更新版：《16 Kids and Counting》、《17 Kids and Counting》……直至《22 Kids and Counting》。2023年起转至Channel 5，成为正式多季系列。节目标题中的数字不再增加，因Sue决定不再生育，"and Counting"部分涵盖了孙辈的出生。
